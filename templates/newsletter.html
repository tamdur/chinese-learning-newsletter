台積電宣布在日本興建第三座晶圓廠

來源：Hacker News

台積電今天宣布，計畫在日本熊本縣興建第三座晶圓廠。這座新工廠預計二〇二八年開始生產，主要製造先進的二奈米晶片。

日本政府會提供大約五千億日圓的補助。熊本縣知事說，這是讓日本重新成為半導體大國的重要一步。

分析師認為，台積電選日本不只是因為補助，也是為了讓供應鏈更多元，不要太集中在一個地方。

TSMC announced today it plans to build a third wafer fabrication plant in Kumamoto Prefecture, Japan. The new factory is expected to start production in 2028, primarily manufacturing advanced 2-nanometer chips.

The Japanese government will provide approximately 500 billion yen in subsidies. The Kumamoto governor called it "an important step toward making Japan a semiconductor powerhouse again."

Analysts believe TSMC chose Japan not just for the subsidies, but also to diversify its supply chain and avoid over-concentration in one location.

聯準會維持利率不變，暗示年底前可能降息

來源：Financial Times

美國聯準會今天開會後決定，維持目前的利率不變。不過主席鮑威爾在記者會上說，如果通貨膨脹繼續下降，今年底以前「很有可能」會降息。

市場聽到消息後反應很正面，美國股市馬上漲了不少。經濟學家說，聯準會這次的態度比大家預期的更溫和。

The U.S. Federal Reserve decided to hold interest rates steady after its meeting today. However, Chair Jerome Powell said at the press conference that rate cuts are "very possible" before year-end if inflation continues to decline.

Markets reacted positively to the news, with U.S. stocks rising significantly. Economists said the Fed struck a more moderate tone than most had expected.

密西根大學籃球隊闖進八強

來源：The Athletic

密西根大學男子籃球隊昨天晚上以七十八比七十五險勝對手，成功闖進全國八強賽。比賽最後十秒非常刺激，密西根靠一記三分球逆轉了比賽。

教練賽後說：「我們的球員在壓力最大的時候表現最好，這就是密西根的精神。」球迷們都非常開心。

The University of Michigan men's basketball team narrowly defeated their opponent 78-75 last night, advancing to the Elite Eight. The final 10 seconds were electrifying — Michigan hit a three-pointer to take the lead.

"Our players perform best under the most pressure — that's the Michigan spirit," the coach said after the game. Fans were thrilled.

科學家發現一種新的深海魚類

來源：Nature

一組日本科學家在太平洋海底將近八千公尺深的地方，發現了一種從來沒見過的魚。這種魚的身體是透明的，可以承受非常大的水壓。

研究團隊說，深海還有很多我們不知道的生物。這次的發現讓科學家對深海生態更加好奇。

A team of Japanese scientists discovered a never-before-seen species of fish nearly 8,000 meters deep on the Pacific Ocean floor. The fish has a transparent body and can withstand extremely high water pressure.

The research team said there are still many deep-sea creatures we know nothing about. This discovery has made scientists even more curious about deep-sea ecosystems.

太空旅行不再是夢想

來源：Ars Technica

一家太空公司宣布，明年開始賣太空旅行的票，讓一般人也有機會到太空看地球。一張票大約要兩百萬美元，飛行時間大約九十分鐘。

雖然價格還是很貴，但跟十年前比已經便宜很多了。專家說，再過幾年，太空旅行可能會像搭飛機一樣普通。

A space company announced it will start selling space travel tickets next year, giving ordinary people the chance to see Earth from space. A ticket costs roughly two million US dollars, with a flight time of about 90 minutes.

While the price is still steep, it's much cheaper than it was ten years ago. Experts say that in a few more years, space travel could become as routine as catching a flight.
