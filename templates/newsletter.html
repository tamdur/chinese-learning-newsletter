台積電宣布在日本興建第三座晶圓廠

來源：Hacker News / 科技

台積電今天宣布，計畫在日本熊本縣興建第三座晶圓廠。這座新工廠預計在二〇二八年開始生產，主要製造先進的二奈米晶片。

日本政府表示，會提供大約五千億日圓的補助。熊本縣知事說，這是「讓日本重新成為半導體大國的重要一步」。

分析師認為，台積電選擇日本，不只是因為補助，也是為了讓供應鏈更多元（duōyuán，多樣化的意思），不要太集中在一個地方。

TSMC announced today plans to build a third wafer fabrication plant in Kumamoto Prefecture, Japan. The new factory is expected to begin production in 2028, primarily manufacturing advanced 2-nanometer chips.

The Japanese government indicated it would provide approximately 500 billion yen in subsidies. The Kumamoto prefectural governor called it "an important step toward making Japan a semiconductor powerhouse again."

Analysts believe TSMC chose Japan not only because of subsidies but also to diversify its supply chain and avoid over-concentration in one location.

聯準會維持利率不變，暗示年底前可能降息

來源：Financial Times / 經濟

美國聯準會今天開會後決定維持目前的利率不變。但是主席鮑威爾在記者會上說，如果通貨膨脹繼續下降，今年底以前「很有可能」會降息。

市場聽到這個消息後反應很正面，美國股市馬上漲了不少。經濟學家說，聯準會這次比大家預期的更「鴿派」（gēpài，意思是比較傾向寬鬆政策）。

The U.S. Federal Reserve decided to hold interest rates steady after its meeting today. However, Chair Jerome Powell said at the press conference that rate cuts are "very possible" before year-end if inflation continues to decline.

Markets reacted positively to the news, with U.S. stocks rising significantly. Economists said the Fed struck a more "dovish" tone — meaning more inclined toward loose monetary policy — than most had expected.

北極海冰面積創四十年來新低紀錄

來源：Carbon Brief / 氣候

最新的衛星資料顯示，今年冬天北極海冰的面積是一九七九年有紀錄以來最小的。科學家說，這跟全球暖化有直接的關係。

海冰減少會影響很多事情。首先，住在北極的動物，像是北極熊，會失去牠們生活的地方。其次，海冰能反射陽光，海冰少了，海水吸收更多熱量，讓暖化更快——科學家叫這個「正回饋」（zhèng huíkuì）。

The latest satellite data shows that Arctic sea ice extent this winter reached its lowest level since records began in 1979. Scientists say this is directly linked to global warming.

The decline in sea ice has far-reaching consequences. Arctic animals like polar bears lose critical habitat. Additionally, since sea ice reflects sunlight, less ice means the ocean absorbs more heat, accelerating warming — a process scientists call "positive feedback."

新研究：每天走路三十分鐘可以大幅降低心臟病風險

來源：Nature / 健康

一項新的大型研究發現，每天走路三十分鐘的人，得心臟病的機率比完全不運動的人低了將近百分之四十。

研究團隊追蹤了超過十萬人，時間長達十年。有趣的是，走路的速度快慢影響不大，重要的是有沒有養成每天走路的習慣。

A major new study found that people who walk for 30 minutes daily have nearly 40% lower risk of heart disease compared to those who are completely sedentary.

The research team tracked over 100,000 people for ten years. Interestingly, walking speed made little difference — what mattered was developing the daily walking habit.

密西根大學籃球隊驚險擊敗乙級強隊，晉級八強

來源：The Athletic / 運動

密西根大學男子籃球隊昨天晚上以七十八比七十五險勝對手，成功晉級全國八強賽。比賽最後十秒非常刺激，密西根靠一個三分球逆轉了比賽。

教練賽後說：「我們的球員在壓力最大的時候表現最好，這就是密西根的精神。」

The University of Michigan men's basketball team narrowly defeated their opponent 78-75 last night, advancing to the Elite Eight. The final 10 seconds were electrifying — Michigan hit a three-pointer to take the lead.

"Our players perform best under the most pressure — that's the Michigan spirit," the coach said after the game.
