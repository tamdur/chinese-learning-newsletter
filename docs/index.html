拉夫羅夫來到亞特蘭大，G20取消大合照

來源：CNN、The Hill

美國今年主辦二十國集團（G20），10月30日在亞特蘭大召開外交部長會議，由國務卿魯比歐主持。最受注意的是俄羅斯外長拉夫羅夫，他是俄國2022年攻打烏克蘭以來，到美國訪問的最高層官員。

歐洲國家不想跟拉夫羅夫一起拍照，還說可能離場，美國只好取消傳統的大合照。德國外長瓦德富爾說：「跟俄國，不能像平常一樣。」

拉夫羅夫說，他和魯比歐短短談了一下，感覺「還不錯」。這場會議是在為12月的邂阿密領袖高峰會做準備。

The United States is hosting the Group of 20 (G20) this year. On October 30, a foreign ministers' meeting was held in Atlanta, chaired by Secretary of State Marco Rubio.

The most closely watched attendee was Russian Foreign Minister Sergei Lavrov, the highest-ranking Russian official to visit the United States since Russia invaded Ukraine in 2022.

European countries did not want to pose for photos with Lavrov and even threatened to walk out, so the United States had no choice but to cancel the traditional group photo.

German Foreign Minister Johann Wadephul said, "With Russia, things cannot be business as usual."

Lavrov said he had a brief chat with Rubio and that it went "pretty well."

The meeting was meant to lay the groundwork for the leaders' summit in Miami in December.

以色列大選沒人過半，阿拉伯政黨成關鍵

來源：CNN、NPR、Al Jazeera

以色列10月27日大選，票已經全部開完，但沒有一方拿到國會120席中過半的61席。總理內塔尼亞胡的陣營拿下53席，对手艾橇科特的陣營拿下48席。

阿拉伯政黨拿下創紀錄的19席，成了關鍵。艾橇科特說，他絕不會加入內塔尼亞胡的政府；如果阿拉伯政黨在國會投票支持他，就有機會過半。不過他選前說過，不和阿拉伯政黨一起執政。

總統赫佐格預計11月4日以後和各黨見面，再決定請誰組政府。如果談不成，以色列可能要再選一次。

Israel's general election on October 27 is over and every ballot has been counted, but no bloc won the 61 seats out of 120 in the Knesset needed for a majority. Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu's bloc took 53 seats, while Gadi Eisenkot's bloc took 48. The Arab parties won a record 19 seats, which makes them the kingmakers.

Eisenkot has said he will never join Netanyahu's government. If the Arab parties vote for him in the Knesset, though, he could reach a majority. Before the election, however, he said he would not govern together with the Arab parties.

President Isaac Herzog is expected to meet with the parties after November 4 and then decide whom to ask to form a government. If no agreement can be reached, Israel may have to hold another election.
