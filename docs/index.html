伊利諾州初選：參議員大戰結果揭曉

來源：Chicago Tribune

伊利諾州三月十七日舉行了初選，這是將近四十年來第一次有空出的聯邦參議員位子，因為老參議員 Durbin 要退休了。

民主黨共有十一個人參加，最大的三位是眾議員 Krishnamoorthi（他募了三千萬美元）、副州長 Stratton，還有眾議員 Kelly。移民問題也成為重要話題。提前投票人數也創了新高，結果在選舉當晚還在計算中。不過伊利諾是深藍州，民主黨的人十一月大選應該也會贏。

Illinois held its primary election on March 17th. For the first time in nearly forty years, there was an open federal Senate seat, because longtime Senator Durbin is retiring.

Eleven Democrats ran in the primary, with the three frontrunners being Representative Krishnamoorthi (who raised $30 million), Lieutenant Governor Stratton, and Representative Kelly. Immigration emerged as a major issue in the race. Early voting numbers set new records, and results were still being counted on election night. However, Illinois is a deep blue state, so the Democratic winner is expected to win the general election in November.

CERN 發現新的重粒子

來源：CERN / Phys.org

歐洲的大型粒子對撞機（就是讓很小的東西互相撞的機器）找到了一種新粒子，叫做 Ξcc⁺。這個粒子跟質子很像，但是重了大約四倍。

二十多年前，美國有一個實驗說找到過它，但別人一直沒辦法重做出來，結果吵了很久。現在 CERN 的團隊用新的機器確認了它的存在，這也是研究強力（把原子裡面的東西黏在一起的力）的重要一步。來自二十多國的一千多位科學家參加了這個計畫。

The Large Hadron Collider at CERN in Europe (a machine that smashes tiny things together) has discovered a new particle called Ξcc⁺. This particle is similar to a proton, but about four times heavier.

More than twenty years ago, an American experiment claimed to have found it, but other scientists couldn't reproduce the results, and the disagreement went on for a long time. Now the CERN team has confirmed its existence using new equipment. This is an important step in studying the strong force (the force that holds stuff together inside atoms). Over a thousand scientists from more than twenty countries participated in this project.

最高法院擋下 Trump 驅逐移民計畫

來源：SCOTUSblog

美國最高法院暫時擋下了 Trump 政府的一個計畫。這個計畫要取消大約三十五萬海地人和六千敘利亞人的「臨時保護身分」（TPS），也就是讓他們留在美國工作的許可。

下面的法官說，政府取消的理由可能是對非白人移民的不友好。最高法院決定直接受理這個案子，結果大概六月底會出來。

The U.S. Supreme Court has temporarily blocked a plan by the Trump administration. The plan would have revoked "Temporary Protected Status" (TPS) for approximately 350,000 Haitians and 6,000 Syrians — permits that allow them to live and work in the United States.

A lower court judge found that the government's stated reasons for revoking the status may have been hostile toward non-white immigrants. The Supreme Court decided to take the case directly, with a decision expected around late June.

Kagi 小網站搜尋推出手機版

來源：Hacker News

Kagi 是一個要付費的搜尋工具，沒有廣告。他們最近推出了「小網站」的手機 app，想法很有意思：他們收集了大約六千到三萬個真正由個人寫的網站，像是個人部落格和獨立創作者的網站。

為什麼要這樣做？因為現在網路上到處都是大公司做的內容，真正有意思的小網站很難被找到。這個消息也在 Hacker News 上得到很多人的注意。

Kagi, a paid search tool without ads, has recently launched a mobile app for "Small Web" — an interesting initiative. They've curated a collection of approximately 6,000 to 30,000 websites that are genuinely written by individuals, such as personal blogs and independent creator sites.

Why take this approach? Because the internet is now saturated with content produced by large corporations, making it difficult to discover truly interesting small websites. This development has also garnered significant attention on Hacker News.

Boyd 當選小熊隊開幕戰先發

來源：Chicago Tribune

小熊隊教練 Counsell 宣布，三月二十六日在 Wrigley 球場對華盛頓的開幕戰，先發投手是 Matthew Boyd。Boyd 今年三十五歲，去年表現超好：十四勝八敗，在主場更是十二勝一敗。

球隊也有一些傷的消息：鈴木誠也在 WBC 受傷，王牌 Steele 也要到五六月才回來。不過好消息是一月簽了 Bregman，五年一億七千五百萬的大約。小熊隊今年被看好是強隊！

Cubs manager Counsell announced that Matthew Boyd will be the starting pitcher for the opening game on March 26th at Wrigley Field against Washington. Boyd is thirty-five years old this year and had an outstanding performance last year: fourteen wins and eight losses, with an impressive twelve wins and just one loss at home.

The team has some injury news: Suzuki Seiya was injured during the WBC, and ace Steele won't return until May or June. However, there's good news — they signed Bregman in January on a five-year, $175 million contract. The Cubs are expected to be a strong contender this year!
