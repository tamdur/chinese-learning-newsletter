密西根作客奧勒岡，先發四分衛還沒決定

來源：ESPN、On3

密西根大學美式足球隊星期六要到奧勒岡的奧森球場（Autzen Stadium）作客。密西根上週以6比15輸給密西根州立大學，戰績掉到5勝4敗。排名第18的奧勒岡是6勝3敗，被看好會贏10分以上。

輸球後，總教練惠廷漢（Kyle Whittingham）開除了進攻教練貝克。誰當先發四分衛（負責傳球的球員），他只說「還沒決定」。消息指出，卡爾（Tommy Carr）可能先上場，安德伍德（Bryce Underwood）也會有機會。

如果再輸，密西根今年打進季後賽的希望就更小了。

Michigan's football team travels to Autzen Stadium on Saturday to play Oregon. Last week Michigan lost 6-15 to Michigan State, dropping its record to 5-4. Oregon, ranked 18th, is 6-3 and is favored to win by more than 10 points.

After the loss, head coach Kyle Whittingham fired offensive coordinator Beck. When asked who will start at quarterback, he would only say the decision has not been made yet. Reports suggest Tommy Carr could get the first chance, with Bryce Underwood also expected to get playing time.

If Michigan loses again, its hopes of reaching the postseason this year will become even slimmer.
