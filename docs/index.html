合約外洩：四大AI公司都在幫美軍做事

來源：The Intercept

美國新聞網站The Intercept打官司拿到一批國防部文件，發現OpenAI、Anthropic、Google和馬斯克的xAI，每家公司都簽下最高兩億美元的合約。

合約去年七月就簽了，內容是替美軍做「幫忙下決定」的AI工具，公司還要教軍人使用，並預測自己的技術有什麼風險。

這些事不是公司自己公布的，是文件外洩之後才被看到。

The U.S. news outlet The Intercept obtained Defense Department documents through litigation, revealing that OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and Elon Musk's xAI each signed contracts worth up to $200 million.

These contracts were signed last July and involve building AI tools to "help with decision-making" for the U.S. military. The companies are also responsible for training military personnel to use them and evaluating potential risks of their technology.

The companies did not disclose any of this themselves—it only became public after the documents were leaked.

歐洲央行升息，美國卻可能降息

來源：CNBC

歐洲央行今天開會，市場幾乎百分之百確定它會升息，把利率從百分之二點二五提高到百分之二點五。

原因是八月歐元區的物價比去年高了百分之三點三，其中能源更漲了百分之十四點三。波斯灣的戰爭讓油價變貴，這是最大的推手。

有趣的是，美國因為新工作機會幾乎掛零，反而可能降息。同一場戰爭，把兩大央行推往相反的方向。

The European Central Bank meets today, and markets are nearly certain it will raise rates, increasing them from 2.25% to 2.5%. The driver is inflation: Eurozone prices in August were 3.3% higher than last year, with energy up 14.3%. The Persian Gulf conflict has driven up oil prices—the main culprit. Here's the irony: the US, facing nearly flat job creation, may instead cut rates. The same conflict is pushing the two central banks in opposite directions.

川普喊話：共和黨贏，每人發五千美元

來源：ABC News

共和黨在德州達拉斯開了史上第一次「期中選舉大會」。川普上台說，只要共和黨十一月保住國會，就發給每個美國大人五千美元，他叫它「川普紅利」。

錢從哪裡來？他說用關稅收到的錢。可是專家算過，這要花掉大約一點三兆美元，而美國政府一年的總預算大約七兆。從去年到現在，關稅收入還不到五千億美元。

Republicans held their first-ever "midterm election convention" in Dallas, Texas. Trump took the stage and announced that if the party keeps control of Congress in November, he'll give every American adult five thousand dollars—a proposal he's branded the "Trump Bonus." The inevitable question: where does the money come from? Trump says tariff revenues. But experts have run the numbers, and there's a significant problem: this plan would cost roughly 1.3 trillion dollars, while the entire U.S. government's annual budget is about 7 trillion. To make matters worse, tariff revenue over the past year hasn't even reached 500 billion dollars.

DeepMind畫出九十億種DNA變化的地圖

來源：Google DeepMind

Google旗下的DeepMind推出「AlphaGenome Atlas」，用AI預測人類DNA裡九十億種可能的單一字母改變，各會造成什麼影響。學術研究可以免費使用。

這些改變大多從來沒在病人身上出現過，也沒人做過實驗。醫生看罕見疾病（很少人得的病）的基因報告時，常常只能寫「意義不明」，現在至少有個參考。

問題是：沒驗證過的預測，看病時該信幾分？

DeepMind, owned by Google, has launched "AlphaGenome Atlas," an AI system that predicts how 9 billion possible single-letter variations in human DNA might affect health. Academic researchers can access it for free.

Most of these genetic changes have never been observed in patients, and none have been experimentally validated. When doctors review genetic reports for rare diseases, they typically can only note "unclear significance"—now they at least have a reference tool to consult.

The key question remains: how much weight should doctors give to predictions that haven't been verified in clinical practice?

賭盤翻臉：密西根在主場反而不被看好

來源：VegasInsider

星期六密西根大學在自家球場迎戰奧克拉荷馬，現場預計超過十萬七千人。

原因很直接：密西根上週靠最後一秒的長傳，才以十三比十二險勝西密西根；奧克拉荷馬卻以五十一比零大勝。記者投票幾乎沒動，錢卻很誠實。

On Saturday, the University of Michigan faces Oklahoma at home, with over 107,000 fans expected. The betting line initially favored Michigan by 2.5 points, but it has completely flipped. Now Oklahoma, the visiting team, is favored by 5.5 to 6.5 points—a 9-point swing in just days. The reason is simple: Michigan barely escaped last week against Western Michigan with a last-second Hail Mary, winning 13-12, while Oklahoma crushed its opponent 51-0. Sports journalists' rankings barely moved, but the money doesn't lie.
