阿拉伯八國指責以色列，庫許納趕赴開羅救和平計畫

來源：CNN

美國特使庫許納八月十六日飛到埃及開羅，見了塞西總統，想救回卡住的加薩和平計畫。他也和哈瑪斯的代表見了面。

同一天，埃及、沙烏地阿拉伯、卡達、約旦、土耳其等八個國家的外交部長一起發表聲明，說以色列不接受這份計畫，是在直接打擊美國帶頭的和平努力，責任要由以色列來負。

那一天，以色列的空襲還在加薩繼續。

U.S. envoy Kushner flew to Cairo on August 16th to meet with President Sisi and attempt to salvage the stalled Gaza peace plan. He also met with Hamas representatives.

On the same day, foreign ministers from eight countries — Egypt, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Jordan, Turkey and others — issued a joint statement asserting that Israel's rejection of the plan constitutes a direct attack on U.S.-led peace efforts, placing full responsibility on Israel.

That same day, Israeli airstrikes continued over Gaza.

美國新規定：聯邦不再出錢付未成年變性醫療

來源：NPR / LGBTQ Nation

美國政府八月十六日確定一條新規定：聯邦的醫療補助和兒童健保，以後不能拿來付未成年人的變性醫療，包括延後發育的藥、荷爾蒙和手術。

新規定十月十三日開始生效。已經在用藥的孩子最多有六個月的緩衝期，讓醫生慢慢把藥減掉。

這兩項計畫是聯邦和各州一起出錢的。各州現在只剩兩條路：停掉這項給付，或是自己全額買單。

On August 16th, the U.S. government finalized a new rule: federal Medicaid and children's health insurance can no longer be used to cover gender transition medical care for minors, including puberty-blocking medications, hormone treatments, and surgery.

The new rule takes effect on October 13th. Children already on these medications have a grace period of up to six months, allowing doctors to gradually discontinue the treatment.

These two programs are jointly funded by the federal government and individual states. States now have only two options: eliminate this coverage, or fund it entirely on their own.

比特幣大跌，挖礦公司股價卻飛上天

來源：24/7 Wall St.

今年比特幣跌了百分之四十四，挖礦公司的股價卻漲了百分之四十三到一百五十二。原因不在挖礦，在人工智慧。

這些公司有廠房、有電力，正好是人工智慧公司最缺的東西。Riot 和一家人工智慧實驗室簽了二十年租約，資料中心的合約總額衝到九十八億美元；Core Scientific 上一季出租場地的收入，已經是挖礦收入的五倍。

分析師現在看的不再是算力（電腦計算的速度），而是簽了多少合約、拉到多少電。

Bitcoin has fallen 44% this year, yet mining company stocks have soared 43% to 152%. The reason has nothing to do with mining — it's artificial intelligence.

These firms possess exactly what AI companies need most: facilities and power. Riot signed a twenty-year lease with an AI lab, driving its data-center contracts to $9.8 billion in total; Core Scientific's site-rental revenue last quarter was already five times its mining revenue.

Analysts are now tracking not hash power (raw computing speed) but signed contracts and secured electricity capacity.

日本第二季成長不到預期一半，央行升息計畫生變

來源：RTTNews / 日本內閣府

日本內閣府八月十七日公布，第二季經濟成長年率只有百分之一點一，不到市場預期百分之二的一半。

問題出在國內。民眾消費幾乎沒動，企業投資還掉了百分之一點二，撐住數字的是出口。

可是物價沒有降下來。日本央行本來打算九月升息，現在得先想清楚：升息會不會把本來就很冷的消費，打得更冷。

Japan's Cabinet Office announced on August 17 that second-quarter economic growth came in at just 1.1 percent annualized — less than half the market's expectation of 2 percent.

The problem is domestic. Consumer spending barely moved, corporate investment actually fell 1.2 percent, and only exports are holding up the overall figures.

But inflation hasn't come down. The Bank of Japan had planned to raise rates in September, and now it must think carefully: would a rate hike make already-cold consumer spending colder still?

跨城大戰今晚開打，小熊主場迎戰白襪

來源：Chicago Sun-Times

芝加哥的兩支球隊今晚在瑞格利球場碰頭。小熊七十二勝五十三敗，白襪六十五勝五十八敗，晚上八點零五分開賽，三天連打三場。

先發投手是小熊的今永昇太，對上白襪的卡斯提歐。

好看的地方在於，這幾年常常只有一隊還在拚，今年八月兩隊都還摸得到季後賽。五月在南區那三場，白襪贏了兩場，這次換小熊當主人。

Chicago's two teams meet at Wrigley Field tonight. The Cubs are 72-53, the White Sox 65-58. First pitch is 8:05 p.m., with three games over three days.

The Cubs will start Shota Imanaga against the White Sox's Castillo.

What makes this compelling is that in recent years usually only one of the two teams has been in contention — but this August, both can still reach the playoffs. Back in May on the South Side, the White Sox won two of three. This time the Cubs are the hosts.

來源：Sky Sports

兵工廠線——八月十六日在卡地夫的公主球場，兵工廠三比零贏了曼城，捧回社區盾（英格蘭新球季的開幕獎盃）。

夏天沒補後衛，薩利巴和廷伯又上不了場，賽前大家都在擔心。結果臨時搭檔莫斯克拉和班懷特，一球都沒讓曼城進。

The Arsenal beat — on August 16th at Cardiff's Principality Stadium, Arsenal defeated Manchester City 3-0 to claim the Community Shield, England's season-opening trophy.

Just 24 seconds into the match, Calafiori received a pass from Lewis-Skelly and tucked the ball into the corner. Havertz doubled the lead with a header in the first half; two minutes into the second half, Odegaard dribbled past Manchester City's defenders and added the third.

Arsenal signed no defender over the summer, and with Saliba and Timber unavailable, there was real concern beforehand. Yet the improvised partnership of Mosquera and Ben White kept Manchester City off the scoresheet entirely.
