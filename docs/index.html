OpenAI 說 2027 年要上市

來源：CNBC

人工智慧公司 OpenAI 的財務長費爾星期三在員工大會上說，公司「2027 年會成為上市公司」，如果生意成長更快，還可能提早。這是公司第一次講出上市的年份。

她說，上市不是終點，只是一個里程碑，也是另一次募資；公司 3 月才剛募到 1220 億美元。上市以後每一季都要公開帳目，外界才能看清楚這些人工智慧公司到底在賺錢還是燒錢。

OpenAI's chief financial officer, Sarah Friar, told an all-hands meeting on Wednesday that the company "will be a public company in 2027" — and possibly sooner if growth accelerates. It is the first time OpenAI has attached a year to a listing. She said an IPO is not the finish line, just a milestone and another fundraise; the company closed a $122 billion round only in March. Once public, it will have to open its books every quarter, letting outsiders finally see whether these AI companies are making money or burning it.

美國國債首度突破 40 兆美元

來源：NPR

美國財政部星期三公布的數字顯示，國債總額已經來到 40.05 兆美元，第一次超過 40 兆，比官方原本的預估早了好幾年，也比 2017 年多了一倍以上。

更頭痛的是利息。今年光是付利息就接近 1.2 兆美元，比國防花的錢還多，成為政府第二大的開銷。最近 30 年期公債的利率升到 19 年來最高，等於新借的每一塊錢都更貴。

Figures released by the U.S. Treasury on Wednesday show the national debt has reached $40.05 trillion, crossing $40 trillion for the first time — years ahead of official projections, and more than double its 2017 level. The bigger headache is interest. Interest payments alone will come to nearly $1.2 trillion this year, more than the government spends on defense, making it the second-largest line in the budget. The 30-year Treasury yield has climbed to a 19-year high, meaning every newly borrowed dollar costs more.

阿聯酋停止與伊朗所有貿易

來源：The Washington Post

阿拉伯聯合大公國（阿聯酋）星期三宣布，停止和伊朗之間所有的買賣和金錢往來，沒有說什麼時候恢復。前一晚有兩枚飛彈落在波斯灣，其中一枚掉進阿聯酋的海域，該國國防部說飛彈是衝著海上船隻來的；伊朗否認發射。

阿聯酋是伊朗第二大的生意夥伴，多年來也是伊朗繞過制裁的主要管道。這一步，比任何一輪正式制裁都要痛。

The United Arab Emirates announced Wednesday that it is halting all trade and financial dealings with Iran, without saying when they might resume. The night before, two missiles came down in the Persian Gulf, one of them landing in Emirati waters; the UAE defense ministry said the missiles were aimed at shipping, while Iran denied firing them. The UAE is Iran's second-largest trading partner and has for years been Tehran's main channel for working around sanctions. This step hurts more than any formal round of sanctions.

美國對加拿大的五成關稅延後三天

來源：PBS NewsHour

原本星期三就要上路的五成關稅（進口貨要加收的稅），被川普在前一晚喊停三天，改到 8 月 22 日。這批關稅打的是加拿大的汽車、酒和乳製品，大約牽涉 200 億美元的貨物。

川普說兩國「已經談成了」，只差把文件弄好；加拿大總理卡尼則說進展很大，但重要的工作還沒做完。這次用的是 1930 年關稅法第 338 條，一條以前從來沒有用過的老法條。

A 50% tariff — an extra tax charged on imported goods — that was due to take effect Wednesday was put on hold by Trump the night before and pushed back three days, to August 22. The duties target Canadian cars, alcohol and dairy products, covering roughly $20 billion of goods. Trump said the two countries "have made a deal" and only the paperwork remains; Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney said there had been substantial progress but that important work was still unfinished. The tariffs rest on Section 338 of the Tariff Act of 1930, an old provision that has never been used before.

A week and a half into Michigan's fall camp, the position that worried new head coach Kyle Whittingham most finally has an answer: Andrew Babalola has won a three-way competition and is likely to start at left tackle — the spot that protects the quarterback — in the season opener. 兵工廠砸 5100 萬英鎊簽下孔薩

來源：ESPN

兵工廠線——兵工廠星期四早上和維拉隊談妥，用大約 5100 萬英鎊簽下英格蘭中後衛孔薩。維拉本來開價 6000 萬，最後兩邊各退一步。

五天前，教練阿爾特塔還公開說不會急著補後衛。但兩名主力薩利巴和廷伯都長期受傷，他只好改口。孔薩要在星期四中午前完成登錄，才趕得上星期五對考文垂的開幕戰。這個夏天球隊已經花掉超過 2 億 1500 萬英鎊。

The Arsenal beat — Arsenal reached an agreement with Aston Villa on Thursday morning to sign England center-back Ezri Konsa for around £51 million. Villa had originally asked for £60 million; in the end both sides gave ground. Five days ago, manager Mikel Arteta said publicly that he was in no rush to add a defender. But with both first-choice center-backs, William Saliba and Jurrien Timber, out long term, he changed his mind. Konsa must complete his registration by Thursday midday to be eligible for Friday's opener against Coventry. Arsenal have now spent more than £215 million this summer.
