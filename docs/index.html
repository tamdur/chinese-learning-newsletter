美國最高法院大幅削弱投票權法

來源：SCOTUSblog

美國最高法院昨天以6比3的票數，在「路易斯安那州訴Callais案」中大幅限制了投票權法第二條的效力。法官Alito寫道，各州不需要為少數族群（人數較少的種族）特別畫出選區。

這個判決推翻了幾十年來保護少數族群投票權的規定。大法官Kagan在反對意見中警告，投票權法已經「幾乎變成一張廢紙」。

The US Supreme Court voted 6-3 yesterday in Louisiana v. Callais to severely restrict the power of Section 2 of the Voting Rights Act. Justice Alito wrote that states are not required to draw districts specifically for racial minorities with smaller populations. The ruling overturned decades of protections for minority voting rights. Justice Kagan warned in her dissent that the Voting Rights Act has become "nearly worthless."

國防部長首次就伊朗戰爭向國會作證

來源：CBS News / CNN

美國國防部長Hegseth昨天首次到國會說明伊朗戰爭的情況，聽證會長達近六個小時。他為封鎖荷莫茲海峽辯護，並要求國會通過1.5兆美元的國防預算。

戰爭已超過60天，花了250億美元。兩黨議員都對Hegseth的強硬態度表達不滿，油價也持續上漲。

US Defense Secretary Hegseth testified before Congress yesterday for the first time about the Iran war, with the hearing lasting nearly six hours. He defended the blockade of the Strait of Hormuz and pressed lawmakers to approve a $1.5 trillion defense budget. The war has now exceeded 60 days and cost $25 billion. Lawmakers from both parties expressed frustration with Hegseth's hardline stance, while oil prices continue to climb.

伊利諾州簽署大眾運輸新法，芝加哥公車地鐵免漲價

來源：CBS Chicago

伊利諾州州長Pritzker簽署了SB 2111法案，每年提供15億美元給CTA、Metra和Pace等大眾運輸系統，避免了原本今年夏天的漲價和減班。

新法還成立「北伊利諾大眾運輸局」，把三家公司放在同一個管理單位下，六月一日開始運作。而且不需要加稅。

Illinois Governor Pritzker signed Senate Bill 2111, which allocates $1.5 billion annually to public transit systems including the CTA, Metra, and Pace. The legislation averts fare increases and service cuts that had been planned for summer. The law also establishes the Northern Illinois Public Transit Authority, consolidating the three agencies under unified management beginning June 1st. The measure requires no tax increases.

Powell宣布留在聯準會，不讓Trump多一個空位

來源：Bloomberg

美國聯準會（中央銀行）主席Powell昨天宣布，他的主席任期五月十五日結束後，會繼續留在理事會當普通理事。

Powell說，Trump政府對聯準會的法律攻擊「讓他別無選擇」。他留下來，Trump就無法在理事會中拿到多數席位。這是1948年以來第一次有卸任主席選擇留任。

Federal Reserve Chair Powell announced yesterday that after his chairman term ends on May 15, he will remain on the board as a regular governor. Powell said that legal attacks on the Fed by the Trump administration "left him no choice." By staying, Trump cannot gain a majority on the board. This is the first time since 1948 that a departing chair has chosen to remain.

密西根冠軍控衛Cadeau宣布留校打大四

來源：Maize n Brew / UM Hoops

密西根大學籃球隊的明星控衛（負責組織進攻的球員）Elliot Cadeau宣布留校再打一年。他在今年三月帶領密西根拿下NCAA全國冠軍，決賽得了19分，還拿下最傑出球員獎。

很多人以為他會去NBA，但他選擇回來。教練Dusty May還從轉學門招到兩名大將，下季陣容非常強。

University of Michigan basketball star point guard Elliot Cadeau announced he will return for his senior year. He led Michigan to the NCAA national championship in March, scoring 19 points in the final game and earning Most Outstanding Player honors. Many expected him to leave for the NBA, but he chose to stay. Coach Dusty May has also added two major recruits through the transfer portal, making next season's roster exceptionally strong.

歐冠四強首回合：兵工廠客場1比1戰平馬競

來源：Arsenal.com

兵工廠線——兵工廠昨晚在馬德里踢了歐冠四強首回合，與馬競1比1打平。Gyokeres在第44分鐘罰進十二碼先進一球，但Alvarez在第56分鐘也靠十二碼追平。

這場比賽爭議不斷，一共判了三次十二碼，第三次被VAR取消。次回合回到酋長球場，兵工廠離20年來首次打進決賽只差一步。

Arsenal beat — Arsenal played the first leg of the Champions League semi-final in Madrid last night, drawing 1-1 with Atlético. Gyokeres converted a penalty in the 44th minute to put the visitors ahead, but Alvarez equalized from the spot in the 56th minute. The match was marred by controversy, with three penalties awarded in total; the third was overturned by VAR. The second leg returns to the Emirates, and Arsenal are one step away from reaching the final for the first time in 20 years.
