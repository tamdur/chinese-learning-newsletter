KPMG：地緣政治風險正在改變全球經濟走向

來源：KPMG Economic Outlook

KPMG（一家大型會計公司）最新的經濟報告指出，荷莫茲海峽（Strait of Hormuz，中東重要的石油運輸通道）幾乎被關閉了，讓油價和運費都大幅上漲。

因為這些地緣（跟地理位置有關的）政治問題，美國的 GDP 成長被下修到大約百分之二。聯準會（美國的中央銀行）預計到明年初只會降息一次，整體經濟前景不太樂觀。

KPMG, a major accounting firm, released a new economic report indicating that the Strait of Hormuz—a critical Middle Eastern oil shipping corridor—has been nearly closed, causing oil prices and freight costs to surge sharply.

Due to these geopolitical issues related to geographic location, the United States GDP growth forecast has been revised downward to approximately 2%. The Federal Reserve, the U.S. central bank, is expected to cut interest rates only once by early next year, and the overall economic outlook remains pessimistic.

古老符文比想像中更早，改寫北歐文字歷史

來源：Science / Archaeology News

科學家在挪威發現了一些刻有符文（runes，古代北歐人用的文字）的石頭。用放射性碳定年法測量後，發現這些石頭大約是西元前五十年到西元二七五年的東西。

這比之前大家以為的早了很多，等於是改寫了北歐文字的歷史。也就是說，北歐人開始使用文字的時間，比我們原本知道的還要早。

Scientists in Norway have discovered stones carved with runes—the ancient alphabet used by Norse people. Using radiocarbon dating to measure them, researchers found these stones date to around 50 BCE to 275 CE.

This is much earlier than experts previously believed, essentially rewriting the history of Norse writing. In other words, Norse people began using written language earlier than we originally thought.
