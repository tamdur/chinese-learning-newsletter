輝達砍半亞洲買家名單，防晶片流入中國

來源：Financial Times

美國晶片公司輝達（Nvidia）把亞洲能買它AI晶片的客戶名單砍掉一半以上，因為美國政府擔心這些晶片會偷偷流到中國。

這幾個月，輝達在新加坡、馬來西亞和日本加強審查，還派自己的員工到客戶的數據中心，親自看合約、問使用的人。有一半以上的舊客戶沒通過，被踢出名單。

Nvidia, the American chip company, has cut more than half of its approved list of Asian buyers for its AI chips, as the U.S. government worries the technology could secretly flow to China. Over the past few months, Nvidia has tightened its vetting process in Singapore, Malaysia, and Japan, even sending its own employees to customers' data centers to personally review contracts and question the people using the chips. More than half of its existing customers failed this scrutiny and were removed from the list.

美國要「拆掉」國際刑事法院，歐盟批不能接受

來源：Al Jazeera

美國國務卿魯比歐（Rubio）宣布，要動用整個政府的力量「拆掉」國際刑事法院（專門審判戰爭罪的國際法庭）。他說這個法院是對美國的威脅。

美國打算用制裁、取消簽證等辦法，還要逼別的國家退出、停止給錢。歐盟馬上反對，說攻擊或威脅法院的人「完全不能接受」。

U.S. Secretary of State Rubio announced plans to mobilize the entire U.S. government to "dismantle" the International Criminal Court, the international tribunal that handles war crimes prosecutions. He characterized the court as a threat to the United States. The U.S. intends to use sanctions, visa cancellations, and other measures, and will also pressure other countries to withdraw from the court and cut off funding. The EU promptly objected, saying that attacks or threats against the court are "completely unacceptable."

一週內兩人被擊斃，美國移民局暫停攔車抓人

來源：NPR

美國移民局（ICE）下令，暫時停止攔車抓非法移民。因為一週內，探員在緬因州和德州各開槍打死一個人。

兩名死者一個來自哥倫比亞，一個來自墨西哥，都不是原本要抓的目標，開槍時探員也沒戴密錄器（記錄畫面的小攝影機）。移民局說這只是暫停，要先讓探員再受訓練。

U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) has ordered a temporary halt to vehicle stops aimed at catching undocumented immigrants. The decision comes after officers fatally shot one person each in Maine and Texas within the span of a week. Both victims—one from Colombia and the other from Mexico—were not the intended targets of the stops, and the officers were not wearing body cameras at the time of the shootings. ICE says the suspension is temporary and will allow time for officers to receive additional training.

37年土壤實驗發現，深層碳被釋放恐加速暖化

來源：ScienceDaily

美國哈佛森林做了一個世界上最久的土壤加溫實驗，已經37年。研究員在地下埋電線，把土加溫，比平常高5度。

結果發現，本來以為很穩、不會分解的深層土壤裡的碳，到了第四個十年也開始被細菌分解，放出更多二氧化碳。這代表暖化可能比很多氣候模型算的還要快。

The Harvard Forest in the United States has been running the world's longest soil warming experiment for 37 years. Researchers buried electrical cables underground to heat the soil, raising temperatures 5 degrees above baseline. The findings revealed something unexpected: deep soil carbon—long thought to be stable and resistant to decomposition—began breaking down by the fourth decade, as bacteria decomposed it and released additional carbon dioxide. This suggests that global warming could progress faster than many climate models currently predict.

小熊爬上外卡龍頭，交易大限前轉為買家

來源：CBS Sports

芝加哥小熊隊打出54勝42敗，站上國家聯盟外卡（季後賽門票）第一名。幾週前他們還因為傷兵多、輸多贏少，看起來要賣人。

現在球隊變強，決定當「買家」，想在8月3日交易大限前補進投手。球隊有好幾個投手受傷，最需要先發投手來幫忙拚季後賽。

The Chicago Cubs posted a 54-42 record and claimed first place in the National League wild card standings. A few weeks ago, with many injured players and more losses than wins, they looked like sellers. Now that the team has gotten stronger, they've decided to become buyers and aim to acquire pitchers before the August 3rd trade deadline.

With several pitchers injured, the team most needs starting pitchers to help make their playoff push.

薩利巴背傷退場，兵工廠後防拉警報

來源：Goal.com

兵工廠線——阿森納（兵工廠）的主力中後衛薩利巴（Saliba），在法國對西班牙的世界盃四強賽只踢了30分鐘，就因為背痛被換下場。

法國最後0比2輸球出局。薩利巴這個背傷已經拖了好幾個月，有報導說他可能要開刀。新球季還沒開始，兵工廠的防線就先亮起紅燈。

The Arsenal beat — Arsenal's star centre-back Saliba played just 30 minutes in France's World Cup semi-final against Spain before being taken off with back pain. France lost 0-2 and crashed out. Saliba's back injury has dragged on for months, with reports now suggesting he may need surgery. As the new season looms, Arsenal's defence is already sounding alarm bells.
