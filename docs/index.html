川普下令：給國會資料前先問白宮

來源：NPR / Washington Post

美國總統川普21日簽了一道行政命令：替政府做事的承包商（拿政府合約的公司），要給國會資料前，必須先通知白宮。白宮認為該保密的東西，也不能交出去。

白宮律師另外規定，公務員要去國會作證，也要先得到同意。

民主黨在期中選舉拿下參眾兩院，明年一月就要上任。這兩道命令，就是要讓他們比較難查政府。接下來很可能會鬧上法院。

On the 21st, U.S. President Trump signed an executive order requiring government contractors (companies that hold government contracts) to notify the White House before handing any material over to Congress. The White House holds that anything it considers confidential should not be released either. White House lawyers have also ruled that civil servants must get permission before testifying before Congress. Democrats won both houses of Congress in the midterm elections and will take office next January. These two orders are meant to make it harder for them to scrutinize the government, and a court fight is likely to follow.

美國堵住晶片漏洞，輝達停賣中國H200

來源：Al Jazeera / Reuters

美國商務部的新規定19日上路：中國公司不能再透過海外的資料中心，偷偷使用美國最先進的AI晶片。在新加坡、馬來西亞、泰國、印尼和巴西的資料中心用這些晶片，現在都要先拿到許可。

輝達（Nvidia）代理執行長克雷斯說，公司會停止在中國賣H200晶片。

同時，美國和新加坡都在調查新加坡的雲端公司Megaspeed，懷疑它透過馬來西亞的子公司，把將近20億美元的輝達晶片送到中國。Megaspeed否認，中國則說會反擊。

Starting on the 19th, a new U.S. Commerce Department rule took effect: Chinese companies can no longer quietly use the most advanced American AI chips through data centers overseas. Data centers in Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia and Brazil that use these chips now need to obtain a license first. Nvidia's acting CEO, Kress, said the company will stop selling H200 chips in China. At the same time, the United States and Singapore are both investigating Megaspeed, a Singapore-based cloud company suspected of using its Malaysian subsidiary to funnel nearly $2 billion worth of Nvidia chips to China. Megaspeed denies the allegations, and China has said it will retaliate.

熊隊今天戰聖徒，威廉斯要少犯錯

來源：ATS.io

7勝2敗、國聯北區第一的芝加哥熊隊，今天中午在士兵球場對上3勝6敗的紐奧良聖徒隊。大家看好熊隊能贏11.5分以上。

四分衛威廉斯（Caleb Williams）最近七場表現不錯，但上一場被抄截（傳球被對手接走）了四次。

聖徒隊最怕對手跑球，被跑出的碼數是聯盟第六多。教練強森很可能多用跑球，這樣也能讓威廉斯少冒險傳球。

Seven-and-two and first in the NFC North, the Chicago Bears host the three-and-six New Orleans Saints at Soldier Field this afternoon. Bettors like Chicago to win by more than 11.5 points. Caleb Williams has played well over his last seven games, but in his most recent outing he was intercepted four times. The Saints are vulnerable to the run, allowing the sixth-most rushing yards in the league. Coach Johnson will likely lean on the ground game, which would also let Williams avoid taking unnecessary risks through the air.

少了兩名主力後衛，兵工廠2比2和紐卡索打平

來源：Arsenal.com / NBC Sports

兵工廠線——兵工廠21日到紐卡索作客，兩名主力中後衛都不能上場，最後2比2打平。

埃澤（Eze）上半場先進球，但紐卡索下半場連進兩球反超。比賽剩下12分鐘時，馬杜埃克（Madueke）把比數追平。

打完11場，兵工廠還是領先6分，不過門將拉亞（Raya）受傷了。星期二，球隊要在主場對上德國的多特蒙德。

The Arsenal beat: Arsenal travelled to Newcastle on the 21st without both of their first-choice centre-backs, and the match ended 2-2. Eze put Arsenal ahead before halftime, but Newcastle scored twice in the second half to take the lead. With 12 minutes left, Madueke equalized. After 11 matches, Arsenal still lead the table by six points, but goalkeeper Raya was injured. On Tuesday, the team host Borussia Dortmund of Germany.
