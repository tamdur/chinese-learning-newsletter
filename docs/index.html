美國與以色列空襲伊朗，荷莫茲海峽危機加深

來源：Reuters, AP

二月底，美國和以色列一起對伊朗發動大規模空襲，伊朗最高領袖在攻擊中死亡。伊朗馬上關閉荷莫茲海峽（全球最重要的石油運輸通道），每天通過的船從77艘降到零。國際油價飆到每桶126美元，目前在112到115之間。不過伊朗還是讓中國和俄羅斯的船通過，用人民幣收費。美國已經從戰略儲備放出4億桶石油，Trump把最後期限延到四月六日。專家說這次的影響比1970年代的石油危機還要嚴重。

The United States and Israel launched a massive airstrike against Iran at the end of February, during which Iran's supreme leader was killed in the attack. Iran immediately closed the Strait of Hormuz, the world's most critical oil shipping channel, reducing daily vessel traffic from 77 ships to zero. International oil prices surged to $126 per barrel, currently fluctuating between $112 and $115. However, Iran continues to allow Chinese and Russian ships to pass through, collecting fees in renminbi. The United States has already released 400 million barrels from its strategic reserves, and Trump has extended the deadline to April 6th. Experts say the impact of this crisis is even more severe than the oil crisis of the 1970s.

油價暴漲打亂全球經濟前景

來源：OECD, Goldman Sachs

荷莫茲海峽關閉讓全球經濟受到很大衝擊。OECD（經濟合作組織）三月報告說，今年全球經濟成長只有2.9%，本來要往上調的計畫完全取消了。美國經濟成長降到2.0%，歐洲更慘，只有0.8%。物價方面也不好，美國通膨（物價上漲速度）預計到4.2%，是主要國家裡最高的。美國聯準會今年大概不會降息了。高盛銀行認為美國進入衰退（經濟倒退）的機會有30%。另外，做肥料的原料漲了四成以上，農民也很擔心。

Soaring oil prices are upending the global economic outlook. The closure of the Strait of Hormuz has dealt a significant blow to the global economy. The OECD's March report states that global economic growth this year will reach only 2.9%, with previously planned upward revisions now completely scrapped. US economic growth has fallen to 2.0%, while Europe fares even worse at just 0.8%. The inflation picture is equally grim—US price increases are projected to reach 4.2%, the highest among major economies. The Federal Reserve is unlikely to cut interest rates this year. Goldman Sachs estimates a 30% chance of the US entering a recession. Additionally, commodity prices for fertilizer production have surged more than 40%, leaving farmers deeply worried.

MCP下載破9700萬次，AI代理時代來了

來源：NVIDIA, Anthropic

你有沒有聽過MCP？它的全名是 Model Context Protocol，簡單來說就是讓AI可以連接各種工具的標準方法，有人叫它「AI的USB」。這個東西2024年11月才推出，當時每月下載只有200萬次，現在已經暴增到9700萬次，成長了47倍。目前有超過一萬個伺服器在用它。Anthropic已經把MCP捐給Linux基金會管理。NVIDIA的老闆黃仁勳在GTC大會上說，我們已經到了「代理式AI」（AI自己做事）的轉折點。大公司像Bloomberg和McLaren都開始用了。

Have you heard of MCP? Its full name is the Model Context Protocol—basically a standard way that lets AI connect to all sorts of tools. Some people call it "the USB of AI." The thing only launched in November 2024, when it had just 2 million monthly downloads. Now it's exploded to 97 million downloads—a 47-fold jump. More than 10,000 servers are using it right now. Anthropic has already donated MCP to the Linux Foundation to manage. NVIDIA's CEO Jensen Huang said at the GTC conference that we've reached an inflection point in "agentic AI"—AI that does things on its own. Big companies like Bloomberg and McLaren are already jumping in.

伊利諾州批准15億美元大修芝加哥交通

來源：Chicago Tribune

好消息！伊利諾州通過了一個大法案，要花15億美元來改造芝加哥的公共交通。新法從六月一日開始，會成立一個叫NITA的新機構，取代用了50年的舊系統。為什麼要改？因為疫情之後坐車的人少了很多，缺了2.3億美元，差一點就要砍掉四成的班次。錢從哪來？主要是汽油稅和地方消費稅。好的是今年不會漲車票也不會減少班次，而且到2030年會有統一的票價系統。

Good news! Illinois has passed a major bill to spend $1.5 billion to overhaul Chicago's public transit. The new law, effective June 1st, will establish a new agency called NITA to replace the old system that has been in place for 50 years. Why the overhaul? After the pandemic, ridership dropped significantly, leaving a $230 million shortfall that nearly forced a 40% reduction in service. Where's the money coming from? Primarily from gasoline taxes and local sales taxes. The positive side: there will be no fare increases this year and no service cuts, and by 2030 there will be a unified fare system.

密西根大學籃球隊太厲害了！昨天在菁英八強賽中以95比62大勝田納西，贏了33分！最強的球員 Lendeborg 拿了27分、7個籃板、4次助攻，而且零失誤。他當初放棄了肯塔基大學700到900萬美元的合約，選擇來密西根。比賽中密西根打出一波21比0的超強攻勢。今年球隊35勝3敗，是史上第一支前四場都贏兩位數而且每場都超過90分的隊伍。四月四日四強賽對手是亞利桑那大學，加油！

Michigan basketball is incredible! Yesterday in the Elite Eight, they dominated Tennessee 95-62, winning by 33 points! Their star player Lendeborg had an outstanding performance with 27 points, 7 rebounds, and 4 assists—and didn't commit a single turnover. He originally turned down a contract worth $7-9 million from the University of Kentucky to come to Michigan. During the game, Michigan went on an impressive 21-0 run. The team now has a record of 35-3, making them the first team in history to win their first four tournament games by double digits while scoring over 90 points in each game. On April 4th, they face the University of Arizona in the Final Four. Go Blue!

兵工廠領先10分，三冠夢還在

來源：Sky Sports, UEFA

【兵工廠線】阿森納現在在英超排第一，31場比賽拿了70分，領先曼城大約9到10分。歐冠那邊也很棒，兩回合3比1打敗勒沃庫森，進入八強。Eze踢了一記超級遠射，Rice也進了球。不過聯賽盃決賽有點可惜，0比2輸給曼城，而且教練那天選了 Kepa 當守門員而不是 Raya，球迷都在討論這個決定。下一場歐冠四月七日對里斯本競技。英超、足總盃、歐冠三冠王的夢還活著！

Arsenal sit atop the Premier League with 70 points from 31 matches, leading Manchester City by roughly 9 to 10 points. In Europe, they're on a roll too, dispatching Leverkusen 3-1 across two legs to reach the quarter-finals. Eze struck a stunning long-range effort and Rice got on the scoresheet. The League Cup final was a disappointment, though—a 2-0 loss to City—and Arteta's choice to start Kepa over Raya that day has fans debating the call. Next up in the Champions League is Sporting Lisbon on April 7th. The treble dream—Premier League, FA Cup, Champions League—is still very much alive.
