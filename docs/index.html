美國再次空襲伊朗，和平談判同時進行

來源：NPR

美國軍方在荷莫茲海峽（Strait of Hormuz）附近發動新一輪打擊，攻擊了伊朗的船隻和飛彈發射點。伊朗說至少四名海軍死亡。

美國稱這是「自衛行動」，但伊朗指控美方違反四月的停火協議。雙方仍在卡達進行和平談判，希望把停火變成長期和平。

The U.S. military launched a new round of strikes near the Strait of Hormuz, targeting Iranian vessels and missile launch sites. Iran said at least four naval personnel were killed. The U.S. characterized the action as "self-defense," but Iran accused the Americans of violating a ceasefire agreement reached in April. Both sides continue peace negotiations in Qatar, hoping to transform the ceasefire into a lasting peace.

亞洲安全高峰會在新加坡開幕

來源：NPR

第23屆香格里拉對話五月29日在新加坡開幕，超過40個國家的國防官員參加。越南總統蘇林（Tô Lâm）發表主題演講，他是第一位在這個高峰會演講的共產黨總書記。

蘇林警告亞太地區正面對三大危機：國際秩序動搖、發展模式改變，以及各國之間的信任下降。

The 23rd Shangri-La Dialogue opened in Singapore on May 29, bringing together defense officials from more than 40 countries. Vietnamese President Tô Lâm delivered the keynote address, becoming the first Communist Party General Secretary to speak at this summit. Lâm warned that the Asia-Pacific region faces three major crises: a shaking international order, shifting development models, and declining trust among nations.

芝加哥市長訪梵蒂岡，視教宗為社會正義盟友

來源：WTTW Chicago

芝加哥市長乾森（Brandon Johnson）帶著50位官員前往梵蒂岡，與出生在芝加哥的教宗良十四世（Pope Leo XIV）會面。他們討論了移民問題和種族賠償（就是為過去的不公平做出補償）。

乾森說，教宗為教會在奴隸制度中的角色道歉，讓他很感動。市長還把城市鑰匙送給教宗，並邀請他到芝加哥的格蘭特公園舉行彌撒。

Chicago Mayor Brandon Johnson traveled to the Vatican with 50 officials to meet with Pope Leo XIV, who was born in Chicago. They discussed immigration and reparations — compensation for past injustices. Johnson said he was deeply moved when the Pope apologized for the Church's role in slavery. The mayor presented the Pope with a symbolic key to the city and invited him to hold Mass at Grant Park in Chicago.

前CIA高官偷走303條金條被捕

來源：NPR

美國聯邦調查局（FBI）在前中央情報局（CIA）高官乳許（David Rush）的家中找到303條金條，價值超過四千萬美元。他以「工作費用」的名義拿走了這些金條，卻沒有歸還。

FBI還在他家找到兩百萬美元現金和35支名牌手錶。更離奇的是，他還假裝自己是海軍飛行員，但他從來沒有飛過飛機。

The Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) discovered 303 gold bars worth more than $40 million at the home of David Rush, a former senior CIA official. Rush had taken the gold bars under the guise of "work expenses" but never returned them. The FBI also found $2 million in cash and 35 luxury watches at his residence. Even more bizarrely, he had impersonated a Navy pilot, despite never having flown an aircraft.

小熊隊終結十連敗，爬回分區第二

來源：Bleacher Nation

芝加哥小熊隊連輸十場後終於回神了！他們在匹茲堡連贏兩場，拿下系列賽。系列最後一場以7比2大勝海盜隊。

匹茲堡出身的乎帕（Ian Happ）連兩場打出全壘打，在家鄉已經連續41場安全上壘。小熊目前31勝26敗，回到國聯中區第二名。

The Chicago Cubs finally snapped their ten-game losing streak! They won both games in Pittsburgh and took the series. In the series finale, they demolished the Pirates 7-2. Ian Happ, who hails from Pittsburgh, hit home runs in back-to-back games and has now reached base safely in 41 consecutive games at home. The Cubs are now 31-26 and have climbed back to second place in the NL Central.

兵工廠今晚爭歐冠冠軍，挑戰歷史雙冠

來源：Arsenal.com

兵工廠線——兵工廠今天在布達佩斯的普斯卡斯球場對戰衛冕冠軍巴黎聖日耳曼（PSG），爭奪歐洲冠軍聯賽獎盃。

兵工廠已經拿下英超冠軍，結束了22年的等待。教練阿爾特塔（Arteta）剛獲選英超年度最佳教練。如果今晚贏球，兵工廠將完成歷史性的聯賽加歐冠雙冠王。

Arsenal beat — Arsenal face defending champion Paris Saint-Germain (PSG) tonight at the Puskás Stadium in Budapest for the European Champions League trophy. Arsenal have already secured the Premier League title, ending a 22-year drought. Manager Arteta was just named Premier League Manager of the Year. A win tonight would complete Arsenal's historic domestic league and Champions League double.
