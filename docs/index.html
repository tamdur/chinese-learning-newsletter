中國的 深度求索發布 V4 模型，不用美國晶片

Bloomberg / MIT Technology Review

中國的人工智慧公司深度求索（DeepSeek）在星期四發布了新的 V4 模型，剛好是他們第一次讓矽谷吃驚的一年後。這次最大的消息是：V4 完全不用美國 Nvidia 的晶片，而是用華為自己做的晶片。

新模型有兩個版本：V4-Pro 可以做很難的程式設計工作，V4-Flash 比較快也比較便宜。兩個都是開放原始碼，任何人都可以用。美國一直限制賣高級晶片給中國，但深度求索用這個模型告訴世界：中國可以自己做。

China's artificial intelligence company DeepSeek released a new V4 model on Thursday, exactly one year after their first breakthrough that caught Silicon Valley's attention. The biggest news this time: V4 doesn't use American Nvidia chips at all—instead it relies on chips made by Huawei. The new model comes in two versions: V4-Pro handles difficult programming tasks, while V4-Flash is faster and cheaper. Both are open-source, available for anyone to use. The US has long restricted sales of advanced chips to China, but with this model DeepSeek is telling the world: China can build this itself.

川普下令攻擊伊朗船隻，荷莫茲海峽危機加深

NBC News / CBS News

美國總統川普下令軍隊攻擊在荷莫茲海峽（Strait of Hormuz）放水雷的伊朗小船。這是自一九七零年代以來最大的石油供應危機，每天少了一千三百萬桶石油。

美國海軍封鎖了伊朗的港口，伊朗也抓了經過海峽的商船。同時，白宮說會在巴基斯坦和伊朗進行第二輪和平談判，但伊朗說沒有這件事。局勢很緊張。

U.S. President Trump has ordered military strikes against Iranian small boats laying mines in the Strait of Hormuz. This represents the largest oil supply crisis since the 1970s, with daily crude supplies down by 13 million barrels. The U.S. Navy has blockaded Iranian ports, while Iran has seized merchant vessels transiting the strait. Meanwhile, the White House has indicated it will pursue a second round of peace negotiations in Pakistan and Iran, but Iran denies any such talks are planned. The situation remains highly tense.

美國國會無法通過監聽法案，四月底將到期

The Hill

美國有一個叫 FISA 702 的法律，讓情報機關可以監聽外國人的通訊。問題是，有時候美國人的資料也會被看到，而且不需要法院許可。

這個法律四月三十日就會到期。共和黨想延長它，但黨內一些重視隱私（privacy）的議員和民主黨一起擋下了投票。他們要求：要看美國人的資料，就必須先拿到法院的許可。只剩五天了，時間很緊。

The United States has a law called FISA 702 that allows intelligence agencies to monitor the communications of foreign nationals. The problem is that American citizens' data sometimes gets caught in the sweep, and no court order is required. This law expires on April 30th. Republicans want to extend it, but privacy-focused members of their own party have joined with Democrats to block a vote. They're demanding a warrant requirement: the government must obtain court approval before accessing Americans' data. With only five days left, time is running out.

伊利諾州健保費漲了，但沒有想像中那麼多

Chicago Sun-Times

美國聯邦政府以前幫人民付一部分的健康保險費，但這個補助（subsidy）去年結束了。很多人擔心保險費會漲百分之七十八。

好消息是，新的數據顯示伊利諾州（Illinois）只漲了百分之二十五，比預想的少很多。買保險的人數也只減少了百分之四。很多人換成比較便宜的方案，所以沒有像大家怕的那麼嚴重。

The federal government previously helped people pay for a portion of their health insurance premiums, but that subsidy ended last year. Many people worried that insurance costs would jump by seventy-eight percent. The good news is that new data shows Illinois experienced only a twenty-five percent increase — far less than feared. The number of people buying insurance dropped by just four percent. Many switched to cheaper plans, so the impact wasn't nearly as severe as everyone had worried.

熊隊選秀第二天：選了 LSU 快速接球手

ESPN

美式足球的選秀（NFL Draft）正在進行。芝加哥熊隊（Bears）在第二天用第八十九順位選了路易斯安那州立大學（LSU）的接球手湯瑪斯（Zavion Thomas）。

湯瑪斯的速度很快，熊隊希望他能幫助球隊的進攻更有力。這次選秀是芝加哥建設年輕陣容（roster）的重要一步。

The NFL Draft is underway. The Chicago Bears selected LSU wide receiver Zavion Thomas with the 89th overall pick on the second day of the draft. Thomas is known for his speed, and the Bears hope he will strengthen the team's offensive weapons. This draft pick represents an important step in Chicago's effort to build a younger roster.

Arsenal.com

兵工廠（Arsenal）今天在主場迎戰紐卡索（Newcastle）。上一場他們輸給了曼城，現在兵工廠和曼城都是七十分，還剩五場比賽。

The good news is that injured star Saka may be available to play — his foot injury has improved considerably. This match is crucial; another loss would make the title dream feel even more distant.
