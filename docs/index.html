聯合國氣候大會在土耳其開幕

來源：Hürriyet Daily News / UNFCCC

一年一次的聯合國氣候大會COP31，11月9日在土耳其的安塔利亞開幕，會議到20日結束。這次很特別：土耳其負責場地，澳洲負責主持談判。

聯合國氣候負責人史蒂爾說，這是一場「執行」大會。重點不是許下新的減碳承諾（答應要做的事），而是把以前答應的事做到，並找到錢來付。

本週稍後，各國領袖還會在伊斯坦堡開會。

The annual UN climate conference, COP31, opened on November 9 in Antalya, Turkey, and runs through the 20th. This year is a bit unusual: Turkey is providing the venue, while Australia is chairing the negotiations. UN climate chief Stiell calls it an "implementation" conference. The focus is not on making new pledges to cut emissions, but on delivering on promises countries have already made and finding the money to pay for them. Later this week, world leaders will also gather in Istanbul.

密西根輸掉州內大戰，四分衛可能換人

來源：University of Michigan Athletics

密西根大學美式足球隊上週末以6比15輸給密西根州立大學，整場沒有達陣（把球帶進對方得分區）。

球隊說，四分衛（指揮進攻的球員）可能再換人。十月底，五星新秀安德伍德因為常常把球丟給对手，被新生卡爾取代。現在，教練可能讓安德伍德回來先發。

11月14日，密西根要去客場挑戰排名第18的奧勒岡大學，外界不看好他們。

Michigan's football team lost 6-15 to Michigan State University last weekend, and the offense failed to score a touchdown (carry the ball into the opponent's end zone) for the entire game. The team says it may replace its quarterback (the player who runs the offense) again. At the end of October, five-star freshman Underwood was benched after repeatedly throwing the ball to the other side, and freshman Carl took over. Now the coaches may bring Underwood back as the starter. On November 14, Michigan travels to face No. 18 Oregon on the road, and few outsiders expect the Wolverines to win.

薩利巴傷後第一次上場比賽

來源：ESPN

兵工廠線——兵工廠中後衛薩利巴11月9日替21歲以下的球隊出賽。這是他十月中背部受傷以後，第一次踢正式比賽。

過去幾週，球迷只聽到他在練球的消息。沒有他，兵工廠靠著替補球員組成的後防，還是守住了聯賽第一。

現在是國家隊比賽的休息期。兵工廠下一場是11月21日對紐卡索，薩利巴很可能在那場回到一隊。

The Arsenal beat: Centre-back Saliba played for the Under-21 side on November 9. It was his first competitive match since he injured his back in mid-October. For weeks, fans had heard only about his training sessions. Without him, Arsenal's back line, made up of replacement players, still held on to top spot in the league. It is now an international break. Arsenal's next match is against Newcastle on November 21, and Saliba is likely to return to the first team for that game.
