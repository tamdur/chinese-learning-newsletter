聯合國氣候大會：土耳其喊出「用電」新目標

來源：Reuters / Hürriyet Daily News

聯合國氣候大會COP31正在土耳其的安塔利亞（Antalya）舉行，今天起兩天是各國領袖的高峰會。土耳其總統艾爾段說，現在不能再做只寫在紙上的承諾（答應要做的事）。

主辦國提出新目標：到2035年，全世界用的能源裡，電要占35%，現在大約只有20%。多用電，像電動車和電爐，就能少燒石油和煤。

不過，錢要誰出、要不要寫明停用化石燃料（煤、石油、天然氣），各國還在吵。這次大會由土耳其主辦、澳洲主持談判，會開到11月20日。

The UN climate conference, COP31, is being held in Antalya, Turkey, and the first two days are a summit for world leaders. Turkish President Erdoğan said the world can no longer rely on promises that exist only on paper. The host country has set a new goal: by 2035, electricity should make up 35% of the world's energy use, up from roughly 20% today. Using more electricity, through things like electric cars and electric stoves, means burning less oil and coal. But countries are still arguing over who should pay, and whether the final deal should explicitly call for phasing out fossil fuels (coal, oil, and natural gas). Turkey is hosting the conference, Australia is presiding over the negotiations, and it runs through November 20.

選後舊國會開工，年底前要過三關

來源：AP / States Newsroom

美國11月3日期中選舉，民主黨拿下了眾議院和參議院。可是新國會明年1月才上任，現在還是由共和黨控制的舊國會做事。這段時間叫做「跛腳鴨」會期（快要下台、力量變小的意思）。

舊國會手上有三件大事：政府的臨時預算12月11日就到期，沒通過的話，部分政府可能要關門；還有每年都要通過的國防法案，以及農業法案。

共和黨只剩大約八個星期，要決定哪些事現在就做，哪些留給明年的民主黨。

In the November 3 midterm elections, the Democrats took control of both the House of Representatives and the Senate. But the new Congress does not take office until January next year, so for now the outgoing Republican-controlled Congress is still in charge. This stretch is known as a "lame-duck" session, meaning a politician who is on the way out and has less power.

The old Congress has three major items on its plate. The government's temporary budget expires on December 11, and if it is not passed, parts of the government could shut down. There is also the defense bill, which must pass every year, and the farm bill.

Republicans have only about eight weeks left to decide which items to finish now and which to leave for the Democrats next year.

公牛隊七連敗，戰績聯盟最差

來源：Chicago Sun-Times / AP

芝加哥公牛隊星期天在主場以121比127輸給費城76人隊，已經連輸七場。戰績1勝8敗，是全NBA最差，也是隊史前九場第三差的開始。

吉迪（Josh Giddey）和布澤利斯（Matas Buzelis）各拿19分。對手的布朗（Jaylen Brown）得29分，老將詹姆斯（LeBron James）也有22分。

今年是新教練帶隊重建的第一年。球迷在問：這是故意輸球、換更好的選秀（挑新球員）順位，還是真的出問題了？下一場是13日對老鷹隊。

The Chicago Bulls lost 121–127 at home to the Philadelphia 76ers on Sunday, their seventh straight defeat. At 1–8, they have the worst record in the NBA, and the start is the third-worst of any Bulls team through nine games in franchise history. Josh Giddey and Matas Buzelis each scored 19 points. For the visitors, Jaylen Brown had 29 and veteran LeBron James added 22. This is the first season of the rebuild under a new head coach, and fans are asking the same question: Is this deliberate tanking to secure a better draft pick, or is something actually broken? The next game is on the 13th against the Atlanta Hawks.

英西大戰，兵工廠隊友變對手

來源：Arsenal.com / LiveScore

兵工廠線——英超休息兩週，兵工廠有19名球員去國家隊比賽。最好看的一場，是11月15日在馬德里的西班牙對英格蘭。

英格蘭隊有萊斯（Declan Rice）和埃澤（Eberechi Eze），萊斯上次沒去，這次回來了。西班牙隊有守門員拉亞（David Raya）、祖比門迪（Martín Zubimendi）和梅里諾（Mikel Merino）。平常一起練球的好朋友，這天要互相搶球。

第二天，德國的哈弗茲（Kai Havertz）也要碰上荷蘭的廷伯（Jurriën Timber）。球迷最希望大家都平安回來，因為21日還要去客場踢紐卡索。

The Arsenal Beat: The Premier League is taking a two-week break, and 19 Arsenal players are off to represent their national teams. The best match of the lot is Spain versus England on November 15 in Madrid. England have Declan Rice and Eberechi Eze, and Rice is back after sitting out the last camp. Spain have goalkeeper David Raya, Martín Zubimendi, and Mikel Merino. Training-ground pals will be battling for the ball against each other that day. The next day, Germany's Kai Havertz also comes up against the Netherlands' Jurriën Timber. Fans will be hoping everyone comes back safe and sound, since Arsenal still have an away trip to Newcastle on the 21st.
