美國利率不變　新主席首次開記者會

來源：IndexBox / CBS News

美國的中央銀行（聯準會）在六月十七日決定不調整利率，維持在百分之三點五到三點七五。

這是新主席沃許第一次主持會議和開記者會。五月的物價漲了百分之四點二，所以大家本來想降的利率不降了，今年底反而可能調高。

The U.S. Federal Reserve decided on June 17th to leave interest rates unchanged, holding them at 3.5 to 3.75 percent. This was the first meeting and press conference presided over by new Chair Warsh. With May inflation rising 4.2 percent, the rate cuts many had expected are now off the table, and there's a possibility rates could be raised by year's end instead.

巴黎科技展開幕　歐洲拚自己的人工智慧

來源：Euronews

歐洲最大的科技展「VivaTech」六月十七日在巴黎開幕，今年剛好滿十年。亞馬遜創辦人貝佐斯和法國總統馬克宏都來了。

兩週前歐盟才提出新計畫，想少靠美國和中國的晶片，自己做人工智慧。今年的「年度國家」是德國。

Europe's largest tech conference, VivaTech, opened in Paris on June 17th, marking exactly a decade since its launch. Amazon founder Jeff Bezos and French President Emmanuel Macron both attended. Just two weeks earlier, the European Union unveiled a new initiative aimed at reducing dependence on American and Chinese semiconductors while building its own artificial intelligence capabilities. Germany has been named this year's "Country of the Year."

七大工業國開完會　意見並不一致

來源：Council of the EU

七個工業大國（G7）的領袖六月十七日在法國埃維昂開完會，談了貿易、烏克蘭和重要礦產。

不過大家意見不同。美國課的關稅讓大家不開心，美國也不想在人工智慧的管理上講太多。其他國家則希望多關心烏克蘭。

Leaders of the Group of Seven major industrial nations concluded their meeting in Evian, France on June 17, discussing trade, Ukraine, and critical minerals. However, opinions diverged on key issues. U.S. tariffs frustrated other members, and America resisted extensive discussion on artificial intelligence regulation. Other nations prioritized greater focus on Ukraine.

伊利諾州簽新法　管大麻類產品

來源：Chicago Sun-Times

伊利諾州州長普里茲克簽了一條新法，要管一種叫「delta-8」的大麻類產品。這種產品以前沒人管，爭論了好幾年。

新法馬上禁止賣給未滿二十一歲的人。十一月起，這些產品會跟一般大麻一樣嚴格管理，包裝也要防小孩打開。

Illinois Governor Pritzker signed a new law to regulate delta-8, a cannabis-derived product that has gone unregulated and sparked debate for years. The law immediately prohibits sales to anyone under twenty-one. Starting in November, these products will face the same strict oversight as conventional cannabis, including child-resistant packaging requirements.

小熊成績下滑　高薪新球員被檢討

來源：ESPN

芝加哥小熊隊今年一開季很強，後來卻一直輸球。現在三十七勝三十五敗，在分區裡掉到第四名。

球迷開始檢討花大錢簽下的球員布瑞格曼。他的合約五年值一億七千五百萬美元，但今年只打出五支全壘打，表現不如預期。

The Chicago Cubs had a strong start to the season this year, but have been losing steadily since then. They now stand at 37 wins and 35 losses, sitting fourth in their division. Fans have begun scrutinizing Bregman, the high-priced player the team signed in the offseason. His five-year contract is worth $175 million, but he has only hit five home runs this season, falling short of expectations.

薩卡帶傷先發　萊斯當上副隊長

來源：Yahoo Sports

兵工廠線——兵工廠隊的球星薩卡六月十七日代表英格蘭出戰克羅埃西亞，是世界盃的第一場比賽。

他的腳跟（阿基里斯腱）三月受過傷，這次帶傷先發算是冒險。同隊的萊斯也被選為英格蘭的副隊長，地位越來越高。

The Arsenal beat — Arsenal's star Bukayo Saka started for England against Croatia on June 17th, their first match of the World Cup. He injured his heel (Achilles tendon) back in March, so starting while still carrying the injury is something of a gamble. His teammate Declan Rice was also named England's vice-captain, a sign of his rising standing.
