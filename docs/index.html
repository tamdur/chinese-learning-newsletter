伊朗為遇害最高領袖辦國葬 美伊會談暫停

來源：CBS News

伊朗開始為最高領袖哈米尼舉行國葬，儀式會從七月四日一直進行到九日。哈米尼在今年二月的美國和以色列空襲中喪生。

伊朗說有一千五百萬到兩千萬人前來送別，還有三十個國家派代表參加。因為要辦葬禮，美國和伊朗暫停談判一週，等葬禮結束後，七月九日才再談。

Iran has begun holding a state funeral for Supreme Leader Khamenei, with the ceremony running from July 4th through the 9th. Khamenei was killed in U.S. and Israeli airstrikes conducted in February of this year. Iran reports that between 15 and 20 million people came to pay their respects, with representatives from 30 countries also in attendance. Due to the funeral proceedings, the United States and Iran have suspended negotiations for one week, with talks scheduled to resume on July 9th after the ceremony concludes.

白宮將訂AI新規則 大公司搶著配合

來源：AI Tools Recap

美國白宮正在和OpenAI、Google、Anthropic三家公司商量，想訂出一套大家自願遵守的規則，管理最新、最強的AI要怎麼公開發布，最快下週就會宣布。

為了討好政府，OpenAI提議把公司百分之五的股份送給美國政府，這些股份大約值四百多億美元。

The U.S. White House is working with OpenAI, Google, and Anthropic to establish a set of voluntary rules governing how the latest and most powerful AI systems should be publicly released, with an announcement expected as early as next week.

To curry favor with the government, OpenAI has proposed donating 5 percent of the company's shares to the U.S. government, valued at approximately $40 billion.

派拉蒙併購華納 英國成最後關卡

來源：Deadline

美國影視公司派拉蒙要花大錢買下華納兄弟。這樁生意已經通過美國政府審查，歐盟也快要同意，現在只剩英國這一關。派拉蒙必須在七月六日前，向英國政府說明清楚。

這是最近媒體公司大合併潮的一部分。之前串流龍頭Netflix也想買華納，最後沒有成功。

American entertainment company Paramount is planning to spend big to acquire Warner Bros. The deal has already cleared U.S. government review, the EU is close to approving it, and now only Britain stands in the way. Paramount must make its case to the British government by July 6th.

This is part of a recent wave of major media company consolidation. Earlier, streaming giant Netflix also attempted to acquire Warner Bros., but ultimately failed.

維州禁賣定位資料 保護隱私

來源：Hacker News

美國維吉尼亞州通過新法律，禁止買賣民眾精準的所在位置（定位）資料，這是全美最嚴格的隱私（個人祕密）保護規定之一，七月一日開始生效。

美國沒有全國性的隱私法，所以常常由各州自己訂規則。這條新法讓專門買賣位置資料的公司，很難再做這種生意。

The U.S. state of Virginia has passed new legislation banning the buying and selling of precise location data about residents—one of the nation's strictest privacy protections to date, effective July 1st. Since the United States lacks a federal privacy law, individual states typically set their own rules. The new law will make it extremely difficult for companies that specialize in trading location data to continue operating.

公牛簽下明星後衛鮑威爾 兩年四千五百萬

來源：NBA.com

芝加哥公牛隊和明星後衛鮑威爾（Norman Powell）談成一份兩年、四千五百萬美元的合約。鮑威爾上季在邁阿密熱火隊，平均每場得二十一點七分，第一次入選明星賽。

這筆簽約也把公牛隊剩下的薪水空間幾乎用光了。

The Chicago Bulls and star guard Norman Powell have agreed to a two-year, $45 million contract. Last season with the Miami Heat, Powell averaged 21.7 points per game and made his first All-Star selection. The signing has nearly exhausted the Bulls' remaining salary cap space.

兵工廠線——阿森納（兵工廠）足球隊宣布，由比利時人德梅斯查克（Pascal De Maesschalck）擔任新的青訓（培養年輕球員）總監，八月一日正式上任。

Arsenal has announced that Belgian Pascal De Maesschalck will become the new youth academy director, officially starting on August 1st. He replaces the fan-favorite veteran Per Mertesacker. Arsenal's youth academy is renowned for developing several first-team stars, including Saka.
