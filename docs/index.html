OpenAI 關掉 Sora 影片工具，迪士尼十億合作也沒了

來源：Variety

OpenAI 在三月二十四日宣布，關掉他們的 AI 影片工具 Sora。這個工具才上線六個月，結果每天要花一千五百萬美元來跑，但總共只賺了兩百一十萬。做一段十秒的影片就要 130 美元，太貴了。

更大的問題是，迪士尼本來要投資十億美元，讓 Sora 用迪士尼和漫威的角色，結果這筆錢也沒了。OpenAI 說他們會把電腦資源轉去做機器人。

OpenAI announced on March 24th that they're shutting down Sora, their AI video tool. The tool had only been live for six months, but it was burning through 15 million dollars a day while generating just 2.1 million in revenue. Creating a ten-second video cost $130 — way too expensive.

The bigger blow was Disney. Disney was planning to invest a billion dollars to let Sora use Disney and Marvel characters, but that deal evaporated. OpenAI said they're redirecting the computing resources toward robotics instead.

法院首次判定 Meta 和 YouTube 讓小孩上網成癮

來源：The New York Times

洛杉磯的法院在三月二十五日做出歷史性的判決：Meta 和 YouTube 讓年輕人上網站成癮，要負法律責任。這是全世界第一次有這樣的結果。

受害人六歲就開始用 YouTube，九歲用 Instagram，有時一天看十六個小時。結果得了焦慮症和憂鬱症。Meta 內部文件也顯示，他們早就知道十三歲以下的小孩在用。法院判賠六百萬美元，還有近兩千件類似的案子在等。

A Los Angeles court made a historic ruling on March 25th: Meta and YouTube are legally responsible for addicting young people to their platforms. This is the first time in the world that such a verdict has been reached.

One victim started using YouTube at age six and Instagram at nine, sometimes watching for sixteen hours a day. As a result, they developed anxiety and depression. Internal Meta documents also showed the company knew children under thirteen were using their platform. The court ordered a $6 million payout, and nearly two thousand similar cases are pending.

小熊的大實驗：新人 Shaw 打右外野

來源：Chicago Tribune

小熊隊今天開幕戰對華盛頓，做了一個很大膽的決定。2023 年第一輪選的新人 Matt Shaw 被放到右外野，但他以前從來沒打過這個位子！

因為鈴木誠也膝蓋受傷上了傷兵名單，球隊只好想辦法。今年的先發陣容有很多年輕新人，像 Shaw、Crow-Armstrong 和 Ballesteros 都先發。球迷很期待看結果怎麼樣。

The Cubs played their opening game against Washington today and made a very bold decision. Matt Shaw, a first-round pick in 2023, was put in right field, but he has never played that position before!

Because Seiya Suzuki went on the injured list with a knee injury, the team had to figure something out. The starting lineup this year has lots of young rookies — Shaw, Crow-Armstrong, and Ballesteros all started. Fans are very eager to see how it turns out.

深海發現 24 種新生物，但採礦工廠也要來了

來源：ZooKeys

科學家在太平洋深海發現了 24 種全新的小生物。這個海底區域在夏威夷和墨西哥之間，面積有六百萬平方公里。研究員說，那裡超過九成的生物還沒有名字。

不過問題來了。今年一月，美國改了法律，讓採礦工廠可以同時申請探查和開採。之前的測試顯示，海底機器工作兩個月後，生物數量少了三成多。科學家正在跟時間賽跑。

Scientists discovered 24 brand-new tiny creatures in the deep Pacific Ocean. This underwater region sits between Hawaii and Mexico and spans six million square kilometers. Researchers say over 90 percent of the creatures living there don't have names yet.

But here's the catch. In January this year, the United States changed its laws to let mining factories apply for exploration and extraction at the same time. Earlier tests showed that after underwater machines worked for two months, the number of creatures dropped by more than 30 percent. Scientists are now in a race against time.

為什麼控制室都是淡綠色的？

來源：99% Invisible

你有沒有注意過，很多工廠、醫院和太空中心的牆都是淡綠色的？這要從一個叫 Faber Birren 的人說起。他是研究顏色的專家，年輕時還把自己房間漆成紅色來做實驗！

後來他幫軍隊的核武工廠選顏色。他發現淡綠色在光的中間，眼睛看了最不會累。1944 年安全委員會採用了他的顏色規定，從此全世界的控制室都跟著用淡綠色了。他說過：「顏色要有用，不只是好看。」

Have you ever noticed that the walls in many factories, hospitals, and space centers are pale green? The story goes back to a man named Faber Birren. He was an expert who studied color, and when he was young, he even painted his own bedroom red as an experiment!

Later, he helped the military choose colors for its nuclear weapons factories. He discovered that pale green sits in the middle of the light spectrum, so it causes the least eye strain when you look at it. In 1944, the safety committee adopted his color standards, and from then on, control rooms around the world started using pale green. He once said: "Color should be useful, not just beautiful."
