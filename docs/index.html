密西根今天客場挑戰俄亥俄州大

來源：Fox Sports

美國大學美式足球最有名的對決「The Game」今天開打！密西根大學（6勝5敗）要到對手的主場，挑戰排名第21的俄亥俄州立大學（7勝4敗）。

這是新教練 Whittingham 的第一年。球隊輸過好幾場只差一點點的比賽，但上週在主場打敗了全勝、排名第4的 UCLA，已經拿到參加年底盃賽的資格。

所以今天比的是面子。對密西根球迷來說，打贏死對頭，比什麼都重要。

College football's most famous rivalry, "The Game," kicks off today! The University of Michigan (6-5) travels to its archrival's home stadium to take on No. 21 Ohio State (7-4).

It is the first season for new head coach Whittingham. The team has lost several games by the narrowest of margins, but last week they beat No. 4 UCLA, which had been unbeaten, at home, and have already earned a spot in a year-end bowl game.

So today is about pride. For Michigan fans, beating their bitter rival matters more than anything else.

拉亞缺席練球，兵工廠備戰曼城

來源：ArsenalNews.co.uk

兵工廠線——守門員拉亞（Raya）在和紐卡索2比2的比賽中受傷，這週沒有參加練球。星期天，兵工廠要在主場對上曼城，這場比賽會影響誰能拿到冠軍。

教練阿特塔（Arteta）說，拉亞不需要開刀，還是有機會上場。好消息是，後衛薩利巴（Saliba）傷好了，回到比賽名單。

不過，薩卡（Saka）雖然已經在練球，大約還要再等三個星期才能比賽。

The Arsenal beat: Goalkeeper Raya was injured in the 2-2 draw with Newcastle and missed training this week. On Sunday, Arsenal host Manchester City at home, a match that could decide who wins the title.

Manager Mikel Arteta says Raya does not need surgery and still has a chance to play. There is good news too: defender Saliba has recovered and is back in the squad.

Saka, meanwhile, has started training again, but he will still need about three more weeks before he can play in a match.
