新預算年度開始，美國政府沒有關門

來源：Fox News

今天是美國政府新預算年度的第一天。去年的今天，政府因為沒錢而關門，一關就是四十三天。

今年不一樣。國會九月初就通過一個短期預算，參議院九十比六、眾議院三百七十比四十八，川普九月二日就簽了字。

不過，這筆錢只撐到十二月十一日。也就是說，真正的預算大戰，要等到十一月期中選舉之後才開打。

Today marks the first day of the US government's new fiscal year. A year ago today, the government shut down due to lack of funding and remained closed for forty-three days.

This year is different. Congress passed a short-term budget in early September, with the Senate voting 90-6 and the House voting 370-48, and Trump signed it on September 2.

However, this funding only lasts until December 11. In other words, the real budget battle won't begin until after the November midterm elections.

美光財報大爆發，AI記憶體自己喊價

來源：CNBC

美國記憶體大廠美光九月三十日公布財報。上一季營收（賣東西收到的錢）有五百四十二億美元，快要是去年同期的五倍，也比專家預期的還高。

最搶手的是 HBM （放在 AI 晶片旁邊的特殊記憶體）。美光說，二〇二七年的 HBM 大部分已經簽約賣掉，而且價格大漲。

這代表 AI 熱潮缺的不只是晶片，記憶體工廠也能決定誰拿到貨、要付多少錢。美光預估下一季營收會到六百一十五億美元。

US memory chipmaker Micron reported earnings on September 30th. Last quarter's revenue came to $54.2 billion—nearly five times the prior year and higher than analyst forecasts.

The breakout is HBM, or high-bandwidth memory used alongside AI chips. Micron says most of its 2027 HBM production is already sold under long-term contracts, with prices surging.

The takeaway is stark: the AI rush isn't just about chip shortages—memory manufacturers now control who gets supply and what they charge. Micron projects next quarter's revenue will reach $61.5 billion.

小熊被教士橫掃，季後賽兩場就結束

來源：MLB.com

小熊隊的季後賽很快就結束了。在三戰兩勝的外卡系列賽，聖地牙哥教士隊兩場全勝，把小熊淘汰出局。

第一場教士以八比零大勝，球星塔提斯和馬查多一共打出兩支全壘打。第二場小熊又以一比四輸球。

小熊的打線完全冷掉：克羅阿姆斯壯和布雷格曼兩人上場打擊十五次，一支安打都沒有，還被三振八次。教士隊下一輪將到密爾瓦基挑戰釀酒人隊。

The Cubs' postseason came to an abrupt end. In the wild card series—a best-of-three matchup—the San Diego Padres swept both games decisively, eliminating the Cubs from the playoffs.

The Padres dominated Game 1 with an 8-0 blowout victory, with stars Tatis and Machado combining for two home runs. The Cubs fell again in Game 2, losing 1-4.

The Cubs' lineup went completely cold. Two batters combined for 15 at-bats without recording a single hit and struck out eight times. The Padres advance to Milwaukee in the next round to face the Brewers.

兵工廠線：十月賽程超忙，三週踢六場

來源：Premier League

兵工廠線——國家隊比賽週還沒結束，兵工廠已經要準備一個很忙的十月。從十月十日到三十一日，球隊要踢六場比賽。

英超先在主場對里茲聯，再去客場踢諾丁漢森林，回家迎戰艾佛頓，月底還要作客利物浦。歐冠則有主場對里爾，和客場挑戰拜仁慕尼黑。

麻煩的是，哈弗茲、萊斯和厄德高都有傷在身。上一場零比三輸給布萊頓之後，這六場會是阿特塔的大考驗。

Arsenal beat — with the international break barely concluded, the squad enters a grueling October. From October 10 through 31, they face six matches.

In the Premier League, Leeds arrive first, followed by a trip to Nottingham Forest, a home fixture against Everton, and a visit to Liverpool to close the month. Europe brings Champions League action: Lille at home and a formidable away test at Bayern Munich.

The problem: Havertz, Rice, and Ödegaard are all carrying injuries. Following the 3-0 defeat to Brighton, these six matches represent a crucial test for Arteta.
