美國轟炸伊朗哈格島，戰爭進入第17天

來源：Washington Post / NPR / Al Jazeera

美國上週轟炸了伊朗最重要的石油出口港——哈格島。伊朗九成的原油都從這裡送出去。Trump 說這是「中東歷史上最強的轟炸之一」，打了超過90個軍事目標。

伊朗的革命衛隊關閉了荷莫茲海峽（全球20%的海上石油經過這裡），國際油價從70美元飆到119美元。目前已有超過2300人死亡。俄羅斯和中國要求聯合國開緊急會議，但伊朗外長說：「我們從來沒要求停火。」

The United States bombed Kharg Island last week, Iran's most important crude oil export port. Ninety percent of Iran's crude oil passes through here. Trump called it "one of the strongest bombings in Middle Eastern history," striking over 90 military targets.

Iran's Revolutionary Guard closed the Strait of Hormuz (through which 20 percent of the world's seaborne oil passes), sending international oil prices skyrocketing from $70 to $119 per barrel. More than 2,300 people have died so far. Russia and China have called for an emergency UN meeting, but Iran's Foreign Minister said: "We have never requested a ceasefire."

密西根狼獾隊拿下NCAA錦標賽第一種子

來源：MGoBlog / ESPN / Detroit News

密西根大學今年打出31勝3敗的好成績，拿到了 NCAA 三月瘋狂錦標賽中西區的第一種子，這是2021年以來第一次！他們在 Big Ten 聯盟打出19勝1敗，客場全勝，是1976年以來第一支做到的球隊。

主力球員 Lendeborg 拿下 Big Ten 年度最佳球員，教練 Dusty May 也拿到最佳教練。雖然聯盟決賽輸給了 Purdue，但整體表現非常強。週四在 Buffalo 開打，Go Blue！

University of Michigan had a fantastic 31-3 record this year and earned the #1 seed in the Midwest region of the NCAA tournament — the first time since 2021! In the Big Ten conference, they went 19-1, and they were undefeated on the road — the first team to do so since 1976.

Star player Lendeborg won Big Ten Player of the Year, and coach Dusty May took home Coach of the Year. Although they lost in the conference championship to Purdue, their overall performance was very strong. The tournament starts Thursday in Buffalo. Go Blue!

伊利諾州今天初選，參議員席位四十年首次開放

來源：ABC7 Chicago / WBEZ / The Hill

伊利諾州今天舉行初選（primary），最受注意的是聯邦參議員的選舉。老參議員 Dick Durbin 做了五屆後退休，這是大約四十年來第一次有空位。

民主黨有三個主要候選人：Krishnamoorthi 錢最多，但副州長 Stratton 最近民調領先，得到州長的支持。如果 Stratton 或 Kelly 當選，會打破參議院非裔女性人數的紀錄。不過芝加哥今天下雪，投票率可能只有15%左右。

Illinois held its primary election today, with the most attention focused on the race for U.S. Senate. Long-serving Senator Dick Durbin retired after five terms, creating an open seat for the first time in roughly forty years.

The Democratic primary features three main candidates: Krishnamoorthi has the most campaign funds, but Lieutenant Governor Stratton has taken a recent polling lead and secured the governor's endorsement. If either Stratton or Kelly wins, it would set a record for the number of Black women serving in the Senate. Heavy snow in Chicago today, however, may keep turnout around just 15%.

賭徒因伊朗飛彈新聞威脅要殺記者

來源：Times of Israel / The Block / CNN

Polymarket 是一個可以用真錢賭新聞結果的網站。上面有一個一千五百萬美元的賭盤（賭注池），賭伊朗飛彈有沒有打中以色列。一位以色列記者報導說飛彈落在耶路撒冷附近，結果賭「沒打中」的人快要輸大錢了。

這些賭徒先要求記者改報導，被拒絕後就寄死亡威脅，還附上他家的地址。Polymarket 已經封鎖了這些帳號，美國參議員也在推動立法禁止這類賭戰爭的行為。

Polymarket is a website where people can bet real money on news outcomes. The site has a $15 million betting pool wagering on whether Iranian missiles hit Israel. When an Israeli journalist reported that missiles landed near Jerusalem, bettors who wagered "no hit" stood to lose enormous sums.

These bettors first demanded the journalist change his reporting. When refused, they sent death threats that included his home address. Polymarket has since suspended these accounts, and U.S. senators are pushing legislation to ban this type of war betting.

研究：貪腐對民主國家的傷害比獨裁國家更大

來源：Frontiers in Political Science / Hacker News

一篇新研究調查了62個國家，發現一件很有趣的事：同樣程度的貪腐（corruption），在民主國家對人與人之間的信任造成的傷害，比在獨裁國家更嚴重。

為什麼？因為民主國家強調公平，所以貪腐讓人覺得被背叛（betrayed）。而且貪腐的官員是大家選出來的，人民會覺得自己也有責任，就更不信任別人。在獨裁國家，大家覺得貪腐是上面的事，跟一般人無關。很矛盾吧？

A new study examining 62 countries uncovered something fascinating: the same level of corruption inflicts greater damage to interpersonal trust in democracies than it does in autocracies.

Why? Because democracies emphasize fairness, so corruption makes people feel betrayed. And because the corrupt officials were chosen by the public, citizens feel they bear some responsibility — which erodes their trust in others even more. In autocracies, people view corruption as something happening at the top, disconnected from ordinary citizens. Pretty ironic, isn't it?
