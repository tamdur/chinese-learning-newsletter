伊朗攻擊美軍基地　美國反擊

來源：CNBC / NPR

伊朗和美國的戰爭變得更嚴重了。六月六日，伊朗朝科威特和巴林（兩個中東國家）的美軍基地發射了七枚飛彈，還放出很多無人機。

飛彈打中科威特的機場，造成一人死亡。美軍擊落無人機後，立刻出兵反擊伊朗。這場仗越打越大，恐怕會把更多國家捲進來。

The conflict between Iran and the United States has escalated. On June 6th, Iran launched seven missiles at U.S. military bases in Kuwait and Bahrain—two Middle Eastern nations—and also deployed numerous drones. The missiles struck Kuwait's airport, killing one person. After U.S. forces shot down the drones, they immediately launched a counterattack against Iran. The fighting is intensifying, and there's concern it could pull more countries into the conflict.

芝加哥熊隊要搬到印第安納

來源：Chicago Sun-Times

芝加哥熊隊（美式足球隊）要離開伊利諾州了。六月五日，球隊的董事會投票決定，要在隔壁的印第安納州哈蒙德市蓋一座新球場。

新球場要花二十億美元，預計二○三一年啟用。熊隊成立一百零六年，這是第一次把主場搬出伊利諾州。

The Chicago Bears are leaving Illinois. On June 5th, the team's board of directors voted to build a new stadium in Hammond, Indiana, just across the border. The new stadium will cost $2 billion and is expected to open in 2031. For the first time in its 106-year history, the Bears are moving their home out of Illinois.

美國參議院通過移民執法大案

來源：NPR

美國參議院開了一整晚的會，最後以五十二票對四十七票，通過了一個七百億美元的移民執法法案。

這筆錢要用來擴大邊境和移民的管理，是近幾年最大的一筆。接下來要送到眾議院表決，能不能過關還不一定。

The U.S. Senate held an all-night session and passed a $70 billion immigration enforcement bill by a vote of 52 to 47. The funding is designated for expanded border and immigration management, marking the largest allocation in recent years. The bill now heads to the House for a vote, where passage remains uncertain.

印度年輕人上街抗議

來源：France 24 / CNN

印度有一群年輕人組了一個叫「蟑螂黨」的運動，在網路上有超過兩千兩百萬人追蹤。

六月六日，幾百個人第一次走上新德里的街頭，要教育部長下台，因為全國考試的題目外洩了。網路上的聲音，真的變成了街頭的力量。

India has a youth movement called the "Cockroach Party" that has amassed over 22 million followers online. On June 6th, hundreds of members took to the streets of New Delhi for the first time, demanding the education minister's resignation over leaked exam questions from a national test. Online voices have transformed into real street power.

他在第九局兩人出局時轟出追平的全壘打。第十局，布許的一支安打送回致勝分，小熊贏下這個系列賽。

The Chicago Cubs defeated the San Francisco Giants 3-2 in extra innings on June 6th. Star Pete Crow-Armstrong went 4-for-5 with two home runs. He hit a game-tying home run in the ninth inning with two outs. In the tenth inning, a single by Busch brought in the winning run, and the Cubs took the series.

兩隊在正規時間一比一平手，哈弗茨幫兵工廠進球，德姆貝萊幫巴黎追平。最後靠點球分勝負，巴黎四比三獲勝，連續兩年拿下冠軍。
