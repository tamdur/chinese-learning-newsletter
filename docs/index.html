川普飛往北京　與習近平舉行高風險峰會

來源：CNBC

美國總統川普五月十三日出發前往北京，與中國領導人習近平會面。這是美中關稅（進口貨物的稅）大戰以來最重要的一次見面。

這次峰會要談的事情很多：貿易、台灣、伊朗戰爭。美國物價上漲了百分之三點八，國內壓力也不小。Tesla的馬斯克和Apple的庫克也會跟著去。

來源：CNBC

US President Trump departed for Beijing on May 13th to meet with Chinese leader Xi Jinping. This is the most significant meeting between the two countries since the US-China tariff war began. The summit has a full agenda: trade, Taiwan, and the Iran conflict. With US inflation at 3.8 percent, there's considerable domestic pressure back home. Tesla's Musk and Apple's Cook are traveling with the delegation. Source: CNBC

Meta宣布裁員八千人　全力轉向AI

來源：The Next Web

Meta（Facebook的母公司）宣布五月二十日起裁掉大約八千名員工，占全公司的百分之十。老闆祖克柏說，公司需要更多人來做AI，而不是做AI可以代替的工作。

另外，Meta還取消了六千個本來要招的新職位。今年科技業已經裁了超過九萬兩千人，AI正在改變整個行業。

來源：The Next Web

Meta, Facebook's parent company, announced on May 20th it would lay off approximately 8,000 employees, representing 10 percent of the total workforce. CEO Zuckerberg said the company needs more people to work on AI rather than doing jobs that AI can replace. Additionally, Meta canceled 6,000 planned new hires. The tech industry has already cut over 92,000 jobs this year as AI reshapes the entire sector. Source: The Next Web

上訴法院暫時恢復川普百分之十全球關稅

美國上訴法院五月十二日決定暫時讓川普的百分之十全球關稅繼續有效。上週另一個法院才說這個稅不合法，現在又被暫停了。

這等於法律上按了一個暫停鍵，讓法官有時間好好想。雙方必須在一週內交出書面意見。關稅的法律戰還沒結束。

來源：ABC News

A U.S. appeals court ruled on May 12 that Trump's 10 percent global tariff can remain in effect temporarily. Last week, another court had said the tariff was illegal, but now it has been suspended again. This is essentially a legal pause button, giving the judge time to think carefully. Both sides must file written arguments within a week. The legal battle over the tariff is far from over. Source: ABC News

芝加哥紅線延伸動工　南區等了三十年

來源：Construction Dive

芝加哥的紅線地鐵延伸工程正式動工了！這個計畫花費五十七億美元，是CTA（芝加哥公車局）有史以來最大的工程。

新路線從九十五街往南延伸到一百三十街，全長約九公里，設四個新站。南區居民等了超過三十年，預計二零三零年完工。

來源：Construction Dive

Chicago's Red Line extension project has officially broken ground. The initiative, costing $5.7 billion, is the largest undertaking in CTA (Chicago Transit Authority) history. The new line extends southward from 95th Street to 130th Street, spanning approximately 9 kilometers with four new stations. South Side residents have waited over three decades for this project, with completion expected in 2030. Source: Construction Dive

Source: ESPN

Ben White膝傷報銷　無緣歐冠決賽

來源：Sky Sports

兵工廠線——兵工廠（Arsenal）確認後衛Ben White在對西漢姆的比賽中傷了膝蓋韌帶（連接骨頭的帶子），整個賽季報銷。

他會錯過五月三十日對PSG（巴黎聖日耳曼）的歐冠（歐洲冠軍聯賽）決賽，這是兵工廠二十年來最大的比賽。球隊還剩兩場英超就快拿到冠軍了，但少了White是一大打擊。

來源：Sky Sports

Arsenal Beat — Arsenal have confirmed that defender Ben White suffered a torn knee ligament (the connective tissue that holds bones together) during the match against West Ham and will miss the remainder of the season. He will miss the Champions League final against PSG (Paris Saint-Germain) on May 30th, the biggest match Arsenal have played in twenty years. With just two Premier League matches left before clinching the title, losing White is a significant blow to the team. Source: Sky Sports
