美最高法院：在美國出生就是公民

來源：AP / Reuters

六月三十日，美國最高法院以六比三，否決了川普總統的命令。川普本來想取消「出生公民權」。

首席大法官羅伯茲說，憲法第十四修正案早就保證，只要在美國出生，不管爸媽是誰，幾乎都是公民。這個看法已經用了一百多年。

On June 30th, the U.S. Supreme Court voted 6-3 to reject President Trump's executive order. Trump had sought to eliminate birthright citizenship.

Chief Justice Roberts stated that the 14th Amendment to the Constitution has long guaranteed that anyone born in the United States is a citizen, regardless of their parents' identity. This interpretation has been applied for over a century.

美國老人保險今起給付減肥藥

來源：AP

從七月一日起，美國的老人健康保險（Medicare）第一次給付減肥藥，像是大家聽過的「瘦瘦針」Ozempic和Wegovy。

以前這些藥很貴，只有糖尿病病人才給付。現在為了減肥也可以用，每個月大約自己付五十美元，好幾百萬老人受益。

Starting today, July 1st, U.S. Medicare is covering weight-loss drugs for the first time, including the widely-known "semaglutide shots" Ozempic and Wegovy.

These medications were previously expensive and only covered for diabetic patients. Now they're available for weight loss as well, with seniors paying around fifty dollars per month out-of-pocket. Millions of older Americans stand to benefit.

伊朗阿曼要在海峽收過路費

來源：ANI

伊朗和阿曼兩國打算在荷莫茲海峽向來往的船收「過路費」。這個海峽很重要，全世界大約五分之一的石油都從這裡經過。

美國很反對。有趣的是，阿曼說這費用是自願付，伊朗卻說一定要付，兩國說法不一樣。

Iran and Oman are planning to collect "passage fees" from ships transiting the Strait of Hormuz. This waterway is critical to global trade — roughly one-fifth of the world's oil passes through it.

The United States is firmly opposed. Interestingly, Oman says the fee would be voluntary, while Iran insists it's mandatory — the two countries are sending conflicting signals.

普丁承認俄國鬧油荒

來源：Reuters

俄國總統普丁很少說國內有困難，但這次他承認，烏克蘭一直用無人機攻擊俄國的煉油廠，害得汽油不夠用。

網路上流傳很多影片，加油站大排長龍，還有人吵架。打仗兩年多，戰爭帶來的麻煩已經蓋不住了。

Russian President Putin rarely admits to domestic difficulties, but this time he acknowledged that Ukraine has been using drones to attack Russian oil refineries, causing a fuel shortage.

Videos circulating online show long lines at gas stations and people arguing. After more than two years of war, the troubles brought by the conflict can no longer be hidden.

公牛隊自由市場開張忙補強

來源：NBC Sports Chicago

NBA的自由球員市場一開張，芝加哥公牛隊就動作頻頻。他們放走後衛瓊斯，和柯林斯簽下兩年一千七百萬美元的合約。

公牛隊還有大約三千一百萬美元可以花，聽說還想找庫明加和鮑威爾，想快點把球隊重建起來。

With NBA free agency in full swing, the Chicago Bulls have been actively making moves. They let go of guard Jones and signed Collins to a two-year, $17 million deal.

The Bulls still have roughly $31 million to spend and are reportedly interested in Kuminga and Powell, hoping to quickly rebuild the team.

兵工廠正式簽下後衛伊卡佩

來源：Arsenal.com / The Athletic

兵工廠線——七月一日，兵工廠（阿森納）正式簽下厄瓜多後衛伊卡佩。球隊花了三千四百五十萬英鎊，和他簽了五年合約，到二〇三一年。

今年二十四歲的伊卡佩，上個球季用租借的方式上場三十九次，幫球隊拿下睽違（等了好久）二十二年的聯賽冠軍。他是今年夏天第一個確定的新人。

Arsenal Line — On July 1st, Arsenal officially signed Ecuadorian defender Hincapié. The club paid £34.5 million and signed him to a five-year contract through 2031.

The 24-year-old Hincapié made 39 appearances on loan last season, helping the team secure the league title for the first time in 22 years. He is the first confirmed new signing for the club this summer.
