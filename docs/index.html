美國和以色列攻打伊朗，油價飆破百元

來源：Al Jazeera

上個月，美國和以色列一起對伊朗發動攻擊，打了九十多個軍事目標。伊朗反擊之後，宣布關閉荷莫茲海峽（全世界最重要的石油運輸通道）。亞洲八成的石油進口都經過那裡，所以油價馬上衝到每桶 126 美元，現在大約 103 美元。

對台灣來說還有一個問題：卡達供應全球三分之一的氦（hé）氣，做晶片一定要用，但伊朗已經攻擊了卡達的設施。大家要注意接下來的影響。

Last month, the United States and Israel launched a joint attack on Iran, striking over ninety military targets. After Iran retaliated, it announced the closure of the Strait of Hormuz—the world's most critical oil shipping channel. Since eighty percent of Asia's oil imports pass through there, oil prices immediately spiked to $126 per barrel; they're now around $103.

For Taiwan, there's an additional concern: Qatar supplies one-third of the world's helium gas, which is essential for chip manufacturing, but Iran has already attacked Qatari facilities. People should watch closely for the fallout.

密西根大學籃球拿下NCAA第一種子

來源：ESPN

密西根大學今年打出 31 勝 3 敗的好成績，拿到 NCAA 錦標賽中西區的第一種子！這是 2021 年以來第一次。他們在大十聯盟打出 19 勝 1 敗，客場全勝，五十年來第一次。

這支球隊很特別，同時派三個身高超過 206 公分的大個子上場。明星球員 Lendeborg 拿了聯盟年度最佳球員，不過他腳踝有點受傷，希望三月十九號首場比賽能上場。Go Blue！

The University of Michigan basketball team had an outstanding season with a 31-3 record, earning the #1 seed in the NCAA tournament's Midwest region! This is their first time achieving this since 2021. They posted a 19-1 record in the Big Ten conference and went undefeated on the road—the first time in fifty years.

This team is special because they can field three players over 206 centimeters tall on the court at the same time. Star player Lendeborg won the Big Ten player of the year award, though he has a slight ankle injury and hopefully will be able to play in their first game on March 19th. Go Blue!

GPT-5.4 會自己操作電腦了

來源：TechCrunch

OpenAI 上週發布了 GPT-5.4，最大的新功能是「電腦操作」——它可以自己動滑鼠、打字，幫你完成電腦上的工作。在測試裡，它的分數是 75%，比人類專家的 72.4% 還高。

不過每家公司各有強項：Google 的 Gemini 3.1 在整體智力測試跟 GPT-5.4 打平，而 Claude Opus 4.6 在寫程式方面還是最強的。AI 的競爭越來越激烈了。

OpenAI released GPT-5.4 last week, with the biggest new feature being "computer operation"—it can move the mouse and type on its own to help you complete tasks on your computer. In testing, it scored 75%, higher than human experts' 72.4%.

However, each company has its own strengths: Google's Gemini 3.1 ties with GPT-5.4 on overall intelligence tests, while Claude Opus 4.6 remains the strongest at writing code. The competition in AI is getting more and more intense.

美國可能取消公司每季財報

來源：Reuters

美國 SEC（證券管理機關）正在考慮一個大改變：上市公司不用再每三個月公布一次財務報告，改成半年一次就好。這個規定從 1970 年就有了，如果改了會很大。

支持的人說，每季報告讓公司只看短期成績，不敢做長期計畫。反對的人擔心，一般投資人會更難了解公司的狀況。英國 2014 年取消過，結果分析師對那些公司的關注變少了。

The U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission is considering a major change: publicly listed companies would no longer need to publish financial reports every three months. Instead, they'd release them twice a year. This requirement has been in place since 1970, so changing it would be significant.

Supporters argue that quarterly reports push companies to focus only on short-term results, discouraging long-term planning. Critics worry that ordinary investors would have a harder time understanding a company's situation. Britain eliminated this requirement in 2014, and the result was that analysts paid less attention to those companies.

小行星上找到生命的材料

來源：Nature Astronomy

日本的「隼鳥二號」太空船從龍宮小行星帶回了一些土壤樣本。科學家昨天在《自然》期刊上公布：裡面找到了 DNA 和 RNA 需要的全部五種基本成分。

這很重要，因為這些樣本是在太空中密封帶回來的，沒有被地球汙染。這代表什麼？也許很久以前，小行星掉到地球上，帶來了生命最早的材料。日本的太空科學又立了一個大功。

Japan's Hayabusa2 spacecraft brought back soil samples from the asteroid Ryugu. Scientists announced yesterday in the journal Nature: they found all five basic components needed for DNA and RNA.

This is important because these samples were sealed in space during their journey back and weren't contaminated by Earth. What does this mean? Perhaps long ago, an asteroid crashed into Earth and brought with it the earliest materials for life. Japan's space science has scored another major victory.
