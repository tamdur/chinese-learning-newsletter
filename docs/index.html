美國經濟亮紅燈：成長放慢、物價還在漲

來源：MarketMinute

美國經濟最近的數字不太好看。GDP成長率被下修到只有0.7%，非常低。同時，物價還是一直在漲，核心通膨（inflation）卡在3.1%不動。這種「經濟不成長但物價一直漲」的情況叫做滯脹（stagflation），是最讓人頭痛的。

更麻煩的是，因為伊朗衝突和荷莫茲海峽（Strait of Hormuz）關閉，油價已經快到每桶一百美元。Goldman Sachs把美國經濟衰退的機率提高到25%。接下來幾個月要小心了。

The latest US economic figures don't look good. GDP growth has been revised down to just 0.7%, which is very low. At the same time, prices keep rising, with core inflation stuck at 3.1%. This situation of "economic stagnation combined with persistent price increases" is called stagflation, and it's the most troublesome scenario.

To make matters worse, due to the Iran conflict and the closure of the Strait of Hormuz, oil prices have climbed close to $100 per barrel. Goldman Sachs has raised the probability of a US economic recession to 25%. We need to be careful over the coming months.

Center Aday Mara had the best performance, scoring 17 points. Although Michigan is the number one seed, this game was very difficult. In their next game, they will face Wisconsin and hope to play more smoothly. March Madness is just getting started!

AI報導越來越像在講道德問題

來源：TechPulse

一項新研究分析了將近七萬篇關於AI的新聞標題，發現一個有趣的現象：媒體討論AI的方式，越來越像在討論對錯的道德問題，而不是科技問題。這種「道德化」的程度，已經跟過去報導基改食品（GMO）和疫苗差不多了。

這件事很重要，因為當大家把AI當成道德議題來看，就會影響政府怎麼管、民眾怎麼想。AI到底是工具還是威脅？答案可能取決於新聞怎麼寫。

A new study analyzing nearly 70,000 news headlines about AI has uncovered an interesting phenomenon: the way media discusses AI increasingly resembles discussions of right and wrong—moral questions—rather than technological ones. The degree of this "moralization" now matches what we've seen in past coverage of genetically modified foods (GMOs) and vaccines.

This matters because when people view AI through a moral lens, it shapes how governments regulate it and what the public thinks. Is AI a tool or a threat? The answer may depend on how the news tells the story.

為什麼天然氣能決定你的電費？

來源：EnergyBrief

伊朗衝突讓天然氣價格大漲，很多國家的電費也跟著漲。但你可能會問：明明有太陽能、風力這些便宜的發電方式，電費為什麼還是跟著天然氣跑？

原因是電力市場用「邊際定價」的方式來算電價。簡單說，每天最後一批被叫來發電的通常是天然氣電廠。而市場規則是：所有電的價格都跟著最貴那一批走。所以就算大部分電來自便宜的能源，天然氣還是能把整體電價拉高。

The Iran conflict sent natural gas prices soaring, and electricity bills in many countries jumped right along with them. But you might ask: if we have cheap ways to generate power like solar and wind, why does the electricity bill still follow natural gas up?

The reason is that electricity markets use a system called "marginal pricing" to calculate electricity rates. Put simply, the last batch of power plants called to generate electricity each day is usually natural gas plants. And the market rule is: all electricity prices follow the price of the most expensive batch. So even if most of the electricity comes from cheap sources, natural gas can still push the overall electricity price higher.

天文學家從爆炸的星星聽到奇怪的「啾啾聲」

來源：CosmosWatch

天文學家發現了一件很奇怪的事。他們在觀察一顆爆炸中的恆星（就是超新星）時，偵測到一種越來越快的訊號，聽起來像「啾」的一聲，科學家叫它chirp。

奇怪的地方在於，這種chirp訊號以前只有在黑洞互相撞擊的時候才會出現。現在竟然在一顆普通的爆炸恆星上看到了。這個發現讓科學家很興奮，因為它可能改變我們對恆星爆炸的理解，也許宇宙裡還有很多我們不知道的事。

Astronomers have discovered something very strange. While observing an exploding star (known as a supernova), they detected a signal that grew increasingly rapid, sounding like a "chirp" — what scientists call a chirp signal.

What's puzzling is that this chirp signal had previously only appeared when black holes collided with each other. Now scientists have spotted it on an ordinary exploding star. This discovery has excited the scientific community because it could change our understanding of stellar explosions and suggests there may be many things about the universe we still don't know.
