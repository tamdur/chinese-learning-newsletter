美國和以色列打伊朗第二十一天：油價大漲，海上運油幾乎停了

來源：Al Jazeera / CNN

美國和以色列的軍隊從二月底開始打伊朗，到現在已經三個星期了。以色列軍隊打了伊朗最大的天然氣田，伊朗也打了卡達的天然氣工廠。結果很嚴重：荷莫茲海峽（全世界兩成的油從這裡走）現在幾乎沒有船在走。油價漲到一桶 115 美元，比開戰前高了八成。到現在還沒有人在談停火。

The militaries of the United States and Israel have been fighting Iran for three weeks now, having started at the end of February. The Israeli military struck Iran's largest natural gas field, and Iran struck a natural gas facility in Qatar in return. The consequences are severe: the Strait of Hormuz, through which twenty percent of the world's oil passes, now has almost no ships transiting it. Oil prices have surged to $115 per barrel, up eighty percent from before the war began. So far, no one is talking about a ceasefire.

美國聯準會不降息，鮑爾：通膨沒有像我們想的那樣下來

來源：CNBC

美國聯準會（就是管錢的地方）決定不降利率，留在 3.5% 到 3.75%。主要原因是東西還是太貴，物價沒降下來。伊朗戰爭讓油價漲很多，結果也讓所有東西更貴。二月美國少了九萬多個工作。官員說今年可能只降一次，但也有七個人說一次也不會降。消息出來以後，美國股票跌了差不多 1.4%。

The U.S. Federal Reserve decided not to cut interest rates, keeping them at 3.5% to 3.75%. The main reason is that things are still too expensive and prices haven't come down. The Iran war caused oil prices to spike a lot, which made everything more expensive. In February, the United States lost over 90,000 jobs. Officials said there might be only one rate cut this year, but seven people said there wouldn't be even one cut. After the news came out, U.S. stocks fell about 1.4%.

丹麥的方法能解決美國房貸問題嗎？

來源：Marginal Revolution

美國很多人有一個問題：他們幾年前用很低的利率買了房子，現在利率變高了，如果賣房子再買新的，要用高利率。結果大家都不想搬家，房子賣的數量少了快六成。丹麥有一個方法：你的房貸和一個債券（一種可以買賣的錢的票）連在一起。利率漲的時候，債券變便宜，你可以用少一點的錢把房貸還完，這樣搬家就不會虧了。

Many Americans face a problem: they bought their homes years ago at very low interest rates, but now rates have climbed. If they sell and buy a new house, they'd have to pay those higher rates. As a result, nobody wants to move, and home sales have dropped by nearly 60%. Denmark has a solution: your mortgage is linked to a bond — a type of tradable financial instrument. When interest rates go up, the bond becomes cheaper, so you can pay off your mortgage with less money, meaning you won't lose money if you move.

密西根大學今天打聖路易大學！全國第一的球隊能繼續贏嗎？

來源：Detroit News

全國排名第一的密西根大學籃球隊今天要打聖路易大學。密西根今年 32 勝 3 敗，在客場十場全贏，是 1976 年以來第一個做到的球隊。第一場他們 101 比 80 大贏，隊員投籃命中率高到 67%。但聖路易也不好打，他們的三分球全國第二，中鋒 Avila 也會投三分，可能把密西根的大個子 Mara 拉到外面。

The #1-ranked University of Michigan men's basketball team faces Saint Louis University today. Michigan is 32-3 this season and has won all ten games on the road — the first team to accomplish that since 1976. In their first matchup, they beat Howard 101-80 behind a dominant 67% shooting performance. But Saint Louis is no pushover: they have the nation's second-best three-point shooting, and center Avila can also shoot threes, which could pull Michigan's big man Mara out on the perimeter.

南極冰河下面發現一大塊花崗岩

來源：ScienceDaily / British Antarctic Survey

英國的科學家在南極一條很大的冰河下面發現了一塊花崗岩（一種很硬的石頭），大約一百公里寬。這條冰河叫 Pine Island Glacier，是南極退得最快的冰河之一。研究人員先在山上看到粉紅色的石頭，不是這裡的，後來用飛機量地下的重力，果然在下面找到了。花崗岩和別的石頭不一樣，會影響冰河滑動的方式，這對了解海平面上升很重要。

Scientists from the United Kingdom discovered a massive granite rock beneath a large glacier in Antarctica, approximately 100 kilometers wide. The glacier, called Pine Island Glacier, is one of the fastest retreating glaciers in Antarctica. Researchers first spotted pink-colored rocks on a mountain that didn't belong there, and later used an aircraft to measure underground gravity, which confirmed they found the granite below. Granite differs from other rock types and affects how glaciers slide, which is important for understanding sea level rise.
