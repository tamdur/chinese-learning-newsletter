伊朗關閉海峽，油價飆到一百二十六美元

來源：Dallas Fed

伊朗跟美國和以色列打起來了。二月底，伊朗軍隊關閉了荷莫茲海峽，全世界大約兩成的石油都要從這裡經過。結果油價飆到每桶一百二十六美元，這是歷史上最大的石油危機。

中國也很緊張，因為三分之一的石油要經過那裡。科威特說情況「超級嚴重」，有一百五十多艘船停在海上不能走。專家說，如果海峽繼續關閉，全世界的經濟成長會掉將近三個百分點。

Iran went to war with the United States and Israel. In late February, Iranian military forces closed the Strait of Hormuz — roughly 20% of the world's oil passes through there. As a result, oil prices surged to $126 per barrel, making this the largest oil crisis in history.

China is also very nervous, because a third of its oil comes through the strait. Kuwait called the situation "beyond catastrophic," with over 150 ships stranded at sea. Experts say if the strait stays closed, global economic growth could drop by nearly 3 percentage points.

密西根籃球隊打進十六強，週五對阿拉巴馬

來源：ESPN

密西根大學男子籃球隊今年超強，已經贏了三十三場，是全國第一種子隊。球隊的明星球員是來自多明尼加的林登伯格，大家都叫他「多明尼加的乒布朗」。

他也是今年的大十聯盟最有價值球員，上一場拿了二十五分。結果密西根輕鬆贏了。週五他們要在芝加哥打阿拉巴馬，如果贏了就是隊史最多勝場紀錄！

The University of Michigan men's basketball team is incredibly strong this year, with 33 wins already — they're the No. 1 seed in the country. The team's star player is Lendeborg from the Dominican Republic, and everyone calls him "The Dominican LeBron."

He's also this year's Big Ten Player of the Year, and scored 25 points last game. Michigan won easily as a result. On Friday they face Alabama in Chicago — if they win, it'll be the most victories in program history!

熱門程式工具遭駭客攻擊，密碼全被偷

來源：Hacker News

一個很多工程師在用的程式工具叫 LiteLLM，每天有三百多萬次下載。駭客先入侵了一個安全檢查的網站，偷到上傳的密碼，然後放了有毒的版本上去。

結果只要你裝了這個版本，電腦裡的密碼、金鑰和雲端帳號全部會被偷走。它也會在你的電腦裡裝一個後門。好在三個小時後網站就把它拿下來了。

A programming tool used by many engineers called LiteLLM gets over 3.4 million downloads per day. Hackers first broke into a security-checking site, stole upload credentials, then published a poisoned version.

As a result, if you installed that version, all the passwords, keys, and cloud account credentials on your computer would be stolen. It also installed a persistent backdoor on your machine. Fortunately, the site took it down after just three hours.

飛彈防禦有多難？數學家說：超級難

來源：Hacker News

美國軍隊在阿拉斯加有四十四枚防禦飛彈，每枚大約要七千五百萬美元。但是打中一枚敵人飛彈的機會只有五成多，所以要用四枚去打一枚。

數學家也說，怎麼分配飛彈去打目標，這個問題在數學上是「超級難」的。結果是，敵人只要射十二枚飛彈，就一定有一枚打不到。如果再加上假的飛彈，問題就更難了。

The U.S. military has 44 defense interceptors in Alaska, each costing about $75 million. But the chance of hitting a single enemy missile is only about 56%, so you need four interceptors per target.

Mathematicians also point out that the problem of how to assign interceptors to targets is "NP-complete" — extremely hard in mathematical terms. The result is that an enemy only needs to launch 12 missiles to guarantee at least one gets through. Add in decoy missiles and the problem becomes even harder.

鹽湖底下發現古老的淡水

來源：ScienceAlert

美國猶他州有一個很大的鹽湖，科學家用直升機在湖底下四公里發現了很多淡水。這些水非常古老，是幾千年前冰河時期的雪水。

研究員也發現一個奇怪的事：淡水是往湖的中間流的，跟一般的方向相反。這個鹽湖這幾年一直在縮小，露出來的湖底有毒的灰塵。科學家在研究這些地下水能不能讓湖底保持潮濕，不讓毒灰塵飛起來。

Utah has a large salt lake, and scientists used helicopters to discover a massive freshwater reservoir about 4 kilometers beneath it. This water is extremely old — snowmelt from the Ice Age thousands of years ago.

Researchers also found something strange: the freshwater flows toward the center of the lake, the opposite of the normal direction. The Great Salt Lake has been shrinking in recent years, exposing toxic dust on the lakebed. Scientists are studying whether this underground water can keep the lakebed moist to prevent toxic dust from blowing into the air.
