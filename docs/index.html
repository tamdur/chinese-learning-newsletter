美國國會通過制裁俄羅斯法案

來源：NPR

美國眾議院在九月十六日，以二百六十二票對一百五十九票，通過了一個制裁俄羅斯的法案。參議院上個月已經同意，法案現在送到總統桌上，等著簽名。

這個法案的做法很特別。它不直接處罰俄羅斯，而是針對買俄羅斯石油和天然氣最多的五個國家，最高可以收到百分之百的關稅（進口貨要交的稅）。中國和印度買得最多，壓力也最大。

The U.S. House of Representatives passed a Russia sanctions bill on September 16, voting 262 to 159. The Senate approved it last month, and the bill now awaits the president's signature. What makes this bill unusual is its approach: rather than directly penalizing Russia, it targets the five countries that purchase the most Russian oil and natural gas, with tariffs reaching as high as 100%. China and India are the largest buyers and face the most pressure.

法官擋下改名，甘迺迪中心決定關門兩年

來源：Washington Post

美國一位聯邦法官九月十五日判決，沒有國會同意，總統的名字不能加在甘迺迪中心的大樓或廣場上，因為這個名字是法律訂下來的。

判決出來幾分鐘後，由總統指派的董事會就投票決定，要把大樓關閉兩年整修，費用大約二億五千萬美元，理由是建築有安全問題。總統則說，名字不加上去，整修的錢就不會撥出來。

On September 15, a federal judge ruled that the president's name cannot be added to the Kennedy Center's building or plaza without congressional approval, since the name is established by law. Minutes after the ruling, the president-appointed board voted to close the building for two years of renovations estimated at $250 million, citing structural safety concerns.

The president responded that without adding his name, the renovation funds would not be allocated.

芝加哥租客保護條例以十二比九過關

來源：Chicago Sun-Times

芝加哥市議會的住宅委員會九月十六日以十二票對九票，通過市長強生提出的「租客保護條例」。不過他事先拿掉了房東最反對的兩件事：房東要有正當理由才能趕走房客，還有要付房客搬家費。

留下來的內容包括建立租屋登記制度、禁止亂收費，還有幫房客打官司。接下來要送全體市議會表決。

Chicago's City Council Housing Committee voted 12 to 9 on September 16 to approve Mayor Brandon Johnson's proposed "Tenant Protection Ordinance." However, the mayor had already removed two provisions that landlords most strongly opposed: requirements that landlords have just cause to evict tenants and that they cover tenant moving costs. The remaining provisions include establishing a rental registration system, prohibiting arbitrary fees, and providing legal assistance to tenants. The ordinance will next go to the full city council for a vote.

谷歌公布湯普森中心新設計

來源：Block Club Chicago

谷歌九月十六日公布芝加哥湯普森中心的新設計圖。這棟大樓一九八五年完工，本來是州政府的辦公室，二零二二年用一億零五百萬美元賣掉。

新設計把十七層樓高的中庭打開，加上大樓梯、座位和店面，廣場上還要放一座九公尺高的雕塑。預計二零二八年開放，大約兩千名員工在這裡上班，一般人也能進去走走。

On September 16, Google unveiled new designs for Chicago's Thompson Center. The building, completed in 1985 as state government offices, was sold for $105 million in 2022. The redesign will open up the 17-story atrium with grand staircases, seating areas, and retail shops, along with a nine-meter-tall sculpture in the plaza. The project is slated to open in 2028 and will house approximately 2,000 employees, while also welcoming the general public.

熊隊史威夫特剛續約就跑出好成績

來源：Bleacher Nation

芝加哥熊隊的跑衛史威夫特上週跑出一百二十四碼，還拿下三次達陣（把球帶進得分區）。熊隊從二零一六年以後，就沒有球員做到過。

更巧的是，這場比賽就在他和球隊談好三年三千三百七十五萬美元續約的兩天後。熊隊現在在國聯北區排第二，九月二十日要對上明尼蘇達。

Chicago Bears running back Swift rushed for 124 yards last week and scored three touchdowns (bringing the ball into the end zone). No Bears player had done that since 2016. More remarkably, this performance came just two days after he and the team agreed to a three-year, $33.75 million contract extension. The Bears are currently in second place in the NFC North and will face Minnesota on September 20.
