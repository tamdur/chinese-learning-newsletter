OpenAI發表GPT-5.5：AI代理時代來了

LLM Stats

OpenAI這個星期發表了新的AI模型GPT-5.5。這個模型最大的特點是它可以自己完成很多步驟的工作，不需要人一步一步地告訴它怎麼做。專家說，這代表AI正在從「工具」變成「助手」。同一個星期，Google和阿里巴巴也發表了類似的產品。整個AI產業都在朝同一個方向前進：讓AI可以獨立做更複雜的事情。

OpenAI unveiled a new AI model called GPT-5.5 this week. Its defining feature is the ability to complete multi-step tasks independently, without requiring humans to guide it through each stage. Experts say this marks AI's transition from "tool" to "assistant." Google and Alibaba released similar products the same week. The entire AI industry is moving in the same direction: enabling AI to tackle increasingly complex tasks on its own.

白宮指控中國公司偷學美國AI技術

NPR

美國白宮星期四發出一份文件，指控中國公司大量「蒸餾」（也就是偷學）美國最先進的AI模型的能力。白宮科技顧問說，這些中國公司用特別的方法把美國AI的知識抽出來，用來訓練自己的模型。同一天，美國國會也通過了一個新法案，要找出並制裁這些公司。這是美中AI競爭的新戰場——從限制晶片出口，擴大到保護AI模型本身。

The White House issued a statement on Thursday accusing Chinese companies of widespread "distillation" — essentially stealing — the capabilities of America's most advanced AI models. White House technology advisers said these Chinese companies are using specialized methods to extract knowledge from American AI systems to train their own models. On the same day, Congress also passed new legislation to identify and sanction these companies. This marks a new battlefield in U.S.-China AI competition — expanding from restricting chip exports to protecting the AI models themselves.

Google發表新一代TPU晶片：專為AI代理設計

Google / Hacker News

Google宣布了第八代TPU晶片，這是Google自己設計的AI專用處理器。這次最特別的是，新晶片專門為「代理型AI」（可以自己做決定、完成任務的AI）設計。傳統的AI晶片主要處理一問一答的對話，但新的TPU可以處理AI代理需要的長時間、多步驟運算。Google在同一個星期推出這個產品，正好和OpenAI的GPT-5.5互相呼應，顯示整個產業都在為AI代理時代做準備。

Google has announced its eighth-generation TPU chip, an AI-specific processor designed in-house. What's particularly notable this time is that the new chip is purpose-built for "agentic AI" — artificial intelligence that can make its own decisions and complete tasks autonomously. Traditional AI chips have mainly handled back-and-forth conversational exchanges, but the new TPU can handle the extended, multi-step computations that agentic AI requires. Google's timing, launching this same week as OpenAI's GPT-5.5, signals that the entire industry is preparing for the age of AI agents.

美國參議院通過七百億美元移民執法預算

NPR

美國參議院在一場通宵投票後，以五十比四十八票通過了一項預算決議。這項決議將為移民及海關執法局和邊境巡邏隊提供七百億美元的經費，為期三年半。這次投票完全按照黨派劃分——所有民主黨議員都投了反對票。這是美國歷史上最大的移民執法投資之一。法案接下來要送到眾議院，但能不能通過還不確定。

After an all-night voting session, the U.S. Senate passed a budget resolution 50-48 that will allocate $70 billion to the Immigration and Customs Enforcement agency and Border Patrol over three and a half years. The vote split entirely along party lines, with all Democrats voting against it. This represents one of the largest investments in immigration enforcement in American history. The measure now heads to the House, where its passage remains uncertain.

The Chicago Cubs defeated the Philadelphia Phillies 8-7 on Wednesday at Wrigley Field, extending their winning streak to nine games — the longest in Major League Baseball this season. In the end, Swanson delivered a walk-off single with the bases loaded, sending the crowd into a frenzy. It's the Cubs' best stretch since 2016.
