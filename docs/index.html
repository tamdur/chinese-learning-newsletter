美國最高法院：總統不能用緊急法加關稅

來源：SCOTUSblog

美國最高法院在二月做出了一個重要的決定，以六比三的票數說：總統不能用緊急經濟法來加關稅（就是進口東西要多付的錢）。結果美國的關稅從百分之十四掉到百分之七。不過 Trump 馬上用了另一個法律，又把關稅拉高到百分之十。政府也可能要退回一千多億美元給公司。網路商店和科技公司的股票大漲，但美國本地工廠的股票反而跌了。

In February, the U.S. Supreme Court made an important ruling, voting 6-3 that the president cannot use emergency economic powers to impose tariffs (the extra fees charged on imported goods). As a result, U.S. tariffs dropped from 14% to 7%. However, Trump quickly invoked another law and raised tariffs back up to 10%. The government may also need to refund over $100 billion to companies. Online retailers and tech company stocks surged, but stocks of American domestic manufacturers declined.

Anthropic 告美國國防部：我們拒做武器 AI

來源：TechCrunch

AI 公司 Anthropic 不讓自己的 AI 用在自動武器或大規模監控（偷看人民）上面。結果國防部把他們列為「有風險的公司」，幾小時後就跟 OpenAI 簽了軍事 AI 合約。Anthropic 因此告上法院，說這是報復。三十多位 OpenAI 和 Google 員工也站出來支持 Anthropic。後來 OpenAI 也在合約裡加了一樣的保護條件。法院三月二十四日會開庭。

AI company Anthropic refused to allow its AI to be used for autonomous weapons or mass surveillance (spying on people). As a result, the Defense Department labeled them a "risky company," and hours later signed a military AI contract with OpenAI instead. Anthropic sued, claiming this was retaliation. Over 30 employees from OpenAI and Google stepped forward in support. Later, OpenAI also added the same protective clause to their contract. The court will hold a hearing on March 24th.

伊朗戰爭第十九天：石油設備被炸

來源：Al Jazeera

以色列和伊朗的戰爭已經打了十九天。以色列軍隊炸了伊朗最大的天然氣田（地下有很多氣的地方），那裡供應伊朗七成的天然氣。伊朗也打回去，打了卡達、沙烏地和阿聯酋的石油設備。結果油價飆到每桶一百一十八美元，比開戰前漲了六成。到現在已有超過兩千人死亡，伊朗拒絕停火。

The war between Israel and Iran has now lasted nineteen days. The Israeli military has bombed Iran's largest natural gas field—an underground reserve that supplies seventy percent of Iran's natural gas. Iran has struck back, targeting oil facilities in Qatar, Saudi Arabia, and the United Arab Emirates. Oil prices have skyrocketed to $118 per barrel, up sixty percent since the war began. Over two thousand people have died so far, and Iran has refused to agree to a ceasefire.

奧斯汀蓋很多房子，房租就降了

來源：Pew Charitable Trusts

美國德州的奧斯汀（Austin）這幾年蓋了非常多新房子，從二零一五到二零二四年加了十二萬間。結果房租真的降了！一年內掉了百分之七，現在中間房租大約一千三百美元，比全國平均還低。他們怎麼做到的？取消停車位規定，讓房子蓋更高，也加快了蓋房的手續。不過新的建案已經在減少，未來房租可能又會漲。

Austin, Texas has built a massive number of new homes over the past few years, adding 120,000 units between 2015 and 2024. And it's working — rents actually fell. In just one year, they dropped by 7 percent. The median rent now sits around $1,300 a month, which is below the national average. How did they pull it off? They eliminated parking requirements, allowed taller buildings, and streamlined the construction approval process. The catch: new construction is already slowing down, so rents could start climbing again in the future.

熱帶泥炭地火災創兩千年新高

來源：University of Exeter / Global Change Biology

一個新的研究發現，熱帶的泥炭地（一種很濕的土，裡面存了很多碳）現在燒得比過去兩千年都嚴重。科學家看了四個大洲的泥炭裡的木炭記錄，發現火本來一千年來越來越少，但上個世紀突然大增。東南亞最嚴重，因為人把水排掉來種棕櫚（做油的樹）。火燒了會放出很多二氧化碳，讓地球更熱，然後又更容易著火。這個問題在氣候模型（電腦預測天氣的工具）裡也還沒被好好算進去。

A new study has found that tropical peatlands—a type of very wet soil containing large amounts of carbon—are now burning more severely than at any point in the past two thousand years. Scientists examined charcoal records from peat deposits across four continents and discovered that fire had actually been declining for the past thousand years, but then surged dramatically in the last century. Southeast Asia is experiencing the worst impacts because people have drained the water to plant palm trees for oil production. When fires burn peatland, they release massive amounts of carbon dioxide, making the planet hotter, which in turn makes fires more likely to occur. This problem is not yet properly accounted for in climate models—the computer tools used to predict weather patterns.
