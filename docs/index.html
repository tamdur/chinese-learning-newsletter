美國政府下令關掉最強AI

來源：CNBC

六月十五日，美國川普政府用「出口管制」的理由，命令AI公司Anthropic關掉它最厲害的兩個模型Fable 5和Mythos 5。

官員說，有人只用「修好這段程式」幾個字，就騙到AI說出怎麼攻擊電腦。公司當天和政府開會，卻沒有結果。這是美國第一次用國家安全的理由，逼公司關掉最先進的AI。

On June 15th, the Trump administration ordered AI company Anthropic to shut down its two most powerful models, Fable 5 and Mythos 5, citing export control regulations. Officials said that someone tricked the AI into explaining how to attack computers using just a few words like "fix this code." The company met with government officials that day but reached no resolution. This marks the first time the U.S. has forced a company to disable its most advanced AI on national security grounds.

美國伊朗談好停戰重開海峽

來源：NPR

打了三個多月的美伊戰爭，六月十四、十五日兩國簽了一份協議。這是巴基斯坦和阿曼幫忙談成的。

協議把停火再延六十天，重新打開荷莫茲海峽（船運石油的重要水道），也停掉黎巴嫩的戰事。六月十九日要在瑞士正式簽字。不過以色列說要繼續留兵在黎巴嫩南部，仍是難題。

After more than three months of US-Iran fighting, the two countries signed an agreement on June 14th and 15th. Pakistan and Oman helped broker the deal. The agreement extends the ceasefire for another sixty days, reopens the Strait of Hormuz (a critical shipping route for oil), and halts the fighting in Lebanon. A formal signing is scheduled for June 19th in Switzerland. However, Israel's insistence on keeping troops in southern Lebanon remains a sticking point.

歐元變紅　美元地位慢慢下滑

來源：歐洲央行

歐洲央行六月十六日的報告說，用歐元借錢和發債券的金額，比去年多了三成，超過一兆一千億美元，是歐元出現以來最高的。

同一時間，美元在國際債券的比例掉到六成，外國政府放在紐約的美國公債，也是二○一二年以來最少。看起來，美元的老大地位正在慢慢變弱。

The European Central Bank's June 16 report showed that the amount of borrowing and bond issuance in euros increased by 30% compared to last year, exceeding 1.1 trillion dollars — the highest level since the euro was introduced. At the same time, the dollar's share of international bonds fell to 60%, and foreign governments' holdings of US Treasury bonds in New York have hit their lowest level since 2012. It appears that the dollar's dominant position is gradually weakening.

美國監聽法過期　國會沒修就休會

來源：NPR

美國有一條法律叫「第七○二條」，讓政府不用法官同意，就能監聽外國人的通訊。

眾議院最後沒有通過延長這條法的案子，議員就放假回家了，法律因此過期。不過三月時批准的舊命令還有效，監聽可以先做到明年三月。

The United States has a law called "Section 702" that allows the government to monitor the communications of foreign nationals without a judge's approval. The House of Representatives failed to pass a bill extending this law, and lawmakers went home on recess, causing the legislation to expire. However, an old order approved in March remains in effect, allowing surveillance to continue until next March.

小熊球星完成完全打擊

來源：MLB.com

芝加哥小熊隊的中外野手柯羅阿姆斯壯，在主場對落磯隊的比賽，一場就打出一壘、二壘、三壘安打和全壘打，完成很難的「完全打擊」。

他是小熊隊史上第十三位做到的人，才二十四歲。那場小熊在九局逆轉，以五比四贏球。

Pete Crow-Armstrong, the Chicago Cubs' center fielder, hit for the cycle in a home game against the Rockies, collecting a single, double, triple, and home run in one game — a rare feat known as a "cycle." At just 24 years old, he became the 13th player in Cubs history to accomplish this. The Cubs rallied in the ninth inning to win 5-4.

兵工廠線——兵工廠隊這次有十五名球員入選二○二六年世界盃，是球隊的新紀錄，而且分在很多不同的國家隊。
