AMD花82億美元買下李飛飛的AI公司

來源：TechCrunch

美國晶片公司AMD宣布，要用自己的股票，花82億美元買下World Labs。這家公司是史丹佛大學教授李飛飛創立的。她被叫做「AI教母」，之後會到AMD當首席科學家。

World Labs做的是「世界模型」。這種AI不只會寫字，還能做出可以走進去的3D世界，也能幫機器人學習。

這是AMD史上第二大的收購（買下別的公司）。以前晶片公司只賣晶片給AI公司，現在AMD直接買下一家，想追上對手輝達。

US chipmaker AMD announced it will acquire World Labs for $8.2 billion using its own stock. The company was founded by Stanford University professor Fei-Fei Li, known as the "godmother of AI," who will join AMD as its chief scientist.

World Labs specializes in "world models"—a type of artificial intelligence that goes beyond text generation. This technology can create immersive 3D worlds that users can explore and help robots learn.

This is AMD's second-largest acquisition in company history. The move marks a significant shift in strategy: while chipmakers traditionally sold chips to AI companies, AMD is now directly acquiring one to keep pace with rival Nvidia.

佛州要法院叫OpenAI先停下來

來源：Axios

美國佛羅里達州的檢察總長James Uthmeier，交給法院一份49頁的文件，申請「緊急禁令」。意思是官司還沒打完，就先請法官叫對方停止某些事。

他要求OpenAI在外部專家檢查安全以前，不能再開發新的AI模型，也不能讓佛州的小孩用ChatGPT。佛州在6月就告了OpenAI，說ChatGPT傷害了孩子。

以前州政府多半只管產品，這次卻想用法院叫一家公司停止研發新AI，這種做法很少見。

Florida's Attorney General James Uthmeier has filed a 49-page document requesting an emergency injunction. He's essentially asking the court to halt OpenAI's actions before the lawsuit is even decided. Specifically, he demands that OpenAI stop developing new AI models and prohibit Florida children from accessing ChatGPT until external experts verify the technology's safety. Florida first sued OpenAI in June, alleging the platform has harmed children. While state governments have historically focused on regulating existing products, attempting to use courts to order a company to halt AI development is largely unprecedented.

政府關門倒數，川普會談沒結果

來源：Punchbowl News、NPR

美國政府的錢只夠用到9月30日。川普找來國會兩黨的四位領袖，談一份新的臨時預算（政府花錢的計畫），可是談完沒有任何進展。政府最快兩天後就可能「關門」停擺。

卡住的原因之一，是白宮剛取消了8.1億美元。這筆錢國會早就通過了，大多要用來幫助難民和移民。白宮故意拖到年度結束，讓錢用不出去。

民主黨因此問：如果現在簽了新預算，白宮之後又取消，那簽了有什麼用？

U.S. government funding runs out on September 30th. Trump convened four leaders from both parties in Congress to discuss a new temporary spending bill, but the talks produced no progress. A government shutdown could happen in as little as two days.

One major sticking point is that the White House recently canceled $810 million in previously approved funding intended largely for refugee and immigrant assistance. The administration deliberately delayed spending the money until year-end, leaving it unspent.

Democrats are therefore questioning: if we sign off on a new budget now and the White House cancels it again later, what's the point of signing?

衣索比亞戰火擴大，提格雷軍攻入阿法爾

來源：Euronews

非洲國家衣索比亞的北部又打起來了。提格雷地區的反抗軍攻進旁邊的阿法爾地區，光是阿法爾就可能有15萬人被迫離開家園。

提格雷在2020到2022年就和政府打過一場大戰，後來簽了和平協定。這次反抗軍想控制通往吉布地港口的道路。衣索比亞沒有海，幾乎所有進出口的貨都要走這條路。

衣索比亞軍方指控（說別人做了壞事）厄利垂亞、蘇丹和埃及在背後支持反抗軍。大家擔心，戰火會擴大到紅海一帶。

Fighting has flared up again in northern Ethiopia. Rebel forces from the Tigray region have attacked into the neighboring Afar region, with an estimated 150,000 people potentially displaced in Afar alone. The two sides fought a major war between 2020 and 2022 before signing a peace agreement. This time, the rebels are seeking to control the road to Djibouti's port—a critical route, since Ethiopia is landlocked and relies almost entirely on it for imports and exports. The Ethiopian military has accused Eritrea, Sudan, and Egypt of backing the rebels. International observers worry the conflict could spread to the broader Red Sea region.

公牛隊換新教練，年輕球隊重新出發

來源：BleacherNation

芝加哥公牛隊9月28日舉行媒體日，新球季的訓練營正式開始。新總教練是巴西人Tiago Splitter，球隊也多了新秀Caleb Wilson、Dailyn Swain，還有Norman Powell和Nicolas Claxton。

三年級前鋒Matas Buzelis已經開始帶Wilson，還說這位第4順位新秀以後會是對手的「大麻煩」。

教練說，今年是成長的一年，會讓年輕球員在錯誤中學習。

The Chicago Bulls held media day on September 28, officially launching the new season's training camp. The new head coach is Brazilian Tiago Splitter. The roster also added rookies Caleb Wilson and Dailyn Swain, along with Norman Powell and Nicolas Claxton. Third-year forward Matas Buzelis has already begun mentoring Wilson, saying the fourth-overall pick will be a major "headache" for opponents going forward. The coach noted that this year is about growth, letting young players learn from their mistakes.

Losing several key players at once is a serious challenge for Arsenal.
