冰川崩落引發洪水，尼泊爾已七百五十人死亡

來源：CNN

八月二十六日，尼泊爾北部一座冰川突然崩落，掉進山谷的河裡。到二十九日為止，已經有七百五十人死亡，其中尼泊爾七百三十四人，西藏十六人，另外還有超過三千人失蹤。

科學家說，這塊冰是從五千二百公尺高的地方掉下來的，一路往下衝了一千二百公尺，還捲走大量石頭和泥沙。這種災難早就畫在地圖上，但山谷裡幾乎沒有警報系統，村民來不及跑。

On August 26, a glacier in northern Nepal suddenly collapsed into a valley river. By August 29, 750 people had been killed—734 from Nepal and 16 from Tibet—with over 3,000 still missing.

Scientists said the ice broke away from an altitude of 5,200 meters and plunged down for 1,200 meters, sweeping vast quantities of rock and debris with it. Though the hazard had long been identified and mapped, the valley had almost no early-warning system, leaving residents no time to evacuate.

美國打算取消 H-1B 配偶工作許可

來源：Bloomberg Law

美國國土安全部把一項新規定排進了工作清單：以後 H-1B 工作簽證持有人的另一半，將不能在美國上班。這等於推翻二○一五年的舊規定。

大約十萬人會受影響，大部分是來自印度的女性，很多人本來就有自己的專業工作。同時政府還打算對每一份 H-1B 申請，收十萬美元以上的費用。

The U.S. Department of Homeland Security has added a new regulation to its work agenda: the spouses of H-1B work visa holders will no longer be permitted to work in the United States. This effectively overturns a rule from 2015.

Approximately 100,000 people will be affected, mostly women from India, many of whom already have their own professional careers. Meanwhile, the government also plans to charge over $100,000 in fees for each H-1B application.

學者公開打賭：AI 能讓經濟一年長百分之十五嗎

來源：Marginal Revolution

經濟學家柯文在部落格上介紹了一個公開的賭約：到二○三三年底以前，美國平均每個人的產出，會不會有某一年成長百分之十五？

這個數字很驚人。二次大戰以後，美國一年大概只長百分之二到三。要跳到十五，代表 AI 真的把世界整個換掉了。目前市場並不看好，願意下注的人很少。

Economist Tyler Cowen introduced a public wager on his blog: before the end of 2033, will America see a single year in which output per person grows by fifteen percent?

The figure is striking. Since the Second World War, America's annual growth has typically been just two to three percent. A jump to fifteen would mean AI has truly remade the whole world. For now the market is skeptical, and few are willing to take the bet.

泰森工廠突然關門，州長承諾拚命找買家

來源：KWQC

八月十三日，泰森食品突然宣布關掉伊利諾州喬斯林的牛肉工廠，兩千五百多名工人幾乎沒收到通知就失業了。

州長普里茲克說，他親自和泰森的執行長通了電話，對方表示公司希望盡快把工廠賣掉。州長說，州政府會「拚了命」去找買家。這座工廠是當地最大的公司之一，但州長沒有權力要公司留下，只能幫忙牽線。

On August 13, Tyson Foods abruptly announced the closure of its beef plant in Joslin, Illinois, leaving more than 2,500 workers jobless with almost no advance notice.

Governor Pritzker said he personally spoke with Tyson's chief executive, who indicated the company hopes to sell the plant as soon as possible. The governor said the state would "fight like hell" to find a buyer. The plant is one of the largest employers in the area, but the governor has no power to make the company stay — he can only help broker a deal.

The Chicago Cubs' record stands at 86 wins and 64 losses. They beat the Mariners to avoid a sweep, and now need just two or three more wins to claim their first playoff ticket since 2020.

They trail the division-leading Brewers by five and a half games and are more likely to advance as a wild card.
