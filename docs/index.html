南非法官皮萊獲得諾貝爾和平獎

來源：Al Jazeera

今年的諾貝爾和平獎，10月9日在挪威宣布，得主是南非法官皮萊（Navi Pillay）。她以前是聯合國的人權負責人。

皮萊在國際法庭當法官時，幫忙讓在戰爭中犯下大罪的人受到審判。最近，她帶領的聯合國小組認為，以色列在加薩犯下了「種族滅絕」（想要消滅一整個民族）。

她是第五位得到這個獎的南非人，前面還有曼德拉等人。頒獎典禮會在12月10日舉行。

This year's Nobel Peace Prize was announced in Norway on October 9. The winner is Navi Pillay, a judge from South Africa who previously served as the United Nations High Commissioner for Human Rights. While she was a judge on international tribunals, Pillay helped bring people who had committed serious crimes during wars to trial. More recently, a UN commission she led concluded that Israel had committed "genocide" (an attempt to destroy an entire people) in Gaza. She is the fifth South African to receive this prize, after figures such as Mandela. The award ceremony will be held on December 10.

颶風伊薩亞斯登陸後變弱，大雨淹水仍危險

來源：ABC News、AccuWeather

今年大西洋第一個颶風（很強的熱帶風暴）「伊薩亞斯」（Isaias），10月9日晚上在美國佛羅里達州的德斯坦（Destin）附近登陸。上岸時是2級颶風，風速每小時大約170公里。

颶風上岸後慢慢變弱，往北穿過阿拉巴馬州。可是它帶來很多雨，有些地方可能下到38公分，很容易突然淹水。

佛州和阿州加起來，已經有超過50萬戶停電。專家提醒：風變小了，不代表就安全了。

Isaias, the first hurricane (a very strong tropical storm) of this year's Atlantic season, made landfall near Destin, Florida, on the evening of October 9. When it came ashore, it was a Category 2 hurricane with winds of about 170 kilometers per hour. After landfall it slowly weakened and moved north across Alabama. But it has brought a lot of rain, and some places could see as much as 38 centimeters, which can lead to sudden flooding. Across Florida and Alabama combined, more than 500,000 households have lost power. Experts warn that just because the winds have weakened does not mean it is safe.

公牛熱身賽小輸灰熊，新秀威爾森拿21分

來源：ESPN

芝加哥公牛隊10月9日在主場打了一場季前熱身賽，以100比104輸給曼菲斯灰熊隊。

今年選秀第4順位的新人威爾森（Caleb Wilson）拿下全隊最高的21分，光是下半場就得了20分，還投進5個三分球。老將鮑威爾（Norman Powell）也有17分。

壞消息是，年輕前鋒布澤利斯（Matas Buzelis）扭傷了腳，提早離場。熱身賽的輸贏不重要，球迷更擔心的是他的傷。

The Chicago Bulls lost a home preseason exhibition game on October 9, falling 104 to 100 to the Memphis Grizzlies. Caleb Wilson, the fourth overall pick in this year's draft, led the team with 21 points, and he scored 20 of them in the second half alone. He also hit five three-pointers. Veteran Norman Powell added 17 points. The bad news came when young forward Matas Buzelis sprained his ankle and had to leave the game early. A preseason result doesn't matter much, and fans are more worried about his injury.
