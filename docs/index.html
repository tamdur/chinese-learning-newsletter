五大科技公司同意讓美國政府測試 AI 模型

來源：Euronews

Google、Microsoft 和 xAI 三家公司正式同意，在新的 AI 模型公開之前，先讓美國商務部的 CAISI（人工智慧安全中心）進行安全測試。加上之前已經加入的 OpenAI 和 Anthropic，現在六大 AI 公司中有五家都接受政府審查。

CAISI 到目前為止已完成超過四十次測試，其中包括一些還沒有上市的最新模型。這代表美國政府已經深入參與 AI 的發展過程。

Google, Microsoft, and xAI have formally agreed to allow the U.S. Commerce Department's CAISI (Center for AI Safety and Innovation) to conduct safety testing before releasing new AI models to the public. Combined with OpenAI and Anthropic, which joined earlier, five of the six major AI companies now accept government review. CAISI has completed over forty tests so far, including some of the latest models not yet on the market. This signals that the U.S. government has become deeply involved in the AI development process.

停火後伊朗對過路船收高價通行費

來源：CBS News

美國和伊朗停火以後，荷莫茲海峽（Strait of Hormuz）並沒有恢復正常。伊朗開始向每艘船收取高達兩百萬美元的通行費，結果幾乎沒有船願意通過。

全球經濟受到很大影響：經濟成長預測被下調到只有百分之二，通膨（物價上漲）超過百分之六。停火了，但經濟戰還在繼續。

After the ceasefire between the United States and Iran, the Strait of Hormuz has not returned to normal. Iran has begun charging ships passage fees as high as $2 million each, with the result that almost no vessels are willing to transit through. The global economy is suffering significantly: economic growth forecasts have been downgraded to just 2 percent, while inflation (rising prices) exceeds 6 percent. The ceasefire holds, but the economic war continues.

川普給歐盟期限：七月四日前完成貿易協議

來源：Al Jazeera

美國總統川普宣布，歐盟必須在七月四日之前完成去年在蘇格蘭談好的貿易協議，否則就要面對更高的關稅。他還威脅要把歐洲汽車的關稅從百分之十五提高到百分之二十五。

歐盟主席 Von der Leyen 說，雙方正在取得好的進展，有信心在期限前完成。

President Trump announced that the European Union must complete a trade agreement negotiated in Scotland last year by July 4th, or face higher tariffs. He also threatened to raise tariffs on European automobiles from 15 percent to 25 percent. EU Commission President Von der Leyen said both sides are making good progress and expressed confidence the deal will be completed before the deadline.

芝加哥一口氣通過近兩千戶新住宅開發案

來源：Block Club Chicago

芝加哥市議會的土地使用委員會休息了三個月後終於重新開會，一次通過了好幾十個建案，總共將帶來近兩千戶新住宅。

這些案子分布在 West Loop、Avondale、Humboldt Park 等社區，包括老人住宅和平價公寓。在房屋短缺的時候，這是好消息。

Chicago's City Council Land Use Committee reconvened after a three-month recess and approved dozens of development projects in a single session, bringing nearly two thousand new housing units to the city. The projects are distributed across neighborhoods including West Loop, Avondale, and Humboldt Park, and include senior housing and affordable apartments. At a time of housing shortages, this is welcome news.

小熊隊九連勝，主場十五連贏創紀錄

來源：Yardbarker

小熊隊五月七日以八比三打敗紅人隊，拿下九連勝，也完成四場橫掃。在 Wrigley Field 的主場連勝來到十五場，是二〇〇八年以來最長。

先發投手今永昇太投了六局只失一分，三振十人。Conforto 三打數三安打，包括一支全壘打。小熊目前戰績亮眼，氣勢正強。

The Cubs defeated the Reds 8–3 on May 7th, extending their winning streak to nine games and completing a four-game sweep. Their home winning streak at Wrigley Field reached 15 games, the longest since 2008. Starting pitcher Shota Imanaga pitched six innings, allowing just one run while striking out 10 batters. Conforto went 3-for-3 with a home run. The Cubs are off to a brilliant start with momentum clearly on their side.

兵工廠二十年後重返歐冠決賽

來源：Arsenal.com

兵工廠線——Arsenal 以兩回合總比數二比一擊敗 Atletico Madrid，闖進歐洲冠軍聯賽決賽！這是兵工廠自二〇〇六年以來第一次打進決賽，等了整整二十年。

Saka 在主場第二回合踢進關鍵一球，鎖定勝局。決賽將在五月三十日於布達佩斯舉行，對手是衛冕冠軍 PSG。

Arsenal beat Atlético Madrid 2-1 on aggregate to reach the Champions League final! This is Arsenal's first final appearance since 2006 — a full twenty years of waiting. Saka scored the decisive goal at home in the second leg, sealing the victory. The final will be held on May 30th in Budapest against defending champion PSG.
