美國對伊朗祭出「史上最重」制裁

來源：CNBC / CNN

美國財政部長貝森特星期一宣布，要對伊朗實施「史上最重」的制裁。這場美國和伊朗的戰爭已經打了快六個月，還沒有要停下來的樣子。

伊朗很生氣，警告說會扣住通過荷莫茲海峽（中東一條很重要的航道）的船隻。不過伊朗總統裴澤斯基安卻說：「我們不能永遠打下去。」這是伊朗高層第一次公開露出想停戰的口氣。

U.S. Treasury Secretary Bessent announced Monday that the United States will impose what it calls the "heaviest sanctions in history" on Iran. The U.S.-Iran conflict has now lasted nearly six months with no end in sight. Iran responded angrily, warning that it would block vessels passing through the Strait of Hormuz, a critical Middle Eastern shipping lane. Yet Iranian President Pezeshkian offered a different message, saying, "We cannot fight forever" — the first time Iran's top leadership has publicly hinted at openness to ending the war.

記憶體大缺貨　輝達AI伺服器要漲價

來源：Bloomberg / 24/7 Wall St.

做AI晶片的輝達（Nvidia）通知大客戶，明年初出貨的AI伺服器要漲價超過一成五。

原因是做記憶體（電腦用來存資料的零件）的工廠供不應求，價格一直往上飛。這是頭一次AI設備變貴，不是因為晶片不夠，而是因為記憶體太貴。專家說這個缺貨的情況，可能要到2027年中才會好轉。

Nvidia, the AI chip maker, has notified major customers that AI servers shipping early next year will cost more than 15 percent extra. The reason is a severe supply shortage of memory chips — the components computers use to store data — with prices climbing steadily. This is the first time AI equipment has grown more expensive not because chips are scarce, but because memory is so costly. Experts say the shortage may not ease until mid-2027.

He simultaneously announced sanctions against 139 individuals and companies linked to Russia. Meanwhile, Russia intensified its air campaign, striking Kherson and Zaporizhzhia with drones and missiles and causing civilian casualties. France, for its part, pledged to speed up missile deliveries to Ukraine.

芝加哥公立學校開學　搭車免費

來源：Chicago Public Schools / WTTW

芝加哥公立學校今天（8月24日）開學，幼稚園到高中的學生都回到教室，學前班的小朋友則是星期二才開始上課。

開學第一天，學校和芝加哥交通局一起請客，讓學生和陪同的大人搭公車、地鐵都不用錢。雖然學校今年還有經費不足的煩惱，但教室總算準時開門了。

Chicago's public schools opened their doors today — August 24th — with kindergarten through high school students returning to classrooms. Pre-K students begin on Tuesday. To mark the first day, the district partnered with the Chicago Transit Authority to offer free bus and train rides for students and the adults accompanying them. Despite ongoing budget troubles this year, the classrooms opened on schedule.

Despite a roster beset by injuries this summer, with roughly a month remaining in the season, the Cubs remain solidly positioned near the top.
