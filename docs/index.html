密西根隊史上最強戰績，大十聯盟錦標賽第一種子

來源：Michigan Sports

密西根大學男籃今年打出 29 勝 2 敗的超強戰績，拿下大十聯盟錦標賽的第一種子。這是球隊歷史上最好的例行賽成績之一。主教練 Dusty May 帶領球隊一路過關，現在全美都在討論密西根能不能拿下全國冠軍。三月瘋（March Madness）就要開始了，今年的密西根真的很有希望。

Michigan men's basketball has posted an outstanding 29-2 record this season and earned the No. 1 seed in the Big Ten Tournament. This ranks among the best regular season records in program history. Head coach Dusty May has led the team through every challenge, and the entire nation is now discussing whether Michigan can capture the national championship. March Madness is about to begin, and this year's Michigan team truly has great promise.

美國二月少了九萬多個工作，大家開始擔心經濟衰退

來源：Economics/Finance

美國二月的就業報告出來了，結果不太好——整個月少了九萬兩千個工作，比大家預期的差很多。投資人很緊張，黃金價格衝到每盎司（ounce）5,185 美元，因為大家把錢放到比較安全的地方。油價也上上下下不穩定。現在很多人在問：美國會不會進入經濟衰退？聯準會（Fed）面對的壓力越來越大了。

The U.S. jobs report for February came in, and the news wasn't great — the country lost 92,000 jobs for the month, much worse than what people were expecting. Investors got nervous, and gold prices shot up to $5,185 per ounce because people were moving their money to safer places. Oil prices bounced around unpredictably too. Now a lot of people are asking: will the U.S. slip into a recession? The Federal Reserve is facing mounting pressure.

Anthropic成立新研究所，專門研究AI法律與政策

來源：Gen AI/AGI

AI 公司 Anthropic 宣布成立一個新的研究所，叫做 Anthropic Institute。這個研究所會研究 AI 相關的法律、對經濟的影響，還有政府該怎麼管理 AI。同一時間，Anthropic 也因為跟美國國防部有關的事情被告上法院。從這些動作可以看出來，Anthropic 越來越積極地跟政府合作，想要影響 AI 的規則怎麼訂。

The AI company Anthropic announced the establishment of a new research institute called the Anthropic Institute. This institute will research laws related to AI, its impact on the economy, and how governments should regulate AI. At the same time, Anthropic is being sued over matters related to the U.S. Department of Defense. These actions show that Anthropic is becoming increasingly active in collaborating with government and wants to influence how AI regulations are set.

AI人臉辨識認錯人，無辜女子被關進監獄

來源：Gen AI/AGI

一名女子被警察抓去關，但她什麼壞事都沒做。原因是 AI 人臉辨識系統把她認成了另一個人。這件事讓很多人再次討論：用 AI 來抓人到底安不安全？人臉辨識技術已經知道有偏差（bias）的問題，有時候會認錯人，特別是對某些族群。這個案子提醒我們，AI 雖然方便，但用在執法上要非常小心。

A woman was arrested by police, but she had done nothing wrong. The reason: an AI facial recognition system mistook her for someone else. This incident has sparked another round of discussion: is it actually safe to use AI to arrest people? Facial recognition technology is already known to have bias problems, and it sometimes gets people wrong, especially with certain groups. This case reminds us that even though AI is convenient, we have to be extremely careful when using it in law enforcement.

腸子跟大腦說話？科學家找到恢復記憶的新方法

來源：Science

史丹佛大學的研究人員發現，我們的腸子會送訊號給大腦，而且這些訊號可能可以幫助恢復記憶力。他們用老鼠做實驗，發現腸道裡的微生物（microbiome）會產生特別的分子，這些分子能讓大腦裡負責記憶的海馬迴（hippocampus）重新正常運作。雖然目前只在老鼠身上成功，但這給治療老年人記憶力下降帶來了新的希望。

Researchers at Stanford University have discovered that our intestines send signals to the brain, and these signals may help restore memory. They conducted experiments on mice and found that microbes in the gut (microbiome) produce special molecules that allow the hippocampus—the brain region responsible for memory—to function normally again. While the findings are so far limited to mice, this offers new hope for treating memory decline in older adults.
