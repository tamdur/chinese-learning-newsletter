美軍直升機在荷莫茲海峽擊沉六艘伊朗快艇

來源：CNN

五月四日，美國派出Apache和Seahawk直升機護送商船通過荷莫茲海峽，伊朗小型快艇試圖攻擊商船，美軍開火擊沉六艘。這是二月海峽關閉以來，雙方第一次直接海上交火。

伊朗否認有船被擊沉，但美國中央司令部指揮官已確認此事。停火情勢非常緊張。

On May 4th, the U.S. deployed Apache and Seahawk helicopters to escort merchant vessels through the Strait of Hormuz. Iranian speedboats attempted to attack the commercial ships, prompting U.S. forces to open fire and sink six vessels. This marks the first direct naval engagement between the two sides since the strait's closure in February. Iran has denied that any of its ships were sunk, but the commander of U.S. Central Command has confirmed the incident. The ceasefire situation remains extremely tense.

美國油價飆到每加侖四點四六美元，恐破五元

來源：NPR

荷莫茲海峽已經關閉快三個月，全球兩成石油運輸受阻。美國汽油均價一週內漲了三十美分，來到每加侖四點四六美元，是二〇二二年七月以來最高。

分析師警告，如果海峽再封一個月，油價可能突破五美元。

The Strait of Hormuz has been closed for nearly three months, blocking roughly 20 percent of global oil shipments. U.S. gasoline prices surged 30 cents in a week, hitting $4.46 per gallon—the highest since July 2022. Analysts warn that if the strait remains sealed for another month, oil prices could breach $5 per gallon.

熊隊新球場法案過了州眾議院，州長批減稅太少

來源：Chicago Sun-Times

伊利諾州眾議院以七十八比三十二票通過大型建設法案，讓NFL芝加哥熊隊可以在Arlington Heights蓋有屋頂的新球場。

但州長Pritzker說法案裡給居民的房屋稅減免「少到可以忽略」。法案接下來送到州參議院，必須在五月三十一日前表決。

The Illinois House of Representatives passed a major construction bill 78-32 that will allow the NFL's Chicago Bears to build a new domed stadium in Arlington Heights. However, Governor Pritzker said the property tax relief for residents included in the bill is "negligibly small." The legislation now heads to the state Senate, where it must be voted on by May 31st.

三大AI公司全面轉向「代理型AI」架構

來源：AI Flash Report

OpenAI、Google和Anthropic在五月發布的新模型顯示，「代理型AI」（能自己規劃步驟、自己修正錯誤的AI）已經成為主流設計。

以前的AI只能回答單一問題，現在的推理模型可以獨立完成好幾個小時的複雜工作。業界說二〇二五年是推理模型的時代，二〇二六年是讓它們真正實用的時代。

OpenAI, Google, and Anthropic's new models released in May show that "agentic AI"—systems capable of planning their own steps and correcting their own errors—has become mainstream design. While earlier AI could only answer single questions, today's reasoning models can independently complete complex work spanning several hours. The industry says 2025 was the era of reasoning models; 2026 is the era of making them truly practical.

小熊橫掃響尾蛇，戰績飆到二十二勝十二敗

來源：Bleacher Nation

芝加哥小熊五月四日以八比四打敗亞利桑那響尾蛇，完成三連勝橫掃。Michael Busch在滿壘時打出三壘安打，Ballesteros轟出全壘打。

小熊目前二十二勝十二敗，主場十一連勝，是今年最好的表現。

On May 4th, the Chicago Cubs defeated the Arizona Diamondbacks 8-4, completing a three-game sweep and winning streak. Michael Busch hit a bases-loaded triple, and Ballesteros homered. The Cubs now stand at 22-12, with an 11-game home winning streak — their best record of the season.

兵工廠線——歐冠四強第二回合今晚在Emirates球場開踢，兵工廠和馬德里競技目前總比分一比一平手。
