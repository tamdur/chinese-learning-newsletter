Google 新演算法 TurboQuant：讓 AI 裝進你的手機

來源：Motley Fool

Google 的研究人員發表了一種叫做 TurboQuant 的新技術。它可以把 AI 大型語言模型需要的記憶體減少大約六倍，而且準確度幾乎不受影響。這代表什麼？以前只能在大型雲端伺服器上跑的頂級 AI，未來可能直接在你的手機或筆電上運行。這會改變 AI 產業的經濟模式，因為公司不再需要花大錢租用雲端算力了。

Google researchers have unveiled a new technique called TurboQuant. It can reduce the memory required by AI large language models by roughly six times while maintaining nearly identical accuracy. What does this mean? Premium AI models that previously could only run on large cloud servers may soon operate directly on your phone or laptop. This will reshape the economics of the AI industry, since companies will no longer need to spend heavily on renting cloud computing power.

美國通膨快到 4%，關稅和油價是主因

來源：MarketMinute

美國的物價又開始快速上漲了。消費者物價指數（CPI）正在接近 4%，這是近年來最高的水準。兩個主要原因：第一，去年加的關稅終於傳到消費者手上，讓商品價格上升了約 0.5% 到 0.7%。第二，中東的衝突推高了能源價格。市場現在預期聯準會今年不會降息了。

American prices are rising rapidly again. The Consumer Price Index (CPI) is approaching 4%, the highest level in recent years. Two main reasons: First, tariffs imposed last year are finally reaching consumers, pushing commodity prices up by roughly 0.5% to 0.7%. Second, conflict in the Middle East has driven up energy prices. The market now expects the Federal Reserve will not cut rates this year.

歐盟和印度簽自由貿易協定，涵蓋 20 億人

來源：European Commission

歐盟和印度在一月底完成了歷史性的自由貿易協定，涵蓋（包含）二十億人和全球將近 25% 的 GDP。這是雙方談了快二十年才談成的。為什麼現在才成功？因為美國加關稅、中國擴大貿易，讓歐盟和印度都覺得必須合作。印度將取消 96.6% 的歐盟商品關稅，這對一向保護國內市場的印度來說是很大的轉變。

The European Union and India completed a historic free trade agreement at the end of January, covering two billion people and nearly 25% of global GDP. This deal came after nearly two decades of negotiations between the two parties. Why did it finally succeed now? Because US tariff increases and China's expanding trade have made both the EU and India feel compelled to cooperate. India will eliminate tariffs on 96.6% of EU goods, a significant shift for a country that has traditionally protected its domestic market.

芝加哥監督委員會質問警察局長：為何沒阻止聯邦移民突擊？

來源：WTTW Chicago

芝加哥的警察監督委員會這週開了一場很緊張的會議，質問警察局長 Snelling：在聯邦政府的「中途閃擊行動」移民大搜捕中，芝加哥警察到底有沒有幫忙聯邦探員？芝加哥是「庇護城市」（不主動配合移民執法的城市），但行動中有 27 名警察被催淚瓦斯弄傷。同時，州政府的調查委員會要求八名川普政府官員來作證。

Chicago's police oversight committee held a tense meeting this week, grilling Police Superintendent Snelling about whether Chicago police assisted federal agents during the federal government's "Operation Midway Blitz" immigration crackdown. Chicago is a "sanctuary city" that doesn't actively cooperate with immigration enforcement, yet 27 officers were injured by tear gas during the operation. Meanwhile, a state investigating committee has demanded that eight Trump administration officials testify.

密西根 91：73 大勝亞利桑那，挺進 NCAA 冠軍賽

來源：MGoBlog

狼獾隊（Wolverines）打出了一場漂亮的比賽！密西根在四強賽以 91比73 大勝亞利桑那。中鋒 Aday Mara 拿下 26 分 9 個籃板，後衛 Elliot Cadeau 交出 13 分 10 次助攻的雙十成績。全隊投籃命中率高達 57%。密西根本季 35 勝 3 敗，週一晚上將對決 UConn 爭奪全國冠軍。

The Wolverines put on a stellar performance! Michigan cruised past Arizona 91-73 in the Final Four. Center Aday Mara led the way with 26 points and 9 rebounds, while guard Elliot Cadeau contributed a double-double with 13 points and 10 assists. The team shot an impressive 57% from the field. Michigan finishes the regular season at 35-3 and will face UConn Monday night for the national championship.
