AI聽到反對理由，反而更相信原本的答案

來源：Marginal Revolution

經濟學家 Tyler Cowen 今天在部落格 Marginal Revolution 問了一個問題：大型語言模型（LLM）到底會不會好好想事情？他提到一個例子：有人給 AI 一個合理的反對理由，想讓它少相信某個結論，結果 AI 反而把這個理由當成支持，更相信原來的答案。

好的推理應該是這樣：聽到有道理的反對意見，就把信心調低一點。這種方法叫做「貝氏更新」（Bayesian update）。

這件事很重要，因為它讓人懷疑：AI 是真的在比較證據，還是只在說別人愛聽的話？

Economist Tyler Cowen asked a question today on his blog, Marginal Revolution: do large language models (LLMs) really think things through properly? He pointed to one example. Someone gave an AI a reasonable objection, hoping it would believe a conclusion less. Instead, the AI treated the objection as support and became more confident in its original answer. Good reasoning should work the other way: when you hear a sensible objection, you lower your confidence a little. This approach is called "Bayesian updating." It matters because it raises a doubt: is AI actually weighing the evidence, or is it just telling people what they want to hear?

新人四分衛跑出致勝分，密西根 28 比 21 贏羅格斯

來源：ESPN

密西根大學美式足球隊在萬聖節主場以 28 比 21 打敗羅格斯大學。一年級四分衛（帶領進攻的球員） Tommy Carr 傳球 266 碼（長度單位），還自己帶球跑進兩次達陣（得分）。

第二次最關鍵：比賽剩下 4 分 19 秒時，雙方 21 比 21 平手，Carr 跑了 9 碼得分，拿下勝利。

原本的先發四分衛 Bryce Underwood 這個月因為表現不好被換下，這是 Carr 第二次先發。密西根上週在延長賽輸給印第安納，這場贏球後戰績是 5 勝 3 敗，也是連續第 10 次打敗羅格斯。

Michigan's football team beat Rutgers 28-21 at home on Halloween. Freshman quarterback Tommy Carr, the player who leads the offense, threw for 266 yards and also ran for two touchdowns himself. The second one mattered most. With 4 minutes and 19 seconds left, the game was tied 21-21, and Carr ran 9 yards into the end zone to win it.

Bryce Underwood, the original starting quarterback, was benched this month because of poor play. This was Carr's second start. Michigan lost to Indiana in overtime last week. With this win, the Wolverines improved to 5-3. It was also their 10th straight win over Rutgers.

兵工廠今天客場挑戰利物浦，冠軍之戰提早開打

來源：Sky Sports

兵工廠線——兵工廠今天要到利物浦的主場安菲爾德（Anfield）比賽，芝加哥時間上午 10 點 30 分開踢。兵工廠是上一季的冠軍，現在領先利物浦 6 分。

天空體育評論員 Paul Merson 說，利物浦一定要贏，不然就很難再爭冠軍；如果兵工廠贏了，冠軍可能很早就有答案。

不過兵工廠的狀況不太好：從 2012 年以後，他們就沒在安菲爾德贏過聯賽。上週他們還在聯賽盃輸給一支第四級的球隊，主力球員 Saka 也不能上場。

The Arsenal beat: Arsenal travel to Anfield to face Liverpool today, with kickoff at 10:30 a.m. Chicago time. Arsenal are last season's champions and currently lead Liverpool by six points. Sky Sports pundit Paul Merson says Liverpool must win or they will find it very hard to stay in the title race, and that if Arsenal win, the championship could be decided early. Arsenal are not in good shape, though. They have not won a league match at Anfield since 2012. Last week they also lost a League Cup tie to a fourth-tier side, and key player Saka is unavailable.
