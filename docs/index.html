美伊和談破裂，川普宣布封鎖荷莫茲海峽

來源：NPR

美國和伊朗在巴基斯坦首都談了二十一個小時，最後還是破裂了。川普馬上宣布，從星期一開始，美國海軍會封鎖伊朗的港口和荷莫茲海峽。這條海峽是全世界最重要的石油運送通道，全球大約兩成的石油要從這裡經過。消息一出來，油價馬上漲到每桶一百美元以上。這是美伊危機以來最大的一次升級，全世界都在看下一步會怎樣。

The US and Iran held talks for twenty-one hours in Pakistan's capital, but ultimately broke down. Trump immediately announced that starting Monday, the US Navy would blockade Iran's ports and the Strait of Hormuz. This strait is the world's most critical oil shipping channel, with roughly 20 percent of global oil passing through it. News of the blockade sent oil prices surging above $100 per barrel. This marks the largest escalation in the US-Iran crisis to date, with the world watching to see what happens next.

Anthropic 指控中國 AI 公司大規模偷學模型

來源：Bloomberg

OpenAI、Anthropic 和 Google 三家最大的 AI 公司決定一起合作，對抗中國 AI 公司偷學它們的技術。Anthropic 說，DeepSeek 等公司用了大約兩萬四千個假帳號，和 Claude 對話了一千六百萬次，把 Claude 的能力「蒸餾」（也就是複製）到自己的模型裡。這個數字很驚人。三家公司平常是對手，現在卻要一起分享情報來防守，這在 AI 界還是第一次。

OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google—the three largest AI companies—have decided to cooperate together to fight against Chinese AI companies stealing their technology. Anthropic says that companies like DeepSeek used approximately twenty-four thousand fake accounts to have 16 million conversations with Claude, "distilling" (that is, copying) Claude's capabilities into their own models. This figure is staggering. The three companies are normally competitors, yet now they must share intelligence together for defense—a first in the AI industry.

美國經濟衰退機率逼近五成，油價又來添亂

來源：Yahoo Finance / JPMorgan

美國最新的工作報告很不好看：減少了九萬兩千個工作，失業率升到百分之四點四。第一季的 GDP 成長只有百分之零點七，幾乎沒在長。評級機構穆迪說，美國經濟衰退（也就是經濟倒退）的機率已經到了百分之四十九。加上伊朗危機讓油價漲到一百美元以上，關稅又推高物價，美國現在面對的是兩面夾擊的壓力。

The U.S. lost 92,000 jobs in the latest employment report, and unemployment rose to 4.4%. First-quarter GDP growth came in at just 0.7%, barely budging. Rating agency Moody's says the probability of a U.S. economic recession — that is, economic contraction — has reached 49%. On top of that, the Iran crisis has pushed oil prices above $100 a barrel, while tariffs are driving up prices. The U.S. is now facing pressure from two directions at once.

伊利諾州通過十五億美元交通法案，芝加哥通勤族有福了

來源：CBS Chicago

伊利諾州通過了一個十五億美元的大型交通法案，這是十多年來最大的一筆交通投資。錢會用在 CTA（芝加哥地鐵和公車）、Metra（通勤火車）和 Pace（郊區公車）上面。法案還成立了「北伊利諾交通局」來統一管理，還取消了車站附近的最低停車位要求，希望鼓勵更多人搭大眾運輸。對每天坐車上班的芝加哥人來說，這是好消息。

Illinois has passed a massive $1.5 billion transportation bill, the largest transit investment in over a decade. The money will be distributed to the CTA (Chicago's subway and bus system), Metra (commuter rail), and Pace (suburban buses). The bill also establishes a new "Northern Illinois Transportation Authority" to provide unified management and eliminates minimum parking requirements near transit stations, hoping to encourage more people to use public transportation. For Chicago commuters who ride to work every day, this is good news.
