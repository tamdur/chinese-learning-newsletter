Anthropic 研究員辭職，美國兩黨要求立法管 AI

來源：Japan Times（Reuters）

人工智慧公司 Anthropic 的研究員 Jacob Coxon 辭職了。他在網路上寫下警告，說各家公司正在拼命做出會自己變聰明的 AI，等於拿大家的性命在賭。

這篇貼文一天內被看了超過一億次，同事 Evan Hubinger 也公開支持他。

美國國會裡，民主黨和共和黨的議員都說，只靠公司自己管自己不夠。連 OpenAI 也主動請國會訂出檢查標準。

Jacob Coxon, a researcher at AI company Anthropic, has resigned. In an online post, he warned that companies are frantically pursuing the development of AI that can make itself smarter — equivalent to gambling with all our lives. The post was viewed over 100 million times within a day, and his colleague Evan Hubinger publicly backed him up. In the US Congress, both Democratic and Republican lawmakers have stated that corporate self-regulation alone is insufficient. Even OpenAI has proactively asked Congress to set inspection standards.

最高法院擋下密蘇里州新選區地圖

來源：CNN Politics

美國最高法院九月十日裁定，密蘇里州十一月的期中選舉不能用新畫的國會選區地圖，要改用四年前的舊地圖。

新地圖對共和黨比較有利，會把這一州的席次從六比二變成七比一，等於拿掉一個民主黨的位子。

今年眾議院誰能過半，只差幾個席位，所以少一席就很要緊。

On September 10th, the U.S. Supreme Court ruled that Missouri's midterm election in November cannot use the newly drawn congressional district map and must instead use the old map from four years ago. The new map would have favored Republicans, shifting the state's congressional seats from 6-2 to 7-1—effectively eliminating one Democratic seat. With this year's battle for House control coming down to just a few seats, losing even one seat is critical.

胡塞武裝攻下紅海摩卡港

來源：NPR

葉門的胡塞武裝九月十日攻下了紅海邊的摩卡港。摩卡離曼德海峽只有八十公里左右，那裡是船要進蘇伊士運河的南邊入口。

全世界大約八分之一的貨物走這條路。胡塞背後是伊朗，波斯灣那頭本來就不平靜，現在紅海這頭又出事。船公司可能要改走非洲南邊，多繞好幾千公里。

Yemen's Houthi forces captured the port of Mocha on the Red Sea on September 10. Mocha lies roughly 80 kilometers from the Strait of Mandeb, the southern gateway through which ships enter the Suez Canal. Approximately one-eighth of the world's cargo travels this route. Behind the Houthis stands Iran, and while the Persian Gulf has long been unstable, tensions are now flaring on the Red Sea as well. Shipping companies may be forced to reroute around Africa's southern coast, adding several thousand kilometers to their voyages.

司法部調查輝達二百億美元授權案

來源：Bloomberg

美國司法部開始調查晶片公司輝達（Nvidia）。去年十二月，輝達付了大約二百億美元，向新公司 Groq 買晶片技術的使用權，Groq 的執行長也跟著到輝達上班。

因為 Groq 表面上還是獨立公司，這筆交易就不必經過政府的合併審查。司法部想弄清楚：這是不是「名義上租技術，其實是買公司」。

The U.S. Department of Justice has launched an investigation into chip maker Nvidia. Last December, Nvidia paid approximately $20 billion to acquire licensing rights to chip technology from Groq, a startup. Groq's CEO subsequently took a position at Nvidia.

Because Groq nominally remained independent, the deal did not require government merger review. The Justice Department is investigating whether this was in fact a company acquisition disguised as a technology licensing agreement.

釀酒人橫掃小熊，分區冠軍沒指望了

來源：Bleacher Nation

密爾瓦基釀酒人以八比六再贏芝加哥小熊，三場全贏。Yelich 打出三分全壘打，投手 Henderson 投五局只被打出兩支安打。

小熊最後一局追回五分，還是差兩分。

現在小熊在國聯中區落後十場，分區冠軍等於沒了，整個球季只剩下拚外卡的機會。

The Milwaukee Brewers beat the Chicago Cubs 8-6 again, sweeping all three games. Yelich hit a three-run home run, and pitcher Henderson threw five innings while allowing just two hits. The Cubs rallied for five runs in the final inning but fell short by two. The Cubs are now ten games back in the National League Central, making a division title all but impossible. The team's only remaining hope for the season is to win a wild-card spot.

兵工廠線——教練 Arteta 在對桑德蘭的賽前記者會上說，後衛 Saliba 腳踝受傷，大約要休息三到四週。

兵工廠本季還沒輸過球，週二在拿坡里靠 Odegaard 進球贏了，也拿下本季第三場零失球。撐住球隊的一直是防守，現在最好的中後衛卻上不了場。

好消息是，Timber 快要回來了。
