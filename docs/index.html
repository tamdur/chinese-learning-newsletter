德國極右派政黨大勝，「防火牆」受考驗

來源：NPR / Reuters

德國東部的薩克森安哈特州星期天投票，極右派的「另類選擇黨」拿到百分之四十三點八的選票，是第二名基民黨百分之十七點二的兩倍多。

德國主流政黨多年來有個不成文的規定，叫「防火牆」，就是絕不跟極右派合作。但這次票數差太多，其他政黨可能湊不出一個不靠它的組合。真的發生的話，這會是一九四五年二戰結束以來，極右派第一次領導德國的一個州。

On Sunday, voters in Saxony-Anhalt, a state in eastern Germany, went to the polls. The far-right Alternative for Germany party captured 43.8% of the vote—more than double the second-place Christian Democratic Union's 17.2%. Germany's mainstream parties have long maintained an unwritten rule known as the "firewall": they will never cooperate with far-right parties.

But the vote gap this time is so wide that the other parties may not be able to form a viable coalition without them. If that happens, it would be the first time since 1945, when World War II ended, that a far-right party has led a German state.

人工智慧完成費馬定理的電腦驗證

來源：Anthropic

人工智慧公司 Anthropic 宣布，他們的模型 Claude 花了十一天，幾乎全靠自己完成了「費馬最後定理」第一份電腦可以檢查的證明，寫下一千三百萬行程式碼，也證明了大約兩萬九千五百個小步驟。

這個定理在一九九五年就被數學家懷爾斯證明過，數學本身不是新的。特別的是，每一步都能讓電腦驗算對不對，所以我們不必先相信人工智慧，也能相信這個結果。

AI company Anthropic announced that their model Claude spent eleven days to complete, almost entirely on its own, the first computer-verifiable proof of Fermat's Last Theorem. The proof spans 13 million lines of code and verifies approximately 29,500 individual steps. Though mathematician Andrew Wiles already proved this theorem in 1995—and the mathematics itself is not new—what makes this achievement remarkable is that every step can be verified by computer. This means we can trust the result without having to first trust AI.

歐盟開始執行人工智慧透明規定

來源：European Commission / AI Weekly

歐盟的《人工智慧法》從八月二日起進入真正執行的階段。在歐洲，聊天機器人一開口就要告訴你它是機器，不是真人；人工智慧做出來的圖片、影片和聲音，也要加上電腦讀得懂的標記。

違規最高可以罰一千五百萬歐元，或是公司全球營業額的百分之三。這是世界上第一次，人工智慧的規定不再只是建議，而是產品一定要做到的事。

The EU's AI Act entered its enforcement phase on August 2. In Europe, chatbots must immediately disclose that they're machines, not real people. AI-generated images, videos, and audio must also carry machine-readable labels. Violations can result in fines up to 15 million euros or 3% of a company's global annual revenue. This marks the world's first time that AI regulations are no longer merely advisory—they're mandatory requirements that products must meet.

芝加哥市長預告「特別宣布」

來源：CBS Chicago

芝加哥市長強生的團隊發出邀請，說九月十三日下午四點有一場「特別宣布」，信上寫著「一起見證歷史」。多數人猜，他要宣布競選連任。

不過他的路不好走。民調支持度只有大約百分之二十三，已經有至少九個人要跟他爭二○二七年二月的市長選舉。市府明年的預算還缺八億八千二百萬美元，他得一邊想辦法省錢，一邊拉票。

Mayor Brandon Johnson's team has sent out invitations for a "special announcement" at 4 PM on September 13th, with the message reading "witness history together." Most observers expect him to announce his re-election campaign. But his path ahead is difficult. His approval rating sits at only about 23%, and at least nine candidates are already competing against him for the mayoral race in February 2027. With next year's city budget facing an 882 million dollar shortfall, he'll have to balance cost-cutting measures with his campaign efforts.

密西根險勝，全國排名引發爭論

來源：Fox Sports

密西根大學美式足球隊球季第一戰在主場只拿十三分，靠最後一秒一記四十七碼的長傳達陣，才以十三比十二贏了西密西根大學。賽前大家都以為他們會大贏。

問題來了：他們球季前被投票排在全國第十六名，那是靠名氣，不是靠成績。現在投票的記者要決定，這樣的表現還留不留得住第十六名。

The University of Michigan football team squeaked past Western Michigan at home with a 13-12 victory in their season opener, managing only thirteen points and surviving on a forty-seven-yard touchdown pass with one second remaining. Everyone had expected them to cruise to an easy win. But here's the rub: they were voted 16th in the nation in the preseason polls—a ranking rooted in reputation rather than actual performance. Now the journalists voting in the polls face a question: does a performance like this justify keeping them at 16th?

兵工廠線：阿森納逆轉切爾西

來源：NBC Sports

兵工廠線——阿森納在主場酋長球場二比一逆轉切爾西。開賽不到一分鐘，羅傑斯就先幫切爾西進球，而他正是夏天切爾西出高價，從阿森納手上搶走的球員。

之後哈維茨追平，隊長厄德高在第五十分鐘踢進致勝球，門將拉亞多次撲救守住比分。夏天賣掉兩名前鋒又沒有補人的阿森納，靠原有的球員拿下九分，和曼城並列，也送給切爾西本季聯賽第一敗。

Arsenal came from behind to beat Chelsea 2-1 at the Emirates. Rogers put Chelsea ahead inside the opening minute—the very player Chelsea paid a premium to sign from Arsenal last summer. Havertz equalized soon after, then captain Odegaard scored the winner in the 50th minute. Goalkeeper Raya made multiple key saves to secure the victory. Arsenal, having sold two forwards in the summer without replacement, have accumulated nine points with their existing squad and drawn level with Manchester City, while also dealing Chelsea their first league defeat of the season.
