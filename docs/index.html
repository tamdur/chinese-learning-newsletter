Google 砸四百億美元投資 Anthropic

來源：Hacker News

Google 的母公司 Alphabet 宣布，計畫投資最多四百億美元給 AI 公司 Anthropic。第一筆一百億已經到位，剩下三百億要看 Anthropic 能不能達到目標。

幾天前 Amazon 也才說要投兩百五十億。Anthropic 目前年收入已超過三百億美元，AI 產業的軍備競賽（比誰花更多錢）越來越激烈。

Alphabet, Google's parent company, announced plans to invest up to $40 billion in AI company Anthropic. The first $10 billion is already committed, with the remaining $30 billion contingent on Anthropic hitting certain milestones. Just days ago, Amazon said it would invest $25 billion. Anthropic's annual revenue has already surpassed $300 billion, as the AI industry's arms race—a competition over who can spend the most—intensifies.

匈牙利變天：奧班執政十六年後落敗

來源：NPR

匈牙利總理奧班在國會大選中慘敗，結束了十六年的執政。對手馬扎爾的 Tisza 黨拿下國會一百三十八席（共一百九十九席），取得超級多數。

奧班承認落敗，並宣布放棄國會議員身分，結束三十六年的政治生涯。這場選舉投票率接近八成，是匈牙利歷史上最高的。

Hungarian Prime Minister Orbán suffered a crushing defeat in parliamentary elections, ending sixteen years in power. The opposition Tisza Party, led by Márton Márki-Zay, secured 138 seats in parliament (out of 199 total), giving it a supermajority. Orbán conceded defeat and announced he would step down from his parliamentary seat, bringing an end to thirty-six years in politics. The election saw a turnout of nearly eighty percent, the highest in Hungarian history.

美伊談判破裂：川普取消特使巴基斯坦行程

來源：Al Jazeera

美國和伊朗的談判再度破裂。伊朗外長阿拉奇離開巴基斯坦後，川普馬上取消了特使 Witkoff 和 Kushner 的出訪計畫。

伊朗說，在美國海軍封鎖（不讓船通過）還在的時候，不會談。川普則說：「想談的話，打電話就好。」四月八日的停火越來越不穩。

US-Iran negotiations have collapsed again. After Iran's foreign minister Araghchi left Pakistan, Trump immediately canceled plans for envoys Witkoff and Kushner to visit the region. Iran said it won't negotiate while the American naval blockade remains in place. Trump responded: "If they want to talk, they can just call." The ceasefire from April 8th is looking increasingly fragile.

芝加哥警察在醫院遭槍擊，一死一重傷

來源：Block Club Chicago

四月二十五日上午，芝加哥 Lincoln Square 的 Swedish Hospital 發生槍擊案。兩名警察押送嫌犯到醫院時，嫌犯拿到槍開火。

一名三十八歲、服務十年的警察不幸身亡，另一名五十七歲的資深警察重傷。嫌犯逃到附近住宅後被逮捕。

On the morning of April 25th, a shooting occurred at Swedish Hospital in Chicago's Lincoln Square neighborhood. As two officers were escorting a suspect into the hospital, the suspect obtained a gun and opened fire. A 38-year-old officer with ten years of service was killed, and a 57-year-old veteran officer was critically wounded. The suspect fled to a nearby residence before being apprehended.

Swanson 再見安打！小熊九連勝創十年最佳

來源：MLB.com Cubs

小熊隊的 Dansby Swanson 在第十局打出再見安打（比賽最後一球就贏了），把 Ian Happ 送回本壘，以八比七打敗費城人隊。

這是小熊的第九場連勝，也是自二〇一六年世界大賽冠軍賽季以來最長的連勝紀錄。接下來要面對道奇和教士的硬仗。

Dansby Swanson of the Cubs hit a walk-off single in the tenth inning, sending Ian Happ home to give Chicago an 8-7 victory over the Philadelphia Phillies. The win marked the Cubs' ninth consecutive victory, their longest winning streak since the 2016 World Series championship season. Tough matchups against the Dodgers and Padres lie ahead.

Eze 神奇進球助兵工廠登頂，卻傳傷病壞消息

來源：Arsenal.com

兵工廠線——Eze 在第九分鐘踢進一記精彩的遠射，幫兵工廠一比零打敗紐卡索，重新登上英超榜首，領先曼城三分。

但壞消息是，Eze 和 Havertz 都受了傷提前離場。週三就是歐冠四強對 Atletico Madrid 的大戰，兵工廠的傷兵問題讓人擔心。

Arsenal beat Newcastle 1-0 with a brilliant long-range strike from Eze in the ninth minute, reclaiming the top spot in the Premier League and moving three points clear of Manchester City. But the bad news: both Eze and Havertz suffered injuries and had to leave the pitch early. With Wednesday's Champions League semi-final against Atletico Madrid looming, Arsenal's injury crisis is a growing concern.
