報告：人工智慧把工作分成兩條路

來源：PwC

會計公司資誠（PwC）今天發表報告，看了二十七個國家超過十億則徵人廣告。

報告說，人工智慧把工作分成兩條路。需要判斷和專業的工作，薪水成長快了百分之四十二。

最會用人工智慧的前百分之二十公司，工作效率比二○一八年高了一百六十三倍，好處越來越集中在少數公司。

PwC, the accounting firm, released a report today after analyzing over one billion job listings across 27 countries.

The report found that artificial intelligence is splitting the job market into two paths. Work requiring judgment and expertise saw salary growth accelerate by 42 percent.

The top 20 percent of companies most adept at using AI saw productivity grow 163 percent compared with 2018, with the benefits increasingly concentrated among a select few companies.
