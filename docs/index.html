日本央行繼續升息，全球唯一

Nikkei / Reuters

日本的中央銀行正在升息（提高借錢的費用），目標是從百分之零點五升到百分之一。這是九十年代以來最高的。原因是日本工人連續三年大幅加薪，物價也在漲。現在全世界只有日本在升息，美國和歐洲都按兵不動。

Japan's central bank is raising interest rates—increasing the cost of borrowing—with a target move from 0.5% to 1%. This is the highest rate since the 1990s. The reason: Japanese workers have secured large wage increases for three consecutive years, and prices are rising. Now Japan alone is raising rates worldwide; the United States and Europe are holding steady.

油價大漲：海峽關閉讓全世界緊張

特別報導 Special Edition

最近油價漲到2022年以來最高的地方。布蘭特原油到了每桶112美元，美國的油價也過了100美元。為什麼會這樣？因為美國和以色列在二月底一起打了伊朗，伊朗就把荷莫茲海峽（一條很重要的海上通道）關起來了。

全世界大約五分之一的石油都要經過這條海峽，每天差不多一千八百萬桶。現在過不去，油就不夠用，價格當然飛漲。高盛銀行說，每桶油裡面已經多了14到18美元的「戰爭加價」。如果海峽到四月中還沒打開，有人說油價可能衝到200美元。美國已經放出史上最大量的備用石油，但還是不夠把價格壓下來。油貴了，東西都會跟著變貴，大家的生活壓力也更大了。

Oil prices have surged to their highest levels since 2022. Brent crude reached $112 per barrel, and American oil prices also exceeded $100. Why? Because the United States and Israel jointly attacked Iran in late February, and Iran responded by closing the Strait of Hormuz—a critically important maritime passage.

Approximately one-fifth of the world’s oil must pass through this strait, roughly eighteen million barrels daily. With the passage now closed, oil supplies are insufficient, and prices have naturally skyrocketed. Goldman Sachs says that each barrel of oil now includes a $14 to $18 “war premium.” If the strait remains closed through mid-April, some analysts say oil could spike to $200 per barrel. The United States has released its largest-ever strategic petroleum reserves, but this still hasn’t been enough to push prices down. As oil becomes more expensive, everything else follows suit, and everyone’s cost of living increases.
