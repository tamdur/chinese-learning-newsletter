Anthropic 推出「玻璃翼」計畫，結盟守護重要軟體

來源：Anthropic

美國的 AI 公司 Anthropic 昨天宣布了一項新計畫，叫做「玻璃翼」(Project Glasswing)。他們找了幾家大科技公司和資訊安全公司一起合作，目標是保護世界上最重要的軟體不被壞人入侵。

參加的公司可以提前使用 Anthropic 還沒公開的新模型「Mythos」。這個版本是特別訓練過的，能幫人快速找出程式裡的漏洞(弱點)。AI 公司一邊擔心模型會被用來攻擊別人的電腦，一邊也希望 AI 能變成防守的好工具。

Anthropic Unveils Project Glasswing Coalition to Defend Critical Software

American AI company Anthropic yesterday announced a new initiative called Project Glasswing. They have partnered with several major technology and cybersecurity firms, with the goal of protecting the world's most important software from attackers.

Participating companies get early access to Anthropic's as-yet-unreleased Mythos model. This version has been specially trained to help people quickly find vulnerabilities (weaknesses) in code. AI companies are worried their models could be used to attack other people's systems, but they also hope AI will become a powerful tool for defense.

歐洲央行上調通膨預估，中東戰火推高油價

來源：Reuters / ECB

歐洲中央銀行(管歐元的銀行)昨天把今年的通膨(物價上漲)預估調高了一些。原因是中東的戰爭讓石油變貴，歐洲的電費和汽油費都跟著上漲。

美國這邊也受到影響。市場現在認為，聯準會今年剩下的時間大概都不會再降息，機率從上週的百分之四十七一下子跳到百分之六十。物價壓力沒退，各國央行只能繼續等。

ECB Raises Inflation Forecast as Middle East War Pushes Up Oil Prices

The European Central Bank (the bank that manages the euro) yesterday raised its inflation (rising prices) forecast for this year. The reason is that the war in the Middle East has made oil more expensive, and Europeans are paying more for electricity and gasoline as a result.

The United States is feeling the effects too. Markets now believe the Federal Reserve probably will not cut rates again this year, with the odds jumping from 47% a week ago to 60% now. With price pressures refusing to ease, central banks everywhere can only keep waiting.

參議院通過戰爭權力案，限制對委內瑞拉動武

來源：AP / Reuters

美國參議院昨天以跨黨派(兩黨合作)的票數通過一項提案，要求總統在對委內瑞拉採取任何新的軍事行動之前，必須先通知國會。白宮非常生氣，說這會讓總統沒辦法快速反應。

這是國會少見地想把戰爭決定權拿回來的動作。提案還要經過眾議院，也可能被川普總統否決。不過光是這次表決本身就顯示，連共和黨內也有人對在南美洲動武感到不安。

Senate Passes War Powers Measure Limiting Military Action Against Venezuela

The US Senate yesterday passed a bipartisan resolution requiring the president to notify Congress before taking any new military action against Venezuela. The White House reacted furiously, arguing it prevents the president from responding quickly.

It is a rare attempt by Congress to reclaim its authority over war-making decisions. The measure still needs to pass the House and could be vetoed by President Trump. Even so, the vote itself shows that even some Republicans are uneasy about military action in South America.

Waymo 無人車開進芝加哥，伊利諾州討論新法

來源：WTTW 芝加哥新聞

Google 旗下的 Waymo 這個星期開始在芝加哥市中心測試無人駕駛計程車。現在車上還是要坐一位安全員，車子先熟悉路況和紅綠燈。以後如果法律允許，車子就可以自己跑。

伊利諾州議會正在討論一項新法案，準備正式讓無人車上路。支持的人說這對老人和行動不方便的人很有幫助；反對的人則擔心工作機會和路上的安全。芝加哥可能很快就會加入舊金山和洛杉磯的行列。

Waymo Robotaxis Begin Testing in Chicago as Illinois Debates New Law

Google's Waymo this week began testing self-driving taxis in downtown Chicago. For now, a safety driver still sits in the car while the vehicles learn the streets and traffic lights. In the future, if the law allows, the cars will drive on their own.

The Illinois legislature is debating a new bill that would formally permit autonomous vehicles on state roads. Supporters say it would help the elderly and people with mobility limitations; opponents worry about job losses and road safety. Chicago may soon join San Francisco and Los Angeles as a Waymo city.

密西根贏了！三十七年來第一座 NCAA 男籃冠軍

來源：密西根大學體育部

週一晚上在印第安納波利斯，密西根大學以六十九比六十三擊敗康乃狄克大學 (UConn)，拿下今年 NCAA 男子籃球全國冠軍。這是密西根自從一九八九年以來的第一座冠軍，整整等了三十七年。

控球後衛卡度 (Elliot Cadeau) 拿下全場最高的十九分，被選為四強賽最有價值球員。大前鋒蘭德柏格 (Yaxel Lendeborg) 也貢獻十八分和九個籃板。密西根將在四月十二日舉行冠軍遊行，整個安娜堡市都快瘋了。

Michigan Wins! First NCAA Men's Basketball Title in Thirty-Seven Years

On Monday night in Indianapolis, the University of Michigan defeated UConn 69–63 to win this year's NCAA men's basketball national championship. It is Michigan's first title since 1989 — a thirty-seven-year wait.

Point guard Elliot Cadeau scored a game-high 19 points and was named Final Four Most Outstanding Player. Forward Yaxel Lendeborg added 18 points and 9 rebounds. Michigan will hold a championship parade on April 12, and all of Ann Arbor is about to lose its mind.

兵工廠線：哈維茲絕殺，客場一比零贏葡萄牙體育

來源：UEFA / Arsenal.com

兵工廠 (Arsenal) 昨晚作客里斯本，踢歐洲冠軍聯賽八強賽第一回合。場上一直是零比零，直到第九十一分鐘，哈維茲 (Havertz) 接到馬丁內利 (Martinelli) 的傳球，冷靜射門得分。

這個客場一比零對兵工廠非常重要，下週的第二回合將回到倫敦的酋長球場。葡萄牙體育踢得很認真，但兵工廠守得扎實，最後把握住一個關鍵的機會，帶走寶貴的勝利。

The Arsenal Beat: Havertz's Late Winner Gives the Gunners a 1–0 Away Win Over Sporting

Arsenal last night traveled to Lisbon for the first leg of their Champions League quarter-final. The match stayed at 0–0 until the 91st minute, when Havertz collected a pass from Martinelli and finished calmly.

This 1–0 away win is huge for Arsenal; next week's second leg returns to the Emirates in London. Sporting played hard, but Arsenal defended solidly and seized one crucial chance to take away a precious victory.
