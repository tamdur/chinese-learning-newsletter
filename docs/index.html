以色列攻擊伊朗第十九天：油價大漲

來源：Al Jazeera

以色列軍隊確認用空襲打死了伊朗的情報部長，這是兩天內被打死的第三位高官。以色列國防部長說，軍隊現在可以不用再問總理，就能攻擊任何伊朗高官。

美國和以色列也攻擊了南帕斯天然氣田，這是全世界最大的天然氣田。結果油價漲到每桶一百一十四美元，美國加油站的油價也在十八天內漲了快三成。伊朗也已經關閉了荷莫茲海峽（重要的石油運輸路線），情況很不樂觀。

Israel's military confirmed it killed Iran's intelligence minister in an airstrike, the third high-ranking official to be killed in two days. Israel's defense minister said the military can now strike any Iranian official without asking the prime minister first.

The US and Israel also attacked the South Pars natural gas field, the world's largest gas field. As a result, oil prices surged to $114 per barrel, and prices at US gas stations have risen nearly 30% in eighteen days. Iran has also shut down the Strait of Hormuz, a critical oil shipping route, and the situation looks dire.

美國最高法院判定：總統不能自己加關稅

來源：SCOTUSblog

美國最高法院以六比三判定，川普總統用來加關稅的那個法律，其實沒有給他這個權力。大法官說，收稅是國會的工作，不是總統的。連川普自己選的法官也站在反對的一邊。

結果怎樣呢？政府改用另一個法律加了百分之十五的稅，但這個稅只能收一百五十天，大約七月就到期了。之前已經收的兩千多億美元關稅，要不要退還，現在也還不知道。

The U.S. Supreme Court ruled 6-3 that the law President Trump relied on to impose tariffs actually doesn't grant him that power. The justices said that collecting taxes is Congress's job, not the president's. Even the judges Trump himself appointed sided with the opposition.

So what happened? The government used another law to impose a 15% tariff instead, but this tax can only be collected for 150 days—it expires around July. Over $200 billion in tariffs that have already been collected may or may not need to be refunded—no one knows yet.

小熊隊簽下布瑞格曼：五年五十二億

來源：Bleacher Report / ESPN

芝加哥小熊隊簽下了三壘手 Alex Bregman，合約是五年一億七千五百萬美元，是球隊歷史上第三大的合約。他是三次明星賽球員，生涯打了兩百零九支全壘打。

小熊隊現在的內野陣容很強，也被看好能在國家聯盟好好拚一下。球隊總薪水大約兩億三千一百萬，差一點就要碰到豪華稅的線了。

The Chicago Cubs have signed third baseman Alex Bregman to a five-year, $175 million contract, making it the third-largest deal in franchise history. The three-time All-Star has hit 209 home runs over his career.

With their strengthened infield lineup, the Cubs are now considered contenders in the National League. The team's total payroll is approximately $231 million, bringing them close to the luxury tax threshold.

芝加哥公車地鐵加強安全 否則失去補助

來源：Chicago Tribune / Sun-Times

芝加哥的公車和地鐵系統 CTA 被聯邦政府警告：如果不改善安全，就會失去五千萬美元的補助。結果 CTA 提出了新計畫：增加百分之七十五的警力，也會在車站加裝更高的閘門。

好消息是，攻擊工作人員的事件少了大約四分之一。但嚴重的暴力事件去年反而創了二十四年新高。坐車的人也因此變少了。

Chicago's public bus and train system, the CTA, has been warned by the federal government that it will lose $50 million in funding if it doesn't improve safety. In response, the CTA has announced a new plan: increase police presence by 75 percent and install taller gates at stations.

The good news is that incidents of attacks on staff have dropped by about 25 percent. However, serious violent crimes actually hit a 24-year high last year. As a result, fewer people are riding transit.

科學家發現：基因啟動前就有隱藏的支架

來源：Nature Genetics / ScienceDaily

英國的科學家發現了一件讓人很驚訝的事。在胚胎（剛開始發育的生命）裡面，DNA 在基因還沒有開始工作之前，就已經搭好了一個立體的支架（像蓋房子的鷹架）。

以前科學家一直以為這個時候的 DNA 是亂的，結果完全不是！它其實非常有秩序，每個基因都被放在對的位子。研究也發現，如果支架壞掉，細胞會以為被病毒攻擊，啟動免疫反應。這個研究發表在《Nature Genetics》上。

British scientists have made a surprising discovery. In embryos (life forms just beginning to develop), DNA already has a three-dimensional scaffold in place (like the framework used in building construction) before genes even start working.

Scientists previously thought that DNA was disorganized at this stage, but that turned out to be completely wrong! It is actually highly ordered, with each gene positioned correctly. The research also found that if the scaffold breaks down, cells mistakenly think they are being attacked by a virus and trigger an immune response. This study was published in Nature Genetics.
