微軟一天市值增加四千五百億美元，創股市紀錄

來源：Washington Post / Bloomberg

微軟（Microsoft）七月三十日股價大漲百分之十六，一天之內市值增加大約四千五百億美元，是股市史上單日增加最多的一次。

原因是它的雲端服務Azure營收成長百分之四十三，一年收入首次超過一千億美元。過去兩星期，大家一直擔心AI花太多錢、賺不回來，這份財報第一次給了清楚的答案。

Microsoft's stock surged 16% on July 30th, adding roughly $450 billion to its market value in a single day — the largest single-day market cap gain in stock market history.

The driver was Azure, the cloud services division, which posted 43% revenue growth and crossed $100 billion in annual revenue for the first time. For the past two weeks, investors have fretted over whether AI investments drain too much cash without delivering returns. This earnings report finally provided a clear answer.

川普宣布加薩協議，哈瑪斯交武器換以軍撤退

來源：NPR/AP

美國總統川普七月三十一日宣布一份加薩協議：哈瑪斯交出所有武器、拆掉地下通道，以色列則分階段撤兵。

之後加薩會由一個專家組成的新政府管理，再由「國際穩定部隊」和巴勒斯坦警察一起維持治安。不過哈瑪斯和以色列都還沒有公開答應，美國官員也說不出時間表。

On July 31st, US President Trump announced a Gaza agreement whereby Hamas surrenders all weapons and dismantles its underground tunnels, while Israel withdraws its military in phases.

Gaza would subsequently be governed by a new administration made up of technical experts, with an international stabilization force and Palestinian police jointly maintaining security. However, neither Hamas nor Israel has publicly agreed, and US officials could offer no timeline.

俄飛彈炸毀美國公司在基輔的無人機工廠

來源：Kyiv Post

俄羅斯七月三十一日用飛彈炸毀基輔附近一座無人機工廠。工廠屬於美國公司Terminal Autonomy，這是開戰以來俄軍第一次直接毀掉美國公司的廠房。

這座廠生產不怕干擾的長程無人機，據了解沒有人死亡。事情發生在川普和澤倫斯基白宮會面三天後，也讓人開始問：美國人持有，到底擋不擋得住俄羅斯？

Russia destroyed a drone factory near Kyiv with a missile on July 31st. The facility belongs to Terminal Autonomy, an American company — the first time since the war began that Russian forces have directly destroyed an American company's plant.

The factory produced long-range drones resistant to electronic jamming, and no deaths were reported. The strike came three days after Trump and Zelensky met at the White House, and it raises a pointed question: does American ownership actually deter Russia at all?

音樂節四天湧入五十萬人，芝加哥全城備戰

來源：Chicago Sun-Times

Lollapalooza音樂節七月三十日在芝加哥格蘭特公園開唱，一連四天，門票全部賣光，估計會有將近五十萬人走進市中心。

因為這幾天可能有雷雨，市府先在三座停車場設好避難點；CTA和Metra都加開班次，哥倫布大道、密西根大道等路段封到八月。今年的壓軸歌手大多是女生，像是Lorde和Charli XCX。

The Lollapalooza music festival opened on July 30th at Chicago's Grant Park for a four-day run. Tickets are completely sold out, and an estimated half a million people are expected to pour into downtown.

With thunderstorms possible over the next few days, the city has set up shelter points in three parking garages in advance; the CTA and Metra are both adding extra service, and stretches of Columbus Drive and Michigan Avenue are closed into August. This year's headliners are mostly women, including Lorde and Charli XCX.

熊隊新球場計畫轉向印第安納州

來源：NFL.com / Chicago Sun-Times

芝加哥熊隊董事會投票決定，推動印第安納州哈蒙德市的新球場計畫，暫時放下伊利諾州的阿靈頓高地。

印第安納州最多願意出十億美元，熊隊自己再拿出二十億美元。真的蓋成的話，這會是熊隊一百零六年來第一次在外州打主場。伊利諾州沒有提出一樣好的條件，州長普利茲克說，球隊得先講清楚自己要什麼。

The Chicago Bears' board of directors voted to advance a new stadium plan in Hammond, Indiana, setting aside the Arlington Heights site in Illinois for now.

Indiana is willing to put up as much as $1 billion, with the Bears contributing $2 billion themselves. If it is actually built, it would be the first time in the Bears' 106-year history that they play home games outside the state. Illinois did not offer comparable terms, and Governor Pritzker said the team first needs to make clear what it actually wants.

兵工廠線：提姆伯拚在開幕戰前復原

來源：Sky Sports / Arseblog

兵工廠線——後衛提姆伯（Jurrien Timber）前陣子動了大腿內側的小手術，現在正努力趕上八月二十一日主場對考文垂的開幕戰。他已經回到球隊一起練習，但還沒完全歸隊。

另一名主力後衛薩利巴因為背傷，可能要休息四到五個月。衛冕冠軍兵工廠很可能在第一場比賽，就少了兩名最好的後衛。

The Arsenal beat — defender Jurrien Timber recently underwent minor adductor surgery and is now pushing to be ready for the home opener against Coventry on August 21st. He is back training with the squad, but is not fully available yet.

Another first-choice defender, Saliba, may need four to five months out with a back injury. The defending champions could well begin their title defence missing two of their best defenders.
