用光控制大腦，三人得諾貝爾醫學獎

來源：Nobel Prize / NPR / STAT

今年的諾貝爾醫學獎在十月五日公布，得主是美國史丹佛大學的戴瑟羅斯，還有德國的赫格曼和納格爾。三人會平分一千二百萬瑞典克朗的獎金。

他們研究一種綠色的藻類（長在水裡的小生物），發現它身上有會感覺到光的蛋白質。科學家把這種蛋白質放進動物的腦細胞，只要用光一照，就能讓細胞打開或關上。

這個方法叫做「光遺傳學」，現在是研究大腦最重要的工具之一，可以幫忙找出記憶、害怕和上癮是怎麼來的。

The Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine for this year was announced on October 5th. The recipients are Deisseroth from Stanford University in the United States, along with Hegemann and Nagel from Germany. The three scientists will share 12 million Swedish kronor in prize money.

They studied a type of green algae—microscopic organisms found in water—and discovered it contains light-sensitive proteins. Scientists inserted these proteins into animal brain cells, and by shining light on them, they could activate or deactivate the cells at will.

This technique is called "optogenetics" and has become one of the most powerful tools for brain research. It allows scientists to uncover how memory, fear, and addiction arise.

美國第一列電池火車，在芝加哥亮相

來源：Mass Transit Magazine / Electrek

芝加哥的通勤火車公司Metra和瑞士的Stadler公司，十月五日在麥考米克展覽中心，展出第一列為美國打造的電池火車。

Metra一共買了八列，要用在南區的Rock Island線。電充飽後可以跑大約七十到一百公里，充電只要二十到三十分鐘。

這些新火車預計最快二〇二七年底開始載客，會取代老舊的柴油火車。它不用在鐵路上方拉電線，可以省下一大筆錢。

Metra, Chicago's commuter rail operator, and Stadler Rail of Switzerland unveiled the first battery-powered train built for the United States at McCormick Place on October 5th.

Metra has ordered eight trains in total, which will operate on the Rock Island Line on the South Side. On a full charge, they can travel roughly 70 to 100 kilometers, and recharging takes just 20 to 30 minutes.

The new trains are expected to begin service as early as late 2027, replacing aging diesel-powered trains. Because they don't require overhead wires along the tracks, they will save a considerable amount of money.
