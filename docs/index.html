三大AI公司要自己訂安全規則

來源：TechCrunch

Google、OpenAI和Anthropic九月二十五日說，要一起成立新組織「前沿AI標準局」（SAFA），專門管最強、最新的AI。它會訂出安全測試、外部檢查和出事回報的規則。

這個組織不歸政府管，最快今年底成立。不久前，白宮才把一份AI行政命令先放一邊，所以美國現在還沒有全國的AI規則。

也就是說，替最強AI訂規矩的，現在是公司自己，不是政府。

On September 25th, Google, OpenAI, and Anthropic announced plans to jointly establish a new organization called the Frontier AI Standards Agency (SAFA), which would oversee the most advanced and cutting-edge AI systems. The organization would establish rules for safety testing, external audits, and incident reporting. Importantly, it would operate independently of government oversight, with the earliest launch date being the end of this year. This announcement comes shortly after the White House shelved an AI executive order, leaving the United States without any national AI regulations. As a result, the rules governing the most powerful AI systems are now being set by the companies themselves, not by the government.

最高法院准各州用聯邦資料查選民

來源：CNN Politics

美國最高法院星期五以6比3決定，各州可以用國土安全部的SAVE資料庫，查選民名單上的人是不是美國公民。

批評的人說，這個資料庫以前常把後來才成為公民的人，錯當成外國人。不過法律規定，選舉前90天內不能大量刪除選民。

同一天，法院也第三次擋下密蘇里州的新選區地圖，說現在換地圖會讓選舉大亂。

The U.S. Supreme Court ruled 6-3 on Friday that states may use the Department of Homeland Security's SAVE database to verify whether voters on election rolls are U.S. citizens.

Critics argue that the database has frequently misclassified people who later became citizens as foreign nationals.

However, federal law prohibits the removal of large numbers of voters within 90 days of an election. On the same day, the Court blocked Missouri's new electoral district map for the third time, saying that changing district boundaries now would create chaos for the election.

芝加哥停車表要轉手，投票延到下週

來源：Block Club Chicago

芝加哥市議會星期五本來要投票，決定要不要讓Stonepeak公司用25.3億美元，買下全市的停車收費表公司。反對的議員用規則把投票延到下週二。

故事要從2008年說起。當時市政府把75年的停車費收入，只用大約11.6億美元就賣掉了，到今天很多人還覺得虧大了。

這次買家會先給市府7500萬美元，放進退休金。市府很需要錢，明年預算還缺8.82億美元。

Chicago's city council was scheduled to vote Friday on whether to allow Stonepeak to purchase the city's parking meter company for $2.53 billion. Opposing aldermen used procedural rules to delay the vote until next Tuesday. The story goes back to 2008, when city officials sold 75 years of parking fee revenue for roughly $1.16 billion—a deal many still consider a massive loss. Under this proposal, the buyer would first give the city $75 million to deposit into the pension fund. The city desperately needs the money; next year's budget faces an $882 million shortfall.

Claude算出九圈物理難題

來源：Anthropic

Anthropic九月二十五日公布，它的AI模型Claude完成了一個粒子物理大計算，算的是很小的粒子互相撞到時，會怎麼彈開。

「圈」是計算的層數，每多一圈就難很多。以前的紀錄是2023年人類做到的八圈。保持紀錄的物理學家Lance Dixon花了兩週檢查，確定Claude算對了。

同一天，Anthropic還公布另一個實驗：讓AI代替人交換書，結果有61%符合主人真正的想法。

On September 25th, Anthropic announced that its AI model Claude had solved a major particle physics problem—calculating how tiny particles scatter when they collide with each other. In physics calculations like these, "loops" represent the layers of computation, and each additional loop dramatically increases the difficulty. The previous record stood at 8 loops, achieved by humans in 2023. Lance Dixon, the physicist who holds the record, spent two weeks reviewing the results and confirmed that Claude's answer was correct. That same day, Anthropic also unveiled another experiment: they had AI select books to exchange on behalf of humans. The AI's selections matched the owners' actual preferences 61% of the time.

小熊隊進季後賽，排名還在爭

來源：Boston Globe

小熊隊星期四已經拿到國聯外卡，連續兩年打進季後賽。但星期五連打兩場，都輸給紅襪隊，比數是3比4和0比2。

兩場打了18局，只得到3分，打線冷得不是時候。現在小熊落後教士隊兩場勝差，還在跟教士隊、費城人隊爭排名。

好消息是，如果戰績一樣，小熊排在這兩隊前面。三戰兩勝的外卡賽9月29日開打，排名高的隊可以在自己的主場打。

The Cubs clinched a National League wild card berth Thursday, securing a postseason spot for the second consecutive year. But Friday brought disappointment—they lost both games to the Red Sox, 3-4 and 0-2. Over 18 innings in the two contests, they managed just 3 runs, with their bats going cold at a critical moment. Now trailing the Padres by two games, the Cubs are still battling San Diego and Philadelphia for playoff positioning.

The silver lining: if the three teams finish with identical records, the Cubs hold the tiebreaker. The best-of-three wild card series begins September 29, with the higher seed earning home-field advantage.

Arsenal Beat — Captain Odegaard's knee is healthy again, and he'll join the team for this Sunday's away fixture at Newcastle. Saka and Calafiori should also feature, though Timber and Merino need more time on the sidelines. Following last week's 0-3 loss to Brighton, Arteta acknowledged that the Rice-Guimarães midfield partnership hasn't quite gelled. Rice is also dealing with a lingering injury. Odegaard's return is perfectly timed: his passing range addresses exactly where the team has been weakest, and it relieves Rice of having to shoulder the burden alone.
