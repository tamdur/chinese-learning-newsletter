支付公司Stripe七十億美元買下AI「轉接台」

來源：Bloomberg

美國支付公司Stripe宣布，花超過七十億美元買下一家叫OpenRouter的公司。這家公司幫大約八百萬名工程師，把程式接到四百多種人工智慧模型，只要一把鑰匙就通。

三個月前，它的身價才十三億美元，現在翻了五倍多。這說明在人工智慧的世界裡，最搶手的位置已經變成中間那個「轉接台」。

American payments company Stripe announced it is acquiring OpenRouter for over 7 billion dollars. The company serves roughly 8 million developers, acting as a universal connector that lets them plug their code into more than 400 different AI models using a single API key.

Three months ago, OpenRouter was valued at just 1.3 billion dollars—now it's worth five times that. The acquisition tells a striking story about today's AI landscape: the most valuable real estate isn't the AI itself, but the middle layer—the “hub” that connects them all.

美伊六十天期限到期 川普放話要炸阿曼

來源：CNN

六月時，川普總統訂下六十天期限，要和伊朗談好核子問題和荷莫茲海峽（中東最重要的航道）的通行。星期一期限到了，雙方什麼也沒談成。

川普說這個期限「不重要」，也不打算給戰爭訂結束時間。他還在訪問中警告：居中傳話的阿曼如果擋路，美國就會轟炸他們。

In June, President Trump set a sixty-day deadline to resolve nuclear issues with Iran and secure passage through the Strait of Hormuz, the Middle East's most critical shipping lane. Monday marked the deadline, but the two sides reached no agreement.

Trump called the deadline “not important” and said he has no intention of setting an end date for the conflict. He also warned in an interview that if Oman—which has been mediating between the two sides—gets in the way, the United States will bomb them.

中國七月數據全面走弱 學者談「日本化」

來源：Bloomberg

中國七月的成績單很難看：工廠生產只成長百分之四點五，比六月慢；零售只成長百分之零點六，不到預估的一半；今年前七個月的投資還減少百分之六點七。

不少經濟學家開始用「日本化」形容中國：不是一時變差，而是可能長期低成長、東西賣不動。十月中國還要和歐盟、美國談貿易。

China's July scorecard looks grim. Factory production grew just 4.5%—slower than June. Retail sales rose only 0.6%, less than half the forecast. And investment for the first seven months of the year actually fell by 6.7%.

Many economists are now describing China's situation as “Japanification”—not a temporary slowdown, but a possible shift toward long-term sluggish growth and weak consumer demand. Trade negotiations with the European Union and the United States still loom in October.

芬蘭八年研究：多看螢幕的孩子 長大反應更好

來源：ScienceDaily

芬蘭東芬蘭大學等單位追蹤兩百六十位孩子八年，等他們長到平均十五點八歲，再用電腦測驗檢查學習、專心和記憶力。

結果和大家想的相反：小時候看螢幕比較多的孩子，長大後的思考反應反而比較好。研究者說，重點可能不是看多久，而是在螢幕上做什麼。伊利諾州卻已立法，要讓手機退出全州教室。

Researchers from the University of Eastern Finland and other institutions tracked 260 children over eight years. When the children reached an average age of 15.8, they administered computer-based tests to assess their learning, concentration, and memory.

The results contradicted conventional expectations: children who spent more time looking at screens when they were young actually showed better cognitive responses as they grew up. Researchers suggested that the crucial factor may not be how much time is spent on screens, but rather what children do on them. Illinois, meanwhile, has already passed legislation to remove mobile phones from classrooms across the state.

The next day the team announced four roster moves: signing veteran defensive end Marcus Davenport and defensive back Deantre Prince, while releasing running back Coleman Bennett and the injured Nikola Kalinic. Final roster cuts are not far off.

麻煩的是，最好的中後衛薩利巴因為背傷要長期休養。上季球隊只失二十七球，現在卻得在曼城、利物浦、切爾西的追趕下，少一個人守。

The Arsenal beat — Arsenal's Premier League season kicks off on Friday, 21 August, at home against newly promoted Coventry. It is the first time in 22 years they begin a campaign as English champions.

The trouble is that their best centre-back, William Saliba, is sidelined with a back injury requiring extended recovery. Last season the team conceded just 27 goals; now they must hold off Manchester City, Liverpool and Chelsea with one fewer defender.
