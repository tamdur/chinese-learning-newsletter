美伊停火談判在巴基斯坦開場，范斯領軍

來源:半島電視台

美國副總統范斯（萬斯）星期五抵達巴基斯坦首都伊斯蘭馬巴德，帶領美方代表團和伊朗開會。這是自一九七九年以來兩國最高層級的見面。

同行的還有維特科夫和庫什納。伊朗方面由國會議長加利巴夫和外長出席。主辦的巴基斯坦事先就把話說低：只要雙方願意再談一輪，就算成功。

難談的問題包括荷姆茲海峽、黎巴嫩要不要放進來，還有伊朗手上的濃縮鈾。兩週前的停火很不穩，這場戰爭已經打了四十二天。

US Vice President Vance arrived Friday in Islamabad, the capital of Pakistan, leading an American delegation to meet with Iran. This is the highest-level meeting between the two countries since 1979. Witkoff and Kushner are traveling with him. Iran is represented by Parliament Speaker Ghalibaf and the foreign minister. Host Pakistan set expectations low in advance: if both sides are willing to come back for another round, that counts as success.

The hard questions include the Strait of Hormuz, whether to bring Lebanon into the talks, and Iran's stockpile of enriched uranium. The ceasefire from two weeks ago has been shaky, and the war has now been going on for forty-two days.

美國三月通膨跳到百分之三點三，油價是主因

來源:美國勞工部/Trading Economics

美國勞工部星期五公布三月的消費者物價報告，比去年同期漲了百分之三點三，是二零二四年五月以來最高。二月才只有百分之二點四。

最明顯的就是油價。汽油漲了百分之十八點九，家用燃油更漲了百分之四十四點二，因為中東打仗讓大家怕油供應不夠。

單月物價漲了百分之零點九，是將近四年來最大的一次跳動。市場現在都在賭聯準會（美國央行）下次降息會往後延。

The US Labor Department released its March consumer price report on Friday, showing prices rose 3.3% from a year earlier — the highest reading since May 2024. February had come in at just 2.4%.

The clearest driver was oil. Gasoline jumped 18.9% and home heating oil surged 44.2%, as war in the Middle East stoked fears of a supply crunch.

Month-on-month, prices climbed 0.9%, the biggest single-month jump in nearly four years. Markets are now betting the Federal Reserve (the US central bank) will push back its next rate cut.

阿提米絲二號太空人返回地球，結束五十三年來首次載人繞月

來源:NASA

美國太空總署的獵戶座太空船星期五晚上八點零七分在聖地牙哥外海降落太平洋，結束大約六十九萬五千英里的繞月之旅。

船上四位太空人是懷斯曼、格洛弗、柯克和加拿大籍的韓森。他們是自一九七二年阿波羅十七號以來，第一批去過月球再回來的人。

他們現在已經登上美軍莫薩號軍艦。這次任務順利，也為下一步阿提米絲三號鋪路。三號的目標是讓太空人真的登陸月球南極。

NASA's Orion spacecraft splashed down in the Pacific Ocean off the coast of San Diego at 8:07 Friday evening, ending a roughly 695,000-mile journey around the moon.

The four astronauts aboard were Wiseman, Glover, Koch, and Canadian astronaut Hansen. They are the first people to travel to the moon and back since Apollo 17 in 1972.

They have now boarded the U.S. Navy ship USS Mosa. The mission went smoothly and paves the way for the next step, Artemis III, which aims to actually land astronauts at the moon's south pole.

普立茲克：聯邦若動海關，芝加哥經濟會垮

來源:芝加哥太陽報

伊利諾州州長普立茲克星期五發火，回應國土安全部部長穆林前一天的說法。穆林暗示聯邦政府可能把庇護城市機場的海關人力抽走，包括歐海爾機場，逼地方配合抓移民。

普立茲克警告，這樣做會「讓美國最忙的機場停擺」，也會打垮芝加哥地區經濟。芝城馬上就要主辦世界盃比賽，壓力特別大。

事情發生的時候，國土安全部的經費停擺已經進入第八週，創下紀錄。

Illinois Governor Pritzker lashed out Friday, responding to remarks made the previous day by Homeland Security Secretary Mullin. Mullin had hinted that the federal government could pull customs staff from airports in sanctuary cities, including O'Hare, to pressure local governments into cooperating with immigration enforcement.

Pritzker warned that such a move would "shut down America's busiest airport" and would also cripple the Chicago-area economy. With Chicago set to host World Cup matches soon, the pressure is especially intense.

The confrontation comes as the Department of Homeland Security's funding lapse enters a record-breaking eighth week.
