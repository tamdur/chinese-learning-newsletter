人工智慧宣稱解開百年數學難題

來源：OpenAI / Quanta Magazine

OpenAI 在九月八日說，公司裡一個還沒公開的新模型，讓大約一萬個小程式一起工作，連續算了八十八個小時，寫出一份數學證明。

題目叫「納維斯托克斯方程式」，用來算水和空氣怎麼流動，天氣預報和飛機設計都靠它。這道題困擾數學家大約九十年，是七大「千禧年難題」之一。

不過數學界還沒檢查完。而且這次證的是加了外力的版本，和真正能拿一百萬美元獎金的題目並不完全一樣。

On September 8th, OpenAI announced that an unreleased new model at the company had orchestrated roughly ten thousand small programs working in tandem for 88 continuous hours to produce a mathematical proof. The problem involves the Navier-Stokes equation, which governs how water and air flow—the foundation of weather forecasting and aircraft design. The puzzle has stumped mathematicians for roughly 90 years and is one of the seven "Millennium Prize Problems." The mathematics community has yet to complete its review, however. What was proved is also a variant with external forces added, not quite the same as the version eligible for the one million dollar prize.

十二國禁止進口以色列屯墾區商品

來源：TIME

加拿大、英國、法國等十二個國家在九月八日一起宣布，要限制或禁止進口以色列屯墾區（以色列人在佔領的約旦河西岸蓋的社區）生產的商品。

他們說，那裡的暴力事件多到從來沒見過。英國外交大臣甚至用了「種族清洗」這樣重的字眼。

以色列很生氣，宣布反擊，包括關閉英國在東耶路撒冷的領事館。屯墾區的商品其實不多，重點是這些國家改用海關規定來表態。

Twelve countries—Canada, Britain, France, and nine others—announced jointly on September 8 that they would restrict or ban imports of goods produced in Israeli settlements, communities that Israelis have built in the occupied West Bank. They cited unprecedented levels of violence in these areas. Britain's Foreign Secretary went so far as to use the term "ethnic cleansing." Israel responded with anger, announcing retaliatory measures, including the closure of Britain's consulate in East Jerusalem. While settlement goods represent a relatively small share of commerce, the significance lies in these countries using customs regulations as a political statement.

美軍擊沉五艘伊朗油輪

來源：CBS News

美國中央司令部九月八日說，伊朗革命衛隊兩天內兩次用飛彈攻擊美國軍艦，美軍因此還手，擊沉了五艘和革命衛隊有關的油輪。

這是這場戰爭裡，美國第一次打伊朗的商用油船，而不是軍事目標。美方說，這些船屬於一個價值好幾十億美元的地下網路，專門替革命衛隊賺錢。

也就是說，美國開始直接打伊朗的錢包。

The U.S. Central Command announced on September 8 that Iran's Revolutionary Guard launched missile attacks on American military vessels twice over two days. In response, the U.S. military struck back by sinking five oil tankers linked to the Revolutionary Guard. This marks the first time in the conflict that the United States has targeted commercial oil tankers rather than military objectives. According to the U.S., these vessels are part of a multi-billion-dollar covert network designed specifically to generate revenues for the Revolutionary Guard. Put another way, America has begun directly targeting Iran's financial interests.

共和黨在達拉斯開期中選舉大會

來源：Republican National Committee

美國共和黨的期中選舉大會今天在德州達拉斯開幕，一共兩個晚上。川普總統今晚發表主題演講，明晚由副總統范斯壓軸。

政黨大會通常只在總統選舉那年才辦，這是美國史上第一次為期中選舉開大會。主辦單位說，每晚大約會有兩萬人到場。

十一月就要投票。共和黨顯然想把一場一場的地方選戰，變成一場全國的比賽。

The Republican Party's midterm election convention opened today in Dallas, Texas, running for two nights. President Trump delivers the keynote speech tonight, with Vice President Vance headlining tomorrow evening. Party conventions typically only take place in presidential election years—this is the first time in U.S. history one has been held for midterms. Organizers expect roughly twenty thousand attendees each night. Voting happens in November. Republicans are clearly seeking to transform scattered local election races into a single nationwide campaign.

熊隊公開阿靈頓高地球場設計圖

來源：CBS News Chicago

芝加哥熊隊九月八日在阿靈頓高地的社區說明會上，第一次公開新球場的設計圖。球場要蓋在以前的阿靈頓賽馬場，佔地三百二十六英畝，有固定屋頂，可以坐六萬人。

旁邊還規劃旅館、商店、一千一百多戶住宅，以及一個新的火車站。球隊說，完工後會帶來大約九千個長期工作。

前陣子熊隊一直拿「搬去印第安納州」當籌碼，這回伊利諾州的方案終於變成看得見的圖。

On September 8, the Chicago Bears publicly unveiled designs for their new stadium at a community meeting in Arlington Heights. The facility would be built on the site of the former Arlington Park racetrack, covering 326 acres, with a fixed roof and seating for 60,000. The development would also include hotels, retail shops, over 1,100 residential units, and a new commuter rail station. The team says the completed project would generate approximately 9,000 permanent jobs. The Bears had recently wielded threats of moving to Indiana as a negotiating tactic, but now Illinois's proposal has finally become something tangible.

兵工廠今晚作客拿坡里　鋒線太薄是隱憂

來源：ESPN

兵工廠線——兵工廠今晚到義大利拿坡里作客，踢新球季歐冠的第一場。真正的看點不是對手，而是自己的攻擊線夠不夠用。

今年夏天，球隊把馬丁內利賣去沙烏地阿拉伯，熱蘇斯轉去巴塞隆納，卻沒有補進任何一名前鋒，只簽下希臘邊鋒佐利斯。

進攻只能靠約克雷斯和薩卡撐住。一季要打三十八場聯賽，再加八場歐冠，這樣的陣容走不走得遠，今晚就是第一道考題。

The Arsenal Beat — Arsenal travels to Naples, Italy tonight for their first Champions League match of the new season. But the real story isn't about the opponent—it's whether their attack can get the job done. This summer, the club sold Martinelli to Saudi Arabia and transferred Jesus to Barcelona without bringing in any replacement striker, only signing Greek winger Tzolis. The attack now hinges on Gyökeres and Saka. With 38 league matches plus 8 Champions League ties ahead this season, whether this squad has enough to go the distance is the first real test—and it starts tonight.
