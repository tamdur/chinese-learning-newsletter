荷姆茲海峽再現油輪，有望重新開放

來源：CNN

波斯灣的荷姆茲海峽，從七月八日打仗以後幾乎關閉，這兩天終於又有商船通過。八月五日有八艘船經過，其中五艘是油輪；戰爭前每天超過一百艘。

伊朗和阿曼正在談一條臨時的安全航道：進來的船走靠近伊朗的一邊，出去的船走阿曼那一邊，先試兩到四個月。美國總統川普說，海峽很快就會全面重開。

The Strait of Hormuz in the Persian Gulf has been nearly closed since fighting erupted on July 8th, but merchant vessels have finally begun passing through again over the past two days. On August 5th, eight ships transited the strait, five of them tankers — a sharp drop from the pre-war average of more than one hundred vessels a day. Iran and Oman are negotiating a temporary safe corridor: inbound ships would travel along Iran's side while outbound ships use Oman's side, with an initial trial period of two to four months. US President Trump said the strait will fully reopen soon.

美參院委員會認定佛奇藐視國會

來源：CNN

美國參議院一個委員會八月六日以八票對五票通過，認定前衛生官員佛奇藐視（看不起、不配合）國會。他作證時一百一十一次搬出憲法第五修正案，一個有關疫情的問題都不回答。

主席、參議員蘭德・保羅沒有交給全院表決，直接把案子送到司法部，要求起訴。司法部說正在研究。怎麼決定，會影響以後每一次國會調查。

A US Senate committee voted 8 to 5 on August 6th to hold former health official Anthony Fauci in contempt of Congress. During his testimony he invoked the Fifth Amendment 111 times and refused to answer a single question about the pandemic. Committee chairman Senator Rand Paul bypassed a full Senate vote and sent the case directly to the Justice Department, asking for prosecution. The Justice Department says it is reviewing the matter. How it decides will shape every future congressional investigation.

芝加哥殯儀館驚見五十七具遺體

來源：Chicago Sun-Times

芝加哥南區九十五街的殯儀館 South Chicago Chapel，被檢查人員找到五十七具沒有好好冷藏、已經腐爛（壞掉）的遺體，有些還被塞在衣櫃和車庫裡，現場滿是老鼠和蟲。

伊利諾州八月七日停掉負責人摩根的執照。但摩根夫婦去年才因為類似問題被關掉一家火化場，當時大約一百具遺體被亂放，他們卻還能繼續做生意。

Inspectors discovered fifty-seven improperly refrigerated, decomposing bodies at South Chicago Chapel, a funeral home on 95th Street on Chicago's South Side. Some had been crammed into closets and the garage, and the premises were overrun with rats and insects.

Illinois suspended the license of the operator, Morgan, on August 7th. But this is not the couple's first offence: only last year a crematory of theirs was shut down over similar violations, when roughly one hundred bodies were found improperly stored — and they were still allowed to keep doing business.

美國建商合併潮再添一樁

來源：StockTitan

美國蓋房子的公司 Dream Finders Homes 八月七日宣布，要用大約二十二億美元現金，買下同行 Beazer Homes，每股三十三點五美元。合併後一年可交屋約一萬三千戶，成為全美第六大建商。

背後的原因是利息很高、土地又貴，中型建商借不到便宜的錢。以後蓋房子的公司愈來愈少、愈來愈大，想買房的人選擇也更少。

US homebuilder Dream Finders Homes announced on August 7th that it will buy rival builder Beazer Homes for about $2.2 billion in cash, at $33.50 a share. The combined company will deliver roughly 13,000 homes a year, making it the sixth-largest builder in the country.

Behind the deal are high interest rates and expensive land: mid-sized builders can no longer borrow cheaply. The result is a homebuilding industry of fewer and larger firms — and fewer choices for people trying to buy a house.

公牛隊補強，新球季不想再墊底

來源：Chicago Sun-Times

芝加哥公牛隊的休季補強大致結束。新上任的籃球事務副總裁葛拉漢用兩年四千五百萬美元簽下老將鮑威爾，搭配年輕的布澤利斯和新人威爾森。

《芝加哥太陽報》八月七日分析說，肯花大錢請老將，代表球隊不打算再故意輸球換選秀籤。離爭冠還很遠，但這一季至少會拚勝場。

The Chicago Bulls have largely wrapped up their offseason. Newly appointed executive vice-president of basketball operations Bryson Graham signed veteran Norman Powell to a two-year, $45 million contract, pairing him with young Matas Buzelis and rookie Caleb Wilson. The Chicago Sun-Times argued on August 7th that a willingness to spend big on a veteran means the team no longer intends to lose on purpose for draft picks. Contention is still a long way off, but this season they will at least be playing for wins.

兵工廠線：羅傑斯改投切爾西

來源：Sky Sports

兵工廠線——兵工廠今年夏天最想要的前鋒羅傑斯，八月七日以一億一千七百萬英鎊加入切爾西，成為身價最高的英國球員。兵工廠不肯出超過一億英鎊，只能看著對手變強。

轉會窗快關了，兵工廠只補進佐利斯和吉馬良斯，卻失去特羅薩德和諾加德。主力後衛薩利巴還要養傷好幾個月，左路攻擊手也還沒買到。

The Arsenal beat — Morgan Rogers, the forward Arsenal wanted most this summer, joined Chelsea on August 7th for £117 million, making him the most expensive English player ever. Arsenal refused to go above £100 million and could only watch a rival get stronger.

With the transfer window closing soon, Arsenal have brought in only Christos Tzolis and Guimarães while losing Leandro Trossard and Christian Nørgaard. First-choice defender William Saliba still needs several months to recover, and the club has yet to sign a left-sided attacker.
