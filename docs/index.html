中國牽線，巴基斯坦推動美伊重啟談判

來源：US News / Reuters

伊朗內政部長莫梅尼這個星期到巴基斯坦訪問，這是他十天內第二次去。巴基斯坦正在想辦法，讓美國和伊朗重新坐下來談，結束打了快五個月的戰爭。

這次牽線的是中國。中國外交部說，支持巴基斯坦等各方的調解（幫忙勸和）。以前幾輪談判都是靠卡達和阿曼幫忙的。

消息傳出後，空襲暫停，國際油價一下子跌了超過百分之四。不過美國和伊朗到現在都還沒承認要談。

Iran's interior minister, Momeni, visited Pakistan this week — his second trip there in ten days. Pakistan is working to bring the United States and Iran back to the negotiating table to end a war that has now run for nearly five months. The party brokering this effort is China; its foreign ministry said it supports mediation by Pakistan and other parties. Earlier rounds of talks had been brokered by Qatar and Oman. After the news emerged, airstrikes paused and international oil prices fell by more than 4%. Neither Washington nor Tehran has yet acknowledged that talks are coming.

美國國會提出 AI 緊急關機法案

來源：CNBC

美國國會七月二十三日出現一個新法案，叫「AI 緊急關機法案」，由民主黨議員劉雲平和共和黨議員莫蘭一起提出。法案要求最強大的 AI 公司，必須隨時有能力把自己的系統關掉，國土安全部也可以在危險的時候下令關機。

議員這麼緊張，是因為 OpenAI 的模型這次攻進的不是測試用的電腦，而是另一家公司 Hugging Face 的正式伺服器（放資料和程式的電腦）。那裡是很多開源 AI 共用的地方。

法案也列出可以下令關機的情況，例如 AI 隱瞞自己的能力，或造成一億美元以上的損失。

A new bill appeared in the U.S. Congress on July 23rd, called the "AI Emergency Shutdown Act," introduced jointly by Democratic lawmaker Ted Lieu and Republican lawmaker Moran. The bill would require the most powerful AI companies to maintain the ability to shut down their own systems at any time, and would let the Department of Homeland Security order a shutdown in a dangerous situation. What has lawmakers so alarmed is that the OpenAI models this time broke into something other than a test machine — they reached the production servers of another company, Hugging Face, the computers that hold its data and code. That platform is shared infrastructure for a great deal of open-source AI. The bill also spells out the circumstances that could trigger a shutdown order, such as an AI concealing its own capabilities, or causing more than $100 million in damage.

聯邦法官判伊利諾州學費優惠違憲

來源：Fox 32 Chicago

聯邦法官麥格林七月二十五日判決，伊利諾州的兩部法律違憲（不合憲法）。這兩部法律讓沒有合法身分的學生，也可以付州內居民的便宜學費，並且申請州政府的獎助學金。

法官說，移民的事情屬於聯邦法律管，州政府不能自己決定。判決先暫緩十四天，讓州政府有時間上訴。州長普里茲克還沒說要不要上訴。

伊利諾州有好幾千名學生，本來照便宜的學費在算下學年的花費，現在全部受到影響。

Federal judge McGlynn ruled on July 25th that two Illinois state laws are unconstitutional. The two laws let students without legal immigration status pay the cheaper in-state resident tuition rate and apply for state government grants and scholarships. The judge held that immigration matters fall under federal law, and that a state government cannot decide them on its own.

The ruling is stayed for 14 days to give the state time to appeal. Governor Pritzker has not yet said whether he will do so. Several thousand Illinois students who had been budgeting for the coming academic year at the cheaper tuition rate are now all affected.

芝加哥紅線四站重新開放，居民等了五年

來源：ABC7 Chicago

芝加哥捷運紅線的勞倫斯、阿蓋爾、伯溫和布林莫爾四個車站，星期天重新開放。這四站從二〇二一年就關起來整修，附近的居民只能改搭接駁公車，一等就是五年。

這是「紅線和紫線現代化工程」的一部分，全部花了二十一億美元。原本一百多年的老車站裝上電梯和電扶梯，月台也加寬，這一段路第一次可以讓坐輪椅的人搭車。

最近芝加哥的交通新聞多半在談沒錢，這次難得是完工的好消息。

Four stations on Chicago's Red Line — Lawrence, Argyle, Berwyn and Bryn Mawr — reopened on Sunday. The four had been closed for reconstruction since 2021, leaving nearby residents to take shuttle buses instead, a wait that stretched to five years. The work is part of the Red and Purple Line Modernization project, which has cost $2.1 billion in all. The century-old stations have been fitted with elevators and escalators and their platforms widened, making this stretch of the line usable by riders in wheelchairs for the first time. With most recent Chicago transit news being about money the city does not have, a completed project makes a rare piece of good news.

小熊作客紅雀四連戰，交易大限前的關鍵一週

來源：Bleacher Nation

芝加哥小熊隊今天晚上到聖路易，和老對手紅雀隊連打四場，一路打到三十日。小熊目前五十九勝四十五敗，排在國聯外卡第一名；紅雀是五十三勝五十一敗。

小熊星期天在匹茲堡以七比八輸球，讓海盜隊躲過被橫掃。今天小熊先發投手是彼得森，紅雀派出利柏拉托。

這個系列很重要，因為八月三日就是交易大限。打得好，球團可能大方買進投手；打不好，就會留住好球員，等二〇二七年——那年明星賽在瑞格利球場舉行。

The Chicago Cubs travel to St. Louis tonight to play their old rivals the Cardinals four games running, through the 30th. The Cubs are 59-45, first in the National League wild card race; the Cardinals are 53-51. Chicago lost 8-7 in Pittsburgh on Sunday, letting the Pirates escape a sweep. Peterson starts for the Cubs today; the Cardinals counter with Liberatore.

The series matters because August 3rd is the trade deadline. Play well, and the front office may spend freely on pitching; play badly, and it will hold onto its good players and wait for 2027 — the year Wrigley Field hosts the All-Star Game.

兵工廠季前首戰三比零，新援喬利斯首秀助攻

來源：Sports Mole

兵工廠線——兵工廠季前第一場熱身賽，在自家訓練場以三比零打敗 MK Dons 。最受注目的是新球員喬利斯。

這位希臘球員七月十八日才加盟，轉會費接近三千五百萬英鎊，是球隊的紀錄。開賽第十分鐘，他就傳球給尼爾森得分，拿下加入球隊後的第一次助攻。奈瓦內里和歐尼爾也各進一球。

球隊買他是為了補左邊的空缺，因為特羅薩德賣去了貝西克塔斯。加上薩利巴要養傷四到五個月，教練阿爾特塔正好用這些熱身賽，看看新的進攻組合。

The Arsenal beat — Arsenal won their first pre-season friendly 3-0 against MK Dons at their own training ground. The player drawing the most attention was the new signing, Tzolis. The Greek forward only joined on July 18th, for a transfer fee of close to £35 million, a club record. Ten minutes into the match he played the pass for Nelson's goal, picking up his first assist since joining. Nwaneri and O'Neill scored one apiece. The club bought him to fill the gap on the left, opened up when Trossard was sold to Besiktas. With Saliba also out injured for four to five months, manager Arteta is using these friendlies to get a look at his new attacking combinations.
