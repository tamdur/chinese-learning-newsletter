美國特使見普丁 俄烏戰爭出現轉機

來源：Washington Post / CNN

美國特使威特科夫和庫許納，星期六在莫斯科和俄羅斯總統普丁談了三個小時。這是俄烏戰爭卡住八個月後，第一次真正的和談。

談完以後，普丁下令從星期六半夜起，三天不攻打烏克蘭首都基輔。烏克蘭總統澤倫斯基也說，到星期一為止不打莫斯科。兩位特使星期天飛到基輔，把結果告訴烏克蘭。

US envoys Whitcomb and Kushner met with Russian President Putin in Moscow on Saturday for three hours. It was the first genuine peace talks after eight months of stalemate in the Russia-Ukraine war. Following the discussions, Putin ordered a three-day ceasefire on attacks against Kyiv, effective from Saturday midnight. Ukrainian President Zelenskyy agreed to hold fire from Moscow through Monday. The two envoys flew to Kyiv on Sunday to report the outcome to Ukrainian officials.

尼泊爾冰河崩塌 死亡人數升到一千三百多人

來源：PBS NewsHour

八月二十六日，喜馬拉雅山一座冰河崩塌，大水沖進尼泊爾。到九月五日，已經有一千三百四十二人死亡，還有將近四千九百人失蹤。

失蹤的人裡有九百多位是水力發電廠工人。救難隊星期六晚上鑽開被泥巴塞住的隧道，送進空氣和攝影機，到現在救出將近三百人。科學家說，喜馬拉雅山變熱的速度大約是全球的兩倍。

On August 26th, a glacier in the Himalayas collapsed and sent massive floods into Nepal. By September 5th, the death toll had reached 1,342, with nearly 4,900 people still missing. Over 900 of those missing were workers at hydroelectric plants. Rescue teams drilled through mud-blocked tunnels on Saturday night, sending in air and cameras to locate survivors, and have rescued nearly 300 people so far. Scientists report that the Himalayas are warming at roughly twice the global rate.

芝加哥明年短少八億八千萬美元

來源：Chicago Sun-Times

芝加哥市長強生九月三日公布明年的預算，發現少了八億八千二百四十萬美元。主要原因是退休金和還債，光是這兩項明年就多出一億六千萬美元。

記者問他會不會裁員或調高房屋稅，他兩個都沒有否認，只說不會把負擔放在工人身上。他要到十月才會正式提出預算。

Chicago Mayor Brandon Johnson released next year's budget on September 3rd and discovered a $882.4 million shortfall. Pensions and debt service are the main drivers—just those two items will require an additional $160 million next year. When reporters asked whether he would cut jobs or raise property taxes, he didn't deny either option, saying only that he wouldn't place the burden on workers. He won't formally present the budget until October.

菲律賓副總統莎拉交保

來源：AP

菲律賓副總統莎拉．杜特蒂九月五日交保（先付一筆錢，暫時不用被關起來）。前一天法院發出逮捕令，因為她被控在二○二四年的網路記者會上說，如果自己被殺，就要派人殺掉總統小馬可仕等三個人。

她付了三十六萬披索，大約五千七百四十美元。她說當時只是擔心自己的安全。如果被判有罪，她可能永遠不能再選公職，包括二○二八年的總統大選。

Philippine Vice President Sara Duterte posted bail on September 5th. A court issued an arrest warrant the day before, charging her with statements made during an online press conference in 2024 in which she allegedly said that if she were killed, she would have President Ferdinand Marcos Jr. and two others killed in retaliation. She paid 360,000 pesos—about $5,740—for bail. She said she was simply expressing concern about her personal safety at the time. If convicted, she could be permanently barred from holding public office, including the 2028 presidential election.

密西根最後一秒長傳 十三比十二險勝

來源：Detroit News

九月五日，密西根大學美式足球隊在主場以十三比十二贏了西密西根大學，這是新總教練惠廷漢的第一場比賽。

比賽最後，四分衛安德伍德丟出長傳沒接到，對手球員已經在場上慶祝，但裁判看了影片，判定還剩一秒。安德伍德再丟一次，隊友布坎南跳起來接到這顆四十七碼的球，密西根贏一分。

On September 5th, the University of Michigan football team defeated Western Michigan University 13-12 at home in the first game under new head coach Whittingham. In the final moments, quarterback Underwood threw a long pass that fell incomplete, and the opposing team was already celebrating on the field. However, the referee reviewed the footage and determined that one second remained on the clock. Underwood threw again, and his teammate Buchanan jumped up to catch the 47-yard pass, giving Michigan a one-point victory.

兵工廠賣掉傑穌斯 前鋒只出不進

來源：Sky Sports / ESPN

兵工廠線——巴西前鋒傑穌斯正式離開兵工廠，轉會到西班牙的巴塞隆納，合約到二○二九年，轉會費大約一千五百萬歐元。

這位二十九歲的球員二○二二年加入球隊，去年幫兵工廠拿下二十二年來第一座英超冠軍。加上幾天前賣掉馬丁尼利，球隊這個夏天走了兩名前鋒，卻一個前鋒都沒買，接下來還要打英超和八場歐冠比賽。

Arsenal Beat — Brazilian striker Gabriel Jesus has officially left Arsenal, transferring to Barcelona in Spain with a contract through 2029. The transfer fee is approximately 15 million euros. The 29-year-old joined the club in 2022 and helped Arsenal capture their first Premier League title in 22 years last season. With the sale of Martinelli just days earlier, the team has let two strikers leave this summer without acquiring a single replacement. They still have Premier League and eight Champions League matches to play.
