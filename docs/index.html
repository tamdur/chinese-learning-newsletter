葉門封鎖沙烏地、油價衝破百元

來源：CNN / Bloomberg

葉門的胡塞武裝說，他們要封鎖沙烏地阿拉伯的港口，還攻擊了兩艘沙烏地油輪，其中一艘起火。

這條紅海通道叫「曼德海峽」，是全球運油的重要關口。加上伊朗早就封了另一個海峽，油價一口氣漲破每桶一百美元，是兩個月來第一次。美國總統川普很生氣，威脅要對伊朗發動大規模攻擊。

Yemen's Houthi militias say they are blockading Saudi Arabia's ports and have attacked two Saudi oil tankers, one of which caught fire.

This Red Sea passage, known as the Strait of Mandeb, is a critical chokepoint for global oil shipping. With Iran already blocking another strait, oil prices have jumped past $100 per barrel for the first time in two months. President Trump expressed fury and threatened to launch a large-scale military strike against Iran.

財政部示警：AI熱潮像網路泡沫

來源：NOTUS

美國財政部有一份內部報告，警告現在大家瘋狂投資AI，情況很像二十多年前的「網路泡沫」。

報告說，AI公司已經和股市、貸款、資料中心、晶片廠、電力公司綁在一起。萬一AI退燒，可能一路影響整個經濟。這是美國政府第一次正式把AI熱潮當成金融風險看待。

The U.S. Treasury Department has released an internal report warning that the current AI investment frenzy mirrors the "dot-com bubble" from over twenty years ago.

According to the report, AI companies have become intertwined with the stock market, lending, data centers, chip manufacturers, and power companies. If the AI boom cools, the impact could ripple through the entire economy. This marks the first time the U.S. government has formally treated the AI boom as a financial risk.

川普對六十國加關稅、理由是強迫勞動

來源：PBS / AP

川普政府宣布，要對六十個貿易夥伴加收關稅，理由是這些國家沒有認真管「強迫勞動」的問題。

大部分國家，包括中國和越南，被加百分之十二點五；英國、加拿大、墨西哥等十七個國家則是百分之十。新關稅星期五生效。之前川普加稅的權力被最高法院打掉，這次他換了新的法律理由。

The Trump administration has announced tariffs on sixty trading partners, arguing that these countries have failed to adequately address forced labor.

Most countries, including China and Vietnam, will face a 12.5 percent increase; seventeen countries including the UK, Canada, and Mexico will see a 10 percent increase. The new tariffs take effect Friday. After the Supreme Court previously blocked Trump's tariff authority, he is now relying on a different legal justification.

司法部撤回對紐時記者的傳票

來源：NPR

美國司法部撤回了對《紐約時報》三名記者的傳票，本來想逼他們到大陪審團作證。

這件事和報導新空軍一號專機的安全疑慮有關。法官說，如果司法部不撤回，他就會依「言論自由」把傳票撤銷。司法部還承認，把傳票寄給記者家人是個錯誤。

The US Department of Justice has withdrawn subpoenas against three New York Times journalists that it had originally sought to use to compel them to testify before a grand jury.

The matter relates to reporting on security concerns regarding the new Air Force One aircraft. A federal judge stated that if the Department of Justice had not withdrawn the subpoenas, he would have dismissed them on freedom of speech grounds. The Department of Justice also acknowledged that sending the subpoenas to the journalists' family members was a mistake.

密西根新四分衛、安德伍德志在最強

來源：On3

密西根大學美式足球隊新教練惠廷罕，宣布由二年級的安德伍德擔任先發四分衛。

安德伍德曾是全美排名第一的高中新秀。他說自己整個休賽季都更聰明地練習，目標是成為密西根隊史上最厲害的球員。秋季集訓正式開始，這也是新教練時代的第一大考驗。

Michigan's new head coach Whittingham has announced that sophomore Underwood will be the starting quarterback.

Underwood was once the nation's top-ranked high school prospect. He says he spent the entire offseason training more strategically, aiming to become the greatest player in Michigan history. With fall camp officially underway, it marks the first major test of the new coaching era.

兵工廠線——阿森納赴美季前熱身

來源：OneFootball

兵工廠線——阿森納足球隊的美國季前巡迴要開始了，第一場在洛杉磯對上伯恩茅斯。

Arsenal's preseason tour of America is underway, with the opening match taking place in Los Angeles against Bournemouth.

This is the new season's first friendly. Manager Arteta has brought twenty-six players, allowing latecomers from the World Cup—White, Timber, and Odegaard among them—to gradually find their feet again, while also giving younger and fringe players like Vieira and Nelson opportunities to make their mark.
