密西根大學今晚對戰阿拉巴馬

來源：Maize n Brew

密西根是今年大學籃球的第一種子隊，成績三十三勝三敗。今晚他們在芝加哥對上阿拉巴馬隊。密西根的五個先發球員都很強，上一場每個人都得了十分以上。

中間最有名的是 Aday Mara，他身高兩百二十一公分，這一季的火鍋數快要打破學校的歷史記錄了。不過阿拉巴馬也不好打——他們上一場投進了十九個三分球。結果很難說，但如果密西根贏了，會是二〇一八年以來走最遠的一次。

Michigan is the No. 1 seed in college basketball this year with a record of 33 wins and 3 losses. Tonight they face Alabama in Chicago. All five of Michigan's starting players are strong — each scored more than 10 points in their last game.

The most notable among them is Aday Mara, who is 221 centimeters tall. His block count this season is close to breaking the school's all-time record. However, Alabama won't be easy to beat — they made 19 three-pointers in their last game. The outcome is hard to say, but if Michigan wins, it will be their deepest tournament run since 2018.

川普開始收百分之十五的全球關稅

來源：Tax Foundation / NPR / Bloomberg

美國最高法院二月的時候說川普之前用的關稅方法不合法。結果白宮換了一個新方法，對所有進口的東西加收百分之十五的稅，但只能用一百五十天。

這是三十多年來最大的加稅，每個家庭大約要多花一千五百美元。大工廠也受影響： Apple 每一季要多花八到十一億美元。七月到期後，國會要決定要不要繼續。

In February, the U.S. Supreme Court ruled that Trump's previous tariff method was illegal. So the White House switched to a new approach, adding a 15% tax on all imported goods, but only for 150 days.

This is the largest tax increase in over thirty years. Each household will have to pay about $1,500 more. Large factories are also affected: Apple will spend an additional $800 million to $1.1 billion per quarter. After it expires in July, Congress will need to decide whether to continue it.

太平洋深海發現二十四種新生物

來源：Inside Climate News / ZooKeys

一個十六人的研究團隊在太平洋海底找到了二十四種新的小生物。這些生物住在夏威夷和墨西哥中間的深海，大約四千公尺深。研究員還發現了一個全新的「超科」（生物分類的大等級），這非常少見。

不過這個地方也面對問題。川普加快了深海開礦的許可。測試結果顯示，開礦的機器動過的地方，生物的數量少了百分之三十七。百分之九十的深海生物還沒有名字，我們都還不認識就可能失去了。

A sixteen-person research team discovered twenty-four new species of small organisms on the Pacific ocean floor. These creatures live in the deep sea between Hawaii and Mexico, at a depth of approximately four thousand meters. Researchers also found an entirely new "superclass" (a major category in biological classification), which is extremely rare.

However, this region faces challenges. Trump has accelerated permits for deep-sea mining. Test results show that in areas where mining machinery has operated, the number of organisms decreased by thirty-seven percent. Ninety percent of deep-sea organisms don't have names yet, and we could lose them before even discovering them.

小熊隊和乘風簽下大約

來源：MLB.com

小熊隊和外野手 Pete Crow-Armstrong 簽了六年一億一千五百萬美元的合約。他去年打了三十一支全壘打、三十五次盜壘，也是全明星賽的先發球員。

他的防守也很厲害，去年有十九次五星等級的接球，是大聯盟的新記錄。今天是開幕日，小熊隊在 Wrigley 球場對國民隊。球迷都很開心，這個約表示球隊是認真要拿冠軍的。

The Cubs signed outfielder Pete Crow-Armstrong to a six-year, $115 million contract. Last year he hit 31 home runs, had 35 stolen bases, and was a starting player in the All-Star Game.

His defense is also impressive — last year he made 19 Gold Glove-caliber catches, a new major league record. Today is Opening Day, and the Cubs are playing the Nationals at Wrigley Field. Fans are excited, as the contract shows the team is serious about winning a championship.

歐盟投票停止掃描私人訊息

來源：Patrick Breyer / TechRadar

歐盟之前想要一個新的法律，要求網路公司掃描每個人的私人訊息。這個計畫叫「聊天控制」，連 WhatsApp 和 Signal 這種加密的軟體也要掃。

但是議員們投票反對了。他們說只能對法官同意的對象才能查。反對的人說，這個系統的錯誤率太高——德國警方的資料顯示，被標記的網路訊息裡面有差不多一半根本沒有問題。不過這件事還沒結束，新版本的「聊天控制」還在談。

The European Union previously wanted a new law that would require internet companies to scan everyone's private messages. This plan, called "Chat Control," would even target encrypted software like WhatsApp and Signal.

However, lawmakers voted against it. They said scanning should only be allowed for targets approved by a judge. Critics argued the system has too high an error rate — data from German police showed that roughly half of the online messages flagged by the system actually contained nothing problematic. Still, the matter isn't settled; a new version of "Chat Control" is still under discussion.
