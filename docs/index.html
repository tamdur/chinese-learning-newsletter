AI股大跌，市值一週少了2.5兆美元

來源：CNN / AP

這個星期，和AI有關的股票大跌。11月5日星期四，美國S&P 500指數一天跌了4.4%，是2025年4月以來最慘的一天。做AI晶片的Nvidia更跌了9.3%。

美國CNN估計，全世界科技股的市值（公司在股市值多少錢）少了大約2.5兆美元。日本和南韓的股市也跌了大約6%。

很多人擔心，民主黨在期中選舉大勝後，會把AI資料中心管得更嚴。星期五股市有反彈，但整個星期S&P 500還是跌了大約2.7%。

AI-related stocks tumbled this week. On Thursday, November 5, the S&P 500 index fell 4.4% in a single day, its worst day since April 2025. Nvidia, the company that makes AI chips, dropped 9.3%. CNN estimates that the market value (how much companies are worth on the stock market) of tech stocks worldwide shrank by about $2.5 trillion. Stock markets in Japan and South Korea also fell by about 6%. Many people worry that after the Democratic Party's big win in the midterm elections, AI data centers will face tighter regulation. Stocks bounced back on Friday, but the S&P 500 still ended the week down about 2.7%.

美國延長對中國晶片管制的暫停期

來源：Baker McKenzie / Dechert

2025年11月，美國和中國談好貿易條件後，美國商務部把一條晶片管制（限制）規定暫停一年。這條規定說，如果一家公司有一半以上屬於黑名單上的中國企業，也會被限制買美國的晶片設備等產品。

這個暫停本來明天（11月9日）就到期。不過根據法律事務所的報告，美國在11月5日已經決定再延長兩個月，到2027年1月10日。

中國那邊有沒有跟著延長，現在還不清楚。AI晶片股正在大跌，這種只是暫時的協議，讓市場更不安心。

In November 2025, after the United States and China agreed on trade terms, the U.S. Department of Commerce suspended for one year a chip export rule (restriction). The rule said that a company more than half owned by Chinese firms on the blacklist would also be restricted from buying American chip equipment and other products. The suspension was due to expire tomorrow, November 9. However, according to a law firm's report, on November 5 the United States decided to extend it by two more months, to January 10, 2027. It is still unclear whether China has extended its side of the arrangement as well. AI chip stocks are falling sharply, and the fact that this is only a temporary deal is making the market even more uneasy.

南非地方選舉，執政黨票數再大跌

來源：CNBC Africa / Reuters

南非11月4日舉行地方選舉。開票到大約98%時，執政黨非洲民族議會（ANC）只拿到大約31%的票。雖然還是第一名，但比2021年的大約46%少了很多。

在最大城市約翰尼斯堡，民主聯盟（DA）拿到大約40%，ANC只剩大約17%。

因為很多城市沒有一個黨過半，幾個黨要一起合作，才能組成市政府。正式結果預計今天公布。

South Africa held local elections on November 4. With about 98% of the ballots counted, the ruling African National Congress (ANC) had won only about 31% of the vote. It still came in first, but that is far down from the roughly 46% it won in 2021. In Johannesburg, the country's largest city, the Democratic Alliance (DA) took about 40%, while the ANC managed only about 17%. Because many cities have no party with a majority, several parties will need to cooperate to form municipal governments. Official results are expected to be released today.

熊隊今晚主場迎戰海盜隊

來源：Chicago Sun-Times / Buccaneers.com

芝加哥熊隊今晚在主場士兵球場，對上坦帕灣海盜隊。比賽在芝加哥時間晚上7點20分開始，NBC全國轉播。

熊隊現在6勝2敗，上週才在客場以34比17打敗上一季的冠軍西雅圖海鷹隊。海盜隊只有3勝5敗，但上週以16比0贏了亞特蘭大，還跑出隊史最多的324碼（美式足球的距離單位）。

不過熊隊比較怕對方跑球，平均每次被跑4.4碼。好球隊就是要贏下這種主場比賽。

The Chicago Bears host the Tampa Bay Buccaneers tonight at Soldier Field. Kickoff is at 7:20 p.m. Chicago time, with a national broadcast on NBC. The Bears are 6-2 and last week won 34-17 on the road against Seattle, last season's champion. The Buccaneers are only 3-5, but last week they shut out Atlanta 16-0 and piled up 324 rushing yards, the most in franchise history. Still, the Bears are more worried about the run game, allowing an average of 4.4 yards per carry. Good teams win home games like this one.

兵工廠1比0小勝赫爾城

來源：Arsenal.com / TNT Sports

兵工廠線——兵工廠11月7日在主場以1比0贏了剛升上英超的赫爾城。上半場兩隊都沒進球，第72分鐘，隊長Odegaard在隊友Madueke射門被擋出後，補射進球。

兵工廠10場拿到27分，在積分榜上領先6分，也拿下本季第7場零失球。

球迷期待的後衛Saliba這次沒有進入比賽名單。他因為背傷，本季還沒上場。教練Arteta說這不是新傷，而是有計畫地讓他慢慢恢復。

The Arsenal beat: Arsenal edged newly promoted Hull City 1-0 at home on November 7. Neither side scored in the first half. In the 72nd minute, captain Odegaard fired home the rebound after Madueke's shot was blocked. Arsenal have 27 points from 10 matches, six points clear at the top of the table, and this was their seventh clean sheet of the season. Fans had hoped to see defender Saliba, but he was left out of the matchday squad. He has not played yet this season because of a back injury. Manager Arteta said this is not a new injury, but a planned, gradual recovery.
