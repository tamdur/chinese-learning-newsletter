荷姆茲談判破局，油價漲破八十四美元

來源：Al Jazeera、CNBC

伊朗上週日表示，除非美國答應六項條件，否則不會和美國直接談重開荷姆茲海峽。同一天，葉門的胡塞武裝用無人機打中沙烏地阿拉伯的吉贊煉油廠，火勢很快被撲滅，沒有人受傷。

布蘭特原油因此漲到每桶八十四點五八美元，三天內漲了超過百分之五。油一變貴，其他東西也跟著貴。美國物價一直降不下來，市場現在猜聯準會（美國的中央銀行）年底前不但不會降息，還可能升息一到兩次。

Iran said on Sunday that it will not negotiate directly with the United States on reopening the Strait of Hormuz unless Washington agrees to six conditions. The same day, Yemen's Houthi forces struck Saudi Arabia's Jazan oil refinery with drones; the fire was quickly put out and no one was injured.

Brent crude consequently climbed to $84.58 a barrel, a rise of more than 5% in three days. When oil gets expensive, everything else follows. American prices have been stubbornly slow to come down, and markets now expect that the Federal Reserve — America's central bank — will not only hold off on cutting rates before year-end but may raise them once or twice.

提格雷再度開戰，衣索比亞和平協議動搖

來源：Amnesty International、Al Jazeera

八月一日起，衣索比亞政府軍和提格雷人民解放陣線的部隊，在靠近蘇丹邊界的謝雷里納一帶重新開火。雙方動用大砲和無人機，好幾百位居民逃過邊界躲到蘇丹。

這是二〇二二年普利托利亞和平協議簽訂後，最嚴重的一次破裂；那場內戰估計死了幾十萬人。國際特赦組織本週警告，當地居民又要面對被傷害的危險。分析家也擔心，蘇丹的兩派人馬會被拉進來，變成整個非洲之角的戰爭。

Since August 1st, Ethiopian government forces and troops of the Tigray People's Liberation Front have been exchanging fire again near Shererina, close to the Sudanese border. Both sides have used artillery and drones, and several hundred residents have fled across the border into Sudan.

This is the most serious breakdown since the Pretoria peace agreement was signed in 2022; that civil war is estimated to have killed several hundred thousand people. Amnesty International warned this week that local residents once again face the risk of serious harm. Analysts also worry that Sudan's two rival factions could be drawn in, turning this into a war across the whole Horn of Africa.

紐約聯準銀行研究：人工智慧改變徵人，沒造成失業潮

來源：Federal Reserve Bank of New York、HR Dive

紐約聯邦準備銀行八月五日發表一份研究。研究部主任亞崔亞整理了銀行裡經濟學家的資料，結論是：人工智慧確實在改變公司想要什麼樣的人，卻還沒讓大家丟掉工作。

調查發現，過去一年只有百分之一的服務業公司說因為人工智慧裁員，製造業則是零。約百分之十二的公司少徵人，但也有百分之十一因為人工智慧多請人。三分之一的公司選擇重新訓練原本的員工。

The Federal Reserve Bank of New York published a research piece on August 5th. Research director Kartik Athreya pulled together data from the bank's economists and concluded that artificial intelligence is indeed changing the kind of people companies want to hire, but it has not yet cost people their jobs.

The surveys found that over the past year only 1% of service-sector firms said they had laid anyone off because of AI, and in manufacturing the figure was zero. About 12% of firms hired fewer people, but 11% hired more because of AI. A third of firms chose to retrain the staff they already had.

上訴法院：白宮宴會廳要國會同意才能蓋

來源：NPR、CBS News

美國聯邦上訴法院八月七日判決，總統不能自己決定在白宮東廂舊址，蓋那座造價四億美元、九萬平方英尺的大宴會廳。三位法官寫道，國會對聯邦的土地和房子有「完全的憲法控制權」，也找不到任何法律授權總統這樣做。

白宮東廂去年秋天就已經被拆掉了。法院把命令先暫停兩個星期，好讓白宮上訴；川普說他會直接告到最高法院。

On August 7th a US federal appeals court ruled that the president cannot decide on his own to build the $400 million, 90,000-square-foot grand ballroom on the site of the old White House East Wing. The three judges wrote that Congress holds "full constitutional control" over federal land and buildings, and that they could find no law authorising the president to do this.

The East Wing was already demolished last autumn. The court has stayed its order for two weeks so the White House can appeal; Trump says he will take the case straight to the Supreme Court.

小熊敲十八支安打，十比二大勝皇家

來源：ESPN、AP

芝加哥小熊八月九日晚上敲出十八支安打，以十比二痛宰堪薩斯城皇家。哈普打出本季第二十號全壘打，捕手阿馬亞補上一支三分砲，布瑞格曼和布希各拿三支安打。

先發投手波伊德投七局失兩分，戰績來到八勝一敗。球團八月三日一口氣做了三筆交易，換來的高斯曼幾天前首次先發就投七局無失分。買來的補強，看來真的有用。

The Chicago Cubs pounded out eighteen hits on the night of August 9th, routing the Kansas City Royals 10-2. Ian Happ hit his 20th home run of the season, catcher Miguel Amaya added a three-run shot, and Alex Bregman and Michael Busch each had three hits.

Starter Matthew Boyd went seven innings, giving up two runs, to improve to 8-1. The front office made three trades in one go on August 3rd, and Kevin Gausman — the arm they brought in — threw seven scoreless innings in his first start days earlier. The reinforcements they bought look like they are genuinely working.

兵工廠線：多特蒙德三比二擊敗兵工廠

來源：Arsenal.com、ESPN

兵工廠線——八月九日的酋長盃，多特蒙德派出三名十八、九歲的年輕球員，一口氣先進三球。兵工廠靠恩瓦內里和約克雷斯的十二碼罰球追到二比三；賽後加賽的十二碼大戰，兵工廠倒是五比四贏了。

同一個下午，球隊才把七千五百萬英鎊買來的布魯諾·吉馬良斯介紹給球迷。問題出在後防：薩利巴要養傷好幾個月，中後衛卻還沒補人。六天後就要對曼城打社區盾，而轉會市場九月一日就關門。

The Arsenal beat — at the Emirates Cup on August 9th, Borussia Dortmund put out three players aged 18 and 19, and they scored the first three goals in short order. Arsenal pulled back to 2-3 through Ethan Nwaneri and a Viktor Gyokeres penalty; in the shootout arranged for after the final whistle, Arsenal did at least win 5-4.

That same afternoon the club presented Bruno Guimaraes, their GBP 75 million signing, to the fans. The problem is at the back: Saliba faces several months out injured, and no centre-back has been signed to replace him. The Community Shield against Manchester City is six days away, and the transfer window shuts on September 1st.
