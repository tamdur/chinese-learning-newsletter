氣候大會落幕 窮國要的錢還在等

來源：Down To Earth / IISD Earth Negotiations Bulletin

聯合國氣候大會COP31在土耳其的安塔利亞舉行，原定11月9日到20日。主辦方一開始就說，這次不是要許新的願望，而是要把以前答應的錢真的拿出來。

結果，各國同意成立一個專門談「氣候資金」的工作計畫。可是，把幫窮國對抗水災、熱浪的錢增加三倍，還有討論貿易規則的提案，都被拿掉了。

「調適目標」（教各國怎麼適應變熱的地球）和讓工人公平轉業的文件，也都沒有談成，只能等下次再說。

The UN climate conference COP31, held in Antalya, Turkey, was originally scheduled for November 9 to 20. From the start, the hosts made clear that this time was not about making new promises, but about actually delivering the money that countries had already pledged. In the end, countries agreed to set up a work programme focused on "climate finance." But a proposal to triple the money helping poor countries cope with floods and heatwaves was dropped, and so was a proposal on trade rules. The "adaptation goal" (guidance on how countries can adjust to a warming planet) and a document on making the shift fair for workers also failed to reach agreement, and will have to wait until next time.

機場列車合約沒招標 得標公司主管捐錢給市長

來源：Chicago Sun-Times

《芝加哥太陽時報》報導，市長強森的團隊今年7月把歐海爾機場「旅客捷運」的合約，直接交給一組公司，沒有公開招標（讓很多公司來比價）。這份合約最多值10億美元。

其中一家是 Harbour 營造公司。合約給出去以後，這家公司的四名主管每人各捐2500美元給強森的競選團隊，看起來違反了市府的道德規定。

市議會最近才投票決定自己來查市府的帳。這件事，可能讓議員更不相信市長。

The Chicago Sun-Times reports that Mayor Johnson's team in July awarded the contract for O'Hare's "Airport People Mover" directly to a group of companies, without a public bidding process (which would have let many firms compete on price). The contract is worth up to $1 billion. One of the firms is Harbour Construction. After the contract was awarded, four of the company's executives each gave $2,500 to Johnson's campaign, which appears to violate the city's ethics rules. The City Council recently voted to conduct its own audit of city finances. The episode could make aldermen even less willing to trust the mayor.

感恩節大戰 熊隊明天作客底特律

來源：Chicago Bears / CBS Chicago

芝加哥熊隊明天（11月26日）感恩節，要到底特律的福特球場挑戰獅子隊。比賽在芝加哥時間中午12點開打，全國電視直播。

兩隊都在搶國聯北區（同一個小組的四支球隊）第一名，這場很可能決定誰能領先。熊隊最近一路連勝，可是這次在對手主場，離上一場比賽只隔幾天，休息時間比平常少很多。

上次兩隊在感恩節碰面是2024年，熊隊以20比23輸了。明天，他們有機會報仇。

The Chicago Bears visit the Detroit Lions at Ford Field tomorrow, November 26, for Thanksgiving. Kickoff is at noon Chicago time, and the game will be broadcast nationally on TV. Both teams are fighting for first place in the NFC North, the four-team division they share, so this game could well decide who takes the lead. The Bears have been on a roll lately, winning game after game. But this time they are playing on the Lions' home field, only a few days after their last game, so they will have much less rest than usual. The two teams last met on Thanksgiving in 2024, when the Bears lost 20-23. Tomorrow, they get a chance to even the score.

兵工廠傷兵多 準備迎戰曼城

來源：Sports Mole / Sky Sports

兵工廠線——兵工廠接下來要對上曼城，這是英超冠軍爭奪戰裡很重要的一場。兵工廠現在排第一，曼城在後面追，很想縮小差距。

麻煩的是，兵工廠的兩名主力中後衛（守在球門前面的球員）都受傷，守門員也有傷。他們這週二才在歐冠對多特蒙德，體力也是問題。

對兵工廠來說，這場考的是板凳深度（替補球員夠不夠強）：換上來的後衛，擋不擋得住曼城？

The Arsenal beat: Arsenal host Manchester City next, a key match in the Premier League title race. Arsenal sit top of the table with City chasing them and eager to close the gap. The problem is that both of Arsenal's starting centre-backs are injured, and so is their goalkeeper. They also played Borussia Dortmund in the Champions League this past Tuesday, so fitness is a concern too. For Arsenal, this match is a test of squad depth: can the replacement defenders hold up against City?
