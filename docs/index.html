一週十二個新模型：三月的 AI 大爆發

來源：Hacker News

三月十日到十六日，六家 AI 公司一口氣推出十二個新模型。其中四個大型系統在七十二小時內同時上線，包括 GPT-5.4、Grok 4.20 和 Gemini 3.1。GPT-5.4 可以一次讀一百萬字，錯誤比上一代少了三分之一。

光是今年第一季就有兩百六十七個新模型發布。分析師說這是「搶先效應」——每家公司都在看對手什麼時候要發布，然後搶在前面。開發者現在每個月就要重新評估一次工具，以前是每三個月。

From March 10 to 16, six AI companies launched twelve new models in rapid succession. Four major systems went live simultaneously within seventy-two hours, including GPT-5.4, Grok 4.20, and Gemini 3.1. GPT-5.4 can process one million characters at once, with one-third fewer errors than the previous generation. In just the first quarter of this year, two hundred sixty-seven new models have been released. Analysts call this the "first-mover effect"—each company watches when its competitors are about to launch, then rushes to announce first. Developers now must reassess their tools every month, whereas previously they did so every three months.

伊朗戰爭推高油價，全球經濟面臨壓力

來源：Financial Times

美國和以色列二月底開始對伊朗的軍事行動後，國際油價從每桶六十五美元飆到一百一十美元。美國加油站的油價也從每加侖（gallon）兩塊九漲到三塊八四。荷莫茲海峽是全球四分之一石油的必經之路，一旦被封鎖情況會更嚴重。

OECD 已經把全球經濟成長預測下調到百分之二點九，美國通膨（物價上漲）預計達百分之四點二，是 G7 國家中最高的。各國央行進退兩難：降息怕物價更高，不降又怕經濟變差。

After US and Israeli military operations against Iran began in late February, international oil prices surged from $65 per barrel to $110. Gas prices at American pumps climbed from $2.90 per gallon to $3.84. The Strait of Hormuz is the chokepoint for a quarter of the world's oil supply; if blocked, the situation would be far worse. The OECD has already downgraded its global economic growth forecast to 2.9%, and US inflation is expected to reach 4.2%—the highest among G7 nations. Central banks face a dilemma: cutting interest rates risks higher prices, while holding steady risks economic deterioration.

AI 不再只是幫手，開始取代工人

來源：Stanford Digital Economy Lab

史丹佛大學研究發現，自從 ChatGPT 出來以後，容易被 AI 影響的入門工作少了百分之十三。二十二到二十五歲的年輕人受到的打擊最大，工作機會下降了百分之十六。

關鍵變化是：AI 現在能自己完成整個工作流程，不只是在旁邊幫忙。MIT 估計 AI 可以取代美國近百分之十二的工人，代表一點二兆美元的年薪。高盛說全球有三億個工作會受影響，稱這是「二〇二六年勞動市場最大的故事」。

A Stanford University study found that since ChatGPT's launch, entry-level jobs susceptible to AI impact have declined by 13 percent. Young people aged 22 to 25 have been hit hardest, with job opportunities down 16 percent. The critical shift: AI can now complete entire workflows independently, not just assist from the sidelines. MIT estimates AI could replace nearly 12 percent of American workers, representing $1.2 trillion in annual wages. Goldman Sachs says 300 million jobs globally will be affected, calling this "the biggest story in the labor market in 2026."

太陽能效率突破百分之百三十，科學家創紀錄

來源：JACS

日本九州大學和德國美因茲大學的團隊做到了一件不可思議的事：一個光子（光的最小單位）可以產生大約一點三個能量載體，效率達到百分之一百三十。這打破了傳統太陽能板的理論上限。

他們用了一種叫「單態分裂」的技術，這個原理從一九六〇年代就知道了，但這是第一次有人真正收到超過百分之百的能量。理論上限是百分之兩百。不過目前還在實驗室階段，離商業使用還有好幾年。

A research team from Kyushu University in Japan and the University of Mainz in Germany achieved something remarkable: a single photon—the smallest unit of light—can generate approximately 1.3 energy carriers, reaching an efficiency of 130 percent. This breaks the theoretical ceiling of traditional solar panels. They used a technique called "singlet fission," a principle known since the 1960s, but this is the first time anyone has actually achieved energy gains exceeding 100 percent. The theoretical maximum is 200 percent. However, the technology remains in the laboratory stage, with several years still needed before commercial use.

密西根大勝田納西，闖進四強

來源：ESPN

密西根大學三月二十九日在芝加哥聯合中心以九十五比六十二大勝田納西，拿到自二〇一八年以來第一張四強門票。三十三分的差距是一九八九年以來最大的。

Lendeborg 拿下二十七分七籃板，獲選區域最有價值球員。Mara 本季阻攻破百，是校史第一人。密西根錦標賽四場都得超過九十分，是三十七年來首見。今晚對亞利桑那，密西根是奪冠熱門之一。

Michigan dominated Tennessee 95-62 on March 29 at the United Center in Chicago, earning their first Final Four berth since 2018. The 33-point margin was the largest in a tournament game since 1989. Lendeborg led the way with 27 points and 7 rebounds, earning Most Valuable Player honors for the region. Mara surpassed 100 blocks this season, a first in school history. Michigan has now scored over 90 points in all four tournament games, a feat not seen in 37 years. Tonight's matchup against Arizona will be crucial, as Michigan is among the championship favorites.
