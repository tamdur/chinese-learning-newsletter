Anthropic 不小心洩露秘密新模型

來源：Fortune

Anthropic 的系統出了問題，差不多三千份沒公開的文件被看到了。其中有一篇文章介紹了一個叫 Claude Mythos 的新 AI 模型，也叫 Capybara。

這個模型已經訓練完成，公司說它是「目前最強的 AI」。它在寫程式、學術推理和網路安全方面的分數都遠超過 Claude Opus 4.6。因為網路安全能力太強，Anthropic 決定先只讓做網路防護的人用。

Anthropic accidentally leaked secret details about a new model. Around three thousand unpublished documents were exposed. One document introduced a new AI model called Claude Mythos, also known as Capybara.

The model has completed training, and the company says it is "currently the most powerful AI." It scores far beyond Claude Opus 4.6 in programming, academic reasoning, and cybersecurity. Because its cybersecurity capabilities are so strong, Anthropic decided to initially limit access to those working on network defense.

Google 推出 Gemini 3 Deep Think 推理模型

來源：Google DeepMind

Google 發布了 Gemini 3 Deep Think，這是一個專門做深度思考的 AI 模型。它在數學比賽中拿到金牌水準，在寫程式比賽中也得到很高的分數。

同時，Google 還推出了 Lyria 3 Pro，可以自動做出三分鐘的音樂。另外，中國的阿里巴巴和小米也在開發開源模型，跟大公司的差距越來越小。

Google has released Gemini 3 Deep Think, a specialized AI model designed for deep reasoning. It achieved gold-medal-level performance in mathematics competitions and scored highly in programming contests.

At the same time, Google also launched Lyria 3 Pro, which can automatically generate three-minute musical compositions. Meanwhile, China's Alibaba and Xiaomi are developing open-source models, with the gap between them and major tech companies narrowing.

芝加哥公共交通迎來五十年最大改革

來源：Capitol News Illinois

伊利諾州長簽了一個重要的法案，成立新的「北伊利諾交通局」來管理 CTA、Metra 和 Pace。每年會多出十五億美元的經費。

這是因為芝加哥的交通系統快沒錢了，如果不改，服務可能要砍掉四成。新計畫還包括統一票價系統和全面改用電動公車。

The Illinois governor signed a major bill establishing a new "Northern Illinois Transportation Authority" to oversee the CTA, Metra, and Pace. The system will receive an additional $1.5 billion in annual funding.

This came about because Chicago's transit system was running out of money, and without reform, services risked being cut by forty percent. The new plan also includes a unified fare system and a comprehensive transition to electric buses.

最高法院擋下關稅，川普怒罵大法官

來源：CNBC

美國最高法院以六比三判定，川普總統不能用緊急法來加關稅，取消了「解放日」關稅。連川普自己提名的兩位大法官也投了反對票。

川普非常生氣，罵他們「不忠、不愛國」。政府要退回一千七百億美元的關稅。不過，川普馬上換了另一條法律，再加百分之十到十五的進口稅。

The Supreme Court blocked Trump's tariffs in a 6-3 decision, ruling that President Trump cannot use emergency powers to impose tariffs, effectively canceling the "Liberation Day" tariffs. Even two Supreme Court justices appointed by Trump himself voted against him.

Trump was furious and attacked them as "disloyal and unpatriotic." The government must refund $170 billion in tariffs. However, Trump quickly pivoted to a different legal authority and imposed new import taxes of 10 to 15 percent.

密西根大勝阿拉巴馬，挺進精英八強

來源：ESPN

一號種子密西根在芝加哥 United Center 以 90 比 77 打敗阿拉巴馬。球星 Lendeborg 拿下 23 分、12 個籃板、7 次助攻，表現非常出色。

上半場阿拉巴馬一度領先九分，但密西根下半場大爆發。替補球員得了 33 分，對手替補只有 6 分。密西根週日將對上田納西，爭取四強門票。

No. 1 seed Michigan defeated Alabama 90-77 at Chicago's United Center to advance to the Elite Eight. Star player Lendeborg delivered an outstanding performance with 23 points, 12 rebounds, and 7 assists.

Alabama led by nine points at halftime, but Michigan erupted in the second half. The Wolverines' bench scored 33 points while Alabama's bench managed only 6. Michigan will face Tennessee on Sunday to compete for a Final Four berth.

美伊戰爭第二個月：戰火擴大，外交仍在進行

來源：綜合報導

美國和以色列對伊朗的軍事行動已經進入第二個月。這場戰爭正在快速擴大。葉門的胡塞武裝向以色列南部發射了一枚飛彈，落點靠近以色列的核子研究設施，不過被成功攔截。同時，伊朗也攻擊了沙烏地阿拉伯境內的美軍基地，造成十多名美軍受傷。開戰以來，已有超過三百名美軍受傷、十三人死亡。

美國國防部正在考慮再派一萬名士兵到中東，目標可能是拿下伊朗九成石油出口經過的哈爾克島。不過外交還沒有停。川普說談判「進展很好」，並把攻擊伊朗石油設施的計畫延後十天到四月六日。國務卿魯比歐則表示，戰爭目標預計「幾週內」完成。目前伊朗已有近兩千人死亡，黎巴嫩也有超過一千人罹難。

The second month of the U.S.-Iran war: fighting escalates as diplomacy continues. Military operations by the United States and Israel against Iran have entered their second month, and the conflict is expanding rapidly. Houthi forces in Yemen fired a missile at southern Israel that landed near an Israeli nuclear research facility but was successfully intercepted. Meanwhile, Iran attacked a U.S. military base in Saudi Arabia, injuring more than a dozen American soldiers. Since the war began, more than three hundred American troops have been injured and thirteen have been killed.

The U.S. Department of Defense is considering deploying an additional ten thousand troops to the Middle East, with the possible target being Kharg Island, through which ninety percent of Iran's oil exports pass. However, diplomacy has not stopped. Trump said negotiations are "progressing very well" and has postponed plans to attack Iranian oil facilities until April 6, a delay of ten days. Secretary of State Rubio stated that war objectives are expected to be completed "within weeks." So far, nearly two thousand Iranians have died, and Lebanon has suffered over one thousand casualties.
