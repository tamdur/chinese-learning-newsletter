以色列空襲黎巴嫩，停火搖晃

來源：Al Jazeera

美國和伊朗星期二才談好兩個星期的停火，第一天就出事了。以色列十分鐘內攻擊了黎巴嫩一百個地方，至少二百五十四人死亡，一千多人受傷。

以色列總理說，停火不包括真主黨（黎巴嫩的武裝團體）。伊朗很生氣，說要再打，還要把荷姆茲海峽再關起來。

Israel Strikes Lebanon, Ceasefire Wobbles

The United States and Iran had just negotiated a two-week ceasefire on Tuesday, and things went wrong on the very first day. Within ten minutes, Israel attacked one hundred locations in Lebanon, killing at least 254 people and wounding more than a thousand.

Israel's prime minister said the ceasefire does not cover Hezbollah (Lebanon's armed group). Iran is furious, saying it will fight again and will close the Strait of Hormuz once more.

油價大跌，道瓊大漲

來源：CNN Business

停火以後，第一批油輪（載石油的大船）平安通過荷姆茲海峽。美國原油從一百一十五美元掉到九十二美元，一天跌了百分之十六。

股市開心得不得了。道瓊指數一天漲了一千三百七十四點，收在四萬七千九百零九點，是今年最大的漲幅。聯準會明年降息的機會也從一成四跳到四成三。

Oil Prices Plunge, Dow Soars

After the ceasefire, the first batch of oil tankers (large ships carrying petroleum) passed safely through the Strait of Hormuz. US crude oil dropped from $115 to $92, falling 16 percent in a single day.

The stock market was thrilled. The Dow Jones index jumped 1,374 points in one day, closing at 47,909 — the biggest gain of the year. The chances of the Federal Reserve cutting interest rates next year also jumped from 14 percent to 43 percent.

甲骨文大裁員三萬人

來源：CIO

星期三早上六點，甲骨文公司開始寄解僱信，美國、加拿大、印度、墨西哥的員工都收到了。這次要裁三萬人，差不多全公司的百分之十八，是公司史上最大的一次。

為什麼？甲骨文要省下八十億美元，去付和開放人工智慧公司那筆一千五百五十六億的運算大單，要蓋三百萬個繪圖晶片的機房。兩個月內，公司已經借了五百八十億的新債。

Oracle's Massive Layoffs Hit 30,000

At six o'clock Wednesday morning, Oracle began sending out termination letters, with employees in the United States, Canada, India, and Mexico all receiving them. This round cuts 30,000 people, roughly 18 percent of the entire company — the largest layoff in company history.

Why? Oracle needs to save $8 billion to help pay for its $155.6 billion compute deal with OpenAI, which requires building data centers housing three million GPUs. In the past two months, the company has already taken on $58 billion in new debt.

臉書推出新模型「繆思火花」

來源：TechCrunch

臉書母公司星期三發表新的人工智慧模型「繆思火花」，是新成立的「超級智慧實驗室」第一個作品，由亞歷山大王帶領。

以前臉書的模型都是開源的，大家都可以拿去用，但這一次不公開。這個模型花了九個月從頭蓋起，之前兩兆參數的「龐然」模型已經被放棄。之後會在雷朋眼鏡、IG、WhatsApp 上都看得到。

Meta Launches New Model "Muse Spark"

Facebook's parent company Meta unveiled a new artificial intelligence model called "Muse Spark" on Wednesday. It is the first product from the newly established Superintelligence Labs, led by Alexandr Wang.

Previously, Meta's models were all open source, and anyone could use them, but this one will not be made public. The model was built from scratch over nine months, and the earlier two-trillion-parameter "Behemoth" model has been abandoned. It will eventually be available on Ray-Ban glasses, Instagram, and WhatsApp.

小熊九比二大勝，霍頓整季報銷

來源：ESPN / MLB.com

昨天的小熊隊一好一壞。貝格曼之前十三個打數一支安打都沒有，昨天一下敲了三支。投手阿薩德投了五又三分之二局沒失分。全隊敲出本季最多的十六支安打，以九比二打敗坦帕灣光芒隊。

可是比賽前幾小時，王牌投手霍頓的核磁共振（一種身體掃描）結果出來，右手肘的韌帶受傷了，整季不能打。芝加哥的投手傷兵又多一個了。

Cubs Win 9-2, Horton Out for Season

Yesterday's Cubs game was a mixed bag. Bregman, who had gone hitless in his previous thirteen at-bats, suddenly banged out three hits. Pitcher Assad threw five and two-thirds scoreless innings. The team's sixteen hits were their season high, and they beat the Tampa Bay Rays 9-2.

But just hours before the game, the MRI results came back for ace pitcher Horton: a ligament injury in his right elbow, and he's out for the entire season. Chicago's pitching injury list just got one name longer.

Havertz's 91st-Minute Winner Stuns Sporting

The Arsenal beat — Havertz came off the bench and headed home the winning goal in the 91st minute, helping Arsenal beat Sporting CP 1-0 away in Lisbon.

This was the first leg of the Champions League quarterfinals. Goalkeeper Raya made several saves to keep a clean sheet. A 1-0 scoreline isn't much, but when the second leg is played back at the Emirates Stadium, that one goal will come in handy.
