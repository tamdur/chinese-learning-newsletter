美國期中選舉明天登場，國會控制權成焦點

來源：Ground News（AP／Reuters）

美國明天（11月3日）舉行期中選舉。眾議院435席全部改選，參議院大約三分之一的席次也要改選，還有36個州要選州長。

民主黨只要多拿下幾席，就能拿回眾議院或參議院的多數。預測市場目前看好民主黨。

這次結果會決定總統最後兩年好不好做事。如果國會變成民主黨主導，就能調查政府，要求官員出來說明。

The United States holds its midterm elections tomorrow, November 3. All 435 seats in the House of Representatives are up for reelection, about one-third of the Senate seats are also being contested, and 36 states will elect governors. Democrats need to win only a few more seats to take back a majority in either the House or the Senate. Prediction markets currently favor the Democrats. The result will shape how easily the president can get things done in his final two years. If Democrats take control of Congress, they will be able to investigate the administration and require officials to come and explain their actions.

OpenAI找錢目標縮小，投資人變冷淡

來源：Venture Daily Digest

據報導，OpenAI這一輪原本想向投資人拿到大約300億美元，但有些投資人反應比預期冷淡，目標降到大約200億美元，少了三分之一。

從12月開始，ChatGPT和寫程式的工具Codex會共用同一個token額度（可以用的量），用多少算多少。

另外，OpenAI也傳出正在和Google談買TPU晶片，想減少對Nvidia的依賴。

According to reports, OpenAI's current funding round originally aimed to raise about US$30 billion from investors, but some investors responded more coolly than expected, so the target was cut to about US$20 billion, a reduction of one-third. Starting in December, ChatGPT and Codex, the coding tool, will draw from a single shared token allowance (the amount of usage available), with consumption counted by how much is actually used. OpenAI has also reportedly been in talks with Google about buying TPU chips, as it looks to reduce its reliance on Nvidia.

伊州明天投票，普立茲克拚第三任

來源：WBEZ Chicago

伊利諾州明天投票。州長普立茲克（JB Pritzker）想拿下第三任，對手又是2022年輸給他的共和黨人貝利（Darren Bailey）。

民調和預測市場都很看好普立茲克。上一位選上第三任的伊州州長，是1980年代的湯普森（Jim Thompson）。如果大勝，他也可能成為2028年總統大選的熱門人選。

同一天，芝加哥也第一次由市民選出公立學校教育委員會的全部21位委員，不再由市長指派。

Illinois votes tomorrow. Governor JB Pritzker is seeking a third term, and his opponent is Republican Darren Bailey, the same man who lost to him in 2022. Polls and prediction markets both strongly favor Pritzker. The last Illinois governor to win a third term was Jim Thompson, back in the 1980s. A landslide win could also make Pritzker a frequently mentioned contender for the 2028 presidential race. On the same day, Chicago will, for the first time, have voters elect all 21 members of the Board of Education, rather than having the mayor appoint them.

兵工廠1比0贏利物浦，14年來第一次

來源：BBC Sport／Sky Sports

兵工廠線——兵工廠星期天到利物浦的主場安菲爾德（Anfield）比賽，以1比0獲勝。這是他們14年來第一次在這裡贏得聯賽。

唯一的進球來自哈維茲（Kai Havertz）。他在英超已經進了46球，成為英超進球最多的德國球員。

幾天前，兵工廠才在聯賽盃PK大戰輸給第四級的弗利特伍德（Fleetwood Town）。這次少了受傷的薩卡（Bukayo Saka），還是撐住了，現在排名第一，領先利物浦9分。

The Arsenal beat: Arsenal won 1-0 at Liverpool's home ground, Anfield, on Sunday. It was their first league win there in 14 years. The only goal came from Kai Havertz, who has now scored 46 Premier League goals, the most of any German player in the competition's history. Just a few days earlier, Arsenal had lost a League Cup penalty shootout to fourth-tier Fleetwood Town. This time, without the injured Bukayo Saka, they held firm. They now sit top of the table, nine points clear of Liverpool.
