最高法院推翻關稅令 總統急換新方案

來源：Perkins Coie

美國最高法院今天做出重大決定，判定總統川普用「國際緊急經濟權力法」加的關稅不合法。法院說，總統不能用緊急法來決定貿易政策，這是國會的工作。

川普很快回應，取消了被推翻的關稅，但馬上用其他法律重新加上一部分。這場關稅大戰還沒結束，但總統的權力已經被法院限制了。

The U.S. Supreme Court made a major ruling today, determining that President Trump's tariffs imposed under the "International Emergency Economic Powers Act" are unlawful. The Court stated that the President cannot use emergency powers to set trade policy—that is Congress's responsibility.

Trump responded swiftly, rescinding the struck-down tariffs but immediately reimposing portions of them under alternative legal authority. The tariff war is far from over, but the President's powers have been constrained by the Court.

法院：禁用 Anthropic 人工智慧違反言論自由

來源：TechCrunch

一位聯邦法官今天判定，川普政府禁止政府機關使用 Anthropic 公司的人工智慧系統違反了憲法第一修正案。這項禁令是去年行政命令的一部分，限制某些人工智慧公司和政府合作。

法官認為，政府不能因為不喜歡一家公司就禁止用它的產品，這是言論自由的問題。這個判決讓人工智慧和政府的關係變得更加複雜。

A federal judge ruled today that the Trump administration's ban on government agencies using Anthropic's artificial intelligence systems violates the First Amendment to the Constitution. The prohibition was part of an executive order last year that restricted certain AI companies from collaborating with the government.

The judge determined that the government cannot ban the use of a company's products simply because it dislikes the company—a matter of free speech. This ruling makes the relationship between artificial intelligence and government even more complex.

阿提米斯二號升空 人類五十年來首次飛越月球

來源：NASA

美國太空總署的阿提米斯二號在四月一日成功發射，四位太空人正在飛往月球的路上。這是一九七二年阿波羅十七號以來，人類第一次離開近地軌道（地球附近的太空區域）。

太空船預計四月六日飛過月球。這次任務雖然不會登陸，但是為未來重返月球鋪好了路。全世界都在關注這趟歷史性的旅程。

NASA's Artemis II successfully launched on April 1st, with four astronauts en route to the Moon. This is humanity's first departure from low Earth orbit—the region of space near Earth—since Apollo 17 in 1972.

The spacecraft is expected to fly past the Moon on April 6th. While this mission won't include a lunar landing, it paves the way for humanity's return to the Moon. The world is watching this historic journey.

伊朗戰爭推高油價：每桶突破111美元

來源：CNBC、Al Jazeera

美國和伊朗的戰爭已經打了一個多月，油價一直在漲。四月二日，美國原油價格一天就漲了11%，衝到每桶111美元，是六年來最大的單日漲幅。

為什麼漲這麼兇？因為伊朗關了荷莫茲海峽，全世界20%的石油要從那裡經過。海峽一關，石油出不來，到月底可能少掉將近十億桶的供應。

川普總統說戰爭還要打好幾個星期，市場聽了很緊張。美國汽油已經漲到每加侖四美元以上，比開戰前貴了30%。分析師警告，如果海峽四月中還沒打開，油價可能衝到200美元。國際能源署說，這是「史上最大的能源安全危機」。

The Iran-US war has been raging for over a month, and oil prices have climbed steadily. On April 2nd, US crude surged 11% in a single day, breaking through $111 per barrel—the largest one-day jump in six years.

Why such a dramatic spike? Iran has closed the Strait of Hormuz, through which 20% of the world's oil passes. With the strait sealed, oil cannot flow out, and supply could drop by nearly a billion barrels by month's end.

President Trump said the war will continue for several more weeks, and markets reacted nervously. US gasoline has risen above $4 per gallon, up 30% since the war began. Analysts warn that if the strait remains closed through mid-April, oil could hit $200 a barrel. The International Energy Agency calls this "the greatest energy security crisis in history."
