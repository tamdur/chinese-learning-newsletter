Google 發表 Gemini Spark 和 新一代 AI 模型

來源：Tom's Guide

Google 在五月十九日的 I/O 大會上發表了 Gemini Spark，這是一個可以全天幫你做事的 AI 助手。它能連上 Gmail 和 Google 的工作工具，在背後自己完成任務。

Google 同時推出 Gemini 3.5 Flash 模型，效果接近最強的 AI，但費用只要三分之一。另外還有每月一百美元的 AI Ultra 方案，給重度使用者用。

Google unveiled Gemini Spark at its May 19th I/O conference, an AI assistant that works for you around the clock. It connects to Gmail and Google's workplace tools, completing tasks autonomously in the background. Google also launched the Gemini 3.5 Flash model, which delivers performance nearly matching the most powerful AI systems but costs only one-third as much. The company also introduced a $100-per-month AI Ultra plan for power users.

川普大法案以一票之差過關

來源：NBC News

美國眾議院（House）在五月二十二日以 215 比 214 的票數通過了川普的「大而美法案」，只差一票就不會過。全部民主黨人都反對，兩位共和黨人也投了反對票。

這個超過一千頁的法案把很多東西包在一起：延長減稅、要求領醫療補助的人要工作，還有移民政策的改變。法案接下來送到參議院（Senate）審查。

The U.S. House of Representatives passed Trump's "America First Act" on May 22nd by a razor-thin margin of 215 to 214 votes. The bill would have failed with just one vote going the other way. All Democrats opposed it, and two Republicans also voted against it. The sprawling legislation, exceeding 1,000 pages, bundles together multiple provisions: extending tax cuts, requiring Medicaid recipients to work, and changes to immigration policy. The bill now heads to the Senate for review.

OpenAI 讓所有人免費用最新 GPT-5.5 Instant

來源：TechCrunch

OpenAI 在五月五日把 ChatGPT 的預設模型換成了 GPT-5.5 Instant，不管是付費還是免費的用戶都能用。這代表幾億人一下子就能用到最先進的 AI。

新模型比上一代少了百分之五十二的錯誤，回答也更短、更準確。這次發表跟 Google I/O 的時間很近，兩家公司的 AI 競爭越來越激烈。

On May 5th, OpenAI switched ChatGPT's default model to GPT-5.5 Instant, making it available to both paid and free users. This means hundreds of millions of people suddenly gained access to the most advanced AI. The new model reduced errors by 52 percent compared to its predecessor, and its responses are shorter and more accurate. The announcement came close in time to Google I/O, as competition between the two companies in AI intensifies.

芝加哥市議會表決小費工人加薪延後案

來源：WTTW Chicago

芝加哥市議會在五月二十日投票決定是否把「小費工人加薪」延到 2028 年才開始。這是一個折衷（雙方各讓一步）方案。

之前市議會想完全停掉加薪，但市長 Johnson 用了否決權（veto）擋下。這次新方案讓餐廳業者暫時不用多付錢，但到 2028 年還是會加薪，市長也同意了。

The Chicago City Council voted on May 20th to decide whether to delay the "tipped worker wage increase" until 2028. This is a compromise measure. Previously, the council had wanted to completely halt the wage increase, but Mayor Johnson blocked it with a veto. The new proposal gives restaurant owners temporary relief from additional costs, but the wage increase will still take effect in 2028, and the mayor has agreed to it.

小熊四連敗，釀酒人搶走分區第一

來源：CBS Sports

小熊隊在五月十九日以 2 比 5 輸給釀酒人隊，這已經是連輸四場了。釀酒人最近十二場贏了十場，以 28 勝 18 敗拿下國聯中區第一。

釀酒人投手 Misiorowski 投了六局沒失分，連續四場先發都是零失分。Turang 打出兩分全壘打。

The Cubs lost to the Brewers 2-5 on May 19th, marking their fourth consecutive defeat. The Brewers have won ten of their last twelve games and now lead the NL Central with a 28-18 record. Brewers pitcher Misiorowski threw six scoreless innings, extending his streak to four consecutive starts without allowing a run. Turang hit a two-run home run.

兵工廠奪得英超冠軍，等了二十二年

來源：ESPN

兵工廠線——Arsenal 終於拿到英超冠軍了！上一次是 2004 年，整整等了二十二年。五月十九日，曼城在客場被伯恩茅斯 1比1 逼平，Arsenal 正式封王。

主教練 Arteta 是第一位以前英超球員身分拿下冠軍的教練。五月三十日他們還要打歐冠決賽，對手是巴黎聖日耳曼，有機會拿雙冠王！

The Arsenal beat — Arsenal has finally won the Premier League title! The last time was in 2004, after waiting exactly twenty-two years. On May nineteenth, Manchester City drew 1-1 away at Bournemouth, and Arsenal officially claimed the crown. Manager Arteta is the first former Premier League player to win the championship as a manager. On May thirtieth, they still have the Champions League final to play, against Paris Saint-Germain, with a chance to win the double!
