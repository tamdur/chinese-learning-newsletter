輝達收購案 面臨更嚴格審查

來源：MLex

晶片公司輝達要花約一百二十九億美元，買下Hugging Face這家公司。全世界做人工智慧的人，都靠它當「模型倉庫」。

美國司法部正在查輝達另一筆兩百億美元的Groq交易，懷疑那其實是併購（買下別人公司），只是換了名字。官員因此對輝達所有交易都更小心。

以前輝達常用「授權加挖角」避開審查，這次卻躲不掉，一定要政府點頭才算數。

Nvidia, the chip giant, plans to spend about $12.9 billion to acquire Hugging Face—the platform that AI developers worldwide rely on as their "model repository."

The U.S. Department of Justice is currently investigating Nvidia's separate $20 billion Groq transaction, suspecting it may actually be an acquisition under a different guise. Officials are now taking a much closer look at all of Nvidia's deals. Previously, Nvidia often used licensing agreements paired with talent recruitment to dodge regulatory scrutiny, but this time it won't slip through—government approval is now required.

日本升息到三十一年新高 日圓反而走弱

來源：The Japan Times

日本央行十八日把利率調高到百分之一點二五，是一九九五年以來最高。九位委員中有兩位投下反對票。

照理說利率變高，日圓應該更值錢。但總裁植田和男沒有答應會再升息，還說升太快會讓市場大亂。

市場因此認為升息已經到頂，日圓反而跌破一美元換一百五十七日圓。

The Bank of Japan raised interest rates to 1.25 percent on the 18th, marking the highest level since 1995. Two of the nine board members cast dissenting votes. In theory, higher interest rates should strengthen the yen. But Governor Kazuo Ueda declined to commit to further rate hikes and warned that rapid rate increases would destabilize the market. Markets concluded that rate hikes have peaked, causing the yen to weaken to 157 per dollar.

巴菲特卸下董事長 交棒給兒子

來源：The Washington Post

九月十八日，九十六歲的巴菲特寫信給股東，宣布辭去波克夏公司董事長，結束六十多年的領導。

董事長由兒子霍華接手，他是農夫，也做公益，不管公司的日常生意。巴菲特改當名譽董事長，仍留在董事會。八個月前，他已把執行長交給亞伯。

他在信裡說：「時間老人永遠會贏。」

On September 18, ninety-six-year-old Buffett wrote to shareholders announcing his resignation as chairman of Berkshire Hathaway, ending more than six decades of leadership. The chairmanship passes to his son Howard, a farmer and philanthropist who will not manage the company's daily operations. Buffett becomes honorary chairman while remaining on the board. Eight months ago, he had already handed the CEO position to Abel. He wrote in the letter: "Father Time always wins."

芝加哥南區居民 反對火車維修場

來源：Block Club Chicago

十八日晚上，橋港、華埠和阿莫廣場的幾百位居民擠滿說明會，反對美國國鐵要蓋的火車維修場。這個計畫要花九億美元。

居民說，維修場離住家只有約一百五十公尺，柴油廢氣和噪音會讓這三個社區更辛苦。

這塊地還和白襪隊小股東伊許比亞的開發案綁在一起，他自己出了約一億兩千五百萬美元。

On the evening of the 18th, hundreds of residents from Bridgeport, Chinatown, and Armour Square packed a town hall meeting to oppose the rail maintenance facility that Amtrak wants to build. The project will cost $900 million. Residents said the maintenance yard would be only about 150 meters from homes, and diesel fumes and noise would add strain to these three neighborhoods. The site is also linked to a development project by White Sox minority owner Ishbia, who has invested about $125 million of his own money.

密西根大學週六主場迎戰UTEP

來源：ClickOnDetroit (WDIV)

密西根大學美式足球隊週六下午三點半在主場迎戰UTEP，這是兩隊第一次交手。

上週六，密西根以十七比十擊敗強隊奧克拉荷馬大學，目前二勝零敗。UTEP開季時曾以零比五十一大輸給同一支球隊。

所以這場比賽比較像一次體檢，看看密西根自己還有哪裡要修。

Michigan's football team faces UTEP at home Saturday at 3:30 p.m., marking the first matchup between the two schools. Last Saturday, Michigan defeated Oklahoma, a strong opponent, 17-10, and currently stands at 2-0. UTEP opened the season with a brutal 0-51 loss to that same Oklahoma team. In that sense, this game is more of a checkup—a chance for Michigan to identify where it still needs to improve.
