Cloudflare 說 AI 讓一千一百人失去工作

來源：Hacker News

網路公司 Cloudflare 宣布裁掉約兩成員工，也就是一千一百人。公司說，AI 工具已經可以做這些人的工作，過去三個月公司內部的 AI 使用量增加了六倍。這是這家公司成立十六年來第一次大規模裁員。

特別的是，公司上一季收入高達六億四千萬美元，是歷史新高，但股價還是跌了兩成多。被裁員工可以領薪水到年底。

Internet company Cloudflare announced layoffs of roughly 20% of its workforce — about 1,100 employees. The company said that AI tools can now perform the work these employees were doing, and internal AI usage has increased sixfold over the past three months. This marks the first major layoff in the company's 16-year history. Notably, the company reported record quarterly revenue of $640 million last quarter, yet its stock price still fell more than 20%. Laid-off employees will receive salary through the end of the year.

波蘭經濟規模突破一兆美元，挑戰全球前二十大

來源：Hacker News

波蘭的 GDP（國內生產總值）在 2025 年達到一兆美元，離全球第二十名只差一步。這個東歐國家在共產時代結束後，靠著加入歐盟和大量製造業，經濟快速成長。

今年波蘭經濟預計成長 3.3%，遠高於歐洲平均的 1.3%。許多公司把工廠從亞洲搬到波蘭，讓它成為歐洲成長最快的國家之一。

Poland's GDP reached $1 trillion in 2025, putting it just one step away from cracking the global top twenty. Since the end of communism, this Eastern European nation has experienced rapid economic growth, powered by EU membership and a thriving manufacturing sector. Poland's economy is projected to grow 3.3% this year, far outpacing Europe's average of 1.3%. Companies are increasingly relocating factories from Asia to Poland, cementing its status as one of Europe's fastest-growing economies.

Redis 作者打造新工具，讓 DeepSeek 大模型在 Mac 上跑

來源：Hacker News

Redis 的作者 antirez 做了一個叫 DS4 的程式，可以讓 DeepSeek V4-Flash 這個有兩千八百多億參數的 AI 模型在 Mac 電腦上跑。以前這麼大的模型只能用雲端伺服器。

用特別的壓縮方法，有 128GB 記憶體的 MacBook 就能跑。在 Mac Studio 上，每秒可以產生約 37 個字，速度很快。

Antirez, the creator of Redis, has built a tool called DS4 that enables the DeepSeek V4-Flash model—an AI with over 280 billion parameters—to run on Mac computers. Previously, models of this scale required cloud servers. Using specialized compression techniques, a MacBook with 128GB of memory can now run it. On a Mac Studio, it generates approximately 37 characters per second, delivering impressive speed.

芝加哥法官即將決定是否調查移民行動

來源：Capitol News Illinois

Cook County（庫克郡）的法官 Reddick 在五月九日聽取了雙方的說法，預計五月十一日做出決定：要不要指派特別檢察官來調查去年的「Operation Midway Blitz」移民行動。

超過四百個團體和居民要求調查聯邦探員是否在行動中做了違法的事。他們說，郡檢察長因為不想得罪聯邦政府，所以一直沒有行動。

Judge Reddick of Cook County heard arguments from both sides on May 9th and is expected to decide on May 11th whether to appoint a special prosecutor to investigate last year's "Operation Midway Blitz" immigration enforcement action. More than 400 organizations and residents have demanded an investigation into whether federal agents engaged in illegal conduct during the operation. They argue that the county's state's attorney has failed to act because she does not want to offend the federal government.

小熊十連勝被終結，遭遊騎兵完封

來源：ESPN

芝加哥小熊的第二次十連勝在昨天畫下句點。德州遊騎兵以 6 比 0 完封小熊，Jung 和 Foscue 各打出一支全壘打。

小熊是 1955 年布魯克林道奇以來，第一支在開季前四十場就拿下兩次十連勝的球隊。目前戰績 27 勝 13 敗，表現仍然非常好。

The Chicago Cubs' second ten-game winning streak came to an end yesterday. The Texas Rangers shut out the Cubs 6-0, with Jung and Foscue each hitting home runs. The Cubs are the first team since the 1955 Brooklyn Dodgers to record two ten-game winning streaks within their first forty games of the season. With a record of 27 wins and 13 losses, they remain in excellent form.
