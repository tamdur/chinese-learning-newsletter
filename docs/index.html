谷歌雲端收入翻倍，AI投資開始回本

來源：Alphabet 第三季財報（SEC 8-K）

谷歌的母公司 Alphabet 在 28 日公布第三季成績：營收 1276 億美元，比去年多了 25%，比大家預期的還要好。

最亮眼的是 Google Cloud（谷歌雲端），收入達到 307 億美元，一年內翻了一倍多。很多公司租用谷歌的電腦和 AI 晶片，來訓練和使用 AI。

這週微軟、亞馬遜也陸續公布成績。今年幾家大公司在 AI 上要花大約 8000 億美元，大家都在看：這些錢到底賺不賺得回來？

Alphabet, Google's parent company, reported its third-quarter results on the 28th: revenue of $127.6 billion, up 25% from a year earlier and better than analysts had expected. The standout was Google Cloud, which brought in $30.7 billion, more than doubling in a single year. Many companies rent Google's computers and AI chips to train and run their own AI. Microsoft and Amazon have also been reporting their results this week. This year, several of the biggest tech companies plan to spend roughly $800 billion on AI, and everyone is asking the same question: will that money ever pay for itself?

聯準會決定不升息，兩人投反對票

來源：Bloomberg、ABC News、Yahoo Finance

美國聯準會（美國的中央銀行） 28 日宣布，利率維持在 3.75% 到 4%，沒有再往上調。九月時，它才意外升息一次。

這次投票是 10 比 2，有兩位委員覺得物價還太高，想要再升息。九月的物價比去年高了 3.7%，離目標的 2% 還很遠。

下週就是期中選舉，但主席華許說：「政治沒有影響這次的決定。」

The U.S. Federal Reserve, the country's central bank, announced on the 28th that it would hold its benchmark rate at 3.75% to 4%, with no further increase. It had raised rates once in September, a move that surprised many. The vote was 10 to 2, with two members believing that prices remain too high and that rates should go up again. Prices in September were 3.7% higher than a year earlier, still well above the Fed's 2% target. Next week brings the midterm elections, but Chair Warsh insisted that "politics did not influence this decision."

湖街橋完工，綠線和粉紅線恢復行駛

來源：Chicago Sun-Times、WBEZ

芝加哥捷運（CTA）的綠線和粉紅線，按計畫在 28 日清晨 4 點走回原本的路線。兩條線從 12 日起改道，工程還比原定晚了兩天。

問題出在湖街橋。這座跨過芝加哥河的老橋已經一百多歲了，高架鐵軌就從上面經過。兩條線要進市中心，都一定要走這裡，橋一關，兩條線一起受影響。

不過，整座橋的大整修還沒結束，汽車和行人要等到 2028 年才能再走。

Chicago Transit Authority (CTA) Green Line and Pink Line trains returned to their original routes as planned at 4 a.m. on the 28th. Both lines had been rerouted since the 12th, and the work ran two days behind schedule.

The problem was the Lake Street Bridge. The century-old bridge crosses the Chicago River, and elevated train tracks run across it. Both lines need this crossing to reach downtown, so when the bridge closed, both were affected at once. Still, the bridge's major overhaul is not finished, and cars and pedestrians will not be able to use it again until 2028.

熊隊碰上冠軍海鷹隊，是真強還是運氣好？

來源：Sports Illustrated、Fox 32 Chicago

芝加哥熊隊目前 5 勝 2 敗，上週四晚上才打敗愛國者隊。下週一（11 月 2 日）晚上，他們要對上一季的超級盃冠軍西雅圖海鷹隊。

四分衛凱勒布．威廉斯（Caleb Williams）是最大話題：這一季只要他先發又打完全場，熊隊就沒輸過。

不過，運彩公司還是看好海鷹隊，認為他們會贏大約 3 分。有人說熊隊之前的對手比較弱，這場正好看看他們是不是真的夠強。

The Chicago Bears are now 5-2, having beaten the New England Patriots just last Thursday night. On Monday night (November 2), they face the Seattle Seahawks, last season's Super Bowl champions. Quarterback Caleb Williams is the biggest storyline: this season, the Bears have not lost a single game when he starts and plays the full game. Still, betting companies favor the Seahawks, predicting they will win by about 3 points. Some people say the Bears' previous opponents were weaker, so this game is a good test of whether they are really that good.

少了薩卡，兵工廠要去利物浦搶分

來源：The False 9、Sports Mole

兵工廠線——英超排第二的兵工廠（18 分），這個週日要到安菲爾德球場，挑戰排第五的利物浦（12 分）。很多人把這場看成搶冠軍的關鍵一戰。

壞消息是，主力邊鋒薩卡大腿受傷。教練阿爾特塔說要休息好幾週，還不知道哪天能回來。

幾天前，兵工廠才在聯賽盃輸給一支第四級聯賽的小球隊。少了薩卡，進球要從哪裡來？週日就知道了。

The Arsenal beat: Arsenal, second in the Premier League with 18 points, travel to Anfield this Sunday to face fifth-placed Liverpool (12 points). Many see this match as a key clash in the title race. The bad news is that star winger Saka has a thigh injury. Manager Arteta says he will be out for several weeks, and it is not yet clear when he will return. Just a few days ago, Arsenal lost to a small club from the fourth tier in the League Cup. Without Saka, where will the goals come from? We'll find out on Sunday.
