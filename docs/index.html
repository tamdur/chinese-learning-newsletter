最新 AI 模型網路攻擊能力大幅提升

來源：來源：Fortune

美國 AI 公司 Anthropic 正在測試一個叫做 Claude Mythos 的新模型。這個消息是因為大約三千份內部文件不小心被公開才知道的。公司說這是他們做過最強的模型，在寫程式和網路安全方面遠遠超過其他 AI。

讓人擔心的是，內部文件警告說這類模型找到系統弱點的速度，已經快過人類防守的速度。Anthropic 會先把 Mythos 給網路安全防守組織使用。國際 AI 安全報告也把網路攻擊列為最大的 AI 風險之一。

US AI company Anthropic is testing a new model called Claude Mythos. The news emerged after approximately three thousand internal documents were accidentally made public. The company says it is their most powerful model yet, far surpassing other AI systems in programming and cybersecurity capabilities.

The concerning part is that internal documents warn that models like this find system vulnerabilities faster than human defenders can respond. Anthropic plans to first provide Mythos to cybersecurity defense organizations. International AI safety reports also rank cyberattacks as one of the greatest AI risks.

伊朗拒絕美國停火方案，油價突破 108 美元

來源：來源：Reuters / Financial Times

伊朗在三月二十五日正式拒絕了美國的十五點停火計畫，說條件「太過分」。美國要求伊朗放棄核武計畫、重新打開荷莫茲海峽（全球石油運送的重要通道）。伊朗外交部長直接說：「我們不要停火。」

這條海峽每天運送大約兩千萬桶石油，佔全球海上運油量的兩成。伊朗三月二日關閉了海峽之後，布蘭特原油漲到每桶 112 美元。Goldman Sachs 估計每桶有 14 到 18 美元是戰爭帶來的額外成本。

Iran formally rejected the U.S. fifteen-point ceasefire plan on March 25, calling the conditions "excessive." The United States demanded that Iran abandon its nuclear weapons program and reopen the Strait of Hormuz, a critical global oil shipping corridor. Iran's foreign minister stated bluntly: "We do not want a ceasefire."

The strait carries approximately twenty million barrels of oil daily, accounting for twenty percent of global seaborne oil shipments. Since Iran closed the strait on March 2, Brent crude has risen to $112 per barrel. Goldman Sachs estimates that $14 to $18 per barrel represents additional war-related costs.

伊利諾州通過 15 億美元交通救命方案

來源：來源：Chicago Tribune / WBEZ

伊利諾州議會連夜通過了一項每年 15 億美元的交通方案，救了芝加哥的 CTA、Metra 和 Pace。疫情時期的聯邦補助已經用完，今年差了 2.3 億，到 2027 年可能差 8.3 億。如果不通過，服務要砍四成，一萬五千人會失業。

錢從哪來？主要是把油稅轉到交通（約 8.6 億），加上地區營業稅加 0.25％和高速公路過路費漲 45 分。新法也計畫在 2029 年統一票價。沒有任何共和黨議員投贊成票。

The Illinois legislature passed an overnight vote approving a $1.5 billion annual transportation package that saved Chicago's CTA, Metra, and Pace transit systems. Federal pandemic subsidies have run out, leaving the systems $230 million short this year and potentially $830 million short by 2027. Without the package, service would be cut by 40 percent and 15,000 jobs would be lost.

Where does the money come from? Primarily by redirecting gas tax revenue to transportation (about $860 million), plus a 0.25 percent increase in regional sales tax and a 45-cent hike in highway tolls. The new law also plans to unify fares by 2029. No Republican legislators voted in favor.

歐洲央行警告市場太樂觀，那斯達克進入修正

來源：來源：Financial Times / ECB

歐洲央行總裁 Lagarde 警告說，市場對未來「太樂觀」了。她說伊朗戰爭是「超出想像的真正衝擊」，專家認為影響可能持續好幾年。她還提到，做晶片需要的氦氣也要經過荷莫茲海峽。

那斯達克指數已經從去年十月高點跌了超過一成，正式進入「修正」。美國聯準會主席 Powell 說今年可能只降息一次，市場甚至擔心會加息。全球央行都陷入兩難：物價漲、經濟慢。

European Central Bank President Lagarde warned that markets are "too optimistic" about the future. She said the Iran war represents "a real shock beyond imagination," and experts believe its impact could persist for years. She also noted that helium required for chip manufacturing must pass through the Strait of Hormuz.

The Nasdaq index has fallen more than 10% from its October peak last year, officially entering "correction" territory. U.S. Federal Reserve Chair Powell said the Fed may only cut rates once this year, with markets even worried about rate hikes. Central banks worldwide face a dilemma: rising prices paired with slowing economic growth.

密西根大學今晚對決阿拉巴馬，挑戰菁英八強

來源：來源：ESPN / NCAA

全美大學籃球錦標賽甜蜜十六強今晚有好戲！密西根大學是全國第一種子，戰績 31 勝 3 敗，大十聯盟冠軍。主力 Nimari Burnett 場均 17.2 分，Danny Wolf 場均 14.1 分加 8.3 個籃板。

對手阿拉巴馬是第五種子，上一場淘汰了四號種子 Purdue。他們打法很快，愛投三分球，Mark Sears 場均 18.8 分。今晚東部時間 9：45 在 TBS 開打，密西根讓 5.5 分。贏了就進菁英八強，是 2021 年以來第一次！

The Sweet Sixteen has plenty of drama in store for college basketball fans tonight! The University of Michigan enters as the nation's top seed with an impressive 31-3 record and Big Ten championship. Leading scorers Nimari Burnett averages 17.2 points per game, while Danny Wolf contributes 14.1 points and 8.3 rebounds per contest.

Their opponent, Alabama, earned the fifth seed after knocking out fourth-seeded Purdue in their last matchup. They play a fast-paced style with a love for three-pointers, led by Mark Sears who averages 18.8 points per game. Tipoff is tonight at 9:45 p.m. Eastern Time on TBS, with Michigan favored by 5.5 points. A win advances them to the Elite Eight for the first time since 2021!

歐洲陽台變發電廠：插上太陽能板就能省電費

來源：Euronews

歐洲天然氣價格最近因為荷莫茲海峽的危機，兩週內漲了五成多。很多人開始想辦法自己發電。

現在有一種「陽台太陽能」產品很受歡迎。做法很簡單：把太陽能板夾在陽台欄杆上，插進牆上的插座就好了。不用請電工，也不用申請許可。一片大約兩百歐元，加上電池也不到一千歐元，兩到六年就能把錢賺回來。

德國是最熱門的市場，過去三年已經裝了超過一百萬組。光是去年就新增了四十二萬組，比前一年多了四成。荷蘭也很厲害，三成多的屋頂都有太陽能板。一位再生能源公司的老闆說：「如果你家有太陽能加電池，就不太需要跟外面買電了。」

European natural gas prices have surged more than 50% over the past two weeks due to the Hormuz Strait crisis. Many people are now looking for ways to generate their own electricity.

A type of "balcony solar" product has become increasingly popular. The process is simple: clamp solar panels onto your balcony railing and plug them into a wall outlet. No electrician needed, no permits required. A single panel costs around 200 euros, and with a battery, the total comes to less than 1,000 euros. You can recoup your investment in two to six years.

Germany is the hottest market, having installed over one million units in the past three years. Last year alone saw 420,000 new installations, a 40% increase from the previous year. The Netherlands is also booming, with more than 30% of rooftops equipped with solar panels. A renewable energy company executive said: "If your home has solar plus a battery, you hardly need to buy electricity from outside anymore."
