谷歌開放Gemma 4模型 挑戰DeepSeek

Google AI Blog

谷歌（Google）這個星期發布了四個新的Gemma 4人工智慧模型，大小從二十三億到三百一十億個參數（parameters）不等。這次最大的改變是使用Apache 2.0開放授權，任何人都可以免費下載和使用。在數學測試中，Gemma 4拿到了百分之八十九的好成績。谷歌這樣做，是因為中國的DeepSeek用開放模型吸引了很多開發者，谷歌不想落後。

Google released four new Gemma 4 artificial intelligence models this week, ranging in size from 2.3 billion to 31 billion parameters. The biggest change this time is the use of Apache 2.0 open licensing, which allows anyone to freely download and use the models. In math tests, Gemma 4 achieved a score of 89 percent. Google is doing this because China's DeepSeek has attracted many developers with its open models, and Google doesn't want to fall behind.

「解放日」一週年 川普再加關稅

Spectrum News

一年前，川普總統宣布「解放日」（Liberation Day），對很多國家加了高關稅。但最高法院後來說這樣做不合法。現在，川普用另外的法律——第三○一條和第二三二條——對中國、歐洲、日本和印度發起新的貿易調查。他還對藥品加了百分之百的關稅，對鋼和鋁的關稅也提高了。美國的工廠工作在過去一年減少了八萬九千個。

A year ago, President Trump announced "Liberation Day," imposing steep tariffs on many countries. However, the Supreme Court later ruled this approach unlawful. Now, Trump is launching new trade investigations against China, Europe, Japan, and India using alternative legal provisions—Sections 301 and 232. He has also imposed 100 percent tariffs on pharmaceuticals and raised tariffs on steel and aluminum. Manufacturing jobs in the US have declined by 89,000 over the past year.

AI寫的論文首次通過學術審查

Nature

日本公司Sakana AI做了一個叫「AI科學家」的系統。這個系統可以自己想研究題目、做實驗、分析數據，最後還能寫出完整的論文。他們把三篇AI寫的論文送去ICLR學術會議審查，其中一篇的分數比很多人寫的論文還高，成功通過了。這是歷史上第一次，完全由AI完成的研究論文通過了正式的同行評審。

Japanese company Sakana AI has developed a system called the "AI Scientist." This system can independently identify research topics, conduct experiments, analyze data, and ultimately write complete academic papers. They submitted three AI-authored papers to the ICLR academic conference for review, and one scored higher than many human-written papers, successfully passing peer review. This marks the first time in history that a paper entirely completed by AI has passed formal academic peer review.

五十多年來首次 太空人飛向月球

NASA

四月一日，NASA的Artemis II任務從佛州發射升空。四位太空人坐在獵戶座（Orion）太空船裡，將花大約十天繞月球飛行一圈。這是一九七二年以來第一次有人飛到月球附近。機組成員包括第一位接近月球的有色人種Victor Glover、第一位女性Christina Koch，和第一位非美國人——加拿大太空人Jeremy Hansen。他們預計四月十日回到地球。

On April 1st, NASA's Artemis II mission launched from Florida. Four astronauts aboard the Orion spacecraft will spend approximately ten days orbiting the moon. This is the first time humans have traveled near the moon since 1972. The crew includes Victor Glover, the first person of color to approach the moon; Christina Koch, the first woman; and Jeremy Hansen, a Canadian astronaut and the first non-American. They are expected to return to Earth on April 10th.
