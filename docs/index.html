谷歌推出新AI　主打「會做事」

來源：TechCrunch

谷歌（Google）在六月八日正式推出新的AI模型「Gemini 3.5 Flash」。它的速度大約快四倍，花的錢卻更少。

谷歌說，這個AI不只會聊天，還能自己一步一步幫人做事、寫程式。這代表大公司的比賽，已經從「會講話」變成「會做事」。

Google officially launched a new AI model called "Gemini 3.5 Flash" on June 8th. It runs about four times faster while costing significantly less. Google says this AI doesn't just chat — it can independently work through tasks step-by-step, write code, and help people get things done. This signals that the competition among tech giants has shifted from "can it talk?" to "can it actually do things?"

菲律賓強震　至少十五人死亡

來源：Al Jazeera

六月八日早上，菲律賓南部的民答那峨島外海發生規模七點八的大地震，是這個國家五十年來最強的一次。

地震讓不少房子倒了，至少十五人死亡，兩百多人受傷。海邊也出現海嘯（海水突然湧上岸），附近的印尼和馬來西亞都發出警報。

Early this morning, a magnitude 7.8 earthquake struck off the coast of Mindanao in southern Philippines — the strongest the country has experienced in fifty years. The quake collapsed numerous buildings, killing at least fifteen people and injuring over two hundred. A tsunami swept ashore in coastal areas, prompting neighboring Indonesia and Malaysia to issue warnings.

習近平訪北韓　七年來第一次

來源：CNN

中國領導人習近平在六月八日到北韓的首都平壤，這是他七年來第一次來。北韓領導人金正恩親自到機場迎接。

就在幾天前，北韓才公開一座新的核子工廠，還說要大量製造核武原料。這次見面，顯示中國和北韓的關係越走越近。

Chinese leader Xi Jinping arrived in Pyongyang, North Korea's capital, on June 8th — his first visit in seven years. North Korean leader Kim Jong Un personally greeted him at the airport. Just days earlier, North Korea had publicly unveiled a new nuclear facility and announced plans to mass-produce nuclear weapons material. The meeting underscores deepening ties between China and North Korea.

歐洲央行降息　利率降到百分之二

來源：ECB

歐洲央行（負責管歐洲錢的大銀行）在六月八日把利率降到百分之二，這是今年第四次降息。

他們說，美國加關稅帶來的漲價沒有想像中那麼嚴重，所以敢把利率調低。利率變低，借錢就比較便宜，能幫經濟多出點力。

The European Central Bank (the big bank that manages Europe's money) lowered interest rates to 2 percent on June 8th, marking the fourth rate cut this year. Officials said that price increases from US tariffs haven't been as severe as feared, so they felt comfortable cutting rates. When interest rates fall, borrowing becomes cheaper, which can give the economy a boost.

小熊投手泰隆受傷提早退場

來源：Washington Post

芝加哥小熊隊的投手泰隆（Taillon），六月七日對上舊金山巨人隊時，第二局才投給一個打者就受傷下場。

他的左腿後面拉傷，自己也說大概要休息養傷。小熊隊最近本來就缺投手，這下更頭痛了。

Chicago Cubs pitcher Taillon left the game early against the San Francisco Giants on June 7th, after facing just one batter in the second inning. He suffered a strain on the back of his left leg and indicated he would need time to recover. The move adds to the Cubs' ongoing pitching shortage.

兵工廠早早補強　後衛辛卡皮加入

來源：TeamTalk

兵工廠線——剛拿下英超冠軍的兵工廠（阿森納），趁著夏天很早就開始補人。

球隊確定簽下後衛辛卡皮（Hincapié），從德國的勒沃庫森隊轉來，總共花了約五千兩百萬歐元。轉會市場六月十五日才開門，教練阿爾特塔想挑戰歐洲冠軍。

Arsenal beat — Fresh off winning the Premier League title, Arsenal is moving fast on summer reinforcements. The club has confirmed the signing of defender Hincapié from Bundesliga side Bayer Leverkusen for approximately €52 million. With the transfer window not opening until June 15th, manager Arteta is building early to challenge for the European crown.
