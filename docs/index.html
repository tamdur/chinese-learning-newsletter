太陽能首度超越煤炭

來源：Electrek

今年五月，美國太陽能發的電第一次比煤炭（一種黑色的燒火石頭）還多。

那個月太陽能佔了全國用電的百分之十二點八，煤炭只有十二點二。不過沒有太陽的時候電夠不夠用，還是個問題。

In May of this year, solar energy generated more electricity than coal in the United States for the first time. That month, solar accounted for 12.8 percent of the nation's electricity use, while coal represented only 12.2 percent. However, ensuring sufficient power supply when there is no sun remains a challenge.

會用AI　薪水多五成

來源：Gloat

最新的調查發現，會用人工智慧（AI）的人，薪水可以多出百分之五十六。

可是同一段時間，科技業因為AI取代人力，兩個月就裁掉三萬兩千人。會用的人賺更多，不會的人卻可能丟了工作。

The latest survey finds that people who can use artificial intelligence (AI) earn fifty-six percent more in salary. Yet during the same period, the tech industry laid off thirty-two thousand workers in two months because AI replaced their jobs. Those who can use it earn more; those who cannot risk losing their jobs entirely.

來源：Vorys

但白宮想由中央統一訂規則，不讓各州自己管。再加上歐洲八月二日也要全面實施AI法，到底AI該誰來管，大家爭個不停。

The United States was set to implement America's first comprehensive AI law in Colorado on June 30th. But the White House wants the federal government to set unified rules instead of allowing individual states to regulate on their own. Meanwhile, Europe is also preparing to fully implement AI legislation on August 2nd. The question of who should govern AI has become a contentious issue.
