習近平在上海成立中國主導的全球人工智慧聯盟

來源：NPR

中國國家主席習近平七月十七日在上海世界人工智慧大會上，宣布成立「世界人工智慧合作組織」，總部設在上海，已有二十九個國家加入，大多是南方國家，像是巴西、印尼和南非，聯合國祕書長古特瑞斯也到場。

習近平說，人工智慧應該像大家一起演奏的交響樂，而不是一個人的獨奏，還答應要幫開發中國家提供五千個訓練機會。這被看成是中國在挑戰美國帶頭的科技規則。

On July 17 at the Shanghai World Artificial Intelligence Conference, Chinese President Xi Jinping announced the establishment of the "World Artificial Intelligence Cooperation Organization," with headquarters in Shanghai. Twenty-nine countries have already joined, mostly nations from the Global South—including Brazil, Indonesia, and South Africa. UN Secretary-General António Guterres attended the event.

Xi said that artificial intelligence should be like a symphony performed by everyone together, rather than a solo by one person. He also pledged to provide five thousand training opportunities for developing countries. This is widely seen as China challenging the technology rules led by the United States.

歐盟要求谷歌向對手開放安卓與搜尋資料

來源：CNBC

歐盟七月十六日根據「數位市場法」發出兩道命令，要谷歌把安卓手機的權限，公平開放給其他人工智慧助理，讓像 ChatGPT 這樣的助理，也能用「嘿」的語音指令來叫醒，並幫忙叫車、回訊息。

谷歌還得把搜尋的排名和點擊資料分享給對手。資料分享要在二〇二七年一月前做到，安卓開放則到二〇二七年七月。谷歌抱怨這樣做會傷害用戶的隱私。

On July 16th, the EU issued two orders under the "Digital Markets Act," requiring Google to give AI assistants fair access to Android phones, so services like ChatGPT can be activated with a "hey" voice command and help users book rides and reply to messages.

Google must also share search rankings and click data with its competitors. Data sharing must be completed by January 2027, with Android access opening by July 2027. Google has protested that this would harm user privacy.

美國國土安全部長威脅不聽話的選務官員

來源：NBC News

美國國土安全部長穆林七月十七日說，各州一定要把選民名冊送進聯邦的公民資料庫檢查，看有沒有非公民登記投票，不照做的州就拿不到聯邦的選舉補助款。

他還警告，拒絕配合的官員可能被罰錢，甚至坐牢。但一位聯邦法官早就下令，禁止用這個資料庫來做這件事。距離期中選舉只剩幾個月，這番話讓聯邦和各州的緊張再升高。

On July 17th, US Department of Homeland Security Secretary Mullin said that all states must submit their voter rolls to the federal citizenship database for verification to determine whether any non-citizens are registered to vote. States that fail to comply will lose federal election funding.

He also warned that officials who refuse to cooperate could face fines and even imprisonment. However, a federal judge had already issued an order prohibiting the use of this database for this purpose. With only a few months remaining until the midterm election, these remarks have escalated tensions between federal and state authorities.

治脊椎傷的藥意外在老鼠身上修復阿茲海默症損傷

來源：ScienceDaily

英國倫敦國王學院的團隊發現，一種本來要治脊椎（背後的脊椎骨）受傷的藥 KCL-286 ，在阿茲海默症（一種讓老人失去記憶的病）的老鼠身上，能修復受損的 DNA ，還能減少腦部發炎。

這種藥可以口服，而且已經通過人體安全測試，一次能對付好幾條致病的路徑，而不是只針對一種蛋白質。雖然目前只在老鼠身上試過，但研究人員說，它有機會更快進入人體試驗。

A team from King's College London has discovered that KCL-286, a drug originally developed to treat spinal cord injuries, can repair damaged DNA and reduce brain inflammation in mice with Alzheimer's disease—a condition that causes memory loss in older people.

The drug can be taken orally and has already passed human safety testing. Rather than targeting a single protein, it addresses multiple disease pathways at once. Though currently tested only in mice, researchers believe it has strong potential to advance to human trials more quickly.

小熊隊簽下五名選秀新人　包括首輪湯森

來源：MLB.com Cubs

芝加哥小熊隊七月十七日簽下二〇二六年選秀的五名新人，領頭的是第一輪、整體第二十三順位挑中的右投手湯森，他來自密西西比大學，簽約金三百一十萬美元，比行情價還低一些。

小熊目前戰績五十四勝四十二敗，排在國聯外卡第一，不過週五在主場以二比五輸給雙城。這批新人是為將來鋪路，和球隊在八月三日交易大限前的補強是兩回事。

On July 17th, the Chicago Cubs signed five 2026 draft picks, headlined by right-handed pitcher Townsend, selected 23rd overall in the first round. He comes from the University of Mississippi and signed a deal worth $3.1 million, slightly below market rate.

The Cubs currently stand at 54 wins and 42 losses, holding down first place in the National League Wild Card race. However, they fell to the Twins 2-5 at home on Friday. These prospects are an investment in the future and are separate from any roster moves the team might make before the August 3rd trade deadline.

兵工廠談成佐利斯轉會　下個目標鎖定羅傑斯

來源：CaughtOffside / Football365

兵工廠線——阿森納已和布魯日隊談成約四千萬歐元的轉會，簽下二十四歲的希臘邊鋒佐利斯，體檢安排在週日、週一，這是今夏繼辛卡皮耶和梅斯利耶之後的第三名新援。佐利斯上季各項賽事攻進二十二球、助攻二十九次，只想加入兵工廠，拒絕了其他球隊。

佐利斯快要搞定，兵工廠的下個主要目標換成阿斯頓維拉的羅傑斯，據說個人條件都快談好了。名記羅曼諾說，教練阿爾特塔和體育總監貝爾塔還沒收手。

Arsenal have agreed a deal to sign 24-year-old Greek winger Christos Tzolis from Club Brugge for approximately 40 million euros, with medicals scheduled for Sunday and Monday. This is the club's third new signing this summer following Hincapié and Meslier. Last season, Tzolis scored 22 goals and provided 29 assists across all competitions, determined only to join Arsenal and spurning offers from other clubs.

With the Tzolis deal nearly done, Arsenal have turned their focus to Aston Villa's Morgan Rogers, with personal terms reportedly close to being agreed. According to insider Fabrizio Romano, manager Arteta and sporting director Berta aren't stopping there.
