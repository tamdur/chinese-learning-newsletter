伯納姆就任英國新首相 公布內閣名單

來源：ITV News

英國換了新首相！伯納姆（Burnham）正式上任，還公布了他的內閣（政府團隊）。他找希利當財政大臣管錢，讓瑞娜負責住房。

他宣布從十月起，家庭用電的稅從百分之五降到零，每個家庭一年可以省下大約四十五英鎊。伯納姆是英國十年來第七位首相，可見這個國家的政局很不穩。

The UK has a new Prime Minister! Burnham officially took office and announced his cabinet (government team). He appointed Healey as Chancellor to manage finances and Rayner to oversee housing. He announced that starting in October, the household electricity tax will be reduced from 5% to 0%, saving each household approximately 45 pounds per year. Burnham is the UK's seventh Prime Minister in ten years, demonstrating how unstable the country's political situation has become.

美國眾議院提前通過撥款案 避免政府關門

來源：NPR

美國國會眾議院以二二○票對二○五票，通過一個臨時撥款案，讓聯邦政府有錢運作到十二月四日。

平常議員都拖到最後一刻才表決，這次卻提前好幾個月就通過，是很少見的事。他們想趕在十一月期中選舉前，避免政府因為沒錢而「關門」停擺。這個案子接下來要送到參議院。

The US House of Representatives passed a temporary spending bill by a vote of 220 to 205, providing the federal government with funding through December 4th. It's remarkably rare for lawmakers to act so far ahead of schedule—normally they drag out the vote until the last possible moment. This time, they fast-tracked passage by several months to prevent a government shutdown before November's midterm elections. The bill now moves to the Senate.

OpenAI 推出更強推理模式 追趕對手

來源：AI News Today

AI 公司 OpenAI 為它的 GPT-5.6 Sol 模型，加上兩種更會「思考」的新模式，叫做 max 和 ultra。

公司說，這讓答題的品質追上對手 Anthropic 的 Fable 5，花的錢卻只要大約一半，速度也快很多。這場 AI 大戰的重點，已經從比誰推出新模型，變成比誰想得深、又比誰便宜。

AI company OpenAI has added two new, more capable reasoning modes—called max and ultra—to its GPT-5.6 Sol model. The company claims this matches the response quality of rival Anthropic's Fable 5, while costing roughly half as much and running considerably faster.

The AI race has shifted focus from who launches the newest model to who can reason more deeply and offer better value.

量子公司 Infleqtion 選芝加哥設全球總部

來源：Governor's Office

伊利諾州州長普里茲克宣布，量子運算公司 Infleqtion 要把全球總部設在伊利諾州。

這家公司會投資大約五千萬美元，在芝加哥南邊一座新的量子科技園區蓋據點，還要至少新增五十個工作機會。州長希望靠這些大公司，把芝加哥打造成全美國的「量子重鎮」。

Illinois Governor Pritzker announced that quantum computing company Infleqtion will establish its global headquarters in Illinois. The company will invest approximately $50 million to build a facility in a new quantum technology park south of Chicago and will create at least 50 new jobs. The governor hopes that by attracting major companies like this, Chicago can become a "quantum hub" for the entire nation.

阿森納傳奇卡索拉宣布退休

來源：Arsenal.com

兵工廠線——阿森納的傳奇球星卡索拉（Cazorla）宣布退休，結束長達二十多年的球員生涯。

這位西班牙中場當年腳踝受重傷，開了十次刀才重新站上球場，是足球史上有名的奮鬥故事。他最後回到從小效力的球隊奧維耶多，還幫球隊升上西班牙甲級聯賽，為生涯留下美好的結尾。

The Arsenal Beat—Arsenal legend Santi Cazorla has announced his retirement, ending a playing career spanning more than two decades. The Spanish midfielder's journey is one of football's most celebrated stories of perseverance: after suffering a severe ankle injury, he underwent ten surgeries before returning to the pitch. He concluded his career where it began, at Oviedo, the club he joined as a youth player, and helped guide them to promotion to Spain's top division, bringing his storied playing career to a beautiful close.
