駭客入侵 GitHub 員工電腦，三千八百個內部程式庫被偷

來源：The Hacker News

全球最大的程式碼平台 GitHub 被駭客攻擊了。一名員工的電腦被裝了有毒的 VS Code 外掛程式（一種工程師常用的工具），讓駭客偷走了超過三千八百個內部程式庫。

一個叫 TeamPCP 的駭客團體已經在網路上開價五萬美元出售這些資料。GitHub 表示客戶資料沒有受到影響，但這件事讓大家很擔心軟體供應鏈的安全。

GitHub, the world's largest code platform, has been hit by a cyberattack. A malicious VS Code extension—a tool commonly used by engineers—was installed on an employee's computer, allowing hackers to steal over 3,800 internal code repositories. A hacker group calling itself TeamPCP has already posted the stolen data for sale online at an asking price of $50,000. GitHub says customer data was not affected, but the incident has raised serious concerns about software supply chain security.

以色列一夜轟炸黎巴嫩逾百處真主黨據點

來源：Al Jazeera

以色列在星期一晚上對黎巴嫩發動大規模（很大範圍的）空襲，打擊超過一百個真主黨目標，包括武器倉庫和指揮中心。黎巴嫩衛生部說至少有 31 人死亡。

這是中東戰事的一次重大升級。同一時間，美國還在跟伊朗談停火。戰爭已經讓超過一百萬黎巴嫩人離開家園。

Israel launched a massive airstrike campaign against Lebanon on Monday evening, targeting over one hundred Hezbollah positions, including weapons depots and command centers. Lebanon's health ministry reported at least 31 deaths. This marks a major escalation in Middle East hostilities. Meanwhile, the US continues ceasefire negotiations with Iran. The conflict has already displaced over one million Lebanese from their homes.

芝加哥市長推「公車站旁蓋更多房子」新規定

來源：City of Chicago

芝加哥市長 Brandon Johnson 提出了「TOD Streets」計畫，要在 CTA 和 Metra 車站附近蓋更多住宅和商店。車站半英里以內的地方都可以適用。

新規定會禁止得來速、加油站等以汽車為主的商店，鼓勵大家走路和搭大眾運輸。市長希望讓芝加哥的街道變得更適合行人。

Chicago Mayor Brandon Johnson has unveiled the "TOD Streets" initiative, aimed at building more housing and commercial spaces near CTA and Metra stations. The new regulations will apply to areas within half a mile of transit hubs. The rules will ban drive-throughs, gas stations, and other car-dependent businesses, while encouraging walking and public transit use. The mayor hopes to make Chicago's streets more pedestrian-friendly.

中國發射神舟二十三號，首位香港太空人升空

來源：NPR

中國在五月二十四日從酒泉發射了神舟二十三號，送三名太空人前往天宮太空站。其中賴嘉盈是第一位來自香港的太空人，她擁有電腦科學博士學位。

這次任務有一個特別的計畫：一名太空人將在太空站住一整年，這將是全世界最長的太空停留之一，用來研究人體在太空的適應能力。

China launched Shenzhou 23 from Jiuquan on May 24th, sending three astronauts to the Tiangong space station. Among them is Lai Jia-ying, the first astronaut from Hong Kong, who holds a doctoral degree in computer science. This mission includes a special plan: one astronaut will stay at the space station for an entire year, making it one of the longest space missions in the world, aimed at studying how the human body adapts to space.

小熊隊九連敗，慘遭海盜隊大勝

來源：ESPN

芝加哥小熊隊星期一以 1 比 12 慘敗給匹茲堡海盜隊，連續輸了第九場比賽，是今年最長的連敗紀錄。

小熊隊季初打出 20 勝 3 敗的好成績，但最近 15 場只贏了 3 場。五月以來有 11 場比賽得分不超過兩分，打線完全沉睡了。

The Chicago Cubs suffered a crushing 1-12 loss to the Pittsburgh Pirates on Monday, extending their losing streak to nine games — the longest of the season. After a strong start with a 20-3 record, the Cubs have won just 3 of their last 15 games. In May alone, they've scored 2 runs or fewer in 11 contests, with the offense completely dormant.

兵工廠線——拿下英超冠軍後，兵工廠馬上把目光轉向五月三十日在布達佩斯對戰 PSG 的歐冠決賽。同時，球隊也開始規劃夏天的轉會。

Negotiations to extend Dutch defender Jurrien Timber are progressing well, with a long-term deal expected to be finalized before the World Cup. Separately, Ben White may depart, and the club is actively searching for a new defender.
