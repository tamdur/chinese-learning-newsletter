美國再度空襲伊朗　停火幾乎破局

來源：NPR

六月二十六日，伊朗用無人機攻擊一艘載著兩百多萬桶原油的大船。美國隨後出動六架飛機，炸了伊朗的飛彈和雷達基地。

這是六月十七日兩國講好停火後，第一次真的開打。那片海運著全球約兩成的石油，大家都很擔心油價會大漲。

On June 26th, Iran attacked a large tanker carrying over 2 million barrels of crude oil with drones. The United States subsequently deployed six aircraft and struck Iranian missile and radar facilities. This was the first actual combat since the two countries agreed to a ceasefire on June 17th. That shipping lane carries roughly one-fifth of the world's oil, and everyone is worried oil prices will spike.

川普簽令　高階公務員更好開除

來源：Federal News Network

六月二十七日，美國總統川普簽了一道命令，把大約八千個高階公務員的職位改了類別。

這些人很多是主管、顧問，會影響政策。改了以後，如果表現不好或不聽命令，政府不用走很多手續就能把人開除。工會擔心這樣會讓公務員變得太政治。

On June 27, President Trump signed an executive order reclassifying approximately eight thousand high-level civil service positions. Many of these are supervisory and advisory roles that influence policy. Under the new classification, the government can fire employees more easily if they underperform or fail to follow orders, without going through lengthy procedures. Labor unions worry this will make civil servants overly political.

雙重熱蓋罩頂　芝加哥體感破百

來源：Chicago Sun-Times

一股叫「雙重熱蓋」（兩片熱空氣蓋住天空）的熱浪，從六月二十八日那週壓向芝加哥。

氣溫會到攝氏三十幾度，加上很濕，身體感覺像華氏一百多度，星期一到三最熱。市政府已經開放圖書館等地當乘涼中心，老人和無家可歸的人最危險。

A heat wave called a "double heat dome"—two layers of hot air pressing down on the sky—is bearing down on Chicago starting the week of June 28th. Temperatures will reach the mid-30s Celsius, and with high humidity, the heat index will feel over 100 degrees Fahrenheit, with Monday through Wednesday the hottest days. The city government has opened libraries and other facilities as cooling centers, with elderly people and homeless individuals at greatest risk.

谷歌投資七千五百萬　幫片廠做拍片工具

來源：Variety

獨立電影公司A24和谷歌的AI團隊DeepMind合作，谷歌投入七千五百萬美元，一起做拍電影用的AI工具。

第一個工具是幫忙畫「分鏡」（拍片前先畫的草圖），不是直接生出影片。兩家說這是要幫忙導演，不是取代他們，創作的主導權還在電影人手上。

Independent film company A24 and Google's AI team DeepMind are collaborating on AI tools for filmmaking, with Google investing $75 million into the partnership. The first tool helps create storyboards—the preliminary sketches drawn before shooting begins—rather than generating finished video directly. Both companies emphasize that the technology is designed to assist directors, not replace them, with creative control remaining firmly in filmmakers' hands.

兵工廠線——阿森納（兵工廠）看上了紐卡索隊的隊長、巴西中場布魯諾。他們先出價六千萬英鎊，被紐卡索回絕了。
