Anthropic辦投資人日，準備上市

來源：Bloomberg

人工智慧公司Anthropic十月十四日在舊金山，請一群大投資人來開會。高盛、摩根士丹利和摩根大通三家銀行，正在幫它準備上市（把公司的股票賣給大家）。

彭博社說，最快十一月九日那週開始找買家，感恩節前就可能開始交易。公司五月的價值大約九千六百五十億美元，現在有人說能到兩兆美元。

前一天，國際貨幣基金組織才說AI股票太貴。這次上市，正好來考驗這句話。

The AI company Anthropic held a meeting in San Francisco on October 14 for a group of major investors. Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley and JPMorgan are helping it prepare for an IPO (selling its shares to the public). Bloomberg reports that the search for buyers could begin as early as the week of November 9, with trading possibly starting before Thanksgiving. The company was valued at roughly $965 billion in May, and some people now say it could reach $2 trillion. Just the day before, the IMF had said AI stocks were too expensive. This listing will be a good test of that claim.

兩位新教練對決，密西根返校日迎戰賓州州立

來源：Sports Illustrated

這週六下午兩點半（芝加哥時間），密西根大學美式足球隊在主場迎接賓州州立大學。這天也是返校日（校友回學校的節日）。兩隊的總教練都是今年才上任：密西根是惠廷漢，賓州州立是坎貝爾。

兩隊都在重新出發，在聯盟裡也都還沒贏過。密西根三勝兩敗，賓州州立三勝三敗。密西根上一場掉球四次，這次在家最要緊的就是把球抱好。

This Saturday at 2:30 p.m. Chicago time, the University of Michigan football team hosts Penn State at home. It is also homecoming, the annual day when alumni return to campus. Both teams have new head coaches this year: Michigan's is Whittingham, and Penn State's is Campbell. Both programs are starting over, and neither has yet won a conference game. Michigan is 3-2 and Penn State is 3-3. Michigan fumbled four times in its last game, so the most important thing at home this time is holding onto the ball.

七分鐘進三球，兵工廠歐冠十四連勝

來源：Flashscore

兵工廠線——兵工廠週二在主場三比零打敗法國的里爾，在歐洲冠軍聯賽拿下十四連勝。三個球都在下半場七分鐘裡進：廷伯、埃茲和薩卡各進一球。

里爾整場連一次射正（踢向球門）都沒有。兵工廠現在有六分，靠進球差排第三。

值得注意的是，三個進球的人都不是中鋒。球隊缺前鋒的問題還在，但別的球員正在補上。

The Arsenal beat: Arsenal beat Lille 3-0 at home on Tuesday, extending their Champions League winning streak to 14 games. All three goals came in the second half within seven minutes: Timber, Eze and Saka each found the net. Lille did not manage a single shot on target over the entire match. Arsenal now have six points and sit third on goal difference. Notably, none of the three goalscorers was a centre-forward. The club's shortage of a striker remains a problem, but other players are stepping up to fill the gap.
