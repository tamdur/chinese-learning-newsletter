輝達拉來六大金主 籌五千億美元建AI機房

來源：CNBC

晶片公司輝達（Nvidia）在八月十日宣布，要和六家華爾街大公司合作，一起找來超過五千億美元的外部資金，去蓋人工智慧（AI）用的大型電腦機房。

這六家包括黑石、貝萊德和高盛。執行長黃仁勳說，他只問了這六家，結果沒有一家說不。

好處是，這筆錢不用算在輝達自己的帳上；壞處是，萬一AI熱潮退了，賠錢的會變成大家的退休金和保險。

Chip maker Nvidia announced on August 10 that it will partner with six major Wall Street firms to jointly raise over $500 billion in outside funding to build large-scale data centres for artificial intelligence.

The six firms include Blackstone, BlackRock and Goldman Sachs. CEO Jensen Huang said he approached only these six companies, and not one of them turned him down.

The upside is that this money doesn't sit on Nvidia's own balance sheet; the downside is that if the AI boom cools, the losses land on everyone's retirement savings and insurance.

土耳其國會通過特赦 庫德武裝團體不買帳

來源：NPR

土耳其國會以四百六十八票對八十八票，通過對庫德工人黨（PKK）成員的「有條件特赦」，也就是要先確認他們全部交出武器才算數。

這場衝突打了四十年，死了大約四萬人。PKK去年已經宣布解散，也辦過交出武器的儀式。

不過法案一通過，PKK馬上說內容有「嚴重的不足」，還要求放出創辦人奧賈蘭。法律寫好了，兩邊卻還沒談好。

Turkey's parliament voted 468 to 88 to pass a conditional amnesty for members of the Kurdistan Workers' Party (PKK) — conditional in that it only counts once they are confirmed to have handed over all their weapons.

The conflict has run for forty years and killed roughly 40,000 people. The PKK announced its dissolution last year and held a weapons-surrender ceremony.

But the moment the bill passed, the PKK said it contained "serious shortcomings" and demanded the release of its founder, Ocalan. The law is written; the two sides still haven't agreed.

派拉蒙嗆搬離加州 州檢察長批勒索

來源：Variety

派拉蒙影業執行長艾利森放話：如果加州檢察長邦塔在十月一日前不肯坐下來談，公司就要開始搬出加州。

加州告派拉蒙，說它花一千一百億美元買下華納兄弟探索，會讓公司大到沒有對手。十月一日之後，派拉蒙每天還要多付華納股東七百萬美元的等待費。

艾利森說，搬走一年可以少繳五億美元的稅。邦塔回嗆：這是想「勒索」加州放行一筆違法的生意。

Paramount Pictures CEO David Ellison has issued a warning: if California Attorney General Rob Bonta won't sit down and talk by October 1, the company will start moving out of California.

California is suing Paramount, arguing that its $110 billion purchase of Warner Bros. Discovery would leave the company so large it has no real rival. After October 1, Paramount also has to pay Warner shareholders an extra $7 million a day in waiting fees.

Ellison says moving could cut its tax bill by $500 million a year. Bonta shot back that this is an attempt to "extort" California into waving through an illegal deal.

日全食今掃過冰島與西班牙 已等一百二十一年

來源：ScienceDaily

今天八月十二日，月亮完全擋住太陽的「日全食」會經過冰島西邊和西班牙北部。

這是西班牙本土一百二十一年來第一次，也是歐洲大陸自一九九九年以來第一次。天最黑的時候只有兩分十八秒，而且快日落才發生，太陽掛得很低。

西班牙估計會湧入幾十萬名追日食的遊客。更難得的是，接下來十八個月，這個國家還會再遇到兩次日食。

Today, August 12, a total solar eclipse — when the moon completely blocks the sun — sweeps across western Iceland and northern Spain.

For mainland Spain it is the first in 121 years, and for continental Europe the first since 1999. The darkest moment lasts only two minutes and eighteen seconds, and it comes close to sunset, with the sun hanging low.

Spain expects hundreds of thousands of eclipse chasers to pour in. Rarer still: over the next eighteen months, the country will see two more solar eclipses.

來源：ClickOnDetroit

密西根大學美式足球新總教練惠廷漢，八月六日開訓時最擔心的是進攻線。練了五次之後，他八月十一日改口說，這一組比他想的好，傳球保護也進步了。

二年級四分衛安德伍德的判斷和出手速度都變快，也更信任前面的隊友。左邊護鋒這個位置，有三個人在搶。

惠廷漢還特別點名接球員布坎南，說他有時候「根本守不住」。

When fall camp opened on August 6, what worried Michigan's new head football coach, Kyle Whittingham, most was the offensive line. Five practices later, on August 11, he changed his tune: the group is better than he thought, and pass protection has improved.

Sophomore quarterback Bryce Underwood is reading the field and releasing the ball faster, and trusting the players in front of him more. At left tackle, three players are competing for the job.

Whittingham also singled out receiver JJ Buchanan, saying that at times he is "simply unguardable."
