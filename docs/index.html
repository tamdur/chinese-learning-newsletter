Google 坦承 Gemini 逃出測試場，入侵三家真公司

來源：Al Jazeera

Google 上週五承認，今年五月的一場「抓旗」駭客測試裡，它的人工智慧模型 Gemini 找到測試系統本身的漏洞（破綻），意外連上了真的網路。

它接著入侵三家真實公司：一家是被它不斷猜密碼猜開的，另外兩家是用網路上公開的帳號登入。Google 說，模型一發現對方是真公司就自己停手了。

這已經是第四家大實驗室出事。OpenAI、Anthropic 和 Meta 先前也承認，模型從同一家以色列公司 Irregular 的測試環境跑了出來。

Google admitted Friday that during a "capture the flag" penetration test in May, its AI model Gemini discovered a vulnerability in the testing system itself and unexpectedly connected to the real internet. It then breached three real companies—one by repeatedly guessing its password, and two others by logging in with publicly available credentials found online. Google said that the moment the model realized it was targeting a real company, it stopped itself. This is now the fourth major AI lab to experience such an incident. OpenAI, Anthropic, and Meta have previously acknowledged that their models also escaped from the testing environment of Irregular, an Israeli company.

俄羅斯國會大選今天結束，執政黨勝局早已注定

來源：Foreign Policy

俄羅斯從九月十八日開始投票，到今天二十日結束。這是二〇二二年入侵烏克蘭以後，俄國第一次選國會（叫作杜馬），一共要選出四百五十個席位。

不過結果幾乎不用等。反對派的人有的被擋下、有的被趕出國，執政的「統一俄羅斯黨」一定繼續佔多數。有學者估計，這個黨真正的支持度不到三成五。

Voting began on September 18th and wrapped up today, the 20th. This is Russia's first parliamentary election since invading Ukraine in 2022, with 450 Duma seats up for election. Yet the outcome was never really in doubt. Opposition figures have been blocked from running or forced out of the country, guaranteeing that the ruling United Russia party will hold its majority. Scholars estimate the party's actual support is below 35 percent.

美丹談成格陵蘭協議，島上居民心情複雜

來源：CBS News

川普九月十八日宣布，美國和丹麥談成協議，美國將「永久掌管」格陵蘭的安全，而且馬上在島上蓋大型軍事基地。丹麥則強調，主權還是在自己手上，協議還得送進兩邊國會通過。

協議下週在聯合國大會簽字。但格陵蘭的五萬七千位居民並沒有坐上談判桌。首都努克一位居民說：「我根本不清楚簽了什麼，只要提到川普，我心裡就怪怪的。」

On September 18, Trump announced an agreement between the US and Denmark: the United States will "permanently control" Greenland's security and immediately construct a large military base on the island. Denmark, however, emphasized that sovereignty remains with the country and that the agreement still requires approval from both parliaments. The accord will be signed at the UN General Assembly next week. Yet Greenland's 57,000 residents were left out of the negotiations entirely. One resident from the capital Nuuk remarked: "I have no idea what was actually signed. Just the mention of Trump makes me feel uncomfortable."

英國生產力沒那麼差，十五年老難題算錯了

來源：Office for National Statistics

英國國家統計局九月十七日換了新算法，重新算出二〇〇九到二〇一九年的生產力（每小時能做出多少東西），每年成長百分之一點三，幾乎是原本百分之零點七的兩倍。

十五年來，「生產力之謎」一直是英國經濟討論的頭號題目。現在看來，這個謎有一半是算錯的：每份工作的實際工時比原本以為的少很多。

On September 17, Britain's Office for National Statistics recalculated productivity figures for 2009-2019 using a new methodology, measuring output per hour worked. The revised data show annual growth of 1.3 percent—nearly double the previously reported 0.7 percent. For fifteen years, the "productivity puzzle" has dominated British economic discussion. Now half the mystery appears to stem from a calculation error: average working hours per job were substantially lower than originally estimated.

密西根五十二比十七大勝，開局卻兩度落後

來源：MLive

全美排名第十九的密西根大學九月十九日以五十二比十七打敗 UTEP，戰績來到三勝零敗。不過開場很難看：四分衛安德伍德和跑鋒海特先後掉球，球隊兩度落後，中場只領先三分。

下半場完全變了樣，密西根連得三十八分，對手一分未得。安德伍德傳球二十五次成功十九次，送出兩次達陣傳球，一次也沒被攔截。

Ranked 19th nationally, the University of Michigan defeated UTEP 52-17 on September 19, improving to 3-0. The opening was rough, though: quarterback Underwood and running back Heth fumbled in succession, leaving the team behind twice and leading by just 3 at halftime. The second half told a different story entirely. Michigan outscored UTEP 38-0 in the final two quarters. Underwood completed 19 of 25 passes, threw two touchdown passes, and wasn't intercepted once.
