紅場沒坦克　俄烏停火三天

來源：CNN

五月九日是俄羅斯的勝利日。普丁在紅場舉辦了近二十年來最小的閱兵：沒有坦克，也沒有飛彈。

同一天，由川普牽線的三天停火開始。這是戰爭擴大後的第一次休兵，兩邊會交換一千名戰俘。大家在看能不能變成真正的和談。

Red Square Without Tanks: Russia-Ukraine Three-Day Ceasefire

May 9 is Russia's Victory Day. Putin held the smallest military parade in Red Square in nearly two decades: no tanks, no missiles.

On the same day, a three-day ceasefire brokered by Trump began. This is the first pause in fighting since the war escalated, and the two sides are exchanging a thousand prisoners. Everyone is watching to see whether it can turn into real peace talks.

美伊快簽一頁紙　戰爭將收尾

來源：Axios / Al Jazeera

美國和伊朗快要簽一張只有十四點的協議，正式結束打了好幾個月的仗。

伊朗會暫停提煉核原料、讓聯合國隨時檢查；美國會取消大部分制裁，把凍結的錢還給伊朗。五月九日，卡達的代表在邁阿密幫兩邊傳話。戰爭害荷姆茲海峽關了一半，油價飆到一百十四美元。

The United States and Iran are close to signing a fourteen-point agreement that would formally end months of fighting.

Iran would suspend uranium enrichment and allow United Nations inspectors access at any time; the United States would lift most sanctions and return Iran's frozen funds. On May 9, Qatari mediators shuttled between the two sides in Miami. The war has closed the Strait of Hormuz by half, sending oil prices surging to $114.

看不見的關稅　比看得見的還貴

來源：UNCTAD / Nairametrics

聯合國的貿易機構五月九日公布新報告：全球百分之八十八的國家，出口時花在「隱形規定」的錢，已經比關稅還多。

這些規定包括產品標準、海關手續、食品安全等。最窮的國家因為達不到標準，每年少賣了一成的貨給大國。報告說，如果大家把規則統一，這些花費可以少三成。

The United Nations' trade body released a new report on May 9: in 88 percent of countries worldwide, the cost of complying with "hidden regulations" when exporting now exceeds what they spend on tariffs.

These regulations include product standards, customs procedures, and food-safety rules. The poorest countries, unable to meet such standards, sell ten percent fewer goods to the major economies each year. The report says that if everyone harmonized the rules, these costs could be cut by thirty percent.

Cloudflare大裁兩成員工

來源：Hacker News

Cloudflare是一家幫很多網站傳送資料的公司。五月九日它宣布要裁掉差不多五分之一的員工。

原因和人工智慧有關。上週的業界報告說，AI助手完成任務的能力一年內從兩成跳到七成多。即使公司還在賺錢，老闆們也想用機器取代人工。

Cloudflare is a company that helps many websites deliver their data. On May 9 it announced that it would lay off roughly one-fifth of its employees.

The reason is tied to artificial intelligence. An industry report last week said AI assistants' ability to complete tasks jumped from 20 percent to over 70 percent in a single year. Even when companies are still profitable, executives want to replace human workers with machines.

小熊隊寫下七十一年來第一遭

來源：CBS Sports / ESPN

芝加哥小熊隊五月九日輸給遊騎兵隊六比零，本季第二次十連勝就此結束。

不過這是寫進歷史的紀錄：自從一九五五年的道奇隊以後，沒有球隊能在前四十場比賽中打出兩次十連勝。小熊現在是二十七勝十二敗，球迷想起了奪下世界大賽的二零一六年。

On May 9 the Chicago Cubs lost to the Rangers 6-0, ending their second ten-game winning streak of the season.

But the streak made history: since the 1955 Dodgers, no team has produced two separate ten-game winning streaks within its first forty games. The Cubs are now 27-12, and fans are remembering 2016 — the year they won the World Series.

電腦說：兵工廠會贏巴黎

來源：Yahoo Sports / Opta

兵工廠線——歐洲冠軍聯賽決賽還有三週就要在布達佩斯開踢。運動數據公司Opta用電腦模擬後說：兵工廠（阿森納）有百分之五十五點七七的機會打敗巴黎聖日耳曼。

球隊本季在歐冠十四場比賽不敗，只丟六球。不過賭盤看好巴黎。兵工廠也只差一分就能拿下二十二年來第一座英超冠軍。

Arsenal beat — The Champions League final kicks off in Budapest in three weeks. The sports-data company Opta ran computer simulations and says Arsenal have a 55.77 percent chance of beating Paris Saint-Germain.

The team has gone unbeaten across fourteen Champions League matches this season, conceding only six goals. The betting markets, however, favor Paris. Arsenal also stand just one point away from claiming their first Premier League title in twenty-two years.
