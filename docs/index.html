用AI造病毒，專打抗藥細菌

來源：Falling Walls Foundation

德國「倒下的牆」基金會（Falling Walls）公布今年「年度科學突破」的入圍名單。最引人注意的是史丹佛大學希伊（Brian Hie）的團隊。

他們用AI寫出幾千段新的DNA，做出16種大自然沒有的「噬菌體」（專門吃細菌的病毒）。它們能殺死一般病毒對付不了的細菌。

另一組入圍的是德國波昂大學的人工視網膜（眼睛裡感光的那一層），讓一些病人重新看見東西。得獎者11月9日在柏林公布。

The German "Falling Walls" Foundation has unveiled this year's nominees for its "Scientific Breakthrough of the Year" award. The most compelling entry comes from Stanford University's Brian Hie and his team.

Using artificial intelligence, they generated thousands of novel DNA sequences to create 16 bacteriophages—viruses that feed on bacteria—that do not exist in nature. These engineered phages can kill bacteria that conventional treatments cannot overcome.

Another nominee is an artificial retina developed by researchers at the University of Bonn in Germany—a synthetic replacement for the light-sensitive layer of the eye—that has restored sight to some patients. The award winner will be announced on November 9th in Berlin.

密西根爭奪第100次小棕壺

來源：FOX 9

密西根大學美式足球隊今天上午11點（芝加哥時間）到明尼蘇達大學比賽，爭奪「小棕壺」（Little Brown Jug）。這是兩隊第100次為了這個水壺開打，它也是美國大學美式足球最老的獎盃。

過去99場，密西根贏了74場，明尼蘇達只贏23場，另外2場打平。密西根已經連贏五場，上次是2024年以27比24贏球。

明尼蘇達最後一次在自己家打敗密西根，已經是1977年的事了。從那以後，密西根在那裡連贏18場。

Michigan's football team takes on the University of Minnesota today at 11 a.m. Chicago time in a battle for the "Little Brown Jug," college football's oldest trophy. This marks the 100th meeting between the two teams for the prize.

In their past 99 matchups, Michigan leads the series with 74 wins to Minnesota's 23, with 2 ties. Michigan has won five straight, with their most recent victory coming in 2024 by a score of 27–24.

Minnesota's last home victory over Michigan dates back to 1977. Since then, Michigan has run off 18 consecutive wins at Minnesota.

兵工廠盯上皇馬小將恩德里克

來源：ArsenalStation

兵工廠線——據球迷網站報導，兵工廠很有機會在一月簽下皇家馬德里的巴西前鋒恩德里克（Endrick）。不過這還只是傳聞，不是談好的交易。

恩德里克今年20歲，在皇馬很少上場。上季他被借到法國里昂隊，21場比賽進了8球，還助攻8次。

兵工廠夏天賣掉好幾名前鋒，現在急著補人。不過切爾西和巴黎聖日耳曼也想要他。

Arsenal Beat — According to fan websites, Arsenal has a good chance of signing Brazilian striker Endrick from Real Madrid in January. However, this remains unconfirmed—no deal has been finalized.

Endrick, 20, has seen limited playing time at Real Madrid. Last season, on loan at French club Lyon, he scored 8 goals and recorded 8 assists across 21 matches.

Arsenal sold several forwards over the summer and is now looking to add depth. Chelsea and Paris Saint-Germain are also interested in the player.
