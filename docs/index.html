烏克蘭出動四百架無人機 襲擊莫斯科油庫

來源：Kyiv Independent

烏克蘭七月二十日晚上，出動超過四百架無人機，攻擊俄羅斯首都莫斯科附近，這是開戰以來最大的攻擊之一。

無人機打中一座油庫和一個大型物流中心，還在黑海擊中六艘船，包括兩艘油輪。這些地方離烏克蘭邊界超過四百公里。

烏克蘭想切斷俄羅斯賣油賺的錢，讓它更難打仗。現在俄羅斯國內的汽油已經開始不夠用。

On the evening of July 20th, Ukraine deployed over 400 drones to attack targets near Moscow, Russia's capital—one of the largest strikes since the war began. The drones hit an oil depot and a large logistics center, and also struck six ships in the Black Sea, including two tankers. These targets lie over 400 kilometers from the Ukrainian border. Ukraine aims to cut off Russia's oil revenue, making it harder for the country to wage war. Domestic gasoline supplies inside Russia are already beginning to run short.

電腦系統出錯 殘障者被擋在健保外

來源：NPR

美國一些州的醫療補助（窮人健保）系統出了問題，錯把有殘障的人擋在外面，讓他們拿不到看病的補助，密西根州就有病人被停掉。

這些電腦系統是德勤（Deloitte）等大公司做的。現在新稅法要求人們要工作才能領補助，各州趕著改系統，錯誤可能會更多。

Medicaid systems in several US states have malfunctioned, wrongly blocking people with disabilities from accessing healthcare benefits. Michigan has already cut off benefits for some patients. These computer systems were built by major companies like Deloitte. With new legislation now requiring work to receive benefits, states are rushing to update their systems—a process likely to generate more errors.

研究：聽AI建議 人更不準卻更有自信

來源：Hacker News

一份新研究發現，人聽了AI給的建議以後，答題的正確率反而變低，可是自己卻覺得更有信心。

研究讓大家回答電影裡的小細節。沒用AI時正確率有百分之二十七，用了AI後掉到百分之九；但覺得自己答對的信心，從三成升到七成六。願意說「我不知道」的人，也從四成四掉到只剩百分之三。

A new study has found that when people take advice from AI, their accuracy in answering questions actually drops—yet they feel far more confident about their answers. Researchers asked participants to answer trivia questions about movie details. Without AI assistance, their accuracy was 27%, but with AI it plummeted to just 9%. Meanwhile, their confidence in being correct jumped from 30% to 76%. The proportion willing to say "I don't know" also fell sharply, from 44% to only 3%.

法官喊停 派拉蒙併華納案暫停十四天

來源：NPR

美國一位聯邦法官下令，把派拉蒙要買下華納兄弟的合併案暫停十四天。這筆交易高達一千一百億美元，是近年最大的媒體合併。

十二個州的檢察長告上法院，說兩家合併後公司太大，會壟斷電影和有線電視市場。法官認為這很可能違反反壟斷法，八月三日會再開庭。

A U.S. federal judge has ordered Paramount's acquisition of Warner Bros. on hold for fourteen days. The $110 billion deal is the largest media merger in recent years. Attorneys general from twelve states filed suit, arguing that the combined company would be too large and dominate the film and cable television markets. The judge found the merger likely violates antitrust law and scheduled another hearing for August 3rd.

密西根體育主任下台 偷暗號醜聞收尾

來源：Detroit News

密西根大學宣布，體育主任曼紐爾（Warde Manuel）會在今年底離開，同時公布一份內部調查報告。

這幾年密西根球隊爆出一連串醜聞：職員史塔利恩斯偷看對手暗號、前教練魏斯被聯邦起訴、總教練摩爾去年十二月被開除又被逮捕。報告說學校沒有及早處理這些問題。曼紐爾從二零一六年就當主任。

The University of Michigan announced that Athletic Director Warde Manuel will step down at the end of this year, as the university released an internal investigation report.

In recent years, Michigan's athletics program has been rocked by a series of scandals: staff member Stalions was caught stealing opponents' signals, former coach Weiss faced federal charges, and head coach Moore was fired and arrested in December of last year.

The report concluded that the university failed to address these issues in a timely manner.

Manuel has served as athletic director since 2016.

兵工廠線：薩利巴傷停五個月 兵工廠追康薩

來源：Football Transfers

兵工廠線——阿森納（別名兵工廠）的主力中後衛薩利巴，在世界盃準決賽對西班牙時傷到背部，預計要休息四到五個月，甚至可能要開刀。

少了他，阿森納趕緊在市場上找人補位。他們看上阿斯頓維拉的後衛康薩（Ezri Konsa），準備出價大約七千萬歐元，把他買來補強防線。

Arsenal Beat — Arsenal's leading center-back Saliba suffered a back injury in the World Cup semifinal against Spain and is expected to miss four to five months, with potential surgery required. With him sidelined, Arsenal moved quickly to find a replacement on the market. They've targeted Aston Villa's Ezri Konsa, preparing a bid of around 70 million euros to strengthen their defense.
