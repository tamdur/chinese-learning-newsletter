中國第三季經濟成長4.5%，比預期好

來源：Reuters

中國政府19日公布，今年第三季經濟比去年同期成長4.5%，比專家預測的4.4%高一點，也比第二季的4.3%好。

不過，這次成長主要靠出口和工廠生產。民眾還是不太愛花錢，1到9月的城市建設投資更減少了7.2%。

今年到目前為止，中國經濟成長4.6%，有達到政府的目標。但這還是靠老方法做到的，不是靠大家買東西。

China's economy grew 4.5% in the third quarter from a year earlier, the government announced on the 19th. That is slightly above the 4.4% that analysts had forecast, and better than the 4.3% recorded in the second quarter. However, the growth came mainly from exports and factory production. Consumers are still reluctant to spend, and urban fixed-asset investment fell 7.2% in the first nine months of the year. So far this year, China's economy has grown 4.6%, meeting the government's target. But it has achieved this the old way, through exports and factories rather than through people buying more.

Anthropic拿出5%股份做公益

來源：Business Standard

人工智慧公司Anthropic在18日宣布，要把公司5%的股份放進一個新的「公益基金」。

如果公司的價值大漲超過一半，董事會還可以再多加2%。這筆錢不能拿去遊說（請官員幫忙說話），也不能捐給政治人物。加上創辦人的捐款，公司大約有20%的股份是留給公共用途。

Anthropic最快可能11月上市。這讓人好奇：一家AI公司除了股東，還要對誰負責？

On the 18th, the AI company Anthropic announced that it would put 5% of the company's shares into a new "public benefit fund." If the company's value rises by more than half, the board can add another 2%. The money cannot be used for lobbying (paying officials to speak on the company's behalf), nor donated to politicians. Counting the founders' donations, about 20% of the company's shares are set aside for public purposes. Anthropic could go public as early as November. That raises a question: beyond its shareholders, who else does an AI company answer to?

巴西選總統，兩人電視辯論互罵

來源：Washington Post

巴西總統魯拉和參議員弗拉維奧·波索納洛，18日晚上第一次在電視上辯論。弗拉維奧是前總統波索納洛的兒子。兩個多小時的辯論氣氛很緊張，兩人都說對方在說謊。

在10月4日的第一輪投票，弗拉維奧拿到47%，贏過魯拉的45%。現任總統落後，是很少見的事。

25日將進行最後投票。預測市場認為，弗拉維奧有79%的機會當選。

On the 18th, Brazilian President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva and Senator Flávio Bolsonaro met in their first televised debate. Flávio is the son of former president Jair Bolsonaro. The debate lasted more than two hours and the mood was tense, with each man accusing the other of lying. In the first round of voting on October 4, Flávio won 47% to Lula's 45%. For a sitting president to be trailing like this is rare. The final vote will be held on the 25th. Prediction markets give Flávio a 79% chance of winning.

米蘭物價抗議連續兩天變暴力

來源：Reuters

因為生活費一直漲，義大利北部出現抗議，還擴大到比利時、葡萄牙和法國。

在米蘭，18日是連續第二天出現暴力。戴面具的人丟石頭、搶商店、放火，警察用催淚瓦斯和水柱回擊。大約5000人參加，損失超過50萬歐元，兩天約有50到60人被抓。

總理梅洛尼批評這些暴力。米蘭市長說，大家的不滿可以理解，但用暴力就是不對。

Protests over rising living costs have spread from northern Italy to Belgium, Portugal, and France. In Milan, the protests turned violent for the second day in a row on the 18th. Masked individuals threw stones, looted shops, and set fires, while police responded with tear gas and water cannons. About 5,000 people took part, and the damage exceeded €500,000. Roughly 50 to 60 people were arrested over the two days. Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni criticized the violence. The mayor of Milan said that people's frustration is understandable, but that violence is not the answer.

威廉斯傷好回歸，熊隊31比23勝獵鷹

來源：NBC Sports

芝加哥熊隊四分衛威廉斯（Caleb Williams）的腿傷好了，18日在亞特蘭大回到球場。他一開始連續傳成12球，全場36次傳球成功30次，推進305碼。

熊隊一度領先24分，但第四節差點被追上，只剩8分。最後靠防守球員戈登擒殺（在四分衛傳球前把他撲倒），還逼對方掉球，才保住勝利。

熊隊現在4勝2敗，獵鷹隊是2勝4敗。

Caleb Williams is back. The Chicago Bears quarterback's leg injury has healed, and he returned to the field in Atlanta on October 18. He started hot, completing his first 12 passes in a row, and finished 30 of 36 for 305 yards. The Bears led by as many as 24 points, but in the fourth quarter the Falcons cut the margin to just eight. What saved the win was the defense: Bears defender Gordon sacked the Falcons quarterback before he could throw, and then forced a fumble. The Bears (4-2) beat the Falcons (2-4) 31-23.

兵工廠1比0小勝森林

來源：Sky Sports

兵工廠線——兵工廠18日在英超客場以1比0贏了諾丁漢森林。

下半場換上場的馬杜埃克在第80分鐘射門，球碰到對手後彈進球門，成為全場唯一進球。這也是兵工廠整場唯一一次射正。

贏球讓兵工廠繼續爭冠軍，森林則掉進降級區。不過，只射正一次，也再次看出球隊缺少好前鋒。接下來，他們要在歐冠對上拜仁。

The Arsenal beat: Arsenal beat Nottingham Forest 1-0 away in the Premier League on the 18th. Substitute Madueke shot in the 80th minute, and the ball hit an opponent and deflected into the net, the only goal of the match. It was also Arsenal's only shot on target all game. The win keeps Arsenal in the title race, while Forest have slipped into the relegation zone. Still, taking just one shot on target again showed that the team lacks a good striker. Next, they face Bayern in the Champions League.
