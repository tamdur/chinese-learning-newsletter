西班牙週日大選，右派可能拿下過半

來源：Brussels Signal / Catalan News

西班牙的選舉活動今天結束，11月29日星期日就要投票。最新民調顯示，保守的人民黨加上極右派的呼聲黨（Vox），合起來可能拿到國會過半的176席。

如果真的這樣，社會黨總理桑切斯就會下台。他是歐盟大國裡，少數還在執政（管理國家）的中間偏左領袖。

還有一件事沒解決：大約16萬3千張海外選票有爭議，最高法院還沒決定要不要算。如果選情很接近，這些票可能會改變結果。

Spain's election campaign ends today, and voters go to the polls this Sunday, November 29. The latest polls suggest that the conservative People's Party and the far-right Vox party together could win 176 seats, a majority in the national parliament. If that happens, Socialist Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez would be forced out of office. He is one of the few center-left leaders still governing a major EU country. One question remains open: about 163,000 overseas ballots are under dispute, and the Supreme Court has not yet decided whether they will be counted. If the race is very close, those votes could decide the outcome.

熊隊最後3秒逆轉獅子隊，登上分區第一

來源：ClickOnDetroit / Sky Sports

芝加哥熊隊感恩節到底特律比賽，第四節一度落後11分，最後以39比35逆轉獅子隊。

比賽只剩3秒時，四分衛 Caleb Williams 傳球給 Rome Odunze，在6碼外達陣（把球帶進得分區）。Williams 全場傳球345碼，Odunze 一個人就拿下兩次達陣。

熊隊現在是9勝2敗，單獨站上國家聯會北區第一名。總教練 Ben Johnson 以前是獅子隊的教練，這場打敗老球隊，贏得特別開心。

The Chicago Bears played the Detroit Lions on Thanksgiving. Down 11 points at one point in the fourth quarter, they came back to win 39-35. With just 3 seconds left, quarterback Caleb Williams threw a pass to Rome Odunze, who caught it for a touchdown from six yards out. Williams threw for 345 yards in the game, and Odunze scored two touchdowns by himself. The Bears are now 9-2 and sit alone atop the NFC North. Head coach Ben Johnson used to coach the Lions, so beating his old team made the win especially sweet.
