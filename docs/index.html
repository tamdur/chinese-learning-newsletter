美軍大規模轟炸伊朗哈爾克島

來源：CNN

美國和以色列對伊朗的戰爭已經進入第十四天。美軍轟炸了哈爾克島（Kharg Island），打擊超過90個軍事目標，包括防空系統、海軍基地和機場。

Trump說他故意沒有炸石油設施，但警告如果伊朗封鎖荷莫茲海峽（Strait of Hormuz），就不會手軟。這座島負責伊朗大約90%的石油出口，所以國際油價已經漲到每桶一百美元以上。目前衝突已造成超過2,600人死亡。

The U.S. and Israel have been at war with Iran for fourteen days. The U.S. military bombed Kharg Island, striking more than 90 military targets, including air defense systems, naval bases, and airports.

Trump said he deliberately avoided hitting oil facilities, but warned that if Iran blocks the Strait of Hormuz, he won't hold back. The island is responsible for about 90% of Iran's oil exports, so international oil prices have already climbed above one hundred dollars a barrel. The conflict has so far caused more than 2,600 deaths.

Boyd獲選小熊開幕戰先發投手

來源：MLB.com

小熊隊宣布，35歲的 Matthew Boyd 將擔任3月26日開幕戰先發投手，在 Wrigley Field 對上國民隊。

Boyd 去年表現超好：14勝，防禦率3.21，還第一次入選全明星賽。他在 Wrigley 的成績特別驚人：12勝1敗，防禦率只有2.51。更厲害的是，他2021到2024年一直受傷，還動了 Tommy John 手術，能這樣回來真的很了不起。總教練 Counsell 說：「這是他自己努力掙來的。」

The Cubs announced that 35-year-old Matthew Boyd will be the starting pitcher for the opening day game on March 26 against the Nationals at Wrigley Field.

Boyd had an outstanding season last year: 14 wins, a 3.21 ERA, and made his first All-Star selection. His performance at Wrigley was particularly impressive: 12 wins and 1 loss with an ERA of just 2.51. What makes it even more remarkable is that he was injured from 2021 to 2024 and underwent Tommy John surgery, so his comeback is truly remarkable. Manager Counsell said: "This is something he earned through his own hard work."

Claude開放百萬token上下文窗口

來源：Anthropic

Anthropic 宣布 Claude Opus 4.6 和 Sonnet 4.6 正式開放一百萬 token 的上下文窗口（就是 AI 一次能讀多少資料），而且不加價。以前用長內容要付大約兩倍的錢，現在不用了。

這代表你可以一次丟整個程式庫給 AI 看，或是同時分析好幾篇研究論文。Google Gemini 也有百萬 token，但超過20萬就要加錢。

Anthropic announced that Claude Opus 4.6 and Sonnet 4.6 now officially support a one-million-token context window (how much data the AI can read at once), and at no extra cost. Previously, using long content required paying roughly double; now it doesn't.

This means you can feed an entire codebase to the AI at once, or analyze multiple research papers simultaneously. Google Gemini also has a million-token capacity, but anything over 200,000 tokens incurs additional charges.

最高法院判IEEPA關稅違法，Trump改用舊法加稅

來源：SCOTUSblog

美國最高法院以6比3裁定，Trump 用IEEPA（國際緊急經濟權力法）來加關稅是違法的。首席大法官 Roberts 寫道，總統不能靠法律裡的兩個字就拿到這麼大的權力。這一下打掉了7個行政命令。

不過 Trump 幾個小時內就改用1974年的貿易法 Section 122，對全球進口加15%的關稅，有效期150天。Goldman Sachs 估計消費者要多付1,750億美元。

The U.S. Supreme Court ruled 6-3 that Trump's use of IEEPA (International Emergency Economic Powers Act) to impose tariffs was illegal. Chief Justice Roberts wrote that a president cannot claim such vast power based on just two words in a law. This struck down 7 executive orders.

However, within hours Trump invoked Section 122 of the 1974 Trade Act, imposing a 15% tariff on all global imports for a 150-day period. Goldman Sachs estimates consumers will pay an extra $175 billion.

科學家發現中子星和黑洞以橢圓軌道合併

來源：Astrophysical Journal Letters

科學家重新分析2020年偵測到的重力波（gravitational wave），發現一個黑洞和一顆中子星（neutron star，密度超高的死掉的星星）是沿著橢圓形軌道合併的。

這很奇怪，因為重力波通常會讓軌道慢慢變圓。橢圓軌道代表它們可能不是正常的雙星系統，而是在很擠的星團（star cluster）裡被推到一起的。研究者說：「軌道的形狀就洩漏了它們的來歷。」

Scientists re-analyzed a gravitational wave detected in 2020 and discovered that a black hole and a neutron star — an extremely dense dead star — merged along an elliptical orbit.

This is strange because gravitational waves normally cause orbits to gradually become circular. An elliptical orbit suggests they may not be a normal binary star system, but rather two objects pushed together in a densely packed star cluster. Researchers said: "The shape of the orbit reveals their origin."
