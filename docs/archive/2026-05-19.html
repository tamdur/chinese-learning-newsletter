川普取消攻打伊朗，雙方接近核武協議

來源：CBS News

美國總統川普週一宣布，他已取消原定週二對伊朗的軍事打擊。他說這是應沙烏地阿拉伯、阿聯酋和卡達等中東盟友的要求。

目前雙方正在談一份框架（大方向）協議，重點是伊朗要停止製造核武器。川普強調，如果談不成，軍隊隨時可以發動大規模攻擊。

President Trump announced on Monday that he has canceled a planned military strike against Iran scheduled for Tuesday. He said this was in response to requests from Middle Eastern allies including Saudi Arabia, the United Arab Emirates, and Qatar. The two sides are currently negotiating a framework agreement focused on Iran ceasing nuclear weapons production. Trump emphasized that if talks fail, the military stands ready to launch a major offensive at any time.

Anthropic 花三億美元買下 Stainless，搶走對手的開發工具

來源：TechCrunch

Anthropic 宣布用超過三億美元買下紐約新創公司 Stainless。Stainless 專門做 SDK（軟體開發工具包），客戶包括 OpenAI、Google 和 Meta。

這筆交易很特別：Anthropic 買到的不只是技術，還拿走了對手天天在用的基礎工具。Anthropic 說會關掉 Stainless 原本提供給外部的服務。

Anthropic announced that it has acquired Stainless, a New York startup, for over $300 million. Stainless specializes in SDKs (software development kits), with clients including OpenAI, Google, and Meta. This deal is unusual: Anthropic isn’t just buying technology—it’s acquiring the foundational tools that competitors rely on daily. Anthropic said it will shut down Stainless’s external-facing services.

史丹佛報告：AI 愈來愈強，管理卻跟不上

來源：Stanford HAI

史丹佛大學發布了 2026 年 AI 指數報告。報告指出，AI 能力快速進步，但各國的管理規定遠遠落後。

一個關鍵數字：「透明度指數」從 58 分掉到 40 分，代表大公司公開的 AI 資訊反而變少了。目前全球有 47 個國家正在制定 AI 相關法律，但做法各不相同。

Stanford University released its 2026 AI Index report, which shows that while AI capabilities are advancing rapidly, regulatory frameworks across countries lag far behind. One striking metric stands out: the “transparency index” dropped from 58 to 40, indicating that major companies are actually disclosing less information about their AI systems. Currently, 47 countries are drafting AI-related legislation, but their approaches vary widely.

芝加哥停車收費表要換新老闆了

來源：Chicago Sun-Times

芝加哥最有名的爭議又回來了。 2008 年，市政府把全市停車收費表以 11.5 億美元租給摩根士丹利集團，租期長達 75 年。

現在這個集團要把經營權賣給紐約的 Stonepeak Partners。這筆交易需要市議會同意，預計這週會提出討論。

Chicago’s most famous controversy is back. In 2008, the city leased its parking meters to the Morgan Stanley group for $1.15 billion, with a lease term stretching 75 years. Now that group wants to sell the operating rights to New York-based Stonepeak Partners. The deal requires city council approval, and discussion is expected this week.

熊隊新賽季賽程公布：全聯盟最難

來源：ESPN

NFL 公布了 2026 年賽程，芝加哥熊隊拿到了全聯盟最難的賽程，對手去年平均勝率高達 .550。

主要原因是 NFC 北區四支球隊去年全部贏多輸少，這在歷史上非常少見。過去九年，賽程最難的球隊有七次打出輸多贏少的成績。

The NFL has released the 2026 schedule, and the Chicago Bears drew the toughest slate in the entire league. Their opponents posted an average winning percentage of .550 last year. The primary reason is that all four teams in the NFC North won more games than they lost last season — an extraordinarily rare occurrence. Over the past nine years, the team with the hardest schedule has finished below .500 seven times.

兵工廠主場最後一戰 1-0 勝伯恩利，冠軍近在眼前

來源：Sky Sports

兵工廠線——兵工廠週一在主場以 1-0 擊敗伯恩利，由哈弗茨在第 36 分鐘頭球破門。這是本季英超最後一場主場比賽。

兵工廠現在領先曼城五分，離 22 年來第一個聯賽冠軍只差一步。最後一場客場對水晶宮， 5 月 24 日開踢。

Arsenal Beat — Arsenal defeated Burnley 1-0 at home on Monday, with Havertz scoring a header in the 36th minute. This was the club’s final home match of the Premier League season. Arsenal now leads Manchester City by five points, just one step away from their first league title in 22 years. Their final away match against Crystal Palace kicks off on May 24th.
