Anthropic第三季收入大增，上市估值引發爭論

來源：Euronews／路透社

人工智慧公司Anthropic告訴投資人，今年第三季收入達到168億美元，比上一季多了大約45%。扣掉一些成本後，公司已經連續兩季賫錢。

不過，上一季的收入成長了一倍多，現在成長速度明顯變慢了。

Anthropic準備在11月上市（把股票賣給大眾），可能是历上最大的一次。投資人正在爭論：公司到底值2兆美元，還是1.5兆美元？國際貨幣基金組織這星期才警告，AI股票可能太貴了。

Anthropic told investors that its revenue in the third quarter of this year reached $16.8 billion, up roughly 45% from the previous quarter. After some costs, the company has been profitable for two straight quarters. Last quarter's revenue, however, had more than doubled, and growth is clearly slowing now. Anthropic is preparing to go public in November (selling shares to the public), which could be the biggest such offering in history. Investors are arguing over whether the company is worth $2 trillion or $1.5 trillion. The International Monetary Fund warned only this week that AI stocks may be too expensive.

衣索比亞與厄利垂亞走到戰爭邊緣

來源：美聯社／法新社、國際危機組織

非洲的衣索比亞和邻國厄利垂亞，這個月初断絕了外交關係。之後有消息說，厄利垂亞的軍隊開進衣索比亞北部的提格雷地區。衣索比亞用無人機攻擊回應，但厄利垂亞說自己沒有大與出兵。

國際危機組織10月13日警告，兩國非常接近全面開戰。背後的原因之一，是沒有海岸的衣索比亞很想要一個紅海港口。大家擔心會重演2020到2022年的提格雷戰爭，那場戰爭害死了幾十萬人。

Ethiopia and its neighbor Eritrea cut diplomatic ties earlier this month. Reports later said Eritrean troops had moved into Tigray, a region in northern Ethiopia. Ethiopia responded with drone strikes, but Eritrea says it has not sent its forces in large numbers. On October 13, the International Crisis Group warned that the two countries were very close to all-out war. One of the reasons behind the tensions is that landlocked Ethiopia badly wants a port on the Red Sea. People fear a repeat of the Tigray War of 2020 to 2022, which killed hundreds of thousands of people.

熊隊威廉斯可能回歸，防守球員卻接連受傷

來源：ChicagoBears.com

芝加哥熊隊四分衛凱勒布·威廉斯，星期天到亞特蘭大打獵鷹隊，被列為「不確定能不能上場」。他大腿後側受傷，連續三場沒先發，但這星期每次練球都全程參加。

壞消息在防守那邊：線衛愛德華茲、艱略特和防守邊鋒奧德因博都不能上場，兩名先發角衛戈登和历蒂文滢也不一定能打。

上星期熊隊在綠灣以7比31大輸。現在防守少了這麼多人，就算威廉斯回來，這場比賽也不好打。

Chicago Bears quarterback Caleb Williams is listed as questionable for Sunday's game at the Atlanta Falcons. He has a hamstring injury and has missed the last three starts, but he has taken part in every practice this week. The defense has the bigger problems. Linebackers Edwards and Elliott and defensive end Odenigbo are all out, and starting cornerbacks Gordon and Stevenson are doubtful. Last week the Bears lost 7-31 in Green Bay. With this many defenders out, the game will be hard to win even if Williams returns.

薩卡越來越會進球

來源：Sky Sports、Arsenal.com

兵工廠線——兵工廠的邊鋒薩卡，這個賽季比以前更會進球了。英國天空體育分析，原因有兩個：他很少受傷，身邊的進攻球員也比以前更強。

星期二兵工廠3比0打敗里爾，薩卡也進了一球。星期天兵工廠要去客場打諾丁漢森林。薩卡在對森林的六場聯賽裡，直接參與了五個進球。

壞消息是，中場祖比門迪和後衛因卡皮耶練球時扁傷腳踝（腳和小腿連接的地方）。祖比門迪希望能趕上比賽，因卡皮耶則要休息比較久。

The Arsenal beat: Arsenal winger Saka is scoring more often this season than ever before. Sky Sports analysis points to two reasons: he has rarely been injured, and the attacking players around him are stronger than in past seasons. On Tuesday Arsenal beat Lille 3-0, and Saka scored in the match. On Sunday Arsenal travel to Nottingham Forest. In six league games against Forest, Saka has been directly involved in five goals. The bad news is that midfielder Martín Zubimendi and defender Piero Hincapié hurt their ankles (where the foot meets the lower leg) in training. Zubimendi hopes to be fit for the match, while Hincapié will be out for a longer stretch.
