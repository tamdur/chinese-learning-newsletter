歐盟選中義大利團隊　自建大型人工智慧

來源：European Commission

歐盟不想一直用美國的人工智慧（會思考的電腦程式），決定自己蓋一個。

六月十九日，歐盟選出由義大利公司領頭的「EUROPA」團隊，要打造一個超大的模型，會說歐盟全部二十四種語言。

這個模型會公開給大家免費用，這是歐洲想靠自己的重要一步。

The European Union doesn't want to keep relying on American artificial intelligence — computer programs that think — so it has decided to build its own. On June 19th, the EU selected the "EUROPA" team, led by an Italian company, to create a massive model capable of speaking all 24 official EU languages. The model will be made publicly available for free, marking an important step in Europe's bid to stand on its own.

來源：CBC / NBC News

美國總統川普和伊朗總統裴澤斯基安簽了一份十四點的協議，兩國先停止打仗六十天。

美國會拿走封鎖伊朗港口的軍艦，重要的荷姆茲海峽（運油的海上通道）也重新開放。

伊朗答應不做核子武器。不過六十天很短，能不能真的和平還很難說。

US President Trump and Iranian President Pezeshkian have signed a fourteen-point agreement to halt the fighting between the two countries for sixty days. The United States will withdraw the warships blockading Iran's ports, and the critical Strait of Hormuz — a vital sea route for shipping oil — will reopen. Iran has agreed not to build nuclear weapons. However, sixty days is a short window, and whether lasting peace can take hold remains uncertain.

經濟學家寫論文　愛找人工智慧幫忙

來源：Marginal Revolution

有名的經濟學家柯文說，現在很多經濟學家做研究時，都會用人工智慧來幫忙。

他們會先把想到的數學點子丟給人工智慧，請它幫忙檢查對不對，再決定要不要寫進論文。

柯文認為，這不是搶人的工作，而是改變了大家做學問的方法。

The well-known economist Cowen says that many economists now use artificial intelligence to help with their research. They first feed their mathematical ideas to the AI, asking it to check whether they are correct, and then decide whether to write them into their papers. Cowen believes this isn't about AI taking people's jobs, but rather a change in how scholars do their work.

芝加哥市長選舉　挑戰者越來越多

來源：WTTW

芝加哥二零二七年的市長選舉越來越熱鬧。前市議員卡德納斯也宣布要參選。

他和門多薩、奎格利等人，都想挑戰現任市長強森。強森的滿意度只有大約百分之三十一，不太高。

大家關心的問題，包括城市缺錢和治安。第一輪投票在明年二月舉行。

Chicago's 2027 mayoral race is heating up. Former alderman Cardenas has announced his candidacy, joining Mendoza, Quigley, and others in challenging incumbent Mayor Johnson. Johnson's approval rating stands at around 31 percent — not particularly high. The issues people care about include the city's budget shortfall and public safety. The first round of voting will be held next February.
