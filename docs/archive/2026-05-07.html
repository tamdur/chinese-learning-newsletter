馬斯克把 xAI 併入 SpaceX，打造史上最大 AI 合併案

來源：CNBC / TechCrunch

馬斯克正式把他的 AI 公司 xAI、社群平台 X 和 SpaceX 合在一起，成立新品牌 SpaceXAI。這筆交易總值 1.25 兆美元，是有史以來最大的企業合併。

馬斯克說，目標是把 AI、火箭和太空網路整合在一起，甚至要在太空蓋資料中心。公司預計今年七月上市。

Musk has officially merged his AI company xAI, social platform X, and SpaceX into a new brand called SpaceXAI. The deal is valued at $1.25 trillion, making it the largest corporate merger in history. Musk says the goal is to integrate AI, rockets, and space networks together, and even build data centers in space. The company is expected to go public in July this year.

DeepSeek 發布 V4-Flash：只用 13B 參數就能跑的超高效 AI

來源：Hugging Face / LLM Stats

中國 AI 公司 DeepSeek 發布了新模型 V4-Flash。這個模型總共有 2840 億個參數（就像大腦裡的連結），但每次處理文字時只啟動 130 億個，大幅節省電腦運算力。

它還能一次讀一百萬個字，而且完全免費開放。這證明了聰明的設計比單純加大模型更重要。

Chinese AI company DeepSeek has released a new model, V4-Flash. The model contains 284 billion parameters in total—think of them like connections in a brain—but activates only 13 billion at a time when processing text, dramatically reducing computational demands. It can also read one million tokens in a single context and is completely free and open. This demonstrates that clever design matters more than simply scaling up model size.

英格蘭銀行利率不動，中東衝突推高油價讓降息無望

來源：Bank of England / CNBC

英格蘭銀行（英國的中央銀行）決定把利率維持在 3.75%，沒有降息。投票結果是八比一，其中一位委員甚至想加息。

主要原因是荷莫茲海峽的衝突讓油價漲到每桶一百美元以上，英國的物價上漲率已經升到 3.3%。專家預測今年可能都不會降息了。

The Bank of England (the UK's central bank) decided to hold interest rates steady at 3.75%, with no rate cut. The voting result was eight to one, with one committee member even pushing for a rate hike. The main reason is that conflict in the Strait of Hormuz has driven oil prices above $100 per barrel, and Britain's inflation rate has risen to 3.3%. Experts predict there may be no rate cuts for the rest of the year.

手術前先用免疫療法，大腸癌病人三年零復發

來源：ScienceDaily / UCL

英國一項臨床試驗發現，大腸癌病人在手術前先接受九週的免疫治療，效果非常好。在 32 位病人中，有 59% 在開刀時已經找不到癌細胞。

追蹤 33 個月後，沒有任何一位病人復發。一般做法是先開刀再化療，但三年內約有 25% 會復發。新方法可能改變未來的治療順序。

A British clinical trial has found that giving colorectal cancer patients nine weeks of immunotherapy before surgery produces remarkable results. Among 32 patients, 59% showed no detectable cancer cells at the time of surgery. After 33 months of follow-up, not a single patient experienced recurrence. The conventional approach—surgery followed by chemotherapy—typically sees about 25% of patients relapse within three years. This new method could fundamentally change the treatment sequence going forward.

小乖連勝八場！延長賽再見勝紅人，登上國聯中區第一

來源：ESPN / Chicago Sun-Times

芝加哥小熊隊在延長賽第十局以 7 比 6 擊敗紅人隊，連續第三天再見勝！ Ian Happ 在第一局打出兩分全壘打，Crow-Armstrong 在第九局轟出追平全壘打。

小熊戰績 19 勝 12 敗，八連勝後獨居國聯中區第一名。球隊氣勢正旺！

The Chicago Cubs defeated the Reds 7-6 in 10 innings, marking their third walk-off victory in as many days! Ian Happ hit a two-run homer in the first inning, and Crow-Armstrong blasted a go-ahead home run in the ninth to tie the game. With the win, the Cubs improve to 19-12 and now sit alone atop the NL Central after their eighth consecutive victory. The team is riding high.

兵工廠二十年後重返歐冠決賽，週日可封王

來源：Arsenal.com / CNN

兵工廠線——Bukayo Saka 在上半場結束前補射進球，兵工廠在主場 1 比 0 擊敗馬德里競技，兩回合總比數 2 比 1，自 2006 年以來首次打進歐冠決賽。

決賽將在五月三十日於布達佩斯舉行。同時，兵工廠在英超領先五分，週日對西漢姆只要贏就能鎖定聯賽冠軍，有機會拿下聯賽加歐冠雙冠王。

Bukayo Saka added a second-half goal as Arsenal defeated Atlético Madrid 1-0 at home, winning 2-1 on aggregate to reach the Champions League final for the first time since 2006. The final will be held in Budapest on May 30th. Meanwhile, Arsenal leads the Premier League by five points, and a win against West Ham on Sunday would clinch the league title, putting a domestic-European double within reach.
