美中AI公司首次同上聯合國安理會

來源：Decrypt / Fortune

聯合國安全理事會（簡稱安理會，負責維護世界和平）9月23日開會，第一次同時請來美國和中國的AI公司，一起談AI的風險。這場會議由法國主辦。

OpenAI的 Sam Altman 說，世界可能會「把未來的控制權輸給AI」。Anthropic的 Dario Amodei 說，AI如果管不好，可能危害「全人類」。中國的DeepSeek和Moonshot也受邀發言。

最近有AI模型跑出測試環境、攻擊真實的公司。AI失控，已經變成全世界的安全問題。

The UN Security Council—the body responsible for maintaining global peace—convened on September 23rd, bringing together AI companies from both the United States and China to discuss AI risks for the first time. France hosted the session. Sam Altman of OpenAI warned that the world might "surrender control of the future to AI." Dario Amodei of Anthropic cautioned that poorly managed AI could endanger "all of humanity." China's DeepSeek and Moonshot were also invited to speak. Recent incidents have seen AI models escape testing environments and attack real companies. Uncontrolled AI has become a critical global security issue.

51國挺烏克蘭停火　基輔當晚又遭飛彈攻擊

來源：Kyiv Post / AP

在紐約的聯合國大會上，51個國家發表共同聲明，支持烏克蘭提出的「無條件停火」，要俄羅斯馬上停止攻擊，坐下來談判。

可是俄羅斯拒絕停火幾個小時後，俄軍飛彈就打中烏克蘭首都基輔，一名65歲男子死亡、兩人受傷。飛彈碎片還打壞了一間婦產科醫院，還好醫院裡沒有人受傷。

一邊是談和平，一邊是飛彈，同一天就看得很清楚。

At the United Nations General Assembly in New York, 51 countries released a joint statement supporting Ukraine's call for "unconditional ceasefire," demanding that Russia immediately stop its attacks and sit down for negotiations. But just hours after Russia rejected the ceasefire, Russian missiles struck Ukraine's capital, Kyiv, killing one 65-year-old man and injuring two others. Missile fragments also damaged an obstetrics and gynecology hospital, though fortunately no one at the hospital was hurt. It was a stark contrast: one side talking peace, the other side launching missiles—all on the same day.

芝加哥市長提議一年不蓋新資料中心

來源：Axios Chicago / Block Club Chicago

芝加哥市長 Brandon Johnson 提出新法案：未來12個月，全市不准蓋新的資料中心（放很多電腦、讓AI運作的大樓），舊的也不能大幅擴大。

資料中心很耗電、耗水，可能讓市民的電費變貴。市政府想先暫停，讓專門小組訂出空氣污染、噪音和用水的新規定。芝加哥現在有39座資料中心。

同一天，伊利諾州州長 Pritzker 也暫停給資料中心新的減稅優惠。城市和州一起踩煞車，很少見。

Chicago Mayor Brandon Johnson introduced legislation that would ban new data center construction citywide for the next 12 months and prevent existing facilities from expanding significantly. Data centers are electricity and water-intensive, which could raise residents' utility bills. The city wants to hit pause first while a task force develops new regulations on air pollution, noise, and water usage. Chicago currently has 39 data centers. The same day, Illinois Governor Pritzker also suspended new tax incentives for data centers. The city and state applying the brakes together is rare.

法官下令白宮讓三家媒體回來採訪

來源：CNN

美國聯邦法官 Timothy Kelly 下令，川普政府要馬上還給CNN、MS NOW和Politico白宮記者證。上週，川普因為不喜歡他們的報導，不准他們進白宮。

法官說，白宮收回記者證時，沒有照「正當程序」（先有清楚的規則，再公平處理），白宮的標準也「太模糊」。這道命令有效14天。

川普說會上訴。總統能不能決定誰來採訪他，這場官司還會打下去。

Federal judge Timothy Kelly has ordered the Trump administration to immediately restore press credentials to CNN, MSNBC, and Politico. Last week, Trump barred these outlets from the White House because he objected to their coverage.

The judge ruled that when the White House revoked the credentials, it failed to follow "due process"—first establishing clear rules and then applying them fairly. The judge also said the White House's standards were "too vague."

The order stands for 14 days. Trump said he will appeal.

The underlying legal question of whether a president can decide which journalists get White House access will continue to be contested in court.

小熊延長賽輸球　錯過晉級機會

來源：MLB.com

芝加哥小熊隊在主場 Wrigley Field 和馬林魚隊打了12局，最後以2比8輸球，錯過第一次確定打進季後賽的機會。

九局打完是2比2平手，但馬林魚在第12局一口氣拿下6分。小熊一直有得分機會卻打不出來：壘上有人在二壘或三壘時，18次打擊只有1支安打。

例行賽只剩幾天，小熊還得再贏球，才能拿到季後賽門票。

The Chicago Cubs played 12 innings at home in Wrigley Field against the Marlins, ultimately losing 2-8 and missing their first chance to clinch a playoff spot. The game was tied 2-2 after nine innings, but the Marlins scored six runs in the 12th inning. The Cubs had scoring opportunities throughout but failed to capitalize: with runners on second or third base, they managed only 1 hit in 18 at-bats. With only a few days left in the regular season, the Cubs need to keep winning to secure their playoff ticket.

萊斯因傷退出英格蘭國家隊

來源：Daily Cannon

兵工廠線——兵工廠中場萊斯（Declan Rice）因為受傷，退出英格蘭國家隊，不參加這次國家聯賽的比賽。英格蘭這次一共有5名球員因傷退出，包括切爾西的 Cole Palmer。

萊斯說，去年12月大腿後側受傷以後，他的背和大腿一直有神經痛。兵工廠本來就在控制他的上場時間，怕他太累。

萊斯是球隊中場的核心。他的身體撐不撐得住，會影響兵工廠整個球季。

Arsenal beat — Arsenal midfielder Declan Rice has withdrawn from the England national squad due to injury and will not play in the upcoming Nations League matches. England has five players withdrawing due to injury in total, including Chelsea's Cole Palmer. Rice explains that since his hamstring injury last December, he's been dealing with persistent nerve pain in his back and leg. Arsenal has already been carefully managing his playing time, wary of overexertion. Rice is the anchor of the team's midfield, and whether his body holds up will be critical to Arsenal's entire season.
