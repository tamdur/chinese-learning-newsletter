聯準會主席警告物價 升息機率大增

來源：CNBC

美國聯準會（中央銀行）主席華許星期五在傑克森霍爾會議上演講。他說物價一年來漲了百分之三點七，最近半年更快到百分之四點一，這個速度不能接受。

華許說：「我們必須確定物價漲幅正在往目標走，不然我們還有工作要做。」市場馬上反應，九月升息的機率從三成五跳到五成五以上，十年期公債利率升到百分之四點七二。對芝加哥人來說，房貸和車貸可能又要變貴了。

Federal Reserve Chair Kevin Warsh delivered a speech Friday at the Jackson Hole conference. He said prices have risen 3.7 percent over the past year, and over the past six months have climbed even faster, at 4.1 percent — a pace he called unacceptable. "We have to be sure that inflation is heading back toward our target," Warsh said, "otherwise we still have work to do." Markets reacted immediately: the odds of a September rate rise jumped from 35 percent to above 55 percent, and the 10-year Treasury yield climbed to 4.72 percent. For people in Chicago, that could mean mortgages and car loans get more expensive again.

美國與委內瑞拉簽下百年石油協議

來源：CNBC

川普星期五宣布，美國和委內瑞拉談成一筆石油協議，要開發十七個油田，地下石油多達六百五十億桶。他說這是「世界史上最大的石油交易」。

這項協議由國務卿魯比歐、國防部長赫格塞斯，和委國代理總統羅德里格斯談成。美方會和一家私人公司合作，拿到五成五的產量，開發權長達一百年。不過委內瑞拉現在是過渡政府，這份合約能不能算數，是接下來的大問題。

President Trump announced on Friday that the United States and Venezuela have reached an oil agreement covering the development of seventeen oil fields holding as much as 65 billion barrels of crude. He called it "the biggest oil deal in world history." The agreement was negotiated by Secretary of State Rubio, Defense Secretary Hegseth, and Venezuela's acting president, Rodriguez. The American side will work with a private company, taking 55 percent of output under development rights running for a hundred years. But Venezuela is currently governed by a transitional administration, so whether the contract will actually hold is the big question ahead.

輝達喊停 AI 雲端分紅計畫

來源：Manila Times / WSJ

晶片公司輝達（Nvidia）七月想出一個新做法：先借錢給 AI 雲端公司買它的晶片，等對方賺錢再分一部分回來，相關金額大約三百六十億美元。

上週輝達突然把這個計畫喊停，推出還不到兩個月。原因不是政府查它，而是自家員工提醒：公司管太多了，連合作對象能租給哪些客戶都要指定，可能違反反壟斷（不准一家獨大）的法律。

The chipmaker Nvidia came up with a new arrangement in July: lend money to AI cloud companies so they could buy its chips, then take a share of the revenue once those customers started earning. The commitments involved came to roughly $36 billion. Last week Nvidia abruptly halted the programme, less than two months after launching it. The reason was not a government investigation — it was a warning from Nvidia's own staff: the company was reaching too far, even specifying which customers its partners were allowed to rent capacity to, which could run afoul of antitrust law (the rules against a single company dominating a market).

車牌鏡頭引爆芝加哥郊區爭議

來源：Chicago Sun-Times

Flock 公司的車牌鏡頭裝滿芝加哥郊區，拍下的幾十億筆紀錄，只要付錢的警察單位都查得到。現在一個鎮接一個鎮在吵，要不要繼續用。

麥亨利郡檢方指控，草原林村的兼職警員柯普用這套系統偷查前伴侶的行蹤，查了好幾百次。伊利諾州的調查也發現，資料分享的把關不夠嚴。艾文斯頓和橡樹園已經解約，其他小鎮還在爭。

Flock's licence-plate cameras blanket Chicago's suburbs, and the billions of records they capture can be searched by any police agency willing to pay. Now one town after another is arguing over whether to keep using them. Prosecutors in McHenry County allege that Copp, a part-time officer in Prairie Grove, used the system to secretly track a former partner's movements — hundreds of searches in all. An Illinois state review also found that the safeguards on data sharing were not strict enough. Evanston and Oak Park have already cancelled their contracts, while other towns are still fighting it out.

密大秋訓結束 四分衛進步明顯

來源：GBM Wolverine

密西根大學美式足球隊結束秋季訓練，九月五日開季對上西密西根大學。全隊最受注目的，是二年級四分衛安德伍德。

前隊友巴特看完最後一場隊內對抗賽後說，安德伍德的腳步變乾淨了，願意待在保護區裡不亂跑，該丟掉的球就丟掉，不再硬傳出去。這些都是好四分衛的樣子。另一件事是接球員希亞姆的傷勢，還要再等消息。

The University of Michigan football team has wrapped up fall camp and opens the season on September 5th against Western Michigan. The player drawing the most attention is sophomore quarterback Bryce Underwood. Former Wolverine Jake Butt, after watching the final intra-squad scrimmage, said Underwood's footwork has gotten cleaner: he is willing to stay in the pocket instead of scrambling, and he throws the ball away when he should rather than forcing it downfield. Those are the marks of a good quarterback. The other open question is receiver Moustapha Thiam's injury, on which there is still no word.

兵工廠線：耶穌未隨一隊練球

來源：Arseblog News

兵工廠線——兵工廠（Arsenal）教練阿特塔在對上阿斯頓維拉前開記者會，證實前鋒耶穌（Gabriel Jesus）沒有跟一隊一起練球。轉會窗再過三天就關了，這等於說球隊會再少一名前鋒。

好消息是隊史最貴的新球員布魯諾（Bruno Guimaraes）已經恢復練習。他大腿內側受傷，錯過開季那場比賽，星期一到維拉球場很可能上場，那會是他第一次先發英超。

The Arsenal beat — Arsenal manager Mikel Arteta, speaking at a press conference ahead of the trip to Aston Villa, confirmed that striker Gabriel Jesus has not been training with the first team. With the transfer window closing in three days, that amounts to saying the squad will be one forward lighter still. The good news is that Bruno Guimaraes, the most expensive signing in the club's history, is back in training. He injured his groin and missed the season opener, but he is likely to feature at Villa Park on Monday — which would be his first Premier League start.
