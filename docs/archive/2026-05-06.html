川普暫停荷莫茲海峽護航行動，稱與伊朗談判有進展

來源：NPR

美國總統川普宣布暫停「自由計畫」護航行動。這項行動才開始一天，伊朗就向阿聯酋發射了十五枚飛彈和四架無人機。川普說他應巴基斯坦的要求暫停，因為和伊朗的談判有「很大進展」。

目前約兩萬三千名海員被困在海峽裡，美國對伊朗港口的封鎖則繼續不變。

President Trump announced a suspension of the "Freedom Operation" escort mission. The operation had been underway for just one day when Iran fired fifteen missiles and four drones at the UAE. Trump said he suspended the mission at Pakistan's request because negotiations with Iran have made "significant progress." Approximately 23,000 sailors are currently stranded in the strait, while the U.S. blockade of Iranian ports remains in place.

國際油價飆破114美元，全球經濟受衝擊

來源：Al Jazeera

荷莫茲海峽的戰火越來越嚴重，布蘭特原油漲到每桶114.44美元，從二月以來已經漲了五成。國際能源署說這是歷史上最大的石油供應中斷，每天少了約一千萬桶以上。

世界銀行警告，能源價格今年可能漲兩成四，全球經濟成長率恐怕會跌到只剩2.7%左右。

Fighting in the Strait of Hormuz is intensifying. Brent crude has surged to $114.44 per barrel, up 50% since February. The International Energy Agency calls it the largest oil supply disruption in history, with roughly 10 million barrels per day offline. The World Bank warns that energy prices could jump 24% this year, pushing global economic growth down to around 2.7%.

Anthropic推出十個金融AI助手，搶進華爾街

來源：Bloomberg

Anthropic發布了十個專門給銀行和保險公司用的AI助手，可以自動做客戶身分審查、財報分析和帳目核對等工作。同時也推出新模型 Claude Opus 4.7，在金融測試中拿下第一名。

摩根大通、高盛、花旗等大銀行都已經在用 Claude，這次是 Anthropic 從研究實驗室走向商業市場最大的一步。

Anthropic has released ten AI assistants tailored for banks and insurance companies, capable of automating customer identity verification, financial statement analysis, and account reconciliation. The company also unveiled a new model, Claude Opus 4.7, which topped financial industry benchmarks. Major banks including JPMorgan Chase, Goldman Sachs, and Citigroup are already using Claude, marking Anthropic's largest stride yet from research laboratory into commercial markets.

AI助手任務成功率一年內從兩成跳到近八成

來源：Air Street Press

一份新的AI報告指出，AI助手處理真實工作的成功率，從一年前的20%大幅跳到現在的77.3%。在網路安全方面更厲害，能解決93%的問題。

不過，實體機器人做家事的成功率只有12%，可見數位世界的AI跟真實世界的機器人之間，還有很大的差距。

A new AI report shows that AI assistants have dramatically improved their success rate in handling real-world tasks, jumping from 20% a year ago to 77.3% today. In cybersecurity, they're even more impressive, solving 93% of problems. However, physical robots performing household chores achieve only a 12% success rate, revealing a significant gap between AI in the digital world and robots operating in the physical world.

Conforto再見全壘打！小熊主場連勝12場

來源：Chicago Sun-Times

Michael Conforto打出生涯第一支再見全壘打，把94英里的速球轟到左外野看台，小熊以5比4逆轉擊敗紅人。在這之前，鈴木誠也也打出一支455英尺的三分砲。

小熊在Wrigley Field的主場連勝來到12場，是2008年以來最長的紀錄。目前戰績23勝12敗，穩坐國聯中區第一。

Michael Conforto hit his first career walk-off home run, launching a 94 mph fastball into the left field bleachers as the Cubs rallied past the Reds 5-4. Earlier in the game, Seiya Suzuki had blasted a 455-foot three-run homer. The Cubs extended their home winning streak at Wrigley Field to 12 games, their longest since 2008. With a record of 23-12, they hold a firm grip on first place in the National League Central.

兵工廠闖進歐冠決賽！等了二十年的夢

來源：NBC Sports

兵工廠線——Bukayo Saka在第44分鐘接到 Trossard 射門被擋回的球，補射得分，兵工廠在主場1比0擊敗馬德里競技，兩回合總比數2比1晉級。

這是兵工廠二十年來第一次打進歐冠決賽，上次是2006年。決賽將在5月30日於布達佩斯舉行。英超方面，兵工廠領先五分，週日贏球就能確定拿下聯賽冠軍。

Bukayo Saka scored in the 44th minute, converting a rebound after Trossard's shot was blocked, as Arsenal defeated Atlético Madrid 1-0 at home to advance 2-1 on aggregate. This marks Arsenal's first Champions League final in twenty years—their last appearance was in 2006. The final will be held in Budapest on May 30th. In the Premier League, Arsenal leads by five points and can clinch the title with a win this Sunday.
