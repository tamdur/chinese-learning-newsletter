美伊接近框架協議，停火期限倒數中

Axios

美國和伊朗（Iran）的停火將在四月二十一日到期。巴基斯坦（Pakistan）陸軍總司令已飛往德黑蘭（Tehran），和埃及、土耳其的調停人一起推動談判。

白宮表示對達成協議「感覺不錯」。不過，伊朗堅持以色列也必須停止攻打黎巴嫩（Lebanon）的真主黨（Hezbollah），美國和以色列都拒絕了這個條件。時間所剩不多。

The US-Iran ceasefire expires on April 21st. Pakistan's army chief has flown to Tehran to push negotiations alongside mediators from Egypt and Turkey. The White House says it "feels good" about reaching a deal. However, Iran insists that Israel must also stop attacking Hezbollah in Lebanon — a condition both the US and Israel have rejected. Time is running out.

史丹佛AI報告：美中差距幾乎消失

Stanford HAI

史丹佛大學（Stanford）發表二零二六年AI指數報告。報告指出，AI模型已在博士等級的科學題目上超過人類，美國和中國的AI能力差距也縮小到只剩不到三分。

更令人擔心的是：AI透明度指數從五十八分降到四十分，AI相關事故增加到三百六十二件，移民到美國的AI研究員人數一年減少了八成。

Stanford University released its 2026 AI Index Report. The report reveals that AI models have surpassed humans on doctoral-level scientific questions, and the capability gap between the United States and China has narrowed to less than three percentage points. More concerning: the AI transparency index dropped from 58 to 40 points, AI-related incidents increased to 362 cases, and the number of AI researchers immigrating to the United States declined by 80 percent year-over-year.

司法部要求撤銷國會山莊暴動重罪判決

NPR

美國司法部向聯邦上訴法院提出申請，要求撤銷「驕傲男孩」（Proud Boys）和「誓言守護者」（Oath Keepers）領袖的煽動叛亂罪定罪。這些是二零二一年一月六日國會山莊攻擊事件中最重的罪名。

川普（Trump）上任第一天就赦免了超過一千五百名暴動者，但煽動叛亂罪的被告只被減刑，沒有被赦免。如果法院同意，這場美國史上最大規模的聯邦調查案中最嚴重的判決將全部消失。

The U.S. Department of Justice has filed a motion with the federal appeals court seeking to overturn seditious conspiracy convictions against the leaders of the Proud Boys and Oath Keepers — the most serious charges brought in connection with the January 6, 2021 attack on the Capitol. Trump issued pardons for more than 1,500 rioters on his first day in office, but those convicted of seditious conspiracy were only granted sentence reductions, not full pardons. If the court agrees, the most severe convictions in what has been the largest federal investigation in American history will be entirely erased.

芝加哥市議會再度挑戰市長，反對派掌權兩大委員會

Chicago Sun-Times

芝加哥市議會選出維加斯（Villegas）擔任土地規劃委員會主席，柯提斯（Curtis）擔任經濟發展委員會主席。兩人都是強森（Johnson）市長的公開批評者。

這兩個委員會掌握城市開發和土地使用的關鍵決定權。市議會多數派和市長的距離越來越遠，早前他們還通過了自己的替代預算案。

The Chicago City Council selected Villegas as chair of the Planning Committee and Curtis as chair of the Economic Development Committee. Both are vocal critics of Mayor Johnson. These two committees control key decisions over city development and land use. The City Council majority has grown increasingly distant from the mayor, having previously passed their own alternative budget proposal.

小熊大勝費城人，今永昇太三振十一次

Bleacher Nation

芝加哥小熊（Cubs）四月十五日以十一比二痛擊費城費城人（Phillies），一掃前一場十三比七慘敗的陰霾。

先發投手今永昇太（Imanaga）投了六局，送出十一次三振，表現強勢。荷爾納（Hoerner）打下個人生涯新高的五分打點，帶動全隊火力。

The Chicago Cubs crushed the Philadelphia Phillies 11-2 on April 15th, shaking off the gloom of a brutal 13-7 loss in their previous game. Starting pitcher Imanaga struck out eleven batters over six innings, delivering a dominant performance. Hoerner drove in a career-high five runs, leading the team's offensive barrage.

兵工廠線：槍手闖進歐冠四強，創下隊史紀錄

Arsenal.com

兵工廠（Arsenal）四月十五日在主場和體育（Sporting CP）零比零踢平，以兩回合一比零的總比數晉級歐洲冠軍聯賽四強。這是隊史第一次連續兩年打進歐冠四強。

儘管萊斯（Rice）、薩卡（Saka）等主力受傷，槍手仍靠穩固的防守過關。下一個對手是馬德里競技（Atletico Madrid），首回合在四月二十九日客場開打。

Arsenal drew 0-0 at home against Sporting CP on April 15th, advancing to the Champions League semi-finals with a 1-0 aggregate victory over two legs. This marks the first time in club history that Arsenal has reached the semi-finals in consecutive years. Despite injuries to key players like Rice and Saka, the Gunners progressed through solid defensive performances. Their next opponent is Atletico Madrid, with the first leg away on April 29th.
