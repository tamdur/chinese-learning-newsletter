川普發90美元健保退款，多數老人拿不到

來源：PBS NewsHour

美國聯邦醫療保險（Medicare，給65歲以上老人的健保）這個星期開始發一次性的90美元退款，用來減少一部分保費。

不過，全美大約7060萬人有這種保險，只有約2080萬人拿得到，還不到三分之一。

錢剛好在十一月期中選舉前幾個星期寄到。批評的人說，這是拉選票的「政治表演」；政府則說，這是幫大家減輕看病的負擔。

Medicare, the federal health insurance program for Americans 65 and older, began sending out a one-time $90 rebate this week to help offset part of their premiums. But while about 70.6 million people in the United States have this coverage, only about 20.8 million of them can actually receive the money, less than a third. The payments are arriving just a few weeks before the November midterm elections. Critics call it a "political show" meant to win votes, while the government says the goal is to ease the burden of medical costs for everyone.

芝加哥一美元賣空地，西區第一批新房完工

來源：Block Club Chicago

芝加哥市政府這個星期在西區的北朗代爾（North Lawndale），為第一批七棟新蓋好的「兩層公寓」舉辦開幕儀式。每棟上下各住一戶，是芝加哥常見的老式房子。

這個計畫叫「缺少的中間」（Missing Middle）。市政府把自己的空地用1美元賣給建商，再補一些錢，讓他們蓋兩到四戶的小樓。

這種房子介於獨棟房子和大公寓之間。過去幾十年，因為法規限制和建造成本太高，幾乎沒人蓋。接下來還有幾百戶要蓋。

Chicago's city government this week held an opening ceremony in North Lawndale on the West Side for the first seven newly built two-flats. Each building has one unit upstairs and one unit downstairs, the classic old-style house common across Chicago. The program is called "Missing Middle." The city sells its vacant lots to developers for one dollar each, then adds some money so they can build small buildings of two to four units. These homes fall between single-family houses and large apartment blocks. For decades, almost no one built them, because of zoning restrictions and high construction costs. Several hundred more units are still to come.

歐巴馬基金會員工宣布組工會

來源：Chicago Sun-Times

歐巴馬基金會和芝加哥南區新開的歐巴馬總統中心，有超過250名員工在10月7日發表公開信，宣布成立「歐巴馬員工聯合會」（Obama Workers United），並加入大型工會AFSCME。

他們說，薪水被凍結，不再調高；健保費用越來越貴；有薪假也變少了。

這件事有點尷尬。歐巴馬是民主黨的前總統，一直公開支持工會，現在輪到他自己的基金會面對員工組工會。

More than 250 employees at the Obama Foundation and the Obama Presidential Center, which recently opened on Chicago's South Side, published an open letter on October 7 announcing the formation of "Obama Workers United" and their affiliation with the large union AFSCME. They say their salaries have been frozen with no more raises, health insurance premiums keep climbing, and paid time off has been cut back. This is a bit awkward. Obama is a former Democratic president who has consistently and publicly backed unions, and now it is his own foundation's staff who are organizing one.

川普想把自家高爾夫球場變成總統度假地

來源：NPR

美國總統川普今天提議，把他在佛羅里達州擁有的一座私人高爾夫球俱樂部，變成官方的總統休息地，就像有名的「大衛營」（Camp David）。川普說，那裡本來就有很多安全設備。

但很多人擔心「利益衝突」（公事和私人好處混在一起）：政府的錢會花在總統自己的生意上。而且球場的土地是向郡政府租的，地方政府也會被拉進來。大衛營則是國家的土地，由海軍管理。

U.S. President Donald Trump today proposed turning a private golf club he owns in Florida into an official presidential retreat, much like the famous Camp David. Trump said the site already has plenty of security equipment. But many people worry about a "conflict of interest" (public duties and private benefits getting mixed together): government money would end up being spent on the president's own business. The golf course land is also leased from the county government, so local authorities would be pulled in as well. Camp David, by contrast, sits on federal land and is managed by the Navy.

威廉斯腿傷再缺陣，貝根特先發戰包裝工

來源：Bleacher Nation / NBC Chicago

芝加哥熊隊四分衛（負責傳球的指揮球員）凱勒布・威廉斯（Caleb Williams）大腿後側肌肉「二級拉傷」，球隊說要一週一週觀察，這個星期天又不能上場。

替補的泰森・貝根特（Tyson Bagent）將連續第二場先發。他上一場已經帶隊打贏紐約噴射機。

這次熊隊要到綠灣，挑戰老對手包裝工。兩隊是NFL歷史最久的一組對手。熊隊目前3勝1敗，這場客場考驗不簡單。

Chicago Bears quarterback Caleb Williams has a Grade 2 strain of his hamstring, and the team says it will be evaluating him week to week. He won't be able to play this Sunday. Backup Tyson Bagent will make his second straight start. He already led the team to a win over the New York Jets last time out. This week the Bears travel to Green Bay to take on their old rivals, the Packers. The two teams have the longest-running rivalry in NFL history. The Bears are 3-1 so far, and this road game will be no easy test.

阿特塔：不要被冠軍壓力綁住

來源：Flashscore

兵工廠線——兵工廠總教練阿特塔（Mikel Arteta）在對里茲聯的賽前記者會上說，球隊最近雖然丟了一些分數，但位置還是很好。他希望球員放輕鬆踢球，不要被爭冠軍的壓力綁住。

大家擔心的後衛廷伯（Timber）和薩利巴（Saliba），阿特塔說他們只是休息，沒有大傷。唯一確定不能上場的是16歲小將道曼（Dowman）。

比賽在本週六（10月10日）於主場酋長球場（Emirates Stadium）進行。里茲聯是本季的黑馬（意外表現很好的隊伍），目前排在前五名。

Arsenal beat: Arteta is not letting the title pressure tie his players down. Speaking at his pre-match press conference ahead of Leeds United, Mikel Arteta said the team has dropped some points recently but still sits in a very good position. He wants his players to relax and play without feeling weighed down by the race for the title.

The concerns about defenders Timber and Saliba are overstated, Arteta said; both are simply resting and have no serious injuries. The only player definitely ruled out is 16-year-old youngster Dowman. The match takes place this Saturday (October 10) at Emirates Stadium. Leeds have been this season's dark horse, performing well beyond expectations, and currently sit in the top five.
