諾貝爾和平獎今天公布得主

來源：Norwegian Nobel Committee

挪威諾貝爾委員會今天在奧斯陸宣布2026年和平獎得主，時間是芝加哥清晨四點。今年一共有287個人和團體被提名。

外界看好的人選，包括烏干達的氣候運動人士 Vanessa Nakate，還有蘇丹的「緊急應變室」。這是一群志工，在內戰中送食物和藥給老百姓。

美國總統川普也公開說他想得這個獎。和平獎由挪威國會選出的委員會決定，所以每年都特別受人注意。

The Norwegian Nobel Committee announced the 2026 Nobel Peace Prize laureate today in Oslo. In Chicago, the announcement came at 4:00 a.m. A total of 287 individuals and organizations were nominated this year. Among the names widely seen as likely contenders were Vanessa Nakate, a climate activist from Uganda, and Sudan's "Emergency Response Room," a group of volunteers who delivered food and medicine to ordinary people during the civil war. U.S. President Donald Trump has also said publicly that he would like to win the prize. The Peace Prize is chosen by a committee selected by the Norwegian Parliament, which is why it draws special attention every year.

移民局翻查幾千萬筆選民資料

來源：CNN

美國 CNN 報導，國土安全部和移民局（ICE）查看了好幾千萬筆選民資料，想找出去投票的非公民。其中一批資料就有4840萬筆。

他們也調查幫人登記投票的民間團體，光是賓州就查了82個。這些內部文件是監督團體 Democracy Forward 公開的。

這個團體的負責人說，總統好像想「自己挑選民」，而不是讓選民選出領導人。政府則說，這是為了防止非公民投票。現在離期中選舉已經不到四個禮拜。

According to CNN, the Department of Homeland Security and Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) have combed through tens of millions of voter records in search of non-citizens who cast ballots. One batch of the data alone contained 48.4 million records. They have also investigated civic groups that help people register to vote; in Pennsylvania alone, they looked into 82 of them. The internal documents were made public by Democracy Forward, a watchdog group. The group's head said the president seems to want to "pick his own voters" rather than letting voters choose their leaders. The government says the effort is meant to prevent non-citizens from voting. The midterm elections are now less than four weeks away.

化學獎頒給「左右手分子」研究

來源：NobelPrize.org

今年的諾貝爾化學獎，頒給法國的 Henri Kagan 和日本的 Kenso Soai。Kagan 今年已經95歲。

很多分子就像我們的左手和右手，長得一樣，方向卻相反。做藥的時候，一邊可能治病，另一邊卻可能有害。兩人找到方法，讓化學反應多做出想要的那一邊。

委員會說，這也幫忙解開一個一百多年的謎：為什麼生物身上的這類分子，幾乎都只有其中一邊。

This year's Nobel Prize in Chemistry goes to Henri Kagan of France and Kenso Soai of Japan. Kagan is already 95 years old this year.

Many molecules are like our left and right hands: they look the same, but they face in opposite directions. When making medicines, one version may treat a disease while the other may be harmful. The two chemists found ways to make chemical reactions produce more of the version that is wanted. The committee said this also helps solve a mystery more than a century old: why the molecules of this kind found in living things almost always come in just one of the two forms.

公牛新秀今晚對決老對手

來源：Chicago Sun-Times

芝加哥公牛今晚七點在聯合中心，和曼菲斯灰熊打季前賽。大家最想看的，是兩個新秀的對決。

公牛在選秀用第4順位選了 Caleb Wilson，灰熊用第3順位選了 Cameron Boozer。兩人從大學就是對手。今年夏天，Wilson 第一場夏季聯賽就拿下35分，創下新紀錄。

公牛正在重建球隊，Wilson 能進步多快，就是這一季最大的看點。

The Chicago Bulls host the Memphis Grizzlies tonight at 7 p.m. at the United Center in a preseason game. The matchup everyone is most eager to see is between two rookies. The Bulls used the fourth overall pick in the draft on Caleb Wilson, while the Grizzlies took Cameron Boozer third. The two have been rivals since their college days. This summer, in his first Summer League game, Wilson scored 35 points, setting a new record. The Bulls are rebuilding, and how quickly Wilson can improve is the biggest thing to watch this season.

兵工廠迎戰傷兵多的里茲

來源：Yorkshire Evening Post

兵工廠線——英超領先4分的兵工廠，星期六在主場酋長球場迎戰里茲聯，芝加哥時間早上六點半開打。

里茲的傷兵（受傷的球員）不少。總教練 Farke 說，邊鋒 Dan James 背部受傷，不能上場；後衛 Joe Rodon 也還在養傷。主力 Harry Wilson 能不能上，還不確定。

兵工廠這邊好多了：White 等三名後衛都回來練球了。里茲本季踢得讓人驚喜，但少了這些人，想在倫敦拿分並不容易。

Arsenal Beat: Arsenal, four points clear at the top of the Premier League, host Leeds United at Emirates Stadium on Saturday, with kickoff at 6:30 a.m. Chicago time. Leeds have a lot of players on the injury list. Head coach Farke says winger Dan James is out with a back injury, and defender Joe Rodon is still recovering. Star player Harry Wilson's availability is still uncertain. Arsenal are in much better shape: White and two other defenders are back training with the squad. Leeds have been a pleasant surprise this season, but without these players, taking points in London will not be easy.
