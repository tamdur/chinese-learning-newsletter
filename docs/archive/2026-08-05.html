密西根初選結果出爐，艾爾賽義德勝出

來源：NBC News

美國密西根州在八月四日舉行初選（政黨先選出自己的代表）。民主黨這邊，醫生出身的艾爾賽義德以很小的差距打敗國會議員史蒂文斯，拿到參議員提名。

艾爾賽義德的想法比較偏左，他能贏，代表黨內選民想要改變。十一月他將對上共和黨的羅傑斯。州長方面，則是民主黨的班森對上共和黨的詹姆斯。

Michigan held its primary elections on August 4th, in which each party first chooses its own candidates. On the Democratic side, Abdul El-Sayed, a physician, narrowly defeated Representative Haley Stevens to take the Senate nomination. El-Sayed's politics lean further to the left, and the win suggests that Democratic primary voters want change. In November, El-Sayed will face Republican Mike Rogers. In the governor's race, Democrat Jocelyn Benson will face Republican John James.

以色列與黎巴嫩羅馬談判進入第七輪

來源：Euronews

以色列和黎巴嫩的代表在八月四日到義大利羅馬坐下來談，這已經是第七輪。談判由美國居中安排，開了三天。

重點是擴大「試辦區」：以色列軍隊分階段從黎巴嫩南部撤走，換來真主黨（黎巴嫩的武裝組織）在那裡交出武器，第一批是三個村子。不過真主黨公開反對，以色列也說不肯再擴大，路還很難走。

Israeli and Lebanese representatives sat down for talks in Rome, Italy on August 4th — already the seventh such round. The negotiations, brokered by the United States, ran for three days. The focus is expanding the "pilot zones": Israeli forces would withdraw from southern Lebanon in stages, in exchange for Hezbollah — the Lebanese armed group — handing over its weapons there, starting with three villages. Hezbollah has publicly opposed the plan, however, and Israel says it will not agree to expand the zones any further, so the road ahead remains hard.

前大法官加入 Anthropic 掌管全球事務

來源：Anthropic / CNBC

美國人工智慧公司 Anthropic 在八月四日宣布，請到古耶擔任第一位「全球事務長」。他當過加州最高法院的大法官，也做過卡內基國際和平基金會的會長。

一家寫程式的公司，卻找外交和法律界的人來當主管，可見人工智慧已經變成各國政府都要管的大事。古耶會直接向公司總裁艾莫黛報告。

On August 4th, the American artificial intelligence company Anthropic announced that it had brought in Mariano-Florentino Cuéllar as its first Chief Global Affairs Officer. Cuéllar previously served as a justice of the California Supreme Court and as president of the Carnegie Endowment for International Peace. That a company which writes software would recruit an executive from the worlds of diplomacy and law shows just how far artificial intelligence has become an issue that governments everywhere now have to manage. Cuéllar will report directly to company president Daniela Amodei.

芝加哥七月凶殺案創五十多年新低

來源：WTTW

芝加哥今年七月有三十七起凶殺案（殺人案件），比去年七月少了百分之十八，是超過五十年來最少的七月。

七月通常是芝加哥一年中最危險的月份，過去十年平均超過七十一起，六年前的七月更是破了一百起。不過警察局長人選還沒定，市長說還要再花幾個月才會決定。

Chicago recorded 37 homicides this July, down 18% from July last year and the fewest of any July in more than fifty years. July is normally the most dangerous month of the year in Chicago, averaging more than 71 over the past decade, and six years ago the July total passed 100. The city has yet to settle on a new police superintendent, however; the mayor says the decision will take several more months.

熊隊布萊恩膝蓋受傷，預計休八到十週

來源：ESPN

芝加哥熊隊的防守球員柯比．布萊恩，週一練習時膝蓋受傷，被車子載離球場。檢查後發現骨頭有小裂痕和瘀傷，要休息八到十週，還好不用開刀。

球隊今年春天用三年四千萬美元把他簽下來，本來要和新人提尼曼一起守最後一線。他一倒下，這個位置只剩菜鳥撐著。熊隊九月十三日開季。

Chicago Bears defensive back Coby Bryant injured his knee at Monday's practice and was carted off the field. Tests found a small fracture and bruising in the bone, and he will need eight to ten weeks of rest — though fortunately no surgery. The team signed him this spring to a three-year, $40 million deal, and he was meant to anchor the last line of the defense alongside rookie Thieneman. With Bryant down, only a rookie is left to hold the position. The Bears open their season on September 13th.

兵工廠中場諾加德 七百萬英鎊轉投艾佛頓

來源：Sky Sports

兵工廠線——丹麥中場球員諾加德快要離開兵工廠了。八月四日，他到艾佛頓做身體檢查，轉會費大約七百萬英鎊。

三十二歲的諾加德去年夏天才從布蘭特福隊加入，身價一千萬英鎊。可是這一年他在英超（英國最高級的足球聯賽）只上場七次，多半坐在板凳上，最後還是拿到了冠軍獎牌。

艾佛頓找他來，是要接替離隊的蓋伊，補上中場的空缺。

The Arsenal beat — Danish midfielder Christian Nørgaard is set to leave Arsenal. On August 4th he underwent a medical at Everton, with a transfer fee of roughly £7 million. Nørgaard, 32, arrived from Brentford only last summer in a £10 million move. Over the past year, though, he appeared in the Premier League — England's top football division — just seven times, mostly from the bench, and still finished with a title medal. Everton have brought him in to replace the departed Idrissa Gana Gueye and fill the gap in midfield.
