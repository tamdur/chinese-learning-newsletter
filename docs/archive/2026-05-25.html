教宗良十四世發表首份AI通諭，與Anthropic共同發布

來源：Vatican News

教宗良十四世今天發表他的第一份通諭（教宗寫給全世界的正式信），名為《偉大的人性》，主題是在AI時代保護人的尊嚴。

特別的是，Anthropic公司共同創辦人Christopher Olah也出席了梵蒂岡的發布會。這份通諭在五月十五日簽署，正好是一百三十五年前良十三世發表社會教義的同一天。

Pope Leo XIV released his first encyclical today — a formal letter from the pontiff to the world — titled "The Greatness of Humanity," which addresses the protection of human dignity in the age of AI. Notably, Christopher Olah, co-founder of Anthropic, attended the Vatican launch event. The encyclical was signed on May 15th, precisely 135 years after Pope Leo XIII issued the founding document of Catholic social teaching on the same date.

陪審團不到兩小時駁回馬斯克告OpenAI案

來源：NPR

加州一個九人陪審團只花不到兩小時就做出決定：全部駁回馬斯克對OpenAI的告訴。陪審團認為馬斯克提告的時間太晚，超過了法律規定的三年期限。

馬斯克在2015年共同創辦OpenAI，他認為公司背棄了原本「為人類服務」的理想，轉而追求利潤。如果他勝訴，OpenAI可能要賠一千五百億美元。馬斯克表示會上訴。

A nine-person jury in California took less than two hours to reach a decision: they dismissed all of Elon Musk's claims against OpenAI. The jury found that Musk filed his lawsuit too late, exceeding the three-year statute of limitations. Musk co-founded OpenAI in 2015 and argued the company abandoned its original mission to serve humanity in pursuit of profit. Had Musk won, OpenAI could have faced a $150 billion judgment. Musk said he plans to appeal.

Google I/O大會發布Gemini 3.5和AI購物助手

來源：Google Blog

Google在年度開發者大會I/O上發布了新AI模型Gemini 3.5 Flash，速度是其他頂級模型的四倍，而且能力也更強。

Google還推出了「萬用購物車」Universal Cart，這是一個AI購物助手，可以自動幫你找到最好的價格和打折資訊。另外還有Android XR智慧眼鏡，顯示Google正把AI放進每一個產品裡。

Google unveiled its new AI model Gemini 3.5 Flash at the annual I/O developer conference, boasting processing speeds four times faster than competing top-tier models while delivering enhanced capabilities. The company also introduced "Universal Cart," an AI shopping assistant that automatically finds the best prices and discount information for you. The announcements also included Android XR smart glasses, demonstrating Google's push to embed AI across its entire product portfolio.

美國與伊朗接近達成停火協議，荷莫茲海峽將重新開放

來源：NPR

美國和伊朗正在談一份停火協議，川普總統說內容「大致已經談好」。協議包括六十天停火、重新開放荷莫茲海峽（全球最重要的石油運輸通道），以及伊朗同意接受聯合國核武檢查。

如果海峽順利開放，全球油價可能下降，對大家的生活是好消息。

The United States and Iran are negotiating a ceasefire agreement, with President Trump saying the terms are "largely worked out." The deal includes a sixty-day ceasefire, reopening the Strait of Hormuz (the world's most critical oil shipping channel), and Iran's agreement to accept UN nuclear inspections. If the strait opens smoothly, global oil prices could fall, which would be welcome news for everyone's daily life.

小熊隊十二場輸十場，緊急換上新秀

來源：MLB.com

小熊隊最近打得很差，十二場比賽輸了十場。總教練Counsell決定調整陣容，把Shaw放上傷兵名單（背部不舒服），從小聯盟叫上來兩位新秀。

其中Ramirez在小聯盟打擊率高達三成一二，Alcantara則已經打了十五支全壘打。球隊希望年輕的力量能幫助止住連敗。

The Cubs have been playing poorly lately, losing 10 out of their last 12 games. Manager Counsell has decided to shake up the roster, placing Shaw on the injured list due to back issues, and calling up two prospects from the minor leagues. Ramirez has been hitting .312 in the minors, while Alcantara has already hit 15 home runs. The team hopes that fresh young talent can help stop the losing streak.

兵工廠線——等了二十二年，這一刻終於來了。隊長Odegaard在水晶宮的主場高舉英超冠軍獎盃，兵工廠以二比一贏得本季最後一場比賽。

The Arsenal beat — after twenty-two years of waiting, the moment finally arrived.
