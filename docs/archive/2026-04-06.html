川普對伊朗的最後期限到了：石油飆漲，戰爭陰影加深

來源：CNBC

今天是四月六日，川普給伊朗的最後期限正式到期。他要求伊朗重新開放荷莫茲海峽（全球五分之一的石油經過那裡），否則將攻擊伊朗的能源設施。伊朗拒絕了，說這是「無力的威脅」。

戰爭已經打了六個星期，超過兩千人死亡。石油價格飆過一百美元，是二〇二二年以來最高。美國國會也面臨壓力，因為《戰爭權力法》的六十天期限快到了，總統需要國會同意才能繼續用兵。

Trump's deadline for Iran has arrived: oil soars, shadows of war deepen

Today, April 6th, marks the formal expiration of Trump's ultimatum to Iran. He demanded that Iran reopen the Strait of Hormuz (through which one-fifth of the world's oil passes), or face U.S. strikes on Iran's energy infrastructure. Iran refused, calling it a "powerless threat."

War has raged for six weeks, with over two thousand dead. Oil prices have surged past one hundred dollars, the highest since 2022. Congress also faces mounting pressure, as the sixty-day limit under the War Powers Act approaches, requiring presidential authorization from Congress to continue military operations.

DeepSeek V4 發布：一兆參數，免費開放

來源：NxCode

中國的 AI 公司深度求索（DeepSeek）發布了 V4 模型。這個模型有一兆個參數，是目前最大的開放模型。它用了一種叫「混合專家」的技術，每次只用三百七十億個參數來回答問題，所以跑起來很快。

最讓人吃驚的是訓練費用：只花了大約五百二十萬美元，比美國的類似模型便宜很多。任何人都可以免費下載使用，這對 AI 產業的競爭格局影響很大。

DeepSeek V4 Released: One Trillion Parameters, Free and Open

Chinese AI company DeepSeek has released its V4 model. The model contains one trillion parameters, making it the largest open-source model to date. It uses a technique called "mixture of experts," deploying only 37 billion parameters per query, which makes it run extremely fast.

Most striking is the training cost: just approximately $5.2 million, far cheaper than comparable American models. Anyone can download and use it for free, which has major implications for competitive dynamics in the AI industry.

川普限制郵寄投票，二十三州聯合提告

來源：White House / Capitol News Illinois

川普簽了一項行政命令，要建立全國選民名冊，並限制郵寄投票。他說這是為了防止選舉作弊，但批評者認為這會讓很多合法選民無法投票。

包括伊利諾州在內的二十三個州馬上聯合提告，說總統沒有權力管各州怎麼辦選舉。伊利諾州檢察長羅爾（Raoul）說，這是「重建時期以來最嚴重的聯邦干預」。今年十一月的期中選舉將是第一場考驗。

Trump Restricts Mail-in Voting; Twenty-Three States File Joint Lawsuit

Trump signed an executive order to establish a national voter registry and restrict mail-in voting. He said this is to prevent election fraud, but critics argue it will prevent many legitimate voters from casting ballots.

Twenty-three states, including Illinois, immediately filed a joint lawsuit, saying the president lacks the power to control how states conduct elections. Illinois Attorney General Raoul said this is "the most serious federal interference since Reconstruction." November's midterm election will be the first test.

全球央行暫停降息，利率回不到疫情前

來源：KPMG / IMF

去年很多國家的中央銀行開始降低利率，但最新資料顯示，百分之七十的央行已經停下來了。利率比新冠疫情前高出不少，短期內很難再降。

美國聯準會今年可能只會降息三次，六月才開始。荷莫茲海峽危機推高了油價，讓通膨（物價上漲）更難控制。國際貨幣基金（IMF）預測今年全球經濟成長百分之三點四，穩定但不算好。

Global Central Banks Pause Rate Cuts, Interest Rates Unlikely to Return to Pre-Pandemic Levels

Many central banks around the world began lowering interest rates last year, but the latest data shows that 70 percent of them have now stopped. Interest rates remain significantly higher than before the COVID-19 pandemic, and it will be difficult to lower them further in the near term.

The U.S. Federal Reserve is likely to cut rates only three times this year, with cuts beginning in June. The Hormuz Strait crisis has driven up oil prices, making inflation (price increases) harder to control. The International Monetary Fund (IMF) forecasts global economic growth of 3.4 percent this year—stable but not particularly strong.

Michigan Battles for the Championship Tonight! Facing UConn

Michigan will face the University of Connecticut (UConn) in Indianapolis tonight, competing for the NCAA men's basketball national championship. This is Michigan's first appearance in a championship game since 1989.

In the semifinals last Friday, Michigan defeated the University of Arizona 91-71, with center Aday Mara scoring 26 points. Michigan has now scored over 90 points in five consecutive games, which is unprecedented. A victory tonight would be the program's second national championship.
