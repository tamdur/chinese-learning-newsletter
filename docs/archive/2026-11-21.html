國際法院判定緬甸犯下種族滅絕罪

來源：Human Rights Watch / AP

聯合國的國際法院在11月19日判定，緬甸軍隊對羅興亞人犯下「種族滅絕」（想要消滅整個民族）的罪行。這是法院歷史上第一次判定一個國家犯下這種罪。

這個案子是非洲小國甘比亞在2019年提出的。2016到2017年，緬甸軍隊的行動害死大約一萬人，還有幾十萬人逃到孟加拉。

法官以9比6通過判決，而且不能上訴。專家說，這次的標準會影響其他種族滅絕的案子。

The International Court of Justice, the United Nations' top court, ruled on November 19 that Myanmar's military committed "genocide" (the intent to destroy an entire people) against the Rohingya. It is the first time in the court's history that it has found a country guilty of this crime. The case was brought in 2019 by Gambia, a small African nation. Between 2016 and 2017, Myanmar's military operations killed roughly 10,000 people, and several hundred thousand more fled to Bangladesh. The judges passed the ruling 9 to 6, and it cannot be appealed. Experts say the standard set in this case will affect other genocide cases.

輝達董事會：不讓黃仁勳回來當執行長

來源：Cointelegraph

晶片大公司輝達的董事會說，他們「不打算」讓創辦人黃仁勳回來當執行長（公司最高的老闆）。黃仁勳在11月12日因為個人行為的問題辭職。

美國總統川普曾經公開支持他回來，但董事會沒有改變想法。現在由克雷斯（Colette Kress）暫時帶領公司，同時尋找新的執行長。

就算沒有黃仁勳，輝達的生意還是很好：上一季賣了1158億美元，是去年同期的兩倍，創下新紀錄。

Nvidia's board says it "does not intend" to bring founder Jensen Huang back as chief executive, the company's top executive. Huang resigned on November 12 over issues related to personal conduct. U.S. President Donald Trump had publicly backed his return, but the board has not changed its mind. For now, Colette Kress is leading the company on an interim basis while the search for a new CEO continues. Even without Huang, Nvidia's business is booming: last quarter the chipmaker sold $115.8 billion worth of products, double the same period a year earlier, a new record.

密西根今天主場迎戰全勝的UCLA

來源：ABC7 Chicago

今天下午，密西根大學美式足球隊在主場迎戰排名第四的UCLA。UCLA今年10場比賽全部贏了，密西根只有5勝5敗。

這場比賽對密西根很重要。只要再贏一場，就能拿到打「盃賽」（球季結束後的特別比賽）的資格。

比賽在芝加哥時間下午2點半開打，CBS會轉播。大家都看好UCLA，但如果密西根能在家打敗一支全勝的強隊，這個球季就有救了。

This afternoon, the University of Michigan football team hosts No. 4 UCLA at home. UCLA has won all 10 of its games this year, while Michigan is only 5-5. This game matters a lot for Michigan. One more win would make the Wolverines eligible for a "bowl game," a special contest played after the regular season ends. Kickoff is at 2:30 p.m. Chicago time, and CBS is broadcasting the game. Most people expect UCLA to win, but if Michigan can beat an unbeaten powerhouse at home, this season can still be saved.

兵工廠今天作客紐卡索

來源：Sport Mole

兵工廠線——國家隊比賽週結束了，聯賽第一名的兵工廠今天要去紐卡索的主場「聖詹姆斯公園」比賽，芝加哥時間上午11點半開打。

兵工廠上一場在家1比0贏了赫爾城，進球的是厄德高（Odegaard）。不過球隊還少了兩名主力中後衛，最會進球的薩卡（Saka）也不能上場。

紐卡索的球迷非常熱情，客隊很難在這裡贏球。而且三天後，兵工廠還要對上德國的多特蒙德。

The Arsenal beat: With the international break over, league leaders Arsenal head to St James' Park to play Newcastle today. Kickoff is 11:30 a.m. Chicago time. Arsenal's last game was a 1-0 home win over Hull City, with the goal scored by Ødegaard. The team is still missing two starting centre-backs, and Saka, the team's top scorer, is not available. Newcastle's fans are famously loud, and visitors rarely win there. Three days from now, Arsenal also face Borussia Dortmund in Germany.
