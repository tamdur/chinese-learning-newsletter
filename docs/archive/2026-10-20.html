芝加哥城市學院清晨談成合約，罷工喊停

來源：WTTW News / Chicago Sun-Times

芝加哥城市學院（City Colleges of Chicago）的老師和職員，原本計畫19日星期一開始罷工。結果在當天清晨大約3點45分，工會和學校談成一份新的四年合約，課照常上。

這個工會有大約1500名成員，96%投票支持罷工。新合約每年加薪，要跟上物價上漲，也保住健康保險。特別的是，合約加入了AI保護，雙方還要一起成立AI委員會，討論學校怎麼使用AI。

不過，合約還要先讓工會成員投票，再交給學校董事會通過。

Teachers and staff at City Colleges of Chicago had planned to go on strike starting Monday, the 19th. But around 3:45 a.m. that morning, the union and the college reached a new four-year contract, and classes will go on as scheduled. The union has about 1,500 members, and 96% of them voted to authorize a strike. The new contract provides annual raises to keep up with rising prices and keeps health insurance in place. Notably, the contract adds protections around AI, and both sides will set up a joint AI committee to discuss how the college uses AI. The contract still has to be approved by union members in a vote first, and then passed by the college's board of trustees.

司法部緊急求法官：加州兩萬張選票先別算

來源：San Francisco Chronicle / NPR

美國司法部19日星期一向法院提出緊急要求，希望法官馬上下令，讓加州先把大約兩萬張郵寄選票放在一邊，不要計算。司法部說，這些選民「可能」不是美國公民。

加州州務卿（管選舉的州政府官員）韋伯（Shirley Weber）說，聯邦政府沒有拿出證據。她指出，內華達州之前也被點名將近一萬六千人，查完發現全都是公民。

到目前為止，法官還沒有做出決定，而期中選舉11月3日就要舉行。

On Monday, October 19, the U.S. Justice Department made an urgent request to the courts, asking a judge to order California to set aside about 20,000 mail-in ballots and not count them for now. The Justice Department said these voters "may" not be U.S. citizens. Shirley Weber, California's secretary of state (the state official in charge of elections), said the federal government has not produced any evidence. She pointed out that Nevada was also named earlier, with nearly 16,000 people flagged, but when the names were checked, all of them turned out to be citizens. So far, the judge has not made a decision, and the midterm election is set for November 3.

考文：算移民的帳，別忘了「交易的好處」

來源：Marginal Revolution

很多人算移民的帳，只看他們繳了多少稅、用了多少公共服務。經濟學家考文（Tyler Cowen）20日在部落格說，這樣算漏掉了很重要的一塊：大家一起工作、買賣東西帶來的好處。

他介紹一篇新研究，研究看了17個富有國家，發現新移民來了以後，有14個國家的本地人過得更好。

考文認為，原因是移民讓市場變大了，買的人和做的人都變多，大家都能分到好處。

Many people tally up the case for immigration by looking only at how much tax immigrants pay and how many public services they use. Economist Tyler Cowen, writing on his blog on the 20th, argues that this accounting leaves out a major piece: the benefits that come from people working together and buying and selling things. He points to a new study of 17 wealthy countries, which found that in 14 of them, native-born residents were better off after new immigrants arrived. Cowen's explanation is that immigration makes the market bigger, with more buyers and more workers, so everyone gets a share of the gains.

考文吃遍巴西：最愛米納斯的豆子

來源：Marginal Revolution / Works in Progress

經濟學家考文為雜誌Works in Progress寫了一篇巴西美食文章。他說，巴西每個地方都有自己的味道。

在米納斯吉拉斯州（Minas Gerais），可以吃到豬肉配豆子。他說這裡的豆子是他吃過最好吃的，連墨西哥都比不上。北部的貝倫（Belém）有亞馬遜河的魚和樹薯（一種長在土裡的根）。他吃過最好的巴西烤肉店，在南部庫里提巴（Curitiba）的郊外。

考文之前也寫過，巴西是全世界賣出最多糧食的國家之一。

Economist Tyler Cowen wrote a piece about Brazilian food for the magazine Works in Progress. He says that every part of Brazil has its own flavor. In Minas Gerais, you can eat pork with beans. He says the beans there are the best he has ever had, and even Mexico cannot compare. In Belém in the north, there is fish from the Amazon River and cassava, a root that grows underground. The best churrasco (Brazilian barbecue) restaurant he has eaten at is on the outskirts of Curitiba in the south. Cowen has also written before that Brazil is one of the countries that sells the most food in the world.

公牛明晚開季，新教練首戰暴龍

來源：Bleacher Nation / Fox Sports

芝加哥公牛隊熱身賽打出3勝2敗，最後一場在16日以133比116贏了灰狼隊。21日星期三，公牛要到多倫多挑戰暴龍隊，芝加哥時間晚上6點半開打。

這是新教練斯普利特（Tiago Splitter）的第一場正式比賽。新秀威爾森（Caleb Wilson）熱身賽平均每場拿15.8分，是最大的看點。

上一季公牛只拿到31勝51敗，這一季還在重建。對球迷來說，年輕球員有沒有進步，可能比輸贏更重要。

The Chicago Bulls finished their preseason at 3-2, and in the last game, on the 16th, they beat the Minnesota Timberwolves 133-116. On Wednesday the 21st, the Bulls travel to Toronto to face the Raptors, with tip-off at 6:30 p.m. Chicago time. It will be the first official game for new head coach Tiago Splitter. Rookie Caleb Wilson, who averaged 15.8 points a game in the preseason, is the main thing to watch. Last season the Bulls went just 31-51, and they are still rebuilding this year. For fans, whether the young players are getting better may matter more than whether the team wins or loses.

兵工廠明天作客慕尼黑，挑戰拜仁

來源：Squawka / Goal / FC Bayern

兵工廠線——兵工廠（Arsenal）21日星期三要到德國慕尼黑，在歐洲冠軍聯賽對上拜仁慕尼黑（Bayern Munich），芝加哥時間下午2點開踢。

兵工廠是這次歐冠裡唯一還沒有丟過球的球隊。可是後衛薩利巴（William Saliba）背部受傷，還不能上場。拜仁也少了兩位主力：奧利塞（Michael Olise）被禁賽，穆西亞拉（Jamal Musiala）也還沒準備好。

不過，拜仁最近兩場主場比賽一共踢進11球。前鋒凱恩（Harry Kane）是兵工廠最要小心的人。

The Arsenal beat: Arsenal travel to Munich on Wednesday the 21st to face Bayern Munich in the Champions League, with kickoff at 2 p.m. Chicago time. Arsenal are the only team left in this Champions League that has not yet conceded a goal. However, defender William Saliba is still unavailable with a back injury. Bayern are also missing two key players: Michael Olise is suspended, and Jamal Musiala is not yet ready. Still, Bayern have scored 11 goals across their last two home matches combined. Striker Harry Kane is the player Arsenal need to watch most closely.
