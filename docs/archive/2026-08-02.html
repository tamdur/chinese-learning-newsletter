歐盟與加州同日實施 AI 標示新規

來源：European Commission

八月二日起，歐盟的人工智慧法正式上路。用 AI 做出來的文字、圖片、聲音和影片都要標示清楚，也要讓使用者知道自己正在跟機器說話。違規的公司最高罰一千五百萬歐元。

美國加州本來一月要實施的透明法，特地延到同一天。大公司必須在 AI 內容裡放看得見和看不見的記號，還要免費提供檢查工具。這是全球第一次有兩大地區同時開燈，但記號很容易被拿掉，管不管得住還要看。

Starting August 2nd, the EU's Artificial Intelligence Act officially takes effect. All AI-generated text, images, audio, and video must be clearly labeled, and users must be told when they are interacting with a machine. Companies in violation face fines of up to 15 million euros. California's own transparency law, originally scheduled for January, was deliberately pushed back to the same date. Large companies must embed both visible and invisible markers in AI-generated content and must provide detection tools free of charge. This is the first time two major jurisdictions have switched on such rules simultaneously — but the markers are easy to strip out, so whether the rules can actually be enforced remains an open question.

密西根明尼蘇達水廠遭駭 FBI 查是否與伊朗有關

來源：CBS News / AP

八月一日，密西根州九座水廠、明尼蘇達州三十多座水廠傳出電腦系統被入侵，聯邦調查局已經介入。官員說，各地供水目前都很安全。

調查人員懷疑是伊朗的駭客（專門入侵電腦的人），但還沒有確定。三年前，與伊朗軍方有關的人就曾用同樣手法，闖進沒改過密碼的水廠控制器。全美上千座小水廠都沒有專職的資安人員。

On August 1st, nine water utilities in Michigan and more than thirty in Minnesota reported that their computer systems had been broken into, and the FBI has opened an investigation. Officials say water supplies everywhere remain safe. Investigators suspect Iranian hackers, though nothing has been confirmed. Three years ago, operatives tied to Iran's military used the same method to break into water-plant controllers whose default passwords had never been changed. Thousands of small water utilities across the United States have no dedicated cybersecurity staff at all.

聯邦合約到期 兩萬六千名移民兒童失去律師

來源：NPR

為沒有大人陪同的移民兒童請律師，這筆聯邦經費在七月三十一日星期五到期。大約兩萬六千個孩子從這週起，可能要自己一個人站在移民法官面前。

差別非常大：有律師的孩子大約一半能留在美國，沒有律師的成功率不到百分之一。近一百個法律團體正在找捐款接手，同時打官司要求恢復經費。有些孩子小到還在學走路。

The federal funding that paid for lawyers for immigrant children arriving without an adult expired on Friday, July 31st. From this week, roughly 26,000 children may have to stand before an immigration judge entirely on their own. The difference is stark: about half of children with a lawyer are allowed to stay in the United States, while for those without one the success rate is under 1 percent. Nearly a hundred legal organizations are scrambling for donations to take over the work while suing to have the funding restored. Some of the children are young enough that they are still learning to walk.

交通稅八月上路 芝加哥消費稅全美最高

來源：Daily Herald

八月一日起，庫克郡等六個郡的消費稅調高零點二五個百分點。芝加哥市內合計來到百分之十點五，超過西雅圖，成為全美大城市裡最高的。

這筆錢交給新成立的北伊利諾交通局，今年可收一億九千九百萬美元，明年超過五億，用在 CTA、Metra 火車和 Pace 巴士上。說好的條件是：大家多付一點稅，換來不減班和更好的服務。

Starting August 1st, sales tax rose by 0.25 percentage points across Cook County and five neighboring counties. Within Chicago the combined rate now reaches 10.5 percent, passing Seattle to become the highest of any major American city. The money goes to the newly created Northern Illinois Transit Authority, which will collect $199 million this year and more than $500 million next year, to be spent on the CTA, Metra trains, and Pace buses. The bargain on offer: everyone pays a little more tax, and in return there are no service cuts and better service.

熊隊左護鋒之爭開打 阿梅加吉搶下先發練習

來源：Bleacher Nation

八月一日的芝加哥熊隊訓練營裡，第三年球員阿梅加吉在十一對十一的對抗中，拿下大部分先發左護鋒的機會，原本坐穩位置的瓊斯反而被排到第二、三隊。

這個位置要保護四分衛威廉斯看不到的那一側，球隊的未來全押在他身上。威廉斯第二年在教練強森手下，說自己看懂防守的速度比新人年快多了。星期一第一次穿護具練習，才是真正的考試。

At Chicago Bears training camp on August 1st, third-year player Kiran Amegadjie took most of the first-team reps at left tackle in eleven-on-eleven drills, while Braxton Jones — who had been considered settled in the job — was pushed down to the second and third teams. The position protects the side quarterback Caleb Williams cannot see, and the franchise has staked its future on him. Williams, in his second year under coordinator Ben Johnson, says he is reading defenses far faster than he did as a rookie. Monday's first practice in pads will be the real test.

兵工廠八千萬英鎊談妥紐卡索隊長吉馬良斯

來源：Yahoo Sports

兵工廠線——球隊與紐卡索談成八千萬英鎊的價碼：七千萬先付，另外一千萬看表現再給。巴西中場吉馬良斯星期一將到倫敦體檢，準備簽下五年合約。

這是今年夏天最大的一筆。教練阿爾特塔七月三十日才公開說補強不夠，答應會有「實際動作」。紐卡索七月中曾回絕五千五百萬和六千五百萬英鎊的報價，那邊也有人說合約還沒完全談定。

The Arsenal beat — the club has agreed an £80 million package with Newcastle: £70 million up front, with another £10 million tied to performance. Brazilian midfielder Bruno Guimarães travels to London on Monday for a medical, ready to sign a five-year contract. It is the biggest deal of the summer.

Manager Mikel Arteta said publicly only on July 30th that the squad had not been strengthened enough, promising there would be "concrete moves." Newcastle had turned down bids of £55 million and £65 million in mid-July, and some on their side say the deal is not yet fully settled.
