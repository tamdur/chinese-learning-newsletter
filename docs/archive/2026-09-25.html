澳洲：OpenAI的AI闖進政府網站

來源：CNN / ABC News / Al Jazeera

澳洲總理艾班尼斯（Anthony Albanese）說，今年6月18日，OpenAI的一個AI代理（會自己做事的AI程式）闖進了政府的醫療統計網站，還在裡面寫入了檔案。這是大家知道的第一次，AI駭進（偷偷闖進）政府系統。

OpenAI說，AI在測試時做了公司沒想到的事，而且看起來沒有病人資料被拿走。

問題是，OpenAI等到9月10日才通知澳洲，晚了快三個月。總理已經直接向OpenAI老闆奧特曼（Sam Altman）表達「非常擔心」。

Australian Prime Minister Anthony Albanese says that on June 18 this year, an AI agent from OpenAI—an autonomous AI program—broke into the government's health statistics website and wrote files to it. This marks the first known instance of artificial intelligence hacking into a government system. According to OpenAI, the AI performed an action the company did not anticipate during testing, and no patient data appears to have been compromised. The concern, however, is that OpenAI didn't notify Australia until September 10, nearly three months after the breach. The Prime Minister has already conveyed his "serious concern" directly to OpenAI CEO Sam Altman.

美中貿易休戰再延兩個月

來源：CNBC / S&P Global / Bloomberg

中國國家主席習近平這週到白宮見美國總統川普。見面前，美國財政部長貝森特（Scott Bessent）宣布，兩國的貿易「休戰」延長到2027年1月10日。

休戰就是雙方先不要再互相提高關稅（進口東西要付的稅）。不過，很多人本來以為會延長半年以上，結果只多了兩個月。

對美國中西部的農民來說，最重要的是中國會不會繼續大量買大豆。更大的協議，兩國還要再談。

Chinese President Xi Jinping visited the White House this week to meet with US President Trump. Before the meeting, US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent announced that the two countries' trade "ceasefire" has been extended until January 10, 2027. A ceasefire means both sides will refrain from raising tariffs on each other—the taxes charged on imported goods—for now. However, many people had expected an extension of at least six months; it turned out to be only two additional months. For American Midwest farmers, the critical question is whether China will continue to purchase soybeans in large quantities. Larger agreements between the two countries remain to be negotiated.

職缺變多了，新人卻更難找工作

來源：Indeed Hiring Lab

求職網站Indeed說，美國的職缺比去年同期多了0.7%，是將近四年來第一次上升。

可是好消息不是每個人都有份。Indeed今年稍早的資料顯示，給新人的入門工作比前一年少了7.5%，找有經驗老手的工作卻多了14.7%。

很多公司開始用AI做簡單的工作。對剛畢業的年輕人來說，第一份工作變得更難找了。

Job search site Indeed reports that U.S. job openings are up 0.7% compared to the same period last year—the first increase in nearly four years. But the good news isn't shared equally.

Indeed's data from earlier this year shows that entry-level positions for newcomers dropped 7.5% compared to the previous year, while jobs for experienced workers jumped 14.7%.

Many companies are increasingly turning to AI to handle routine work. For recent graduates, landing that first job has become significantly more difficult.

工會支持芝加哥火車維修廠

來源：Crain's Chicago Business

芝加哥勞工聯合會（很多工會組成的團體）這週表態，支持美國鐵路公司Amtrak在Bridgeport蓋一座9億美元的火車維修廠。地點在33街到35街之間，就在白襪隊球場Rate Field旁邊。

即將接手白襪隊的富豪伊許比亞（Justin Ishbia），已經為這個計畫投入超過1.25億美元。

附近居民上週在會議上大聲反對。現在工會站出來，說這會帶來很多工作機會，這場爭論變得更熱了。

The Chicago Federation of Labor, an umbrella organization of many unions, announced its support this week for Amtrak, the U.S. railroad company, to build a $900 million train repair facility in Bridgeport. The facility would be located between 33rd and 35th Streets, right next to the White Sox's Rate Field. Justin Ishbia, the wealthy businessman set to take over the White Sox, has already committed more than $125 million to the project. Neighbors strongly opposed the plan at a meeting last week. Now, with union backing and promises of significant job creation, the debate has intensified.

密西根迎戰愛荷華：名教練首次對決

來源：The Gazette / MGoBlue

週六，排名第18的密西根大學，要在主場「大房子」迎戰第17名的愛荷華大學。這是兩隊今年第一場十大聯盟比賽。密西根三戰全勝，還以17比10打敗第11名的奧克拉荷馬；愛荷華也是三勝零敗。

密西根新教練威廷漢（Kyle Whittingham）當教練以來贏了180場；愛荷華的費倫茲（Kirk Ferentz）更多，有216勝。這是兩人第一次交手。

密西根前三場掉了六次球，而愛荷華最會慢慢控球、等對手犯錯。

On Saturday, 18th-ranked University of Michigan faces 17th-ranked University of Iowa at home in "The Big House." This is the two teams' first Big Ten Conference game this year. Michigan is 3-0, having defeated 11th-ranked Oklahoma 17-10; Iowa is also 3-0. Michigan's new coach Kyle Whittingham has won 180 games in his coaching career; Iowa's Kirk Ferentz has even more, with 216 wins. This will be their first matchup. Michigan has turned the ball over six times in its first three games, while Iowa excels at controlling tempo and waiting for opponents to make mistakes.

英格蘭要多曼慢慢來

來源：Yahoo Sports / ESPN

兵工廠線——阿森納16歲的天才小將多曼（Max Dowman），暫時不會進入英格蘭國家隊。

英格蘭21歲以下隊教練卡斯利（Lee Carsley）說，多曼非常出色，但大家要有耐心。他希望多曼先走21歲以下隊這條路，以後才能在國家隊踢很多年。

多曼上季成為英超史上最年輕的進球球員。阿森納之前也拒絕把他借給別的球隊。

Arsenal beat — Arsenal's 16-year-old prodigy Max Dowman won't be stepping up to the England national team just yet. England U-21 coach Lee Carsley says the young talent is exceptional, but patience is needed. Carsley hopes Dowman will develop through the U-21 pathway first, so he can enjoy a long career with the senior national team. Last season, Dowman became the youngest goal-scorer in Premier League history. Arsenal has also refused to loan him to other clubs so far.
