兵工廠主場2比0贏艾佛頓

來源：Sky Sports / FotMob

兵工廠線——兵工廠週六在主場酋長球場，以2比0打敗艾佛頓，聯賽不敗的紀錄又多了一場。

這場贏得很輕鬆。之前他們踢得很辛苦：先是全場只有一球踢向球門，以1比0險勝諾丁漢森林；接著在歐冠和拜仁2比2打平。

接下來，10月27日要去客場踢聯賽盃，11月1日再去挑戰利物浦。那是這個秋天聯賽最難的一關。

The Arsenal beat: Arsenal beat Everton 2-0 at home at the Emirates on Saturday, extending their unbeaten league run by another match.

The win came easily. Their recent outings had been harder going. First, they managed only one shot on target in a 1-0 squeaker over Nottingham Forest. Then they drew 2-2 with Bayern in the Champions League.

Next up, they travel for a League Cup tie on October 27, then visit Liverpool on November 1. That is the toughest test in the league this autumn.
