微軟與OpenAI結束獨家合作

來源：CNBC

微軟和OpenAI宣布結束從2019年開始的獨家合作關係。以後OpenAI可以在Amazon和Google的雲端平台賣產品，不再只能用微軟的Azure。

微軟也不用再付分潤（分享賺到的錢）給OpenAI，但仍保留股份。這代表AI競爭進入新階段，兩家公司都想要更多自由。

Microsoft and OpenAI announced the end of their exclusive partnership that began in 2019. Going forward, OpenAI can sell its products on Amazon and Google's cloud platforms, no longer restricted to Microsoft's Azure.

Microsoft will no longer pay revenue-sharing fees to OpenAI, though it retains its equity stake. This marks a new phase in AI competition, with both companies seeking greater independence.

美國國土安全部停擺75天後終於復工

來源：NBC News

美國國土安全部（DHS）停擺了創紀錄的75天，終於在4月30日恢復運作。川普簽了撥款法案，讓機場安檢、海岸防衛隊和災難救助重新拿到經費。

但移民執法機關仍沒有被列入這次的預算，國會兩黨對於削減開支的爭議還沒結束。

The U.S. Department of Homeland Security (DHS) has resumed operations after a record 75-day shutdown on April 30th. Trump signed an appropriations bill that restored funding to airport security screenings, the Coast Guard, and disaster relief efforts.

However, immigration enforcement agencies were not included in this budget allocation, and the bipartisan congressional dispute over spending cuts remains unresolved.

Snapchat母公司裁員一千人，直說是AI取代

來源：TechCrunch

Snapchat的母公司Snap裁掉了1000名員工，佔全公司人數的16%。執行長Spiegel直接說：因為AI進步了，更少的人就能做一樣的事。

目前Snap有超過65%的新程式碼是AI寫的。這是少數大公司老闆公開承認「AI讓人失業」的案例。

Snap, the parent company of Snapchat, has laid off 1,000 employees, representing 16% of its workforce. CEO Spiegel was direct about the reason: AI has advanced to the point where fewer people can accomplish the same work.

Currently, more than 65% of Snap's new code is written by AI. This stands out as a rare instance of a major company executive publicly acknowledging that AI is displacing workers.

美國司法部調查伊利諾州36個學區性別課程

來源：ABC7 Chicago

美國司法部民權部門對伊利諾州36個學區展開調查，要看學校有沒有在教性別認同的內容，以及有沒有通知家長。

這是聯邦政府對伊利諾州教育規模最大的一次介入，涵蓋從幼兒園到高中的課程，直接影響芝加哥地區多所學校。

The U.S. Department of Justice's Civil Rights Division has launched an investigation into 36 school districts in Illinois to determine whether schools are teaching content related to gender identity and whether they are notifying parents.

This represents the federal government's largest intervention into Illinois education to date, covering curriculum from kindergarten through high school and directly affecting multiple schools in the Chicago area.

密西根大學與冠軍教練May續約五年

來源：Bleacher Report

密西根大學籃球隊教練Dusty May帶隊拿下全國冠軍後，學校馬上跟他簽了五年新約。May兩年內贏了64場，追平了歷史紀錄。

他接手時球隊戰績只有8勝24敗，現在卻是全美第一。May說：「我不會走的。能在這裡當教練是我的榮幸。」

After leading the Michigan basketball team to a national championship, coach Dusty May signed a five-year extension with the university. In just two years, May has compiled 64 wins, tying a school record.

When he took over, the team had a record of 8 wins and 24 losses. Now they're ranked number one in the nation. May said: "I'm not going anywhere. It's an honor to coach here."

兵工廠被VAR搶走勝利，客場1-1平馬競

來源：Al Jazeera

兵工廠線——歐冠四強首回合，兵工廠在馬德里和馬競1-1打平。Gyokeres射入十二碼先開紀錄，但Alvarez在下半場也靠十二碼追平。

最爭議的是第77分鐘，Eze在禁區被犯規，裁判原本判了十二碼，卻被VAR推翻。教練Arteta賽後非常生氣。次回合5月5日回到主場。

Arsenal — In the first leg of the Champions League semi-final, Arsenal played to a 1-1 draw against Atlético Madrid in Madrid. Gyokeres scored from the penalty spot first, but Alvarez equalized from the penalty in the second half.

The most controversial moment came in the 77th minute when Eze was fouled in the box. The referee initially awarded a penalty, but VAR overturned the decision. Manager Arteta was furious after the match. The second leg returns home on May 5th.
