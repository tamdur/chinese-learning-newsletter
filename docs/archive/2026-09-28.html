川普拒絕伊朗停火，油價大漲

來源：Euronews、CNBC

美國總統川普拒絕了伊朗提出的七天停火計畫。伊朗說，只要停火，就會重新打開荷姆茲海峽，這是全世界運油最重要的水道。

川普說：「他們想談，但這不是我想要的交易。」消息一出，布蘭特原油每桶漲到107美元以上，一天就漲了超過3%。

油價變貴，很多東西也可能跟著變貴。股市因此下跌，大家擔心物價又要上升。

US President Trump has rejected a seven-day ceasefire plan proposed by Iran. Iran stated that if a ceasefire were agreed upon, it would reopen the Strait of Hormuz, the world's most critical oil shipping route. Trump said, "They want to negotiate, but this is not the deal I want."

Following the announcement, Brent crude oil surged above $107 per barrel—a jump of more than 3% in a single day.

With oil prices climbing, many other commodities are likely to follow suit. Stock markets have declined as a result, and investors are worried that inflation could rise again.

美中成立「超級智慧」對話

來源：Axios

中國國家主席習近平到白宮訪問，這是他11年來第一次。美中兩國同意成立「超級智慧對話」，一起討論AI帶來的好處和危險。

兩國也要建立一條專門的聯絡線路。如果AI出了大問題，雙方可以馬上通話。美國官員把它比作冷戰時美國和蘇聯之間的「紅色電話」。

下一次對話會在2026年11月以前舉行。不過，什麼樣的事才算「AI出事」，現在還不清楚。

China's President Xi Jinping visited the White House—his first trip in eleven years. The US and China have agreed to establish a "Super Intelligence Dialogue" to jointly discuss the benefits and risks of AI. The two countries will also set up a dedicated communication hotline so both sides can contact each other immediately if a major AI issue arises. US officials have compared it to the "red phone" that connected the US and Soviet Union during the Cold War. The next dialogue is scheduled to take place before November 2026. However, it remains unclear what exactly would constitute "an AI incident."

澳洲可能升息，利率創15年新高

來源：ABC News (Australia)

澳洲央行（中央銀行）9月29日要開會。34位經濟學家裡，有33位認為它會升息（提高利率），從4.35%調到4.6%，是2011年以來最高。

這會是澳洲今年第四次升息。原因是油價和電費很高，物價一直降不下來。升息以後，一般房貸利率會到大約6.5%。

伊朗停火談不成，油價又往上衝。離中東很遠的澳洲，也躲不掉這波影響。

Australia's central bank will meet on September 29. Of 34 economists surveyed, 33 expect a rate increase from 4.35% to 4.6%—the highest since 2011. This will be Australia's fourth rate hike this year, as soaring oil and electricity costs keep inflation stubbornly elevated. The increase would push typical mortgage rates to around 6.5%.

Failed ceasefire talks with Iran have sent oil prices surging again. Even Australia, far from the Middle East, cannot escape the impact.

白宮用「拖時間」砍掉國會的錢

來源：PBS NewsHour

美國政府的年度預算在9月30日到期。白宮在最後五天，要求取消國會已經批准的大約8億1千萬美元。

這叫做「口袋撤銷」（撤銷就是取消）：時間太短，國會來不及投票，錢就自動過期了。被砍的錢大多是用來幫助難民和移民的。

政府監察單位早就說這種做法違法。共和黨參議員柯林斯和伊利諾州的民主黨參議員德賓，都出來批評。

The U.S. government's annual budget expires on September 30. With only five days remaining, the White House is requesting the cancellation of approximately $810 million that Congress had already approved. This tactic, known as a "pocket rescission," relies on timing: Congress simply doesn't have enough time to vote before the funds automatically expire. The cuts primarily target money intended to help refugees and immigrants. Government watchdog agencies have long flagged this practice as illegal. Both Republican Senator Collins and Illinois Democratic Senator Durbin have publicly criticized the move.

小熊隊季後賽再戰教士隊

來源：MLB.com

芝加哥小熊隊的外卡賽對手確定了：聖地牙哥教士隊。三場兩勝，全部在聖地牙哥打，星期二開打。

教士隊9月打出17勝6敗，在主場是50勝30敗，非常強。不過，今年小熊對上他們是5勝1敗。

小熊的克羅阿姆斯壯（Pete Crow-Armstrong）今年打出45支全壘打，還有40次盜壘（趁機跑向下一個壘）。他是小熊隊史上第一人，也是大聯盟史上第七位做到的球員。

The Chicago Cubs' wild-card opponent is confirmed: the San Diego Padres. Best-of-three series, all games in San Diego, starting Tuesday. The Padres went 17-6 in September with a 50-30 home record—very strong. However, the Cubs are 5-1 against them this year. Cubs' Pete Crow-Armstrong has hit 45 home runs this season and stolen 40 bases. He's the first in Cubs history and the seventh player in Major League Baseball history to achieve this milestone.

約克雷斯：「我想多上場」

來源：Sports Illustrated

兵工廠線——兵工廠前鋒約克雷斯（Viktor Gyökeres）說，上場時間太少讓他很失望。這一季英超已經打了450分鐘，他只踢了19分鐘。

他說：「當然希望多踢一點，但我們有很多好球員。」他也說，夏天從來沒想過要離開球隊。

不過，教練最愛用的哈維茲在德國隊比賽時拉傷大腿。約克雷斯的機會，可能就要來了。

Arsenal Beat — Arsenal forward Viktor Gyökeres said limited playing time has left him frustrated. With 450 minutes of Premier League football played this season, he has featured for just 19 minutes. "Of course I'd like to play more, but we have a lot of quality players," he said. He also said he never considered leaving the club over the summer. However, Havertz, the manager's preferred forward, suffered a thigh injury while playing for Germany. Gyökeres' opportunity may soon come.
