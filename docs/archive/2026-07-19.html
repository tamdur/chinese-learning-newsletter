伊朗攻擊約旦基地　兩名美軍身亡

來源：Bloomberg

美國和伊朗的戰爭又打了八個晚上。十八日，伊朗的飛彈打中約旦（中東的一個國家）一座美軍基地，兩名美國軍人死亡，還有一人失蹤。

這是三月以來第一次有美軍在戰場上喪命。伊朗拒絕了停火，美國也馬上還手，讓大約一萬人沒了乾淨的水。

The U.S.-Iran conflict has intensified over eight more nights. On the 18th, Iranian missiles struck a U.S. military base in Jordan (a Middle Eastern country), killing two American soldiers and leaving one missing.

This marks the first combat death of American troops since March. Iran rejected ceasefire attempts, and the U.S. immediately retaliated, leaving roughly ten thousand people without access to clean water.

蘋果超越輝達　重回全球第一

來源：CNBC / Bloomberg

大家對人工智慧（AI）的熱情開始退燒。投資人擔心花大錢蓋AI設備，卻賺不回來，於是紛紛賣股票。

星期五，做晶片的輝達跌了百分之三點五，蘋果趁機用四點八八兆美元的身價，拿回了「全球最值錢公司」的寶座。

Enthusiasm for artificial intelligence (AI) is beginning to cool. Investors worry they're spending huge sums on AI infrastructure but won't recoup their returns—so they're dumping shares left and right.

On Friday, chip maker Nvidia fell 3.5 percent. Apple seized the moment, reclaiming the title of world's most valuable company with a valuation of $4.88 trillion.

美國解禁　公務手機可裝抖音

來源：US News / AP

美國司法部宣布，政府員工又可以在公務手機上裝抖音（TikTok，一款短影片APP）了。

二〇二二年，美國因為怕洩漏祕密，禁止員工使用。現在抖音的美國部分已經交給美國人管，拖了好幾年的爭議總算落幕。

The US Department of Justice announced that government employees can now install TikTok—a short-form video app—on their work phones again.

In 2022, the United States banned employees from using it over concerns about potential leaks of classified information. Now that TikTok's US operations have been handed over to American management, the years-long dispute has finally come to an end.

剛果雨林　發現新猴子

來源：Hacker News

科學家在非洲剛果的雨林裡，發現了一種從來沒見過的猴子。牠全身黑黑的，嘴巴旁邊有橘色的花紋，當地人叫牠「Likweli」。

這是七十五年來，非洲第五種被找到的新猴子。牠住的地方很小，數量也不多，科學家很擔心牠會絕種。

Scientists have discovered a previously unknown species of monkey in the rainforests of Congo in Africa. It is entirely black with orange markings around its mouth, and local people call it "Likweli."

This is the fifth new monkey species discovered in Africa over the past seventy-five years. With an extremely limited habitat and small population, scientists are concerned it may face extinction.

明星賽回娘家　小熊主場辦盛會

來源：MLB.com

美國職棒大聯盟公布了二〇二七年明星賽的標誌。明年七月十三日，這場大賽將在芝加哥小熊隊的瑞格利球場舉行。

這座老球場已經辦過三次明星賽，這是第四次，也是目前辦最多次的球場。新標誌上有球場紅色的大招牌，還有芝加哥市旗上的藍色星星。

Major League Baseball has unveiled the logo for the 2027 All-Star Game. The showcase will be held at Wrigley Field, home of the Chicago Cubs, on July 13th next year.

This historic ballpark has already hosted the All-Star Game three times; this will be the fourth occasion, making it the stadium that has hosted the game most frequently. The new logo features the ballpark's iconic red marquee along with the blue stars from the Chicago city flag.

兵工廠線　阿森納改補後防

來源：Pain in the Arsenal

兵工廠線——阿森納（Arsenal）今年夏天本來忙著找前鋒，邊鋒佐利斯（Tzolis）也快要加入了。

不過主力後衛薩利巴（Saliba）在世界盃受了傷，球隊只好趕快補強後防。教練阿爾特塔現在想找一名左後衛和一名中場，還看上了一位法國後衛。

Arsenal was originally focused on hunting for a new striker this summer, with winger Tzolis set to arrive.

But the plan shifted when key defender Saliba suffered an injury at the World Cup, forcing the club to urgently shore up the defense.

Manager Arteta is now on the hunt for a left-back and a midfielder, and has also set his sights on a French defender.
