OpenAI發布GPT-5.4，考試成績超過大多數人類

來源：OpenAI / The Verge

OpenAI今天推出了新的AI模型GPT-5.4。這個模型去考律師考試和醫生考試，成績比百分之九十五的人類還好。這是AI能力的一個大進步。很多人開始討論：如果AI這麼厲害，律師和醫生的工作會不會受到影響？不過專家說，通過考試和真的做好工作是不一樣的。

OpenAI released a new AI model called GPT-5.4 today. The model took the lawyer licensing exam and the medical licensing exam, scoring better than ninety-five percent of human test-takers. This represents a major leap forward in AI capabilities. Many people have begun discussing: if AI is this capable, will the work of lawyers and doctors be affected? However, experts say that passing an exam and actually doing a job well are two different things.

習近平和川普四月要見面，談晶片和稀土

來源：The Diplomat / Reuters

美國和中國的領導人預計這個月會見面，這是近年來最重要的一次會議。兩國在晶片和稀土（做電子產品需要的特別礦物）上互相依賴。美國有先進的晶片技術，中國控制了全球百分之七十的稀土加工。雙方還會談台灣問題和AI規則。

The leaders of the United States and China are expected to meet this month in what would be the most significant meeting in recent years. The two countries depend on each other for chips and rare earths — special minerals needed to make electronic products. The US has advanced chip technology, while China controls about 70 percent of global rare earth processing. The two sides will also discuss the Taiwan issue and AI regulations.

NASA太空船準備好了，四月飛向月球

來源：NASA / Scientific American

NASA今天確認，Artemis II太空任務按照計畫進行，四月就會發射。這是一九七二年以來，人類第一次坐太空船飛到月球旁邊。四位太空人會繞月球飛一圈，但不會降落。這次任務是為了測試，下一次才會真的讓人踏上月球表面。

NASA confirmed today that the Artemis II space mission is proceeding as planned, with launch set for April. This will be humanity's first crewed journey near the Moon since 1972. Four astronauts will orbit the Moon, but will not land. This mission is a test; the next one will actually put humans on the lunar surface.

The U.S. House of Representatives passed the full-year 2026 budget today by a vote of 217 to 214. Earlier, the Senate had modified the bill, which led to a partial government shutdown. Now the budget heads to the President's desk for signature. It includes increased defense spending and continued support for America's semiconductor manufacturing initiative.

伊朗戰爭第三十三天：油價飆升，荷莫茲海峽仍然關閉

特別報導 — CNN, Al Jazeera, CNBC

美國和以色列對伊朗的戰爭已經打了三十三天了。今天最大的消息是：一枚從伊朗發射的飛彈（missile）打中了卡達外海的一艘油輪，幸好沒有人受傷。同時，伊朗的無人機攻擊了科威特機場的油庫，引發大火。荷莫茲海峽（Strait of Hormuz）仍然關閉，全球大約百分之二十的石油平常要從這裡經過。

油價已經漲到很嚇人的地步。布蘭特原油接近每桶一百二十美元，美國汽油的平均價格突破了每加侖四美元，是二〇二二年以來最高的。分析師警告說，如果海峽在四月中之前不重新開放，油價可能會再大幅上漲，最壞的情況下甚至可能衝到兩百美元一桶。菲律賓已經因為能源不夠宣布進入緊急狀態。

川普總統說戰爭可能在兩三個星期內結束，但伊朗外交部長阿拉奇說他不相信美國。伊朗沒有回應美國提出的十五點要求，還說這些條件「不切實際」。看起來短時間內很難有和平的結果，油價大概還會繼續讓大家頭痛。

The U.S.-Israel war against Iran has now lasted thirty-three days. Today's biggest news: a missile fired from Iran hit an oil tanker in Qatari waters, though fortunately no one was injured. Meanwhile, Iranian drones attacked an oil depot at Kuwait's airport, triggering a massive fire. The Strait of Hormuz remains closed, though roughly twenty percent of the world's oil normally passes through this waterway.

Oil prices have risen to alarming levels. Brent crude is approaching one hundred twenty dollars per barrel, and average U.S. gasoline prices have exceeded four dollars per gallon — the highest since 2022. Analysts warn that if the strait doesn't reopen before mid-April, oil prices could surge much further, potentially even reaching two hundred dollars per barrel in a worst-case scenario. The Philippines has already declared a state of emergency due to energy shortages.

President Trump said the war could end in two or three weeks, but Iran's foreign minister Araghchi said he doesn't believe the United States. Iran has not responded to America's fifteen-point demands, calling the conditions "unrealistic." It appears peace is unlikely in the near term, and oil prices will probably continue to cause headaches for everyone.

Claude Code 原始碼意外外洩，藏了一隻虛擬寵物

Bloomberg

三月三十一日，Anthropic 在發布（發出）Claude Code 2.1.88 版時出了大錯。一個除錯用的檔案不小心被放進公開的 npm 套件裡，結果五十一萬行程式碼全部被看到了。在 Anthropic 來得及處理之前，這些程式碼已經在 GitHub 上被複製了超過五萬次。

最有趣的發現是什麼？工程師們在程式碼裡找到了一個叫「Claude Buddy」的隱藏功能——這是一隻住在你終端機（terminal）裡的虛擬寵物，有點像電子雞。每個用戶的寵物都是用帳號自動產生的，有十八種動物、五種稀有等級，還有百分之一的機會拿到閃亮版。它會看你和 Claude 的對話，然後用對話泡泡說出自己的想法。

今天 Anthropic 發布了 2.1.89 版修復問題，同時正式開放了 Buddy 功能。公司表示這次外洩「沒有涉及客戶資料」，只是打包時的人為錯誤。不過社群已經玩瘋了——有人甚至用最稀有的寵物組合發了一個加密貨幣。

On March 31st, Anthropic made a serious mistake while releasing Claude Code version 2.1.88. A debug file was accidentally included in the public npm package, exposing all 510,000 lines of source code. Before Anthropic could respond, the code had been copied over 50,000 times on GitHub.

What was the most interesting discovery? Engineers found a hidden feature in the code called "Claude Buddy"—a virtual pet that lives in your terminal, somewhat like a digital pet toy. Each user's pet is automatically generated based on their account, with eighteen animal types, five rarity levels, and a one percent chance of getting a shiny version. It watches your conversations with Claude and expresses its thoughts through speech bubbles.

Today Anthropic released version 2.1.89 to fix the issue and officially opened the Buddy feature to the public. The company stated that the leak "did not involve customer data," only a human packing error. However, the community has already gone wild—someone even launched a cryptocurrency using the rarest pet combination.
