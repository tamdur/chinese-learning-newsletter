美國住房大法案半夜生效　川普沒簽名

來源：NPR

美國一項大型住房法案，在七月十日半夜正式生效。特別的是，川普總統沒有簽名，也沒有否決（擋下），法案就自動通過了。

他不簽是為了抗議另一個卡住的法案。這項新法有四十多條規定，要鼓勵多蓋房子、也限制大公司大量買走一般住家，是幾十年來最重要的住房法。

A major US housing bill took effect at midnight on July 10th. What's notable is that President Trump neither signed it nor vetoed it — the bill passed automatically.

He refused to sign as a protest against another stalled bill. The new law contains over forty provisions designed to encourage more home construction and limit large corporations from bulk-buying residential properties. It's the most significant housing legislation in decades.

俄羅斯一直猛攻，烏克蘭的飛彈快用完了，所以這對澤倫斯基是好消息。不過要辦好授權手續，可能要等上一年，飛彈才真的造得出來。

At a NATO summit held in Turkey, Trump promised to allow Ukraine to manufacture American "Patriot" interceptor missiles domestically — weapons used to shoot down incoming missiles.

With Russia launching relentless attacks and Ukraine's missile supplies dwindling, this is welcome news for Zelenskyy. However, completing the authorization process could take about a year before Ukraine can actually begin producing the missiles.

上訴法院維持伊利諾州攻擊槍禁令

來源：WTTW News

美國第七巡迴上訴法院在七月十日以二比一，維持伊利諾州禁止攻擊型槍枝和大容量彈匣的法律，推翻了下級法院的判決。

這條法律是在二〇二二年海蘭帕克國慶遊行槍擊案之後通過的。最近聯邦最高法院也答應要審類似案子，全國很快會有一場大辯論。

The U.S. Court of Appeals for the Seventh Circuit upheld Illinois's law banning assault-style firearms and large-capacity magazines in a 2-to-1 decision on July 10th, reversing a lower court's ruling.

The law was enacted following the 2022 Highland Park Fourth of July parade shooting. The U.S. Supreme Court has recently agreed to hear a similar case, setting the stage for a major nationwide debate.

SK海力士上市首日大漲近一成三

來源：The Motley Fool

南韓記憶體晶片公司SK海力士，七月十日在美國股市掛牌，第一天股價就漲了百分之十二點八。

這次共募到兩百六十五億美元，是外國公司在美國上市金額最高的一次。因為AI很紅，它做的高速記憶體又是輝達的重要供應商，投資人搶著要買。

South Korean memory chip company SK Hynix went public on the U.S. stock market on July 10th, with its stock price surging 12.8% on the first trading day.

The offering raised $26.5 billion in total — the largest IPO by a foreign company in the U.S. to date. With AI in high demand and SK Hynix serving as a key supplier of high-speed memory to Nvidia, investors rushed to snap up shares.
