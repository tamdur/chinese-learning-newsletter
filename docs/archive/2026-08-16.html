以色列空襲黎巴嫩，十一人死亡

來源：CNN / France 24

以色列上週六空襲黎巴嫩南部，至少十一人死亡，其中三個是小孩。這是六月和真主黨（黎巴嫩的武裝組織）停火以來，死傷最重的一次。以軍說，這是報復真主黨先攻擊他們的士兵。

同一時間，伊朗的無人機又打中一艘經過荷姆茲海峽的油輪。阿拉伯聯合大公國說，這是「公然違反」國際法。六月談好的停火，正從海上和陸上兩邊裂開。

Israel carried out airstrikes on southern Lebanon last Saturday, killing at least eleven people, three of them children. It was the deadliest toll since the ceasefire with Hezbollah—a Lebanese armed group—took effect in June. The Israeli military said the strikes were retaliation for Hezbollah's prior attack on its soldiers. Meanwhile, Iranian drones struck an oil tanker transiting the Strait of Hormuz. The United Arab Emirates called it a “blatant violation” of international law. The ceasefire agreed in June is now unravelling on both the maritime and the land front.

產業報告：Anthropic 年營收增為三倍

來源：AI industry roundup

一份AI產業回顧整理指出，Anthropic 的年化營收（把現在的速度換算成一整年）已超過三百億美元，是去年底約九十億美元的三倍多；每年付超過一百萬美元的企業客戶，也超過一千家。

這些數字不是今天才公布的，但值得看：這個月大家都在談AI借錢蓋機房，很少人看真正收進來的錢。而這筆收入，還是遠遠少於花出去的投資。

An AI industry roundup reports that Anthropic's annualised revenue—its current run rate extrapolated over a full year—now exceeds $30 billion, more than triple the roughly $9 billion at the end of last year. The number of corporate customers spending over $1 million a year has also passed 1,000. These figures were not disclosed today, but they are worth noting: this month everyone has been talking about AI companies borrowing to build data centres, and few have looked at the money actually coming in. Even so, that revenue still falls far short of the capital being spent to serve it.

民主黨同一天通過兩種相反主張

來源：Washington Post / AP

美國民主黨全國委員會八月十五日在德州奧斯汀開夏季會議，先通過一項決議，要國會廢掉移民及海關執法局（ICE）；接著又通過另一項，只要求改革這個單位。

廢除案在委員會以二十五票對十二票通過。兩案都過，等於全黨說不出一個共同答案。距離期中選舉只剩幾個月，這道裂縫可能一路延燒到二○二八年的初選。

The Democratic National Committee held its summer meeting in Austin, Texas on August 15th. It first passed a resolution calling on Congress to abolish Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE), then passed another calling only for the agency to be reformed. The abolition measure carried in committee by 25 votes to 12. By passing both, the party effectively failed to state a single common answer. With only months to go before the midterms, the rift could carry all the way into the 2028 primary.

日相只送供品，中韓仍抗議

來源：South China Morning Post / Al Jazeera

八月十五日是日本在二次大戰投降的八十一週年。防衛大臣小泉進次郎等四位閣員到靖國神社參拜，首相高市早苗只送上供品，沒有親自去。

這座神社供奉日本的戰死者，裡面也有十四名甲級戰犯，所以每次參拜都變成外交事件。中國外交部提出「強烈抗議」，南韓說「深感遺憾」。高市送供品而不去，是在算清楚要用多少歷史。

August 15th marked the 81st anniversary of Japan's surrender in the Second World War. Defence Minister Koizumi Shinjiro and three other cabinet members visited Yasukuni Shrine, while Prime Minister Takaichi Sanae sent a ritual offering rather than attending in person. The shrine enshrines Japan's war dead, and among them are fourteen Class-A war criminals, which is why every visit becomes a diplomatic incident. China's Foreign Ministry lodged a “strong protest,” and South Korea expressed “deep regret.” By sending an offering instead of going herself, Takaichi is calculating exactly how much history to invoke.

紅雀新人前三打席連三轟，小熊吞敗

來源：ESPN

聖路易紅雀二十二歲新人拜耶茲（Joshua Báez）八月十五日在小熊主場瑞格利球場初登大聯盟，前三個打席全部打出全壘打，是大聯盟一百五十年來第一人。

第一支打的就是他看到的第一顆球，飛了四百四十九英尺。當天有三十位家人到場看他。紅雀以八比四贏球；小熊在國聯中區仍落後釀酒人三場。

St. Louis Cardinals rookie Joshua Báez, 22, made his major league debut on August 15th at Wrigley Field, the Cubs' home ballpark, and homered in each of his first three at-bats—the first player to do so in the 150-year history of Major League Baseball. The first came on the very first pitch he saw and travelled 449 feet. Thirty members of his family were in the stands to watch. The Cardinals won 8-4; the Cubs remain three games behind the Brewers in the National League Central.

兵工廠今開季戰曼城，社區盾在卡地夫

來源：Sports Illustrated / Goal.com

兵工廠線——上季拿下英超冠軍的兵工廠，今天在威爾斯的卡地夫對上曼城，踢社區盾（英格蘭新球季的第一場正式比賽），芝加哥時間早上九點開球。

這九十分鐘像是今年夏天的總驗收：球隊花了約一億六千七百萬歐元補中場，簽下蘇比門迪和布魯諾，但教練阿爾特塔公開要的邊鋒一直沒買，後防也刻意不補人。熱身賽五場只贏一場，後衛薩利巴和廷伯都有傷不能上。曼城這邊也是新局：馬雷斯卡接替瓜迪奧拉後的第一場正式比賽。

The Arsenal beat—Arsenal, last season's Premier League champions, face Manchester City today in Cardiff, Wales, in the Community Shield, the first official match of the new English season, kicking off at 9am Chicago time. These ninety minutes are a reckoning for the summer's work: the club spent roughly €167 million reinforcing midfield, signing Zubimendi and Bruno Guimarães, but the winger manager Mikel Arteta publicly asked for was never bought, and the defence was deliberately left unreinforced. Arsenal won only one of five pre-season friendlies, and defenders Saliba and Timber are both injured and unavailable. For City it is also a new era: Enzo Maresca's first competitive match after replacing Pep Guardiola.
