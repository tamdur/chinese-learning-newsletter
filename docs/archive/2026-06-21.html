伊朗宣布關閉荷姆茲海峽　停火協議破局

來源：NBC News

六月二十日，伊朗宣布關閉荷姆茲海峽（運油的重要水道），說是因為以色列又去轟炸了黎巴嫩南部。

這條海峽運送全世界大約兩成的石油，一關閉，油價可能會大漲。幾天前的協議才讓海峽重開，現在又關上。

不過美國軍方說，海峽其實還開著，當天過往的商船還變多了。

On June 20th, Iran announced it was closing the Strait of Hormuz—a critical shipping lane for oil—claiming it was in response to Israel's renewed bombing campaign in southern Lebanon.

The strait carries roughly one-fifth of the world's oil supply. If closed, oil prices could spike significantly. A ceasefire agreement just days earlier had reopened the strait; now it's been shut again.

However, the U.S. military disputed this, saying the strait actually remained open, with commercial traffic even increasing that day.

歐盟AI新法八月上路　卻追不上新科技

來源：CFR

八月二日起，歐盟的人工智慧法律就要全面生效，是全世界第一部完整管AI的法律。

但這部法律當初是為了管「幫人做事」的AI，沒想到現在出現了能自己行動的AI。

六月十五日發表的國際AI安全報告就說，這種會自己動的AI最難管，才是最大的難題。

Starting August 2nd, the EU's artificial intelligence law will take full effect, making it the world's first comprehensive AI regulation.

But the law was originally designed to govern AI that performs tasks for humans — nobody anticipated AI systems that can act autonomously.

An international AI safety report released on June 15th identified self-operating AI as the hardest to regulate and the biggest challenge ahead.

文件外流　美擬給警察人臉辨識手機程式

來源：NPR

美國國土安全部一份外流的文件顯示，政府想給大約一千三百個地方警局一款手機程式。

警察用手機掃人的臉，就能和超過兩億五千萬筆資料比對，找出要抓的移民。

這些照片會留十五年，連被掃到的美國公民也一樣，讓很多人擔心隱私。

A leaked Department of Homeland Security document reveals the U.S. government wants to distribute a mobile app to roughly thirteen hundred local police departments.

Officers could use their phones to scan a person's face and compare it against a database of over 250 million records to identify immigrants targeted for arrest.

These photos would be retained for fifteen years—including those of U.S. citizens caught in the scans—raising widespread privacy concerns.

北美貿易協定七月大檢視　前途未定

來源：KPMG

美國、墨西哥和加拿大的貿易協定，七月一日要做六年一次的大檢視。

這次要決定協定要不要再延十六年，還是改成每年都要重談。過去一年關稅（進口貨要繳的稅）大亂，大家都在看結果。

專家說，因為不確定，北美的經濟成長可能會放慢到百分之一點六。

The trade agreement between the United States, Mexico, and Canada faces a major review on July 1st—the first comprehensive checkup in six years.

The review will decide whether the agreement gets extended for another sixteen years, or switches to annual renegotiations instead. Over the past year, tariffs—taxes on imported goods—have been in chaos, and everyone is watching to see what happens.

Experts warn that the uncertainty could slow North American economic growth to just 1.6 percent.

公牛手握選秀第四順位　新團隊開局

來源：Bleacher Nation

芝加哥公牛隊下週就要參加NBA選秀，這次手上有第四順位，是好幾年來最高的。

球隊換了新的管理團隊，也換了新的總教練。這個選秀是新團隊的第一個大決定，也代表公牛要慢慢重新打造球隊了。

The Chicago Bulls will participate in the NBA Draft next week, and this time they have the fourth overall pick—the highest they've had in several years.

The team has brought in a new front office and a new head coach. This draft represents the new team's first major decision, and it signals that the Bulls are beginning a gradual rebuild.

兵工廠線——阿森納（兵工廠）正式出價，要買巴黎聖日耳曼的邊鋒巴爾科拉，他才二十三歲。

幾天前兵工廠才在歐冠決賽輸給巴黎，現在想補強左路，這是這個夏天第一筆真的行動。

Arsenal beat — Arsenal has made an official bid for Paris Saint-Germain's winger Barcola, who is only twenty-three years old.
