拉脫維亞大選，總理的黨大勝

來源：Al Jazeera、Euronews

拉脫維亞是歐洲北邊的小國，和俄羅斯相鄰。星期六選國會，八成多的票開出來後，總理庫爾伯格斯（Andris Kulbergs）的「聯合名單」拿到百分之三十六的票，排第一。

第二名是常批評歐盟的「拉脫維亞優先」，拿到百分之十三，比上次多了快一倍。

聯合名單的票還不到一半，要找別的黨一起組成政府。庫爾伯格斯說，他不想和拉脫維亞優先合作。

Latvia is a small country in Northern Europe, bordering Russia. In Saturday's parliamentary election, with over 80 percent of votes counted, Prime Minister Andris Kulbergs' "Unity List" secured 36 percent of the vote, placing it first.

Coming in second is "Latvia First," which frequently criticizes the EU, winning 13 percent of the vote—nearly double its previous result.

With the Unity List falling short of a majority, it will need to partner with other parties to form a government. Kulbergs said he does not wish to cooperate with Latvia First.

密西根四次失誤，輸掉小棕壺

來源：Fox 9、ClickOnDetroit

密西根大學美式足球隊星期六到明尼蘇達比賽，以十四比二十輸球。這是兩隊第一百次爭奪「小棕壺」獎盃，明尼蘇達自二〇一四年後第一次贏回它。

密西根全場失誤四次。四分衛安德伍德（Bryce Underwood）被抄截（球被對手搶走）三次，還有一次沒接好對方踢來的球，送給對手三分。

新教練惠廷翰上週才以一分輸給愛荷華，現在連輸兩場。失誤的老問題，還是沒有解決。

Michigan lost to Minnesota 14–20 on Saturday. This was the hundredth contest between the two teams for the Little Brown Jug trophy, which Minnesota won back for the first time since 2014.

Michigan committed four turnovers in the game. Quarterback Bryce Underwood threw three interceptions, and the team fumbled on another play, giving Minnesota a field goal.

Coach Whittingham lost to Iowa by just one point last week, and now Michigan has lost two straight games. The team's chronic turnover problem remains unresolved.
