伊利諾州簽署全美首例前沿AI安全法

來源：Transparency Coalition

伊利諾州州長普里茲克今天簽署一項新法律SB 315。這讓伊利諾州成為全美第一個規定AI公司要接受「第三方」（也就是外面獨立公司）安全檢查的州。

這條法律針對OpenAI、Anthropic這些做最強AI的公司。它們每年都要請人來查安全問題，還要公開說明怎麼防止AI闖大禍，也要保護敢說真話的員工。法律二零二七年一月一日生效。

Illinois Governor Pritzker signed a new law, SB 315, today. This makes Illinois the first state in the nation to require AI companies to undergo third-party safety audits conducted by independent outside firms. The law targets leading AI developers like OpenAI and Anthropic. They will be required to conduct annual safety reviews, publicly disclose how they prevent AI systems from causing major harm, and protect employees who speak up truthfully. The law takes effect on January 1, 2027.

聯準會新主席取消未來利率指引

來源：The Motley Fool

美國聯準會（管全國利率的機構）新主席沃許，決定不再事先告訴大家利率會怎麼走，也不再公布每位官員預測利率的「點陣圖」。

沃許說，以前的預測害聯準會在二零二一、二二年看錯了物價上漲。這個大改變讓債券市場很不安，因為投資人失去了猜下一步的線索。下次利率決定在七月二十九日。

The U.S. Federal Reserve's new chair, Warsh, has decided to stop providing advance guidance on where interest rates will head. The Fed will also no longer publish the "dot plot" showing each official's interest rate forecast. Warsh said the Fed's previous projections led them to badly misjudge rising prices in 2021 and 2022. This dramatic shift has rattled the bond market, as investors lose the signposts they've relied on to anticipate the next move. The Fed's next rate decision is scheduled for July 29th.

SK海力士赴美掛牌 規模創史上最大

來源：Investing.com

南韓記憶體大廠SK海力士今天開始為在美國那斯達克掛牌募資，金額高達二百九十四億美元，是史上最大的一次，超過二零一四年阿里巴巴的紀錄。

這家公司做的「高頻寬記憶體」是AI晶片的重要零件。募到的錢會拿去蓋新廠、買設備。股票星期四定價、星期五開始交易，消息帶動南韓股市今天上漲百分之一點八。

South Korean memory chipmaker SK Hynix began raising money today for a Nasdaq listing worth $29.4 billion—the largest such offering in history, surpassing Alibaba's 2014 record. The company makes high-bandwidth memory, a critical component for AI chips. The funds raised will go toward building new factories and buying equipment. The stock is priced Thursday and begins trading Friday; the news pushed South Korea's stock market up 1.8% today.

中俄啟動「海上聯合2026」軍演

來源：Bloomberg

中國和俄羅斯今天在山東青島外海，開始一場叫「海上聯合2026」的海軍演習，一直練到七月十三日，之後還要一起到太平洋巡邏。

俄羅斯派出一艘巡洋艦、一艘護衛艦和一艘潛水艇；中國則出動北部戰區的軍艦。這場演習讓大家看到兩國的軍事合作越來越緊密。

China and Russia launched the "Joint Sea 2026" naval exercise today off the coast of Qingdao, Shandong, running through July 13th, after which the two will conduct a joint patrol in the Pacific. Russia deployed a cruiser, a frigate, and a submarine, while China sent warships from its Northern Theater Command. The exercise puts on display the ever-tightening military cooperation between the two countries.
