美國七月零售銷售大跌，消費信心跟著下滑

來源：Bloomberg

美國政府八月十四日公布，七月的零售銷售比六月少了百分之零點六，是二零二五年五月以來最大的跌幅。原本大家以為會小小上升。

同一天，密西根大學的調查顯示，八月的消費者信心指數從五十五點二掉到五十一，跌了大約百分之八。伊朗戰爭讓油價和食物變貴，大家不敢花錢。

消費是美國經濟的引擎。物價還很高，中央銀行降息怕物價更高，不降息又怕經濟變得更冷。

The U.S. government announced on August 14 that retail sales in July dropped 0.6% from June—the sharpest decline since May 2025. Economists had expected a modest gain.

The same day, the University of Michigan reported that its consumer confidence index fell from 55.2 to 51 in August—a drop of roughly 8%. The war with Iran has driven up oil and food prices, making consumers reluctant to spend.

Consumer spending drives the U.S. economy. With inflation still elevated, the Federal Reserve faces a dilemma: cutting rates risks pushing prices higher, while holding steady threatens a deeper slowdown.

博通股價重挫，人工智慧的借錢模式引發擔心

來源：24/7 Wall St.

美國銀行八月十四日發出報告，說晶片公司博通和兩家投資公司合開的人工智慧借貸平台，到二零二九年可能要承擔高達三千七百億美元的保證責任。

消息一出，博通股價當天跌了五到六個百分點，信用評等也被調低。

這個平台第一筆生意大約三百五十億美元，是替一家人工智慧公司蓋機房。原本被看成聰明的新做法，現在變成大家眼中的風險。

Bank of America released a report on August 14 saying that an artificial-intelligence lending platform jointly established by chipmaker Broadcom and two investment firms could carry guarantee liabilities as high as $370 billion by 2029.

Once the news broke, Broadcom's share price fell five to six percent that day, and its credit rating was downgraded.

The platform's first deal, worth roughly $35 billion, was to build a data center for an AI company. What was originally seen as a clever new approach is now viewed as a risk.

林肯號官兵家屬喊苦，川普說時間還不夠長

來源：Washington Post

美國航空母艦林肯號在中東已經連續出海超過兩百四十天，船上有五千多名官兵。家屬八月十四日公開說，船上浴室發霉、水被污染，還有人心理出了狀況。

記者問川普總統這樣是不是太久了，他回答「還遠遠不夠長」。國防部長赫格塞斯說這些報導被扭曲了，海軍則不肯公布官兵的心理健康資料。

這場戰爭打了一個多月，大家談的多半是油價和飛彈，這次談的是人。

The American aircraft carrier USS Abraham Lincoln has been continuously at sea in the Middle East for more than 240 days, with over 5,000 sailors aboard. On August 14, family members spoke out publicly, saying showers on the ship have gone moldy, the water has been contaminated, and some of the crew are struggling psychologically.

When reporters asked President Trump whether the deployment had lasted too long, he replied, "Not nearly long enough." Defense Secretary Hegseth said the reports had been distorted, while the Navy declined to release mental-health data on its personnel.

This war has been going on for more than a month, and the discussion has mostly been about oil prices and missiles. This time it is about the people.

伊利諾州自己出錢救水災，今年龍捲風破紀錄

來源：WTTW / Capitol News Illinois

伊利諾州州長普利茲克八月十四日簽下一項新法案，讓芝加哥的西北和西南「住宅權益計畫」推出為期三年的補助，幫淹水受損的屋主修房子。他當天也到受災的社區走了一趟。

伊利諾州今年已經出現超過兩百個龍捲風，是州內有紀錄以來最多，全美國只有面積大很多的德州追得上。

因為聯邦政府拒絕了州政府的救災補助申請，伊利諾只好自己想辦法幫民眾。

Illinois Governor Pritzker signed a new bill on August 14 letting Chicago's Northwest and Southwest Home Equity Programs offer a three-year rebate to help homeowners repair flood damage. He also toured storm-hit communities that day.

Illinois has recorded more than 200 tornadoes this year—the most in state history—with only far larger Texas keeping pace nationally.

After the federal government rejected the state's disaster-aid request, Illinois has had to find its own way to help residents.

小熊三比零完封紅雀，後援投手梅頓恐報銷

來源：Cubs Insider / MLB.com

芝加哥小熊八月十四日在主場以三比零完封聖路易紅雀。新來的先發投手霍姆斯第一次在主場上場，一口氣解決前十六名打者，全場只被敲出一支安打，退場時觀眾起立鼓掌。

鈴木誠也在六局擊出本季第二十一支全壘打，一棒送回三分。小熊戰績來到七十一勝五十一敗。

不過同一天，後援投手梅頓被診斷出膝蓋肌腱部分斷裂，這一季大概打不了了。球隊的隱憂，從先發移到了後援。

The Chicago Cubs shut out the St. Louis Cardinals 3-0 at home on August 14. In his first home start, new starting pitcher Clay Holmes retired the first 16 batters in a row and allowed just one hit all night, drawing a standing ovation as he left the field.

Seiya Suzuki hit his 21st home run of the season in the sixth inning, driving in three runs with one swing. The Cubs improved to 71 wins and 51 losses.

But on the same day, relief pitcher Phil Maton was diagnosed with a partially torn knee tendon and will likely miss the rest of the season. The team's worry has shifted from the rotation to the bullpen.

兵工廠線：中後衛只剩三人，阿爾特塔不急著補人

來源：CaughtOffside

兵工廠線——兵工廠隊教練阿爾特塔八月十四日在賽前記者會上說，中後衛薩利巴因為世界盃後的背傷要長期休養，另一名後衛廷伯還要好幾個星期才能回來。

這樣一來，能上場的資深中後衛只剩加布里埃爾、卡拉菲奧里和懷特三個人。不過阿爾特塔說得很清楚，球隊不會急著到轉會市場買人。

八月十六日，兵工廠要在卡地夫對上曼城，爭奪社區盾。

The Arsenal beat—Arsenal manager Mikel Arteta said at a pre-match press conference on August 14 that center-back William Saliba faces a long spell out with a back injury picked up after the World Cup, while defender Jurrien Timber is still several weeks from returning.

That leaves just Gabriel, Calafiori, and Ben White as available senior center-backs. But Arteta was clear—the club will not be rushing into the transfer market to buy someone.

On August 16, Arsenal face Manchester City in Cardiff for the Community Shield.
