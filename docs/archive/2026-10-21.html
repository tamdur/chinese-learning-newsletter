美國說和伊朗談和「非常困難」

來源：Al Jazeera

伊朗革命衛隊說，有兩艘油輪撞上伊朗放的水雷（放在水裡會爆炸的武器），起火燃燒。伊朗外交部長則說，美國不接受條件，海峽就不會打開。

俄羅斯外交部長拉夫羅夫最近也才去過伊朗。這場戰爭背後，俄國的影子愈來愈清楚。

US Secretary of State Marco Rubio said that reaching a peace agreement with Iran would be “extremely difficult.” As long as Iran keeps attacking ships in the Strait of Hormuz (the waterway into and out of the Persian Gulf), he said, the United States will keep up economic pressure on Iran. The Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps said two oil tankers had hit mines Iran had laid in the water (explosive weapons that detonate on contact) and caught fire. Iran’s foreign minister, for his part, said the strait will not reopen as long as the US refuses to accept Iran’s conditions. Russian Foreign Minister Sergei Lavrov had also recently visited Iran. Behind this war, Russia’s shadow is growing ever clearer.

羅馬尼亞新政府上任，結束五個月僵局

來源：Al Jazeera

羅馬尼亞新總理尼庫列斯庫和內閣21日正式上任，結束了五個多月的僵局（卡住、沒辦法前進的情況）。他以前是記者和外交官，不屬於任何政黨。國會投票時，他拿到280票，遠超過需要的233票。

羅馬尼亞在黑海旁邊，和烏克蘭相鄰，也是北約成員。新政府最大的難題是赤字（花的錢比收的錢多）。它必須在12月讓國會通過2027年的預算，不然可能又會垮台。

It must get parliament to pass the 2027 budget in December, or it could fall apart again.
