荷莫茲海峽危機：油價飆到一百二十六美元

來源：Dallas Fed / CNBC

伊朗跟美國和以色列的戰爭已經打了快四個星期了。因為打仗，荷莫茲海峽變得很危險，運油的船不敢走，結果全世界大約百分之二十的石油供應都被影響了。油價飆到每桶一百二十六美元，比戰爭以前貴了一倍多。

專家說，如果海峽關閉一整季，全球經濟成長會少將近三個百分點。科威特的官員也警告說，海峽如果一直不能用，結果會「非常非常嚴重」。亞洲國家受害最大，因為那邊百分之八十的石油是從海峽運來的。

The war between Iran, the US, and Israel has been going on for nearly four weeks now. Because of the fighting, the Strait of Hormuz has become very dangerous — oil tankers don't dare pass through it. As a result, roughly 20% of the world's oil supply has been disrupted. Oil prices have surged to $126 per barrel, more than double what they were before the war.

Experts say that if the strait stays closed for a full quarter, global economic growth would drop by nearly 3 percentage points. Kuwaiti officials have also warned that if the strait remains unusable, the consequences will be "extremely, extremely severe." Asian countries are the hardest hit, since 80% of their oil comes through the strait.

密西根狼獾隊大勝，甜蜜十六強對上阿拉巴馬

來源：ESPN

密西根大學男子籃球隊以九十五比七十二大勝聖路易大學，成功闖進甜蜜十六強！這是狼獾隊今年第三十三場勝利，他們也是全國第一種子。

球員Lendeborg得了二十五分，投籃命中率非常高。密西根隊整場比賽都在領先，最多領先二十五分。結果很漂亮。下一場他們要在芝加哥對上阿拉巴馬大學，球迷們都很期待。

The University of Michigan men's basketball team crushed Saint Louis University 95-72, advancing to the Sweet 16! This was the Wolverines' 33rd win this season, and they're also the nation's No. 1 seed.

Player Lendeborg scored 25 points with an incredibly high shooting percentage. Michigan led the entire game, at one point by as much as 25 points — a dominant result. Next up, they'll face the University of Alabama in Chicago, and fans can't wait.

AI工具被駭客入侵，偷走用戶密碼

來源：Hacker News / GitHub

一個叫LiteLLM的AI程式工具在網路上被駭客動了手腳。駭客把壞的程式碼放進去，結果任何人下載這個工具，電腦裡面的密碼、雲端金鑰和SSH密鑰都會被偷偷送到駭客的伺服器。

這次攻擊影響了兩個版本。網站上的工程人員發現後馬上提醒大家：如果你有下載，要趕快換掉所有密碼。這也是今年最嚴重的軟體供應鏈攻擊之一。專家提醒，從網站下載工具以前，一定要先檢查來源。

An AI programming tool called LiteLLM was tampered with by hackers on the internet. They inserted malicious code, and as a result, anyone who downloaded the tool had their passwords, cloud keys, and SSH keys secretly sent to the hackers' server.

The attack affected two versions. Engineers on the site immediately warned everyone: if you downloaded it, change all your passwords right away. This is also one of the most serious software supply chain attacks this year. Experts remind us to always verify the source before downloading tools from the web.

為什麼飛彈防禦這麼難？數學家說出答案

來源：Hacker News

一位數學家寫了一篇文章解釋，為什麼攔截飛彈這件事在數學上非常困難。他說，要把防禦武器分配給每一顆飛彈，這個問題是「NP完全」的，意思是就算用最快的電腦也很難算出最好的答案。

更麻煩的是，攻擊的一方可以放出假飛彈來騙人。結果防守的軍隊需要的攔截武器數量會暴增。文章說，美國目前只有四十四枚攔截飛彈，最多只能擋十一枚敵方飛彈。考慮到現在的伊朗戰爭，這也讓人擔心。

A mathematician wrote an article explaining why intercepting missiles is extremely difficult from a mathematical standpoint. He says that assigning defensive weapons to each incoming missile is an "NP-complete" problem — meaning even the fastest computers struggle to find the optimal solution.

What makes it worse is that the attacking side can launch decoy missiles to fool the defense. As a result, the defending military needs a dramatically larger number of interceptors. The article notes that the US currently has only 44 interceptor missiles, enough to stop just 11 enemy missiles at most. Given the ongoing Iran war, this is quite worrying.

大鹽湖底下藏著一大片淡水

來源：ScienceAlert

美國猶他州的大鹽湖，大家都知道它很鹹。但是科學家最近發現，湖底下面竟然藏著一大片淡水！這片淡水可能深達三到四公里，面積也非常大。

研究團隊用飛機上的電磁設備掃描湖底，結果發現地下的岩石裡面充滿了淡水。科學家說，這些水將來也許可以用來解決當地的問題。大鹽湖這幾年一直在縮小，乾掉的湖底會產生有毒的灰塵，如果能用地下淡水把那些地方弄濕，就能減少空氣污染。

Everyone knows that the Great Salt Lake in Utah is extremely salty. But scientists recently discovered that beneath the lake lies a massive freshwater reservoir! This freshwater could extend 3 to 4 kilometers deep, covering a very large area.

The research team used electromagnetic equipment mounted on aircraft to scan the lake bed, and found that underground rocks were saturated with freshwater. Scientists say this water could potentially be used to address local problems in the future. The Great Salt Lake has been shrinking in recent years, and the exposed lakebed produces toxic dust. If underground freshwater could be used to wet those areas, it would help reduce air pollution.
