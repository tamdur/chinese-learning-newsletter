美國對墨西哥加稅 八月一日開始

來源：Tax Foundation

美國政府宣布，八月一日起，對鄰國墨西哥的商品加收百分之三十的關稅（進口稅）。墨西哥是美國最大的貿易夥伴之一。

專家算出，今年這一連串的加稅，是美國從一九九三年以來最大的一次加稅，平均每個家庭一年要多付大約一千五百美元。這也讓大家更擔心物價會變貴。

The U.S. government announced that starting August 1st, it will impose a 30 percent tariff on goods from neighboring Mexico. Mexico is one of America's largest trading partners.

Experts estimate that this year's series of tariff increases represents the largest tariff hike the United States has implemented since 1993. On average, each household will have to pay approximately 1,500 dollars more per year. This has also heightened concerns about rising prices.

伊朗宣布關閉荷姆茲海峽 油價大漲

來源：Al Jazeera

伊朗宣布關閉荷姆茲海峽（世界最重要的石油通道），但是美軍不承認，說商船還在通行，雙方各說各話。這裡是全球大約五分之一石油的必經之路。

消息一出，油價大漲超過百分之四，每桶接近七十九美元。原本每天有十八到二十二艘船通過，現在只剩下六艘。

Iran announced the closure of the Strait of Hormuz, the world's most critical oil passage, but the U.S. military disputes this claim, saying commercial ships are still transiting. Both sides are telling different stories. The strait is the essential route for roughly one-fifth of global oil supplies.

Following the announcement, oil prices surged more than 4 percent, approaching $79 per barrel. Daily traffic through the strait has collapsed from 18 to 22 ships to just six.

AI需求「幾乎無限」 電力成最大難關

來源：CNBC

最近科技股大跌，很多人怕AI是泡沫。但是業界大老葛辛格（Pat Gelsinger）說，AI的需求「幾乎無限」，真正的難關已經不是晶片，而是電力夠不夠用。

有一家做光學零件的公司Lumentum，產品甚至已經賣到二〇二七年以後，好幾年的訂單都排滿了。

Tech stocks have slumped recently, prompting some to worry that AI is a bubble. But according to industry veteran Pat Gelsinger, AI demand is "almost unlimited." The real bottleneck isn't chips anymore—it's power supply.

Take Lumentum, an optical components manufacturer. Its products are already booked through 2027 and beyond, with several years' worth of orders completely filled.

芝加哥郊區立起「小巴勒斯坦路」路牌

來源：ABC7 Chicago

芝加哥郊區的橋景鎮（Bridgeview）哈林大道，掛上了「小巴勒斯坦路」的榮譽路牌。這裡住著全美國最大的巴勒斯坦裔社區之一。

原本因為政治因素被拖延，最後還是順利掛牌。路牌也紀念二〇二三年在仇恨攻擊中被殺害的六歲男孩瓦迪亞（Wadea）。

Harlem Avenue in Bridgeview, a Chicago suburb, now displays an honorary street sign reading "Little Palestine Road." The area is home to one of the largest Palestinian American communities in the country.

The sign had been delayed due to political considerations, but was ultimately successfully installed. It also honors Wadea, a six-year-old boy who was killed in a hate attack in 2023.

With the August 3rd trade deadline approaching, the Cubs are expected to aggressively pursue pitcher acquisitions to shore up their rotation. That same day, the team designated Woodford for assignment and reinstated Maton to the roster.

兵工廠線——阿森納（兵工廠）想簽下紐卡索的隊長布魯諾（Bruno Guimaraes）。巴西在世界盃出局後，布魯諾告訴球隊他想加入阿森納，讓紐卡「非常震驚」。
