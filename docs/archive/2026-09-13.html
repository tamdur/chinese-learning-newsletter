AI 老闆喊放慢腳步，兩大對手都點頭

來源：TechCrunch

人工智慧公司 Anthropic 的老闆艾莫岱，九月十二日發表文章〈我們必須放慢前沿（最新最強的技術）〉。他說模型變強的速度，已經比研究人員看懂它、管住它的速度還快。

他提的第一步，是讓公司外面的獨立檢查人員進來，拿到跟員工差不多的權限。不到一天，OpenAI 的奧特曼和馬斯克都公開贊成，奧特曼還說自己公司也會照做。

也有人寫公開信反問：如果是真心的，就先把自己的模型公開給大家看。

On September 12, Dario Amodei, CEO of AI company Anthropic, published an article titled "We Must Slow Down the AI Frontier." He argued that AI models are now advancing faster than researchers can understand and control them. His proposed first step: bring in independent external auditors with access equivalent to that of company employees.

In less than a day, both OpenAI's Sam Altman and Elon Musk publicly endorsed the proposal. Altman said his company would implement the same approach. Not everyone agreed, however. Critics published an open letter with a pointed question: if you're sincere about this, why not start by making your own models public for everyone to examine?

紅海海路與陸路同時斷，沙烏地石油三十年最少

來源：Rappler

葉門的胡塞武裝占領了丕林島。這個小島就在紅海南邊入口曼德海峽的中間，等於讓他們握住了海峽的兩邊。

同一時間，沙烏地阿拉伯的東西向輸油管被無人機炸中，只好停止輸送。這條管子長一千兩百公里，本來就是為了萬一荷莫茲海峽被封住，讓石油可以繞路走。

現在海路和陸路一起斷，沙烏地能送到市場的原油，掉到三十年來最少。

Yemen's Houthi forces have taken control of Perim Island, located at the entrance to the Strait of Mandab in the southern Red Sea—effectively giving them command over both sides of the passage. At the same time, Saudi Arabia's east-west oil pipeline has been struck by drones and forced to suspend operations. The 1,200-kilometer pipeline was originally built as a backup route in case the Strait of Hormuz were ever blocked, allowing oil to bypass the chokepoint. With both sea and land routes now cut off, the amount of Saudi crude reaching international markets has fallen to its lowest level in three decades.

AI 代理人當駭客，四十八國三百九十五個單位被入侵

來源：BleepingComputer

資安研究人員發現，一群說俄語的駭客，用 AI 代理人（會自己動手做事的程式）去攻擊別人的電腦。這些程式建在 OpenAI 的 Codex 和 DeepSeek 模型上面，人幾乎只要在旁邊看。

他們利用列印管理軟體 PaperCut 的漏洞，在四十八個國家、三百九十五個單位入侵了四百四十台主機，受害最多的是學校，共兩百零四家。最快的一次，從什麼都沒有到拿下整個網路，只花六小時。

Cybersecurity researchers have discovered a group of Russian-speaking hackers using AI agents—autonomous programs capable of acting independently—to attack computers. Built on OpenAI's Codex and DeepSeek models, these programs required almost no human intervention beyond observation. The attackers exploited a vulnerability in PaperCut, a print management software, to compromise 440 servers across 395 organizations in 48 countries. Schools bore the heaviest toll, accounting for 204 of the affected institutions. In the fastest attack, they went from initial access to full network takeover in just six hours.

伊利諾「臨終選擇法」上路，成中西部第一州

來源：Capitol News Illinois

一個為身心障礙者發聲的團體告上法院，想擋下伊利諾州的新法律。聯邦法官塔普不同意，這條「臨終選擇法」就在九月十二日開始實施，伊利諾成為中西部第一個讓醫師幫末期病人結束生命的州。

法律的條件很嚴：要兩位醫師分別判斷病人只剩不到六個月，病人要頭腦清楚、能自己吃藥、沒有被人逼，還得等上一段時間。醫師也不能自己先開口提。

A disability rights advocacy group sued to block Illinois's new law, but Federal Judge Tapp rejected the challenge. The End-of-Life Choices Act went into effect on September 12th, making Illinois the first Midwestern state to allow physicians to help terminally ill patients end their lives. The law's requirements are strict: two doctors must independently determine that the patient has fewer than six months to live; the patient must be mentally competent, capable of taking the medication themselves, acting without coercion, and willing to wait a specified period. Doctors also cannot raise the option first.
