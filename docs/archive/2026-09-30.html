美光今天公布財報，AI記憶體賣到缺貨

來源：Micron guidance / analyst previews

美國記憶體晶片大廠美光（Micron），今天下午要公布最新一季的成績單。公司預估這一季營收（賣東西收到的錢）大約500億美元，是去年同一季113億美元的四倍多。

賺這麼多，是因為AI。AI電腦除了要算得快的晶片，旁邊還要放一種叫HBM的高速記憶體，快速送資料。美光說，這種記憶體到2027年的貨都已經被訂光了。

大家發現，AI卡住的地方不只是處理器，記憶體也不夠用。今天的數字，會告訴市場AI熱潮還有多熱。

U.S. memory chip giant Micron is releasing its latest quarterly earnings report this afternoon. The company projects this quarter's revenue at roughly $50 billion, more than four times last year's same-quarter figure of $11.3 billion.

This surge is driven by AI. AI computers need not only fast-processing chips, but also high-speed memory called HBM that rapidly shuttles data. Micron says demand for this memory has booked out through 2027.

The industry is discovering that AI's bottleneck isn't just processing power—memory is equally constrained. Today's numbers will tell the market just how hot the AI rush remains.

密西根週六搶「小棕壺」，兩隊第100次對決

來源：Sports Illustrated

密西根大學美式足球隊上週輸給愛荷華大學，這個星期六（10月3日）要到客場挑戰明尼蘇達大學。

兩隊搶的獎盃叫「小棕壺」，是大學美式足球最老的獎盃比賽，今年剛好第100次。密西根過去贏了74次，已經連贏5場。

不過，明尼蘇達的四分衛（負責傳球的球員）Drake Lindsey去年傳出18次達陣（把球帶進得分區），被看好是NFL選秀的熱門人選。密西根可不能大意。

Michigan's football team lost to Iowa last week and travels to Minnesota this Saturday, October 3rd, for a conference matchup.

The two teams are competing for the Little Brown Jug, the oldest rivalry trophy in college football. This year marks their 100th meeting. Michigan has won 74 of those games historically and currently holds a five-game winning streak.

Minnesota quarterback Drake Lindsey poses a serious threat, however. He threw for 18 touchdowns last year and is regarded as a top prospect for the NFL draft. Michigan cannot take this challenge lightly.
