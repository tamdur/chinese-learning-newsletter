紐約機場飛機撞上消防車，兩名機員死亡

來源：Associated Press

星期天晚上，一架從加拿大飛來的客機在紐約 LaGuardia 機場降落後，撞上了一輛正在過馬路的消防車。塔台人員已經多次叫消防車停下來，但結果還是來不及。

飛機上有 72 名乘客和 4 名機組人員，超過 40 人送醫，其中 9 人傷勢嚴重。兩位年輕的機長不幸死亡。這是這個機場 34 年來第一次死亡事故，也讓超過 600 個航班被取消。

A passenger aircraft arriving from Canada struck a fire truck crossing the tarmac at LaGuardia Airport in New York on Sunday evening. Air traffic control had repeatedly ordered the fire truck to stop, but there was insufficient time to avoid the collision.

The aircraft carried 72 passengers and 4 crew members, with over 40 people transported to hospitals, 9 of them with serious injuries. Two young pilots tragically died. This was the first fatal accident at the airport in 34 years, and it resulted in the cancellation of over 600 flights.

川普宣布暫停打伊朗五天

來源：Reuters

美國和伊朗的戰爭已經打了 24 天。川普說雙方在談，所以軍隊會暫停打電廠和石油設備五天。但伊朗說根本沒有在談，也說川普是在操作石油市場。

川普的條件是：打開荷莫茲海峽、交出核武材料、不做核武器。結果伊朗反而威脅要在整個海灣放水雷。到目前為止，伊朗已有超過 3,200 人死亡，國會也在問軍隊什麼時候要走。

The United States and Iran have been at war for 24 days. Trump says the two sides are talking, so the military will pause attacks on power plants and oil facilities for five days. But Iran says there are no talks happening at all, and claims Trump is just manipulating the oil market.

Trump's conditions are: open the Strait of Hormuz, hand over nuclear materials, and stop making nuclear weapons. Instead, Iran is threatening to lay mines throughout the entire Gulf. So far, Iran has suffered over 3,200 deaths, and Congress is asking the military when it plans to leave.

小熊隊後天開打！新球員 Bregman 加入

來源：ESPN

芝加哥小熊隊 3 月 26 日在 Wrigley Field 開打，對手是國民隊。今年最大的消息是簽了 Alex Bregman，五年 1.75 億美元，他會打三壘。

不過王牌投手 Justin Steele 要到夏天才能回來，結果開幕戰由 Matthew Boyd 先發。年輕球員 Ballesteros 春訓打得很好，也讓大家很期待。分析員說小熊隊今年有機會拿分區冠軍！

The Chicago Cubs open on March 26 at Wrigley Field against the Nationals. The biggest news this year is signing Alex Bregman on a five-year, $175 million deal — he'll play third base.

However, ace pitcher Justin Steele won't return until summer, so Matthew Boyd gets the opening day start. Young player Ballesteros has looked great in spring training, getting everyone excited. Analysts say the Cubs have a shot at the division title this year!

伊朗在海峽放水雷，油價大漲

來源：Financial Times

美國情報單位確認，伊朗在荷莫茲海峽放了大約 12 顆水雷。全世界 20% 的石油從這裡經過，所以網站上到處都在討論油價。結果油價最高衝到每桶 126 美元。

更麻煩的是，美軍的掃雷船目前在馬來西亞，不在對的地方。從戰爭開始到現在，汽油已經漲了一加侖一塊美元。不過暫停消息一出來，股市也馬上漲了一千多點。

The U.S. intelligence community has confirmed that Iran placed approximately 12 naval mines in the Strait of Hormuz. Since 20% of the world's oil passes through here, people everywhere online are talking about oil prices. Oil prices spiked to as high as $126 per barrel.

To make matters worse, the U.S. Navy's minesweeper ships are currently in Malaysia and not in the right place. Since the war started, gas prices have risen by a dollar per gallon. However, once news of a pause came out, the stock market immediately jumped more than a thousand points.

全世界最老的洞穴畫在印尼被發現

來源：Nature

科學家在印尼的一個洞穴裡找到了一個手印，有 67,800 年那麼老，是全世界最古老的洞穴畫。研究員在 2015 年就發現了，結果最近才在 Nature 上發表。

這個手印很特別：手指尖被故意畫成像爪子的形狀，這在全世界也是第一次看到。這個發現比之前的記錄老了一萬多年，也幫我們了解古人怎麼從印尼到澳洲的。

Scientists discovered a handprint in a cave in Indonesia that is 67,800 years old, making it the oldest cave painting in the world. Researchers found it in 2015, but the results were only recently published in Nature.

The handprint is special: the fingertips were deliberately drawn to look like claws, which has never been seen anywhere in the world before. This discovery is more than 10,000 years older than the previous record, and it also helps us understand how ancient people traveled from Indonesia to Australia.
