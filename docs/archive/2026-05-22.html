Anthropic 發布 Claude Sonnet 4，主打自動工作能力

來源：Anthropic

Anthropic 公司在5月22日推出了新的 AI 模型 Claude Sonnet 4。這個模型能一次讀二十萬個字，特別適合讓 AI 自己完成多步工作。

GitHub 已經用它來做 Copilot 的寫程式工具。同時，美國政府也和 Anthropic 等公司談好，以後新模型上線前，政府可以先試用。

Anthropic released a new AI model, Claude Sonnet 4, on May 22nd. The model can process 200,000 characters at once, making it particularly well-suited for enabling AI to complete multi-step tasks autonomously. GitHub has already adopted it for Copilot's code-writing capabilities. Meanwhile, the U.S. government has reached an agreement with Anthropic and other companies allowing it to test new models before their public release.

GitHub 被駭客入侵，三千八百個內部程式庫被偷

來源：Hacker News

全球最大的程式碼平台 GitHub 被一個叫 TeamPCP 的駭客組織（就是網路壞人）攻擊。他們在一個 VS Code 工具裡藏了病毒，員工安裝後就中了。

大約三千八百個內部程式庫被偷走，駭客開價五萬美元出售。GitHub 說客戶的資料沒有受到影響。

GitHub, the world's largest code platform, was attacked by a hacker group called TeamPCP. They hid malware in a VS Code tool, and employees who installed it became infected. About 3,800 internal code repositories were stolen, and the hackers are asking $50,000 to sell them. GitHub says customer data was not affected.

芝加哥「布洛德維尤六人」抗議者全部撤告

來源：Block Club Chicago

去年在芝加哥郊區的 Broadview 移民拘留（關人的地方）中心外，六名抗議者被控阻擋移民官員。案件原本下週就要開庭。

但檢察官承認大陪審團過程有嚴重錯誤：第一次陪審團不同意起訴後，檢察官把反對的人換掉再投一次。法官因此撤銷了全部指控。

All six protesters in the "Broadview Six" case have had their charges dropped. Last year, outside the Immigration and Customs Enforcement detention center in Broadview, a suburb of Chicago, six protesters were charged with obstructing immigration officials. The case was set to go to trial next week. But prosecutors admitted there were serious errors in the grand jury process: after the first jury declined to indict, prosecutors replaced the dissenting jurors and voted again. The judge therefore dismissed all charges.

民主黨公布選舉檢討報告，黨主席卻說不認同

來源：NPR

美國民主黨終於公布了2024年大選的檢討報告，一共192頁。報告說黨需要更重視中部和南部的選民。

但很不尋常的是，黨主席 Ken Martin 同一天就公開批評這份報告，說「我不認同裡面的內容」，報告的摘要和結論甚至是空白的。

The US Democratic Party finally released its 192-page review of the 2024 election. The report says the party needs to pay more attention to voters in the Midwest and South. But in an unusual turn, party chair Ken Martin publicly criticized the report on the same day, saying "I don't agree with its contents." The report's summary and conclusions are even left blank.

密西根 Johnson Jr. 確定參加 NBA 選秀，七呎二新人加入

來源：Maize n Brew

密西根大學的大前鋒 Morez Johnson Jr. 宣布留在2026年 NBA 選秀，不回學校打球了。他幫密西根拿到了自1989年以來的第一座全國冠軍，預計在第17順位被選走。

好消息是，密西根已經找到替補：七呎二的中鋒 Moustapha Thiam 從 Cincinnati 轉來，他上季平均每場阻攻2.6次，全國排第七。

Michigan power forward Morez Johnson Jr. announced he will stay in the 2026 NBA Draft and not return to school. He helped Michigan capture its first national championship since 1989, and is projected to be selected around the 17th pick. The good news for Michigan: the program has already found a replacement. Seven-foot-two center Moustapha Thiam transferred from Cincinnati, averaging 2.6 blocks per game last season—seventh in the nation.

兵工廠公布英超冠軍遊行計畫

來源：Arsenal.com

兵工廠線——兵工廠確認將在5月31日下午兩點舉行英超冠軍遊行，路線經過北倫敦 Islington 區。這是球隊22年來第一次拿到聯賽冠軍，也是隊史第14座。

遊行安排在歐冠決賽的隔天——兵工廠將在5月30日於布達佩斯對戰巴黎聖日耳曼。如果贏了，遊行就是雙冠慶祝！

Arsenal beat — Arsenal has confirmed it will hold a Premier League championship parade on May 31st at 2 p.m., with the route passing through Islington in North London. This is the team's first league title in 22 years, and the 14th in club history. The parade is scheduled for the day after the Champions League final — Arsenal will face Paris Saint-Germain in Budapest on May 30th. If they win, the parade becomes a double-trophy celebration!
