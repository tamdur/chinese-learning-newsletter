美國三度空襲伊朗，波斯灣捲入戰火

來源：NPR

六月的停火已經破裂。伊朗攻擊一艘經過荷姆茲海峽的貨船後，美國第三次出手，打擊了大約一百四十個伊朗的軍事目標。

這一次伊朗不只回擊美軍基地，還向卡達、阿聯和巴林發射飛彈和無人機。卡達攔下攻擊，但碎片還是傷了三個人，其中一個是小孩。原本以為結束的戰爭，如今變成整個波斯灣的衝突。

June's ceasefire has now collapsed. After Iran attacked a cargo ship passing through the Strait of Hormuz, the United States launched its third strike, hitting approximately 140 Iranian military targets. This time, Iran not only retaliated against US military bases but also launched missiles and drones at Qatar, the UAE, and Bahrain. Qatar managed to intercept the attack, but debris still wounded three people, including a child. What was thought to be over has now engulfed the entire Persian Gulf.

荷姆茲海峽停運，全球油價震盪

來源：Al Jazeera

戰火重新燒起來後，沒有一艘大型油輪敢通過荷姆茲海峽。這條海峽運送全世界大約五分之一的石油，是最重要的關卡。

國際能源署警告，如果一直被封鎖，每天可能少掉一千四百萬桶石油，是石油市場史上最大的一次中斷。布蘭特原油的價格也跟著上下起伏，超過百分之五。

Since fighting resumed, no major oil tanker dares to cross the Strait of Hormuz. The strait carries roughly one-fifth of the world's oil supply and is the world's most critical chokepoint. The International Energy Agency warns that if it remains blocked, the world could lose as much as 14 million barrels of oil per day—the largest disruption in oil market history. Brent crude prices have fluctuated in response, with swings exceeding 5 percent.

美國參議員葛瑞姆病逝，享年七十一歲

來源：Washington Post

美國南卡羅來納州的共和黨參議員葛瑞姆，週六晚上因為突然生病過世，享年七十一歲。

他過世前幾天才剛去烏克蘭的基輔，和總統澤倫斯基見面。葛瑞姆在外交上一向強硬，戰爭期間去過烏克蘭十次。他本來還要競選連任，現在南卡州得重新找人接下這個位子。

Republican Senator Lindsey Graham of South Carolina died Saturday evening following a sudden illness, at the age of 71. Just days before his death, he had traveled to Kyiv, Ukraine, where he met with President Zelensky. Graham was known for taking a firm stance on foreign policy and visited Ukraine ten times during the war. He had been planning to run for reelection, but now South Carolina must find a replacement for his Senate seat.

蘋果控告OpenAI偷商業機密

來源：CNBC

蘋果公司控告OpenAI，說它偷走蘋果的商業機密，拿去做自己的AI硬體產品。

蘋果指出，OpenAI的硬體主管以前是蘋果的副總裁，他叫來面試的人把蘋果的零件帶去，「秀出來」給大家看。另一名前員工還偷走一台蘋果筆電。這兩家原本合作做ChatGPT和Siri的公司，如今鬧上法院。

Apple is suing OpenAI for allegedly stealing its trade secrets to develop AI hardware products. According to the suit, OpenAI's hardware chief—a former Apple vice president—asked job interview candidates to bring Apple components so he could show them to the team. A second former employee also made off with an Apple laptop. The two firms, which once worked together to bring ChatGPT to Siri, now find themselves in court against each other.

密西根美式足球簽下四星新秀馬吉歐

來源：247Sports

密西根大學美式足球隊在七月十一日簽下四星進攻線球員馬吉歐(Lincoln Mageo)。他來自加州，身高約一百九十公分，體重接近兩百九十磅。

他最後選了密西根，放棄華盛頓和猶他大學。這一簽，讓密西根二零二七年的新生陣容更強，這一批新人已經是全美最頂尖的隊伍之一。

The University of Michigan football team signed four-star offensive lineman Lincoln Mageo on July 11th. The California native stands approximately 190 centimeters tall and weighs close to 290 pounds. He ultimately chose Michigan over scholarship offers from Washington and the University of Utah. The signing strengthens Michigan's 2027 recruiting class, which is already ranked among the best in the nation.

阿森納將破隊史紀錄簽下佐利斯

來源：FootballTransfers

兵工廠線——阿森納即將用大約三千五百萬英鎊，從比利時的布魯日隊買下希臘邊鋒佐利斯(Christos Tzolis)。

這是球隊史上最貴的一筆簽約，也是有史以來身價最高的希臘球員。阿森納先把特羅薩德賣去土耳其的貝西克塔斯，空出位子，才談成這筆交易。佐利斯上一季踢了三十六場，進十七球、助攻二十三次。

Arsenal Beat — Arsenal is set to pay approximately £35 million to Belgian club Club Brugge for Greek winger Christos Tzolis. This will be the most expensive signing in club history and the highest transfer fee ever for a Greek player. Arsenal first sold Trossard to Turkish club Beşiktaş to make room before sealing the deal. Last season, Tzolis made 36 appearances, scoring 17 goals and providing 23 assists.
