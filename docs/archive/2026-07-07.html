哈瑪斯宣布解散加薩管理機構

來源：Al Jazeera

控制加薩快二十年的哈瑪斯，在七月六日宣布解散管理加薩的政府機構。負責人法拉也遞出辭呈，希望把權力交給一個由聯合國和美國支持的委員會來管理。

不過以色列說，這只是「拖延時間」，因為哈瑪斯沒有提到要放下武器，成員也還留在原本的位子上。

Hamas, which has controlled Gaza for nearly two decades, announced on July 6th the dissolution of the government institution administering Gaza. Farrah, the official in charge, also submitted his resignation, hoping to transfer power to a committee backed by the United Nations and the United States. However, Israel dismissed this as merely "stalling for time," arguing that Hamas made no mention of laying down arms and its members remain in their original positions.

白宮新規把關 GPT-5.6 上市

來源：White House OSTP

美國白宮訂出一套「自願」的人工智慧規則，第一次用政府的標準，來決定 OpenAI 最新的 GPT-5.6 能不能公開給大家使用。

按照規定，AI 公司要先讓政府試用最強的模型，最多三十天，通過以後才能上市。OpenAI 說，不希望這種做法變成以後的常態。

The U.S. White House has established a set of "voluntary" artificial intelligence rules that, for the first time, use government standards to determine whether OpenAI's latest GPT-5.6 can be publicly released. Under the regulations, AI companies must first allow the government to test the most powerful model for up to thirty days before it can be approved for market launch. OpenAI stated that it hopes this approach does not become standard practice going forward.

來源：Anthropic

人工智慧公司 Anthropic 在七月推出新模型 Claude Sonnet 5，說它是目前最會「自己動手做事」的版本。

更讓人注意的是，有報導指出 Anthropic 的營收已經超過對手 OpenAI。它的年營收在短短幾個月內，從九十億美元衝到三百億美元，成長速度非常驚人。

Anthropic, the artificial intelligence company, launched Claude Sonnet 5 in July, calling it their most capable version yet at autonomous action-taking. But what's even more striking is reporting that Anthropic's revenue has now surpassed competitor OpenAI. In just a few months, the company's annual revenue has jumped from $9 billion to $30 billion—a truly remarkable growth trajectory.

芝加哥深隧道防洪　今年第六次快滿

來源：Block Club Chicago

芝加哥用來防大水的「深隧道」系統，這幾天又快裝滿了。西南邊的麥庫克水庫今年已經第六次滿載，而在二○二一到二○二五這五年間，也才滿過五次。

七月三、四日兩天就下了將近三英吋的雨，差不多是一個月的雨量。專家說，這和氣候變遷有關。

Chicago's "Deep Tunnel" system for preventing major flooding is filling up again these days. The McCook Reservoir on the southwest side has already reached full capacity six times this year — compared to just five times during the entire five-year period from 2021 to 2025. On July 3rd and 4th alone, nearly three inches of rain fell, equivalent to roughly a month's typical rainfall. Experts say this trend is connected to climate change.
