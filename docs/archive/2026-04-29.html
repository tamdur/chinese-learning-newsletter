阿聯酋退出 OPEC，石油市場大震動

來源：CNBC

阿聯酋（阿拉伯聯合大公國）宣布五月一日起正式退出 OPEC（石油輸出國組織），結束將近六十年的會員身分。這是第一個退出的波斯灣大國。

阿聯酋貢獻了 OPEC 將近百分之十七的石油收入，它希望自己決定石油生產量，目標是每天五百萬桶。在美國對伊朗開戰的時候，這個消息讓全世界的石油市場很緊張。

The United Arab Emirates announced it will formally withdraw from OPEC (Organization of the Petroleum Exporting Countries) on May 1st, ending nearly six decades of membership. It is the first major Persian Gulf nation to leave the organization. The UAE has contributed roughly seventeen percent of OPEC's oil revenues, and it wants autonomy to set its own production targets, aiming for five million barrels per day. The announcement has rattled global oil markets at a time when the United States is at war with Iran.

Google 把 AI 交給美國軍方，Anthropic 拒絕

來源：TechCrunch

Google 同意讓美國國防部在機密（不公開的）網路上使用它的 AI 系統。另一家 AI 公司 Anthropic 則公開拒絕了同樣的要求，擔心 AI 會被用在大規模監控和自動武器上。

國防部因此把 Anthropic 列為「供應鏈風險」，兩邊已經打上了官司。同時，Google 內部有九百多名員工簽名反對這筆交易。

Google has agreed to allow the U.S. Department of Defense to use its AI systems on classified (non-public) networks. Another AI company, Anthropic, publicly refused the same request, fearing that AI could be deployed for mass surveillance and autonomous weapons. The Defense Department subsequently listed Anthropic as a "supply chain risk," and the two sides are now in litigation. Meanwhile, more than nine hundred Google employees have signed a letter opposing the deal.

The war between the United States and Iran has entered its sixtieth day. Iran's Foreign Minister Araghchi traveled to St. Petersburg, Russia, to meet with President Putin. Putin stated that Russia would do everything in its power to help end this war. Meanwhile, Pakistan has been mediating between the two sides, but they remain at odds over the Strait of Hormuz issue, and peace has not yet arrived.

美國最高法院審理移民保護案

來源：Roll Call

美國最高法院今天聽取了一個重要案件：川普政府要取消大約三十三萬海地人和四千敘利亞人的「臨時保護身分」（TPS）。有了這個身分，他們可以留在美國工作，不會被送回去。

政府說法院管不了這件事，但反對的人認為政府沒有照規定來做。結果可能在六月底公布。

The U.S. Supreme Court heard arguments today in a significant case: the Trump administration's push to rescind "Temporary Protected Status" (TPS) for approximately 330,000 Haitians and 4,000 Syrians. With this status, they can remain in the United States, work legally, and avoid deportation. The government argues the courts have no jurisdiction over this matter, while opponents contend the administration failed to follow proper procedures. A ruling is expected by late June.
