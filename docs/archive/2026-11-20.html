川普、習近平、普丁在深圳首次三方會談

來源：Arab News（美聯社）

美國總統川普、中國領導人習近平和俄羅斯總統普丁，19日在深圳開會。這是三個人第一次一起坐下來談，地點在亞太經合會（APEC）的場邊。

會前沒有先定好要談什麼，大家猜重點是烏克蘭戰爭和核子武器。到目前為止，還沒有公開任何結果。

烏克蘭和歐洲都不在場。他們很擔心，大國會不問他們，就自己決定烏克蘭的未來。

U.S. President Donald Trump, Chinese leader Xi Jinping, and Russian President Vladimir Putin met in Shenzhen on the 19th. It was the first time the three of them sat down together, on the sidelines of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) summit. No agenda had been set in advance, but people expect the main topics to be the war in Ukraine and nuclear weapons. So far, no results have been made public. Ukraine and Europe were not in the room. They are worried that the major powers will decide Ukraine's future among themselves without asking them.

芝加哥議會45比5通過 要自己查市府帳本

來源：WTTW News

芝加哥市議會18日開特別會議，用45票對5票，同意請外面的公司，獨立檢查市政府的財務。

議員們不太相信市長強生（Brandon Johnson）給的數字。之前他們已經否決（投票不同意）市長向大公司加稅的計畫，2027年的預算因此還差8億8200萬美元，年底前一定要補上。

45比5這麼大的差距，說明議會幾乎一致不信任市長的帳。預算之爭，已經變成信任問題。

On the 18th, the Chicago City Council held a special meeting and voted 45 to 5 to hire outside firms to independently audit the city government's finances. Council members have little faith in the figures Mayor Brandon Johnson has provided. They had already voted down the mayor's plan to raise taxes on large corporations, which leaves the 2027 budget $882 million short. That gap must be closed before the end of the year.

A margin of 45 to 5 suggests the council is almost unanimous in its distrust of the mayor's books. The budget fight has become a question of trust.

Anthropic股票上市第一天 收盤漲17%

來源：The National News

AI公司Anthropic的股票，19日在那斯達克（Nasdaq）開始買賣，代號ANT。上市價是每股83美元，一共拿到813億美元，是史上最大的上市案。

一開盤，股價就跳到111.5美元，漲了大約34%。但是後來越走越低，最後收在97.17美元，比上市價高約17%。

這一天有超過8億4600萬股換手，打破SpaceX的紀錄。有人認為，股價後來往下掉，是因為投資人擔心AI股票已經太貴了。

Shares of Anthropic, the AI company, began trading on the Nasdaq on the 19th under the ticker ANT. The IPO price was $83 a share, raising a total of $81.3 billion, the largest IPO in history. At the opening bell the stock jumped to $111.50, up about 34%. It then slid steadily and closed the day at $97.17, still about 17% above the IPO price. More than 846 million shares changed hands that day, breaking SpaceX's record. Some observers think the stock's late-session fall reflected investor worries that AI stocks had already become too expensive.

熊隊休息回來 靠防守迎戰聖徒

來源：Sports Illustrated

芝加哥熊隊7勝2敗，休息一週後，星期天要在主場軍人球場（Soldier Field）對上紐奧良聖徒隊。聖徒隊只有3勝6敗。

熊隊已經連贏四場，最大的武器是防守。在「第三檔」（美式足球每一輪的第三次進攻機會）擋住對手這件事上，熊隊的數據是22.8%，排名全聯盟第一。

這代表熊隊現在不只會得分，也很會擋人。

The Chicago Bears, now 7-2, are back from their bye week and host the New Orleans Saints this Sunday at Soldier Field. The Saints sit at 3-6. The Bears have won four straight games, and their biggest weapon right now is defense. On third downs (the third attempt in each series of downs), opponents have converted just 22.8% of the time, the best mark in the league. In other words, the Bears are not only scoring points. They are also very good at keeping opponents off the field.

兵工廠作客紐卡索前 阿特塔說明傷兵

來源：Flashscore

兵工廠線——兵工廠星期六要去紐卡索比賽。教練阿特塔（Mikel Arteta）在賽前記者會上，說明了薩卡（Saka）、厄德高（Odegaard）和馬杜埃克（Madueke）三位球員的身體狀況。

兵工廠現在是英超第一名，已經連贏五場，包括在客場1比0贏了利物浦。

不過，兩位主力中後衛在國家隊比賽期間受傷，還不能上場。下星期一，球隊還要在歐冠主場迎戰多特蒙德。

The Arsenal beat — Arsenal travel to Newcastle on Saturday. At his pre-match press conference, manager Mikel Arteta gave an update on the fitness of Saka, Ødegaard and Madueke. Arsenal are now top of the Premier League after five straight wins, including a 1-0 away victory over Liverpool. However, two of their first-choice centre-backs picked up injuries during international duty and are still unavailable. Next Monday, the team also host Borussia Dortmund in the Champions League.
