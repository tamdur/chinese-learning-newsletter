藍色起源火箭在測試中爆炸

來源：CNBC

美國太空公司藍色起源（Blue Origin）的 New Glenn 火箭，在佛羅里達州進行引擎測試時爆炸。這枚高 57 公尺的火箭在點火幾秒後就被火球吞沒（完全包住），所幸沒有人受傷。

這是自 2016 年 SpaceX 火箭爆炸以來，該基地最嚴重的事故。藍色起源原本計畫今年發射 12 次，現在計畫恐怕要大幅延後。

Blue Origin's New Glenn rocket exploded during an engine test in Florida. The 57-meter-tall rocket was engulfed in flames just seconds after ignition, though no one was injured. This is the most serious incident at the facility since SpaceX's 2016 rocket explosion. Blue Origin had planned 12 launches this year, but the schedule will likely be significantly delayed.

AI代理人正在改變全球工作市場

來源：Economy Middle East

一份新報告指出，到 2026 年底，超過八成的企業軟體都會加入 AI 代理人（能自己做決定的程式）。會用 AI 工具的人，薪水比不會的人高出 56%。

更讓人擔心的是，全球大約兩成的公司會用 AI 來減少中間管理層，超過一半的主管職位可能消失。這不再只是工廠的問題，而是辦公室的問題了。

A new report indicates that by the end of 2026, over 80% of enterprise software will incorporate AI agents — programs capable of making their own decisions. People who can use AI tools earn 56% more than those who cannot. More troubling is that roughly one-fifth of companies globally plan to use AI to reduce middle management, with over half of supervisory positions potentially disappearing. This is no longer just a factory problem — it's now an office problem.

加拿大轉向瑞典買軍機，不再只靠美國

來源：Euronews

加拿大總理卡尼（Mark Carney）宣布，將向瑞典的 Saab 公司購買一批軍用偵察（監視）飛機，而不是選擇美國的 Boeing 或 L3Harris。

卡尼說，加拿大跟美國的關係已經永久改變，必須在經濟和國防上找更多夥伴。加拿大也在考慮減少原本向美國訂購的 88 架 F-35 戰鬥機，改買瑞典的 Gripen。

Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney announced that Canada will purchase a fleet of military reconnaissance and surveillance aircraft from Sweden's Saab company, rather than choosing American options from Boeing or L3Harris. Carney stated that Canada's relationship with the United States has fundamentally changed, and Canada must seek additional partners in both economic and defense matters. Canada is also considering reducing its original order of 88 F-35 fighter jets from the United States, opting instead for Sweden's Gripen aircraft.

芝加哥市議會通過聯合中心七十億開發案減稅

來源：Chicago Sun-Times

芝加哥市議會投票通過，給聯合中心（United Center）附近的「1901 計畫」五千五百萬美元的減稅。這個七十億美元的大計畫，要把停車場變成有住宅、商店、旅館和六千人音樂廳的新區域。

第一期預計 2026 年開工， 2028 年完成，可帶來近兩千個建築工作和 600 個長期工作。這是芝加哥西區歷史上最大的開發案之一。

Chicago's City Council voted to approve a $55 million tax break for the "1901 Project" near the United Center. The $7 billion mega-development will transform a parking lot into a new neighborhood with residential units, retail shops, a hotel, and a 6,000-seat concert hall. The first phase is expected to break ground in 2026 and finish by 2028, bringing nearly 2,000 construction jobs and 600 permanent positions. It ranks among the largest development projects in Chicago's West Side history.

密西根籃球簽下三名轉學好手，衛冕之路起步

來源：247Sports

衛冕（保衛冠軍）的密西根大學籃球隊，從轉學門戶（transfer portal）簽下三名新球員：中鋒 Thiam、前鋒 Estrella 和 Reed。這批轉學生排名全國第九。

其中 Thiam 上季平均得 12.8 分、7.1 個籃板。去年密西根靠轉學生拿下冠軍，今年顯然想再來一次。

Michigan's defending national champion basketball team has signed three transfer portal players: center Thiam, forward Estrella, and Reed. This transfer class ranks ninth nationally. Thiam averaged 12.8 points and 7.1 rebounds last season. Having won the championship last year with transfers, Michigan clearly intends to repeat the formula.

兵工廠女隊奪下史上首屆 FIFA 女子冠軍盃

來源：Arsenal.com

兵工廠線——兵工廠女隊在加時賽以 3比2 擊敗巴西的 Corinthians，成為 FIFA 女子冠軍盃史上第一個冠軍。Foord 在加時第 104 分鐘踢進致勝球。

這讓兵工廠的賽季更加輝煌：男隊已拿下英超冠軍，明天還要在布達佩斯跟 PSG 爭歐冠冠軍，挑戰歷史性的雙冠王。

Arsenal's women's team defeated Brazil's Corinthians 3-2 in extra time to become the first-ever champion of the FIFA Women's Club World Cup. Foord scored the winning goal in the 104th minute of extra time. The victory caps off a historic season for Arsenal: the men's team has already claimed the Premier League title, and tomorrow they face PSG in Budapest for the Champions League crown, chasing a historic domestic and European double.
