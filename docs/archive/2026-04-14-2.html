IMF算帳：川普關稅一年，美國稅率升到 50 年來最高

來源：Council on Foreign Relations

川普的「解放日」關稅政策滿一年了。國際貨幣基金組織（IMF）算出，美國平均關稅已經升到 11%，是 1970 年代以來最高。

關稅讓美國物價多漲了約 0.5 到 0.75 個百分點，全球貿易成長也從去年的 5.1% 掉到今年的 2.8%。

來源：Council on Foreign Relations

Trump's "Liberation Day" tariff policy has now been in effect for one year. The International Monetary Fund has calculated that the average U.S. tariff rate has climbed to 11%—the highest level since the 1970s.

The tariffs have added approximately 0.5 to 0.75 percentage points to U.S. price inflation, while global trade growth has slowed from 5.1% last year to 2.8% this year.

Source: Council on Foreign Relations
