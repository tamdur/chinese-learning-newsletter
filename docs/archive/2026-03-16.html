普渡大學爆冷擊敗密西根，拿下十大聯盟冠軍

來源：Yahoo Sports

密西根今年打得超好，拿到全國第三名的排名，但在十大聯盟錦標賽決賽卻被普渡爆冷打敗，比數 72-80。

下半場一開始，普渡就打出 22-8 的進攻，完全控制了比賽。密西根的替補球員整場只得 8 分，跟整季的表現差很多。不過密西根戰績 31勝 3敗，今天選拔日應該還是能拿到一號種子。

Michigan had an outstanding season this year, earning the nation's third ranking, but fell to Purdue in a stunning upset at the Big Ten Tournament final, 72-80.

Early in the second half, Purdue exploded on a 22-8 run that completely controlled the game. Michigan's bench players scored just 8 points for the entire game, far below their season average. Still, with a 31-3 record, Michigan should secure a No. 1 seed when Selection Sunday rolls around.

OpenAI 拿下美國國防部兩億美元合約，Anthropic 被排除

來源：The Intercept / Reuters

OpenAI 跟美國國防部簽了大約兩億美元的合約，要幫軍方做 AI 相關的工作。這是 OpenAI 第一次接機密（秘密的）軍事案子。

另一邊，Anthropic 就沒這麼幸運了。國防部長要求 Anthropic 取消禁止大規模監控和自動武器的限制，但 Anthropic 拒絕了，結果被禁止參加聯邦合約。這件事讓大家開始討論：AI 公司到底能不能用合約來限制政府怎麼用 AI？

OpenAI has signed a contract worth approximately $200 million with the U.S. Department of Defense to provide AI-related work for the military. This marks OpenAI's first involvement in classified military projects.

Anthropic, meanwhile, has not been as fortunate. The Secretary of Defense demanded that Anthropic remove restrictions prohibiting large-scale surveillance and autonomous weapons, but Anthropic refused. As a result, it has been barred from federal contracts. This situation has sparked discussion about whether AI companies can use contractual terms to limit how governments use their AI systems.

摩根大通：美國經濟衰退機率升到 35%

來源：J.P. Morgan Research / Bloomberg

摩根大通（J.P. Morgan）最近說，美國在 2026 年進入經濟衰退的機率有 35%。雖然不是最可能的情況，但風險比以前高了。

最大的問題是關稅（tariffs）。新的關稅大概會讓美國人多花 4300 億美元，等於 GDP 的 1.4%。企業因為貿易戰不確定，不太敢請新人。股市今年幾乎沒漲，只有能源和公用事業還在漲。

JPMorgan Chase recently said there's a 35% chance the U.S. enters an economic recession in 2026. While it's not the most likely scenario, the risk is higher than before.

The biggest problem is tariffs. New tariffs will probably cost Americans an additional $430 billion, equivalent to 1.4% of GDP. Companies are hesitant to hire new workers because of trade war uncertainty. The stock market has barely risen this year, with only energy and utilities continuing to climb.

小熊隊鈴木誠也 WBC 膝傷，開幕戰出賽成疑

來源：ESPN / Chicago Tribune

芝加哥小熊隊的外野手鈴木誠也（Seiya Suzuki）在世界棒球經典賽中受傷了。他滑進二壘的時候傷到右膝，之後就離開了比賽。

鈴木這次 WBC 表現不錯，打了 2 支全壘打。小熊隊 3 月 26 日就要開幕了，不到兩個禮拜。球隊的王牌投手 Steele 也在養傷，如果鈴木也不能上場，小熊的開季就有點麻煩了。

Chicago Cubs outfielder Seiya Suzuki suffered a knee injury during the World Baseball Classic. He injured his right knee while sliding into second base and left the game.

Suzuki had been performing well in the WBC, hitting 2 home runs. The Cubs' opening day is March 26th — less than two weeks away. The team's ace pitcher Steele is also recovering from injury, so if Suzuki can't play, the Cubs could face some serious trouble to start the season.

史上最亮快速電波爆發，來自 1.3 億光年外

來源：Nature / Science

科學家偵測到有史以來最亮的快速電波爆發（fast radio burst）。這個訊號只持續了 0.2 秒，但釋放的能量等於太陽 4 天的總量。

它來自 1.3 億光年以外一個叫 NGC 4141 的星系。最奇怪的是，科學家監測了 6 年，這個訊號再也沒出現過。大家本來以為這種爆發會重複發生，所以這次的發現讓天文學家很驚訝。

The brightest fast radio burst ever detected has been captured by scientists. The signal lasted only 0.2 seconds, but released energy equivalent to four days' worth of the sun's total output.

It came from a galaxy called NGC 4141 located 130 million light-years away. What's strangest is that scientists monitored it for 6 years, and the signal never appeared again. Astronomers had expected these bursts to repeat, so this discovery came as a surprise.
