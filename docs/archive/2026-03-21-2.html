法國軍艦位置被跑步軟體公開

來源：Le Monde / Hacker News

法國海軍的航空母艦「Charles de Gaulle」正在地中海做軍事工作。結果，一個船上的軍隊人員在甲板（船的上面）上跑了七公里，還把跑步記錄放到 Strava 網站上。這個網站會公開 GPS 位置，所以大家都看得到軍艦在哪裡。

法國的記者也用衛星照片來確認。軍隊說這不合規定。這也不是第一次了——Strava 已經三次讓軍事地點被發現了。

The French Navy's aircraft carrier "Charles de Gaulle" was conducting military operations in the Mediterranean Sea. As it turned out, a soldier on board ran seven kilometers on the deck (the top part of the ship) and then posted the running record to the Strava website. This website publicly displays GPS locations, so everyone could see where the warship was.

French journalists also used satellite photos to confirm the location. The military said this violated regulations. This wasn't the first time either — Strava has already revealed military positions three times.

密西根大學大勝開場 NCAA 比賽

來源：Michigan Athletics

一號種子隊密西根大學以 101 比 81 打敗了 Howard 大學。這是 NCAA 大學籃球比賽的第一場。密西根的球員 Morez Johnson Jr. 得了 21 分，投了八次全部進，也搶了 10 個籃板。

全隊的投球命中率是 67.2%，打破了隊上的比賽記錄。結果也是密西根自 1992 年以來第一次在大學比賽得到一百分以上。下一場對手是 Georgia 或 Saint Louis。

The No. 1 seed University of Michigan dominated Howard University with a score of 101-81 in the first game of the NCAA college basketball tournament. Michigan player Morez Johnson Jr. led the way with 21 points, hitting all eight of his shot attempts while grabbing 10 rebounds.

The team shot 67.2% from the field, breaking the team's game record. The result also marks the first time since 1992 that Michigan has scored over 100 points in a college game. Their next opponent will be either Georgia or Saint Louis.

馬斯克被判對 Twitter 投資人說謊

來源：CNBC

美國的法院做出了一個大的判決。九個人的小組認定 Elon Musk 在 2022 年買 Twitter 的時候，在網站上說了不對的話。他說這個交易「先停下來」，結果 Twitter 的股票就掉了大約 40%。

法院說他可能要賠大約 26 億美金。這也是很少見的事：一個大公司的老闆被一般人組成的小組判有錯。 Musk 說他會上訴（再請更高的法院看）。

A U.S. court has made a major ruling. A jury of nine people determined that Elon Musk made false statements on the platform when he was buying Twitter in 2022. He said the deal should "pause," and as a result Twitter's stock price dropped by about 40%.

The court said he may have to pay about $2.6 billion. This is also a rare event: a jury of ordinary people has found the boss of a major company at fault. Musk said he would appeal (ask a higher court to review the case).

加拿大人越來越窮？十年的數字說明了

來源：Marginal Revolution

加拿大的經濟看起來還好，但每個人平均分到的錢其實十年來只長了 3.2%。同一段時間，美國長了 20.2%。加拿大也第一次掉到了全世界的平均以下。

為什麼？因為人口長得太快了。外來人員從不到一百萬長到三百萬。全國的錢還是在長，但分到每個人就少了。結果，去食物銀行（免費拿食物的地方）的人也多了一倍。

Canada's economy looks fine on the surface, but the average income per person has grown only 3.2% over the past decade. In the same period, the US grew 20.2%. Canada has also fallen below the global average for the first time.

Why? Because the population grew too fast. Foreign-born residents increased from under one million to three million. The country's total wealth still grows, but when divided among more people, each person gets less. As a result, the number of people visiting food banks (places to get free food) has doubled.

瘧疾蟲裡有會自己轉的小水晶

來源：ScienceDaily

瘧（一種很多人得的病）疾每年在全世界讓六十萬人死。美國的研究員發現，瘧疾的蟲在吃紅血球的時候，會做出很小的水晶。這些水晶會自己一直轉，就像小小的火箭一樣。

研究員說，水晶轉的力量來自一種化學反應，跟火箭用的也一樣。這是生物裡第一次找到這種東西。因為只有這個蟲才有，工廠以後也許能做出只打蟲、不傷人的新藥。

Malaria, a disease that many people get, kills 600,000 people every year around the world. American researchers discovered that the malaria parasite creates tiny crystals when it eats red blood cells. These crystals spin on their own, just like little rockets.

Researchers say the spinning force of the crystals comes from a chemical reaction, the same kind that rockets use. This is the first time scientists have found this kind of thing in a living organism. Because only this parasite has it, factories may be able to make new medicines in the future that kill the bug without harming people.
