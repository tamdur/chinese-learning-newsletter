委內瑞拉連兩次強震　至少兩百多人死亡

來源：CNN

六月二十四日，南美洲的委內瑞拉北部發生兩次大地震，第一次是七點二級，過了三十九秒又來了七點五級。這種「雙胞震」很少見。

目前至少兩百三十五人死亡，四千多人受傷。因為當地的房子很舊又不夠堅固，專家擔心死亡人數可能超過一萬人。美國已經派出救援隊前去幫忙。

On June 24th, two major earthquakes struck northern Venezuela in South America. The first was magnitude 7.2, followed just 39 seconds later by a 7.5. This kind of "twin quake" is extremely rare.

At least 235 people have died so far, with over 4,000 injured. Because many local buildings are old and poorly constructed, experts worry the death toll could exceed 10,000. The United States has already dispatched rescue teams to assist.

美國下令　Anthropic關閉外國人使用頂尖AI

來源：Fortune

美國商務部命令人工智慧（就是會思考的電腦程式）公司Anthropic，不可以讓任何外國人使用它最強的兩個模型Fable5和Mythos5。

這兩個模型六月九日才推出，三天後就被叫停。政府把AI當成武器來管，這是第一次，以後外國人想用美國頂尖AI可能更難了。

The U.S. Department of Commerce has ordered artificial intelligence company Anthropic to prevent foreign users from accessing its two most powerful models, Fable5 and Mythos5.

The two models launched on June 9th and were shut down to foreign users just three days later. This marks the first time the government has treated AI as a weapon requiring export controls. Going forward, foreigners seeking access to America's cutting-edge AI systems may face even stricter barriers.

伊朗要求過海峽的船買保險

來源：Fortune

伊朗規定，所有要通過荷莫茲海峽的船，都要向它新成立的海峽管理局買保險，還要走指定的路線。

前六十天保險免費，但專家認為以後會變成過路費，每艘船可能要付兩百萬美元。這條海峽是全球石油運輸的重要通道，這個做法讓各國很擔心。

Iran has mandated that all ships passing through the Strait of Hormuz must purchase insurance from its newly established strait management authority and follow designated shipping routes.

Insurance is free for the first sixty days, but experts believe it will eventually become a transit toll, potentially costing each vessel two million dollars. Since the strait is a critical global oil shipping corridor, this move has alarmed nations worldwide.

才半年　伊利諾州龍捲風就破紀錄

來源：CBS Chicago

今年才過了一半，伊利諾州（芝加哥所在的州）就出現了一百七十二個龍捲風，打破了二○二四年全年一百四十二個的紀錄。

這個數字是平均的兩倍多，也是全美國最多的。氣象專家說天氣型態改變是主因，也讓大家想問：暖化是不是把龍捲風帶到了中西部？

Halfway through the year, Illinois—the state where Chicago is located—has already experienced 172 tornadoes, breaking the full-year record of 142 set in 2024.

This figure is more than double the average and the highest in the entire United States. Weather experts say changing weather patterns are the primary cause, which raises an obvious question: has global warming brought more tornadoes to the Midwest?

史旺森一天打回十一分　寫下小熊隊紀錄

來源：ESPN

芝加哥小熊隊的游擊手史旺森，在同一天的兩場比賽裡，打回十一分，幫球隊連贏紐約大都會隊兩場。

他打破了名人堂球星桑托在一九七○年創下的一天十分紀錄。三場比賽他總共打回十五分，是小熊隊百年來最厲害的表現。

Chicago Cubs shortstop Dansby Swanson drove in eleven runs across two games in a single day, helping the team sweep the New York Mets with consecutive victories.

He broke Hall of Famer Ron Santo's single-day record of ten RBIs, set in 1970. Across three games, Swanson drove in a total of fifteen runs, marking the most dominant performance in Cubs franchise history.

兵工廠正式買下後衛辛卡皮耶

來源：Arsenal.com

兵工廠線——阿森納隊花了三千四百五十萬英鎊，把厄瓜多的左後衛辛卡皮耶正式買下來，合約到二○三一年。

這位二十四歲的球員本來是用租借的方式來隊，上個球季出賽三十九場，幫球隊拿下二十二年來第一座英超冠軍，表現很好，所以球隊決定把他留下來。

The Arsenal beat — Arsenal have officially signed Ecuador's left-back Piero Hincapié for £34.5 million on a deal through 2031.

The 24-year-old initially joined on loan and made 39 appearances last season, helping the club secure their first Premier League title in 22 years. Impressed by his performances, Arsenal decided to make the move permanent.
