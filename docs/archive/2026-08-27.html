OpenAI 自製晶片 說比輝達更省電

來源：CNBC

在美國的晶片研討會上，OpenAI 公布了自己設計的第一款晶片，名字叫「墨西哥辣椒」（Jalapeño）。他們說這款晶片在回答問題時，每一度電能做的事，是輝達（Nvidia）Blackwell 系統的一點五到一點九倍。

不過這些數字是 OpenAI 自己測的，外面還沒有別人做過同樣的測試。也有分析師說，這樣比並不公平，因為新晶片用了更新的記憶體。這款晶片今年底才會少量上線。

At a chip conference in the United States, OpenAI unveiled its first proprietary chip, called "Jalapeño." The company claims this chip can accomplish 1.5 to 1.9 times more work per watt of electricity than Nvidia's Blackwell system when answering questions.

However, these figures come from OpenAI's own testing, and no independent verification has been conducted yet. Some analysts argue the comparison is unfair because the new chip uses more advanced memory technology. The chip won't begin limited rollout until the end of this year.

芝加哥市長簽令 嚴管資料中心

來源：City of Chicago

芝加哥市長強生（Brandon Johnson）本週簽下行政命令。以後有人要在市內蓋資料中心（裝滿電腦、幫人工智慧算資料的大房子），市府會用更嚴的標準審查空氣和噪音。他也請市議會暫停批准新的資料中心。

市長說，這是為了乾淨的空氣和水，還有大家的電費。但市府現在正缺錢，而資料中心本來是新稅金的來源之一。

Chicago Mayor Brandon Johnson signed an executive order this week. Going forward, anyone proposing to build a data center in the city—large facilities packed with computers that process data for artificial intelligence—will face tighter city review of air quality and noise impacts. He also asked the city council to suspend approvals of new data centers.

The mayor cited protection of clean air and water, along with controlling residents' electricity costs, as justification. But the city currently faces a budget shortfall, and data centers had been expected to become a new source of tax revenue.

Meta 和解 伊利諾州可拿七億多美元

來源：Chicago Sun-Times

美國四十七個州控告臉書和 Instagram 的母公司 Meta，說它故意把軟體設計得讓青少年上癮（停不下來），傷害他們的心理健康。二十六日雙方和解，Meta 要付大約一百七十億美元，其中伊利諾州可拿到將近七億六千八百萬美元。

除了賠錢，Meta 也答應為青少年的帳號預設每天的使用時間上限，並加強年齡確認。

Forty-seven U.S. states sued Meta, the parent company of Facebook and Instagram, alleging it deliberately designed its software to addict teenagers and damage their mental health. On the 26th, the two sides reached a settlement in which Meta will pay approximately $17 billion, with Illinois receiving nearly $768 million.

In addition to the financial settlement, Meta also agreed to implement default daily usage time limits for teen accounts and strengthen age verification.

傑克森洞會議登場 等聯準會新主席開口

來源：Tech Times

美國聯準會（負責決定全國利率的機構）一年一度的傑克森洞會議，二十七日開始。新主席沃許（Kevin Warsh）將在二十八日星期五上午，發表上任後第一場重要演講。

七月的會議上有三位委員主張升息，市場現在認為九月升息的機會大約是三分之一。他怎麼說，會直接影響芝加哥人的房貸和借錢成本。

The U.S. Federal Reserve—the agency responsible for setting the nation's interest rates—is holding its annual Jackson Hole conference, which began on the 27th. New Fed Chair Kevin Warsh will deliver his first major speech since taking office on Friday morning, the 28th.

Three committee members advocated for a rate hike at the July meeting, and markets now estimate roughly a one-in-three chance of an increase in September. What Warsh says will directly affect Chicagoans' mortgage rates and borrowing costs.

密大籃球隊歐洲熱身賽 開局兩連敗

來源：The Detroit News

上一季拿下全國冠軍的密西根大學籃球隊，這個月到立陶宛打熱身賽。這是新教練博因頓（Mike Boynton）接手後的頭幾場比賽：二十三日以八十四比九十六輸給立陶宛國家隊，隔天又以七十七比八十二輸給對方的二隊。

球隊今天在克羅埃西亞打第三場。冠軍陣容幾乎全部留下，二年級的麥肯尼和大一新生麥考伊都得了十幾分。

Michigan's basketball team, last season's national champions, is in Lithuania this month for warm-up games. These are the opening matches under new coach Mike Boynton: on the 23rd, the team lost 84–96 to the Lithuanian national team, and the following day fell 77–82 to Lithuania's reserve squad.

The team plays their third game today in Croatia. Nearly the entire championship roster has returned, with sophomore McKenney and freshman McCoy both scoring in double figures.

兵工廠想用約克雷斯加現金 換阿爾瓦雷斯

來源：OneFootball

兵工廠線——轉會市場八月三十一日就要關門。英國媒體報導，兵工廠打算用前鋒約克雷斯（Viktor Gyokeres）加上大約六千八百五十萬英鎊，向馬德里競技換來阿根廷前鋒阿爾瓦雷斯（Julian Alvarez）。

有意思的是，約克雷斯去年才剛加入，上一季還是隊上進球最多的人，現在卻變成了換人的條件。

The Arsenal beat — with the transfer market closing on August 31st, British media reports that Arsenal is preparing to offer striker Viktor Gyokeres plus roughly £68.5 million to Atlético Madrid for Argentine forward Julian Alvarez.

What's interesting is that Gyokeres only joined last year and was the team's top goalscorer last season, yet now he's part of the package being offered.
