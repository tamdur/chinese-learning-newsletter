GPT-5.4 上線：一百萬個 Token，AI 進步速度驚人

更讓人吃驚的是，三月初短短一週內就有十幾個重大 AI 產品發布。這種進步速度會改變很多人寫程式和分析文件的方式。

OpenAI released the latest GPT-5.4, which can process one million tokens at a time (the basic unit of text), with one-third fewer errors. This means it can read an entire book or a large amount of code in one go.

What's even more surprising is that in just one week in early March, there were over a dozen major AI product releases. This pace of progress will change how many people write code and analyze documents.

美國海軍拒絕護送商船過荷莫茲海峽

來源：Reuters / AP

波斯灣的荷莫茲海峽（Strait of Hormuz）是全球最重要的石油通道，大約兩成的全球石油都經過這裡。但因為和伊朗的衝突加劇（加劇：變得更嚴重），美國海軍拒絕了護送商船的要求。

伊朗說他們用水下無人機攻擊了油輪。以前美國海軍都會護送，現在不做了，這是一個很大的改變。油價和全球市場都可能受到影響。

The Strait of Hormuz in the Persian Gulf is the world's most critical oil passage, with approximately 20 percent of global oil flowing through here. However, due to escalating conflicts with Iran, the U.S. Navy has refused requests to escort merchant vessels.

Iran says they used underwater drones to attack oil tankers. The U.S. Navy used to provide escorts in the past, but now they are declining to do so—a significant change. Oil prices and global markets could be affected.

Hacker News 禁止 AI 寫留言：「這裡是人和人的對話」

來源：Hacker News / Y Combinator

知名科技論壇 Hacker News 正式宣布：不可以用 AI 來寫或修改留言。這篇公告得到將近四千個讚，可見很多人在意這件事。

大家討論的重點是：用 AI 幫忙想法和讓 AI 直接寫，界線在哪裡？而且這種規定要怎麼執行？在 AI 越來越強的時代，怎麼保持「真人對話」是個大問題。

The well-known tech forum Hacker News has officially announced that users cannot use AI to write or edit comments. This announcement received nearly four thousand upvotes, showing that many people care about this issue.

The main point of discussion is: where is the line between using AI to help with your thoughts and letting AI write directly? And how would such a rule even be enforced? As AI becomes increasingly powerful, maintaining "real human conversation" is becoming a major challenge.

研究：大麻會讓人「記住」沒發生的事

來源：Psychopharmacology / Science Daily

一項新的研究發現，大麻裡的 THC 不只是讓記憶變差，還會讓人產生「假記憶」。受試者會非常有信心地「記得」根本沒發生過的事。

另外，他們也更容易忘記「要做的事」，比如下午要打電話給朋友。這對法律上的目擊證人（目擊：親眼看到）和日常生活的決定都有很大的影響。

A new study found that THC in cannabis doesn't just impair memory — it also creates "false memories." Test subjects became confident that they remembered things that never actually happened.

Additionally, they were more likely to forget "things to do," like calling a friend in the afternoon. This has significant implications for eyewitness testimony in legal cases and everyday decision-making.
