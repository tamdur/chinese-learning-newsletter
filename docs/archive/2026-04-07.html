伊朗拒絕美國停火提議

來源：NPR

伊朗今天拒絕了美國提出的四十五天停火方案，要求戰爭永久結束並解除制裁。川普總統把最後通牒延到星期二晚上八點，還威脅要炸毀伊朗所有的橋和電廠。

更嚇人的是，伊朗第一次公開說要攻擊阿拉伯聯合大公國境內的一座先進人工智慧資料中心，作為報復。

Iran today rejected the 45-day ceasefire proposal put forward by the United States, demanding a permanent end to the war and the lifting of sanctions. President Trump extended his ultimatum to 8 p.m. Tuesday and further threatened to destroy all of Iran's bridges and power plants. More alarmingly, Iran for the first time publicly stated that it would, as retaliation, strike an advanced artificial intelligence data center located within the United Arab Emirates.

輝達成立開源人工智慧聯盟

來源：NVIDIA Newsroom

晶片大廠輝達（Nvidia）今天宣布成立「Nemotron聯盟」，要和八家人工智慧實驗室一起打造開放的前沿模型。

成員包括法國的Mistral、搜尋新秀Perplexity，還有Thinking Machines等。這個聯盟想用分享資料和算力的方式，和OpenAI、Google這些封閉路線的大公司抗衡。

Chip giant Nvidia today announced the formation of the "Nemotron Alliance," teaming up with eight artificial intelligence labs to build open frontier models. Members include France's Mistral, search upstart Perplexity, and Thinking Machines, among others. The alliance aims to counter closed-approach giants like OpenAI and Google by sharing data and computing power.

歐元區通膨升到百分之二點五

來源：Polymarket / ECB

歐元區三月的物價年增率升到百分之二點五，比歐洲央行訂的百分之二目標還高。主要原因是伊朗戰爭推高了油價。

現在市場估計四月底開會時，央行有百分之七十三的機率會維持利率不動，但升息的可能也明顯變大了。

The Eurozone's annual inflation rate rose to 2.5% in March, exceeding the European Central Bank's 2% target. The main driver was the war in Iran, which has pushed oil prices higher. Markets now estimate a 73% probability that the central bank will hold interest rates steady at its late-April meeting, though the likelihood of a rate hike has also grown noticeably.

歐巴馬總統中心博物館六月開幕

來源：Chicago Today

位於芝加哥傑克森公園的歐巴馬總統中心星期一宣布，博物館門票將在五月六日開賣，六月十九日正式對外開放。

這個計畫二零一六年就公布了，但一直被官司、施工延誤和超支拖累。十年之後，這座紀念館終於要和市民見面。

The Obama Presidential Center, located in Chicago's Jackson Park, announced Monday that museum tickets will go on sale May 6, with the center officially opening to the public on June 19. The project was first unveiled in 2016, but has been dogged by lawsuits, construction delays, and cost overruns. A decade later, this memorial center is finally set to greet the city's residents.

公牛隊高層大地震 兩主管遭開除

來源：Chicago Sun-Times

芝加哥公牛隊在例行賽剩下四場、戰績二十九勝四十九敗、又連輸七場的情況下，老闆萊恩斯多夫星期一開除了籃球事務副總裁卡尼索瓦斯和總經理艾佛斯利。

卡尼索瓦斯從二零二零年就掌管球隊。老闆據說想留下總教練多諾文，等於是要整個重新開始。

The Chicago Bulls, with four games left in the regular season, a record of 29-49, and a seven-game losing streak, saw owner Jerry Reinsdorf fire Vice President of Basketball Operations Arturas Karnisovas and General Manager Marc Eversley on Monday. Karnisovas had been running the team since 2020. The owner reportedly wants to keep head coach Billy Donovan, which amounts to starting over from scratch.
