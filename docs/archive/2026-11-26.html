芝加哥又一名和平員中槍，幾週來第四人

來源：Chicago Tribune

本週，芝加哥南岸區（South Shore）一名「社區和平員」被人開了六槍。他被送到芝加哥大學醫學中心，情況穩定。

和平員是在街頭工作的人，很多就住在這些社區。市政府和慈善團體付錢請他們在吵架變成開槍之前出面勸和。這是芝加哥不靠警察、減少暴力的重要方法。

可是幾週來，已經有四名和平員中槍。不久前，43歲的Prince Johnson在Riverdale被打死。大家開始問：這份工作還安全嗎？

This week, a "community peacekeeper" in Chicago's South Shore neighborhood was shot six times. He was taken to the University of Chicago Medical Center, and his condition is stable. Peacekeepers work on the streets, and many of them live in the neighborhoods they serve. The city and charitable groups pay them to step in and talk people down before arguments turn into gunfire. It is an important way Chicago is trying to reduce violence without relying on police. But in just a few weeks, four peacekeepers have now been shot. Not long ago, 43-year-old Prince Johnson was killed in Riverdale. People are starting to ask: is this job still safe?

感恩節老傳統：熊隊到底特律挑戰獅子隊

來源：Fox Sports / AP

今天是美國感恩節，芝加哥熊隊中午在底特律的Ford Field對上獅子隊。兩隊在感恩節對戰，是美式足球最老的節日傳統之一。

熊隊最近很強，上週以21比13打敗聖徒隊，已經連贏五場。獅子隊則是NFC戰績最好的球隊。

歷史站在熊隊這邊：過去五次感恩節出賽，熊隊贏了四次，其中三次打敗獅子隊。不過兩隊上次在感恩節碰面是2024年，獅子隊以23比20贏球。

Today is Thanksgiving in the United States, and at noon the Chicago Bears face the Detroit Lions at Ford Field in Detroit. Thanksgiving matchups between the two teams are among the oldest traditions in American football. The Bears have been playing well lately. Last week they beat the Saints 21-13, and they have now won five games in a row. The Lions, meanwhile, have the best record in the NFC. History is on the Bears' side: in their last five Thanksgiving appearances, they have won four times, three of them against the Lions. The two teams last met on Thanksgiving in 2024, however, when the Lions won 23-20.
