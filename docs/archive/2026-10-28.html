期中選舉剩六天，伊州郵寄選票明天截止申請

來源：Reuters／Illinois Policy Institute

美國期中選舉11月3日登場，只剩六天。這次眾議院全部席次、35席參議員和36位州長都要改選。

路透社和Ipsos的最新民調顯示，川普的支持率只有31%，是他兩次任期中最低的。如果今天投票，47%的人說會選民主黨，選共和黨的只有35%。

住在芝加哥的人要注意：想用郵寄投票，明天10月29日是最後申請日。伊利諾州這次要選出一位新的聯邦參議員，芝加哥也第一次由選民選出全部的教育委員會成員。

The U.S. midterm elections kick off on November 3, just six days away. This time, all House seats, 35 Senate seats, and 36 governorships are up for grabs. The latest Reuters/Ipsos poll puts President Trump's approval rating at just 31%, the lowest of his two terms. If the vote were held today, 47% said they would back the Democrats, compared with 35% for the Republicans. Chicago residents take note: if you want to vote by mail, tomorrow, October 29, is the last day to apply. Illinois is electing a new U.S. senator this cycle, and for the first time, Chicago voters will elect the entire Board of Education.

公牛連輸兩場，今晚主場迎戰尼克

來源：Bleacher Nation

芝加哥公牛今晚7點在聯合中心主場對上紐約尼克。新教練史普利特（Tiago Splitter）上任後，公牛開季1勝3負，已經連輸兩場。

星期一在波士頓，公牛上半場還領先12分，第三節卻一直失分，最後以99比134大輸塞爾提克35分。前一場輸給巫師，也是在第三節出問題。

尼克是東區的強隊。正在重建的公牛今晚能不能守住下半場，是球迷最想看的事。

The Chicago Bulls host the New York Knicks tonight at 7 p.m. at the United Center. Under new head coach Tiago Splitter, the Bulls have started the season 1-3 and have now lost two games in a row. On Monday in Boston, Chicago led by as many as 12 points in the first half, but it kept giving up points in the third quarter and lost 134-99 to the Celtics, a 35-point blowout. The game before that, they lost to the Wizards, and that one also fell apart in the third quarter. The Knicks are one of the strong teams in the East. Whether the rebuilding Bulls can hold on in the second half tonight is what fans most want to see.

兵工廠輸給第四級球隊，聯賽盃出局

來源：ESPN／VAVEL

兵工廠線——兵工廠星期二在聯賽盃第四輪，到客場對上英格蘭第四級聯賽的弗利特伍德（Fleetwood Town），結果在互射12碼時以3比5落敗。

弗利特伍德第21分鐘先進球，兵工廠的約克雷斯（Gyökeres）在第50分鐘追平，兩隊踢成1比1。比賽中還因為球場上空出現無人機，停了8分多鐘。

16歲的小將道曼（Dowman）在比賽後段因為踢人，被紅牌趕出場。教練阿特塔說：「這是我們很難過的一天。」兵工廠星期日要去客場挑戰利物浦。

The Arsenal beat: Arsenal lost to Fourth Tier side Fleetwood Town on penalties in the League Cup fourth round on Tuesday. The match was played away at Fleetwood, and it ended 1-1 after 90 minutes. Fleetwood took the lead in the 21st minute, and Arsenal's Gyökeres equalized in the 50th. Play was stopped for more than eight minutes when a drone was spotted over the pitch. Late in the game, 16-year-old Dowman was shown a red card for a kick at an opponent. Arsenal lost the shootout 5-3. Manager Arteta said, "It was a very difficult day for us." Arsenal travel to Liverpool on Sunday.
