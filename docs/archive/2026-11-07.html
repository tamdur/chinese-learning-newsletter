共和黨重畫選區，結果沒有幫上忙

來源：AP

川普總統想幫共和黨守住眾議院，所以推動幾個州在選舉前重畫選區（也就是重新切分投票的地區）。美聯社在十一月三日期中選舉後分析，發現這招大多沒有用。

在阿拉巴馬、佛羅里達和德州，新地圖沒有帶來想要的席次。密蘇里州的新地圖被法院擋下，有些地方的選民也直接投票說不。

更糟的是，民主黨在加州也重畫地圖，反而從共和黨手上搶走好幾個席次。

President Trump wanted to help Republicans hold on to the House, so several states moved to redraw their congressional district maps (in other words, redraw the boundaries of the areas that cast votes) before the election. An Associated Press analysis after the November 3 midterms found that the strategy mostly did not work.

In Alabama, Florida, and Texas, the new maps did not deliver the seats Republicans were hoping for. A new map in Missouri was blocked by the courts, and in some places, voters also rejected the redrawn maps directly at the ballot box.

Worse still for Republicans, Democrats redrew the map in California too, and instead of helping the GOP, it ended up flipping several seats away from Republican hands.

薩利巴傷後歸隊，今天會上場嗎？

來源：Sky Sports

兵工廠線——兵工廠後衛薩利巴因為背部受傷，缺席了好幾場比賽。這個星期他終於回到一隊，跟大家一起完整訓練。

週三在歐冠（歐洲冠軍聯賽）客場對布拉格斯拉維亞，他第一次重新進入比賽名單，不過一直坐在板凳上，沒有上場。教練阿特塔說，希望他幾週內就能回到場上。

今天兵工廠在主場對赫爾城，芝加哥時間早上九點開踢。薩利巴會再坐板凳，還是正式回到場上？這是今天最值得看的地方。

The Arsenal beat: Arsenal defender Saliba has missed several matches with a back injury. This week he finally returned to the first team and trained fully with the squad.

On Wednesday, in the Champions League away match against Slavia Prague, he was named in the matchday squad for the first time, but he stayed on the bench and did not play. Coach Arteta said he hopes Saliba will be back on the pitch within a few weeks.

Today Arsenal host Hull City at home, with kickoff at 9 a.m. Chicago time. Will Saliba sit on the bench again, or will he officially return to the field? That is the thing to watch most closely today.
