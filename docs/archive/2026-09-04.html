美國八月只增兩萬多份工作，聯準會降息機率大增

來源：CNBC / 美國勞工統計局

美國政府公布，八月只增加兩萬兩千份工作，比原本預估的七萬五千份少很多。失業率升到百分之四點三，是近五年來最高。

更糟的是，六月的數字還被改成減少一萬三千份。市場現在認為，聯準會（美國的中央銀行）在九月十七日降息的機會高達八成八。上個月大家還以為會升息呢。

The U.S. government reported that only 22,000 jobs were added in August, a sharp miss against the expected 75,000. The unemployment rate jumped to 4.3%, the highest level in nearly five years. To make matters worse, June's job figures were revised downward to show a loss of 13,000 positions. Markets are now pricing in an 88% probability that the Federal Reserve will cut rates on September 17th. A month ago, those same traders were betting on a rate hike instead.

OpenAI 推出新模型，首次列為最高風險等級

來源：Axios / Bloomberg / CNBC

OpenAI 在九月三日推出新模型 GPT-6 Astra。這是該公司第一個被自己評為「重大風險」的模型，因為它能自己找出電腦系統的漏洞（弱點），還能做出攻擊工具。

但公司這次沒有把它收起來，而是先給 Daybreak 計畫裡的資安公司用，只准防守、不准攻擊，另外拿出十億美元幫忙保護水電等重要設施。

On September 3rd, OpenAI released a new model, GPT-6 Astra. For the first time, the company has rated one of its own models as posing a "major risk"—because it can independently identify vulnerabilities in computer systems and build attack tools. Rather than shelving it, OpenAI instead released it first to cybersecurity firms within the Daybreak program, with strict constraints: defensive use only. The company also announced $1 billion in funding to help protect critical infrastructure, including power grids and water systems.

美財政部擬取消部分私校免稅資格

來源：NPR

美國財政部星期四提出新規定：私立學校和大學如果因為學生的種族而給特別的獎學金或名額，就可能失去免稅資格。最多有一萬八千所學校會受影響。

財政部長貝森特說，學校把這些做法改叫「公平」或「包容」，也改不了歧視（不公平對待）的本質。新規定若通過，要到明年五月以後才上路。

The U.S. Treasury Department announced new regulations on Thursday that could strip private schools and universities of their tax-exempt status if they award scholarships or admissions slots based on students' race. As many as 18,000 schools could be affected. Treasury Secretary Bessent said that schools can relabel these practices as "fairness" or "inclusion," but that doesn't change their discriminatory nature. If approved, the regulations would not take effect until after May next year.

謝德水族館又有兩隻白鯨死亡

來源：Block Club Chicago

芝加哥謝德水族館星期三又有兩隻白鯨死去。二十歲的「小雨」上個月才從加拿大一座關門的樂園搬來，三十四歲的「貝多芬」則在館裡住了幾十年。

八天前，另一隻搬來的白鯨也突然死了。水族館說死因還不清楚，正在檢查。三個星期前大家還在為這場救援高興，現在卻有人問：把牠們運來，真的對嗎？

Two more beluga whales died at Chicago's Shedd Aquarium on Wednesday. Little Rain, only 20 years old, had just arrived last month from a defunct amusement park in Canada, while Beethoven, 34, had lived at the facility for decades. Eight days earlier, another relocated beluga had died suddenly. The aquarium says the cause of death remains unclear and is still under investigation. Three weeks ago, everyone was celebrating this rescue operation—but now some are asking whether bringing them here was really the right call.

密大今晚開季，新教練首度帶隊上陣

來源：Yahoo Sports

全美排名第十六的密西根大學，星期六晚上在自家球場迎戰西密西根大學。這是新教練惠廷漢帶隊的第一場比賽，賭盤看好密大贏二十七點五分。

不過對手不好惹：西密大上季十勝四敗，拿下中美聯盟冠軍，跑陣很強，正好打向密大整個八月都在擔心的前線。練了一個月的話，今晚要驗收了。

Michigan, ranked 16th nationally, hosts Western Michigan at home Saturday night. It's new coach Whitingham's first game at the helm, and oddsmakers favor Michigan by 27.5 points.

But Western Michigan is no pushover. Last season they went 10-4, won the Mid-American Conference championship, and have a powerful running game—precisely the defensive line Michigan has been concerned about all August. After a month of practice, tonight is the moment of truth.

兵工廠線：馬丁尼利轉會，創球會賣人新高

來源：Daily Cannon

兵工廠線——巴西前鋒馬丁尼利已完成體檢，將以大約七千萬歐元轉會沙烏地的利雅德新月，簽約四年。這是兵工廠賣球員收過最高的錢，比舊紀錄多了快一倍。

二十五歲的他十八歲就加入球隊，共出賽二百七十八場、進六十二球。歐洲的轉會窗已經關了，沙烏地那邊還開著，所以教練阿爾特塔少了一名前鋒，卻補不了人。

Arsenal Line — Brazilian forward Martinelli has completed his medical and will transfer to Al-Hilal in Saudi Arabia for approximately 70 million euros, signing a four-year contract.

This is the highest amount Arsenal has ever received for selling a player, nearly double the previous record.

At 25 years old, he joined the team at 18 and has made 278 appearances, scoring 62 goals.

Europe's transfer window has already closed, but Saudi Arabia's remains open, so coach Arteta has lost a forward but cannot bring in a replacement.
