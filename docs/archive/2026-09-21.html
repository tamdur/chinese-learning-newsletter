四大AI公司被控聯手放慢進度

來源：CBS News

四位付錢使用ChatGPT、Claude、Grok和Gemini的美國民眾，九月十八日在舊金山的聯邦法院控告Anthropic、OpenAI、Google和馬斯克的xAI，說這四家公司私下講好一起放慢AI的進步，違反了公平競爭的法律。

事情要從九月十二日說起。那天Anthropic的執行長寫文章，呼籲大家一起放慢速度、把安全放第一，同一天另外三家的老闆都公開說贊成。

原告說，公司自己想慢下來可以，但四家講好一起慢，付月費的人就吃虧了。

Four American users who pay for ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, and Gemini filed a federal lawsuit in San Francisco on September 18 against Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, and Elon Musk's xAI. They allege the four companies secretly coordinated to slow AI progress in violation of antitrust law. The dispute stems from September 12, when Anthropic's CEO published an article urging everyone to slow down and put safety first—and that same day, the leaders of the other three companies publicly endorsed the position. The plaintiffs argue that while companies can choose to slow down on their own, when four competitors coordinate to do so together, paying subscribers are left worse off.

烏克蘭無人機打中莫斯科煉油廠

來源：Bloomberg

俄國說，烏克蘭在一天內發射超過一千六百架無人機，其中一百五十二架飛向首都莫斯科，是今年最大的一次。

離克里姆林宮只有十五公里的卡波特尼亞煉油廠（把石油變成汽油的工廠）被打中起火，濃煙全城都看得見。這座廠供應莫斯科大約四成的汽油。

攻擊發生在俄國國會選舉投票的最後一天。以前莫斯科人是在電視上看戰爭，現在要擔心加油站沒油。

Russia reports that Ukraine launched more than 1,600 drones in a single day, with 152 targeting Moscow — the largest such attack this year. The Kapotnya refinery, just 15 kilometers from the Kremlin, was struck and caught fire, with thick smoke visible across the city. The facility converts crude oil into gasoline and provides roughly 40 percent of Moscow's fuel supply.

The attack came on the final day of Russia's parliamentary election voting. For Moscow residents, the conflict that was once watched on television has become a real concern — now they worry about empty gas pumps.

白宮拿掉CNN的電視輪值

來源：CNN Business

白宮把CNN從星期一的電視輪值名單上拿掉。幾十年來，ABC、CBS、CNN、NBC和福斯五家電視台自己輪流拍總統，畫面再分給大家用。

這是川普上星期五說要禁止CNN、MS NOW和Politico進入白宮之後的新一步。星期六，三家的記者真的被擋在門外。

三家媒體正準備打官司，說這違反憲法保障的新聞自由。

The White House has removed CNN from Monday's television rotation schedule. For decades, five television networks—ABC, CBS, CNN, NBC, and Fox—have taken turns filming the president under a pool arrangement, with the footage shared among all outlets.

This latest action follows Trump's announcement on Friday that he would ban CNN, MS NOW, and Politico from entering the White House. On Saturday, reporters from these three organizations were indeed turned away at the door.

The three media outlets are now preparing legal action, arguing that the ban violates constitutionally protected press freedom.

美中商談降關稅，習近平本週訪白宮

來源：Modern Diplomacy

習近平預定九月二十四日到白宮和川普見面，還有國宴。在這之前，美國財政部長貝森特上週末和中國副總理何立峰談降關稅。

雙方談的是農產品和能源，大約涉及三百億美元的貿易，想把快到期的休戰延長。桌上還有稀土（做磁鐵和電池要用的礦物）出口限制、買波音飛機，還有新加進來的AI風險對談。

一個月前美方還在加稅，現在方向反過來了。

Xi Jinping is scheduled to visit the White House and meet Trump on September 24th, with a state dinner included. Before that, U.S. Treasury Secretary Bessent met with China's Vice Premier He Lifeng last weekend to discuss tariff reductions. The two sides discussed agricultural products and energy, involving roughly $30 billion in trade, hoping to extend the ceasefire that's about to expire. Also on the table: restrictions on rare earth exports (minerals used in magnets and batteries), Boeing aircraft purchases, and newly added talks on AI risks. A month ago the U.S. was still raising tariffs; now the direction has completely reversed.

熊隊四分衛威廉斯大腿拉傷

來源：NBC Sports

芝加哥熊隊四分衛威廉斯（Caleb Williams）星期天第四節帶球往前衝時自己滑倒，大腿後側拉傷，坐車下場。

熊隊在士兵球場以三比九輸給明尼蘇達維京人。受傷前，威廉斯傳球二十六次成功十五次，推進一百三十八碼，還被抄走一球。

教練強森說，星期一做完檢查才知道要休多久。球隊希望只是最輕的第一級。

Chicago Bears quarterback Caleb Williams slipped while running with the ball in the fourth quarter Sunday, suffering a hamstring strain and being carted off the field. The Bears lost to the Minnesota Vikings 3-9 at Soldier Field. Before the injury, Williams completed 15 of 26 passes for 138 yards and threw an interception. Coach Johnson said the team would know more about the severity after Monday's examination. The team hopes it's only a Grade 1 strain, the mildest form.

兵工廠線：阿森納看上多特蒙德前鋒拜爾

來源：Arsenal / CaughtOffside

兵工廠線——阿森納傳出正在觀察德國多特蒙德的前鋒拜爾（Maximilian Beier），準備一月轉會時出手。

這是夏天賣掉熱蘇斯和馬丁內利、卻沒買任何前鋒之後，第一個說得出名字的目標。二十三歲的拜爾加入多特蒙德後進了二十一球、助攻十九次。

前一天，阿森納在布萊頓零比三慘敗，一球未進。教練阿爾特塔承認，球隊每一次拚搶都輸人。

Arsenal has reportedly been monitoring Borussia Dortmund striker Maximilian Beier, with plans to move for him in January. This is the first named target after the club sold Jesus and Martinelli in the summer without acquiring a striker replacement.

The 23-year-old Beier has scored 21 goals and recorded 19 assists since joining Borussia Dortmund.

Just the day before, Arsenal suffered a humiliating 0-3 defeat to Brighton without scoring. Manager Arteta acknowledged that the team lost every physical contest.
