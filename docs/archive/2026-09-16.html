美國聯準會可能升息 二〇二三年以來第一次

來源：Kiplinger / Federal Reserve

美國的中央銀行（聯準會）今天下午開會決定利率。市場猜他們會把利率調高到百分之三點七五到四，這是二〇二三年以來第一次升息。

奇怪的是，美國經濟並不熱。八月幾乎沒有增加新工作，失業率百分之四點三。可是伊朗戰爭讓油價變貴，物價還是漲了大約百分之三點七。

主席華許要在經濟走弱的時候壓住物價，非常難。十年期公債的利率已經先漲過百分之五，以後買房子、借錢都會更貴。

The Federal Reserve, America's central bank, held a meeting this afternoon to decide on interest rates. Markets expect the Fed to raise rates to between 3.75% and 4%, marking the first rate increase since 2023. Oddly, the American economy is not performing well. August saw almost no job growth, with unemployment at 4.3%. Yet the Iran war has made oil prices expensive, so inflation still rose about 3.7%. Chair Warsh faces an extraordinarily difficult task: controlling inflation while the economy weakens. The 10-year Treasury yield has already climbed above 5%, making mortgages and borrowing more expensive in the future.

谷歌開放工程師用對手的模型

來源：Business Insider

谷歌（Google）以前規定，公司裡的工程師寫程式只能用自己家的 Gemini。這個星期，公司改了規定，讓所有工程師都能在 Antigravity 這個工具裡，使用對手Anthropic的 Claude Opus 5。

谷歌說，Gemini 還是公司最主要的模型。不過一家自己有模型、有晶片、有機房的大公司，卻承認員工用別人的模型寫程式比較好，這件事本身就很說明問題。

Google previously had a policy that engineers in the company could only use their own Gemini model for writing code. This week, the company changed that policy, allowing all engineers to use their rival Anthropic's Claude Opus 5 within the Antigravity tool. Google says Gemini is still the company's primary model. But for a major company that has its own models, chips, and data centers to admit that its employees write better code using a competitor's model—that alone speaks volumes.

芝加哥移民大抓捕滿一年 研究算出損失

來源：ABC7 Chicago

去年九月，聯邦政府在芝加哥展開大規模移民抓捕，名字叫「中途島閃擊」。兩個多月裡抓了三千八百四十一人，其中只有一成五以前犯過罪。

滿一年這天，州長普里茲克到小村區公布伊利諾大學的研究：庫克郡的餐廳和商店少做了十二點六億美元生意，地方政府少收一億零七百萬美元的稅。

研究說，很多人不敢出門吃飯買東西。而芝加哥明年的預算，本來就已經少了八點八億美元。

Last September, the federal government launched a large-scale immigration enforcement operation in Chicago called "Operation Midway Blitz." Over the course of two months, 3,841 people were arrested, but only 15 percent had prior criminal records. On the one-year anniversary, Governor Pritzker visited Little Village to announce findings from a University of Illinois study: restaurants and shops in Cook County lost 1.26 billion dollars in business, and local government tax revenue dropped by 107 million dollars. The study found that many people were afraid to venture out to eat or shop. Meanwhile, Chicago's budget for the coming year already faces a shortfall of 880 million dollars.

剛果警察驅散反對修憲的遊行

來源：Reuters

非洲的剛果民主共和國星期二有好幾個城市上街遊行，反對修改憲法。大家擔心，改憲法是為了讓總統齊塞克迪可以再做第三任。

在首都金夏沙，遊行的人和支持政府的人起了衝突；在南邊的礦業城市盧本巴希，警察放催淚瓦斯、對空開槍，抓走好幾個人。

齊塞克迪今年六十二歲，任期到二〇二八年。剛果出產全世界七成的鈷（做電池用的金屬），一亂起來，全球都會感覺到。

Several cities across the Democratic Republic of Congo took to the streets on Tuesday to protest constitutional amendments. There is widespread concern that the amendments are intended to allow President Tshisekedi to run for a third term. In the capital Kinshasa, protesters clashed with pro-government supporters. In Lubumbashi, a mining city in the south, police deployed tear gas, fired shots into the air, and arrested several people. Tshisekedi is 62 years old and his current term runs until 2028. Congo produces 70 percent of the world's cobalt—a metal essential for batteries—so any instability there reverberates globally.

小熊勝勇士 魔術數字剩下七

來源：NBC Chicago

芝加哥小熊隊星期一晚上在瑞格利球場，以七比三打敗亞特蘭大勇士隊，「魔術數字」降到七：再湊滿七場自己贏或對手輸，就確定進季後賽。

這場投手彼得森投六局只掉一分、三振七次；克勞阿姆斯壯五次打擊打出四支安打，含一支全壘打，打回四分。

小熊目前排在國聯外卡第一，還剩十幾場比賽。球隊已經六年沒打過季後賽了。

The Chicago Cubs defeated the Atlanta Braves 7-3 Monday night at Wrigley Field, dropping their magic number down to seven. They now need seven more combined wins or opponent losses to clinch a playoff berth.

Pitcher Peterson threw six innings, allowing just one run with seven strikeouts. Crow-Armstrong went 4-for-5 with a home run, driving in four runs.

The Cubs currently lead the National League wild-card standings with roughly a dozen games remaining. The team hasn't made the postseason in six years.

英超認錯：兵工廠該有一個罰球

來源：Read Arsenal

兵工廠線——英超的「關鍵判決小組」看完錄影後說，兵工廠在維拉球場那場，應該得到一個十二碼罰球。

那是第二輪，兵工廠一比零贏球。快結束時，古馬蘭斯衝進禁區被維拉後衛馬特森撞倒，裁判沒吹。小組五個人全部認為該判罰球，而且馬特森該吃第二張黃牌下場。

可是這個小組只能事後說明，不能改結果。兵工廠開季四戰全勝，還在第一名。

From the Arsenal Desk — The Premier League's review panel confirmed after watching footage that Arsenal should have been awarded a penalty in the Villa Park match. It was Matchday 2, and Arsenal won 1-0. Late in the game, Guimarães was brought down in the box by Villa defender Maatsen, but the referee didn't call it. All five panel members agreed the penalty should have been given, and Maatsen should have received a second yellow card and been sent off. However, the panel can only comment after the fact—it cannot change results. Arsenal started the season with four straight wins and remains in first place.
