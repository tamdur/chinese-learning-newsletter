高凌風的迪斯可：被當成原版的翻唱

來源：South China Morning Post

1980年，台灣還在戒嚴（政府用軍隊管理社會）的時期，一般人不能隨便去舞廳跳舞。可是，歌手高凌風還是帶起了一股迪斯可熱潮。

他把西方的舞曲填上中文歌詞，例如 Ring My Bell。他的動作非常快：一首1983年的西洋歌，幾個月後就有了中文版，歌名的意思是「愛像青橄欖」。

對當時很多台灣人來說，高凌風唱的就是原曲；真正的原版，反而沒有人聽過。

In 1980, Taiwan was still under martial law (a period when the government ran society with the military), and ordinary people could not simply go to dance halls whenever they liked. Still, singer Kao Ling-feng set off a disco craze. He wrote Chinese lyrics for Western dance tracks, such as "Ring My Bell." He moved fast: a Western song from 1983 had a Chinese version within a few months, and its Chinese title meant "Love Is Like Green Olives." For many Taiwanese at the time, the version Kao Ling-feng sang was the original. The true original, ironically, was something almost nobody had heard.

會消失的鏡子小屋

來源：Dwell

在芬蘭北部拉普蘭的伊納里，湖邊有一間用鏡面玻璃做的小屋 Lucia Smart，只有大約24平方公尺。四周都是白樺樹（一種白色樹皮的樹）和湖水。

建築師把鋼架做得很細，連燈都藏在架子裡。從外面看，玻璃映出樹林和湖面，小屋好像不見了；從裡面看，幾乎四面八方都是風景。

小屋先在工廠做好，再整間運到湖邊。建築師說，這是一個「躲起來的地方」。

At Inari in Lapland, northern Finland, a small mirror-glass cabin called Lucia Smart stands by a lake. It is only about 24 square meters. All around it are white birch trees (a kind of tree with white bark) and the lake water. The architect kept the steel frame very slim and hid even the lights inside the shelving. From outside, the glass reflects the forest and the lake, so the cabin seems to vanish. From inside, you are surrounded by scenery on almost every side. The cabin was built first in a factory and then transported, whole, to the lakeshore. The architect calls it a "place to hide away."

從門口到音樂廳：鄉下歌聲的旅程

來源：Newberry Library

11月11日星期三下午四點，紐伯瑞圖書館（60 W. Walton St.）請來民俗學家 Stephen Wade。他也彈班鳩琴（一種圓身的美國弦樂器）。

他寫過一本書，介紹美國國會圖書館錄下的普通人歌聲。這次的新書說的是：鄉下人坐在家門口隨口唱的歌，怎麼被保存下來，一步步走進音樂廳。

活動免費，有現場演奏，結束後還可以請他簽書。

On Wednesday, November 11, at 4 p.m., the Newberry Library (60 W. Walton St.) will host folklorist Stephen Wade, who also plays the banjo, a round-bodied American stringed instrument. He wrote a book about the everyday voices preserved by the Library of Congress. His new book traces how songs that country people sang casually while sitting in their doorways were saved and, step by step, made their way into concert halls. The event is free, includes a live performance, and afterward attendees can have their books signed by him.

會自己畫圖的機器博物館

來源：drawingmachines.org

芝加哥藝術學院的建築教授 Pablo Garcia，花了好幾年翻找專利（政府對發明的保護）文件和博物館收藏，做出網站 drawingmachines.org。這裡收了幾百種畫圖機器，從1800年代初一直到今天。

最有趣的是擺錘（會來回擺動的重物）畫圖機，像1893年一台「畫幾何圖形的玩具」，還有投錢就能玩的版本。

擺錘帶著筆在紙上搖，越擺越小，美麗的曲線就自己出現了。

Pablo Garcia, an architecture professor at the School of the Art Institute of Chicago, spent several years digging through patent documents (government protections for inventions) and museum collections to build the website drawingmachines.org. It catalogs several hundred drawing machines, from the early 1800s to today. The most fascinating are the pendulum drawing machines, which use heavy weights that swing back and forth. One example is an 1893 "geometric drawing toy," and there were even coin-operated versions you could pay to play. A pen attached to the pendulum sways over the paper, and as the swing gets smaller and smaller, beautiful curves appear on their own.
