從偶像到聲音地圖：林強的《別境》

來源：KFJC (90.1 FM) / bié Records

1990年，林強唱紅了〈向前走〉，是當時台灣最紅的年輕歌手之一。後來他走下舞台，改替導演侯孝賢的電影寫音樂，2001年靠《千禧曼波》拿下金馬獎最佳原創電影音樂。

新專輯《別境》共有十一首曲子。他親自走遍台灣，錄下各地的自然聲音，再慢慢織成像電影一樣的電子音樂。聽的時候，好像跟著他在島上散步。

從流行偶像到安靜的聲音探險家，現在大家叫他「台灣實驗電子音樂教父」。

In 1990, Lin Chiang topped the charts with "Walk Forward" (向前走), making him one of Taiwan's biggest young singers at the time. He later left the stage to write music for director Hou Hsiao-hsien's films, and in 2001 his score for Millennium Mambo won the Golden Horse Award for Best Original Film Score. His new album, Beyond Places (《別境》), has eleven tracks. He traveled around Taiwan himself, recorded the natural sounds of different places, and slowly wove them into electronic music that feels like a film. Listening to it is like taking a walk across the island with him. Having gone from pop idol to quiet sound explorer, he is now widely known as the "godfather of Taiwan's experimental electronic music."

猜猜春天幾點到：羅瓦涅米的破冰打賭

來源：LEO Network（引自 Lapin Kansa）

在芬蘭北部的羅瓦涅米，凱米河冬天會結成厚厚的冰。每年春天，當地的獅子會在冰上放一個記號，就在有名的「伐木工人蠟燭橋」上游。

大家下注（花錢打賭），猜冰塊裂開、把記號沖過大橋的日子，連幾點幾分都要猜。2026年，冰特別早破，記號在半夜2點32分漂過大橋。

三月來玩時，河面還是一片白色的冰。站在橋上，你就站在冬天和春天的交界。

In Rovaniemi, in northern Finland, the Kemijoki River freezes into a thick sheet of ice every winter. Each spring, the local Lions Club places a marker on the ice just upstream of the famous Lumberjack's Candle Bridge. People bet money on the day the ice will break up and carry the marker under the bridge, and they have to guess the hour and minute too. In 2026 the ice broke unusually early, and the marker floated past the bridge at 2:32 a.m. When visitors come in March, the river is still a white expanse of ice. Standing on the bridge, you are standing right at the boundary between winter and spring.

鏡頭後的六十年：普立茲攝影師來到海德公園

來源：Chicago Humanities Festival

11月7日星期六，芝加哥人文節和芝加哥大學一起舉辦「海德公園日」。攝影記者大衛・肯納利會在 Ida Noyes Hall 聊他拿相機的六十年。

1972年，他用越戰的照片得到普立茲獎（美國新聞界最高的獎）。後來他成為福特總統的白宮攝影師，站在很近的地方，拍下許多歷史時刻。

活動免費，但要先上網報名。同一天，洛克斐勒教堂還有豎琴家瑪麗・拉蒂摩的演出。

On Saturday, November 7, the Chicago Humanities Festival and the University of Chicago are jointly hosting "Hyde Park Day." Photojournalist David Kennerly will talk at Ida Noyes Hall about six decades behind the camera. In 1972, he won the Pulitzer Prize (the highest honor in American journalism) for his photographs of the Vietnam War. Later he became White House photographer for President Gerald Ford, standing very close to the action and capturing many historic moments. The event is free, but you must sign up online in advance. The same day, Rockefeller Chapel will also host a performance by harpist Mary Lattimore.

騙子刻的木頭電腦：419eater 的戰利品室

來源：419eater.com

2003年，一個網名叫「Shiver Metimbers」的人建立了 419eater.com。這裡的志工專門反過來捉弄詐騙犯（用假故事騙錢的人），用假身分陪他們聊上好幾個月。

網站最有名的故事裡，一位志工假裝是藝術品買家，說服騙子 John Boko 用木頭親手刻出一台和真的一樣大的 Commodore 64 電腦。他以為這樣就能拿到一大筆藝術獎學金，還拍照、把作品寄出去當證明。

騙人的人，最後成了被騙的藝術家。

In 2003, a person using the online handle "Shiver Metimbers" founded 419eater.com. Volunteers on the site turn the tables on scammers, the people who swindle others with made-up stories, and they spend months chatting with them under fake identities.

The site's most famous story involves a volunteer who posed as an art buyer and talked a scammer named John Boko into carving a wooden replica of a Commodore 64 computer, full life-size. Boko believed this would win him a large arts grant, so he took photos and mailed the piece off as proof. The con artist ended up as the deceived artist.
