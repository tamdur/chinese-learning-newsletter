從生物系到哥德搖滾：楊乃文的《沉默》

來源：Mando Gap (Substack)

一九九九年，台灣魔岩唱片推出一張大膽的專輯：楊乃文的《沉默》。楊乃文在澳洲雪梨長大，原本在大學讀生物學，卻放棄一切回到台北做音樂。

這張專輯混合了哥德搖滾、電子節拍和澳洲酒吧搖滾的粗獷（意思是野性、有力）感覺，由製作人林暐哲操刀。她沙啞的嗓音在當時的華語樂壇非常獨特，為她贏得金曲獎最佳女歌手。《沉默》也被選入台灣兩百大經典專輯。

In 1999, Taiwan's Rock Records released a bold album: "Silence" by Nai-Wen Yang. Raised in Sydney, Australia, Yang had been studying biology at university before abandoning everything to return to Taipei and pursue music. The album blended gothic rock, electronic beats, and the raw energy of Australian pub rock, produced by Lin Wei-Zhe. Her distinctive raspy voice was remarkably unique in the Mandarin music scene at the time, earning her the Golden Melody Award for Best Female Singer. "Silence" was also selected as one of Taiwan's 200 greatest classic albums.

在冰凍的波羅的海上滑冰：奧蘭群島

來源：Visit Åland

奧蘭群島位於芬蘭和瑞典之間，由六千五百個小島組成。它屬於芬蘭，但居民說瑞典語，有自己的旗幟和議會。一九二一年起，這裡就是非武裝區。

三月時海冰最厚，可以在島嶼之間的冰面上長距離滑冰。經典路線從十四世紀的卡斯特霍姆城堡出發，滑過結冰的海灣，到達古老的堡壘遺跡（意思是留下來的舊建築），全程約兩到三小時。滑完冰後，別忘了嚐嚐當地的煙燻鱸魚和在地啤酒。

The Åland Islands, located between Finland and Sweden, comprise 6,500 small islands. Though part of Finland, residents speak Swedish and have their own flag and parliament. Since 1921, the archipelago has been demilitarized. March brings the thickest sea ice, allowing long-distance skating across the frozen waters between islands. The classic route departs from the 14th-century Kastelholm Castle, glides across the frozen bay, and reaches ancient fortress ruins — a journey of roughly two to three hours. After skating, don't miss the local smoked sea bass and regional craft beer.

Wrigley Field 的歷史一刻：首屆HBCU棒球經典賽

來源：The TRiiBE

五月二日，芝加哥 Wrigley Field 將舉辦第一屆HBCU（傳統黑人大學）棒球經典賽，由阿拉巴馬 A&M 大學對上 Prairie View A&M 大學。這座球場是當年 Jackie Robinson 打過球的球場中，唯一還在使用的一座。

門票免費，但非常搶手，目前有候補名單。比賽那週還有大學行進樂隊表演、青少年棒球營和遊行。

On May 2nd, Chicago's Wrigley Field will host the inaugural HBCU (Historically Black Colleges and Universities) Baseball Classic, featuring Alabama A&M University against Prairie View A&M University. This ballpark is the only one still in use among the stadiums where Jackie Robinson played. Tickets are free but in high demand, with a waitlist currently in place. The week of the game will also feature college marching bands, youth baseball camps, and a parade.

五萬首民間旋律的植物圖鑑

來源：Traditional Tune Archive

一九八七年，一個叫 Andrew Kuntz 的人開始獨自整理傳統小提琴曲目。他的計畫叫「小提琴手的同伴」，到了二〇一〇年變成一個巨大的網站資料庫，現在收錄超過五萬首曲子。

每首曲子都有詳細記錄：來源、別名、文化故事，涵蓋愛爾蘭、英國和北美的民間音樂。這就像一本活的植物圖鑑，只不過記錄的不是植物，而是旋律。

In 1987, a man named Andrew Kuntz began single-handedly organizing traditional fiddle tunes. His project, called "The Fiddler's Companion," evolved into a massive online database by 2010, and now contains more than 50,000 songs. Each tune is meticulously documented: its sources, alternate names, and cultural stories, covering folk music from Ireland, Britain, and North America. It's like a living field guide to plants, except instead of cataloging flora, it catalogs melodies.
