兩架鋼琴，四十八年的對話

來源：聯合報

1978年，德國鋼琴家魏樂富來到台灣，認識了鋼琴家葉綠娜。兩人結婚後，在1979年組成台灣最早的雙鋼琴組合，一起彈了四十八年。

今年三月，他們在台北國家音樂廳演出《青春舞曲》。這首曲子是音樂教育家林榮德請魏樂富寫的。他們用「預置鋼琴」（在琴弦上放小東西，改變聲音）來彈，向美國作曲家凱吉致敬，讓老曲子發出奇特的新聲音。

想挖寶的人，可以找他們的唱片《幻境》和《戲舞》。

In 1978, German pianist Werner Wei (魏樂富) came to Taiwan and met pianist Ye Lü-na (葉綠娜). After they married, the two formed Taiwan's first piano duo in 1979 and played together for forty-eight years. This March, they performed "Dance of Youth" (青春舞曲) at the National Concert Hall in Taipei. The piece was commissioned from Wei by Lin Rong-de (林榮德), a music educator. They played it on a "prepared piano," placing small objects on the strings to change the sound, a tribute to the American composer John Cage that gives old tunes a strange new voice. Anyone looking for a hidden gem can seek out their albums Illusion (幻境) and Play and Dance (戲舞).

阿爾托的磚牆實驗室

來源：Alvar Aalto Foundation

1952年到1954年，芬蘭建築大師阿爾托在湖區的一座小島上，替自己蓋了一間夏天住的房子。以前只能坐他自己設計的小船過去。

這間房子其實也是他的實驗室。他把院子的牆分成大約五十塊，每一塊用不同的磚、瓷磚和排法，看看它們過了芬蘭的冬天會變成什麼樣子，再用在真正的建築上。

這間房子只在夏天開放。三月去芬蘭的話，可以到附近城市的阿爾托博物館，那裡一年到頭都開。

Between 1952 and 1954, Finnish master architect Alvar Aalto built a summer house for himself on a small island in a lake district. The only way to get there was by a small boat he had designed himself. The house was also his laboratory. He divided its courtyard wall into roughly fifty sections, each built with a different brick, tile, and bond pattern, to see how they would hold up through a Finnish winter, and then used what he learned in his real buildings. The house is open only in summer. If you visit Finland in March, you can go to the Aalto Museum in a nearby city, which is open year-round.

學當自家街區的導遊

來源：Chicago for Chicagoans

11月15日星期天下午一點到五點，志工團體 Chicago for Chicagoans 在羅傑斯公園圖書館，開一堂免費的「導遊入門課」。

不需要經驗。課上會教你怎麼找老資料、怎麼在大家面前說話、怎麼安排路線。想拿證書的人，可以考一個小測驗，再帶一次練習導覽，讓老導遊打分數。

這個團體的導遊，很多就是這樣學出來的。他們帶大家走海德公園等老街區，想付多少就付多少。

On Sunday, November 15, from 1 to 5 p.m., the volunteer group Chicago for Chicagoans is holding a free "Intro to Tour Guiding" class at the Rogers Park Library. No experience is needed. The class covers how to find old archival records, how to speak in front of a crowd, and how to plan a route. Anyone who wants a certificate can take a short quiz, then lead a practice tour while an experienced guide scores them. Many of the group's guides learned the job this way. They lead visitors through older neighborhoods such as Hyde Park, and people pay whatever they wish.

一個人畫出的北美口音地圖

來源：aschmann.net

語言學家 Rick Aschmann 花了幾十年，自己做出一張北美英語口音地圖，把美國和加拿大各地的方言都畫在上面。

地圖連到六百多段真人說話的影片。點一下某個地方，就能聽到當地人怎麼說 about 或 bag。有些口音的分界線，只隔著幾公里外的小鎮。

他也老實寫出地圖還缺什麼，網頁上還留著一條條更新紀錄。這是一個人用心做出來的寶藏。

Linguist Rick Aschmann spent decades creating a map of North American English accents on his own, charting the dialects of communities across the United States and Canada. The map links to more than 600 video clips of real people speaking. Click on a location and you can hear how locals pronounce words like "about" or "bag." Some accent boundaries run between towns only a few miles apart. Aschmann is also upfront about what the map still lacks, and the site keeps a running log of its updates. It is a treasure that one person built with great care.
