澎湖的海，錄成一張唱片

來源：Discrepant

2013年上半年，住在台灣多年的法國錄音家 Yannick Dauby，在澎湖開了一間小小的「實驗聲音工作室」。他白天在海邊撿東西，也記錄海底的生物。

他把水下麥克風錄到的聲音、港口的風聲、喪禮音樂和收音機的雜音放在一起，再用海邊撿來的舊東西即興演奏。

這張專輯叫《一流水》，讓我們聽見離島（本島外的小島）海裡和岸上的聲音。

In the first half of 2013, Yannick Dauby, a French sound recordist who had lived in Taiwan for years, opened a small 'experimental sound studio' in Penghu. During the day, he scavenged the shore for objects and recorded the sounds of underwater creatures. He combined recordings captured by submerged microphones, harbor winds, funeral music, and radio static, then improvised using weathered objects he'd collected from the beach. The album, titled 'A Stream of Water,' lets us hear the voices of the sea and shore of the outlying islands—those small islands beyond the main island.

一頭馴鹿，一點也不浪費

來源：Lapland North

在芬蘭北方的伊納里村，薩米族（北歐的原住民）廚師 Heikki Nikula 開了一家餐廳，叫 Aanaar。食材大多來自附近的漁夫、養馴鹿（北方一種長角的鹿）的人和採野菜的人。

他不只用馴鹿肉，連心、血、舌頭和肝都拿來做菜。這來自薩米人的老想法：土地給的剛剛好，什麼都不能浪費。

這家餐廳在2020年被選為芬蘭年度餐廳，一年四季都開，就在河邊。

In the village of Inari in northern Finland, Sámi chef Heikki Nikula opened a restaurant called Aanaar. The ingredients mostly come from local fishermen, reindeer herders, and wild herb foragers. He doesn't just use reindeer meat—he also cooks with the heart, blood, tongue, and liver. This reflects an old Sámi principle: the land provides exactly what's needed, and nothing goes to waste. In 2020, the restaurant was named Finland's Restaurant of the Year. It operates year-round on the riverbank.

在城市裡，過兩天修道院的日子

來源：Monastery of the Holy Cross

芝加哥 Bridgeport 區有一座聖十字修道院，住著本篤會的修士（一生住在修道院裡修行的人）。這裡原本是立陶宛人的老教堂。

10月9日到11日，修道院開放一個週末，讓大家住進來。客人有自己的小房間，跟修士一起唱葛利果聖歌（一種很老的教會歌曲），參加彌撒（天主教的禮拜），也一起吃飯。

不用離開城市，就能走進一千多年前傳下來的生活。

In Chicago's Bridgeport neighborhood stands Holy Cross Monastery, home to Benedictine monks—people devoted to spending their entire lives within monastery walls in spiritual practice. This building was originally an old Lithuanian church. From October 9–11, the monastery opens for a special weekend, welcoming guests to stay. Visitors receive their own small rooms, join the monks in singing Gregorian chants (an ancient form of church chant), attend Mass (Catholic worship), and share meals together. Without leaving the city, you can step into a way of life that has been passed down over a thousand years.

一個人的日晷百科

來源：mysundial.ca

日晷（用太陽影子看時間的工具）是很老的時鐘。加拿大工程師 Carl Sabanski 在2001年做了一座五公尺多長的石頭日晷，放在小鎮 Pinawa。

後來，他花了很多年做網站 mysundial.ca。網站上有世界各地讀者寄來的日晷照片，也教你算出在自己家鄉，日晷上的線該怎麼畫。

網站還有可以印出來、剪下來的紙日晷，北美日晷協會也拿去給學生上課。

A sundial—a tool that uses the sun's shadow to tell time—is an ancient type of clock. Canadian engineer Carl Sabanski built a stone sundial over five meters long in 2001 and installed it in the small town of Pinawa. Later, he spent many years developing the website mysundial.ca. The site features sundial photographs submitted by readers from around the world and teaches you how to calculate and draw the shadow lines for a sundial in your own hometown. The site also offers printable, cut-out paper sundials that the North American Sundial Society uses for classroom instruction.
