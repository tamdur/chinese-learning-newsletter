唱自己的歌：楊弦點燃的民歌之火

來源：The News Lens International Edition

一九七五年，一個學工程的年輕人楊弦，把詩人余光中的九首詩配上曲子，做出了《中國現代民歌集》這張唱片。三個月內就賣光了一萬張。

那時候的大學生大多在聽外國的流行歌。楊弦喊出「唱自己的歌」，讓很多年輕人開始用自己的話寫歌，唱出台灣的樣子，掀起了「校園民歌」的風潮。

兩年後，原住民歌手胡德夫在同一個演唱會上唱了〈美麗島〉。這首歌後來因為太有力量，被政府禁止過。

In 1975, a young engineering student named Yang Xian set nine poems by poet Yu Guangzhong to music and released the album "Contemporary Chinese Folk Songs." Ten thousand copies sold out within three months. Most university students back then were listening to foreign pop music. But Yang Xian's rallying cry — "sing your own songs" — inspired many young people to write songs in their own voices, singing of Taiwan and igniting the "campus folk song" movement. Two years later, indigenous singer Hu Defu sang "Formosa" at that same concert. The song was so powerful that it was subsequently banned by the government.

聖誕老人號：一夜火車開進北極圈

來源：VR (Finnish State Railways)

在芬蘭，有一班有名的夜車，從首都赫爾辛基開到北邊的羅瓦涅米（一座靠近北極圈的城市），要坐大約十一到十二個小時。

車上有兩層的臥鋪車廂。旅客晚上在城市上車，聽著鐵軌的聲音睡著，早上醒來時，窗外已經變成蓋滿白雪的森林。有的房間裡還有自己的淋浴間和廁所，早餐也會送到房間門口。

芬蘭人其實不太喜歡「聖誕老人號」這個名字，但這段慢慢橫越整個國家的旅程，真的很受大家喜愛。

In Finland, there's a famous overnight train that runs from the capital, Helsinki, northward to Rovaniemi—a city nestled near the Arctic Circle—covering the journey in about eleven to twelve hours. The train features double-decker sleeper cabins. Passengers board in the city at night, fall asleep to the gentle rhythm of the rails, and wake the next morning to find snow-covered forests stretching across the landscape outside their windows. Some rooms even come with their own shower and toilet, and breakfast is delivered right to your cabin door. While the Finns aren't particularly fond of calling it the "Santa Claus Train," this leisurely journey meandering across the entire country has truly captured everyone's hearts.

人人可下：芝加哥西洋棋大賽等你來

來源：US Chess / mychess.events

七月十七到十九日，在芝加哥北邊的惠靈（Wheeling），有一場西洋棋比賽。

這場比賽最特別的地方，是把棋手按實力分成七組，從最厲害的大師組，一直到剛學不久的新手組。就算你是第一次比賽，也可以報名下五盤棋，而且不會碰到高手，還有機會贏得現金獎。

沒辦法待整個週末的人，也可以選只比兩天的快棋。

From July 17–19, Wheeling—just north of Chicago—will host a chess tournament. What makes this event special is that players are organized into seven groups based on skill level, ranging from advanced masters down to beginners. Even if you've never competed before, you can sign up to play five games, face only opponents at your own level, and have a shot at winning cash prizes. If you can't make it for the full weekend, a two-day rapid chess option is also available.

沉默的穀倉：一位攝影師的告別

來源：Looking at the West (Andrew McAllister)

二零一二年，攝影師麥卡利斯特（Andrew McAllister）用一顆新鏡頭，拍了美國愛達荷州一座舊穀倉（存放稻穀的高大建築）。一個多月後，那座穀倉失火燒掉了。

他發現，穀倉不見以後，整個小鎮好像也少了一塊回憶。從那時起，他開始了「穀倉調查」，一座一座地拍下西部小鎮裡那些正在消失的老建築，把它們當成快要被人忘記的故事，好好留了下來。

In 2012, photographer Andrew McAllister used his new lens to photograph an old barn in Idaho—a tall structure built to store grain. More than a month later, the barn caught fire and burned down. He realized that when the barn disappeared, the entire town seemed to lose a piece of its memory. From then on, he began the "Barn Investigation," systematically photographing the old buildings disappearing from small towns across the West, treating them as nearly-forgotten stories and preserving them for posterity.
