被禁的歌，在未來的島上醒來

來源：A Closer Listen

SABIWA是在高雄出生、住在柏林的聲音藝術家。2023年4月，她在英國唱片公司 Phantom Limb 推出專輯《Island no. 16》。

她把台灣傳統民歌的唱法，和現場錄音、雜音、電子聲音混在一起。最長的一首〈Dog Smells Your Future〉大約十五分鐘，裡面藏著一首戒嚴（政府嚴格管控人民的時期）時被禁的老歌。

舊旋律被埋在一層層聲音下面，像在想像一個未來：這首歌換了樣子，卻還活著。

SABIWA is a sound artist born in Kaohsiung and now based in Berlin.

In April 2023, she released the album Island no. 16 on Phantom Limb, a British record label. She blends the singing style of Taiwanese folk songs with live field recordings, noise, and electronic sounds. The longest track, "Dog Smells Your Future," runs about fifteen minutes and hides an old song that was banned during martial law (a period when the government tightly controlled the public). The original melody is buried under layer after layer of sound, as if imagining a future in which the song has changed its form but is still alive.

在泥巴裡踢足球的芬蘭小鎮

來源：Suopotkupallo.fi

芬蘭凱努地區有個小鎮叫許林薩爾米（Hyrynsalmi）。1990年代末，當地的越野滑雪選手想出一個怪方法練體力：在深到腰的沼澤（很溼的爛泥地）裡踢足球。

後來這變成「沼澤足球世界盃」，每年七月舉行，約有150隊參加，是金氏世界紀錄認可辦得最久的同類比賽。每場上下半場各只有十分鐘，踢完還能去免費的三溫暖洗掉泥巴。

三月去雖然看不到比賽，但它讓人看見芬蘭鄉下的幽默：越辛苦，越好玩。

In the Kainuu region of Finland there is a small town called Hyrynsalmi. In the late 1990s, local cross-country skiers came up with an unusual way to build their stamina: playing soccer in a swamp, in muddy ground that reached their waists. That eventually grew into the Finnish Swamp Football World Championship, held every July with around 150 teams taking part. It is the longest-running event of its kind recognized by Guinness World Records. Each match has two halves of just ten minutes, and afterward players can wash off the mud in a free sauna. Visiting in March, you won't see any games, but the town still shows the humor of rural Finland: the harder the work, the more fun it is.

用聲音做出一個角色

來源：Rough House Puppet Arts

11月30日星期一晚上6點到9點，芝加哥的偶戲團體 Rough House Puppet Arts 在 Free Street Storefront（4625 S Ashland Ave）辦一場工作坊，名叫「Puppeteering Your Voice」。

帶課的是樂手 Charlie Malave，他會教大家怎麼替木偶配音，也教擬音（用身邊的東西做出音效）。一個木偶像不像活的，常常要看它的聲音。

費用隨你付，從0到60美元，也有免費名額。

On Monday, November 30, from 6 to 9 p.m., Chicago puppetry group Rough House Puppet Arts will hold a workshop called "Puppeteering Your Voice" at Free Street Storefront (4625 S Ashland Ave). The class is led by musician Charlie Malave, who will teach participants how to give a voice to a puppet and also how to do foley, making sound effects with everyday objects. A puppet's sound often determines whether it seems alive. Tuition is pay-what-you-can, from $0 to $60, and there are also free spots.

被拆散的祈禱書，在網路上重逢

來源：Fragmentarium

1450到1500年間，法國昂熱有人做了一本手繪的祈禱書，叫「時禱書」。1964年7月6日，它在蘇富比拍賣會上被拆開來賣，書頁散到世界各地。

瑞士的網站 Fragmentarium 專門收集中世紀手抄本的碎片。它把原本連在一起的兩頁在網路上拼回來：一頁在紐西蘭的但尼丁，另一頁在美國南卡羅來納州的溫斯羅普大學。

兩頁相隔一萬五千公里，但用網站的「雙頁檢視」，任何人都能像翻一本完整的書一樣來回翻看。

Between 1450 and 1500, someone in Angers, France, produced a hand-painted prayer book called a "Book of Hours." On July 6, 1964, it was taken apart and sold at a Sotheby's auction, and its pages scattered around the world.

Fragmentarium, a Swiss website, specializes in collecting fragments of medieval manuscripts. It has digitally reunited two pages that were once bound together: one leaf is in Dunedin, New Zealand, and the other is at Winthrop University in South Carolina. The two pages are about 15,000 kilometers apart, yet using the site's "double-page view," anyone can flip back and forth between them as if leafing through a complete book.
