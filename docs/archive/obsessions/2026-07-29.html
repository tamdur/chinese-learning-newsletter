把罵人的話，變成搖滾的旗幟

來源：Taiwan Panorama

一九八九年，幾個大學生組了樂團，名字取自台灣的濁水溪，叫「濁水溪公社」。那時候「台客」是一句罵人的話，用來笑人土氣。一九九九年，他們發行專輯《台客的復仇》，大方把這個詞搶回來。

專輯裡混了那卡西（早年台灣酒館的走唱音樂）、龐克搖滾和台灣民謠，唱河水污染，也唱街頭抗議。他們的現場演出又吵又亂，故意惹人注目。十多年後，「台客」真的變成很多人驕傲的自稱。

In 1989, a group of university students formed a band named after Taiwan's Zhuoshui River: LTK Commune (濁水溪公社). At the time, "taike" was an insult — a word used to mock people as rustic and unsophisticated. In 1999 they released an album called Revenge of the Taike, boldly taking the word back.

The album mixed nakasi — the sing-along music of old Taiwanese taverns — with punk rock and Taiwanese folk songs, singing about river pollution and about street protests. Their live shows were loud and deliberately chaotic, designed to turn heads. A little over a decade later, "taike" had genuinely become a name many people used for themselves with pride.

一場沒有發生的戰爭，留下石頭的牙齒

來源：Salpa Line Museum / Visit Kotka-Hamina

一九四〇到一九四四年，芬蘭沿著東邊邊界蓋了一千二百公里長的「薩爾帕防線」。裡面有七百二十八座水泥碉堡（打仗時躲人的堅固小屋），還有工人一塊塊敲出來的花崗岩擋車石，人們叫它「薩爾帕的牙齒」。

它要擋的敵人始終沒有來。今天這些石頭和壕溝安靜地留在米希基萊的森林裡，離赫爾辛基約兩個半小時車程。室內博物館冬天休息，戶外步道卻全年免費開放。三月的雪地上走這一趟，安靜得讓人難忘。

From 1940 to 1944, Finland built a 1,200-kilometre defensive line along its eastern border, known as the Salpa Line. It contained 728 concrete bunkers — sturdy shelters for soldiers during combat — along with granite anti-tank obstacles that workers chiselled out block by block, which people called "Salpa's teeth."

The enemy the line was built to stop never came. Today the stones and trenches sit quietly in the forest at Miehikkälä, about two and a half hours' drive from Helsinki. The indoor museum closes for the winter, but the outdoor trail stays open and free year-round. Walking it across the March snow is quiet in a way that stays with you.

滿月之夜，火舞者回到彩虹海灘

來源：Chicago Full Moon Jam

二〇二六年八月二十七日晚上七點到十點，芝加哥的「滿月聚會」要在南區的彩虹海灘公園舉行。活動全由志工籌辦，跟著月亮的圓缺走，每個月換一座海灘。

黃昏時，大家先圍成一圈打鼓；接著火舞者拿起火焰，隨著鼓聲在密西根湖黑黑的水邊旋轉。不用報名、不用門票、不准喝酒。多年來，它幾乎全靠一個人告訴一個人，慢慢傳開。

On August 27, 2026, from 7 to 10 p.m., Chicago's Full Moon Jam will be held at Rainbow Beach Park on the South Side. The event is run entirely by volunteers, follows the cycles of the moon, and moves to a different beach each month.

At dusk, everyone gathers in a circle to drum; then the fire dancers take up their flames and spin along the dark edge of Lake Michigan in time with the drumming. No registration, no admission, no alcohol. For years it has spread almost entirely by word of mouth, one person telling another.

一直往下捲，直到海的最深處

來源：neal.fun (Neal Agarwal)

neal.fun 上有一個網頁叫「深海」。你只要一直往下捲動，就會從海面沉到一萬零九百九十四公尺深的馬里亞納海溝底部，旁邊還有深度表一路跳動。

沿路的生物都停在真實的深度：藍鯨在淺處，抹香鯨在兩千兩百五十公尺，鐵達尼號的殘骸在三千八百公尺，再往下是大王魷魚和燈籠魚。這個網頁是尼爾一個人做的，沒有廣告，也不用登入。

There's a webpage on neal.fun called "The Deep Sea." All you have to do is keep scrolling down, and you sink from the surface to the bottom of the Mariana Trench, 10,994 metres deep, with a depth gauge ticking away beside you.

The creatures along the way sit at their real depths: blue whales near the surface, a sperm whale at 2,250 metres, the wreck of the Titanic at 3,800 metres, and further down, giant squid and anglerfish. The page was made by Neal Agarwal on his own — no ads, and no login.
