豬頭神的六個宇宙：血肉果汁機的GIGO

來源：Blow 吹音樂

台中樂團「血肉果汁機」在二○一五年推出第一張專輯GIGO。他們把從小聽到大的廟會陣頭和牽亡歌，放進吵鬧的重金屬音樂裡。嗩吶（一種很響的吹管樂器）一出聲，就像神明繞境走進了搖滾現場。

專輯的故事更妙：五個宇宙把壞情緒倒進第六個宇宙，最後生出一位豬頭神。唱片封面做成道教經書的樣子。上線三十分鐘就衝上排行榜第一，還拿下第六屆金音獎最佳搖滾專輯。

In 2015, Taichung-based band Meat Juice Machine released their debut album, GIGO. They took the temple procession soundtracks and funeral chants they'd grown up hearing and hurled them into a wall of heavy metal noise. When the suona—a piercing wind instrument—kicks in, it's like a deity's parade just marched into a rock concert. The album's concept is wildly inventive: five universes dump their negativity into a sixth universe, which then spawns a pig-headed god. The cover is styled as a Taoist scripture. It hit number one on the charts within thirty minutes of release and won Best Rock Album at the 6th Golden Indie Music Awards.

一塊黑麥派，帶著故鄉逃難

來源：Slow Food Foundation Ark of Taste

芬蘭最有名的點心叫「卡累利阿派」。薄薄的黑麥皮，裡面包米粥，上面抹蛋奶油。它出生在十七世紀的農家，最早的紀錄在一六八六年。那麼冷的地方，只有黑麥長得好。

一九三九年冬季戰爭，約四十二萬卡累利阿人離開家鄉，把做派的方法一起帶走。從此這塊小派變成全芬蘭的味道。到赫爾辛基，可以去一九一四年開幕的哈卡涅米市場，吃一個剛出爐的。

Finland's most famous pastry is the Karelian pie—a thin rye crust wrapped around rice porridge and topped with egg butter. Born in 17th-century farmhouses, its earliest documented record dates to 1686. In such a cold climate, rye was virtually the only grain that could grow reliably. When the Winter War broke out in 1939, approximately 420,000 Karelians fled their homeland, carrying their pie-making methods with them. From that diaspora came a transformation: what had once been a regional specialty became the flavor of all Finland. Today, visitors to Helsinki can taste a freshly baked Karelian pie at the Hakaniemi Market, which opened in 1914.

星空下的啤酒節：天文館的成人之夜

來源：Adler Planetarium

九月十七日星期四晚上，芝加哥的阿德勒天文館要辦「火箭啤酒節」。這是每個月一次的成人之夜，只招待二十一歲以上的客人。

現場有德國風格的樂隊Die Musikmeisters演奏，還有兩家本地啤酒廠的酒。專家會談民間的太空旅行公司，和人類要怎麼飛向更遠的地方。天氣好的話，還能用館裡的望遠鏡看天空，背後就是整片城市夜景。

On Thursday evening, September 17th, Chicago's Adler Planetarium is hosting Rocket Beer Fest, a monthly adults-only night welcoming guests 21 and older. The event features live music from the German-style band Die Musikmeisters and beer from two local breweries. Expert panelists will discuss private space tourism companies and how humanity can venture farther into space. Weather permitting, visitors can use the planetarium's telescopes to observe the night sky, with the glittering city skyline as a backdrop.

玻璃上的好萊塢：一個人守住的手繪特效

來源：Matte Shot (nzpetesmatteshot.blogspot.com)

在電腦特效出現以前，電影裡的高山和城堡，很多是畫家畫在玻璃上的，這叫「接景」。紐西蘭人Peter Cook花了十幾年，一個人寫下超過一百九十篇文章，記住這門快被忘記的手藝。

他一格一格分析老電影。例如一九四一年的《大國民》，片中那座又大又舊的大宅，就是畫家邦尼斯特用筆畫出來的。

Before computer special effects existed, many mountains and castles in films were painted by artists directly onto glass—a technique called matte painting. A New Zealander named Peter Cook spent over a decade single-handedly writing more than 190 articles to preserve this rapidly disappearing craft. He analyzed old films frame by frame. For example, in 1941's Citizen Kane, the sprawling, decrepit mansion seen in the film was actually hand-painted by artist Bonestell.
