美人魚不需要王子：鄭宜蘋的噪音宇宙

來源：CDM Create Digital Music

台北藝術家Betty Apple（鄭宜蘋）在2024年三月發行了她的第一張個人專輯《Taiwan Bay Be》。她原本學劇場，後來在台北藝術大學跟著王福瑞學噪音音樂。

整張專輯重新講了小美人魚的故事：美人魚發現她不需要王子，就像台灣正在找到自己的聲音。她把工業電子樂和噪音混在一起，用各種奇怪的東西當樂器，創造出十一首大膽又自由的作品。

Taipei artist Betty Apple (鄭宜蘋) released her debut solo album "Taiwan Bay Be" in March 2024. Originally trained in theater, she studied noise music under Wang Fu-rui at Taipei National University of the Arts. The entire album retells the story of the Little Mermaid: the mermaid discovers she doesn't need a prince, much like Taiwan finding its own voice. She blends industrial electronic music with noise, using all manner of unconventional objects as instruments, creating eleven bold and liberated compositions.

裸麥魚包：芬蘭湖區烤了五百年的味道

來源：Finnish Intangible Cultural Heritage Wiki

在芬蘭湖區的庫奧皮奧（Kuopio），有一種從中世紀就流傳下來的食物叫做Kalakukko。它是用裸麥（一種北歐穀物）做成大麵包，裡面塞滿小湖魚和豬肉，慢烤八個小時，連魚骨頭都融化了。

2002年它拿到了歐盟的傳統特產保護認證。三月去庫奧皮奧的市場，可以買到用柴燒烤爐（燒木頭的烤爐）做的新鮮魚包，熱騰騰地用紙包著吃。

In Kuopio, a town in Finland's lake district, there is a food called Kalakukko that has been passed down since the Middle Ages. It's a large loaf made from rye—a cereal grain common in Northern Europe—stuffed with small lake fish and pork, then slow-baked for eight hours until even the fish bones dissolve. In 2002, it earned the European Union's protected designation of origin for traditional specialties. In March, you can buy freshly baked fish bread at Kuopio's market, made in wood-fired ovens and served piping hot wrapped in paper.

用身體說話：芝加哥文化中心免費舞蹈工作坊

來源：City of Chicago — DCASE

4月27日星期六下午三點，編舞家Keisha Janae會在芝加哥文化中心舉辦一場免費工作坊，叫做「讚美之屋的語言」。

參加的人不需要任何舞蹈經驗。大家圍成一圈，一起分享故事、做動作、畫畫和唱歌。這些靈感來自美國黑人信仰傳統。名額有限，想去要早點報名。

On Saturday, April 27th at 3 p.m., choreographer Keisha Janae will host a free workshop at the Chicago Cultural Center called "The Language of the House of Praise." No dance experience is necessary to participate. Attendees will gather in a circle to share stories, create movements, draw, and sing together. The inspiration draws from African American faith traditions. Spots are limited, so early registration is recommended.

用文字畫畫的工具：GlyphDrawing.Club

來源：GlyphDrawing.Club Blog

GlyphDrawing.Club是兩位芬蘭人做的免費網站，讓你用文字符號（就是鍵盤上的字）來畫圖。喜歡字體設計或文字藝術的人一定會愛上它。

他們的部落格更值得一看。有一篇文章介紹芬蘭字體師Malmiola在1937年用金屬印刷線條排出了作曲家西貝流士的肖像畫，那幅原作至今還保存在赫爾辛基的印刷博物館裡。

GlyphDrawing.Club is a free website created by two Finnish designers that lets you draw pictures using text characters—the symbols on your keyboard. Anyone who loves typeface design or text-based art will be captivated by it. Their blog is equally worth exploring. One article tells the story of Finnish typographer Malmiola, who in 1937 arranged metal printing lines to create a portrait of composer Sibelius. The original artwork is still preserved today in the Printing Museum in Helsinki.
