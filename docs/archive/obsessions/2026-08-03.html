地下室裡的台灣歌

來源：Moon Glyph (Bandcamp)

一九六二年，陳醫師在嘉義聽見隔壁樓傳來吉他聲，馬上跑去請人教他彈。一九七一年，他到美國聖路易市當醫生，因為太想家，就寫歌給還是嬰兒的兒子，自己錄成一支支伴唱影片。

幾十年後，兒子陳修生找出這些老錄音，和樂團夥伴加上吉他、電子琴和鼓，做成二○一六年的迷幻（像做夢一樣）流行專輯《台灣民謠風》。他笑說，這就是他小時候以為的台灣傳統音樂。

In 1962, in Chiayi, Dr. Chen heard guitar music drifting over from the building next door and immediately went to ask for lessons. In 1971 he moved to St. Louis to practice medicine. So homesick that he could hardly bear it, he began writing songs for his infant son and recording them himself as karaoke videos.

Decades later his son, Hewson Chen, dug out those old recordings and — with his bandmate — added guitar, synthesizer, and drums, turning them into the 2016 psychedelic pop album Taiwanese Folk Style. He jokes that this was simply what he assumed traditional Taiwanese music sounded like as a child.

森林深處的歌謠村

來源：Ilomantsi Museums

芬蘭東邊靠近俄羅斯邊界的伊洛曼齊，有一座「吟唱者之村」。一八四九年，學者隆洛特就是從這一帶的老歌手口中，收集詩句寫成芬蘭史詩《卡勒瓦拉》，故事裡有一位可怕的女巫女王露希。

村裡一九六四年蓋的木屋，照著卡累利阿地方的傳統樣式建造。夏天有人彈著康特勒琴（芬蘭的傳統琴）現場吟唱；冬天要小團體先預約才進得去。這裡離赫爾辛基約五百公里。

In Ilomantsi, in eastern Finland near the Russian border, stands the Rune Singer's Village. In 1849, the scholar Elias Lönnrot collected verses from the old singers of this region to compose the Finnish epic Kalevala, which features a fearsome witch-queen named Louhi.

The village's wooden house, built in 1964, follows the traditional building style of the Karelia region. In summer there is live singing accompanied by the kantele (a traditional Finnish zither); in winter, small groups must reserve ahead to get in. The site lies about 500 kilometres from Helsinki.

地道牆上的芝加哥壁畫

來源：Chicago History Museum

八月九日星期天上午十一點，藝術家華金斯帶大家走過海德公園大道和康乃爾大道之間的火車地道，看牆上的壁畫，全程約一個半到兩小時。

這裡有富勒一九七三年畫的〈海德公園精神〉，也有沃克一九七七年的〈善意的孩子〉。沃克是芝加哥六、七○年代社區壁畫運動的開創者之一。集合地點在東五十七街的公寓前，參加的人還能免費進芝加哥歷史博物館一星期。

On Sunday, August 9th at 11 a.m., artist Juarez Hawkins will lead a walk through the railway underpasses between Hyde Park Boulevard and Cornell Avenue to look at the murals on the walls. The tour runs about an hour and a half to two hours.

Here you will find Astrid Fuller's 1973 Spirit of Hyde Park and William Walker's 1977 Children of Goodwill. Walker was one of the founders of Chicago's community mural movement of the 1960s and '70s. The meeting point is in front of the apartment building on East 57th Street, and participants also get a week of free admission to the Chicago History Museum.

從網路垃圾堆裡挖出的寶藏

來源：cameronsworld.net

一九九○年代，有個免費網站 GeoCities，讓任何人都能做自己的網頁，最多時放了三千八百萬個。二○○九年雅虎把它關掉，這些網頁幾乎全部消失。

二○一五年，設計師卡麥隆從剩下的資料裡救出將近七百個會動的小圖、閃亮的字條和計數器，把它們拼成一張大網頁。點一下任何一個小圖，就會跳出視窗，帶你回到當年真正的舊網頁，背景還放著老音樂。

In the 1990s there was a free website called GeoCities that let anyone build their own webpage — at its peak it held 38 million of them. In 2009 Yahoo shut it down, and almost all of those pages vanished.

In 2015, the designer Cameron Askin rescued nearly 700 animated GIFs, glittering text banners, and hit counters from what remained, and assembled them into one enormous page. Click any of the little images and a window pops open, carrying you back to a real page from that era — with the old music still playing in the background.
