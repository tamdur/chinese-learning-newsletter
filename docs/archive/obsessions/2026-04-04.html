羅大佑的第一張專輯：之乎者也

來源：Wikipedia

一九八二年，羅大佑發了第一張專輯《之乎者也》。這四個字是文言文裡沒有意思的語氣詞，他用來笑那些老派的人。國民黨的電台不讓播。

主打歌《鹿港小鎮》唱出從鄉下到城市的年輕人的孤單，賣了十萬張以上。電吉他配上傳統的嗩吶（一種中國管樂器），搖滾和民謠混在一起。有人叫他台灣的 Bob Dylan。這張專輯打開了華語流行音樂的新路。

In 1982, Lo Ta-yu released his debut album zhī hū zhě yě (之乎者也). These four characters are meaningless particles from classical Chinese, which he used to mock old-fashioned people. The KMT-controlled radio stations refused to broadcast it.

The lead single "A Small Town in Lukang" captured the loneliness of young people moving from the countryside to the city, and sold over 100,000 copies. Electric guitar mixed with the traditional Chinese wind instrument suona created a blend of rock and folk music. Some called him the Bob Dylan of Taiwan. This album opened a new path for Chinese-language pop music.

芬蘭冰釣：人人都可以的自由

來源：Visit Finland

芬蘭有一個特別的法律叫「每個人的權利」：任何人都可以在公共的湖上冰釣，不用買許可證。三月是最好的時候：冰夠厚，魚很活，白天也比較長。

你只需要一根短釣竿和一個鉤子。在冰上鑽一個洞，等魚上鉤，釣到的鱸魚可以直接在冰上煮來吃。 Rovaniemi 和 Inari 附近都有導遊帶你去體驗。

Finland has a special law called "Everyone's Right": anyone can ice fish on public lakes without buying a permit. March is the best time: the ice is thick enough, fish are active, and the days are getting longer.

You only need a short fishing rod and a hook. Drill a hole in the ice, wait for the fish to bite, and the perch you catch can be cooked and eaten right there on the ice. Near Rovaniemi and Inari, guides can take you to experience it.

梵蒂岡天文學家來芝加哥演講

來源：Adler Planetarium

四月二十二日晚上七點， Adler 天文館請來一位特別的客人： Brother Guy Consolmagno，梵蒂岡天文台的前台長。他是 MIT 的行星科學家，也是天主教的修士。

講題是「被丟掉的世界」——那些差一點就對的天文學想法。門票包含博物館參觀，活動到八點半結束。科學和信仰的交會，很少見。

On the evening of April 22nd at 7 PM, the Adler Planetarium is hosting a special guest: Brother Guy Consolmagno, former director of the Vatican Observatory. He is a planetary scientist from MIT and also a Catholic priest.

The lecture topic is "Discarded Worlds" — astronomical ideas that almost got it right. Admission includes museum access, and the event concludes at 8:30 PM. The intersection of science and faith is rarely seen.

五萬九千座燈塔，一張地圖

來源：lighthouses.app

一位工程師生病在家的那個星期，做了一個網站：把美國海岸防衛隊的燈塔資料放上互動地圖。五萬九千多個燈塔、浮標（海上的指路燈）和信號站，每一個都用動畫顯示它真正的閃光方式。

資料來自二〇二五年的官方燈塔清單。燈塔是正在消失的技術，這張地圖是一個人對它的愛。

A software engineer, home sick one week, built a website: putting U.S. Coast Guard lighthouse data onto an interactive map. Over fifty-nine thousand lighthouses, buoys (guides to ships at sea), and signal stations — each one animated to show its actual flash pattern.

The data comes from the official 2025 lighthouse registry. Lighthouses are a disappearing technology, and this map is one person's love letter to them.
