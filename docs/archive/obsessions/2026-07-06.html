菊花夜行軍：在菸樓裡誕生的客家搖滾

來源：Discogs / 報導者

二〇〇一年，一群住在高雄美濃的客家人，組成了「交工樂隊」，做出一張很特別的專輯《菊花夜行軍》。他們沒有進大唱片公司，而是在一間「菸樓」（以前用來烤菸葉的老房子）裡錄音，還全部用客家話來唱。

專輯講一個年輕人阿成，從城市回到家鄉種花。台灣加入世界貿易組織後，便宜的外國花大量進來，本地花農很難過日子。這張專輯在二〇〇二年得到金曲獎最佳樂團。

In 2001, a group of Hakka people living in Meinong, Kaohsiung, formed the Labor Exchange Band (Jiaogong) and released a distinctive album called The Night March of the Chrysanthemums. Rather than signing with a major record label, they recorded in a tobacco house—an old structure once used to cure tobacco leaves—and sang entirely in Hakka. The album tells the story of a young man named A-Cheng who returns from the city to his hometown to grow flowers. After Taiwan joined the World Trade Organization, cheap imported flowers flooded the market, making it increasingly hard for local flower farmers to get by. The album won Best Band at the Golden Melody Awards in 2002.

走進別人的森林：芬蘭的「自然享用權」

來源：Yle

在芬蘭，有一條很老的規矩，叫做「自然享用權」。它讓每一個人，都可以在別人的土地上走路、露營，還可以採野果，不用先問主人。

每年夏天，成千上萬的泰國農夫飛到芬蘭北邊，採野生的雲莓和藍莓。這些野果沒辦法用農田種出來。對來度蜜月的人來說，這條規矩讓你可以離開步道，自由走進芬蘭的大森林。

In Finland, there is a very old rule called "Everyman's Right." It lets anyone walk across someone else's land, camp there, and pick wild berries without first asking the owner. Every summer, thousands of Thai farmers fly to northern Finland to harvest wild cloudberries and bilberries. These wild berries cannot be grown on farms. For a couple on their honeymoon, this rule offers the freedom to leave the marked trails and wander freely into Finland's vast forests.

在作家博物館裡當一晚的偵探

來源：American Writers Museum

七月八日星期三傍晚五點到七點半，芝加哥的「美國作家博物館」要辦一場推理遊戲。館裡有一件「寶物」不見了，客人要問別人問題、找線索，一起破案。

博物館歡迎大家穿古裝來玩，還準備了一種叫「修正案」的特別調酒。這是給大人（二十一歲以上）的活動，門票十八元起。

On Wednesday, July 8th, from 5 to 7:30 PM, Chicago's American Writers Museum will host a mystery game. A "treasure" has gone missing in the museum, and guests must ask one another questions, hunt for clues, and solve the case together. The museum welcomes visitors to come dressed in period costume, and has prepared a special cocktail called "The Amendment." It's an adults-only event (21 and up), with tickets starting at $18.

一個人畫出的「發明大地圖」

來源：historicaltechtree.com

有一位叫 Étienne Fortier-Dubois 的人，自己做了一個很好玩的網站，叫做「歷史科技樹」。他把兩千三百多種發明畫成一張大地圖，從三百萬年前的石器，一直到今天的人工智慧。

這張地圖不只告訴你東西是什麼時候發明的，還告訴你每一樣發明，是靠哪些更早的發明才做得出來。像遊戲裡的「科技樹」一樣，人人都可以幫忙加進新東西。

A person named Étienne Fortier-Dubois built a delightful website called the "Historical Tech Tree." He mapped over 2,300 inventions onto a single sprawling diagram, stretching from stone tools three million years ago all the way to artificial intelligence today. The map shows not just when each invention was created, but also which earlier inventions made it possible. Like a tech tree in a video game, anyone can help contribute new entries.
