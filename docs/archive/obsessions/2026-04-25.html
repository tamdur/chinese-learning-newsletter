山裡的電子琴聲：陳世興的四張專輯

來源：Discogs Digs

一九八八年，台灣吉他手陳世興離開台北，搬到山上生活。他用 Casio 合成器（電子音樂機器）和 Roland 吉他，同時發行四張專輯：《空》、《山》、《靈》、《雨》。這是台灣第一個專門做氛圍（ambient）音樂的廠牌 Prime Records 的開始。

山裡的風聲和冥想（靜坐思考）改變了他的音樂，從搖滾變成緩慢、安靜、帶有東方味道的聲音。有人說他像日本的喜多郎，但他的音樂更奇特，更像台灣的山。

In 1988, Taiwanese guitarist Chen Shixing left Taipei and moved to the mountains. Armed with a Casio synthesizer and a Roland guitar, he released four albums in quick succession: "Emptiness," "Mountain," "Spirit," and "Rain." This marked the beginning of Prime Records, Taiwan's first label dedicated to ambient music. The wind in the mountains and meditation transformed his sound from rock into something slow, quiet, and tinged with Eastern character. Some compared him to Japan's Kitaro, but his music was more distinctive—it sounded like Taiwan's mountains themselves.

滑下山坡迎接春天：芬蘭的懺悔節

來源：thisisFINLAND

每年二月底或三月初，芬蘭人會慶祝 Laskiainen，也就是懺悔節（一個古老的節日）。傳統上，你的雪橇（在雪上滑行的車）滑得越遠，那年的作物就長得越高。

今天，大人小孩都會去城市的山坡比賽滑雪橇，再吃一種叫 laskiaispulla 的奶油麵包，裡面有杏仁醬或果醬，配上熱豌豆湯。這是芬蘭人一起宣告「春天快來了」的方式。

Every late February or early March, Finns celebrate Laskiainen, also known as Shrove Tuesday—an ancient holiday. Traditionally, the farther your sledge travels down the snow, the taller your crops will grow that year. Today, both adults and children head to city slopes to race their sleds, then enjoy a pastry called laskiaispulla—a cream bun filled with almond paste or jam—paired with hot pea soup. It's how Finns collectively announce that spring is on its way.

努比亞的動物與神話：芝加哥大學免費講座

來源：Eventbrite / Institute for the Study of Ancient Cultures (UChicago)

五月六日晚上七點到九點，芝加哥大學古代文化研究所（ISAC）會舉辦一場免費的公開講座。主講人是 Bruce Williams，他花了幾十年寫了八本書，記錄古代努比亞（非洲東北的古文明）的考古發現。

那晚也有新書簽名會，書名是《古代努比亞動物圖鑑》，收集了古代藝術中真實和神話中的動物。不用買票，直接去就好。

On the evening of May 6th from 7 to 9 p.m., the University of Chicago's Institute for the Study of Ancient Cultures (ISAC) will host a free public lecture. The speaker is Bruce Williams, who has spent decades writing eight books documenting archaeological discoveries from ancient Nubia, an early civilization in northeastern Africa. That evening will also feature a book signing for his new work, "Ancient Nubian Animal Iconography," which collects both real and mythological animals from ancient art. No tickets required — just show up.

一個三角形裡有多少秘密？六萬個點的百科全書

來源：Clark Kimberling, University of Evansville

數學家 Clark Kimberling 在美國伊凡斯維爾大學工作，三十多年來他只做一件事：找出三角形裡面所有特別的點。他建了一個網站叫「三角形中心百科全書」，目前已經記錄超過六萬個點。

每一個點都有精確的定義和座標（位置數字）。一個簡單的三角形，竟然藏著這麼深的世界。這個網站是一個人對幾何（形狀的數學）的熱愛，變成了一座小小的紀念碑。

Mathematician Clark Kimberling, who works at the University of Evansville in the United States, has spent over thirty years doing one thing: discovering every special point hidden inside triangles. He built a website called the "Encyclopedia of Triangle Centers," which now documents more than 60,000 points. Each point has a precise definition and coordinates (positional numbers). A simple triangle, it turns out, conceals a remarkably deep world. This website stands as a small monument to one person's passion for geometry—the mathematics of shapes.
