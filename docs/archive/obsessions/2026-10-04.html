倉庫裡的台灣迪斯可

來源：Teleport City / Aberrant Records

一九七〇到八〇年代，西方的迪斯可（一種跳舞音樂）傳到台灣。一群女歌手用國語來唱，配上台灣自己的編曲，聽起來比原版更奇妙。

後來錄音帶和卡拉OK流行，這些黑膠唱片被丟進倉庫。台北的唱片行 THT Records 一箱一箱去翻，把它們找了回來。

二〇二〇年，Aberrant Records 出版了合輯《Taiwan Disco》，讓世界聽見這段被忘記的舞曲年代。

From the 1970s to the 1980s, Western disco—a type of dance music—arrived in Taiwan. A group of female singers performed it in Mandarin, layered with Taiwan's own arrangements, creating something even more distinctive than the originals.

Later, as cassette tapes and karaoke took over, these vinyl records were stuffed into warehouses. A Taipei record shop called THT Records hunted through the boxes, one by one, and rescued them from obscurity.

In 2020, Aberrant Records released the compilation album "Taiwan Disco," bringing this forgotten chapter of dance music back to the world's attention.

海上漂來的「私酒魚雷」

來源：Tulli (Finnish Customs) / Customs Museum

一九一九到一九三二年，芬蘭禁止賣酒。可是想喝酒的人沒有變少，走私（偷偷運貨）反而越來越多。

大船停在芬蘭海域外面，小快艇再拖著像魚雷一樣的密封酒桶衝上岸。海關的船一靠近，就把繩子割斷，讓酒桶沉進海裡。

從赫爾辛基坐渡輪不遠，就是海上要塞（保護城市的堡壘）芬蘭堡。島上的海關博物館免費參觀，還能看到這種「私酒魚雷」的模型。

From 1919 to 1932, Finland banned alcohol sales. But the desire to drink didn't vanish—smuggling actually grew more and more rampant.

Large ships anchored just outside Finnish waters, while small speedboats towed torpedo-shaped, sealed barrels of alcohol toward shore. The moment customs boats approached, smugglers would cut the ropes and let the barrels sink into the sea.

Just a short ferry ride from Helsinki lies Suomenlinna, a sea fortress built to protect the city. The island's customs museum is free to enter and displays a model of these ingenious smuggling "torpedoes."

火裡重生的玻璃

來源：Open House Chicago (Chicago Architecture Center)

10月17日星期六上午11點到下午3點，東加菲爾德公園區的 Firebird Community Arts 免費開放，這是芝加哥建築開放日的活動之一。

大家可以看老師和年輕人現場吹玻璃。這些年輕人都曾受過槍擊暴力的傷害。他們站在上千度的火爐前，把燒紅的玻璃變成作品，也慢慢治好心裡的傷。

地址：2651 W. Lake St.，走進去就能看。

On Saturday, October 17th, from 11 a.m. to 3 p.m., Firebird Community Arts in the East Garfield Park neighborhood is free to visit as part of Chicago's Architecture Open House event.

Visitors can watch instructors and young artists blow glass live. These young people have all experienced the trauma of gun violence. Standing before furnaces heated to over a thousand degrees, they transform glowing glass into art—and in the process, slowly heal their inner wounds.

Location: 2651 W. Lake St. — just walk in to see for yourself.

替商場寫下最後一頁

來源：DeadMalls.com

二〇〇〇年，兩位曾在百貨公司上班的人 Peter Blackbird 和 Brian Florence，做了一個網站：DeadMalls.com。

這裡專門記錄美國快要關門或已經關門的購物商場，已經有將近四百五十篇。每一篇都寫出商場的歷史、一家家店怎麼離開，還有讀者寄來的照片。

網站到今年還在更新。讀者就像醫生，替商場寫「死亡報告」，記下網購和人口搬走怎麼讓商場一間間變空。

In 2000, two former department store employees named Peter Blackbird and Brian Florence launched a website: DeadMalls.com.

The site specializes in documenting American shopping malls that are closing or have already closed, featuring nearly 450 articles to date. Each entry tells the mall's story, chronicles how stores vacated one by one, and includes photos submitted by readers.

The website continues to be updated through today. Readers serve as documentarians, writing the "death reports" of malls and recording how e-commerce and population shifts have emptied them out one by one.
