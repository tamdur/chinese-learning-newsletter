弦樂遇上唱盤：六個女生的實驗

來源：Douban Music / Taiwan Beats

二○一五年，六位學古典音樂的女生組成了 SEMIFUSA 樂團。兩把小提琴、兩把中提琴、兩把大提琴，再加上一台唱盤機。

製作人是拿過世界冠軍的 DJ QuestionMark。他把「搓碟」（用手來回轉唱片發出聲音）直接放進弦樂裡，一下子像爵士，一下子像拉丁舞曲。這張同名專輯也讓他第一次入圍金曲獎最佳專輯製作人。

In 2015, six classically trained women formed the ensemble SEMIFUSA: two violins, two violas, two cellos — and a turntable. The producer was DJ QuestionMark, a world champion turntablist, who folded scratching (working a record back and forth by hand to make sound) directly into the string arrangements, so the music swings from jazz to Latin dance floor and back. The self-titled album earned him his first Golden Melody Award nomination for Best Album Producer.

砍開山頂，露出芬蘭最暗的夜空

來源：DarkSky International

二○一四年，芬蘭東部一座農場在砍樹時，不小心把山頂清空了。主人抬頭一看，夜空一點遮擋也沒有。

這片山頂叫 Syrjävaara，現在是九十公頃的暗空公園，用儀器量出來的黑暗程度，是全芬蘭紀錄裡最暗的幾個之一。附近三個小鎮一起保護這片夜色，山上有小天文台，也有咖啡館，客人捧著熱咖啡等極光。

In 2014, a farm in eastern Finland was logging when it accidentally cleared its own hilltop. The owner looked up and found the night sky with nothing in the way. The hill is called Syrjävaara, and it is now a 90-hectare dark sky park; measured with instruments, its darkness ranks among the deepest ever recorded in Finland. Three nearby towns work together to protect the night here. On the hill there is a small observatory and a café, where visitors cradle hot coffee and wait for the aurora.

拳台、老爺車，一起開進奧格登大道

來源：Chicago Youth Boxing Club

八月二十三日星期日，芝加哥西區的北朗代爾有一場免費派對，地點在朗代爾基督教健身中心。

芝加哥青少年拳擊俱樂部訓練社區小孩已經二十年，這天把拳台搬到街上比賽。同一天也慶祝六十六號公路一百歲，一整排老爺車停在奧格登大道旁，還有音樂和小吃。走進去就好，不用買票。

On Sunday, August 23, there is a free party in North Lawndale on Chicago's West Side, at the Lawndale Christian Fitness Center. The Chicago Youth Boxing Club has been training neighborhood kids for twenty years, and on this day it moves the ring outdoors for live bouts. The same day marks Route 66's hundredth birthday, with a row of vintage cars parked along Ogden Avenue, plus music and food. Just walk in — no ticket needed.

一個人，替全世界的蒸汽火車點名

來源：SteamLocomotive.com

SteamLocomotive.com 是火車迷 Wes Barris 一手做的網站。他把還留在世上的蒸汽火車，一輛一輛記下來。

美國、加拿大、英國、墨西哥都有。每一輛都寫清楚：哪一年造的、哪家工廠造的、車輪怎麼排列，還有現在停在哪裡。有的還在觀光鐵路上跑，有的在公園裡慢慢生鏽。

SteamLocomotive.com is a site built single-handedly by railway enthusiast Wes Barris. One by one, he records every steam locomotive still left in the world — in the United States, Canada, Britain, and Mexico. Each entry sets it all down: the year it was built, the works that built it, how its wheels are arranged, and where it stands today. Some still run on heritage railways; others sit slowly rusting in a park.
