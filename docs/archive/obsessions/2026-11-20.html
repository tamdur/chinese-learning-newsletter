浴室裡的慢歌

來源：Wikipedia / Dork

deca joins 是來自台北的樂團，2013年成立。他們先叫 FUBAR，後來改名「灰矮星」，直到2017年1月才定下現在的名字。

三個月後，他們推出專輯《浴室》。樂團說，這是故意離開尖銳的搖滾，走向更柔軟、懶洋洋的聲音。樂評聽到裡面混著獨立搖滾、爵士和迷幻音樂。

這張專輯在台灣和國外都大受好評，也定下了他們朦朧（霧霧的、看不清楚）又親密的招牌聲音。

deca joins is a band from Taipei, formed in 2013. They first called themselves FUBAR, then renamed themselves Grey Dwarf (灰矮星), and did not settle on their current name until January 2017. Three months later, they released the album Bathroom (浴室). The band says it was a deliberate move away from sharp rock toward a softer, lazier sound. Critics heard a blend of indie rock, jazz, and psychedelia in it. The album was widely praised in Taiwan and abroad, and it set the band's signature sound: hazy and intimate.

一台失敗的遊戲機

來源：Vapriikki Museum Centre

在芬蘭坦佩雷市的 Vapriikki 博物館中心，芬蘭遊戲博物館在2026年10月重新開幕。

最重要的展覽叫「美好的失敗」，主角是諾基亞2003年推出的 N-Gage 遊戲機。它賣得很差，卻幾乎全由坦佩雷的工程師打造。博物館認為，正是這次失敗，練出了後來做出《憤怒鳥》和《部落衝突》的人才。

館裡有一百多款遊戲，其中六十多款可以親手玩，連老諾基亞手機也有。週二到週日開放，成人票16歐元。

At the Vapriikki museum center in Tampere, Finland, the Finnish Games Museum reopened in October 2026. Its main exhibition, "Beautiful Failures," centers on the N-Gage, a game console Nokia launched in 2003. It sold poorly, yet it was built almost entirely by engineers in Tampere. The museum argues that this failure is what trained the people who later made Angry Birds and Clash of Clans. The museum has more than 100 games, and over 60 of them can be played by hand, including old Nokia phones. It is open Tuesday through Sunday, and adult admission is 16 euros.

屋頂上的冰壺

來源：The Gwen Hotel

芝加哥「華麗一英里」旁的 The Gwen 飯店，每年冬天都把屋頂變成高山小屋風的天地。這裡有不用真冰的冰壺（在冰上推大石頭的運動）場、小木屋和火爐桌，還能看城市夜景。

一小時的冰壺時段每人約25美元，包含器材、簡單教學和一份季節點心。場地通常從十一月底開到三月初，可以在 OpenTable 上訂位。

Every winter, The Gwen hotel, next to Chicago's Magnificent Mile, turns its rooftop into a mountain-lodge wonderland. Up there you'll find a curling area that uses no real ice (curling is the sport of sliding heavy stones across ice), along with cabins and fire-pit tables, plus views of the city at night.

A one-hour curling session costs about $25 per person and includes equipment, a short lesson, and a seasonal snack. The space usually runs from late November to early March, and you can book a spot on OpenTable.

同一片星空，二十八種故事

來源：Visual Cinnamon

荷蘭天文學家 Nadieh Bremer 後來成了資料圖像藝術家。2018年5月到7月，她做了一個互動星空網站「Figures in the Sky」。

網站把28種文化畫的星座，像中國、毛利、阿茲特克和北歐，全都疊在同一群星星上。點一下參宿四（獵戶座裡的紅色大星），就會看到它同時屬於17種文化的星座，每種用不同顏色畫出。

星星資料來自免費的天文軟體 Stellarium。她一個人做完，還順手修正了一些錯誤。

Dutch astronomer Nadieh Bremer later became a data visualization artist. From May to July 2018, she built an interactive star map website called "Figures in the Sky." The site layered the constellations drawn by 28 cultures, including Chinese, Māori, Aztec, and Norse, over the same set of stars. Clicking on Betelgeuse (the large red star in Orion) shows that it belongs to the constellations of 17 cultures at once, each drawn in a different color. The star data comes from Stellarium, a free astronomy software program. She completed the project on her own and, along the way, fixed a few errors.
