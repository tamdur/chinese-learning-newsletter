生祥樂隊《圍庄》：用客語唱出土地的怒吼

來源：beehype

二〇一六年，林生祥和他的樂隊發行了《圍庄》，一張完全用客家話演唱的雙專輯。這張專輯的靈感來自高雄美濃的真實故事：石化工廠要蓋在農田旁邊，村民起來抗議。

音樂非常特別：月琴（一種圓形的傳統樂器）和嗩吶碰上龐克（punk）吉他和雷鬼（reggae）節奏，再加上北管（廟會音樂）的元素。每家唱片公司都拒絕了，最後靠群眾募資才成功。二〇一七年拿下金曲獎評審團大獎。

In 2016, Lin Shengxiang and his band released "Weizhuang," a double album performed entirely in Hakka. The album draws inspiration from a true story in Meinong, Kaohsiung: a petrochemical factory was set to be built beside farmland, prompting villagers to protest.

The music is strikingly original: the moon lute—a round traditional instrument—and the suona collide with punk guitar and reggae rhythms, layered with elements of beiguan, the temple fair music tradition. Every major record label rejected it, so the band turned to crowdfunding and succeeded. In 2017, it won the Golden Melody Award for Best Album (Judging Panel Award).

努塔耶爾維玻璃村：芬蘭最老的手工玻璃工坊

來源：Visit Finland

在坦佩雷南邊的鄉間，有一座一七九三年建立的小村莊叫努塔耶爾維（Nuutajärvi）。這裡是芬蘭最古老的玻璃工坊，到現在還在運作。

工業生產在二〇一四年結束後，村子變成了藝術家的社區。獨立的玻璃工匠在老窯房裡工作，遊客可以看他們親手吹製著名的 Toikka 玻璃鳥。村裡有自己的旅館和小餐廳，從赫爾辛基開車大約兩小時，很適合蜜月旅行中安排一晚。

In the countryside south of Tampere sits a small village called Nuutajärvi, established in 1793. It is Finland's oldest glassworks, and it continues to operate to this day.

After industrial production ended in 2014, the village transformed into an artist community. Independent glass artisans work in the old kilns, where visitors can watch them hand-blow the famous Toikka glass birds. The village has its own inn and small restaurant, located about two hours' drive from Helsinki—a perfect spot to arrange a night's stay during a honeymoon trip.

Doc10 紀錄片影展：十部片、十個真實故事

來源：doc10.org

芝加哥唯一的純紀錄片影展 Doc10 將在四月二十四日到五月三日舉行，地點在林肯廣場的 Davis 戲院和市中心的乘斯乃爾（Siskel）電影中心。今年是第十一屆。

影展只放映十部精心挑選的紀錄片，過去的選片曾獲奧斯卡提名。今年最後一天放映的是《刀》，關於作家乘魯西迪（Rushdie）被刺殺未遂的故事，映後有他本人的現場問答。票價每場二十美元，常常很快賣完。

Chicago's only pure documentary film festival, Doc10, will run from April 24 to May 3, held at the Davis Theater in Lincoln Square and the Siskel Film Center downtown. This year marks the eleventh edition.

The festival screens only ten carefully curated documentaries, past selections of which have received Oscar nominations. The final day's film is "The Knife," about the attempted assassination of author Salman Rushdie, followed by a live Q&A with Rushdie himself. Tickets are twenty dollars per screening and often sell out quickly.

Erowid：一對夫妻花三十年建的知識寶庫

來源：erowid.org

Erowid 是一個一九九五年創立的非營利網站，由一對夫妻維護至今。它收集了超過四萬篇關於各種精神活性（讓心理產生變化的）物質的第一人稱報告，從咖啡因到草藥都有。

每篇報告都經過仔細審核和分類。這個網站代表了早期網路的精神：知識應該免費、開放、不帶偏見。它的設計非常樸素，內容卻極其豐富，是一個關於人類意識體驗的獨特圖書館。

Erowid is a nonprofit website founded in 1995 and maintained by a married couple to this day. It houses over 40,000 first-person accounts of various psychoactive substances—compounds that alter mental states—ranging from caffeine to herbal remedies.

Each report is carefully reviewed and categorized. The site embodies the spirit of the early internet: knowledge should be free, open, and unbiased. Its design is remarkably plain, yet its content is extraordinarily rich—a unique library of human consciousness and experience.
