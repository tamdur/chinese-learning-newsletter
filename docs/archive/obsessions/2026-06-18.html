一張嚇壞政府的台語唱片

來源：Wikipedia / 台灣文化部

一九八九年，台灣剛解嚴（政府放寬管制）不久，「黑名單工作室」推出了專輯《抓狂歌》。

這張唱片花了五年才完成，把搖滾、饒舌和台語街頭對話混在一起。其中一首〈民主阿草〉公開叫人上街，馬上被禁止播放。

如今它被看作「新台語歌」的起點，二○一九年還得到金曲獎的特別貢獻獎。

In 1989, shortly after Taiwan lifted martial law—the government relaxed its controls—the "Blacklist Studio" released the album "Songs of Madness." The record took five years to complete, blending rock, rap, and Taiwanese street vernacular. One track, "Democracy Bumpkin," explicitly called people into the streets and was immediately banned from broadcast. Today it is regarded as the starting point of "new Taiwanese song," and in 2019 it received a Special Contribution Award at the Golden Melody Awards.

橘色剪刀的故鄉，變成藝術村

來源：History Tools

一六四九年，一位商人在芬蘭南部的河邊蓋了一座鐵工廠。這就是「菲斯卡」（Fiskars）的起點，後來這裡做出了聞名世界的橘色手把剪刀。

工業離開後，空下來的紅磚老屋變成了工作室和畫廊。今天約有一百位手藝人住在這裡，有吹玻璃的、做陶器的和打鐵的。

三月時，村子蓋著雪，遊客很少，離首都只要一小時，很適合慢慢逛。

In 1649, a merchant built an iron factory by a river in southern Finland. This was the beginning of Fiskars, which would later become famous worldwide for its orange-handled scissors. After industry left, the vacant red-brick buildings were transformed into studios and galleries. Today, about a hundred artisans live here—glass blowers, potters, and blacksmiths among them. In March, the village is blanketed in snow with few tourists, yet it's only an hour from the capital, making it perfect for a leisurely visit.

用畫蓋過塗鴉的三十年海牆

來源：Rogers Park Business Alliance

從一九九三年起，芝加哥羅傑斯公園區的居民用一個特別的方法對付湖邊長牆上的幫派塗鴉：不是把它塗掉，而是畫上自己的畫。

每年的「牆上藝術家節」人人都能畫一小格。這面六百英尺的牆三十多年來年年重畫，當年的幫派塗鴉再也沒回來。

今年主題是「我們都是鄰居」，六月二十、二十一日在羅耀拉海灘登場，還有音樂和舞蹈。

Since 1993, residents of Chicago's Rogers Park have tackled gang graffiti on a lakefront wall with an unusual approach: rather than paint over it, they paint their own art on top of it. Every year at the "Artists of the Wall" festival, anyone can paint their own small section. Over more than three decades, this six-hundred-foot wall gets repainted annually, and the gang tags have never returned. This year's theme is "We Are All Neighbors," taking place at Loyola Beach on June 20th and 21st, complete with music and dance performances.

人類四千年怎麼把音樂寫下來

來源：Mae Mai (Jon Silpayamanant's blog)

一位住在美國的泰裔大提琴家，花了很多年，一個人做了一份「音樂記譜（把音樂寫成符號）時間表」，總共兩千多條。

最早的一條是三千多年前、刻在泥板上的古老歌曲，也收了中國《禮記》裡的鼓譜。他特別不把西方放在中心，大部分內容來自亞洲、非洲和中東。

這份表到二○二六年還在更新，是他一個人的世界音樂資料庫的一部分。

A Thai-American cellist living in the United States has spent many years compiling a "music notation timeline"—a single-handedly assembled record of over two thousand entries documenting how humans have written down music. The earliest entry dates back more than three thousand years: an ancient song carved into clay tablets. The collection also includes drum notation from China's Classic of Rites. He has deliberately avoided centering Western notation, instead drawing most entries from Asia, Africa, and the Middle East. The timeline continues to be updated as of 2026 and forms part of his personal world music database.
