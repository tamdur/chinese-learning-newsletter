被收回的唱片：楊祖珺與美麗島

來源：Wikipedia (Taiwan) / 台灣流行音樂維基館

一九七九年一月，民歌手楊祖珺發行了她的第一張專輯，由新格唱片出版。

裡面有兩首歌被官方盯上：「美麗島」和「少年中國」。三月，唱片送進中國廣播公司，七月新聞局就下令全面回收、禁止販賣。

留下來的唱片太少，原版黑膠（早期的圓形唱片）成了收藏家眼中的寶貝。「美麗島」後來成為台灣民主運動的歌，也給了那年高雄事件的雜誌名字。

In January 1979, folk singer Yang Tsu-chun released her debut album on Xinge Records.

Two of its songs caught the authorities' attention: “Formosa” and “Young China”. In March the record was sent to the China Broadcasting Corporation; by July the Government Information Office had ordered a total recall and banned its sale.

So few copies survived that original vinyl pressings became treasures in the eyes of collectors. “Formosa” went on to become an anthem of Taiwan's democracy movement, and gave its name to the magazine behind that year's Kaohsiung Incident.

消失的冰上公路

來源：Europa.tips / Väylä (Finnish Transport Infrastructure Agency)

芬蘭北部的海路托島，以前每到冬天渡船就停開，車子改走一條十公里長的冰上公路，直接開過結冰的海面。

二○二五到二六年的冬天，是這條冰路最後一次開放。二○二六年六月二十九日，一條八點四公里長的堤道通車，上面有兩座七百五十公尺的橋。

想開冰路的人還有一個地方：北卡累利阿的科利冰路，長七公里，是歐洲最長的內陸冰路。冰厚四十公分才開放，限速每小時五十公里，不能超車，也不能停車。

Hailuoto Island in northern Finland used to shut down its ferry every winter, and cars took a ten-kilometre ice road instead, driving straight across the frozen sea.

The winter of 2025-26 was the last time that ice road opened. On 29 June 2026 an 8.4-kilometre causeway carrying two 750-metre bridges opened to traffic.

Anyone still wanting to drive on ice has one more option: the Koli ice road in North Karelia, seven kilometres long and the longest inland ice road in Europe. It opens only once the ice is forty centimetres thick, with a speed limit of fifty kilometres an hour, no overtaking and no stopping.

音樂教室裡的塔羅課

來源：Friendly Music Community (Berwyn, IL)

八月三十日星期天傍晚六點到七點半，芝加哥西邊柏文鎮的一間社區音樂空間，要上一堂進階塔羅牌課。

老師會把七十八張牌上的人物，和猶太神祕學的「生命樹」圖對照，教已經懂基本牌義的人，怎麼專業地處理沉重的問題。

請自己帶偉特牌，附近的書店 The Pile 就有賣。四堂課一共一百美元，滿十八歲就能報名，不必有經驗。

On Sunday 30 August, from 6 to 7:30 in the evening, a community music space in Berwyn, west of Chicago, is holding an advanced tarot class.

The instructor will match the figures on the 78 cards against the “Tree of Life” diagram of Jewish mysticism, teaching people who already know the basic card meanings how to handle heavy questions professionally.

Bring your own Rider-Waite deck — The Pile, a bookshop nearby, sells them. Four classes cost $100 in total, anyone eighteen or over can sign up, and no experience is required.

免費送出的摺紙密碼

來源：Origami By Boice (obb.design)

波特蘭的摺紙藝術家 Boice Wong 十三歲那年，看見一位交換學生摺出一條龍，從此愛上摺紙。他在自己的網站上，把最複雜作品的「摺線圖」免費送給大家。

很多職業摺紙家把摺線圖當成祕密收著。這種圖看起來只是一堆亂線，其實是精準的地圖，告訴你該在方形紙上壓哪裡，平面才會立起來，變成龍或人。

二○二一年，他拿下「摺紙奧運」銀牌，是第一個得獎的美國人。

Boice Wong, an origami artist in Portland, fell in love with paper folding at thirteen, when he watched an exchange student fold a dragon. On his own website he gives away, for free, the crease patterns behind his most complex pieces.

Many professional folders keep their crease patterns to themselves. A pattern looks like nothing but a tangle of lines, but it is in fact a precise map: it tells you where to press on a square of paper so that the flat sheet stands up and becomes a dragon, or a person.

In 2021 he took silver at the “Origami Olympics” — the first American ever to medal there.
