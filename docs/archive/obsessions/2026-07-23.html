冰冷電音裡的完美情歌

來源：Wikipedia（中文）／豆瓣音樂

二〇〇〇年，陳珊妮做出第六張專輯《完美的呻吟》。這一年她三十歲，把過去的民謠搖滾放下，走進電子音樂。

香港雙人組Minimal和吉他手徐千秀，替她編出冰冷又漂亮的聲音，唱出一首首成熟的情歌。專輯剛出來時賣得不好，卻在多年後被大家看見。在台灣一九九三到二〇〇五年的百大專輯裡，它排名第八十五。

In 2000, Chen Shani released her sixth album, "Perfect Moan." At thirty, she left her folk-rock days behind and ventured into electronic music. Hong Kong duo Minimal and guitarist Xu Qianxiu composed a cold yet beautiful sound for her, producing mature love songs throughout. The album didn't sell well upon release, but found its audience years later. In Taiwan's top 100 albums from 1993 to 2005, it ranked 85th.

兩百年玻璃村，親手吹一件作品

來源：Nuutajärven Lasi／Visit Tampere

在芬蘭坦佩雷附近，有一座叫努塔耶爾維（Nuutajärvi）的玻璃村。它建於一七九三年，是芬蘭最老、還在運作的玻璃工廠，村裡有十八世紀的鐘樓和老莊園。

旅客可以在師傅的帶領下，拿起吹管，把一團一千一百度的熱玻璃，慢慢吹成自己的作品。設計師托伊卡有名的玻璃小鳥，也是在這裡做出來的。

Near Tampere, Finland, sits a glass village called Nuutajärvi. Founded in 1793, it's Finland's oldest glass factory still in operation, with an 18th-century bell tower and historic estates dotting the grounds. Visitors can work alongside master craftspeople, picking up a blow pipe to slowly transform a ball of molten glass—heated to 1,100 degrees—into their own piece. The designer Toikka's celebrated glass birds were crafted in this very workshop.

在紐伯里圖書館，找回你的家族樹

來源：Newberry Library

八月一日星期六早上九點半，芝加哥的紐伯里圖書館（Newberry Library）有一堂免費的家譜（家族的紀錄）課，叫「家譜入門」。

館員會教大家怎麼用圖書館的老資料，開始畫自己的家族樹。不用報名，現場有四十個位子，先到先坐。課後還可以請館員幫忙，找到和祖先有關的移民、戶口和教會紀錄。

At 9:30 a.m. on Saturday, August 1st, Chicago's Newberry Library is hosting a free genealogy workshop called "Introduction to Family Trees." Librarians will show you how to use the library's historical materials to start mapping your own family tree. No registration required—just 40 seats available on a first-come, first-served basis. After class, staff can help you track down immigration papers, census records, and church documents about your ancestors.

全世界最懂鞋帶的一個網站

來源：Ian's Shoelace Site（fieggen.com）

住在澳洲墨爾本的伊恩（Ian Fieggen），花了二十多年，做出全世界最完整的鞋帶網站。裡面有一百多種綁鞋帶的教法、超過兩千八百張照片。

他在一九九八年還發明了「伊恩結」，只要一半的時間，就能綁出牢牢的蝴蝶結。網站甚至解釋，為什麼有些綁法容易鬆掉，有些不會。

Ian Fieggen, who lives in Melbourne, Australia, has spent over twenty years building the world's most comprehensive shoelace website. It features over a hundred different shoelace-tying techniques and more than twenty-eight hundred photographs. In 1998, he also invented the "Ian Knot," which allows you to tie a secure bow in just half the time. The website even explains why some tying methods come undone easily while others remain secure.
