拼貼的安魂曲：珂拉琪《MEmento·MORI》

來源：Wikipedia (zh-hant) / Fresh Music SG

2021年，台灣雙人樂團珂拉琪發表第一張專輯《MEmento·MORI》。這個名字把英文的「我」，藏進拉丁文「記得你終將死去」的中間。

他們把重金屬搖滾、電腦合成的人聲，和廟裡的誦經（念經的聲音）拼在一起。歌詞混用台語、阿美族語、英語和日語，一下輕唱，一下嘶吼（大聲喊叫）。

最有名的〈萬千花蕊慈母悲哀〉，唱一位母親等著白色恐怖（過去政府抓人的年代）中失蹤的兒子。副歌喊向觀音，意外在網路上爆紅。

In 2021, Taiwanese duo Collage released their debut album MEmento·MORI. The title embeds the English word "me" inside the Latin phrase "remember you will die." They blend heavy metal rock, synthesized vocals, and temple chanting into their sound. Lyrics mix Taiwanese Hokkien, Amis, English, and Japanese, swinging between soft singing and raw screams. Their breakthrough track, "Ten Thousand Flowers, A Grieving Mother," depicts a mother waiting for her son, lost during Taiwan's White Terror. The chorus invokes Guanyin, and the song unexpectedly became a viral hit online.

柳枝小女巫：芬蘭的棕枝主日

來源：Elävän perinnön wikiluettelo (Finnish National Living Heritage Wiki)

棕枝主日（復活節前的星期日）這天，芬蘭的孩子會打扮成小女巫：臉上畫雀斑（臉上的小點點），穿舊花衣，手拿裝飾過的柳枝，一家一家去敲門。

他們念著「柳枝給你，糖果給我」，換到甜點。這個習俗合了兩件老事：東正教用柳枝代替棕櫚枝祝福，還有北歐人取笑復活節女巫亂飛的老傳說。兩者到1980年代才變成今天的樣子。

2027年的復活節在3月28日，棕枝主日就是3月21日，那時來芬蘭，剛好遇得上。

On Palm Sunday—the Sunday before Easter—Finnish children dress up as little witches: they draw freckles (little dots) on their faces, wear old, colorful clothes, carry decorated pussy-willow branches, and go from house to house knocking on doors. They chant, "Pussy willows for you, candy for me," trading the branches for sweets.

This tradition blends two old customs: the Orthodox Church's blessing with pussy-willow branches in place of palm fronds, and Northern European folklore about witches flying chaotically on Easter. These two elements didn't merge into today's celebration until the 1980s.

Easter in 2027 falls on March 28, making Palm Sunday March 21—if you visit Finland then, you'll catch it just right.

封街丟餃子：羅傑斯公園的波蘭餃節

來源：Jarvis Square Events / Rogers Park Business Alliance

8月8日星期六下午一點到晚上十點，芝加哥羅傑斯公園的賈維斯大道1500號那一段會封街，辦一年一度的波蘭餃節。

主角是 pierogi，一種波蘭水餃，有馬鈴薯配起司的，也有酸白菜配蘑菇的。攤位還賣「波蘭餃瑪格麗特」調酒。

當天最好玩的是丟餃子比賽，還有現場波卡（一種輕快的舞曲）樂團和手作攤位，地點就在紅線賈維斯站旁邊。

On Saturday, August 8th, from 1 p.m. to 10 p.m., a stretch of Jarvis Avenue at 1500 in Chicago's Rogers Park will close for the annual Pierogi Fiesta. The main attraction is pierogi — Polish dumplings available with potato and cheese filling, or with sauerkraut and mushrooms. Vendors will also sell "Pierogi Margarita" cocktails. The day's highlight is the pierogi-tossing contest, plus live polka bands and craft vendors set up right next to the Jarvis Red Line stop.

一個人的三億年：金絲雀的異星演化史

來源：Serina (Dylan Bajda / Sheather888)

2015年1月17日起，畫家 Dylan Bajda 一個人開始寫一顆叫「賽蓮娜」的假想衛星的自然史。

那裡只播下幾種地球生物：草、向日葵、螞蟻、孔雀魚，還有唯一的陸上脊椎動物（身上有骨頭的動物），就是家養金絲雀。三億年間，牠們分出上百種後代：不會飛的巨鳥、會潛水的，還有代替哺乳動物當獵食者的。

全部由他一人畫圖、撰寫，像一本真的自然史百科。2025年1月，這個網站默默滿十歲。

Starting in January 2015, artist Dylan Bajda began a solo project: documenting the natural history of an imagined satellite called Serina. He seeded this world with just a handful of Earth species—grass, sunflowers, ants, guppies, and one terrestrial vertebrate: the domesticated canary. Over three hundred million years, these creatures diversified into more than a hundred descendant species: flightless giants, diving varieties, and canaries that evolved into apex predators, filling ecological niches once occupied by mammals. All drawn and written by Bajda himself, it reads like an authentic natural history encyclopedia. In January 2025, the website quietly turned ten years old.
