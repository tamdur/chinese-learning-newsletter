大象體操的《水底》：讓數學搖滾學會漂浮

來源：Bandcamp / Topshelf Records

高雄的張凱婷和張凱翔是姐弟，加上鼓手涂嘉欽，組成「大象體操」。他們玩的是「數學搖滾」，節拍複雜又精準，像時鐘一樣準。

二〇一八年的專輯《水底》不一樣。這次沒有人當老大，每首歌輪流由不同的人主導。貝斯線不再像齒輪（機器裡的輪子），反而像水流，還加入了爵士和拉丁音樂的節奏。每個音還是扣得很緊，聽起來卻像漂在水裡。

From Kaohsiung, siblings Kai-ting and Kai-hsiang Chang form Elephant Gym alongside drummer Chia-chin Tu. They play "math rock" — rhythms that are intricately complex yet executed with metronomic precision. Their 2018 album Underwater marks a shift. This time no single member takes the lead; instead, different members steer each track. The bass lines abandon their gear-like rigidity for something more fluid — water-like, drawing in jazz and Latin rhythms. Every note is still locked tightly to the next, yet the whole thing feels like it is floating rather than ticking.

蕾絲之城：勞馬水手妻子的三百年手藝

來源：Visit Rauma

芬蘭西岸有個叫「老勞馬」的木造小鎮，是聯合國認定的世界遺產。一七〇〇年代中期，鎮上大約一千五百人，其中兩三百人是專業的蕾絲（一種很細的花邊布）師傅，多半是女人。丈夫出海跑船時，她們就用荷蘭來的細線編蕾絲賺錢。

二〇二三年，這門手藝被列入芬蘭的活文化遺產名單。老船東的家「瑪蕾拉」現在是博物館，一整年都有人示範怎麼用木棒和軟墊做蕾絲，三月去也看得到。

On Finland's west coast sits a wooden town called "Old Rauma," a UNESCO World Heritage site. In the mid-1700s, the town's roughly 1,500 residents included two to three hundred professional lace makers — a craft practiced mostly by women. While their husbands were away at sea, these women earned income by weaving lace from imported Dutch thread. In 2023 the craft was added to Finland's living cultural heritage list. The former shipowner's residence, "Marela," is now a museum where artisans demonstrate lace-making with wooden bobbins and pillow cushions year-round — a sight you can catch in March too.

誇張的衣著，與取笑他們的人

來源：Newberry Library Public Programs

八月二十七日星期四晚上六點到七點，芝加哥的紐貝瑞圖書館有一場免費演講。可以到現場的魯格斯廳，也可以用視訊參加。

主題是歷史上那些穿著最誇張的人，以及當時的漫畫家和寫小冊子的人怎麼取笑他們。紐貝瑞圖書館從一八八七年起就免費開放，這場演講會用到館裡收藏的諷刺（用好笑的方式批評）畫和珍貴老書。

On Thursday, August 27th, from 6 to 7 p.m., the Newberry Library in Chicago is hosting a free lecture. You can attend in person at Ruggles Hall or join by video. The subject is history's most dramatically dressed figures, and how the cartoonists and pamphleteers of their day ridiculed them. The Newberry has been free and open to the public since 1887, and the talk draws on the library's collection of satirical prints and rare old books.

把撕開的古書一頁一頁拼回去的網站

來源：Fragmentarium

中世紀的手抄書很珍貴，卻有書商故意把它們拆開，一頁一頁分開賣，因為買一頁的人比買一整本的人多。這種事一直做到一九五〇年代。

二〇一五年，瑞士弗里堡大學的一小群人做了「Fragmentarium」網站。世界各地的圖書館和收藏家可以把散落的書頁照片上傳，在電腦上排回原來的順序。英國國家圖書館用它拼回一七三一年大火燒剩的古英文殘頁，有些書頁分開幾百年後終於又在一起。

Medieval manuscripts were precious, but some book dealers deliberately took them apart and sold the pages one by one, because there were more buyers for a single page than for a whole book. The practice continued into the 1950s. In 2015 a small group at the University of Fribourg in Switzerland built the "Fragmentarium" website. Libraries and collectors worldwide can upload photographs of scattered leaves and reassemble them in their original order on screen. The British Library used it to piece back together Old English fragments that survived a 1731 fire — some pages reunited at last after centuries apart.
