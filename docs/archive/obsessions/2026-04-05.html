陳珊妮：用音樂說出我們的眼淚

來源：Golden Melody Awards

陳珊妮是台灣最有名的音樂製作人之一。她會唱歌、會寫歌、也會幫別人做音樂。她對每一個聲音都非常認真，要求很高。

2004年的專輯《後來 我們都哭了》拿下金曲獎最佳國語專輯和最佳製作人兩個大獎。這張專輯把日本流行、電子音樂和巴西吉他混在一起，聽起來又美又特別。

表面上風格一直在變，但底下是很強的紀律。這就是陳珊妮的厲害。

Chen Shan-ni is one of Taiwan's most renowned music producers. She sings, writes songs, and produces music for others. She takes every sound incredibly seriously and maintains exacting standards.

Her 2004 album "After That, We All Cried" won two major Golden Melody Awards: Best Mandarin Album and Best Producer. The album blended Japanese pop, electronic music, and Brazilian guitar in a way that sounded both beautiful and distinctly original.

On the surface, her style constantly evolves, but underneath lies formidable discipline. That is what makes Chen Shan-ni exceptional.

在結冰的海上溜冰：芬蘭群島之旅

來源：Visit Turku Archipelago

芬蘭西南方的圖爾庫群島有四萬個小島，冬天海水結成厚冰，變成一片天然的溜冰場。芬蘭人用又長又窄的冰刀，在島與島之間滑行。

三月的陽光照在白色冰面上，風景非常美。有導遊帶你從納古島出發，滑過開闊的冰海。回來以後，大家圍著火喝咖啡，還可以在冰上釣魚。

The Turku Archipelago in southwestern Finland contains forty thousand small islands. In winter, seawater freezes into thick ice, transforming the landscape into a natural skating rink. Finns use long, narrow ice skates to glide between islands.

In March, sunlight reflects off the white ice, creating stunning scenery. Guides lead visitors from Nagu Island across the open frozen sea. Upon returning, everyone gathers around a fire to drink coffee, and you can even fish on the ice.

芝加哥茶節：一起來喝好茶

來源：Chicago Tea Festival

4月11到12日，第三屆芝加哥茶節在 Jefferson Park 的哥白尼中心舉行。三十多家茶商帶來世界各地的好茶，從烏龍到陳年普洱，從日本抹茶到手工茶具。

現場有專家講茶的歷史、怎麼選茶、怎麼泡茶。如果你住在芝加哥又喜歡喝茶，這是一個很棒的週末活動。

From April 11 to 12, the third annual Chicago Tea Festival will be held at the Copernicus Center in Jefferson Park. More than thirty tea merchants will bring fine teas from around the world, from oolong to aged pu-erh, from Japanese matcha to handcrafted tea utensils.

On-site experts will speak about the history of tea, how to select tea, and how to brew it properly. If you live in Chicago and enjoy drinking tea, this is a wonderful weekend activity.

全世界的英語口音，都在這裡

來源：IDEA International Dialects of English Archive

IDEA（國際英語方言檔案庫）是一個很特別的網站。一位叫 Paul Meier 的口音老師花了幾十年，收集了一千六百多段錄音。每個人念同一段文字，但口音都不一樣。

從美國南方到印度，從蘇格蘭到日本，幾百種口音都有。每段錄音還記下說話者的年紀和家鄉。對語言學家、演員、或只是好奇的人來說，這是一座寶庫。

IDEA (International Dialects of English Archive) is a rather special website. An accent coach named Paul Meier spent decades collecting over 1,600 audio recordings. Each person reads the same passage, but with different accents.

From the American South to India, from Scotland to Japan, hundreds of accents are represented. Each recording also notes the speaker's age and hometown. For linguists, actors, or simply curious people, it's a treasure trove.
