草東沒有派對：一張憤怒的專輯，一代人的聲音

來源：Album of the Year

二〇一六年，台灣樂團草東沒有派對自己出錢發行了首張專輯《醜奴兒》。專輯名字來自宋朝詞牌（一種古詩的格式），歌詞寫的卻是現代年輕人的困境：薪水不漲，未來看不到。

十二首歌混合了數學搖滾和後龐克的能量，在金曲獎拿下三座獎。沒有唱片公司幫忙，實體專輯卻賣出一萬六千張。這是太陽花世代最真實的吶喊。

In 2016, the Taiwanese band No Party for Cao Dong self-funded the release of their debut album "The Servile." The album's title borrows from a Song Dynasty poetic form, but the lyrics capture the struggles of modern young people: stagnant wages and an invisible future. Twelve songs blend the energy of math rock and post-punk, earning the band three Golden Melody Awards. Without a record label's backing, the physical album sold 16,000 copies. It was the rawest voice of the Sunflower Generation.

穿上紅色浮衣，漂在北極冰海上

來源：The Icebreaking Company

芬蘭北部的凱米港停著一艘（一條船）退休的破冰船「桑波號」。它一九六〇年在赫爾辛基建造，現在帶遊客出海，在結冰的波的尼亞灣裡破出一條水道。

最特別的是：船員幫你穿上全身紅色防寒浮衣，然後你跳進冰水，靜靜漂浮在碎冰之間。三月冰層最厚，是體驗最好的時候。

In the northern Finnish port of Kemi sits the Sampo, a retired icebreaker that has found new purpose. Built in Helsinki in 1960, the vessel now carries tourists out to sea, carving a passage through the frozen waters of the Bay of Bothnia. But here's what makes it truly special: the crew outfits you in a full-body red thermal survival suit, then you plunge into the ice water and drift silently among the broken floes. March brings the thickest ice — and the best experience.

雙人腳踏車衝進密西根湖

來源：Red Bull / Do312

六月十三日上午十點到下午三點半，Red Bull Spin Off 將在芝加哥十二街海灘舉辦。四十組隊伍要騎著自己做的雙人腳踏車，通過浮在水上的障礙賽道。

評審看三樣東西：車子的創意、表演精神、還有速度。免費觀看，帶野餐墊來湖邊就好。

From 10 a.m. to 3:30 p.m. on June 13th, Red Bull Spin Off will take place at Chicago's 12th Street Beach. Forty teams will ride custom-built tandem bicycles through obstacle courses floating on the water. Judges will score three things: the creativity of the bike, the performance spirit, and speed. Entry is free to watch — just bring a picnic blanket down to the lake.

程式語言之父的織布資料庫

來源：University of Arizona CS Department

Ralph Griswold 發明了早期程式語言 SNOBOL4，也創建了亞利桑那大學的電腦科學系。退休後，他迷上了織布的幾何學，花了好幾年掃描（把紙變成電子檔）十九世紀的織布手冊，把幾千種圖案存進大學的伺服器。

他二〇〇六年去世了，但那些檔案還在。後來一位工程師用這些資料建了 Handweaving.net，現在收藏超過七萬六千種織布圖案，是全世界最大的織布資料庫。

Ralph Griswold invented the early programming language SNOBOL4 and founded the computer science department at the University of Arizona. After retiring, he became fascinated by the geometry of weaving and spent years scanning nineteenth-century weaving manuals — converting paper into digital files — and storing thousands of patterns on the university's servers. He passed away in 2006, but those files remained. Later, an engineer used this data to build Handweaving.net, which now houses over seventy-six thousand weaving patterns, making it the world's largest weaving database.
