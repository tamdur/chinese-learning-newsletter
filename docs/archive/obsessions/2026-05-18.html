一張專輯改變了台語音樂的命運

來源：No Man Is An Island

一九九〇年十二月，台灣解嚴（取消軍事管制）後兩年，歌手林強推出了《向前走》。這是台灣第一張台語搖滾專輯，由陳昇和李宗盛共同製作。

在那之前，台語歌多半是慢節奏的悲傷情歌。林強把電子合成器、金屬吉他和饒舌混在一起，用台語唱出年輕人的活力。專輯賣了超過四十萬張，還拿下金曲獎年度最佳歌曲，開啟了「新台語歌運動」的時代。

In December 1990, two years after Taiwan lifted martial law, singer Lin Qiang released the album Forward. It was Taiwan's first Taiwanese-language rock album, produced jointly by Chen Sheng and Lee Zong-sheng. Before this, Taiwanese-language songs were mostly slow-tempo sad love ballads. Lin Qiang mixed electronic synthesizers, metal guitars, and rap together, singing the vitality of young people in Taiwanese. The album sold over 400,000 copies and won the Golden Melody Award for Best Song of the Year, ushering in the era of the "New Taiwanese Language Song Movement."

從海上斗篷到牆上藝術：芬蘭的絨毯故事

來源：Kotona

芬蘭有一種特別的厚絨毯，叫做 ryijy。很久以前，維京時代的漁夫把它當作海上的斗篷（防風外衣）和睡袋。到了十七世紀，它變成結婚的重要物品：新娘親手織出美麗的圖案，新人在婚禮上站在毯子上宣誓。

每個地區有自己的花紋：海邊的是斜格子，內陸的有生命之樹。二十世紀後，藝術家把 ryijy 掛上客廳的牆，讓它成為芬蘭現代藝術的一部分。

Finland has a special thick rug called ryijy. Long ago, Viking-era fishermen used it as a sea cloak (windproof outer garment) and sleeping bag. By the seventeenth century, it had become an essential wedding item: brides wove beautiful patterns by hand, and couples stood on the rug during their wedding ceremony to exchange vows. Each region had its own designs: coastal areas featured diagonal checks, while inland areas displayed the tree of life. In the twentieth century, artists hung ryijy on their living room walls, transforming it into part of Finnish modern art.

在絲綢上畫出你的色彩

來源：Lillstreet Art Center

五月三十日，芝加哥 Ravenswood 區的 Lillstreet Art Center 將舉辦一場絲綢染畫工作坊。參加者會學到用染料直接畫在絲網上，再把圖案印到絲綢布上的方法。

每個人可以帶走兩條自己設計的絲綢口袋方巾，所有材料都包含在內。每次印出來的圖案都不一樣，是真正獨一無二的作品。

On May 30th, the Lillstreet Art Center in Chicago's Ravenswood neighborhood will host a silk dyeing and painting workshop. Participants will learn to paint directly onto silk screens with dyes, then transfer the design onto silk fabric. Everyone will take home two silk pocket scarves of their own design, with all materials included. Each print comes out differently, making every piece truly one-of-a-kind.

當程式碼變成詩

來源：The HTML Review

The HTML Review 是一本每年出刊一次的網路文學雜誌，由兩位編輯 Shelby Wilson 和 Maxwell Neely-Cohen 主持。這裡的作品不只是放在網頁上的文字，而是用 HTML 程式碼本身來創作。

詩可以跟著你的滑動變化，小說會隨著點擊改變顏色。第五期在二〇二六年春天上線，收錄了約十七件作品。每位作者都擁有自己的程式碼，所以每一件都像是一本獨立的小書。

The HTML Review is an online literary magazine published once yearly, curated by editors Shelby Wilson and Maxwell Neely-Cohen. The works here are more than text placed on a webpage — they are created using HTML code itself as the medium. Poetry shifts as you scroll, fiction changes color with each click. Issue five launched in spring 2026 with roughly seventeen pieces. Each author owns their code, making every work feel like its own self-contained book.
