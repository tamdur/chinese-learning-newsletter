從台語流行到工業噪音：林強的大膽轉變

來源：Rate Your Music

一九九零年，林強用台語唱出「向前走」，賣了超過四十萬張，一夜成名。可是他不想當普通的流行歌手。

三年後，他飛去倫敦，和製作人 John Fryer 一起錄了「娛樂世界」。這張專輯（就是音樂唱片）充滿電子節拍和工業噪音（機器般的重低音），和之前的風格完全不同。銷量很差，但林強因此成為台灣實驗電子音樂的教父，也開始替侯孝賢的電影做配樂。

In 1990, Lin Qiang sang "Move Forward" in Taiwanese, selling over 400,000 copies and becoming a household name overnight. But he had no interest in being an ordinary pop singer. Three years later, he flew to London and recorded "Entertainment World" with producer John Fryer. The album was filled with electronic beats and industrial noise—machine-like heavy bass—a complete departure from his previous style. Sales were dismal, but Lin Qiang became the godfather of experimental electronic music in Taiwan and began composing film scores for director Hou Hsiao-hsien.

芬蘭湖裡的稀有海豹：三月正是生寶寶的季節

來源：Atlas Obscura

大約一萬年前，芬蘭的塞馬湖和波羅的海斷開了。一群環斑海豹被留在湖中，慢慢變成了世界上唯一的淡水海豹。牠們的眼睛比海裡的親戚更大，目前只剩大約四百三十隻。

三月是母海豹生寶寶的時候，牠們會在湖邊的雪堆裡挖洞當窩。如果三月去蜜月旅行，要注意附近的林南薩里國家公園會設立禁止進入的保護區，讓海豹安心生產。

About 10,000 years ago, Finland's Lake Saimaa became separated from the Baltic Sea. A group of ringed seals became trapped in the lake and gradually evolved into the world's only freshwater seals. They have larger eyes than their ocean-dwelling cousins, and only about 430 remain today. March is when mother seals give birth, digging dens in snowdrifts along the lake's shore. If you're planning a honeymoon trip in March, be aware that the nearby Linnansaari National Park establishes protected no-entry zones to allow the seals to give birth undisturbed.

大衛乘拜恩的腦科學劇場：芝加哥限定體驗

來源：Theater of the Mind Chicago / Goodman Theatre

你知道 Talking Heads 樂團的 David Byrne 嗎？他和作家 Mala Gaonkar 一起設計了一個叫「Theater of the Mind」的沉浸式（讓你整個人走進去的）展覽。

地點在芝加哥的 Reid Murdoch Building，每次只讓十六個人進場。七十五分鐘的旅程中，你會參加真正的腦科學實驗，挑戰你對記憶和感覺的理解。演出到七月十二日為止，票價六十九到九十九美元。

You know David Byrne from Talking Heads? He and writer Mala Gaonkar have designed an immersive exhibition called "Theater of the Mind." It's located in Chicago's Reid Murdoch Building, and only sixteen people are admitted per session. Over seventy-five minutes, you'll participate in actual neuroscience experiments that challenge your understanding of memory and perception. The show runs through July 12th, with tickets priced from sixty-nine to ninety-nine dollars.

一個人建的銅管樂團比賽資料庫

來源：Brass Band Results

英國有一個叫 Tim 的工程師，他以前在銅管樂團（用喇叭、法國號等金屬樂器演奏的樂團）吹奏。出於熱愛，他一個人建了一個網站，收集全世界銅管樂團比賽的成績。

資料庫裡有超過三十二萬筆紀錄，涵蓋（包含）將近六千場比賽和一萬多個樂團。最早的紀錄可以追溯（回頭找）到一八五三年的英國公開賽。每個樂團的頁面都記載了他們演奏的曲子、指揮是誰、還有名次。

In the United Kingdom, an engineer named Tim used to perform in a brass band—an ensemble that plays metal instruments like trumpets and French horns. Driven by his passion for the music, he single-handedly built a website that collects competition results from brass bands around the world. The database contains over 320,000 records, covering nearly 6,000 competitions and more than 10,000 bands. The earliest record traces back to the 1853 British Open Championship. Each band's page documents the pieces they performed, who conducted them, and their placement in the competition.
