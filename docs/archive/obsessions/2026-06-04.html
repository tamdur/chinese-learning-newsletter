用郵寄把台灣送進世界噪音圈

來源：White Fungus

一九九三年，台灣剛解嚴（結束長達三十八年的管制）沒多久，聲音藝術家王福瑞就辦了島上第一個專門做噪音的廠牌「NOISE」。

他把音樂錄進卡帶，親手做好外包裝，用郵寄送給歐洲和日本的藝術家。他的作品〈V-zone〉用金屬般的回音和電子雜訊，聽起來既緊張又特別。

這些手工卡帶後來被當成藝術品收藏，並在二○一三年於台北立方空間的「潛伏的浪」展覽中展出。

In 1993, shortly after Taiwan lifted martial law — ending nearly thirty-eight years of strict controls — sound artist Wang Fu-rui established the island's first noise-dedicated record label, "NOISE." He recorded music onto cassettes, handcrafted the packaging, and mailed copies to artists across Europe and Japan. His work "V-zone" layered metallic echoes and electronic noise into something simultaneously tense and distinctive. These handmade cassettes later became prized as artworks, eventually exhibited in the 2013 "Undercurrent Waves" show at Taipei Cube Space.

只剩四個孩子會說的語言，如何活了回來

來源：National Geographic

一九八六年，全世界只剩四個孩子會說伊納利薩米語。這是唯一只在芬蘭境內使用的薩米語，講的人住在北方拉普蘭的伊納利湖邊。

當地人學習紐西蘭毛利人的做法，開辦只說這種語言的幼兒園，又訓練大人當老師。到了二○二五年，已經有大約五百人會說了。

三月來這裡，伊納利小鎮安靜又悠閒。鎮上的西達博物館得過二○二四年的歐洲年度博物館獎。

In 1986, only four children in the entire world spoke Inari Sami. This is the only Sami language used exclusively within Finland, spoken by people living along Lake Inari in the northern Lapland region. Local residents adopted methods from New Zealand's Māori people, opening kindergartens where only this language was spoken and training adults to become teachers. By 2025, approximately five hundred people could speak it. In March, the small town of Inari is quiet and leisurely. The town's Siida Museum won the European Museum of the Year award in 2024.

露營入門課：在工具圖書館學搭帳篷

來源：Block Club Chicago

這個六月，芝加哥一個叫「Switchback」的戶外裝備交換團體，要在西加菲公園區的「工具圖書館」開一堂免費的露營入門課。

課堂上會教你怎麼搭帳篷、怎麼生火、安全使用露營爐，還有在野外過夜該怎麼穿衣服。

上完課，你可以看看圖書館可租的露營用具，也能便宜買到二手裝備。不用報名，直接去就行。

This June, a Chicago outdoor gear exchange group called "Switchback" is holding a free beginner camping class at the Tool Library in the West Garfield Park area. The class will teach you how to set up a tent, build a fire, safely use a camping stove, and what to wear for an overnight stay in the wilderness. After class, you can check out camping equipment available for rental at the library, or pick up secondhand gear at a discount. No registration required—just show up.

一位教授的千件古老醫療器具網路博物館

來源：MoHMA – Museum of Historical Medical Artifacts

布勞福斯醫師是一位核子醫學教授，花了三十年收藏一千多件古老的醫療器具：放水蛭（一種會吸血的蟲）的罐子、手術用的鋸子，還有數不清的聽診器。

沒有博物館願意收，也沒有出版社願意幫他印那本有一千張照片的書。於是他在二○○七年自己蓋了一座網路博物館mohma.org。

每一件器具都有仔細的說明，可以照種類或年代慢慢看。實物後來捐給了大學，但這個網站仍是最完整的紀錄。

Dr. Blaufox is a nuclear medicine professor who spent thirty years collecting over a thousand antique medical instruments: jars for storing leeches (blood-sucking insects used in treatment), surgical saws, and countless stethoscopes. No museum wanted them, and no publisher would print his book with a thousand photographs. So in 2007 he built his own online museum at mohma.org. Each instrument has detailed documentation and can be browsed by category or era. The physical collection was eventually donated to a university, but the website remains the most complete record.
