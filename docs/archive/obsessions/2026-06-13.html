早了三十年的台灣城市流行樂

來源：Bitetone (Substack)

一九九五年，五個台灣大學畢業的年輕人組成了WHY NOT樂團，發行了《無法度按捺》這張唱片。他們用台語唱歌，把爵士樂、薩克斯風和四個人的和聲放在一起。

這種聲音太新，大家還沒準備好，樂團很快就解散了。但鼓手後來成了五月天的鼓手，鍵盤手開了唱片公司。這張唱片就像一顆種子，悄悄養出了後來的台灣獨立音樂。

In 1995, five young Taiwanese university graduates formed the band WHY NOT and released the album "無法度按捺." They sang in Taiwanese, blending jazz, saxophone, and four-part harmonies. The sound was ahead of its time—audiences weren't ready, and the band dissolved quickly. But the drummer later became the drummer for Mayday, and the keyboardist went on to start a record label. That album was like a seed, quietly nurturing the independent music scene that would flourish in Taiwan afterward.

百年紅磚市場，赫爾辛基的美味

來源：Hietalahden Kauppahalli (official site)

在芬蘭的首都赫爾辛基，有一座一九〇三年蓋好的紅磚市場，叫海塔拉提市場。它的牆面有漂亮的曲線，裡面原本有一百多個木頭小攤子。

當年為了乾淨衛生，市場外面禁止擺攤，只有賣魚能留在外頭。如今這裡賣著芬蘭、日本、法國和菲律賓的食物，比海邊那座給遊客的老市場更熱鬧、更有在地味。

In Helsinki, the capital of Finland, there stands a red-brick market built in 1903 called the Hietalahti Market. Its walls curve in elegant lines, and it once housed more than a hundred wooden stalls inside. Back then, to maintain cleanliness and hygiene, vendors were forbidden from setting up outside the market—except for fishmongers, who were allowed to sell their catch outdoors. Today, the market bustles with food from Finland, Japan, France, and the Philippines, feeling livelier and more authentically local than the older harborside market catering to tourists.

走進百年玻璃花房的後台

來源：Newberry Library

六月十七日下午一點到兩點，紐貝里圖書館會帶大家參觀芝加哥的加菲公園溫室（一種玻璃蓋成的大花房）。這次還能看到平常不開放的後面種花房。

這座溫室在一九〇八年完工，由景觀設計師顏森設計。他把植物分成不同主題的房間來擺放，在當時是很了不起的想法。活動開放給所有人參加。

On the afternoon of June 17th, from 1 to 2 p.m., the Newberry Library will lead a tour of the Garfield Park Conservatory in Chicago—a grand glass-enclosed greenhouse. This visit offers a rare chance to see the back propagation house, normally closed to the public. The conservatory was completed in 1908 and designed by landscape architect Jens Jensen. His innovation of organizing plants into thematically arranged rooms was a remarkable achievement for its time. The tour is open to everyone.

用電話簿畫出來的畫

來源：GitHub / MetaFilter Projects

一九六〇年，法國藝術家莫雷萊把一張畫布分成四萬個小格子。他請太太念出電話簿上的號碼，遇到雙數就把格子塗藍色，底色是紅色。

寫程式的史威加特做了一個網站，用電腦重現這個做法，一秒就能畫出好幾百張，顏色和大小都能自己改。它讓人看見一件事：連電話簿這種普通東西，也能變成美麗的藝術。

In 1960, French artist François Morellet divided a canvas into 40,000 small squares. He asked his wife to read numbers from a telephone directory, and whenever she said an even number, he would color the square blue against a red background. Programmer Al Sweigart created a website that recreates this method using a computer, generating hundreds of images in seconds, with customizable colors and sizes. It reveals something remarkable: even something as ordinary as a telephone directory can be transformed into beautiful art.
