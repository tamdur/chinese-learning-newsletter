八部合音：布農族的祈禱歌

來源：風潮音樂 Wind Music

一九九二年，學者吳榮順帶著錄音機走進中央山脈的布農族部落，錄下《布農族之歌》，那年得到金鼎獎。

最有名的是「祈禱小米豐收歌」。幾位男子圍成一圈，聲音慢慢往上滑，像蜜蜂飛，也像水流，不用一般民歌的音階（音的高低順序）。

一九五二年，日本學者黑澤隆朝把這種歌聲寄給聯合國，全世界的學者因此重新思考：多聲部的音樂是怎麼開始的？

In 1992, the scholar Wu Rong-shun carried a tape recorder into the Bunun tribal settlements of the Central Mountain Range and recorded "Songs of the Bunun," which won the Golden Tripod Award that year.

The most famous track is "Pasibutbut," the millet harvest prayer song. Several men form a circle and their voices slide slowly upward — like bees in flight, like flowing water — without using the scale of ordinary folk songs (the sequence of high and low notes).

In 1952, the Japanese scholar Kurosawa Takatomo sent this singing to the United Nations, and scholars around the world were prompted to reconsider: how did polyphonic music begin?

塞馬湖：雪堆裡出生的小海豹

來源：University of Helsinki

大約九千五百年前，土地上升，芬蘭的塞馬湖和大海分開，一群環斑海豹從此住在淡水裡。新研究說，牠們的祖先早在六萬年前就分家了。

全世界只剩不到五百隻。每年二月中到三月中，約一百隻小海豹在湖邊的雪堆裡出生。雪少的冬天，志工會幫忙鏟出雪堆，讓母海豹有地方做窩。

三月來這裡，正好是小海豹出生的季節，不過要等到春天冰化了，才看得見牠們。

About 9,500 years ago, land uplift separated Finland's Lake Saimaa from the sea, and a population of ringed seals has lived in fresh water ever since. New research says their ancestors split off as far back as 60,000 years ago.

Fewer than 500 remain in the world. Each year from mid-February to mid-March, about 100 pups are born in the snowdrifts along the lakeshore. In winters with little snow, volunteers help shovel out drifts so mother seals have somewhere to den.

Coming here in March falls right in pup season — though you have to wait until the ice melts in spring before you can actually see them.

開鎖之夜：北區的鎖具俱樂部

來源：Pumping Station One / TOOOL Chicago

八月五日星期三晚上八點，芝加哥的開鎖社團在北區的創客空間「一號抽水站」聚會，地點在艾爾斯頓大道三五一九號。

活動免費，任何人都可以來。老手會教新來的人怎麼開鎖，也把工具借給沒帶的人。他們研究鎖的原理，是為了好玩，不是為了偷東西。不用先報名，直接走進去就好。

On Wednesday, August 5th at 8 p.m., Chicago's lockpicking club meets at Pumping Station One, a makerspace on the North Side, at 3519 N Elston Ave.

The event is free and anyone may come. Experienced members teach newcomers how to pick locks and lend tools to those who don't have their own. They study how locks work for the fun of it, not to steal anything. No registration needed — just walk in.

老樹名單：一個人手寫的年齡簿

來源：Rocky Mountain Tree-Ring Research

網路上有個叫「老樹名單」的網頁，排版簡單得像三十年前。主人是年輪學家（研究樹木年輪的學者）彼得．布朗。

他把全世界各種樹的最高年齡一一記下來。第一名是加州的狐尾松，五千零六十五歲。每一筆都要真的數過年輪才算數，還附上原本的研究論文。

這是一小群科學家幾十年的功夫。密西西比河以東的老樹，由哈佛森林的裴德森另外整理。

There is a webpage on the internet called OLDLIST, its layout as plain as something from thirty years ago. Its keeper is the dendrochronologist (a scholar who studies tree rings) Peter M. Brown.

He records the maximum age of every kind of tree in the world, one by one. First place goes to a bristlecone pine in California, 5,065 years old. Every entry only counts if the rings have actually been counted, and each comes with the original research paper attached.

This is the work of a small group of scientists over several decades. The old trees east of the Mississippi River are catalogued separately by Neil Pederson of Harvard Forest.
