林強的錄音盒子：一座島的聲音地圖

來源：In Sheep's Clothing Hi-Fi / Pure Person Press

2020年，DJ Angie QQ 請「台灣電子音樂之父」林強給她幾段台灣的錄音。沒想到，他一口氣寄來好幾千段！

這些聲音都是他多年來細心記下的：家人喝咖啡聊天、廟會、機車、鳥叫和雨聲。Angie QQ 請各國音樂人把它們變成安靜、像夢一樣的音樂，做成專輯《Sounds of Taiwan》。第一首〈Family Time〉，就是林強一家人在飯桌上聊天的聲音。

她還打算接著做《台灣的鳥》、《台灣的廟》，甚至《台灣的機車》。

In 2020, DJ Angie QQ asked Lin Chiang, the "father of Taiwanese electronic music," for a few recordings of Taiwan. To her surprise, he sent several thousand clips in one go! The sounds were ones he had patiently recorded over many years: family members chatting over coffee, temple fairs, scooters, birdsong, and rain. Angie QQ invited musicians from around the world to turn them into quiet, dreamlike music, released as the album "Sounds of Taiwan." The first track, "Family Time," is simply the sound of Lin Chiang's family chatting at the dinner table. She also plans to keep going with "Birds of Taiwan," "Temples of Taiwan," and even "Scooters of Taiwan."

芬蘭人的惡夢：請離我遠一點

來源：Yle

芬蘭奧盧的設計師卡羅莉娜，想向外國朋友說明「芬蘭人是什麼樣子」，就畫起了漫畫。主角馬蒂只有一個願望：別人離他遠一點。

他怕閒聊，怕被稱讚，等公車時跟別人站得好幾公尺遠。很多人大叫：「這就是我！」2016年，漫畫出成書《芬蘭人的惡夢》。中文網路上還出現新詞「精芬」，意思是心裡住著一個芬蘭人。

去芬蘭時記得：排隊留一個手臂的距離，見面握手不擁抱。大家安靜不說話，也不是沒禮貌。

Carolina, a designer in Oulu, Finland, started drawing comics to explain to her foreign friends what Finns are like. Her main character, Matti, has only one wish: for other people to keep their distance. He dreads small talk, dreads being complimented, and when he waits for the bus, he stands several meters away from everyone else. Many readers cried out, "That's me!" The comics were published as a book in 2016, titled The Finnish Nightmare. On Chinese social media, a new word has also appeared: 精芬 (jīngfēn), meaning someone who has a Finn living inside their heart. If you visit Finland, remember to leave about an arm's length between yourself and the person in front of you in line, and to shake hands rather than hug when you meet. Finns are quiet and don't make much conversation, but this is not a sign of bad manners.

如果艾密特．提爾活了下來

來源：Museum of Contemporary Photography (MoCP)

1955年，十四歲的芝加哥男孩艾密特．提爾到密西西比州看親戚，被人帶走殺害。他的死，點燃了美國黑人爭取平等的運動。

芝加哥當代攝影博物館的展覽，問了一個問題：如果他活下來，人生會是什麼樣子？策展人（安排展覽的人）莎拉．路易斯和提爾的表哥一起，用館裡七十多位攝影師的照片，拼出他沒能過完的一生。其中一組餐廳照片，讓人想起媽媽為他準備的那份午餐。

展覽到12月19日，免費參觀，地址在密西根大道600號。

In 1955, 14-year-old Chicago boy Emmett Till went to visit relatives in Mississippi, where he was abducted and murdered. His death ignited the American Black civil rights movement. An exhibition at the Museum of Contemporary Photography in Chicago asks a simple question: if he had survived, what would his life have been like? Curator Sarah Elizabeth Lewis and Till's cousin drew on photographs by more than seventy photographers from the museum's collection to piece together the life he never got to live. One set of restaurant photographs brings to mind the lunch his mother had packed for him. The exhibition runs through December 19, admission is free, and it is located at 600 South Michigan Avenue.

鞋帶教授的二十年

來源：Ian's Shoelace Site (fieggen.com)

澳洲軟體工程師伊恩．菲根，自稱「鞋帶教授」。從2003年起，他一個人經營一個網站，只講一件事：怎麼綁鞋帶。

網站裡收了幾十種穿鞋帶的方法，每一種都有他親手畫的圖，名字也取得簡單好懂。他還自己發明了兩種結：「伊恩快速結」和「伊恩安全結」。

二十多年過去了，很多人上網查「鞋帶為什麼老是鬆掉」，最後都會找到這裡。

Ian Fieggen, an Australian software engineer, calls himself the "Shoelace Professor." Since 2003, he has run a single website on his own, and it covers only one subject: how to tie your shoelaces. The site holds several dozen lacing methods, each with a diagram he drew himself, and each given a simple, easy-to-remember name. He also invented two knots of his own, the "Ian Fast Knot" and the "Ian Secure Knot." More than twenty years later, many people who search online for "why do my shoelaces keep coming undone" still end up finding his site.
