獵人：奶奶聽得懂的歌

來源：Wind Music / Wikipedia (zh)

「你可以寫一首我聽得懂的歌嗎？」二〇〇〇年，布農族歌手王宏恩（藝名Biung）記住了奶奶在聖誕節說的這句話。

他剛大學畢業，就用布農族語寫詞、作曲，自己製作了第一張專輯《獵人》。整張唱片只有簡單的吉他和打擊樂器，旋律裡藏著布農族傳統祭歌（祭典時唱的歌，像八部合音）的影子。

這張專輯入圍了二〇〇一年金曲獎，也替後來的原住民歌手打開了一扇門。

"Can you write a song I can understand?" In 2000, Bunun indigenous singer Wang Hong-en (stage name Biung) held onto these words his grandmother spoke to him on Christmas.

Fresh out of university, he wrote lyrics and composed music in the Bunun language, producing his debut album, "The Hunter." The entire record was built on nothing but simple guitar and percussion, its melodies carrying echoes of traditional Bunun ceremonial songs—the kind sung at festivals with their distinctive eight-part harmonies.

The album earned a nomination for the 2001 Golden Melody Awards and opened doors for indigenous singers to follow.

三國石堆：十秒走過三個國家

來源：Atlas Obscura

在芬蘭拉普蘭的基爾皮斯亞爾維（Kilpisjärvi）村外，湖中央立著一座水泥界碑（標出國界的石柱），名叫「三國石堆」。芬蘭、瑞典、挪威三國的邊界在這裡相遇，這是全世界最北的三國交界點，現在的界碑立於一九二六年。

夏天得走小橋才到得了。可是三月的湖面結得又厚又硬，你可以直接走上去，繞著界碑走一圈，十秒內就踏進了三個國家。

步道就從村子裡出發，從基魯納開車約四小時。

Outside the village of Kilpisjärvi in Lapland, Finland, a concrete border marker stands in the middle of a lake. Known as the "Three-Country Stone Pile," this monument marks where the borders of Finland, Sweden, and Norway meet — the world's northernmost tri-point boundary. The current marker was installed in 1926.

In summer, a small bridge is required to reach it. But in March, when the lake freezes solid, you can walk straight across. Circle the marker and you'll have stepped through all three countries in ten seconds.

The trail departs from the village itself, roughly a four-hour drive from Kiruna.

三百五十張桌子的玩具兵大會

來源：Chicagoland Toy Soldier Show

九月二十日星期日，芝加哥近郊紹姆堡的萬豪飯店，會擺出三百五十張攤位桌。主辦人說，這是全世界最大的玩具兵展。

桌上有一百多年前的古董兵，也有現在做的小兵人。展場還有比賽，分成兩組：一組比自己動手做的場景模型，另一組只比上色的功夫。

現場也開「場景模型入門」課，讓人親手試做。任何人都能進場，不必是收藏家。

On Sunday, September 20th, the Marriott Hotel in Schaumburg, near Chicago, will host 350 vendor booths. Organizers say it's the world's largest toy soldier exhibition.

The booths will display antique soldiers from over a hundred years ago as well as modern miniature figures. The show also features competitions in two categories: one for handcrafted diorama scenes and another for painting technique.

There's also a beginner's workshop on scene modeling where you can try building one yourself. The event is open to everyone—collectors and newcomers welcome.

信任的演化：一個人做出來的遊戲

來源：ncase.me

加拿大人凱斯（Nicky Case）一個人做出了網頁遊戲《信任的演化》，靈感來自一九八四年的名著《合作的演化》。

你和電腦對手玩交換硬幣的遊戲。有的對手叫「複製貓」，你怎麼對它，它就怎麼對你；有的是「記仇者」；有的是「偵探」。規則一改，信任就慢慢長出來，或是整個垮掉。

玩到最後，你會開始想：一個社會為什麼會變得可靠？凱斯還做了一個網站 explorabl.es ，收集別人做的這種作品。

Canadian Nicky Case single-handedly created the web game "The Evolution of Trust," inspired by the 1984 classic "The Evolution of Cooperation."

You play a coin-exchange game against computer opponents with different personalities. Some are "Copycats" who mirror your every move; others are "Grudgers" who remember slights; some are "Detectives" who probe for patterns. Tweak the rules, and trust either slowly emerges or collapses entirely.

By the end, you're left pondering a deeper question: why does a society become trustworthy? Case has also built explorabl.es, a website that curates similar interactive works by other creators.
