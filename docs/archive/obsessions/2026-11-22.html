大支的第一張專輯：說出來的代價

來源：自由時報

2002年，台南出生的饒舌（一種邊說邊唱的音樂）歌手大支，發行了專輯《舌燦蓮花》（意思是很會說話）。很多人說，這是華語世界第一張個人嘻哈專輯。那時候，大部分台灣人還沒聽過饒舌。

他用國語和台語輪流唱，直接談台灣獨立和社會問題。結果，他收到死亡威脅，唱片圈也不再找他。他等了九年，才出第二張專輯。

今天，大家叫他「台語饒舌教父」。

In 2002, Da Zhi, a rapper (rap is music where the performer speaks and sings at the same time) born in Tainan, released his album Shé Càn Lián Huā ("Silver Tongue," an idiom for being a gifted talker). Many people say it was the first solo hip-hop album in the Chinese-speaking world. At the time, most Taiwanese had never heard rap. He alternated between Mandarin and Taiwanese Hokkien in his songs, and he spoke directly about Taiwanese independence and social problems. As a result, he received death threats, and the music industry stopped working with him. He waited nine years before releasing his second album. Today, people call him the "godfather of Taiwanese-language rap."

湖邊修道院的威士忌

來源：Valamo Monastery

在芬蘭的湖區，有一座新瓦拉莫修道院（修士一起生活、祈禱的地方）。它是芬蘭唯一的東正教（基督教的一支）修道院。1940年，修士們離開被蘇聯占領的卡累利阿，搬到這片湖邊。

從2014年起，修士們一起經營酒廠。他們把威士忌放進裝過教堂葡萄酒的舊木桶，讓酒慢慢變香。

遊客可以參觀地下酒窖（放酒的房間），品嚐修士做的酒，晚上還能住在修道院的客房，聽湖邊的安靜。

In the lake district of Finland stands New Valamo Monastery, a place where monks live and pray together. It is the only Orthodox Christian monastery in Finland. In 1940, the monks left Karelia, which had been occupied by the Soviet Union, and moved to this lakeside. Since 2014, the monks have run a distillery together. They age their whisky in old barrels that once held church wine, letting the spirit mellow slowly. Visitors can tour the underground cellar where the casks are kept, taste the spirits the monks make, and at night even stay in the monastery's guest rooms and listen to the quiet of the lake.

穿上醜毛衣，跑過街頭壁畫

來源：Runstreet / Time Out Chicago

今年十二月，Runstreet 的「藝術路跑」會回到芝加哥的 Fulton Market。這是他們今年最後一場。

大家穿著很醜的節日毛衣，慢慢跑5公里。一路上常常停下來，在壁畫（畫在牆上的大圖）前拍照，包括有名的 B_Line 壁畫牆。這不是比賽，比較像一起跑步逛畫展。

這一區以前是肉品市場。最醜的毛衣還能得獎！

This December, Runstreet's "art run" returns to Chicago's Fulton Market, and it's their last one of the year. Runners wear the ugliest holiday sweaters they can find and jog an easy 5 kilometers. Along the way, they often stop to take photos in front of murals, including the famous B_Line mural wall. It isn't a race so much as a group run through an art show. The neighborhood used to be a meatpacking district. And the ugliest sweater can still win a prize!

海上漂來的樂高

來源：Lego Lost at Sea / Brick Fanatics

1997年2月13日，一道大浪打中貨船 Tokio Express，62個貨櫃（裝貨的大鐵箱）掉進英國外海。其中一個裝了將近480萬個樂高零件，有章魚、救生艇和小小的刀。

大約2010年，Tracey Williams 在海灘上撿到這些樂高。她在網路上請大家拍照、記錄，再跟船上的貨單比對。現在已經有上千人一起找。

科學家說，這些塑膠可能會在海裡留上100到1300年。

On February 13, 1997, a huge wave struck the cargo ship Tokio Express, and 62 containers (large steel boxes used to carry freight) fell into the sea off the coast of England. One of them held nearly 4.8 million LEGO pieces, including octopuses, lifeboats, and tiny knives. Around 2010, Tracey Williams found some of these LEGO pieces on a beach. She asked people online to send photos and reports, then checked them against the ship's cargo manifest. Now more than a thousand people are searching together. Scientists say the plastic may stay in the sea for 100 to 1,300 years.
