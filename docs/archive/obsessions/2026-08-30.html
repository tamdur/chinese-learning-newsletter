煤礦爆炸那一夜的歌

來源：Taiwan Panorama

一九八四年六月二十日，台北土城的海山煤礦（挖煤的地方）發生爆炸，七十四位工人死去，大多是原住民。歌手胡德夫也去救人。回家那一晚，他對著錄音機，唱下〈為什麼〉這首歌。沒有樂團，也沒有錄音室。

歌詞是排灣族詩人莫那能寫的。同一年，台灣第一個原住民權利團體成立了。

胡德夫從一九七〇年代就開始唱歌，卻等到二〇〇五年才出第一張專輯《匆匆》。裡面的〈太平洋的風〉，第二年拿下金曲獎。

On 20 June 1984, an explosion tore through the Haishan coal mine in Tucheng, Taipei, killing seventy-four workers — most of them Indigenous. The singer Hu Defu joined the rescue. That night, back at home, he sang a song called “Why” straight into a tape recorder. No band, no studio.

The lyrics were written by Monaneng, a Paiwan poet. That same year, Taiwan's first Indigenous rights organisation was founded.

Hu Defu had been singing since the 1970s, yet he did not release his first album, “In a Hurry,” until 2005. Its track “Wind of the Pacific” won a Golden Melody Award the following year.

沒有人訂位的小屋

來源：Finland, Naturally

芬蘭北部的帕拉斯國家公園裡，散落著許多沒有人看管的小木屋，芬蘭話叫 autiotupa。門不上鎖，也不能預訂，由國家的森林局照顧。走路或滑雪的人都能進去住一晚，不用錢。

每間大約六個床位、一個燒木頭的爐子，外面還有一間放柴火（燒的木頭）的小房子。

規矩只有一條：最晚到的人一定有地方睡。天黑後若又來了人，先到的人就要讓出空間，甚至自己到外面搭帳篷。三月的拉普蘭雪還厚，白天也長，這些小屋正好是滑雪路上的休息站。

Scattered through Pallas National Park in northern Finland are small wooden cabins that nobody tends — called autiotupa in Finnish. The doors are never locked and cannot be reserved; the state forestry service looks after them. Anyone walking or skiing can spend a night there, free.

Each holds about six bunks and a wood-burning stove, with a small shed outside for firewood.

There is only one rule: whoever arrives last is guaranteed a place to sleep. If more people turn up after dark, those who came earlier make room — even pitching their own tent outside. In March the snow in Lapland is still deep and the days are long, which makes these cabins the natural rest stops along a ski route.

開鎖的人，星期三見

來源：Pumping Station: One

每個月第一個星期三晚上八點，芝加哥的開鎖社團 TOOOL 會在 Elston 大道的 Pumping Station: One 聚會。下一次是九月二日。

活動公開，不用錢。老手拿出真的鎖，教大家怎麼打開；沒帶工具的人，他們會借你。只要有新面孔出現，就從最基本的教起。

他們想說的，是鎖匠（修鎖的師傅）都知道的一件事：大部分的鎖，比主人以為的還要好開。懂得開鎖，是一門手藝，不是把戲。

On the first Wednesday of every month at 8 p.m., Chicago's lock-picking club TOOOL meets at Pumping Station: One on Elston Avenue. The next one is 2 September.

The meeting is open and free. Old hands bring out real locks and teach anyone how to open them; if you turn up without tools, they will lend you some. Whenever a new face appears, they start again from the basics.

What they are really getting at is something every locksmith knows: most locks are easier to open than their owners believe. Knowing how is a craft, not a trick.

一個人的實驗室

來源：self-experiments.org

住在紐約州羅徹斯特的 Alex Chernavsky 是一位資訊工程師。多年來，他把自己當成實驗對象：吃下某種藥或營養品，再測自己的反應速度、心情和記憶。

他學醫學試驗的做法，連自己都不知道吃的是真藥，還是安慰劑（沒有藥效的假藥）。

所有結果都放在他親手做的網站上，格式一樣：假設、做法、資料、結論。有效的寫，沒效的也寫。他發現糖尿病的藥 metformin 讓他反應變快，語氣卻和那些什麼都沒發現的實驗一模一樣。

Alex Chernavsky, an IT professional living in Rochester, New York, has spent years treating himself as the experimental subject: he takes a drug or supplement, then measures his own reaction time, mood and memory.

He borrows the methods of clinical trials, arranging things so that even he does not know whether he is taking the real drug or a placebo (a fake with no active effect).

Every result goes onto a website he built by hand, always in the same format: hypothesis, method, data, conclusion. The ones that worked are written up, and so are the ones that did not. He found that metformin, a diabetes drug, sharpened his reaction time — and reported it in exactly the same tone as the trials that found nothing at all.
