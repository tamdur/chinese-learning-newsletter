人魚變成機器：貝蒂蘋果的聲音宇宙

來源：Bandcamp / CDM Create Digital Music

貝蒂蘋果（本名鄭宜蘋，一九八六年生）在台北學聲音藝術，老師是噪音（很吵的實驗音樂）前輩王福瑞。她也曾和日本噪音大師 Merzbow 同台演出。

二零二四年三月一日，她發表第一張專輯《Taiwan Bay Be》。她把十年來的身體表演，變成又硬又快的電子舞曲，也把人魚的童話改寫成科幻故事，問了一個問題：人死了以後，聲音會去哪裡？

Betty Apple (born Cheng Yi-Ping, 1986) studied sound art in Taipei under Wang Fujui, a pioneering figure in noise — the loud, experimental genre. She has also shared a stage with Merzbow, the Japanese master of noise. On March 1, 2024, she released her debut album Taiwan Bay Be. Drawing on a decade of body-performance work, she turned that practice into hard, fast electronic dance music, and rewrote the mermaid fairy tale as science fiction, asking one question: where does sound go after a person dies?

開車走過結冰的海：芬蘭最後一條冰路

來源：Europa Tips / Finnish Transport Infrastructure Agency

芬蘭北部的歐盧（Oulu）外海有一座小島，叫海盧奧托。幾百年來，島上的人冬天開車走過結冰的海面。這條「冰路」長七公里，是芬蘭最長的一條，路邊插著樹枝當記號，工人每個月量三次冰有多厚。

天氣變暖，能走的時間從四、五個月縮短到只剩四、五個星期。二零二六年底，一座八點四公里的新橋完工，冰路就要退休了。想走冰路，得去芬蘭別的地方。

Off the coast of Oulu in northern Finland lies a small island called Hailuoto. For centuries, islanders have driven across the frozen sea each winter. This "ice road" stretches seven kilometers — Finland's longest — with tree branches marking the route and workers measuring the ice thickness three times a month. Warming temperatures have compressed the usable season from four or five months down to just four or five weeks. By the end of 2026, an 8.4-kilometer bridge will be finished and the ice road will be retired. Anyone wanting to drive on a frozen sea will need to go elsewhere in Finland.

門是開著的：葬儀社裡的悲傷圈

來源：Chicago Death Doula Collective / Midtown Funerals

芝加哥爾文公園路三九一八號的 Midtown 葬儀社（辦喪事的地方），每個月第三個星期二晚上七點到八點，會把大門打開，變成大家說話的客廳。

兩位陪伴臨終者的工作者珊迪和潔西卡，在這裡帶一個免費的「悲傷圈」。不用報名，走進來就好。心裡的失去，不管是上個月的，還是三十年前的，都可以帶來。每個月第二個星期二有西班牙語場。下一次是二零二六年八月十八日。

Midtown Funeral Home at 3918 Irving Park Road in Chicago opens its doors every third Tuesday of the month, from 7 to 8 p.m., turning the space into a living room where people come to talk. Two end-of-life doulas, Sandi and Jessica, lead a free "grief circle" here. No registration needed — just walk in. Whatever loss you carry, whether from last month or thirty years ago, you are welcome to bring it. A Spanish-language session is held on the second Tuesday of each month. The next circle is August 18, 2026.

創業家就是英雄：一個部落格挖了幾十年

來源：Dollars and Dragons

Dollars and Dragons 是一個很舊、很簡單的部落格，沒有廣告。作者莫龍（Cyril Morong）是美國聖安東尼奧學院退休的經濟學老師。

幾十年來他只挖一個想法：做生意的人，走的是神話學家坎伯說的「英雄旅程」，先聽見召喚，再走進考驗，最後帶著寶物回家。一九九七年他在加拿大蒙特婁的會議上發表這個看法，二零二五年還在寫，用經濟學讀荷馬的《奧德賽》。

Dollars and Dragons is an old, very plain blog with no advertising. Its author, Cyril Morong, is a retired economics professor from San Antonio College in the United States. For decades he has mined a single idea: that people in business are walking the "hero's journey" described by the mythologist Joseph Campbell — first hearing the call, then entering the ordeal, and finally returning home with a treasure. He presented this idea at a conference in Montreal, Canada in 1997, and was still writing in 2025, reading Homer's Odyssey through the lens of economics.
