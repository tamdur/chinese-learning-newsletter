山裡的錄音機：陳世興的新世紀音樂

來源：FOND/SOUND

陳世興本來是搖滾樂團的吉他手。一九八零年代，他搬到台灣東部的山裡，開始把蟲鳴、溪水和鳥叫錄下來，再配上電子琴慢慢的長音。

一九九四年，他在小唱片公司「鐘石唱片」發行了《發現後山的藝術》。這種音樂不是為了電台，而是給朋友泡茶時慢慢聽的。

台灣的新世紀音樂幾乎沒人記錄，直到最近，挖舊唱片的人才把它重新找回來。

Chen Shi-hsing was originally a guitarist in a rock band. In the 1980s, he relocated to the mountains of eastern Taiwan, where he began recording insect chirps, flowing water, and birdsong, layering them with slow, sustained tones from an electronic keyboard. In 1994, he released "Discovering Arts Behind the Mountains" on the small independent label Prime Records. This wasn't music meant for radio—it was designed for friends to listen to slowly while brewing tea together. Taiwan's new age music has gone almost entirely undocumented, but recently record collectors have begun excavating and rediscovering these forgotten works.

黑香腸的規矩：去一趟，回一趟

來源：Wikipedia / Visit Tampere

芬蘭坦佩雷的老市場裡，有一攤賣黑香腸的肉店，開了一百年。黑香腸用豬血、豬肉和碎黑麥做成，外皮烤得脆脆的。

買的時候，當地人不說要幾公斤，而是直接說出一個價錢；或者說一句「menopaluu」，意思是「去和回來」，因為一整條香腸彎起來，正像一個來回的彎道。

香腸要趁熱吃，配黃芥末和紅莓果醬。市場星期一到星期六都開，三月來的旅人可以順路嘗嘗。

In the old market of Tampere, Finland, sits a butcher stall selling black sausage—a business that has thrived for a hundred years. Black sausage is made from pork blood, pork meat, and cracked rye, with a crispy roasted skin.

When buying, locals don't specify a quantity in kilos. Instead, they simply state a price, or say "menopaluu"—a word meaning "there and back"—because when a whole sausage is curved, it resembles a round-trip bend in the road.

The sausage is best eaten hot, served with yellow mustard and lingonberry jam.

The market operates Monday through Saturday. Visitors coming in March can swing by and give it a try.

芝加哥的降靈會，重新上演

來源：We Play With The Dead

九月十九日起，芝加哥北克拉克街三九一四號的小劇場，要上演一齣戲，叫《降靈會》（古時候和死去的人說話的聚會）。

一百多年前，很多美國人相信靈媒（自稱能和鬼魂說話的人）真的能把親人請回來。台上的演員會把當年騙人的手法一一示範，也把這段歷史說清楚。

這不是鬼屋遊戲。觀眾要滿二十一歲，還可能被請上台一起參與。票價大約四十美元，週末場一直演到十月三十一日。

Starting September 19, a small theater at 3914 North Clark Street in Chicago will stage a play called "The Séance"—a gathering where people attempt to communicate with the dead. Over a century ago, many Americans believed that mediums—people who claimed they could speak with ghosts—could genuinely bring back their departed relatives. The performers onstage will demonstrate the deceptive tricks used back then and explain this fascinating chapter of history. This is not a haunted house attraction. Audiences must be at least 21 years old and may be invited to participate onstage. Tickets cost about forty dollars, with weekend performances running through October 31st.

會自己改變的世界地圖

來源：Histochart

有個網站叫 Histochart，只要拉動時間軸，整個世界的國界就會跟著改變：帝國變大又崩塌，殖民地分家，國家合併。

做這個網站的人說，自己「根本不是歷史學家」。他從二零一五年開始做，十年來整個打掉重做了好幾次，只因為他很想弄清楚，地球上那些線到底怎麼移動。

沒有廣告，也沒有公司，只有一個人的好奇心。

There's a website called Histochart. Drag the timeline and the world's borders transform—empires grow and collapse, colonies break away, countries merge. The person who built it says he's not a historian at all. He started in 2015, and over the past decade he's completely torn it down and rebuilt it multiple times, all because he really wanted to understand one thing: how do those lines on Earth actually move? No ads, no company—just one person's curiosity.
