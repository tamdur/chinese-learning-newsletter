林強的大轉彎：從民謠巨星到電子音樂先鋒

來源：Discogs

一九九零年，林強用一首台語歌「向前走」賣出四十萬張，還拿下金曲獎年度歌曲。但他對流行音樂圈感到厭倦（不想再做了），飛到倫敦，找來曾替 Nine Inch Nails 製作的 John Fryer。

一九九四年的專輯「娛樂世界」混合了電子舞曲、工業搖滾和環境音樂。當時幾乎沒人買，但三十年後回頭看，它走在台灣音樂前面好幾十年。

In 1990, Lin Qiang sold 400,000 copies of his Taiwanese-language song "Moving Forward" and won the Golden Melody Award for Song of the Year. But exhausted by the pop music scene, he flew to London and enlisted John Fryer, who had produced for Nine Inch Nails. His 1994 album "Entertainment World" blended electronic dance music, industrial rock, and ambient sound. Few bought it at the time, but three decades later, it's clear the album was decades ahead of Taiwan's music landscape.

三月的芬蘭：白樺樹醒來的季節

來源：Waldwissen.net

芬蘭人把三月叫做「Mahlakuu」，意思是「樹液（樹的汁水）之月」。冬天快結束時，銀白色的白樺（一種樹）開始流出淡淡甜味的汁水。這個傳統從一五零零年代就有了。

古時候，每棵被採集的樹都有自己的名字，女孩們會用第一滴春天樹液洗臉，希望夏天不會曬傷。三月蜜月旅行，正好遇上這個古老的春天儀式。

Finns call March "Mahlakuu," which means "tree sap month." As winter nears its end, the silvery birch trees begin to release a delicate, subtly sweet sap. This tradition dates back to the 1500s. In ancient times, each tree being tapped had its own name, and girls would wash their faces with the first drops of spring sap, hoping to avoid sunburn during the summer. A March honeymoon happens to coincide with this ancient spring ritual.

拿著地圖和指南針，走進冰河時代的山丘

來源：Chicago Area Orienteering Club

四月二十六日，芝加哥定向越野俱樂部在 Gilberts 的 Freeman Kame 森林保護區舉辦比賽。這裡的地形是古代冰河留下的小山丘和水塘，很特別。

參加的人只能用地圖和指南針，在樹林裡找到藏起來的檢查點。不能用手機導航！新手歡迎參加，早上九點半有免費教學課。

On April 26th, the Chicago Orienteering Club is holding a race at Freeman Kame Forest Preserve in Gilberts. The terrain here is special — dotted with small hills and ponds left behind by ancient glaciers. Participants navigate only with map and compass, searching through the woods to find hidden checkpoints. No smartphone navigation allowed! Beginners are welcome. Free instruction starts at 9:30 a.m.

一千兩百條紅線：一位教授的瘋狂收藏

來源：The Vault of Culture

你看過偵探電影裡那面牆嗎？照片、文件、紅色的線把所有線索連在一起。伊利諾大學的英文教授 Shawn Gilmore 花了好幾年，把這個畫面在電影、電視和遊戲裡出現的每一次都記錄下來。

他的資料庫叫做「敘事紅線理論」，已經超過一千兩百筆。從老黑白電影到最新的獨立遊戲，一個人對一件小事的熱情，就是網路最好的樣子。

You know that wall in detective movies? The one with photos, documents, and red lines connecting all the clues together. Shawn Gilmore, an English professor at the University of Illinois, spent years documenting every single time this visual appeared in movies, television shows, and video games. His database, called "Narrative Red String Theory," has grown to over twelve hundred entries. From old black-and-white films to the latest indie games, one person's passion for a small detail is exactly what makes the internet great.
