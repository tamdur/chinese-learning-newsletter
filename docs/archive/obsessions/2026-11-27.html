看不見的樂團，聽得見的世界

來源：NOWnews

台灣的「EyeMusic」樂團，五位成員都是視障（看不見或看不清楚）音樂家。他們自己寫了一部音樂劇，叫《保溫箱的藝術家》。二〇二二年首演後，去過台北、高雄和台中。

現在，這部劇的九首歌全都上了串流平台。曲風從流行、爵士、古典，一路跳到歌劇、放克和嘻哈。所有的音樂，都是他們用耳朵一個音一個音聽出來、寫出來的。

創團成員劉子瑜，後來成為台灣第一位拿到鋼琴碩士的視障音樂家。

EyeMusic, a band from Taiwan, has five members who are all visually impaired musicians, meaning they are either blind or have very limited sight. They wrote a musical themselves called The Artists of the Incubator (《保溫箱的藝術家》). After its premiere in 2022, the show travelled to Taipei, Kaohsiung and Taichung. All nine of its songs are now on streaming platforms. The styles range from pop, jazz and classical to opera, funk and hip-hop. Every piece of music was built by ear, one note at a time, by the performers themselves. Founding member Liu Zi-yu later became the first visually impaired musician in Taiwan to earn a master's degree in piano.

一籃莓果，換來整片森林

來源：Finland.fi

一九一四年，芬蘭的寡婦（丈夫已經過世的女人）伊爾瑪．林德格倫，和三位女性在森林裡採越橘（一種小小的紅色莓果）。地主走過來，要她們把莓果交出來。

官司一路打到最高法院。法官說：在別人的土地上採莓果，不算犯罪。這個案子，成了芬蘭「每個人的權利」的源頭。

今天，任何人都可以在芬蘭的森林裡散步、滑雪、露營。三月來度蜜月，你們可以走進結冰湖邊的樹林；只是生火或開車進去，還是要先問過地主。

In 1914, Ilma Lindgren, a Finnish widow (a woman whose husband had already died), was picking lingonberries (small red berries) in the forest with three other women. A landowner walked over and demanded that they hand over the berries. The case went all the way to the Supreme Court, which ruled that picking berries on someone else's land was not a crime. The case became the origin of Finland's "everyman's rights." Today, anyone can walk, ski, or camp in Finland's forests. Honeymooners visiting in March can stroll through the trees beside frozen lakes; lighting a fire or driving in, however, still requires the landowner's permission.

湖邊的望遠鏡，免費看月亮

來源：Navy Pier

十二月，一群愛看星星的志工「Chi-Town Skygazers」，會在海軍碼頭的南邊架起望遠鏡，請大家免費看天空。

日期是十二月十六日到二十三日，還有二十六、二十七日。星期日到星期四，從傍晚五點半到七點半；星期五、六延長到八點半。

不用報名，走過去就能看。在密西根湖上，月亮好像就在眼前。天氣不好時，活動可能取消。

In December, a group of volunteer stargazers called the Chi-Town Skygazers will set up telescopes south of Navy Pier and invite the public to look at the sky for free. The sessions run December 16 through 23, plus December 26 and 27. From Sunday through Thursday, they go from 5:30 to 7:30 in the evening; on Friday and Saturday, the hours are extended to 8:30. No registration is needed, so just walk over and look. Out on Lake Michigan, the moon seems close enough to touch. If the weather is bad, the event may be canceled.

誰在管這塊地？一個人的世界地圖

來源：Political Geography Now

地圖上的國界，常常和真實情況不一樣。美國奧勒岡州的地圖製作者艾文．森塔尼，從二〇〇九年開始，幾乎一個人經營網站「Political Geography Now」。

他追蹤世界上有爭議（大家意見不同、互相爭吵）的土地：哪裡的前線在移動，哪個地方想要獨立，連哥斯大黎加和尼加拉瓜搶的一小片海灘都不放過。

他畫出「現在到底是誰管這塊地」的地圖，連BBC的記者也會來參考。二〇二二年，美國地理學家協會還頒獎給他。

Borders on a map often don't match what is really happening on the ground. Evan Centanni, a mapmaker in Oregon, has run the website Political Geography Now almost entirely on his own since 2009. He tracks the world's disputed lands, the places where people disagree and quarrel over who should control them: where the front lines are moving, which regions want independence, and even the small stretch of beach that Costa Rica and Nicaragua have fought over. He draws maps that show who actually controls a piece of land right now, and reporters from the BBC come to him for reference. In 2022, the American Association of Geographers even gave him an award.
