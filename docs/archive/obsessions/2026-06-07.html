蝴蝶飛了：排灣古調的新聲音

來源：Bandcamp / Taiwu Ancient Ballads Troupe

二○○二年，一位排灣族老師查馬克來到屏東大武山下的小學。他向村裡的老人家，一字一字把快要消失的古老歌謠學回來。

這就是「泰武古謠傳唱」的由來。他們是第一個登上世界音樂節大舞台的台灣團體，也拿過好幾座金曲獎。

二○二二年的專輯《蝴蝶飛了》做了七年。四代人的歌聲，配上雷鬼、電子和饒舌，讓古老的聲音活了起來。

In 2002, a Paiwan teacher named Chamak came to a small school at the foot of Dawu Mountain in Pingtung. He went to the village elders and learned back, word by word, ancient songs that were on the verge of disappearing. This is how the "Taiwu Ancient Ballads Troupe" came about. They were the first Taiwanese group to take the stage at a world music festival, and they've won several Golden Melody Awards. The 2022 album "Flight of the Butterfly" took seven years to make. Voices from four generations, combined with reggae, electronic music, and rap, brought the ancient sounds back to life.

為最稀有的海豹堆一個雪窩

來源：Visit Finland

在芬蘭的賽馬湖裡，住著世界上最稀有的海豹。大約八千年前冰河退了，牠們被困在湖裡，再也回不了海。現在全世界只剩下大約四百隻。

冬天時，海豹媽媽會在湖邊挖一個雪窩（雪做的小家）生寶寶。要是雪太少，小海豹就很危險。

在普馬拉這個小鎮，遊客可以坐安靜的電動雪車，和當地人一起用手幫海豹堆雪窩，最後還能喝一碗熱魚湯。

In Finland's Lake Saimaa lives the world's rarest seal. About eight thousand years ago, when the ice age glaciers retreated, they became trapped in the lake and could never return to the sea. Today, only around four hundred remain in the entire world. In winter, mother seals dig snow dens—small shelters made of snow—along the lake's edge to give birth. If there isn't enough snow, the pups face serious danger. In the small town of Puumala, visitors can ride in quiet electric snow vehicles and join locals in hand-building snow dens for the seals. Afterward, they're treated to a warm bowl of fish soup.

划船走一趟非裔歷史水路

來源：Block Club Chicago

六月十九日是美國的「六月節」，紀念黑人得到自由的日子。配合這個節日，芝加哥有一場划船活動。

大家從河邊的船坡出發，划著獨木舟，走一趟「非裔歷史水路」。這條河記得當年逃向自由的人、勇敢的黑人飛行員和爭取人權的前輩。

活動適合新手，有人帶領，不過要先報名。這是認識芝加哥另一面的好方法。

June 19th is Juneteenth in the United States, commemorating the day Black Americans gained their freedom. To mark this holiday, Chicago is hosting a paddling excursion. Participants will depart from a riverside boat launch and paddle canoes along the "African American Heritage Water Trail." This river holds the memories of those who once fled toward freedom, courageous Black aviators, and civil rights pioneers. The activity welcomes beginners and includes guided instruction, though registration is required in advance. It's an excellent way to discover another side of Chicago's history.

二十二萬份文件，變成一張水質地圖

來源：FutureHeist / MetaFilter

美國德州有將近一萬五千個供水系統，每年都要交水質報告。可是這些報告被分成二十二萬多份文件，藏在難用的網頁後面，一般人幾乎看不到。

有一位工程師把十五年的資料全部抓下來，標在一張地圖上。點一點，就能看到哪裡的水有重金屬或其他問題。

有些地方的數字，竟然超過安全標準好幾十倍。本來沒人看得懂的公開資料，終於人人都能用了。

Texas has nearly fifteen thousand water supply systems, and each one files a water quality report every year. But these reports are scattered across more than two hundred twenty thousand documents, buried behind clunky websites that most people never access. One engineer downloaded fifteen years of data and plotted it all on a single map. A click reveals which areas have heavy metals or other contaminants in their water. Some locations show readings dozens of times above safe levels. Public information that was once incomprehensible is finally usable for everyone.
