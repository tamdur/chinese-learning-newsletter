江湖卡夫卡：醒來變成蟲的人

來源：Blow 吹音樂

2022年，客家搖滾歌手林生祥和作詞人鍾永豐，推出專輯《江湖卡夫卡》。這是兩人第十次合作。

他們借用卡夫卡的小說《變形記》：一個人早上醒來，變成了一隻蟲，說不出話，也逃不出殼。歌裡的蟲，就是台灣政治江湖裡的官員。

音樂也跟著「變形」。林生祥放下習慣的和弦，和日本鍵盤手早川徹一起寫出陌生的音階；嗩吶（一種像喇叭的樂器）不再跟著旋律走，而像一隻瘋狗，在變形的身體裡扭來扭去。

In 2022, Hakka rock musician Lin Sheng-hsiang and lyricist Zhong Yong-feng released the album Kafka in the Rivers and Lakes. It marked their tenth collaboration together. The album draws from Kafka's novella The Metamorphosis: a man awakens one morning transformed into an insect, unable to speak or escape his shell. In their songs, the insect becomes a symbol for officials in Taiwan's political underworld. The music itself undergoes a parallel transformation. Lin Sheng-hsiang abandoned his familiar chord progressions, collaborating with Japanese keyboardist Hayakawa Tetsu to craft unfamiliar scales; the suona (a trumpet-like wind instrument) no longer follows the melody but instead writhes like a crazed dog within the transformed body.

在結冰的湖上，滑向遠方

來源：Visit Punkaharju

芬蘭東部的塞馬湖，冬天會結成厚厚的冰。在小鎮蓬卡哈留附近，工作人員每年都會在湖面上整理出十六公里長的溜冰路線。

路線分成四段，有適合全家的小圈，也有給高手的長路。途中有營火可以取暖，還有一家鬆餅咖啡館。兩頭都能租冰刀鞋，一日票只要八歐元。

溜冰季從一月開始，大約到三月中結束。三月來度蜜月，也許正好趕上春天化冰前的最後幾天，不在冰場繞圈，而是在湖上一路滑向遠方。

Lake Saimaa in eastern Finland freezes thick with ice each winter. Near the small town of Punkaharju, staff members groom a 16-kilometer skating route across the lake surface every year. The route is divided into four sections—small loops perfect for families and longer stretches for experienced skaters. Along the way there are bonfires to warm up by and a pancake café. Ice skates can be rented at both ends, with a day pass costing just eight euros. The skating season runs from January through mid-March. Come for a honeymoon in March and you might just catch the final days before the spring thaw, and instead of circling a rink, you can glide across the lake into the distance.

石頭迷的星期六晚上

來源：Chicago Rocks and Minerals Society

10月10日星期六晚上七點半，芝加哥岩石礦物學會在北郊的 Skokie 舉行每月聚會，地點是聖彼得教會。免費參加，誰都可以直接走進去。

不管你喜歡化石、礦石，還是只是對地球好奇，這裡都歡迎。從小孩到老手，大家在這裡交換找石頭的故事。

學會的會刊名字很可愛，叫《The Pick and Dop Stick》，說的是挖石頭的尖頭工具，和磨寶石時用來夾住石頭的小棒子。

On Saturday evening, October 10th at 7:30 p.m., the Chicago Rock and Mineral Society held its monthly gathering in Skokie on the North Shore, at St. Peter's Church. Admission is free, and anyone can simply walk in. Whether you're into fossils, minerals, or just curious about the Earth, you're welcome here. From children to seasoned collectors, everyone swaps stories about hunting for rocks. The society's newsletter has a charming name — "The Pick and Dop Stick" — which refers to the pointed tools used for digging up rocks and the small sticks used to hold stones in place while polishing gems.

從小溪石頭長出來的字

來源：Spencerian.com

從1850年到1925年左右，美國人寫商業信，都用一種彎彎圓圓的草寫字，叫「史賓賽體」。可口可樂和福特汽車的標誌，都是從它變來的。打字機出現後，這種字差點消失。

1970年代末，寫字高手麥可・蘇爾找到一位九十歲的老師傅，直接跟他學，把這門手藝接了下來。

他的網站 Spencerian.com 記下了這段歷史。據說發明人史賓賽，是看著俄亥俄州小溪裡被水磨圓的石頭，畫出字裡的圓圈。

From around 1850 to 1925, American business correspondence was written in an elegant, flowing script known as Spencerian. The logos of Coca-Cola and Ford both evolved from this distinctive style. When typewriters arrived, the practice nearly vanished.

In the late 1970s, master penman Michael Sull found a ninety-year-old craftsman still practicing the art and learned directly from him, reviving this nearly-extinct tradition. His website, Spencerian.com, documents this remarkable story.

According to tradition, the style's creator Spencer drew his circular letterforms by observing smooth river stones in an Ohio stream, shaped by the patient flow of water.
