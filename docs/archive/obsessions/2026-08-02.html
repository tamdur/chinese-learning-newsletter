用琴弦做出來的電音

來源：Douban Music / Taiwan Beats

二〇一五年十月，台灣的世界冠軍唱盤手 DJ QuestionMark，為一支六人女子弦樂團製作了專輯《SEMIFUSA》。團員全是學古典音樂出身，兩把小提琴、兩把中提琴、兩把大提琴。

專輯裡所有聽起來像電子音樂的聲響，其實都是把她們拉琴的聲音錄下來，再切開、重組而成，完全沒有用合成器（電子樂器）。爵士、城市流行與拉丁節奏在裡面交錯。這張唱片也讓他入圍第二十七屆金曲獎最佳製作人。

In October 2015, Taiwan's world champion turntablist DJ QuestionMark produced an album called SEMIFUSA for a six-member all-female string ensemble. The members were all classically trained — two violins, two violas, and two cellos. Everything on the album that sounds like electronic music is actually made from recordings of them playing their instruments: the sounds were captured, then cut up and reassembled, with no synthesizers used at all. Jazz, city pop, and Latin rhythms weave through the record. The album also earned him a nomination for Best Producer at the 27th Golden Melody Awards.

大砲一響，郵差才敢過海

來源：Eckerö Mail and Customs House / Visit Åland

芬蘭西邊的奧蘭群島上，有一棟一八二八年蓋好的郵政海關大樓。設計者是打造赫爾辛基市中心的建築師恩格爾，這裡曾是俄國最西邊的據點。

一六三八年到一八九五年，當地農夫輪流划船，把信送過奧蘭海。冬天海面結冰，他們就拖著船，或趕著馬雪橇走過去。早春冰快裂開時，兩岸的大砲會開火，告訴人們今天能不能走。大樓現在是小博物館，三月來正好看得到海冰。

On the Åland Islands west of Finland stands a postal and customs building completed in 1828. Designed by Carl Ludvig Engel — the architect who shaped Helsinki's city center — it once served as Russia's westernmost outpost. From 1638 to 1895, local farmers took turns rowing boats to carry mail across the Åland Sea. In winter, when the water froze solid, they would drag their boats across the ice or drive horse-drawn sleighs over it. In early spring, as the ice began to crack, cannons on both shores would fire — a signal telling people whether it was safe to cross that day. Today the building is a small museum, and March is exactly the right time to come and see the sea ice.

平地跑道上的肩與胯

來源：Chicago-Style Roller Derby

八月二十二日星期六，芝加哥式滾輪德比聯盟要在帕拉泰恩的鹽溪運動中心（647 S Consumers Ave）打今年第四場。場地離市中心大約三十英里。

這個業餘聯盟二〇二二年由兩支球隊合併而成。傍晚五點半開門，六點開哨。選手穿著隊服和護具，在平地跑道上用臀部把對手撞開。不懂規則也沒關係，任何人走進去都能看。

On Saturday, August 22, Chicago-Style Roller Derby will host its fourth bout of the year at the Salt Creek Sport Center in Palatine (647 S Consumers Ave). The venue sits about thirty miles from downtown. This amateur league was formed in 2022 from the merger of two teams. Doors open at 5:30 PM and the first whistle is at 6. Skaters in team jerseys and full protective gear use their hips to knock opponents aside on a flat track. If you don't know the rules, no problem — anyone can walk in and watch.

五千種鯰魚的家譜

來源：PlanetCatfish.com

一九九六年開始，一位網名叫 Jools 的養魚迷，帶著一小群同好做出了「鯰魚目錄」。到今天，它收了四十四科、五千兩百多種鯰魚（一種長鬍鬚的魚），配上兩萬多張照片。

每一種都對照國際瀕危物種名單。網站上還有數十年沒中斷的「本月鯰魚」專欄，以及會員登記的五萬多筆飼養紀錄。沒有任何機構出錢，全靠愛好者一筆一筆寫成。

Since 1996, an aquarium enthusiast known online as Jools, together with a small group of fellow hobbyists, has built the Cat-eLog. Today it covers forty-four families and more than five thousand two hundred species of catfish — the long-whiskered fish — accompanied by over twenty thousand photographs. Each species is cross-referenced against the IUCN Red List. The site also features a "Catfish of the Month" column that has run uninterrupted for decades, along with more than fifty thousand husbandry records logged by members. No institution funded it — it was built entirely by enthusiasts, one entry at a time.
