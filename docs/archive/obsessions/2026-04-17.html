被禁的聲音：黑名單工作室與《抓狂歌》

來源：Takes on 200

一九八九年，台灣解除戒嚴（軍事管制）才兩年，一張專輯震動了整個音樂界。黑名單工作室把台語民謠、搖滾、饒舌和電子合成器混在一起，還加入街頭的聲音，做出從沒人聽過的東西。

第一首歌《計程車》用喇叭聲問：「你要去哪裡？」歌詞太尖銳（批評很直接），國民黨禁止電台播放。這張專輯後來被認為「新台語歌運動」的起點。

In 1989, just two years after Taiwan lifted martial law, an album shook the entire music world. Blacklist Studio mixed Taiwanese folk music, rock, rap, and electronic synthesizers together, adding street sounds to create something no one had ever heard before. The first song, "Taxi," uses horn sounds to ask: "Where are you going?" The lyrics were so sharp and direct in their criticism that the Kuomintang banned radio stations from playing it. This album later came to be seen as the starting point of the "New Taiwanese Music Movement."

星期四的豌豆湯：芬蘭人五百年不變的午餐

來源：thisisFINLAND

每個星期四，芬蘭全國的學校、軍營和餐廳都供應同一道菜：黃色豌豆湯配豬肉，再加一份烤鬆餅配果醬。這個傳統來自中世紀：天主教規定星期五禁食，所以星期四要吃飽一點。

後來禁食取消了，湯卻留下來了。三月到赫爾辛基旅行的話，找一間小餐廳，在桌上加一點芥末醬，和所有芬蘭人吃一樣的東西。

Every Thursday, schools, military barracks, and restaurants across Finland serve the same dish: yellow pea soup with pork, accompanied by a slice of pancake with jam. This tradition dates back to the Middle Ages: Catholic doctrine forbade eating meat on Fridays, so Finns ate heartily on Thursdays to prepare. The fasting rule eventually disappeared, but the soup remained. If you visit Helsinki in March, find a small restaurant, add a dollop of mustard to your bowl, and eat exactly what every Finn is eating.

經濟學也能演出來：Planet Money 芝加哥現場秀

來源：Chicago Humanities Festival

四月二十三日晚上七點半，美國公共廣播的人氣節目 Planet Money 來到芝加哥。主持人 Wailin Wong 和作家 Alex Mayyasi 將在 Athenaeum Center 舞台上說全新的經濟故事。

芝加哥大學的兩位教授也會加入對談，把播客（podcast）變成一場有趣的公開演講。活動是芝加哥人文節的一部分，散場後還有簽書會。

On the evening of April 23rd at 7:30 p.m., Planet Money, a popular show from American Public Radio, is coming to Chicago. Hosts Wailin Wong and writer Alex Mayyasi will take the stage at the Athenaeum Center to tell brand new economic stories. Two professors from the University of Chicago will also join the conversation, transforming the podcast into an engaging public lecture. The event is part of the Chicago Humanities Festival, and there will be a book signing after the show.

一位圖書館員的食物時間線

來源：Food Timeline

Lynne Olver 是美國紐澤西州的一位圖書館員。她花了十六年，一個人建了一個叫 Food Timeline 的網站，記錄從史前時代到現代的食物歷史。她親自回答了超過兩萬五千個讀者問題，網站吸引了三千五百萬人次瀏覽。

Olver 在二零一五年過世後，維吉尼亞理工大學的食物研究團隊接手維護這個網站，讓她的心血繼續留在網路上。

Lynne Olver was a librarian in New Jersey who spent sixteen years building a website called Food Timeline entirely on her own, documenting the history of food from prehistoric times to the modern era. She personally answered more than twenty-five thousand questions from readers, and the site attracted thirty-five million visits. After Olver passed away in 2015, a food research team at Virginia Tech took over maintaining the website, ensuring that her life's work continues to live on the internet.
