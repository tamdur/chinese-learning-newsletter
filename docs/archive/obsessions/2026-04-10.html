閃靈樂團：用黑金屬說台灣的故事

來源：Chthonic 閃靈

二〇〇五年，台灣的閃靈樂團發行了專輯《賽德克巴萊》。這張專輯把交響樂、黑金屬和二胡混在一起，講的是一九三〇年霧社事件的故事——賽德克族（台灣原住民）起來反抗日本統治。主唱林昶佐用嘶吼（大聲喊叫式的唱法）唱出這段沉重的歷史，女歌手陳珊妮的聲音則像冷風一樣穿過每首歌。這張專輯帶閃靈走上了歐美的大舞台，讓世界聽見台灣很少被說出的歷史。

In 2005, Taiwan's Chthonic released the album "Seediq Bale." This album blended symphonic music, black metal, and the erhu (two-stringed Chinese violin), telling the story of the 1930 Wushe Incident—when the Seediq people, Taiwan's indigenous inhabitants, rose up to resist Japanese colonial rule. Lead vocalist Lin Chao-tso delivered this weighty history through harsh vocals (a shouted singing style), while female vocalist Chen Shan-ni's voice cut through each song like a cold wind. The album brought Chthonic onto the Western stage, allowing the world to hear a chapter of Taiwan's history rarely told elsewhere.

坦佩雷短片影展：紅磚工廠裡的電影盛會

來源：Tampere Film Festival

每年三月，芬蘭的坦佩雷市會舉辦北歐最大、最老的短片影展。這個影展從一九七〇年開始，五天內會吸引大約兩萬人來看。放映的地方是舊時代留下來的紅磚工廠，厚重的牆壁配上銀幕上的光影，氣氛（感覺）很特別。如果你在二〇二七年三月去芬蘭，影展在三月十日到十四日，是體驗芬蘭城市生活的好機會。

Every March, the Finnish city of Tampere hosts the largest and oldest short film festival in Northern Europe. Dating back to 1970, the festival draws approximately 20,000 visitors over five days. The screenings take place in old red brick factories from a bygone era, where thick walls paired with the play of light on screen create a uniquely special atmosphere. If you're planning to visit Finland in March 2027, the festival runs from March 10-14, making it an excellent opportunity to experience Finnish urban life.

CIVL Fest：芝加哥獨立音樂場地的大串聯

來源：CIVL / Do312

四月十六日到二十五日，芝加哥將舉辦 CIVL Fest，一個由獨立音樂場地聯盟辦的全城音樂節。超過四十場演出分散在 Empty Bottle、Thalia Hall、The Hideout、Schubas 和 Rosa's Lounge 等場地。這些都是芝加哥最有個性的小型場地，不是大型體育館，而是社區裡真正有生命力的音樂空間。每場演出各自售票，沒有統一的通行證。

From April 16 to 25, Chicago will host CIVL Fest, a citywide music festival organized by an alliance of independent music venues. Over forty performances will be scattered across venues like Empty Bottle, Thalia Hall, The Hideout, Schubas, and Rosa's Lounge. These are some of Chicago's most distinctive intimate spaces—not massive arenas, but genuinely vibrant music venues embedded in the community. Each performance has its own separate ticket sales; there is no unified festival pass.

百兔工作室：在帆船上寫程式的兩個人

來源：Hundred Rabbits

Rekka 和 Devine 是兩位藝術家，他們住在一艘（一條）帆船上，用很少的電力和沒有網路的條件下寫軟體。他們的工作室叫 Hundred Rabbits（百兔），做出了 Orca（一種音樂寫作工具）和 Uxn（一套小型電腦系統）等開放原始碼的軟體。當你的工具必須在海上也能用，你就會從最基本的地方重新想像電腦可以是什麼。

Rekka and Devine are two artists living aboard a sailboat who write software with minimal electricity and no internet connection. Their studio, Hundred Rabbits, has created open-source software including Orca, a music composition tool, and Uxn, a minimal computing system. When your tools must work at sea, you're forced to reimagine computing from first principles.
