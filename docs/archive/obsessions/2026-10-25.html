用母語跳舞的排灣之歌

來源：Taipei Times

2019年12月，排灣族歌手阿爆推出專輯《母親的舌頭》。十一首歌全部用排灣語唱，沒有一句國語。

主打歌下面是一直跳動的浩室（house）舞曲節拍，上面是老排灣歌的旋律。她還和一位饒舌歌手合唱，一邊排灣語，一邊台語，唱出兩個不同族群的戀人。

2020年，這張專輯拿下金曲獎年度專輯，是第一張得到這個大獎的原住民語專輯。評審說，就算聽不懂歌詞，也會被感動。

In December 2019, Paiwan singer A-Bao released her album Mother's Tongue. All eleven songs are sung in Paiwan, without a single line of Mandarin. Beneath the lead track runs a relentless house beat, and above it float the melodies of old Paiwan folk songs. She also duets with a rapper, switching between Paiwan and Taiwanese Hokkien to tell the story of two lovers from two different peoples. In 2020, the album won the Golden Melody Award for Album of the Year, the first album in an Indigenous language to take that top prize. The judges said that even listeners who could not understand the lyrics would be moved.

寡婦村：走過冰海的送信路

來源：Wikipedia / Postimuseo

從1600年代到1850年代，瑞典和芬蘭之間的皇家信件，要越過大約53公里寬的奧蘭海。

送信的是當地農夫。海水沒結冰時就划船；冬天結冰了，就用馬和雪橇（在雪上滑的車）拉著郵船走過冰面；冰太薄時，只能用手拖。1710年的冬天，埃克勒島就有十七個男人死去。斯托比村因此被叫做「寡婦村」（寡婦是丈夫死了的女人）。

1828年蓋的郵局今天還在，變成一間博物館。島上的老路每一公里，仍立著紅色的木柱。

From the 1600s to the 1850s, royal mail between Sweden and Finland had to cross the Åland Sea, roughly 53 kilometers wide. The letters were carried by local farmers. When the sea was not frozen, they rowed boats. In winter, when the sea froze, they pulled mail boats across the ice using horses and sleighs (carts that glide over snow). When the ice was too thin for that, they had to haul the boats by hand. In the winter of 1710, seventeen men died on the island of Eckerö. Because of this, the village of Stöbbo came to be known as the "Village of Widows" (a place where the husbands had died, leaving women behind). The post office, built in 1828, still stands today and has been turned into a museum. Along the island's old road, red wooden posts still mark every kilometer.

圍成方形，一起大聲唱老歌

來源：Comfort Station Logan Square

10月27日星期二晚上七點到九點，洛根廣場的 Comfort Station （2579 N. Milwaukee Ave.）有「聖豎琴」（Sacred Harp）合唱聚會。

大家面對面坐成一個空心的方形，輪流站到中間帶頭，用四個聲部大聲唱老聖歌。沒有樂器，聲音有點粗、有點不和諧，卻很有力量。

這種唱法來自1844年美國南方鄉下的歌本。不會唱歌也沒關係，現場會借你歌本，完全免費。

On Tuesday, October 27, from 7 to 9 p.m., Comfort Station (2579 N. Milwaukee Ave.) in Logan Square will host a Sacred Harp singing session. Participants sit face to face in a hollow square, taking turns stepping into the middle to lead, and sing old hymns loudly in four vocal parts. There are no instruments, and the sound is a bit rough and out of tune, yet it has real power. This style of singing comes from a songbook published in 1844 in the rural American South. You don't need to know how to sing: the venue will lend you a songbook on the spot, free of charge.

認得每一條鯨魚的網站

來源：Happywhale

Happywhale 是2015年由兩個人開始的網站。賞鯨客、漁夫和科學家上傳鯨魚尾巴的照片，網站就會比對，找出是哪一條鯨魚。

每條鯨魚尾巴下面的黑白花紋都不一樣，就像人的指紋。靠著七十多萬張照片，網站發現：2003年在巴西拍到的一條大翅鯨，22年後出現在一萬五千多公里外的澳洲東邊，是史上最遠的紀錄。

你上傳一張旅遊照，也可能找到「你的」鯨魚走過的路。

Happywhale is a website that a pair of people started in 2015. Whale watchers, fishermen, and scientists upload photos of whale tails, and the site matches them to work out which individual whale is pictured. The black-and-white markings on each whale's tail are unique, much like human fingerprints. Drawing on more than 700,000 photos, the site has documented a record-breaking journey: a humpback whale photographed in Brazil in 2003 turned up 22 years later off Australia's east coast, more than 15,000 kilometers away, the longest movement ever recorded for the species.

Upload a vacation snapshot, and you might find out where "your" whale has traveled.
