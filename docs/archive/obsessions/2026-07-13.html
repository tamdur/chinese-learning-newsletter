母親的舌頭：一張排灣族的電音靈魂樂

來源：樂手巢 YSOLIFE

台東的排灣族歌手阿爆，在二零一九年底發行了《母親的舌頭》。這張專輯全部用排灣族語演唱，還是她和媽媽王秋蘭一起寫的。小時候媽媽常說她的舌頭「還不夠準」，長大後她把這句叮嚀（叮嚀就是再三提醒）變成了歌。

專輯把古老的排灣族吟唱，和靈魂樂、電子樂、饒舌（快速唸出來的歌）混在一起，聲音又新又美。二零二零年，它拿下金曲獎年度專輯，是第一張得到這個大獎的原住民語專輯。

A-Bao, a Paiwan singer from Taitung, released "Mother's Tongue" at the end of 2019. The album is sung entirely in the Paiwan language and was co-written with her mother, Wang Qiu-Lan. Growing up, her mother often told her that her tongue "wasn't accurate enough," and years later she transformed this constant reminder into a song.

The album blends ancient Paiwan chanting with soul, electronic music, and hip-hop (rapid-fire lyrics), creating a sound that is both fresh and beautiful. In 2020, it won the Golden Melody Award for Album of the Year—the first indigenous-language album ever to receive this prestigious honor.

紙業大亨的祕密收藏：芬蘭湖區的藝術寶庫

來源：Serlachius Museums

在芬蘭湖區一個叫曼塔的小鎮，住的人不到六千。紙業大亨塞拉修斯，卻在這裡默默建起北歐最重要的私人藝術收藏。

畫作就掛在他從前的湖邊大宅裡，望著美麗的湖水。這裡有芬蘭黃金時代的名家，也有五百多年前的歐洲油畫。大宅裡的餐廳用當地當季的食材做菜，被認為是歐洲最好的博物館餐廳之一。

In Mänttä, a small town in Finland's lake region with fewer than six thousand residents, paper magnate Serlachius quietly built Northern Europe's most significant private art collection.

The paintings hang in his former lakeside mansion, overlooking beautiful waters. The collection features masters from Finland's golden age alongside European oil paintings from over five centuries ago. The mansion's restaurant, which prepares dishes using local seasonal ingredients, is considered one of Europe's finest museum restaurants.

帶著壞掉的烤麵包機來，不用帶錢包

來源：Aurora Public Library District

七月十八日星期六，中午十一點到下午兩點，芝加哥西邊奧羅拉的桑托里圖書館，要辦一場免費的「修理咖啡館」。

熱心的志工會幫大家修好壞掉的檯燈、電扇、咖啡機，還有破洞的衣服和掉了扣子的褲子。他們不是要賣你零件，而是要教你自己動手修。這樣的活動在大芝加哥地區越來越多，目的是讓好東西別急著被丟掉。

On Saturday, July 18th, from 11 a.m. to 2 p.m., the Santori Library in Aurora, west of Chicago, will host a free "Repair Café."

Devoted volunteers will help you repair broken desk lamps, fans, coffee makers, clothes with holes, and pants missing buttons. They're not selling you parts — they're teaching you how to fix things yourself. Events like this are becoming increasingly common across the greater Chicago area, with the goal of keeping good things from being thrown away too hastily.

射箭的黃金年代，藏在一個玩家的目錄檔案裡

來源：VintageArchery.org

有一個小小的網站，主要靠加州一位愛好者查普爾整理，把二十世紀中期射箭最紅時的原廠目錄，一本一本掃描保存下來。

從一九三零到一九七零年代，貝爾、皮爾森、布朗寧等許多做弓的老牌子，都按著年份排好。除了目錄，網站還留下老雜誌和公司的故事。這些弓廠後來很多都關門或被併購了，若不是這個網站，這些回憶可能早就不見了。

A small website, primarily curated by California-based archery enthusiast Chappell, has painstakingly scanned and preserved the original factory catalogs from archery's golden age in the mid-twentieth century.

From the 1930s through the 1970s, the catalogs of major bowmakers—Bear, Pearson, Browning, and others—are organized by year. Beyond the product catalogs, the site also archives vintage magazines and company histories. Many of these bow manufacturers eventually closed or were acquired, and without this website, these memories would have vanished long ago.
