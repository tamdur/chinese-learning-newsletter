一首詩，十五種聲音

來源：VERSE Magazine

台灣有個音樂計畫叫《滾動的詩》，把一百年來的十五首台灣現代詩，交給十五組獨立樂團重新譜曲（配上音樂）。最特別的是詩人鴻鴻和「神棍樂團」的合作。

這個樂團把西方搖滾和嗩吶、二胡、鑼鈸這些傳統樂器混在一起。他們把鴻鴻自嘲是「魯蛇」（失敗者）的詩〈不要再對我打槍〉，變成一首好記又偷偷帶刺的電子搖滾，聽起來像操場上的兒歌一樣順口。

Taiwan has a music project titled "Like a Rolling Poem" that enlists fifteen independent bands to compose music for fifteen Taiwanese modern poems spanning a hundred years. Most striking is the collaboration between poet Hong Hong and the band ZenKwun. The band fuses Western rock with traditional Chinese instruments—suona, erhu, gongs, and cymbals. They took Hong Hong's poem "Don't Shoot at Me Again," in which the poet self-deprecatingly refers to himself as a "loser," and transformed it into a catchy yet subtly barbed electronic rock track that's as easy to hum as a playground nursery rhyme.

結冰的瀑布，六十公尺高的冰牆

來源：Polar Lapland

在芬蘭羅瓦涅米東南方一百多公里的科羅烏歐瑪峽谷，夏天從崖壁流下的小溪，一到冬天就結成十幾道高聳的冰瀑，最高的有六、七十公尺。攀冰的人還幫它們取名字：適合新手的叫「長毛象瀑布」，最難的叫「棕色激流」。

這裡的冰從一月到四月中都又亮又完整，所以三月很適合來。你可以報名一日嚮導攀冰，也可以穿上冰爪，走那條五公里、繞過整排冰瀑的峽谷步道。

In Korouoma Gorge, located more than a hundred kilometers southeast of Rovaniemi in Finland, streams that flow down the cliff walls in summer transform into a dozen or more towering ice waterfalls by winter, with the tallest reaching sixty to seventy meters. Ice climbers have even given them names: the route suitable for beginners is called "Mammoth Falls," while the most challenging is "Brown Rapids." The ice remains bright and pristine from January through mid-April, making March an ideal time to visit. You can sign up for a guided ice climbing day trip, or you can put on crampons and hike the five-kilometer gorge trail that loops past the entire row of ice waterfalls.

走進百年電車車廂

來源：Pullman National Historical Park

七月二十五、二十六日星期六和星期日，芝加哥的普爾曼國家歷史公園要辦一年一度最大的鐵道活動，慶祝伊利諾中央鐵路電氣化滿一百年。

遊客可以走進目前唯一還留著、一九二六年的電氣通勤車廂，還有三節古董車廂：一九五○年的臥鋪觀景車、一九三四年的休息車，還有一九五七年的「好萊塢海灘」。這些車廂由博物館運來，每三十分鐘一場導覽，當日票二十五美元，名額有限。

On Saturday and Sunday, July 25-26, Chicago's Pullman National Historical Park will host its largest annual railroad event, celebrating 100 years since the Illinois Central Railroad's electrification. Visitors can board the only surviving 1926 electric commuter car, along with three vintage cars: a 1950 sleeper-observation car, a 1934 sleeper-lounge car, and a 1957 "Hollywood Beach." These cars have been transported to the site by the railway museum, with guided tours running every thirty minutes. Day-of tickets cost $25, with limited capacity.

兩萬個陌生人的夢

來源：DreamBank.net

DreamBank.net 是一個免費、可以搜尋的夢境檔案庫，由心理學家和程式設計師在美國一所大學經營。網站收了超過兩萬份一字不漏記下來的夢，其中有人寫了幾十年，像一位叫芭布的女士，記下自己三千多個夢。

任何人都能在裡面查一個詞出現的次數，比方說「憤怒」或「飛行」，在一位盲人的夢和一位老兵的夢裡各出現幾次。這是個奇特又安靜動人的工具，讓我們看看潛意識（自己沒察覺的內心）長什麼樣子。

DreamBank.net is a free, searchable dream archive maintained by a psychologist and a programmer at an American university. The site holds over 20,000 carefully transcribed dreams, with some contributors documenting theirs for decades—like a woman named Barb, who recorded more than 3,000 of her own. Anyone can search the archive for how often a word appears—"anger" or "flying," for instance—and see how many times it occurs in a blind person's dreams compared to a war veteran's. It's a strange and quietly moving tool that lets us glimpse what the unconscious mind—the inner life we don't consciously perceive—actually looks like.
