飛到洛杉磯錄音的《平凡》

來源：FOND/SOUND

一九九一年，台灣音樂人黃韻玲做了一件很少人敢做的事：她堅持飛到美國洛杉磯，在錄過許多西方名團的錄音室，完成她的第四張專輯《平凡》。

這張唱片不急不徐，融合了爵士（一種輕鬆的西方音樂）和巴西的慢歌風味，配上她冷靜的歌聲，像午後的陽光。在那個大家追求熱鬧的年代，它安靜卻難忘。

In 1991, Taiwanese musician Kay Huang did something few dared to attempt: she insisted on flying to Los Angeles, to a recording studio that had worked with many renowned Western bands, to complete her fourth album, "Ordinary."

The record unfolds without rush, blending jazz — a relaxed form of Western music — with Brazilian slow-song sensibilities, layered beneath her composed voice, like afternoon sunlight. In an era when everyone chased excitement, it stood quiet yet unforgettable.

每年長出新土地的海島

來源：The Crowded Planet

芬蘭唯一列入世界自然遺產的地方，是瓦薩附近的克瓦肯群島。它入選不是因為風景，而是因為這裡的土地每年還在慢慢升高。

很久以前，三公里厚的大冰層壓著這塊地，冰融化後，地面慢慢彈回來。每年大約長出一百五十個足球場那麼大的新土地。三月海面結冰，你可以走到百年前還不存在的小島上。

Finland's only UNESCO World Natural Heritage site is the Kvarken archipelago near Vaasa. It was selected not for its scenery, but because the land here continues to rise slowly each year.

Long ago, a three-kilometer-thick ice sheet pressed down on this region. After the ice melted, the ground began to slowly rebound. Approximately one hundred fifty football fields' worth of new land emerge each year. When the sea freezes in March, you can walk out to islands that didn't exist a century ago.

在林肯公園找出五十種能吃的植物

來源：do312 (Block Club Chicago Events)

七月十八日，芝加哥唯一的全職採食專家大衛會帶大家在林肯公園的北塘邊走兩個小時。他保證，大家至少能認出五十種就長在城市裡、可以吃的植物。

大衛以前替芝加哥頂級餐廳尋找特別的食材，後來改帶民眾導覽。夏天還能看到野生的菇類。早上十點在北塘餐廳前集合，一人四十美元。

On July 18th, David, Chicago's only full-time foraging expert, will lead a two-hour walk along the north pond at Lincoln Park. He guarantees that participants will identify at least fifty edible plants growing right here in the city.

David previously sourced specialty ingredients for Chicago's top restaurants, but has since shifted to leading public tours. Summer brings wild mushrooms into view. The group meets at 10 a.m. in front of the North Pond restaurant; admission is forty dollars per person.

一張會穿越時間的世界地圖

來源：OpenHistoricalMap

有一群愛好歷史的人，合力做了一個很特別的網站，叫做 OpenHistoricalMap。任何人都可以替街道、國界和城市標上開始和結束的年代。

這張大地圖從六千年前一直畫到今天。你只要拉動時間的滑桿，就能看著古羅馬的道路一條一條出現，看城市和商路隨著時代慢慢改變。

A group of history enthusiasts came together to create a remarkable website called OpenHistoricalMap. Anyone can label streets, borders, and cities with their start and end dates.

This vast map stretches back six thousand years to the present day. By dragging a time slider, you can watch the roads of ancient Rome appear one by one, and see cities and trade routes transform gradually across the centuries.
