自己作主的聲音：黃韻玲《平凡》

來源：FOND/SOUND

一九九一年，黃韻玲飛到紐約，錄下專輯《平凡》。這是她第一張完全由自己製作的唱片。那個年代，台灣很少有女生能自己寫歌、編曲，又當製作人。

專輯裡的〈關不掉的收音機〉帶著森巴（一種巴西舞曲）的節奏，輕快又聰明，成了台灣流行音樂的經典。到今天，許多樂迷還說：那是黃韻玲真正長大的一張唱片。

In 1991, Kay Huang flew to New York and recorded her album "Ordinary." It was the first record she had fully produced herself. In that era, Taiwan had very few women who could write songs, arrange music, and work as a producer all at once. The track "The Radio You Can't Turn Off" carries a samba rhythm—light and clever—and became a classic of Taiwan's pop music. To this day, many fans still say it was the album where Kay Huang truly came of age.

空氣吉他世界冠軍：芬蘭奧盧的夏天

來源：Air Guitar World Championships

芬蘭北部的奧盧市，從一九九六年起，每年八月舉辦「空氣吉他世界大賽」。參賽的人手上沒有樂器，只能對著空氣彈六十秒，評審看的是「空氣感」：技術、台風和創意。

大賽的口號是「製造空氣，不要戰爭」。他們相信，大家一起彈空氣吉他，戰爭就會停下來。二〇二六年是第三十屆，十三個國家的高手同場比賽，芬蘭選手拉烏提歐連續第二年奪冠。

Every August since 1996, the city of Oulu in northern Finland has hosted the Air Guitar World Championships. Competitors perform for sixty seconds with no actual instrument in hand, simply mimicking the motions in thin air. Judges evaluate their "air guitar sense"—technique, stage presence, and creativity. The competition's motto is "Make Air, Not War," reflecting a heartfelt belief that when everyone plays air guitar together, war will stop. 2026 marks the thirtieth edition, with elite performers from thirteen countries competing. Finnish competitor Rautio claimed the title for the second consecutive year.

芝加哥地下室裡的布袋戲

來源：Chicago International Puppet Theater Festival

九月十四日開始，芝加哥國際偶戲節開了一門六週的晚間課。老師威廉斯帶大家從零開始，親手做出自己的手偶。

課裡有歐洲手套偶戲的技巧，也有台灣布袋戲的手法。布袋戲流傳了好幾百年，一隻手就能讓小小的戲偶點頭、轉身、打架。每週演一段新戲，最後一堂表演給同學和朋友看。不用任何經驗。

Starting September 14th, the Chicago International Puppet Theater Festival is offering a six-week evening class. Teacher Williams guides you from scratch in creating your own hand puppets. The curriculum blends European hand puppet techniques with methods from Taiwanese glove puppet theater (Bu Dai Xi). Glove puppetry has been performed for hundreds of years—with one hand, you can make a tiny puppet nod, turn, and fight. Each week you'll perform a new scene, and in the final class, you'll showcase your work to classmates and friends. No experience required.

五百種玩法：一個人守了三十年的紙牌圖書館

來源：Pagat.com

一九九五年，英國數學家麥克勞架了一個小網站 pagat.com，只想記下自己知道的紙牌遊戲規則。

三十年後，這裡收了全世界五百多種紙牌遊戲的規則，都是他一個人整理的。他寫信給各國的玩家，一條一條問清楚。

網站的樣子還停在九〇年代，簡單的白底黑字。這是故意的：沒有廣告，不追蹤你，打開很快，找東西也快。

In 1995, British mathematician McLeod built a small website, pagat.com, simply to document the card game rules he knew. Thirty years later, it holds rules for over five hundred card games from around the world—all organized by him alone. He wrote to players in different countries, asking them question by question to get every detail right. The website still looks like it's from the '90s: plain white background, black text. Intentional. No ads. Doesn't track you. Loads fast. Finding things is fast.
