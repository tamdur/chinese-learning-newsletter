絲竹空的《紙鷹》：二胡遇上爵士樂

來源：All About Jazz

「絲竹空」這個名字，來自中國古老的室內樂：絲弦、竹管和空間。樂團由鋼琴家彭郁雯在二零零五年於台北成立。

二零零九年的專輯《紙鷹》裡，吳政君拉二胡，陳弢（音同逃）彈阮琴和柳琴，配上爵士樂的節奏。樂評人說，這些旋律帶著早期移民離鄉來到台灣的悲傷與希望，聽起來既是中國的，也完全是爵士的。

The name "Silk and Bamboo Space" comes from China's ancient chamber music tradition: silk strings, bamboo pipes, and space. The ensemble was founded by pianist Peng Yuwen in Taipei in 2005.

On their 2009 album "Paper Eagle," Wu Zhengjun plays the erhu, while Chen Tao plucks the ruan and liuqin, all set against the rhythms of jazz. Music critics noted that these melodies carry the sorrow and hope of early migrants leaving home to come to Taiwan—sounding at once authentically Chinese and thoroughly jazz.

塞馬湖的雪窩小海豹

來源：thisisFINLAND

芬蘭的塞馬湖住著世界上最稀有的動物之一：塞馬環斑海豹。全世界只剩大約五百隻，而且只生活在這一座淡水湖裡。

三月正是灰色小海豹出生的時候，牠們在冰上的雪洞裡誕生。當地義工整個冬天都在堆雪，為海豹媽媽做出舒服的產房。從薩翁林納出發的觀察之旅，正好在蜜月的季節。

Finland's Lake Saimaa is home to one of the world's rarest animals: the Saimaa ringed seal. Only about five hundred remain in the entire world, and they live nowhere else but in this single freshwater lake.

March is when the gray seal pups are born, arriving in snow dens carved into the ice. Local volunteers spend the entire winter building snow to create comfortable birthing chambers for the mother seals. Observation tours departing from Savonlinna hit their sweet spot right at the height of this season.

芝加哥街區海報藝術家開講

來源：Block Club Chicago

四月十八日下午一點到兩點，哈洛德華盛頓圖書館將舉辦一場免費講座，主角是海報畫家史蒂夫·沙納布魯克。

他已經為芝加哥街區畫了將近八十張海報，風格像一九四零年代的國家公園旅遊畫。他也會介紹自己從路牌字體設計出來的新字型。需要先報名，座位不多了。

On April 18th from 1 to 2 p.m., the Harold Washington Library will host a free lecture featuring poster artist Steve Shanabruch.

He has painted nearly eighty posters throughout Chicago neighborhoods, working in a style reminiscent of 1940s national park travel posters. He will also introduce a new typeface he developed based on road sign lettering. Registration is required, and seats are filling up quickly.

一個人的顏料大百科

來源：artistpigments.org

網站artistpigments.org是某個人利用空閒時間做的。他整理了七萬八千多種顏料，來自一千三百多個品牌，包括水彩、油畫等三十四種。

他還親手做了四百多張色卡，放在窗邊半年，測試顏色會不會退色。你可以按色調、透明度來搜尋。這不像商店目錄，比較像一個人一生的熱愛。

The website artistpigments.org is a passion project built by one person in their spare time. He has catalogued over 78,000 pigments sourced from more than 1,300 brands, covering 34 different types including watercolors and oils.

He personally created over 400 color swatches and left them by a window for six months to test whether the colors would fade. You can search by hue and transparency. It doesn't feel like a store catalog—it feels like a lifetime of devotion.
