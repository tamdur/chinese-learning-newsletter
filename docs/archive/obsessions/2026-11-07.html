被偷走的歌聲：郭英男的〈老人飲酒歌〉

來源：The News Lens 關鍵評論網

一九八八年，台東馬蘭部落的阿美族老人郭英男，和太太郭秀珠一起去法國表演。他們不用樂器，好幾個聲音一高一低，疊在一起唱。

這段歌聲被人錄下來做成唱片，卻沒有寫上他們的名字。一九九三年，樂團「謎」把它放進名曲〈Return to Innocence〉，一九九六年亞特蘭大奧運也用了這首歌。郭英男在電視上聽到，才認出那是自己的聲音。

經過三年的官司（在法院爭對錯），後來的唱片終於寫上：這是阿美族的傳統歌謠。

In 1988, Guo Yingnan, an elderly Amis man from the Malan tribe in Taitung, and his wife, Guo Xiuzhu, traveled to France to perform. They used no instruments. Several voices, some high and some low, layered over one another as they sang.

The recording was later pressed onto a record, but it carried none of their names. In 1993, the group Enigma put the piece into their hit "Return to Innocence," and in 1996 the Atlanta Olympics used the song as well. Guo Yingnan heard it on television and recognized that it was his own voice.

After three years of litigation, a court battle over who was in the right, a later release finally credited the recording: it is a traditional Amis folk song.

森林裡的魚麵包：芬蘭卡拉庫柯

來源：Wikipedia / Atlas Obscura

在芬蘭湖泊很多的薩沃地區，人們會把一條條完整的小魚，包進厚厚的黑麥麵皮裡，再用小火慢慢烤，最久要烤八個小時。

烤這麼久，連魚骨頭都變軟，可以直接吃下去。以前，砍樹的工人和漁夫會帶著它走進森林，好幾天都不會壞，就像古時候的便當。

三月去庫奧皮奧時，別忘了在市場找找這個圓圓的魚麵包。

In Savo, a Finnish region full of lakes, people wrap whole small fish in thick rye dough and bake them slowly over low heat for up to eight hours.

Baked this long, even the fish bones turn soft enough to eat. In the past, lumberjacks and fishermen carried them into the forest, where they kept for days, much like a packed lunch in ancient times.

When you visit Kuopio in March, don't forget to look for these round fish loaves at the market.

在資料庫裡找祖先：諾斯布魯克的家譜講座

來源：Jewish Genealogical Society of Illinois

你知道曾曾祖父叫什麼名字嗎？十一月十五日星期日下午兩點到四點，伊利諾州猶太家譜學會（研究家族歷史的團體）在諾斯布魯克的貝斯艾爾會堂舉辦免費講座，也可以在家用 Zoom 參加。

講者 Robinn Magid 是 JRI-Poland 網站的會長。這個網站由志工一筆一筆整理，收集了舊波蘭土地上猶太人的出生、結婚和死亡紀錄。

她會教大家，怎麼在這個很少人知道的資料庫裡，找到自己的家人。

Do you know the name of your great-great-grandfather? On Sunday, November 15, from 2 to 4 p.m., the Illinois Jewish Genealogical Society (a group that researches family history) is holding a free lecture at Beth El Congregation in Northbrook. You can also join on Zoom from home.

The speaker, Robinn Magid, is president of the JRI-Poland website. The site is run by volunteers who have patiently gathered birth, marriage, and death records of Jews in the former Polish lands, one record at a time.

She will show everyone how to find their own family in this database, which few people know about.

帆船上的永遠軟體：百兔工作室

來源：100r.co

「百兔」（Hundred Rabbits）只有兩個人：Devine Lu Linvega 和 Rekka Bell。他們住在一艘叫 Pino 的帆船上，在海上做電腦工具。

海上常常缺電，也沒有網路。有一次，修圖軟體 Photoshop 一定要連上網路檢查，可是他們在大海中間，根本連不上。於是他們決定自己寫工具。

他們做出 Uxn，一台只有三十二個指令（給電腦的命令）的小小虛擬電腦（只活在程式裡的電腦）。他們希望它像石頭一樣不會變，很多年後還能打開來用。

Hundred Rabbits is a two-person team: Devine Lu Linvega and Rekka Bell. They live aboard a sailboat called Pino and make computer tools while at sea.

At sea, power is often scarce and there is no internet. Once, the photo-editing software Photoshop needed an internet connection to check its license, but they were in the middle of the ocean and simply could not get online. So they decided to write their own tools.

They built Uxn, a tiny virtual computer (a computer that exists only inside software) with just thirty-two instructions (commands given to a computer). They hoped it would be like a stone, unchanging, so that it could still be opened and used many years later.
