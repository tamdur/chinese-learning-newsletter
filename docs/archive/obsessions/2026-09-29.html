用電子琴畫一座山

來源：Discogs Digs

1988年，台灣作曲家陳世興一口氣做了四張專輯：《空》、《山》、《靈》、《雨》。這是一家新唱片公司的第一步。他們說，台灣需要比流行歌更細、比古典音樂更放鬆的聲音。

《山》只用了一台家用電子琴、一台取樣機（把聲音錄下來再彈出來的機器）和一把電吉他。有的歌像風吹過竹笛，有的是安靜的吉他，聽起來一點也不舊。

四張專輯後來合成《空山靈雨》，借用了唐朝詩人王維「空山新雨後」的詩意。

In 1988, Taiwanese composer Chen Shixing released four albums in rapid succession: Empty, Mountain, Spirit, and Rain. It was a new record label's debut. They believed Taiwan needed a sound more nuanced than pop music yet more relaxed than classical. Mountain was made with just a home synthesizer, a sampler (a device that records sounds and plays them back), and an electric guitar. Some songs evoke wind whistling through bamboo flutes; others feature quiet guitar—nothing about them sounds dated. The four albums were later compiled as Empty Mountain, Spirit, and Rain, drawing on the poetic essence of Tang Dynasty poet Wang Wei's line "After new rain in the empty mountains."

同一片海，三種過法

來源：Wikivoyage

芬蘭西南邊的群島海，有大約四萬個小島，是世界上島最多的群島。一條250公里長的「群島環路」，用橋和小渡船把島連在一起。

冬天，過海的方法跟著冰改變。海還沒結冰時，坐渡船；冰太厚、船開不動，卻又不夠結實時，改坐冰上快艇（靠大風扇推動、能在冰上和水上滑行的船）；最冷的時候，冰變得很硬，成了「冰路」，汽車可以直接開過海。

三月來這裡，也許一個星期就能試到兩種過法。

Off southwest Finland lies an archipelago with roughly 40,000 islands—the world's most island-dense region. A 250-kilometer "archipelago loop" connects them via bridges and ferries. Winter transforms the crossing. Before the sea freezes, you take the ferry. When ice thickens but remains too fragile for vehicles, you switch to hovercraft—fan-powered boats that glide across both ice and water. When cold peaks and ice solidifies into a true "ice road," cars can drive straight across the sea. Come in March, and you might experience two different crossing methods in a single week.

舊地圖的五十歲生日

來源：Newberry Library

芝加哥地圖學會今年滿五十歲。10月30日到31日，紐伯瑞圖書館（Newberry Library）要辦一場稀有地圖和書的市集來慶祝。

10月31日星期六，早上9點到下午5點，大家都能免費入場。你可以動手做地圖，聽一場愛荷華州地圖歷史的講座，也可以跟著學會最早的會員，看他們最愛的地圖。

美國各地的店家還會帶來幾百年前的地圖和地圖集，讓你親手翻看。

The Chicago Map Society is turning fifty this year. From October 30th to 31st, the Newberry Library will host a rare maps and books market to celebrate. On Saturday, October 31st, from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., admission is free for everyone.

You can make your own maps by hand, attend a lecture on the history of Iowa maps, or join the society's earliest members as they share their favorite maps. Dealers from across the United States will also bring maps and atlases from centuries past, allowing you to handle and explore them firsthand.

臥室裡的王國

來源：The Kingdom of Talossa (talossa.com)

1979年12月26日，美國密爾瓦基一個14歲的男孩麥迪森（Robert Ben Madison），在媽媽過世不久後，宣布自己的臥室是一個國家，叫「塔洛薩王國」。這個名字在芬蘭話裡是「在房子裡」。

這不只是小孩的遊戲。他發明了一種語言，有三萬五千個基本字，還寫了憲法（國家最重要的法律）和自己的歷史。到了2023年，全世界還有157位「國民」真的在投票選舉。

它的網站記下了四十多年來的每一次改變，全是一群人因為喜愛而做的。

On December 26, 1979, a 14-year-old boy from Milwaukee named Robert Ben Madison announced that his bedroom was a nation: the Kingdom of Talossa. The name, borrowed from Finnish, means "in the house." But this was far more than a child's game. Madison invented a complete language—35,000 basic words—drafted a constitution, and authored an entire national history. By 2023, the kingdom claimed 157 active citizens worldwide, all of whom participated in real elections. The project's website chronicles more than four decades of continuous development, all driven by a devoted community of creators.
