破地獄醒來了

來源：Far Out Magazine

台北二人樂團「破地獄」成立於2013年。團名來自道教的喪禮（送別死者的儀式），用來救出卡在生與死之間的靈魂。

他們早期做安靜的電子聲音，後來加入重金屬和工業噪音。疫情之後，他們沉默了三年。

今年一月，新專輯《Post Purgatory》在日本發行。八首歌混用台語、國語和英語。樂團說，這張專輯在講時間：它能治好傷口，也能毀掉一切。

Break Hell, a two-person band from Taipei, was formed in 2013. The name comes from a Daoist funeral rite, a ceremony that sends the dead on their way and frees souls stuck between life and death. The band began with quiet electronic sounds and later added heavy metal and industrial noise. After the pandemic, they went silent for three years. In January, their new album, Post Purgatory, came out in Japan. Its eight songs mix Taiwanese Hokkien, Mandarin, and English. The band says the album is about time: it can heal a wound, and it can also destroy everything.

雪地裡的免費小屋

來源：nationalparks.fi

在芬蘭北部的國家公園裡，有五百多間「荒野小屋」。走路或滑雪來的旅人，都可以免費住一兩晚，不用預約。

這些小屋的起源，可以追到十八世紀路邊給旅人休息的地方。今天屋裡的木柴，是用芬蘭人付的稅買的。

這裡有一條沒寫下來的規矩：先到的人要先離開，把位子留給天黑後才趕到、又冷又濕的下一位旅人。

In the national parks of northern Finland, there are more than 500 "wilderness huts." Travelers who arrive on foot or on skis can stay for a night or two for free, without making a reservation. The origins of these huts go back to the 18th century, when roadside shelters gave travelers a place to rest. Today, the firewood inside the huts is paid for with the taxes Finns pay. There is also an unwritten rule: whoever arrives first should be the first to leave, so that the spot is free for the next traveler, the one who reaches the hut after dark, cold and soaked through.

最後一場三天問答大會

來源：Sporcle

問答網站 Sporcle 的年度大會，將在11月13日到15日來到芝加哥郊外的紹姆堡（Schaumburg）。主辦單位說，這是最後一次連辦三天，明年就要改變樣子。

最重要的比賽叫「大腦之戰」，最多六人一隊，像在酒吧玩問答一樣。週末還有好幾場獎金一千美元的對決。

門票七十九美元起，任何愛冷知識的人都能參加。

Sporcle, the trivia website, will hold its annual convention in Schaumburg, a suburb of Chicago, from November 13 to 15. The organizers say this is the last time it will run for three days in a row, and next year the event will look different. The main competition is called the "Brain Battle," with teams of up to six people playing much like pub trivia. The weekend also includes several head-to-head contests with $1,000 prizes. Tickets start at $79, and anyone who loves random facts can join in.

被時間遺忘的唱片與卡帶

來源：Museum of Obsolete Media

英國圖書館員 Jason Curtis 花了快二十年，一個人做出「過時媒體博物館」網站，記錄了八百五十多種被淘汰（不再有人用）的錄音和錄影工具。

最好玩的是很少人聽過的失敗品。1956年，克萊斯勒汽車在車上裝了唱片機，可是路上的坑洞讓唱針一直跳，不到兩年就消失了。

每一種都附上照片，說明它怎麼出生、誰支持它，又為什麼死去。

British librarian Jason Curtis spent nearly twenty years building the "Museum of Obsolete Media" website on his own. It documents more than 850 sound and video recording tools that have been discontinued, meaning nobody uses them anymore. The most entertaining entries are the failures that very few people have ever heard of. In 1956, Chrysler put a record player in its cars, but potholes kept making the needle skip, and within less than two years the idea was gone. Each entry comes with photos that explain how the format was born, who backed it, and why it died.
