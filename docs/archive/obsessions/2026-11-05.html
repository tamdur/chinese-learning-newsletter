一首歌，划過半個地球

來源：Songlines

阿美族歌手 Putad 平常是龐克樂團「漂流出口」的主唱。在 2022 年的專輯《Our Island》裡，她唱起祖先傳下來的古調。

歌曲〈Listwar Zanset〉把她的歌聲，放在馬達加斯加的老樂器和模里西斯的節奏上。這些島嶼說著不同的語言，卻有同一群祖先：大約五千年前，南島民族坐著小船從台灣出發，一路划到非洲外海和復活節島。

這張專輯入圍第三十三屆金曲獎。聽它，就像參加一場跨越大海的家族重聚。

Putad, a singer from the Amis people, normally fronts the punk band 漂流出口 (Piaoliu Chukou). On her 2022 album Our Island, she sings ancestral melodies handed down through her family.

In the song "Listwar Zanset," her voice sits atop old instruments from Madagascar and rhythms from Mauritius. These islands speak different languages, yet they share the same ancestors: about 5,000 years ago, Austronesian peoples set out from Taiwan in small boats and paddled all the way to the waters off Africa and to Easter Island.

The album was nominated for the 33rd Golden Melody Awards. Listening to it feels like attending a family reunion that stretches across the sea.

一步跨過三個國家

來源：Finland Naturally

在芬蘭北方的拉普蘭，靠近小村子基爾皮斯耶爾維，有一座「三國界碑」（標出國界的記號）。芬蘭、瑞典和挪威的國界，就在這裡碰在一起。這裡也是芬蘭本土最西邊的一點。

現在的水泥界碑是 1926 年重建的。繞著它走一小圈，幾步之內就能站在三個國家。

三月時，四周的湖水結成厚冰，可以走路、滑雪或騎雪上摩托車過去。出發前，記得先問清楚冰層和天色。

In Lapland, in northern Finland, near the small village of Kilpisjärvi, there is a "three-country border marker" (a marker that shows where national borders meet). The borders of Finland, Sweden, and Norway come together right here. It is also the westernmost point of mainland Finland.

The concrete marker standing today was rebuilt in 1926. Take a short walk around it, and within a few steps you can stand in three countries.

In March, the lake water around it freezes into thick ice, so you can walk, ski, or ride a snowmobile out to it. Before you set off, be sure to check the ice conditions and the weather.

星期六早上，咖啡、蛋糕和德國電影

來源：DANK Haus

林肯廣場的德國文化中心 DANK Haus，這個月有一個溫暖的小活動。 11 月 14、21 和 28 日的星期六，十點半先喝咖啡、吃自己做的蛋糕，十一點看一部短片，中午再看一部德國長片。

地點在二樓 206 室。會員五美元，一般人七美元。不會說德語也沒關係。這棟百年老房子在 4740 N. Western Ave.，是芝加哥德國移民文化的家。冷天裡，這是很舒服的週末早晨。

The German Cultural Center DANK Haus in Lincoln Square has a warm little event this month. On Saturdays, November 14, 21, and 28, guests start at 10:30 with coffee and homemade cake, then watch a short film at 11:00, and at midday there is a full-length German film.

The event takes place on the second floor, Room 206. Members pay $5 and non-members pay $7. You don't need to speak German. The century-old building at 4740 N. Western Ave. is a home for Chicago's German immigrant culture. On a cold day, it makes for a very pleasant weekend morning.

把鳥的歌寫成樂譜的人

來源：Homerton250 Archive Blog

大衛・辛德利（1933–2018）是英國劍橋大學霍默頓學院的音樂老師。他花了幾十年，把鳥叫的錄音放慢，再一個音一個音寫成樂譜。

他有一首鋼琴曲，來自 1961 年 5 月 14 日錄下的一隻林百靈（一種會唱歌的小鳥）。他還幫電視節目猜出已經滅絕（完全消失）的渡渡鳥叫聲。 1992 年，他用一位毛利老人學鳥叫的錄音，讓紐西蘭垂耳鴉的聲音再次響起。

現在，學院的網上檔案還保存著他的手稿。

David Hindley (1933–2018) taught music at Homerton College, University of Cambridge. For decades he slowed down recordings of birdsong and then transcribed them into sheet music, one note at a time.

He wrote a piano piece based on a woodlark (a songbird) recorded on May 14, 1961. He also helped a television program work out what the dodo, an extinct bird that had vanished completely, sounded like. In 1992, using recordings of a Māori elder who had learned bird calls, he brought the voice of the huia, a New Zealand crow-like bird, back to life on record.

Today, the college's online archive still holds his manuscripts.
