巴奈的夜婆：等了十幾年的台語歌

來源：VERSE

巴奈是台灣原住民歌手。她曾在凱道上住了兩千多天，替原住民爭取土地。可是，她從來沒有用台語唱過歌。

製作人柯智豪從2009年開始寫歌，花了十幾年，才在2023年完成《夜婆》。「夜婆」就是台語的蝙蝠（晚上飛出來的小動物）。牠在夜裡說著一段見不到太陽的愛情。

這張專輯在2024年得到金曲獎最佳台語專輯。

Parnai is a Taiwanese Indigenous singer who spent over 2,000 days camped on Ketagalan Boulevard, fighting for Indigenous land rights. Yet she had never sung in Taiwanese before. Producer Ko Chih-hao began writing songs for her in 2009, and after more than a decade, finally completed "Night Bat" in 2023. "Night Bat" is the Taiwanese word for bat—a creature of the night. In the album, it tells of a love that dwells in darkness, never touched by sunlight. The album won the Golden Melody Award for Best Taiwanese Language Album in 2024.

會幫人呼吸的椅子

來源：Alvar Aalto Foundation

1929年，芬蘭建築師阿爾托設計帕米歐療養院（讓病人休息養病的醫院）。他不只蓋房子，連裡面的椅子也自己設計。

他和木工一起把芬蘭的樺木（一種白色樹皮的樹）蒸軟，彎成兩個圓圈，一根鐵管也不用。椅背的角度能讓肺病病人呼吸得更輕鬆，頭靠的地方還切了小縫，讓空氣流通。

今天，這張椅子還在圖爾庫的工廠用同樣的方法彎出來。

In 1929, Finnish architect Alvar Aalto designed the Paimio Sanatorium, a hospital where patients come to rest and recover. He didn't just design the building — he also designed all the furniture inside it himself. Working with woodworkers, he steamed Finnish birch wood (a tree with distinctive white bark) until it was soft, then bent it into two curved loops without using a single metal rod. The angle of the backrest helps tuberculosis patients breathe more easily, and the headrest area has small slots cut into it to allow air to flow freely. Today, this chair is still being made in the Turku factory using the same technique.

老修車廠裡的笑話與美酒

來源：Fever

芝加哥有一間一百年的老修車廠，現在叫 Chop Shop。10月9日星期五，這裡有《An Idiot's Guide to Wine》的最後兩場表演，晚上六點和八點半。

一位喜劇演員（讓人發笑的表演者）會帶大家喝六種來自世界各地的葡萄酒。一邊喝，一邊聽笑話，七十分鐘就能學會分辨酒的味道，一點也不無聊。

只有二十一歲以上的人才能進場。票只在 Fever 網站上賣。

Chicago has a century-old car repair shop, now called Chop Shop. On Friday, October 9th, it will host the final two performances of "An Idiot's Guide to Wine" at 6 PM and 8:30 PM. A comedian will lead everyone through a tasting of six wines from around the world. While sipping, you'll hear jokes—and in just seventy minutes, you'll learn to distinguish wine flavors in a way that's genuinely entertaining. Only those 21 and older may attend. Tickets are sold exclusively on the Fever website.

刻在石頭上的好品德

來源：Virtues of the Dead (epigraphs.net)

有一個網站叫「Virtues of the Dead」，收集了兩千七百多段英國教堂裡的墓誌銘（刻在墓碑上的話）。每個月還會介紹一座特別的墓。

最特別的是，它不按名字排，而是按照死者被稱讚的品德來分類，像是「聰明」、「和善」、「認真」。

這些石頭也告訴我們誰被記住：在三百多筆記錄裡，貴族有39位，僕人（替人做家事的人）卻只有4位。

There's a website called "Virtues of the Dead" that has collected over 2,700 epitaphs from British churches—the words inscribed on tombstones. Each month it also spotlights a particularly noteworthy grave. What's most striking about it is the arrangement: rather than organizing by name, it groups epitaphs according to the virtues the deceased were praised for—"intelligence," "kindness," "diligence." These stones also reveal who gets remembered: among over 300 records, there are 39 nobles, but only 4 servants (people who worked in households).
