關不掉的收音機：黃韻玲的《平凡》

來源：FOND/SOUND

1991年，黃韻玲不再只為別人寫歌。她飛到美國洛杉磯的錄音室，做出自己的專輯《平凡》。

這張唱片受到英國和巴西音樂的影響，聲音溫柔又時髦（時髦就是很流行、有品味）。最有名的一首歌叫「關不掉的收音機」，唱一台你不聽、它也一直播放的收音機。這樣細緻的聲音，在當時的台灣很少見。

In 1991, Kay Huang stopped writing songs just for others. She flew to a recording studio in Los Angeles and created her own album, "Ordinary." Influenced by British and Brazilian music, the album had a sound that was both gentle and fashionable. The most famous song, "The Radio You Can't Turn Off," depicts a radio that keeps playing whether you're listening or not. Such refined sound was quite rare in Taiwan at that time.

詩人太太的蛋糕：芬蘭小鎮的冬日甜點

來源：Baking Heritage

波爾沃（Porvoo）是芬蘭第二古老的小鎮，街上的招牌同時寫著芬蘭文和瑞典文兩種語言。

這裡是「魯內貝里蛋糕」的故鄉。傳說在1840年代，國家詩人的太太用家裡剩下的材料，做出這個蛋糕。它加了杏仁和一點酒，上面放著一圈紅色的果醬。每年一月，全芬蘭都買得到，最熱鬧的日子是二月五號。

Porvoo is Finland's second-oldest town, where shop signs display both Finnish and Swedish. This charming place is the birthplace of the Runeberg cake. According to legend, in the 1840s, the wife of Finland's national poet created this cake using leftover ingredients from her kitchen. The cake combines almonds and a splash of alcohol, topped with a ring of red jam. Available throughout Finland every January, it sees its biggest rush on February 5th.

免費的福音歌聲：千禧公園的夏夜

來源：City of Chicago DCASE

七月二十四、二十五號兩天，芝加哥的福音音樂節又回到千禧公園了。

今年請來了幾位有名的歌手，像Tasha Cobbs Leonard和Hezekiah Walker。節目也讓本地的年輕人上台，和大人一起唱歌。不用買票，下雨也照樣舉行，大家都可以來聽。

On July 24-25, Chicago's gospel music festival returns to Millennium Park. This year features several renowned singers, including Tasha Cobbs Leonard and Hezekiah Walker. The program also gives local young people the chance to perform alongside established artists. Admission is free, and the festival runs rain or shine — everyone is welcome.

一位英文老師的蘑菇地圖

來源：MushroomExpert.Com

麥可·郭（Michael Kuo）是美國一所大學的英文老師。二十多年來，他一個人做了一個網站，介紹北美三百多種蘑菇。

網站上有他自己拍的照片，也教你怎麼分辨每一種蘑菇。他不是科學家，只是太喜歡蘑菇了。現在連真正的專家寫報告時，都會引用他的網站。

Michael Kuo teaches English at an American university. For more than twenty years, he has single-handedly built a website introducing over three hundred mushroom species across North America. The site features his own photographs and teaches visitors how to identify each type of mushroom. He isn't a scientist—he's simply passionate about mushrooms. Today, even professional experts reference his website when writing their research reports.
