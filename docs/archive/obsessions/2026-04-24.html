透明雜誌：台北地下搖滾的靈魂之聲

來源：Bandcamp / Touming Magazine

二〇一〇年，台北四人樂團「透明雜誌」發行了首張專輯《我們的靈魂樂》，收錄十四首歌。吉他充滿能量，鼓聲飛快，聽起來像美國龐克名團 Fugazi，但又帶著意想不到的放克節奏。

主唱洪申豪用中文寫下青春的焦慮（不安的感覺）和詩一般的畫面，讓這張唱片有了獨特的台灣味道。這張專輯在日本也很受歡迎，發行十年後還推出了透明黑膠（唱片）再版。

In 2010, Taipei four-piece band Transparent Magazine released their debut album Our Soul Music, featuring fourteen tracks. The guitar work bristles with energy, the drums rattle along at breakneck speed, conjuring the spirit of American punk legends Fugazi — but layered with unexpected funk rhythms. Singer Hong Shen-hao writes in Mandarin about the anxieties of youth and paints scenes with poetic clarity, giving the record a distinctly Taiwanese character. The album found an enthusiastic audience in Japan as well, earning a vinyl reissue a decade after its initial release.

焦油之路：芬蘭奧盧的航海記憶

來源：Oulu2026 (European Capital of Culture)

十八世紀時，芬蘭北部的小城奧盧（Oulu）是全世界最大的松焦油（松樹提煉的黑色油）出口地。內陸森林裡的焦油，靠小船沿河運到港口，再送到世界各地保護木造船身。

現在奧盧是二〇二六年歐洲文化之都，推出了「焦油故事」步行路線，沿途十個地點都有 QR 碼，掃描後可以聽到作家武歐里（Katariina Vuori）寫的歷史故事。三月來訪，還能感受冬季海岸的寧靜氣氛。

In the 18th century, Oulu, a small city in northern Finland, was the world's largest exporter of pine tar — a black oil derived from pine trees. Tar from inland forests was transported downriver by small boats to the port, then shipped around the world to protect wooden ship hulls. Today, Oulu is Europe's Capital of Culture for 2026 and has launched the "Tar Stories" walking route, with ten locations along the way, each marked with a QR code that plays historical narratives written by author Katariina Vuori. A March visit also offers the chance to experience the quiet atmosphere of the winter coast.

五月清晨，去蒙特羅斯看候鳥過境

來源：Chicago Ornithological Society

五月一日早上七點半，芝加哥鳥類學會將在蒙特羅斯角（Montrose Point）帶領一場春季候鳥散步。這裡有一條叫做「魔法樹籬」的矮樹帶，只有一百多公尺長，卻記錄過三百多種鳥類。

春天時，色彩鮮豔的林鶯（一種小型候鳥）會在這裡停留休息，離你近到伸手可及。散步約兩英里，到十一點結束，所有程度都歡迎，但需要事先報名。

On the morning of May 1st at 7:30 a.m., the Chicago Ornithological Society will lead a spring migratory bird walk at Montrose Point. There's a shrub corridor here called the "Magic Hedge" — just over a hundred meters long — that has recorded more than three hundred bird species. In spring, vibrantly colored warblers stop to rest here, close enough that you could almost reach out and touch them. The walk covers about two miles and wraps up by 11 a.m. All experience levels are welcome, but advance registration is required.

一個人的古地圖百科：四十五年的熱愛

來源：Cartographic Images (myoldmaps.com)

吉姆·席博德（Jim Siebold）花了四十五年，一個人建起了一部古地圖百科全書。網站收錄了四百七十多篇研究文章，從兩千五百年前巴比倫的泥板地圖，到文藝復興時期的木刻印刷地圖，全部超過一萬一千頁。

每篇文章都是詳細的 PDF，記錄了製圖者、年代和學術評論。席博德說，他從來沒遇過另一個和他一樣痴迷（非常喜歡）古地圖的人。這個網站從一九九四年就默默上線了，等著有緣人來發現。

Jim Siebold spent forty-five years building a comprehensive encyclopedia of ancient maps single-handedly. His website contains over 470 research articles spanning from Babylonian clay tablet maps from 2,500 years ago to Renaissance woodcut printed maps — more than 11,000 pages in total. Each article is a detailed PDF documenting the cartographer, era, and scholarly commentary. Siebold says he's never met another person who shares his obsession with ancient maps. The website has been quietly online since 1994, waiting for kindred spirits to discover it.
