陳珊妮：打破規則的音樂才女

來源：Rate Your Music

二〇〇四年十月，陳珊妮在小小的獨立廠牌「五四三音樂站」發行了專輯《後來，我們都哭了》。這張專輯表面聽起來像精緻的藝術流行樂，但裡面藏著澀谷系（日本的一種混合風格）和電子音樂的聲音。

二〇〇五年，她在第十六屆金曲獎同時拿下最佳國語專輯和最佳專輯製作人兩個大獎，成為第一位得到製作人獎的女性。她證明了：台灣的小廠牌也能做出改變歷史的音樂。

In October 2004, Chen Shan-ni released the album "Later, We All Cried" on the tiny independent label "543 Music Station." On the surface, the album sounds like refined art pop, but it conceals the sounds of Shibuya-kei—a Japanese hybrid genre—and electronic music. In 2005, she won both Best Mandarin Album and Best Album Producer at the 16th Golden Melody Awards, becoming the first woman to receive the Producer Award. She proved that Taiwan's small independent labels could create music that changes history.

走在結冰的海上：赫爾辛基冬季群島之旅

來源：Visit Finland

每年冬天，芬蘭灣的海水會結成厚厚的冰。這時候，赫爾辛基有專門的導覽（帶路的人）帶小組出發，穿上全身防寒衣，搭氣墊船越過碎冰，再走上冰凍的海面，前往無人居住的小島。

三月是最好的時間：白天越來越長，冰還夠厚。最後，大家在冰上生火烤肉，享受這片安靜的白色世界。

Every winter, the waters of the Bay of Finland freeze into thick sheets of ice. During this season, Helsinki offers guided tours where small groups don their full-body thermal suits and board inflatable boats to traverse the broken ice fields, eventually stepping out onto the frozen sea to reach uninhabited islands. March is the ideal time: the days grow longer while the ice remains thick enough to safely support visitors. The experience culminates with a bonfire on the ice where everyone grills meat and savors the serene white landscape.

原住民的故事：免費短片影展在芝加哥放映

來源：Evanston RoundTable

五月二十九日到三十日，「原住民影像節」將在埃文斯頓公共圖書館免費放映一百零二分鐘的短片。所有影片都由美國原住民導演拍攝，包括尤皮克族和梅蒂斯族等社群的作品。

同一批影片也會在 Logan Square 的 Comfort Station 放映，歡迎所有人參加。

From May 29 to 30, the "Indigenous Film Festival" will screen 102 minutes of short films free of charge at the Evanston Public Library. All films are directed by Native American filmmakers, including works from communities such as the Yupik and Métis peoples. The same collection of films will also be screened at Comfort Station in Logan Square, and everyone is welcome to attend.

一個人的計算機博物館

來源：The Old Calculator Web Museum

在口袋計算機出現之前，人們用的是桌上型電子計算機，有些跟打字機一樣大。美國工程師 Rick Bensene 花了四十多年收集和修復這些機器，並把它們放上網站。

這個網站從一九九七年開始運作，是網路上最老的計算機博物館。每一台機器都有照片、電路圖和修復筆記，記錄了一段快要被忘記的科技歷史。

Before pocket calculators appeared, people used desktop electronic calculators, some as large as typewriters. American engineer Rick Bensene has spent over forty years collecting and restoring these machines, and putting them online. His website, which began operating in 1997, is the oldest computer museum on the internet. Each machine has photographs, circuit diagrams, and repair notes that document a piece of technological history that's in danger of being forgotten.
