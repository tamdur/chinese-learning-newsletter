林強飛到倫敦，做出一張太早的唱片

來源：Bandcamp Daily

一九九三年，台灣的台語搖滾歌手林強飛到英國倫敦，找來幫過很多名團的製作人，做出唱片《娛樂世界》。

他把台灣民謠和電子音樂混在一起，聲音太新奇，當時幾乎沒有人買。但現在大家都說，這是九零年代台灣最重要的音樂之一。後來林強幫電影配樂，還用《刺客聶隱娘》得到坎城影展的大獎。

In 1993, Taiwanese Hokkien rock singer Lim Giong flew to London and worked with a producer who had collaborated with many renowned bands to create the album "Entertainment World." He blended Taiwanese folk music with electronic sounds, and the result was so novel that hardly anyone bought it at the time. But now everyone agrees it was one of the most important pieces of Taiwanese music from the 1990s. Later, Lim Giong went on to compose film scores, and won a major award at the Cannes Film Festival for his work on "The Assassin."

爺爺和孫子相隔五十八年蓋出的木頭教堂

來源：Finnish Architecture Navigator

一七六三年，芬蘭中部一位農夫木匠（會做木工的人），用一根根松木蓋出了佩泰耶韋西老教堂。

教堂全用木頭，裡面卻學了歐洲石頭大教堂的樣子。五十八年後，他的孫子才把鐘樓加上去。一九九四年它成為世界遺產。三月時教堂只能先預約才進得去，兩個人幾乎可以包場慢慢看。

In 1763, a farmer-carpenter in central Finland built the Petäjävesi Old Church using pine logs, one after another. The church was constructed entirely of wood, yet its interior design mimicked the style of Europe's grand stone cathedrals. Fifty-eight years later, his grandson finally added the bell tower. In 1994 it became a UNESCO World Heritage site. In March, the church can only be entered by reservation, and with just two visitors, you can practically have the place to yourself for a leisurely tour.

滿月的海灘：火舞、鼓聲和夕陽

來源：Chicago Full Moon Jam

六月二十九日，芝加哥的「滿月慶典」回到福斯特海灘，今年已經是第二十二年了。

活動在太陽下山前開始，一直到晚上十點。舞者轉動著火圈，鼓手對著大湖打鼓。這是免費的，由志工舉辦，你也可以帶自己的樂器一起敲。結束後大家把海灘收得乾乾淨淨。

On June 29th, Chicago's Full Moon Jam returns to Foster Beach for its twenty-second year. The event runs from sunset until 10 p.m. Fire dancers spin flaming hoops while drummers beat rhythms toward Lake Michigan. It's free, volunteer-run, and you're welcome to bring your own instrument to join in. When it's over, everyone leaves the beach spotless.

一個人花三十年，整理五萬首老曲子

來源：Traditional Tune Archive

一九九六年，一位叫安德魯的人開始把傳統小提琴曲子一首一首記下來。

將近三十年後，這個網站「傳統曲調檔案館」已經收了五萬多首愛爾蘭、英國和北美的民謠。每一首不只有樂譜，還告訴你它最早出現在哪裡、有哪些不同的名字。直到今天，還有很多樂手在用它。

In 1996, a man named Andrew began recording traditional fiddle tunes one by one. Nearly thirty years later, his website, the "Traditional Tune Archive," has collected more than fifty thousand folk melodies from Ireland, England, and North America. Each tune includes not just sheet music, but also information about where it first appeared and what alternative names it goes by. To this day, many musicians continue to use it.
