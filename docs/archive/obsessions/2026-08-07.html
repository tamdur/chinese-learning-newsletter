兩套鼓的雷聲：落差草原WWWW的《盤》

來源：Bandcamp (Prairie WWWW / 落差草原WWWW)

落差草原WWWW是二零一零年在雲林成立的實驗樂團。早期的專輯《泥土》裡，他們只用不插電的樂器，還把野外錄到的風聲和蟲聲放進歌中。

二零一八年的《盤》完全變了樣。兩套鼓同時敲打，節拍故意不整齊，再加上電子合成器，把生活裡的雜音也當成音樂的材料。〈樹靈〉這首歌還留著萬物有靈（相信山和樹都有生命）的想像，聲音卻已經全面用電。團員說，這就是亞熱帶島嶼的脈搏。

Prairie WWWW is an experimental band founded in Yunlin in 2010. On their early album Ní-Thôo (Soil), they used only acoustic instruments and wove field recordings of wind and insect sounds into their songs.

Their 2018 album Pán marks a complete departure. Two drum kits pound simultaneously, their rhythms deliberately misaligned, while electronic synthesizers transform everyday sounds into compositional material. The track "Tree Deity" still carries animistic imagery—the belief that mountains and trees possess life—yet its soundscape has become entirely electronic. The band calls this the pulse of a subtropical island.

為了呼吸而設計的椅子

來源：Paimio Sanatorium

一九二九年，三十歲的芬蘭建築師阿爾托贏得比賽，在小鎮帕伊米奧蓋一座有一百八十四張病床的肺結核療養院。他把整棟房子當成一件「醫療器具」。

洗手台被設計成不會發出水聲，免得吵醒旁邊的病人。牆的顏色和燈光，是為了躺上好幾個月的眼睛而挑的。那張彎曲木片做的椅子，椅背角度經過計算，能打開病人的呼吸道，讓咳嗽輕鬆一些。

這裡現在是世界遺產，全年開放參觀，也能住上一晚。離土庫市約三十分鐘車程。

In 1929, Finnish architect Alvar Aalto, then thirty years old, won a competition to design a tuberculosis sanatorium with 184 beds in the small town of Paimio. He conceived the entire building as a single "medical device."

The sinks were designed to run silently, so as not to wake patients in adjacent beds. The wall colors and lighting were carefully chosen for eyes that would spend months lying in bed. The curved plywood chair had a backrest angled to open the patient's airways and make coughing easier.

Today it is a UNESCO World Heritage site, open year-round for visitors, and guests can stay overnight. It is about thirty minutes by car from Turku.

洗衣店後面的紙牌情人

來源：Chicago Magic Lounge

芝加哥北克拉克街五零五零號，門面看起來只是一家自助洗衣店。推開暗門，裡面藏著一間魔術俱樂部。

到八月二十六日為止，每個星期三晚上七點，捷克紙牌高手普謝尼奇卡在這裡演出《五十二個情人》，整場表演只用一副撲克牌。八月十二日和十九日都還有場次。開演前，店裡的魔術師會走到桌邊，在你眼前變近距離魔術。票價四十四美元起，第一排五十美元。

At 5050 North Clark Street in Chicago, the storefront looks like just a self-service laundromat. Push open the hidden door, and inside is a magic club.

Through August 26, every Wednesday at 7 p.m., Czech card master Ondrej Psenicka performs "52 Lovers" here, with the entire show using only a single deck of playing cards. There are still shows on August 12 and 19. Before each performance, the club's resident magicians come to your table and perform close-up magic right before your eyes. Tickets start at $44, with front row seats at $50.

你在看，數字才會走

來源：Rise Up Labs

打開這個網頁，十個數字同時開始跳動：出生的嬰兒、賺到的錢、被播放的歌、排出的二氧化碳；另一邊是死亡人數、消失的物種和軍事花費。這些數字都來自聯合國、全球碳預算等真實統計。

最特別的是，你一離開分頁，數字就停住；回來才又開始跑。它們只因為你在看才存在。這個網頁出自一個兩人工作室，還有〈天空〉、〈距離〉等姊妹作品。

Open this webpage and ten numbers start ticking simultaneously: babies born, money earned, songs played, carbon dioxide emitted—and on the other side, death counts, disappearing species, military spending. All these figures come from real data: the United Nations, the Global Carbon Budget, and similar sources.

What's most striking is this twist: the moment you leave the tab, the numbers freeze; they resume only when you return. They exist only because you're watching. This webpage comes from a two-person studio that also has sister projects like "Sky" and "Distance."
