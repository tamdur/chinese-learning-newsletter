藏了四十年的歌聲：鄧麗君最後的回聲

來源：The Conversation

日本東京，一位唱片公司的工程師整理作曲家宮城隆的舊錄音帶時，發現一卷帶子裡藏著鄧麗君從沒發表過的歌聲，大約錄於一九八〇年代中期。

那個年代空白錄音帶很貴，舊帶子幾乎都會被錄掉，所以這段歌聲能留下來，非常難得。作詞家荒木豐久替它取名「情歌最愛夜霧時」，二〇二五年六月發行，正好是鄧麗君過世三十年。

冷戰時期，她的歌曾經偷偷傳進海峽對岸的收音機裡。三十年後，她又唱了一次。

In Tokyo, a record-company engineer sorting through composer Miyagi Takashi's old master tapes found an unreleased Teresa Teng vocal hidden on one of the reels, recorded some time in the mid-1980s. Blank tape was expensive in those days and old reels were almost always recorded over, so the survival of this performance is extraordinarily rare. The lyricist Araki Toyohisa gave it the title "Love Songs Are Best in the Foggy Night." It was released in June 2025, exactly thirty years after Teng's death. During the Cold War her songs were once smuggled onto radios on the other side of the strait. Thirty years on, she sings once more.

一條腰帶，說出你的家鄉

來源：Craft Museum of Finland

芬蘭中部的于韋斯屈萊有一座手工藝博物館，館裡的「民族服裝中心」，是全國研究傳統服裝最可靠的地方。

他們說，這套衣服真正的招牌不是裙子，而是腰上那條織帶。織法叫「板織」，在芬蘭已經有三千年歷史。每個地方的花紋、顏色和長度都不一樣，內行人看一眼腰帶，就能說出你家在哪個村子。

從赫爾辛基坐火車過去大約三小時，正好順路走進芬蘭的湖區。

In Jyväskylä, in central Finland, there is a craft museum whose National Costume Center is the country's most authoritative source on traditional dress. The real signature of the costume, they say, is not the skirt but the woven belt at the waist. The technique, called tablet weaving, goes back three thousand years in Finland. The patterns, colours and lengths differ from place to place, and someone who knows what to look for can glance at your belt and name the village you come from. It is about three hours by train from Helsinki — a trip that takes you straight into Finland's lake district.

草原大道：走進芝加哥消失的豪宅年代

來源：Glessner House

一百多年前，芝加哥十六街到二十二街之間，短短六個街區蓋了將近九十棟豪宅。百貨大王馬歇爾．菲爾德、肉品大亨阿默、火車車廂大王普爾曼，都住在這條草原大道上。

九月十三日星期日下午兩點到四點，歷史學家威廉．泰爾會帶大家走完這六個街區，還會走進第二長老教會，看裡面的彩色玻璃窗。

只收十五個人，票價三十元。

More than a century ago, in the six short blocks between 16th and 22nd Streets, Chicago built nearly ninety mansions. The department-store magnate Marshall Field, the meatpacking tycoon Philip Armour, and the railway-carriage king George Pullman all lived on Prairie Avenue. On Sunday, September 13th, from 2 to 4 p.m., the historian William Tyre will walk visitors through all six blocks and into the Second Presbyterian Church to see its stained-glass windows. Only fifteen places; tickets are $30.

一個人，十四年，五萬首老曲子

來源：The Traditional Tune Archive

一九九六年，美國小提琴手安德魯．昆茲開始做一件傻事：把他找得到的每一首愛爾蘭、蘇格蘭和北美傳統舞曲，一首一首寫下來。曲子的來歷、別名、老故事，全都記上。

他做了十四年。後來一個人做不完，就和夥伴把它改成網站「傳統曲調檔案館」，現在收了超過五萬首曲子，免費給大家看。

輸入一個老曲名，你會看到它三百年來走過的路。

In 1996 the American fiddler Andrew Kuntz set out on a quietly absurd project: to write down, one by one, every Irish, Scottish and North American traditional dance tune he could find — where each came from, what else it was called, the old stories attached to it. He kept at it for fourteen years. When it grew past what one person could manage, he and a collaborator turned it into a website, the Traditional Tune Archive, which now holds more than fifty thousand tunes and is free to everyone. Type in the name of an old tune and you can see the road it has travelled over three hundred years.
