郵寄而生的噪音王國

來源：數位荒原 Heath

一九九三年，聲音藝術家王福瑞在台北成立了「NOISE」，這是台灣第一個實驗音樂品牌。那時島上幾乎沒有人聽這種音樂。

沒有市場，他就靠寄信交朋友，把自己做的卡帶（錄音帶）和小刊物，寄給歐洲、美國、日本的音樂人，大家互相交換作品。

他的「Noisenet」合輯連起世界各地幾百位噪音創作者，也為台北今天熱鬧的實驗聲音場景打下了根。

In 1993, sound artist Wang Fu-jui founded "NOISE" in Taipei—Taiwan's first experimental music label. At the time, hardly anyone on the island listened to this kind of music. With no market to build on, he cultivated friendships through the mail, sending cassettes and small magazines he produced to musicians across Europe, America, and Japan, exchanging work with one another. His "Noisenet" compilation brought together hundreds of noise artists worldwide and laid the groundwork for the vibrant experimental sound scene that Taipei boasts today.

在森林裡跳舞的憂傷探戈

來源：Tangomarkkinat / Yle News

探戈這種舞，很多人以為只屬於南美洲。可是在遙遠的芬蘭，它也成了國家的象徵。

一九一三年探戈傳到芬蘭後，變慢了，也變得憂傷（心裡難過），唱的不是熱情，而是失去的愛和望不到邊的森林。

從一九八五年起，小鎮塞納約基每年七月都辦探戈節。這個點子最早是兩對夫妻在三溫暖（蒸氣浴）裡想出來的，現在每年吸引十萬多人，還會選出「探戈國王和王后」。

Many people assume tango belongs only to South America. Yet in distant Finland, it has become a national symbol. When tango arrived there in 1913, it transformed—slowing down and growing melancholic, expressing not passion but lost love and endless forests. Since 1985, the small town of Seinäjoki has hosted a tango festival every July. The idea originally came from two couples in a sauna, and it now draws over 100,000 visitors annually, complete with the crowning of a Tango King and Queen.

十五家餐廳，兩座冠軍：芝加哥漢堡大戰

來源：Roscoe Village Burger Fest

七月十七到十九號，芝加哥的羅斯科村（Roscoe Village）要辦漢堡節。十五家餐廳會在貝爾蒙和達門街口擺攤，比誰做的漢堡最好吃。

特別的是，這裡會選出兩位冠軍：客人可以用手機傳簡訊投票，選出「大家最愛」；另外還有一群評審，自己選出「評審最愛」。

進場只要捐大約十塊美金，整個週末都有現場音樂，也有給小朋友玩的區域。

From July 17 to 19, Chicago's Roscoe Village is hosting a burger festival. Fifteen restaurants will set up booths at the corner of Belmont and Damen to compete over whose burgers taste the best. What makes it special is that two champions will be crowned: customers can text-vote on their phones for "People's Choice," while a panel of judges selects "Judges' Choice." Entry is just a donation of about ten dollars, and there's live music throughout the weekend, plus a kids' play area.

被刪掉的城市：一張消失網路世界的地圖

來源：deletedcity.net

以前有個叫 GeoCities 的網站，讓三千五百萬人蓋自己的網頁，像一個個小家。二〇〇九年，公司把它整個刪掉，十年的東西差點全部不見。

荷蘭設計師 Richard Vijgen 用別人搶救下來的備份，把它畫成一座會動的城市地圖。每個社區有多大，就看它的檔案多不多。

你可以一直放大，最後看到一個個沒人住的舊網頁，上面閃亮的小圖和留言簿都還在。這件作品後來還在好幾個有名的美術館展出。

There used to be a website called GeoCities that let 35 million people build their own webpages, like little homes. In 2009, the company deleted it entirely, and a decade of content nearly vanished forever. Dutch designer Richard Vijgen took backups that others had rescued and transformed them into an animated city map. Each neighborhood's size reflects how many files it contains. You can zoom in endlessly, eventually discovering individual abandoned old webpages, their glittering graphics and guestbooks still preserved. The work was later exhibited in several renowned art museums.
