客家牛哥的新靈魂之聲

來源：Taiwan B (Substack)

黃子軒在台灣客家民謠圈唱了十年，二〇二四年終於發行第一張個人專輯《牛騷》。這個名字是雙關語：聽起來像New Soul，字面上卻是「牛在叫」，把鄉下客家的畫面帶進了現代音樂。

專輯裡用客語唱著新靈魂樂、巴薩諾瓦（一種巴西音樂）和電子民謠，聲音大膽又新鮮。這張專輯在二〇二五年第三十六屆金曲獎拿下最佳客語專輯和最佳客語歌手兩項大獎。

Huang Zi-xuan has sung in Taiwan's Hakka folk music circles for ten years. In 2024, he finally released his debut solo album, "Niu Sao" (牛騷). The title is a play on words — it sounds like "New Soul," but literally means "the ox is calling," bringing rural Hakka imagery into contemporary music. The album features songs in Hakka that blend new soul music, bossa nova (a Brazilian music style), and electronic folk, with a bold and fresh sound. The album won two awards at the 36th Golden Melody Awards in 2025: Best Hakka-Language Album and Best Hakka-Language Singer.

北歐最大的木造老城

來源：Featuring Finland

芬蘭西海岸有一座叫做「老勞馬」（Old Rauma）的小鎮，是北歐保存最完整的木造城鎮。中世紀的街道上擠著六百多棟木屋，一九九一年被列入聯合國世界遺產。

這裡的人還說著自己的方言，跟標準芬蘭語差很多，甚至有用方言寫的詩和小說。三月時白雪覆蓋小鎮，遊客很少，可以安靜地散步，順便在老店裡看看手工梭織蕾絲。

On the west coast of Finland sits a small town called Old Rauma, the best-preserved wooden town in Northern Europe. Medieval streets are lined with more than six hundred wooden houses, and the town was designated a UNESCO World Heritage site in 1991. The residents speak their own dialect, which differs significantly from standard Finnish, and there are even poems and novels written in the local language. In March, snow blankets the town and tourists are few, making it possible to stroll quietly through the streets and browse handmade shuttle-woven lace in the old shops.

走進萊特的私人世界

來源：Wednesday Journal (Oak Park)

五月十六日，芝加哥近郊的橡樹園（Oak Park）將舉辦一年一度的「萊特建築散步」（Wright Plus Housewalk）。八棟平常不開放的私人老房子會打開大門，讓大家走進去參觀。

這些房子是建築大師萊特（Frank Lloyd Wright）和他的草原學派（Prairie School）同行設計的。票已賣完，但當天早上八點在萊特故居還有七十五張現場票，先到先買。

On May 16th, Oak Park, a suburb near Chicago, will host its annual "Wright Plus Housewalk." Eight private historic homes that are normally closed to the public will open their doors for visitors. These houses were designed by architectural master Frank Lloyd Wright and his Prairie School contemporaries. Tickets have sold out, but on the morning of the event, seventy-five walk-up tickets will be available starting at 8 a.m. at Wright's home, first come, first served.

一個人畫出三百五十五種世界地圖

來源：map-projections.net

你知道同一個地球可以用多少種方式畫成平面地圖嗎？一位叫 Tobias Jung 的德國人沒有受過地圖學訓練，卻花了十多年建了一個網站，收集了三百五十五種「地圖投影法」（把圓的地球變成平面的方法）。

網站上可以把任何兩種投影放在一起比較，總共有六萬多種組合。他還自己發明新的投影方式，連大學學者都引用他的研究。

Did you know the same Earth can be rendered as a flat map in countless different ways? A German named Tobias Jung, who has no formal training in cartography, spent over a decade building a website that catalogues three hundred fifty-five different "map projections" — methods for transforming our round planet into a flat surface. The site lets you compare any two projections side by side, creating more than sixty thousand possible combinations. He's even invented new projection methods of his own, and university scholars now cite his research.
