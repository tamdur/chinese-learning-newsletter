透明雜誌：台北地下音樂的靈魂之聲

來源：Touming Magazine Bandcamp

二〇一〇年，台北樂團「透明雜誌」發行了專輯《我們的靈魂樂》。他們在地下場地 Underworld 演出，是台北龐克（punk）圈「四大天王」之一。

這張專輯最特別的地方是：在強烈的吉他噪音下面，藏著乾淨好聽的旋律（melody）。他們受到 Pixies 和日本樂團 Number Girl 影響，在日本也有很多歌迷。

In 2010, the Taipei band Transparent Magazine released their album "Our Soul Music." They performed at the underground venue Underworld and were one of the "Four Kings" of Taipei's punk scene. What made this album special was that beneath the intense guitar noise lay clean, melodic passages. Influenced by Pixies and Japanese band Number Girl, they had built a substantial fanbase in Japan as well.

卡累利亞派：一塊餡餅裡的戰爭記憶

來源：Gastro Obscura / Atlas Obscura

在芬蘭，每間麵包店都賣一種小餡餅：外皮是黑麥做的，裡面包著米粥（rice porridge），吃的時候要抹上蛋奶油。這就是卡累利亞派。

它的故事很特別。一九四〇年蘇聯佔領了卡累利亞地區，四十二萬人被迫搬到芬蘭各地，帶著家鄉的食譜。從此，一個小地方的點心變成了全國的美食。

In Finland, every bakery sells a small pastry: the crust is made of rye, filled with rice porridge, and eaten with a dollop of egg butter. This is the Karelian pasty. Its story is remarkable. In 1940, the Soviet Union occupied the Karelia region, forcing 420,000 people to relocate across Finland, carrying their homeland's recipes with them. From that moment on, a local delicacy became a national treasure.

千禧公園屋頂啤酒節

來源：Choose Chicago

五月八日星期五，芝加哥千禧公園的 Harris Theater 屋頂將舉辦「Beer Under Glass」啤酒品嘗會。超過八十家伊利諾州的小型啤酒廠會帶來一百五十到兩百種啤酒。

活動時間是下午五點半到八點半，門票約五十七到六十七美元。你可以一邊喝啤酒，一邊看芝加哥市中心的天際線。

On Friday, May 8th, the rooftop of Harris Theater at Millennium Park in Chicago will host "Beer Under Glass," a beer tasting event. More than eighty small breweries from Illinois will bring between 150 and 200 craft beers. The event runs from 5:30 p.m. to 8:30 p.m., with tickets priced around $57 to $67. You can enjoy beers while taking in views of Chicago's downtown skyline.

世界概況檔案庫：一個人救回三十六年的資料

來源：worldfactbookarchive.org

美國中情局（CIA）曾經公開一本《世界概況》，記錄全球兩百八十一個國家的基本資料。二〇二六年二月，它突然被關閉了。

幸好，一位研究者早已把一九九〇到二〇二五年的每一版都存了下來，整理成超過一百萬筆資料。你可以搜尋、比較不同年份的變化，還能做出地圖。

The United States Central Intelligence Agency (CIA) once published the World Factbook, a compendium documenting basic information about all 281 countries worldwide. In February 2026, it was suddenly taken offline. Fortunately, a researcher had already archived every edition from 1990 to 2025, organizing the data into over one million records. You can now search, compare changes across different years, and even generate maps.
