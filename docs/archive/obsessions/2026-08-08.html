藏起來的鄉愁：搖滾樂裡的台北七個地方

來源：Blow 吹音樂

二〇二五年，台北的四人搖滾樂團 COLD DEW 發行了第一張專輯《臺北人文地景》。靈感來自白先勇一九七一年的小說《台北人》。

專輯裡有七首歌，每一首都對著台北的一個地方：西門町、大稻埕、沙崙海水浴場、新店碧潭，還有木柵的富德公墓（安放過世親人的地方）。音樂混著藍調、民謠、電子與吵雜的聲音。

團員說，這是「努力掩藏的鄉愁」。就像小說裡的人物，臉上笑著，心裡卻藏著回不去的家。

In 2025, the four-piece Taipei rock band COLD DEW released their debut album, Taipei Human Landscapes (《臺北人文地景》), inspired by Pai Hsien-yung's 1971 story collection Taipei People. The album has seven songs, each one addressed to a particular place in Taipei: Ximending, Dadaocheng, Shalun Beach, Bitan in Xindian, and the Fude Public Cemetery in Muzha, where the dead are laid to rest. The music blends blues, folk, electronics and noise. The band calls it “a diligently concealed homesickness” — like the characters in the novel, smiling outwardly while carrying inside a home they can never return to.

啄木鳥的咖啡：芬蘭樹上的戰時飲料

來源：Mycelyum Inc.

第二次世界大戰時，芬蘭買不到咖啡和糖。人們走進森林，找一種長在樺樹（一種樹皮白白的樹）上的黑色香菇，叫做樺褐孔菌。

把它曬乾、磨成粉，就能煮出很像咖啡的飲料。芬蘭人叫它「啄木鳥茶」，因為它會慢慢把樹幹吃空，就像啄木鳥在樹上打洞。

今天在芬蘭的咖啡店還買得到。它有點甜，帶著堅果香，也不含咖啡因，而芬蘭人喝咖啡的量是全世界第一名。

During the Second World War, Finland could not get coffee or sugar. People went into the forests looking for a black fungus that grows on birch trees (the trees with the white bark), called chaga. Dried and ground into powder, it brews into a drink much like coffee. Finns called it “woodpecker tea,” because the fungus slowly hollows out the trunk it feeds on, the way a woodpecker bores holes in a tree. You can still buy it in Finnish cafés today. It is faintly sweet, with a nutty aroma, and contains no caffeine — in a country that drinks more coffee per person than any other on earth.

沒有祖國的語言：芝加哥的世界語之夜

來源：La Esperanto-Societo de Ĉikago

八月十一日星期二，傍晚五點半到七點，芝加哥世界語學會在湖景區的王爾德酒吧餐廳（北百老匯大道三一三〇號）辦一場聚會。

聚會的名字是 Manĝu, Manĝu! ，意思是「吃吧，吃吧！」。這個學會一九五四年就成立了。活動免費，一句都不會說的人也歡迎。

世界語是一八八七年柴門霍夫醫生發明的。他希望大家說話時，不必用某一個國家的話當共同語言。

On Tuesday, August 11th, from 5:30 to 7pm, the Esperanto Society of Chicago holds a gathering at Wilde Bar & Restaurant in Lakeview (3130 N Broadway). The meet-up is called Manĝu, Manĝu! — “Eat, eat!” The society was founded back in 1954. The event is free, and people who cannot speak a single word are welcome too. Esperanto was invented in 1887 by Dr L. L. Zamenhof, who hoped people could talk to one another without having to use any one country's language as the common tongue.

開一台不存在的車：一個人的二十五個網站

來源：Steven Irby / singleservingsites.cool

網路工程師史蒂芬・厄比給自己出了一道題目：二十五個星期，做出二十五個網站。

第十八個叫 Lofi Car 。打開它，畫面像一台老汽車的駕駛座，你轉動收音機的旋鈕（可以轉的圓形開關）挑音樂，窗外的霓虹燈（彩色的燈）公路一直往後跑。

裡面還藏著幾個祕密按鈕，等人去發現。這種簡單卻讓人開心的小東西，正是一個人做網站最迷人的地方。

The web builder Steven Irby set himself a challenge: twenty-five weeks, twenty-five websites. Number eighteen is called Lofi Car. Open it and the screen looks like the driver's seat of an old car — you turn the radio knob to choose the music while a neon-lit highway streams backwards past the window. A few secret buttons are hidden inside, waiting to be found. That kind of simple, cheerful little thing is exactly what makes one person building websites so appealing.
