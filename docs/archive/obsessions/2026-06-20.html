飛到好萊塢錄的《平凡》

來源：FOND/SOUND

一九九一年，台灣音樂人黃韻玲飛到美國洛杉磯，在許多好萊塢名片用過的錄音室，錄下了專輯《平凡》。

當時台灣流行的是偶像和情歌，這張慢慢的、帶著爵士味的唱片很特別，剛出時沒人注意。後來黃韻玲為張學友等大歌手寫歌，這張專輯也在多年後才被大家珍惜。

In 1991, Taiwanese musician Kay Huang (Huang Yun-ling) flew to Los Angeles and recorded her album "Ordinary" in a studio that had been used for many Hollywood records. At the time, Taiwan's pop scene was dominated by idol pop and love ballads, so this slow, jazz-tinged album was something unusual — and it went almost unnoticed when it first came out. Kay later wrote songs for major singers like Jacky Cheung, and this album only came to be cherished years down the line.

三月空蕩的世界最大木教堂

來源：Finnish Architecture Navigator

在芬蘭的凱里邁基，有一座全世界最大的木頭教堂，坐得下三千人。一八四○年代，當地人嫌第一個設計太小，才蓋得這麼大。

裡面的柱子被畫成大理石的樣子，看起來像石頭大教堂。因為沒有暖氣，三月天冷時大家改去旁邊的小教堂，這座大堂就靜靜空著，走進去格外安靜。

In Kerimäki, Finland, stands the world's largest wooden church, with seating for three thousand people. In the 1840s, locals felt the first design was too small, which is why it was built on such a grand scale. The pillars inside are painted to look like marble, giving the impression of a stone cathedral. Because there is no heating, when the cold of March sets in everyone moves to a small chapel next door, leaving this great hall standing quietly empty — strikingly silent to walk through.

在百年德國會館看世界盃

來源：Block Club Chicago

在芝加哥的老社區，有一棟一九二七年的德國會館「DANK Haus」，裡面有博物館、圖書室和德文學校。

六月二十五日，這裡辦「足球熱」派對，大家一起看德國隊的世界盃球賽。這裡不像運動酒吧，來的多是德裔家庭和附近的鄰居，有點心、飲料，氣氛溫暖又熱鬧。

In an old Chicago neighborhood stands DANK Haus, a German cultural center built in 1927, home to a museum, a library, and a German-language school. On June 25th, the center is hosting a "Football Fever" party where everyone gathers to watch the German national team's World Cup match. Unlike a sports bar, the crowd here is mostly German-heritage families and neighbors from nearby, with snacks and drinks and a warm, lively atmosphere.

飛行員把所有航班變成會轉的地球

來源：jameshard.ing

詹姆斯·哈丁是一位客機飛行員。他把自己十幾年來飛過的每一趟航班記錄，做成了一個會轉動的立體地球網站。

每飛完一趟，網站就會自動更新。地球會一趟一趟地畫出他飛過的路線，還有圖表告訴你他去過哪些機場、飛過哪些飛機。這是只有真心喜歡飛行的人才會做的事。

James Harding is a commercial airline pilot. He took his records of every flight he has flown over the past decade or so and turned them into a website with a rotating 3D globe. Each time he finishes a flight, the site updates automatically. The globe traces out his routes one by one, and charts tell you which airports he has visited and which aircraft he has flown. This is the kind of thing only someone who genuinely loves flying would make.
