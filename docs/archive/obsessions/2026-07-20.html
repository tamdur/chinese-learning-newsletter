古琴大師被遺忘的南風專輯

來源：FOND/SOUND

他後來成為台灣最有名的古琴（一種古老的七弦樂器）大師。但在一九八九年，袁中平先做了一張奇特又好聽的專輯，叫做《南風旅程》。

這張唱片的靈感，來自他一個人走過台灣的高山和小村子。專輯裡幾乎每種樂器都是他自己彈的。

像〈蜻蜓蝌蚪四重奏〉這樣的歌，用聲音學蟲子叫、流水和天氣，沒有固定的旋律。它曾經被人忘記，現在又被喜歡找老唱片的人重新發現。

He later became Taiwan's most celebrated guqin (an ancient seven-string instrument) master. But in 1989, Yuan Jung-Ping first made a strange and beautiful album called "South Wind Journey." The album drew inspiration from his solitary travels through Taiwan's mountains and small villages. Nearly every instrument on the album was played by him. Pieces like "Dragonfly Tadpole Quartet" use sound to evoke insect calls, flowing water, and weather, without any fixed melody. The album was eventually forgotten, but now vintage record enthusiasts are rediscovering it.

睡在牢房裡的旅館

來源：Hotel Katajanokka

在赫爾辛基一座安靜的小島上，有一棟用紅磚（紅色的磚頭）蓋成的監獄，建於一八三七年。它關過等待審判的人，戰爭後還關過芬蘭的總統。

這座監獄在二零零二年關閉，整修後在二零零七年變成旅館。以前的牢房現在是客人的房間，牆壁厚達一公尺，房間裡非常安靜。

住在這裡，走一小段路就能到有名的大教堂和海港。

On a quiet island in Helsinki stands a former prison, built from red brick in 1837. It once housed people awaiting trial and, after the war, even Finland's president. The prison shut down in 2002 and, following restoration, reopened as a hotel in 2007. The old cells have become guest rooms—walls a full meter thick keep the rooms remarkably quiet. A short walk takes you to the famous cathedral and harbor.

親手釀一瓶自己的酒

來源：Wild Blossom Meadery & Winery

在芝加哥南邊的Beverly 區，有一間開在舊消防隊裡的釀酒廠，叫做 Wild Blossom。它是芝加哥唯一自己養蜜蜂、用蜂蜜釀酒的地方，已經開了二十年。

這個星期六，七月二十五日，他們有一堂釀酒課。你可以在發酵桶（讓酒變熟的大桶子）中間，親手調配並裝好一瓶帶回家的酒。

兩個人一起上課要一百四十九美元。

On Chicago's south side in the Beverly neighborhood, there's a brewery housed in an old fire station called Wild Blossom. It's Chicago's only place that raises its own bees and brews mead, and it's been open for twenty years. This Saturday, July 25th, they're offering a winemaking class where you can personally blend and bottle your own wine to take home, working among the fermentation tanks—the large barrels where wine matures. For two people, the class costs $149.

一個人抄下八百首老曲子

來源：oldtimefiddletunes.net

John Lamancusa 是一位大學的工程教授。幾十年來，他在音樂節和聚會上用耳朵聽，把超過八百首美國傳統的小提琴曲子，一首一首抄了下來。

每一首他都配上樂譜和電腦播放的檔案，全部免費放在網站上，沒有廣告，也不用登記帳號。

這是他一生的心血，送給每一個在找一段熟悉旋律的人。

John Lamancusa is a university engineering professor. Over several decades, he has attended music festivals and gatherings, listening by ear and transcribing over eight hundred traditional American fiddle tunes, one by one. For each tune, he has added sheet music and a digital audio file, all freely available on his website—no ads, no registration required. It is the work of a lifetime, a gift to anyone searching for a familiar melody.
