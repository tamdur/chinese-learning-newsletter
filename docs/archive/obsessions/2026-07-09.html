武俠反派化身工業電音

來源：Resident Advisor / L.I.E.S. Records

在台北工作的音樂人 Tzusing，2017年推出第一張專輯《東方不敗》。這個名字來自金庸的小說《笑傲江湖》，是一個反派，他為了練成天下無敵的劍法，不惜改變自己的身體。

Tzusing 把冷硬的工業電子音樂，和亞洲、中東的民間旋律混在一起。第一首歌用鋼弦的震動聲，配上像打雷一樣的鼓聲，開創了台北獨特的「世界電音」。

Tzusing, a Taipei-based musician, released his debut album "The Invincible East" in 2017. The title draws from Jin Yong's martial arts novel "Laughing Proud Wanderer," referencing its antagonist—a character who transformed his own body to achieve an unbeatable sword technique. Tzusing blends the cold, industrial edge of electronic music with folk melodies from Asia and the Middle East. The opening track combines the resonant vibration of steel strings with thunderous drums, establishing Taipei's distinctive "world electronic" sound.

玻璃小鎮裡飛出的鳥

來源：Nuutajärven Lasi

在芬蘭的森林深處，有一個叫努塔耶爾維（Nuutajärvi）的小鎮，做玻璃已經超過230年。這裡有一個「鳥巢」展覽，收藏了幾百種玻璃做的鳥。

這些鳥是設計師托伊卡（Oiva Toikka）的心血。他在1972年做出第一隻，之後一輩子都在設計新的鳥，讓它們變成全世界收藏家的最愛。三月來訪時，還能坐在爐火前，近距離看師傅吹玻璃。

Deep in the forests of Finland sits a small town called Nuutajärvi, where glass has been made for over 230 years. Here you'll find the "Bird's Nest" exhibition, home to hundreds of hand-blown glass birds—the life's work of designer Oiva Toikka. He created his first bird in 1972 and devoted the rest of his life to designing new ones, earning them a place in collections around the world. Visit in March and you can sit by the furnace fire to watch master glassmakers at work up close.

公園裡的手作紙與小誌

來源：Chicago Printers Guild

7月20日星期六下午兩點，芝加哥印刷公會在麥金利公園（McKinley Park）辦一場免費活動，叫「Books & Beats」。

大人小孩都可以來，親手做紙、玩版畫，還能做出自己的一本小誌（一種自己編印的小書）。現場還有音樂表演。不用報名，也不用經驗，來了就能把手弄得沾滿墨水，開心玩一個下午。

On Saturday, July 20th at 2 PM, the Chicago Printers Guild is hosting a free event called "Books & Beats" at McKinley Park. Adults and children alike can come make paper by hand, experiment with printmaking, and create their own zine (a self-published small book). There will also be live music. No registration required and no prior experience necessary—just show up ready to get your hands inky and enjoy an afternoon of fun.

一個人記錄的兩萬座燈塔

來源：Lighthouse Directory (ibiblio.org)

退休的數學教授羅利特（Russ Rowlett），從1999年開始建立一個燈塔資料庫。到2009年，他已經記錄了全世界超過兩萬四千座燈塔。

每一座都有地圖位置、歷史和照片。這個網站沒有廣告，也不賺錢，到現在還常常更新，是他純粹出於喜愛做出來的心血。

Russ Rowlett, a retired mathematics professor, began building a lighthouse database in 1999. By 2009, he had cataloged over 24,000 lighthouses worldwide. Each entry includes its map location, historical information, and photographs. The website contains no advertisements and generates no revenue, yet it remains actively maintained to this day—a true labor of love undertaken purely from his passion for lighthouses.
