廟宇裡的電子鬼魅

來源：PopMatters

夢東是台北一對兄弟組成的樂團。二〇二〇年，他們做了一張叫《秘神》的專輯（一整套的歌）。這些歌是為他們自己拍的攝影書配的音樂，照片拍的是台灣各地的廟宇、木乃伊（不會腐爛的和尚身體）和奇怪的老房子。

音樂裡有低沉的電子聲，還有八、九〇年代老電玩和老電影的聲音。它不學傳統民謠，而是玩台灣早年那種說鬼神、有點嚇人又好玩的流行文化。

Meng Dong is a Taipei-based band of two brothers. In 2020, they released an album titled Secret Deities. The songs were created as a soundtrack to a photography book they shot themselves, documenting temples, preserved mummified monks, and weathered old houses scattered across Taiwan. The music blends deep electronic tones with the sonic textures of 1980s and '90s arcade games and vintage films. Rather than drawing on traditional folk music, it taps into Taiwan's early popular culture—that playfully eerie fascination with ghosts and spirits.

會被極光叫醒的夜車

來源：VR (Finnish Railways)

每到冬天的晚上，芬蘭都有一班火車從首都赫爾辛基出發，開一整夜，十二個小時後到北邊的羅瓦涅米。新的雙層車廂裡有小房間，上層的房間還有自己的淋浴間和廁所，早餐會送到門口。

從九月到三月，火車會開過北極圈。這時你可能會在睡夢中，被窗外的北極光（天上五顏六色的光）叫醒。所以大家又叫它「極光號」。

Every winter night, a train departs from Helsinki, Finland's capital, and travels through the darkness for twelve hours before reaching Rovaniemi in the far north. The new double-decker train cars have small cabins, with upper-level rooms featuring their own shower and toilet, and breakfast delivered to your door. From September to March, the train passes through the Arctic Circle. During these months, you might wake in the middle of the night to see the Northern Lights—those shimmering, multicolored ribbons of light dancing across the sky—framed by your window. That's why everyone has nicknamed it the 'Aurora Train.'

天文館裡的星空露營

來源：Adler Planetarium

八月二十日，芝加哥的阿德勒天文館（看星星的地方）要辦一場只給大人參加的夜間派對，叫「阿德勒營」。當晚有露營、看星星、喝調酒和現場音樂，天氣好的話還能用大望遠鏡看夜空。

那裡也有一個新的展覽，講天文學和占星術（用星星算命）本來是同一門學問，後來在一五〇〇到一八〇〇年間才分開。展品裡有一本一四九四年的星座書，很珍貴。

On August 20th, Chicago's Adler Planetarium is hosting an adults-only evening party called "Adler Camp." The night will feature camping, stargazing, cocktails, and live music—and if weather permits, visitors can peer through large telescopes to view the night sky. The planetarium is also showcasing a new exhibition about how astronomy and astrology (using stars to divine the future) were originally one discipline but didn't separate until sometime between 1500 and 1800. The exhibits include a rare 1494 star chart.

一張讓你起雞皮疙瘩的歌單

來源：GitHub — remidefleurian/chim

聽到某些音樂，我們會忽然起雞皮疙瘩，心裡一陣感動，這叫「顫慄」（身體發抖的感覺）。有一位研究大腦的科學家做了一張表，把幾十年來科學家研究過、會讓人起雞皮疙瘩的每一首歌都記下來。

一開始就有一千多首歌，每一首都可以查到它是從哪篇研究來的。這張表放在網路上，只要有新的研究，就會再加進去。

When we hear certain songs, we sometimes get sudden goosebumps and feel a wave of emotion—a sensation called 'frisson' (that trembling feeling in your body). A neuroscientist studying the brain created a chart documenting all the songs that researchers have identified over decades as triggering this response. The list started with over a thousand songs, and each one can be linked back to the research paper that discovered it. The chart is published online and continuously updated whenever new research emerges.
