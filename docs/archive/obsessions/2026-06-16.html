甜梅號：在速食店桌上取名的傳奇樂團

來源：Taipei Times / Discogs

一九九八年三月，原本的主唱沒來練團，剩下的三個人卻發現：沒有他也能玩音樂。他們在台北一家速食店的桌子上，為自己取名「甜梅號」。

二〇〇一年的第一張專輯《是不是少了什麼》沒有人唱歌，全部用吉他說故事。層層疊疊的聲音慢慢堆高，從溫柔突然變得激烈，開創了台灣的後搖滾（一種沒有歌詞的搖滾樂）。

這張專輯後來很難買到。二〇一二年樂團演出時，歌迷為了聽這些少見的歌，還提早排隊。

In March 1998, their original vocalist failed to show up for rehearsal. The three remaining members discovered they could make music without him. At a table in a fast-food restaurant in Taipei, they named themselves Sugar Plum Ferry. Their debut album in 2001, "Is Something Missing?", featured no vocals — the guitars told the entire story. Layers of sound built gradually, shifting from gentle to suddenly intense, pioneering post-rock in Taiwan — a genre of instrumental rock without lyrics. The album eventually became difficult to find. When the band performed in 2012, fans arrived early and waited in line just to hear these rare songs.

雪殼：三月芬蘭能走在雪上的奇蹟

來源：Finnish Way of Hiking

每年三月，芬蘭白天會回暖，晚上又結冰。這樣一來，厚厚的雪表面會凍成一層硬殼，芬蘭人叫它hankikanto。

以前沒有馬路通到北方時，這層硬雪就是大家的「高速公路」。人們可以滑著雪，朝任何方向快速前進，去遠方搬乾草、或是拜訪別的村子。

今天的旅人特地在三月來這裡。在明亮的春陽下，踩在一公尺深的雪上卻不會陷下去，這是其他季節都沒有的體驗。

Every March, Finland experiences a unique phenomenon: daytime temperatures rise above freezing while nights dip below zero. This daily cycle transforms the thick snow surface into a hard crust that Finns call hankikanto. Before roads connected the north, this frozen crust served as a natural "highway." People could ski across it in any direction at speed, traveling to distant villages or hauling hay across the landscape. Today's travelers come deliberately in March to experience something unavailable any other season: walking atop a meter-deep snowpack in bright spring sunshine without sinking through.

玻璃花房變溜冰場：海軍碼頭的復古夏夜

來源：Block Club Chicago

芝加哥海軍碼頭的「水晶花房」是一座高大的玻璃屋。從疫情（大家生病的那段時期）關門到現在，六月十八日這個星期四終於重新開放，變成湖邊的溜冰場。

這是芝加哥第一座蓋在湖邊的輪式溜冰場，一次可以容納一百個人。現場有DJ放著迪斯可和九〇年代的老歌，透過玻璃牆還能看見密西根湖。

票價從十七點五美元起，每天開放，一直到勞動節為止。

The Crystal Gardens at Chicago's Navy Pier is a tall glass house. After closing during the pandemic, it finally reopens this Thursday, June 18th, transforming into a lakefront roller rink. This is Chicago's first roller skating rink built directly on the lake, accommodating up to one hundred people at a time. A DJ spins disco and nineties classics, and through the glass walls you can see Lake Michigan. Tickets start at $17.50, and it's open daily through Labor Day.

一個人的電網地圖：看見英國的電從哪來

來源：iamkate.com

凱特·莫利是英國西南部的一位工程師。她自己做了一個網站，即時（當下這一刻）顯示全英國的電力是從哪裡來的。

風力、太陽能、核能、天然氣，還有六條海底電纜（海底下的電線）連到法國和挪威等國家，全都看得到。網站每幾分鐘更新一次，還會告訴你這些電產生了多少污染。

這是她一個人用愛做的作品，沒有廣告，過去一年卻有將近兩千兩百萬人來看。

Kate Morley is an engineer in southwest England. She built a website by herself that shows in real time where Britain's electricity comes from, updated every few minutes. You can see wind power, solar energy, nuclear power, natural gas, and six undersea cables connecting to countries like France and Norway. The site also tells you how much pollution each source generates. It's a labor of love with no ads, yet nearly 22 million people have visited it over the past year.
