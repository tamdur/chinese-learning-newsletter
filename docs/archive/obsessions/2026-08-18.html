《地平線上的脈動》：台灣電子音樂的地下風景

來源：ato.archives (Bandcamp)

2025年7月18日，唱片廠牌 ato.archives 發行了合輯《地平線上的脈動》。策畫的人是住在台灣的音樂家 Ryan J Raffa。他花了八年，記錄台北一群玩「模組合成器」（用電線把小機器接在一起，做出聲音的樂器）的朋友。

專輯裡有九首歌。Great Human Energy 的〈毛蟲〉像蟲子慢慢爬；shengkai 的〈Briny Mist〉像海上的霧；MADZINE 的作品像繩子打結又解開。這個小圈子的聲音，外面的人很少聽見。

On 18 July 2025, the record label ato.archives released Pulses on the Horizon, a compilation curated by Ryan J Raffa, a musician based in Taiwan. Over eight years, Raffa documented a circle of friends in Taipei who play modular synthesizers — instruments made by connecting small electronic devices together with cables.

The album contains nine tracks. Great Human Energy's “Caterpillar” sounds like an insect slowly crawling; shengkai's “Briny Mist” evokes sea mist; MADZINE's piece is like rope being knotted and untied. The sound of this small community rarely reaches people outside it.

詩人的蛋糕：一年只賣三十六天

來源：Wikipedia / Visit Porvoo

芬蘭的國民詩人魯內貝里（Runeberg）最愛一種小圓蛋糕。傳說是他太太用剩下的麵包屑、餅乾屑和杏仁做出來的。不過現在研究食物歷史的學者說，1840年代波爾沃（Porvoo）的糕點師傅 Lars Astenius 才是真正的發明人。

蛋糕上有一圈覆盆子果醬和一點糖霜。全芬蘭只在1月1日到2月5日賣。但在詩人的家鄉波爾沃，老城區的 Café Fanny 和 Café Helmi 一整年都做。三月從赫爾辛基坐車來走一趟，就能吃到別的季節吃不到的味道。

Finland's national poet Runeberg adored a certain small round cake. Legend says his wife made it from leftover breadcrumbs, cookie crumbs and almonds. But food historians now tell a different story — they credit Lars Astenius, a pastry chef in Porvoo in the 1840s, as the true inventor.

The cake is crowned with a ring of raspberry jam and a dab of icing. Across Finland it is sold only from 1 January to 5 February. Yet in Porvoo, the poet's hometown, two cafés in the old town — Café Fanny and Café Helmi — bake it year-round. Make the trip from Helsinki in March and you can taste a flavour unavailable in any other season.

街上的古典樂：渴耳朵音樂節第十一年

來源：Access Contemporary Music

8月22日星期六下午兩點到晚上十一點，23日星期日下午一點到晚上九點，芝加哥 Ravenswood 區的 Wilson 大道會封起來，變成「Thirsty Ears」音樂節。這是全芝加哥唯一的古典音樂街頭市集，免費入場，建議捐十美元。

最精采的是星期六黃昏的「默片之聲」：作曲家寫好新的配樂，樂手就在街上現場演奏，旁邊同時放映沒有聲音的老電影。街上還有餐車、啤酒和小朋友的活動。

On Saturday 22 August from 2pm to 11pm, and Sunday 23 August from 1pm to 9pm, Wilson Avenue in Chicago's Ravenswood neighbourhood closes to become the Thirsty Ears festival. It is Chicago's only classical music street fair, free to enter with a suggested $10 donation.

The highlight is Saturday evening's “Sound of Silent Film”: composers write new scores, and musicians perform them live on the street while an old silent film screens alongside. There are also food trucks, beer and activities for children.

一本用打油詩寫成的字典，2066年完工

來源：OEDILF

2004年開始，全世界有超過兩千兩百六十位志工，想把英文裡的每一個字，都寫成一首「五行打油詩」（一種押韻又有節奏的英文短詩）。每一首都要大家一行一行討論、通過，才能放上網站。

到今天，他們已經寫了十二萬多首，字母卻只走到 Ir。照這個速度，第一版要等到2066年2月16日才寫得完。

Since 2004, more than 2,260 volunteers worldwide have been trying to write a limerick — a five-line rhyming poem with a bouncy rhythm — for every single word in English. Each one must be discussed line by line and approved by the community before it goes up on the site.

To date they have written more than 120,000 limericks, but alphabetically they have only reached “Ir”. At this rate, the first complete edition will not be finished until 16 February 2066.
