渡過黑水的藍調

來源：中央社

客家歌手黃連煜唱歌三十年了。2022年，他的專輯《滅人山》拿下金曲獎三個大獎。

「滅人山」來自老歌〈渡台悲歌〉。很久以前，客家人坐船渡過危險的海峽（兩塊陸地中間的海），再爬進陌生的高山，心裡很害怕。黃連煜用這座山，比作走進中年。

主打歌找來卑南族（台灣東部的原住民族）歌手桑布伊一起唱，花了快兩年才完成。樂評人馬世芳說，他是台灣少見真正的藍調歌手。

Hakka singer Huang Lien-yu has been performing for thirty years. In 2022, his album Mie Ren Shan (a name that roughly means "the mountain that wears people down") won three Golden Melody Awards. The title comes from an old song, "Lament for Crossing to Taiwan." Long ago, Hakka people crossed the dangerous strait by boat, then climbed into unfamiliar mountains, filled with fear. Huang uses that mountain as a metaphor for entering middle age. For the lead track, he brought in Puyuma singer Sanbuyi, a member of an Indigenous people from eastern Taiwan, and the two took almost two years to finish the song. Music critic Ma Shih-fang says Huang is one of the few truly authentic blues singers in Taiwan.

一條麵包裡的七十隻蟋蟀

來源：This is Finland

2017年11月1日，芬蘭改了法律，可以賣蟲給人吃。麵包公司 Fazer 馬上推出「蟋蟀麵包」。

每條麵包放了大約70隻蟋蟀（一種會叫的小蟲）磨成的粉，賣3.99歐元，比一般麵包貴。結果在赫爾辛基的店裡，幾個小時就賣光了。

同一時間，坦佩雷的廚師也辦起吃蟲晚餐：番茄上放著小蟲，還有蟋蟀粉做的肉丸。

On November 1, 2017, Finland changed its law to allow insects to be sold as food. The bakery company Fazer immediately launched "Cricket Bread." Each loaf contains powder ground from about 70 crickets (small insects that chirp), and it sells for €3.99, more than ordinary bread. In Helsinki, the loaves sold out within a few hours. At the same time, chefs in Tampere held insect dinners, serving tomatoes topped with small insects and meatballs made with cricket powder.

一起把《奧德賽》讀完

來源：National Hellenic Museum

10月29日到30日，芝加哥的國家希臘博物館要辦「荷馬馬拉松」。大家輪流大聲讀荷馬的長詩《奧德賽》，從頭讀到尾。

讀的人可以用英文，也可以用希臘文、西班牙文、波蘭文。不用報名，也不用錢，走進去就能聽，也能自己讀一段。

今年博物館還辦過一場假的法庭，審判（決定有沒有罪）詩中的英雄奧德修斯。結果，他被判無罪。

On October 29 and 30, the National Hellenic Museum in Chicago will host a "Homer Marathon." Everyone takes turns reading aloud Homer's epic poem The Odyssey, from beginning to end. Readers may use English, Greek, Spanish, or Polish. There is no registration and no charge. Walk in to listen, or read a passage yourself. This year, the museum also staged a mock trial to judge (decide guilt or innocence) Odysseus, the poem's hero. In the end, he was found not guilty.

風沙中的大搬家

來源：America's Great Migrations Project

1930年代，美國中部颳起「黑色風暴」。大風把乾土吹上天，農田全毀了。

華盛頓大學的歷史學家葛瑞格里，花了二十多年，一個人做出免費的互動地圖網站。你可以一個縣一個縣點下去，看1935到1940年間，三、四十萬名農民從奧克拉荷馬等州搬到加州，落腳在哪裡。

課本只畫一個箭頭，這裡卻看得見每一個地方。

In the 1930s, "black blizzards" swept across America's Midwest. Gusts of wind lifted the dry topsoil into the sky, and the farmland was ruined. Over more than twenty years, Washington University historian Gregory built a free, interactive map website by himself. You can click through it county by county to see where the 300,000 to 400,000 farmers who moved from states like Oklahoma to California between 1935 and 1940 ended up settling. Textbooks draw only a single arrow, but here you can see every individual place.
