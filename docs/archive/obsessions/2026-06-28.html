迪斯可大王高凌風：把西洋歌變成台灣味

來源：South China Morning Post

一九八零年，台灣藝人高凌風出了一張迪斯可（一種很會讓人想跳舞的音樂）唱片。他把好幾首西洋紅歌填上中文詞，放進同一張黑膠唱片裡。

那時候的台灣人大多沒聽過原版。一九八三年，他把一首七分鐘的英國名曲剪成三分鐘，還把失戀唱得像青橄欖一樣又苦又澀。

這段被人忘記的音樂故事，最近又紅了起來，連芝加哥的唱片行都買得到。

In 1980, Taiwanese performer Kao Ling-feng released a disco album—a genre of music that makes people want to dance. He took several hit songs from the West, added Chinese lyrics, and pressed them onto a single vinyl record. Most Taiwanese at the time had never heard the originals. In 1983, he trimmed a seven-minute British classic down to three minutes, transforming a song about heartbreak into something bittersweet and raw, like biting into an unripe olive. This forgotten chapter of musical history has recently surged back into popularity, with copies now available even in record shops across Chicago.

雪地裡的貂熊：芬蘭森林的三月守候

來源：Erä-Eero Wildlife Centre

在芬蘭東部的大森林裡，有一座叫 Erä-Eero 的野生動物中心。這裡被說是全世界最適合看野生貂熊（一種少見又很強壯的動物）的地方。

客人會在湖邊有暖氣的小屋裡過夜，靜靜等動物出現。三月最剛好：白天變長了，深色的貂熊在白雪上特別好認。

運氣好的話，還能看到老鷹、野狼，甚至是剛睡醒的棕熊。

In the vast forests of eastern Finland sits a wildlife center called Erä-Eero, widely regarded as the world's best place to observe the wolverine—a rare and formidable animal. Guests spend their nights in heated cabins overlooking a lake, waiting patiently for wildlife to appear. March is ideal: daylight stretches longer, and the wolverine's dark fur stands out sharply against the white snow. With luck, visitors may also spot eagles, wolves, or even a brown bear just emerging from hibernation.

走進老火車：普爾曼鐵路日

來源：U.S. National Park Service

七月二十五、二十六日，芝加哥南邊的普爾曼國家歷史公園會舉辦「鐵路日」。客人可以走進三節保存下來的老火車廂，其中一節是一九五零年的臥鋪車。

導覽員會帶大家走看這座公司小鎮，講這裡的工人怎麼組成第一個由黑人帶頭、並且爭取到合約的大工會。

當天買票是二十五美元，十二歲以下的小孩免費。

On July 25 and 26, Pullman National Historic Site, south of Chicago, will host "Railroad Day." Visitors can step inside three preserved vintage train cars, including a 1950 sleeper car. Tour guides will lead guests through this company town, telling the story of how workers here organized the first major labor union led by Black workers that successfully negotiated a contract. Admission is $25 per ticket; children under 12 are free.

十八年的網路話題，變成一張圖

來源：hackernewstrends.com

有一個人自己做了一個小工具，叫 Hacker Trends。他把一個很有名的科技論壇十八年來的四千五百萬篇貼文和留言，全部整理成圖表。

你只要打一個字，比如某個程式語言或某家公司的名字，就能看到大家這些年來聊它聊得多不多。

你還能把幾個字放在一起比，看不同的人工智慧模型怎麼一個接一個紅起來。

Someone created a small tool called Hacker Trends. They took forty-five million posts and comments from a famous tech forum spanning eighteen years and organized everything into charts. You just type a word—say, a programming language or a company name—and you can see how much people have been talking about it over the years. You can even compare multiple terms side by side, watching how different AI models caught fire one after another.
