八部合音，遇見大提琴

來源：World Music Network / NPR / RootsWorld

一九五二年，學者錄下台灣布農族的「祈禱小米豐收歌」（求小米長得好的歌）。族人沒有樂譜，也沒有指揮，卻能同時唱出八條不同的聲音，慢慢調整到合在一起。

二〇〇〇年，美國大提琴家大衛．達林走進台東山裡的霧鹿部落。他把琴聲放進這片歌聲中，錄成二〇〇三年的專輯 Mudanin Kata。人聲和琴聲有時互相拉扯，有時美得讓人靜下來。

In 1952, scholars recorded the Bunun people of Taiwan singing the "Prayer for Millet Abundance"—a song asking for millet to flourish. Without sheet music or a conductor, the tribal members produced eight distinct vocal lines, gradually adjusting them until they harmonized. In 2000, American cellist David Darling entered the Wulu settlement in the mountains of Taitung. He wove his cello into this singing and recorded the result as the 2003 album Mudanin Kata. The human voices and cello sometimes strain against each other, and sometimes their beauty is profound enough to quiet the listener.

內衣、飲料，和漫長的冬夜

來源：thisisFINLAND (Finnish Ministry for Foreign Affairs)

二〇一五年，芬蘭是世界上第一個推出「國家表情符號」的國家。六十個圖案裡，有兩個畫著 kalsarikännit：一個人穿著內衣待在家，手裡拿著飲料，哪裡都不想去。

這個字由芬蘭語的「內衣」和「喝醉」合成。芬蘭外交部大方地說，這是熬過漫長黑夜的好辦法。二〇一八年還有人為它寫了一本書。

這也提醒旅人：芬蘭人很看重安靜的獨處。一個待在家的晚上，和一場熱鬧的聚會一樣珍貴。

In 2015, Finland became the first country in the world to launch an official set of national emojis. Among the sixty designs, two depicted kalsarikännit: a person at home in underwear, holding a drink, with no desire to go anywhere. The word combines the Finnish for "underwear" and "to get drunk." The Finnish Foreign Ministry cheerfully explained that this was a perfect way to endure the long dark nights. Someone even wrote a book about it in 2018. It's a telling reminder for visitors: Finns deeply value quiet solitude. An evening at home is just as precious as a lively gathering.

學會飛：碼頭邊的空中舞蹈課

來源：Aerial Dance Chicago

九月十二日，芝加哥的空中舞蹈團 Aerial Dance Chicago 在海軍碼頭舉辦一年一度的免費體驗。先看一段表演，接著換觀眾上場。

大家可以抓住長長的綢帶（垂在空中的布），學著往上爬、在半空中把身體停住。不用經驗，不分年紀，也不用付一毛錢。

這場活動已經連辦好幾年，專門為完全的新手打開一扇門。

On September 12th, Chicago's aerial dance company Aerial Dance Chicago hosts its annual free experience at Navy Pier. First comes a performance, then the audience gets their turn. Everyone can grab the long silk ribbons hanging in the air, learn to climb up, and hold their body suspended in mid-air. No experience required, any age welcome, completely free. The event has been running for years, specifically designed to open the door for absolute beginners.

一個人的服裝史博物館

來源：costumes.org

一九九六年，戲劇教授 Tara Maginnis 在舊金山看到一台能查資料的旅遊機器，心想：衣服的歷史也該有這樣一台。

她一個人動手，每天寫一頁，寫了整整一年，做出網站 costumes.org。裡面從中世紀排到二十世紀，附上老時裝圖片，還教人怎麼把戲服縫出來。

快三十年了，她仍然獨自照顧這個網站，一封一封回讀者的信。

In 1996, drama professor Tara Maginnis saw an information kiosk for tourists in San Francisco and thought: the history of clothing deserves something like this. Working alone, writing one page a day, she spent an entire year building costumes.org. The site spans from the Middle Ages to the twentieth century, featuring vintage costume photographs and guides on how to sew period garments. Nearly three decades later, she still maintains the site solo, answering readers' letters one at a time.
