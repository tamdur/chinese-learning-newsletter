林強的台灣聲音地圖

來源：Pure Person Press (Bandcamp)

「向前走」是一九九〇年代最紅的台語歌，唱出台灣人心裡的希望。寫這首歌的林強後來不當歌星了，他幫導演侯孝賢配樂，也背起錄音機走遍全島。

二〇二〇到二〇二一年，他錄下西螺大橋上幾千位媽祖進香客的腳步、老糖廠裡搖椅的聲音，還有竹林裡孩子的笑聲。二〇二五年十一月，這些聲音交給謝明諺等音樂家重新創作，變成專輯《台灣的聲音》。

"Marching Forward" was the biggest Taiwanese-language song of the 1990s, giving voice to the hope Taiwanese people carried in their hearts. Lim Giong, who wrote it, later stopped being a pop star. He went on to compose film scores for director Hou Hsiao-hsien, and he shouldered a field recorder and walked the whole island.

Between 2020 and 2021 he recorded the footsteps of thousands of Mazu pilgrims crossing the Xiluo Bridge, the sound of a rocking chair in an old sugar factory, and children laughing in a bamboo grove. In November 2025 those sounds were handed to musicians including Minyen Hsieh to rework, becoming the album "Sounds of Taiwan."

坦佩雷市場裡的黑香腸

來源：City of Tampere / Visit Tampere

芬蘭坦佩雷市的市場大廳蓋於一九〇一年，是北歐最大的室內市場。裡面的肉攤賣一種黑香腸，用豬肉、豬血和黑麥粉做成，配著越橘（一種紅色小果子）果醬和芥末一起吃，這樣吃已經有三百多年。

當地人不秤重量，而是直接說要買幾歐元。想要一整條彎成馬蹄形的，就說「來回車票」。市場整年都開，三月去芬蘭也吃得到。

The market hall in Tampere, Finland was built in 1901 and is the largest indoor market in the Nordic countries. The meat counter inside sells a black sausage made from pork, pig's blood, and rye flour, eaten with lingonberry jam (a small red berry) and mustard — a way of eating it that goes back more than three hundred years.

Locals don't order by weight; they simply say how many euros' worth they want. If you want a whole one bent into a horseshoe, you ask for a "round-trip ticket." The market is open all year, so you can still get it on a March visit to Finland.

蟲蟲嘉年華：學蜜蜂跳搖擺舞

來源：Peggy Notebaert Nature Museum

八月四日下午一點到四點，芝加哥林肯公園裡的自然博物館要辦「蟲蟲嘉年華」。

在館裡的昆蟲動物園，你可以親手摸摸本地的昆蟲和蜘蛛，也能用顯微鏡看牠們的樣子。最好玩的是學蜜蜂跳「搖擺舞」：蜜蜂走出像數字八的路線，告訴同伴食物在哪個方向、有多遠。伊利諾大學的研究員也會說明，昆蟲的身體怎麼幫人想出新的工程設計。

On August 4, from 1 to 4 in the afternoon, the nature museum in Chicago's Lincoln Park is holding "Bugapalooza."

At the museum's bug zoo you can touch local insects and spiders with your own hands, and look at them under a microscope. The best part is learning the honeybee's "waggle dance": the bee traces a path shaped like a figure eight to tell its hive-mates which direction the food is and how far away. Researchers from the University of Illinois will also explain how insects' bodies help people come up with new engineering designs.

一個人的世界燈塔名冊

來源：Lighthouse Directory (ibiblio.org)

一九九九年，美國北卡羅來納大學一位數學教授羅利特開始拍燈塔，本來只是自己的興趣。到了二〇〇九年，他的個人網站已經把全世界的燈塔都收了進來。

今天這份名冊記下兩萬四千六百多座燈塔，照國家和海岸線排好，很多還附上他親自拍的照片和歷史說明。網站放在大學的免費數位圖書館裡，沒有公司出錢，他一個人做了二十五年，到現在還在更新。

In 1999 Russ Rowlett, a mathematics professor at the University of North Carolina in the United States, started photographing lighthouses — at first just as a personal hobby. By 2009 his personal website had gathered in lighthouses from all over the world.

Today the directory records more than 24,600 lighthouses, arranged by country and coastline, many of them with photographs he took himself and notes on their history. The site sits in the university's free digital library, with no company paying for it. He has done it alone for twenty-five years, and he is still updating it.
