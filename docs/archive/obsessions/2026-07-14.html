跳舞時代——台灣流行歌的幕後推手陳君玉

來源：Taipei Times — Taiwan in Time

陳君玉在一九零六年出生於台北大稻埕，父親是拉人力車的工人，他小學沒念完就輟學（半路離開學校）。可是在一九三三年，他當上古倫美亞唱片公司文藝部的主任。他找來作曲家鄧雨賢、歌手純純、作詞人李臨秋，一起做出台語流行歌。

他自己最有名的歌叫「跳舞時代」，開頭就唱：「我是文明女，東西南北自由行。」在那個年代，這句話非常新潮。陳君玉在一九六三年過世，後來大家慢慢忘了他。可是這首歌一直流傳下去，二零零三年得到金馬獎的紀錄片，就用它來當片名。

Chen Jun-yu was born in 1906 in Dadaocheng, a Taipei neighborhood. His father worked as a rickshaw puller, and Chen himself left school before finishing primary education. Yet by 1933, he had become the director of arts at Columbia Records, where he brought together composer Deng Yu-xian, singer Chun Chun, and lyricist Li Lin-qiu to create Taiwanese pop songs.

His most celebrated song was "The Dance Era," which opens with the lyrics: "I am a modern woman, free to go wherever I please." For its time, such a sentiment was strikingly progressive.

Chen Jun-yu passed away in 1963, and over time he faded from public memory. Yet his song endured. In 2003, a documentary that won the Golden Horse Award chose "The Dance Era" as its title, preserving his legacy.

森林上桌——赫爾辛基的十六人餐廳Grön

來源：Restaurant Grön / MICHELIN Guide

在芬蘭首都赫爾辛基，有一家只有十六個座位的小餐廳，叫做Grön。二零二六年，它拿到米其林兩顆星，是全芬蘭第二家。主廚托尼親自到城市附近的森林和海邊，採集野生的香草、菇類和各種植物。

餐廳的架子上，擺滿他做的醃菜（用鹽和醋泡過的菜）和發酵食物。因為芬蘭有「自然人人可享」的權利，大家都能自由走進森林採集。三月來這裡，可以在一個晚上，嚐到整片北方森林的味道。不過座位很少，一定要早點訂位。

In Helsinki, Finland's capital, there is a small restaurant with only sixteen seats called Grön. In 2026, it earned a two-star Michelin rating, making it the second in all of Finland. Chef Tony personally ventures into the forests and coastlines near the city to forage for wild herbs, mushrooms, and various plants. The restaurant's shelves are filled with his homemade pickles and fermented foods. Because Finland recognizes the right of "universal access to nature," everyone is free to walk into the forest and forage. Visit in March, and in a single evening you can taste the flavors of the entire northern forest. Just remember: seats are limited, so you'll need to book well in advance.

免費電車巡遊——布朗維爾六家藝廊之夜

來源：Time Out Chicago / Bronzeville Art District

這個星期五，七月十七號，芝加哥南邊的布朗維爾（Bronzeville）要舉辦免費的電車藝廊之旅，今年是第二十季。從六月到九月，每個月的第三個星期五都會舉行，不用買票，只要先報名就好。

一台雙層巴士會載著大家，繞過六個地方，包括Gallery Guichard等藝廊。每一站都有導覽員介紹作品，還有現場音樂和飲料。晚上五點四十五分，在47街436號的Gallery Guichard開門，想去的人要早點排隊。

This Friday, July 17th, Bronzeville on Chicago's South Side will host a free trolley art gallery tour—the 20th season of this annual event. Running from June through September, it takes place on the third Friday of each month. There's no admission fee; you just need to sign up in advance. A double-decker bus will transport visitors around six locations, including galleries like Gallery Guichard. At each stop, a guide introduces the artworks, and the evening features live music and refreshments. The tour opens at 5:45 PM at Gallery Guichard, located at 436 East 47th Street. If you're interested in attending, arrive early to line up.

挖出老唱片——一個人的世界稀有唱片檔案

來源：Excavated Shellac

從二零零七年開始，住在洛杉磯的收藏家強納森·沃德，經營一個叫Excavated Shellac的網站。他把珍貴的七十八轉老唱片轉成音樂檔案，還寫下仔細的研究筆記。

這些唱片來自很多冷門的地方，像是索馬利亞、葉門、喀麥隆和烏茲別克。他一張一張追查，找出這些地方最早的錄音。後來這個計畫還做成四張CD的專輯，得到葛萊美獎的提名。可是這個免費網站到二零二五年都還在更新，一直是最主要的檔案。

Since 2007, Jonathan Ward, a collector based in Los Angeles, has been running a website called Excavated Shellac. He digitizes rare 78 rpm records and accompanies them with meticulously researched notes. The recordings originate from far-flung and overlooked places—Somalia, Yemen, Cameroon, Uzbekistan—and Ward has painstakingly tracked down what appear to be the earliest recordings from each location. The project eventually spawned a four-CD album series that earned Grammy nominations. Yet the free website itself remains the primary archive, continuously updated through 2025.
