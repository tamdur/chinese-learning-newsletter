文夏：混血歌的起點

來源：The News Lens

一九五六年，台南歌手文夏唱出〈飄浪之女〉。這張唱片來自台南的小公司「亞洲唱片」，也打開了台灣「混血歌」的年代。

文夏沒有學過作曲，一開始只在電台唱歌。他把日本演歌的曲子配上台語歌詞，再加上曼波、恰恰這些跳舞的節奏，聽起來又新又熟悉。

他一生錄了大約一千二百首歌，其中三百多首是日本歌改寫的。從小小的錄音室，他打下了台語流行歌的基礎。

In 1956, Tainan singer Wen Hsia released "Girl of the Drifting Waves." The record came from Asia Records, a small Tainan label, and it opened the era of Taiwan's "hybrid songs." Wen Hsia had never studied composition. At first, he sang only on the radio. He set Taiwanese lyrics to melodies borrowed from Japanese enka, then added dance rhythms like mambo and cha-cha, so the result sounded both fresh and familiar. Over his lifetime he recorded about 1,200 songs, more than 300 of them adapted from Japanese tunes. From those small recording sessions, he laid the foundation for Taiwanese-language popular music.

在石頭裡挖出的教堂

來源：MyHelsinki / Docomomo Finland

在赫爾辛基，有一座教堂不是蓋在石頭上，而是炸開花崗岩（一種很硬的石頭），直接在石頭裡面挖出來的。設計它的是一對建築師兄弟。

這座「岩石教堂」因為戰爭拖了很久，一九六九年才完成。裡面的牆是粗粗的原石，頭上是淺淺的銅屋頂，一圈天窗讓陽光沿著石牆流下來。

石牆讓聲音特別好聽，所以這裡常有音樂會。想避開人潮，可以一早去，或是晚上來聽一場演出。

In Helsinki, there is a church that was not built on top of stone but carved directly out of solid rock. Its builders blasted into granite (a very hard stone) and hollowed out the interior. It was designed by a pair of architect brothers. Because of the war, the "Rock Church," as it is known, took a long time to finish and was not completed until 1969. Inside, the walls are left as rough, raw stone, and above them sits a shallow copper roof. A ring of skylights lets sunlight trickle down along the stone walls. The stone walls give the room an especially beautiful sound, so concerts are held here often. To avoid the crowds, go early in the morning, or come in the evening to hear a performance.

相撲力士來到芝加哥

來源：Sports Travel Magazine

美國第一個職業相撲聯盟「相撲冠軍聯盟」，今年十月十日在德州奧斯汀開打。

第二站就是芝加哥！十一月七日，比賽在 Credit Union 1 Arena 舉行。高大的力士（相撲選手）在圓圈裡用力互推，先被推出圈外或倒下的人就輸了。

現在已經可以買票，下一站要等到十二月在加州。在美國中西部看現場職業相撲，這樣的機會以前幾乎沒有過。

The Sumo Champion League, America's first professional sumo league, kicked off on October 10 this year in Austin, Texas. Chicago is the second stop! The match will be held at the Credit Union 1 Arena on November 7. Towering rikishi (sumo wrestlers) shove against each other with all their might inside the ring, and whoever is pushed out of the circle or knocked to the ground first loses. Tickets are already on sale, and the next stop, in California, isn't until December. Until now, a chance to see professional sumo live in the American Midwest has been almost unheard of.

用文字畫畫的老網路

來源：asciiart.eu

在表情符號出現以前，有一群人只用鍵盤上的字母和符號畫圖，這叫 ASCII 藝術。

從一九九三年到二〇一三年，他們把作品貼在 Usenet 新聞群組（早期的網路討論區），每張圖下面都簽上自己的網名。

後來群組安靜了，放資料的電腦一台台關掉。 ASCII Art Archive 的主人把十二萬五千多則留言保存下來，一個字都沒改。現在打開它，就像翻開一本網路小世界的簽名簿。

Before emoticons came along, a group of people drew pictures using nothing but the letters and symbols on their keyboards. This was called ASCII art. From 1993 to 2013, they posted their work to Usenet newsgroups (early online discussion boards), and signed each picture with their online handle. Later the groups fell silent, and the computers storing the data were switched off one by one. The keeper of the ASCII Art Archive has preserved more than 125,000 posts, without changing a single character. Opening it now is like leafing through the guestbook of a small online world.
