林強與千禧曼波的電子聲音

千禧曼波 OST (2001)

二〇〇一年，侯孝賢的電影《千禧曼波》在坎城影展亮相。為它寫音樂的人是林強——一個從台語流行歌手變成電子音樂先鋒（走在最前面的人）的藝術家。

這張配樂用閃亮的電子節拍和飄浮的聲響，畫出台北年輕人在深夜裡漂流的感覺。代表曲〈一個純潔的人〉聽起來像城市的呼吸。雖然叫做配樂，但它本身就是一張了不起的電子專輯。

In 2001, director Hou Hsiao-hsien's film "Millennium Mambo" premiered at the Cannes Film Festival. The composer who wrote its music was Lin Qiang—an artist who transformed from a Taiwanese pop singer into an electronic music pioneer (someone who walks at the forefront).

This soundtrack paints the feeling of young Taipei residents drifting through the late night with shimmering electronic beats and floating sounds. The signature track "A Pure Person" sounds like the city's breath. Though called a soundtrack, it stands as a remarkable electronic album in its own right.

在冰海上漂浮：芬蘭破冰船桑波號

Icebreaker Sampo, Kemi

在芬蘭北部的凱米港，有一艘退休的破冰船叫做「桑波號」。每年一月到三月，它載著遊客開進結冰的波的尼亞灣。船頭壓碎白色的冰面，發出巨大的聲響。

最奇妙的部分在後面：客人穿上亮橘色的防寒衣，然後跳進零下的北極海水裡，像軟木塞一樣浮在冰塊之間。天空是灰白色的，世界安靜得不真實。

In the port of Kemi in northern Finland, there is a retired icebreaker called the Sampo. Every January through March, it carries tourists into the frozen Gulf of Bothnia. The ship's bow crushes the white ice, producing a thunderous sound.

The most remarkable part comes after: passengers put on bright orange thermal suits, then jump into the sub-zero Arctic seawater, floating among the ice floes like corks. The sky is gray-white, and the world is unnaturally quiet.

拇指大小的壁爐：芝加哥迷你模型展

Chicago International Miniature Show

四月十日到十二日，芝加哥歐乘機場旁邊的 Marriott 飯店會舉辦一場迷你模型展。這個展已經辦了四十八年，每次有兩百五十位藝術家參加。

他們做的東西小到讓人不敢相信：只有拇指大小的壁爐，裡面的火真的會亮；完美的小房子、小家具、小街道。門票十二美元，但你會在裡面待上好幾個小時。

From April 10 to 12, a miniature model exhibition will be held at the Marriott Hotel next to Chicago O'Hare Airport. This show has been running for 48 years, with 250 artists participating each time.

The things they create are so small you can hardly believe it: fireplaces the size of a thumb, with real glowing fire inside; perfect little houses, tiny furniture, miniature streets. Admission is 12 dollars, but you'll spend hours in there.

一個人的印刷博物館：Circuitous Root

circuitousroot.com

大衛·麥克米倫是一個業餘的鑄字師（做金屬字的人）和鐵匠。他的網站 Circuitous Root 是一座關於老印刷技術的寶庫。

裡面有熱金屬鑄字、活版印刷、電報機的故事，還有一八四二年的字體設計專利。每一頁都是他在工作室裡親手記錄的筆記。小心：打開這個網站，一個下午就沒了。

David MacMillan is an amateur type caster (a person who makes metal type) and blacksmith. His website, Circuitous Root, is a treasure trove about old printing technologies.

Inside you'll find stories about hot metal type casting, letterpress printing, telegraph machines, and a typeface design patent from 1842. Every page is a note he's recorded by hand in his workshop. Fair warning: open this website and your entire afternoon will disappear.
