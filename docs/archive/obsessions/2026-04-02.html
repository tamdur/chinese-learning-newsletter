蛋堡《家常音樂》——嘻哈走進金曲殿堂

Taiwan Beats

蛋堡（Soft Lipa）是台灣嘻哈歌手，本名杜振熙。2020年女兒出生後，他在家裡做了一張專輯，叫《家常音樂》。這張唱片把爵士樂和嘻哈混在一起，聽起來很放鬆，像在家煮菜一樣自然。結果它在第32屆金曲獎拿下最佳男歌手、最佳專輯、最佳音樂錄影帶三項大獎——嘻哈音樂第一次做到。評審說，華語音樂正式進入了「嘻哈的大時代」。

Soft Lipa is a Taiwanese hip-hop artist whose real name is Du Zhenxi. After his daughter was born in 2020, he recorded an album at home called "Home-Style Music." The record blends jazz and hip-hop together, sounding relaxed and as natural as cooking at home. It went on to win three major awards at the 32nd Golden Melody Awards: Best Male Singer, Best Album, and Best Music Video — a first for hip-hop music. The judges said that Mandarin music had officially entered the "golden age of hip-hop."

伊納里——薩米人的文化首都

Visit Inari / Siida Museum

伊納里（Inari）在北極圈以北350公里，是芬蘭薩米人的文化中心。薩米人是歐洲唯一被認可的原住民，在這裡住了幾千年。鎮上的「西達博物館」（Siida）2024年拿下歐洲年度最佳博物館，展覽帶你認識薩米人的歷史。三月底還有「馴鹿盃」賽跑——200頭馴鹿在結冰的湖面上比賽，從1959年辦到現在。晚上可以住玻璃屋頂小屋，躺著看極光。

Located 350 kilometers north of the Arctic Circle, Inari is the cultural center of Finland's Sámi people. The Sámi are Europe's only recognized indigenous population, and have lived here for thousands of years. The town's Siida Museum won the title of Europe's Best Museum in 2024, with exhibitions that introduce visitors to Sámi history. At the end of March, the Reindeer Cup race takes place — 200 reindeer compete on the frozen lake, a tradition running since 1959. In the evening, you can stay in a glass-roofed cabin and watch the northern lights from bed.

國際爵士日在芝加哥——走進傳奇的論壇咖啡廳

Choose Chicago

4月25日，芝加哥將成為聯合國「國際爵士日」的全球主辦城市，今年也是Miles Davis（邁爾斯·戴維斯）的100歲生日。重點活動在Bronzeville的「論壇咖啡廳」（The Forum）。這棟1897年的老建築，當年Louis Armstrong和Nat King Cole都在這裡演出過。當天有免費導覽、爵士巴士之旅和現場表演，爵士週一直到5月2日。全部免費。

On April 25th, Chicago will serve as the United Nations' global host city for International Jazz Day, marking what would have been Miles Davis's 100th birthday. The centerpiece event takes place at The Forum in Bronzeville, an 1897 building where Louis Armstrong and Nat King Cole once performed. The day features free tours, jazz bus rides, and live performances, with Jazz Week running through May 2nd. Everything is free.
