陳珊妮的藝術流行實驗

來源：Rate Your Music

二○○四年十月，陳珊妮發行第六張專輯《後來我們都哭了》。這張專輯把弦樂、電子音樂和慵懶（放鬆的）節奏混合在一起，風格接近日本的「澀谷系」音樂。封面照片拍攝於國父紀念館，帶有哥德式的暗色美感。

隔年，她在第十六屆金曲獎拿下最佳國語專輯和最佳專輯製作人，成為史上第一位獲得製作人獎的女性。

In October 2004, Sandii Chen released her sixth album, "Later We All Cried." The album blends strings, electronic music, and languid rhythms, drawing stylistic inspiration from Japan's "Shibuya-kei" sound. The cover photograph, shot at the Sun Yat-sen Memorial Hall, carries a gothic aesthetic with dark, moody tones. The following year, she won Best Mandarin Album and Best Album Producer at the 16th Golden Melody Awards, becoming the first woman in history to receive the producer award.

三月的費斯卡村：雪中的設計小鎮

來源：Finland, Naturally

費斯卡村位於赫爾辛基以西約一個半小時車程。一六四九年，一位荷蘭商人在這裡建立了煉鐵（把鐵礦變成鐵的）工廠。後來這個品牌以橘色手把的剪刀聞名世界。

一九八○年代，公司把河邊空出來的紅磚工廠改成藝廊和工作室。今天有超過二十間全年開放。三月造訪，幾乎沒有其他遊客，只有你和覆著白雪的安靜村莊。

Fiskars Village is located about an hour and a half west of Helsinki. In 1649, a Dutch merchant established an iron foundry here—a factory that transformed raw iron ore into usable iron. The brand later became famous worldwide for scissors with orange handles. In the 1980s, the company converted the vacant red brick factories along the river into galleries and studios. Today, more than twenty remain open year-round. A visit in March offers solitude—few other tourists, just you and the quiet village blanketed in white snow.

木頭雕塑也能跳舞

來源：City of Chicago / DCASE

五月十四日（星期三）晚上六點，芝加哥文化中心的舞蹈工作室對外開放。駐村藝術家將展示一件特別的作品：與真人一樣大的木頭雕塑，同時是舞伴、道具和舞台風景。

藝術家會解說如何設計這些物件，以及和舞者一起測試的過程。免費入場，提供手語翻譯。

On the evening of May 14th (Wednesday) at 6 p.m., the dance studio at the Chicago Cultural Center opens its doors to the public. The artist-in-residence will present a special work: life-sized wooden sculptures that double as dance partners, props, and stage scenery all at once. The artist will explain the design process behind these objects and share what it was like testing them out alongside the dancers. Admission is free, and ASL interpretation will be provided.

鑄排機的最後百科全書

來源：Linotype.wiki

Linotype鑄排機（用熱金屬排字的機器）從一八八○年代到一九八○年代，幫全世界的報紙印刷了一百年的文字。現在會操作這些機器的人越來越少了。

荷蘭人 Maarten Renckels 建立了 Linotype.wiki，一個人記錄所有關於這些機器的知識：字體資料庫、現存機器的位置、維修指南。他正在和時間賽跑，趕在最後的老師傅離開之前，把他們的經驗留下來。

The Linotype casting machine — a hot metal typesetting device — printed a century of words for newspapers worldwide, from the 1880s through the 1980s. Now, fewer and fewer people know how to operate these machines. Dutch enthusiast Maarten Renckels created Linotype.wiki, a one-man effort to document all knowledge about these machines: a typeface database, locations of surviving machines, and repair guides. He is racing against time to preserve the experience of the last master operators before they are gone.
