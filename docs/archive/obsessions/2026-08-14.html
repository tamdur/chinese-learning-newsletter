舞池裡的女聲：台灣迪斯可的失落年代

來源：Aberrant Records / The Analog Vault

一九七零年代的台灣還在戒嚴（政府管得特別嚴的時期），可是台北夜總會裡的音樂很狂野。一群女歌手錄了迪斯可和放克唱片，只印一次就不見了。

二零二零年，一家專門挖掘老唱片的公司走遍台北的舊唱片行，把這些歌找回來，做成一張合輯（把很多歌收在一起的唱片）。裡面有華麗的弦樂、破音的電吉他，還有國語歌聲飄在鼓點上。

日本有城市流行樂，韓國有演歌，台灣的迪斯可一樣特別，只是很少人記得。

In the 1970s, Taiwan was under martial law — a period of rigid government control — yet Taipei's nightclubs thrummed with uninhibited music. A group of female singers recorded disco and funk records, each pressed in a single run, then vanished.

In 2020, a label dedicated to rescuing forgotten vinyl worked through Taipei's old record shops, recovered these songs, and compiled them into an anthology. The collection is full of lush strings, fuzzed-out electric guitar, and Mandarin vocals riding atop pounding drums.

Japan has city pop, Korea has enka. Taiwan's disco was every bit as distinctive — it is just that almost no one remembers it.

松焦油之都：奧盧最富有的那一百年

來源：Oulu2026.eu / Turkansaari Open Air Museum

一八六五年，芬蘭北部的城市奧盧運出八萬桶松焦油（松樹慢慢燒出來的黑油），是當時全世界最大的焦油港。英國、法國和美國的船隊都靠它來防水。

焦油是奧盧河邊的村民在森林土坑裡燒出來的，再用平底木船順著河水送到港口。這門生意讓窮困的農村過上好日子。

今天在河中小島上的圖爾坎薩里露天博物館，還看得到當年的運油木船和重建的土坑。三月來芬蘭的旅人多半直奔赫爾辛基或拉普蘭，奧盧常常被跳過。

In 1865, the northern Finnish city of Oulu shipped out eighty thousand barrels of pine tar — the black oil produced by slowly burning pine — making it the largest tar port in the world at the time. The fleets of Britain, France and America all relied on it for waterproofing.

Villagers along the Oulu River burned the tar in forest pits, then floated it down to the harbour in flat-bottomed wooden boats. The trade lifted impoverished rural villages into prosperity.

Today, at the Turkansaari open-air museum on an island in the river, you can still see the original tar boats and the reconstructed pits. Travellers coming to Finland in March mostly head straight for Helsinki or Lapland — Oulu is usually skipped.

芝加哥的第一場尼泊爾街頭節

來源：Block Club Chicago

八月十六日星期日晚上七點，芝加哥北邊的艾傑瓦特社區要辦全市第一場尼泊爾街頭節，地點在肯莫爾大道五六零二號，免費參加。

主辦的是非營利組織「尼泊爾援助」。當晚有尼泊爾食物、現場音樂和舞蹈。尼泊爾移民在芝加哥人數慢慢變多，卻很少被人注意，這是他們第一次辦這樣的節慶。

同一個週末，城裡還有規模大得多的水上飛行表演。小節慶就藏在大活動旁邊。

On Sunday, August 16th at 7 p.m., the Edgewater neighborhood on Chicago's North Side will host the city's first-ever Nepali Street Festival, at 5602 N Kenmore Ave. Admission is free.

The nonprofit Nepalese Aid is organizing the event, with Nepali food, live music and dance. Chicago's Nepali immigrant community has grown steadily but draws little attention. This is their first festival of its kind.

That same weekend, the city hosts the far larger Air and Water Show. The small festival sits quietly alongside the big spectacle.

一個人的橋樑百科：二十年，尋遍全美老鐵橋

來源：HistoricBridges.org

二零零三年，美國密西根州的工程師霍爾斯開了一個網站，本來只想記錄家鄉的老橋。

二十多年後，它變成北美橋樑工程的百科全書。他寫長文介紹罕見的桁架（用鐵條交叉搭成的橋身骨架）樣式，像佩格拉姆式、凱洛格式，全國只剩下幾座還站著。

其中一篇談弓弦式桁架，那是最老也最少見的鐵橋。橋頂那道彎彎的弧線，其實是十九世紀工程師省事的辦法，開車經過的人幾乎沒發現。

In 2003, Nathan Holth, an engineer from Michigan, started a website meant only to document the old bridges of his home state.

Two decades on, it has become an encyclopedia of North American bridge engineering. Holth writes long essays on rare truss designs — the Pegram truss, the Kellogg truss — types where only a handful of examples are still standing nationwide.

One essay examines the bowstring truss, the oldest and rarest iron bridge type of all. That gentle curve along the top of the bridge was in fact a nineteenth-century engineer's shortcut — something drivers crossing it almost never notice.
