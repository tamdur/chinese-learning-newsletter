陳珊妮：自己當自己的導演

來源：Wikipedia

陳珊妮是台灣少見的女性音樂製作人，在那個大家以為調音台只屬於男生的年代，她成了金曲獎史上第一位得到「最佳專輯製作人」的女生。

二○○八年，她推出《如果有一件事是重要的》，把古典弦樂、電子搖滾和 DJ 音效層層疊在一起，還翻唱了日本歌姬美空雲雀的〈蘋果花〉，並把蕭邦的〈離別曲〉改成和田馥甄合唱的新歌。隔年她拿下最佳國語女歌手，評審說她「演活了自己的聲音」。

Chen Shan-ni is a rare female music producer in Taiwan. In an era when people assumed the mixing console belonged only to men, she became the first woman in Golden Melody Awards history to win Best Album Producer. In 2008, she released "If There Is One Thing That Matters," layering classical strings, electronic rock, and DJ effects together. She also covered Japanese diva Misora Hibari's "Apple Blossom" and reworked Chopin's "Farewell Waltz" into a new duet with Hebe Tien. The following year she won Best Mandarin Female Singer, with judges praising her for having "brought her own voice to life."

桑波號：讓遊客漂在冰海上的老破冰船

來源：Visit Finland

桑波號在一九六○年於芬蘭赫爾辛基的船廠造好，接下來近三十年，它認真地在波的尼亞灣裡破開厚厚的海冰，替船隻開路。

一九八七年退休後，凱米市把它買下，改成給遊客搭的破冰船。今天四小時的行程裡，旅客可以看老船身壓碎一公尺厚的海冰，還能穿上橘色的防寒衣，親自爬到冰上，讓身體浮在結冰的海裡。它每年吸引約一萬名來自五十多國的遊客，一直開到三月底。

Built in 1960 at a shipyard in Helsinki, Finland, the Sampo spent nearly three decades dutifully breaking through thick sea ice in the Bay of Bothnia, opening routes for vessels. After its retirement in 1987, the city of Kemi purchased it and converted it into a tourist icebreaker.

During today's four-hour voyage, passengers can watch the aging ship's hull crush meter-thick sea ice, and can also don bright orange thermal suits to climb directly onto the ice and float in the frozen sea.

The ship attracts roughly 10,000 visitors from more than 50 countries each year, operating through the end of March.

詩的擂台賽：紐伯里圖書館慶祝四十年

來源：Newberry Library

一九八六年，詩人馬克‧史密斯在芝加哥的綠磨坊酒吧辦了「上城詩擂台」，他讓觀眾替每首詩打分數，把安靜的朗讀變成大家一起參與的比賽。

七月二十五日下午一點到四點，紐伯里圖書館要慶祝這個活動滿四十年，還會展出史密斯多年來收藏的計分卡和海報。史密斯本人會到場說故事，現場也有詩人表演，還準備了點心。上城詩擂台是全美最長壽的詩歌活動，至今仍每月在綠磨坊登場。

In 1986, poet Marc Smith launched the Uptown Poetry Slam at Chicago's Green Mill Tavern, transforming quiet poetry readings into an interactive competition where the audience scored each poem. To celebrate the event's fortieth anniversary, the Newberry Library will host a gathering on July 25th from 1 PM to 4 PM, featuring an exhibition of scorecards and posters from Smith's collection spanning decades.

Smith himself will attend to share stories, poets will perform live, and refreshments will be served. The Uptown Poetry Slam remains America's longest-running poetry event, still taking place monthly at the Green Mill.

Rockstar：用搖滾情歌寫成的程式語言

來源：Rockstar / Esolang wiki

二○一八年，工程師兼業餘搖滾樂手迪倫‧比堤做了一種很特別的程式語言，它的每一行程式，讀起來都像八○年代的搖滾情歌。

像「我的心是一塊石頭」其實是在宣告一個變數。這原本只是他在網路上開的玩笑，沒想到大家真的動手做出能運作的版本。現在它被收在 esolangs.org 這個網站上，那裡從二○○五年起，由志工整理了七千多種故意設計得很奇怪的程式語言。

In 2018, an engineer and amateur rock musician named Dylan Beattie created a unique programming language in which every line of code reads like an 1980s rock love song. For example, "My heart is a stone" is actually a variable declaration. It started as a joke he posted online, but to his surprise, people actually built a working version. Today it lives on esolangs.org, a site where volunteers have cataloged over 7,000 deliberately bizarre programming languages since 2005.
