聲音裡的台灣

來源：Pure Person Press / In Sheep's Clothing

2020年，NTS電台主持人Angie QQ請台灣電子音樂大師林強，分享他多年收集的台灣聲音。這些錄音裡有廟裡的祈禱、風聲，還有小孩的笑聲。

Angie QQ再邀請Point Hsu、CFCF等音樂人，用這些聲音做成新的音樂。專輯叫做《Sounds of Taiwan》，2025年11月發行，還特地在台灣製作，把聲音送回家。

In 2020, NTS radio host Angie QQ invited Taiwan's electronic music master Lim Giong to share the Taiwanese sounds he had collected over many years. These recordings captured temple prayers, wind, and children's laughter.

Angie QQ then brought in musicians like Point Hsu and CFCF to transform these sounds into new music. The album, titled "Sounds of Taiwan," was released in November 2025. Importantly, it was produced entirely in Taiwan — bringing the sound back home.

大理石裡的和平

來源：Stone Ideas / Alvar Aalto Foundation

芬蘭大師阿爾托設計的芬蘭廳，1971年蓋在赫爾辛基的湖邊。1975年，來自冷戰兩邊的33位國家元首在這裡簽下和平協議。

阿爾托用義大利白色大理石蓋牆，可是芬蘭天氣忽冷忽熱，石頭很快就變形、變色。經過三年整修，2025年終於換上更耐冷的石頭，重新開放。現在這裡還能參觀、喝咖啡，甚至住上一晚。

Finnish architect Alvar Aalto's Finlandia Hall was built on the lakeshore in Helsinki in 1971. In 1975, 33 heads of state from both sides of the Cold War signed a peace accord there.

Aalto chose Italian white marble for the walls, but Finland's harsh climate — cycling between extreme heat and cold — quickly warped and discolored the stone. After three years of restoration, the hall reopened in 2025 with more cold-resistant stone. Today, visitors can tour the building, have coffee, and even stay overnight.

走進十六世紀的英國村莊

來源：Bristol Renaissance Faire

布里斯托文藝復興市集，把十六世紀的英國小村莊搬到威斯康辛州，離芝加哥開車大約一小時。7月18、19日這個週末的主題是「田園風」，有花園造型的服裝比賽，還有草藥師示範。

這裡平常就有騎士比武、真人下棋，還能大口吃烤火雞腿。市集開到9月7日，每天早上十點到晚上七點，任何人都能來。

The Bristol Renaissance Faire transports a 16th-century English village to Wisconsin, about an hour's drive from Chicago. This coming weekend, July 18–19, features a "cottagecore" theme, complete with garden-inspired costume contests and demonstrations by herbalists.

The faire regularly features jousting tournaments, human-scale chess games, and hearty roasted turkey legs. It runs through September 7, open daily from 10 a.m. to 7 p.m., and welcomes everyone.

火柴盒裡的小世界

來源：Oh, What a Match!

1980年代的紐約，餐廳和飯店會送客人印著店名的小火柴盒。娜迪雅那時開始收集，幾十年後，她女兒愛麗絲也在紐約愛上同樣的東西。

2017年聖誕節，母女倆才發現彼此都有這個嗜好（很喜歡的習慣）。她們一起做了個部落格，專門介紹火柴盒的歷史，還有世界各地的收藏家。

In 1980s New York, restaurants and hotels gave guests small matchboxes imprinted with their names. Nadia began collecting them then, and decades later, her daughter Alice fell in love with the same hobby in New York.

Not until Christmas 2017 did mother and daughter discover they both shared this passion. Together, they started a blog dedicated to exploring matchbox history and collectors around the world.
