落日飛車：絲一般滑順的城市之歌

來源：Bandcamp Daily

台北樂團「落日飛車」在二〇一八年推出第二張專輯《Cassa Nova》。這張唱片讓他們成為亞洲最受歡迎的城市流行（City Pop）樂團之一。

仔細聽：電鋼琴的聲音像玻璃一樣亮，貝斯慢慢地走，薩克斯風像布料一樣滑過。他們把七、八十年代的靈魂樂和放克，換上了新的錄音方式。

歌曲〈Slow / Oriental〉的影片，還拿下第三十屆金曲獎最佳音樂錄影帶。

In 2018, Taipei band Sunset Rollercoaster released their second album, Cassa Nova, becoming one of Asia's most beloved city pop acts. Listen closely: the electric piano shines bright as glass, the bass walks with patient grace, and the saxophone glides as smooth as silk. They revived the soul and funk of the '70s and '80s with contemporary production techniques. Their music video for "Slow / Oriental" won Best Music Video at the 30th Golden Melody Awards.

玻璃村裡飛出的鳥

來源：Visit Finland

在芬蘭烏爾亞拉（Urjala）的森林裡，有一個叫努塔耶爾維（Nuutajärvi）的玻璃村。這裡的工廠從一七九三年開始做玻璃，是芬蘭最老的玻璃工廠，直到二〇一四年才關門。

一九七二年，藝術家托伊卡（Oiva Toikka）開始在這裡做玻璃鳥。每一隻都是用嘴吹出來的，顏色和樣子都不一樣，現在全世界都有人收藏。

老工廠現在變成玻璃學校，你可以站在旁邊看師傅吹玻璃。從赫爾辛基去坦佩雷的路上，很適合停下來看看。

In the forests of Urjala, Finland, sits a glassmaking village called Nuutajärvi. The factory began producing glass in 1793, making it Finland's oldest glassworks until it closed in 2014. Starting in 1972, artist Oiva Toikka began creating glass birds there. Each bird is hand-blown, with unique colors and shapes, and they're now collected by enthusiasts worldwide. The old factory has since transformed into a glass school where you can watch craftspeople blow glass right before your eyes. If you're traveling from Helsinki to Tampere, it's worth a stop along the way.

在老爵士酒吧裡「演」新聞

來源：TimeOut Chicago

芝加哥上城區的綠磨坊（Green Mill）是一家很老的爵士酒吧。在禁酒時代（美國不准賣酒的年代），黑幫老大卡彭（Al Capone）的手下常來這裡。

每個星期六下午三點，這裡會變成一本「現場雜誌」，叫做 The Paper Machete。記者、喜劇演員和音樂人輪流上台，用說話和表演來談這週的真實新聞。這種做法來自一九三〇年代美國的「活報紙」劇團。

演出兩個小時，每週上台的人都不一樣，而且免費。下一場在十月十日。

The Green Mill in Chicago's North Side is an old jazz bar. During Prohibition—the era when alcohol sales were banned in America—associates of gangster Al Capone frequently visited. Every Saturday at 3 PM, it transforms into a "live magazine" called The Paper Machete. Journalists, comedians, and musicians take turns performing, using speech and performance to discuss the week's real news. This approach traces back to American "living newspaper" theater groups from the 1930s. The show runs for two hours, the performers change each week, and admission is free. The next show is on October 10th.

鐵達尼號上的每一個人

來源：Encyclopedia Titanica

一九九六年，一位叫辛德（Philip Hind）的人自己開始做一個網站：Encyclopedia Titanica。他想說出鐵達尼號上每一個人的故事。

快三十年後，許多愛歷史的志工為乘客和船員一個一個寫下小傳，有照片、船上住的位置，還有當年的報紙。

例如乘客 Harry Thorn，上船時用了假名字「H. Johnston」。這種小事，課本裡找不到。網站的討論區從一九九九年開到現在，後人和研究者還在分享新發現。

In 1996, Philip Hind launched his own website: Encyclopedia Titanica. His mission was to tell the stories of everyone aboard the Titanic. Nearly thirty years later, countless history enthusiasts have contributed detailed biographies for passengers and crew members, complete with photographs, their locations on the ship, and newspaper accounts from that era. Take passenger Harry Thorn, for example—he boarded under the false name 'H. Johnston.' These small details are exactly what you won't find in textbooks. The website's discussion forum, launched in 1999, continues to thrive to this day, with descendants and researchers constantly sharing new discoveries.
