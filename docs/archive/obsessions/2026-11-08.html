9m88的《平庸之上》：台北的城市流行夢

來源：Taiwan News

2017年，台灣歌手9m88在網路上翻唱日本老歌〈Plastic Love〉。這首1984年的「城市流行樂」忽然紅了，很多人因此認識了她。

2019年8月，她自己出版第一張專輯《平庸之上》。她把爵士樂和靈魂樂的唱法，放進八〇年代閃亮又懷舊（想念過去）的電子聲音裡。

那時，台北有一群年輕人沒活過八〇年代，卻把日本老歌當成最酷的新發現。這張專輯，就是那股風潮的聲音。

In 2017, Taiwanese singer 9m88 posted a cover of "Plastic Love," an older Japanese song, online. The track came out in 1984 as "city pop," and it suddenly became popular again. Many people discovered her through it. In August 2019, she released her first album, Beyond Mediocrity (平庸之上). On it, she brought jazz and soul vocal styles into the bright, nostalgic electronic sounds of the 1980s. At that time, a group of young people in Taipei had never lived through the 1980s, yet they treated old Japanese songs as the coolest new discovery. This album is the sound of that trend.

芬蘭史詩誕生的小木屋

來源：Lohja Museum

在赫爾辛基西邊大約一小時車程的薩馬蒂村，有一間小木屋。1802年，隆洛特就在這裡出生。屋子是他當裁縫（做衣服的人）的爸爸親手蓋的。

隆洛特長大後當了鄉下醫生。他走路到卡累利阿地區，收集民間老歌，編成芬蘭的國家史詩（很長的故事詩）《卡勒瓦拉》。

從1880年代起，小屋就是博物館，裡面還放著他小時候的搖籃，和一把芬蘭古琴。小屋只在某些季節開放，三月想去，要先查好時間。

In the village of Sammatti, about an hour's drive west of Helsinki, there is a small wooden cabin. Elias Lönnrot was born here in 1802. His father, a tailor, built the house with his own hands. Lönnrot grew up to become a country doctor. On foot, he traveled to the Karelia region and collected old folk songs, which he shaped into the Kalevala, Finland's national epic. From the 1880s onward, the cabin has been a museum. It still holds the cradle he slept in as a child and a Finnish kantele, a traditional zither-like instrument. The cabin is open only in certain seasons, so if you plan to visit in March, check the hours beforehand.

藏在三樓的玉與青銅：免費之夜

來源：Choose Chicago

11月20日星期五，傍晚5點到8點，橋港區（Bridgeport）的亞洲藝術傳承博物館免費開放。晚上6點左右，通常還有導覽。

這間博物館很小，很多芝加哥人都不知道。要從西35街的門進去，再搭電梯到三樓。裡面收藏了中國的玉器、青銅器和織品（布料作品），年代跨越好幾千年。

地址是南摩根街3500號。這是每個月第三個星期五的免費之夜，很適合下班後安靜地走一走。

On Friday, November 20, from 5 to 8 p.m., the Asian Art Heritage Museum in Bridgeport is open for free. A guided tour usually takes place around 6 p.m. The museum is very small, and many Chicagoans don't know it exists. Enter through the door on West 35th Street, then take the elevator to the third floor. Its collection includes Chinese jade, bronze vessels, and textiles, spanning several thousand years. The address is 3500 South Morgan Street. This is the free evening held on the third Friday of every month, a good chance to stroll quietly after work.

海底電纜的守護人

來源：atlantic-cable.com

伯恩斯以前是英國BBC的工程師。從1994年起，他一個人經營網站 atlantic-cable.com，記錄海底電纜（放在海底的粗電線）的歷史。這是網路上最老的個人歷史網站之一。

網站講到1858年第一條橫越大西洋的電纜。它只用了幾個星期就壞了，因為負責的電機師想讓訊息傳得更快，把電壓調得太高。

網站裡有十九世紀的老書、手畫的路線地圖，還有伯恩斯親自找到、拍下的老電纜切面照片。

Burns was once an engineer at the BBC in Britain. Starting in 1994, he has run the website atlantic-cable.com by himself, recording the history of submarine cables (the heavy electrical wires laid on the seabed). It is one of the oldest personal history websites on the internet. The site tells the story of the first cable across the Atlantic, laid in 1858. It broke down within a few weeks, because the electrical engineer in charge wanted messages to travel faster and turned the voltage up too high. The site includes nineteenth-century books, hand-drawn route maps, and photographs of old cable cross-sections that Burns tracked down and photographed himself.
