被偷走的歌聲，回家了

來源：關鍵評論網、Wikipedia

1988年，法國學者在台東馬蘭，錄下阿美族老夫妻郭英男和郭秀珠唱的〈飲酒歡樂歌〉。

五年後，德國樂團 Enigma 沒有問過他們，就把這段歌聲放進〈Return to Innocence〉。這首歌後來成了1996年奧運的宣傳曲，全世界都聽過，卻沒人知道是誰在唱。

兩人告上法院。1998年，台灣的魔岩唱片為他們做了專輯《生命之環》，把老人家沒有樂器伴奏的歌聲，配上電子音樂。隔年官司和解（雙方談好條件）。這一次，歌聲終於掛上自己的名字。

In 1988, a French scholar in Malan, Taitung recorded an Amis couple—Kuo Ying-nan and Kuo Hsiu-chu—singing 'The Song of Joyful Drinking.' Five years later, the German band Enigma wove their recording into 'Return to Innocence' without asking permission. The song later became the theme for the 1996 Olympics, heard worldwide, but no one knew who was actually singing. The couple took the matter to court. In 1998, Taiwan's Magic Rock Records produced an album for them called 'Circle of Life,' pairing their unaccompanied voices with electronic music. The next year, the lawsuit was settled—both sides reached an agreement on the terms. This time, finally, the voices bore their own names.

赫爾辛基的盤子，走到街邊

來源：Design Forum Finland、MyHelsinki

在赫爾辛基東邊的阿拉比亞區，有一家1873年開的陶瓷工廠，曾是歐洲最大的餐具廠。

它的博物館原本在工廠八樓，現在搬到新大樓的一樓，免費參觀。展品從十九世紀的老碗盤，一路排到設計師卡伊·弗蘭克在1950年代做的 Teema 餐具。它簡單、安靜，卻影響了整個芬蘭設計。

從市中心坐電車就到，旁邊還有 Iittala 的店。想避開人潮的兩個人，可以在這裡慢慢走上半天。

In the Arabia district on Helsinki's east side sits a ceramics factory founded in 1873—once Europe's largest tableware maker. Its museum, originally located on the factory's eighth floor, has relocated to the ground level of a new building with free admission. Exhibits range from nineteenth-century bowls and plates to Teema, the tableware designed by Kaj Franck in the 1950s. Though simple and understated, Teema shaped all of Finnish design. A tram ride from downtown, with an Iittala shop nearby, it's a perfect haven for a couple seeking to escape the crowds—ideal for a leisurely half-day wander.

魚養菜，菜養魚

來源：Plant Chicago

10月17日星期六早上10點到中午，芝加哥 Back of the Yards 區的非營利組織 Plant Chicago，要開一堂「魚菜共生」入門課。

什麼是魚菜共生？魚的大便變成菜的肥料，菜的根再把水洗乾淨，送回給魚。一個水箱，養出魚，也種出菜。他們認為，這是城市自己種糧食的好方法。

老師是營運主任 Eric Weber。地點在 4459 S. Marshfield Ave.，要先報名；住在 60609 郵遞區號的人免費。

On Saturday, October 17, from 10 a.m. to noon, Plant Chicago—a nonprofit in Chicago's Back of the Yards neighborhood—will hold an introductory workshop on aquaponics. Here's how it works: fish waste becomes fertilizer for vegetables, and the vegetable roots filter and clean the water, which returns to the fish. One tank yields both fish and vegetables. The organization sees this as a smart way for cities to grow their own food. The instructor is Operations Director Eric Weber. The workshop takes place at 4459 S. Marshfield Ave. Pre-registration is required. Residents of the 60609 zip code attend free.

說錯的未來，一個人的檔案館

來源：Boys Who Cried AI

有一個小網站叫 Boys Who Cried AI，是 Patrick Grady 一個人做的。他收集了三十多則關於人工智慧、卻沒說中的預言，最早的一則來自1958年。

每一則都寫清楚：誰說的、有什麼頭銜（身分和職位）、說了什麼、期限是哪一天，還有為什麼沒成真。例如1965年，學者賽門說，二十年內，機器能做「人能做的任何工作」。

網站不是在笑人，而是提醒我們：太有把握的預言，會決定錢和政策往哪裡走。

There is a small website called Boys Who Cried AI, created by Patrick Grady as a one-person project. He has collected more than thirty predictions about artificial intelligence that never came to pass, with the earliest dating back to 1958. Each entry is meticulously documented: who made the prediction, their title and credentials, what was claimed, the deadline given, and why it failed to materialize. For instance, in 1965, scholar Herbert Simon predicted that within twenty years, machines would be capable of doing "any work that humans can do." The site isn't meant to ridicule these thinkers—rather, it reminds us that overconfident predictions have the power to direct where money and policy actually flow.
