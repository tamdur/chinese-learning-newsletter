落日飛車：一場慢下來的告別

來源：Album of the Year / Apple Music

台北的樂團落日飛車，把日本八〇年代的城市流行歌和薩克斯風（一種銅管吹奏樂器）變成台灣獨立音樂的招牌。

二〇二五年八月，他們發表新專輯《Quit Quietly》。主唱曾國宏說，這是向某個階段告別，以後少說話，多寫歌。

一樣的爵士和弦，一樣是黃浩庭的薩克斯風，速度卻慢了下來，像日記，不像舞會。正當亞洲各地的樂團都在模仿他們的聲音時，他們自己先走開了。

Taipei's band Sunset Rollercoaster made 1980s Japanese city pop and the saxophone—a brass wind instrument—the signature sound of Taiwan's indie music.

In August 2025, they released a new album, Quit Quietly. Lead singer Tseng Kuo-hung called it a farewell to one chapter; going forward, they'll talk less and write more songs.

Same jazz chords, same Huang Hao-ting on saxophone—but slower now, sounding like diary entries instead of a night out. Just as bands across Asia are copying their signature sound, Sunset Rollercoaster has already moved on.

沒有地主的法律：芬蘭的森林人人可走

來源：The World / PRX

一九二〇年代，芬蘭湖區有位採野莓的女子林格倫，被地主阻止採果。她告上法院，贏了。到一九三〇年，這件事變成正式法律，叫做「每個人的權利」。

不管你是本地人還是遊客，都能在幾乎任何一塊土地上走路、滑雪、露營一兩晚、採野果。每年夏天約有七千名泰國工人到北部的拉普蘭採莓，因為野莓在摘下來之前不屬於任何人。

三月來蜜月的人也一樣：可以踩著雪走過任何一片森林和湖岸，不必問誰是主人。

In the 1920s, Lindgren, a woman who gathered wild berries in Finland's lake country, was stopped by a landowner from picking fruit. She took the case to court and won. By 1930, this incident had become formal law, known as "everyone's right."

Whether you're a local or a visitor, you can walk, ski, camp for a night or two, and pick wild berries on almost any land. Every summer, about seven thousand Thai workers come to northern Lapland to harvest berries—because wild berries belong to no one until they're picked.

Couples honeymooning in March have the same freedom: you can walk through any forest and along any lakeshore in the snow without asking who owns the land.

老宅裡的爵士與詩：艾奇沃特的沙龍之夜

來源：Eventbrite / True Muse Inc

八月九日星期天下午五點到九點，藝術團體 True Muse 在艾奇沃特的柯爾文老宅辦「第二個星期天沙龍」。這棟一九〇九年蓋的大房子在雪利登路上，是芝加哥的古蹟（受保護的老建築）。

六點，唐諾文．米克森四重奏開始演奏爵士；七點四十五分，詩人塔拉．貝茲和 Pugs Atomz 上台朗讀。現場有酒和小點心。

創辦人索羅泰爾說，這不是表演，是大家一起參與的聚會，而且辦在一棟多數芝加哥人一輩子走不進去的房子裡。

On Sunday, August 9th from 5 to 9 p.m., the arts collective True Muse will host its "Second Sunday Salon" at the historic Colvin House in Edgewater. This grand house, built in 1909 and located on Sheridan Road, is a Chicago landmark—a protected historic building.

At 6 p.m., the Donovan Mixon Quartet takes the stage with jazz; at 7:45 p.m., poets Tara Betts and Pugs Atomz give readings. Wine and light snacks will be served.

Founder Solotaire says this isn't a performance but a gathering everyone takes part in—one held inside a house that most Chicagoans will never have the chance to enter.

十五萬首電腦老歌，被一群志工救了回來

來源：Amiga Music Preservation (AMP)

一九九九年，一群玩電腦的老手做了一個網站，叫 Amiga Music Preservation。他們要保存的是「模組音樂」，也就是八、九〇年代的人在家用電腦上，一個音符一個音符敲出來的曲子，用來配電腦動畫和破解程式的開頭畫面。

今天這個資料庫收了將近十五萬首曲子、超過一萬五千位作曲者。它還把每個人的本名和當年的代號、待過的團體對起來，把一個地下世界的口耳相傳，變成可以查詢的家譜。

In 1999, a group of longtime computer hobbyists created a website called Amiga Music Preservation to preserve "module music"—the tunes people composed note by note on home computers in the 1980s and 90s, used as soundtracks for computer animations and the opening screens of cracked software.

Today the database holds nearly 150,000 pieces by more than 15,000 composers. It also links each person's real name with the alias they used back then and the groups they passed through, turning an underground world's oral tradition into a searchable genealogy.
