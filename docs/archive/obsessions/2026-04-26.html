廟口的搖滾樂：拍謝少年的北管搖滾

來源：BeatRoute

台北三人樂團「拍謝少年」只用台語寫歌。他們花了五年做出二〇一七年的專輯，把北管（台灣廟會傳統音樂）和搖滾混在一起。嗩吶（一種很大聲的管樂器）、鑼鼓和電吉他一起響起來。

在〈神的孩子〉這首歌裡，北管的遊行隊伍突然出現在歌曲中間，再變回吉他的聲音。聽起來就像廟裡的神明走進了搖滾演唱會。

Taipei's three-member band "Apologize Youngsters" writes all their songs in Taiwanese. They spent five years creating their 2017 album, blending beiguan—Taiwan's traditional temple fair music—with rock and roll. The suona, a piercing woodwind instrument, crashes together with gongs, drums, and electric guitar. In the song "God's Children," a beiguan parade suddenly emerges in the middle of the track, then shifts back to guitar sounds. It's as if a deity from the temple has walked straight into a rock concert.

芬蘭冬夜裡的光：坦佩雷燈節

來源：Tampereen Valoviikot

芬蘭第二大城坦佩雷從一九六六年開始舉辦「光之週」燈節，用燈光對抗漫長的冬天黑夜。現在活動長達五個月，到三月初才結束。河邊有免費的燈光藝術，街上有光影投射。

白天也值得走走。芬萊森老紡織（織布）廠已經變成電影院和餐廳。皮尼基觀景塔只要兩歐元門票，還有賣了四十年的甜甜圈。

Tampere, Finland's second-largest city, has hosted the "Week of Light" festival since 1966, using illuminations to fight back against the long winter darkness. Today the festival runs for five months, ending in early March. Along the riverbanks, free light art installations and projected shadows brighten the streets. The daytime is worth exploring too. The old Finlayson textile mill has been converted into a cinema and restaurants. The Pyynikki observation tower charges just two euros for entry and still sells doughnuts that have been made there for forty years.

科學博物館的二手衣之夜

來源：Block Club Chicago

四月三十日晚上六點到十點，芝加哥科學與工業博物館會變成一個大型二手衣市場。這個活動叫 Thrift Lab，門票三十五美元，只給大人參加。

裡面有十六個古著（二手衣）攤位、衣服修補教學，還有用縫紉（針線）方法命名的調酒。最有趣的是，整個活動的點子最早是一位青少年實習生的課堂作業。

On the evening of April 30th, from 6 to 10 p.m., the Museum of Science and Industry in Chicago will transform into a massive secondhand clothing marketplace. The event is called Thrift Lab, with tickets priced at thirty-five dollars for adults only. Inside, you'll find sixteen vintage clothing vendors, garment repair workshops, and cocktails named after sewing techniques. Most interestingly, the entire concept originated as a classroom assignment from a teenage intern.

五千種音階的收藏家

來源：Huygens-Fokker Foundation

鋼琴有十二個音，但世界上的音樂不只這些。荷蘭科學家 Manuel Op de Coul 從一九九九年開始，一個人收集了超過五千兩百種調音系統，做成免費軟體 Scala。

從古希臘的畢達哥拉斯音律，到印尼甘美朗（傳統打擊樂）的音階，全都在裡面。這是一個人用二十五年回答一個問題：如果音樂不只有十二個音，還能怎樣？

The piano has twelve notes, but the world's music is not limited to them. Dutch scientist Manuel Op de Coul has spent since 1999 collecting over five thousand two hundred tuning systems by himself, turning them into free software called Scala. From the Pythagorean tuning of ancient Greece to the scales of Indonesian gamelan—traditional percussion music—they are all there. It is one man's twenty-five-year answer to a single question: if music is not limited to twelve notes, what else is possible?
