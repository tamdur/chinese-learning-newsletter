從台東到阿姆斯特丹：李泰祥與美麗的哀愁

來源：YouTube

一九八五年，來自台東的阿美族作曲家李泰祥飛到阿姆斯特丹，指揮皇家大會堂管弦樂團，錄下了專輯《美麗的哀愁》。這張專輯把台灣民歌和西方交響樂結合在一起，包括他最有名的〈橄欖樹〉——三毛作詞，齊豫唱紅的經典歌曲。

李泰祥一輩子沒有自己的房子，一直住在租來的小房間裡。他最後二十五年帶著帕金森氏症（一種讓手會發抖的病）繼續創作，用音樂定義了一個時代。

In 1985, composer Li Taixiang, an Amis indigenous musician from Taitung, flew to Amsterdam to conduct the Royal Concertgebouw Orchestra and recorded the album "Beautiful Sorrow." The album fused Taiwanese folk songs with Western orchestral music, featuring his most celebrated work, "Olive Tree"—a classic song with lyrics by Sanmao and made famous by singer Qi Yu. Li Taixiang never owned a home throughout his life, always living in rented small rooms. In his final twenty-five years, while battling Parkinson's disease—a condition that causes hand tremors—he continued composing, defining an era through his music.

芬蘭馬：從戰場到雪地雪橇

來源：Ranch Kuulas

芬蘭馬是芬蘭唯一的本土馬種，二〇〇七年被選為國馬。牠們身體結實，特別能忍受寒冷。冬季戰爭時，超過兩萬一千匹芬蘭馬在雪地森林中運送士兵和物資，最後犧牲了。

戰後數量一度從四十萬匹掉到不到一萬五千匹，差點消失。後來靠賽馬活動才慢慢恢復。三月的拉普蘭，你可以坐在芬蘭馬拉的雪橇（在雪上滑行的車）上，穿過白雪覆蓋的雲杉森林。

The Finnish horse is Finland's only native horse breed and was designated the national horse in 2007. These animals have sturdy builds and are exceptionally cold-hardy. During the Winter War, over 21,000 Finnish horses transported soldiers and supplies through snowy forests, ultimately perishing in the conflict. After the war, their population plummeted from 400,000 to fewer than 15,000, bringing the breed to the brink of extinction. The breed's recovery came slowly through harness racing activities. In March, in Lapland, you can ride in a sleigh pulled by Finnish horses, gliding through snow-covered spruce forests.

摩天大樓的真正起源：芝加哥人文節講座

來源：Chicago Humanities Festival

五月九日下午一點半，歷史學家 Gregory Dreicer 將在芝加哥人文節的 Athenaeum Center 演講。他的新書《American Bridge》挑戰了大家熟悉的故事：摩天大樓並不是某個美國天才單獨發明的。

他發現，支撐高樓的鋼骨架技術其實來自歐洲和世界各地的工程師。對芝加哥這座以天際線（skyline）聞名的城市來說，這是一場值得聽的演講。

On May 9th at 1:30 p.m., historian Gregory Dreicer will give a lecture at the Athenaeum Center as part of the Chicago Humanities Festival. His new book, "American Bridge," challenges the familiar narrative: the skyscraper was not invented by a single American genius. He discovers that the steel frame technology supporting tall buildings actually came from engineers across Europe and around the world. For Chicago, a city renowned for its skyline, this is a lecture worth attending.

一個人的公路百科全書

來源：Kurumi.com

美國康乃狄克州有一個叫 Scott Oglesby 的公路歷史迷，花了幾十年建造了一個網站 kurumi.com，記錄美國的公路建設。最特別的是他的「交流道圖鑑」，把每一種高速公路交流道的形狀都畫出來，像昆蟲圖鑑一樣分類。

網站還有廢棄公路的歷史，和一個一九九八年用 Java 寫的路牌製作工具。這就是早期網路最美好的樣子：一個人因為熱愛，把全部的知識放上網。

In Connecticut, a road history enthusiast named Scott Oglesby has spent decades building kurumi.com, a website documenting America's highway construction. What makes it special is his "interchange encyclopedia"—a visual catalog where he draws the shape of every type of highway interchange, organized like an insect field guide. The site also features the history of abandoned roads and a road sign maker tool written in Java back in 1998. It's the internet at its finest: one person's passionate knowledge shared freely with the world.
