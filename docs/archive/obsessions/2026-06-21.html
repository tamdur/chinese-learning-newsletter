王海玲的《偈》：一張望向星空的校園民歌

來源：Takes on 200

一九八零年，製作人李壽全為校園民歌手王海玲做了一張很特別的專輯。名字叫《偈》（佛教裡的詩句），這個字太難了，封面上還得幫大家標出怎麼念。

主打歌用的是大詩人鄭愁予的詩，由蘇來譜曲。別的民歌愛唱田園風光，它卻望向遠遠的星空。它在「台灣最佳兩百張專輯」裡排第五十九名，現在還能在線上聽到。

In 1980, producer Li Shou-chuan created a truly distinctive album for campus folk singer Wang Hailing. It was called "Ji" (偈, "Buddhist verse")—a character so difficult that the cover had to include a pronunciation guide for listeners.

The title track drew from the poetry of the great poet Zheng Chouyu, with music composed by Su Lai. While other folk songs typically celebrated pastoral scenery, this one gazed toward the distant stars. The album ranked 59th on the "200 Greatest Taiwanese Albums" list and remains available to stream online today.

不看重量，看價錢：坦佩雷的黑香腸

來源：Visit Tampere

在芬蘭坦佩雷市的湖邊市集，有一種當地人最驕傲（覺得很光榮）的食物：用血和黑麥做的黑香腸。最老的店家塔波拉，從一九五三年就用家傳配方在賣了。

買的方法很特別：你不是說要幾條、多重，而是告訴老闆你想花多少錢。熱熱的香腸配上紅色的越橘果醬，就是這座工人城市的味道。

At the lakeside market in Tampere, Finland, there's a food that locals take the greatest pride in: a black sausage made from blood and rye. The oldest shop, Tapola, has been selling it since 1953 using a family recipe passed down through generations.

The way you buy it is unusual: instead of telling the shopkeeper how many links you want or how much they should weigh, you tell him how much money you want to spend. Steaming-hot sausage paired with red lingonberry jam—that's the taste of this working-class city.

門廊變舞台：羅斯科村的鄰里音樂節

來源：Eventbrite / Lakeview Roscoe Village Chamber of Commerce

六月二十八日星期天下午一點到五點，芝加哥的羅斯科村會辦一場好玩的活動。這一區的住戶把自己家門前的門廊（房子前的小平台）借出來，當成音樂舞台。

每位音樂人表演四十五分鐘，客人可以一家走過一家，慢慢聽、慢慢找喜歡的團。大約一千五百人參加，不用進任何表演廳，整個街區就是會場。

On Sunday, June 28th, from 1 to 5 PM, the Roscoe Village neighborhood in Chicago will host a delightful event. Residents of the area lend out the front porches of their homes—the little platforms in front of the house—to serve as music stages.

Each musician performs for forty-five minutes, and guests can stroll from house to house, listening at their own pace and discovering bands they like. About fifteen hundred people take part. There's no need to enter any concert hall; the entire neighborhood is the venue.

一個人做的網站：讓你聽懂義大利詩

來源：italianpoetry.it

有一個叫「義大利詩」的網站，是一個人因為喜歡而默默做出來的。他挑出但丁、佩脫拉克這些大詩人的作品，親自念出來錄成聲音。

最棒的是：聲音念到哪個字，那個字就會亮起來，義大利文和英文同時亮。把滑鼠移到字上，還會看到小小的說明。沒有學校、沒有錢贊助，只有一個人的熱情。

There's a website called "Italian Poetry" that one person quietly built out of pure passion. He selected works by Dante, Petrarch, and other great poets, then recorded himself reading them aloud.

The best part: as the audio plays, each word lights up in real time—both the Italian text and the English translation highlight together. Hover over any word and you'll see a brief explanation. No institution backing it, no funding—just one person's enthusiasm.
