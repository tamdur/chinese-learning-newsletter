夢東：深夜電視的幽靈迪斯可

來源：Guruguru Brain / Bandcamp

台北兄弟 Hom Yu 和 Jiun Chi 組成的夢東，在 2020 年於東京廠牌 Guruguru Brain 發行了《秘神》。他們把 1980 年代的恐怖電視劇、佛經念誦、道教法器、飛碟節目的旁白通通取樣進來。

底下是老合成器、鼓機、衝浪吉他的簡單節奏。名曲〈717〉像深夜開車穿過西門町的霓虹。這是台客迷幻的新招魂術。

Mong Tong, formed by Taipei brothers Hom Yu and Jiun Chi, released "Mystery" in 2020 on the Tokyo label Guruguru Brain. They sampled it all in: 1980s horror TV dramas, Buddhist sutra chanting, Taoist ritual instruments, UFO program narration.

Underneath are the simple rhythms of vintage synthesizers, drum machines, and surf guitar. The standout track "717" feels like driving through Ximending's neon lights late at night. This is a new spirit-summoning ritual for Taiwanese psychedelia.

芬蘭婚毯：跪過的羊毛

來源：Designmuseo / Hvitträsk

十八、十九世紀的芬蘭鄉下，新娘會親手織一張厚厚的羊毛毯，叫做 ryijy（婚禮毯）。上面打滿兩人的名字、婚禮那一年、愛心、皇冠、鬱金香和生命之樹。婚禮當天夫妻跪在毯上，當晚把它掛到床頭。

想看真品，從赫爾辛基坐二十分鐘火車到 Kirkkonummi，走進建築師 Eliel Saarinen 的湖畔家 Hvitträsk。大廳裡仍掛著他設計、妻子 Loja 親手織的大毯：一張由夫妻合作的婚禮毯。

In 18th and 19th century rural Finland, a bride would weave by hand a thick wool blanket called a ryijy (wedding blanket). It was covered with the couple's names, the year of the wedding, hearts, crowns, tulips, and trees of life. On the wedding day the couple knelt on the blanket, and that night they hung it at the head of the bed.

To see the real thing, take a twenty-minute train from Helsinki to Kirkkonummi and walk into Hvitträsk, the lakeside home of architect Eliel Saarinen. Hanging in the great hall is still a large blanket he designed and his wife Loja wove by hand: a wedding blanket made through the collaboration of husband and wife.

帶你的樂器來即興

來源：Elastic Arts

芝加哥 Avondale 區的店面空間 Elastic Arts，二十年來一直是城裡即興音樂的心臟。四月十六到十八日舉辦 Imminence 音樂節，十八日星期六下午一點有一場完全免費的公開即興工作坊。

大提琴家 Ishmael Ali、薩克斯風手 Molly Jones、作曲家 Ben Zucker 和打擊樂手 Bill Harris 主持。帶任何樂器、不論程度都可以來。這是幾條街外誕生的 AACM 傳統：陌生人圍成小圈、散開、再圍起來。地址 3429 W Diversey Ave，早點到。

Elastic Arts, a storefront space in Chicago's Avondale neighborhood, has been the heart of the city's improvised music scene for twenty years. The Imminence Festival runs April 16–18, and on Saturday the 18th at 1 p.m. there's a completely free public improvisation workshop open to all.

Cellist Ishmael Ali, saxophonist Molly Jones, composer Ben Zucker, and percussionist Bill Harris will lead the session. Bring any instrument; all skill levels welcome. This is the AACM tradition born just a few blocks away: strangers gather in small circles, scatter, and gather again. 3429 W Diversey Ave — get there early.

陰天就熄燈的網站

來源：Low-Tech Magazine

比利時記者 Kris De Decker 從 2007 年起經營 Low-Tech Magazine，寫那些被人忘掉的老技術：中世紀的踏輪起重機、冰屋、無火燜燒鍋、中國的風帆獨輪車、氣送郵件的管道。

2018 年他把網站搬到巴塞隆納陽台上的一片太陽能板。圖片壓成粗粒的土黃色，背景有一支電池表。連續幾天陰雨，整個網站就熄燈。網址是 solar.lowtechmagazine.com。

Belgian journalist Kris De Decker has been running Low-Tech Magazine since 2007, writing about forgotten old technologies: medieval treadwheel cranes, ice houses, fireless cookers, China's wind-powered sail wheelbarrows, pneumatic mail tubes.

In 2018, he moved the site onto a single solar panel on a balcony in Barcelona. Images are compressed into grainy ochre, and a battery meter sits in the background. After several consecutive days of overcast rain, the whole site goes dark. The address is solar.lowtechmagazine.com.
