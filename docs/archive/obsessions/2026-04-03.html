台灣迪斯乳——七〇年代女王們的秘密舞池

來源：Discogs

在台灣跳舞曾經是違法的。但在一九七〇和八〇年代，一群女歌手偷偷創造了台灣自己的迪斯乳和乘放克音樂。吳秀珠、乘崔台青和乘鄒娟娟用模糊的吉他、大樂隊的銅管和太空風格的合成器，做出了全亞洲最瘋狂的舞曲。

二〇二〇年的合輯把這些被遺忘的音樂重新帶回來。芝加哥的唱片行也買得到。這是台灣流行音樂的「失落的一章」——在九〇年代的華語流行之前，台灣女人早就在創造最自由的音樂了。

Dancing in Taiwan was once illegal. But in the 1970s and 80s, a group of female singers secretly created Taiwan's own disco and funk music. Wu Xiuzhu, Cui Taiqing, and Zou Juanjuan crafted some of Asia's wildest dance tracks using hazy guitars, big-band brass, and spacey synthesizers.

A 2020 compilation brought these forgotten songs back. Record shops in Chicago carry it too. This is the "lost chapter" of Taiwan pop music — before 1990s Mandarin pop took over, Taiwanese women were already creating the most liberated music of all.

芬蘭探戈——世界上最憂傷的舞蹈

來源：Finnish Music Quarterly

芬蘭有自己的探戈，和阿根廷的完全不一樣。芬蘭探戈幾乎都是小調，用手風琴演奏，歌詞總是在講「思念」這件事。這個傳統從一九一三年開始，在第二次世界大戰時變得更深沉。

每年夏天，小城塞納約基會舉辦十萬人的探戈節。在赫爾辛基，冬天也有舞蹈教室和社交舞會。三月去芬蘭的話，可以嘗試上一堂芬蘭探戈課——大部分觀光客不知道這個浪漫的體驗。

Finland has its own tango, completely different from Argentina's. Finnish tango is almost entirely in the minor key, played on accordion, and the lyrics always revolve around "longing." This tradition began in 1913 and became even more profound during World War II.

Every summer, the small city of Seinäjoki hosts a tango festival with a hundred thousand attendees. In Helsinki, there are also dance classes and social dances during the winter. If you go to Finland in March, you can try taking a Finnish tango lesson — most tourists don't know about this romantic experience.

博覽會芝加哥——海軍碼頭的國際當代藝術

來源：EXPO CHICAGO

四月九日到十二日，一百三十家畫廊會聚集在海軍碼頭的節慶大廳，帶來世界各地的當代藝術。門票四十美元一天。

今年有兩個特別的主題展區：一個和底特律藝術學院合作，探索密西西比河流域的藝術；另一個和即將開幕的歐巴馬總統中心合作。還有免費的公開講座，包括藝術家乘和知名策展人的對談。

From April 9 to 12, one hundred thirty galleries will converge at Navy Pier's Festival Hall, bringing contemporary art from around the world. Admission is forty dollars per day.

This year features two special themed sections: one in collaboration with the Detroit Institute of Arts exploring art from the Mississippi River basin; another partnering with the soon-to-open Obama Presidential Center. Free public lectures are also available, including conversations with artists and renowned curators.

音樂地圖——一個人花十年畫出流行音樂的族譜

來源：Musicmap.info

一位比利時建築師花了超過十年的時間，做出了一個互動式的流行音樂類型族譜。從一八七〇年到現在，你可以看到每一種音樂風格怎麼出現、分裂、合併和影響彼此。

這個網站沒有廣告，沒有追蹤器，完全免費。它贏得了「美麗資訊獎」，還在巴塞隆納的音樂節上展出。這就是網路最美好的精神——一個人因為熱愛，做出了讓所有人都能享受的東西。

A Belgian architect spent over a decade creating an interactive family tree of popular music genres. From 1870 to the present day, you can see how each musical style emerged, split apart, merged, and influenced one another.

The website has no ads, no trackers, and is completely free. It won the "Beautiful Information Award" and was featured at a music festival in Barcelona. This embodies the internet at its best — one person, driven by passion, created something that everyone can enjoy.
