《海口味》：一張台灣漁港的地圖

來源：Taiwan.md

2012年，台南的三人樂團「拍謝少年」推出第一張專輯《海口味》。這九首歌，他們寫了六年。

他們把以前的後搖滾，變成台語搖滾：吵雜的吉他、迷幻的聲音，還有很強的節奏。封面上畫著虱目魚（台南最有名的一種魚），像在說：我們就是海口人。

〈深海的你〉唱海的可怕，〈台十七〉唱那條串起南部漁村的海邊公路。整張專輯，就是一張台灣漁港的地圖。

In 2012, the three-member Tainan band Sorry Youth (拍謝少年) released their debut album Seafood (海口味). These nine songs had taken six years to write. They transformed their earlier post-rock into Taiwanese-language rock—distorted guitars, psychedelic tones, and driving rhythm. The cover features milkfish, a fish iconic to Tainan, as if declaring: we are harbor people. "You of the Deep Sea" captures the terror of the ocean; "Highway 17" traces the coastal road that strings together the fishing villages of the south. The whole album reads as a map of Taiwan's fishing harbors.

三十萬間木屋，從芬蘭運向世界

來源：ArchDaily

1940年，芬蘭成立了一家公司，名字的意思是「木屋公司」。那時戰爭讓卡累利阿（芬蘭東邊被奪走的土地）的難民沒有家，政府得很快蓋出很多房子。

工廠先把木板量好、切好，運到現場，幾天就能組起一間房子。到1956年，他們已經把大約三十萬間木屋賣到五十多個國家，其中一萬間是賠給蘇聯的。

2021年威尼斯建築展的芬蘭館，重新說起這段被忘記的故事。

In 1940, Finland established a company—its name simply meant "the wooden house company"—to solve an urgent national crisis. War had displaced refugees from Karelia, territory stripped from Finland's eastern border, leaving them homeless. The government needed to build thousands of dwellings quickly. The company's solution was elegant: manufacture wooden panels and components in the factory, ship them to the building site, and assemble a complete house within days. The model proved remarkably successful. By 1956, they had exported approximately 300,000 wooden houses to over fifty countries, including 10,000 units delivered as war reparations to the Soviet Union. This remarkable but largely forgotten chapter was revived at the 2021 Venice Architecture Biennale, where Finland's pavilion told the story anew.

走進大理石大廳：拉薩爾街的金錢建築

來源：Chicago Detours

芝加哥有個導覽團 Chicago Detours ，帶大家花兩小時，走進拉薩爾街金融區的大樓，看那些路人平常進不去的大廳。

路線從南拉薩爾街39號的大理石銀行大廳開始，經過期貨交易所和洛克利大樓，最後走進南迪爾伯恩街140號華麗的馬凱特大樓。

導覽員會解釋：以前的銀行家為什麼要用金箔（很薄的金片）、銅櫃台和很高的天井？因為他們想讓你相信，錢放在這裡很安全。門票可以上網預訂。

Chicago Detours is a tour company that takes visitors on two-hour walks through the financial district's grand buildings on LaSalle Street, showing them the magnificent halls that ordinary pedestrians normally can't enter. The route begins at the marble banking hall at 39 South LaSalle Street, passes through the futures exchange and the Rookery Building, and concludes in the elaborate Marquette Building at 140 South Dearborn Street. The tour guides explain why the old bankers adorned these spaces with gold leaf, copper counters, and soaring skylights—they wanted visitors to believe their money would be safe here. Tickets can be booked online.

雜訊裡的聲音檔案館

來源：shortwavearchive.com

2012年，一個喜歡短波收音機的人，自己開了一個網站。他把收到的廣播錄下來，原封不動放上網，連沙沙的雜訊都留著。

裡面有海盜電台、宗教電台，還有奇怪的「數字電台」，只念一串一串的數字，據說是要傳給國外情報員的暗號。

這個網站到現在幾乎每天更新，變成一座空中電波的聲音博物館。

In 2012, a shortwave radio enthusiast created his own website. He recorded the broadcasts he received and posted them online unchanged, preserving even the crackling static. It includes pirate stations, religious broadcasters, and mysterious "numbers stations" that simply broadcast strings of numbers—reportedly coded messages meant for intelligence operatives overseas. The site updates almost daily and has become a living sound archive of the airwaves.
