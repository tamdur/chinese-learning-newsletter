颱風夜裡的溫柔：落日飛車《Soft Storm》

來源：Atwood Magazine

小時候，台灣的夏天常有颱風。風雨一來，家裡就停電，全家人點著蠟燭，擠在一起等天亮。

台北樂團「落日飛車」把這個大家共同的回憶，做成了2020年的第三張專輯《Soft Storm》。他們沒有寫風雨有多可怕，反而寫出「和家人一起躲在屋裡」的安心感。

輕飄飄的吉他、柔軟的電子琴聲，帶著1980年代的味道。專輯還請來韓國歌手OHHYUK等人一起唱。在混亂的2020年，這是一場溫柔的暴風雨。

When I was a child, summers in Taiwan often brought typhoons. When the wind and rain arrived, the power would go out at home, and the whole family would light candles and huddle together, waiting for morning. Taipei band Sunset Rollercoaster (落日飛車) turned this shared memory into their third album, Soft Storm, released in 2020. They don't dwell on how frightening the storms were. Instead, they capture the sense of safety in staying indoors with family. The light-footed guitar and soft electric organ carry the flavor of the 1980s. The album also features Korean singer OHHYUK and others. In the chaotic year of 2020, this was a gentle storm.

聖誕老人住在「耳朵山」

來源：Life in Lapland

在芬蘭，聖誕老人叫做Joulupukki，意思是「聖誕山羊」。很久以前，他頭上長角，一家一家地向人要禮物和剩下的食物，一點也不可愛。

1927年，電台兒童節目主持人Markus Rautio告訴孩子們：聖誕老人偷偷住在拉普蘭的「耳朵山」（Korvatunturi）。這座山有三個山頭，像大耳朵一樣，能聽見北風送來的孩子願望。

這座山真的存在，就在芬蘭和俄羅斯的邊界上。想去要先拿到邊防隊的書面許可，所以寄到那裡的信，都會轉送到羅瓦涅米的聖誕老人村。

In Finland, Santa Claus is called Joulupukki, which means "Christmas Goat." Long ago, he had horns on his head and went from house to house demanding gifts and leftover food, and he was anything but cute. In 1927, Markus Rautio, a host of a children's radio program, told the kids that Santa secretly lived on "Ear Mountain" (Korvatunturi) in Lapland. The mountain has three peaks shaped like big ears, and it can hear the children's wishes carried on the north wind. The mountain is real, and it sits right on the border between Finland and Russia. Anyone who wants to visit must first get written permission from the border guard, so letters mailed there are forwarded to Santa Claus Village in Rovaniemi.

從紀念小樹到千禧公園的聖誕樹

來源：WTTW News

芝加哥每年的官方聖誕樹，不是從樹場買來的，而是由市民報名，從一般人家的後院選出來。

今年選中的是一棵將近50英尺（約15公尺）高的挪威雲杉（一種四季常綠的樹）。2004年，這家人的爺爺過世，有人在葬禮後送他們一小盆樹苗。隔年，他們把它種在院子裡。二十年後，它長成了大樹。

現在，這棵樹正在千禧公園立起來。11月20日星期五，城市會在這裡舉行點燈典禮，任何人都可以來看。

Chicago's official Christmas tree is never bought from a tree farm. Instead, residents apply, and the city selects it from a family's backyard. This year's pick is a Norway spruce (an evergreen) nearly 50 feet (about 15 meters) tall. In 2004, the family's grandfather died, and after the funeral someone gave them a small potted seedling. The next year, they planted it in their yard. Twenty years later, it has grown into a large tree. Now the tree is being raised in Millennium Park. On Friday, November 20, the city will hold its lighting ceremony there, and anyone is welcome to come see it.

農田底下的冷戰祕密

來源：The Time Chamber

英國網站The Time Chamber由Ed和Nick等幾位愛好者經營。他們把英國被丟下不用的冷戰雷達站和地下碉堡（很堅固的防守建築），一個一個仔細記錄下來。

冷戰時，人們很怕核子戰爭。英國在各地挖了小小的地下觀測站，用來監測核彈爆炸。

2009年，他們說服了Cuckfield的地方議會，修好一座藏在農夫草地裡的觀測站。那裡只剩一個水泥入口，他們就用自己收藏的冷戰儀器，把缺少的東西一件件裝回去。2010年，它第一次開放給大家參觀。

The British website The Time Chamber is run by a handful of enthusiasts, including Ed and Nick, who have been carefully documenting Britain's abandoned Cold War radar stations and underground bunkers (very sturdy defensive structures), one by one. During the Cold War, people were deeply afraid of nuclear war, so Britain dug small underground observation posts across the country to monitor nuclear bomb explosions. In 2009, they persuaded the Cuckfield local council to restore one such post, hidden in a farmer's field. Only a concrete entrance remained, so they used their own collection of Cold War equipment to put back the missing pieces one at a time. In 2010, it was opened to the public for the first time.
