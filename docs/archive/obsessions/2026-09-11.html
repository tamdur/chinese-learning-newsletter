兩百年的老戲曲，撞上重金屬

來源：WONDER 覺誌

「不是路」本來是北管（一種傳統戲曲音樂）裡的一首老曲子，流傳了兩百多年。台北樂團百合花在二○二一年，用這個名字做了一張專輯。

他們把南管唱腔、月琴唸歌、重金屬吉他和迪斯可放進同一首歌。奇妙的是，老戲曲的唱法擺在放克的貝斯上，一點也不奇怪，好像兩種聲音本來就有同一個心跳。

這張專輯拿下第三十三屆金曲獎的最佳台語專輯。

"Not a Path" was originally a traditional melody from beiguan—a form of classical Chinese opera music—that had been passed down for over two centuries. In 2021, the Taipei band Lilium released an album named after this tune. They merged nanyin vocal styles, moon lute recitations, heavy metal guitar, and disco into a single song. What's remarkable is that the classical opera vocals placed over a funk bassline don't sound strange at all—it's as if the two sound worlds had always shared the same heartbeat. The album won Best Taiwanese Album at the 33rd Golden Melody Awards.

帶著聖像逃難的修道院

來源：Valamo Monastery

一九四○年冬天，戰爭逼近，大約一百九十位修士離開了拉多加湖上的瓦拉姆修道院。他們帶著兩位聖人的聖像（畫在木板上的宗教畫），逃到芬蘭湖區的海納韋西，在湖邊蓋了新瓦拉姆。

今天這裡每年有十一萬人來訪。你可以學畫聖像、看展覽，也可以在一九七七年落成的主教堂裡聽早禱。院裡還有一座酒莊，用水果和莓果釀酒，成了最有名的伴手禮。小旅館也還在接待客人。

In the winter of 1940, as war crept closer, about 190 monks fled the Valaam Monastery on Lake Ladoga. They carried with them icons of two saints—religious paintings on wooden panels—and escaped to Heinävesi in Finland's lake region, where they built a new Valaam by the water's edge. Today, the monastery welcomes over 110,000 visitors annually. You can study icon painting, view exhibitions, and attend morning prayers in the cathedral that was completed in 1977. The monastery also runs a winery that produces fruit and berry wines, which have become its most celebrated souvenirs. A small guesthouse continues to accommodate visitors.

火爐邊的玻璃課

來源：Firebird Community Arts

芝加哥東加菲爾德公園有一間舊倉庫，裡面是「火鳥社區藝術」的工作室。十歲以上、沒有經驗的人都能報名玻璃課，把燒紅的玻璃吹成紙鎮或雙層小碗，隔一個星期再回來拿。

同一個工作室還有一個計畫，訓練在槍擊中活下來的年輕人當玻璃學徒。所以教你的老師，可能不是從美術系來的，而是從療傷的路上走過來的。

Tucked in an old warehouse in Chicago's East Garfield Park is the Firebird Community Arts studio. Anyone 10 or older with no prior experience can take a glass-blowing class, shaping molten glass into paperweights or double-walled bowls—you come back a week later to pick up your finished piece. The studio also runs a program training young survivors of gun violence as glass apprentices. Your instructor might not have studied fine art, but rather walked their own path of healing.

九百種泥土的顏色

來源：Early Futures

在美國北喀斯開山的一間小木屋裡，一位藝術家從二○一七年開始收集赭石（一種含鐵的土，人類最老的顏料）。到今天，她已經編號超過九百份樣本，來自世界各地的河床、採石場和路邊。

每一份都有筆記，記下它從哪裡來、誰撿的、以前和現在被拿來做什麼。這些土大多是陌生人寄給她的，有科學家、考古學家，也有純粹好奇的路人。

In a small cabin in Washington's North Cascades, an artist has been collecting ochre—iron-rich earth, humanity's oldest pigment—since 2017. Today, she has catalogued over nine hundred samples from riverbeds, quarries, and roadsides around the world. Each sample comes with notes documenting where it originated, who collected it, and what it was and is used for. Most of these soils were sent to her by strangers: scientists, archaeologists, and simply curious passersby.
