月琴詩人的電影之聲

來源：FOND/SOUND

一九八六年，來自北投的民謠歌手陳明章，為侯孝賢的電影《戀戀風塵》寫了配樂。他用月琴（一種圓形的傳統彈撥樂器）彈出河洛民間旋律，聽起來像從山村裡挖出來的聲音。隔年，這張專輯在法國拿下最佳音樂獎，是台灣電影配樂第一次得到國際大獎。

多年後，有人在陳明章家陽台的鳥籠下面找到了原始錄音帶，已經在太陽底下曬了好幾年。這些錄音能留下來，本身就是一個奇蹟。

In 1986, folk singer Chen Ming-zhang from Beitou composed the score for Hou Hsiao-hsien's film "Dust in the Wind." He played Taiwanese folk melodies on the moon lute—a round, traditional plucked instrument—producing sounds that seemed unearthed from remote mountain villages. The following year, the album won Best Music at a French film festival, marking Taiwan's first international award for a film score. Years later, someone discovered the original recording tapes beneath a birdcage on Chen's balcony, where they had been baking in the sun for years. That these recordings survived at all was itself a miracle.

五百年的婚禮啤酒

來源：Vice

芬蘭有一種古老的啤酒叫做 Sahti，已經有五百年歷史。它沒有過濾，加了杜松（一種常綠樹的果實）調味，過去只在婚禮和豐收時才會釀造。傳統上，都是由村裡的女性來釀酒。

今天，這個傳統在芬蘭內陸的「 Sahti 帶」地區最為活躍，小村莊至今仍舉辦自釀比賽。距離赫爾辛基約九十分鐘車程的 Lammi 小鎮，有一家一九八七年開業的小型釀酒廠，全年都能喝到這種古老的滋味。

Finland has an ancient beer called Sahti that dates back five hundred years. It's unfiltered and flavored with juniper—the berry of an evergreen tree—and was traditionally brewed only for weddings and harvest celebrations. In the old days, the women of the village did all the brewing. Today, the tradition thrives most vibrantly in the "Sahti Belt" region of inland Finland, where small villages still hold home-brewing competitions. In the town of Lammi, about ninety minutes' drive from Helsinki, a small brewery that opened in 1987 serves this ancient flavor year-round.

用形狀唱歌的人

來源：Comfort Station Logan Square

在芝加哥 Logan Square 的 Comfort Station，每個月第二和第四個星期二晚上七點，一群人圍成方形，一起唱一種有兩百多年歷史的美國讚美詩。這叫做 Sacred Harp，樂譜上的音符不是圓的，而是三角形、長方形、菱形等形狀。沒有指揮，每個人面對面同時唱出四個聲部。

下次活動是六月九日和二十三日，現場提供樂譜，不需要任何音樂經驗，任何人都可以參加。

At Comfort Station in Chicago's Logan Square, every second and fourth Tuesday evening at seven o'clock, a group gathers in a square formation to sing together a style of American hymn with over two hundred years of history. It's called Sacred Harp, and the musical notes on the sheet are not round circles, but rather triangles, rectangles, diamonds, and other shapes. There is no conductor; each person faces the others while simultaneously singing four different vocal parts. The next gatherings are June 9th and 23rd. Sheet music is provided on-site, and no musical experience is required—anyone can participate.

一個人的電影圖書館

來源：FilmGrab

FilmGrab 是一個由一位女性獨自經營的網站，收集了將近四千部電影的劇照（電影畫面截圖）。她在二〇一〇年開始做這件事，那時剛畢業卻碰上不景氣，只能在小成本電影劇組工作。

每一張圖都是她親手挑選、標記、上傳的，完全沒有用任何程式或人工智慧。到現在已經超過一億八千萬次瀏覽。她說這是「給光影、構圖和視覺故事的一封情書」。

FilmGrab is a website run single-handedly by one woman, housing nearly four thousand film stills—screenshots captured directly from movies. She started the project in 2010, fresh out of school during an economic downturn, working on low-budget film sets. Every image has been personally selected, tagged, and uploaded by her, entirely without automation or artificial intelligence. The site has now accumulated over 180 million views. She describes it as "a love letter to light, composition, and visual storytelling."
