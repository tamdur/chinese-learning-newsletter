恆春老歌手的月琴聲

來源：Taipei Times

陳達（1906-1981）是屏東恆春的老歌手。他眼睛不好，大家叫他「紅目達仔」。他很窮，常常一個人走在路上，彈著舊月琴（一種圓圓的琴），邊彈邊唱。

1967年，音樂學者許常惠和史惟亮到恆春找老歌，發現了他。1971年，史惟亮替他錄了一張唱片《民族樂手：陳達和他的歌》。

他的聲音很粗，像被海風吹過的老木頭。他最有名的歌是〈思想起〉。1976年，他到台北唱歌，正想寫「自己的歌」的大學生，都把他當成老師。

Chen Da (1906-1981) was an old singer from Hengchun in Pingtung. His eyes were weak, so people called him "Red-Eyed Da." He was very poor and often walked the roads alone, playing an old moon lute (a round-bodied stringed instrument) and singing as he went. In 1967, the music scholars Hsu Chang-hui and Shih Wei-liang travelled to Hengchun to look for old songs and discovered him. In 1971, Shih recorded an album for him, "Folk Musician: Chen Da and His Songs." His voice was rough, like old driftwood weathered by the sea wind. His most famous song is "Sixiangqi" (Thinking Back). In 1976 he came to Taipei to perform, and university students who were trying to write "songs of their own" treated him as a teacher.

小人馬提教你芬蘭禮貌

來源：Wikipedia

2015年，芬蘭設計師科霍寧開始畫一個小漫畫，主角叫馬提。他很怕跟不認識的人聊天，也怕別人站得太近。

這套漫畫叫「芬蘭人的惡夢」，在網路上很紅，2019年還出了書。書裡藏著真的芬蘭規矩：等公車時，人跟人要隔一個手臂遠；聊天時大家都不說話，也不算尷尬（不自在）。

三月去赫爾辛基度蜜月，搭電車時安靜一點、別靠太近，就是給芬蘭人最好的禮物。

In 2015, Finnish designer Kohonen began drawing a small comic strip starring a character named Matti. Matti is terrified of chatting with strangers and hates it when people stand too close. The series, titled "The Finn's Nightmare," became a hit online, and a book collection followed in 2019. The book is full of real Finnish etiquette: while waiting for a bus, people keep about an arm's length between them, and during conversation, silence is perfectly fine and nobody considers it awkward. If you honeymoon in Helsinki in March, the best gift you can give a Finn is to keep quiet on the tram and not crowd them.

一隻烤雞的祕密晚餐

來源：bar Berria (Tock)

芝加哥的 Logan Square 有一個「快閃」餐廳，叫 bar Berria。它沒有自己的店面，每一份菜單都只做一個晚上。

12月4日（星期五）晚上7點半，主廚 Sieger Bayer 和 Emily Abram 要辦「烤雞晚餐」：烤麵包、當季蔬菜、配菜和甜點，大家圍在一起分著吃。

一張票90美元，可以自己帶酒。要在 Tock 網站上買票，就像搶一場小音樂會的票。

In Chicago's Logan Square neighborhood, there is a pop-up restaurant called bar Berria. It has no storefront of its own, and each menu is served for just one night. On Friday, December 4, at 7:30 p.m., chefs Sieger Bayer and Emily Abram will host a "roast chicken dinner": toasted bread, seasonal vegetables, side dishes, and dessert, all shared family-style with everyone gathered around the table. Tickets are $90 each, and diners may bring their own wine. Tickets are sold through the Tock website, and getting one is a bit like scrambling for seats at a small concert.

乳酪皮上的小世界

來源：Microbial Foods

「微生物食物」（Microbial Foods）是一個小網站，由美國學者 Benjamin Wolfe 的研究室製作。他們把發酵（讓細菌慢慢改變食物）的食物，當成一座活的博物館。

網站上的「微生物圖鑑」像球員卡，一張一張介紹小生物：有一種黴菌（發霉的菌）會在乳酪皮上長出黃色的毛，還有一種紅色的酵母，會把乳酪染成粉紅色。

這些卡片來自他們2014年的研究：他們看了10個國家、137塊乳酪皮。原來一塊乳酪上，每天都有小生物在打架，也在合作。

"Microbial Foods" is a small website built by the lab of American scholar Benjamin Wolfe. The lab treats fermented foods, where bacteria slowly transform what they're made of, as a living museum. The site's "Microbe Field Guide" works like a set of trading cards, introducing the tiny organisms one by one. One mold (the kind of fungus that goes fuzzy) grows yellow fuzz on the rind of cheese, and a red yeast turns cheese pink. These cards come from the lab's 2014 research, in which they examined 137 cheese rinds from 10 countries. It turns out that on a single piece of cheese, tiny organisms are fighting and cooperating every day.
