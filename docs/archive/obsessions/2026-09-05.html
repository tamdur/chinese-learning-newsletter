一張寄到五個國家的專輯

來源：NME

台北歌手 9m88 本名唐玉琦，原本讀服裝設計，後來到紐約學爵士樂（一種美國音樂）。二零二二年，她把自己唱好的歌，寄給五個國家的音樂人。

美國的 Oddisee、日本的 StarRo、韓國的 SUMIN，每個人都用自己的方式，把她的歌重做一次。其中一首叫〈Star〉，後來變成她和美國歌手 Silas Short 合唱的慢歌。

從前大家記得的是她的復古（老派）都市流行風，這張專輯卻把台北變成全世界深夜音樂網裡的一站。

Taipei singer 9m88 (real name Tang Yuqi) originally studied fashion design before moving to New York to study jazz. In 2022, she sent her recorded songs to musicians across five different countries. Oddisee from the United States, StarRo from Japan, and SUMIN from Korea each remade her songs in their own distinctive ways. One track, "Star," eventually became a slow ballad duet with American singer Silas Short. While audiences had previously known her for her retro urban pop sound, this album transformed Taipei into a hub on the global late-night music network.

魚骨做的琴：芬蘭的坎特勒

來源：Wikipedia / Finnish Heritage Museum

芬蘭的民族史詩（很長的古老故事詩）《卡勒瓦拉》裡，白鬍子的智者維納莫寧，用一條大魚的下巴骨做成第一把琴，再拿馬尾的毛當弦。他一彈，整片森林都安靜下來聽。

十九世紀，芬蘭還被俄國管著。收集這部史詩的隆洛特自己也會彈這種琴，坎特勒於是成了芬蘭人想念家園的記號。

今天它有五條弦的小琴，也有四十條弦的大琴，在赫爾辛基的音樂廳裡還聽得到。

In Finland's national epic, the ancient narrative poem the Kalevala, the white-bearded sage Väinämöinen crafted the first harp from the jawbone of a great fish, stringing it with horsehair. When he played, the entire forest fell silent to listen. In the nineteenth century, Finland remained under Russian rule. Lönnrot, who collected this epic, was himself a skilled kantele player, and so the instrument became a symbol of the Finnish people's longing for their homeland. Today the kantele exists in many forms — small five-stringed harps and large forty-stringed instruments alike — and its music can still be heard in the concert halls of Helsinki.

七十個攤位的黑膠挖寶日

來源：Chicagoland Record & CD Collectors Show

二零二六年九月二十日星期天，芝加哥西南邊的 Countryside 宴會中心會擺出一百二十二張長桌，七十多位攤主帶著黑膠唱片和 CD 來賣。

早上七點是給認真挖寶的人進場，要多付一點錢。九點到下午四點開放給所有人，門票五塊美金。

主辦人 John Reidy 已經辦了幾十年，這是美國中西部最大的唱片交換會之一。

On Sunday, September 20, 2026, the Countryside Banquet Hall in southwest Chicago will host more than seventy vendors at 122 long tables selling vinyl records and CDs. The event opens early at 7 a.m. for serious collectors willing to pay an extra fee for early access. General admission runs from 9 a.m. to 4 p.m. for five dollars. Organizer John Reidy has been running this event for several decades — it's one of the largest record swap meets in the American Midwest.

會讀洞的織布機

來源：tillvonahnen.de

一位叫 Till von Ahnen 的工程師，自己用 Rust 這種程式語言，做出一台一八零一年法國提花織布機的模擬器（用電腦假裝成那台機器），還把做法寫成文章放上網。

那台老機器靠一串打了洞的卡片，決定每一次要把哪些線提起來，再複雜的花紋也能存下來、重複織出。

後來英國人巴貝奇設計計算機時，存記憶的辦法就是跟它學的。最早的程式語言，原來是織出來的。

Engineer Till von Ahnen built a simulator of the 1801 French Jacquard loom using Rust — essentially recreating the machine in code — and published an article online detailing how he pulled it off.

That ancient loom relied on a series of punched cards to determine which threads to lift on each pass. Remarkably, this simple system could encode and faithfully reproduce even the most intricate patterns. When the British mathematician Charles Babbage later designed his calculating engine, he borrowed this very same method for storing memory. The earliest programming language, as it turns out, was woven.
