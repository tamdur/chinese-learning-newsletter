阿爆：用母語唱出金曲獎歷史

來源：Golden Melody Awards / Abao

二〇二〇年，排灣族歌手阿爆發行了專輯「母親的舌頭」。這張專輯把排灣族傳統歌謠和電子音樂混在一起，聽起來既古老又新鮮。最動人的部分是：歌詞由阿爆和她的媽媽一起寫的。媽媽還記得老一輩的話，女兒把它變成歌——兩代人合力搶救一種正在消失的語言。這張專輯拿下金曲獎兩個大獎，是原住民語言專輯第一次得到跨類別的肯定。

In 2020, Paiwan singer A-Bao released the album "Mother's Tongue." This album blended traditional Paiwan folk songs with electronic music, creating a sound that felt both ancient and fresh. The most moving aspect was this: the lyrics were written together by A-Bao and her mother. Her mother remembered the words of the older generation, and the daughter transformed them into songs — two generations working together to rescue a language on the verge of extinction. The album won two major Golden Melody Awards, marking the first time an indigenous-language album received cross-category recognition. Producer Dizparity used electronic beats to bring ancient sounds into modern ears.

芬蘭荒野小屋：滑雪、極光、三溫暖

來源：Metsähallitus / Parks & Wildlife Finland

芬蘭北部有一條很有名的步道，叫做Hetta-Pallas，全長五十五公里，穿過拉普蘭的雪白山丘。每隔十到十五公里就有一間荒野小屋，住一晚只要十歐元。三月是最好的時候。白天變長了，可以慢慢滑雪；晚上天空常常出現綠色的極光，像一條光做的河流。其中一間小屋叫Hannukuru，有燒木柴的三溫暖。想像一下：滑了一天的雪，坐在熱騰騰的桑拿裡，窗外是結冰的山。這就是芬蘭人說的幸福。

Finland's northern region features a famous hiking trail called Hetta-Pallas, stretching fifty-five kilometers through Lapland's snow-covered mountains. Wilderness cabins dot the route every ten to fifteen kilometers, with nightly stays costing just ten euros. March is the ideal time to visit. Days grow longer, allowing for leisurely cross-country skiing; evenings frequently bring green auroras dancing across the sky, like rivers made of light. One cabin, Hannukuru, features a wood-burning sauna. Picture this: after a full day of skiing, you sit in a steaming sauna while frozen peaks frame the window outside. This, the Finns say, is happiness.

芝加哥菲律賓美食節回來了

來源：Chi-ibigan / Ravenswood Event Center

Chi-ibigan，芝加哥的菲律賓美食節，四月四日在Ravenswood Event Center登場。去年第一次辦就在四十五分鐘內賣光，今年從一層擴大到三層。一樓是鹹食區，有Kasama、Bayan Ko等名店的料理。三樓是甜點區。二樓最熱鬧——卡拉OK、麻將、DJ，還有整隻烤乳豬。菲律賓烤乳豬叫lechón，皮脆肉嫩，是節日的主角。如果你想去，記得早點買票——上次可是一下就沒了。

Chi-ibigan, Chicago's Filipino food festival, arrives on April 4th at the Ravenswood Event Center. Last year's inaugural event sold out in just forty-five minutes, and this year it's expanding from one floor to three. The ground floor features savory dishes from acclaimed restaurants like Kasama and Bayan Ko. The third floor is dedicated to desserts. The second floor is where the energy peaks — karaoke, mahjong, a DJ, and whole roasted suckling pigs. In Filipino cuisine, this dish is called lechón, prized for its crispy skin and tender meat, and it's the star of the festival. If you're planning to go, buy your tickets early — they disappeared instantly last time.
