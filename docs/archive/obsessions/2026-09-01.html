電線裡的島嶼：台灣模組合成器九人合輯

來源：ato.archives (Bandcamp)

二〇二五年七月十八日，台北的小廠牌 ato.archives 發行了一張合輯，叫《地平線上的脈動》。九首曲子，全都用模組合成器做成；那是一種要插上許多電線，才會發出聲音的機器。

選曲的人是 Ryan J Raffa。他在台灣的合成器圈子裡待了八年，認識了這些朋友。專輯裡有吵鬧的〈毛蟲〉，也有錄進青蛙叫聲的〈The Frog〉。沒有歌詞，沒有人唱歌，只有電壓變成音樂。

On July 18, 2025, a small independent label in Taipei called ato.archives released a compilation album titled “Pulses on the Horizon.” All nine tracks were created using modular synthesizers—machines that require patching numerous cables together to produce sound.

The curator was Ryan J Raffa, who has spent eight years in Taiwan's synthesizer community and knows these musicians personally. The album features the raucous track “Caterpillar,” alongside “The Frog,” which incorporates actual frog calls. There are no lyrics, no vocals—only voltage transformed into music.

藏在鋼裡的祕密：羅瓦涅米的芬蘭腰刀

來源：Finland, Naturally

幾乎每個芬蘭人都有一把 puukko，一種短短的腰刀。殺魚、削木頭、劈生火的柴，全靠它。

在北方城市羅瓦涅米，鐵匠羅塞利花了幾十年，調出自己的高碳鋼配方。他打鐵的力氣很大，老鐵鋪的水泥地被敲出一個很深的坑。鋼要燒到多熱、又要多慢冷卻，這個祕方連工廠裡的師傅都不完全知道。

羅瓦涅米還有一九二八年開的 Marttiini 刀廠。比起聖誕老人村，這裡也許更像真正的拉普蘭。

Nearly every Finn owns a puukko, a short belt knife used for everything—cleaning fish, carving wood, splitting kindling for fire.

In the northern city of Rovaniemi, the blacksmith Roselli spent decades perfecting his own high-carbon steel formula. He hammered with such force that the cement floor of his old smithy was worn into a deep pit. Exactly how hot the steel must get, and how slowly it must cool, is a recipe even the craftsmen in his own workshop never fully knew.

Rovaniemi is also home to the Marttiini knife factory, founded in 1928. It may capture the essence of true Lapland far better than the Santa Claus Village does.

工廠開門的兩天：拉文斯伍德藝術漫步

來源：Greater Ravenswood Chamber of Commerce

九月十二、十三日，早上十一點到下午六點，芝加哥北邊的 Ravenswood 大道會變成一條藝術長廊。從 Lawrence 街到 Irving Park 街，平常不開放的工廠、鐵工房和工作室，這兩天全部打開大門。

今年是第二十四屆。街上有七十五位以上的創作者擺攤；Lillstreet 藝術中心開版畫、陶藝和織布的體驗課；兩個舞台輪流演爵士樂和藍草音樂（美國鄉間的弦樂）；還有短片放映和餐車。入口在 Ravenswood 和 Berteau 路口，免費入場。

On September 12th and 13th, from 11 a.m. to 6 p.m., Ravenswood Avenue on Chicago's North Side turns into an art corridor. From Lawrence to Irving Park, factories, metalworking shops, and studios that are normally closed to the public open their doors for the weekend.

This is the 24th year of the event. More than 75 makers will have booths along the street; Lillstreet Art Center offers hands-on workshops in printmaking, ceramics, and weaving; two stages take turns hosting jazz and bluegrass; and there are short-film screenings and food trucks. The entrance is at Ravenswood and Berteau, and admission is free.

轉動地球，聽見遠方的電台

來源：Radio Garden (radio.garden)

打開 radio.garden，螢幕上是一顆會轉的地球，上面撒了一萬七千多個綠點。每一個綠點，都是此刻正在播音的電台。點下去，你就掉進奈及利亞的談話節目，或是蒙古鄉下的民謠電台。

它本來是二〇一六年的一件展覽作品，由設計師 Jonathan Puckey 為荷蘭聲音與影像研究所製作。後來阿姆斯特丹的小團隊 Moniker 把它變成免費網站。他們說，聽廣播應該像旅行，而不是像查資料。

Open radio.garden and you see a spinning globe on your screen, scattered with over seventeen thousand green dots. Each dot is a radio station broadcasting right now. Click on one and you tumble into a talk show from Nigeria, or a folk station in rural Mongolia.

It started as an exhibition piece in 2016, created by designer Jonathan Puckey for the Netherlands Institute for Sound and Vision. Later, the small Amsterdam team Moniker turned it into a free website. They say listening to the radio should feel like traveling, not like looking something up.
