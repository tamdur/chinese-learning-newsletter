夢東兄弟：用老合成器重新打造廟會音樂

NTS Radio / Guruguru Brain Records

台北有一對兄弟，組了一個叫「夢東」的樂團。他們做的音樂很特別：把台灣廟會的聲音，和舊式合成器（一種電子樂器）混在一起。他們稱自己的風格為「亞洲宇宙音樂」。

二〇二〇年的專輯《Mystery》聽起來像是走進一座未來的廟：道教的鑼鼓聲在電子音效裡飄動。專輯還附了一本原創畫冊。英國的 NTS Radio 也注意到了他們——台灣廟口的聲音，正在走向世界。

Taipei is home to a pair of brothers who formed a band called "Dream East." Their music is quite distinctive: they blend the sounds of Taiwan's temple festivals with vintage synthesizers — a type of electronic instrument. They call their style "Asian Cosmic Music."

Their 2020 album "Mystery" sounds like stepping into a temple of the future: the gongs and drums of Taoism drift through electronic effects. The album came with an original art book. British radio station NTS Radio took notice too — the sounds from Taiwan's temple streets are reaching the world.

睡在玻璃屋裡看極光

Kakslauttanen Arctic Resort / Arctic TreeHouse Hotel

三月的芬蘭北部，是看極光（北方天空的彩色光）最後的好時機。最舒服的看法，是躺在暖暖的玻璃圓頂小屋裡，透過屋頂看天空。

Kakslauttanen 度假村在九〇年代發明了這種玻璃屋，外面零下三十度也不會結霜（起霧）。另一個好選擇是 Arctic TreeHouse Hotel，設計更現代。很多玻璃屋裝了極光警報器——光一出現就會叫醒你。從赫爾辛基飛過去只要九十分鐘。

Late March in Finnish Lapland is the final good window for viewing the northern lights — the colorful glow dancing across the northern sky. The most comfortable way to watch is to lie in a warm glass dome cabin and gaze at the sky through the roof.

Kakslauttanen resort pioneered this glass cabin design in the 1990s — even at thirty degrees below zero, the glass won't frost over. Another excellent option is Arctic TreeHouse Hotel, which has a more modern design. Many glass cabins are equipped with aurora alarms that wake you the moment the lights appear. It's only a ninety-minute flight from Helsinki.

芝加哥遇見維也納：免費藝術展

Chicago Cultural Center

四月十日到七月十九日，芝加哥文化中心有一場免費展覽，名叫《裝飾與資訊》。十四位藝術家一起探索芝加哥和維也納之間的思想交流。

這兩座城市的關係比你想的還深：維也納建築師魯斯曾說「裝飾是罪」，而芝加哥學派的經濟學也改變了世界。開幕是四月十日晚上六點，地點在華盛頓街七十八號。

From April 10 to July 19, the Chicago Cultural Center is hosting a free exhibition called "Ornament and Information." Fourteen artists together explore the intellectual exchange between Chicago and Vienna.

The relationship between these two cities runs deeper than you might think: Vienna architect Loos once said "ornament is crime," while the Chicago School of economics transformed the world. The opening is on the evening of April 10 at 6 p.m., at 78 Washington Street.
