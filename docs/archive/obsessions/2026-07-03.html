被偷走的歌聲，又唱了回來

來源：Wikipedia / The News Lens

一九九六年，奧運（世界運動大會）的主題曲〈回到純真〉紅遍全球。但很少人知道，那段歌聲來自台灣阿美族的農夫郭英男。德國樂團「謎」偷偷取樣了他唱的婚禮祝歌，卻沒問過他。

郭英男一家打官司，在一九九九年討回了公道。之後他錄下自己的專輯《生命之環》，把最原始的歌聲，配上簡單的電子音樂。這一次，歌聲屬於他自己。

二○○二年他過世前，還拿下了金曲獎。

In 1996, the Olympic Games' theme song "Return to Innocence" became a global sensation. But few people knew that the voice belonged to Kuo Ying-nan, a farmer from Taiwan's Amis indigenous community. The German band Enigma had secretly sampled a wedding blessing song he had sung—without asking his permission. Kuo and his family sued and won their case in 1999. Afterward, he recorded his own album, "The Circle of Life," pairing his most authentic voice with simple electronic music. This time, the voice was truly his own. Before his death in 2002, he also won the Golden Melody Award.

一日餐廳：人人都能當老闆

來源：Atlas Obscura

在芬蘭，每年有四天，任何人都可以開一家餐廳。這叫「餐廳日」，二○一一年從首都赫爾辛基開始。

原本一位朋友想開小店，卻被一堆規定卡住。大家後來發現：只開一天的店，芬蘭法律根本不管。於是廚房、內衣店、甚至舊公車站，都變成了一日餐廳，賣什麼、賣多少錢，都自己決定。

如今它已傳到七十個國家。三月來蜜月，說不定正好碰上。

In Finland, there are four days each year when anyone can open a restaurant. Called "Restaurant Day," it started in 2011 in the capital, Helsinki. The idea began when a friend wanted to open a small shop but ran into bureaucratic obstacles. People later realized that Finnish law doesn't actually regulate one-day-only establishments. So kitchens, lingerie shops, and even abandoned bus stations became pop-up restaurants, where owners decided what to sell and how much to charge. Today, the concept has spread to seventy countries. If you're honeymooning in March, you might just catch one.

在珍亞當斯的後院，學澆水

來源：Jane Addams Hull-House Museum

七月十一日星期六，芝加哥的珍亞當斯赫爾館博物館，要開一堂免費的花園課，教大家怎麼澆水，讓植物長得好。帶課的是來自皮爾森區的社區菜園志工。

這堂課連著博物館的展覽「常青之城」。一百多年前，赫爾館就幫來到芝加哥的移民開闢（開墾）菜園和遊樂場。不用有種菜的經驗，任何人都能來。

On Saturday, July 11, the Jane Addams Hull House Museum in Chicago will offer a free gardening workshop on how to water plants so they thrive. The class is led by a community garden volunteer from the Pilsen neighborhood and ties in with the museum's exhibition "Perennial City." Over a century ago, Hull House helped immigrants arriving in Chicago establish vegetable gardens and playgrounds. No gardening experience necessary—everyone is welcome.

搶救二十二萬個快要消失的遊戲

來源：Flashpoint Archive

二○二○年，Adobe 關掉了 Flash 播放器，許多人小時候在網路上玩的遊戲，眼看就要永遠不見。

一位叫 Ben 的愛好者不甘心，開始一個一個把它們存下來，連遊戲需要的伺服器回應也一起保存。後來有五十多位志工加入。

到二○二六年七月，他們已經救回二十二萬多個網頁遊戲和動畫，重建了網路失去的那段時光。

In 2020, Adobe shut down Flash Player, and countless games that people had played online as children seemed destined to disappear forever. A fan named Ben refused to accept this loss and began saving them one by one, preserving not just the games themselves but also the server responses they required to function. Eventually, more than fifty volunteers joined his effort. By July 2026, they had rescued over 220,000 web games and animations, restoring an entire era of internet history that was almost lost.
