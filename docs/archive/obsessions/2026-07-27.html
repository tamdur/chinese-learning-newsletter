最後一張被查禁的唱片

來源：The News Lens（關鍵評論網）

一九九零年，台灣解嚴才三年，龐克（一種又吵又直接的搖滾樂）歌手趙一豪推出了第一張專輯《把我自己掏出來》。吵鬧的吉他加上沙啞的吼叫，讓新聞局說它歌詞粗俗，把唱片全部收走。

為了重新上架，唱片公司只好改歌名：《把我自己掏出來》變成《把我自己收回來》，〈震動〉改叫〈創造〉。這場查禁，反而讓這張唱片成了台灣地下搖滾的起點。

In 1990, just three years after Taiwan lifted martial law, punk singer Chao Yi-hao released his debut album 「Take Myself Out.」 With its loud guitars and raw, hoarse vocals, the Government Information Office denounced the lyrics as vulgar and confiscated every copy. To get the album back into stores, the record company had no choice but to rename the songs: 「Take Myself Out」 became 「Take Myself Back,」 and 「Vibration」 was changed to 「Creation.」 The censorship, ironically, is what made the record the starting point of Taiwan's underground rock scene.

湖中小島上的磚牆實驗室

來源：Finnish Design Shop / Design Stories

芬蘭建築師阿爾托（Alvar Aalto）和太太艾莉莎，在派揚內湖的松林小島上，蓋了一棟夏天的房子。院子的牆被分成大約五十格，每格試一種不同的磚頭和瓷磚，像一本材料樣本書，後來都用在他的大建築上。

當年上島，只能坐他自己設計的小木船。要提醒的是，屋內導覽只在夏天開放，三月去只能在外面看。可以順道去附近的阿爾托美術館和賽奈察洛市政廳，那兩處整年都開。

The Finnish architect Alvar Aalto and his wife Elissa built a summer house on a pine-covered island in Lake Päijänne. The courtyard walls were divided into roughly fifty panels, each testing a different brick and tile — in effect a materials sample book, and the results went on to be used in his major buildings. Back then the only way onto the island was a small wooden boat Aalto designed himself. One thing to note: interior tours run only in summer, so a March visit means seeing the house from the outside. You can pair it with the nearby Alvar Aalto Museum and Säynätsalo Town Hall, both open year-round.

走進老闆為工人蓋的城

來源：Friends of Pullman National Historical Park (Eventbrite)

八月二日星期天下午一點，普爾曼國家歷史公園之友會帶大家走一趟。從展覽館出發，走一哩路，大約九十分鐘。

一八八零年代，火車車廂大老闆普爾曼在芝加哥南邊蓋了一整座城給工人住，房租直接從工資裡扣掉。一八九四年工資被砍、房租卻沒降，就爆發了有名的普爾曼大罷工。這片街區到二零二二年才正式成為國家公園。

On Sunday, August 2nd at 1 PM, the Friends of Pullman National Historical Park lead a walking tour. It sets out from the Exhibit Hall, covers about a mile, and takes roughly 90 minutes. In the 1880s, the railcar magnate George Pullman built an entire town south of Chicago for his workers to live in, with rent deducted straight from their wages. In 1894 wages were cut but rent was not, and the famous Pullman Strike broke out. The neighborhood only officially became a national park in 2022.

一個人畫了二十五年的口音地圖

來源：aschmann.net

阿希曼（Rick Aschmann）原本是聖經翻譯員，語言學全靠自學。他花了二十多年，只用一套很普通的繪圖軟體，一層一層畫出北美英語的口音地圖，沒有用任何專業製圖工具。

地圖裡的聲音來自將近一千段網友寄來的錄音，有政治人物、福音歌手，也有賽車手。他說，只要聽五個單字怎麼唸，就能猜出你是哪裡人。到今天，還是有陌生人不斷寄錄音給他。

Rick Aschmann started out as a Bible translator and taught himself linguistics from scratch. Over more than twenty years, using nothing but an ordinary drawing program — no professional cartography tools at all — he built up a map of North American English accents layer by layer. The sounds behind the map come from nearly a thousand recordings sent in by people online: politicians, gospel singers, race car drivers. He says five words is all it takes to guess where you are from. Strangers are still sending him recordings today.
