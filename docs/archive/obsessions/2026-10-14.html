從金門前線回來的歌聲

來源：Sound Traces

一九六一年，南王民聲康樂隊從金門勞軍（到軍中表演給士兵看）回來。這個隊伍有十二個人，大多是台東南王村的卑南族人。

他們一回來就走進台北的錄音室，錄下《台灣山地同胞舞曲集》。這張唱片由鈴鈴唱片發行，是戰後最早的原住民流行唱片。

五位女歌手唱著卑南族的跳舞歌，七位男樂手在旁邊伴奏。在那個緊張的年代，部落的歌聲就這樣被留了下來。

In 1961, the Nanwang Minsheng Entertainment Troupe returned from a morale-boosting tour of Kinmen, performing for the soldiers stationed there. The group had twelve members, most of them Puyuma people from Nanwang Village in Taitung.

Right after they got back, they went straight into a recording studio in Taipei and cut Dance Songs of Taiwan's Mountain Compatriots. The record was released by Ling Ling Records and was the earliest Indigenous pop record of the postwar era.

Five women singers performed Puyuma dance songs while seven male musicians provided accompaniment. In those tense years, the songs of the village were preserved in this way.

穿上橘色衣服，漂在冰海上

來源：Wikipedia / Visit Finland

芬蘭北邊的凱米（Kemi）有一艘老破冰船，叫做「桑波號」。它在一九六〇年於赫爾辛基造好，工作了快三十年，替貨船打開結冰的海路。

一九八七年，凱米市把它買下，不讓它被拆掉。現在它每年載大約一萬名遊客出海。

最特別的時刻，是穿上橘色的保暖救生衣，直直地漂在冰冷的海水裡，身體卻一點也不濕。三月時，這裡的冰還可能超過一公尺厚。

In Kemi, in northern Finland, there is an old icebreaker called the Sampo. It was built in Helsinki in 1960 and spent nearly thirty years clearing icy sea lanes so cargo ships could get through.

In 1987, the city of Kemi bought the ship to keep it from being scrapped. Today it carries about 10,000 visitors out to sea every year.

The most memorable moment is pulling on an orange thermal survival suit and floating upright in the freezing water, somehow without getting wet at all. In March, the ice here can still be more than a meter thick.

走進藍調大師的家

來源：WTTW News

十月十七、十八日，芝加哥的「開放建築日」又來了。兩百多棟平常不開放的建築，都可以免費參觀，不用預約。

今年最讓人期待的新地點，是藍調（一種美國黑人音樂）大師 Muddy Waters 的老家。他從一九五四年住到一九七三年，常常請樂手朋友來家裡一起玩音樂。

現在，他的曾孫女正把這棟房子改成博物館、小舞台和錄音室。地址：4339 S. Lake Park Ave.，先到先進。

On October 17 and 18, Chicago's Open House Chicago weekend is back. More than two hundred buildings that are normally closed to the public can be toured for free, and no reservation is needed.

This year's most anticipated new site is the former home of Muddy Waters, the blues master (blues is a style of American Black music). He lived there from 1954 to 1973, and he often invited musician friends over to the house to play music together.

Now his great-granddaughter is turning the house into a museum, a small stage, and a recording studio. Address: 4339 S. Lake Park Ave. First come, first served.

一個人守護的彈珠台百科

來源：PinWiki

二〇一一年，一個很受歡迎的彈珠台修理網站不再對大家開放。玩家 Casey Gardner 擔心這些知識會消失，就自己做了免費的線上百科 PinWiki。十幾年來，大部分的費用都是他自己付的。

網站裡藏著很多小故事。例如一九三〇年代，有人發明了抓「搖機台」的裝置：一顆小球放在杯子裡的柱子上，機器一搖，球就掉下來。

彈珠台的老祖先，是十八世紀歐洲的一種桌上遊戲。

In 2011, a popular pinball repair website stopped being open to the public. Player Casey Gardner worried that this knowledge would disappear, so he built PinWiki, a free online encyclopedia, himself. For more than a decade, he has paid most of the costs out of his own pocket.

The site is full of little stories. For example, in the 1930s, someone invented a device for catching people who shook the machine: a small ball sat in a cup on a post, and when the machine was jolted, the ball dropped.

The ancestor of the pinball machine was a tabletop game from eighteenth-century Europe.
