借走的歌聲：郭英男與奧運主題曲

來源：Wikipedia (Chinese) / The News Lens

一九八八年，台東馬蘭部落的阿美族長者郭英男和妻子，錄下一首古老的《飲酒歡樂歌》。五年後，德國樂團把這段歌聲放進自己的新歌裡。一九九六年亞特蘭大奧運，全世界都聽見了，卻沒有人知道他的名字。

郭英男打了三年官司，一九九九年才和解。這場官司讓台灣開始立法保護原住民的傳統歌曲。一九九八年，他出版專輯《生命之環》，收了十首阿美族的歌；年老的嗓音（說話唱歌的聲音）配上寬廣的電子音樂，聽起來像海浪。

In 1988, Guo Yingnan, an Amis elder from Malan village in Taitung, and his wife recorded an ancient song titled 'Song of Drinking Joy.' Five years later, a German band sampled this recording and incorporated it into their new track. In 1996, when the song was featured at the Atlanta Olympics, the world heard it—but no one knew his name. Guo Yingnan pursued a legal battle for three years before reaching a settlement in 1999. This lawsuit became a turning point, prompting Taiwan to enact legislation protecting indigenous traditional songs. In 1998, he released the album 'Circle of Life,' featuring ten Amis songs. His weathered voice, layered beneath expansive electronic music, evokes the sound of ocean waves.

空下來的鐵工廠，長出一座藝術村

來源：Fiskars Village

赫爾辛基往西開一小時，有個小村子叫菲斯卡（Fiskars）。這裡從一六四九年就開始打鐵，一九八零年代工廠搬走後，村子幾乎空了。藝術家用很便宜的租金搬進空工廠，把它變成設計小鎮。

現在村裡六百個居民，大約三分之一加入了 Onoma 這個藝術家合作社，一起經營商店、畫廊和工作室。冬天特別安靜，一八三六年開張的老旅館還亮著燈，正好停下來住一晚。

An hour's drive west of Helsinki sits a small village called Fiskars. Iron-working has been the trade here since 1649, but when the factories moved away in the 1980s, the village emptied out. Artists moved into the vacant factories at bargain rents and transformed the place into a design town. Today, 600 residents call it home, and about one-third have joined Onoma, an artists' cooperative that runs shops, galleries, and studios together. Winter here is especially quiet—and the old inn that opened in 1836 still has its lights on, making it the perfect spot to stop for a night.

一座湖中小島，一家人的一百三十年

來源：Chicago Maritime Museum

九月二十九日星期一，芝加哥航海博物館請來作家蘇珊・華瑟曼，談她的新書《下船之後：北馬尼圖島的故事》。當天有茶會、演講，也可以請她簽名。

她家從一八九三年起，每年夏天都住在密西根湖上這座偏遠的小島。老木屋有一部分，是用那一年芝加哥世界博覽會剩下的材料蓋的。書裡寫大湖的航線，怎麼決定了小島的伐木（砍樹）、農作和度假生活。

On Monday, September 29th, the Chicago Maritime Museum hosts author Susan Wasserman to discuss her new book, "After Disembarking: The Story of North Manitou Island." The day features a tea reception and lecture, with opportunities to request her autograph. Her family has spent every summer on this remote island in Lake Michigan since 1893. Part of the old wooden house was built using leftover materials from that year's Chicago World's Fair. The book explores how the Great Lake's shipping routes shaped the island's logging, farming, and summer life.

一個人的陀螺博物館

來源：Rod Cross Home Page

澳洲雪梨大學的退休物理學家羅德・克羅斯，把自己的大學網頁做成了一座小小的博物館。他用手畫的圖和親手做的實驗，解釋陀螺（會旋轉的玩具）為什麼轉得快時站得直，一慢下來就馬上倒。這個問題，物理學家想了一百多年才解開。

同一個網站上還有幾十篇他寫的文章，談網球的旋轉、球棒的甜蜜點、球為什麼會彈跳。沒有廣告，也不收錢，只有一個科學家的好奇心。

Rod Cross, a retired physicist at the University of Sydney, has turned his university webpage into a small museum. Using hand-drawn diagrams and experiments of his own design, he explains why a spinning top stands upright when spinning fast, then topples the moment it slows—a puzzle physicists spent more than a century unraveling. The site also hosts dozens of articles he's written about tennis ball spin, the sweet spot on a bat, and why balls bounce. No advertisements, no charge—simply one scientist's curiosity.
