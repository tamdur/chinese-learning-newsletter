當爵士鋼琴遇上笛子：絲竹空的紙鳶

來源：All About Jazz

在台北，有一個叫「絲竹空」的樂團，名字的意思是「絲線、竹子和空」。團長彭郁雯在美國伯克利音樂學院學過爵士鋼琴，回來後把爵士和中國傳統樂器混在一起。笛子、二胡、阮（一種圓形的彈撥樂器）都加入了。

他們的專輯《紙鳶》（紙做的風箏）第一首歌來自一千七百年前的古詩，笛子的聲音飄在爵士鋼琴上面，像風箏飛在天上。

In Taipei, there's an ensemble called "Silk Bamboo Space," a name meaning "silk threads, bamboo, and emptiness." The group's leader, Peng Yu-wen, studied jazz piano at Berkeley College of Music in the United States, and upon returning, began blending jazz with traditional Chinese instruments. The bamboo flute, erhu, and ruan (a round plucked instrument) all found their place in the ensemble. On their album "Paper Kite" (a kite made of paper), the opening track draws from an ancient poem from seventeen hundred years ago, with the flute's voice floating atop the jazz piano like a kite soaring through the sky.

芬蘭荒野裡的免費小屋

來源：thisisFINLAND (finland.fi)

芬蘭的國家公園裡藏著五百多間免費的荒野小屋，芬蘭語叫 autiotupa。屋裡有柴火、煮飯的工具，全部由政府出錢準備。不用預約，不用付錢，可以住一到兩晚。

三月的時候，你可以在帕拉斯—于列斯國家公園滑雪，白天在雪地裡走，晚上到下一間小屋休息。沒有人群，只有安靜的森林。

Finland's national parks hide over 500 free wilderness cabins, called autiotupa in Finnish. Each cabin comes stocked with firewood and cooking equipment, all provided by the government. No reservations needed, no fees—you can stay one or two nights. In March, you can ski through Pallas-Yllästunturi National Park, hiking across the snow by day and retreating to the next cabin each evening. No crowds, just the quiet of the forest.

親手做一個書盒：Spudnik Press 工作坊

來源：Spudnik Press

六月七日中午十二點到下午六點，芝加哥 West Hubbard Street 的 Spudnik Press 有一堂手工書盒課。設計師 Amira Hegazy 會教你做蛤殼盒（像貝殼一樣打開的盒子）和書套，用來保護你珍惜的作品。

不需要任何經驗。盒子不只是包裝，它本身就是一件設計作品。

On June 7th from noon to 6 p.m., Spudnik Press on West Hubbard Street in Chicago is offering a workshop on handmade book boxes. Designer Amira Hegazy will teach you how to make clamshell boxes (boxes that open like shells) and book sleeves to protect your treasured work. No prior experience needed. These boxes are more than mere packaging—they are design pieces in their own right.

一個人花二十八年寫的撲克牌百科

來源：The World of Playing Cards (wopc.co.uk)

英國人 Simon Wintle 從一九九六年開始建一個網站，專門記錄全世界撲克牌的歷史，到現在已經有四千八百八十篇文章。

你可以在這裡發現：今天的四種花色來自十三世紀埃及的紙牌，而那些紙牌又是從中國的錢牌來的。你還會知道，任天堂在一八八九年是靠手工做花牌起家的。

British man Simon Wintle started building a website in 1996 dedicated to recording the history of playing cards from around the world. By now, it contains 4,880 articles. Here you can discover that today's four suits originated from thirteenth-century Egyptian playing cards, which themselves came from Chinese money cards. You'll also learn that Nintendo got its start in 1889 by handcrafting flower cards.
