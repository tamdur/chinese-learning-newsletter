一天一首歌的考驗

來源：China Daily

一九八〇年代，年輕的黃韻玲在台灣當羅大佑的助理。羅大佑給她一個很難的功課：一年裡，每天都要寫一首新歌。

她後來說，那是「很可怕的經驗」。可是這些歌累積下來，變成她一九八六年在滾石唱片發行的第一張專輯《憂傷男孩》。

這張唱片把溫柔的情歌和早期的電子合成器聲音放在一起，聽起來又新又真。她後來成為台灣很受尊敬的音樂製作人。

In the 1980s, a young Huang Yun-ling was working in Taiwan as an assistant to Luo Dayou. Luo gave her a tough assignment: write a new song every day for a full year. She later called it a "terrifying experience." But the songs added up, and they became her first album, Sad Boy (憂傷男孩), released by Rock Records in 1986. The record paired tender love songs with the sounds of early electronic synthesizers, and it sounded fresh and sincere at the same time. She later became one of Taiwan's most respected music producers.

把拉普蘭的冰放進玻璃杯

來源：Iittala

一九六〇年代末，芬蘭設計師塔皮奧·維卡拉想用玻璃留住拉普蘭冰塊融化時的樣子。

他先用手在模子上刻出冰面的紋路，再和伊塔拉（Iittala）的吹玻璃師傅一起試了一千多個小時。

一九六八年，「Ultima Thule」玻璃杯誕生了，成為世界上最有名的杯子之一。三月去芬蘭，拿起一杯，就像握著一片冬天。

In the late 1960s, Finnish designer Tapio Wirkkala wanted to use glass to capture the way Lapland ice looks as it melts. He first pressed the texture of the ice surface into a mold by hand, then spent more than a thousand hours experimenting with the glassblowers at Iittala. In 1968, the "Ultima Thule" tumbler was born, becoming one of the most famous drinking glasses in the world. Visit Finland in March, pick one up, and it feels like holding a piece of winter.

在芝加哥親手做一隻甲蟲標本

來源：The Insect Asylum (Eventbrite)

芝加哥Avondale區有一家奇特的小店，叫「昆蟲收容所」（The Insect Asylum）。每個月第三個星期六，這裡都開甲蟲標本課。

下一堂課在十二月十九日。不需要經驗，你可以自己選甲蟲，價錢從八十五美元起。花一百美元，還能上兩個小時，親手打開寶石甲蟲閃亮的翅膀。

下課時，你會帶走一隻插在針上、可以直接擺出來的標本。

On Chicago's Avondale side, there is a quirky little shop called The Insect Asylum. On the third Saturday of every month, it runs a beetle-pinning class. The next one is on December 19. No experience is needed. You can choose your own beetles, with prices starting at $85. For $100, you get two hours to spread the shimmering wings of a jewel beetle with your own hands. When class ends, you take home a pinned specimen on a needle, ready to display.

哪些花不能帶進家門？

來源：Plant-Lore Archive

英國植物學家羅伊·維克里花了四十多年，在網站Plant-Lore上收集和植物有關的民間傳說，可以照地區和植物種類查找。

一九八〇年代的一次調查發現，人們最怕帶進屋裡的花是山楂花（一種開白色小花的樹），佔所有「不吉利」回報的百分之二十三。很多人記得，小時候拿山楂花進門會被大人罵。

有人認為，這是因為山楂花的味道有點像死去的動物。

British botanist Roy Vickery spent more than forty years collecting folklore about plants for the website Plant-Lore, which can be searched by region and by plant species.

A survey in the 1980s found that the flower people were most afraid to bring indoors was hawthorn blossom (a tree with small white flowers), accounting for 23 percent of all "unlucky" responses. Many people remember being scolded by adults as children for carrying hawthorn blossom through the front door. Some believe this is because hawthorn flowers smell a little like a dead animal.
