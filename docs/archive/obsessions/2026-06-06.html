牛騷New Soul：客家老調，變成年輕人的靈魂樂

來源：Taiwan Beats

兩次拿下金曲獎的黃子軒，在二○二四年初推出第一張個人專輯《牛騷》。「牛騷」的客家話念起來，就像英文的「New Soul」，是一個很巧的雙關語（一句話兩種意思）。

他把客家老民謠的旋律，和靈魂樂、電子樂融在一起。以前的客語歌多是唱給老人聽；這張專輯想找回的，是心裡有客家血脈、卻沒聽過這種音樂的年輕人。

Huang Zi-xuan, a two-time Golden Melody Award winner, released his debut solo album "牛騷" (Niu Sao) in early 2024. "牛騷" sounds like the English phrase "New Soul" when spoken in Hakka, making it a clever bilingual pun—one phrase with two meanings.

He blends the melodies of traditional Hakka folk songs with soul and electronic music. In the past, Hakka-language songs were mostly performed for older listeners; this album aims to reconnect with young people who carry Hakka heritage in their hearts but have never heard this kind of music before.

世界第一愛喝咖啡：芬蘭人為什麼只喝淺焙

來源：Wise Nose

芬蘭是全世界最會喝咖啡的國家，每個人一年喝掉的咖啡豆重達十二公斤。他們幾乎只喝淺焙（烘得比較淡的咖啡），因為早期用煮的方法太強，深焙會變得很苦，久了就成了習慣。

在芬蘭，喝咖啡是大事。上班的咖啡休息時間受法律保護；到人家家裡作客，如果主人請你喝咖啡，最好別推辭，不然會很失禮。

Finland is the world's greatest coffee-drinking nation, with each person consuming roughly twelve kilograms of coffee beans annually. Finns drink almost exclusively light roasts—coffee roasted to a lighter color—because their early brewing methods were too intense, and dark roasts became unbearably bitter. Over time, this preference hardened into tradition.

In Finland, coffee drinking is serious business. Workplace coffee breaks are legally protected; when you visit someone's home, if your host offers you coffee, you'd better accept. Declining would be considered quite rude.

火中取寶：芝加哥的樂燒陶器之夜

來源：Mighty Mud Classes

芝加哥的陶藝教室 Mighty Mud，六月要辦一場「樂燒」陶器之夜，費用五十五美元，五歲以上都能參加。你挑一件做好的陶器，自己上色，再看老師放進高溫的窯裡燒。

燒到發紅時，老師會把陶器夾出來，丟進裝滿木屑的桶子裡蓋起來。煙火的變化會在表面留下黑色和金屬般的光澤，每一件都不一樣。當晚就能帶回家，不過只能擺著看，不能裝食物。

Mighty Mud, a pottery studio in Chicago, is hosting a Raku firing night in June for fifty-five dollars per person. Anyone five and older can participate. You choose a finished piece of pottery, paint it yourself, then watch the instructor place it in a high-temperature kiln.

When the piece turns red, the instructor removes it with tongs and places it in a bucket filled with wood shavings, then covers it. The smoke and flames create black markings and metallic luster on the surface—each piece comes out unique. You can take your work home the same evening, though it's for display only and shouldn't be used to hold food.

一個人的偵探：翻出百年老報，改寫西洋棋歷史

來源：Chess Marginalia

「Chess Marginalia」這個網站，只由一個人經營。他回頭去翻十九世紀的德國棋刊和舊報紙，去查那些被大家抄了一百多年、卻沒人確認過的說法。

他找到的證據指出，有名的「丹麥開局」其實是一位名叫德雷爾（Dreier）的人發明的，推翻了以前的故事。這就是網路最迷人的一面：有人純粹因為熱愛，把小事做到最深。

The website "Chess Marginalia" is run by just one person. He goes back through nineteenth-century German chess journals and old newspapers, checking claims that everyone has been copying for over a hundred years without anyone actually verifying them.

The evidence he found suggests that the famous "Danish Opening" was actually invented by someone named Dreier, overturning the previous account. This is the internet at its most compelling: someone doing small things with absolute depth, driven purely by passion.
