讓稻田開口唱歌

來源：天下雜誌

2006年，美濃（高雄的客家小鎮）出生的歌手林生祥，和作詞人鍾永豐，一起做了客家話專輯《種樹》。歌裡，大樹、稻田和農夫都在說話，說出土地和天氣慢慢改變的心事。

那一年，林生祥到沖繩（日本南方的島）學節奏和呼吸，把美濃山歌的味道，和沖繩海邊的音樂混在一起。

隔年，鍾永豐靠這張專輯拿下金曲獎最佳作詞人。很多人說，這張唱片是林生祥「跟著土地一起長大」的音樂起點。

In 2006, singer Lin Sheng-xiang, born in Meinong (a Hakka town in Kaohsiung), teamed up with lyricist Chung Yung-feng to make Planting Trees (種樹), a Hakka-language album. In the songs, big trees, rice paddies and farmers all speak, voicing their worries about how the land and the weather are slowly changing. That same year, Lin traveled to Okinawa (a southern island of Japan) to study its rhythms and breathing, mixing the flavor of Meinong mountain songs with the music of Okinawa's seaside. The next year, Chung won the Golden Melody Award for Best Lyricist on the strength of this album. Many people say the record was the musical starting point of Lin's journey of "growing up together with the land."

芬蘭人的惡夢：請離我遠一點

來源：Wikipedia

住在芬蘭奧盧（芬蘭北部的城市）的設計師 Karoliina Korhonen，畫了漫畫《芬蘭人的惡夢》。主角馬蒂最怕的事，是跟陌生人聊天、突然被稱讚，還有在公車站跟別人站得太近。

沒想到，這部漫畫在中國爆紅。網友發明了「精芬」這個詞，意思是「心裡住著一個芬蘭人」，用來笑自己愛安靜、愛獨處。

三月去芬蘭玩，記得：排隊站遠一點，少聊天。在芬蘭，安靜不是冷淡，而是舒服。

Karoliina Korhonen, a designer living in Oulu (a city in northern Finland), drew a comic called A Finn's Nightmare. The protagonist, Matti, is most afraid of chatting with strangers, of suddenly being complimented, and of standing too close to someone at a bus stop. Unexpectedly, the comic became a huge hit in China. Netizens coined the term 精芬 (jīng fēn), meaning "a Finn lives in my heart," which they use to laugh at themselves for loving quiet and solitude. Planning a trip to Finland in March? Remember: stand a little farther away in lines, and talk less. In Finland, quiet isn't coldness. It's comfort.

一階一階，爬上芝加哥的天空

來源：Shirley Ryan AbilityLab

11月8日星期天早上，芝加哥威利斯大樓要舉行第18屆 SkyRise 爬樓梯活動，這是全美國最高的室內爬樓梯比賽。參加的人要走2,149階、105段樓梯，一路爬到103樓的觀景台。

坐輪椅的人也能參加，用手搖車（用手轉動前進的車）完成一樣的高度。人在遠方的人，也可以線上參加。

報名費50美元，還要幫忙募款（為好事收集捐款）。大家平均要爬四十五分鐘，10歲以上就能報名。

On the morning of Sunday, November 8, the Willis Tower in Chicago will host the 18th annual SkyRise stair climb, the tallest indoor stair race in the United States. Participants will climb 2,149 steps across 105 flights of stairs, all the way up to the observation deck on the 103rd floor. Wheelchair users can take part too, covering the same height in hand-cycles (wheelchairs that are turned by hand). People who are far away can also join online. The entry fee is $50, and participants are also asked to help raise money for charity. On average, climbers take about forty-five minutes to finish, and anyone aged 10 or older can sign up.

一個人，收集全世界的徽章

來源：Heraldry of the World

1995年，一位荷蘭學生做了一個小網站，專門收集城市和國家的徽章（代表一個地方的圖案）。三十年後，這個網站已經有好幾萬個徽章，到現在還是由荷蘭人 Ralf Hartemink 一個人，利用空閒時間慢慢整理。

每個徽章都有自己的一頁，告訴你上面的獅子、高塔和波浪線是什麼意思。幾百年前，小鎮就是用這些圖案，向世界說出自己的驕傲。

In 1995, a Dutch student built a small website devoted to collecting the coats of arms (emblems that represent a place) of cities and countries. Thirty years later, the site holds tens of thousands of them, and it is still maintained by one person, the Dutchman Ralf Hartemink, who works on it slowly in his spare time. Each coat of arms has its own page that explains what the lions, towers, and wavy lines on it mean. Hundreds of years ago, small towns used these very designs to announce their pride to the world.
