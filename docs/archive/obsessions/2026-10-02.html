「就在今夜」：台灣搖滾開門的那一年

來源：Marie Claire 美麗佳人

1982年，五人樂團「丘丘合唱團」推出第一張專輯《就在今夜》。吉他手邱晨寫歌，主唱是藝名「娃娃」的金智娟。

在這之前，台灣流行的是「校園民歌」，女歌手的聲音大多又輕又甜。娃娃的嗓子（喉嚨發出的聲音）卻有點沙啞，很有力量，再加上更重的鼓聲和電吉他，讓人大吃一驚。

很多人說，這張唱片讓台灣音樂從民歌走進了真正的搖滾樂團時代。

In 1982, the five-member band Qiuqiu Chorus released their debut album "Right Here Tonight." Guitarist Qiu Chen wrote the songs, with lead vocals from Jin Zhijuan, performing under the stage name "Wa Wa." Before this, Taiwan's popular music featured "campus folk songs," and female singers typically had light, sweet voices. But Wa Wa's voice was somewhat hoarse yet powerfully strong. Combined with heavier drums and electric guitar, it shocked audiences. Many say this album brought Taiwan's music from the folk era into the true age of rock bands.

送不出去的山頂

來源：GeographyRealm

芬蘭最高的地方在北邊拉普蘭的哈爾蒂山（Halti），高1,324公尺。可是，這座山真正的山頂在國界另一邊，屬於挪威。

2017年芬蘭獨立滿一百年，挪威有人想把國界移一點，把山頂當作生日禮物送給芬蘭。很多人支持，但挪威政府說，依照憲法（國家最重要的法律），國土不能隨便送人。

所以到今天，芬蘭還是沒有自己最高山的山頂。三月正好可以滑雪，一站一站住山屋，慢慢走到那裡。

Finland's highest point is Mount Halti in Lapland in the north, standing at 1,324 meters. But here's the thing: the actual summit of the mountain lies just across the border in Norway. In 2017, when Finland celebrated 100 years of independence, some Norwegians thought it would be a nice gesture to shift the border slightly and give the peak to Finland as a birthday gift. Many people supported the idea, but the Norwegian government said no—the constitution (the nation's supreme law) doesn't allow territory to be given away so casually. So to this day, Finland still doesn't own the summit of its own highest mountain. March is a perfect time to ski up there, staying in mountain huts along the way, slowly making your way to the peak.

藍調之父的家，開門了

來源：WTTW News

10月17日和18日，芝加哥建築中心舉辦免費的「芝加哥開放日」，全市三十個社區、兩百多棟建築都會開門。

其中最特別的一棟在肯伍德區（4339 S. Lake Park Ave.）。「芝加哥藍調之父」穆迪‧華特斯從1954年到1973年住在這裡，常在地下室和樂手一起練歌。

現在，他的曾孫女（孫子的女兒）正把這棟老房子變成博物館。不用買票，也不用報名，直接去就好。

On October 17 and 18, the Chicago Architecture Center is hosting a free "Chicago Open House," with thirty neighborhoods and over two hundred buildings throughout the city opening their doors.

Among them, the most remarkable is located in the Kenwood neighborhood (4339 S. Lake Park Ave.). "The Father of Chicago Blues," Muddy Waters, lived here from 1954 to 1973, often practicing with musicians in the basement of the house. Now, his great-granddaughter—his grandson's daughter—is transforming the old home into a museum.

No ticket is required, and there's no need to register. Simply come by.

一座網路上的棒球墓園

來源：TheDeadBallEra.com

1999年，網頁設計師法蘭克‧魯索（Frank Russo）開了一個網站「死球時代」。他想找到每一位已經過世的大聯盟球員，親自去拍下他們的墓（人死後埋在土裡的地方）。

二十多年來，他記下了一千多個墓的位置，從大明星貝比‧魯斯，到早就被人忘記的板凳球員都有。

後來他把這些研究寫成一本書，但網站一直免費開放。每一頁，都是他一個墓、一個墓走出來的。

In 1999, web designer Frank Russo launched a website called "Dead-Ball Era." His mission was to find every deceased Major League Baseball player and personally photograph their graves (the places where people are buried underground). Over the past twenty-odd years, he has documented the locations of more than a thousand graves—from superstars like Babe Ruth to long-forgotten bench players. He later published his research as a book, but the website has remained free and open to everyone. Every page is the product of him walking from grave to grave.
