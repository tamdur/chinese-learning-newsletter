沒有吉他的搖滾樂：鐵之貝克的鋼琴革命

來源：Taipei Times

一九九九年，三個台北年輕人做了一個大膽的決定：組一個搖滾樂團，但不要吉他手。鐵之貝克（Tizzy Bac）用鋼琴當主角，再加上特別處理過的貝斯，創造出別人學不來的聲音。

二〇〇九年的第三張專輯「如果看見地獄，我就不怕魔鬼」更加大膽。專輯名字來自一個十歲小女孩的作文。同名歌曲裡，鋸琴（一種用鋸子拉出的樂器）的聲音像鬼一樣飄過，然後整首歌突然爆發，氣勢被比作皇后樂團（Queen）。

In 1999, three young people from Taipei made a bold decision: form a rock band without a guitarist. Tizzy Bac put the piano at center stage, paired it with specially processed bass, and created a sound no one else could replicate. Their third album in 2009, "If I See Hell, I Won't Fear the Devil," pushed even further. The album's title came from an essay written by a ten-year-old girl. In the title track, the sound of a musical saw—an instrument played by bowing a saw blade—drifts through like a ghost, then the entire song suddenly explodes, with critics comparing its force to Queen.

芬蘭的滑雪節：用甜麵包迎接春天

來源：thisisFINLAND

每年二月底，芬蘭人會慶祝「滑雪節」（Laskiainen）。二〇二七年是二月二十三日，剛好在三月蜜月之前。大家帶著雪橇（一種在雪上滑行的工具）到公園的山坡上滑下來，滑得越遠，今年的收成就越好。

滑完之後，大家吃豌豆湯暖身，再享用「滑雪麵包」（laskiaispulla）——一種有小豆蔻（一種香料）味道的甜麵包，裡面塞著杏仁醬和滿滿的鮮奶油。赫爾辛基的麵包店現在還推出開心果口味的豪華版本！

Every late February, Finns celebrate "Laskiainen," or Skiing Day. In 2027, it falls on February 23rd, just before the March honeymoon season. People bring sleds to the park's slopes and race downhill—the farther you slide, the better your harvest will be that year. After sledding, everyone warms up with pea soup, then indulges in "laskiaispulla," a sweet cardamom-flavored bread stuffed with almond paste and generous dollops of fresh whipped cream. Bakeries in Helsinki are now even offering a deluxe pistachio version!

白鯨與爵士樂：乘涼在乾德水族館

來源：Shedd Aquarium

今年夏天，芝加哥的乾德水族館（Shedd Aquarium）每週三晚上變成大人的音樂天堂。從六月三日到九月二日，下午五點到晚上十點，你可以一邊看白鯨游泳、摸魟（一種扁平的魚）魚，一邊聽現場爵士樂。

芝加哥居民門票只要二十美元，大部分展覽都會開放。在湖邊的露台上吹著風、聽著音樂，這是芝加哥最特別的夏夜體驗之一。

This summer, Chicago's Shedd Aquarium transforms into an adult music haven every Wednesday evening. From June 3rd through September 2nd, from 5 p.m. to 10 p.m., you can watch beluga whales swim and touch stingrays while listening to live jazz. Chicago residents get in for just twenty dollars, and most exhibits will be open. On the lakeside terrace with a breeze in your hair and music in your ears, it's one of Chicago's most distinctive summer night experiences.

一個人花八年，把全世界唯一的鐵路信號雜誌搬上網

來源：jonroma.net

從一九〇八年到一九四八年，世界上只有一本雜誌專門介紹鐵路信號系統——就是火車怎麼知道什麼時候該停、什麼時候該走的技術。這本雜誌叫做 Railway Signaling。

有一個人花了整整八年，把四十年份的每一篇文章都掃描（用機器拍下來）、打字、分類，放到網路上讓所有人免費閱讀。這就是網路最美的地方：一個人的熱情，可以保存住一整個時代的知識。

From 1908 to 1948, the world had only one magazine dedicated exclusively to railway signaling systems — the technology that tells trains when to stop and when to go. That magazine was called Railway Signaling. One person spent eight full years scanning every article from four decades of issues, transcribing them, categorizing them, and uploading them to the internet for anyone to read for free. This is the internet at its best: one person's passion preserving an entire era's worth of knowledge.
