山中的聲音：陳世興的四張環境音樂專輯

來源：FOND/SOUND

一九八八年，搖滾吉他手陳世興離開城市，搬到台灣的山裡。他在那裡遇到一位西藏僧人，也發現了Brian Eno的音樂。短短一年內，他就完成了四張專輯：《空》、《山》、《靈》、《雨》。

這些專輯由「乾乾唱片」發行，這家小廠牌（唱片公司）想創造一種「新中國音樂」。很多人把陳世興比作台灣的Kitaro，但他的風格更安靜、更簡單，像山裡的空氣一樣透明。

In 1988, rock guitarist Chen Shi-hsing left the city and moved into the mountains of Taiwan. There he met a Tibetan monk and discovered the music of Brian Eno. Within a single year, he completed four albums: Emptiness, Mountain, Spirit, and Rain. These albums were released by "Qianqian Records," a small label seeking to create a new form of "Chinese music." Many people compared Chen Shi-hsing to Taiwan's Kitaro, but his style is quieter and simpler—transparent as the air in the mountains.

坦佩雷的黑香腸：芬蘭最驕傲的街頭小吃

來源：Visit Tampere

芬蘭第二大城坦佩雷（Tampere）有一種出名的食物：黑香腸（mustamakkara）。它用豬肉、豬血和黑麥做成，顏色很深，味道很濃。

在市場廣場的小攤子上，當地人站在冷風中，吃著熱騰騰的黑香腸，配上越橘（一種北歐小紅莓）果醬和一杯冰牛奶。據說名廚Anthony Bourdain把牛奶換成伏特加，讓當地人大為搖頭。

Finland's second-largest city, Tampere, is famous for one particular food: black sausage, or mustamakkara. Made from pork, pork blood, and rye, it has a deep color and intense flavor. At small stalls in the market square, locals stand in the cold wind eating piping-hot black sausage topped with lingonberry jam and washed down with a cold glass of milk. Legend has it that celebrity chef Anthony Bourdain once swapped the milk for vodka—a move that left locals shaking their heads in disapproval.

奇珍異物博覽會：在芝加哥學做標本

來源：TimeOut Chicago

六月六日到七日，芝加哥的McCormick Place將舉辦全世界最大的「奇珍異物博覽會」。現場有各種稀奇的東西：動物標本、頭骨收藏，還有老式醫療器具。

最特別的是，你可以報名動物標本製作課（把動物做成永久保存的樣子），親手做一隻雙頭小鴨標本。不需要經驗，費用兩百三十五美元。

On June 6 and 7, Chicago's McCormick Place will host the world's largest "Odditorium" — a showcase of curiosities and rarities. The event features an eclectic collection: taxidermied animals, skull collections, and antique medical instruments. The standout attraction is a hands-on taxidermy workshop where you can learn to preserve animals and create your own two-headed duckling specimen. No experience necessary. The course costs $235.

XWord Info：一個人建的填字遊戲資料庫

來源：XWord Info

二〇〇七年，工程師Jim Horne開始好奇：《紐約時報》的填字遊戲裡藏著什麼規律？於是他建了XWord Info這個網站。

如今，網站收錄了從一九四二年到今天的近兩萬八千份填字遊戲。一位叫David Steinberg的少年帶領六十多位志工，花了四年把早期的紙本資料全部打成電子檔。這就是一個人的熱情能做到的事。

In 2007, engineer Jim Horne became curious: what patterns lay hidden in the New York Times crossword puzzles? So he built XWord Info, a website that now archives nearly 28,000 crosswords from 1942 to today. A teenager named David Steinberg led over 60 volunteers who spent four years digitizing all the early paper records. This is what one person's passion can accomplish.
