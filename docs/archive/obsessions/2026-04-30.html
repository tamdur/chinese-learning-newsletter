古箏與薩克斯風的自由對話

來源：Ka Dao Yin Bandcamp

「慢」是台北樂團「卡到音」在 2013 年發行的專輯，曾獲得金曲獎。這個四人組合用鋼琴、薩克斯風、古箏和笙（一種用嘴吹的中國樂器）來即興演奏。

專輯名字來自台灣馬路上的「慢」字標誌。他們的音樂就像那個字一樣，不急不忙，讓古老的民謠在自由即興中慢慢變成新的聲音。

"Slow" is an album released in 2013 by the Taipei ensemble "Stuck Tone," which won a Golden Melody Award. This four-member group improvises using piano, saxophone, guzheng, and sheng (a Chinese wind instrument played with the mouth). The album's name comes from the "slow" road signs found on Taiwanese streets. Their music, like that character, is unhurried and patient—allowing ancient folk melodies to gradually transform into new sounds through free improvisation.

芬蘭織毯：從維京船上到婚禮地毯

來源：Kotona Living

芬蘭有一種叫 ryijy 的傳統織毯，已經有一千多年的歷史。它最早是維京人在船上用的毛毯，後來到了十七世紀，變成了婚禮上最重要的東西。新人會站在織毯上結婚，上面的圖案有愛心、皇冠和生命之樹。

在赫爾辛基附近的 EMMA 美術館，搭地鐵只要二十分鐘，就能看到從古老到現代的 ryijy 收藏。

Finland has a traditional woven rug called ryijy that dates back more than a thousand years. It originally started as a blanket used by Vikings on their ships, but by the seventeenth century it had become the most important item at weddings. Newlyweds would stand on the woven rug to be married, with patterns featuring hearts, crowns, and the tree of life. At the EMMA art museum near Helsinki, just a twenty-minute metro ride away, you can see a collection of ryijy rugs spanning from ancient to modern times.

海德公園的河狸慶典

來源：Illinois Beaver Alliance

五月二日星期六，芝加哥海德公園的 Nichols Park 將舉辦免費的「河狸慶典」，時間是下午一點到四點。

河狸會築壩（用樹枝蓋水壩），創造出濕地，讓水變乾淨，也能減少水災。近年來河狸悄悄回到伊利諾州的河邊，這是當地生態最令人開心的好消息之一。現場有兒童活動和環保團體展覽。

On Saturday, May 2nd, Nichols Park in Chicago's Hyde Park will host a free "Beaver Festival" from 1 to 4 p.m. Beavers build dams using branches to create wetlands, clean water, and reduce flooding. In recent years, beavers have quietly returned to the riverbanks of Illinois—one of the most heartening pieces of good news for the region's ecosystem. The event will feature children's activities and exhibits from environmental organizations.

一萬場演唱會的秘密錄音

來源：Internet Archive — Live Music Archive

芝加哥樂迷 Aadam Jacobs 從 1984 年開始，偷偷帶著錄音機去看演唱會。四十多年來，他錄了超過一萬場，包括 1989 年 Nirvana 第一次來芝加哥表演的珍貴（非常難得的）錄音。

他在 2024 年底把全部錄音帶捐給了 Internet Archive，志工們正在把這些錄音整理上傳，到現在已經有兩千多筆，任何人都可以免費聽。

Chicago music fan Aadam Jacobs has been secretly bringing a recorder to concerts since 1984. Over more than four decades, he recorded over 10,000 shows, including a rare and precious recording of Nirvana's first performance in Chicago in 1989. At the end of 2024, he donated all his recordings to the Internet Archive, where volunteers are organizing and uploading them. More than 2,000 recordings are now available for anyone to listen to for free.
