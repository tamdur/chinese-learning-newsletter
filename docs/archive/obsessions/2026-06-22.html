濁水溪公社：台灣最生氣的一張專輯

來源：Taiwan Panorama

一九九九年，「濁水溪公社」這個樂團推出了《台客的復仇》。當別的歌手都在唱漂亮的流行歌，他們卻全部用台語唱。

這張專輯把吵鬧的電吉他、辦桌那卡西、布袋戲的聲音全部混在一起。他們把「台客」這個原本罵人的詞，變成一種驕傲。

In 1999, the band Turbid Water Commune (LTK Commune) released Revenge of the Taike. While other singers were performing polished pop songs, they sang entirely in Taiwanese (Hokkien). The album mixed raucous distorted electric guitars, banquet-hall nakasi, and puppet-theater sound effects all together. They transformed "Taike" — originally a slur — into a badge of pride.

努塔耶爾維：森林裡的玻璃村

來源：Nuutajärvi Glass

一七九三年，有人在芬蘭的深山森林裡蓋了一座玻璃工廠。那裡沒有路、沒有火車，只有很多樹，可以燒火做玻璃。

這座工廠做了兩百多年，到二○一四年才停下來。現在藝術家搬了進來，遊客可以看他們吹玻璃，也可以在小旅館住一晚。

In 1793, someone built a glassworks in the depths of Finland's forest. There were no roads, no trains — only trees, which could be burned to fuel the glassmaking. The factory operated for over two hundred years until it closed in 2014. Today, artists have moved in, and visitors can watch them blow glass and stay overnight at a small inn.

在芝加哥學做奶油手機殼

來源：Eventbrite

六月二十七日星期六晚上六點到九點，芝加哥的手作家會在一家叫enid的店教大家做「奶油裝飾」。

這是一種日本手藝：用像奶油的材料，加上可愛的小飾品，把東西變漂亮。你可以帶自己的手機殼來，一邊做一邊吃糖果。十六歲以上都能參加。

On Saturday, June 27th, from 6 to 9 p.m., a local maker in Chicago will teach a class at a shop called enid on how to make "decoden" (cream decorations). This is a Japanese craft: using a material that looks like whipped cream, along with cute little charms, to make everyday objects beautiful. You can bring your own phone case and snack on candy while you work. Open to anyone 16 and older.

一個人寫了十五年的食物百科

來源：Food Timeline

林恩．奧爾弗（Lynne Olver）是美國的一位圖書館員。她花了十五年，一個人做了一個免費的網站「食物時間線」，記錄從古到今的食物歷史。

光是講冰淇淋的那一篇，就有三萬多字。她還免費回答了兩萬五千個問題。她在二○一五年過世後，一所大學接手，讓網站繼續開著。

Lynne Olver was an American librarian who spent fifteen years single-handedly building a free website called the Food Timeline, documenting the history of food from ancient times to the present day. Her entry on ice cream alone contains more than thirty thousand words. She also answered twenty-five thousand questions for free. After her death in 2015, a university took over the project and kept the site running.
