後山的聲音：陳世興為東海岸寫下的音樂

來源：FOND/SOUND

陳世興是在台北出生的吉他手。一九八零年代中期，他聽到英國音樂家布萊恩·伊諾那種很安靜的音樂，就想用聲音畫出心裡的風景。

一九九四年，他為紀錄片《發現後山的藝術》寫了配樂。「後山」說的是台灣東邊的花蓮和台東，離台北很遠。

他用電子合成的音色和慢慢流動的旋律，不是畫面，而是花蓮寬闊的陽光，和在島嶼邊緣安靜創作的藝術家。

Chen Shi-hsing is a guitarist born in Taipei. In the mid-1980s, he heard the quiet music of British musician Brian Eno and wanted to paint the landscapes in his heart with sound.

In 1994, he composed the score for a documentary called "Discovering the Arts Behind the Mountains." The "backcountry" (literally "behind the mountains") refers to Hualien and Taitung on Taiwan's east coast, far from Taipei.

Using electronic synthesized tones and slowly flowing melodies, he captured not the images themselves, but the broad sunlight of Hualien and the quiet creative work of artists on the island's edge.

最後一次：開車越過結冰的大海

來源：Europa.Tips

在芬蘭北部的奧盧外海，有一座小島叫海盧奧托，島上住不到一千人。到了冬天，海水結冰，人們就開車走在七公里長的「冰路」上過海。

這條冰路是政府維護的，車速不能超過五十公里，車和車之間要隔五十公尺。二零二五到二零二六這個冬天，是它最後一次開放。

因為一座新的長堤（連接島和陸地的路堤）會在二零二六年底完工。二零二七年三月來的人，會看到島上紅色的木屋和一八七二年的老燈塔，不用再過冰面了。

Off the coast of Oulu in northern Finland sits a small island called Hailuoto, home to fewer than a thousand people. When winter arrives and the sea freezes over, residents drive across a seven-kilometer "ice road" to reach the mainland.

The ice road is maintained by the government. The speed limit is fifty kilometers per hour, and vehicles must keep fifty meters apart. This winter, from 2025 to 2026, will be its final season of operation.

A new causeway connecting the island to the mainland will be completed by the end of 2026. Visitors arriving in March 2027 will see the island's red wooden houses and its old lighthouse from 1872 — without ever needing to drive across the frozen sea again.

龍舟劃過芝加哥河：中國城的夏日盛會

來源：Chicago Chinatown Chamber of Commerce

六月二十日，芝加哥中國城的坪湯紀念公園會舉辦龍舟比賽。三十三支隊伍會划著畫得很漂亮的船，在芝加哥河上比速度。

比賽開始前，會先有傳統的舞獅表演。這個活動從二零零零年開始辦，為的是幫中國城的識字教育（教人讀書寫字）籌錢。

從早上八點到下午五點，公園裡還有很多表演和攤位。這是免費的，誰都可以來看。

On June 20th, Ping Tom Memorial Park in Chicago's Chinatown will host a dragon boat race. Thirty-three teams will paddle beautifully decorated boats and race for speed on the Chicago River.

Before the competition begins, there will be a traditional lion dance performance. This event has been held since 2000 to raise money for literacy education programs in Chinatown.

From 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., the park will feature many performances and vendor booths. Admission is free and open to everyone.

腳踏車為什麼不會倒？一個人親手寫的網站

來源：ciechanow.ski

波蘭工程師巴托什做了一個很特別的網站。他用會動的立體圖，把很難的物理講得清清楚楚。圖上的每一行程式，都是他自己一個字一個字寫出來的。

在講腳踏車的那一篇裡，他告訴我們：腳踏車不倒，其實不是因為輪子在轉。真正的原因，是前輪的設計，還有騎車的人一直在輕輕調整方向。你可以自己拉圖上的滑桿來試試看。

他一年只寫幾篇，每一篇都要花好幾個月。最新的一篇是講月亮，二零二四年十二月才完成。

A Polish engineer named Bartosz created a remarkable website. Using animated 3D diagrams, he explains difficult physics with exceptional clarity. Every single line of code on the page was written by him by hand, character by character.

In his article about bicycles, he reveals that a bike stays upright not because its wheels are spinning. The real reason lies in the design of the front wheel and the rider's constant, subtle adjustments to direction. You can drag the sliders in the diagram yourself to experiment.

He writes only a few essays per year, and each one takes several months to complete. His most recent piece, about the moon, was finished in December 2024.
