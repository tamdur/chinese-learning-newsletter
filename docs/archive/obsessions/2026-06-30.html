楊乃文的《Silence》：用沙啞嗓音唱出黑夜

來源：Music and Literature is Life / Discogs / Wikipedia

一九九九年九月，台灣歌手楊乃文推出了專輯《Silence》。她在澳洲的小酒館唱歌長大，嗓音低沉又沙啞，在當時的台北很特別。

製作人林暐哲把搖滾、吉他和電子音樂混在一起。同名歌曲裡，男聲像影子一樣飄進來，十分陰暗迷人。

這張專輯在二〇〇〇年為她贏得金曲獎最佳女歌手，直到今天仍被選進台灣百大專輯。

In September 1999, Taiwanese singer Faith Yang (Yang Naiwen) released the album "Silence." She grew up singing in small bars in Australia, her voice low and husky — something distinctly unusual in Taipei at the time. Producer Lin Wei-zhe wove together rock, guitar, and electronic music. In the title track, a male voice drifts in like a shadow, darkly enchanting. The album won her the Golden Melody Award for Best Female Singer in 2000, and it remains a fixture on Taiwan's list of greatest albums to this day.

在芬蘭小島上，親手打造一把傳統小刀

來源：Visit Finland / Hanhiniitty Smithy

在芬蘭最大的塞馬湖區，有一座小島，島上的鐵匠（打鐵的師傅）馬丁從一九八八年就開始打刀了。

他開了一門四天的課，每次只收六個人。兩個人可以一起把鐵燒紅、敲打成刀，再用白樺木或鹿角做刀柄，最後親手縫一個皮套。

要坐免費的渡船才能到這座小島。這門課全年都能約，很適合三月的蜜月旅行。

On an island in Finland's largest lake region, Saimaa, there's a blacksmith named Martti who has been forging traditional knives since 1988. He offers a four-day workshop that takes only six students at a time. Two people work together to heat the iron until it glows red, hammer it into shape, fashion a handle from birch wood or reindeer antler, and finally stitch a leather sheath by hand. You reach the island by taking a free ferry. The course is available year-round and makes for a memorable honeymoon activity in March.

花朵走上街頭：芝加哥的日式插花散步展

來源：Japanese Culture Center Chicago

七月十二、十三日，芝加哥南港大道會變成一座露天花藝館。兩個日本插花流派的老師和學生，會把作品擺進店面和餐廳的櫥窗裡。

這是第五年舉辦，免費參觀，可以自己慢慢逛。每天中午十二點半還有人帶著大家導覽。

有些店家還會擺出日本書法，一家酒舖也會請大家免費品嚐清酒。

On July 12th and 13th, Chicago's Southport Corridor will transform into an open-air flower art gallery. Teachers and students from two Japanese ikebana schools will display their arrangements in shop windows and restaurant storefronts. This is the fifth year the event is being held, and admission is free — you can stroll through at your own pace. Each day at 12:30 p.m., guided tours will be available. Some shops will also feature Japanese calligraphy, and a liquor shop will offer free sake tastings.

一個人花了二十五年，記下每一個老鬧鐘

來源：ClockHistory.com

十四歲那年，比爾的爺爺教他怎麼上發條、調整鬧鐘。從此他就愛上了收集美國老牌子Westclox的床頭鬧鐘。

二〇〇〇年，他建了一個網站叫ClockHistory，把每一款鬧鐘的樣子、價錢和出廠年份都記下來。

二十五年過去，這個網站收了兩百多件專利和老廣告，成了全世界收藏家最信賴的資料庫。

At fourteen, Bill's grandfather taught him how to wind and adjust alarm clocks. From that moment, he fell in love with collecting vintage bedside alarms from the American brand Westclox. In 2000, he built a website called ClockHistory, documenting the appearance, price, and manufacturing year of every model. Twenty-five years later, the site has amassed over two hundred patents and vintage advertisements, becoming the world's most trusted database for collectors.
