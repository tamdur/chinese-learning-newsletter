一張專輯改變了台語流行音樂

來源：Wikipedia / Marching Forward

一九九〇年十二月，林強發行了他的第一張專輯《向前走》。當時，台語歌大多是慢節奏的悲傷情歌。林強把搖滾吉他和嘻哈的說唱節奏放進台語歌裡，用街頭的語言唱出年輕人的心聲。

這張專輯賣了超過四十萬張，還在第三屆金曲獎拿到年度最佳歌曲。它開啟了「新台語歌運動」，讓大家知道台語也能唱出最新、最潮的聲音。

In December 1990, Lin Qiang released his debut album "Moving Forward." At the time, most Taiwanese-language songs were slow-paced sad love ballads. Lin Qiang introduced rock guitars and hip-hop rap rhythms into Taiwanese-language music, singing the voices of young people in street vernacular. The album sold over 400,000 copies and won Song of the Year at the third Golden Melody Awards. It launched the "New Taiwanese Language Music Movement," showing everyone that Taiwanese could express the newest and trendiest sounds.

冰湖上的七百年漁網

來源：Finnish Heritage Agency

在芬蘭東部，有一座叫做普魯維西湖的清澈大湖。每年冬天，大約三十個漁村的人會在冰面上鑽（打洞）好幾個洞，把長長的網放進水裡捕魚。這個方法從一三〇〇年就有記錄，已經超過七百年了。

他們捕的是一種小魚，芬蘭語叫 muikku，已獲得歐盟的地理標誌（產地保護認證）。三月是冰融化前最後的捕魚季，也是收穫最豐富的時候。

In eastern Finland, there is a clear large lake called Lake Puruvesi. Every winter, people from about thirty fishing villages drill holes through the ice and lower long nets into the water to catch fish. This method has been documented since 1300, making it over seven hundred years old. The fish they catch is a small species called muikku in Finnish, which has received geographical indication protection from the European Union. March is the final fishing season before the ice melts, and also the most abundant harvest time.

版畫與手作書的一日市集

來源：Chicago Printers Guild

四月十九日星期六，芝加哥藝術學院（SAIC）的版畫系會舉辦一年一度的藝術書與版畫市集。在這裡，學生和老師直接賣自己的作品。

你會找到各種好玩的東西：手工裝訂的小書、實驗風格的獨立小誌（zine），還有用活字印刷做的精美版畫。芝加哥印刷工會也會到場。

On Saturday, April 19th, the School of the Art Institute of Chicago (SAIC) will host its annual art book and printmaking market. Here, students and faculty sell their work directly to visitors. You'll find all sorts of interesting things: hand-bound chapbooks, experimental independent zines, and beautiful letterpress prints. The Chicago Printing Guild will also be on hand.

用考古的方式拆解老遊戲

來源：Computer Archeology (computerarcheology.com)

一位叫 Topher Cantrell 的工程師花了好多年，一個人建了一個網站叫 Computer Archeology。他把經典電子遊戲的程式碼打開來，像考古學家挖遺跡（古老的地方）一樣，一行一行寫下說明。

他研究了《太空侵略者》、《大蜜蜂》等遊戲，發現了藏在程式裡的秘密訊息和小錯誤。整個網站只用最簡單的 HTML 做成，充滿了對老遊戲的熱愛。

An engineer named Topher Cantrell spent years building a website called Computer Archeology, working largely by himself. He opens up the source code of classic video games and documents them line by line, the way an archaeologist excavates an ancient site. He has studied games like Space Invaders and Galaga, uncovering hidden messages and small bugs embedded in the code. The entire website is built with the simplest HTML, brimming with love for vintage games.
