夢裡的大象：高雄樂團遇見客家歌聲

來源：NME

大象體操是2012年在高雄成立的三人樂團。他們玩的是「數學搖滾」，節奏常常忽快忽慢，很不規則。團名裡的「大象」，指的是貝斯手KT彈出像唱歌一樣的旋律。

2022年的第三張專輯《夢境》，他們請來兩位特別的客人：爵士歌手9m88，還有用客家話唱農村生活的林生祥。

在最後一首歌裡，林生祥的歌聲和彎彎曲曲的貝斯線合在一起，讓冷冷的數學搖滾，變得像兩代人溫暖的對唱。

Elephant Gymnastics is a three-piece band formed in Kaohsiung in 2012. They play "math rock," with tempos that often speed up and slow down without warning, making their music very irregular. The "elephant" in the band's name refers to bassist KT, whose playing produces melodies that sing like a voice.

On their third album, Dreamscape (夢境), released in 2022, they invited two special guests: jazz singer 9m88, and Lin Sheng-xiang, who sings about rural life in Hakka.

In the final song, Lin Sheng-xiang's voice blends with the winding bass line, turning cold math rock into something like a warm duet between two generations.

唱哭歌的農婦：赫爾辛基第一座女性銅像

來源：Helsinki Art Museum (HAM)

帕拉斯克（Larin Paraske）生於1833年，是卡累利阿（芬蘭和俄羅斯交界的地方）的農村婦女，也是民歌手。她能背出三萬兩千多行古老詩歌，還會唱只由女人在婚禮和喪禮（為過世的人辦的儀式）上唱的哭歌。

1887年，學者發現了她。後來她到赫爾辛基表演，連作曲家西貝流士都來聽。

1949年，她的銅像立在市中心大街旁的公園裡，成為赫爾辛基第一座為女性立的公共雕像，從芬蘭廳走過去只要幾分鐘。

Larin Paraske was born in 1833. She was a rural woman from Karelia, the region straddling the border between Finland and Russia, and a folk singer. She could recite more than 32,000 lines of ancient poetry, and she also knew the laments, the mourning songs that only women sang at weddings and funerals (rituals held to honor the dead).

In 1887, scholars discovered her. She later performed in Helsinki, where even the composer Jean Sibelius came to listen.

In 1949, a bronze statue of her was placed in a park beside a downtown street. It was the first public statue in Helsinki to honor a woman, and it is only a few minutes' walk from Finlandia Hall.

蒂芬尼圓頂下的巴洛克回聲

來源：Apollo's Fire / Early Music America

11月8日星期天下午五點，得過葛萊美獎的克里夫蘭古樂團「阿波羅之火」（Apollo's Fire），會在芝加哥文化中心免費演出「跨越大西洋的回聲」。

他們用巴洛克（三百多年前的歐洲音樂時代）的老樂器，像大鍵琴、古提琴和長頸的魯特琴，再加上來自千里達的女高音德比克，帶大家聽這種音樂怎麼從歐洲傳到美洲。

演出地點在東華盛頓街78號的文化中心，大廳上方有美麗的蒂芬尼玻璃圓頂。不用買票，記得早點到。

On Sunday, November 8, at 5:00 p.m., Apollo's Fire, the Cleveland-based baroque orchestra that has won a Grammy, will give a free concert called "Echoes Across the Atlantic" at the Chicago Cultural Center.

They will play baroque instruments (from the European musical era more than three hundred years ago), such as the harpsichord, the viola da gamba, and the long-necked lute. They will be joined by Trinidadian soprano Debique, and the program traces how this music traveled from Europe to the Americas.

The concert takes place at the Cultural Center at 78 East Washington Street, under the stunning Tiffany glass dome above the main hall. Admission is free, but arrive early.

每一滴雨的回家路

來源：mghydro.com

在這個免費網站的世界地圖上點一下，它馬上就會畫出一塊「集水區」（下雨後，水都會流到同一個地方的那片土地）。它用全世界的地形高低資料，找出眼睛看不見的山脊線（山頂連成的線），算出每一滴雨會往哪裡流。

做這個工具的人只有一位：水文學家（研究水怎麼流動的專家）馬修．赫伯格。他在2022年10月推出這個網站，後來在法國索邦大學拿到博士。他說，這是他最驕傲的作品。

Click anywhere on the world map on this free website, and it immediately draws a "catchment basin" (the stretch of land where, after rain, all the water flows to the same place). Using worldwide elevation data, it finds the invisible ridgelines (the lines connecting mountain peaks) and works out where every raindrop will flow.

The site was built by one person: hydrologist (a scientist who studies how water moves) Matthew Heberger. He launched the website in October 2022, and later earned his PhD at the Sorbonne University in France. He says it is his proudest creation.
