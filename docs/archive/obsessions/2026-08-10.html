撿來的台北，唱成台語搖滾

來源：The News Lens 關鍵評論網

二〇二四年，台灣的百合花樂團推出第三張專輯《萬事美妙》，全部用台語演唱。北管、南管這些傳統戲曲音樂，被他們放進搖滾、藍調和放克裡。

主唱林奕碩說，他寫歌像在撿別人不要的東西：蟾蜍山（台北的一座小山）葉媽媽的故事、寶藏巖（山邊的老社區）住戶的閒聊、南門市場舊書店的老書。這些快要消失的台北碎片，都被他拼進歌裡。

前一張專輯《不是路》，拿過第三十三屆金曲獎最佳台語專輯。

In 2024 the Taiwanese band Lilium released their third album, Wonderful Everything, sung entirely in Taiwanese. They wove Beiguan and Nanguan — traditional opera musics — into rock, blues and funk.

Frontman Lim E-siok describes his songwriting as picking up things other people have thrown away: the story of Mama Ye from Toad Mountain, a small hill in Taipei; overheard talk among residents of Treasure Hill, the old community on its slope; old books from a secondhand bookshop at Nanmen Market. These fragments of a disappearing Taipei all end up stitched into his songs.

The band's previous album, Not the Way, won Best Taiwanese Album at the 33rd Golden Melody Awards.

拉普蘭的紫水晶山

來源：Amethystmine.fi

芬蘭北部的皮哈羅斯托國家公園裡，有一座叫蘭皮瓦拉的山丘。這裡是全歐洲唯一開放給一般人參觀的紫水晶礦場（挖寶石的地方）。

冬天要坐一種有暖氣的車廂上山，由雪車在前面拉著走，大約一個半小時才會到。導遊先講這片紫色石頭二十億年前怎麼形成，再發給每個人一把小鏟子。

自己挖出來的石頭，只要放得進手掌，就能免費帶回家，一人一顆。三月也開放。

In Pyha-Luosto National Park in northern Finland there is a fell called Lampivaara. It holds the only amethyst mine in all of Europe that is open to the general public.

In winter you ride up in a heated carriage towed by a snow machine, a journey of about an hour and a half. The guide first explains how this purple stone formed two billion years ago, then hands each visitor a small trowel.

Whatever you dig out is yours to take home free, so long as it fits in your palm — one stone per person. The mine is open in March too.

公園裡的和平劇場

來源：Time Out Chicago

八月十九日晚上，芝加哥西邊奧斯汀區的拉福雷特公園，會變成一座露天劇場。這是「和平之書」藝術節，有短劇、舞蹈和朗誦，全部免費。

所有作品都是芝加哥的藝術家和青少年為這個活動新寫的，主題只有一個：和平。不少年輕人寫的，就是自己社區裡的槍枝暴力。

主辦的劇團今年成立滿三十年。他們每年把演出帶到不同的公園，而不是留在劇院裡。

On the evening of August 19th, La Follette Park in Chicago's Austin neighbourhood becomes an open-air theatre. This is the Peacebook festival: short plays, dance and spoken word, all of it free.

Every piece is newly written for the event by Chicago artists and teenagers, on a single theme — peace. A good deal of what the young writers turn in is about gun violence in their own neighbourhoods.

The theatre company behind it turns thirty this year. Each year it takes the performances out to different parks rather than keeping them in a theatre.

有人替倫敦的鴿子畫了一張地鐵圖

來源：London Pigeons

十幾年來，一位倫敦人自己經營一個網站，只寫一件事：鴿子。別人覺得鴿子髒，他卻認真研究牠們的生活。

他最有名的作品叫「鳥糞線」。他花了整整一個月，跑到五座鐵路和公路橋下，收集並秤重鴿子的糞便（大便），再推算出全倫敦的橋每個月大約累積兩公噸。

最後他把結果畫成一張假的地鐵路線圖，樣子和倫敦地鐵的經典路線圖一模一樣。

For more than a decade a Londoner has run a website of his own about exactly one thing: pigeons. Where other people see vermin, he has taken their lives seriously enough to study them properly.

His best-known piece of work is called the Guano Line. He spent a full month under five railway and road bridges, collecting and weighing pigeon droppings, and from that worked out that London's bridges accumulate roughly two tonnes of the stuff every month.

He then drew the results as a spoof Underground map, made to look exactly like the classic London Tube diagram.
