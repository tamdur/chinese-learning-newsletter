三天三夜，寫出台灣第一座國際配樂獎

來源：Wikipedia / cmcmusic.com.tw

一九八七年，導演侯孝賢拍電影《戀戀風塵》，找上從來沒寫過配樂（電影裡的音樂）的陳明章。陳明章騎著父親的偉士牌機車，載著歌手許景淳沿著海邊公路來到九份，仔細看過電影裡的山城。

回去以後，他三天三夜沒睡，把整部配樂寫完也錄好。他不照西方流行歌的做法，而是用歌仔戲（台灣的傳統戲曲）那種自由、不數拍子的唱法來寫。

這部音樂後來在法國南特三大洲影展得到最佳配樂獎，是台灣電影音樂第一次拿到國際大獎。

In 1987, director Hou Hsiao-hsien was filming "Dust in the Wind" and approached Chen Ming-zhang, who had never written a film score before. Chen Ming-zhang rode his father's Vespa scooter with singer Xu Jing-qing along the coastal highway to Jiufen, where he carefully studied the mountain town featured in the film. Back home, he worked without sleep for three days and three nights, composing and recording the entire score. Rather than following Western pop-music conventions, he wrote using the free, unmeasured vocal style of Taiwanese opera. The score later won the Best Score award at the Nantes Three Continents Film Festival in France, marking the first time Taiwanese film music had received an international major award.

「請安靜」：芬蘭把寂靜當成高級品

來源：Helsinki Times

芬蘭國家旅遊局把整個國家的招牌分成三塊：「野性自由」、「文化節拍」，還有一塊只有三個字：「請安靜。」

芬蘭每平方公里只住大約十六個人，人少到可以把真正的安靜當成商品來賣：風聲、被雪蓋住的松林、結冰的河。

所以三月去芬蘭度蜜月，不妨把安靜也排進行程：找一間荒野小屋住下，什麼活動都不安排。

Finland's National Tourism Board has divided the country's brand into three pillars: "Wild Freedom," "Cultural Rhythm," and one with just three words: "Please Be Quiet." With only about sixteen people per square kilometer, Finland has so few residents that it can actually market genuine silence as a premium commodity—the sound of wind, snow-covered pine forests, frozen rivers. So if you're planning a honeymoon to Finland in March, consider adding silence to your itinerary: find a wilderness cabin, check in, and schedule absolutely nothing.

一日限定：森林裡的五十種野菇

來源：Chicago Botanic Garden / Illinois Mycological Association

二〇二六年九月六日星期日，早上十點到下午四點，伊利諾真菌學會（研究菇類的社團）在芝加哥植物園辦一年一次的蘑菇展。

展出的五十到一百種野菇，都是會員那一週在附近森林裡採來的。展期固定在勞動節連假，剛好碰上秋菇最多的時候。

現場有會員教大家認菇，分出哪些能吃、哪些有毒。看展免費，但植物園的停車費照算。

On Sunday, September 6, 2026, from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m., the Illinois Mycological Society—a club dedicated to mushroom research—will host its annual mushroom exhibition at the Chicago Botanic Garden. The display will feature fifty to one hundred wild mushroom species, all collected by members from nearby forests during the preceding week. The event is held every year during the Labor Day weekend, timing that coincides perfectly with peak autumn mushroom season. Members will be on hand to teach visitors how to identify different mushrooms and distinguish between edible and poisonous varieties. Admission is free, though parking fees at the botanic garden apply.

兩個人守了二十五年的夢境圖書館

來源：DreamBank.net

一九九九年，柏克萊的研究生施奈德向指導老師、睡眠學者杜姆霍夫提議：把幾十年來收集的夢境筆記全部放上網。

這就是 DreamBank。裡面有超過兩萬篇夢的紀錄，做夢的人從七歲到七十四歲；有人連續五十年每晚寫下自己的夢，也有盲人的夢，還有二戰老兵一再夢見的惡夢。

網站附了搜尋工具，任何人都能查「掉下去」或「牙齒」在所有夢裡出現的樣子。二十五年來，它一直只靠這兩個人維持。

In 1999, UC Berkeley graduate student Schneider proposed to his advisor, sleep researcher Domhoff, that they put all the dream notes they'd collected over decades online. This became DreamBank. It contains over 20,000 dream records from people aged 7 to 74—some recorded their own dreams every night for fifty years straight. The collection includes dreams from blind people and recurring nightmares from World War II veterans. The site has search tools that let anyone see how words like "falling" or "teeth" appear across all the dreams. For twenty-five years, just these two people have kept it running.
