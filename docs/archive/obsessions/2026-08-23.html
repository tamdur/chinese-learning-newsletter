六百元的吉他，彈出台灣的聲音

來源：陳明章音樂 cmcmusic

一九八六年，陳明章用一把二手吉他，為侯孝賢的電影《戀戀風塵》配樂。那把琴只花了六百元台幣。他和歌手許景淳，三天三夜就錄完整張配樂。

他年輕時跟恆春老歌手陳達學月琴（一種傳統兩弦樂器），也學歌仔戲。他把這些聲音搬到吉他上，自己發明「月琴調」和「海洋調」，讓西方樂器唱出台灣的味道。

一九八七年，這份配樂在法國南特影展得到最佳電影音樂獎，是台灣電影音樂第一次得到國際大獎。

In 1986, Chen Ming-chang used a secondhand guitar—which cost only 600 New Taiwan dollars—to compose the soundtrack for Hou Hsiao-hsien's film "Dust in the Wind." He and singer Hsu Ching-chun recorded the entire score in just three days and three nights. As a young man, Chen had studied the moon lute, a traditional two-stringed instrument, with veteran Hengchun folk singer Chen Da, and had also learned Taiwanese opera. He transposed those sounds onto the guitar, inventing his own "moon lute tuning" and "ocean tuning"—tunings that let a Western instrument sing with an unmistakably Taiwanese flavour. In 1987 the soundtrack won Best Film Music at the Nantes Three Continents Festival in France, the first international prize ever won by Taiwanese film music.

逃離戰火的修道院，如今在湖邊做酒

來源：Valamo Monastery

一九四零年二月，冬季戰爭（蘇聯攻打芬蘭）打到眼前，一群東正教修士抱著聖像逃出拉多加湖上的老修道院。他們在芬蘭東部湖區的海納韋西落腳，蓋起新瓦拉莫修道院。

這是芬蘭唯一的男子東正教修道院。今天，修士們經營全國最大的修道院酒廠，每年用莓果做出大約十二萬公升的甜酒，還有果醬廚房和餐廳。

旅客可以在這裡住一晚，沿著湖邊樹林散步。在多數人信路德教派的芬蘭，這裡是安靜又特別的一角。

In February 1940, as the Winter War—the Soviet invasion of Finland—closed in, a group of Orthodox monks fled the old monastery on Lake Ladoga, clutching their icons. They resettled in Heinävesi, in the lake district of eastern Finland, and built the New Valamo Monastery, Finland's only Orthodox monastery for men. Today the monks run the country's largest monastic distillery, producing roughly 120,000 litres of berry liqueur each year, alongside a jam kitchen and a restaurant. Visitors can stay overnight and walk the lakeside woods. In overwhelmingly Lutheran Finland, this is a quiet and singular corner.

洗衣店後面的最後一場魔術

來源：Chicago Magic Lounge

八月二十六日星期三晚上七點，捷克魔術師普謝尼奇卡在芝加哥魔術廳表演《52 Lovers》。這是這一季的最後一場，演完他就離開芝加哥了。

整場表演只用一副撲克牌。他的手法乾淨到連其他魔術師都看不出他是怎麼做的，臉上還一直沒有表情。

魔術廳藏在安德森維爾一家真的洗衣店後面，門口沒有招牌。票價大約四十四到五十美元。

On Wednesday evening, August 26th at 7 PM, Czech magician Ondřej Pšenička performs "52 Lovers" at the Chicago Magic Lounge. It is the final show of the run—after this he leaves Chicago. The entire performance uses a single deck of cards. His sleight of hand is so clean that even other magicians can't work out how he does it, and his face stays completely expressionless throughout. The Lounge is hidden behind a working laundromat in Andersonville, with no sign at the door. Tickets run about $44 to $50.

一個網頁，裝下還沒有人聽過的音階

來源：Scale Workshop

鋼琴一個八度有十二個音，但這不是唯一的答案。Scale Workshop 是一個免費網頁，二零一九年由英國作曲家 Sevish 推出。不用買樂器或軟體，打開瀏覽器，用鍵盤就能彈出自己設計的音階。

最聰明的一步是把音階資料直接放在網址裡。把連結寄給朋友，對方一點開就聽到你剛發明的調音。各地的微分音（比半音更小的音）愛好者因此能即時分享作品。

現在由 Lumi Pakkanen 帶領的社群維護。老師也用它告訴學生：一個八度分成十二個音，從來不是唯一的走法。

A piano octave contains twelve notes, but that is not the only answer. Scale Workshop is a free website launched in 2019 by British composer Sevish. No instrument or software purchase needed—just open your browser and use the keyboard to play scales you design yourself. The cleverest touch is storing the scale data directly in the URL. Send the link to a friend and the moment they open it, they hear the tuning you just invented. Microtonal enthusiasts—people working with intervals smaller than a semitone—can share their work instantly this way. It is now maintained by a community led by Lumi Pakkanen. Teachers use it too, to show students that dividing an octave into twelve notes was never the only way.
