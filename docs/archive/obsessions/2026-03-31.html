綠帽：用電子音樂切開文化的刀

來源：Resident Advisor

馬來西亞出生、住在台北和上海的音樂人 Tzusing，二〇二三年在柏林的 PAN 廠牌發行了專輯《綠帽》。這個名字來自唐代的故事：戴綠帽代表男人被背叛（不忠）的羞恥。

十二首歌把儒家男性面子、不忠和父權（男性主導的社會）的雙重標準，變成猛烈的電子節拍。裡面混合了南非 gqom、trap 和工業音樂的聲音——用舞曲做文化考古，很少人能做到這樣。

Tzusing, a musician born in Malaysia who lives in both Taipei and Shanghai, released the album "Green Hat" on the Berlin-based PAN label in 2023. The title draws from a Tang dynasty tale: wearing a green hat symbolizes the shame of male infidelity and betrayal.

Across twelve tracks, the album transforms Confucian masculine honor, infidelity, and patriarchal double standards into ferocious electronic beats. The soundscape weaves together South African gqom, trap, and industrial music—using dance music as cultural archaeology in a way few artists can achieve.

赫爾辛基的水上桑拿天堂

來源：Discovering Finland

Allas Sea Pool 就在赫爾辛基市場廣場旁邊，漂浮在波羅的海上。這裡有五間桑拿房、一個加溫到二十七度的游泳池，還有一個直接引入海水的冷水池。

三月的玩法：先在玻璃牆桑拿裡看海上小島，等身體熱了，跳進接近冰點的海水，雪花落在臉上。最後到屋頂酒吧，喝香檳看海景。這就是芬蘭人說的幸福。

Allas Sea Pool sits right beside Helsinki's Market Square, floating on the Baltic Sea. It features five saunas, a heated swimming pool warmed to twenty-seven degrees, and a cold-water pool fed directly with seawater.

The March experience: Start in a glass-walled sauna overlooking small islands in the sea. Once your body is hot, jump into near-freezing seawater with snowflakes falling on your face. Finally, head to the rooftop bar for champagne and ocean views. This is what Finns call happiness.

世界最大蒂芙尼玻璃圓頂下的免費音樂會

來源：Eventbrite/DCASE

四月二十三日下午五點到八點半，芝加哥文化局在 Preston Bradley Hall 舉辦免費的「圓頂之下」音樂會。地點在 78 E. Washington，頭頂就是全世界最大的蒂芙尼彩色玻璃圓頂。

除了現場音樂，還有衣服交換活動：帶五件舊衣服來，帶五件新衣服走。所有年齡都歡迎，現場有手語翻譯。在芝加哥最美的天花板下聽歌換衣服，不用花一毛錢。

On April 23rd from 5 to 8:30 p.m., the Chicago Department of Cultural Affairs will host a free "Under the Dome" concert at Preston Bradley Hall. The venue is located at 78 E. Washington, and directly overhead is the world's largest Tiffany stained glass dome.

In addition to live music, there will be a clothing swap: bring five old clothes items and leave with five new ones. All ages are welcome, and sign language interpretation will be provided. Listen to music and swap clothes under one of Chicago's most beautiful ceilings—completely free of charge.
