甜梅號：被兵役打斷的台北搖滾夢

來源：Bandcamp Daily

二○○一年，三個台大學生組成「甜梅號」，發行了第一張專輯《是不是少了什麼》。十二首沒有歌詞的曲子，用很多層吉他聲，一下子安靜，一下子吵鬧。

專輯一出，團員就被叫去當兵，樂團只好暫停，唱片也很快絕版（不再生產）。今天，這張專輯成了台北地下搖滾的傳說。

In 2001, three National Taiwan University students formed "Sugar Plum Ferry" and released their debut album "Lack of Something." Twelve instrumental tracks layered guitars densely, swinging between whisper-quiet passages and wall-of-sound noise.

The album dropped, then the band members were drafted for military service. The group went on indefinite hiatus, and the record quickly went out of print. Today, that album has become legend in Taipei's underground rock scene.

老勞馬：木頭小城與漁婦的蕾絲

來源：Discovering Finland

芬蘭西岸的老勞馬（Old Rauma）是全北歐最大的木造小城，被聯合國列為世界遺產。城裡有六百多棟十八、十九世紀的木屋，到現在還有人住著。

從一七○○年代開始，漁夫出海時，太太們就用木棒編蕾絲賺錢。今天還有一百五十位居民在編。冬天遊客少，石板路和彩色木屋特別安靜。

Old Rauma, on Finland's west coast, is the largest wooden town in all of northern Europe and has been designated a UNESCO World Heritage Site. The town is home to over six hundred wooden houses dating from the eighteenth and nineteenth centuries, and people still live in them today.

Starting in the 1700s, while fishermen were out at sea, their wives would earn money by making lace with wooden bobbins. Today, around one hundred and fifty residents still practice the craft. In winter, when tourists are scarce, the slate-paved streets and colorful wooden houses become particularly quiet.

芝加哥新地標：五層樓的魔術大宅

來源：Block Club Chicago

四月十八日，芝加哥的老房子「麥考密克大宅」變身成「手與眼」魔術劇場開幕。五層樓、三十七個房間，花了五千萬美金整修。

客人會走進「沙漏層」和「地下層」這些秘密通道，看世界一流的近距離魔術，並享用三小時晚餐。票價從兩百二十五美金起跳。

On April 18th, Chicago's historic McCormick Mansion transforms into the grand opening of The Hand & The Eye, an intimate magic theater. The five-story, 37-room venue underwent a $50 million restoration.

Guests will navigate through secret passages like the "Hourglass Floor" and "Underground Level," experiencing world-class close-up magic while enjoying a three-hour dinner experience. Tickets start at $225.

速記迷的寶庫：一個人蒐集的書寫史

來源：stenophile.com

有一個叫西恩（Sean）的人，自己做了一個網站叫「Stenophile」，專門蒐集速記（一種快速寫字的方法）系統。

以前辦公室的人都要學速記，像是有名的皮特曼和格雷格兩種寫法。西恩連日本的速記也收進來，還自己發明了一套叫「Stenohand」的新系統。

There is a person named Sean who created a website called "Stenophile" dedicated to collecting shorthand systems — a method of rapid handwriting.

In the past, office workers had to learn shorthand, with famous systems like Pitman and Gregg. Sean has even collected Japanese shorthand systems, and he invented his own new system called "Stenohand."
