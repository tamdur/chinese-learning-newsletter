音樂精靈的告別之作：《平凡》

來源：Discogs / reissue archive

一九九一年九月，黃韻玲在滾石唱片發行了《平凡》，那是她離開滾石前的最後一張專輯。

大家叫她「音樂精靈」：歌是她寫的，曲是她編的，樂器是她彈的，連和聲也是她自己唱。

這張唱片有明亮的旋律，配上活潑的貝斯，最後一首歌就叫「福爾摩沙」，那是台灣的老名字。它絕版了很多年，後來被挖唱片的人找回來，重新壓成黑膠。

In September 1991, Huang Yun-ling (黃韻玲) released Ordinary on Rolling Stone Records — her last album for the label before she left it.

Everyone called her the "Music Sprite": she wrote the songs, arranged them, played the instruments, and even sang her own harmonies.

The record has bright melodies over a lively bass, and the closing track is simply called "Formosa," the old name for Taiwan. Out of print for years, it was dug back up by record collectors and pressed to vinyl again.

北國小鎮的憂傷舞步

來源：Tangomarkkinat / Helsinki Times

探戈（一種南美洲來的舞）在一九一三年傳進芬蘭，慢慢變了樣：曲調轉成小調，聽起來帶著淡淡的憂傷。

從一九八五年起，農村小鎮塞納約基每年七月辦「探戈市集」，是世界上最老的探戈節。十萬多人湧進來，投票選出探戈國王和王后。

更早以前，芬蘭人在湖邊和森林旁的木頭舞亭跳舞，趁著夏天明亮的夜晚認識彼此。冬天一到，舞就搬進鎮上的舞廳。

Tango — a dance from South America — arrived in Finland in 1913 and gradually changed shape: the tunes turned to minor keys, carrying a faint melancholy.

Since 1985 the farming town of Seinäjoki has held Tangomarkkinat every July, the oldest tango festival in the world. More than a hundred thousand people pour in and vote to crown a Tango King and Queen.

Long before that, Finns danced in wooden pavilions beside lakes and forests, getting to know one another in the bright summer nights. When winter came, the dancing moved indoors to the town dance halls.

地底下的芝加哥：走進迷宮的兩小時

來源：Choose Chicago / Inside Chicago Walking Tours

芝加哥市中心的大樓底下，藏著一張叫「地下人行道」的走道網，把辦公大樓、車站和旅館連在一起。

二零二六年九月十二日，有一場叫「張開眼睛」的導覽，帶大家走兩個小時，幾乎全程在室內。路上會看到彩色玻璃、地下游泳池、市政廳旁的壁畫，還有從地底下看畢卡索的大雕像。

票價三十五美元，一團最多二十人，訂好位子以後才告訴你在哪裡集合。

Beneath the buildings of downtown Chicago runs a hidden network of walkways called the Pedway, linking office towers, train stations and hotels.

On September 12, 2026, a tour called "Open Your Eyes" takes people through it for two hours, almost entirely indoors. Along the way you see stained glass, an underground swimming pool, murals beside City Hall, and a view of Picasso's big sculpture from below street level.

Tickets are $35, groups are capped at twenty people, and they only tell you the meeting point after you book.

每個故事都發生在真實的地方

來源：Reading Maps

英國人 Aires Loutsaris 白天是上班族，下班後只做一件事：把小說人物走過的路，一條一條畫到真實的地圖上。

八十天環遊世界的路線、《白鯨記》捕鯨船的航線、《黑暗之心》沿著剛果河往上游的旅行，還有戴洛維夫人在倫敦走路的那一天，都在裡面。

這個網站叫 Reading Maps ，路線分成陸路和海路，也分成流亡、求生、成長。現在已經超過一千條，任何人都能免費看。

Aires Loutsaris, from Britain, has an office job by day; after work he does just one thing — plotting the routes that fictional characters walked, one by one, onto real maps.

The eighty-day trip around the world, the whaling ship's voyage in Moby-Dick, the journey up the Congo in Heart of Darkness, and the single day Mrs Dalloway spent walking through London are all in there.

The site is called Reading Maps. Routes are sorted into overland and sea, and into exile, survival and coming-of-age. There are more than a thousand of them now, and anyone can browse them for free.
