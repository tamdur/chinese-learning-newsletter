谷歌買晶片，還拿到馬維爾股票

來源：CNBC

晶片公司馬維爾給了谷歌一張「認股權」（用固定價格買股票的權利），谷歌可以用每股約二百零七美元，買下最多五千九百萬股，價值大約一百二十二億美元。

這些股票不是一次給完。谷歌每向馬維爾多買五億美元的晶片，才解鎖一批。馬維爾則要為谷歌設計人工智慧晶片，訂單到二零三三年可能高達一千二百億美元。

消息一出，馬維爾股價漲了一成，老供應商博通卻跌了三個百分點以上。

Chip company Marvell has granted Google a warrant — the right to buy shares at a fixed price — allowing it to purchase up to 59 million shares at about $207 each, worth roughly $12.2 billion.

The shares are not handed over all at once. Google unlocks another batch for every additional $500 million of chips it buys from Marvell. In return, Marvell will design AI chips for Google, with orders that could reach $120 billion by 2033.

On the news, Marvell's stock rose 10 percent, while long-time supplier Broadcom fell more than three percentage points.

程式套件被下毒，編譯就中招

來源：Rust Blog

八月二十日，三個很多人用的 Rust 程式套件被放上惡意版本。它們偷偷多了一個假的相依套件，只要「編譯」（把程式碼變成電腦能執行的檔案）專案，攻擊者的程式就會自動下載並執行，連跑都不用跑。

其中 arrayref 歷來被下載兩億四千五百萬次，出現在超過三成五的受檢環境中。Rust 安全小組收到通報後，約九十分鐘內就把壞版本全部下架。

調查發現，攻擊手法和最近北韓的行動很像，可能是維護者的帳號被盜。

On 20 August, malicious versions of three widely used Rust software packages were published. Each had quietly gained a fake dependency, so that merely "compiling" a project — turning source code into a file the computer can execute — would automatically download and run the attacker's code. The program never even had to be launched.

One of them, arrayref, has been downloaded 245 million times over its lifetime and appears in more than 35 percent of the environments that were scanned. After the Rust security team was notified, it pulled every bad version within about 90 minutes.

Investigators found the method closely resembles recent North Korean operations, and believe the maintainer's account was compromised.

聯準會七月紀錄：九比三，三人要升息

來源：CNBC

八月十九日公布的七月會議紀錄顯示，聯準會決定把利率維持在百分之三點五到三點七五，票數是九比三。克里夫蘭的哈瑪克、明尼亞波利斯的卡什卡利、達拉斯的羅根都主張升息零點二五個百分點。

紀錄還寫著，不只這三人，「許多」與會者都認為，如果通膨降不下來，可能還是得再升息。

另外，主席沃許提議把一年八次的會議減成六次，目前還沒決定。

The minutes of the July meeting, released on 19 August, show that the Federal Reserve's decision to hold interest rates at 3.5 to 3.75 percent passed by a vote of nine to three. Hammack of Cleveland, Kashkari of Minneapolis and Logan of Dallas all argued for raising rates by a quarter of a percentage point.

The minutes also record that it was not just those three: "many" participants thought another rate rise might still be needed if inflation does not come down.

Separately, Chair Warsh proposed cutting the number of meetings from eight a year to six. No decision has been made.

芝加哥出局，無緣二零二八民主黨大會

來源：WTTW News

民主黨全國委員會八月二十日宣布，二零二八年全國代表大會的主辦城市第一輪就刷掉芝加哥和亞特蘭大，只剩波士頓、費城和丹佛。

芝加哥二零二四年才辦過一次，也是美國辦過最多次政黨大會的城市。委員會沒有說明為什麼淘汰。

亞特蘭大市長批評，同時拿掉兩座城市是「拋棄黑人選民」。對芝加哥來說，失去的是好幾億美元的旅客消費和全國曝光。

The Democratic National Committee announced on 20 August that Chicago and Atlanta had both been cut in the very first round of host-city selection for the 2028 national convention, leaving Boston, Philadelphia and Denver.

Chicago hosted the convention as recently as 2024, and has held more party conventions than any other American city. The committee gave no explanation for the eliminations.

Atlanta's mayor criticised the decision, calling the removal of both cities at once an abandonment of Black voters. For Chicago, what is lost is several hundred million dollars of visitor spending and a turn in the national spotlight.

熊隊訓練營收尾，兩大問題沒答案

來源：ESPN

ESPN 八月二十一日報導，芝加哥熊隊的年輕外接員伯登因為鼠蹊部（大腿根部）受傷，整個季前賽都不會上場，不過球隊認為他趕得上九月十三日的開幕戰。

左邊護鋒的位置也還沒定下來。原本被看好的特拉皮洛還在養傷，可能要到訓練營結束才回得來；威爾斯和阿梅加吉耶只能輪流跟先發練球，誰也沒勝出。

ESPN reported on 21 August that Chicago Bears young receiver Luther Burden III will miss the entire preseason with a groin injury, though the team believes he will be ready for the season opener on 13 September.

The left tackle job is unsettled too. Ozzy Trapilo, once the favourite for it, is still recovering and may not be back until camp ends; Jedrick Wills and Kiran Amegadjie are taking turns with the starters, and neither has won the job.

兵工廠線：阿特塔還想再簽一名邊鋒

來源：Sky Sports

兵工廠線——聯賽今晚在主場對考文垂開打，轉會窗還剩十天，教練阿特塔說球隊還有事情要做。

兵工廠正在談一名邊鋒，這名球員上季有二十一次進球或助攻，預算最高可能到八千六百萬英鎊。這個位置阿特塔找了一整個夏天都沒買到。

他也說，就算已經用五千一百萬英鎊談成後衛孔薩，球隊還想再補一名後衛；而青訓出身的路易斯史凱利「百分之百會留下」。

The Arsenal beat — the league season kicks off tonight at home against Coventry, ten days remain in the transfer window, and manager Mikel Arteta says the club still has work to do.

Arsenal are in talks for a winger who produced 21 goals or assists last season, with a budget that could run as high as £86 million. It is the position Arteta hunted all summer without landing anyone.

He also said that even after agreeing a £51 million deal for defender Ezri Konsa, the club wants to add another defender — and that academy graduate Myles Lewis-Skelly will "100 percent stay".
