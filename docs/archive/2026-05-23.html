美國情報總監乘戰時辭職，因丈夫罹癌

來源：Fox News

美國國家情報總監Tulsi Gabbard宣布辭職，六月三十日生效。她在辭職信中說，丈夫Abraham被查出一種非常少見的骨癌（骨頭的癌症），她必須回家照顧他。

她是川普第二任期第四位離開的閣員。美國正在打伊朗戰爭，這時候情報總監出缺，影響很大。

US Director of National Intelligence Tulsi Gabbard has announced her resignation, effective June 30th. In her resignation letter, she stated that her husband Abraham has been diagnosed with a rare form of bone cancer, and she must return home to care for him. She is the fourth cabinet member to depart during Trump's second term. Her departure comes at a critical moment, as the US is engaged in war with Iran, making the vacant intelligence director position a significant loss.

新規定：想拿綠卡？先回國申請

來源：NPR

川普政府宣布，以後在美國的外國人想申請綠卡（永久居留證），必須先回到自己的國家，在那裡辦手續。過去五十年來，人們都可以留在美國直接申請。

不過，持有H-1B工作簽證的高技術人才不受影響。每年大約六十萬人會被這條新規定影響，而且可能面對好幾年的等待。

The Trump administration has announced that foreigners in the United States seeking to apply for a green card (permanent residency) must now return to their home country to complete the process there. For the past fifty years, people have been able to apply while remaining in the United States. However, holders of H-1B work visas for highly skilled workers are exempt from this new rule. Approximately 600,000 people annually will be affected by this policy, and many could face waits of several years.

軍方人員已經取消了國殤日長假，準備隨時行動。

The United States is preparing a fresh round of military strikes against Iran. The conflict has now stretched on for nearly three months since late February. On Wednesday, the US delivered an "final offer" to Iran, warning that further attacks would follow if rejected. Military personnel have cancelled Memorial Day leave and stand ready for immediate action.

打伊朗飛彈不夠，美暫停對台一百四十億美元軍售

來源：Al Jazeera

美國代理海軍部長曹雄在國會聽證會上說，因為伊朗戰爭用掉太多飛彈，美國暫停賣給台灣一百四十億美元的武器。

專家說，美軍已經用掉超過一半的戰前飛彈庫存（存放的飛彈數量），要花一到四年才能補回來。這讓人擔心美國還有沒有能力同時保護台灣。

U.S. Acting Secretary of the Navy Cao Xiong told Congress that the United States is pausing a $14 billion weapons sale to Taiwan because the Iran conflict has depleted too many missiles. Experts say the U.S. military has already used more than half of its pre-war missile stockpile and will need one to four years to replenish supplies. The move raises concerns about whether the United States has the capacity to defend Taiwan simultaneously.
