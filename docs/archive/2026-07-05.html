五角大廈與AI公司為「自動武器」鬧翻

來源：Tech Times

法院最近公開了一批電子郵件，讓大家看到美國國防部（五角大廈）和AI公司Anthropic的爭吵。國防部想把Claude這個AI用在「自己會開火的武器」上，但公司老闆Amodei不肯，說現在的AI還不夠可靠。

後來國防部把這家公司列入「黑名單」，不少人懷疑這是一種報復。

A court recently released emails that exposed a dispute between the U.S. Department of Defense (the Pentagon) and AI company Anthropic. The Pentagon wanted to deploy Claude, an AI system, in autonomous weapons that fire on their own, but company founder Amodei refused, arguing that current AI lacks sufficient reliability. The Defense Department subsequently added the company to its blacklist, prompting widespread suspicion that this was retaliatory.

中國美團推出AI 全用國產晶片訓練

來源：VentureBeat

中國的外送公司美團，六月三十日公開了一個很大的AI，叫做LongCat-2.0。它最特別的地方，是完全用中國自己做的晶片（電腦裡的小零件）訓練出來的，沒有用到美國Nvidia的晶片。

這個AI很會寫程式，在OpenRouter網站上很受歡迎。這也讓大家看到，中國就算沒有美國晶片，一樣能做出很強的AI。

China's food delivery company Meituan publicly released a major AI model called LongCat-2.0 on June 30th. What makes it particularly noteworthy is that it was trained entirely using chips manufactured domestically in China—without relying on American Nvidia processors. The AI is particularly skilled at code generation and has become popular on the OpenRouter website. This development demonstrates that China can create powerful AI systems even without access to American semiconductor technology.

芝加哥七月四日暴風雨 逾20萬戶停電

來源：CBS Chicago

七月四日連假，強烈的暴風雨掃過芝加哥一帶。風速最快超過每小時80英里，吹倒很多樹木，也淹了不少道路，害得國慶煙火只好取消。

電力公司ComEd有超過20萬戶停電。在Kendall郡，一位47歲的女士被倒下的樹木壓住，不幸過世。

A powerful severe storm swept through the Chicago area on the July 4th holiday. Wind speeds exceeded 80 miles per hour at their peak, toppling numerous trees and flooding many roads, forcing the cancellation of the Independence Day fireworks. The power company ComEd reported over 200,000 customers without electricity. In Kendall County, a 47-year-old woman was struck by a falling tree and died.

美國籍教宗 七月四日為移民發聲

來源：Washington Post

第一位美國籍的教宗良（Leo），在七月四日美國國慶這天，特地去了義大利的小島Lampedusa。這座島是很多移民冒險渡海、進入歐洲的第一站。

教宗寫信呼籲美國人用「愛心和慷慨（大方）」對待移民，說歡迎他們不只是做好事，也是尊重每一個人。

Pope Leo, the first American-born pontiff, traveled to the Italian island of Lampedusa on July 4th, America's Independence Day.

The island serves as the first point of entry for countless migrants attempting dangerous sea crossings to reach Europe. In a letter, the Pope urged Americans to welcome migrants with "love and generosity," saying that such welcome is not only a good deed but also a way of respecting every person.

五星新秀McCoy 決定留在密西根打球

來源：UM Hoops

密西根大學籃球隊最近很亂：帶隊拿到冠軍的教練Dusty May上個月突然離開，去了NBA的達拉斯獨行俠隊，球隊只好先請Boynton當代理教練。

不過好消息來了：五星新秀、後衛Brandon McCoy在七月四日公開表示，他還是要留在密西根。他是全美排名第十的高中好手。

Michigan basketball has been in turmoil: Coach Dusty May, who led the team to a championship, suddenly left last month for the NBA's Dallas Mavericks, forcing the program to install Boynton as interim coach. But there's good news: five-star recruit and guard Brandon McCoy publicly announced on July 4th that he's staying at Michigan. He's ranked in the top ten nationally.

兵工廠賣掉Trossard 進帳2000萬歐元

來源：Arseblog News

兵工廠線——阿森納（外號兵工廠）談好了一筆交易，要把前鋒Trossard賣給土耳其的貝西克塔斯隊，價錢是2000萬歐元。

這位31歲的比利時球員，2023年加入後表現一直很穩。賣掉他以後，教練Arteta就有錢、也有空位去補強前鋒，目標之一是Morgan Rogers。

Arsenal beat—Arsenal has finalized a deal to sell forward Trossard to Turkish side Beşiktaş for €20 million.

The 31-year-old Belgian has been consistent since joining in 2023.

With Trossard sold, manager Arteta now has the funds and roster space to strengthen the forward line, with Morgan Rogers among his targets.
