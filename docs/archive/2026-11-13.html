伊利諾州開始管THC軟糖和飲料

來源：Capitol News Illinois

從11月12日起，伊利諾州開始執行新的工業大麻法。以前，很多加油站和便利商店都在賣含有THC（會讓人「嗨」起來的成分）的軟糖和飲料。

新規定說，每一包THC超過0.4毫克的產品都算是大麻，只有拿到執照的大麻店才能賣。

州長普立茲克（Pritzker）說，這些規定「早就該有了」。聯邦的新規定要到12月才上路，伊利諾州先走一步。

Starting November 12, Illinois began enforcing a new law on industrial hemp. Until now, many gas stations and convenience stores have been selling gummies and drinks containing THC, the compound that makes people feel "high." Under the new rules, any product with more than 0.4 milligrams of THC per package counts as cannabis, and only licensed cannabis dispensaries may sell it. Governor Pritzker said these rules were "long overdue." Federal rules will not take effect until December, so Illinois is moving first.

俄烏九個月來首次見面，只談成換俘

來源：Foreign Exchanges

俄羅斯和烏克蘭的代表，11月10日和11日在阿布達比（阿拉伯聯合大公國的首都）談了兩天。這是兩國九個月來第一次直接見面，由美國在中間幫忙。

結果，雙方只同意交換數百名戰俘（打仗時被抓走的士兵）。至於停火和土地問題，兩邊還是談不攏。

美國特使說，接下來幾週還會再談。不過，要真正結束這場戰爭，看來還有很長的路。

Representatives of Russia and Ukraine met for two days, on November 10 and 11, in Abu Dhabi, the capital of the United Arab Emirates. It was the first direct meeting between the two countries in nine months, with the United States helping to broker it. The only result was an agreement to exchange several hundred prisoners of war. On a ceasefire and on territory, the two sides remain far apart. A US envoy said talks will resume in the coming weeks. Still, it appears that ending the war will take a long time.

小熊隊克勞阿姆斯壯拿下國聯MVP

來源：Chicago Sun-Times

小熊隊中外野手克勞阿姆斯壯（Pete Crow-Armstrong）11月12日拿下國家聯盟最有價值球員（MVP），打敗了道奇隊的大谷翔平。

他今年打出45支全壘打，還有41次盜壘（趁機跑到下一個壘）。小熊隊慶祝的影片裡，還出現了熊隊四分衛凱勒布‧威廉斯（Caleb Williams）。

不過也有壞消息：報導說，鈴木誠也不打算接受小熊隊給的一年合約，可能會離開。

Cubs center fielder Pete Crow-Armstrong won the National League Most Valuable Player award on November 12, beating the Dodgers' Shohei Ohtani. This year he hit 45 home runs and stole 41 bases, running to the next base whenever he could get away with it. A Cubs celebration video also featured Bears quarterback Caleb Williams. There is some bad news, though: reports say Seiya Suzuki does not intend to accept the one-year contract the Cubs have offered and may leave the team.

兵工廠盼薩卡12月初回歸

來源：3 Added Minutes

兵工廠線——據報導，兵工廠希望薩卡（Saka）能在12月2日對布倫特福德（Brentford）時回到場上。他10月21日在歐冠對拜仁時大腿受傷，上場不到10分鐘就被換下。

少了這位主力，兵工廠還是英超第一名，領先第二名6分。前10場贏了9場，是隊史最好的開局。

國家隊比賽週結束後，兵工廠21日要打紐卡索，24日在歐冠對多特蒙德。薩卡回來之前，球隊得先守住這個領先。

Arsenal beat: Arsenal reportedly hope Bukayo Saka can return to the pitch when they face Brentford on December 2. He pulled a thigh muscle on October 21 in the Champions League against Bayern and was substituted off after less than 10 minutes on the field. Even without their key man, Arsenal remain top of the Premier League, six points clear of second place. They have won nine of their first ten games, the best start in club history. After the international break, Arsenal play Newcastle on the 21st and host Borussia Dortmund in the Champions League on the 24th. Until Saka is back, the team has to hold onto this lead.
