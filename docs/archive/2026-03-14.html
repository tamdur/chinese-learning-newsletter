密西根險勝俄亥俄，晉級大十聯盟四強

來源：ESPN

大十聯盟錦標賽八強戰，頭號種子密西根（30勝2敗）以71比67險勝俄亥俄州大。這場比賽打得很緊張，最後靠Aday Mara拿下全隊最高的17分才驚險過關。

密西根週六將在準決賽對上伊利諾或威斯康辛。今年的密西根真的很強，但錦標賽什麼事都可能發生。

In the Big Ten Tournament quarterfinals, top seed Michigan (30-2) narrowly defeated Ohio State 71-67. The game was tightly contested throughout, with Michigan securing the narrow victory behind Aday Mara's team-leading 17 points.

Michigan will face either Illinois or Wisconsin in Saturday's semifinal. Michigan has been incredibly strong this season, but anything can happen in tournament play.

Anthropic控告美國國防部，抗議遭禁止政府合約

來源：Fortune

AI公司Anthropic被Trump政府禁止參與所有美國政府合約，公司在3月12日正式提告。國防部官員私下承認，這個決定是「意識形態」的，沒有證據顯示Anthropic做錯什麼。

問題的核心是：Anthropic拒絕讓自家AI用於自主武器和大規模監控（就是用科技監視很多人）。這件事反映出AI公司跟政府之間越來越大的緊張關係。

AI company Anthropic has been banned by the Trump administration from participating in all U.S. government contracts. The company filed a formal lawsuit on March 12th. Department of Defense officials privately admitted that this decision was "ideological" and that there is no evidence that Anthropic did anything wrong.

The core issue is this: Anthropic refuses to allow its AI to be used for autonomous weapons and mass surveillance (using technology to monitor many people). This reflects growing tension between AI companies and the government.

油價突破100美元，伊朗戰爭衝擊全球石油供應

來源：CNBC

布蘭特原油連續第二天收在103美元，因為伊朗的戰爭讓荷莫茲海峽（全球最重要的石油運輸通道）的船運受阻。

國際能源署說這是史上最大的供應中斷，影響全球大約20%的石油供應。美股道瓊指數也大跌740點。能源價格上漲對全世界的經濟都不是好消息。

Brent crude oil closed above $103 for the second consecutive day as Iran's military conflict disrupts shipping through the Strait of Hormuz, the world's most critical petroleum transport corridor.

The International Energy Agency says this represents the largest supply disruption on record, affecting roughly 20% of the world's oil supply. The Dow Jones index also fell sharply by 740 points. Rising energy prices spell trouble for economies worldwide.

研究：人們反對AI已變成道德問題，不是技術問題

來源：Marginal Revolution

一項新研究發現，社會對AI的看法已經「道德化」了，程度跟基改食品和疫苗差不多。什麼意思呢？就是很多人反對AI不是因為技術原因，而是覺得AI本身就是「不對的」。

大多數反對者說，就算證明AI是安全又有用的，他們也不會改變想法。這很有趣——當一個議題變成道德問題，事實和數據就很難改變人的立場。

A new study finds that society's view of AI has become "moralized" — comparable to how people view genetically modified foods and vaccines. What does that mean? Many people oppose AI not because of technical reasons, but because they believe AI itself is inherently "wrong."

Most opponents say that even if you proved AI was safe and useful, they wouldn't change their minds. That's interesting — when an issue becomes a moral one, facts and data struggle to shift people's positions.

科學家用月球土壤成功種出鷹嘴豆

來源：ScienceDaily

科學家在模擬（模仿）月球土壤裡成功種出了鷹嘴豆！他們在土裡加了蚯蚓堆肥和一種特別的真菌（幫助植物吸收養分的菌），讓鷹嘴豆順利長大。

為什麼選鷹嘴豆？因為它可以把空氣中的氮（植物需要的養分）固定在土裡，等於自己幫自己施肥。未來如果人類住在月球，也許真的能自己種菜吃。

Scientists successfully grew chickpeas in simulated lunar soil! They added earthworm compost and a special fungus to the soil—the kind that helps plants absorb nutrients—and the chickpeas grew well.

Why chickpeas? Because they can fix nitrogen from the air directly into the soil, which means they essentially fertilize themselves. In the future, if humans live on the Moon, maybe they'll actually be able to grow their own vegetables.
