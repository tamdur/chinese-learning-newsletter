穆迪把美國信用評級從最高等級降下來

來源：Moody's Ratings

信用評級機構穆迪（Moody's）在五月十六日把美國的信用等級從 AAA 降到 Aa1。美國從一九一七年起就一直是最高等級，現在三大機構全都不再給美國最高分了。

穆迪說原因是美國政府欠的錢越來越多，利息負擔也越來越重，而國會一直沒辦法解決這個問題。

On May 16th, credit rating agency Moody's downgraded the United States' credit rating from AAA to Aa1. The U.S. had maintained the top rating since 1917, and now all three major rating agencies have removed America from the highest tier. Moody's cited rising government debt and increasing interest burdens as the reason, noting that Congress has been unable to address the problem.

川普下令從德國撤走五千名美軍

來源：AP News

美國總統川普在五月十七日下令，要把至少五千名駐（住在那裡的）德國的美軍撤回來。這個決定讓北約（NATO）盟友非常緊張。

俄羅斯跟烏克蘭的戰爭還在打，歐洲國家現在必須加快自己的國防計畫。

On May 17th, U.S. President Trump ordered the withdrawal of at least 5,000 American troops stationed in Germany. The decision has put NATO allies on edge. With the war between Russia and Ukraine still ongoing, European nations now must accelerate their own defense plans.

熱門文章：很多公司得了「AI瘋狂症」

來源：Hacker News

一篇在 Hacker News 上拿到一千六百多個讚的文章說，現在很多公司患了「AI 瘋狂症」——明明不需要，卻花大錢投資 AI 計畫。

作者指出，只有五分之一的 AI 投資有帶來好處，老闆們因為怕被別人超過、加上投資人的壓力，就不停花錢。

A post that garnered over 1,600 upvotes on Hacker News argues that many companies have come down with "AI fever" — splurging on AI initiatives they don't actually need. The author points out that only one in five AI investments have delivered real benefits, yet executives keep spending money out of fear of falling behind competitors and pressure from investors.

美國司法部要求交出十萬用戶資料

來源：Hacker News

美國司法部要求蘋果和 Google 交出超過十萬名用戶的個人資料。這些人都下載了一個叫 EZ Lynk 的汽車改裝程式。

政府說這個程式幫車主關掉排放控制，違反空氣法。但隱私權專家擔心，這樣大規模要求用戶資料是在監視一般人。

The US Department of Justice is demanding that Apple and Google hand over personal data for more than 100,000 users. These are people who downloaded an app called EZ Lynk, which modifies vehicles. The government says the app helps car owners disable emissions controls, violating the Clean Air Act. But privacy experts worry that such large-scale demands for user data amount to surveillance of ordinary people.

來源：ESPN

芝加哥跨城大戰（Crosstown Classic）來到第三場決勝戰。小熊第一場十比五贏，白襪第二場八比三扳回來。

今年兩隊的成績都在五成以上，是二〇二〇年以來第一次，球迷們特別期待今天的比賽。

The Chicago Crosstown Classic reaches its decisive third game today. The Cubs won the first game 10-5, and the White Sox evened the series with an 8-3 victory in game two. Both teams are sitting above .500 this season, the first time since 2020, and fans are especially eager for today's matchup.
