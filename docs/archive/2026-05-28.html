中國四家 AI 公司十二天內連發四款開放模型

來源：Air Street Press

四月裡，中國四家 AI 公司在短短十二天內，各自推出一款開放的寫程式 AI 模型：GLM-5.1、MiniMax M2.7、Kimi K2.6 和 DeepSeek V4。

這些模型在程式測試中的表現已經接近歐美最強的模型，但使用費用只要三分之一。而且它們全部開放權重（就是讓大家免費下載來用），這對 OpenAI 和 Anthropic 的收費模式是很大的挑戰。

In April, four Chinese AI companies each released an open coding AI model within just twelve days: GLM-5.1, MiniMax M2.7, Kimi K2.6, and DeepSeek V4. These models perform nearly as well as the strongest Western models in coding benchmarks, but cost only one-third as much to run. And they're all open-weight — meaning anyone can download and use them for free — which poses a significant challenge to OpenAI and Anthropic's paid business models.

以色列空襲黎巴嫩釀三十一死，停火名存實亡

來源：NPR / Al Jazeera

雖然美國在四月幫忙談成了停火，以色列五月二十七日還是對黎巴嫩發動了大規模空襲，打了超過一百個目標，造成至少三十一人死亡，其中包括四名小孩。

總理 Netanyahu 說要「加大力道」打擊真主黨。自三月以來，黎巴嫩已有三千兩百人喪生，停火幾乎已經失去意義。

Despite a ceasefire brokered by the United States in April, Israel launched a massive air assault on Lebanon on May 27th, striking more than one hundred targets and killing at least thirty-one people, including four children. Prime Minister Netanyahu said he would "intensify" efforts against Hezbollah. Since March, Lebanon has seen thirty-two hundred deaths, and the ceasefire has become almost meaningless.

熊隊新球場可享十五億美元減稅，議員吵翻

來源：Chicago Sun-Times

芝加哥熊隊想在 Arlington Heights 蓋新球場，一份新出的報告發現，伊利諾州的「大型開發案」法案會讓熊隊在四十年內省下超過十五億美元的房產稅，每年大約少繳三千九百萬。

熊隊說球場會花二十億蓋，但還要州政府出八億五千萬蓋馬路和水管。州議會只剩五天就要休會，能不能通過還不知道。

The Chicago Bears want to build a new stadium in Arlington Heights. A new report found that Illinois's "major development" bill would save the Bears over $1.5 billion in property taxes over forty years—roughly $39 million per year. The Bears say the stadium will cost $2 billion to build, but they're also asking the state to contribute $850 million for roads and utilities. The state legislature has only five days left before it recesses, and it's unclear whether the bill will pass.

伊利諾州資料中心每天喝掉數百萬加侖水

來源：NBC Chicago

伊利諾州越來越多資料中心（放電腦伺服器的大樓），它們需要大量的水來幫機器散熱。一座大型資料中心一天就能用掉五百萬加侖，跟一個中型城鎮差不多。

問題是這些水用來散熱後會蒸發掉，沒辦法回到水系統裡。州政府的管理速度完全跟不上，科技投資和環境保護之間的矛盾越來越大。

Illinois is home to an ever-growing number of data centers—massive buildings filled with computer servers—and they require enormous quantities of water to cool their machines. A single large data center can consume five million gallons per day, roughly equivalent to the usage of a mid-sized city. The catch: after the water is used for cooling, it evaporates and never returns to the water system. State regulators have struggled to keep pace, and the tension between technological investment and environmental stewardship continues to mount.

小熊隊十連敗，從第一掉到最後

來源：Bleacher Nation

小熊隊的連敗已經到了十場。球季開始時他們打出二十七勝十二敗，是國聯中區第一名，結果最近十六場只贏了兩場，直接掉到最後一名。

球隊已經把二號新秀 Pedro Ramirez 從小聯盟叫上來，這位二十二歲的二壘手上來四場就打出不錯的成績，是球迷目前少數的好消息。

The Cubs have now lost ten straight. They started the season strong with a 27-12 record, sitting atop the National League Central, but have won just two of their last sixteen games and have plummeted to last place. The team has called up number-two prospect Pedro Ramirez from the minor leagues—the 22-year-old second baseman has posted solid numbers in four games so far, offering fans one of the few bright spots in an otherwise bleak stretch.

兵工廠週六爭歐冠，巴黎傷兵成隱憂

來源：Arsenal.com / FOX Sports

兵工廠線——拿下英超冠軍後，兵工廠現在全力準備五月三十日在布達佩斯的歐冠決賽，對手是衛冕冠軍巴黎聖日耳曼。這是兵工廠二十年來第一次打進歐冠決賽。

好消息是巴黎有傷兵問題：Dembele 和 Hakimi 都因傷缺席了賽前友誼賽，雖然已回到訓練場，但 Hakimi 可能無法先發上場。

After clinching the Premier League title, Arsenal is now in full preparation mode for the Champions League final on May 30th in Budapest, facing defending champion Paris Saint-Germain. This is Arsenal's first Champions League final appearance in twenty years. The good news is that Paris has an injury problem: both Dembele and Hakimi missed the pre-match friendly due to injury, and while they've returned to training, Hakimi may not be fit to start.
