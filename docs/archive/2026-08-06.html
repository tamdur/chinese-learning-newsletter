普洛斯併購西格羅，成為全球最大倉庫房東

來源：Bloomberg

美國倉庫公司普洛斯用大約一百四十億英鎊，買下英國的倉庫公司西格羅。西格羅先前拒絕過三次，這回終於點頭，其中三十五億英鎊用現金支付。

合併後的公司價值大約一千四百一十億美元，是世界上最大的倉庫房東。為什麼要合併？因為人工智慧資料中心正在搶同樣的土地和電力，倉庫業者壓力愈來愈大。

US warehouse company Prologis has acquired British warehouse operator Segro for approximately 14 billion pounds. Segro had rejected three previous offers before finally agreeing to the deal this time, with 3.5 billion pounds paid in cash. The combined company will be valued at around 141 billion dollars, making it the world's largest warehouse landlord. Why the merger? AI data centers are competing for the same land and electricity, putting mounting pressure on warehouse operators.
