盧拉小勝當選巴西總統，對手不認輸

來源：InfoMoney / Tribuna PR

巴西10月25日舉行總統決選。盧拉（Lula）拿下約51.8%的票，打敗得票48.2%的參議員 Flávio Bolsonaro，第四次當上總統。

但是 Bolsonaro 不承認輸了。投票當天，最高法院一位法官把投票時間延長兩小時。他說有些投票所早就關門，這樣不公平，要求取消選舉結果。

有趣的是，他的團隊自己也曾因為下雨，要求延長投票。接下來要看巴西的法院能不能穩住局面。

Brazil held its presidential runoff on October 25. Lula won about 51.8% of the vote, defeating Senator Flávio Bolsonaro, who took 48.2%, and won the presidency for a fourth time. But Bolsonaro is not conceding defeat. On election day, a Supreme Court justice extended voting by two hours. He said some polling stations had closed early, which was unfair, and asked for the election result to be annulled. Ironically, Bolsonaro's own team once asked for voting hours to be extended because of rain. Next, the question is whether Brazil's courts can keep the situation under control.

經濟學家：多數大公司還沒從 AI 賺到錢

來源：Apollo / TechCrunch

投資公司 Apollo 的首席經濟學家 Torsten Slok 發現，除了幾家科技巨頭，美國其他大公司花很多錢買 AI，卻還看不到好處。

他把標普500指數裡最大的七家科技公司拿掉，看剩下的493家。這些公司每賣100元，大約只賺10元，三年來幾乎沒変；七大科技公司卻從15元升到25元。

國際貨幣基金（IMF）本月才警告：如果 AI 賺不到錢， AI 股票可能大跌。 Slok 的數字，正好是在回答這個問題。

Torsten Slok, chief economist at the investment firm Apollo, has found that apart from a handful of tech giants, America's big companies are spending heavily on AI without seeing much in return. He removed the seven largest companies in the S&P 500 and looked at the remaining 493. For every $100 those companies bring in, they keep only about $10 in profit, a figure that has barely moved in three years. The seven tech giants, by contrast, have risen from $15 to $25. Just this month, the International Monetary Fund warned that if AI cannot make money, AI stocks could fall sharply. Slok's numbers speak directly to that question.

以色列明天投票，兩邊都難過半

來源：Ynetnews / Times of Israel

以色列10月27日要選出國會（Knesset）的120個議員。總理納坦雅胡（Netanyahu）這次可能會下臺。

最後幾份民調顯示，前軍隊總長 Gadi Eisenkot 的 Yashar 黨拿到約22到23席，比納坦雅胡的聯合黨（Likud）還多。但是兩邊都不到過半的61席。

所以，選完以後才是真正的比賽：誰能找到夠多政黨一起組政府，誰就能當總理。阿拉伯政黨的14到16席，可能変成關鍵。

Israelis go to the polls on October 27 to elect the 120 members of the Knesset, the national parliament. Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu may lose power this time. The final polls show that Yashar, the party of former military chief of staff Gadi Eisenkot, is winning about 22 to 23 seats, more than Netanyahu's Likud party. But neither side is close to the 61 seats needed for a majority. That means the real contest starts after the vote: whoever can gather enough parties to form a government will become prime minister. The 14 to 16 seats held by Arab parties could turn out to be the key.

小獎金找印度奇才：祭司工程師救古書

來源：Marginal Revolution

經濟學家 Tyler Cowen 創立的 Emergent Ventures India，在部落格 Marginal Revolution 公布第20批得獎名單。這個計畫不投資大機構，而是直接給有特別想法的人一筆小錢。

其中一位是來自蘇拉特（Surat）的 Zahan Turel。他是軟體工程師，也是家族第八代的祖教（古代波斯的宗教）祭司。他要把族人的老手稿和儀式紀錄存進電腦，免得它們消失。

另一位 Vaibhav Sai 想用 AI 設計生物分子。這個計畫相信：找对人，比找对機構更重要。

Emergent Ventures India, a program founded by economist Tyler Cowen, has published the names of its 20th batch of winners on his blog, Marginal Revolution. The program does not invest in large institutions. Instead, it gives small grants directly to people with unusual ideas. One winner is Zahan Turel from Surat. He is a software engineer and also the eighth-generation priest of his family's Zoroastrian faith (an ancient Persian religion). He plans to digitize his community's old handwritten manuscripts and ritual records so they do not disappear. Another winner, Vaibhav Sai, wants to use AI to design biomolecules. The program rests on a simple belief: finding the right people matters more than finding the right institutions.

密西根男籃熱身賽66比83輸休士頓

來源：MGoBlue / Detroit News

排名第16的密西根大學男籃，10月25日在大急流城（Grand Rapids）打最後一場熱身賽，以66比83輸給排名第10的休士頓大學。

這場球打得很激烈，一共吹了50次犯複。密西根上半場曾落後10分，靠一波15比1追上來，後來又被拉開。 Roddy Gayle Jr. 拿下全隊最高的14分。

熱身賽不算成績。11月2日对 Oakland，才是正式開季。

Ranked 16th, the University of Michigan men's basketball team played its final exhibition game on October 25 in Grand Rapids and lost 66-83 to No. 10 Houston. The game was physical, with 50 fouls called in total. Michigan trailed by 10 points at one point in the first half and fought back with a 15-1 run, but Houston pulled away again later.

Roddy Gayle Jr. led the team with 14 points.

Exhibition games don't count toward the record. The season officially opens on November 2 against Oakland.
