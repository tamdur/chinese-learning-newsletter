美國最高法院判川普關稅違法，退款明天開始

來源：The Hill

美國海關明天開始把錢退給進口商。最高法院今年二月以 6 比 3 判定川普用 IEEPA（緊急經濟法）加的關稅超過總統的權力，所以違法。

大約 33 萬家公司可以分到總共 1660 億美元的退款。這是美國歷史上最大的一筆政府退款。

Starting tomorrow, U.S. Customs will begin issuing refunds to importers. In February this year, the Supreme Court ruled 6-3 that the tariffs Trump imposed using the IEEPA (International Emergency Economic Powers Act) exceeded presidential authority and were therefore illegal. Approximately 330,000 companies will share a total of $166 billion in refunds. This is the largest government refund in U.S. history.

俄烏三天停火到期，雙方互指對方違規

來源：Al Jazeera

美國幫忙談成的三天停火今天結束了，但俄國和烏克蘭都說是對方先開火。俄國說烏克蘭違規一萬六千多次；烏克蘭總統澤倫斯基說俄國根本沒在遵守。

這次停火是為了紀念俄國「勝利日」，但今年的閱兵是二十多年來最小的，可以看出戰爭對俄國軍力的影響。

The three-day ceasefire brokered by the United States came to an end today, but both Russia and Ukraine are blaming each other for breaking it first. Russia claims Ukraine violated the truce over 16,000 times, while Ukrainian President Zelensky says Russia never honored it in the first place. The ceasefire was meant to mark Russia's "Victory Day," but this year's military parade was the smallest in more than two decades, a stark illustration of the war's toll on Russian military strength.

Anthropic 砺 2000 億美元與 Google 合作建 AI 基礎設施

來源：The Information

AI 公司 Anthropic 和 Google Cloud 簽了五年合約，承諾花 2000 億美元買 Google 的晶片和雲端服務。這是科技史上最大的雲端合約之一。

光是 2026 年，Anthropic 的伺服器花費就要 200 億美元。這筆錢佔 Google 雲端訂單的四成以上，可見 AI 競爭已經變成燒錢大戰。

AI company Anthropic and Google Cloud have signed a five-year contract committing $200 billion to purchase Google's chips and cloud services. It's one of the largest cloud deals in tech history. In 2026 alone, Anthropic's server spending will reach $20 billion. This accounts for over 40 percent of Google Cloud's orders, showing how the AI competition has become an all-out spending war.

郵輪爆漢他病毒疫情，乘客已散布多國

來源：WHO

世界衛生組織確認，荷蘭郵輪 MV Hondius 上爆發安地斯漢他病毒，目前有六例確診、三人死亡。這種病毒是唯一可以人傳人的漢他病毒。

乘客已在西班牙下船，部分美國乘客已飛回國。各國正在追蹤這些旅客的健康狀況。

The World Health Organization has confirmed an outbreak of Andes hantavirus on the Dutch cruise ship MV Hondius, with six confirmed cases and three deaths so far. This virus is the only hantavirus capable of human-to-human transmission. Passengers disembarked in Spain, with some American passengers having already flown back to the United States. Countries are now tracking the health status of these travelers.

公牛隊在選秀抽簽中拿到第四順位

來源：Sports Illustrated

芝加哥公牛隊在 NBA 選秀抽簽中拿到第四順位，比原本預計的第九順位好很多。抽簽在海軍碼頭舉行，公牛只有 4.5% 的機會拿到前四。

今年選秀有四個被認為是改變球隊命運的大物。公牛正在重建，這個順位是很大的好消息。

The Chicago Bulls landed the fourth overall pick in the NBA draft lottery, a significant improvement from their projected ninth spot. The drawing took place at Navy Pier, where the Bulls had just a 4.5% chance of securing a top-four selection. This year's draft features four prospects widely considered franchise-altering talents. For a Bulls team in the midst of a rebuild, this pick represents major good fortune.

兵工廠客場 1-0 勝西漢姆，冠軍近在眼前

來源：ESPN

兵工廠線——Trossard 在第 83 分鐘接到 Odegaard 的傳球，射入致勝球，兵工廠在客場 1-0 擊敗西漢姆。補時階段西漢姆的進球被 VAR 取消，兵工廠驚險過關。

兵工廠現在領先五分，只剰兩場比賽。再贏一場就能拿到 22 年來第一座聯賽冠軍。

Arsenal Line — Trossard struck the winner in the 83rd minute, converting a pass from Odegaard as Arsenal secured a 1-0 away victory over West Ham. A West Ham goal in stoppage time was ruled out by VAR, allowing Arsenal to escape with the three points. Arsenal now leads by five points with just two matches remaining. One more win will secure their first league title in 22 years.
