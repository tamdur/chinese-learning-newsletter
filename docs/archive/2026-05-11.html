美國最高法院判川普關稅違法，退款明天開始

來源：The Hill

美國海關明天開始把錢退給進口商。最高法院今年二月以 6 比 3 判定川普用 IEEPA（緊急經濟法）加的關稅超過總統的權力，所以違法。

大約 33 萬家公司可以分到總共 1660 億美元的退款。這是美國歷史上最大的一筆政府退款。

Starting tomorrow, U.S. Customs will begin issuing refunds to importers. In February this year, the Supreme Court ruled 6-3 that the tariffs Trump imposed using the IEEPA (International Emergency Economic Powers Act) exceeded presidential authority and were therefore illegal. Approximately 330,000 companies will share a total of $166 billion in refunds. This is the largest government refund in U.S. history.

俄烏三天停火到期，雙方互指對方違規

來源：Al Jazeera

美國幫忙談成的三天停火今天結束了，但俄國和烏克蘭都說是對方先開火。俄國說烏克蘭違規一萬六千多次；烏克蘭總統澤倫斯基說俄國根本沒在遵守。

這次停火是為了紀念俄國「勝利日」，但今年的閱兵是二十多年來最小的，可以看出戰爭對俄國軍力的影響。

The three-day ceasefire brokered by the United States came to an end today, but both Russia and Ukraine are blaming each other for breaking it first. Russia claims Ukraine violated the truce over 16,000 times, while Ukrainian President Zelensky says Russia never honored it in the first place. The ceasefire was meant to mark Russia's "Victory Day," but this year's military parade was the smallest in more than two decades, a stark illustration of the war's toll on Russian military strength.

Anthropic 砸 2000 億美元與 Google 合作建 AI 基礎設施

來源：The Information

AI 公司 Anthropic 和 Google Cloud 簽了五年合約，承諾花 2000 億美元買 Google 的晶片和雲端服務。這是科技史上最大的雲端合約之一。

光是 2026 年，Anthropic 的伺服器花費就要 200 億美元。這筆錢佔 Google 雲端訂單的四成以上，可見 AI 競爭已經變成燒錢大戰。

AI company Anthropic and Google Cloud have signed a five-year contract committing $200 billion to purchase Google's chips and cloud services. It's one of the largest cloud deals in tech history. In 2026 alone, Anthropic's server spending will reach $20 billion. This accounts for over 40 percent of Google Cloud's orders, showing how the AI competition has become an all-out spending war.

熊隊球場法案過了眾議院，但球隊說不夠

來源：CBS Chicago

芝加哥熊隊想蓋一座新球場，但到底蓋在哪裡還沒定。伊利諾州眾議院以 78 比 32 票通過了一個大型建設法案，希望把球隊留在阿靈頓高地。但熊隊說這個方案還不夠好。

另一邊，印第安納州的哈蒙德市願意用超過十億美元的公共資金幫熊隊蓋新球場。伊利諾州參議院必須在五月底前做出決定，不然熊隊可能真的要搬家了。

The Chicago Bears want to build a new stadium, but where it will be located remains undecided. The Illinois House of Representatives passed a major construction bill 78-32, hoping to keep the team in Arlington Heights. However, the Bears say the proposal isn't good enough. Meanwhile, the city of Hammond, Indiana is willing to use over a billion dollars in public funds to help the Bears build a new stadium. The Illinois Senate must make a decision by the end of May, or the Bears may actually relocate.

小熊隊十連勝被中斷，今晚挑戰亞特蘭大

來源：ESPN

芝加哥小熊隊本季第二次十連勝被德州遊騎兵隊終結了。遊騎兵連續兩場把小熊完封（讓對手零分），比數分別是 3-0 和 6-0。

小熊目前戰績 27 勝 12 敗，是自 1955 年布魯克林道奇隊以來，第一支在開季四十場內拿下兩次十連勝的球隊。今晚他們到亞特蘭大打客場，看能不能重新找回火力。

The Chicago Cubs' second ten-game winning streak of the season was snapped by the Texas Rangers. The Rangers shut out the Cubs in back-to-back games, winning 3-0 and 6-0. The Cubs now stand at 27 wins and 12 losses, making them the first team since the Brooklyn Dodgers in 1955 to record two separate ten-game winning streaks within their first forty games of the season. Tonight they head to Atlanta for a road game, hoping to reignite their offense.

兵工廠客場 1-0 勝西漢姆，再贏兩場就奪冠

來源：ESPN

兵工廠線——Trossard 在第 83 分鐘接到 Odegaard 的傳球，射入致勝球，兵工廠在客場 1-0 擊敗西漢姆。最刺激的是第 95 分鐘，西漢姆的 Callum Wilson 踢進了追平球，但VAR（影片輔助裁判）判定犯規，進球不算。

兵工廠現在領先第二名五分，只剩兩場比賽。再贏兩場，就能拿到自 2004 年以來的第一座英超冠軍。

Arsenal's Trossard scored in the 83rd minute to secure a 1-0 away victory over West Ham. The drama peaked in the 95th minute when West Ham's Callum Wilson appeared to level the score, but VAR ruled it a foul and disallowed the goal. Arsenal now leads second place by five points with just two matches remaining. Two more wins will bring them their first Premier League title since 2004.
