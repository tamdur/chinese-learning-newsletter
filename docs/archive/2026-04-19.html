伊朗再次關閉荷莫茲海峽，向船隻開火

來源：PBS

伊朗在4月18日再次關閉荷莫茲海峽（全球最重要的石油運輸通道），並向經過的油輪開火。就在前一天，伊朗才剛短暫重新開放海峽，讓油價跌了9%。

這次事件中，兩艘印度商船被打中，印度已召見伊朗大使抗議。美國海軍封鎖仍在進行，離4月21日的停火期限只剩三天，局勢越來越緊張。

Iran closed the Strait of Hormuz again on April 18th—the world's most critical oil shipping channel—and opened fire on passing tankers. Just a day earlier, Iran had briefly reopened the strait, which caused oil prices to plummet 9%. In this incident, two Indian merchant vessels were hit, prompting India to summon Iran's ambassador in protest. The US naval blockade continues, with only three days remaining until the April 21st ceasefire deadline, as tensions escalate.

However, Anthropic has acknowledged that this model does not yet match their unreleased Mythos model.

AI 開店做生意：三年租約，目標賺錢

來源：Hacker News

舊金山一家新創公司 Andon Labs 簽了一份三年的店面租約，給了一個叫 Luna 的 AI 十萬美元和完全的自主權，唯一的要求就是賺錢。

Luna 自己決定開精品店，選商品、定價格、用 Zoom 面試員工，還請畫家在牆上畫商標。不過也出了不少錯，例如在 TaskRabbit 上不小心選了阿富汗當地點。

A San Francisco startup called Andon Labs signed a three-year storefront lease and gave an AI named Luna $100,000 and complete autonomy with a single mandate: make money. Luna decided on its own to open a boutique, selecting merchandise, setting prices, conducting employee interviews over Zoom, and even hiring a painter to design a logo on the wall. But it made plenty of mistakes along the way—like accidentally selecting Afghanistan as a location on TaskRabbit.

研究：出生公民權讓移民青少年犯罪減少七成

來源：Marginal Revolution

一篇新的研究報告發現，移民家庭的小孩如果出生就拿到公民身分，長大後犯罪的比例會少70%。這份研究用的是德國的資料：2000年以後出生的移民小孩自動獲得德國國籍。

經濟學家 Tyler Cowen 指出，這對美國正在討論的出生公民權政策很有參考價值。

A new research report finds that children born to immigrant families who receive citizenship at birth are 70% less likely to commit crimes as adults. The study uses data from Germany, where children born to immigrants after 2000 automatically acquired German citizenship. Economist Tyler Cowen notes that this has significant implications for the birthright citizenship policy currently under debate in the United States.

小熊最近三場總共得了33分，戰績回升到10勝9敗，開季低迷的氣氛總算一掃而空。

The Chicago Cubs dominated the New York Mets 12-4 to capture their third straight win. Ballesteros blasted a three-run homer, while Hoerner and Happ each added solo shots as the lineup exploded offensively. Over their last three games, the Cubs have scored 33 runs total, lifting their record to 10-9 and finally dispelling the gloom that hung over the early season.

兵工廠客場對決曼城，冠軍近在眼前

來源：Al Jazeera / ESPN

兵工廠線——兵工廠今天前往曼城的伊蒂哈德球場，帶著70分領先曼城的64分。如果贏球，英超冠軍幾乎就確定了，這會是自2004年以來的第一次。

不過挑戰不小：Saka、Merino 和 奧丹加（Ødegaard）都因傷缺席，而曼城最近九場不敗。幾天前兵工廠剛打進歐冠四強，正在挑戰聯賽加歐冠的雙冠王。

Arsenal travel to Manchester City's Etihad Stadium today, carrying a 70-point lead over City's 64 points. A win would virtually seal the Premier League title — the club's first since 2004. But the challenge is formidable: Saka, Merino, and Ødegaard are all sidelined by injury, while City are unbeaten in their last nine matches. Arsenal reached the Champions League semi-finals just days ago and are chasing a domestic-European double.
