Anthropic上市估值可能降到1.5兆美元

來源：Reuters

AI公司Anthropic準備在美國的Nasdaq股市上市（把公司的股票賣給大家）。路透社從四位知道內情的人那裡得知，想買股票的投資人，正把公司的估值（公司值多少錢）從2兆美元壓低到大約1.5兆美元。

原因是Anthropic第三季的收入成長變慢了。公司在10月26日送出上市文件，向投資人介紹公司的說明會，預計11月中開始。

這是第一家上市的頂尖AI實驗室。以前價錢由少數大投資人決定，現在要由股市裡的買家說了算。

AI company Anthropic is preparing to list its shares on the US Nasdaq stock exchange. Reuters has learned from four people familiar with the matter that investors looking to buy in are pushing the company's valuation down from about $2 trillion to roughly $1.5 trillion.

The reason is that Anthropic's revenue growth slowed in the third quarter. On October 26, the company filed its listing documents, and it plans to begin its investor presentations, known as a roadshow, in mid-November.

This is the first leading AI lab to go public. Until now, its price was set by a small number of large investors. Going forward, the price will be decided by buyers on the stock market.

英國央行今天決定利率，市場預料升息

來源：Bloomberg／FXStreet

英國的中央銀行「英格蘭銀行」今天中午（芝加哥早上6點）宣布新的利率。市場普遍認為，利率會從3.75%升到4%。

如果真的升息，這會是2023年8月以來第一次。原因是伊朗戰爭讓油價大漲，英國物價漲了大約3.5%，比2%的目標高很多。

九月開會時，九位委員裡已經有三位想升息。這次大家也會看投票的人數怎麼分。

The Bank of England announces its interest rate decision today at noon London time (6 a.m. in Chicago). Markets widely expect the rate to rise from 3.75% to 4%.

If the hike happens, it would be the first increase since August 2023. The cause is the Iran war, which has sent oil prices soaring and pushed UK inflation up to about 3.5%, well above the bank's 2% target.

At the September meeting, three of the nine policymakers already wanted to raise rates. This time, people will also be watching how the votes split.

法國五大工會今天全國大罷工

來源：Sortiraparis／Économie Matin

法國五個大工會（CFDT、CGT、FO、Unsa、FSU）今天一起發起全國罷工（停止工作來抗議）。他們要求提高薪水和退休金，也反對政府的新預算少花540億歐元。

法國鐵路公司SNCF的四個工會都參加了，火車、市區交通和機場都可能大亂。

工會說，油錢、吃的和房租都在漲，薪水卻跟不上。上個月17日的抗議，就有55萬人走上街頭。

Five of France's major labor unions (CFDT, CGT, FO, Unsa, and FSU) have today launched a nationwide strike, a work stoppage to protest. They are demanding higher wages and better pensions, and they oppose the government's new budget, which cuts spending by 54 billion euros.

All four unions at the French national railway, SNCF, are taking part, so trains, city transit, and airports could face serious disruption.

The unions say that the prices of fuel, food, and rent keep rising, while wages have not kept up. At the protest last month on the 17th, 550,000 people took to the streets.

密西根換教練又換人，週五再戰UConn

來源：MGoBlue.com／Athlon Sports

密西根大學男子籃球隊今年四月以69比63打敗UConn，拿下全國冠軍。這週五（11月6日），兩隊要在波士頓的TD Garden再打一次。

不過這場「重賽」只有名字一樣。教練Dusty May去了NBA的達拉斯獨行俠隊，由助理教練Mike Boynton Jr.接手。冠軍賽的十位先發球員，只剩三位會上場；密西根只留下控球後衛Elliot Cadeau。

大學球員轉學很容易，冠軍隊幾個月就換了一批人。新球隊週一已經打敗Oakland，拿下第一勝。

Michigan's men's basketball team beat UConn 69–63 in April to win the national championship. This Friday (November 6), the two teams will meet again at the TD Garden in Boston.

But this "rematch" shares only its name with the original. Coach Dusty May has left for the NBA's Dallas Mavericks, and assistant coach Mike Boynton Jr. has taken over. Of the ten starters from the title game, only three will still be on the court. Michigan has kept just point guard Elliot Cadeau.

Transfers are easy in college, and a champion can turn over much of its roster within a few months. The new squad already beat Oakland on Monday for its first win.

兵工廠客場2比1險勝布拉格斯拉維亞

來源：Arsenal.com／Sky Sports

兵工廠線——兵工廠週三在歐洲冠軍聯賽到捷克作客，以2比1打敗布拉格斯拉維亞。Noni Madueke第9分鐘先進球；Viktor Gyökeres第38分鐘和Mikel Merino互相傳球後，從很小的角度射進第二球。

下半場兵工廠打得不好，對手在第90分鐘追回一球。教練Arteta說，球隊沒有打出「平常的水準」。後衛Ezri Konsa在第60分鐘左右因為身體有狀況被換下場。

四場比賽後，兵工廠拿到10分，排名第三。

Arsenal won 2-1 away to Slavia Praha in the Champions League on Wednesday. Noni Madueke scored in the 9th minute. In the 38th minute, Viktor Gyökeres scored the second goal from a very tight angle after a one-two with Mikel Merino.

Arsenal played poorly in the second half, and the hosts pulled a goal back in the 90th minute. Manager Arteta said the team did not play at its "usual level." Defender Ezri Konsa was substituted off around the 60th minute because of a physical problem.

After four matches, Arsenal have 10 points and sit third in the table.
