美國六月只增五萬七千個工作 遠低於預期

來源：CNBC / BLS

美國政府在七月二日公布，六月只增加了五萬七千個工作，大約只有專家預測的一半。四月和五月的數字也被往下修改。

失業率降到百分之四點二。奇怪的是，股市反而上漲，因為投資人覺得工作變少，中央銀行就不會急著升息。目前利率停在百分之三點五到三點七五。

On July 2, the US government reported that June added only 57,000 jobs—roughly half what experts had predicted. The April and May figures were also revised downward. The unemployment rate declined to 4.2%. Strangely, the stock market rose on the news, as investors reasoned that fewer jobs would discourage the Federal Reserve from hastily raising interest rates. Current interest rates remain between 3.5% and 3.75%.

美國移民局五天逮捕一萬人

來源：Associated Press

美國移民局（負責抓沒有合法身分的移民）在五天內抓了超過一萬人，平均一天兩千多人，比去年十二月一天約一千三百人高出許多。

官員希望把這個速度變成「新常態」，但改用比較低調的方式，不再像之前那樣大陣仗。六月被關進拘留所的人也增加到大約三萬九千人。

U.S. immigration authorities arrested over 10,000 people in five days, averaging more than 2,000 arrests per day—a dramatic increase from roughly 1,300 per day in December of last year.

Officials hope to make this pace the "new normal," but plan to pursue it with a quieter approach, moving away from the large-scale operations seen previously.

The number of people detained in June also rose to approximately 39,000.

Meta 要賣多餘的 AI 運算能力

來源：Bloomberg

臉書母公司 Meta 宣布成立新事業「Meta Compute」，要把自己多出來的 AI 資料中心能力賣給別的公司。這樣它就從「買運算能力」變成「賣運算能力」，直接和亞馬遜、微軟、谷歌的雲端服務競爭。

Meta 今年蓋資料中心的預算高達一千兩百五十億到一千四百五十億美元。消息一出，股價上漲超過百分之七。

Meta, Facebook's parent company, has announced a new business division called "Meta Compute" that will sell its excess AI data center capacity to other companies. This marks a shift for Meta from being a buyer of computing power to a seller, putting it in direct competition with Amazon, Microsoft, and Google's cloud services. Meta's budget for building data centers this year reaches between $125 billion and $145 billion. Following the announcement, the stock price climbed more than 7 percent.

科學家首次造出會生長分裂的人造細胞

來源：Quanta Magazine

美國明尼蘇達大學的團隊，第一次用完全不是活的化學材料，做出一個會生長、會複製 DNA、還會分裂的人造細胞，取名「SpudCell」。生長和分裂正是生命繁殖的兩大特徵。

不過它還不算真正的生命，必須一直餵它養分才能活，而且只能複製大約五代。這項研究由亞達瑪拉博士帶領。

Researchers at the University of Minnesota have for the first time created an artificial cell from entirely non-living chemical materials that can grow, replicate DNA, and divide. They named it 'SpudCell.' While growth and division are the two defining characteristics of biological reproduction, this creation does not yet constitute true life. It requires continuous feeding with nutrients to survive and can only replicate for approximately five generations. The research was led by Dr. Adamala.

武切維奇離開公牛 重返魔術隊

來源：Hoops Rumors

資深中鋒武切維奇決定離開芝加哥公牛隊，回到奧蘭多魔術隊，簽下一年三百九十萬美元的合約。他說自己在奧蘭多有房子，就算別隊出更多錢，他還是想回去。

他曾經在公牛隊打了六個賽季，上一季被交易到塞爾提克。這次離開，代表公牛正式開始重建球隊。

Veteran center Vucevic has decided to leave the Chicago Bulls and return to the Orlando Magic on a one-year contract worth $3.9 million. He stated that he owns a home in Orlando and preferred to return there regardless of how much money other teams might have offered. After playing six seasons with the Bulls, he was traded to the Celtics last season. This departure signals that the Bulls are formally beginning a team rebuild.

阿森納積極追求尤文圖斯前鋒伊爾迪茲

來源：FootballTransfers

兵工廠線——阿森納（兵工廠隊）看上尤文圖斯的年輕前鋒伊爾迪茲，據說已經和對方接觸。這名球員才二十一歲，在土耳其被世界盃淘汰後成為目標。

不過尤文圖斯開價超過一億歐元，而且不太想賣，因為他的合約簽到二○三○年。阿森納想加強左邊的攻擊，也在留意其他人選。

The Arsenal beat—Arsenal has set their sights on Juventus's young striker Yildiz, reportedly already in contact with the club. The 21-year-old became a target after Turkey's World Cup elimination. However, Juventus are holding firm at over 100 million euros and are reluctant to sell, as his contract runs through 2030. Arsenal are looking to strengthen their left-wing attack and are also monitoring other candidates.
