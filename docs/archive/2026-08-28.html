輝達傳出要用一百二十九億美元買下 Hugging Face

來源：CNBC / Bloomberg

晶片公司輝達（Nvidia）傳出快談成一筆大生意：用大約一百二十九億美元，買下人工智慧平台 Hugging Face。這會是輝達史上最大的一次收購。

Hugging Face 像是人工智慧界的公共圖書館，放了大約兩百五十萬個模型，有一千三百萬名開發者在用，一直被當成中立的地方。

現在賣晶片的公司想當館長，而 OpenAI、Google 這些大客戶正在自己做晶片，想少靠輝達一點。

Chip maker Nvidia is reportedly close to sealing a major deal: acquiring the AI platform Hugging Face for approximately $12.9 billion. This would be the largest acquisition in Nvidia's history. Hugging Face functions like a public library for the AI world, hosting roughly 2.5 million models and serving 13 million developers — and it has long been regarded as neutral ground. Now the company that sells the chips wants to be the librarian, even as major customers like OpenAI and Google build their own chips to rely less on Nvidia.

美國準備對中國再加百分之七點五關稅

來源：Hoodline / Bloomberg

美國正在準備對中國商品再加百分之七點五的關稅（進口商品要繳的稅），理由是中國工廠生產太多、賣得太便宜。加上去以後，總稅率剛好到百分之二十。

這個數字是算好的。七月時美國答應中國，關稅最高不超過百分之二十；現在是百分之十二點五，剛好還差七點五。

時間點也很重要：中國國家主席習近平九月二十四日要到白宮訪問，這是十多年來第一次。

The United States is preparing to impose an additional 7.5% tariff on Chinese goods — a tariff being a tax paid on imports — citing overproduction by Chinese factories and prices that are too low. Once added, the total rate would land at exactly 20%. That figure is no accident. In July the US promised China that tariffs would go no higher than 20%; the current rate is 12.5%, leaving precisely 7.5 points of room. The timing matters too: Chinese President Xi Jinping is due at the White House on September 24th, the first such visit in more than a decade.

Anthropic 推出新標準，讓 Claude 直接操作機器人

來源：Bloomberg

人工智慧公司 Anthropic 在八月二十七日發表一套新的軟體標準，叫做「模型硬體標準」（MHS），讓 Claude 這類人工智慧可以直接和機器人、工廠機器、實驗室儀器（做實驗用的機器）溝通。

以前機器怎麼用，都寫在紙本說明書裡，或只有少數有經驗的人知道。MHS 把這些寫成電腦看得懂的資料，還能規定機器手臂最快只能轉多快，比較安全。

目前還在測試階段，Hugging Face 和樹莓派（Raspberry Pi）已經開始支援。

On August 27th the AI company Anthropic released a new software standard called the Model Hardware Standard (MHS), which lets AI systems like Claude communicate directly with robots, factory machinery and laboratory instruments. Until now, how to operate a machine lived in a paper manual, or only in the heads of a few experienced operators. MHS turns that knowledge into data a computer can read, and it can also set safety limits — for example, capping how fast a robotic arm is allowed to move. The standard is still in testing, with Hugging Face and Raspberry Pi already supporting it.

蘇丹快速支援部隊攻下邊界城鎮，戰火往兩邊擴大

來源：Sudan Tribune

蘇丹（非洲東北部的國家）的內戰又有新變化。八月二十七日，政府軍在青尼羅省的凱利鎮擋下「快速支援部隊」的攻擊。幾天前，這支武裝部隊才剛拿下靠近衣索比亞邊界的兩座城鎮：蓋桑和庫爾穆克。

國際移民組織說，已經有將近一萬四千人從這兩座城逃走。

拿下邊界，等於多了一條對外的補給路線；同時他們還在圍攻歐貝德市，那座城如果失守，通往首都喀土穆的路就開了。

Sudan's civil war has taken a new turn. On August 27th, government forces repelled an attack by the Rapid Support Forces on the town of Al-Keli in Blue Nile state. Only days earlier, the paramilitary group had captured two towns near the Ethiopian border: Geissan and Kurmuk. The International Organization for Migration says nearly 14,000 people have fled those two towns. Taking the border gives the RSF another supply route to the outside world; at the same time they are besieging El-Obeid, a city whose fall would open the road to the capital, Khartoum.

熊隊三十一日要裁掉三十八人，後防最讓人擔心

來源：Chicago Sun-Times

芝加哥熊隊要在八月三十一日星期天下午六點前，把球員名單從九十一人減到五十三人，等於要送走三十八個人。

最麻煩的是後防。安全衛布萊恩八月七日開刀，膝蓋要養四到六個月；角衛戈登小腿受傷，還在傷兵名單上。星期三和泰坦隊一起練球時，兩名先發角衛又都下場。

跑衛和接球員的最後幾個位置也還沒定，星期六還有一場季前賽要打。

The Chicago Bears must cut their roster from 91 players down to 53 by 6 p.m. on Sunday, August 31st — 38 players sent away. The biggest problem is the secondary. Safety Coby Bryant had surgery on August 7th and needs four to six months for his knee; cornerback Kyler Gordon has a calf injury and remains on the physically-unable-to-perform list. At Wednesday's joint practice with the Titans, both starting cornerbacks went down as well. The last few spots at running back and receiver are still unsettled, and there is one more preseason game to play on Saturday.

歐冠抽籤出爐，兵工廠八個對手全定了

來源：Arsenal.com

兵工廠線——二○二六到二七球季歐洲冠軍聯賽的聯賽階段抽籤已經完成，兵工廠（阿森納）的八個對手是：皇家馬德里、拜仁慕尼黑、多特蒙德、皇家貝提斯、里爾、拿坡里、薩巴赫和布拉格斯拉維亞。

皇家馬德里會來酋長球場，兵工廠則要飛去慕尼黑、塞維亞、拿坡里和布拉格。

聯賽階段從九月八日打到明年一月底。麻煩的是，轉會市場九月二日就關門，這支球隊的前鋒人數比暑假開始時還少。

The Arsenal beat — the league-phase draw for the 2026-27 Champions League is complete, and Arsenal's eight opponents are Real Madrid, Bayern Munich, Borussia Dortmund, Real Betis, Lille, Napoli, Sabah and Slavia Prague. Real Madrid come to the Emirates, while Arsenal must travel to Munich, Seville, Naples and Prague. The league phase runs from September 8th to the end of January. The complication: the transfer window shuts on September 2nd, and this squad has fewer forwards than it had at the start of the summer.
