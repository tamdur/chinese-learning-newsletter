AI公司推出新模型Opus 5 價格只要一半

來源：CNBC

人工智慧公司Anthropic在七月二十四日推出新的AI模型「Claude Opus 5」。這是他們兩個月內第四次發表新模型，速度很快。

公司說，這個新模型的能力幾乎跟最強的「Fable 5」一樣好，可是價格只要一半。它還多了一個「用力」開關，讓使用者自己選要回答快一點，還是想得更深。這讓各家AI公司的競爭變得更激烈。

Artificial intelligence company Anthropic launched a new AI model called 'Claude Opus 5' on July 24th. This is their fourth new model release within two months—a rapid pace. The company states that the new model's capabilities are nearly as strong as their most powerful 'Fable 5,' but at half the price. It also adds a new 'effort' toggle that lets users choose whether they want faster responses or deeper thinking. This intensifies competition among AI companies.

美國眾議院通過禁止議員炒股法案

來源：GovTrack

美國眾議院在七月二十四日，以二百三十二票對一百九十八票，通過「禁止內線交易法」，不准國會議員買賣個別公司的股票。這是很多人盼了很久的改革。

不過共和黨也在法案裡加了一條有爭議的規定：投票時要出示有照片的身分證。民主黨很不滿。表決完，眾議院就休會到八月三十一日。

On July 24th, the U.S. House of Representatives passed the "Ban on Congressional Stock Trading Act" by a vote of 232 to 198, prohibiting members of Congress from buying and selling shares of individual companies. This is a reform many have long awaited. However, Republicans also added a controversial provision to the bill: requiring voters to show photo identification when voting. Democrats were not pleased. After the vote, the House adjourned until August 31st.

俄羅斯飛彈打中基輔軍武展 至少十人死亡

來源：Kyiv Independent

七月二十四日白天，俄羅斯發射彈道飛彈，打中烏克蘭首都基輔附近的一場國防武器展覽，造成至少十人死亡、大約一百人受傷。

這種白天的攻擊很少見。俄羅斯說是故意打的，因為展覽上有拿來攻擊俄國的無人機。烏克蘭已經開始調查這起事件。

Russian ballistic missiles struck a defense weapons exhibition near Kyiv on the afternoon of July 24th, killing at least ten people and injuring approximately one hundred. Such daytime attacks are uncommon. Russia claimed the strike was intentional, citing the presence of drones at the exhibition that are used to attack Russian territory. Ukraine has launched an investigation into the incident.

芝加哥七家平價超市 週六恐關門

來源：Block Club Chicago

芝加哥南區和西區的七家「Save A Lot」平價超市，可能在七月二十五日（週六）關門。經營的公司Yellow Banana說，因為政府減少了食物補助，店裡的生意掉了大約兩成七。

這些店本來是要幫助買菜不方便的社區。三年前，市政府還出了一千三百五十萬美元幫忙開店。

Seven Save A Lot budget supermarkets in Chicago's South and West sides may close on Saturday, July 25th. The operating company, Yellow Banana, says business has plummeted by approximately 27 percent as a result of government cuts to food assistance. The stores were originally intended to serve neighborhoods with limited grocery access. Three years ago, the city government invested $13.5 million to help open them.

小熊隊克羅阿姆斯壯 締造美技接殺紀錄

來源：MLB.com

芝加哥小熊隊的中外野手克羅阿姆斯壯（Pete Crow-Armstrong），打破了大聯盟「五星級接殺」（最難接的球）的紀錄。

他生涯已經完成三十三次，超越了前紀錄保持人基爾梅爾的三十二次。二十四歲的他，只打了大約兩個半球季就做到了。小熊隊目前在國聯外卡排名領先。

Chicago Cubs center fielder Pete Crow-Armstrong has broken a Major League Baseball record for five-star catches—Statcast's designation for the most difficult defensive plays. He has completed 33 of these spectacular catches in his career, surpassing the previous record holder's 32. At just 24 years old, Crow-Armstrong achieved this milestone in only about two and a half seasons. The Cubs are currently leading the National League wild card standings.

兵工廠快簽下布魯諾 卻錯過羅傑斯

來源：FootballTransfers

兵工廠線——阿森納（兵工廠）快要簽下紐卡索的隊長布魯諾（Bruno Guimaraes）了。之前紐卡索連五千五百萬和六千五百萬英鎊的報價都拒絕，現在雙方終於快談成。

不過同一天，阿森納想要的另一名球員羅傑斯（Morgan Rogers）卻選擇加入切爾西。另外，從布魯日隊簽來的措里斯也快完成了，轉會費大約三千四百萬英鎊。

Arsenal is on the verge of signing Newcastle captain Bruno Guimaraes. Newcastle had previously rejected offers of £55 million and £65 million, but now the two sides are finally close to reaching a deal. However, on the same day, Morgan Rogers, another player Arsenal had targeted, instead chose to join Chelsea. Additionally, Christos Tzolis, signing from Club Brugge, is also nearing completion of his transfer, with a fee of approximately £34 million.
