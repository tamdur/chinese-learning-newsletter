密西根大學籃球隊大勝晉級十六強

來源：MGoBlog

美國大學籃球比賽中，一號種子隊密西根以 95 比 72 大勝聖路易斯，成功進入十六強。球隊今年戰績 33 勝 3 敗，非常強。

隊員 Lendeborg 拿下 25 分，他的灌籃非常精彩。七呎三吋的中鋒 Mara 也有 16 分、5 個籃板和 4 次火鍋，差一點就打破學校的火鍋紀錄。結果對手教練也說：「他們太強了，誰打都很難贏。」下一場在星期五，地點在芝加哥。

In the NCAA tournament, the No. 1 seed Michigan Wolverines dominated Saint Louis 95–72 to advance to the Round of 16. The team has an impressive 33–3 record this season.

Guard Lendeborg led the way with 25 points, delivering several highlight-reel dunks. Center Mara, who stands 7'3", contributed 16 points, 5 rebounds, and 4 blocks—nearly breaking the school's single-game block record. Even the opposing coach acknowledged the gap: "They're just too strong. It's tough for anyone to beat them." Michigan's next game is Friday in Chicago.

美伊開戰三週，油價還會漲多少？

來源：Marginal Revolution

美國和伊朗開戰三週了，石油市場受到很大的影響。美軍空軍炸了伊朗最大的石油出口站，那裡每天送出一百五十萬桶石油。伊朗也關了荷莫茲海峽（全世界兩成石油要從那裡過），結果油輪少了七成。

國際油價已經漲了四成，到每桶一百多美元，是四年來最高。如果四週內達成協議，油價可能回到七十美元。但如果繼續打下去，可能漲到一百三到兩百美元，全世界經濟也會出問題。

The United States and Iran have been at war for three weeks now, and the oil market has taken a serious hit. The US Air Force bombed Iran's largest oil export terminal, which normally ships out 1.5 million barrels per day. Iran has also closed the Strait of Hormuz — through which 20% of the world's oil passes — resulting in a 70% drop in tanker traffic.

International oil prices have already jumped 40%, reaching over $100 per barrel — the highest level in four years. If a deal is reached within four weeks, oil could fall back to $70. But if the fighting continues, prices could spike to $130–$200 per barrel, which would create serious problems for the global economy.

白宮公布 AI 法律方向

來源：White House / CBS News

美國白宮在三月二十日公布了一份四頁的 AI 法律計畫，給國會參考。重點有七個，包括小孩安全、網路上的問題、工作影響和各州的法律。白宮不想設立新的管理單位，也不想管太多。

最大的爭議是：白宮希望各州不能自己管 AI 工廠（開發公司），也不想讓開發人員因為別人亂用而被罰。科技公司很高興，但批評的人說結果就是沒人管。有一位參議員已經寫了另一個更嚴格的法案。

The White House released a four-page AI legal framework on March 20th for Congress to consider. The plan has seven key points, including child safety, online harms, job impacts, and state-level regulations. The White House doesn't want to create new regulatory agencies and prefers a light-touch approach.

The biggest controversy: the White House wants to prevent states from regulating AI developers independently, and doesn't want developers to be penalized for how others misuse their technology. Tech companies are pleased, but critics argue this means no one is actually in charge. One senator has already drafted a stricter alternative bill.

馬斯克被判誤導推特投資人

來源：BBC

三月二十日，舊金山的九位陪審員認定馬斯克在買推特的時候，故意在網站上發了兩則不實的消息，讓股價下跌。他在 2022 年答應用 440 億美元買推特，後來又想不買了，結果最後還是按原價買下。

不過陪審團也認為他不是有計畫的詐騙。估計要賠大約二十一到二十六億美元。馬斯克的團隊說會上訴，他目前身價大約八千一百億美元，所以這筆錢對他來說也不算多。

On March 20th, nine jurors in San Francisco found that Musk intentionally posted two false messages on the website when buying Twitter, causing the stock price to fall. He promised to buy Twitter for $44 billion in 2022, but later wanted to back out. In the end, he still bought it at the original price.

However, the jury also determined that he was not engaged in planned fraud. He is estimated to owe approximately $2.1 to $2.6 billion in damages. Musk's team says they will appeal. His current net worth is approximately $810 billion, so this sum is not a significant amount for him.

一億四千九百萬年前的鳥改寫飛行歷史

來源：Smithsonian Magazine

中國的科學家在福建省找到了一種很小的古代鳥，大約跟鵪鶉（一種小鳥）一樣大，活在一億四千九百萬年前的恐龍時代。研究結果發在《自然》雜誌上。

這隻鳥最特別的地方是：牠有短尾巴，不像恐龍那樣的長尾巴。短尾巴讓鳥飛得更穩。以前我們以為最早的短尾鳥是一億三千萬年前的，現在往前推了兩千萬年。同一個地點也找到了另外兩隻鳥的化石，說明那時候的鳥種類比我們想的還多。

Chinese scientists discovered a tiny ancient bird in Fujian Province about 149 million years ago during the age of dinosaurs. The bird was roughly the size of a quail. The findings were published in the journal Nature.

The most remarkable feature of this bird was its short tail, unlike the long tails that dinosaurs had. The short tail allowed the bird to fly more steadily. We previously thought the earliest short-tailed birds appeared 130 million years ago, but now we've pushed that date back by 20 million years. Two other bird fossils were also found at the same location, showing that bird species were more diverse than we had thought at that time.
