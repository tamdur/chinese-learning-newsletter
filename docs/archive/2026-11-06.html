英國央行升息到4%

來源：Bank of England

英國的中央銀行「英格蘭銀行」11月5日宣布，把利率從3.75%調高到4%。這是它2023年以來第一次升息。

九位委員中有八位贊成，還有一位想升得更多。原因是中東戰爭讓油價和電費上漲，東西跟著變貴。英格蘭銀行估計，明年年中物價漲幅可能接近5%。

升息會讓借錢買房子變貴，但可以幫忙壓住物價。

The Bank of England, Britain's central bank, announced on November 5 that it is raising its benchmark interest rate from 3.75% to 4%. It is the first rate increase since 2023. Eight of the nine members of its policy committee voted for the hike, and one wanted to raise rates even further. The reason is that the Middle East war has pushed up oil and electricity prices, making goods across the board more expensive. The Bank of England estimates that inflation could approach 5% by the middle of next year. Higher interest rates will make borrowing money to buy a house more expensive, but they can help hold prices down.

密西根主場迎戰州內對手

來源：On3、Dimers、ClickOnDetroit

密西根大學美式足球隊星期六（11月7日）在主場迎戰同一州的密西根州立大學，兩隊要搶「保羅班揚獎盃」。

上週密西根以28比21險勝羅格斯大學。教練惠廷翰說，大一四分衛（指揮進攻的球員）湯米·卡爾會繼續先發。對手今年只有2勝5敗。

預測網站算出密西根贏球機會約85.6%。不過對兩邊的球迷來說，這場比賽比什麼都重要。

The University of Michigan football team hosts its in-state rival, Michigan State University, this Saturday (November 7), with the two schools competing for the Paul Bunyan Trophy. Last week Michigan narrowly beat Rutgers, 28-21. Head coach Whittingham says true freshman quarterback Tommy Carl will keep his starting job. The opponent has only two wins and five losses this year. A prediction website puts Michigan's chance of winning at about 85.6%. But for fans on both sides, this game matters more than anything else.
