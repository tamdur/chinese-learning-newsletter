密西根主場迎戰印第安納

來源：Dimers

密西根大學美式足球隊週六中午在安娜堡（Ann Arbor）主場，迎戰上一季的全國冠軍印第安納大學。

印第安納目前六勝一敗，唯一輸的一場是以15比19小輸給俄亥俄州立大學。密西根四勝兩敗，上週剛以21比18贏了賓州州立大學。

運動彩券看好客隊印第安納，預測他們大約會多贏七分。在自己家當弱隊，說明十大聯盟的強弱已經不一樣了。

The University of Michigan football team hosts Indiana University, last season's national champion, at noon Saturday in Ann Arbor. Indiana is 6-1 so far, and its only loss was a narrow 15-19 defeat to Ohio State. Michigan is 4-2 and beat Penn State 21-18 last week. Betting markets favor the visiting Hoosiers by about seven points. For Michigan to be the underdog at home says a lot about how much the Big Ten's pecking order has shifted.

薩利巴快回來了

來源：Sportsmole

兵工廠線——後衛薩利巴（Saliba）背部受傷，已經缺陣一段時間，現在終於快要回來了。

報導說，他可能在週六主場對艾佛頓（Everton）的英超比賽復出，也可能等到三天後去弗利特伍德（Fleetwood）踢聯賽盃。另外，前鋒哈弗茨（Havertz）大腿後側又受傷了。

兵工廠目前在英超排第二，週三對拜仁的歐冠比賽，薩利巴沒有上場。阿爾特塔會冒險早點用他，還是讓他慢慢來？

Arsenal beat — Defender William Saliba, who has been out for a while with a back injury, is finally close to a return. Reports say he could come back in Saturday's home Premier League match against Everton, or he may wait until three days later to play Fleetwood in the League Cup. Separately, Kai Havertz has suffered another hamstring injury. Arsenal currently sit second in the Premier League, and Saliba did not play in Wednesday's Champions League match against Bayern. Will Arteta take the risk and bring him back early, or let him ease back in slowly?
