德州參議員大戰，民主黨拚三十年來首勝

來源：Fox 4 News、Newsweek

今天是美國期中選舉的投票日。全國最受注意的一場，是德州的參議員選舉：共和黨的帕克斯頓，對上民主黨的州議員塔拉里科。

民主黨從1994年以後，就沒在德州的全州選舉贏過。但這次民調非常接近：10月30日的一份民調，帕克斯頓48.7%，塔拉里科47.6%。帕克斯頓在2023年被州議會彈劾（議會想把官員趕下台），最後過關。

共和黨現在在參議院有53席。如果丟掉德州，民主黨搶回參議院的機會就大很多。德州投票所當地晚上7點關門。

Today is Election Day for the US midterms. The race drawing the most attention nationwide is the Texas Senate contest, where Republican Paxton faces Democrat Talarico, a state legislator. Democrats have not won a statewide election in Texas since 1994, but this year the polling is extremely close. In an October 30 poll, Paxton had 48.7% and Talarico had 47.6%. Paxton was impeached by the state legislature in 2023, when lawmakers tried to remove him from office, but he ultimately survived the vote. Republicans currently hold 53 seats in the Senate. If they lose Texas, Democrats' chances of winning back the Senate improve considerably. Polling stations in Texas close at 7 p.m. local time.

芝加哥第一次全民選出教育委員會

來源：Chicago Sun-Times、WBEZ

今天，芝加哥人第一次自己選出公立學校教育委員會的全部21個位子，包括主席。從1995年起，這些人都是市長決定的。

這次有四十多位候選人。新委員明年1月上任，馬上要面對大問題：芝加哥公立學校這學年缺了大約7億3千萬美元。很多候選人說，不會把房屋稅加到最高來補洞。

這是全美第四大的學區，以後學校的錢怎麼花，要看選民今天的選擇。

Today, Chicago voters elected all 21 seats on the Board of Education for the first time ever, including the board president. Since 1995, the mayor had picked every one of these members. More than forty candidates ran for the seats. The new board members take office next January, and they will face a major problem right away: Chicago Public Schools is about $730 million short for this school year. Many candidates have said they will not raise property taxes to the maximum to fill the gap. This is the fourth-largest school district in the United States, and how its money is spent from now on depends on the choices voters made today.

司法部派千名觀察員到投票所

來源：Louisiana Illuminator、Pennsylvania Capital-Star

美國司法部說，今天會派大約1000名選舉觀察員，到全美50個州的投票所。2022年期中選舉時，只去了24個州的64個地方。

觀察員只能在旁邊看，不能插手投票。司法部不肯先公布會去哪裡，目前只知道賓州費城等4個郡會有人去。

今年秋天，聯邦政府對選舉管得越來越多。民權團體NAACP已經告上法院，要司法部交出這個計畫的資料。

The U.S. Justice Department said today it will send about 1,000 election observers to polling places in all 50 states. In the 2022 midterm elections, it sent observers to 64 locations in just 24 states. Observers may only watch from the sidelines and may not interfere with voting. The department has refused to say in advance where they will go, and so far the only known locations are four counties, including Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. This fall, the federal government is taking an ever-larger role in running elections. The civil rights group NAACP has already sued, demanding that the Justice Department hand over materials related to the plan.

芝加哥公投：要不要向聯邦討錢？

來源：NBC Chicago、WTTW

今天芝加哥的選票上有一題：市政府該不該用一切合法的方法，向聯邦政府要回一筆錢？

這跟去年秋天的「中途閃電行動」有關。聯邦政府派人到芝加哥大抓移民，市長強森說，這讓城市多花了很多錢，商店生意也變差。市議會在7月通過，把這題放上選票。

不過，這次投票沒有法律效力（投完不一定要照做），只是表達民意。也有人懷疑，城市根本沒辦法逼聯邦付錢。

Today's Chicago ballot includes a question: should the city government use every legal means available to try to get money back from the federal government? The question is tied to last autumn's "Operation Midway Blitz." When federal agents carried out large-scale immigration raids in Chicago, Mayor Johnson said the city ended up spending a great deal of money, and local shops saw their business suffer. The City Council voted in July to put the question on the ballot. However, the vote has no legal force (the city is not required to act on the result); it is simply a way of expressing public opinion. Some people also doubt that the city can actually make the federal government pay.

熊隊34比17大勝上季冠軍海鷹

來源：ESPN、chicagobears.com

芝加哥熊隊星期一晚上到西雅圖，以34比17大勝上一季的冠軍海鷹隊，戰績變成6勝2敗。

上週大家還在問：熊隊之前贏的多是弱隊，是不是真的強？這場給了答案。四分衛威廉斯傳球247碼，沒有失誤；跑衛斯威夫特跑進3次達陣。

熊隊的防守也很兇。海鷹在關鍵的第三次進攻，11次只成功1次，全場只推進252碼。

The Chicago Bears traveled to Seattle on Monday night and beat last season's champion, the Seahawks, 34 to 17, moving their record to 6 wins and 2 losses. Last week, people were still asking whether the Bears were really good, since most of their earlier wins had come against weak teams. This game gave the answer. Quarterback Caleb Williams threw for 247 yards without a turnover, and running back D'Andre Swift ran in three touchdowns. The Bears' defense was just as fierce. On third down, which was critical, Seattle converted only once in 11 tries, and for the whole game the Seahawks moved the ball just 252 yards.

沒有薩卡，兵工廠週三出戰歐冠

來源：Sports Mole、NBC Sports

兵工廠線——剛在安菲爾德1比0贏了利物浦、英超領先9分的兵工廠，馬上又要出發。星期三（11月4日），他們要到捷克，在歐洲冠軍聯賽客場挑戰布拉格斯拉維亞。

球星薩卡大腿受傷，還是不能上場；後衛薩利巴也傳出無法出賽。中場埃澤也有小傷，能不能踢還不確定。

兵工廠這季歐冠還沒輸過，斯拉維亞已經連續18場歐洲比賽沒贏。教練阿特塔要在贏球和讓主力休息之間，找到平衡。

The Arsenal beat: Arsenal, who just beat Liverpool 1-0 at Anfield and lead the Premier League by nine points, are off again. On Wednesday (November 4), they travel to the Czech Republic to take on Slavia Prague in the Champions League. Star winger Saka is still out with a thigh injury, and defender Saliba has also been reported unavailable. Midfielder Eze has a minor knock too, and it is not yet clear whether he will be able to play. Arsenal have not lost a Champions League match this season, while Slavia have not won in 18 consecutive European matches. Manager Arteta has to find a balance between winning the game and resting his key players.
