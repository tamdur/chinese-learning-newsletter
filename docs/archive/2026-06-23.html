川普宣布以色列伊朗停火 伊朗答應讓核子檢查員回國

來源：NPR

美國總統川普六月二十三日宣布，以色列和伊朗會在隔天開始停火，結束打了好幾個月的戰爭。這場仗從今年年初打到現在，連帶讓全世界的油價跳上跳下。

在瑞士談了好久之後，伊朗答應讓國際原子能總署（負責檢查核子設施的機構）的檢查員重新進到國內看一看。不過像荷莫茲海峽要怎麼通行、伊朗的核子計畫以後怎麼辦，這些事都還沒講清楚。

President Trump announced on June 23rd that Israel and Iran will begin a ceasefire the following day, ending a war that has raged for several months. The conflict, which has stretched from early this year to the present, has caused global oil prices to swing wildly. After lengthy negotiations in Switzerland, Iran has agreed to allow inspectors from the International Atomic Energy Agency—the organization responsible for monitoring nuclear facilities—to return to the country for inspections. However, key questions remain unresolved, including how shipping through the Strait of Hormuz will be managed and what Iran's nuclear program will look like going forward.

Google 新模型Gemini 3.5 Pro登場 一次能讀兩百萬字

來源：TechTimes

Google這個禮拜推出最新的人工智慧模型Gemini 3.5 Pro。它最厲害的地方，是一次可以讀進兩百萬個token（差不多等於很長很長的一本書），是目前正式上線的模型裡最多的。

它還有一個叫Deep Think的功能，會花更多時間慢慢想難題。每個月付二十美元可以用大容量，想用會深思的模式，得付到兩百五十美元。這也讓六月變成各家比拼新模型的熱鬧月份。

Google unveiled its latest artificial intelligence model, Gemini 3.5 Pro, this week. Its standout feature is the ability to process up to two million tokens at once — roughly equivalent to a very lengthy book — making it the largest context window among models currently in public release. The model also includes a feature called Deep Think, which devotes extra processing time to carefully reasoning through difficult problems. A monthly subscription of twenty dollars grants access to the expanded capacity, while the deep reasoning mode requires a subscription tier of two hundred fifty dollars per month. This milestone has made June an especially competitive month for AI companies launching new models.

英國首相施凱爾辭職 工黨要改選新黨魁

來源：NPR / Al Jazeera

英國首相施凱爾六月二十二日宣布辭職，距離工黨兩年前大勝、上台執政還不到兩年。會走到這一步，是因為極右派的「改革黨」快速壯大，加上地方選舉成績很差。

工黨會重新選出黨魁，七月九日開始登記，預計九月國會開議前就會有新領袖。大家普遍看好曼徹斯特市長伯納姆出線。如果是他，英國十年內就換了七個首相，看得出這個國家政治有多不穩。

UK Prime Minister Starmer announced his resignation on June 22nd, less than two years after Labour's landslide victory and taking office. The decision came amid rapid growth of the far-right Reform Party and Labour's poor performance in local elections. The party will select a new leader, with registration opening July 9th, and a successor expected before Parliament reconvenes in September. Manchester Mayor Burnham is widely favored to win the contest. If he does, Britain will have had seven prime ministers in a decade — a stark measure of the country's political instability.

美國最高法院不受理 七州人民不能自己提告護投票權

來源：NPR

美國最高法院六月二十二日決定不重新審理一個案子，等於讓下級法院的判決留著。這個判決說，在七個州裡，一般人民和團體不能自己提告，去執行「選舉權法案」。

受影響的七個州包括阿肯色、愛荷華、明尼蘇達等。過去人民自己提告，一直是保護投票權最主要的方法。現在這條路被擋住，又剛好碰上期中選舉要到了，影響特別大。

On June 22, the U.S. Supreme Court declined to review a case, effectively allowing a lower court ruling to stand. That ruling determined that in seven states, ordinary citizens and advocacy groups cannot file their own lawsuits to enforce the Voting Rights Act. The seven affected states include Arkansas, Iowa, and Minnesota. Historically, private citizens filing lawsuits has been the primary mechanism for protecting voting rights. Now that avenue is blocked, and the timing is particularly consequential as midterm elections approach.

小熊隊克羅阿姆斯壯六月火燙 打進國聯MVP話題

來源：Da Windy City / Yahoo Sports

芝加哥小熊隊的中外野手克羅阿姆斯壯（Pete Crow-Armstrong）這個六月打得超級燙。他的打擊率高達四成二九，全壘打九支，這兩項都是大聯盟第一，這個禮拜還完成了「完全打擊」。

他才二十四歲，不只打擊好，守備也是全聯盟最頂尖的。他的整體貢獻值排大聯盟第三，只輸給大谷翔平，讓他成了今年國聯最有價值球員（MVP）的熱門人選。

Pete Crow-Armstrong, the Chicago Cubs' center fielder, has been on fire this June. His batting average stands at .429, and he's hit 9 home runs — both leading Major League Baseball. He also completed the cycle this week. At just 24 years old, he's not only excelling at the plate but also displaying elite defense across the league. His overall contribution ranks third in MLB, behind only Shohei Ohtani, making him a frontrunner for the National League MVP award this year.

兵工廠線：阿森納搶下16歲新星 今夏第二筆簽約

來源：Sky Sports

兵工廠線——兵工廠（阿森納）打敗曼聯和曼城，搶下年僅十六歲的小將蒙加（Jeremy Monga）。這位左路小將上季已經幫萊斯特城上場三十次，還曾在十五歲就踢英超，是大家搶著要的新星。

這是兵工廠今夏的第二筆簽約。球隊還在追一名身價大約五千萬英鎊的中場球員，由教練阿爾特塔親自出馬談。趁著薩卡等人去踢世界盃，兵工廠出手又快又果決。

Arsenal beat Manchester United and Manchester City to land 16-year-old left-winger Jeremy Monga. The young prospect made 30 appearances for Leicester City last season and featured in the Premier League at just 15 years old, making him one of the most sought-after talents in English football. This is Arsenal's second signing of the summer. The club is also pursuing a central midfielder valued at approximately £50 million, with manager Mikel Arteta personally leading negotiations. While Saka and others are away at the World Cup, Arsenal has moved quickly and decisively in the transfer market.
