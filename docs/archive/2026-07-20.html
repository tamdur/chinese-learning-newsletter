AI破解三十年凸優化數學難題

來源：Hacker News

今天很多研究人員都在談一件事：一個叫做 GPT-5.6 的人工智慧，解開了一個放了大約三十年、一直沒人能解決的數學難題。這個難題屬於「凸優化」（一種找出最好答案的數學方法）。

過去 AI 大多只是幫忙做小事，這次它卻自己想出全新的答案。大家覺得，這代表 AI 已經從助手，變成能真正做研究的夥伴了。

Researchers everywhere are talking about the same thing today: an AI system called GPT-5.6 has solved a mathematical puzzle in convex optimization that has resisted solution for about thirty years. Convex optimization is a mathematical method for finding the best possible answer to complex problems. What makes this remarkable is that AI didn't merely assist with the work—it independently conceived an entirely new solution. Many see this as a turning point: AI has transformed from a mere assistant into a genuine research partner.

油價衝破九十美元

來源：Bloomberg

因為荷莫茲海峽（波斯灣一個很重要的海上通道）封鎖越來越嚴重，今天國際油價大漲近百分之四，每桶超過九十美元。一個星期前才大約七十九美元。

這個海峽平常運送全球超過兩成的石油，現在每天只剩幾艘船通過。專家擔心會出現「停滯性通膨」，也就是物價變貴、但經濟又長不快。

As blockades of the Hormuz Strait—a key maritime passage through the Persian Gulf—intensify, international oil prices surged nearly four percent today, pushing crude above ninety dollars per barrel. Just a week ago, it was trading around seventy-nine dollars. The strait normally transports more than one-fifth of global oil, but now only a handful of ships pass through daily. Experts worry stagflation could emerge—rising prices coupled with sluggish economic growth.

美國收緊綠卡新規定

來源：Texas Tribune

美國國土安全部今天正式公布新的「公共負擔」規定。以後移民如果用過政府的醫療補助、食物券或是住房補助，申請綠卡（永久居留權）時可能會被拒絕。

這個新規定會在九月十八號開始生效。它讓大家更注意，到底什麼樣的人才能拿到在美國長住的資格。

The U.S. Department of Homeland Security formally announced new "public charge" regulations today. Immigrants who have received government medical assistance, food stamps, or housing assistance may be denied when applying for a green card (permanent residency). The new rule takes effect on September 18th. It raises important questions about exactly who qualifies for long-term residency in the United States.

歐盟禁止銷毀賣不掉的衣服

來源：European Commission

歐盟今天開始實施一項新規定：大公司不可以再把賣不掉的衣服和鞋子燒掉或丟到垃圾場。以後這些沒賣完的東西，必須拿去轉送、再賣或回收。

過去很多時尚品牌為了保護形象，寧願把多做的衣服銷毀。這條新法律要考驗，規定能不能改變「快時尚」大量生產的習慣。

The EU is implementing a new rule starting today: major companies can no longer burn or landfill unsold clothing and shoes. Going forward, any leftover inventory must be donated, resold, or recycled. Historically, many fashion brands destroyed excess stock to preserve their brand image. This new law will test whether regulation can reshape the mass-production culture of fast fashion.

小熊隊換來投手希瓦利

來源：MLB.com

芝加哥小熊隊今天從運動家隊換來一位有經驗的右投手希瓦利（Aaron Civale），還拿到一些現金，代價是送出小聯盟投手莫菲特。

小熊隊最近一個月打出二十勝八敗，戰績排在國聯外卡第一，但因為投手受傷，先發陣容變薄。這次交易能幫球隊補強，拚八月三號交易截止前的最後衝刺。

The Chicago Cubs acquired experienced right-handed pitcher Aaron Civale from the Athletics today, along with some cash, in exchange for sending out minor league pitcher Moffett. The Cubs have posted a 20-8 record over the past month, putting them in first place in the National League wild card standings, but pitcher injuries have thinned out their starting rotation. The trade strengthens the roster as the team makes its final push toward the August 3rd trade deadline.

兵工廠三將奪世界盃冠軍

來源：Arsenal.com

兵工廠線——西班牙昨天在世界盃決賽以一比零打敗阿根廷，加時賽第一百零六分鐘由費蘭·托雷斯攻進致勝球。

兵工廠（阿森納）的梅里諾、蘇比門迪和門將拉亞都是西班牙隊員，一起拿到冠軍。這次兵工廠共有七名球員在世界盃拿到獎牌，比任何一支英超球隊都多，看得出球隊實力很堅強。

Arsenal Line — Spain defeated Argentina 1-0 in yesterday's World Cup final, with Ferran Torres scoring the winning goal in the 106th minute of extra time. Arsenal's Merino, Zubimendi, and goalkeeper Raya were all Spanish players who won the championship together. This time Arsenal had seven players win medals at the World Cup, more than any other Premier League team, demonstrating the club's considerable strength.
