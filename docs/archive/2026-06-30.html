人工智慧熱潮推動企業大併購

來源：Bain & Company

一家叫貝恩的顧問公司說，今年前五個月，全球企業買下別家公司的金額高達二點四兆美元，比去年多了四成一。

這股熱潮跟人工智慧有關。因為人工智慧的電腦機房很耗電，兩家美國電力公司就打算花一千一百九十億美元合併，好蓋出更多電廠。

A consulting firm called Bain says that in the first five months of this year, the total value of global corporate acquisitions reached $2.4 trillion, a 41 percent increase from last year. This wave of deal-making is tied to artificial intelligence. Because AI data centers consume enormous amounts of electricity, two major US power companies are planning to merge for $119 billion to build more power plants.

The surge in M&A activity reflects how companies are racing to position themselves in the AI era. Energy infrastructure has become a critical bottleneck — tech giants need reliable, abundant power to run their compute-intensive models, making utility companies and power generation assets suddenly strategic targets. This merger represents just one front in the broader scramble to control the resources that will define the next decade of technology.

However, Iran stated that they were only there to discuss how to implement the ceasefire, and denied that they were engaging in direct negotiations with the United States. Within Iran, direct talks with the United States are an extremely sensitive matter.

但是川普總統不肯簽名。他說，要等國會先通過他想要的投票改革法案，他才願意簽住房法案。

Congress passed a major housing bill aimed at building more homes to make them more affordable for everyone. The bill was highly popular, passing in the House 358 to 32. But President Trump refuses to sign it. He says he will only sign the housing bill after Congress first passes a voting reform bill that he wants.

南非排外期限將到　上萬外國人逃離

來源：France 24

南非有些反對外國人的團體，自己訂了六月三十日這個期限，要沒有證件的外國人離開。這個期限其實沒有法律效力。

不過已經有一萬三千多人嚇得逃走，很多來自辛巴威和馬拉威。南非警察全國出動，軍隊也待命，擔心會出現暴力。

Some anti-foreigner groups in South Africa have set their own deadline of June 30th, demanding that undocumented foreigners leave the country. This deadline has no legal force. However, more than 13,000 people have already fled in fear, many from Zimbabwe and Malawi. South African police are deployed nationwide, and the military is on standby, concerned that violence could erupt.
