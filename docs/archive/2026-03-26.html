美國最高法院：川普關稅不合法

來源：Tax Foundation / CNBC

美國最高法院在二月二十日以六比三的結果，認定川普用來加關稅的法律根本沒有提到「關稅」兩個字。結果這些關稅已經收了超過一千六百億美元，每個家庭平均多花了大約五百七十美元。

不過，白宮也沒有放棄。他們馬上換了另一條法律，先用百分之十的全球關稅撐一百五十天，同時也開始新的調查來準備長期關稅。鋼鐵和車的關稅沒受影響。這場貿易戰還沒結束，只是換了戰場。

The US Supreme Court ruled 6-3 on February 20th that the legal authority Trump used to impose tariffs didn't actually mention the word "tariff" at all. As a result, these tariffs have already collected over $160 billion, costing the average household about $570 more.

However, the White House hasn't given up. They immediately switched to a different law, implementing a 10% global tariff for 150 days while also launching a new investigation to prepare for longer-term tariffs. Tariffs on steel and cars remain unaffected. This trade war isn't over—it's just shifted to a new battlefield.

新版 ARC-AGI-3 測試出爐，最強 AI 也只拿一成

來源：ARC Prize

ARC Prize 在三月二十五日推出了 ARC-AGI-3，這是二〇一九年以來第一次大改。這個測試要看 AI 能不能自己從遊戲裡找出規則，完全沒有說明。一共有一百五十多個手工做的關卡，每個有八到十層。

結果很有意思：人類可以拿到百分之百，但最好的 AI 只有百分之十二。那些大型語言模型更差，不到百分之一。表現比較好的反而是用搜尋和強化學習的方法。獎金超過兩百萬美元，而且所有答案都要公開。

ARC Prize released ARC-AGI-3 on March 25th, the first major overhaul since 2019. This test examines whether AI can figure out the rules on its own from games, with absolutely no instructions. There are over 150 hand-crafted levels, each with 8 to 10 stages.

The results are fascinating: humans can achieve 100%, but the best AI only managed 12%. Large language models did even worse, scoring below 1%. The approaches that performed better were actually search and reinforcement learning methods. The prize pool exceeds $2 million, and all solutions must be made public.

小熊隊 Crow-Armstrong 簽了一億一千五百萬大約

來源：MLB.com Cubs

芝加哥小熊隊的外野手 Pete Crow-Armstrong 才二十四歲，就和球隊簽下六年一億一千五百萬美元的合約，到二〇三二年。這是球員在還有五年隊控制時，拿過最大的合約。

他去年打出三十一支全壘打、三十五次盜壘、三十七支二壘打，是小熊隊歷史上第一個在同一年做到三十加三十加三十的球員。防守也很厲害，他拿下十九個五星美技接殺，是 Statcast 的新紀錄。隊友和教練都說他帶給球隊很大的能量。

Pete Crow-Armstrong, a 24-year-old outfielder for the Chicago Cubs, just signed a six-year, $115 million deal with the team through 2032. This is the largest contract a player has received while still having five years of team control remaining.

Last year, he hit 31 home runs, had 35 stolen bases, and collected 37 doubles — making him the first player in Cubs history to achieve 30+ in all three categories in the same season. His defense is also exceptional. He recorded 19 five-star catches, setting a new Statcast record. Teammates and coaches say he brings tremendous energy to the team.

中國禁止 Manus AI 創辦人出國，Meta 收購案卡住

來源：Marginal Revolution

Meta 去年底想用大約二十億美元買下 Manus AI，但北京現在不讓公司的兩位創辦人出國。Manus 原本在中國成立，去年把公司搬到新加坡，也關了中國的辦公室。

中國政府在查他們把 AI 技術從中國搬到新加坡時，有沒有拿出口許可。這種「新加坡洗白」的做法越來越多：中國 AI 公司先搬家，再找西方的錢。出國禁令本來是給犯罪嫌疑人員用的，現在也用在 AI 人才身上，結果整個交易幾乎停了。

Meta had planned to acquire Manus AI for approximately $2 billion late last year, but Beijing is now preventing the company's two founders from traveling abroad. Manus was originally founded in China but relocated to Singapore last year and closed its Chinese offices.

The Chinese government is investigating whether the founders obtained proper export licenses when moving the AI technology from China to Singapore. This pattern—what some call "Singapore whitewashing"—is becoming increasingly common: Chinese AI companies relocate first, then seek Western funding. Exit bans, traditionally used against criminal suspects, are now being applied to AI talent, and the result has effectively frozen the entire deal.

小行星龍宮樣本找到全部五種 DNA 組成

來源：Nature Astronomy / Phys.org

日本的研究人員在小行星「龍宮」的樣本裡，找到了組成 DNA 和 RNA 的全部五種基本材料，叫做核鹼基。這些樣本是隼鳥二號太空船直接從小行星帶回來的，不是掉到地上的隕石（從天上掉下來的太空石頭），所以不用擔心被地球的東西弄髒。

科學家也發現一個特別的結果：這五種材料的比例差不多一樣多，在其他太空石頭裡從來沒看過。這個發現支持一個想法：很久以前，小行星可能把生命需要的材料帶到了地球。這篇論文三月發在網站 Nature Astronomy 上。

Researchers from Japan found all five basic components that make up DNA and RNA in samples from the asteroid Ryugu. These are called nucleobases. The samples were brought back directly from the asteroid by the Hayabusa2 spacecraft, not meteorites that fell to Earth (space rocks that came from the sky), so there's no need to worry about them being contaminated by things on Earth.

Scientists also discovered something particularly interesting: the proportions of these five materials were roughly equal, something that had never been seen before in other space rocks. This finding supports an idea: long ago, asteroids may have brought the materials needed for life to Earth. This paper was published in March on the website Nature Astronomy.
