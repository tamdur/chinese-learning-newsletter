亞太峰會在深圳開場

來源：China Global South Project

亞太經濟合作會議（APEC）今天在中國深圳開始，一共開兩天。這是中國第三次當主人。

大家最關心的是美國總統川普和中國國家主席習近平。川普預計今天晚上到，明天和習近平見面，談貿易和AI晶片。俄羅斯總統普丁也會來，三個人可能第一次一起開會。

不過專家說，這次大概只有小進展，大的協議要等十二月在美國佛州開的G20會議。

The Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) summit opened today in Shenzhen, China, and will run for two days. It is China's third time hosting the event. Most attention is on US President Donald Trump and Chinese President Xi Jinping. Trump is expected to arrive tonight and meet Xi tomorrow to discuss trade and AI chips. Russian President Vladimir Putin will also attend, and the three leaders may meet together for the first time. Experts, however, say this round will likely bring only small progress. Any major agreements will have to wait for the G20 meeting in December in Florida.

黃仁勳辭職後，輝達找新老闆

來源：South China Morning Post

輝達（Nvidia）創辦人黃仁勳在十一月十二日突然辭職，結束了33年的領導。現在由財務長克瑞斯（Colette Kress）暫時當執行長。

董事會正在找新的執行長。英國《金融時報》說，董事會已經找過Arm的老闆哈斯（Rene Haas）。川普也公開說，蘋果前執行長庫克（Tim Cook）很適合。董事會則說，現在談人選還太早。

輝達是做AI晶片最重要的公司。沒有接班人（接下工作的人），讓投資人很緊張。

Nvidia founder Jensen Huang suddenly resigned on November 12, ending his 33 years at the helm. For now, CFO Colette Kress is serving as acting CEO, and the board is searching for a permanent replacement. The Financial Times reports that the board has already approached Rene Haas, the head of Arm. President Trump has also publicly said that former Apple CEO Tim Cook would be a good fit. The board, for its part, says it is too early to discuss candidates. Nvidia is the leading maker of AI chips, and with no successor yet ready to take the job, investors are nervous.

留住熊隊，州議員回來開會

來源：Chicago Sun-Times

伊利諾州議員這個星期回到州首府春田市（Springfield）開三天會，十二月一日到三日再開三天。

最重要的事是留住芝加哥熊隊，不讓球隊搬到印第安納州。一個幫忙蓋新球場的法案越來越多人支持，不過大概要到十二月才會投票。

另一個大題目是AI資料中心。這些中心要用很多電，州長普立茲克（Pritzker）提出七點計畫，希望議員這次訂出管理規則。

Illinois lawmakers returned to the state capital, Springfield, this week for three days of session, with three more days scheduled for December 1 to 3. The most pressing matter is keeping the Chicago Bears from moving to Indiana. A bill to help pay for a new stadium is gaining support, but a vote probably won't come until December.

Another big issue is AI data centers. These facilities use enormous amounts of electricity, and Governor JB Pritzker has put forward a seven-point plan, hoping lawmakers will finally set rules for how they are managed.

伊朗冬天缺天然氣，領導人怕民心不穩

來源：Al-Monitor / Reuters

伊朗還在和美國打仗，現在冬天又來了。路透社報導，伊朗政府說，今年冬天天然氣不夠的量，會比去年多四成以上。

原因是戰爭一開始，四座處理天然氣的工廠被打壞了。全國31個省裡，有11個省特別容易缺氣。

伊朗地下的天然氣是世界上最多的國家之一，卻不夠自己用。領導人怕人民生氣上街，正在和俄羅斯談買天然氣。

Iran is still fighting the United States, and now winter has arrived. Reuters reports that the Iranian government says its natural gas shortfall this winter will be more than 40 percent larger than last year's. The cause is that four plants that process natural gas were damaged when the war began. Of the country's 31 provinces, 11 are especially vulnerable to gas shortages. Iran has some of the largest natural gas reserves in the world, yet it does not have enough to meet its own needs. Officials fear that angry citizens will take to the streets, so they are negotiating with Russia to buy natural gas.

密西根男籃狂得123分

來源：Associated Press (via ABC News)

排名第20的密西根大學男籃，15日在主場以123比79大勝威廉與瑪麗學院，是1990年以來得分最多的一場。

密西根下半場拿下75分，創下學校紀錄。全隊有8個人得分超過10分，新生科斯特洛（Quinn Costello）也拿下生涯最高的16分。

這場球說明，這支球隊靠的是全隊的力量，不是只靠一個明星。下一場，他們星期六要去費城挑戰維拉諾瓦大學。

The No. 20-ranked University of Michigan men's basketball team routed William & Mary 123-79 at home on the 15th, the most points Michigan has scored in a game since 1990. The Wolverines put up 75 points in the second half alone, a school record. Eight players scored in double figures, and freshman Quinn Costello also set a career high with 16 points. The game showed that this team runs on collective strength rather than leaning on a single star. Next up, they travel to Philadelphia on Saturday to take on Villanova.
