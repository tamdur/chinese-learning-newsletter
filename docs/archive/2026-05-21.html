美國眾議院以一票之差通過川普大法案

來源：NPR

美國眾議院以 215 比 214 的票數，通過了川普的「一個大而美的法案」，只贏一票。這份超過一千頁的法案將延長減稅、加快推動醫療補助的工作要求，還會取消大部分清潔能源補助。

兩位共和黨議員投了反對票，可見黨內合作非常不容易。法案現在送到參議院，還要再過一關。

The House of Representatives passed Trump's "big and beautiful" bill by a razor-thin margin of 215 to 214 votes, winning by just one. The bill, which exceeds a thousand pages, will extend tax cuts, accelerate Medicaid work requirements, and eliminate most clean energy subsidies. Two Republican members voted against it, underscoring how difficult party unity has become. The bill now heads to the Senate for another vote.

習近平與普丁簽署「多極世界」宣言

來源：Al Jazeera

中國國家主席習近平和俄羅斯總統普丁在北京開了兩天的高峰會，簽了一份 47 頁的「多極（多個強國共同治理）世界」宣言，還有超過 40 份合作協議。

這場會議就在川普訪問北京之後幾天。習近平批評「霸權主義」，明顯是在說美國。

Chinese President Xi Jinping and Russian President Putin held a two-day summit in Beijing, signing a 47-page declaration on a "multipolar world" governed by multiple great powers, along with over 40 cooperation agreements. The meeting took place just days after Trump's visit to Beijing. Xi criticized "hegemony," an unmistakable reference to the United States.

荷莫茲海峽危機：油價回落到一百美元

來源：CNBC

全球最重要的石油運輸通道荷莫茲海峽，從二月底被伊朗封鎖到現在。上週油價一度漲到每桶 107 美元，但現在回落到約 100 美元。

原因是美國向伊朗提出了和平方案，市場正在等伊朗的回覆。伊朗外交部說還在考慮中。

The Strait of Hormuz, the world's most critical oil shipping corridor, has been blockaded by Iran since late February. Last week, oil prices briefly spiked to $107 per barrel, but have since fallen to around $100. The reason: the United States has put forward a peace proposal to Iran, and markets are waiting for Tehran's response. Iran's Foreign Ministry says it is still considering the offer.

GitHub 遭駭客入侵，三千八百個內部程式庫被偷

來源：The Hacker News

全球最大的程式碼平台 GitHub 宣布被駭客（網路入侵者）攻擊了。一個叫 TeamPCP 的組織在員工的電腦上放了一個有毒的 VS Code 外掛程式，偷走了約 3，800 個內部程式庫。

GitHub 表示客戶的資料沒有受到影響，已經在第一時間更換了重要密碼。被偷的資料已被放上網路，開價九萬五千美元出售。

GitHub, the world's largest code platform, announced it was attacked by hackers (internet intruders). An organization called TeamPCP placed a malicious VS Code plugin on employee computers and stole approximately 3,800 internal code repositories. GitHub stated that customer data was not affected and changed critical passwords immediately. The stolen data has been posted online for sale at a price of $95,000.

小熊輸釀酒人，丟掉國聯中區第一

來源：MLB.com

芝加哥小熊週二在瑞乖利球場以 2 比 5 輸給密爾瓦基釀酒人。釀酒人投手 Misiorowski 投了六局沒失分，三振八人。

小熊最近十場輸了八場，已經掉到國聯中區第二名，落後釀酒人半場勝差。

The Chicago Cubs lost to the Milwaukee Brewers 2-5 on Tuesday at Wrigley Field. Brewers pitcher Misiorowski threw six scoreless innings with eight strikeouts. The Cubs have lost eight of their last ten games and have dropped to second place in the NL Central, half a game behind the Brewers.

兵工廠等了 22 年，終於拿下英超冠軍

來源：Arsenal.com

兵工廠線——等了 22 年，兵工廠終於又是英超冠軍了！曼城週二被伯恩茅斯 1比1 逼和，數學上已經追不上兵工廠。

主教練 Arteta 帶隊連續三年拿第二之後，今年終於突破。本季兵工廠 37 場贏了 25 場，五月 25 日將舉行捧盃儀式。

Arsenal beat — After 22 years, Arsenal has finally won the Premier League title again! Manchester City was held to a 1-1 draw by Bournemouth on Tuesday, mathematically eliminated from catching Arsenal. Manager Arteta, after finishing second for three consecutive years, has finally broken through. This season Arsenal has won 25 of 37 matches. The trophy presentation ceremony will be held on May 25th.
