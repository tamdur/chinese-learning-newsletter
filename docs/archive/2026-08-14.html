OpenAI 推出資安模型，只開放給通過審查的客戶

來源：The Hacker News

OpenAI 在八月十三日推出新的資安模型 GPT-5.6-Cyber，專門用來找出電腦程式裡的漏洞（破洞）。它比一般版本更少拒絕這類任務，在公司自己的測試裡，能完成百分之九十五的高難度資安工作，一般模型只有百分之一點五。

研究人員用它檢查 Chrome 瀏覽器的 V8 引擎，找到兩個還沒修好的漏洞，已經通報給谷歌。這個模型不公開賣，只給通過審查的客戶使用，所以審查的人變成了真正的安全關卡。

On August 13, OpenAI unveiled a new cybersecurity model called GPT-5.6-Cyber, designed specifically to identify vulnerabilities (or "holes") in computer code. It refuses such tasks far less often than the standard version. In the company's own testing, it successfully completed 95 percent of challenging cybersecurity tasks — compared to just 1.5 percent for the standard model.

Researchers used the model to examine Chrome's V8 engine and found two previously unpatched vulnerabilities, which they've reported to Google. The model isn't sold publicly; only customers who pass a security review can access it. In effect, the reviewers themselves have become the real security checkpoint.

烏克蘭無人機深入八百英里，炸俄國大煉油廠

來源：AP News

八月十三日，烏克蘭的無人機飛到俄國的巴什科爾托斯坦，攻擊薩拉瓦特煉油廠，引起大火。那裡離烏克蘭邊界大約一千三百公里，是俄國最大的煉油廠之一。當地官員說，有兩位平民受傷。

這是三天內第四次深入攻擊。烏克蘭現在有計畫地打俄國的煉油廠，想切斷它的汽油。俄國的煉油量已經掉到二○○二年以來最低，至少十六個地區又開始缺油。

On August 13th, Ukrainian drones reached Russia's Bashkortostan region and attacked the Salavat refinery, triggering a major fire. The site is approximately 1,300 kilometers from the Ukrainian border and is one of Russia's largest refineries. Local officials said two civilians were injured.

This marks the fourth deep strike in three days. Ukraine is now systematically targeting Russian refineries in an effort to cut off its gasoline supply. Russia's refining output has fallen to its lowest level since 2002, and at least sixteen regions are facing fuel shortages.

美股創新高，油價反而下跌

來源：CNBC

八月十三日，美國標準普爾五百指數收在七七九八點，第一次站上七千八百點，創下新高。同一天，油價卻跌了超過百分之二，布蘭特原油收在每桶八十七美元。

奇怪的是，伊朗當天攻擊了兩艘阿聯酋的油輪，地點就在荷莫茲海峽，那是世界最重要的石油通道。以前這種消息會讓油價大漲，現在投資人好像不再害怕，他們覺得需求變弱比供應被擋更重要。

On August 13th, the U.S. S&P 500 Index closed at 7,798 points, breaking through 7,800 for the first time and setting a new record high. On the same day, oil prices fell more than 2%, with Brent crude closing at $87 per barrel.

What's striking is that Iran attacked two Emirati tankers that same day in the Strait of Hormuz, the world's most critical oil chokepoint. In the past, such news would have sent oil prices surging. Now, however, investors appear unperturbed. They regard weakening demand as more consequential than blocked supply.

DeepSeek 推出新模型，同時大幅漲價

來源：Reuters

中國人工智慧公司 DeepSeek 在八月十三日正式推出新版模型 V4-Pro，一次可以讀一百萬個字詞，做事的能力更強。

但同一天它也宣布，從八月十六日起要調高收費，有些項目漲了十倍以上，還分成尖峰和離峰兩種價錢，離峰只要一半。這家公司本來就是靠便宜出名，現在漲價等於承認：連最便宜的廠商，也扛不住電腦運算的成本。

Chinese AI company DeepSeek officially launched its new V4-Pro model on August 13th. The model can read one million tokens at once and is stronger at getting things done.

But on the same day, it also announced price increases effective August 16th, with some items rising more than tenfold. It introduced tiered pricing for peak and off-peak hours, with off-peak rates at half the peak price. DeepSeek built its reputation on being cheap. Raising prices is essentially an admission: even the most affordable vendor cannot absorb the cost of compute.

熊隊季前賽首戰，威廉斯不上場

來源：Chicago Sun-Times

芝加哥熊隊確認，先發四分衛威廉斯（Caleb Williams）不會出戰星期六對克里夫蘭布朗隊的季前賽，改由替補的巴金特（Tyson Bagent）先發，預計打完上半場。

總教練強森說：「我需要在季前賽看威廉斯嗎？不用。我每天練習都看得到他。」球隊也宣布，接球員柏登因為大腿內側受傷，整個季前賽都不能上場。

The Chicago Bears confirmed that starting quarterback Caleb Williams will not play in Saturday's preseason game against the Cleveland Browns. Backup Tyson Bagent will start instead and is expected to play through halftime.

Head coach Ben Johnson said, "Do I need to see Williams in the preseason? No. I see him at practice every day." The team also announced that wide receiver Luther Burden III will miss the entire preseason with a groin injury.

兵工廠對曼城，社區盾為新球季揭幕

來源：Goal.com

兵工廠線——上季英超冠軍兵工廠，八月十六日將在威爾斯卡地夫的公國球場對上曼城，爭奪社區盾，這是英格蘭新球季的第一場正式比賽。

教練阿爾特塔的陣容不好排：後衛薩利巴和廷伯都有傷，可能由莫斯克拉和班懷特頂上；賴斯和薩卡剛踢完世界盃，預計先休息，改由馬杜埃凱和蘇比門迪先發。

The Arsenal beat — Last season's Premier League champions Arsenal take on Manchester City on August 16th at Cardiff's Principality Stadium for the Community Shield, the first official match of England's new season.

Manager Arteta faces a tricky selection puzzle: defenders William Saliba and Jurrien Timber are both injured and may be replaced by Cristhian Mosquera and Ben White. Declan Rice and Bukayo Saka, fresh off the World Cup, are expected to rest, with Noni Madueke and Martin Zubimendi starting in their place.
