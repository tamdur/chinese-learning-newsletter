小飛機撞上北京第一高樓

來源：South China Morning Post

六月二十六日傍晚，一架小飛機撞上北京最高的大樓「中信大廈」。這棟大樓高五百二十八公尺，撞出一個大洞，碎片掉到街上，大家趕快逃出來。

這架小飛機從北京東邊的機場起飛。奇怪的是，中國官方沒有說明，新聞也不太敢報，飛機為什麼會飛進守得很嚴的北京天空，到現在還是個謎。

On the evening of June 26th, a small aircraft collided with the CITIC Tower, Beijing's tallest building. The structure, which stands 528 meters high, suffered a large impact hole, and debris scattered onto the streets below as people rushed to evacuate. The aircraft had taken off from an airport on Beijing's eastern side. Curiously, Chinese authorities have offered no explanation, and news outlets have been hesitant to report, leaving unanswered the question of how a plane managed to penetrate Beijing's heavily guarded airspace — a mystery that remains unsolved.

美國開新公司的人創新高

來源：Marginal Revolution

今年美國人很愛開公司。一到五月，就有大約兩百九十萬個人申請開新公司，是有紀錄以來最多的一次。光是五月就比去年多了百分之十。

這很特別，因為全世界的經濟並不好。學者在想，會不會是人工智慧（AI）讓開公司變得更容易了。

Americans are really keen on starting companies this year. From January to May, approximately 2.9 million people filed applications to start new businesses — the highest number on record. May alone saw a 10 percent increase compared to last year. This is remarkable, especially given that the global economy is not performing well. Scholars are wondering whether artificial intelligence (AI) might be making it easier to start a company.

美國牽線　以色列黎巴嫩簽約

來源：Al Jazeera

六月二十六日，美國國務卿魯比歐幫忙牽線，讓以色列和黎巴嫩簽了一份約定。內容是要武裝組織真主黨停火、退出黎巴嫩南部。

可是真主黨當天就說不同意，還警告可能會打內戰。以色列也說，在真主黨交出武器以前，軍隊不會走。這份約定恐怕很難真的做到。

On June 26th, U.S. Secretary of State Rubio brokered a deal between Israel and Lebanon. The agreement calls for the armed group Hezbollah to cease fire and withdraw from southern Lebanon. But Hezbollah rejected the terms that same day and warned of possible civil war. Israel also stated that its military would not leave until Hezbollah surrenders its weapons. The agreement looks unlikely to succeed.

禽流感愛躲在乳牛的乳房

來源：ScienceDaily

科學家找到了一個答案：為什麼H5N1禽流感（鳥類的流行感冒）會在乳牛之間傳得這麼快。

研究發現，這種病毒最喜歡待的地方不是牛的肺，而是乳房。所以病毒主要不是用咳嗽傳開，而是靠擠奶的機器。只要把機器洗乾淨，就能幫忙擋住病毒。

Scientists have found an answer to why H5N1 avian flu spreads so quickly among dairy cows. Research shows that the virus prefers to hide not in the lungs, but in the udders. This means the virus spreads mainly not through coughing, but through milking machines. Simply washing the machines thoroughly can help block transmission.

教練跳槽　密大找代理教練

來源：Maize n Brew

密西根大學籃球隊今年才拿下全國冠軍，沒想到總教練梅伊（Dusty May）突然離開，跑去當NBA小牛隊的教練。

六月二十七日，學校先請助理教練博因頓（Mike Boynton）先當「代理教練」。用「代理」兩個字，代表學校還想慢慢找正式的新教練。

The University of Michigan basketball team just won the national championship this year, but head coach Dusty May suddenly left to become a coach for the NBA's Dallas Mavericks. On June 27th, the school appointed assistant coach Mike Boynton as "interim head coach." The use of the word "interim" indicates the school is still taking its time to find a permanent new head coach.

兵工廠想花大錢買兩名前鋒

來源：GoonerNews

兵工廠線——阿森納（兵工廠）想一次買進兩名好前鋒：維拉隊的羅傑斯，還有巴黎隊的巴爾科拉。

這兩人加起來可能要花將近兩億英鎊，是很大的一筆錢。阿森納隔了二十二年才拿到聯賽冠軍，現在想補強攻擊，但大家也在問：錢要從哪裡來？

Arsenal — The Gunners are eyeing two top strikers in a single swoop: Rogers from Aston Villa and Barcola from Paris. Together, the pair could cost nearly 200 million pounds, a substantial outlay. Having won the league title for the first time in twenty-two years, Arsenal is now looking to bolster its attack, but questions linger: where will the money come from?
