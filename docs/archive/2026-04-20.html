美軍在荷莫茲海峽扣押伊朗貨船

來源：NPR

美國海軍驅逐艦「USS Spruance」在四月十九日攔下伊朗貨船「Touska」號。這艘船不理會六小時的警告，硬闖美國海上封鎖線，美軍最後開砲打壞它的引擎室。

伊朗軍方說這是「海上搶劫」，發誓要報復。雙方的停火（暫時停止打仗）將在星期三到期，情勢非常緊張。

The U.S. Navy destroyer USS Spruance intercepted the Iranian cargo ship Touska on April 19th. The vessel ignored six hours of warnings and broke through the American naval blockade, prompting U.S. forces to fire on its engine room. Iran's military called it "maritime piracy" and vowed retaliation. The ceasefire between the two sides expires Wednesday, and tensions are running extremely high.

美國法院判定Ticketmaster違法壟斷

來源：NPR

美國一個聯邦（國家級）陪審團在四月十五日判定Live Nation和Ticketmaster違法壟斷（一家公司控制整個市場）。陪審團認為他們控制了大型演唱會場地約八成六的售票，每張票多收了消費者 1.72 美元。

現在三十三個州要求法官考慮把這兩家公司拆開。這是多年來最重要的反壟斷案件之一。

A U.S. federal jury ruled on April 15th that Live Nation and Ticketmaster engaged in illegal monopolistic practices—when a single company controls an entire market. The jury found that they controlled ticket sales for approximately 86 percent of major concert venues, overcharging consumers by $1.72 per ticket. Now thirty-three states are urging the judge to consider breaking apart these two companies. This is one of the most significant antitrust cases in years.

國際油價飆升到每桶九十六美元

來源：Axios

美軍扣押伊朗貨船之後，國際油價（布蘭特原油）在星期天晚上大漲約七個百分點，來到每桶 96.88 美元。荷莫茲海峽是全球最重要的石油運送通道，星期天沒有任何油輪通過。

聯合國警告，依靠進口石油的開發中國家受到的壓力最大。

International oil prices (Brent crude) surged roughly seven percentage points to $96.88 per barrel on Sunday evening, following a U.S. military seizure of an Iranian cargo vessel. The Strait of Hormuz, the world's most critical petroleum shipping corridor, saw no tanker traffic pass through on Sunday. The United Nations has warned that developing nations reliant on imported oil face the greatest pressure.

Google推出Gemma 4，開放AI模型追上頂尖水準

來源：LLM Stats

Google在四月初發布了Gemma 4，這是一種免費開放的 AI 模型，任何人都可以下載使用。它在全球開放模型排名中拿到第三名。

四月是歷史上 AI 模型發布最密集的一個月，Gemma 4、Llama 4、Qwen 3.5 同時競爭。免費模型和付費模型的差距正在快速縮小。

Google launched Gemma 4 in early April, a free, open-source AI model that anyone can download and use. It ranks third globally among open models. April has become the most densely packed month for AI model releases in history, with Gemma 4, Llama 4, and Qwen 3.5 all competing simultaneously. The gap between free and paid models is narrowing rapidly.

小熊隊延長賽再見勝，橫掃大都會隊

來源：ESPN

芝加哥小熊隊在四月十九日以 2 比 1 打敗紐約大都會隊，完成三連勝橫掃。比賽打到第十局延長賽，Hoerner 打出高飛犧牲打，送回致勝分。

大都會隊已經連輸十一場，是他們自 2004 年以來最長的連敗紀錄。

The Chicago Cubs defeated the New York Mets 2-1 on April 19th in ten innings, completing a three-game sweep. Hoerner delivered the game-winning run with a sacrifice fly in the extra inning. The Mets have now lost eleven consecutive games, their longest losing streak since 2004.

兵工廠客場輸曼城，領先只剩三分

來源：ESPN

兵工廠線——兵工廠四月十九日在客場以 1 比 2 輸給曼城。Cherki 先幫曼城進球，Havertz 很快追平，但 Haaland 在第六十五分鐘踢進致勝球，這是他本季聯賽第二十三球。

兵工廠已經連輸兩場，領先曼城只剩三分，而且曼城少打一場。冠軍之爭越來越緊張了。

Arsenal's away match against Manchester City on April 19th ended in a 1-2 defeat. Cherki opened the scoring for City, but Havertz quickly equalized. However, Haaland sealed the win in the 65th minute with his 23rd league goal of the season. Arsenal has now lost two consecutive matches, and their lead over Manchester City has shrunk to just three points — with City having a game in hand. The title race is tightening considerably.
