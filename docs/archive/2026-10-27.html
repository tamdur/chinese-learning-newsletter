三大央行同一週決定利率

來源：Raisin、Reuters、Eurostat

這個星期，世界上三家最重要的中央銀行（管國家利率的銀行），要在兩天內決定利率。

美國聯準會星期三公布。路透社問了一百位經濟學家，九十四位認為利率會停在百分之三點七五到四。不過，很多人認為十二月還會再升息（把利率調高）。

歐洲央行星期四開會。歐元區九月的物價比去年漲了百分之三點八，能源更漲了快兩成。日本央行也會在三十日公布。三家的決定，會讓我們知道這一波升息是不是快到頂了。

This week, the world's three most important central banks, the institutions that set national interest rates, will each decide on rates within two days. The US Federal Reserve announces its decision on Wednesday. Reuters polled 100 economists, and 94 of them expect the Fed to hold its rate steady at 3.75% to 4%. Still, many people think it could raise rates again in December. The European Central Bank meets on Thursday. Prices in the euro area in September were 3.8% higher than a year earlier, and energy prices rose by nearly 20%. The Bank of Japan will also announce its decision on the 30th. Taken together, the three decisions should show whether this wave of rate hikes is close to its peak.
