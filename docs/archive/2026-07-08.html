聯合國成立人工智慧委員會 科技老闆與各國領袖同桌

來源：Tech Times

聯合國七月八日在瑞士日內瓦，第一次召開「人工智慧為善」委員會的會議。這是聯合國第一個專門管人工智慧的組織。

最特別的是，輝達老闆黃仁勳、亞馬遜老闆等大公司主管，和各國領袖坐在一起開會。委員會有四十四位成員，要一起訂出安全又公平的規則。不過有人擔心，這樣會讓大公司太有發言權。

The UN held its first meeting of the "AI for Good" committee on July 8th in Geneva, Switzerland. This marks the UN's first organization dedicated specifically to overseeing artificial intelligence. What's particularly striking is that major tech executives—including Nvidia's Jensen Huang and Amazon's CEO—sat alongside world leaders at the table. The committee comprises forty-four members tasked with jointly establishing rules that are both safe and fair. Some observers worry, however, that this arrangement grants large corporations too much influence in the process.

馬克宏訪敘利亞 大馬士革爆炸傷十八人

來源：Al Jazeera

法國總統馬克宏七月七日到敘利亞首都大馬士革訪問時，市區發生兩起爆炸，至少十八人受傷，其中四人是警察。

炸彈分別放在垃圾桶和一輛停著的車子裡。馬克宏是二〇二四年阿薩德政權倒台後，第一位到訪的西方大國領袖，本想幫助敘利亞重建，但爆炸讓大家看到當地還很不安全。

French President Macron's visit to Damascus, the Syrian capital, on July 7th was marked by two explosions that wounded at least 18 people, including four police officers. The bombs were placed in a trash bin and in a parked car. Macron was the first major Western leader to visit Syria since the Assad regime's collapse in 2024, intending to help with the country's reconstruction—but the blasts underscored how dangerously unstable the capital remains.

以色列再次降息 利率降到百分之三點五

來源：Bank of Israel

以色列央行七月六日把基準利率從百分之三點七五，調降到百分之三點五，這是連續第二次降息。

央行說，因為美國和伊朗停火，油價往下掉，物價上漲的壓力也變小了。這也是這場衝突結束後，帶來的第一個明顯的經濟好處。

On July 6th, Israel's central bank lowered its benchmark interest rate from 3.75% to 3.5%, marking the second consecutive rate cut. The bank attributed the move to the U.S.-Iran ceasefire, which has pushed oil prices lower and eased inflationary pressures. This represents the first tangible economic benefit to emerge since the end of the conflict.

芝加哥年中短缺一億三千萬 企業稅之爭再起

來源：Chicago Sun-Times

芝加哥市長強生的團隊七月七日公布，市政府今年年中出現一億三千一百七十萬美元的預算缺口。

為了補這個洞，有人主張恢復向企業徵收的「人頭稅」。商業團體反對，但一些進步派人士認為，這比刪減服務更公平。

Chicago Mayor Brandon Johnson's team announced on July 7th that the city is facing a mid-year budget shortfall of $131.7 million. To address the gap, some officials are advocating for the return of a "head tax" on businesses. Business groups oppose the proposal, but progressive advocates argue it is more equitable than cutting services.

小熊克羅阿姆斯壯 連兩年入選明星賽

來源：MLB.com

芝加哥小熊隊中外野手克羅阿姆斯壯，連續第二年入選國家聯盟明星隊，今年也是小熊隊唯一的代表。

他六月表現火燙，打擊率三成八一，還打出十一支全壘打。明星賽七月十四日將在費城舉行。今年三月，小熊才和他簽下六年、一億一千五百萬美元的合約。

Chicago Cubs center fielder Pete Crow-Armstrong has been selected to the National League All-Star team for the second consecutive year, and is the Cubs' sole representative this season. He had a hot June, posting a .381 batting average and hitting 11 home runs. The All-Star Game will be held in Philadelphia on July 14. In March this year, the Cubs signed him to a six-year, $115 million contract.
