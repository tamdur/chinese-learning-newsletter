OpenAI推出青少年版ChatGPT，靠系統猜你幾歲

來源：TechCrunch

八月十八日，OpenAI推出給十三到十七歲使用的「青少年版」ChatGPT。最特別的是，它不等你自己說年齡，而是用一套「年齡預測」系統來猜：看你常聊什麼、什麼時間上網、帳號開多久。系統覺得你未滿十八歲，就自動把你換過去。

這個版本不談自殺、自我傷害和情愛話題，也不會說自己有感情，另外加了「學習模式」，一步一步問問題，帶學生自己想答案。有人說這是保護，也有人擔心這是另一種監視。

On August 18th, OpenAI launched a "teen version" of ChatGPT for users ages 13 to 17. What's special about it is that it doesn't wait for you to state your age — instead, it uses an "age prediction" system to guess, by looking at what you typically chat about, what times you're online, and how long your account has existed. If the system thinks you're under 18, it automatically switches you over.

This version avoids discussing suicide, self-harm, and romantic topics, and won't claim to have feelings. It also adds a "study mode" that asks questions step by step, guiding students to work out their own answers. Some see this as protection; others worry it's a new form of surveillance.

白宮舞廳工程喊停，最高法院兩天內要決定

來源：SCOTUSblog

白宮東邊的側樓去年被拆掉，政府想在原地蓋一座九萬平方英尺的舞廳，要花四億美元，錢全部來自私人捐款。八月七日上訴法院判決：這麼大的工程沒有國會點頭，總統不能自己決定，地面上的工程必須停下來。

政府向最高法院緊急求助，說工程已經完成六成五，「回不了頭」了。反對的一方八月十八日也遞出文件，請大法官不要放行。停工命令八月二十一日生效，大法官只剩兩天可以想。

The east wing of the White House was demolished last year. The government wants to build a 90,000-square-foot ballroom on the site, at a cost of $400 million funded entirely by private donations. On August 7th, an appeals court ruled that a project of this scale cannot proceed without congressional approval — the president cannot decide it alone, and above-ground work must stop.

The government has gone to the Supreme Court for emergency relief, arguing that the project is already 65 percent complete and there is "no turning back." The opposing side also filed papers on August 18th, asking the justices not to let it proceed. The construction halt takes effect on August 21st, leaving the justices just two days to decide.

英國與Google啟動AI試驗，幫飛機避開凝結尾

來源：NATS / Google

飛機飛過留下的白色長線叫「凝結尾」（飛機後面拉出的細長白雲）。它會把熱關在地球裡，造成的暖化大約占航空業影響的三分之一，比燒油放出的二氧化碳還多。

八月十八日，Google、英國航管公司NATS、氣象局和劍橋、帝國理工的學者一起啟動「藍天行動」，花五百萬英鎊、做三十個月。做法是用AI預測哪一層空氣容易生成凝結尾，再請飛機稍微改變高度繞開。試驗場地是北大西洋的尚維克空域，那裡就占了全球凝結尾暖化的百分之五。

The long white streaks left behind by aircraft are called "contrails" (the thin white clouds that trail behind a plane). They trap heat in the Earth's atmosphere, and the warming they cause accounts for roughly one-third of aviation's climate impact — more than the carbon dioxide from burning the fuel itself.

On August 18th, Google, the UK air traffic control provider NATS, the Met Office, and researchers from Cambridge and Imperial College London launched "Operation Blue Skies," a £5 million project running for thirty months. The approach uses AI to predict which layers of air are prone to contrail formation, then asks aircraft to change altitude slightly to steer around them. The test site is the Shanwick oceanic airspace over the North Atlantic, which alone accounts for five percent of global contrail warming.

普利茲克簽災難聲明，十五個郡可申請聯邦補助

來源：Chicago Sun-Times

伊利諾今年天氣特別兇。國家氣象局確認州內已經出現大約兩百零四個龍捲風，全美最多，還有一次又一次的暴雨和淹水。州長普利茲克八月十八日簽下災難聲明，範圍包含十五個郡。

這張紙很重要：它讓州政府的交通、環保和商務部門調出人力和資源，也讓這些郡能去申請聯邦的「社區發展」災後補助。伊利諾先前直接向聯邦要救災錢被拒絕，這次等於換一扇門敲。

Illinois has had an unusually violent year of weather. The National Weather Service has confirmed roughly 204 tornadoes across the state — the most in the country — along with repeated downpours and flooding. Governor Pritzker signed a disaster proclamation on August 18th covering fifteen counties.

That piece of paper matters: it lets the state's transportation, environmental protection, and commerce departments release personnel and resources, and it lets those counties apply for federal Community Development disaster block grants. Illinois had earlier asked Washington directly for disaster money and been turned down; this time it amounts to knocking on a different door.

小熊游擊手史旺森側腹拉傷，預計休四到六週

來源：MLB.com Cubs

小熊隊游擊手史旺森（Dansby Swanson）上週日對紅雀隊揮棒姿勢怪怪的，第三局就下場，檢查結果是左邊側腹（腰的兩側）二級拉傷。他被放進傷兵名單，要休四到六週，例行賽剩下的日子幾乎都在養傷。季後賽九月二十九日開打，他還有機會趕上。

接手游擊的是霍納，他從二壘移過去。有意思的是，霍納週一在瑞格利球場對白襪隊，才在九局下打出追平分安打，球隊才在延長賽七比五贏球。

Cubs shortstop Dansby Swanson swung awkwardly last Sunday against the Cardinals and left the game in the third inning. Tests showed a Grade 2 strain of his left oblique (the muscles along the sides of the waist). He has been placed on the injured list and will need four to six weeks, meaning he will spend nearly all of what's left of the regular season recovering. The playoffs begin on September 29th, so he still has a chance to make it back.

Taking over at shortstop is Hoerner, moving across from second base. Fittingly, it was Hoerner who, on Monday at Wrigley Field against the White Sox, hit the game-tying single in the bottom of the ninth before the team won 7-5 in extra innings.

兵工廠線：新球季首戰主場對考文垂

來源：Football Meister

兵工廠線——上季的英超冠軍兵工廠，八月二十一日星期五在主場酋長球場踢新球季第一戰，對手是剛升上來的考文垂。考文垂上季在英冠拿下冠軍，領先第二名十一分，隔了很多年才回到頂級聯賽。

教練阿爾特塔手上還缺兩人：廷伯（大腿內側和骨盆）和薩利巴（下背）都還沒好。預計先發的前場三人是薩卡、哈佛茨和新加入的佐利斯。佐利斯身價三千四百萬英鎊，社區盾那場送出兩次助攻，這會是他第一場英超先發。

The Arsenal beat — Arsenal, last season's Premier League champions, open the new season at home at the Emirates on Friday, August 21st, against newly promoted Coventry. Coventry won the Championship last season by eleven points over the runners-up, returning to the top flight after many years away.

Manager Arteta is still without two players: Timber (adductor and pelvis) and Saliba (lower back) are both not yet fit. The expected front three are Saka, Havertz, and new signing Christos Tzolis. Tzolis, signed for £34 million, provided two assists in the Community Shield, and this would be his first Premier League start.
