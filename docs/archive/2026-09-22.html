馬斯克新模型出爐 用衛星資料訓練

來源：MarkTechPost

馬斯克的公司星期一推出新的人工智慧模型，叫做 Grok 4.7。它有二點一兆個參數（模型裡可以調整的數字），比上一代大了四成，但價錢和速度都沒變，而且一上線就能用，不用排隊。

最特別的是訓練資料。公司用了同一個老闆旗下太空公司的星鏈衛星紀錄和工程失敗報告，希望模型更懂真實的機器。這種資料，別家公司想買也買不到。

Musk's company launched a new AI model on Monday called Grok 4.7. It contains 2.1 trillion parameters—the adjustable numbers that make up the model—40% more than the previous generation, yet retains the same price and speed. It's available immediately upon launch with no waiting period. What's most remarkable is the training data. The company used Starlink satellite records and engineering failure reports from the space company under the same owner, aiming to help the model better understand real-world machinery. This type of data simply isn't available for purchase—other companies can't buy it no matter how badly they want to.

和談有望 油價跌破一百美元

來源：Bloomberg

打了七個月的美伊戰爭，這星期出現轉機。伊朗總統佩澤斯基安到紐約參加聯合國大會，川普說他願意見面談一談。

市場馬上有反應。星期一美國原油大跌百分之四點五，收在每桶九十五點七八美元，四天下來跌了超過百分之九。海上封鎖其實還在，二十多艘美國軍艦仍擋著伊朗的石油出口。但油價降下來，對物價和利率都是好消息。

After seven months of US-Iran conflict, a breakthrough emerges this week. Iran's President Pezeshkian came to New York to attend the UN General Assembly, and Trump said he's willing to meet and talk. Markets responded immediately. On Monday, US crude oil dropped 4.5%, closing at $95.78 per barrel—down more than 9% over four days. The maritime blockade remains in effect, with over twenty American warships still blocking Iran's oil exports. But falling oil prices are good news for both inflation and interest rates.

聯邦法院翻案 密蘇里州改用共和黨地圖

來源：Courthouse News Service

美國第八巡迴上訴法院星期一裁定，密蘇里州十一月的期中選舉要用共和黨畫的新選區地圖。這和州最高法院前不久的判決剛好相反：同一張地圖，兩個法院說法完全不一樣。

舊地圖下共和黨在該州的國會席次是六比二，新地圖還能再多拿一席。法院把裁定暫停到九月二十八日下午五點，等聯邦最高法院出手。這個案子已經是第三次送上最高法院了。

The U.S. 8th Circuit Court of Appeals ruled Monday that Missouri's November midterm elections must use a new congressional district map drawn by Republicans. The decision directly contradicts a state Supreme Court ruling from just weeks earlier — the same map, yet the two courts reached entirely opposite conclusions. Republicans hold a 6-to-2 advantage in the state's congressional seats under the old map; the new map would give them one additional seat. The court has stayed its ruling until 5 p.m. on September 28, pending action by the U.S. Supreme Court. This is the third time the case has reached the nation's highest court.

芝加哥市長選戰 挑戰者錢多三十倍

來源：Axios Chicago

最新的政治獻金（捐給候選人的錢）報告顯示，伊利諾州州務卿吉安努利亞斯手上有超過兩千一百萬美元，現任市長強森卻不到七十萬。

吉安努利亞斯的錢多來自工會和企業，光是四個工會今年就各捐了一百萬。強森靠的是小額捐款，共四十八萬二千美元。伊利諾州沒有捐款上限，這樣的差距會決定誰跑得完全市的選戰。

The latest campaign finance report shows a massive funding gap: Illinois Secretary of State Giannulis has over $21 million on hand, compared to incumbent Mayor Johnson's less than $700,000. Giannulis's money comes largely from unions and corporations—just four unions have each contributed $1 million this year. Johnson has relied on small donations, raising $482,000 from that source. With Illinois having no donation limits, this financial divide could prove decisive in determining who can afford to wage a full citywide campaign.

密西根教練：掉球才是最大毛病

來源：Yahoo Sports / Maize n Brew

星期六就要打大十聯盟的開季戰，對手是全國排名第十七的愛荷華。密西根大學教練惠廷漢卻先談掉球：三場比賽，球隊就把球掉在地上六次。

他說：「我們每天都在強調，真的沒辦法再多做什麼了。」他也說，球一直這樣掉，想爭冠軍根本不可能。愛荷華正好是那種分數低、靠場上位置吃飯的隊伍。

Saturday brings Michigan's Big Ten conference opener against nationally-ranked No. 17 Iowa. But Michigan's coach is focusing first on turnovers: in three games, the team has fumbled the ball six times. "We emphasize this every day," he said. "There's really nothing more we can do." He also said that if the team keeps fumbling like this, there's simply no way to compete for a championship. Iowa is exactly the kind of team that plays low-scoring football and wins through field position control.

兵工廠線：阿爾特塔說慘敗是一堂大課

來源：Last Word on Sports

兵工廠線——兵工廠零比三輸給布萊頓，這是衛冕（守住冠軍）球季的第一場敗仗。總教練阿爾特塔說，這是「很大的一課」，球隊離該做到的基本功「差得很遠」。

他把問題指向中場。賴斯和吉馬良斯第一次在聯賽一起先發，卻搶不到落地的第二球，後防也傳不出布萊頓的高壓逼搶。不過阿爾特塔也說，兩人才一起踢了幾分鐘，「這份默契一定會愈來愈好」。

Arsenal Line — Arsenal lost 0-3 to Brighton, the defending champions' first defeat of the season. Manager Arteta called it "a big lesson," saying the team fell "far short" of the basic fundamentals they should be achieving. He blamed midfield. Rice and Guimarães started together in the league for the first time, but couldn't secure the second ball, and the defense couldn't break out of Brighton's high press. Still, Arteta noted that the two have only shared the pitch for a few minutes, and "that understanding will definitely improve."
