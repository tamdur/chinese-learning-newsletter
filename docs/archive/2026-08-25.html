最高法院放行川普郵寄投票命令

來源：NPR

美國最高法院八月二十四日以六比三，取消了波士頓法官塔瓦尼六月發出的禁令。這讓川普總統限制郵寄投票的命令，可以在二十三個州和華盛頓特區先開始運作。

不過命令還沒完全上路。八月十一日另一道禁令，還擋著郵局改規則。三位大法官不同意，他們說這只是暫時的，並沒有回答總統到底能不能管各州怎麼辦選舉。

By a 6-3 vote on August 24, the U.S. Supreme Court lifted an injunction issued in June by Boston judge Tavani.

The ruling allows President Trump's order restricting mail-in voting to begin operating in 23 states and Washington, D.C. The order has not fully taken effect, however: a separate injunction from August 11 still blocks the Postal Service from changing its rules.

Three justices dissented, arguing that the decision is only temporary and does not answer whether the president actually has the authority to direct how states run their elections.

川普放話：加拿大汽車關稅要加到五成

來源：CNBC

上週六，美國才對約兩百億美元的加拿大貨品課了五成關稅，像是紅酒、水泥和曲棍球桿。八月二十四日，他又說要從 2027 年一月一日起，把汽車、卡車、零件和鋼鐵也加到五成。

川普的理由是美國對加拿大有六百億美元的貿易赤字（買得比賣得多）。總理卡尼已經說會還手。汽車零件常常來回過境好幾次，中西部的工廠和安大略的車廠，其實在同一條生產線上。

Last Saturday the U.S. imposed 50 percent tariffs on roughly $20 billion of Canadian goods — wine, cement and hockey sticks among them. On August 24 Trump said he would extend the same 50 percent rate to cars, trucks, parts and steel starting January 1, 2027. His stated reason is a $60 billion U.S. trade deficit with Canada (buying more than it sells).

Prime Minister Carney has already said Canada will hit back. Auto parts routinely cross the border several times before a vehicle is finished, so Midwestern factories and Ontario assembly plants are effectively on the same production line.

AI 模型共享平台 Hugging Face 傳出價一百三十億美元

來源：TechCrunch

Hugging Face 是全世界最大的 AI 模型共享平台，很多人叫它「AI 界的 GitHub」。研究人員和小公司都在上面放模型和資料。

八月二十四日有報導說，它請了銀行幫忙問問看誰想買，開價大約一百三十億美元。2023 年它只值四十五億美元，今年還拒絕了輝達五億美元的投資。目前沒有買家現身，公司也沒有回應。

Hugging Face is the world's largest platform for sharing AI models — many people call it "the GitHub of AI." Researchers and small companies use it to host models and datasets.

On August 24 it was reported that the company had hired a bank to sound out potential buyers, at an asking price of around $13 billion. In 2023 it was worth just $4.5 billion, and this year it turned down a $500 million investment from Nvidia. So far no buyer has emerged, and the company has not commented.

法國民調：極左梅蘭雄可能對上勒龐

來源：France 24 / Reuters

法國選總統要投兩輪。八月二十四日公布的一份民調顯示，極左派「不屈法國」的梅蘭雄，在五種情況裡有三種能進第二輪，對手是極右派的勒龐。他的支持度從四月的百分之十二升到十六以上。

中間派的菲力普說，這個風險「真實而且嚴重」。過去二十多年，第二輪一向是中間選民聯手擋住極右派的時候。若兩邊都是極端，這個機制就沒了。距離投票還有二十個月。

France elects its president in two rounds. A poll published on August 24 shows Jean-Luc Mélenchon, of the hard-left France Unbowed, reaching the second round in three of five modeled scenarios, against the far right's Marine Le Pen. His support has risen from 12 percent in April to above 16 percent.

The centrist Édouard Philippe called the risk "real and serious." For more than two decades, the second round has been the moment when mainstream voters combine to block the far right. If both finalists are from the extremes, that mechanism disappears. The vote is still twenty months away.

密西根新教頭排好名單　卻不公開

來源：SI.com

密西根大學美式足球隊今年換了新總教練惠廷漢。八月的秋季集訓快結束了，他說第一場比賽的先發名單已經排好，但不打算公開，要讓對手西密西根大學上場時自己去發現。

四分衛早就定了，還是二年級的安德伍德。惠廷漢承認唯一還沒定的是替補四分衛，目前由新生卡爾暫時排第二。整隊的目標是爭十大聯盟冠軍。新球季還沒開打。

The University of Michigan football team has a new head coach this year, Kyle Whittingham. With August's fall camp winding down, he says the starting lineup for the opener is already set but that he does not intend to release it — he wants opponent Western Michigan to find out on the field.

The quarterback job was settled long ago: sophomore Bryce Underwood. Whittingham admits the only spot still unresolved is backup quarterback, currently held provisionally by a freshman, Carr. The team's stated goal is a Big Ten title. The new season has not yet begun.

The Arsenal beat — with one week left in the transfer window, most of the nearly £200 million Arsenal spent this summer went into the spine: Guimarães in midfield, and defender Konsa at around £50 million. Up front they added just one winger, Tzolis.

At the other end, Trossard has already moved to Turkey, and Martinelli is in talks to leave, though nothing has been agreed. Names like Álvarez and Osimhen remain rumors for now.

If no one else arrives before the window shuts, the defending champions will go into the season with fewer forwards than they had in June — and still have to get through 38 league matches plus European football, with thin cover on the left and behind Saka.
