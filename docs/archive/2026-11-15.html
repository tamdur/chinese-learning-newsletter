芝加哥預算卡關，市長新稅被擋

來源：WTTW News

芝加哥市議會的預算聽證會這週結束了，但市長 Brandon Johnson 和多數議員還是談不攏。

2027 年的預算有 8.82 億美元的缺口。Johnson 想向大公司收「人頭稅」，按員工人數收錢。可是有 28 位議員寫信說，這個計畫已經「沒救了」。預算要 26 票才能通過，所以這條路走不通。

市政府必須在年底前通過預算，大約只剩七週。議員選舉也只剩一百天左右，大家壓力都很大。

Chicago's budget hearings wrapped up this week, but Mayor Brandon Johnson and a majority of the City Council still cannot find common ground. The 2027 budget has an $882 million gap. Johnson wants to levy a "head tax" on large companies, charging them by the number of employees. But 28 aldermen have written a letter saying the plan is "beyond saving." The budget needs 26 votes to pass, so this route is a dead end. The city must approve a budget before year-end, which leaves only about seven weeks. Aldermanic elections are also roughly 100 days away, and everyone is feeling the pressure.

六十萬顆輝達晶片卡在香港

來源：Reuters

美國已經允許輝達（Nvidia）把 H200 人工智慧晶片賣到中國，但現在卡關的反而是北京。

路透社報導，大約 60 萬顆 H200 晶片正放在香港，因為中國政府還沒同意大量進口。香港的資料中心也不夠多，用不了這麼多晶片。

同時，美國商務部正在考慮新規定，限制別人從遠方使用放在國外的輝達機器。輝達自己也已經預估，在中國的資料中心生意會是零。

The United States has already allowed Nvidia to sell its H200 AI chips to China, but it is now Beijing that is holding things up. According to a Reuters report, about 600,000 H200 chips are sitting in Hong Kong because the Chinese government has not yet agreed to large-scale imports. Hong Kong also lacks enough data centers to make use of that many chips. Meanwhile, the US Commerce Department is considering new rules that would restrict people from using chips located overseas from a distance. Nvidia itself has projected that its data center business in China will be zero.

經濟學家選出年度好電影

來源：Marginal Revolution

美國經濟學家 Tyler Cowen 很愛看電影。他在部落格上公布了 2026 年最好的電影名單，他最喜歡的是《Sirat》。

這部電影是西班牙導演 Oliver Laxe 拍的，故事發生在摩洛哥的沙漠。一位父親帶著兒子，到沙漠裡的音樂派對尋找失蹤（不見了）的女兒。

《Sirat》在 2025 年的坎城影展得過評審團獎，也有不少影評人把它排在第一名。

American economist Tyler Cowen is a devoted moviegoer. On his blog, he published his list of the best films of 2026, and his top pick is Sirat. The film was directed by Spanish filmmaker Oliver Laxe and is set in the deserts of Morocco. A father travels with his son to a music party in the desert to search for his missing daughter. Sirat won the Jury Prize at the 2025 Cannes Film Festival, and many critics have also ranked it number one.

密西根籃球回主場，想走出輸球

來源：Michigan Athletics

上一季的全國冠軍密西根大學籃球隊，今天在主場 Crisler Center 對上 William & Mary，芝加哥時間下午一點開打。

11 月 6 日，密西根和冠軍賽的老對手 UConn 再次碰面，結果以 77 比 85 輸球。UConn 上半場一度領先 18 分，Braylon Mullins 一個人就拿了 23 分。

這是密西根這季第一次輸球。球隊的陣容重新組過，大家都想看看，這群新組合跌倒後能不能馬上站起來。

Last season's national champion, Michigan men's basketball, opened a home game today at Crisler Center against William & Mary, with tip-off at 1 p.m. Chicago time. On November 6, Michigan met UConn, an old rival from the national championship game, and lost 77–85. UConn led by as many as 18 points in the first half, and Braylon Mullins scored 23 on his own. It was Michigan's first loss of the season. The roster has been reshuffled, and everyone wants to see whether this new group can get back on its feet quickly after a fall.
