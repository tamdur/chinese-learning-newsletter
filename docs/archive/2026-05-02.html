川普拒絕伊朗停火提案，談判陷入僵局

來源：Al Jazeera

美國總統川普五月一日正式拒絕伊朗最新的停火提案。伊朗要求先開放荷莫茲海峽（全球五分之一的石油從這裡經過），但川普堅持伊朗不能有核武器。

巴基斯坦負責中間調解（幫雙方溝通），但雙方在核問題上無法達成共識，談判已經停下來。川普暗示如果伊朗不同意，美軍可能重新開始打擊。

President Trump formally rejected Iran's latest ceasefire proposal on May 1st. Iran demanded that the Strait of Hormuz be reopened first—one-fifth of the world's oil passes through it—but Trump insisted that Iran cannot possess nuclear weapons. Pakistan has been mediating between the two sides to facilitate communication, but they cannot reach consensus on the nuclear issue, and negotiations have stalled. Trump hinted that if Iran does not agree, the U.S. military may resume strikes.

伊朗戰爭耗光武器，美國告知歐洲盟友交貨要延遲

來源：Financial Times

美國已正式通知英國、波蘭、立陶宛和愛沙尼亞四個盟友：已經付了錢的武器會延遲交貨。原因是伊朗戰爭消耗了大量美軍的彈藥和武器庫存。

波羅的海和北歐國家也受到影響。這些國家靠美國武器來保護自己，現在很擔心安全問題。

The United States has formally notified four allies — Britain, Poland, Lithuania, and Estonia — that weapons they have already paid for will face delayed delivery. The reason: the Iran war has depleted substantial ammunition and weapons stockpiles from the U.S. military. Baltic and Northern European countries are also affected by the delays. These nations rely on American weapons for their defense, and many are now concerned about their security.

Google 推出 Gemini 3.1 Ultra，刷新 AI 記憶力紀錄

來源：LLM Stats

Google 發布了最新的 AI 模型 Gemini 3.1 Ultra，它的「上下文視窗」（一次能讀多少資料）達到兩百萬 token，等於一次讀完一千五百頁文件，是目前公開模型中最大的。

這讓 Google、OpenAI 和 Anthropic 三家公司的 AI 競爭更加激烈。

Google has released its latest AI model, Gemini 3.1 Ultra, with a "context window"—the amount of data it can read at once—reaching two million tokens, equivalent to reading through fifteen hundred pages of documents in a single pass. This makes it the largest context window among publicly available models, intensifying the AI competition among Google, OpenAI, and Anthropic.

內布拉斯加率先要求醫療補助者必須工作，伊利諾十五萬家庭失去食物補助

來源：NPR

五月一日，內布拉斯加州成為全美第一個執行「醫療補助工作要求」的州：領取 Medicaid 的人每月要工作或上課八十小時，否則會失去健康保險。最多四萬人可能受影響。

同一天，伊利諾州約十五萬個家庭開始失去 SNAP 食物補助（政府幫買食物的錢），因為川普的新法案收緊了資格。

On May 1st, Nebraska became the first state in the nation to implement "Medicaid work requirements": people receiving Medicaid must work or attend school for eighty hours per month, or lose their health insurance. Up to forty thousand people could be affected. That same day, approximately one hundred fifty thousand families in Illinois began losing SNAP food assistance (government money for food purchases) because of Trump's new legislation tightening eligibility requirements.

小熊六比五險勝響尾蛇，但傷兵不斷

來源：Bleacher Nation

小熊隊五月一日以六比五擊敗響尾蛇隊，戰績來到十九勝十二敗。Michael Busch 打出兩支安打、送回兩分，是今天的功臣。

但壞消息是傷兵變多了：投手 Justin Steele 手肘再次受傷，至少休息兩週；二壘手 Nico Hoerner 因為脖子不舒服提前退場。球隊雖然在贏，但健康問題讓人擔心。

The Cubs narrowly defeated the Diamondbacks 6-5 on May 1st, improving their record to 19 wins and 12 losses. Michael Busch was the hero of the day, recording two hits and driving in two runs. But there's troubling news on the injury front: pitcher Justin Steele has suffered another elbow injury and will be sidelined for at least two weeks; second baseman Nico Hoerner left the game early due to neck discomfort. While the team is winning, the mounting health concerns are cause for worry.
