參議院罕見表決　要川普停止對伊朗的戰爭

來源：NPR

六月二十三日，美國參議院以五十票對四十八票通過一項決議，要求川普總統停止對伊朗的戰爭。

這是一九七三年以來，國會兩院第一次一起通過這種決議，有四名共和黨議員跑去支持。雖然停火已經開始，這次表決主要是象徵性的，卻是國會對總統出兵權力少見的一次反擊。

On June 23rd, the U.S. Senate passed a resolution 50-48 calling on President Trump to cease military operations against Iran.

This marks the first time since 1973 that both chambers of Congress have passed such a resolution together, with four Republican senators crossing over to support it. Although a ceasefire has already begun, the vote is largely symbolic, yet it represents a rare instance of Congress pushing back against the President's war powers.

史上最大醫療詐騙案　伊利諾州十九人被告

來源：Chicago Sun-Times

六月二十三日，美國司法部公布了史上最大的醫療詐騙（用假帳單騙錢）案，全國共有四百五十五人被告，假帳單金額超過六十五億美元。

其中十九人來自伊利諾州，包括一家橡樹草坪鎮診所的老闆，他被控向州政府的醫療補助計畫騙了大約九千五百萬美元。

On June 23rd, the U.S. Department of Justice announced the largest healthcare fraud case in history, with 455 defendants across the nation charged with submitting false medical bills totaling more than $6.5 billion.

Nineteen of those defendants are from Illinois, including the owner of a clinic in Oak Lawn, who is accused of defrauding the state's Medicaid program of approximately $95 million.

美國推新計畫　加快新藥試驗追趕中國

來源：Marginal Revolution

六月二十四日，美國衛生部推出「試驗先鋒行動」，要讓食藥署和幾個研究單位一起合作，加快新藥的試驗速度。

官員直說，這是為了追趕中國。中國把生技當成國家大事，試驗流程又快又順。美國希望把早期試驗的時間縮短半年到一年，守住領先地位。

On June 24th, the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services launched the "Trial Pioneer Initiative," designed to accelerate the pace of drug testing by bringing together the FDA and several research institutions in a coordinated effort.

Officials were blunt about the motivation: they're trying to catch up with China. China treats biotech as a national priority, with streamlined trial processes that move quickly and smoothly. The U.S. hopes to cut early-stage trial timelines by six months to a year, determined to hold onto its competitive edge.

要執照的工作變多　恐拖累就業

來源：Marginal Revolution

一份新分析指出，在美國，需要政府執照才能做的工作越來越多。一九五零年代大約只有百分之五的人需要執照，現在已經升到百分之二十五。

以前只有醫生、律師要執照，如今幾百種工作都要。分析說這讓人更難入行，也難換州工作，還把價格推高，品質卻不一定變好。

A new analysis shows that in the United States, more and more jobs require government licensing. Back in the 1950s, only about 5 percent of workers needed a license; today that figure has climbed to 25 percent.

Licensing used to be confined to doctors and lawyers, but now hundreds of occupations require it. The analysis argues this makes it harder for people to enter fields, difficult to switch states for work, and drives up prices without necessarily improving quality.

奪冠後閃人　梅伊離開密西根轉戰小牛

來源：Off Tackle Empire / ESPN

密西根大學籃球教練梅伊（Dusty May）要離開了，去當NBA達拉斯小牛隊的總教練。而他幾個月前才帶領球隊拿下全國冠軍。

他成為自一九八八年的布朗以來，第一位剛奪冠就直接跳到NBA的大學教練。這下密西根的休賽期大亂，傳出將由助理教練博因頓暫時接手。

Michigan basketball coach Dusty May is leaving to become the head coach of the NBA's Dallas Mavericks, just months after leading the team to a national championship.

He becomes the first college coach since Brown in 1988 to jump directly to the NBA immediately after winning a title. The move throws Michigan's offseason into chaos, with reports indicating that assistant coach Boynton will take over temporarily.

薩卡傷癒　趕上英格蘭世界盃

來源：Arsenal.com

兵工廠線——阿森納和英格蘭的邊鋒薩卡（Saka）確定傷癒，可以為英格蘭踢世界盃了。

他的腳跟（阿基里斯腱）從三月起就有毛病，球季尾聲一直讓人擔心。教練圖赫爾說他「準備好了，不再會痛」。薩卡是英格蘭進攻的關鍵，對球隊和國家都是好消息。

Arsenal beat — Winger Saka of Arsenal and England has been cleared to play, meaning he will be fit for England's World Cup campaign.

His heel, the Achilles tendon, had been troubling him since March, and lingering concerns dogged the end of the season. Coach Tuchel said he was "ready to go, no more pain." Saka is crucial to England's attack, good news for both club and country.
