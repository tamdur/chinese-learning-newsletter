世界財經大會在曼谷開幕，AI泡沫成焦點

來源：IMF / Nation Thailand / Bangkok Post

國際貨幣基金（IMF）和世界銀行的年會，今天在泰國曼谷開幕，會一直開到18日。來自191個國家的財政部長和中央銀行總裁都來了。泰國上一次主辦是1991年，已經過了35年。

IMF總裁喬治艾娃說，現在有三件大事在影響世界經濟：AI、很貴的能源，還有破紀錄的國家債務（欠的錢）。這一週，IMF也會公布新的金融穩定報告。

最近幾家科技大公司借了很多錢，蓋AI資料中心。有人擔心這是「泡沫」（像肥皂泡，看起來很大，卻可能一下就破掉）。大家都在看IMF會怎麼說。

The annual meetings of the International Monetary Fund (IMF) and the World Bank opened today in Bangkok, Thailand, and will run through the 18th. Finance ministers and central bank governors from 191 countries are attending. Thailand last hosted the meetings in 1991, 35 years ago. IMF Managing Director Kristalina Georgieva says three major forces are shaping the world economy right now: AI, expensive energy, and record national debt. This week, the IMF will also publish its new Global Financial Stability Report. Several big tech companies have recently borrowed heavily to build AI data centers, and some people worry this is a "bubble," like a soap bubble that looks huge but could pop at any moment. Everyone is waiting to see what the IMF will say.

諾貝爾經濟學獎今天公布，誰會得獎？

來源：NobelPrize.org / NPR / Polymarket

今年諾貝爾獎的最後一個獎——經濟學獎，今天在瑞典公布，時間是芝加哥清晨4點45分。我們寫這篇文章的時候，得獎名單還沒出來。

大家猜誰會得獎呢？在預測網站上，研究市場和價格的帕克斯（Ariel Pakes）最被看好，大約有21%的機會。史丹佛的艾希（Susan Athey）、研究人為什麼會做出不聰明決定的學者，還有研究央行政策的伍德福（Michael Woodford），也都有人看好。

去年的獎頒給了莫基爾、阿吉翁和霍伊特，因為他們解釋了新發明怎麼讓經濟一直成長。今年會輪到誰呢？

The last prize of this year's Nobel season, the economics prize, is being announced today in Sweden, at 4:45 a.m. Chicago time. As we write this, the winners have not yet been named, so who will take it? On prediction markets, Ariel Pakes, who studies markets and prices, is the favorite, with roughly a 21% chance. Susan Athey of Stanford, a scholar of why people sometimes make unwise decisions, and Michael Woodford, who studies central bank policy, also have their backers. Last year's prize went to Joel Mokyr, Philippe Aghion and Peter Howitt, who explained how new inventions keep economies growing. Whose turn is it this year?

熊隊在綠灣大敗，7比31輸給包裝工

來源：ESPN / Chicago Sun-Times

芝加哥熊隊星期天到綠灣挑戰包裝工隊，結果以7比31大輸。這是熊隊2006年以來第一次在蘭博球場被看好會贏，沒想到卻輸得這麼慘。

代替受傷的威廉斯先發的四分衛巴根特，傳了21次球只成功12次，只推進87碼，還兩次被對手把球搶走，第四節就被換下場。熊隊全場一共丟掉5次球。

包裝工四分衛洛夫傳球推進367碼。兩隊現在都是3勝2敗。好消息是，教練強森說，威廉斯快要回來了。

The Chicago Bears traveled to Green Bay on Sunday to take on the Packers and lost badly, 31 to 7. It was the first time since 2006 that the Bears had been picked to win at Lambeau Field, and they fell apart completely. Starting in place of the injured Williams, quarterback Bagent threw 21 passes and completed only 12 of them, gaining just 87 yards. He had the ball taken away by the defense twice, and he was pulled from the game in the fourth quarter. The Bears turned the ball over five times in all. Packers quarterback Love threw for 367 yards. Both teams are now 3-2. The one bit of good news came from Coach Johnson, who said Williams is close to coming back.

兵工廠明天迎戰里爾，歐冠首場主場戰

來源：Arsenal.com / UEFA / Sports Mole

兵工廠線——兵工廠星期二要在酋長球場迎戰法國的里爾。這是本季歐洲冠軍聯賽第一場主場比賽，芝加哥時間下午2點開踢。

兵工廠上一場歐冠在客場1比0贏了拿坡里，週末又在落後之後追回來，2比1打敗里茲。里爾第一輪卻以2比3輸給西班牙的貝蒂斯。

壞消息是，後衛因卡皮耶在打完里茲後收操時扭到腳踝，被扶下場，明天能不能上場還不確定。外界猜教練阿特塔會換掉好幾名先發球員，讓主力休息。

The Arsenal beat: Arsenal host France's Lille at the Emirates Stadium on Tuesday. It is the club's first home Champions League match of the season, with kickoff at 2 p.m. Chicago time. Arsenal's last Champions League game was a 1-0 away win over Napoli, and at the weekend they came from behind to beat Leeds 2-1. Lille, for their part, lost 3-2 to Spain's Real Betis in their opener. The bad news is that defender Piero Hincapié had to be helped off after rolling his ankle during the cool-down following the Leeds game, and it is still uncertain whether he will be available tomorrow. Observers expect manager Mikel Arteta to rotate several starters and rest his key players.
