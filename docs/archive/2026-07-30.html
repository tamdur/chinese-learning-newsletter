美國利率不動，股市大跌

來源：CNBC

美國的中央銀行（聯準會）在七月二十九日決定利率不動，維持在百分之三點五到三點七五。但這次有三位官員投下反對票，認為應該馬上升息。

聯準會說，物價漲得太快，已經連續五年超過百分之二的目標，中東打仗讓油價變貴也是原因之一。

消息一出，道瓊指數當天跌了一千一百五十三點，三十年期公債的利率升到百分之五點二一，是十九年來最高。這表示美國人買房子借錢，要付更多利息。

The US central bank (the Federal Reserve) decided on July 29th to hold interest rates steady at 3.5 to 3.75 percent. However, three officials dissented, believing rates should be raised immediately.

The Federal Reserve said prices are rising too fast, having exceeded the 2 percent target for five consecutive years, and noted that the war in the Middle East pushing up oil prices is one of the causes.

When the news broke, the Dow Jones fell 1,153 points that day, and the 30-year Treasury yield rose to 5.21 percent, the highest in 19 years. That means Americans borrowing money to buy a home will pay more interest.

日本熊本強震，至少二十三人死亡

來源：Washington Post

七月二十八日下午，日本南部的熊本縣發生規模七點一的大地震。到三十日為止，至少二十三人死亡、六十三人受傷。

災情最重的是嘉島町的永旺購物中心。地震時裡面大約有三千人，大家疏散到停車場後，商場另一邊發生瓦斯爆炸，一大片建築倒了下來。

政府要二十六萬人撤離。救難隊日夜搜救，因為受困的人多半要在七十二小時內找到，活下來的機會才大。

On the afternoon of July 28th, a magnitude 7.1 earthquake struck Kumamoto Prefecture in southern Japan. As of the 30th, at least twenty-three people have died and sixty-three have been injured.

The hardest-hit site was the AEON shopping mall in Kashima. About three thousand people were inside during the earthquake. After everyone had evacuated to the parking lot, a gas explosion occurred on the other side of the mall and a large section of the building collapsed.

The government has ordered 260,000 people to evacuate. Rescue teams are searching day and night, because those trapped are far more likely to survive if they are found within seventy-two hours.

藏在文件裡的AI病毒會自己傳染

來源：Hacker News

資安研究員公布一種新的攻擊方法：把指令藏在普通的Word文件裡，用白色的字或註解讓人看不見。當你打開檔案，請微軟的Copilot幫忙寫摘要（重點整理），這個助手就會乖乖照著指令做事。

更麻煩的是，它還會把同樣的指令寫進助手產生的下一份文件，文件寄給誰就傳染給誰。

這種攻擊沒有任何程式碼，只是一段文字，所以傳統的防毒軟體很難擋。這則消息在Hacker News上得到三百五十三分。

Security researchers have disclosed a new attack method: hiding instructions inside ordinary Word documents, using white text or comments to make them invisible. When you open the file and ask Microsoft's Copilot to write a summary, the assistant obediently follows the hidden instructions.

What is worse, it also writes those same instructions into the next document the assistant produces, so whoever the document is sent to gets infected in turn.

This attack contains no program code at all — just a passage of text — which makes it very hard for traditional antivirus software to block. The story scored 353 points on Hacker News.

佛奇拒絕回答，參議院要投票追究

來源：NPR

七月二十九日，美國防疫專家佛奇（Anthony Fauci）到參議院說明新冠病毒的來源，卻一個問題都不回答。他超過一百次引用憲法第五修正案，這條規定人民可以不說出對自己不利的話。

佛奇說，主持會議的參議員保羅（Rand Paul）是想設陷阱，讓他說錯話後被告偽證（說謊作證），還罵對方失控。

保羅則宣布，下星期要開會投票，控告佛奇藐視（不理會）國會。

On July 29th, American public health expert Anthony Fauci appeared before the Senate to explain the origins of the coronavirus, but refused to answer a single question. He invoked the Fifth Amendment more than one hundred times — the constitutional provision that lets people decline to say anything that could incriminate them.

Fauci said that Senator Rand Paul, who chaired the hearing, was trying to set a trap so that he would misspeak and then be charged with perjury (lying under oath), and he accused Paul of being out of control.

Paul, for his part, announced that next week he would convene a vote to charge Fauci with contempt of Congress.

密西根新教頭首次面對大十聯盟媒體

來源：On3

大十聯盟的媒體日七月二十八日到三十日在芝加哥舉行。密西根大學今天中午上台，新總教練惠廷漢（Kyle Whittingham）第一次以密大教頭的身分面對記者。

他在猶他大學帶隊二十一年，這次接手的卻是一支剛經過醜聞（不光彩的事）、連體育主任都辭職的球隊。

和他一起出席的有四分衛安德伍德、跑衛馬歇爾和防守球員皮爾斯。大家最想知道的，是這支球隊能不能重新站起來。

The Big Ten's media days are being held in Chicago from July 28th to 30th. The University of Michigan took the stage at noon today, with new head coach Kyle Whittingham facing reporters for the first time as Michigan's head coach.

He led the team at the University of Utah for twenty-one years, but the side he is taking over now has just been through a scandal — and even its athletic director has resigned.

Appearing alongside him were quarterback Underwood, running back Marshall, and defensive player Pierce. What everyone most wants to know is whether this team can get back on its feet.

阿爾特塔：兵工廠需要更好的球員

來源：Arseblog News

兵工廠線——英超冠軍兵工廠的總教練阿爾特塔（Mikel Arteta）這個夏天第一次公開談球員買賣。他說：「我們想把這家球會帶到不同的層次，這需要更好的陣容、更好的球員。」

今年夏天，球隊只簽下門將梅斯利耶和邊鋒佐利斯，卻送走三名球員，走的比來的多。

阿爾特塔說，他們已經看清楚哪些位置可以加強，很快就會有「具體的動作」。

The Arsenal beat — Mikel Arteta, manager of Premier League champions Arsenal, spoke publicly about transfers for the first time this summer. He said: "We want to take this club to a different level, and that requires a better squad and better individuals."

This summer the team has signed only goalkeeper Illan Meslier and winger Christos Tzolis, while sending three players away — more departures than arrivals.

Arteta said they have already worked out which positions can be strengthened, and that "concrete moves" will come very soon.
