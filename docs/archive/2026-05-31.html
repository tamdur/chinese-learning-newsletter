熊隊球場法案今天到期，搬到印第安納？

來源：Chicago Sun-Times

伊利諾州議會今天（5月31日）是最後一天開會，芝加哥熊隊的新球場法案還沒通過。法案要用公共經費在 Arlington Heights 蓋新球場，但州長和市長意見不同。

熊隊警告：如果法案沒過，他們會考慮搬到印第安納州的 Hammond。議員正在想辦法縮小法案範圍，希望能在午夜前投票通過。

Illinois' legislative session ends today, May 31st, and Chicago's Bears stadium bill hasn't passed. The proposal would use public funds to build a new stadium in Arlington Heights, but the governor and mayor disagree on the approach. The Bears have warned that without the bill's passage, they'll consider relocating to Hammond, Indiana. Lawmakers are scrambling to pare down the legislation, hoping to secure a vote before midnight.

美國警告伊朗：不簽就打

來源：CBS News / CNN

美國國防部長 Hegseth 在新加坡的安全會議上說，如果和伊朗的停火談判（雙方談好不再打）失敗，美軍隨時可以再打。

川普要求伊朗銷毀440公斤的高純度鈾（做核武的材料），才願意簽協議。自二月荷莫茲海峽被封鎖以來，油價因停火希望已跌了兩成。

Defense Secretary Hegseth said at a security conference in Singapore that if ceasefire negotiations with Iran fail, the U.S. military is ready to strike again. Trump is demanding that Iran destroy 440 kilograms of highly enriched uranium—the material needed for nuclear weapons—before he will sign any agreement. Since the Strait of Hormuz was blockaded in February, oil prices have already fallen 20 percent on hopes for a ceasefire.

以色列攻入黎巴嫩，占領古城堡

來源：Times of Israel

以色列軍隊在黎巴嫩南部占領了有名的博福特城堡，這是十字軍時代蓋的古堡，地點很重要。軍隊過了利塔尼河，正向真主黨的重要據點 Nabatieh 推進。

同一天，真主黨用無人機打死了一名以色列士兵。這是以軍26年來第一次拿下這座城堡。

Israeli forces have taken control of the famous Beaufort Castle in southern Lebanon, an ancient fortress built during the Crusades that holds significant strategic value. The military has crossed the Litani River and is advancing toward Nabatieh, a key Hezbollah stronghold. On the same day, Hezbollah killed an Israeli soldier with a drone strike. This marks the first time Israeli forces have captured the castle in 26 years.

無人機打中歐洲最大核電廠

來源：IAEA / Times of Israel

烏克蘭的札波羅熱核電廠是歐洲最大的核電廠，有六座反應爐（產生核能的設備）。一架無人機打中了第六號機組的渦輪大樓，牆壁被炸出一個洞。

國際原子能總署說核心安全系統沒有受損，沒有輻射外洩，但警告攻擊核電廠就像「玩火」。這是兩年來第一次有無人機打進電廠範圍。

Ukraine's Zaporizhzhia Nuclear Power Plant is Europe's largest, with six reactors that generate nuclear energy. A drone struck the turbine building of Unit 6, blasting a hole in the wall. The International Atomic Energy Agency said the core safety systems were undamaged and there was no radiation leak, but warned that attacks on nuclear plants are like "playing with fire." This is the first time in two years that a drone has penetrated the plant's perimeter.

小熊終於贏了！十連敗結束

來源：Yahoo Sports

芝加哥小熊終於結束了痛苦的十連敗！外野手 Ian Happ 打出三分全壘打，整場送回五分，帶領小熊以10比4大勝海盜隊。

小熊接著又贏了系列最後一場，目前戰績31勝26敗，在國聯中區排第二。他們是大聯盟史上第二支同一季有兩次十連勝又有十連敗的球隊。

The Chicago Cubs have finally ended their agonizing ten-game losing streak! Outfielder Ian Happ hit a three-run home run and drove in five runs total, leading the Cubs to a dominant 10-4 victory over the Pirates. The Cubs followed up with another win in the series finale and now stand at 31-26, sitting in second place in the NL Central. They are the second team in Major League history to experience both a ten-game winning streak and a ten-game losing streak in the same season.
