川普本週四訪北京，美中高峰會談貿易與伊朗

來源：CNBC

美國總統川普將在週四抵達北京，與中國國家主席習近平舉行為期兩天的高峰會。雙方要談的事很多：關稅（進出口的額外費用）、台灣、伊朗戰爭，還有AI規則。

川普還帶了一群大企業老闆同行，包括馬斯克和蘋果的Tim Cook。今天美中官員已經在首爾開始準備會議，討論貿易問題。這是今年最重要的一場外交會面。

President Trump will arrive in Beijing on Thursday for a two-day summit with Chinese President Xi Jinping. There is much to discuss: tariffs (additional fees on imports and exports), Taiwan, the Iran conflict, and AI regulations. Trump is also bringing a delegation of major business executives, including Elon Musk and Apple's Tim Cook. US and Chinese officials have already begun preparing for the meeting in Seoul today, discussing trade issues. This is the year's most important diplomatic meeting.

四家中國AI公司十二天內推出開放模型，挑戰西方

來源：Digital Bright Future

四月底到五月初，短短十二天內，中國四家AI公司接連發布新的開放程式碼模型：DeepSeek V4、Kimi K2.6、GLM-5.1和MiniMax M2.7。這些模型在寫程式的測試中，表現和美國最強的模型差不多。

更重要的是，使用這些中國模型的費用只要西方的三分之一。同時有這麼多公司一起推出高水準的模型，這在以前從來沒有發生過。

Between late April and early May, within a span of just twelve days, four Chinese AI companies released new open-source models in rapid succession: DeepSeek V4, Kimi K.2.6, GLM-5.1, and MiniMax M2.7. In coding benchmarks, these models performed comparably to America's strongest models. More significantly, using these Chinese models costs only one-third as much as Western alternatives. This marks the first time so many companies have simultaneously released high-quality models at this level.

川普拒絕伊朗停火提案，和平談判危機加深

來源：CNN

美國與伊朗的停火談判出了大問題。川普說伊朗提出的十四點方案「完全不能接受」，還形容停火正在「靠大量急救維持生命」，只有百分之一的機會能活下來。

伊朗方面回應說「已做好一切準備」。有消息指出，川普正在更認真地考慮恢復（重新開始）軍事行動。這場危機也會成為他這週和習近平會面的重要話題。

US-Iran ceasefire negotiations have hit a major snag. Trump said Iran's fourteen-point proposal is "completely unacceptable" and described the ceasefire as being kept alive on "massive life support," with only a one percent chance of survival. Iran responded by saying it is "fully prepared." Reports indicate that Trump is seriously considering resuming military operations. The crisis is also expected to be a major topic when he meets with Xi Jinping this week.

熊隊球場法案過了眾議院，但球隊說不夠

來源：CBS Chicago

芝加哥熊隊想蓋一座新球場，但到底蓋在哪裡還沒定。伊利諾州眾議院以 78 比 32 票通過了一個大型建設法案，希望把球隊留在阿靈頓高地。但熊隊說這個方案還不夠好。

另一邊，印第安納州的哈蒙德市願意用超過十億美元的公共資金幫熊隊蓋新球場。伊利諾州參議院必須在五月底前做出決定，不然熊隊可能真的要搬家了。

The Chicago Bears want to build a new stadium, but where it will be located remains undecided. The Illinois House of Representatives passed a major construction bill 78-32, hoping to keep the team in Arlington Heights. However, the Bears say the proposal isn't good enough. Meanwhile, the city of Hammond, Indiana is willing to use over a billion dollars in public funds to help the Bears build a new stadium. The Illinois Senate must make a decision by the end of May, or the Bears may actually relocate.

小熊隊十連勝被中斷，今晚挑戰亞特蘭大

來源：ESPN

芝加哥小熊隊本季第二次十連勝被德州遊騎兵隊終結了。遊騎兵連續兩場把小熊完封（讓對手零分），比數分別是 3-0 和 6-0。

小熊目前戰績 27 勝 12 敗，是自 1955 年布魯克林道奇隊以來，第一支在開季四十場內拿下兩次十連勝的球隊。今晚他們到亞特蘭大打客場，看能不能重新找回火力。

The Chicago Cubs' second ten-game winning streak of the season was snapped by the Texas Rangers. The Rangers shut out the Cubs in back-to-back games, winning 3-0 and 6-0. The Cubs now stand at 27 wins and 12 losses, making them the first team since the Brooklyn Dodgers in 1955 to record two separate ten-game winning streaks within their first forty games of the season. Tonight they head to Atlanta for a road game, hoping to reignite their offense.

兵工廠 1-0 勝西漢姆，再贏兩場就奪冠

來源：Sky Sports

兵工廠線——兵工廠靠 Trossard 在第 83 分鐘的進球，客場 1-0 擊敗西漢姆。最刺激的是第 95 分鐘，西漢姆的 Callum Wilson 踢進了追平球，但VAR（影片輔助裁判）判定犯規，進球不算。

兵工廠現在領先第二名五分，只剩兩場比賽。再贏兩場，就能拿到自 2004 年以來的第一座英超冠軍。

Arsenal's Trossard scored in the 83rd minute to secure a 1-0 away victory over West Ham. The drama peaked in the 95th minute when West Ham's Callum Wilson appeared to level the score, but VAR ruled it a foul and disallowed the goal. Arsenal now leads second place by five points with just two matches remaining. Two more wins will bring them their first Premier League title since 2004.
