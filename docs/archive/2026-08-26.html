調查：伊利諾州警察暗中幫忙遣送移民

來源：Injustice Watch / The Intercept

伊利諾州有一條「信任法」，不准地方警察幫聯邦移民局抓移民。可是八月二十五日發表的一份調查發現，全州一百零二個縣裡，超過三分之一的警察、警長和檢察官都在偷偷幫忙。

從二零二五年一月到現在，至少有一百五十人因此被送進遣送程序。做法包括告訴移民局某人什麼時候放出來，或在監獄門口交人。

這條法律最大的漏洞是沒有人檢查，受害的人只能在被遣送之後自己去告。

Illinois has a "TRUST Act" that prohibits local police from helping federal immigration authorities arrest immigrants. But an investigation published on August 25 found that across the state's 102 counties, more than a third of police officers, sheriffs and prosecutors have been quietly helping anyway.

From January 2025 until now, at least 150 people have been routed into deportation proceedings as a result. The methods include tipping ICE off about when someone is due to be released, or handing people over at the jailhouse door.

The law's biggest loophole is that nobody checks: the people harmed can only sue on their own after they have already been deported.

湯森路透推出自家法律AI模型

來源：Thomson Reuters / Law.com

有一百七十五年歷史的湯森路透，八月二十四日推出自己的大型語言模型，名字就叫「湯森」。

公司花了兩年、大約四千萬美元，把旗下法律資料庫Westlaw幾十年的判決和文章，疊在一個開放的基礎模型上，再請自家的律師和編輯幫忙訓練。

這是老牌資料公司不甘心只當AI公司客戶的最好例子。他們賭的不是模型多聰明，而是別人拿不到的資料。

Thomson Reuters, a company with 175 years of history, launched its own large language model on August 24, named simply "Thomson."

The company spent two years and roughly $40 million layering decades of court decisions and articles from its Westlaw legal database onto an open base model, then had its own lawyers and editors help train it.

It is the clearest example yet of an old-line data company refusing to be merely a customer of the AI firms. Their bet isn't on how clever the model is — it's on data nobody else can get.

俄軍無人機兩星期內三度炸邊界關口

來源：Kyiv Independent

八月二十五日深夜，一架俄羅斯無人機打中烏克蘭南部的維諾格拉迪夫卡關口，海關設施受損，人和車都得改走別的關口。

這是兩個星期內第三次。烏克蘭外交部長西比加說，這是「很明顯的模式」，俄國是故意要切斷烏克蘭往西邊的出口。

關口對面的摩爾多瓦是歐盟候選國，不是北約成員，幾乎沒有防空能力，已經多次抗議領空被侵犯。

Late on the night of August 25, a Russian drone struck the Vynohradivka border crossing in southern Ukraine, damaging customs facilities and forcing people and vehicles to reroute through other crossings.

It was the third such strike in two weeks. Ukrainian Foreign Minister Sybiha called it "an obvious pattern," saying Russia is deliberately trying to cut off Ukraine's westward exits.

Moldova, on the other side of the crossing, is an EU candidate country but not a NATO member. With almost no air defenses of its own, it has repeatedly protested violations of its airspace.

格陵蘭冰川斷出曼哈頓大小的冰島

來源：Weather.com / ESA

歐洲太空總署的衛星照片顯示，格陵蘭西北邊的彼得曼冰川在八月四日斷掉一大塊，面積七十六點四平方公里，跟紐約曼哈頓差不多大，最厚有一百五十公尺。

這是這座冰川自二零一二年以來最大的一次崩解。

這塊冰本來就浮在海上，所以海平面不會馬上上升。可是浮冰像煞車，擋住後面的冰；那裡的冰如果全化掉，海平面會升高約三十公分。科學家說，真正的原因是溫暖的海水鑽到冰底下。

Satellite images from the European Space Agency show that the Petermann Glacier in northwestern Greenland shed a huge slab on August 4 — 76.4 square kilometers in area, about the size of Manhattan, and up to 150 meters thick.

It is the glacier's largest calving event since 2012.

The ice was already floating on the sea, so sea level will not rise immediately. But floating ice acts like a brake, holding back the ice behind it; if all the ice in that basin melted, sea level would rise by about 30 centimeters. Scientists say the real cause is warm ocean water working its way in beneath the ice.

熊隊最後一練：萊特受傷，強森召集開會

來源：Windy City Gridiron

芝加哥熊隊八月二十五日進行訓練營最後一場開放球迷觀看的練習。先發右邊鋒（保護四分衛的進攻線球員）萊特練到一半，按著右手臂走回室內。

更受注意的是練習中途，隊上最好的防守球員、角衛強森把大家叫過來開會，說今天練得還不夠好。近端鋒克梅特說，之後那是整個訓練營裡比較好的一段。

星期六對泰坦隊打完熱身賽，熊隊就要裁到五十三人。

The Chicago Bears held the last fan-attended practice of training camp on August 25. Starting right tackle Wright — the offensive lineman who protects the quarterback — walked back inside partway through, holding his right arm.

What drew more attention came mid-practice, when cornerback Johnson, the team's best defensive player, called everyone together for a meeting and said the day's work wasn't good enough. Tight end Kmet said the stretch that followed was one of the better ones of the whole camp.

After Saturday's preseason game against the Titans, the Bears have to cut down to 53 players.

兵工廠線——阿森納八月二十五日正式簽下英格蘭後衛康薩，價錢五千五百萬英鎊，比當初傳出的多了四百萬，合約四年，還可以再加一年。

But the registration paperwork didn't make it in time for the opening match, so the first time he pulls on an Arsenal shirt will most likely be August 31 at Villa Park, against the Villa side that sold him and in front of the fans who watched him play for five years.
