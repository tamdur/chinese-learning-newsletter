般若波羅蜜多心經

無眼耳鼻舌身意，無色聲香味觸法，無眼界，乃至無意識界。無無明，亦無無明盡，乃至無老死，亦無老死盡。

There are no eyes, ears, nose, tongue, body, or mind; no sights, sounds, smells, tastes, touches, or objects of mind; no realm of sight, and so on up to no realm of consciousness. There is no ignorance and no end of ignorance, and so on up to no old age and death and no end of old age and death.

孟子：離婁下

逄蒙學射於羿，盡羿之道，思天下惟羿為愈己，於是殺羿。孟子曰：「是亦羿有罪焉。」公明儀曰：「宜若無罪焉。」曰：「薄乎云爾，惡得無罪？鄭人使子濯孺子侵衛，衛使庾公之斯追之。子濯孺子曰：『今日我疾作，不可以執弓，吾死矣夫！』問其僕曰：『追我者誰也？』其僕曰：『庾公之斯也。』曰：『吾生矣。』其僕曰：『庾公之斯，衛之善射者也，夫子曰「吾生」，何謂也？』曰：『庾公之斯學射於尹公之他，尹公之他學射於我。夫尹公之他，端人也，其取友必端矣。』庾公之斯至，曰：『夫子何為不執弓？』曰：『今日我疾作，不可以執弓。』曰：『小人學射於尹公之他，尹公之他學射於夫子。我不忍以夫子之道反害夫子。雖然，今日之事，君事也，我不敢廢。』抽矢扣輪，去其金，發乘矢而後反。」

Feng Meng studied archery under Yi. When he had mastered all of Yi's techniques, he reflected that the only person in the world superior to himself was Yi, and so he killed Yi. Mencius said: "Yi himself bears some blame in this." Gongming Yi said: "It would seem he was blameless." Mencius said: "The blame is slight, that is all — how can he be without blame? The people of Zheng sent Zi Zhuo Ruzi to raid Wei, and Wei sent Yu Gong Zhi Si to pursue him. Zi Zhuo Ruzi said: 'Today my illness has flared up and I cannot hold my bow. I am a dead man!' He asked his charioteer: 'Who is pursuing me?' His charioteer said: 'Yu Gong Zhi Si.' He said: 'Then I shall live.' His charioteer said: 'Yu Gong Zhi Si is the best archer in Wei. How can you say you will live?' He said: 'Yu Gong Zhi Si learned archery from Yin Gong Zhi Ta, and Yin Gong Zhi Ta learned archery from me. Now Yin Gong Zhi Ta is an upright man, and those he befriends must be upright as well.' When Yu Gong Zhi Si arrived, he said: 'Master, why are you not holding your bow?' He said: 'Today my illness has flared up and I cannot hold my bow.' Yu Gong Zhi Si said: 'I learned archery from Yin Gong Zhi Ta, and Yin Gong Zhi Ta learned from you. I cannot bear to use your own art to harm you. Nevertheless, today's affair is my lord's business, and I dare not abandon it.' He drew out his arrows, knocked off their metal tips against the wheel, shot four arrows without their heads, and then turned back."

六祖大師法寶壇經：頓漸品第八

時，祖師居曹溪寶林，神秀大師在荊南玉泉寺。于時兩宗盛化，人皆稱南能北秀，故有南北二宗頓漸之分，而學者莫知宗趣。師謂眾曰：「法本一宗，人有南北。法即一種，見有遲疾。何名頓漸？法無頓漸，人有利鈍，故名頓漸。」然秀之徒眾，往往譏南宗祖師，不識一字，有何所長。秀曰：「他得無師之智，深悟上乘。吾不如也。且吾師五祖，親傳衣法。豈徒然哉！吾恨不能遠去親近，虛受國恩。汝等諸人，毋滯於此，可往曹溪參決。

At that time, the Sixth Patriarch was residing at Baolin Monastery in Caoxi, while Great Master Shenxiu was at Yuquan Temple in Jingnan. Both schools were flourishing, and people commonly referred to them as 'Huineng of the South' and 'Shenxiu of the North,' giving rise to the distinction between the Southern and Northern schools and their 'sudden' versus 'gradual' approaches. Yet students could not discern the true meaning of either school. The Master addressed the assembly: 'The Dharma is originally one school; it is people who come from south or north. The Dharma is of one kind; it is perception that is slow or fast. Why speak of sudden and gradual? The Dharma itself has no sudden or gradual — people are sharp or dull, hence the names sudden and gradual.' Meanwhile, Shenxiu's followers frequently disparaged the Patriarch of the Southern school, saying he could not read a single character and had nothing noteworthy about him. Shenxiu said: 'He has attained the wisdom that needs no teacher and has deeply awakened to the Supreme Vehicle. I am not his equal. Moreover, our teacher the Fifth Patriarch personally transmitted the robe and Dharma to him — how could that be without reason? I regret only that I cannot travel far to be near him, and that I receive imperial favor in vain. All of you should not linger here — go to Caoxi and seek the decisive teaching.'
