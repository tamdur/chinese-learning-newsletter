般若波羅蜜多心經

Heart Sutra

觀自在菩薩，行深般若波羅蜜多時，照見五蘊皆空，度一切苦厄。

Avalokitesvara Bodhisattva, when practicing the profound Prajna Paramita, illuminated the five aggregates and saw that they are all empty, thus overcoming all suffering and distress.

孟子・離婁上

Mencius

樂正子從於子敖之齊。樂正子見孟子。孟子曰：「子亦來見我乎？」曰：「先生何為出此言也？」曰：「子來幾日矣？」曰：「昔昔。」曰：「昔昔，則我出此言也，不亦宜乎？」曰：「舍館未定。」曰：「子聞之也，舍館定，然後求見長者乎？」曰：「克有罪。」孟子謂樂正子曰：「子之從於子敖來，徒餔啜也。我不意子學古之道，而以餔啜也。」孟子曰：「不孝有三，無後為大。舜不告而娶，為無後也，君子以為猶告也。」

Yue Zhengzi followed Prince Ao to Qi. Yue Zhengzi went to see Mencius. Mencius said: "So you too have come to see me?" Yue Zhengzi said: "Master, why do you say such a thing?" Mencius asked: "How many days have you been here?" Yue Zhengzi replied: "I arrived the day before yesterday." Mencius said: "The day before yesterday? Then is it not fitting that I should say such a thing?" Yue Zhengzi said: "I had not yet settled my lodgings." Mencius said: "Have you ever heard that one must first settle one's lodgings before seeking an audience with an elder?" Yue Zhengzi said: "I was in the wrong." Mencius said to Yue Zhengzi: "You came here following Prince Ao merely for the food and drink. I did not expect that you had studied the Way of the ancients only to pursue food and drink." Mencius said: "There are three things that are unfilial, and having no heir is the greatest. Shun married without telling his parents because he feared having no heir. The gentleman considers this the same as if he had told them."

六祖大師法寶壇經・機緣品第七

Platform Sutra

以知五蘊法，及以蘊中我，外現眾色象，一一音聲相，平等如夢幻，不起凡聖見，不作涅槃解，二邊三際斷。常應諸根用，而不起用想，分別一切法，不起分別想。劫火燒海底，風鼓山相擊，真常寂滅樂，涅槃相如是。吾今彊言說，令汝捨邪見，汝勿隨言解，許汝知少分。」

志道聞偈大悟，踊躍作禮而退。

行思禪師，生吉州安城劉氏。

‘In an instant there is no mark of arising; in an instant there is no mark of ceasing. There is no further arising-and-ceasing to be extinguished—and so quiescent cessation is manifest right now. At the very moment of this manifestation, there is no quantity of manifestation to be measured—this is what is called permanent bliss. This bliss has no receiver, nor is there a non-receiver. How could there be such a thing as one substance with five functions? Much less should you say that nirvana suppresses all dharmas and prevents them from ever arising.
