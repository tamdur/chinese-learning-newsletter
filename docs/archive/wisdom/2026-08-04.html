般若波羅蜜多心經

Heart Sutra · Tuesday segment (2 of 7)

舍利子，色不異空，空不異色，色即是空，空即是色，受想行識，亦復如是。

Shariputra, form is not different from emptiness, emptiness is not different from form. Form is emptiness, emptiness is form. The same is true of feelings, perceptions, mental formations, and consciousness.

孟子・滕文公下

Mencius · 滕文公下 · passage 118 of 269

曰：「仲子，齊之世家也。兄戴，蓋祿萬鍾。以兄之祿為不義之祿而不食也，以兄之室為不義之室而不居也，辟兄離母，處於於陵。他日歸，則有饋其兄生鵝者，己頻顣曰：『惡用是鶃鶃者為哉？』他日，其母殺是鵝也，與之食之。其兄自外至，曰：『是鶃鶃之肉也。』出而哇之。以母則不食，以妻則食之；以兄之室則弗居，以於陵則居之。是尚為能充其類也乎？若仲子者，蚓而後充其操者也。」

Mencius said: "Zhongzi belongs to one of Qi's most prominent families. His elder brother Dai received an emolument of ten thousand zhong from the fief of Gai. Zhongzi regarded his brother's emolument as unrighteous and would not eat from it; he regarded his brother's house as unrighteous and would not live in it. He left his brother and mother and went to live in Yuling. One day he returned home and found that someone had given his brother a live goose. He knit his brows and said: 'What use is this honking thing?' Another day, his mother killed the goose and served it to him, and he ate it. His brother came in from outside and said: 'That is the meat of that honking thing.' Zhongzi went out and vomited it up. He will not eat what his mother provides, but he eats what his wife provides; he will not live in his brother's house, but he lives in Yuling. Can this be considered fully carrying out his principles? Someone like Zhongzi — only by becoming an earthworm could he truly fulfill his standards."

六祖大師法寶壇經・機緣品第七

Platform Sutra · 機緣品第七 · passage 118 of 178

師自黃梅得法，回至韶州曹侯村，人無知者。有儒士劉志略，禮遇甚厚。志略有姑為尼，名無盡藏，常誦《大涅槃經》。師暫聽，即知妙義，遂為解說。尼乃執卷問字，師曰：「字即不識，義即請問。」尼曰：「字尚不識，焉能會義？」師曰：「諸佛妙理，非關文字。」尼驚異之，遍告里中耆德云：「此是有道之士，宜請供養。」有魏武侯玄孫曹叔良及居民，競來瞻禮。時，寶林古寺，自隋末兵火已廢，遂於故基重建梵宇，延師居之。

After the Master received the Dharma at Huangmei, he returned to Caohou Village in Shaozhou. No one there knew of him. There was a Confucian scholar named Liu Zhilue who treated him with great courtesy and respect. Zhilue had an aunt who was a nun named Wujinzang ('Inexhaustible Treasury'). She constantly recited the Mahaparinirvana Sutra. After listening briefly, the Master immediately understood its subtle meaning and proceeded to explain it to her. The nun then held up the text and asked about specific characters. The Master said: 'I cannot read the characters, but please ask me about the meaning.' The nun said: 'If you cannot even read the characters, how can you understand the meaning?' The Master said: 'The wondrous principle of all Buddhas has nothing to do with written words.' The nun was astonished, and she told all the village elders: 'This is a man who has attained the Way. We should invite him to stay and make offerings to him.' A descendant of Marquis Cao of the Wei — Cao Shuliang — along with local residents came eagerly to pay respects and bow. At that time the ancient Baolin Monastery, which had been destroyed by fire during the wars at the end of the Sui dynasty, was rebuilt on its old foundations, and the Master was invited to reside there.
