般若波羅蜜多心經

觀自在菩薩，行深般若波羅蜜多時，照見五蘊皆空，度一切苦厄。

Avalokitesvara Bodhisattva, when practicing the profound Prajna Paramita, illuminated the five aggregates and saw that they are all empty, thus overcoming all suffering and distress.

孟子：告子下

淳于髡曰：「先名實者，為人也；後名實者，自為也。夫子在三卿之中，名實未加於上下而去之，仁者固如此乎？」孟子曰：「居下位，不以賢事不肖者，伯夷也；五就湯，五就桀者，伊尹也；不惡汙君，不辭小官者，柳下惠也。三子者不同道，其趨一也。一者何也？曰：仁也。君子亦仁而已矣，何必同？」

Chunyu Kun said: 'Those who put reputation and substance first are acting for others; those who put them last are acting for themselves. You, Master, were ranked among the three senior ministers, yet before your reputation and achievements could benefit either the ruler above or the people below, you left. Is this really how a benevolent man behaves?' Mencius replied: 'One who holds a low position and refuses to serve an unworthy lord with his talents—that was Bo Yi. One who went to Tang five times and to Jie five times—that was Yi Yin. One who did not disdain a corrupt lord or decline a petty office—that was Liuxia Hui. These three men followed different paths, but their destination was the same. And what was that? Benevolence. A gentleman need only be benevolent. Why must everyone follow the same path?'

金剛般若波羅蜜經：不受不貪分第二十八

「須菩提！若菩薩以滿恆河沙等世界七寶持用布施；若復有人，知一切法無我，得成於忍，此菩薩勝前菩薩所得功德。何以故？須菩提！以諸菩薩不受福德故。」須菩提白佛言：「世尊！云何菩薩不受福德？」「須菩提！菩薩所作福德，不應貪著。是故說不受福德。」

"Subhuti, if a bodhisattva used the seven treasures filling worlds as numerous as the sands of the Ganges for giving; and if another person, knowing that all dharmas are without self, perfected patience — this bodhisattva's merit would surpass that of the former. Why? Subhuti, because bodhisattvas do not cling to merit." Subhuti addressed the Buddha: "World-Honored One, how is it that bodhisattvas do not cling to merit?" "Subhuti, a bodhisattva should not be attached to the merit they create. Therefore it is said they do not cling to merit."
