般若波羅蜜多心經

無罣礙故，無有恐怖，遠離顛倒夢想，究竟涅槃。三世諸佛，依般若波羅蜜多故，得阿耨多羅三藐三菩提。

Without hindrance, there is no fear. Far from all inverted views, one attains nirvana. All buddhas of past, present, and future rely on Prajna Paramita and attain supreme, perfect enlightenment.

孟子 — 萬章上

曰：「象不得有為於其國，天子使吏治其國，而納其貢稅焉，故謂之放，豈得暴彼民哉？雖然，欲常常而見之，故源源而來。『不及貢，以政接于有庳』，此之謂也。」咸丘蒙問曰：「語云：『盛德之士，君不得而臣，父不得而子。』舜南面而立，堯帥諸侯北面而朝之，瞽瞍亦北面而朝之。舜見瞽瞍，其容有蹙。孔子曰：『於斯時也，天下殆哉，岌岌乎！』不識此語誠然乎哉？」

Mencius said: "Xiang was not permitted to govern his own state. The Son of Heaven appointed officials to administer the state and collect its tribute and taxes. That is why some called it exile — for how could he be allowed to tyrannize the people? Even so, Shun wished to see him constantly, and so Xiang came to court in a steady stream. The phrase 'not waiting for the regular tribute period, he conducted governance through visits to You Bi' refers to this." Xianqiu Meng asked: "A saying goes: 'A man of supreme virtue cannot be treated as a subject by his sovereign, nor as a son by his father.' When Shun faced south as ruler, Yao led the feudal lords to face north and pay court to him, and the Blind Man also faced north and paid court. When Shun received the Blind Man, his expression was uneasy. Confucius said: 'At that moment, the realm was in peril — truly precarious!' Is this saying really true?"

修道之人，倘不以智慧照破煩惱，無始生死憑何出離？」師曰：「煩惱即是菩提，無二無別。若以智慧照破煩惱者，此是二乘見解。羊鹿等機，上智大根，悉不如是。」簡曰：「如何是大乘見解？」師曰：「明與無明，凡夫見二；智者了達，其性無二。無二之性，即是實性。實性者，處凡愚而不減，在賢聖而不增，住煩惱而不亂，居禪定而不寂。不斷不常，不來不去，不在中間及其內外，不生不滅，性相如如，常住不遷，名之曰道。

'If a practitioner does not use wisdom to break through affliction, how can one escape the beginningless cycle of birth and death?' The Master said: 'Affliction is itself bodhi — they are not two, not separate. To use wisdom to break through affliction is the understanding of the Two Vehicles. Those of the capacities symbolized by sheep and deer carts — people of superior wisdom and great spiritual roots do not see it this way.' Xue Jian asked: 'What then is the Mahayana understanding?' The Master said: 'Light and non-light — ordinary people see them as two. The wise penetrate to the truth that their nature is not two. This non-dual nature is the true nature. The true nature is not diminished in the foolish and ordinary, not increased in the worthy and sagely. It is not disturbed amid affliction, not stilled in meditation. It is neither cut off nor eternal, neither coming nor going, not in the middle nor inside nor outside, neither arising nor ceasing. Its nature and characteristics are thusness, permanently abiding and unchanging. This is called the Way.'
