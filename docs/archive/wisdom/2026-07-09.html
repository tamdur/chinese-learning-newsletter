般若波羅蜜多心經

無眼耳鼻舌身意，無色聲香味觸法，無眼界，乃至無意識界。無無明，亦無無明盡，乃至無老死，亦無老死盡。

There are no eyes, ears, nose, tongue, body, or mind; no sights, sounds, smells, tastes, touches, or objects of mind; no realm of sight, and so on up to no realm of consciousness. There is no ignorance and no end of ignorance, and so on up to no old age and death and no end of old age and death.

孟子 · 滕文公上

有為神農之言者許行，自楚之滕，踵門而告文公曰：「遠方之人聞君行仁政，願受一廛而為氓。」文公與之處，其徒數十人，皆衣褐，捆屨、織席以為食。陳良之徒陳相與其弟辛，負耒耜而自宋之滕，曰：「聞君行聖人之政，是亦聖人也，願為聖人氓。」陳相見許行而大悅，盡棄其學而學焉。陳相見孟子，道許行之言曰：「滕君，則誠賢君也；雖然，未聞道也。賢者與民並耕而食，饔飧而治。今也滕有倉廩府庫，則是厲民而以自養也，惡得賢？」

A man named Xu Xing, who professed the teachings of the Divine Farmer, came from Chu to Teng. He went to the gate and told Duke Wen, 'A man from afar has heard that you are practicing benevolent government, and wishes to receive a homestead and become your subject.' Duke Wen gave him a place to live. His followers, several dozen in number, all wore rough hemp, and they wove sandals and mats for their livelihood. Chen Xiang, a disciple of Chen Liang, and his brother Xin, carrying their plows on their backs, came from Song to Teng, saying, 'We have heard that you are practicing the government of a sage — you must be a sage yourself. We wish to become subjects of a sage.' Chen Xiang met Xu Xing and was greatly delighted; he abandoned all his former learning and became Xu Xing's student. Chen Xiang came to see Mencius and relayed Xu Xing's words: 'The ruler of Teng is truly a worthy ruler; nevertheless, he has not yet heard the Way. A worthy ruler should plow alongside his people and eat with them, preparing his own meals while governing. But now Teng has granaries and treasuries — this is to oppress the people in order to feed himself. How can he be called worthy?'

六祖大師法寶壇經 · 疑問品第三

自心地上覺性，如來放大光明；外照六門清淨，能破六欲諸天；自性內照，三毒即除；地獄等罪一時銷滅，內外明徹不異西方。不作此修，如何到彼？」

大眾聞說，了然見性，悉皆禮拜，俱歎善哉。唱言：「普願法界眾生，聞者一時悟解。」

師言：「善知識！若欲修行，在家亦得，不由在寺。在家能行，如東方人心善；在寺不修，如西方人心惡。但心清淨，即是自性西方。」

韋公又問：「在家如何修行？願為教授。

'Upon the ground of your own mind, the awakened nature — the Tathagata — radiates great light. Outwardly it illuminates the six gates, purifying them and shattering the six desire-heavens. When one's own nature shines inward, the three poisons are instantly removed. All karmic offenses such as those deserving of hell are dissolved at once. When inner and outer are bright and clear, this is no different from the Western Pure Land. If you do not cultivate in this way, how will you ever get there?' The great assembly, hearing this teaching, clearly saw their own nature. They all prostrated in reverence, exclaiming in unison: 'Excellent!' They proclaimed: 'May all sentient beings throughout the Dharma realm, upon hearing this, awaken and understand at once.' The Master said: 'Good friends! If you wish to practice, you can do so at home — you need not be in a monastery. Practicing at home is like an Easterner whose mind is virtuous; not practicing in a monastery is like a Westerner whose mind is unwholesome. Just purify the mind, and that itself is the Western Pure Land of your own nature.' Prefect Wei asked again: 'How should one practice at home? Please instruct us.'
