般若波羅蜜多心經

舍利子，色不異空，空不異色，色即是空，空即是色，受想行識，亦復如是。

Shariputra, form is not different from emptiness, emptiness is not different from form. Form is emptiness, emptiness is form. The same is true of feelings, perceptions, mental formations, and consciousness.

孟子 — 萬章上

萬章曰：「堯以天下與舜，有諸？」孟子曰：「否。天子不能以天下與人。」「然則舜有天下也，孰與之？」曰：「天與之。」「天與之者，諄諄然命之乎？」曰：「否。天不言，以行與事示之而已矣。」曰：「以行與事示之者如之何？」

Wan Zhang said: "Did Yao give the realm to Shun? Is this true?" Mencius said: "No. The Son of Heaven cannot give the realm to another person." "Then if Shun possessed the realm, who gave it to him?" Mencius said: "Heaven gave it to him." "When Heaven gave it to him, did Heaven instruct him with repeated and explicit commands?" Mencius said: "No. Heaven does not speak — it simply reveals its will through actions and affairs." Wan Zhang said: "How does Heaven reveal its will through actions and affairs?"

六祖大師法寶壇經 — 付囑品第十

師一日喚門人法海、志誠、法達、神會、智常、智通、志徹、志道、法珍、法如等，曰：「汝等不同餘人，吾滅度後，各為一方師。吾今教汝說法，不失本宗：先須舉三科法門，動用三十六對，出沒即離兩邊。說一切法，莫離自性。忽有人問汝法，出語盡雙，皆取對法，來去相因。究竟二法盡除，更無去處。三科法門者，陰、界、入也。陰是五陰——色、受、想、行、識是也。

One day the Master summoned his disciples Fahai, Zhicheng, Fada, Shenhui, Zhichang, Zhitong, Zhiche, Zhidao, Fazhen, Faru, and others, and said: 'You are different from other people. After my passing, each of you will be a teacher in your own region. I will now teach you how to expound the Dharma without losing the essential teaching of our school. First you must raise the Three Categories of Dharma Teachings, employ the Thirty-Six Pairs of Opposites, and in all expression and activity, depart from both extremes. In teaching any dharma, do not depart from the self-nature. If someone suddenly asks you about the Dharma, speak always in pairs, each taking its opposite, cause and effect arising mutually. Ultimately, when both elements of each pair are eliminated, there is nowhere else to go. The Three Categories of Dharma Teachings are the aggregates, the sense-realms, and the sense-fields. The aggregates are the five aggregates: form, sensation, perception, mental formations, and consciousness.'
