般若波羅蜜多心經 — Heart Sutra — Thursday

無眼耳鼻舌身意，無色聲香味觸法，無眼界，乃至無意識界。無無明，亦無無明盡，乃至無老死，亦無老死盡。

There are no eyes, ears, nose, tongue, body, or mind; no sights, sounds, smells, tastes, touches, or objects of mind; no realm of sight, and so on up to no realm of consciousness. There is no ignorance and no end of ignorance, and so on up to no old age and death and no end of old age and death.

孟子 — Mencius — 公孫丑下

時子因陳子而以告孟子，陳子以時子之言告孟子。孟子曰：「然。夫時子惡知其不可也？如使予欲富，辭十萬而受萬，是為欲富乎？季孫曰：『異哉子叔疑！使己為政，不用，則亦已矣，又使其子弟為卿。人亦孰不欲富貴？而獨於富貴之中，有私龍斷焉。』古之為市也，以其所有易其所無者，有司者治之耳。有賤丈夫焉，必求龍斷而登之，以左右望而罔市利。人皆以為賤，故從而征之。征商，自此賤丈夫始矣。孟子去齊，宿於晝。

Shi Zi relayed the message through Chen Zi, and Chen Zi told Mencius. Mencius said: "Very well. But how would Shi Zi know that this cannot be done? If I desired wealth, would I have refused a hundred thousand and accepted ten thousand — is that the behavior of someone who desires wealth? Ji Sun once said: 'How strange is Zi Shu Yi! He pushed himself into government, and when he was not employed, that should have been the end of it. But then he got his sons and brothers appointed as ministers. Who does not desire wealth and rank? But he cornered the market on wealth and rank all by himself.' In ancient times, the marketplace was simply a place where people exchanged what they had for what they lacked, overseen by an official. But there was a base fellow who would always seek out a prominent mound to climb, looking left and right to snare all the profit in the market. Everyone thought him contemptible, and so they taxed him. The taxation of merchants began with this base fellow." Mencius left Qi and lodged at Zhou.

六祖大師法寶壇經 — Platform Sutra — 般若品第二

元有般若之智，與大智人更無差別，因何聞法不自開悟？緣邪見障重、煩惱根深。猶如大雲覆蓋於日，不得風吹，日光不現。般若之智亦無大小，為一切眾生自心迷悟不同，迷心外見，修行覓佛；未悟自性，即是小根。若開悟頓教，不能外修，但於自心常起正見，煩惱塵勞常不能染，即是見性。善知識！內外不住，去來自由，能除執心，通達無礙。能修此行，與般若經本無差別。

「善知識！一切修多羅及諸文字，大小二乘，十二部經，皆因人置。

'They originally possess prajna-wisdom no different from that of people of great wisdom. So why, upon hearing the Dharma, do they not awaken by themselves? It is because the obstacles of wrong views are heavy and the roots of affliction run deep. It is like when great clouds cover the sun: without wind to blow them away, the sunlight cannot appear. Prajna-wisdom is itself neither great nor small. It is because all beings' own minds are deluded or awakened differently. Those whose deluded minds look outward and seek the Buddha through external cultivation — these have not yet awakened to their own nature, and are called people of lesser capacity. If one awakens to the sudden teaching, one does not pursue external cultivation, but constantly gives rise to right views within one's own mind, so that afflictions and defilements can never stain it — this is seeing one's nature. Good friends, neither dwelling within nor without, coming and going freely, able to remove the clinging mind, penetrating without obstruction — one who can cultivate this practice is fundamentally no different from the Prajna Sutra.' 'Good friends, all sutras and written texts, both the Lesser and Greater Vehicles, and the twelve divisions of the canon, were all established because of people.'
