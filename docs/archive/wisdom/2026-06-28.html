般若波羅蜜多心經

故知般若波羅蜜多，是大神咒，是大明咒，是無上咒，是無等等咒，能除一切苦，真實不虛。故說般若波羅蜜多咒，即說咒曰：揭諦揭諦，波羅揭諦，波羅僧揭諦，菩提薩婆訶。

Therefore, know that Prajna Paramita is the great transcendent mantra, the great bright mantra, the supreme mantra, the unequalled mantra, which can remove all suffering and is true, not false. Therefore he proclaimed the Prajna Paramita mantra, saying: Gate gate paragate parasamgate bodhi svaha.

孟子・公孫丑下

高子以告。曰：「夫尹士惡知予哉？千里而見王，是予所欲也；不遇故去，豈予所欲哉？予不得已也。予三宿而出晝，於予心猶以為速。王庶幾改之。王如改諸，則必反予。夫出晝而王不予追也，予然後浩然有歸志。予雖然，豈舍王哉？王由足用為善。王如用予，則豈徒齊民安，天下之民舉安。王庶幾改之，予日望之。予豈若是小丈夫然哉？諫於其君而不受，則怒，悻悻然見於其面。去則窮日之力而後宿哉？」尹士聞之曰：「士誠小人也。」

Gao Zi reported this to Mencius. Mencius said: "How could Yin Shi understand me? I traveled a thousand li to see the king — that was what I desired. To leave because I was not received — was that what I desired? I had no choice. I stayed three nights before leaving Zhou, and in my heart I still felt it was too soon. I hoped the king might yet change his mind. If the king had changed, he would surely have called me back. Only when I left Zhou and the king did not send after me did I finally resolve wholeheartedly to go home. But even so, how could I give up on the king? The king is still capable of doing good. If the king were to use me, it would not be just the people of Qi who would find peace — the people of the whole world would find peace. I kept hoping the king might change. Every day I watched for it. Do you think I am like some petty man who, when his remonstrance to his ruler is rejected, gets angry, shows his resentment on his face, and when he leaves, exhausts a whole day's travel before stopping for the night?" When Yin Shi heard this, he said: "I am truly a small man."

六祖大師法寶壇經・般若品第二

若起邪迷、妄念顛倒，外善知識雖有教授，救不可得。若起正真般若觀照，一剎那間，妄念俱滅。若識自性，一悟即至佛地。善知識！智慧觀照，內外明徹，識自本心。若識本心，即本解脫。若得解脫，即是般若三昧，即是無念。何名無念？若見一切法，心不染著，是為無念。用即遍一切處，亦不著一切處。但淨本心，使六識出六門，於六塵中無染無雜，來去自由，通用無滯，即是般若三昧、自在解脫，名無念行。

'If you give rise to deviant confusion, false thoughts, and inverted views, then even though an external good teacher offers instruction, you cannot be saved. But if you give rise to true and correct prajna-contemplation, in a single instant all false thoughts are extinguished. If you recognize your own nature, with one awakening you arrive directly at the ground of buddhahood. Good friends, when wisdom illuminates, inside and outside become thoroughly clear, and you recognize your own original mind. If you recognize your original mind, you are fundamentally liberated. If you attain liberation, this is prajna-samadhi, which is no-thought. What is no-thought? If you see all dharmas yet the mind is not stained or attached — this is no-thought. Its function reaches everywhere, yet it is not attached anywhere. Simply purify your original mind, letting the six consciousnesses go out through the six gates, and amid the six sense-objects remain undefiled and unmixed, coming and going freely, functioning without obstruction — this is prajna-samadhi, freedom and liberation, and is called the practice of no-thought.'
