般若波羅蜜多心經

無眼耳鼻舌身意，無色聲香味觸法，無眼界，乃至無意識界。無無明，亦無無明盡，乃至無老死，亦無老死盡。

There are no eyes, ears, nose, tongue, body, or mind; no sights, sounds, smells, tastes, touches, or objects of mind; no realm of sight, and so on up to no realm of consciousness. There is no ignorance and no end of ignorance, and so on up to no old age and death and no end of old age and death.

孟子 · 滕文公下

孟子曰：「非其道，則一簞食不可受於人；如其道，則舜受堯之天下，不以為泰，子以為泰乎？」曰：「否。士無事而食，不可也。」曰：「子不通功易事，以羡補不足，則農有餘粟，女有餘布；子如通之，則梓匠輪輿皆得食於子。於此有人焉，入則孝，出則悌，守先王之道，以待後之學者，而不得食於子。子何尊梓匠輪輿而輕為仁義者哉？」曰：「梓匠輪輿，其志將以求食也；君子之為道也，其志亦將以求食與？」

Mencius said: "If it is not in accordance with the Way, then one should not accept even a single basket of rice from another person. But if it is in accordance with the Way, then Shun accepted the whole empire from Yao without considering it excessive — would you consider it excessive?" Peng Geng said: "No. But it is wrong for a scholar to eat without working." Mencius said: "If you do not allow the exchange of goods and services so that surpluses make up for deficits, then farmers will have excess grain and weavers will have excess cloth. But if you do allow such exchange, then carpenters, wheelwrights, and carriage-makers can all get food from you. Now suppose there is a person who is filial at home, respectful abroad, who guards the Way of the ancient kings to pass it on to future learners — and yet he cannot get food from you. Why do you honor carpenters and wheelwrights but look down on those who practice benevolence and righteousness?" Peng Geng said: "The carpenters and wheelwrights have the intention of seeking food by their work. When a gentleman pursues the Way, is his intention also to seek food?"

六祖大師法寶壇經 · 懺悔品第六

善知識！各隨我語，一時道：『弟子等，從前念今念及後念，念念不被愚迷染。從前所有惡業愚迷等罪，悉皆懺悔，願一時銷滅，永不復起。弟子等，從前念今念及後念，念念不被憍誑染。從前所有惡業憍誑等罪，悉皆懺悔，願一時銷滅，永不復起。弟子等，從前念今念及後念，念念不被嫉妬染。從前所有惡業嫉妬等罪，悉皆懺悔，願一時銷滅，永不復起。』善知識！已上是為無相懺悔。

'Good friends! Each of you repeat after me, saying together: We disciples — in past thoughts, present thoughts, and future thoughts, thought after thought — vow never to be tainted by delusion and ignorance. We fully repent all past evil karma born of ignorance and delusion. May it all be eliminated at once and never arise again. We disciples — in past thoughts, present thoughts, and future thoughts, thought after thought — vow never to be tainted by arrogance and deceit. We fully repent all past evil karma born of arrogance and deceit. May it all be eliminated at once and never arise again. We disciples — in past thoughts, present thoughts, and future thoughts, thought after thought — vow never to be tainted by jealousy and envy. We fully repent all past evil karma born of jealousy and envy. May it all be eliminated at once and never arise again.' Good friends! The above is the formless repentance.
