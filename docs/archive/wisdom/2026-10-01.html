般若波羅蜜多心經

無眼耳鼻舌身意，無色聲香味觸法，無眼界，乃至無意識界。無無明，亦無無明盡，乃至無老死，亦無老死盡。

There are no eyes, ears, nose, tongue, body, or mind; no sights, sounds, smells, tastes, touches, or objects of mind; no realm of sight, and so on up to no realm of consciousness. There is no ignorance and no end of ignorance, and so on up to no old age and death and no end of old age and death.

孟子：萬章上

萬章問曰：「或曰：『百里奚自鬻於秦養牲者，五羊之皮，食牛，以要秦穆公。』信乎？」

Wan Zhang asked: "Some say: 'Baili Xi sold himself to a cattle feeder of Qin for five ram skins, and fed cattle in order to gain an audience with Duke Mu of Qin.' Is this true?"

六祖大師法寶壇經：付囑品第十

汝等悲泣，蓋為不知吾去處；若知吾去處，即不合悲泣。法性本無生滅去來，汝等盡坐，吾與汝說一偈，名曰真假動靜偈。汝等誦取此偈，與吾意同，依此修行，不失宗旨。」眾僧作禮，請師說偈。偈曰：

一切無有真，不以見於真，若見於真者，是見盡非真。若能自有真，離假即心真，自心不離假，無真何處真？有情即解動，無情即不動，若修不動行，同無情不動。若覓真不動，動上有不動，不動是不動，無情無佛種。

'You weep because you do not know where I am going. If you knew, you would not weep. The Dharma-nature is originally without arising or ceasing, without coming or going. All of you sit down — I will give you a verse called "The Verse of True and False, Movement and Stillness." If you recite and take up this verse, your understanding will accord with mine. Practice according to it and you will not lose the essential teaching.' The assembly bowed and requested the Master to speak the verse. The verse said: "Nothing whatsoever is truly real; do not try to see the real through appearances. If you think you see something real, that seeing is entirely unreal. If you can find the real within yourself, then departing from the false, the mind itself is real. If your own mind does not depart from the false, there is no real — so where would you find it? Sentient beings understand movement; the non-sentient do not move. If you cultivate the practice of non-movement, you become as unmoving as something non-sentient. If you seek the truly unmoving, within movement there is non-movement. But non-movement that is merely non-movement — the non-sentient have no Buddha-seed."
