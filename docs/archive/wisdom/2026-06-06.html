般若波羅蜜多心經

無罣礙故，無有恐怖，遠離顛倒夢想，究竟涅槃。三世諸佛，依般若波羅蜜多故，得阿耨多羅三藐三菩提。

Without hindrance, there is no fear. Far from all inverted views, one attains nirvana. All buddhas of past, present, and future rely on Prajna Paramita and attain supreme, perfect enlightenment.

孟子・公孫丑下

孟子曰：「天時不如地利，地利不如人和。三里之城，七里之郭，環而攻之而不勝。夫環而攻之，必有得天時者矣；然而不勝者，是天時不如地利也。城非不高也，池非不深也，兵革非不堅利也，米粟非不多也；委而去之，是地利不如人和也。故曰：域民不以封疆之界，固國不以山谿之險，威天下不以兵革之利。得道者多助，失道者寡助。寡助之至，親戚畔之；多助之至，天下順之。以天下之所順，攻親戚之所畔；故君子有不戰，戰必勝矣。」

Mencius said: "Favorable weather is not as good as advantageous terrain, and advantageous terrain is not as good as unity among the people. A city wall of three li, with outer walls of seven li, may be surrounded and attacked yet not taken. To surround and attack it, the attackers must have had favorable weather; yet they still could not win — this shows that favorable weather is not as good as advantageous terrain. When the city walls are not low, the moats not shallow, the weapons and armor not poor, the grain supplies not scarce — yet the defenders abandon it all and flee — this shows that advantageous terrain is not as good as unity among the people. Therefore it is said: Confining the people does not depend on borders and boundaries; securing the state does not depend on mountains and rivers; overawing the world does not depend on military might. He who has the Way finds many allies; he who lacks the Way finds few. When allies are fewest, even one's own relatives turn away; when allies are most numerous, the whole world submits. When one who commands the world's allegiance attacks one whose own kin have turned against him — therefore the gentleman either does not fight at all, or when he fights, he is certain to win."

惠明至，提掇不動，乃喚云：『行者！行者！我為法來，不為衣來。』惠能遂出，坐盤石上。惠明作禮云：『望行者為我說法。』惠能云：『汝既為法而來，可屏息諸緣，勿生一念。吾為汝說。』明良久。惠能云：『不思善，不思惡，正與麼時，那箇是明上座本來面目？』惠明言下大悟。復問云：『上來密語密意外，還更有密意否？』惠能云：『與汝說者，即非密也。汝若返照，密在汝邊。』明曰：『惠明雖在黃梅，實未省自己面目。

When Huiming arrived and tried to pick up the robe and bowl, he could not move them. He called out, 'Lay brother! Lay brother! I have come for the Dharma, not for the robe.' Huineng came out and sat upon the rock. Huiming bowed and said, 'I beg the lay brother to expound the Dharma for me.' Huineng said, 'Since you have come for the Dharma, put aside all external conditions and do not give rise to a single thought. I will explain it to you.' After Huiming stood in silence for a long while, Huineng said, 'Without thinking of good, without thinking of evil — at just this moment, what is your original face, Venerable Ming?' At these words, Huiming was greatly awakened. He then asked, 'Beyond the secret words and secret meaning just spoken, is there anything further?' Huineng said, 'What I have told you is not secret. If you turn your light inward, the secret is right where you are.' Huiming said, 'Although I was at Huangmei, I had never truly realized my own original face.
