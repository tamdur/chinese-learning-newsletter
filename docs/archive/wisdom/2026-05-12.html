般若波羅蜜多心經 — 第二段

舍利子，色不異空，空不異色，色即是空，空即是色，受想行識，亦復如是。

Shariputra, form is not different from emptiness, emptiness is not different from form. Form is emptiness, emptiness is form. The same is true of feelings, perceptions, mental formations, and consciousness.

孟子 — 梁惠王下

樂正子入見，曰：「君奚為不見孟軻也？」曰：「或告寡人曰，『孟子之後喪踰前喪』，是以不往見也。」曰：「何哉君所謂踰者？前以士，後以大夫；前以三鼎，而後以五鼎與？」曰：「否。謂棺槨衣衾之美也。」曰：「非所謂踰也，貧富不同也。」樂正子見孟子，曰：「克告於君，君為來見也。嬖人有臧倉者沮君，君是以不果來也。」曰：「行或使之，止或尼之。行止，非人所能也。吾之不遇魯侯，天也。臧氏之子焉能使予不遇哉？」

Yue Zhengzi went in to see the duke and said, "Why did Your Lordship not go to see Mencius?" The duke said, "Someone told me that Mencius's funeral for his mother exceeded his funeral for his father, and so I did not go." Yue Zhengzi said, "What do you mean by 'exceeded'? Do you mean that the first was conducted with the rites for a common officer and the second with those for a grandee? That the first used three sacrificial vessels and the second used five?" The duke said, "No. I meant the superior quality of the coffin, outer coffin, and burial garments." Yue Zhengzi said, "That is not 'exceeding'—it is simply the difference between being poor and being better off." Yue Zhengzi went to see Mencius and said, "I told the duke about you, and he was going to come see you. But one of his favorites, Zang Cang, dissuaded him, and so he did not come after all." Mencius said, "When a man acts, there is something that drives him forward; when he stops, there is something that holds him back. Whether he acts or stops is not entirely within human control. That I did not meet the Marquis of Lu was Heaven's doing. How could that fellow of the Zang family have kept me from meeting him?"

金剛般若波羅蜜經 — 無斷無滅分第二十七

「須菩提！汝若作是念：『如來不以具足相故，得阿耨多羅三藐三菩提。』須菩提！莫作是念：『如來不以具足相故，得阿耨多羅三藐三菩提。』」「須菩提！汝若作是念，發阿耨多羅三藐三菩提心者，說諸法斷滅。莫作是念！何以故？發阿耨多羅三藐三菩提心者。於法不說斷滅相。」

"Subhuti, if you have this thought: 'The Tathagata did not attain anuttara-samyak-sambodhi by means of the perfection of marks' — Subhuti, do not think this way: 'The Tathagata did not attain anuttara-samyak-sambodhi by means of the perfection of marks.'" "Subhuti, if you have this thought, then one who aspires to anuttara-samyak-sambodhi would be declaring all dharmas to be annihilated. Do not think this way! Why? One who aspires to anuttara-samyak-sambodhi does not declare the mark of annihilation regarding any dharma."
