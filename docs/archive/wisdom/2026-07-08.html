般若波羅蜜多心經 — Heart Sutra — Wednesday

舍利子，是諸法空相，不生不滅，不垢不淨，不增不減。是故空中無色，無受想行識。

Shariputra, all dharmas are marked with emptiness — they are neither born nor destroyed, neither defiled nor pure, neither increasing nor decreasing. Therefore, in emptiness there is no form, no feelings, perceptions, mental formations, or consciousness.

孟子 — Mencius — 滕文公上

使畢戰問井地。孟子曰：「子之君將行仁政，選擇而使子，子必勉之！夫仁政，必自經界始。經界不正，井地不鈞，穀祿不平。是故暴君汙吏必慢其經界。經界既正，分田制祿可坐而定也。夫滕壤地褊小，將為君子焉，將為野人焉。無君子莫治野人，無野人莫養君子。請野九一而助，國中什一使自賦。卿以下必有圭田，圭田五十畝。餘夫二十五畝。死徙無出鄉，鄉田同井。出入相友，守望相助，疾病相扶持，則百姓親睦。方里而井，井九百畝，其中為公田。八家皆私百畝，同養公田。公事畢，然後敢治私事，所以別野人也。此其大略也。若夫潤澤之，則在君與子矣。」

The duke sent Bi Zhan to ask about the well-field system. Mencius said, 'Your ruler is about to practice benevolent government and has chosen you for the task — you must do your utmost! Benevolent government must begin with proper boundaries. If boundaries are not correct, the well-fields will be unequal and grain stipends will be unfair. Therefore tyrannical rulers and corrupt officials always neglect proper boundaries. Once boundaries are correctly set, the division of fields and regulation of stipends can be settled without difficulty. Now Teng is small in territory, but there will be gentlemen and there will be field workers. Without gentlemen, there is no one to govern the field workers; without field workers, there is no one to feed the gentlemen. In the countryside, let the tax be one-ninth under the aid system; in the capital, let it be one-tenth, self-assessed. From ministers on down, each must have a sacrificial field of fifty mu. Additional adult males receive twenty-five mu. Whether in death or removal, no one leaves their district. Those who share a well-field in their district go out and come in as companions, keep watch and help each other, and support one another in sickness — then the people will live in affection and harmony. A well-field is one li square, comprising nine hundred mu; the center is the public field. Eight families each hold a hundred mu privately and together cultivate the public field. Only after the public work is done may they attend to private affairs — this is what distinguishes the field workers. This is the general outline. As for refining and perfecting it, that rests with your ruler and with you.'

六祖大師法寶壇經 — 疑問品第三

性在身心存，性去身壞。佛向性中作，莫向身外求。自性迷即是眾生，自性覺即是佛。慈悲即是觀音，喜捨名為勢至，能淨即釋迦，平直即彌陀；人我是須彌，貪欲是海水，煩惱是波浪，毒害是惡龍，虛妄是鬼神，塵勞是魚鱉。貪瞋是地獄，愚癡是畜生。善知識！常行十善，天堂便至。除人我，須彌倒；去貪欲，海水竭；煩惱無，波浪滅；毒害除，魚龍絕。

'When the nature is present, body and mind endure; when the nature departs, the body disintegrates. The Buddha is made within the nature — do not seek outside the body. When one's own nature is deluded, one is a sentient being; when one's own nature is awakened, one is a Buddha. Compassion is Avalokiteshvara; joyful giving is Mahasthamaprapta; the ability to purify is Shakyamuni; equanimity and directness are Amitabha. Self-and-other discrimination is Mount Sumeru; greed and desire are the ocean waters; afflictions are the waves; malice is the evil dragons; falsehood is the ghosts and spirits; worldly defilements are the fish and turtles. Greed and anger are the hells; ignorance is the animal realm. Good friends! Always practice the ten virtues and heaven is right here. Remove self-and-other discrimination, and Mount Sumeru topples. Eliminate greed and desire, and the ocean waters dry up. When afflictions are gone, the waves are stilled. When malice is removed, the fish and dragons vanish.'
