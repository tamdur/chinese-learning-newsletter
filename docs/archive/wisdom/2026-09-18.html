般若波羅蜜多心經

無苦集滅道，無智亦無得。以無所得故，菩提薩埵，依般若波羅蜜多故，心無罣礙。

There is no suffering, no cause of suffering, no cessation, no path; no wisdom and no attainment. With nothing to attain, a bodhisattva relies on Prajna Paramita, and the mind is without hindrance.

孟子 · 萬章上

萬章曰：「舜流共工于幽州，放驩兜于崇山，殺三苗于三危，殛鯀于羽山，四罪而天下咸服，誅不仁也。象至不仁，封之有庳。有庳之人奚罪焉？仁人固如是乎？在他人則誅之，在弟則封之。」曰：「仁人之於弟也，不藏怒焉，不宿怨焉，親愛之而已矣。親之欲其貴也，愛之欲其富也。封之有庳，富貴之也。身為天子，弟為匹夫，可謂親愛之乎？」「敢問或曰放者，何謂也？」

Wan Zhang said: "Shun banished Gong Gong to You Zhou, exiled Huan Dou to Chong Mountain, destroyed the San Miao at San Wei, and executed Gun on Yu Mountain. These four were punished and the whole realm submitted, for it was the punishment of the wicked. Yet Xiang was the most wicked of all, and Shun enfeoffed him in You Bi. What had the people of You Bi done to deserve that? Is this how a benevolent man acts — punishing wickedness in others but enfeoffing it in his own brother?" Mencius said: "A benevolent man does not harbor anger or nurse grudges against his brother — he simply loves him with familial affection. Loving him, he wishes him to be honored; being affectionate toward him, he wishes him to be wealthy. Enfeoffing him in You Bi was to give him both wealth and honor. If Shun were Son of Heaven while his brother remained a commoner, could that be called familial love?" Wan Zhang asked: "May I ask what those who call it exile mean?"

六祖大師法寶壇經 · 宣詔品第九

』何故？無所從來，亦無所去。無生無滅，是如來清淨禪。諸法空寂，是如來清淨坐。究竟無證，豈況坐耶。」簡曰：「弟子回京，主上必問。願師慈悲，指示心要，傳奏兩宮及京城學道者。譬如一燈，然百千燈，冥者皆明，明明無盡。」師云：「道無明暗，明暗是代謝之義。明明無盡，亦是有盡，相待立名故。《淨名經》云：『法無有比，無相待故。』」簡曰：「明喻智慧，暗喻煩惱。

'Why? Because there is nowhere the Tathagata comes from, and nowhere he goes. No arising and no ceasing — this is the Tathagata's pure dhyana. The emptiness and stillness of all dharmas — this is the Tathagata's pure sitting. Ultimately there is nothing to be verified, so how much less could it depend on sitting?' Xue Jian said: 'When I return to the capital, Their Majesties will certainly ask. I hope the Master will compassionately point out the essentials of mind, so I may convey them to both palaces and to those studying the Way in the capital. It is like one lamp lighting hundreds and thousands of lamps — all those in darkness become illumined, and the light is endless.' The Master said: 'The Way has neither light nor darkness. Light and darkness refer to alternation and succession. To say "the light is endless" — that too has an end, because the terms are established in mutual dependence. The Vimalakirti Sutra says: "The Dharma has no comparison, because it is not relative."' Xue Jian said: 'Light is a metaphor for wisdom; darkness is a metaphor for affliction.'
