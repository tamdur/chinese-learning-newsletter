般若波羅蜜多心經

故知般若波羅蜜多，是大神咒，是大明咒，是無上咒，是無等等咒，能除一切苦，真實不虛。故說般若波羅蜜多咒，即說咒曰：揭諦揭諦，波羅揭諦，波羅僧揭諦，菩提薩婆訶。

Therefore, know that Prajna Paramita is the great transcendent mantra, the great bright mantra, the supreme mantra, the unequalled mantra, which can remove all suffering and is true, not false. Therefore he proclaimed the Prajna Paramita mantra, saying: Gate gate paragate parasamgate bodhi svaha.

Mencius said: "No. These are not the words of a gentleman — they are the talk of rustic folk east of Qi. When Yao was old, Shun served as regent. The 'Canon of Yao' says: 'After twenty-eight years, Fang Xun passed away. The hundred families mourned as if for their own parents. For three years, throughout the four seas, all music was silenced.' Confucius said: 'Heaven does not have two suns; the people do not have two kings.' If Shun had already become Son of Heaven and then also led the feudal lords of the realm in observing three years of mourning for Yao, there would have been two Sons of Heaven at once." Xianqiu Meng said: "That Shun did not treat Yao as a subject — I have heard your explanation of that. But the Odes say: 'Under all of heaven, there is no land that is not the king's; to the borders of the land, there is no one who is not the king's subject.' Since Shun had become Son of Heaven, may I ask how the Blind Man could not be his subject?"

六祖大師法寶壇經 — 宣詔品第九

」簡曰：「師說不生不滅，何異外道？」師曰：「外道所說不生不滅者，將滅止生，以生顯滅，滅猶不滅，生說不生。我說不生不滅者，本自無生，今亦不滅，所以不同外道。汝若欲知心要，但一切善惡都莫思量，自然得入清淨心體，湛然常寂，妙用恒沙。」簡蒙指教，豁然大悟。禮辭歸闕，表奏師語。 其年九月三日，有詔獎諭師曰：「師辭老疾，為朕修道，國之福田。師若淨名托疾毘耶，闡揚大乘，傳諸佛心，談不二法。

Xue Jian said: 'The Master teaches neither arising nor ceasing — how does this differ from the non-Buddhists?' The Master said: 'What the non-Buddhists call "neither arising nor ceasing" uses ceasing to stop arising and uses arising to reveal ceasing — their ceasing is not truly ceasing, and their arising they merely claim is non-arising. What I call "neither arising nor ceasing" means there was originally no arising, and now there is also no ceasing. This is why it differs from the non-Buddhists. If you wish to know the essentials of mind, simply do not think about anything good or bad, and you will naturally enter the pure essence of mind — profoundly still and constantly serene, with wondrous functions as numerous as the sands of the Ganges.' Xue Jian received this teaching and experienced a great, expansive awakening. He bowed, took his leave, and returned to the palace, where he submitted a memorial reporting the Master's words. In the ninth month, on the third day of that year, an imperial decree was issued commending the Master: 'The Master has declined due to age and illness, cultivating the Way on our behalf — he is a field of merit for the nation. The Master is like Vimalakirti feigning illness in Vaishali, expounding the Great Vehicle, transmitting the Buddha Mind, and teaching the Dharma of non-duality.'
