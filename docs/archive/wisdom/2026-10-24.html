般若波羅蜜多心經

無罣礙故，無有恐怖，遠離顛倒夢想，究竟涅槃。三世諸佛，依般若波羅蜜多故，得阿耨多羅三藐三菩提。

Without hindrance, there is no fear. Far from all inverted views, one attains nirvana. All buddhas of past, present, and future rely on Prajna Paramita and attain supreme, perfect enlightenment.

孟子：告子上

孟子曰：「水信無分於東西。無分於上下乎？人性之善也，猶水之就下也。人無有不善，水無有不下。今夫水，搏而躍之，可使過顙；激而行之，可使在山。是豈水之性哉？其勢則然也。人之可使為不善，其性亦猶是也。」告子曰：「生之謂性。」孟子曰：「生之謂性也，猶白之謂白與？」曰：「然。」「白羽之白也，猶白雪之白；白雪之白，猶白玉之白與？」曰：「然。」「然則犬之性，猶牛之性；牛之性，猶人之性與？」

Mencius said: "Water indeed does not distinguish between east and west. But does it not distinguish between up and down? The goodness of human nature is like water's tendency to flow downward. There is no person who is not good, just as there is no water that does not flow downward. Now, if you strike water and splash it up, you can make it leap over your forehead; if you dam it and force it along, you can make it stay on a mountain. But is this the nature of water? It is the force of circumstances that makes it so. That people can be made to do what is not good—their nature is treated in just the same way." Gaozi said: "What is inborn is what we call nature." Mencius said: "When you say what is inborn is nature, is that like saying white is white?" Gaozi said: "Yes." "Then is the whiteness of white feathers the same as the whiteness of white snow, and the whiteness of white snow the same as the whiteness of white jade?" "Yes." "Then is the nature of a dog the same as the nature of an ox, and the nature of an ox the same as the nature of a human being?"

如法受持分第十三

如來說：世界，非世界，是名世界。須菩提！於意云何？可以三十二相見如來不？」「不也，世尊！不可以三十二相得見如來。何以故？如來說：三十二相，即是非相，是名三十二相。」「須菩提！若有善男子、善女人，以恆河沙等身命布施；若復有人，於此經中，乃至受持四句偈等，為他人說，其福甚多！」。

The Tathagata says: a world is not a world, and is therefore called a world. Subhuti, what do you think? Can one perceive the Tathagata through the thirty-two physical marks? No, World-Honored One! One cannot perceive the Tathagata through the thirty-two marks. Why? Because what the Tathagata calls the thirty-two marks are not marks, and are therefore called the thirty-two marks. Subhuti, if a good man or good woman were to sacrifice as many lives as there are grains of sand in the Ganges River, and another person were to receive and uphold even just four lines of verse from this sutra and explain them to others, the merit of the latter would be far greater.
