般若波羅蜜多心經

觀自在菩薩，行深般若波羅蜜多時，照見五蘊皆空，度一切苦厄。

Avalokitesvara Bodhisattva, when practicing the profound Prajna Paramita, illuminated the five aggregates and saw that they are all empty, thus overcoming all suffering and distress.

孟子

孟子曰：「今之事君者曰：『我能為君辟土地，充府庫。』今之所謂良臣，古之所謂民賊也。君不鄉道，不志於仁，而求富之，是富桀也。『我能為君約與國，戰必克。』今之所謂良臣，古之所謂民賊也。君不鄉道，不志於仁，而求為之強戰，是輔桀也。由今之道，無變今之俗，雖與之天下，不能一朝居也。」白圭曰：「吾欲二十而取一，何如？」孟子曰：「子之道，貉道也。萬室之國，一人陶，則可乎？」曰：「不可，器不足用也。」

What today is called a good minister would in ancient times be called a robber of the people. If a ruler does not incline toward the Way or set his will on benevolence, to seek to strengthen his military is to aid a tyrant like Jie. Following today's path without changing today's customs, even if you gave such a ruler the whole world, he could not hold it for a single morning.' Bai Gui said: 'I would like to tax at one part in twenty. What do you think?' Mencius said: 'Your way is the way of the northern barbarians. In a city of ten thousand households, would it suffice to have a single potter?' Bai Gui said: 'No—there would not be enough wares.'

What do you seek?' Huineng replied, 'Your disciple is a commoner from Xinzhou in Lingnan. I have come from afar to pay respects to the Master. I seek only to become a Buddha — nothing else.' The Patriarch said, 'You are from Lingnan, and a barbarian besides. How could you possibly become a Buddha?' Huineng said, 'Although people are from the south or north, the Buddha-nature fundamentally has no south or north.
