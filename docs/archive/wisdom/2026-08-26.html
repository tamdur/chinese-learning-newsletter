般若波羅蜜多心經

舍利子，是諸法空相，不生不滅，不垢不淨，不增不減。是故空中無色，無受想行識。

Shariputra, all dharmas are marked with emptiness — they are neither born nor destroyed, neither defiled nor pure, neither increasing nor decreasing. Therefore, in emptiness there is no form, no feelings, perceptions, mental formations, or consciousness.

孟子 · 離婁下

孟子曰：「舜生於諸馮，遷於負夏，卒於鳴條，東夷之人也。文王生於岐周，卒於畢郢，西夷之人也。地之相去也，千有餘里；世之相後也，千有餘歲。得志行乎中國，若合符節。先聖後聖，其揆一也。」子產聽鄭國之政，以其乘輿濟人於溱洧。孟子曰：「惠而不知為政。歲十一月徒杠成，十二月輿梁成，民未病涉也。君子平其政，行辟人可也。焉得人人而濟之？故為政者，每人而悅之，日亦不足矣。」

Mencius said: "Shun was born in Zhufeng, moved to Fuxia, and died in Mingtiao — he was a man of the eastern barbarian regions. King Wen was born in Qizhou and died in Biying — he was a man of the western barbarian regions. Their lands were over a thousand li apart; their eras were over a thousand years apart. Yet when each fulfilled his purpose in the Central States, they matched like the two halves of a tally. The earlier sage and the later sage — their standard was one and the same." Zi Chan managed the government of the state of Zheng, and used his own carriage to ferry people across the Zhen and Wei rivers. Mencius said: "That was kind, but it showed he did not understand governance. If in the eleventh month footbridges are built, and in the twelfth month carriage bridges are completed, the people will not suffer from difficult crossings. When a ruler governs fairly, he may even clear people from his path as he goes out. How can he ferry everyone across personally? Therefore, if a ruler tried to please every single person, he would never have enough hours in the day."

六祖大師法寶壇經 · 機緣品第七

」師曰：「什麼物？恁麼來？」曰：「說似一物即不中。」師曰：「還可修證否？」曰：「修證即不無，污染即不得。」師曰：「只此不污染，諸佛之所護念。汝既如是，吾亦如是。西天般若多羅讖，汝足下出一馬駒，踏殺天下人。應在汝心，不須速說。」讓豁然契會，遂執侍左右一十五載，日臻玄奧。後往南嶽，大闡禪宗。

永嘉玄覺禪師，溫州戴氏子。少習經論，精天台止觀法門。因看《維摩經》發明心地。

The Master asked, "What is this thing, and how did it come here?"

Huairang replied, "To call it any particular thing would miss the mark."

The Master asked, "Then can it still be cultivated and realized?"

He replied, "Cultivation and realization are not absent — but defilement cannot touch it."

The Master said, "It is precisely this freedom from defilement that all the Buddhas guard and hold in mind. You are like this, and I too am like this. Prajñātāra of India prophesied that from beneath your feet a colt would come forth who would trample the whole world underfoot. Keep this in your heart; there is no need to speak of it too soon."

At these words Huairang suddenly awakened and understood. He then attended upon the Master for fifteen years, drawing nearer each day to the profound mystery. Later he went to Mount Nanyue, where he greatly expounded the Chan school.

Chan Master Yongjia Xuanjue was a son of the Dai family of Wenzhou. In his youth he studied the sutras and treatises, and became expert in the Tiantai method of calming and contemplation (śamatha-vipaśyanā). Through reading the Vimalakīrti Sutra, he illuminated the ground of his own mind.
