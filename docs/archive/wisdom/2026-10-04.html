般若波羅蜜多心經

故知般若波羅蜜多，是大神咒，是大明咒，是無上咒，是無等等咒，能除一切苦，真實不虛。故說般若波羅蜜多咒，即說咒曰：揭諦揭諦，波羅揭諦，波羅僧揭諦，菩提薩婆訶。

Therefore, know that Prajna Paramita is the great transcendent mantra, the great bright mantra, the supreme mantra, the unequalled mantra, which can remove all suffering and is true, not false. Therefore he proclaimed the Prajna Paramita mantra, saying: Gate gate paragate parasamgate bodhi svaha.

孟子

「伊尹曰：『何事非君？何使非民？』治亦進，亂亦進。曰：『天之生斯民也，使先知覺後知，使先覺覺後覺。予，天民之先覺者也；予將以此道覺此民也。』思天下之民匹夫匹婦有不與被堯舜之澤者，若己推而內之溝中，其自任以天下之重也。

"Yi Yin said: 'What ruler is not worth serving? What people are not worth commanding?' He advanced whether there was order or chaos. He said: 'Heaven produced these people so that those who understand first may awaken those who understand later, and those who are awakened first may awaken those who are awakened later. I am among Heaven's people one who has been awakened first; I shall use this Way to awaken these people.' He felt that if even a single common man or woman in the world had not received the blessings of Yao and Shun, it was as if he himself had pushed them into a ditch — such was the weight of responsibility he took upon himself for the whole world."

六祖大師法寶壇經

達磨所傳信衣，中宗賜磨衲寶鉢，及方辯塑師真相，並道具等，主塔侍者尸之，永鎮寶林道場。

師自言，從上以來，佛佛惟傳本體，師師密付本心。過去七佛：毘婆尸佛、尸棄佛、毘舍浮佛、拘留孫佛、拘那含牟尼佛、迦葉佛、釋迦文佛。

此土初祖菩提達磨、二祖慧可大師、三祖僧璨大師、四祖道信大師、五祖弘忍大師、六祖惠能大師。

此六代祖師，已上諸佛及祖，皆傳無相法，汝等見性，勿生疑念。

六祖大師法寶壇經終。

The robe transmitted from Bodhidharma, the mona robe and precious bowl bestowed by Emperor Zhongzong, the true likeness of the Master sculpted by Fangbian, together with his monastic implements — all were kept by the attendant in charge of the stupa, to be permanently enshrined at the Baolin monastery. The Master himself had said that from the beginning, from Buddha to Buddha, only the original essence has been transmitted; from teacher to teacher, only the original mind has been secretly entrusted. The seven Buddhas of the past are: Vipashyin Buddha, Shikhin Buddha, Vishvabhu Buddha, Krakucchanda Buddha, Kanakamuni Buddha, Kashyapa Buddha, and Shakyamuni Buddha. The first patriarch of this land was Bodhidharma, the second patriarch was Great Master Huike, the third patriarch was Great Master Sengcan, the fourth patriarch was Great Master Daoxin, the fifth patriarch was Great Master Hongren, and the sixth patriarch was Great Master Huineng. These six generational patriarchs, together with all the Buddhas and ancestors above, all transmitted the formless Dharma. May you all see your true nature and give rise to no doubts. The Dharma Treasure Platform Sutra of the Sixth Patriarch is concluded.
