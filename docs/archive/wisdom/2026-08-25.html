般若波羅蜜多心經

舍利子，色不異空，空不異色，色即是空，空即是色，受想行識，亦復如是。

Shariputra, form is not different from emptiness, emptiness is not different from form. Form is emptiness, emptiness is form. The same is true of feelings, perceptions, mental formations, and consciousness.

孟子 · 離婁上

孟子曰：「仁之實，事親是也；義之實，從兄是也。智之實，知斯二者弗去是也；禮之實，節文斯二者是也；樂之實，樂斯二者，樂則生矣；生則惡可已也，惡可已，則不知足之蹈之、手之舞之。」孟子曰：「天下大悅而將歸己。視天下悅而歸己，猶草芥也。惟舜為然。不得乎親，不可以為人；不順乎親，不可以為子。舜盡事親之道而瞽瞍厎豫，瞽瞍厎豫而天下化，瞽瞍厎豫而天下之為父子者定，此之謂大孝。」

Mencius said: "The substance of benevolence is serving one's parents; the substance of righteousness is obeying one's elder brother. The substance of wisdom is to understand these two things and never depart from them; the substance of ritual propriety is to regulate and adorn these two things; the substance of music is to take joy in these two things. When that joy arises, it cannot be stopped; and when it cannot be stopped, one unconsciously begins to dance with one's feet and wave one's hands." Mencius said: "When the whole realm is greatly delighted and is about to turn to you—to regard the whole realm's delight and allegiance as if it were mere grass and rubbish: only Shun was like this. If you cannot win the love of your parents, you cannot be considered a true person; if you cannot be in harmony with your parents, you cannot be considered a true son. Shun exhausted every means of serving his parents, and in the end the Blind Man was pleased. When the Blind Man was pleased, the whole realm was transformed. When the Blind Man was pleased, the standard for the relationship between fathers and sons throughout the realm was set. This is what is called great filial piety."

六祖大師法寶壇經 · 機緣品第七

聞曹溪法席盛化，徑來參禮，遂問曰：「當何所務，即不落階級？」師曰：「汝曾作什麼來？」曰：「聖諦亦不為。」師曰：「落何階級？」曰：「聖諦尚不為，何階級之有？」師深器之，令思首眾。一日，師謂曰：「汝當分化一方，無令斷絕。」思既得法，遂回吉州青原山，弘法紹化。

懷讓禪師，金州杜氏子也。初謁嵩山安國師，安發之曹溪參扣。讓至禮拜，師曰：「甚處來？」曰：「嵩山。

Hearing that the Dharma seat at Caoxi was flourishing and its teaching widely transforming, he went straight there to pay his respects, and then asked, "What should one work at, so as not to fall into gradual stages?" The Master said, "What have you been doing?" He replied, "I do not even engage in the Holy Truths." The Master said, "Then into what stage do you fall?" He replied, "If one does not even engage in the Holy Truths, what stages could there be?" The Master esteemed him deeply, and appointed Xingsi to head the assembly. One day the Master said to him, "You should go and spread the teaching in a region of your own; do not let it be cut off." Having received the Dharma, Xingsi then returned to Mount Qingyuan in Jizhou, where he propagated the Dharma and carried on the work of transformation.

Chan Master Huairang was a son of the Du family of Jinzhou. He first called upon National Master An of Mount Song, and An sent him on to Caoxi to inquire further. When Huairang arrived and made his bows, the Master said, "Where have you come from?" He said, "Mount Song."
