般若波羅蜜多心經 — Heart Sutra — Tuesday

舍利子，色不異空，空不異色，色即是空，空即是色，受想行識，亦復如是。

Shariputra, form is not different from emptiness, emptiness is not different from form. Form is emptiness, emptiness is form. The same is true of feelings, perceptions, mental formations, and consciousness.

孟子・離婁上 — Mencius 130

孟子曰：「伯夷辟紂，居北海之濱，聞文王作，興曰：『盍歸乎來！吾聞西伯善養老者。』太公辟紂，居東海之濱，聞文王作，興曰：『盍歸乎來！吾聞西伯善養老者。』二老者，天下之大老也，而歸之，是天下之父歸之也。天下之父歸之，其子焉往？諸侯有行文王之政者，七年之內，必為政於天下矣。」

Mencius said: "Bo Yi fled from the tyrant Zhou and dwelt on the shores of the North Sea. When he heard that King Wen had risen, he stirred and said: 'Why not go home? I have heard that the Chief of the West is good at caring for the elderly.' Tai Gong also fled from Zhou and dwelt on the shores of the East Sea. When he heard that King Wen had risen, he stirred and said: 'Why not go home? I have heard that the Chief of the West is good at caring for the elderly.' These two elders were the most venerable in all the realm, and they turned to King Wen—meaning the fathers of the realm turned to him. When the fathers of the realm turn to someone, where will their sons go? If any feudal lord were to carry out the government of King Wen, within seven years he would surely govern the whole realm."

六祖大師法寶壇經・機緣品第七 — Platform Sutra 130

』彼曰：『汝見虛空有相貌否？』對曰：『虛空無形，有何相貌？』彼曰：『汝之本性，猶如虛空，了無一物可見，是名正見；無一物可知，是名真知。無有青黃長短，但見本源清淨，覺體圓明，即名見性成佛，亦名如來知見。』學人雖聞此說，猶未決了，乞和尚開示。」師曰：「彼師所說，猶存見知，故令汝未了。吾今示汝一偈：不見一法存無見，大似浮雲遮日面，不知一法守空知，還如太虛生閃電。

'He then asked: "Do you see empty space as having any form or appearance?" I answered: "Empty space has no form — what appearance could it have?" He said: "Your original nature is just like empty space — there is not a single thing to be seen, and this is called right view. There is not a single thing to be known, and this is called true knowledge. There is no blue or yellow, no long or short — just see the original source as pure and clear, the awakened substance as perfectly luminous, and this is called seeing one's nature and becoming a Buddha, also known as the Tathagata's knowledge and insight." Although I heard this teaching, I still have not reached clarity. I beg the Master to reveal it to me.' The Master said: 'What that teacher said still retains notions of seeing and knowing, which is why you have not reached clarity. Now I will show you with a verse: Not seeing a single dharma while holding to a view of nothingness is like a floating cloud obscuring the face of the sun. Not knowing a single dharma while clinging to empty knowing is like lightning flashing in the open sky.'
