般若波羅蜜多心經

舍利子，是諸法空相，不生不滅，不垢不淨，不增不減。是故空中無色，無受想行識。

Shariputra, all dharmas are marked with emptiness — they are neither born nor destroyed, neither defiled nor pure, neither increasing nor decreasing. Therefore, in emptiness there is no form, no feelings, perceptions, mental formations, or consciousness.

孟子·告子下

曰：「奚有於是？亦為之而已矣。有人於此，力不能勝一匹雛，則為無力人矣；今曰舉百鈞，則為有力人矣。然則舉烏獲之任，是亦為烏獲而已矣。夫人豈以不勝為患哉？弗為耳。徐行後長者謂之弟，疾行先長者謂之不弟。夫徐行者，豈人所不能哉？所不為也。堯舜之道，孝弟而已矣。子服堯之服，誦堯之言，行堯之行，是堯而已矣；子服桀之服，誦桀之言，行桀之行，是桀而已矣。」曰：「交得見於鄒君，可以假館，願留而受業於門。」

Mencius said: 'What does height have to do with it? You simply need to act. If someone here cannot lift a single young chicken, he is a man without strength. But if he can lift three thousand catties, then he is a man of great strength. So one who can bear the load of Wu Huo is simply another Wu Huo. Why should anyone worry about being incapable? The problem is simply not trying. Walking slowly behind an elder is called brotherly respect; walking quickly ahead of an elder is called disrespect. Is walking slowly something people cannot do? It is simply something they choose not to do. The Way of Yao and Shun is nothing more than filial piety and brotherly respect. Wear the clothes of Yao, speak the words of Yao, act as Yao acted, and you are simply another Yao. Wear the clothes of Jie, speak the words of Jie, act as Jie acted, and you are simply another Jie.' Cao Jiao said: 'I shall seek an audience with the lord of Zou and ask to stay, so I may study under you.'

金剛般若波羅蜜經·淨心行善分第二十三

「復次，須菩提！是法平等，無有高下，是名『阿耨多羅三藐三菩提』；以無我、無人、無眾生、無壽者，修一切善法，則得阿耨多羅三藐三菩提。須菩提！所言善法者，如來說即非善法，是名善法。」

"Furthermore, Subhuti, this Dharma is equal and without high or low. This is what is called anuttara-samyak-sambodhi. By cultivating all wholesome dharmas with no self, no person, no being, and no one who lives a lifespan, one attains anuttara-samyak-sambodhi. Subhuti, what are called wholesome dharmas — the Tathagata says they are not wholesome dharmas; they are merely called wholesome dharmas."
