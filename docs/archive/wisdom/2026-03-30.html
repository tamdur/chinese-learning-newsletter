般若波羅蜜多心經 — Heart Sutra — Monday

觀自在菩薩，行深般若波羅蜜多時，照見五蘊皆空，度一切苦厄。

Avalokitesvara Bodhisattva, when practicing the profound Prajna Paramita, illuminated the five aggregates and saw that they are all empty, thus overcoming all suffering and distress.

孟子 — 梁惠王上

梁惠王曰：「寡人之於國也，盡心焉耳矣。河內凶，則移其民於河東，移其粟於河內。河東凶亦然。察鄰國之政，無如寡人之用心者。鄰國之民不加少，寡人之民不加多，何也？」孟子對曰：「王好戰，請以戰喻。填然鼓之，兵刃既接，棄甲曳兵而走。或百步而後止，或五十步而後止。以五十步笑百步，則何如？」曰：「不可，直不百步耳，是亦走也。」

King Hui of Liang said, 'I devote my whole heart to governing my state. When there is famine in Henei, I move the people to Hedong and transfer grain to Henei. When there is famine in Hedong, I do the same. When I examine the policies of neighboring states, none governs as attentively as I do. Yet the people of neighboring states do not decrease, and my people do not increase — why is this?' Mencius replied, 'Your Majesty is fond of war, so let me use a war analogy. When the drums thunder and the blades clash, some soldiers throw down their armor and flee. Some stop after a hundred paces, some after fifty paces. What would you think if those who ran fifty paces laughed at those who ran a hundred?' The king said, 'That would not be right. They merely did not run a hundred paces — but they ran all the same.'

禪宗 — 信心銘

契心平等所作俱息狐疑淨盡正信調直一切不留無可記憶虛明自照不勞心力非思量處識情難測真如法界無他無自要急相應唯言不二不二皆同無不包容十方智者皆入此宗宗非促延一念萬年無在不在十方目前極小同大忘絕境界極大同小不見邊表有即是無無即是有若不如是必不須守一即一切一切即一但能如是何慮不畢信心不二不二信心言語道斷非去來今

Align the mind with equanimity, and all striving comes to rest. Doubt is cleansed completely; true faith stands upright. Nothing whatsoever is retained, nothing that can be remembered. Empty, luminous, self-illuminating, no effort of the mind is needed. This is not a place of thinking — consciousness and sentiment cannot fathom it. In the true suchness of the Dharma-realm, there is no other and no self. To swiftly accord with this, only say: not two. In not-two, all is the same; nothing is excluded. The wise in all directions all enter this teaching. This teaching is beyond haste or delay; one thought encompasses ten thousand years. It is nowhere, yet it is everywhere; the ten directions stand before your eyes. The infinitely small equals the infinitely large, when the boundaries of the world are forgotten. The infinitely large equals the infinitely small, when edges can no longer be seen. Existence is precisely emptiness; emptiness is precisely existence. If it is not like this, you must not hold to it. One is all; all is one. If you can simply be like this, why worry that it is not complete? Faith and mind are not two; not-two is the faithful mind. Words cannot open this road; it is not of the past, the present, or the future.
