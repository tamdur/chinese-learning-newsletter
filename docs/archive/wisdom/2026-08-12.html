般若波羅蜜多心經

舍利子，是諸法空相，不生不滅，不垢不淨，不增不減。是故空中無色，無受想行識。

Shariputra, all dharmas are marked with emptiness — they are neither born nor destroyed, neither defiled nor pure, neither increasing nor decreasing. Therefore, in emptiness there is no form, no feelings, perceptions, mental formations, or consciousness.

孟子・離婁上

今也小國師大國而恥受命焉，是猶弟子而恥受命於先師也。如恥之，莫若師文王。師文王，大國五年，小國七年，必為政於天下矣。《詩》云：『商之孫子，其麗不億。上帝既命，侯于周服。侯服于周，天命靡常。殷士膚敏，祼將于京。』孔子曰：『仁不可為眾也。夫國君好仁，天下無敵。』今也欲無敵於天下而不以仁，是猶執熱而不以濯也。《詩》云：『誰能執熱，逝不以濯？』」

"Nowadays small states take great states as their teachers yet are ashamed to accept direction from them—this is like a student being ashamed to accept instruction from his teacher. If they feel ashamed, nothing would be better than to take King Wen as their model. Those who model themselves on King Wen—a great state will achieve supremacy in five years, a small state in seven years, and surely will govern the whole realm. The Odes say: 'The descendants of Shang, their numbers were more than hundreds of thousands. But when the Lord on High gave the mandate, they submitted to Zhou. They submitted to Zhou, for Heaven's mandate is not constant. The officers of Yin, elegant and capable, assist with libations in the capital.' Confucius said: 'Benevolence cannot be overcome by mere numbers. If a state's ruler loves benevolence, he will have no rival in the realm.' Now, those who wish to be without rival in the realm yet do not practice benevolence are like those who try to handle something hot without washing their hands in water. The Odes say: 'Who can handle something hot without rinsing with water?'"

'Furthermore, the sutra speaks of three carts — the goat cart, the deer cart, and the ox cart — and the white ox cart. How are these to be distinguished? I humbly ask the Master to explain once more.' The Master said: 'The sutra's meaning is perfectly clear; it is you yourself who turn away from it in confusion. The reason people of the Three Vehicles cannot fathom the Buddha's wisdom is that the problem lies in their calculating and measuring. The more they exhaust their thought and speculation, the further away they get. The Buddha originally taught for the sake of ordinary beings, not for those already enlightened. If people refuse to accept this principle, let them leave the assembly. What they fail to realize is that they are already sitting in the white ox cart, yet they go outside the gate looking for three other carts. Moreover, the sutra plainly tells you: "There is only the One Buddha Vehicle; there are no other vehicles, whether two or three." All the countless skillful means, all the various causes, conditions, parables, and expressions — all these are for the sake of the One Buddha Vehicle. Why can you not see? The three carts are provisional, established for the past; the One Vehicle is real, established for the present.'
