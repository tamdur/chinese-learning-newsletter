般若波羅蜜多心經

無眼耳鼻舌身意，無色聲香味觸法，無眼界，乃至無意識界。無無明，亦無無明盡，乃至無老死，亦無老死盡。

There are no eyes, ears, nose, tongue, body, or mind; no sights, sounds, smells, tastes, touches, or objects of mind; no realm of sight, and so on up to no realm of consciousness. There is no ignorance and no end of ignorance, and so on up to no old age and death and no end of old age and death.

孟子 · 滕文公上

滕定公薨。世子謂然友曰：「昔者孟子嘗與我言於宋，於心終不忘。今也不幸至於大故，吾欲使子問於孟子，然後行事。」然友之鄒問於孟子。孟子曰：「不亦善乎！親喪固所自盡也。曾子曰：『生事之以禮；死葬之以禮，祭之以禮，可謂孝矣。』諸侯之禮，吾未之學也；雖然，吾嘗聞之矣。三年之喪，齊疏之服，飦粥之食，自天子達於庶人，三代共之。」

When Duke Ding of Teng died, the heir apparent said to Ran You, 'Once Mencius spoke with me in Song, and I have never been able to forget his words. Now that this great misfortune has befallen me, I wish to send you to consult Mencius before we proceed.' Ran You went to Zou and asked Mencius. Mencius said, 'Is this not excellent! In mourning for one's parents, one should certainly give it one's utmost. Zengzi said: "Serve them with propriety while they live; bury them with propriety when they die; sacrifice to them with propriety — this may be called filial." As for the rites of the feudal lords, I have not studied them; nevertheless, I have heard of them. The three-year mourning period, garments of coarse cloth with hemmed edges, and a diet of thin gruel — from the Son of Heaven down to the common people, this was the same across all three dynasties."

師復曰：「今於大梵寺說此頓教，普願法界眾生言下見性成佛。」時，韋使君與官僚道俗，聞師所說，無不省悟，一時作禮，皆歎善哉。

'This verse is the sudden teaching, also called the great Dharma-ship. Those who hear it in delusion take kalpas upon kalpas; those who awaken do so in a single instant.' The Master further said: 'Now here at Dafan Temple I have taught this sudden teaching. I sincerely wish that all beings throughout the dharma-realm may, upon hearing these words, see their nature and attain buddhahood.' At that time, Magistrate Wei and the officials, monks, and laypeople who heard the Master's teaching all without exception attained insight. Together they bowed in respect and exclaimed with admiration: 'Excellent! How wonderful!'
