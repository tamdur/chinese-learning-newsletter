般若波羅蜜多心經

無眼耳鼻舌身意，無色聲香味觸法，無眼界，乃至無意識界。無無明，亦無無明盡，乃至無老死，亦無老死盡。

There are no eyes, ears, nose, tongue, body, or mind; no sights, sounds, smells, tastes, touches, or objects of mind; no realm of sight, and so on up to no realm of consciousness. There is no ignorance and no end of ignorance, and so on up to no old age and death and no end of old age and death.

孟子 · 萬章下

王勃然變乎色。曰：「王勿異也。王問臣，臣不敢不以正對。」王色定，然後請問異姓之卿。曰：「君有過則諫，反覆之而不聽，則去。」

The King flushed with anger. Mencius said: "Let the King not take this amiss. The King asked his minister, and his minister dares not answer with anything but the truth." When the King's expression calmed, he asked about ministers of a different surname. Mencius said: "If the ruler commits faults, they remonstrate. If he repeatedly refuses to listen, they leave."

金剛般若波羅蜜經 · 尊重正教分第十二

「復次，須菩提！隨說是經，乃至四句偈等，當知此處，一切世間，天、人、阿修羅，皆應供養，如佛塔廟。何況有人，盡能受持讀誦。須菩提！當知是人成就最上第一希有之法，若是經典所在之處，即為有佛，若尊重弟子。」

"Furthermore, Subhuti, wherever this sutra is spoken, even as few as four lines of verse, know that that place should be revered by all the world — by gods, humans, and asuras — as if it were a shrine of the Buddha. How much more so for one who can fully receive, uphold, read, and recite it. Subhuti, know that such a person achieves the supreme, foremost, and rarest of dharmas. Wherever this sutra is found, there the Buddha is present, and it should be honored as one would honor his revered disciples."
