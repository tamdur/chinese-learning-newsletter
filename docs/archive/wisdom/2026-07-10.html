般若波羅蜜多心經

無苦集滅道，無智亦無得。以無所得故，菩提薩埵，依般若波羅蜜多故，心無罣礙。

There is no suffering, no cause of suffering, no cessation, no path; no wisdom and no attainment. With nothing to attain, a bodhisattva relies on Prajna Paramita, and the mind is without hindrance.

孟子 · 滕文公上

孟子曰：「許子必種粟而後食乎？」曰：「然。」「許子必織布而後衣乎？」曰：「否。許子衣褐。」「許子冠乎？」曰：「冠。」曰：「奚冠？」曰：「冠素。」曰：「自織之與？」曰：「否。以粟易之。」曰：「許子奚為不自織？」曰：「害於耕。」曰：「許子以釜甑爨，以鐵耕乎？」曰：「然。」「自為之與？」曰：「否。以粟易之。」

Mencius said, 'Does Master Xu eat only grain he has grown himself?' 'Yes.' 'Does Master Xu wear only cloth he has woven himself?' 'No. Master Xu wears rough hemp.' 'Does Master Xu wear a cap?' 'Yes.' 'What kind of cap?' 'A plain cap.' 'Does he weave it himself?' 'No. He trades grain for it.' 'Why does Master Xu not weave it himself?' 'It would interfere with his farming.' 'Does Master Xu cook with pots and pans and plow with iron?' 'Yes.' 'Does he make them himself?' 'No. He trades grain for them.'

師言：「吾與大眾說無相頌。但依此修，常與吾同處無別；若不依此修，剃髮出家於道何益？頌曰：

心平何勞持戒，行直何用修禪！恩則孝養父母，義則上下相憐，讓則尊卑和睦，忍則眾惡無諠，若能鑽木出火，淤泥定生紅蓮。苦口的是良藥，逆耳必是忠言，改過必生智慧，護短心內非賢。日用常行饒益，成道非由施錢，菩提只向心覓，何勞向外求玄。聽說依此修行，西方只在目前。

The Master said: 'I will give the assembly a formless verse. Simply practice according to it, and you will always be with me, no different from being together. But if you do not practice according to it, then even shaving your head and leaving home — what good does it do for the Way?' The verse says: 'When the mind is level, what need is there to uphold precepts? When conduct is straight, what use is practicing meditation? In kindness, care for your parents with filial devotion. In righteousness, let those above and below feel mutual compassion. In yielding, those of high and low station live in harmony. In patience, the host of evils makes no disturbance. If you can drill wood and produce fire, then from the muddy pond the red lotus will surely bloom. Bitter medicine is truly good medicine; words harsh to the ear are surely loyal counsel. Correcting faults gives birth to wisdom; defending shortcomings reveals an unworthy heart. In daily life, always practice generosity and benefit others — the Way is not achieved merely by giving money. Bodhi is to be sought only within your own mind — why labor to seek mysteries outside? If you listen to this teaching and practice accordingly, the Western Pure Land is right before your eyes.'
