般若波羅蜜多心經 — Heart Sutra — Wednesday

舍利子，是諸法空相，不生不滅，不垢不淨，不增不減。是故空中無色，無受想行識。

Shariputra, all dharmas are marked with emptiness — they are neither born nor destroyed, neither defiled nor pure, neither increasing nor decreasing. Therefore, in emptiness there is no form, no feelings, perceptions, mental formations, or consciousness.

孟子 — 公孫丑上

Passage 48

曰：「敢問夫子之不動心，與告子之不動心，可得聞與？」「告子曰：『不得於言，勿求於心；不得於心，勿求於氣。』不得於心，勿求於氣，可；不得於言，勿求於心，不可。夫志，氣之帥也；氣，體之充也。夫志至焉，氣次焉。故曰：『持其志，無暴其氣。』」「既曰『志至焉，氣次焉』，又曰『持其志無暴其氣』者，何也？」曰：「志壹則動氣，氣壹則動志也。今夫蹶者趨者，是氣也，而反動其心。」「敢問夫子惡乎長？」

Gongsun Chou asked: "May I ask about the difference between your unmoved heart, Master, and Gaozi's unmoved heart?" Mencius said: "Gaozi said: 'If you do not succeed with words, do not seek it in the heart; if you do not succeed with the heart, do not seek it in the vital energy.' Not to seek in vital energy what you cannot attain in the heart—that is acceptable. But not to seek in the heart what you cannot attain with words—that is unacceptable. The will is the commander of the vital energy; the vital energy fills the body. The will is paramount, and the vital energy comes next. Therefore I say: 'Hold firm to your will, and do not abuse your vital energy.'" Gongsun Chou asked: "You say the will is paramount and the vital energy comes next, yet you also say to hold firm to your will and not abuse your vital energy—why is that?" Mencius said: "When the will is concentrated, it moves the vital energy; when the vital energy is concentrated, it moves the will. For instance, stumbling and hurrying are matters of the vital energy, yet they in turn agitate the heart." Gongsun Chou asked: "May I ask where your particular strength lies, Master?"

六祖大師法寶壇經 — 行由品第一

Passage 48

時，有一客買柴，使令送至客店；客收去，惠能得錢，却出門外，見一客誦經。惠能一聞經語，心即開悟，遂問：『客誦何經？』客曰：『《金剛經》。』復問：『從何所來，持此經典？』客云：『我從蕲州黃梅縣東禪寺來。其寺是五祖忍大師在彼主化，門人一千有餘；我到彼中禮拜，聽受此經。大師常勸僧俗，但持《金剛經》，即自見性，直了成佛。』惠能聞說，宿昔有緣，乃蒙一客，取銀十兩與惠能，令充老母衣糧，教便往黃梅參禮五祖。

At that time, a customer bought firewood and had me deliver it to his lodging. After the customer took the firewood and I received my payment, I went out the door and encountered another man reciting a sutra. The moment I heard the words of the sutra, my mind was opened to awakening. I asked, 'What sutra are you reciting?' The man said, 'The Diamond Sutra.' I asked further, 'Where have you come from, carrying this scripture?' He replied, 'I come from Dongchan Monastery in Huangmei County, Qizhou. The Fifth Patriarch, Great Master Hongren, presides there, with over a thousand disciples. I went there to pay my respects and received this sutra. The Master constantly urges both monks and laypeople to simply uphold the Diamond Sutra, and they will see their own nature and directly realize Buddhahood.' Hearing this, I felt a karmic connection from past lives. Then a patron gave me ten taels of silver for my mother's clothing and provisions, and told me to go to Huangmei to pay respects to the Fifth Patriarch.
