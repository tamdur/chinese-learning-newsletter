般若波羅蜜多心經 — Saturday

無罣礙故，無有恐怖，遠離顛倒夢想，究竟涅槃。三世諸佛，依般若波羅蜜多故，得阿耨多羅三藐三菩提。

Without hindrance, there is no fear. Far from all inverted views, one attains nirvana. All buddhas of past, present, and future rely on Prajna Paramita and attain supreme, perfect enlightenment.

孟子 — 梁惠王上

『孰能一之？』對曰：『不嗜殺人者能一之。』『孰能與之？』對曰：『天下莫不與也。王知夫苗乎？七八月之間旱，則苗槁矣。天油然作雲，沛然下雨，則苗浡然興之矣。其如是，孰能禦之？今夫天下之人牧，未有不嗜殺人者也，如有不嗜殺人者，則天下之民皆引領而望之矣。誠如是也，民歸之，由水之就下，沛然誰能禦之？』」齊宣王問曰：「齊桓、晉文之事可得聞乎？」

'"Who can unify it?" I replied, "One who does not delight in killing can unify it." "Who would give their allegiance to such a person?" I replied, "Everyone in the world would give their allegiance. Does Your Majesty know about the young rice shoots? If there is a drought in the seventh or eighth month, the shoots wither. But when the sky masses thick clouds and sends down a torrent of rain, the shoots spring up vigorously. When this happens, who can stop it? Among the shepherds of men in the world today, there is not one who does not delight in killing. If there were one who did not delight in killing, all the people in the world would crane their necks looking toward him. If this were truly so, the people would flow to him like water flowing downward — in a torrent that no one could hold back."' 金剛般若波羅蜜經 — 如理實見分第五

「須菩提。於意云何？可以身相見如來不？」「不也，世尊，不可以身相得見如來。何以故？如來所說身相，即非身相。」佛告須菩提：「凡所有相，皆是虛妄。若見諸相非相，即見如來。」

"Subhuti, what do you think? Can the Tathagata be seen by means of bodily characteristics?" "No, World-Honored One. The Tathagata cannot be seen by means of bodily characteristics. Why? What the Tathagata calls bodily characteristics are not bodily characteristics." The Buddha told Subhuti: "All that has characteristics is illusory. If you see all characteristics as non-characteristics, then you see the Tathagata."
