般若波羅蜜多心經

舍利子，是諸法空相，不生不滅，不垢不淨，不增不減。是故空中無色，無受想行識。

Shariputra, all dharmas are marked with emptiness — they are neither born nor destroyed, neither defiled nor pure, neither increasing nor decreasing. Therefore, in emptiness there is no form, no feelings, perceptions, mental formations, or consciousness.

孟子．告子上

孟子曰：「人之於身也，兼所愛。兼所愛，則兼所養也。無尺寸之膚不愛焉，則無尺寸之膚不養也。所以考其善不善者，豈有他哉？於己取之而已矣。體有貴賤，有小大。無以小害大，無以賤害貴。養其小者為小人，養其大者為大人。今有場師，舍其梧檟，養其樲棘，則為賤場師焉。養其一指而失其肩背，而不知也，則為狼疾人也。飲食之人，則人賤之矣，為其養小以失大也。飲食之人無有失也，則口腹豈適為尺寸之膚哉？」

Mencius said: "People love every part of their bodies. Since they love every part, they nourish every part. Since there is not an inch of skin they do not love, there is not an inch of skin they do not nourish. To judge whether one nourishes well or poorly, is there any other way than to examine one's own choices? The body has parts of greater and lesser importance, parts that are major and minor. Do not let the minor harm the major; do not let the less important harm the more important. One who nourishes the minor part becomes a petty person; one who nourishes the major part becomes a great person. Consider a gardener who neglects his fine paulownia and catalpa trees but tends his thorns and brambles—he would be a worthless gardener. Someone who takes care of one finger while losing his shoulders and back, without realizing it, is a confused and reckless person. Those who care only about eating and drinking are looked down upon by others, because they nourish the minor at the expense of the major. If a person who cares about eating and drinking lost nothing else thereby, would the mouth and belly be merely an inch of skin?"

金剛般若波羅蜜經．究竟無我分第十七

何以故？須菩提！實無有法名為菩薩。是故佛說一切法，無我、無人、無眾生、無壽者。」「須菩提！若菩薩作是言：『我當莊嚴佛土』，是不名菩薩。何以故？如來說：莊嚴佛土者，即非莊嚴，是名莊嚴。」「須菩提！若菩薩通達無我、法者，如來說名真是菩薩。」。

Why? Subhuti, there is in reality no dharma called 'bodhisattva.' Therefore the Buddha says that all dharmas are without self, without person, without sentient being, without life span. Subhuti, if a bodhisattva says, 'I shall adorn buddha-lands,' that one is not a bodhisattva. Why? What the Tathagata calls adorning buddha-lands is not adorning, and is therefore called adorning. Subhuti, if a bodhisattva thoroughly comprehends the selflessness of self and dharmas, the Tathagata calls that one a true bodhisattva.
