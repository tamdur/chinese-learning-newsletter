般若波羅蜜多心經

舍利子，色不異空，空不異色，色即是空，空即是色，受想行識，亦復如是。

Shariputra, form is not different from emptiness, emptiness is not different from form. Form is emptiness, emptiness is form. The same is true of feelings, perceptions, mental formations, and consciousness.

孟子 · 萬章下

曰：「敢問招虞人何以？」曰：「以皮冠。庶人以旃，士以旂，大夫以旌。以大夫之招招虞人，虞人死不敢往。以士之招招庶人，庶人豈敢往哉。況乎以不賢人之招招賢人乎？欲見賢人而不以其道，猶欲其入而閉之門也。夫義，路也；禮，門也。惟君子能由是路，出入是門也。《詩》云：『周道如砥，其直如矢；君子所履，小人所視。』」萬章曰：「孔子，君命召，不俟駕而行。然則孔子非與？」曰：「孔子當仕有官職，而以其官召之也。」

Wan Zhang said: "May I ask, what is the proper way to summon a gamekeeper?" Mencius said: "With a leather cap. A commoner is summoned with a plain banner, a scholar with a dragon-and-snake banner, a grand officer with a feathered pennant. When a gamekeeper was summoned with the signal meant for a grand officer, the gamekeeper would rather die than go. When a commoner is summoned with the signal meant for a scholar, how would the commoner dare go? How much worse is it to summon a worthy man with the signal meant for an unworthy man! Wanting to see a worthy man but not following the proper way is like wanting him to enter while keeping the door shut. Righteousness is the road; ritual is the door. Only a gentleman can travel this road and pass through this door. The Book of Songs says: 'The highways of Zhou are smooth as a whetstone, straight as an arrow. They are what the gentleman treads, and what the common people gaze upon.'" Wan Zhang said: "When Confucius received a summons from his ruler, he set out without waiting for his carriage to be yoked. Was Confucius then wrong?" Mencius said: "Confucius was then holding office with official duties, and he was summoned in his capacity as an officeholder."

金剛般若波羅蜜經 · 莊嚴淨土分第十

佛告須菩提：「於意云何？如來昔在燃燈佛所，於法有所得不？」「不也，世尊！如來在燃燈佛所，於法實無所得。」「須菩提！於意云何？菩薩莊嚴佛土不？」「不也，世尊！何以故？莊嚴佛土者，即非莊嚴，是名莊嚴。」「是故，須菩提！諸菩薩摩訶薩，應如是生清淨心，不應住色生心，不應住聲、香、味、觸、法生心，應無所住而生其心。」「須菩提！譬如有人，身如須彌山王，於意云何？是身為大不？」須菩提言：「甚大，世尊！何以故？佛說非身，是名大身。」

The Buddha told Subhuti: "What do you think? When the Tathagata was with Dipankara Buddha, did he attain any dharma?" "No, World-Honored One. When the Tathagata was with Dipankara Buddha, he in truth attained no dharma." "Subhuti, what do you think? Does a bodhisattva adorn buddha-lands?" "No, World-Honored One. Why? Adorning buddha-lands is not adorning; it is called adorning." "Therefore, Subhuti, all bodhisattva-mahasattvas should give rise to a pure mind thus: they should not give rise to a mind that abides in form, nor should they give rise to a mind that abides in sound, smell, taste, touch, or mental objects. They should give rise to a mind that abides nowhere." "Subhuti, suppose there were a person whose body was as great as Mount Sumeru. What do you think — would that body be great?" Subhuti said: "Very great, World-Honored One. Why? The Buddha has said it is not a body; that is what is called a great body."
