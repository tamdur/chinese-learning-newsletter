般若波羅蜜多心經 — Heart Sutra — Tuesday

舍利子，色不異空，空不異色，色即是空，空即是色，受想行識，亦復如是。

Shariputra, form is not different from emptiness, emptiness is not different from form. Form is emptiness, emptiness is form. The same is true of feelings, perceptions, mental formations, and consciousness.

Subhuti, what do you think? When the Tathagata was with Dipankara Buddha, was there any dharma through which he attained anuttara-samyak-sambodhi? No, World-Honored One! As I understand the meaning of what the Buddha has said, when the Buddha was with Dipankara Buddha, there was no dharma through which he attained anuttara-samyak-sambodhi. The Buddha said: So it is, so it is! Subhuti, there is in reality no dharma through which the Tathagata attained anuttara-samyak-sambodhi. Subhuti, if there were any dharma through which the Tathagata attained anuttara-samyak-sambodhi, then Dipankara Buddha would not have given me the prediction: 'In a future life, you shall become a buddha named Shakyamuni.' It is precisely because there is no dharma through which one attains anuttara-samyak-sambodhi that Dipankara Buddha gave me the prediction, saying: 'In a future life, you shall become a buddha named Shakyamuni.'
