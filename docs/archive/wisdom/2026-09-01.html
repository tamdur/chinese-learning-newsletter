般若波羅蜜多心經

第二段（週二） · Segment 2 of 7

舍利子，色不異空，空不異色，色即是空，空即是色，受想行識，亦復如是。

Shariputra, form is not different from emptiness, emptiness is not different from form. Form is emptiness, emptiness is form. The same is true of feelings, perceptions, mental formations, and consciousness.

孟子 · 離婁下

第 144 段 · Passage 144 · ctext.org

孟子曰：「禹惡旨酒而好善言。湯執中，立賢無方。文王視民如傷，望道而未之見。武王不泄邇，不忘遠。周公思兼三王，以施四事；其有不合者，仰而思之，夜以繼日；幸而得之，坐以待旦。」孟子曰：「王者之迹熄而詩亡，詩亡然後春秋作。晉之乘，楚之檮杌，魯之春秋，一也。其事則齊桓、晉文，其文則史。孔子曰：『其義則丘竊取之矣。』」孟子曰：「君子之澤五世而斬，小人之澤五世而斬。予未得為孔子徒也，予私淑諸人也。」

Mencius said: "Yu detested fine wine and loved good counsel. Tang held to the mean and appointed the worthy without regard to their origin. King Wen looked upon the people as if they were wounded, and gazed toward the Way as if he had not yet seen it. King Wu did not neglect those near nor forget those far. The Duke of Zhou aspired to combine the virtues of all three kings and carry out all four of their practices. Whenever something did not accord, he would look upward and think, continuing through the night into the day. When he was fortunate enough to find the answer, he would sit and wait for dawn." Mencius said: "When the traces of the true kings faded, the Odes ceased. When the Odes ceased, the Spring and Autumn Annals was composed. Jin had its Sheng, Chu its Taowu, and Lu its Spring and Autumn — they were all one and the same type of record. Their subject matter was the affairs of Duke Huan of Qi and Duke Wen of Jin, and their style was that of official history. Confucius said: 'As for its moral judgments, I ventured to make those myself.'" Mencius said: "The influence of an exemplary person lasts five generations before it is cut off; the influence of a petty person also lasts five generations before it is cut off. I was not able to be a disciple of Confucius directly — I have refined myself through those who were."

六祖大師法寶壇經 · 機緣品第七

第 144 段 · Passage 144

忽有一僧來禮拜，云：「方辯是西蜀人，昨於南天竺國，見達磨大師，囑方辯速往唐土。吾傳大迦葉正法眼藏及僧伽梨，見傳六代，於韶州曹溪，汝去瞻禮。方辯遠來，願見我師傳來衣鉢。」師乃出示，次問：「上人攻何事業？」曰：「善塑。」師正色曰：「汝試塑看。」辯罔措。過數日，塑就真相，可高七寸，曲盡其妙。師笑曰：「汝只解塑性，不解佛性。」師舒手摩方辯頂，曰：「永為人天福田。

Suddenly a monk arrived and bowed in reverence, saying: "I am Fangbian, a man of Western Shu. Yesterday, in the country of South India, I saw the Great Master Bodhidharma, who instructed Fangbian to hurry to the land of Tang. He said: 'The True Dharma Eye Treasury that I transmitted from Mahakasyapa, together with the samghati robe, has now been passed down through six generations, to Caoxi in Shaozhou. Go there and pay your respects.' Fangbian has come from far away, and wishes to see the robe and bowl handed down to my Master."

The Master brought them out and showed him. Then he asked, "What craft does the venerable one practice?"

Fangbian said, "I am skilled at sculpting."

The Master said gravely, "Then try sculpting something."

Fangbian was at a loss. After several days he finished a true likeness of the Master, some seven inches high, rendered with exquisite skill in every detail.

The Master laughed and said, "You understand only the nature of sculpting; you do not understand Buddha-nature."

The Master reached out his hand and stroked the crown of Fangbian's head, saying, "May you forever be a field of merit for humans and devas.
