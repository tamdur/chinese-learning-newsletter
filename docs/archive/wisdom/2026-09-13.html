般若波羅蜜多心經

故知般若波羅蜜多，是大神咒，是大明咒，是無上咒，是無等等咒，能除一切苦，真實不虛。故說般若波羅蜜多咒，即說咒曰：揭諦揭諦，波羅揭諦，波羅僧揭諦，菩提薩婆訶。

Therefore, know that Prajna Paramita is the great transcendent mantra, the great bright mantra, the supreme mantra, the unequalled mantra, which can remove all suffering and is true, not false. Therefore he proclaimed the Prajna Paramita mantra, saying: Gate gate paragate parasamgate bodhi svaha.

孟子．萬章上

萬章問曰：「舜往于田，號泣于旻天，何為其號泣也？」孟子曰：「怨慕也。」萬章曰：「父母愛之，喜而不忘；父母惡之，勞而不怨。然則舜怨乎？」

Wan Zhang asked: "When Shun went to the fields and wept and cried out to compassionate Heaven, why did he weep and cry out?" Mencius said: "Out of resentful longing." Wan Zhang said: "When parents love you, you rejoice and never forget them; when parents dislike you, you toil without resentment. Was Shun then resentful?"

六祖大師法寶壇經．頓漸品第八

年十三，自玉泉來參禮。師曰：「知識遠來艱辛，還將得本來否？若有本則合識主。試說看。」會曰：「以無住為本，見即是主。」師曰：「這沙彌爭合取次語？」會乃問曰：「和尚坐禪，還見不見？」師以柱杖打三下，云：「吾打汝痛不痛？」對曰：「亦痛亦不痛。」師曰：「吾亦見亦不見。」神會問：「如何是亦見亦不見？」師云：「吾之所見，常見自心過愆，不見他人是非好惡，是以亦見亦不見。汝言：『亦痛亦不痛。

There was a young boy named Shenhui, a son of the Gao family of Xiangyang. At the age of thirteen, he came from Yuquan Temple to pay his respects. The Master said: 'Friend, you have come a long way through hardship. Have you brought the fundamental with you? If you possess the fundamental, you should know its master. Try to speak.' Shenhui said: 'I take non-abiding as the fundamental, and seeing as the master.' The Master said: 'This little novice — how dare you speak so glibly?' Shenhui then asked: 'When the Master sits in meditation, does he see or not see?' The Master struck him three times with his staff and said: 'When I strike you, does it hurt or not hurt?' Shenhui replied: 'It both hurts and doesn't hurt.' The Master said: 'I also both see and don't see.' Shenhui asked: 'What do you mean by both seeing and not seeing?' The Master said: 'What I see is that I constantly see the faults and errors of my own mind. I do not see the right and wrong, the good and bad, of others. This is why I say I both see and don't see. Now what about your saying it both hurts and doesn't hurt?'
