般若波羅蜜多心經

無眼耳鼻舌身意，無色聲香味觸法，無眼界，乃至無意識界。無無明，亦無無明盡，乃至無老死，亦無老死盡。

There are no eyes, ears, nose, tongue, body, or mind; no sights, sounds, smells, tastes, touches, or objects of mind; no realm of sight, and so on up to no realm of consciousness. There is no ignorance and no end of ignorance, and so on up to no old age and death and no end of old age and death.

孟子 · 公孫丑下

孟子為卿於齊，出吊於滕，王使蓋大夫王驩為輔行。王驩朝暮見，反齊滕之路，未嘗與之言行事也。公孫丑曰：「齊卿之位，不為小矣；齊滕之路，不為近矣。反之而未嘗與言行事，何也？」曰：「夫既或治之，予何言哉？」孟子自齊葬於魯，反於齊，止於嬴。充虞請曰：「前日不知虞之不肖，使虞敦匠事。嚴，虞不敢請。今願竊有請也，木若以美然。」

When Mencius was a minister in Qi, he was sent on a condolence mission to Teng. The king appointed Gai's governor, Wang Huan, as his escort. Wang Huan called on him morning and evening, but throughout the journey between Qi and Teng, Mencius never once discussed the affairs of the mission with him. Gongsun Chou said: "The position of a Qi minister is not insignificant, and the road between Qi and Teng is not short. Yet you traveled back and forth without ever discussing the mission with him — why?" Mencius said: "He had already taken charge of everything himself. What was there for me to say?" When Mencius returned from burying his mother in Lu and was back in Qi, he stopped at Ying. Chong Yu said: "Previously, not knowing how unworthy I was, you entrusted me with managing the coffin construction. At that solemn time I did not dare raise questions. Now I would like to ask: didn't the wood seem rather fine?"

六祖大師法寶壇經 · 般若品第二

善知識！摩訶般若波羅蜜是梵語，此言大智慧到彼岸。此須心行，不在口念。口念心不行，如幻、如化、如露、如電；口念心行，則心口相應，本性是佛，離性無別佛。何名摩訶？摩訶是大。心量廣大，猶如虛空，無有邊畔，亦無方圓大小，亦非青黃赤白，亦無上下長短，亦無瞋無喜，無是無非，無善無惡，無有頭尾。諸佛剎土，盡同虛空。世人妙性本空，無有一法可得。自性真空，亦復如是。善知識！莫聞吾說空，便即著空。

'Good friends, Mahaprajnaparamita is a Sanskrit term meaning "great wisdom that reaches the other shore." This must be practiced with the mind, not merely recited with the mouth. If the mouth recites but the mind does not practice, it is like an illusion, a phantom, dewdrops, or a lightning flash. If the mouth recites and the mind also practices, then mind and mouth correspond, and one's original nature is itself Buddha — apart from this nature there is no other Buddha. What does "maha" mean? Maha means "great." The capacity of the mind is vast and great, like empty space, without boundaries. It is neither square nor round, neither great nor small. It is not blue, yellow, red, or white. It has no above or below, no long or short. It has no anger or joy, no right or wrong, no good or evil, no head or tail. All buddha-lands are ultimately like empty space. The wondrous nature of worldly people is originally empty — there is not a single dharma that can be grasped. The true emptiness of self-nature is also like this. Good friends, do not hear me speak of emptiness and then become attached to emptiness.'
