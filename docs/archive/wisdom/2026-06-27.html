般若波羅蜜多心經

無罣礙故，無有恐怖，遠離顛倒夢想，究竟涅槃。三世諸佛，依般若波羅蜜多故，得阿耨多羅三藐三菩提。

Without hindrance, there is no fear. Far from all inverted views, one attains nirvana. All buddhas of past, present, and future rely on Prajna Paramita and attain supreme, perfect enlightenment.

孟子・公孫丑下

孟子去齊。尹士語人曰：「不識王之不可以為湯武，則是不明也；識其不可，然且至，則是干澤也。千里而見王，不遇故去。三宿而後出晝，是何濡滯也？士則茲不悅。」

Mencius left Qi. Yin Shi said to others: "If he did not realize that the king could not become a Tang or a Wu, then he is not perceptive. If he realized it yet still came, then he was just angling for gain. He traveled a thousand li to see the king; finding him unreceptive, he left. Yet he lingered three nights before departing Zhou — why was he so slow? This is what I find displeasing."

六祖大師法寶壇經・般若品第二

』善知識！我於忍和尚處，一聞言下便悟，頓見真如本性。是以將此教法流行，令學道者頓悟菩提。各自觀心，自見本性。若自不悟，須覓大善知識、解最上乘法者，直示正路。是善知識有大因緣，所謂化導令得見性。一切善法，因善知識能發起故。三世諸佛、十二部經，在人性中本自具有。不能自悟，須求善知識指示方見；若自悟者，不假外求。若一向執謂須他善知識方得解脫者，無有是處。何以故？自心內有知識自悟。

'Good friends, when I was with the Fifth Patriarch, upon hearing a single phrase I immediately awakened and directly saw the true suchness of my original nature. This is why I am spreading this teaching, so that students of the Way may suddenly awaken to bodhi. Each of you, look into your own mind and see your own nature. If you cannot awaken by yourself, you must seek out a great good teacher — one who understands the Supreme Vehicle Dharma — to point you directly to the right path. Such a good teacher has great karmic affinity, which is to say: guiding and teaching others so they may see their nature. All wholesome dharmas can arise because of a good teacher. The buddhas of the three times and the twelve divisions of the canon are inherently complete within human nature. If you cannot awaken by yourself, you must seek a good teacher's guidance to see it. But if you are one who awakens on your own, do not seek outside. If you stubbornly insist that only through another good teacher can you attain liberation, that is impossible. Why? Within your own mind there is already a knowing awareness that awakens of itself.'
