般若波羅蜜多心經

舍利子，色不異空，空不異色，色即是空，空即是色，受想行識，亦復如是。

Shariputra, form is not different from emptiness, emptiness is not different from form. Form is emptiness, emptiness is form. The same is true of feelings, perceptions, mental formations, and consciousness.

孟子 · 公孫丑下

孟子之平陸。謂其大夫曰：「子之持戟之士，一日而三失伍，則去之否乎？」曰：「不待三。」「然則子之失伍也亦多矣。凶年饑歲，子之民，老羸轉於溝壑，壯者散而之四方者，幾千人矣。」曰：「此非距心之所得為也。」曰：「今有受人之牛羊而為之牧之者，則必為之求牧與芻矣。求牧與芻而不得，則反諸其人乎？抑亦立而視其死與？」曰：「此則距心之罪也。」

Mencius went to Pinlu and said to its governor: "If one of your soldiers deserted his post three times in a single day, would you dismiss him?" The governor said: "I would not wait for three times." "But you yourself have deserted your post many times over. In years of famine and hardship, your people — the old and weak left dying in ditches, the able-bodied scattered to the four directions — have numbered in the thousands." The governor said: "This is not something that I, Ju Xin, have the power to remedy." Mencius said: "Suppose someone were entrusted with another person's cattle and sheep to tend. He would surely need to find pasture and fodder for them. If he could not find pasture and fodder, would he return the animals to their owner, or would he simply stand by and watch them die?" The governor said: "This is indeed my fault."

六祖大師法寶壇經 · 行由品第一

願聞先聖教者，各令淨心，聞了各自除疑，如先代聖人無別。」

一眾聞法，歡喜作禮而退。

Those who wish to hear the teaching of the ancient sages, let each of you purify your mind. Having heard it, let each of you cast away your doubts and be no different from the sages of the past.'

The entire assembly, having heard the Dharma, rejoiced, bowed, and withdrew.
