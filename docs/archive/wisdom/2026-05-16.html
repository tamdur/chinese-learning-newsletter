般若波羅蜜多心經 — Heart Sutra — Saturday

無罣礙故，無有恐怖，遠離顛倒夢想，究竟涅槃。三世諸佛，依般若波羅蜜多故，得阿耨多羅三藐三菩提。

Without hindrance, there is no fear. Far from all inverted views, one attains nirvana. All buddhas of past, present, and future rely on Prajna Paramita and attain supreme, perfect enlightenment.

孟子 — 公孫丑上 — Mencius

曰：「文王何可當也？由湯至於武丁，賢聖之君六七作。天下歸殷久矣，久則難變也。武丁朝諸侯有天下，猶運之掌也。紂之去武丁未久也，其故家遺俗，流風善政，猶有存者；又有微子、微仲、王子比干、箕子、膠鬲皆賢人也，相與輔相之，故久而後失之也。尺地莫非其有也，一民莫非其臣也，然而文王猶方百里起，是以難也。齊人有言曰：『雖有智慧，不如乘勢；雖有鎡基，不如待時。』

Mencius said: "How can anyone be compared to King Wen? From Tang to Wu Ding, there arose six or seven worthy and sagely rulers. The world had belonged to the Yin dynasty for a long time, and what has lasted long is hard to change. Wu Ding held court over all the lords and possessed the empire as easily as turning it in his palm. The tyrant Zhou was not far removed from Wu Ding's time—the old families and inherited customs, the lingering influence of good governance, still survived. Moreover, there were the Viscount of Wei, Zhong of Wei, Prince Bi Gan, the Viscount of Ji, and Jiao Ge—all worthy men who assisted the dynasty together. So it was a long time before the dynasty was lost. Every foot of land belonged to Yin, every person was its subject—and yet King Wen had to rise from a territory of only a hundred li. That is why it was so difficult. The people of Qi have a saying: 'Though you have wisdom, it is better to ride the momentum of circumstances; though you have good tools, it is better to wait for the right season.'"

金剛般若波羅蜜經 — 一合理相分第三十 — Diamond Sutra

「須菩提！若善男子、善女人，以三千大千世界碎為微塵，於意云何？是微塵眾寧為多不？」須菩提言「甚多，世尊！何以故？若是微塵眾實有者，佛則不說是微塵眾。所以者何？佛說微塵眾，則非微塵眾，是名微塵眾。」「世尊！如來所說三千大千世界，則非世界，是名世界。何以故？若世界實有者，則是一合相。如來說一合相，則非一合相，是名一合相。」「須菩提！一合相者，則是不可說；但凡夫之人貪著其事。」

"Subhuti, if a good man or good woman were to grind the three thousand great thousand worlds into fine dust, what do you think — would that mass of fine dust be great?" Subhuti said: "Very great, World-Honored One! Why? If that mass of fine dust were truly real, the Buddha would not call it a mass of fine dust. Why is this? What the Buddha calls a mass of fine dust is not a mass of fine dust — it is merely called a mass of fine dust." "World-Honored One! What the Tathagata calls the three thousand great thousand worlds is not a world — it is merely called a world. Why? If a world were truly real, it would be a composite unity. What the Tathagata calls a composite unity is not a composite unity — it is merely called a composite unity." "Subhuti, a composite unity is something that cannot be spoken of; yet ordinary people are greedily attached to such things."
