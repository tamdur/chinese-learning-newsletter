般若波羅蜜多心經

舍利子，色不異空，空不異色，色即是空，空即是色，受想行識，亦復如是。

Shariputra, form is not different from emptiness, emptiness is not different from form. Form is emptiness, emptiness is form. The same is true of feelings, perceptions, mental formations, and consciousness.

孟子・公孫丑下

不得已而之景丑氏宿焉。景子曰：「內則父子，外則君臣，人之大倫也。父子主恩，君臣主敬。丑見王之敬子也，未見所以敬王也。」曰：「惡！是何言也！齊人無以仁義與王言者，豈以仁義為不美也？其心曰『是何足與言仁義也』云爾，則不敬莫大乎是。我非堯舜之道，不敢以陳於王前，故齊人莫如我敬王也。」景子曰：「否，非此之謂也。禮曰：『父召，無諾；君命召，不俟駕。』固將朝也，聞王命而遂不果，宜與夫禮若不相似然。」

Having no choice, Mencius went to stay at the Jingchou household. Master Jing said: "Within the family there is the bond of father and son; outside there is the bond of ruler and minister — these are the great relationships of human life. Between father and son, affection is paramount; between ruler and minister, respect is paramount. I have seen the king show respect to you, but I have not seen how you show respect to the king." Mencius said: "What! What kind of talk is this? Among the people of Qi, no one speaks to the king about benevolence and righteousness. Do you think it is because they consider benevolence and righteousness not admirable? In their hearts they think, 'This king is not worth talking to about benevolence and righteousness' — that is all. There is no greater disrespect than that. As for me, I would not dare present anything before the king that was not the Way of Yao and Shun. Therefore no one in Qi respects the king as much as I do." Master Jing said: "No, that is not what I meant. The rites say: 'When a father summons, do not answer merely yes; when a ruler commands your presence, do not wait for the carriage.' You were about to go to court, but upon hearing the king's summons you did not follow through — this seems inconsistent with propriety."

六祖大師法寶壇經・行由品第一

』遂出至廣州法性寺，值印宗法師講《涅槃經》。時有風吹旛動，一僧曰：『風動。』一僧曰：『旛動。』議論不已。惠能進曰：『不是風動，不是旛動，仁者心動。』一眾駭然。印宗延至上席，徵詰奧義。見惠能言簡理當，不由文字，宗云：『行者定非常人。久聞黃梅衣法南來，莫是行者否？』惠能曰：『不敢。』宗於是作禮，告請傳來衣鉢出示大眾。宗復問曰：『黃梅付囑，如何指授？』惠能曰：『指授即無；惟論見性，不論禪定解脫。

He then went to Faxing Temple in Guangzhou, where the monk Yinzong was lecturing on the Nirvana Sutra. At that time, the wind was blowing a banner, and one monk said, 'The wind is moving.' Another monk said, 'The banner is moving.' They argued back and forth without end. Huineng stepped forward and said, 'It is not the wind that moves, nor the banner that moves — it is your minds that move.' The entire assembly was astonished. Yinzong invited Huineng to the seat of honor and questioned him on the profound meaning of the teaching. Seeing that Huineng's words were simple yet perfectly apt, and not dependent on written texts, Yinzong said, 'You are certainly no ordinary person. I have long heard that the robe and Dharma of Huangmei came south — could it be you?' Huineng said, 'I would not presume to claim so.' Yinzong then bowed and asked Huineng to show the robe and bowl to the assembly. Yinzong asked further, 'How did the master at Huangmei instruct you when he transmitted the teaching?' Huineng said, 'There was no special instruction. We only discussed seeing one's own nature — not meditation or liberation.'
