般若波羅蜜多心經

心經 — 星期日

故知般若波羅蜜多，是大神咒，是大明咒，是無上咒，是無等等咒，能除一切苦，真實不虛。故說般若波羅蜜多咒，即說咒曰：揭諦揭諦，波羅揭諦，波羅僧揭諦，菩提薩婆訶。

Therefore, know that Prajna Paramita is the great transcendent mantra, the great bright mantra, the supreme mantra, the unequalled mantra, which can remove all suffering and is true, not false. Therefore he proclaimed the Prajna Paramita mantra, saying: Gate gate paragate parasamgate bodhi svaha.

孟子 告子上

孟子．告子上

孟子曰：「無或乎王之不智也，雖有天下易生之物也，一日暴之、十日寒之，未有能生者也。吾見亦罕矣，吾退而寒之者至矣，吾如有萌焉何哉？今夫弈之為數，小數也；不專心致志，則不得也。弈秋，通國之善弈者也。使弈秋誨二人弈，其一人專心致志，惟弈秋之為聽。一人雖聽之，一心以為有鴻鵠將至，思援弓繳而射之，雖與之俱學，弗若之矣。為是其智弗若與？曰非然也。」

Mencius said: "Do not be puzzled by the king's lack of wisdom. Even the most easily grown thing in the world—if you expose it to heat for one day and freeze it for ten days, nothing can survive. The occasions on which I see the king are rare, and as soon as I withdraw, those who 'freeze' him arrive. What can I do about any sprouts that may appear? Now take the game of chess—it is a minor art, but without complete concentration and single-minded devotion, you cannot master it. Chess Master Qiu was the best chess player in the country. Suppose he is teaching two students. One concentrates with total devotion, listening only to Chess Master Qiu. The other, though listening, keeps thinking that a swan is about to fly by, and wonders about drawing a bow to shoot it down. Though he studies alongside the first student, he will never be as good. Is this because his intelligence is inferior? I say it is not."

金剛般若波羅蜜經／究竟無我分第十七

究竟無我分第十七

爾時，須菩提白佛言：「世尊！善男子、善女人，發阿耨多羅三藐三菩提心，云何應住？云何降伏其心？」佛告須菩提：「善男子、善女人，發阿耨多羅三藐三菩提心者，當生如是心：我應滅度一切眾生。滅度一切眾生已，而無有一眾生實滅度者。何以故？須菩提！若菩薩有我相、人相、眾生相、壽者相，則非菩薩。所以者何？須菩提！實無有法，發阿耨多羅三藐三菩提心者。

At that time, Subhuti addressed the Buddha: World-Honored One, if a good man or good woman gives rise to the aspiration for anuttara-samyak-sambodhi, how should they abide? How should they subdue their mind? The Buddha told Subhuti: A good man or good woman who gives rise to the aspiration for anuttara-samyak-sambodhi should give rise to this thought: I should bring all sentient beings to final nirvana. Yet when all sentient beings have been brought to final nirvana, in truth not a single sentient being has been brought to final nirvana. Why? Subhuti, if a bodhisattva holds the mark of self, the mark of person, the mark of sentient being, or the mark of life span, then that one is not a bodhisattva. Why is this? Subhuti, there is in reality no dharma called 'giving rise to the aspiration for anuttara-samyak-sambodhi.'
