般若波羅蜜多心經

無眼耳鼻舌身意，無色聲香味觸法，無眼界，乃至無意識界。無無明，亦無無明盡，乃至無老死，亦無老死盡。

There are no eyes, ears, nose, tongue, body, or mind; no sights, sounds, smells, tastes, touches, or objects of mind; no realm of sight, and so on up to no realm of consciousness. There is no ignorance and no end of ignorance, and so on up to no old age and death and no end of old age and death.

孟子 — 梁惠王下

「今燕虐其民，王往而徵之。民以為將拯己於水火之中也，籠食壺漿，以迎王師。若殺其父兄，係累其子弟，毀其宗廟，遷其重器，如之何其可也？天下固畏齊之彊也。今又倍地而不行仁政，是動天下之兵也。王速出令，反其旄倪，止其重器，謀於燕眾，置君而後去之，則猶可及止也。」鄒與魯鬰。穆公問曰：「吾有司死者三十三人，而民莫之死也。誤之，則不可勝誤；不誤，則疾視其長上之死而不救，如之何則可也？」

"Now Yan was oppressing its people, and Your Majesty went and punished it. The people thought you were going to rescue them from fire and flood, and they welcomed your army with baskets of food and jars of drink. But if you kill their fathers and elder brothers, put their sons and younger brothers in chains, destroy their ancestral temples, and carry off their precious vessels—how can that be acceptable? The world already fears the strength of Qi. Now you double your territory without practicing benevolent government—this will set the armies of the world in motion against you. I urge Your Majesty to issue orders at once: release the old and young captives, stop removing the precious vessels, consult with the people of Yan, install a new ruler, and then withdraw. If you do this, it may still be possible to avert disaster." There was a clash between Zou and Lu. Duke Mu asked, "Thirty-three of my officials were killed, and not a single commoner died to save them. If I punish the people, there are too many to punish. If I do not punish them, they simply looked on as their superiors were killed without lifting a hand. What should be done?"

金剛般若波羅蜜經 — 無法可得分第二十二

須菩提白佛言：「世尊！佛得阿耒多羅三藐三菩提。為無所得耶？」佛言：「如是，如是。須菩提！我於阿耒多羅三藐三菩提乃至無有少法可得，是名阿耒多羅三藐三菩提。」

Subhuti addressed the Buddha, saying: "World-Honored One! When the Buddha attained anuttara-samyak-sambodhi, was there truly nothing attained?" The Buddha said: "Just so, just so. Subhuti, with regard to anuttara-samyak-sambodhi, there is not even the slightest dharma to be attained. This is what is called anuttara-samyak-sambodhi."
