般若波羅蜜多心經

舍利子，色不異空，空不異色，色即是空，空即是色，受想行識，亦復如是。

Shariputra, form is not different from emptiness, emptiness is not different from form. Form is emptiness, emptiness is form. The same is true of feelings, perceptions, mental formations, and consciousness.

孟子 · 滕文公上

「設為庠序學校以教之：庠者，養也；校者，教也；序者，射也。夏曰校，殷曰序，周曰庠，學則三代共之，皆所以明人倫也。人倫明於上，小民親於下。有王者起，必來取法，是為王者師也。《詩》云『周雖舊邦，其命惟新』，文王之謂也。子力行之，亦以新子之國。」

'Establish xiang, xu, xue, and xiao schools to educate the people. Xiang means "to nourish"; xiao means "to teach"; xu means "archery" [and by extension, discipline]. The Xia dynasty called them xiao, the Yin called them xu, the Zhou called them xiang, while xue was the term shared by all three dynasties. The purpose of all of them was to illuminate the human relationships. When human relationships are made clear by those above, the common people below will be drawn together in affection. Should a true king arise, he would surely come to learn from you — you would be the teacher of a true king. The Odes say: "Although Zhou was an old state, its mandate was new" — this refers to King Wen. If you exert yourself in this, you too will make your state new.'

六祖大師法寶壇經 · 疑問品第三

念念見性，常行平直，到如彈指，便覩彌陀。使君但行十善，何須更願往生？不斷十惡之心，何佛即來迎請？若悟無生頓法，見西方只在剎那。不悟念佛求生，路遙如何得達。惠能與諸人，移西方於剎那間，目前便見。各願見否？」

眾皆頂禮云：「若此處見，何須更願往生？願和尚慈悲，便現西方，普令得見。」

師言：「大眾！世人自色身是城，眼耳鼻舌是門，外有五門，內有意門。心是地，性是王。王居心地上，性在王在，性去王無。

'In thought after thought, see your own nature; always act with fairness and directness — and arrival comes in a finger-snap, and you will see Amitabha. Prefect, just practice the ten virtues — what need is there to vow for rebirth? If you do not sever the mind of the ten evils, what Buddha would come to welcome you? If you awaken to the sudden teaching of the unborn, you see the West in an instant. Without awakening, reciting the Buddha's name and seeking rebirth — the road is so long, how could you ever arrive? Huineng will now, for all of you, move the Western Pure Land here in an instant, so you can see it right before your eyes. Does everyone wish to see it?' The assembly all prostrated and said: 'If we can see it right here, what need is there to vow for rebirth elsewhere? We beg the Master, in his compassion, to manifest the West so that all may see it.' The Master said: 'Great assembly! The worldly person's own physical body is the city. The eyes, ears, nose, and tongue are the gates. There are five gates on the outside and one gate of mind-consciousness within. The mind is the ground; the nature is the sovereign. The sovereign dwells upon the ground of the mind. When the nature is present, the sovereign is present; when the nature departs, the sovereign is gone.'
