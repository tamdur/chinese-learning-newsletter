般若波羅蜜多心經 — Heart Sutra — Wednesday

舍利子，是諸法空相，不生不滅，不垢不淨，不增不減。是故空中無色，無受想行識。

Shariputra, all dharmas are marked with emptiness — they are neither born nor destroyed, neither defiled nor pure, neither increasing nor decreasing. Therefore, in emptiness there is no form, no feelings, perceptions, mental formations, or consciousness.

孟子 · 梁惠王上 — Mencius, Book 1A §13

來源：ctext.org

王說曰：「《詩》云：『他人有心，予忖度之。』夫子之謂也。夫我乃行之，反而求之，不得吾心。夫子言之，於我心有戚戚焉。此心之所以合於王者，何也？」曰：「有復於王者曰：『吾力足以舉百鈞』，而不足以舉一羽；『明足以察秋毫之末』，而不見輿薪，則王許之乎？」曰：「否。」

The king was pleased and said, 'The Ode says: "What other men have in their hearts, I measure by reflection." This describes you, Master. For I was the one who acted, yet when I turned inward to examine myself, I could not grasp my own motive. When you, Master, spoke of it, my heart stirred with recognition. How is it that this heart of mine accords with true kingship?' Mencius said, 'Suppose someone reported to Your Majesty: "My strength is sufficient to lift three thousand catties, but not sufficient to lift a single feather; my eyesight is sharp enough to discern the tip of an autumn hair, but I cannot see a cartload of firewood." Would Your Majesty accept this?' The king said, 'No.'

金剛般若波羅蜜經 · 一相無相分第九 — Diamond Sutra

來源：CBETA 電子佛典

「須菩提！於意云何？須陀洹能作是念：『我得須陀洹果』不？」須菩提言：「不也，世尊！何以故？須陀洹名為入流，而無所入；不入色、聲、香、味、觸、法，是名須陀洹。」「須菩提！於意云何？斯陀含能作是念：『我得斯陀含果』不？」須菩提言：「不也，世尊！何以故？斯陀含名一往來，而實無往來，是名斯陀含。

"Subhuti, what do you think? Can a stream-enterer have the thought 'I have attained the fruit of stream-entry'?" Subhuti said: "No, World-Honored One. Why? A stream-enterer is called 'one who has entered the stream,' yet there is nothing entered. One does not enter into form, sound, smell, taste, touch, or mental objects — this is what is called a stream-enterer." "Subhuti, what do you think? Can a once-returner have the thought 'I have attained the fruit of once-returning'?" Subhuti said: "No, World-Honored One. Why? A once-returner is called 'one who comes and goes once more,' yet in truth there is no coming or going — this is what is called a once-returner."
