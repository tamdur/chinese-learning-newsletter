般若波羅蜜多心經

舍利子，色不異空，空不異色，色即是空，空即是色，受想行識，亦復如是。

Shariputra, form is not different from emptiness, emptiness is not different from form. Form is emptiness, emptiness is form. The same is true of feelings, perceptions, mental formations, and consciousness.

孟子．告子上

孟子曰：「仁，人心也；義，人路也。舍其路而弗由，放其心而不知求，哀哉！人有雞犬放，則知求之；有放心，而不知求。學問之道無他，求其放心而已矣。」孟子曰：「今有無名之指，屈而不信，非疾痛害事也，如有能信之者，則不遠秦楚之路，為指之不若人也。指不若人，則知惡之；心不若人，則不知惡，此之謂不知類也。」孟子曰：「拱把之桐梓，人苟欲生之，皆知所以養之者。至於身，而不知所以養之者，豈愛身不若桐梓哉？弗思甚也。」

Mencius said: "Benevolence is the heart of a person; righteousness is the path of a person. To abandon that path and not follow it, to lose that heart and not know to seek it—how sad! When people's chickens and dogs stray, they know to search for them. But when their heart strays, they do not know to search for it. The way of learning is nothing other than seeking the heart that has strayed." Mencius said: "Suppose there is someone whose ring finger is bent and cannot straighten. It is not painful and it does not interfere with his work. Yet if there were someone who could straighten it, he would not consider the road to Qin or Chu too far to travel, because his finger is not as good as other people's. When a finger is not as good as others', one knows to be troubled by it. But when one's heart is not as good as others', one does not know to be troubled. This is called not knowing what matters." Mencius said: "If people have a paulownia or catalpa tree of only a span or two in girth and wish it to grow, they all know how to nourish it. But when it comes to their own selves, they do not know how to nourish themselves. Surely it is not that they care about themselves less than about a paulownia tree? They simply do not think."

Why is this? 'Tathagata' means the suchness of all dharmas. If someone says that the Tathagata attained anuttara-samyak-sambodhi — Subhuti, in reality there is no dharma through which the Buddha attained anuttara-samyak-sambodhi. Subhuti, what are called 'all dharmas' are not all dharmas, and are therefore called all dharmas. Subhuti, it is like a person with a great body.
