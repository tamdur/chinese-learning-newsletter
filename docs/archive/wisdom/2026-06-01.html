般若波羅蜜多心經 — Heart Sutra — Monday

觀自在菩薩，行深般若波羅蜜多時，照見五蘊皆空，度一切苦厄。

Avalokitesvara Bodhisattva, when practicing the profound Prajna Paramita, illuminated the five aggregates and saw that they are all empty, thus overcoming all suffering and distress.

孟子 — Mencius (公孫丑上)

孟子曰：「子路，人告之以有過則喜。禹聞善言則拜。大舜有大焉，善與人同。舍己從人，樂取於人以為善。自耕、稼、陶、漁以至為帝，無非取於人者。取諸人以為善，是與人為善者也。故君子莫大乎與人為善。」

Mencius said: "When someone pointed out a fault to Zilu, he was delighted. When Yu heard good counsel, he bowed in gratitude. The great Shun surpassed them both: he took goodness as something to share with others. He set aside his own ways to follow others, and delighted in learning from others in order to do good. From the time he was a farmer, a potter, and a fisherman, all the way until he became emperor, there was nothing he did not learn from others. To take from others in order to do good—this is to help others do good. Therefore, there is no greater thing for a noble person than to help others do good."

六祖大師法寶壇經 — Platform Sutra (行由品第一)

』祖知悟本性，謂惠能曰：『不識本心，學法無益；若識自本心，見自本性，即名丈夫、天人師、佛。』三更受法，人盡不知，便傳頓教及衣鉢，云：『汝為第六代祖，善自護念，廣度有情，流布將來，無令斷絕。聽吾偈曰：

有情來下種，因地果還生，無情既無種，無性亦無生。

「祖復曰：『昔達磨大師，初來此土，人未之信，故傳此衣，以為信體，代代相承；法則以心傳心，皆令自悟自解。

The Patriarch, knowing that Huineng had awakened to the original nature, said to him, 'If you do not recognize your original mind, studying the Dharma is of no benefit. If you recognize your original mind and see your original nature, then you are called a great person, a teacher of gods and humans, a Buddha.' At the third watch the Dharma was transmitted, and no one knew. The Patriarch also passed on the Sudden Teaching, along with the robe and bowl, saying, 'You are now the Sixth Patriarch. Guard your thoughts well, liberate sentient beings far and wide, and spread the teaching into the future so that it will not be cut off. Listen to my verse: With sentient beings, a seed is sown; / From this ground, the fruit is born. / Without sentience there is no seed; / Without a nature, there is no birth.' The Patriarch also said, 'Long ago, when the Great Master Bodhidharma first came to this land, people did not yet trust him. Therefore this robe was transmitted as a token of faith, passed down from generation to generation. But the Dharma is transmitted mind to mind, each person arriving at their own awakening and understanding.
