般若波羅蜜多心經

無眼耳鼻舌身意，無色聲香味觸法，無眼界，乃至無意識界。無無明，亦無無明盡，乃至無老死，亦無老死盡。

There are no eyes, ears, nose, tongue, body, or mind; no sights, sounds, smells, tastes, touches, or objects of mind; no realm of sight, and so on up to no realm of consciousness. There is no ignorance and no end of ignorance, and so on up to no old age and death and no end of old age and death.

孟子 — 梁惠王下

孟子對曰：「有。惟仁者為能以大事小，是故湯事葛，文王事昆夷；惟智者為能以小事大，故大王事獯鬻，句踐事吳。以大事小者，樂天者也；以小事大者，畏天者也。樂天者保天下，畏天者保其國。《詩》云：『畏天之威，于時保之。』」王曰：「大哉言矣！寡人有疾，寡人好勇。」

Mencius replied, "There is. Only a benevolent ruler can serve a smaller state while leading a larger one. That is why Tang served the state of Ge, and King Wen served the Kunyi tribes. Only a wise ruler can serve a larger state while leading a smaller one. That is why King Tai served the Xunyu, and Goujian served the state of Wu. He who serves a smaller state while leading a larger one delights in Heaven. He who serves a larger state while leading a smaller one stands in awe of Heaven. He who delights in Heaven protects the whole world. He who stands in awe of Heaven protects his own state. The Odes say, 'Standing in awe of Heaven's majesty, thereby he preserved it.'" The king said, "What great words! But I have a weakness—I love courage."

金剛般若波羅蜜經 — 離相寂滅分第十四

又說：一切眾生，即非眾生。須菩提！如來是真語者、實語者、如語者、不誑語者、不異語者。」「須菩提！如來所得法，此法無實無虛。須菩提！若菩薩心住於法而行布施，如人入闇，則無所見；若菩薩心不住法而行布施，如人有目，日光明照，見種種色。」「須菩提！當來之世，若有善男子、善女人，能於此經，受持讀誦，則為如來，以佛智慧，悉知是人，悉見是人，皆得成就無量無邊功德。」。

And further says: all sentient beings are not sentient beings. Subhuti, the Tathagata is one who speaks truth, speaks reality, speaks things as they are, does not speak deceptively, and does not speak inconsistently. Subhuti, the dharma the Tathagata has attained is neither real nor unreal. Subhuti, if a bodhisattva practices generosity while the mind dwells on dharmas, it is like a person entering darkness — they cannot see anything. If a bodhisattva practices generosity while the mind does not dwell on dharmas, it is like a person with eyes in bright sunlight — they can see all manner of forms. Subhuti, in ages to come, if a good man or good woman is able to receive, uphold, read, and recite this sutra, the Tathagata, with his buddha-wisdom, will fully know and fully see that such a person will achieve immeasurable and boundless merit.
