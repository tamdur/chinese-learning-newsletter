般若波羅蜜多心經

無罣礙故，無有恐怖，遠離顛倒夢想，究竟涅槃。三世諸佛，依般若波羅蜜多故，得阿耨多羅三藐三菩提。

Without hindrance, there is no fear. Far from all inverted views, one attains nirvana. All buddhas of past, present, and future rely on Prajna Paramita and attain supreme, perfect enlightenment.

孟子・滕文公下

孟子曰：「湯居亳，與葛為鄰，葛伯放而不祀。湯使人問之曰：『何為不祀？』曰：『無以供犧牲也。』湯使遺之牛羊。葛伯食之，又不以祀。湯又使人問之曰：『何為不祀？』曰：『無以供粢盛也。』湯使亳眾往為之耕，老弱饋食。葛伯率其民，要其有酒食黍稻者奪之，不授者殺之。有童子以黍肉餉，殺而奪之。《書》曰：『葛伯仇餉。』此之謂也。為其殺是童子而征之，四海之內皆曰：『非富天下也，為匹夫匹婦復讎也。』『湯始征，自葛載』，十一征而無敵於天下。東面而征，西夷怨；南面而征，北狄怨，曰：『奚為後我？』民之望之，若大旱之望雨也。歸市者弗止，芸者不變，誅其君，弔其民，如時雨降。民大悅。《書》曰：『徯我后，后來其無罰。』『有攸不惟臣，東征，綏厥士女，匪厥玄黃，紹我周王見休，惟臣附于大邑周。』其君子實玄黃于匪以迎其君子，其小人簞食壺漿以迎其小人，救民於水火之中，取其殘而已矣。《太誓》曰：『我武惟揚，侵于之疆，則取于殘，殺伐用張，于湯有光。』不行王政云爾，苟行王政，四海之內皆舉首而望之，欲以為君。齊楚雖大，何畏焉？」

Mencius said: "When Tang dwelt in Bo, he was neighbor to the lord of Ge. The lord of Ge was dissolute and did not sacrifice. Tang sent someone to ask: 'Why do you not sacrifice?' He replied: 'I have no animals for the offerings.' Tang sent him oxen and sheep. The lord of Ge ate them but still did not sacrifice. Tang again sent someone to ask why. He replied: 'I have no grain for the offerings.' Tang sent the people of Bo to plow his fields for him, and the old and weak brought them food. But the lord of Ge led his people to waylay those who brought wine, food, millet, and rice, and robbed them — killing those who would not hand them over. A boy bringing millet and meat was killed and robbed. The Book of Documents says: 'The lord of Ge treated the food-bringers as enemies.' This is what it refers to. Tang punished him for killing that boy, and all within the four seas said: 'He is not seeking to enrich himself with the empire — he is avenging ordinary men and women.' 'Tang's campaigns began with Ge.' In eleven campaigns he had no rival in the empire. When he marched east, the western tribes complained; when he marched south, the northern tribes complained, saying: 'Why does he leave us for last?' The people longed for him as people in a great drought long for rain. Those going to market were not stopped, and those weeding their fields went on as before. He punished the rulers but comforted the people, like timely rain falling. The people were overjoyed. The Book of Documents says: 'We have waited for our lord; when our lord comes, we shall suffer no more.' 'There were those who would not submit. He campaigned east, bringing peace to the men and women there. They came bearing baskets of dark silk and yellow silk to welcome him, saying: "Welcome our lord of Zhou, that we may receive his blessings and become subjects of the great city of Zhou."' Their gentlemen came with baskets of silk to welcome his gentlemen; their common people came with baskets of food and jugs of drink to welcome his common people. He saved the people from fire and flood, seizing only the tyrants. The Great Declaration says: 'Our martial power is displayed; we invade his territory, seize the tyrants, and our punitive glory shines — more glorious even than Tang.' If one does not practice kingly government, so be it. But if one does, all within the four seas will raise their heads in hope, wishing to make him their ruler. Though Qi and Chu are great, what is there to fear?"

六祖大師法寶壇經・懺悔品第六

自心眾生無邊誓願度，自心煩惱無邊誓願斷，自性法門無盡誓願學，自性無上佛道誓願成。善知識！大家豈不道，眾生無邊誓願度。恁麼道，且不是惠能度。善知識！心中眾生，所謂邪迷心、誑妄心、不善心、嫉妬心、惡毒心，如是等心，盡是眾生。各須自性自度，是名真度。何名自性自度？即自心中邪見煩惱愚癡眾生，將正見度。既有正見，使般若智打破愚癡迷妄眾生，各各自度。邪來正度，迷來悟度，愚來智度，惡來善度；如是度者，名為真度。

'Sentient beings of my own mind, limitless in number, I vow to deliver. Afflictions of my own mind, inexhaustible in number, I vow to cut off. Dharma teachings of my own nature, boundless in number, I vow to study. The supreme Buddha Way of my own nature, I vow to attain.' Good friends! Do we not all say, 'Sentient beings, limitless in number, I vow to deliver'? But saying this does not mean that Huineng delivers them. Good friends! The 'sentient beings' within the mind are deviant thoughts, deluded thoughts, unwholesome thoughts, jealous thoughts, and malicious thoughts — minds such as these are all sentient beings. Each person must deliver them with their own self-nature. This is called true deliverance. What does it mean to deliver them with one's own self-nature? It means to use right views to deliver the sentient beings of wrong views, afflictions, and ignorance within your own mind. Once you have right views, use prajna wisdom to shatter the sentient beings of ignorance and delusion. Each delivers themselves. When deviance comes, correct it through right views; when confusion comes, dissolve it through awakening; when ignorance comes, counter it with wisdom; when evil comes, transform it with goodness. Deliverance such as this is called true deliverance.
