般若波羅蜜多心經

來源：https://pages.ucsd.edu/~dkjordan/chin/chtxts/ShinJing.html

舍利子，色不異空，空不異色，色即是空，空即是色，受想行識，亦復如是。

Shariputra, form is not different from emptiness, emptiness is not different from form. Form is emptiness, emptiness is form. The same is true of feelings, perceptions, mental formations, and consciousness.

孟子 · 滕文公下

來源：https://ctext.org/mengzi

戴盈之曰：「什一，去關市之征，今茲未能。請輕之，以待來年，然後已，何如？」孟子曰：「今有人日攘其鄰之雞者，或告之曰：『是非君子之道。』曰：『請損之，月攘一雞，以待來年，然後已。』如知其非義，斯速已矣，何待來年。」公都子曰：「外人皆稱夫子好辯，敢問何也？」

Dai Yingzhi said: "A tax of one in ten, and the abolition of tariffs and market taxes — I cannot manage it this year. May I reduce them gradually, and wait until next year to complete it? What do you think?" Mencius said: "Suppose there is a man who steals one of his neighbor's chickens every day. Someone tells him: 'This is not the way of a gentleman.' He replies: 'Let me reduce it — I will steal one chicken a month, and wait until next year to stop completely.' If you know something is wrong, you should stop it at once. Why wait until next year?" Gongduzi said: "Outsiders all say that you, Master, are fond of disputation. May I ask why?"

六祖大師法寶壇經 · 懺悔品第六

來源：六祖大師法寶壇經

善知識！各自觀察，莫錯用心。經文分明言自歸依佛，不言歸依他佛。自佛不歸，無所依處。今既自悟，各須歸依自心三寶，內調心性，外敬他人，是自歸依也。

「善知識！既歸依自三寶竟，各各志心，吾與說一體三身自性佛，令汝等見三身了然，自悟自性。總隨我道：『於自色身，歸依清淨法身佛。於自色身，歸依圓滿報身佛。於自色身，歸依千百億化身佛。』善知識！色身是舍宅，不可言歸。

Good friends! Each of you, examine yourselves and do not misuse your minds. The sutra text clearly says to take refuge in one's own Buddha, not in some other Buddha. If you do not take refuge in your own Buddha, there is nothing to rely upon. Now that you have awakened for yourselves, each of you must take refuge in the Three Treasures of your own mind: inwardly regulate your mind-nature, outwardly show respect to all people. This is self-refuge. Good friends! Now that you have taken refuge in the Three Treasures of your own nature, each of you focus your minds. I will explain for you the single-substance, three-body, self-nature Buddha, so that you may clearly see the three bodies and awaken to your own nature for yourselves. Repeat after me together: 'In this very physical body, I take refuge in the pure Dharma-body Buddha. In this very physical body, I take refuge in the perfect Reward-body Buddha. In this very physical body, I take refuge in the myriad Transformation-body Buddha.' Good friends! The physical body is a dwelling place; it cannot be spoken of as a refuge.
