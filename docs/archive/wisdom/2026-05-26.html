般若波羅蜜多心經 — Heart Sutra — Tuesday

舍利子，色不異空，空不異色，色即是空，空即是色，受想行識，亦復如是。

Shariputra, form is not different from emptiness, emptiness is not different from form. Form is emptiness, emptiness is form. The same is true of feelings, perceptions, mental formations, and consciousness.

孟子 — 公孫丑上 — Mencius (gong-sun-chou-i)

曰：「敢問其所以異？」曰：「宰我、子貢、有若智足以知聖人。汙，不至阿其所好。宰我曰：『以予觀於夫子，賢於堯舜遠矣。』子貢曰：『見其禮而知其政，聞其樂而知其德。由百世之後，等百世之王，莫之能違也。自生民以來，未有夫子也。』有若曰：『豈惟民哉？麒麟之於走獸，鳳凰之於飛鳥，太山之於丘垤，河海之於行潦，類也。聖人之於民，亦類也。出於其類，拔乎其萃，自生民以來，未有盛於孔子也。』」

Gongsun Chou asked: "May I ask in what way they differed?" Mencius said: "Zai Wo, Zigong, and You Ruo were wise enough to recognize a sage. Even in their partiality, they would not stoop to flattering the one they admired. Zai Wo said: 'In my observation, the Master far surpasses Yao and Shun.' Zigong said: 'By seeing a state's rituals, one can know its governance; by hearing its music, one can know its virtue. Looking back over a hundred generations, one can assess a hundred generations of kings—and none can be found to contradict this. Since the beginning of humankind, there has never been anyone like the Master.' You Ruo said: 'Is it only among humans? The unicorn is to running beasts, the phoenix is to flying birds, Mount Tai is to small hills, the rivers and seas are to puddles—they are all of the same kind, yet stand far above. The sage is to the people what these are—of the same kind, yet towering above the rest. Since the beginning of humankind, there has never been one greater than Confucius.'"

禪宗經典 — 六祖大師法寶壇經 — Platform Sutra

行由品第一

秀復思惟：『五祖明日見偈歡喜，即我與法有緣；若言不堪，自是我迷，宿業障重，不合得法。』聖意難測，房中思想，坐臥不安，直至五更。祖已知神秀入門未得，不見自性。

「天明，祖喚盧供奉來，向南廊壁間，繪畫圖相，忽見其偈，報言：『供奉却不用畫，勞爾遠來。經云：「凡所有相，皆是虛妄。」但留此偈，與人誦持。依此偈修，免墮惡道；依此偈修，有大利益。』令門人炷香禮敬，盡誦此偈，即得見性。門人誦偈，皆歎善哉。

Shenxiu thought further: 'If the Fifth Patriarch sees the verse tomorrow and is pleased, then I have an affinity with the Dharma. If he says it is no good, then I am deluded, my past karma is heavy, and I am not fit to receive the Dharma.' The sage's intention was hard to fathom. In his room, Shenxiu turned the matter over in his mind, sitting and lying in unease, all the way until the fifth watch. The Patriarch already knew that Shenxiu had not yet entered the gate and had not seen his own nature. At dawn, the Patriarch called for the artist Lu and went to the south corridor wall to have the murals painted. Suddenly he saw the verse. He said to Lu, 'There is no need to paint after all. I am sorry you have come so far. The scripture says: "All that has form is illusory." Let us keep this verse instead, for people to recite and uphold. Those who practice according to this verse will avoid falling into evil destinies; those who practice according to this verse will gain great benefit.' He ordered the community to light incense, pay respect, and recite the verse, so that they might see their own nature. The disciples recited the verse and all exclaimed, 'Excellent!'
