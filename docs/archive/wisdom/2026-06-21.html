般若波羅蜜多心經

故知般若波羅蜜多，是大神咒，是大明咒，是無上咒，是無等等咒，能除一切苦，真實不虛。故說般若波羅蜜多咒，即說咒曰：揭諦揭諦，波羅揭諦，波羅僧揭諦，菩提薩婆訶。

Therefore, know that Prajna Paramita is the great transcendent mantra, the great bright mantra, the supreme mantra, the unequalled mantra, which can remove all suffering and is true, not false. Therefore he proclaimed the Prajna Paramita mantra, saying: Gate gate paragate parasamgate bodhi svaha.

孟子 · 公孫丑下

曰：「未也。沈同問『燕可伐與』？吾應之曰『可』，彼然而伐之也。彼如曰『孰可以伐之』？則將應之曰：『為天吏，則可以伐之。』今有殺人者，或問之曰『人可殺與』？則將應之曰『可』。彼如曰『孰可以殺之』？則將應之曰：『為士師，則可以殺之。』今以燕伐燕，何為勸之哉？」燕人畔。王曰：「吾甚慚於孟子。」陳賈曰：「王無患焉。王自以為與周公，孰仁且智？」王曰：「惡！是何言也？」

Mencius said: "No. Shen Tong asked, 'May Yan be attacked?' I answered, 'Yes,' and they went ahead and attacked. But if he had asked, 'Who may attack it?' I would have answered: 'Only one acting as Heaven's agent may attack it.' It is like this: if someone commits murder and another asks, 'May this person be killed?' I would answer, 'Yes.' But if asked, 'Who may kill him?' I would answer: 'Only the Chief Justice may kill him.' Now Qi attacked Yan — this was one Yan attacking another Yan. Why would I have urged that?" The people of Yan rebelled. The king said: "I am deeply ashamed before Mencius." Chen Jia said: "Your Majesty need not worry. Does Your Majesty consider himself more benevolent and wise than the Duke of Zhou?" The king said: "What! What kind of talk is that?"

六祖大師法寶壇經 · 般若品第二

一念愚即般若絕，一念智即般若生。世人愚迷，不見般若，口說般若，心中常愚。常自言：『我修般若。』念念說空，不識真空。般若無形相，智慧心即是。若作如是解，即名般若智。何名波羅蜜？此是西國語，唐言到彼岸，解義離生滅。著境生滅起，如水有波浪，即名為此岸；離境無生滅，如水常通流，即名為彼岸，故號波羅蜜。善知識！迷人口念，當念之時，有妄有非。念念若行，是名真性。悟此法者，是般若法；修此行者，是般若行。

'One thought of ignorance and prajna is cut off; one thought of wisdom and prajna arises. Worldly people are ignorant and deluded, unable to see prajna. They speak of prajna with their mouths, yet in their minds they remain ignorant. They constantly say, "I cultivate prajna," and speak of emptiness thought after thought, yet do not recognize true emptiness. Prajna has no form or appearance — the wisdom-mind itself is prajna. If you understand it this way, this is called prajna-wisdom. What does "paramita" mean? It is a Western Indian term meaning "arriving at the other shore" in Chinese. Its meaning is: understanding that transcends birth and extinction. When you cling to sense-objects, birth and extinction arise — this is like water with waves, and is called "this shore." When you are free from sense-objects, there is no birth or extinction — this is like water flowing freely and unobstructed, and is called "the other shore." Hence the term "paramita." Good friends, deluded people recite with their mouths, but at the very moment of recitation there are both false and wrong thoughts. If you practice it thought after thought, this is called true nature. One who awakens to this dharma has awakened to the prajna-dharma; one who cultivates this practice cultivates the prajna-practice.'
