般若波羅蜜多心經 — Heart Sutra — Saturday

無罣礙故，無有恐怖，遠離顛倒夢想，究竟涅槃。三世諸佛，依般若波羅蜜多故，得阿耨多羅三藐三菩提。

Without hindrance, there is no fear. Far from all inverted views, one attains nirvana. All buddhas of past, present, and future rely on Prajna Paramita and attain supreme, perfect enlightenment.

孟子 — 公孫丑上

「何謂知言？」曰：「詖辭知其所蔽，淫辭知其所陷，邪辭知其所離，遁辭知其所窮。生於其心，害於其政；發於其政，害於其事。聖人復起，必從吾言矣。」「宰我、子貢善為說辭，冉牛、閔子、顏淵善言德行。孔子兼之，曰：『我於辭命則不能也。』然則夫子既聖矣乎？」

Gongsun Chou asked: "What do you mean by 'understanding words'?" Mencius said: "When words are one-sided, I can see what they obscure. When words are excessive, I can see the trap they have fallen into. When words are deviant, I can see how they have departed from the truth. When words are evasive, I can see the desperation behind them. When such distortions arise in a person's mind, they damage his governance; when they manifest in his governance, they damage his undertakings. Were a sage to arise again, he would surely agree with my words." Gongsun Chou said: "Zai Wo and Zigong excelled at rhetoric; Ran Niu, Min Zi, and Yan Hui excelled at virtuous conduct. Confucius combined both abilities, yet said: 'When it comes to rhetoric, I am not capable.' Does this mean you are already a sage, Master?"

One day the Patriarch summoned all his disciples and said, 'Let me tell you: the matter of birth and death is the great concern of worldly beings. Yet all day long you only seek the field of merit and do not seek to escape the bitter sea of birth and death. If your own nature is deluded, how can merit save you? Each of you, go and look into your own wisdom. Take the prajna-nature of your original mind and each compose a verse. Bring it to me. If one of you awakens to the great meaning, I will transmit the robe and Dharma to you, making you the Sixth Patriarch. Go quickly! Do not delay! Deliberation is useless in this matter. One who sees their nature must see it the instant the words are spoken. One who can do this could see it even while wielding a sword in battle.'
