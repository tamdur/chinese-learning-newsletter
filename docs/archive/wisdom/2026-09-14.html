般若波羅蜜多心經 — Monday

觀自在菩薩，行深般若波羅蜜多時，照見五蘊皆空，度一切苦厄。

Avalokitesvara Bodhisattva, when practicing the profound Prajna Paramita, illuminated the five aggregates and saw that they are all empty, thus overcoming all suffering and distress.

孟子 — 萬章上

曰：「長息問於公明高曰：『舜往于田，則吾既得聞命矣；號泣于旻天，于父母，則吾不知也。』公明高曰：『是非爾所知也。』夫公明高以孝子之心，為不若是恝，我竭力耕田，共為子職而已矣，父母之不我愛，於我何哉？帝使其子九男二女，百官牛羊倉廩備，以事舜於畎畝之中。天下之士多就之者，帝將胥天下而遷之焉。為不順於父母，如窮人無所歸。天下之士悅之，人之所欲也，而不足以解憂；好色，人之所欲，妻帝之二女，而不足以解憂；富，人之所欲，富有天下，而不足以解憂；貴，人之所欲，貴為天子，而不足以解憂。人悅之、好色、富貴，無足以解憂者，惟順於父母，可以解憂。人少，則慕父母；知好色，則慕少艾；有妻子，則慕妻子；仕則慕君，不得於君則熱中。大孝終身慕父母。五十而慕者，予於大舜見之矣。」

Mencius said: "Chang Xi once asked Gongming Gao: 'That Shun went to the fields — I have heard your explanation of that. But that he wept and cried out to compassionate Heaven and to his parents — that I do not understand.' Gongming Gao replied: 'This is beyond your comprehension.' In Gongming Gao's view, a filial son's heart could not be so indifferent as to say: 'I have done my best to plow the fields and fulfill my duty as a son — that is all. If my parents do not love me, what concern is that of mine?' The Emperor sent his nine sons and two daughters, along with a full complement of officials, oxen, sheep, and granaries, to serve Shun amid the fields. Most of the scholars in the realm went to join him, and the Emperor was prepared to hand over the entire realm to him. Yet because he could not gain the approval of his parents, Shun was like a destitute man with nowhere to turn. The admiration of all the scholars in the realm — something everyone desires — was not enough to relieve his sorrow. Beautiful women — something everyone desires — and being married to the Emperor's two daughters was not enough to relieve his sorrow. Wealth — something everyone desires — and possessing all the wealth of the realm was not enough to relieve his sorrow. Honor — something everyone desires — and being honored as the Son of Heaven was not enough to relieve his sorrow. The admiration of others, beautiful women, wealth, and honor — none of these could relieve his sorrow. Only gaining the approval of his parents could relieve it. When young, a person longs for his parents. When he becomes aware of the attraction of beauty, he longs for young and lovely women. When he has a wife and children, he longs for his wife and children. When he enters public service, he longs for his sovereign, and if he fails to gain his sovereign's favor, he burns with anxiety. But a person of the greatest filial devotion longs for his parents his entire life. In the great Shun, even at fifty still longing for his parents — there I see such devotion."

六祖大師法寶壇經 — 頓漸品第八

』如何？汝若不痛，同其木石；若痛，則同凡夫，即起恚恨。汝向前見、不見是二邊，痛、不痛是生滅。汝自性且不見，敢爾弄人！」神會禮拜悔謝。師又曰：「汝若心迷不見，問善知識覓路。汝若心悟，即自見性依法修行。汝自迷不見自心，却來問吾見與不見。吾見自知，豈代汝迷？汝若自見，亦不代吾迷。何不自知自見，乃問吾見與不見？」神會再禮百餘拜，求謝過愆。服勤給侍，不離左右。

'If it doesn't hurt, you are like wood or stone. If it does hurt, you are no different from an ordinary person and will give rise to anger and resentment. Your earlier answers of seeing and not-seeing are dualistic, and your hurt and not-hurt belong to arising and ceasing. You have not yet even seen your own nature, and yet you dare to come here and play games with people!' Shenhui bowed in repentance and gratitude. The Master continued: 'If your mind is confused and you cannot see, then ask a good spiritual friend to show you the way. If your mind is awakened, then see your own nature and practice in accord with the Dharma. You yourself are confused and cannot see your own mind, yet you come asking me whether I see or not. If I see, I know it for myself — how could that resolve your confusion? If you could see for yourself, that would equally not resolve my confusion. Why don't you know and see for yourself, instead of asking me whether I see or not?' Shenhui bowed more than a hundred times, begging forgiveness for his faults. He then served the Master diligently as an attendant, never leaving his side.
