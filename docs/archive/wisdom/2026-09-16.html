般若波羅蜜多心經

舍利子，是諸法空相，不生不滅，不垢不淨，不增不減。是故空中無色，無受想行識。

Shariputra, all dharmas are marked with emptiness — they are neither born nor destroyed, neither defiled nor pure, neither increasing nor decreasing. Therefore, in emptiness there is no form, no feelings, perceptions, mental formations, or consciousness.

孟子．萬章上

萬章曰：「父母使舜完廩，捐階，瞽瞍焚廩。使浚井，出，從而揜之。象曰：『謨蓋都君咸我績。牛羊父母，倉廩父母，干戈朕，琴朕，弤朕，二嫂使治朕棲。』象往入舜宮，舜在床琴。象曰：『鬱陶思君爾。』忸怩。舜曰：『惟茲臣庶，汝其于予治。』不識舜不知象之將殺己與？」曰：「奚而不知也？象憂亦憂，象喜亦喜。」曰：「然則舜偽喜者與？」

Wan Zhang said: "Shun's parents sent him to repair the granary roof, then removed the ladder, and the Blind Man set fire to the granary. They sent him to dredge a well, and after he climbed out, they covered it over. Xiang said: 'The credit for plotting to bury our lord-like brother is all mine! The oxen and sheep shall go to Father and Mother, the granaries to Father and Mother. The shields and spears shall be mine, the lute shall be mine, the carved bow shall be mine, and I shall make the two sisters-in-law attend to my bed.' Xiang went into Shun's palace, and there was Shun sitting on the bed playing the lute. Xiang said: 'I have been thinking of you with deep longing,' but he looked embarrassed. Shun said: 'I have all these subjects and officials — please help me govern them.' I wonder, did Shun not know that Xiang intended to kill him?" Mencius said: "How could he not know? When Xiang was troubled, Shun was troubled; when Xiang was happy, Shun was happy." Wan Zhang said: "Then was Shun's happiness merely pretended?"

The Master, seeing that followers of various schools were raising questions with hostile intent and gathering in large numbers at his seat, spoke to them with compassion: 'Students of the Way should eliminate all thoughts of good and evil. That which cannot be named, yet is named — call it self-nature. This non-dual nature is what is called true nature. Upon this true nature, all teachings are established. At these words, you should see it directly for yourselves.' Upon hearing this, all present bowed in reverence and requested to serve him as their teacher.
