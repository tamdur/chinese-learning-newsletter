般若波羅蜜多心經

無罣礙故，無有恐怖，遠離顛倒夢想，究竟涅槃。三世諸佛，依般若波羅蜜多故，得阿耨多羅三藐三菩提。

Without hindrance, there is no fear. Far from all inverted views, one attains nirvana. All buddhas of past, present, and future rely on Prajna Paramita and attain supreme, perfect enlightenment.

孟子・公孫丑下

陳臻問曰：「前日於齊，王餽兼金一百而不受；於宋，餽七十鎰而受；於薛，餽五十鎰而受。前日之不受是，則今日之受非也；今日之受是，則前日之不受非也。夫子必居一於此矣。」孟子曰：「皆是也。當在宋也，予將有遠行。行者必以贐，辭曰：『餽贐。』予何為不受？當在薛也，予有戒心。辭曰：『聞戒。』故為兵餽之，予何為不受？若於齊，則未有處也。無處而餽之，是貨之也。焉有君子而可以貨取乎？」

Chen Zhen asked: "Previously in Qi, the king offered you a gift of one hundred yi of fine gold and you refused it. In Song, you were offered seventy yi and accepted. In Xue, you were offered fifty yi and accepted. If the earlier refusal was right, then the later acceptances were wrong; if the later acceptances were right, then the earlier refusal was wrong. Master, it must be one or the other." Mencius said: "Both were right. When I was in Song, I was about to go on a long journey. For a traveler, one gives a travel gift. The accompanying words were: 'A gift for your journey.' Why should I not accept? When I was in Xue, I was on guard for my safety. The words were: 'I have heard you are taking precautions, so here is money for arms.' Why should I not accept? But in Qi, there was no such occasion. To give a gift without occasion is to buy someone off. How could a gentleman allow himself to be bought?"

The aggregates and the elements — ordinary people see them as two, but the wise understand that their nature is not two. This non-dual nature is itself the Buddha-nature."' Hearing this, Yinzong was delighted. Pressing his palms together, he said, 'My own lecturing on the sutra is like broken tiles, while your discourse on its meaning is like pure gold.' Thereupon he shaved Huineng's head and asked to become his disciple. Huineng then began teaching the East Mountain Dharma Gate beneath the bodhi tree.

Huineng said, 'Since receiving the Dharma at East Mountain, I endured every hardship, my life hanging by a thread. That today I am able to share this gathering with you — the magistrate, officials, monks, nuns, Taoists, and laypeople — is surely due to the karmic connections of countless past lives, and to the good roots we have planted together through making offerings to the Buddhas in former existences. Only through this could you now hear the causes and conditions of receiving the Sudden Teaching. This teaching was transmitted by the ancient sages — it is not Huineng's own wisdom.
