般若波羅蜜多心經

舍利子，是諸法空相，不生不滅，不垢不淨，不增不減。是故空中無色，無受想行識。

Shariputra, all dharmas are marked with emptiness — they are neither born nor destroyed, neither defiled nor pure, neither increasing nor decreasing. Therefore, in emptiness there is no form, no feelings, perceptions, mental formations, or consciousness.

孟子 · 萬章下

孟子謂萬章曰：「一鄉之善士，斯友一鄉之善士；一國之善士，斯友一國之善士；天下之善士，斯友天下之善士。以友天下之善士為未足，又尚論古之人。頌其詩，讀其書，不知其人，可乎？是以論其世也。是尚友也。」齊宣王問卿。孟子曰：「王何卿之問也？」王曰：「卿不同乎？」曰：「不同。有貴戚之卿，有異姓之卿。」王曰：「請問貴戚之卿。」曰：「君有大過則諫，反覆之而不聽，則易位。」

Mencius said to Wan Zhang: "The best scholar in a village befriends the best scholars in the village. The best scholar in a state befriends the best scholars in the state. The best scholar in the world befriends the best scholars in the world. And when befriending all the best scholars in the world is not enough, he goes further and considers the ancients. He recites their poems and reads their writings — but can he do so without knowing who they were as persons? Therefore he studies their times as well. This is what it means to seek friends upward through history." King Xuan of Qi asked about ministers. Mencius said: "What kind of ministers is the King asking about?" The King said: "Are ministers not all the same?" Mencius said: "They are not. There are ministers who are royal kinsmen, and ministers of a different surname." The King said: "May I ask about ministers who are royal kinsmen?" Mencius said: "If the ruler commits great faults, they remonstrate. If he repeatedly refuses to listen, they replace him."

金剛般若波羅蜜經 · 無為福勝分第十一

「須菩提！如恆河中所有沙數，如是沙等恆河，於意云何？是諸恆河沙，寧為多不？」須菩提言：「甚多，世尊！但諸恆河，尚多無數，何況其沙。」「須菩提！我今實言告汝：若有善男子、善女人，以七寶滿爾所恆河沙數三千大千世界，以用布施，得福多不？」須菩提言：「甚多，世尊！」佛告須菩提：「若善男子、善女人，於此經中，乃至受持四句偈等，為他人說，而此福德，勝前福德。」

"Subhuti, if there were as many Ganges Rivers as there are grains of sand in the Ganges, what do you think — would the grains of sand in all those Ganges Rivers be many?" Subhuti said: "Very many, World-Honored One. The Ganges Rivers alone would already be countless, how much more so the sand in them all." "Subhuti, I now tell you truly: if a good man or good woman filled as many three-thousand-fold great-thousand worlds as there are grains of sand in all those Ganges Rivers with the seven precious treasures and used them for giving, would the merit obtained be great?" Subhuti said: "Very great, World-Honored One." The Buddha told Subhuti: "If a good man or good woman receives and upholds from this sutra even as few as four lines of verse and explains them to others, this merit surpasses the former."
