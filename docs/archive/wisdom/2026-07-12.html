般若波羅蜜多心經

故知般若波羅蜜多，是大神咒，是大明咒，是無上咒，是無等等咒，能除一切苦，真實不虛。故說般若波羅蜜多咒，即說咒曰：揭諦揭諦，波羅揭諦，波羅僧揭諦，菩提薩婆訶。

Therefore, know that Prajna Paramita is the great transcendent mantra, the great bright mantra, the supreme mantra, the unequalled mantra, which can remove all suffering and is true, not false. Therefore he proclaimed the Prajna Paramita mantra, saying: Gate gate paragate parasamgate bodhi svaha.

孟子 · 滕文公上

「當堯之時，天下猶未平，洪水橫流，氾濫於天下。草木暢茂，禽獸繁殖，五穀不登，禽獸偪人。獸蹄鳥跡之道，交於中國。堯獨憂之，舉舜而敷治焉。舜使益掌火，益烈山澤而焚之，禽獸逃匿。禹疏九河，瀹濟漯，而注諸海；決汝漢，排淮泗，而注之江，然後中國可得而食也。當是時也，禹八年於外，三過其門而不入，雖欲耕，得乎？后稷教民稼穡。樹藝五穀，五穀熟而民人育。人之有道也，飽食、煖衣、逸居而無教，則近於禽獸。聖人有憂之，使契為司徒，教以人倫：父子有親，君臣有義，夫婦有別，長幼有序，朋友有信。放勳曰：『勞之來之，匡之直之，輔之翼之，使自得之，又從而振德之。』聖人之憂民如此，而暇耕乎？

'In the time of Yao, the world was not yet settled. Floodwaters flowed recklessly, inundating the land. Plants and trees grew in wild profusion, birds and beasts multiplied, the five grains did not ripen, and the birds and beasts encroached upon the people. Trails of animal hooves and bird tracks crisscrossed the Central States. Yao alone was troubled by this and raised up Shun to bring order. Shun appointed Yi to take charge of fire; Yi set fire to the mountains and marshes, and the birds and beasts fled into hiding. Yu channeled the nine rivers, cleared the courses of the Ji and the Ta, and directed them to the sea; he opened the Ru and the Han, cleared the Huai and the Si, and directed them into the Yangtze — only then could the Central States be farmed and fed. During that time, Yu spent eight years abroad and passed his own door three times without entering. Even had he wished to farm, could he have? Hou Ji taught the people to plant and harvest; they cultivated the five grains, and when the grains ripened, the people were nourished. But human beings have a Way: if they are well-fed, warmly clothed, and live at ease but receive no education, they come close to being animals. The sages were troubled by this and appointed Xie as Minister of Education to teach the people human relationships: affection between parent and child, righteousness between ruler and minister, distinct roles between husband and wife, proper order between elder and younger, and trust between friends. Yao said: "Encourage them, guide them, correct them, straighten them, help them, support them, enable them to find it in themselves, and then further uplift their virtue." The sages' concern for the people was like this — could they have had time to farm?'

六祖大師法寶壇經 · 定慧品第四

師示眾云：「善知識！我此法門，以定慧為本。大眾！勿迷，言定慧別。定慧一體，不是二。定是慧體，慧是定用。即慧之時定在慧，即定之時慧在定。若識此義，即是定慧等學。諸學道人，莫言先定發慧、先慧發定各別。作此見者，法有二相。口說善語，心中不善。空有定慧，定慧不等。若心口俱善、內外一如，定慧即等。自悟修行，不在於諍。若諍先後，即同迷人，不斷勝負，却增我法，不離四相。善知識！定慧猶如何等？猶如燈光。

The Master addressed the assembly: 'Good friends! This Dharma teaching of mine takes samadhi and prajna as its foundation. Great assembly, do not be confused into thinking that samadhi and prajna are separate. Samadhi and prajna are one substance, not two. Samadhi is the substance of prajna; prajna is the function of samadhi. At the very moment of prajna, samadhi is within prajna. At the very moment of samadhi, prajna is within samadhi. If you understand this principle, then you understand the equal cultivation of samadhi and prajna. All you students of the Way, do not say that samadhi comes first and gives rise to prajna, or that prajna comes first and gives rise to samadhi, as if they were separate. Holding such a view implies that the Dharma has a dual nature. If the mouth speaks good words but the mind within is not good, then samadhi and prajna are empty — they are not equal. If mind and mouth are both good, inner and outer are unified as one, then samadhi and prajna are equal. Self-awakening and self-practice — that is what matters, not disputation. If you argue about which comes first or last, you are like a deluded person, never settling the contest of winning and losing, only increasing attachment to self and dharmas, never departing from the four marks. Good friends! What is samadhi-prajna like? It is like the lamp and its light.'
