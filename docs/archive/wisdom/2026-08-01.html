般若波羅蜜多心經

般若波羅蜜多心經 · Saturday 段

無罣礙故，無有恐怖，遠離顛倒夢想，究竟涅槃。三世諸佛，依般若波羅蜜多故，得阿耨多羅三藐三菩提。

Without hindrance, there is no fear. Far from all inverted views, one attains nirvana. All buddhas of past, present, and future rely on Prajna Paramita and attain supreme, perfect enlightenment.

孟子 · 滕文公下

《孟子》滕文公下 · 第 114 段

「聖王不作，諸侯放恣，處士橫議，楊朱、墨翟之言盈天下。天下之言，不歸楊，則歸墨。楊氏為我，是無君也；墨氏兼愛，是無父也。無父無君，是禽獸也。公明儀曰：『庖有肥肉，廄有肥馬，民有飢色，野有餓莩，此率獸而食人也。』楊墨之道不息，孔子之道不著，是邪說誣民，充塞仁義也。仁義充塞，則率獸食人，人將相食。吾為此懼，閑先聖之道，距楊墨，放淫辭，邪說者不得作。作於其心，害於其事；作於其事，害於其政。聖人復起，不易吾言矣。

"No sage king has arisen. The feudal lords are dissolute and willful. Unemployed scholars engage in reckless argument. The words of Yang Zhu and Mo Di fill the world. The teachings of the world, if not following Yang, follow Mo. Yang's doctrine of 'for oneself' denies the ruler; Mo's doctrine of 'universal love' denies the father. To deny father and ruler is to be no better than the beasts. Gongming Yi said: 'In the kitchen there is fat meat; in the stable there are fat horses; yet the people have the look of hunger, and in the wilds there are those who have starved to death — this is leading beasts to devour men.' If the Way of Yang and Mo does not cease and the Way of Confucius is not made manifest, then these heresies will deceive the people and block up benevolence and righteousness. When benevolence and righteousness are blocked, beasts are led to devour men, and men will devour one another. I am alarmed by this. I defend the Way of the ancient sages, oppose Yang and Mo, and banish their licentious words, so that the authors of heresies cannot prevail. When heresy arises in the mind, it harms one's conduct; when it arises in one's conduct, it harms one's governance. Should a sage arise again, he would not change my words.

六祖大師法寶壇經 · 懺悔品第六

《六祖大師法寶壇經》懺悔品第六 · 第 114 段

思量惡事，化為地獄；思量善事，化為天堂。毒害化為龍蛇，慈悲化為菩薩，智慧化為上界，愚癡化為下方。自性變化甚多，迷人不能省覺，念念起惡，常行惡道。迴一念善，智慧即生，此名自性化身佛。善知識！法身本具，念念自性自見，即是報身佛。從報身思量，即是化身佛。自悟自修自性功德，是真歸依。皮肉是色身，色身是舍宅，不言歸依也。但悟自性三身，即識自性佛。吾有一無相頌，若能持誦，言下令汝積劫迷罪一時銷滅。

When you think of evil things, it transforms into hell. When you think of good things, it transforms into heaven. Malice and harm transform into dragons and serpents. Compassion and kindness transform into bodhisattvas. Wisdom transforms into the higher realms. Ignorance transforms into the lower realms. The transformations of self-nature are very many. Deluded people, unable to awaken and reflect, thought after thought give rise to evil and constantly walk the path of evil. But if they turn around with a single thought of goodness, wisdom is immediately born. This is called the Transformation-body Buddha of one's own nature. Good friends! The Dharma-body is originally complete in everyone. Seeing your own nature for yourself in each thought-moment — that is the Reward-body Buddha. When from the Reward-body there arises deliberation — that is the Transformation-body Buddha. Awakening, cultivating, and developing the merit and virtue of your own nature for yourself: this is true refuge. The skin and flesh are the physical body; the physical body is a dwelling — it is not spoken of as a refuge. Simply awaken to the three bodies of your own nature, and you will recognize the self-nature Buddha. I have a verse without characteristics. If you can recite and uphold it, it will instantly eliminate the accumulated offenses of kalpas upon kalpas of delusion.
