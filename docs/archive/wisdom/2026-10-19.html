般若波羅蜜多心經

觀自在菩薩，行深般若波羅蜜多時，照見五蘊皆空，度一切苦厄。

Avalokitesvara Bodhisattva, when practicing the profound Prajna Paramita, illuminated the five aggregates and saw that they are all empty, thus overcoming all suffering and distress.

孟子．萬章下

曰：「為其多聞也，則天子不召師，而況諸侯乎？為其賢也，則吾未聞欲見賢而召之也。繆公亟見於子思，曰：『古千乘之國以友士，何如？』子思不悅，曰：『古之人有言：曰事之云乎，豈曰友之云乎？』子思之不悅也，豈不曰：『以位，則子，君也；我，臣也。何敢與君友也？以德，則子事我者也。奚可以與我友？』千乘之君求與之友，而不可得也，而況可召與？齊景公田，招虞人以旌，不至，將殺之。志士不忘在溝壑，勇士不忘喪其元。孔子奚取焉？取非其招不往也。」

Mencius said: "If it is because of his wide learning, then even the Son of Heaven does not summon his teacher — how much less should a feudal lord? If it is because of his worthiness, then I have never heard of wanting to see a worthy man by summoning him. Duke Mu of Lu frequently went to see Zisi and said: 'In ancient times, how did rulers of states with a thousand chariots befriend scholars?' Zisi was displeased and said: 'The ancients had a saying — they spoke of serving them, not of befriending them!' Zisi's displeasure meant: 'In terms of rank, you are the ruler and I am the subject — how dare I presume to be your friend? In terms of virtue, you should be serving me — how can you presume to be my friend?' If the ruler of a state with a thousand chariots sought friendship with Zisi and could not obtain it, how much less could he summon him? Duke Jing of Qi was hunting and summoned his gamekeeper with a pennant. The gamekeeper did not come, and the Duke was about to have him killed. 'A man of resolve never forgets he may end up in a ditch. A brave man never forgets he may lose his head.' What did Confucius find admirable in this? He admired the fact that the gamekeeper would not go when summoned by the wrong signal."

金剛般若波羅蜜經．一相無相分第九

』世尊！我若作是念：『我得阿羅漢道。』世尊則不說須菩提是樂阿蘭那行者。以須菩提實無所行，而名須菩提，是樂阿蘭那行。」。

"World-Honored One, if I were to think 'I have attained the way of the arhat,' then the World-Honored One would not say that Subhuti delights in the practice of dwelling in peace. Because Subhuti in truth has no such practice, Subhuti is said to delight in the practice of dwelling in peace."
