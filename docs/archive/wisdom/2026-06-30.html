般若波羅蜜多心經

舍利子，色不異空，空不異色，色即是空，空即是色，受想行識，亦復如是。

Shariputra, form is not different from emptiness, emptiness is not different from form. Form is emptiness, emptiness is form. The same is true of feelings, perceptions, mental formations, and consciousness.

孟子 · 公孫丑下

曰：「非也。於崇，吾得見王。退而有去志，不欲變，故不受也。繼而有師命，不可以請。久於齊，非我志也。」

Mencius said: "No. At Chong, I obtained an audience with the king. When I withdrew, I already had the intention to leave, and I did not wish to change that intention — so I did not accept a salary. Then military orders came, and I could not request leave. My long stay in Qi was not what I had intended."

六祖大師法寶壇經 · 般若品第二

善知識！吾有一無相頌，各須誦取，在家出家，但依此修。若不自修，惟記吾言，亦無有益。聽吾頌曰：

說通及心通，如日處虛空，惟傳見性法，出世破邪宗。法即無頓漸，迷悟有遲疾，只此見性門，愚人不可悉。說即雖萬般，合理還歸一，煩惱暗宅中，常須生慧日。邪來煩惱至，正來煩惱除，邪正俱不用，清淨至無餘。菩提本自性，起心即是妄，淨心在妄中，但正無三障。世人若修道，一切盡不妨，常自見己過，與道即相當。

'Good friends, I have a formless verse that each of you must recite and remember. Whether you have left home or remain a householder, just practice according to it. If you do not practice it yourselves and merely memorize my words, it will be of no benefit. Listen to my verse:' 'Mastery in speech and mastery in mind are like the sun dwelling in empty space. Transmit only the Dharma of seeing one's nature — this appears in the world to shatter wrong teachings. The Dharma itself has neither sudden nor gradual; it is delusion and awakening that are slow or fast. This gate of seeing one's nature cannot be fathomed by the foolish. Though explanations may be given in ten thousand ways, when they accord with principle they return to one. In the dark house of afflictions, one must constantly give birth to the sun of wisdom. When wrong views come, afflictions arise; when right views come, afflictions are removed. When neither wrong nor right is employed, purity reaches its completest extent. Bodhi is originally one's own nature; to give rise to mental activity is itself delusion. The pure mind exists within delusion; simply correct it and the three obstructions vanish. If worldly people cultivate the Way, nothing whatsoever is an obstacle. Always see your own faults — this accords with the Way.'
