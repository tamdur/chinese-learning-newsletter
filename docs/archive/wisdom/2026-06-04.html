般若波羅蜜多心經

無眼耳鼻舌身意，無色聲香味觸法，無眼界，乃至無意識界。無無明，亦無無明盡，乃至無老死，亦無老死盡。

There are no eyes, ears, nose, tongue, body, or mind; no sights, sounds, smells, tastes, touches, or objects of mind; no realm of sight, and so on up to no realm of consciousness. There is no ignorance and no end of ignorance, and so on up to no old age and death and no end of old age and death.

孟子・公孫丑上

孟子曰：「伯夷隘，柳下惠不恭。隘與不恭，君子不由也。」

Mencius said: "Bo Yi was too narrow; Liu Xia Hui was too lacking in respect. The noble person follows neither extreme."

六祖大師法寶壇經・行由品第一

惠能生在邊方，語音不正，蒙師傳法，今已得悟，只合自性自度。』祖云：『如是，如是！以後佛法，由汝大行。汝去三年，吾方逝世。汝今好去，努力向南。不宜速說，佛法難起。』

「惠能辭違祖已，發足南行。兩月中間，至大廋嶺。逐後數百人來，欲奪衣鉢。一僧俗姓陳，名惠明，先是四品將軍，性行麁慥，極意參尋。為眾人先，趁及惠能。惠能擲下衣鉢於石上，云：『此衣表信，可力爭耶？』能隱草莽中。

Huineng was born in a remote region and speaks with an imperfect accent. Yet having received the Dharma from the Master, I am now awakened. It is only fitting that I ferry myself with my own nature.' The Patriarch said, 'Just so, just so! Hereafter, the Buddha-Dharma will spread widely through you. Three years after you leave, I will depart this world. Go well now, and strive toward the south. But do not begin teaching too soon — the Buddha-Dharma is difficult to establish.'

After Huineng took leave of the Patriarch, he set out southward on foot. Within two months, he reached the Dayu Mountain Range. Several hundred people were pursuing him from behind, intent on seizing the robe and bowl. One monk, surnamed Chen in lay life and named Huiming, had formerly been a general of the fourth rank. He was rough in temperament and the most determined of the pursuers, outrunning all the rest to catch up with Huineng. Huineng set the robe and bowl down on a rock and said, 'This robe is a symbol of faith. Should it be fought over by force?' Then he hid himself in the undergrowth.
