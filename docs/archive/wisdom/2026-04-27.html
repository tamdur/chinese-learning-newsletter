般若波羅蜜多心經

觀自在菩薩，行深般若波羅蜜多時，照見五蘊皆空，度一切苦厄。

Avalokitesvara Bodhisattva, when practicing the profound Prajna Paramita, illuminated the five aggregates and saw that they are all empty, thus overcoming all suffering and distress.

孟子 · 梁惠王下

齊宣王問曰：「人皆謂我毀明堂。毀諸？已乎？」孟子對曰：「夫明堂者，王者之堂也。王欲行王政，則勿毀之矣。」王曰：「王政可得聞與？」對曰：「昔者文王之治岐也，耕者九一，仕者世祿，關市譏而不征，澤梁無禁，罪人不孥。老而無妻曰鰥。老而無夫曰寡。老而無子曰獨。幼而無父曰孤。此四者，天下之窮民而無告者。文王發政施仁，必先斯四者。《詩》云：『哿矣富人，哀此煢獨。』」王曰：「善哉言乎！」

King Xuan of Qi asked, "Everyone tells me to tear down the Hall of Light. Should I tear it down or not?" Mencius replied, "The Hall of Light is the hall of a true king. If Your Majesty wishes to practice kingly government, then do not tear it down." The king said, "May I hear about kingly government?" Mencius replied, "In ancient times, when King Wen governed Qi, farmers were taxed one part in nine, descendants of officials received hereditary salaries, travelers were inspected at passes and markets but not taxed, fish traps at weirs were open to all, and the families of criminals were not punished for their crimes. The old and wifeless are called widowers. The old and husbandless are called widows. The old and childless are called the solitary. The young and fatherless are called orphans. These four are the most destitute people in the world, with no one to turn to. When King Wen put benevolent government into practice, he always gave priority to these four. The Odes say, 'The rich may get along, but pity those who are alone and desolate.'" The king said, "Well said!"

金剛般若波羅蜜經

爾時，須菩提白佛言：「世尊！善男子、善女人，發阿耨多羅三藐三菩提心，云何應住？云何降伏其心？」佛告須菩提：「善男子、善女人，發阿耨多羅三藐三菩提心者，當生如是心：我應滅度一切眾生。滅度一切眾生已，而無有一眾生實滅度者。何以故？須菩提！若菩薩有我相、人相、眾生相、壽者相，則非菩薩。所以者何？須菩提！實無有法，發阿耨多羅三藐三菩提心者。

At that time, Subhuti addressed the Buddha: World-Honored One, if a good man or good woman gives rise to the aspiration for anuttara-samyak-sambodhi, how should they abide? How should they subdue their mind? The Buddha told Subhuti: A good man or good woman who gives rise to the aspiration for anuttara-samyak-sambodhi should give rise to this thought: I should bring all sentient beings to final nirvana. Yet when all sentient beings have been brought to final nirvana, in truth not a single sentient being has been brought to final nirvana. Why? Subhuti, if a bodhisattva holds the mark of self, the mark of person, the mark of sentient being, or the mark of life span, then that one is not a bodhisattva. Why is this? Subhuti, there is in reality no dharma called 'giving rise to the aspiration for anuttara-samyak-sambodhi.'
