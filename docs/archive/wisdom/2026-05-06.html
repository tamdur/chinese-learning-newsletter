般若波羅蜜多心經

舍利子，是諸法空相，不生不滅，不垢不淨，不增不減。是故空中無色，無受想行識。

Shariputra, all dharmas are marked with emptiness — they are neither born nor destroyed, neither defiled nor pure, neither increasing nor decreasing. Therefore, in emptiness there is no form, no feelings, perceptions, mental formations, or consciousness.

孟子 — 梁惠王下

齊人伐燕，取之。諸侯將謀救燕。宣王曰：「諸侯多謀伐寡人者，何以待之？」孟子對曰：「臣聞七十里為政於天下者，湯是也。未聞以千里畏人者也。《書》曰：『湯一征，自葛始。』天下信之。『東面而征，西夷怨；南面而征，北狄怨。曰，奚為後我？』民望之，若大旱之望雲霓也。歸市者不止，耕者不變。誅其君而弔其民，若時雨降，民大悅。《書》曰：『徯我后，后來其蘇。』

The people of Qi attacked Yan and annexed it. The other feudal lords planned to rescue Yan. King Xuan said, "Many of the feudal lords are plotting to attack me. How should I deal with them?" Mencius replied, "I have heard of one who, with only seventy li of territory, brought order to the whole world—that was Tang. I have never heard of one who, with a thousand li of territory, lived in fear of others. The Documents say, 'Tang's campaigns began with Ge.' The whole world trusted him. 'When he marched east, the western tribes complained; when he marched south, the northern tribes complained, saying, Why does he put us last?' The people longed for him as people in a great drought long for clouds and rainbows. Those going to market were not delayed; those plowing the fields were not disturbed. He punished the rulers and comforted the people, and his coming was like timely rain. The people were overjoyed. The Documents say, 'We awaited our lord; when the lord comes, we shall be revived.'"

金剛般若波羅蜜經 — 非說所說分第二十一

「須菩提！汝勿謂如來作是念：『我當有所說法。』莫作是念。何以故？若人言：如來有所說法，即為謗佛，不能解我所說故。須菩提！說法者，無法可說，是名說法。」爾時，慧命須菩提白佛言：「世尊！頗有眾生，於未來世，聞說是法，生信心不？」佛言：「須菩提！彼非眾生，非不眾生。何以故？須菩提！眾生眾生者，如來說非眾生，是名眾生。」

"Subhuti, do not say that the Tathagata has this thought: 'I have something to teach.' Do not think this way. Why? If someone says the Tathagata has something to teach, that person slanders the Buddha and cannot understand what I have said. Subhuti, teaching the Dharma means there is no Dharma to teach — this is called teaching the Dharma." At that time, the wise Subhuti addressed the Buddha, saying: "World-Honored One! Will there be beings in the future who, hearing this teaching, give rise to faith?" The Buddha said: "Subhuti, they are neither beings nor non-beings. Why? Subhuti, what is called 'beings' — the Tathagata says they are not beings; they are merely called beings."
