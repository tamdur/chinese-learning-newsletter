孟子 — 離婁下

公行子有子之喪，右師往弔，入門，有進而與右師言者，有就右師之位而與右師言者。孟子不與右師言，右師不悅曰：「諸君子皆與驩言，孟子獨不與驩言，是簡驩也。」孟子聞之，曰：「禮，朝廷不歷位而相與言，不踰階而相揖也。我欲行禮，子敖以我為簡，不亦異乎？」

Gong Xing Zi was in mourning for his son. The Right Minister came to pay condolences. When he entered the gate, some went forward to speak with the Right Minister, and others went to where the Right Minister was seated to speak with him. Mencius did not speak with the Right Minister, and the Right Minister was displeased, saying: "All the other gentlemen spoke with Huan, yet Mencius alone did not. He is slighting me." When Mencius heard of this, he said: "According to ritual, at court one does not cross positions to converse, and one does not skip steps to bow. I wished to observe proper ritual, and yet Zi'ao considers me rude — is that not strange?"

The Master said: 'Fixing the mind and contemplating stillness — this is sickness, not Chan. Sitting for long periods merely confines the body — what benefit does it serve for understanding truth? Listen to my verse: In life, sitting and never lying down; in death, lying down and never sitting — in both cases, just a set of stinking bones. What basis is this for spiritual practice?' Zhicheng bowed again and said: 'I studied the Way under Great Master Shenxiu for nine years without attaining realization. Now, hearing the Master's single statement, it accords with my original mind. The matter of birth and death is of great urgency. I beg the Master, in great compassion, to teach me further.' The Master said: 'I have heard that your teacher instructs students in the method of precepts, concentration, and wisdom. I wonder how your teacher explains the characteristics of precepts, concentration, and wisdom? Tell me.' Zhicheng said: 'Great Master Shenxiu says: refraining from all evil is called precepts; practicing all good is called wisdom; purifying one's own mind is called concentration.'
