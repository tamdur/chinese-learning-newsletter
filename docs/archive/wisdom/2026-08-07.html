般若波羅蜜多心經

無苦集滅道，無智亦無得。以無所得故，菩提薩埵，依般若波羅蜜多故，心無罣礙。

There is no suffering, no cause of suffering, no cessation, no path; no wisdom and no attainment. With nothing to attain, a bodhisattva relies on Prajna Paramita, and the mind is without hindrance.

孟子 · 離婁上

「故曰，為高必因丘陵，為下必因川澤。為政不因先王之道，可謂智乎？是以惟仁者宜在高位。不仁而在高位，是播其惡於眾也。上無道揆也。下無法守也，朝不信道，工不信度，君子犯義，小人犯刑，國之所存者幸也。

"Therefore it is said: to build high, one must rely on hills and mounds; to build low, one must rely on rivers and marshes. Can it be called wise to govern without relying on the Way of the former kings? For this reason, only the benevolent should occupy high positions. When someone without benevolence occupies a high position, it is to spread his wickedness among the multitude. When those above have no principles of the Way to guide them, those below have no laws to uphold, the court does not trust the Way, craftsmen do not trust their measures, gentlemen violate righteousness, and common people violate the law—it is sheer luck if the state survives at all.

六祖大師法寶壇經 · 機緣品第七

聽吾偈曰：禮本折慢幢，頭奚不至地？有我罪即生，亡功禮無比。」師又曰：「汝名什麼？」曰：「法達。」師曰：「汝名法達，何曾達法？」復說偈曰：「汝今名法達，勤誦未休歇，空誦但循聲，明心號菩薩。汝今有緣故，吾今為汝說，但信佛無言，蓮華從口發。」達聞偈，悔謝曰：「而今而後，當謙恭一切。弟子誦《法華經》，未解經義，心常有疑。和尚智慧廣大，願略說經中義理。」師曰：「法達！法即甚達，汝心不達。經本無疑，汝心自疑。

'Listen to my verse: Bowing is meant to topple the banner of pride — why then does your head not touch the ground? When there is a sense of self, offenses arise at once; forget all merit, and your bowing is beyond compare.' The Master then asked: 'What is your name?' He answered: 'Fada.' The Master said: 'Your name means "Dharma Penetrator" — yet when have you ever penetrated the Dharma?' He then spoke another verse: 'Your name is Fada, yet you tirelessly recite without rest. Chanting by rote merely follows the sound; one who illuminates the mind is called a bodhisattva. Because you have this karmic connection, I will now explain it for you. Simply trust that the Buddha speaks without words, and the lotus flower will blossom from your mouth.' Hearing the verse, Fada repented and apologized, saying: 'From this day forward, I shall be humble and respectful toward all. I have been reciting the Lotus Sutra but have not understood its meaning, and my mind has always been plagued by doubts. Master, your wisdom is vast — please briefly explain the essential principles of the sutra.' The Master said: 'Fada! The Dharma is quite penetrating — it is your own mind that has not penetrated it. The sutra itself holds no doubts; it is your own mind that creates doubt.'
