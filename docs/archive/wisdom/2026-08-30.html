般若波羅蜜多心經

故知般若波羅蜜多，是大神咒，是大明咒，是無上咒，是無等等咒，能除一切苦，真實不虛。故說般若波羅蜜多咒，即說咒曰：揭諦揭諦，波羅揭諦，波羅僧揭諦，菩提薩婆訶。

Therefore, know that Prajna Paramita is the great transcendent mantra, the great bright mantra, the supreme mantra, the unequalled mantra, which can remove all suffering and is true, not false. Therefore he proclaimed the Prajna Paramita mantra, saying: Gate gate paragate parasamgate bodhi svaha.

Zhihuang had no reply. After a long silence, he asked, “Whose heir is your teacher?” Xuance said, “My teacher is the Sixth Patriarch of Caoxi.”

Zhihuang asked, “What does the Sixth Patriarch take to be meditative concentration?”

Xuance said, “What my teacher teaches is this: it is wondrously clear and perfectly still, its essence and its function alike being suchness itself. The five aggregates are fundamentally empty; the six sense-objects have no real existence. There is no going out and no coming in, no concentration and no distraction. The nature of meditation abides nowhere — so leave off abiding in the stillness of meditation. The nature of meditation is unborn — so leave off giving rise to thoughts about meditation. Let the mind be like empty space, yet without even the notion of empty space as a measure.”

Hearing this, Zhihuang went straight to call upon the Master. The Master asked, “What brings you here, good sir?” Zhihuang related everything that had happened.

The Master said, “It is truly as he said. If only your mind is like empty space, without clinging to any view of emptiness — responding and functioning without obstruction, moving or still with no calculating mind, all sense of ordinary and sagely forgotten, both subject and object extinguished together, nature and appearances alike suchness itself — then there will be no time when you are not in concentration.”
