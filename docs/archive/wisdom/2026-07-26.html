般若波羅蜜多心經

故知般若波羅蜜多，是大神咒，是大明咒，是無上咒，是無等等咒，能除一切苦，真實不虛。故說般若波羅蜜多咒，即說咒曰：揭諦揭諦，波羅揭諦，波羅僧揭諦，菩提薩婆訶。

Therefore, know that Prajna Paramita is the great transcendent mantra, the great bright mantra, the supreme mantra, the unequalled mantra, which can remove all suffering and is true, not false. Therefore he proclaimed the Prajna Paramita mantra, saying: Gate gate paragate parasamgate bodhi svaha.

孟子・滕文公下

孟子謂戴不勝曰：「子欲子之王之善與？我明告子。有楚大夫於此，欲其子之齊語也，則使齊人傅諸？使楚人傅諸？」曰：「使齊人傅之。」曰：「一齊人傅之，眾楚人咻之，雖日撻而求其齊也，不可得矣；引而置之莊嶽之間數年，雖日撻而求其楚，亦不可得矣。子謂薛居州，善士也。使之居於王所。在於王所者，長幼卑尊，皆薛居州也，王誰與為不善？在王所者，長幼卑尊，皆非薛居州也，王誰與為善？一薛居州，獨如宋王何？」

Mencius said to Dai Busheng: "You want your king to become good? Let me explain it plainly. Suppose a Chu nobleman here wants his son to speak the language of Qi — would he have a man of Qi tutor the boy, or a man of Chu?" "A man of Qi," Dai answered. Mencius said: "If one man of Qi tutors him while a crowd of Chu men jabber around him, even if you beat the boy daily demanding he speak Qi dialect, it will be impossible. But if you took him and placed him in the Zhuang-Yue district of Qi for several years, even if you beat him daily demanding he speak Chu dialect, that too would be impossible. You say Xue Juzhou is a good man and have placed him at court. But if everyone at court, old and young, high and low, were a Xue Juzhou, then with whom would the king do wrong? And if no one at court, old or young, high or low, is a Xue Juzhou, then with whom would the king do right? What can a single Xue Juzhou do about the king of Song?"

六祖大師法寶壇經・懺悔品第六

又煩惱無邊誓願斷，將自性般若智，除却虛妄思想心是也。又法門無盡誓願學，須自見性，常行正法，是名真學。又無上佛道誓願成，既常能下心，行於真正，離迷離覺，常生般若。除真除妄，即見佛性，即言下佛道成。常念修行，是願力法。

「善知識！今發四弘願了，更與善知識授無相三歸依戒。善知識！歸依覺，兩足尊。歸依正，離欲尊。歸依淨，眾中尊。

Furthermore, 'Afflictions, inexhaustible in number, I vow to cut off' means to use the prajna wisdom of your own nature to eliminate false and deluded thoughts. Furthermore, 'Dharma teachings, boundless in number, I vow to study' means that you must see your own nature and always practice the true Dharma. This is called true study. Furthermore, 'The supreme Buddha Way, I vow to attain' means that once you can constantly humble your mind and practice what is true and correct, departing from both delusion and enlightenment, constantly giving rise to prajna, and setting aside both the true and the false — then you see the Buddha-nature, and in that very moment the Buddha Way is attained. Always be mindful of cultivation and practice — this is the power of the vows. Good friends! Now that we have made the Four Great Vows, I will further confer upon you the formless threefold refuge precepts. Good friends! Take refuge in enlightenment, the most honored among those replete in both blessings and wisdom. Take refuge in the true Dharma, the most honored among those free from desire. Take refuge in purity, the most honored among communities.
