般若波羅蜜多心經

舍利子，色不異空，空不異色，色即是空，空即是色，受想行識，亦復如是。

Shariputra, form is not different from emptiness, emptiness is not different from form. Form is emptiness, emptiness is form. The same is true of feelings, perceptions, mental formations, and consciousness.

孟子 · 滕文公下

孟子曰：「仕。傳曰：『孔子三月無君，則皇皇如也，出疆必載質。』公明儀曰：『古之人三月無君則弔。』」「三月無君則弔，不以急乎？」曰：「士之失位也，猶諸侯之失國家也。禮曰：『諸侯耕助，以供粢盛；夫人蠶繅，以為衣服。犧牲不成，粢盛不潔，衣服不備，不敢以祭。惟士無田，則亦不祭。』牲殺器皿衣服不備，不敢以祭，則不敢以宴，亦不足弔乎？」「出疆必載質，何也？」

Mencius said: "They did. The records say: 'When Confucius went three months without a ruler to serve, he became anxious and uneasy; when he left a state, he always carried his ceremonial gifts with him.' Gongming Yi said: 'Among the ancients, if one went three months without a ruler, friends would come to offer condolences.'" "If three months without a ruler calls for condolences, is that not overly urgent?" Mencius said: "A scholar losing his position is like a feudal lord losing his state. The Rites say: 'The feudal lords plow the communal fields to supply the sacrificial grain; their consorts tend silkworms and reel silk to make the sacrificial garments. If the sacrificial animals are not fit, the grain is not pure, and the garments are not prepared, they dare not sacrifice. Likewise, if a scholar has no land, he too cannot sacrifice.' If the animals, vessels, and garments are not prepared so that one dares not sacrifice, then one dares not feast either — is that not also worthy of condolences?" "Why did Confucius always carry ceremonial gifts when leaving a state?"

At that time, the Great Master saw that scholars and commoners from Guangzhou, Shaozhou, and all four directions had gathered in the mountains to hear the Dharma. He ascended the platform and addressed the assembly: 'Come, good friends! This matter must arise from within yourselves. At all times, in every thought-moment, purify your own minds. Cultivate and practice by yourselves, see your own Dharma-body, see the Buddha of your own mind, and deliver and discipline yourselves — only then will you not have come here in vain. Since you have come from afar and we are gathered here together, we all share a karmic connection. Now let each of you kneel. I will first transmit the five-fold Dharma-body incense of your own self-nature, and then confer the formless repentance.' The assembly knelt.
