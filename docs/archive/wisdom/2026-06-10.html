般若波羅蜜多心經

舍利子，是諸法空相，不生不滅，不垢不淨，不增不減。是故空中無色，無受想行識。

Shariputra, all dharmas are marked with emptiness — they are neither born nor destroyed, neither defiled nor pure, neither increasing nor decreasing. Therefore, in emptiness there is no form, no feelings, perceptions, mental formations, or consciousness.

孟子 · 公孫丑下

曰：「豈謂是與？曾子曰：『晉楚之富，不可及也。彼以其富，我以吾仁；彼以其爵，我以吾義，吾何慊乎哉？』夫豈不義而曾子言之？是或一道也。天下有達尊三：爵一，齒一，德一。朝廷莫如爵，鄉黨莫如齒，輔世長民莫如德。惡得有其一，以慢其二哉？故將大有為之君，必有所不召之臣。欲有謀焉，則就之。其尊德樂道，不如是不足與有為也。故湯之於伊尹，學焉而後臣之，故不勞而王；桓公之於管仲，學焉而後臣之，故不勞而霸。今天下地醜德齊，莫能相尚。無他，好臣其所教，而不好臣其所受教。湯之於伊尹，桓公之於管仲，則不敢召。管仲且猶不可召，而況不為管仲者乎？」

Mencius said: "Is that really the point? Zengzi said: 'The wealth of Jin and Chu is beyond my reach. They have their wealth; I have my benevolence. They have their rank; I have my righteousness. What do I lack?' Surely Zengzi would not have said this if it were not right — it must represent one valid principle. In the world there are three things universally honored: rank, age, and virtue. At court, nothing compares to rank; in the village, nothing compares to age; for governing the world and leading the people, nothing compares to virtue. How can one who possesses only one of these look down upon the other two? Therefore a ruler who intends to accomplish great things must have ministers he does not summon. When he wishes to consult them, he goes to them. If his reverence for virtue and delight in the Way does not reach this level, he is not worth working with. Thus Tang first learned from Yi Yin, and only then made him a minister — and so he became king without difficulty. Duke Huan first learned from Guan Zhong, and only then made him a minister — and so he became hegemon without difficulty. Now the states are similar in territory and equal in virtue, and none can rise above the others. For no other reason than this: they prefer to make ministers of those they can instruct, not those from whom they can learn. Tang would not have dared summon Yi Yin, nor Duke Huan summon Guan Zhong. If even Guan Zhong could not be summoned, how much less one who would not stoop to be a Guan Zhong?"

六祖大師法寶壇經 · 行由品第一

』宗曰：『何不論禪定解脫？』能曰：『為是二法，不是佛法。佛法是不二之法。』宗又問：『如何是佛法不二之法？』惠能曰：『法師講《涅槃經》，明佛性，是佛法不二之法。如高貴德王菩薩白佛言：「犯四重禁、作五逆罪，及一闡提等，當斷善根佛性否？」佛言：「善根有二：一者常，二者無常，佛性非常非無常，是故不斷，名為不二。一者善，二者不善，佛性非善非不善，是名不二。

Yinzong asked, 'Why not discuss meditation and liberation?' Huineng said, 'Because those are dualistic practices, and the Buddha-Dharma is the teaching of non-duality.' Yinzong asked again, 'What is the non-dual teaching of the Buddha-Dharma?' Huineng said, 'In your lecture on the Nirvana Sutra, you explain that Buddha-nature is the non-dual teaching of the Buddha-Dharma. As the noble Bodhisattva King of High Virtue said to the Buddha: "Do those who have violated the four grave precepts, committed the five heinous crimes, or who are icchantikas — have they cut off their good roots and Buddha-nature?" The Buddha said: "Good roots are of two kinds: one is permanent, the other impermanent. But Buddha-nature is neither permanent nor impermanent, and therefore is not cut off — this is called non-duality. One kind is good, the other is not good. But Buddha-nature is neither good nor not good — this is called non-duality.
