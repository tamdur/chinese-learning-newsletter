般若波羅蜜多心經

舍利子，色不異空，空不異色，色即是空，空即是色，受想行識，亦復如是。

Shariputra, form is not different from emptiness, emptiness is not different from form. Form is emptiness, emptiness is form. The same is true of feelings, perceptions, mental formations, and consciousness.

孟子：萬章上

萬章問曰：「《詩》云：『娶妻如之何？必告父母。』信斯言也，宜莫如舜。舜之不告而娶，何也？」孟子曰：「告則不得娶。男女居室，人之大倫也。如告，則廢人之大倫，以懟父母，是以不告也。」萬章曰：「舜之不告而娶，則吾既得聞命矣；帝之妻舜而不告，何也？」曰：「帝亦知告焉則不得妻也。」

Wan Zhang asked: "The Odes say: 'How should one take a wife? One must first inform one's parents.' If anyone should have believed these words, surely it was Shun. Yet Shun married without informing his parents — why?" Mencius said: "Had he informed them, he would not have been allowed to marry. The union of man and woman in marriage is the greatest of human relationships. Had he informed them, he would have had to abandon this greatest of human relationships, which would have bred resentment toward his parents. That is why he did not inform them." Wan Zhang said: "That Shun did not inform his parents before marrying — I have heard your explanation of that. But why did the Emperor give his daughters to Shun without informing Shun's parents?" Mencius said: "The Emperor also knew that if he informed them, the marriage would not be permitted."

六祖大師法寶壇經：頓漸品第八

一日，師告眾曰：「吾有一物，無頭無尾，無名無字，無背無面。諸人還識否？」神會出曰：「是諸佛之本源，神會之佛性。」師曰：「向汝道：『無名無字』，汝便喚作本源佛性。汝向去有把茆蓋頭，也只成箇知解宗徒。」祖師滅後，會入京洛，大弘曹溪頓教，著《顯宗記》，盛行于世。
師見諸宗難問咸起惡心，多集座下愍而謂曰：「學道之人，一切善念惡念應當盡除。無名可名，名於自性，無二之性，是名實性。

One day, the Master addressed the assembly: 'I have one thing — it has no head and no tail, no name and no label, no back and no front. Do any of you recognize it?' Shenhui stepped forward and said: 'It is the original source of all Buddhas, and it is Shenhui's Buddha-nature.' The Master said: 'I just told you it has no name and no label, and you immediately call it the original source and Buddha-nature. Even if you go on to have a thatched hut over your head someday, you will be nothing more than a follower of intellectual understanding.' After the Patriarch's passing, Shenhui went to the capital region of Luoyang, where he widely propagated the sudden teaching of Caoxi. He wrote the Xianzong Ji (Record of Revealing the True Teaching), which became widely known throughout the land.
