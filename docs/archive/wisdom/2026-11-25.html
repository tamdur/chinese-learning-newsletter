般若波羅蜜多心經

舍利子，是諸法空相，不生不滅，不垢不淨，不增不減。是故空中無色，無受想行識。

Shariputra, all dharmas are marked with emptiness — they are neither born nor destroyed, neither defiled nor pure, neither increasing nor decreasing. Therefore, in emptiness there is no form, no feelings, perceptions, mental formations, or consciousness.

孟子．告子下

孟子曰：「子過矣。禹之治水，水之道也。是故禹以四海為壑，今吾子以鄰國為壑。水逆行，謂之洚水。洚水者，洪水也，仁人之所惡也。吾子過矣。」孟子曰：「君子不亮，惡乎執？」魯欲使樂正子為政。孟子曰：「吾聞之，喜而不寐。」公孫丑曰：「樂正子強乎？」曰：「否。」「有知慮乎？」曰：「否。」「多聞識乎？」曰：「否。」「然則奚為喜而不寐？」曰：「其為人也好善。」

Mencius said: 'You are wrong, sir. When Yu controlled the floods, he worked with the natural course of water. That is why Yu used the four seas as his drainage basin. But you, sir, use the neighboring states as your drainage basin. When water flows backward, it is called a deluge. A deluge is a flood—something a benevolent person detests. You are wrong, sir.' Mencius also said: 'If a gentleman is not sincere, how can he hold firm to anything?' The state of Lu wanted to appoint Yue Zheng Zi to manage the government. Mencius said: 'When I heard this, I was so happy I could not sleep.' Gongsun Chou asked: 'Is Yue Zheng Zi a man of vigor?' Mencius said: 'No.' 'Is he a man of wisdom and foresight?' 'No.' 'Is he a man of wide learning?' 'No.' 'Then why were you so happy you could not sleep?' Mencius said: 'Because he is a man who loves goodness.'

「祖一日喚諸門人總來：『吾向汝說，世人生死事大，汝等終日只求福田，不求出離生死苦海；自性若迷，福何可救？汝等各去，自看智慧，取自本心般若之性，各作一偈，來呈吾看。若悟大意，付汝衣法，為第六代祖。火急速去，不得遲滯，思量即不中用；見性之人，言下須見。若如此者，輪刀上陣，亦得見之。』眾得處分，退而遞相謂曰：『我等眾人，不須澄心用意作偈，將呈和尚，有何所益？神秀上座，現為教授師，必是他得。

One day the Patriarch summoned all his disciples and said, 'Let me tell you: the matter of birth and death is the great concern of worldly beings. Yet all day long you only seek the field of merit and do not seek to escape the bitter sea of birth and death. If your own nature is deluded, how can merit save you? Each of you, go and look into your own wisdom. Take the prajna-nature of your original mind and each compose a verse. Bring it to me. If one of you awakens to the great meaning, I will transmit the robe and Dharma to you, making you the Sixth Patriarch. Go quickly! Do not delay! Deliberation is useless in this matter. One who sees their nature must see it the instant the words are spoken. One who can do this could see it even while wielding a sword in battle.' The assembly, having received these instructions, withdrew and said to one another, 'We in the assembly need not bother calming our minds to compose a verse and present it to the Master — what good would it do?
