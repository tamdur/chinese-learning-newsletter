般若波羅蜜多心經

無眼耳鼻舌身意，無色聲香味觸法，無眼界，乃至無意識界。無無明，亦無無明盡，乃至無老死，亦無老死盡。

There are no eyes, ears, nose, tongue, body, or mind; no sights, sounds, smells, tastes, touches, or objects of mind; no realm of sight, and so on up to no realm of consciousness. There is no ignorance and no end of ignorance, and so on up to no old age and death and no end of old age and death.

孟子 · 滕文公上

他日又求見孟子。孟子曰：「吾今則可以見矣。不直，則道不見；我且直之。吾聞夷子墨者。墨之治喪也，以薄為其道也。夷子思以易天下，豈以為非是而不貴也？然而夷子葬其親厚，則是以所賤事親也。」徐子以告夷子。夷子曰：「儒者之道，古之人『若保赤子』，此言何謂也？之則以為愛無差等，施由親始。」

Another day, Yi Zhi again sought to see Mencius. Mencius said, 'Now I can see him. If I am not straightforward, the Way will not be made clear, so let me be direct. I have heard that Yi Zhi is a Mohist. In Mohist funeral practice, frugality is taken as the guiding principle. Yi Zhi intends to change the world with this — surely he considers lavish funerals wrong and unworthy. And yet Yi Zhi buried his own parents lavishly. This means he treated his parents with what he himself considers base.' Xu Bi reported this to Yi Zhi. Yi Zhi said, 'According to the Confucian Way, the ancients "cared for the people as if caring for a newborn" — what does this mean? I take it to mean that love knows no gradations, but its practice begins with one's parents.'

六祖大師法寶壇經 · 定慧品第四

於自念上，常離諸境，不於境上生心。若只百物不思，念盡除却，一念絕即死，別處受生，是為大錯。學道者思之。若不識法意，自錯猶可，更誤他人；自迷不見，又謗佛經，所以立無念為宗。善知識！云何立無念為宗？只緣口說見性，迷人於境上有念，念上便起邪見，一切塵勞妄想從此而生。自性本無一法可得，若有所得，妄說禍福，即是塵勞邪見，故此法門立無念為宗。善知識！無者無何事？念者念何物？無者無二相，無諸塵勞之心。

'In your own thoughts, always remain free from all objects; do not give rise to mental activity in response to objects. If you merely try to think of nothing at all, eliminating all thoughts completely, then the moment thought ceases entirely you die and are reborn elsewhere — this is a grave error. Students of the Way, reflect on this carefully. If you do not understand the meaning of this Dharma, you will go wrong yourself, which is one thing — but worse, you will mislead others. Unable to see because of your own delusion, you will even slander the Buddhist sutras. Therefore no-thought is established as the doctrine. Good friends! Why is no-thought established as the doctrine? Simply because those who speak of seeing their own nature are still, when encountering objects, caught up in thoughts, and upon those thoughts, wrong views arise. All the dust and defilements and deluded thinking spring from this. In its own nature, there is originally not a single dharma that can be grasped. If there is anything grasped, then one falsely speaks of fortune and misfortune — and that itself is dust, defilement, and wrong views. Therefore this Dharma teaching establishes no-thought as its doctrine. Good friends! "No" — no what? "Thought" — thought of what? "No" means free from duality, free from all the minds of dust and defilement.'
