般若波羅蜜多心經

無眼耳鼻舌身意，無色聲香味觸法，無眼界，乃至無意識界。無無明，亦無無明盡，乃至無老死，亦無老死盡。

There are no eyes, ears, nose, tongue, body, or mind; no sights, sounds, smells, tastes, touches, or objects of mind; no realm of sight, and so on up to no realm of consciousness. There is no ignorance and no end of ignorance, and so on up to no old age and death and no end of old age and death.

孟子：離婁下

曾子居武城，有越寇。或曰：「寇至，盍去諸？」曰：「無寓人於我室，毀傷其薪木。」寇退，則曰：「修我牆屋，我將反。」寇退，曾子反。左右曰：「待先生，如此其忠且敬也。寇至則先去以為民望，寇退則反，殆於不可。」沈猶行曰：「是非汝所知也。昔沈猶有負芻之禍，從先生者七十人，未有與焉。」子思居於衛，有齊寇。或曰：「寇至，盍去諸？」子思曰：「如伋去，君誰與守？」

Zengzi lived in Wucheng when there was a raid from Yue. Someone said: "The raiders are coming — should you not leave?" He said: "Do not let anyone lodge in my house or damage the trees and shrubs." When the raiders withdrew, he said: "Repair my walls and roof — I shall return." When the raiders withdrew, Zengzi returned. His attendants said: "You were treated by the people with such loyalty and respect, yet when the raiders came you were the first to leave, setting an example for the people, and when they withdrew you returned. This seems not quite right." Shen Youxing said: "This is beyond your understanding. In the past, Shen You faced the calamity of the firewood carrier. Of the seventy followers of the Master, not one was involved in it." Zi Si was living in Wei when there was a raid from Qi. Someone said: "The raiders are coming — should you not leave?" Zi Si said: "If I leave, with whom will the ruler defend the state?"

六祖大師法寶壇經：頓漸品第八

後投僧出家，具戒精進。一日，憶師之言，遠來禮覲。師曰：「吾久念汝，汝來何晚？」曰：「昨蒙和尚捨罪，今雖出家苦行，終難報德，其惟傳法度生乎？弟子常覽《涅槃經》，未曉常無常義。乞和尚慈悲，略為解說。」師曰：「無常者，即佛性也。有常者，即一切善惡諸法分別心也。」曰：「和尚所說，大違經文。」師曰：「吾傳佛心印，安敢違於佛經？」曰：「經說佛性是常；和尚却言無常。善惡之法乃至菩提心，皆是無常；和尚却言是常。

Later he joined the sangha, was fully ordained, and practiced with diligence. One day, remembering the Master's words, he traveled from afar to pay his respects. The Master said: 'I have been thinking of you for a long time. Why have you come so late?' Xingchang said: 'Previously the Master pardoned my offense. Though I have now left home and practice austerities, I can never fully repay such virtue. Perhaps the only way is to transmit the Dharma and save sentient beings? I have been reading the Nirvana Sutra regularly, but I do not understand the meaning of permanence and impermanence. I humbly beg the Master to explain briefly out of compassion.' The Master said: 'Impermanence is itself Buddha-nature. Permanence is the discriminating mind that separates all dharmas into good and evil.' Xingchang said: 'What the Master says greatly contradicts the sutra text.' The Master said: 'I transmit the Buddha's mind-seal. How would I dare contradict the Buddha's sutras?' Xingchang said: 'The sutra says Buddha-nature is permanent, yet the Master says it is impermanent. It says good and evil dharmas, even the bodhi-mind, are all impermanent, yet the Master says they are permanent.'
