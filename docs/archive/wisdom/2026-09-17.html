般若波羅蜜多心經

無眼耳鼻舌身意，無色聲香味觸法，無眼界，乃至無意識界。無無明，亦無無明盡，乃至無老死，亦無老死盡。

There are no eyes, ears, nose, tongue, body, or mind; no sights, sounds, smells, tastes, touches, or objects of mind; no realm of sight, and so on up to no realm of consciousness. There is no ignorance and no end of ignorance, and so on up to no old age and death and no end of old age and death.

孟子：萬章上

曰：「否。昔者有饋生魚於鄭子產，子產使校人畜之池。校人烹之，反命曰：『始舍之圉圉焉，少則洋洋焉，攸然而逝。』子產曰『得其所哉！得其所哉！』校人出，曰：『孰謂子產智？予既烹而食之，曰：得其所哉？得其所哉。』故君子可欺以其方，難罔以非其道。彼以愛兄之道來，故誠信而喜之，奚偽焉？」萬章問曰：「象日以殺舜為事，立為天子，則放之，何也？」孟子曰：「封之也，或曰放焉。」

Mencius said: "No. In ancient times, someone sent a gift of live fish to Zi Chan of Zheng. Zi Chan ordered the pond keeper to raise them in the pond. The pond keeper cooked and ate them instead, then reported back: 'When I first released them they were sluggish, but soon they became lively and swam off happily into the deep.' Zi Chan said: 'They found their place! They found their place!' The pond keeper went out and said: 'Who says Zi Chan is clever? I cooked and ate them, and he said: They found their place! They found their place!' Thus a gentleman can be deceived by what accords with principle, but cannot be fooled by what violates it. Xiang came to Shun with the manner of one who loves his brother, and so Shun genuinely believed him and was happy — what pretense was there?" Wan Zhang asked: "Xiang made it his daily business to try to kill Shun. When Shun became Son of Heaven, why did he merely exile Xiang?" Mencius said: "He enfeoffed him — though some say it was exile."

六祖大師法寶壇經：宣詔品第九

神龍元年上元日，則天、中宗詔云：「朕請安、秀二師宮中供養。萬機之暇，每究一乘。二師推讓云：『南方有能禪師，密授忍大師衣法，傳佛心印，可請彼問。』今遣內侍薛簡，馳詔迎請，願師慈念，速赴上京。」師上表辭疾，願終林麓。薛簡曰：「京城禪德皆云：『欲得會道，必須坐禪習定。若不因禪定而得解脫者，未之有也。』未審師所說法如何？」師曰：「道由心悟，豈在坐也。經云：『若言如來若坐若臥，是行邪道。

On the day of the Lantern Festival in the first year of the Shenlong era, Empress Wu Zetian and Emperor Zhongzong issued a decree: 'We have invited Masters Huian and Shenxiu to the palace for offerings. In our leisure from the myriad affairs of state, we often contemplate the One Vehicle. Both masters have deferred, saying: "In the south there is Chan Master Huineng, who secretly received the robe and Dharma from the great Master Hongren, transmitting the Buddha Mind Seal. You should invite him instead." We now dispatch the palace attendant Xue Jian with an urgent decree to welcome and invite you. We hope the Master will be compassionate and swiftly come to the capital.' The Master submitted a memorial declining due to illness, wishing to end his days in the forests. Xue Jian said: 'The Chan worthies in the capital all say: "To realize the Way, one must practice seated meditation and concentration. No one has ever attained liberation without dhyana concentration." I wonder what the Master's teaching is on this?' The Master said: 'The Way is realized through the mind — how could it depend on sitting? The sutra says: "If anyone says the Tathagata either sits or lies down, that person walks an errant path."'
