般若波羅蜜多心經 — Thursday

無眼耳鼻舌身意，無色聲香味觸法，無眼界，乃至無意識界。無無明，亦無無明盡，乃至無老死，亦無老死盡。

There are no eyes, ears, nose, tongue, body, or mind; no sights, sounds, smells, tastes, touches, or objects of mind; no realm of sight, and so on up to no realm of consciousness. There is no ignorance and no end of ignorance, and so on up to no old age and death and no end of old age and death.

孟子 — 告子下

曰：「夫道，若大路然，豈難知哉？人病不求耳。子歸而求之，有餘師。」公孫丑問曰：「高子曰：『《小弁》，小人之詩也。』」孟子曰：「何以言之？」曰：「怨。」曰：「固哉，高叟之為《詩》也！有人於此，越人關弓而射之，則己談笑而道之；無他，疏之也。其兄關弓而射之，則己垂涕泣而道之；無他，戚之也。小弁之怨，親親也。親親，仁也。固矣夫，高叟之為《詩》也！」曰：「《凱風》何以不怨？」

Mencius said: 'The Way is like a broad highway—is it hard to find? People simply fail to seek it. Go home and seek it; you will have teachers enough.' Gongsun Chou said: 'Master Gao says that the "Xiao Bian" ode is the poem of a petty man.' Mencius asked: 'Why does he say that?' 'Because it expresses resentment.' Mencius said: 'How rigid old Master Gao is in his reading of the Odes! Suppose someone here were shot at by a stranger from Yue with a drawn bow—one could talk about it with a laugh, for no other reason than that the attacker is a stranger. But if one's own brother drew a bow and shot, one would speak of it in tears—for no other reason than the closeness of the bond. The resentment of "Xiao Bian" comes from love for one's kin. And love for kin is benevolence itself. How rigid indeed is old Master Gao's reading of the Odes!' Gongsun Chou asked: 'Then why does the "Kai Feng" ode not express resentment?'

金剛般若波羅蜜經 — 福智無比分第二十四

「須菩提！若三千大千世界中，所有諸須彌山王，如是等七寶聚，有人持用布施；若人以此《般若波羅蜜經》，乃至四句偈等，受持讀誦，為他人說，於前福德百分不及一，百千萬億分、乃至算數譬喻所不能及。」

"Subhuti, if someone gathered together heaps of the seven treasures equal in number to all the Mount Sumerus in the three thousand great thousand worlds and used them for giving; and if another person received, upheld, read, and recited even as few as four lines of verse from this Prajnaparamita Sutra and explained it to others — the merit of the former would not equal even one hundredth, one billionth, nor any fraction reachable by calculation or analogy of the latter."
