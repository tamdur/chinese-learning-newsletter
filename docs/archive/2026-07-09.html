美國再度空襲伊朗 川普宣布停火結束

來源：NPR

美國總統川普說，六月和伊朗談好的停火「結束了」。他罵伊朗的領導人是「壞人」，還下令再次出兵攻打伊朗。

伊朗馬上還手，打了美軍在科威特和巴林的基地，讓戰火燒到別的國家。

這次出兵沒有先問過國會（管法律的最高機關），油價因此大漲，一桶漲到大約七十八美元。

US President Trump said the ceasefire agreed with Iran in June has "ended." He called Iran's leaders "bad guys" and ordered military forces to strike Iran again. Iran immediately retaliated, attacking US military bases in Kuwait and Bahrain, spreading the conflict to other countries. This military action proceeded without consulting Congress, the nation's highest legislative body, causing oil prices to surge sharply to approximately $78 per barrel.

OpenAI 開放新模型 速度創下紀錄

來源：OpenAI / VentureBeat

OpenAI 在七月九日開放新的 GPT-5.6 模型給大家使用，最強的一款叫做 Sol。

真正的重點不是模型本身，而是它跑得超快。Sol 用一家叫 Cerebras 的公司做的特別晶片，一秒可以吐出七百五十個字，比一般的顯示卡快六到十五倍。

這代表 AI 花錢的重點，正從「訓練」慢慢轉到「使用」。

OpenAI released a new GPT-5.6 model for public use on July 9th, with its most powerful version called Sol. The real headline isn't the model itself, but rather its speed. Sol runs on custom chips made by Cerebras and can generate 750 tokens per second—6 to 15 times faster than standard GPUs. This reflects a fundamental shift in AI economics: spending priorities are gradually moving from training toward inference costs.

IMF 下修今年全球成長 歸咎伊朗戰爭

來源：Al Jazeera / IMF

國際貨幣基金（IMF，一個管全球經濟的組織）說，因為伊朗戰爭讓能源變貴，今年全世界的經濟成長會變慢。

他們把今年的成長預測，從百分之三點五降到百分之三；物價上漲則從百分之四點一升到百分之四點七。

這是第一次有人把這場戰爭的代價，用清楚的數字算給大家看。

The International Monetary Fund (IMF) says rising energy prices from the Iran war are slowing global economic growth this year. The organization has cut its growth forecast from 3.5% to 3%, while raising its inflation projection from 4.1% to 4.7%. This marks the first time the war's economic costs have been quantified clearly and publicly.

好萊塢千億大合併 多州聯手出手擋

來源：Storyboard18

美國好幾個州要聯手擋下一筆一千一百億美元的大合併，也就是 Skydance-Paramount 要買下華納兄弟的案子。

帶頭的是加州的檢察長（管法律的官員），他們擔心合併之後，好萊塢會被同一家公司管得太多。

有趣的是，這個案子上週才剛通過聯邦政府的審查，現在戰場又換到了各州。

Several U.S. states are joining forces to block a massive $110 billion merger—Skydance-Paramount's bid to acquire Warner Bros. California's Attorney General is leading the effort, concerned that the merger would leave Hollywood's output overly concentrated under one company's control. Remarkably, the deal just passed federal government review last week, and now the battle has shifted to the state level.

密西根球員發光 NBA 夏季聯賽

來源：On3

有十一位密西根大學的前球員，正在打 NBA 夏季聯賽（七月九日到十九日）。這是他們拿下全國冠軍後的好成果。

最亮眼的是選秀第十一順位的 Lendeborg。第一場對上湖人，他投籃全部命中，拿下十九分，三分球四投四中。

下一場對馬刺，他又拿到十一分和八個籃板。

Eleven former University of Michigan players are currently competing in the NBA Summer League (July 9-19). It's an impressive showing following their national championship. The standout performance has come from Lendeborg, the 11th overall draft pick. In his first game against the Lakers, he was perfect from the field, scoring 19 points with a 4-for-4 night from three-point range. In his next game against the Spurs, he added 11 points and 8 rebounds.

吉馬良斯想投奔兵工廠 阿森納準備再出價

來源：CaughtOffside / Sky Sports

兵工廠線——紐卡索的隊長吉馬良斯（Bruno Guimaraes）親口告訴球隊，他想加入兵工廠，也就是阿森納。

阿森納之前出過兩次價，五千五百萬和六千五百萬英鎊都被拒絕，現在準備再加碼到六千五百萬到九千萬英鎊。

教練阿爾特塔把他當成今年夏天最想要的中場球員。問題是，紐卡索能不能留住這位一心想走的隊長。

The Arsenal beat — Newcastle captain Bruno Guimaraes has personally told the club he wants to join Arsenal. Arsenal previously tabled two bids, at 55 million and 65 million pounds, both rejected. Now they're preparing to raise their offer to between 65 and 90 million pounds. Manager Arteta views him as the midfielder he most wants this summer. The question is whether Newcastle can retain a captain who is determined to leave.
