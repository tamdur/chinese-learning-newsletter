歐洲央行：物價還會高一陣子

來源：Reuters

歐洲中央銀行（簡稱歐洲央行）總裁拉加德說，歐元區的物價在接下來幾季還會很高。九月的通膨（物價上漲）是3.8%，主要是因為伊朗戰爭讓能源變貴。

今年歐洲央行已經升息兩次。拉加德說，目前還沒看到薪水跟著大漲，但她不能保證十二月一定會再升息。

這代表利率可能繼續往上走，借錢的人要多留意。

European Central Bank (ECB) President Christine Lagarde says prices in the eurozone will stay high for several more quarters. Inflation (the rise in prices) was 3.8% in September, mainly because the Iran war has made energy more expensive. The ECB has already raised interest rates twice this year. Lagarde says wages have not yet risen sharply, but she cannot promise there will be no rate hike in December. That means interest rates may keep climbing, so people who borrow money should pay close attention.

教宗到阿根廷：人比經濟重要

來源：AP / CNN

第一位美國籍教宗良十四世，八日抵達阿根廷。這是1987年以來，第一次有教宗到訪。就連來自阿根廷的前教宗方濟各，當教宗後也沒回去過。

教宗見了總統米雷伊。米雷伊大砍政府花費，教宗卻說，人應該比經濟更重要，也請大家保護民主。

九日是國定假日，教宗在首都布宜諾斯艾利斯為家庭舉行大型彌撒（天主教的禮拜）。

Pope Leo XIV, the first American-born pope, arrived in Argentina on the 8th. It is the first papal visit to the country since 1987. Even Pope Francis, who is from Argentina, has never returned there since becoming pope.

The pope met with President Milei. Milei has been slashing government spending, but the pope said that people should matter more than the economy. He also asked everyone to protect democracy.

The 9th is a national holiday, and the pope will hold a large Mass for families in the capital, Buenos Aires.

中國再停對美加稅兩個月

來源：Bloomberg / Digitimes

中國九日宣布，原本暫停對美國商品多收的24%關稅（買外國貨要繳的稅），會再停兩個月，到明年一月十日。這項決定十日開始生效。

不過，美國商品還是要繳10%的關稅。中國也會繼續暫停向停在中國港口的美國船收特別費用。

美中九月就說好延長貿易休戰，這次是中國正式照做。兩國能不能在一月前談出更大的協議，還不知道。

China announced on the 9th that it will extend its suspension of the additional 24% tariff on U.S. goods (the extra tax Chinese buyers pay on imported products) by two more months, to January 10 of next year. The decision takes effect on the 10th. U.S. goods will still face a 10% tariff. China will also keep suspending the special fees it charges U.S. ships docked at Chinese ports. In September, the two countries agreed to extend their trade truce, and this time China is making that formal. It is still unclear whether the two sides can reach a bigger deal before January.

熊隊最後一秒險勝海盜

來源：NBC News

芝加哥熊隊八日晚上在主場，以27比24打敗坦帕灣海盜隊，拿下四連勝，戰績變成7勝2敗。

這場贏得很驚險。四分衛威廉斯被抄截四次，全隊一共失誤五次。海盜隊還在最後不到三分鐘時追平比分。

還好威廉斯傳出一記38碼長傳，讓踢球員桑托斯在時間到時踢進致勝射門。

The Bears edged the Tampa Bay Buccaneers 27-24 at home on the night of the 8th, extending their winning streak to four games and improving their record to 7-2. It was a nail-biter. Quarterback Williams was intercepted four times, and the team committed five turnovers in total. With less than three minutes left, the Buccaneers tied the score. Fortunately, Williams threw a 38-yard pass that set up kicker Santos to kick the winning field goal as time expired.
