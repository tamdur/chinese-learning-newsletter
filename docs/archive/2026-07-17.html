中國Kimi K3登場，開源模型挑戰美國

來源：VentureBeat

中國的月之暗面公司（Moonshot AI）在七月十六日推出新的人工智慧模型Kimi K3。它有二點八兆個參數，是目前世界上最大的開源模型，任何人都能免費下載來用。

在一項測驗真實工作能力的比賽中，Kimi K3排名第三，只輸給兩個美國模型，還贏過Claude Opus 4.8。很多工程師寫程式時也最喜歡用它。這讓大家發現，中國追上美國的速度比想像中還要快。

China's Moonshot AI released a new artificial intelligence model called Kimi K3 on July 16. With 2.8 trillion parameters, it is currently the world's largest open-source model, available for anyone to download and use for free. In a benchmark testing real-world capabilities, Kimi K3 ranked third, losing only to two American models while outperforming Claude Opus 4.8. Many engineers also prefer to use it when writing code. This suggests that China is catching up to the United States faster than previously thought.

剛果伊波拉疫情，擴散速度史上最快

來源：France24

世界衛生組織警告，非洲剛果民主共和國的伊波拉（一種很危險的傳染病）疫情，已經超過兩千人確診、七百五十人死亡。

這次疫情只花四十天就到達一千人，比二○一八年那次的兩百三十五天快很多，是有紀錄以來擴散最快的一次，也是史上第三大。醫生已經在七月二日開始試驗一種新藥，希望能救更多人。

The World Health Organization is warning that the Ebola outbreak in the Democratic Republic of Congo has already exceeded 2,000 confirmed cases and 750 deaths. This outbreak reached 1,000 cases in just forty days—much faster than the 235 days it took in 2018—making it the fastest-spreading on record and the third-largest in history. Doctors began testing a new drug on July 2nd, hoping to save more lives.

美國縮短學生與記者簽證年限

來源：Al Jazeera

美國國土安全部公布新規定，改掉過去讓學生和記者「只要還在唸書或工作就能一直留下」的做法。

以後留學生和交流學者最多只能待四年，記者則是九十到兩百四十天，中國記者只有九十天。這條規定今天正式公告，九月十五日開始實施。幾十萬名國際學生如果想繼續留下，就必須重新申請。

The US Department of Homeland Security has announced new regulations that end the previous practice allowing students and journalists to stay indefinitely "as long as they were still studying or working." Going forward, international students and exchange scholars can remain for a maximum of four years, while journalists face limits of 90 to 240 days—with Chinese journalists restricted to just 90 days. The regulation was officially announced today and takes effect on September 15th. Hundreds of thousands of international students who wish to continue their stay will need to reapply.

加拿大野火煙霧，芝加哥關閉海灘泳池

來源：Chicago Sun-Times

加拿大有超過八百處野火，煙霧一路飄到芝加哥，讓星期四的空氣品質指數飆到七百五十三，是全世界大城市裡最糟的。

市政府因此關閉所有海灘和五十座戶外泳池，這是新冠疫情以來第一次。芝加哥火焰隊的比賽也延期，球星萊萬多夫斯基（Lewandowski）沒能上場。這也顯示氣候變暖後，加拿大的煙每年夏天都吹進美國中西部。

Canada has over 800 wildfires, with smoke drifting all the way to Chicago. On Thursday, the air quality index spiked to 753, the worst among major cities in the world. The city government closed all beaches and 50 outdoor pools in response—the first such closure since the COVID pandemic. The Chicago Flames game was also postponed, with star player Lewandowski unable to play. This underscores how, in an era of climate warming, Canadian smoke sweeps into America's Midwest every summer.

熊隊備戰新球季，盼避免開季慢熱

來源：Bleacher Nation

芝加哥熊隊的老將將在七月二十八日到哈拉斯球場報到，開始季前訓練。

今年的焦點包括左邊鋒特拉皮洛（Trapilo）的膝蓋傷勢恢復情況、球隊可能找回老將克隆尼（Clowney），還有歐鄧澤（Odunze）在摩爾離隊後升為頭號接球員。總教練班強森最想解決的，就是熊隊這幾年老是開季打得慢熱的毛病。

Chicago Bears veterans will report to Halas Hall on July 28 to begin preseason training. This year's main storylines include left tackle Trapilo's knee injury recovery, the possibility of re-signing veteran Clowney, and Odunze taking over as the primary receiver with Moore's departure. Head coach Ben Johnson's top priority is fixing the Bears' recurring problem of slow season starts in recent years.

兵工廠季前熱身賽開踢

來源：Yahoo Sports

兵工廠線——阿森納（Arsenal）宣布二○二六到二七年球季的第一場季前熱身賽，將在七月十七日今天，於梅多公園球場對上博勒姆伍德隊，正式展開新球季的準備。

今年夏天球隊大多會留在歐洲訓練，因為很多球員剛打完世界盃，這樣可以減少他們來回奔波的時間。

Arsenal beat — Arsenal has announced its first pre-season friendly of the 2026-27 season, taking place today, July 17th, at Meadow Park against Boreham Wood, marking the official start of preparations for the new campaign. Most of the squad will base itself in Europe for summer training, given that many players have just completed World Cup commitments, which reduces the demands of traveling back and forth.
