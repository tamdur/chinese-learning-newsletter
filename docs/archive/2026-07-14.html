人工智慧股大跌，油價也上漲

來源：Yahoo Finance

星期二全球股市大跌。很多人開始擔心，投入人工智慧（一種聰明的電腦技術）的大錢，不一定能賺得回來。晶片（電腦裡最重要的零件）公司輝達、超微和美光都跌了不少，那斯達克指數一天就跌超過百分之三。

同一時間，美國和伊朗又打起來，油價上漲，讓大家更害怕。以前市場總是不理會這些警告，這一次終於怕了。

On Tuesday, global stock markets took a beating. Many investors grew worried that the billions invested in artificial intelligence—a kind of intelligent computer technology—might not actually pay off. Chip makers, which produce computers' most vital components, all fell sharply: Nvidia, AMD, and Micron all slid significantly, and the Nasdaq dropped more than 3% in a single day. Meanwhile, the US and Iran flared up in conflict again, oil prices climbed, and fear spread. Markets have historically brushed off these warnings, but this time they finally got scared.

來源：NPR

美國和伊朗的停火破局，戰爭又開始了。美國派軍艦封鎖荷莫茲海峽（中東一條很重要的海上通道），不讓伊朗的船進出。

川普還說，其他國家的船要通過，得付貨物價值百分之二十的費用。聯合國反對，說海峽是大家共用的，不能隨便收錢。

The ceasefire between the US and Iran has collapsed, and fighting has resumed. The United States has deployed warships to blockade the Strait of Hormuz, a vital shipping channel in the Middle East, preventing Iranian vessels from entering or leaving. Trump has also stated that ships from other nations wishing to transit must pay a fee equivalent to twenty percent of their cargo's value. The United Nations has objected, saying the strait is a shared waterway that cannot be used for arbitrary charges.

大腦做決定比想像中更早

來源：ScienceDaily

美國伊利諾大學研究發現，大腦做決定的時間，比科學家以前想的還要早。決定的訊號很早就出現在負責感覺的腦區。

以前大家以為，決定是在後面才做好，再傳回來。這個新發現推翻了舊想法，也提醒我們，現在的人工智慧設計可能少了一個重要的環節。

Researchers at the University of Illinois have found that the brain makes decisions much earlier than scientists previously believed. Decision signals appear surprisingly early in brain regions responsible for sensory processing.

The prevailing view had held that decisions were finalized in deeper brain structures and then transmitted back to sensory areas. This new discovery overturns that understanding and suggests that current artificial intelligence models may be missing a crucial component.

芝加哥住房補助恐被砍

來源：Block Club Chicago

美國聯邦政府打算減少「長期支持性住房」的經費。這個計畫幫沒有家的人租房子，還提供照顧服務。

芝加哥有超過三千位居民，可能因此失去住處，重新流落街頭。伊利諾州一共有五千多人受影響，四千萬美元的補助有危險。地方單位說，這是幫助街友最有效的方法。

The U.S. federal government plans to cut funding for "long-term supportive housing," a program designed to help homeless people secure rental housing and receive supportive services. The potential cuts could put more than 3,000 Chicago residents back on the streets. Statewide, Illinois would see over 5,000 people affected, with $40 million in aid at risk. Local officials say this program represents the most effective way to help the homeless.
