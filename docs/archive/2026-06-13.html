龍捲風侵襲芝加哥一帶　數十萬戶停電

來源：National Weather Service

六月十一日晚上，至少七個龍捲風（很強的旋轉大風）掃過伊利諾州北部和印第安納州。

這是芝加哥今年最嚴重的風災。數十萬戶停電，機場許多班機取消。在丹維爾，一所小學的屋頂被吹到馬路上。所幸沒有人死亡，工人正在搶修電力。

On the evening of June 11th, at least seven tornadoes swept through northern Illinois and Indiana. This was Chicago's most severe wind disaster of the year. Hundreds of thousands of households lost power, and many flights were canceled at the airport. In Danville, the roof of an elementary school was blown onto the road. Fortunately, there were no deaths, and workers are rushing to restore power.

歐洲央行兩年多來首次升息

來源：European Central Bank

六月十一日，歐洲央行把利率（借錢要付的利息）調高到百分之二點二五。

這是二○二三年以來第一次升息，之前一直在降息。原因是伊朗戰事影響石油運送，能源變貴，物價又開始上漲，央行只好出手。

On June 11th, the European Central Bank raised interest rates to 2.25%. This is the first rate increase since 2023, after a prolonged period of rate cuts. The move comes as conflict in Iran disrupts oil shipping, driving up energy costs and triggering renewed inflation pressures, forcing the central bank to act.

芝加哥退休金缺口三百六十億　市議會逼問市長

來源：Chicago Sun-Times

芝加哥欠下的退休金（付給退休員工的錢）缺口已經到三百六十億美元。

市長強森想把今年要付的一大筆錢分成兩半付，說是房屋稅收得慢。市議會不滿，對手門多薩也拿這件事當選舉話題。

Chicago's pension shortfall—the gap between what the city owes retirees and what it has set aside—has ballooned to $36 billion. Mayor Johnson wants to split this year's large payment into two installments, citing slower-than-expected property tax revenue. The city council is unhappy with the plan, and his opponent Mendoza has seized on the issue as a campaign talking point.

美最高法院將裁定移民執法大案

來源：Just Security

美國最高法院預計在六月底前，對一件移民大案做出裁定。

這個案子要決定總統在遣返（把人送回原本國家）移民這件事上，權力到底有多大。這是政府大規模遣返計畫的法律根據，關係到幾百萬移民的命運。

The US Supreme Court is expected to issue a ruling by the end of June on a major immigration case. The case will decide how much power the president has in carrying out deportations—sending immigrants back to their home countries. This is the legal foundation for the government's large-scale deportation plans, affecting the fate of millions of immigrants.

密西根美式足球招兵大豐收

來源：Sports Illustrated

密西根大學的美式足球隊被一位知名分析師選為六月的「招生贏家」。

在教練摩爾帶領下，球隊二○二六年的新生已經有二十名球員口頭答應加入。六月六日還有五星好手歐霍來參觀，球隊氣勢正旺。

The University of Michigan football team has been named June's "recruiting winner" by a prominent analyst. Under coach Moore's leadership, the team has secured verbal commitments from twenty players for the 2026 freshman class. On June 6th, five-star prospect Ojo visited campus, adding to the team's momentum.

兵工廠夏季補強　鎖定中場大將

來源：JustArsenal

兵工廠線——在歐冠決賽輸給巴黎聖日耳曼之後，兵工廠（阿森納隊）把目光轉向轉會市場。

教練阿爾特塔想補強中場，球隊已經在和羅馬隊的法國球員柯內談轉會，身價大約四千三百萬英鎊。夏天的補強才剛開始。

The Arsenal beat — after losing the Champions League final to Paris Saint-Germain, Arsenal has turned its attention to the transfer market. Manager Arteta wants to strengthen the midfield, and the club is already in talks with Roma over French midfielder Koné, valued at around £43 million. The summer reinforcement campaign is just getting started.
