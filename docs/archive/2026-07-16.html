川普黃金時段演講　談選舉安全

來源：Al Jazeera

美國總統川普將在星期四晚上發表全國電視演講，公布剛解密的情報。他說有外國想干擾美國選舉，還說投票機器有漏洞。白宮把這叫做「選舉安全」，因為年底就要期中選舉了。

不過，許多專家提醒，這些說法早在幾年前就被證明不是真的，擔心會讓人民對投票失去信心。

President Trump will deliver a nationwide television address Thursday evening to disclose recently declassified intelligence. He claims foreign countries are attempting to interfere with US elections and that voting machines have security vulnerabilities. The White House describes this initiative as addressing "election security" with midterm elections approaching at year's end.

However, many experts warn that these claims were debunked years ago and worry the statements could undermine public confidence in voting.

手機就能跑的大型AI　新模型登場

來源：Hacker News

一家叫 PrismML 的公司推出新的人工智慧模型 Bonsai，大小約二百七十億個參數，卻能直接在手機上運作，不用連網路或大型電腦。在 iPhone 上，它每秒可以產生約十一個字。

這代表強大的 AI 開始走向「隨身」和「離線」，更能保護個人隱私。今天它登上科技論壇的熱門第一名。

A startup called PrismML has unveiled an AI model named Bonsai that runs directly on your phone with roughly 27 billion parameters—no internet connection or powerful computer needed. On an iPhone, it can generate about 11 characters per second.

This marks a turning point: powerful AI is becoming portable and offline-enabled, with better privacy protection built in. The model topped tech forums' trending lists today.

直布羅陀拆邊界圍籬　併入申根區

來源：Euronews

位在西班牙南邊的英國屬地直布羅陀，在七月十五日半夜拆掉了和西班牙之間的邊界圍籬（就是隔開兩地的鐵欄杆）。這是根據歐盟和英國新簽的條約，讓直布羅陀加入「申根區」，人們往來不用再蓋護照章。

以後在機場和港口，會由英國和西班牙的人員一起查驗。這也解決了英國脫離歐盟後，留下的一個大難題。

Gibraltar, a British territory south of Spain, dismantled the border fence between itself and Spain at midnight on July 15th—the metal barrier that had separated the two territories. This follows a new treaty signed between the EU and the UK, allowing Gibraltar to join the "Schengen Area," eliminating the need for passport stamps when traveling between the two.

Going forward, immigration checks at airports and ports will be conducted jointly by British and Spanish personnel. The move also resolves a major problem that emerged following Britain's exit from the European Union.

芝加哥撥四億美元　建球場周邊設施

來源：Block Club Chicago

芝加哥市議會通過，用四億二千四百萬美元的公共經費，替「The 78」新開發區蓋停車場和道路。這裡未來是芝加哥火焰足球隊的新球場。其中約二億美元，要蓋一座能停一千二百輛車的停車場。

有居民不滿，說球場是私人的，市府卻拿大筆公款幫忙，尤其現在市府預算還有缺口。

Chicago's City Council has approved spending $424 million in public funds to build parking facilities and roads for the 'The 78' development, which will host the Chicago Fire's new soccer stadium. About $200 million of that will fund a parking garage capable of holding 1,200 vehicles.

The move has drawn criticism from residents, who argue that the stadium is privately owned but the city is nonetheless investing substantial public money—a particularly contentious issue given the city's current budget shortfalls.

小熊克羅阿姆斯壯　加入二十／二十俱樂部

來源：MLB.com Cubs

芝加哥小熊隊的中外野手克羅阿姆斯壯，在明星賽前就打出二十支全壘打，加上盜壘二十次，成為今年第一位加入「二十／二十俱樂部」的球員。

他今年二十四歲，是隊史上少數連續兩年做到的人。他的打擊率、盜壘都是隊上第一，還連續第二年入選明星賽，帶著小熊隊爭取季後賽門票。

Chicago Cubs center fielder Crow-Armstrong hit 20 home runs and stole 20 bases before the All-Star Game, becoming the first player to join the '20/20 club' this season.

At 24 years old, he is among the rare few in franchise history to achieve this feat in consecutive years. Leading the team in both batting average and stolen bases, he has made the All-Star Game for the second year in a row, helping the Cubs pursue a playoff berth.

兵工廠鎖定努薩　備妥四千萬歐元

來源：FootballTransfers.com

兵工廠線——阿森納（球迷叫它兵工廠）準備出價約四千萬歐元，想簽下德國萊比錫紅牛隊的邊鋒努薩。努薩是挪威人，剛在世界盃幫國家隊踢進八強，表現亮眼。

不過萊比錫想要六千萬歐元以上，所以還談不成。特羅薩德離隊後，兵工廠左路缺人，努薩正好可以補上這個空缺。

Arsenal Beat — Arsenal is preparing a bid of around €40 million to sign winger Nusa from RB Leipzig. Nusa is Norwegian and recently impressed at the World Cup, helping his national team reach the quarter-finals.

However, Leipzig is demanding €60 million or more, so a deal hasn't been finalized. After Trossard's departure, Arsenal needs cover on the left wing, and Nusa could fill that gap.
