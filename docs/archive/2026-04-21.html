美伊停火到期，核子談判陷入僵局

來源：NPR / CNN / Al Jazeera

美國和伊朗的兩週停火在今天到期，但雙方還沒有達成新的協議。美國要求伊朗停止提煉鈾（製造核武器的材料）二十年，伊朗只願意停五年。

川普總統說延長停火「很不可能」。全世界都在看荷乘玆海峽（Strait of Hormuz）會不會再被封鎖，因為全球大量石油都從那裡經過。

The two-week ceasefire between the United States and Iran expired today, but the two sides have failed to reach a new agreement. The US demands that Iran halt uranium enrichment—a key material for nuclear weapons—for twenty years, while Iran is willing to agree only to a five-year freeze. President Trump said extending the ceasefire was "highly unlikely." The world is watching to see whether the Strait of Hormuz will be blocked again, since a vast portion of global oil passes through it.

土耳其連續校園槍擊案，總統宣布加強槍枝管制

來源：CBS News / Al Jazeera

上週土耳其在兩天內發生兩起校園槍擊案。其中最嚴重的一起在卡赫拉曼馬拉什省，一名十四歲學生用父親的槍在學校開火，造成十人死亡、十二人受傷，是土耳其史上最慘重的校園槍擊事件。

總統乘爾多安週一宣布將加強槍枝管制，並加重處罰讓孩子拿到槍的家長。

Turkey experienced two school shootings within a single week. The most severe occurred in Kahramanmaraş Province, where a fourteen-year-old student opened fire at his school using his father's gun, killing ten people and injuring twelve — the deadliest school shooting in Turkish history. On Monday, President Erdoğan announced plans to strengthen gun control measures and impose harsher penalties on parents who allow their children access to firearms.

NVIDIA 發布 Nemotron 3 開放 AI 模型系列

來源：NVIDIA Newsroom

NVIDIA 發布了 Nemotron 3，這是一組專門為「代理式 AI」（讓 AI 自己完成多步任務）設計的開放模型。系列有三種大小：Nano、Super 和 Ultra。

其中 Super 版本有一千兩百億個參數，處理速度比上一代快五倍。Cursor、Perplexity 等公司已經開始使用。NVIDIA 想把硬體的優勢延伸到軟體生態系統。

NVIDIA has released Nemotron 3, a suite of open models specifically designed for "agentic AI"—enabling AI systems to autonomously complete multi-step tasks. The series comes in three sizes: Nano, Super, and Ultra. The Super version, with 120 billion parameters, processes information five times faster than its predecessor. Companies like Cursor and Perplexity are already adopting the technology. NVIDIA aims to extend its hardware advantages into the software ecosystem.

芝加哥公車局撤換保全，改派警察巡邏

來源：Chicago Sun-Times

芝加哥公車局（CTA）終止了和保全公司的合約，裁掉一百五十九名沒有帶槍的保全人員。這筆錢會改用來請更多警察、警犬小組和危機處理人員。

原因是公車和地鐵上的暴力攻擊達到二十四年來的最高紀錄，聯邦政府威脅要削減 CTA 的經費。

The Chicago Transit Authority (CTA) has terminated its contract with a security firm, laying off 159 unarmed security officers. The savings will be redirected toward hiring more police officers, K-9 units, and crisis response personnel. The shift comes as violent attacks on buses and trains have reached a 24-year high, and the federal government has threatened to cut CTA funding.

兵工廠女隊奪下首屆 FIFA 女子冠軍盃

來源：Arsenal.com / FIFA.com

兵工廠線——兵工廠女隊在 FIFA 女子冠軍盃決賽中，以三比二擊敗巴西的 Corinthians，成為這項新賽事的第一個冠軍。

比賽打到延長賽才分出勝負。替補上場的 Caitlin Foord 在第一百零四分鐘踢進致勝球，讓兵工廠永遠刻在獎盃上。

Arsenal beat Corinthians of Brazil 3-2 in the FIFA Women's Club World Cup final, becoming the inaugural champions of the new tournament. The match went to extra time before a winner emerged. Substitute Caitlin Foord scored the decisive goal in the 104th minute, etching Arsenal's name onto the trophy forever.
