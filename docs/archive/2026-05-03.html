中國智譜公司用華為晶片訓練出新 AI 模型

來源：LLM Stats

中國的智譜 AI 公司發布了新模型 GLM-4.7，這是第一個完全用華為「昇騰」晶片訓練的頂級模型，不需要任何美國 NVIDIA 的晶片。

這代表中國自己的晶片技術已經能做出世界級的 AI，美國的出口管制（不讓賣高級晶片給中國）的效果正在被打上問號。

China's Zhipu AI company has released a new model called GLM-4.7, the first top-tier model trained entirely on Huawei's "Ascend" chips, without requiring any American NVIDIA hardware.

This demonstrates that China's own chip technology is now capable of producing world-class AI, raising questions about the effectiveness of US export controls that restrict the sale of advanced chips to China.

川普拒絕伊朗十四點停火方案

來源：NPR

伊朗透過巴基斯坦提出了一份十四點停火方案，要求美軍撤離、解除制裁（處罰）、並在三十天內結束戰爭。

川普總統說他「無法想像這會被接受」，並警告空襲可能恢復。同時，伊朗正在推動限制荷莫茲海峽（全球石油運輸要道）通行的法律，和平希望更加渺茫。

Iran submitted a fourteen-point ceasefire proposal through Pakistan, demanding the withdrawal of US troops, the lifting of sanctions, and an end to the war within thirty days.

President Trump said he "couldn't imagine this being accepted" and warned that airstrikes could resume. Meanwhile, Iran is pushing legislation to restrict passage through the Strait of Hormuz—the world's critical oil shipping route—making prospects for peace even more remote.

美國下令從德國撤走五千名駐軍

來源：NPR

美國國防部長 Hegseth 下令在六到十二個月內從德國撤回約五千名美軍。這是因為德國總理 Merz 公開批評美國在伊朗戰爭中的做法。

川普暗示未來還會撤更多，西班牙和義大利可能是下一個。歐洲盟友對美國的信任正在動搖。

U.S. Defense Secretary Hegseth has ordered the withdrawal of approximately 5,000 American troops from Germany within six to twelve months. The move follows German Chancellor Merz's public criticism of U.S. actions in the Iran conflict.

Trump has hinted at further withdrawals to come, with Spain and Italy potentially next in line. European allies' confidence in the United States is beginning to falter.

西北大學用 3D 列印造出能和腦細胞溝通的人工神經

來源：ScienceDaily

西北大學的工程師用 3D 列印技術做出了人工神經元，可以發出電訊號讓真正的腦細胞回應。研究發表在《自然：奈米技術》期刊。

這些人工神經比傳統的金屬電極更柔軟，也更像真的神經，未來有可能用來治療腦部疾病或幫助失去聽力和視力的人。

Engineers at Northwestern University used 3D printing technology to create artificial neurons that can emit electrical signals to trigger responses from real brain cells. The research was published in the journal Nature Nanotechnology.

These artificial neurons are softer than traditional metal electrodes and more closely resemble actual nerve tissue. In the future, they could potentially be used to treat brain diseases or help restore hearing and vision to those who have lost it.

The Cubs have won fourteen of their last eighteen games, running a hot streak with a season record of 21 wins and 12 losses.
