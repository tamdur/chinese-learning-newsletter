Google 明天開大會，新 Gemini 模型準備登場

來源：TechTimes / Android Authority

Google 每年最大的開發者大會 I/O 明天在加州開幕，預計會發表新一代 Gemini 人工智慧模型。消息指出，新模型的能力大約跟 OpenAI 的 GPT-5.5 同一等級，但還比不上 Anthropic 的 Mythos。

現在 AI 三大公司的競爭越來越快。OpenAI 上週才把舊的 GPT-4 系列全部停用，Google 馬上就端出新菜。這場 AI 大戰還沒有要停的意思。

Google's biggest annual developer conference, I/O, opens tomorrow in California with an expected unveiling of the next-generation Gemini artificial intelligence model. Sources indicate the new model's capabilities are roughly on par with OpenAI's GPT-5.5, but still fall short of Anthropic's Mythos. The competition among the three AI giants is accelerating. OpenAI only last week discontinued its entire GPT-4 series, and Google is already rolling out new offerings. This AI arms race shows no signs of slowing down.

油價飆到每桶 107 美元，荷莫茲海峽危機加深

來源：World Bank / CNBC

國際油價今天漲到每桶 107.72 美元，是 2022 年以來最高。過去一個月漲了 23%，原因是中東戰爭讓荷莫茲海峽（連接波斯灣的重要水道）受到嚴重干擾。

全世界大約兩成的石油都要經過這條海峽，目前每天少了一千零五十萬桶的供應。油價持續上漲會讓各國的物價跟著變貴。

International oil prices surged to $107.72 per barrel today, the highest level since 2022. Over the past month, prices have climbed 23%, driven by Middle East conflict that has severely disrupted the Strait of Hormuz, the critical waterway connecting the Persian Gulf. Roughly one-fifth of the world's oil passes through this strait, and current supply shortfalls amount to 10.5 million barrels per day. Sustained oil price increases will push up inflation across countries worldwide.

以色列擊殺哈瑪斯軍事首領哈達德

來源：NPR / CBC

以色列軍方宣布，已在加薩的空襲（從空中攻擊）中擊殺了哈瑪斯軍事部門的最高領導人哈達德。他被稱為「幽靈」，是 2023 年十月七日攻擊事件的主要策劃者之一。

哈達德是自去年十月停火以來，被以色列擊殺的最高級別哈瑪斯人物。這次行動也造成六名平民死亡，包括他的妻子和女兒。

Israel's military announced that it has killed Yahya Sinwar, the top leader of Hamas's military wing, in an airstrike in Gaza. Known as "the Ghost," Sinwar was one of the principal architects of the October 7, 2023 attacks. He is the highest-ranking Hamas figure killed by Israel since the ceasefire began last October. The operation also resulted in six civilian deaths, including his wife and daughter.

共和黨參議員擋下川普選舉法案

來源：Notus / NBC News

美國參議院的共和黨議員擋下了川普力推的「拯救美國法案」。這個法案要求投票時必須出示公民身分證明，還要取消郵寄投票。

四名共和黨議員跟民主黨站在一起投下反對票，法案連 50 票都拿不到。這是川普第二任期內少見的黨內反對，顯示他在國會的影響力有限。

Republican senators in the U.S. Senate blocked Trump's heavily promoted "Save America Act." The bill would have required voters to present citizenship identification and eliminated mail-in voting. Four Republican senators voted against it alongside Democrats, and the bill fell short of the 50 votes needed to pass. This represents a rare instance of intra-party opposition during Trump's second term, demonstrating his limited influence in Congress.

熊隊新賽季賽程出爐，全聯盟最難

來源：CBS Sports / ESPN

NFL 公布了 2026 年的賽程，芝加哥熊隊拿到全聯盟最難的賽程。對手上季合計勝率高達 .550，是 32 支球隊裡最高的。

其中有八場要對上去年的季後賽球隊，包括感恩節打獅隊、聖誕節打包裝工隊。熊隊球迷要有心理準備，這一季不好打。

The NFL released its 2026 schedule, and the Chicago Bears drew the toughest slate in the league. Their opponents combined for a .550 winning percentage last season — the highest among all 32 teams. Eight of those matchups are against teams that made the playoffs last year, including Thanksgiving against the Lions and Christmas against the Packers. Bears fans should brace themselves: it's going to be a rough season.
