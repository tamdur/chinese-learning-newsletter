OpenAI淘汰舊模型，全面改用GPT-5世代

來源：OpenAI

OpenAI宣布把GPT-4o、GPT-4.1和o4-mini從ChatGPT全部下架。原因是每天只剩0.1%的人還在用舊版，大家都已經轉到新的GPT-5系列了。

這些模型才推出一年多就被淘汰（不再使用），顯示AI進步的速度非常快。不過開發者仍然可以透過API使用舊模型。

OpenAI announced that it is removing GPT-4o, GPT-4.1, and o4-mini from ChatGPT entirely, transitioning fully to the GPT-5 generation. The reason is that only 0.1% of users still use the older versions daily — everyone else has already switched to the new GPT-5 series. These models became obsolete just one year after their release, illustrating how rapidly AI progress is advancing. However, developers can still access the older models through the API.

川習會結束，雙方說法不一致

來源：Al Jazeera

川普和習近平在北京的兩天會議結束了，但兩邊對結果的說法完全不同。川普說達成了「很棒的貿易協議」，北京卻說沒有正式協議，還警告美國不要碰台灣問題。

具體成果只有中國同意買200架Boeing飛機，比川普要求的500架少很多。全球股市因為失望而下跌。

Trump and Xi Jinping's two-day meeting in Beijing has ended, but the two sides are giving completely different accounts of the results. Trump said they reached a "tremendous trade deal," while Beijing said there was no formal agreement and warned the US not to touch the Taiwan issue. The only concrete outcome is China's agreement to buy 200 Boeing aircraft, far fewer than the 500 planes Trump had requested. Global stock markets fell due to disappointment.

停火失敗後，俄羅斯發動戰爭以來最猛空襲

來源：The Washington Post

美國安排的三天停火結束後，俄羅斯在30小時內發射了731枚飛彈和無人機，是開戰以來最大規模的空襲。

其中一次攻擊打中了基輔的公寓大樓，造成至少24人死亡。外界擔心停火反而給了俄國重新準備的時間。

After the U.S.-brokered three-day ceasefire collapsed, Russia launched its largest-scale aerial assault since the war began, firing 731 missiles and drones within a 30-hour window. One strike hit an apartment building in Kyiv, killing at least 24 people. There are concerns that the ceasefire may have simply given Russia time to regroup and prepare.

Boston Dynamics機器狗裝上Google大腦

來源：Google DeepMind

Boston Dynamics和Google DeepMind合作，把Gemini Robotics這個AI模型裝進了他們的四腳機器狗Spot裡面。

以前Spot只能走固定路線，現在它可以聽懂人說的話、自己判斷周圍環境，像是找到地上的水或危險物品。這是目前最大規模的AI加工業機器人合作。

Boston Dynamics and Google DeepMind have partnered to install Gemini Robotics, an AI model, into Spot, their four-legged robot dog. Previously, Spot could only navigate predetermined routes, but now it can understand human speech and make independent judgments about its surroundings — such as identifying water or hazards on the ground. This represents the largest-scale collaboration between AI and industrial robotics to date.

小乖打破低潮，城際大戰10比5大勝白襪

來源：BVM Sports

小熊隊在城際大戰（Crosstown Classic）以10比5打敗白襪隊，結束了連續五場打擊低潮。捕手Carson Kelly在第七局打出關鍵安打，全場送回4分打點。

小熊全隊打出14支安打，每個先發球員都有上壘。白襪主場湧入38,723名球迷，是今年白襪第一次滿場。

The Cubs broke out of a five-game offensive slump in the Crosstown Classic, defeating the White Sox 10-5. Catcher Carson Kelly delivered a key hit in the seventh inning, driving in 4 runs. The Cubs produced 14 hits overall, with every starting player reaching base. The White Sox drew 38,723 fans to their home stadium, marking their first sellout of the season.

兵工廠只差一勝，就能拿下22年來首座英超冠軍

來源：MSN Sports

兵工廠線——Arsenal目前以79分領先Manchester City 5分，只剩兩場比賽。下週一在主場對已降級的Burnley，只要贏球就可能封王。

如果成功，這將是Arsenal自2004年以來第一次拿到英超冠軍，結束22年的等待。球隊同時還在準備5月30日的歐冠決賽，有機會拿下雙冠王。

Arsenal currently leads Manchester City by 5 points with 79 points, with only two matches remaining. Next Monday at home against already-relegated Burnley, a victory could seal the title. If successful, this will be Arsenal's first Premier League championship since 2004, ending a 22-year wait. The team is simultaneously preparing for the European final on May 30th, with a chance to claim the double.
