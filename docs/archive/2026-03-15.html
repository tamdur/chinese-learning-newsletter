密西根大學絕殺三分球晉級十大聯盟冠軍賽

來源：ESPN

密西根大學昨天在十大聯盟錦標賽半決賽中，以 68-65 險勝威斯康辛。比賽最後只剩 0.4 秒，Lendeborg 投進了一記絕殺三分球，全場瘋狂！

Aday Mara 拿下 16 分、8 個籃板、5 次阻攻（就是把對方的球打掉）。威斯康辛的 Austin Rapp 下半場連續投進 5 個三分球，差點翻盤。密西根今天下午將對上 Purdue，爭奪冠軍。今年戰績 31 勝 2 敗，太猛了。

Michigan University hit a buzzer-beating three-pointer to advance to the Big Ten Championship game. In yesterday's Big Ten Tournament semifinal, Michigan narrowly defeated Wisconsin 68-65. With just 0.4 seconds remaining, Lendeborg buried a clutch three-pointer, sending the arena into a frenzy.

Aday Mara recorded 16 points, 8 rebounds, and 5 blocks. Wisconsin's Austin Rapp was on fire in the second half, draining 5 consecutive three-pointers and nearly mounting a comeback. Michigan will face Purdue this afternoon in the championship game. With a record of 31 wins and 2 losses, the team has been dominant this season.

Morgan Stanley 會議：AI 將消滅整個工作類別

來源：Fortune

在舊金山的 Morgan Stanley 科技大會上，OpenAI 的 Sam Altman 說，未來幾年內，一家公司可能只要一到五個人，其他工作都交給 AI。xAI 的共同創辦人 Jimmy Ba 更直接：他說 2026 年是「對人類未來最重要的一年」。

一份調查顯示，過去一年企業因為 AI 平均減少了 4% 的員工。像 Shopify 已經連續八到十季減少人力，但收入還在成長。大家一直問的問題是：「我們的小孩以後要做什麼？」

At Morgan Stanley's technology conference in San Francisco, Sam Altman from OpenAI stated that within the coming years, a company might only need one to five people, with AI handling all other work. Jimmy Ba, co-founder of xAI, was even more direct: he said 2026 is "the most important year for humanity's future."

A survey shows that companies reduced their workforce by an average of 4% over the past year due to AI. Companies like Shopify have cut headcount for eight to ten consecutive quarters while still growing revenue. The question everyone keeps asking is: "What will our children do in the future?"

中國通過第十五個五年計畫：科技自主是重點

來源：Reuters

中國全國人大上週通過了新的五年計畫（2026-2030）。GDP 成長目標是 4.5-5%，是歷來最低的。最大的重點是科技自主：晶片補助約 700 億美元，目標是 2030 年數位經濟佔 GDP 的 12.5%。

計畫裡直接提到要應對「危險的風暴」——明顯是在講美國的貿易壓力。中國在太陽能、風力發電、電動車電池都是全球第一，但分析師說，想靠消費來帶動經濟，從來沒有國家成功過。

China's national legislature passed a new five-year plan (2026-2030) last week. The GDP growth target is 4.5-5%, the lowest on record. The biggest priority is technological self-sufficiency: chip subsidies of roughly $70 billion, with a goal of the digital economy making up 12.5% of GDP by 2030.

The plan directly addresses the need to respond to "dangerous storms" — an obvious reference to U.S. trade pressure. China leads globally in solar energy, wind power, and EV battery production, but analysts say no country has ever successfully driven economic growth through consumption alone.

諾貝爾經濟學家 Christopher Sims 過世

來源：New York Times

諾貝爾經濟學獎得主 Christopher Sims 在 3 月 14 日過世，享年 83 歲。他是普林斯頓大學教授，2011 年和 Thomas Sargent 一起拿到諾貝爾獎。

他最有名的貢獻是發明了 VAR 模型——一種分析經濟數據因果關係的方法。現在全世界的中央銀行都在用這個工具。他也曾預測歐洲的債務危機，比大多數人都早。他說過：「學術界的經濟學家對政策的幫助沒有很大。」

Nobel Prize-winning economist Christopher Sims passed away on March 14th at the age of 83. He was a professor at Princeton University and shared the Nobel Prize in Economics in 2011 with Thomas Sargent.

His most famous contribution was inventing the VAR model — a method for analyzing causal relationships in economic data. Central banks around the world now use this tool. He also predicted Europe's debt crisis earlier than most people. He once said: "Academic economists don't provide much help with policy."

5500 年前的骨頭找到最古老的梅毒基因

來源：Science

科學家在哥倫比亞發現了一具 5500 年前的人骨，從裡面取出了梅毒（一種傳染病）細菌的 DNA。這比之前的紀錄早了 3000 年，是目前最古老的。

有趣的是，這個細菌跟現代的梅毒不一樣，是一個早期分支，大約在 13700 年前就分開了。這個發現支持了一個老理論：梅毒最早來自美洲，後來哥倫布的船隊把它帶到了歐洲。

Scientists in Colombia discovered human remains dating back 5,500 years containing DNA from the bacterium that causes syphilis, a contagious disease. This finding pushes back the earliest known record by 3,000 years.

Intriguingly, this bacterium differs from modern syphilis strains — it represents an early branch that diverged approximately 13,700 years ago. The discovery supports a longstanding theory: syphilis originated in the Americas and was later introduced to Europe by Columbus's expeditions.
