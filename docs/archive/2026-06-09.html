以色列和伊朗停手　川普推停火

來源：Arab News

打了一個星期後，以色列和伊朗這兩個國家都停手了。六月九日，美國總統川普說，兩邊都想馬上停火（不再打仗）。

他還打電話給以色列總理，叫他先別出手。不過伊朗說，如果以色列繼續打黎巴嫩南部，它就會再打回去。

After a week of fighting, Israel and Iran have both stood down. On June 9th, US President Trump said both sides want to stop the war immediately.

He also called the Israeli Prime Minister and told him to hold back for now. However, Iran said that if Israel continues fighting in southern Lebanon, it will strike back.

xAI推出寫程式和做影片新工具

來源：xAI

馬斯克的公司xAI在六月推出兩樣新東西。一個叫Grok Build，能幫人自動寫電腦程式；另一個能把圖片變成影片。

這個月的AI很熱鬧，谷歌、OpenAI和Anthropic都快要推出新的模型，大家比得很兇。

Elon Musk's company xAI unveiled two new tools in June. One is called Grok Build, which helps people automatically write computer code; the other can transform images into videos.

This month has been bustling with AI activity, with Google, OpenAI, and Anthropic all on the verge of releasing new models. The competition is fierce.

歐洲央行：美元還是老大

來源：European Central Bank

六月九日，歐洲央行（管歐元的銀行）發表一份報告。報告說，全世界各國存的錢裡，大約五成七是美元，歐元只有兩成。

雖然很多人都在說要少用美元，但這幾年下來，美元的地位幾乎沒有變弱。

On June 9th, the European Central Bank (the bank that manages the euro) released a report. The report said that of all the money countries around the world hold in reserves, roughly 57 percent is in US dollars, while the euro accounts for only 20 percent.

Although many people have been talking about using fewer US dollars, over the past several years the dollar's position has barely weakened at all.

美國籍教宗在西班牙國會演講

來源：NPR

第一位美國籍教宗良十四世，六月八日在西班牙國會演講。這是教宗第一次對西班牙的國會議員說話。

他說，社會需要「道德的更新」，也要尊重每一個人，包括移民。議員們站起來拍手了七分鐘。

Pope Leo XIV, the first American-born pontiff, delivered a speech to Spain's parliament on June 8th. This was the first time a pope has addressed Spanish lawmakers.

He said society needs a "moral renewal" and must respect every person, including immigrants. Lawmakers stood and applauded for seven minutes.

小熊投手受傷多　蕭歸隊

來源：Bleacher Nation

芝加哥小熊隊六月九日讓內野手蕭恩（Matt Shaw）傷好歸隊。他之前背痛，休息了兩個多星期。

不過球隊的投手傷了好幾個，這幾天還要去客場打洛磯隊。那個球場很好打出全壘打，小熊壓力不小。

The Chicago Cubs brought infielder Matt Shaw back from injury on June 9th. He had been sidelined for over two weeks with a back strain.

However, the team is dealing with multiple pitcher injuries heading into an away series against the Rockies. That ballpark is known for favoring home runs, putting considerable pressure on the Cubs.

兵工廠賽季後改組　辭退醫療主管

來源：Arsenal.com

兵工廠線——兵工廠（阿森納）在歐洲冠軍賽決賽輸給巴黎後，開始大改組。

六月八日，球隊辭退了醫療主管伊克巴醫生。這一季很多球員受傷，總教練阿爾特塔想把幕後團隊也一起換新。

Arsenal Beat — Arsenal begins a major overhaul following their loss to Paris in the European Champions League final.

On June 8th, the club dismissed Dr. Iqbal, the head of medical services. With many players sidelined by injuries this season, manager Arteta wants to refresh the backroom staff as well.
