民主黨拿下美國眾議院

來源：NBC News

美國期中選舉的結果出來了！民主黨預計多拿下大約29席，超過過半需要的218席，拿回眾議院的控制權，結束了共和黨四年來的多數。

這代表川普總統接下來兩年，要面對一個「分裂的政府」。民主黨可以調查政府官員，在政府怎麼花錢上也有很大的發言權，還能擋下總統想通過的法案。

The results of the U.S. midterm elections are in! The Democratic Party is projected to gain roughly 29 seats, well past the 218 needed for a majority, and will take back control of the House of Representatives, ending the Republican majority that has lasted four years. This means President Trump will face a "divided government" for the next two years. Democrats will be able to investigate government officials, will have a great deal of say over how the government spends money, and will be able to block bills the president wants to pass.

緬因州爆冷門，柯林斯落選

來源：ABC7 Chicago

緬因州民主黨人特洛伊·傑克森（Troy Jackson）打敗了共和黨參議員蘇珊·柯林斯（Susan Collins）。柯林斯當了快30年的參議員，是共和黨裡少數的溫和派。

這是今年選舉最大的冷門之一。昨晚好幾場關鍵的參議院選戰都倒向民主黨，但參議院最後由哪一黨控制，還要等計票結果。參議院很重要，因為法官和部長都要經過它同意。

Maine Democrat Troy Jackson defeated Republican Senator Susan Collins. Collins has served in the Senate for nearly 30 years and was one of the few moderates left in the Republican Party. It is one of the biggest upsets of this year's election. Several key Senate races went to the Democrats last night, but which party will control the Senate still depends on the final vote count. The Senate matters because judges and cabinet secretaries need its approval.

芝加哥首次選出教育委員會主席

來源：ABC7 Chicago

芝加哥人第一次自己投票，選出公立學校（CPS）教育委員會的主席：潔西卡·比格斯（Jessica Biggs）。過去二十多年，這個委員會都由市長決定。

比格斯是四位候選人中，唯一已經在委員會裡的人，得到庫克郡委員會主席普雷克溫克（Toni Preckwinkle）和工會的支持。這次委員會全部21個席位都由選民決定。新團隊一上任，就要面對學校缺錢的大問題。

For the first time, Chicagoans voted to choose the president of the Chicago Public Schools (CPS) Board of Education: Jessica Biggs. For more than two decades, the board was appointed by the mayor. Biggs, the only one of the four candidates who already sat on the board, won the backing of Cook County Board President Toni Preckwinkle and the labor unions. This time, voters decided all 21 seats on the board. The new team takes office facing a major problem: the schools are short of money.

史特拉頓可望當選伊州參議員

來源：ABC7 Chicago

伊利諾州副州長茱莉安娜·史特拉頓（Juliana Stratton）預計拿下伊州空出來的聯邦參議員席位。唐娜·米勒（Donna Miller）也贏得伊州第2選區的眾議員選舉。

芝加哥這次投票不太順利，有些投票所的選票用完了，也有投票機壞掉。不過大家投票很熱烈，到下午就有將近25萬名芝加哥人投了票。

Illinois Lieutenant Governor Juliana Stratton is expected to win the U.S. Senate seat being vacated in Illinois. Donna Miller also won the U.S. House election in Illinois's 2nd District. Chicago's voting did not go smoothly this time: some polling places ran out of ballots, and some voting machines broke down. Still, turnout was enthusiastic, and by the afternoon nearly 250,000 Chicagoans had cast their ballots.

密西根開季獲勝，星期五再戰康大

來源：mgoblue.com / Detroit News

去年的全國冠軍密西根大學，星期一在主場以84比68打敗奧克蘭大學，開始衛冕（保住冠軍）之路。蓋爾（Roddy Gayle Jr.）和麥肯尼（Trey McKenny）各拿17分，新生卡斯特洛（Quinn Costello）打大學第一場比賽就拿下13分。

密西根已經連續24年贏得開季第一戰。不過真正的考驗在星期五：他們要在波士頓對上排名第2的康乃狄克大學（UConn），重演去年的冠軍賽。上個月的熱身賽，密西根才以66比83輸給休士頓。

Michigan, last year's national champion, opened its title defense Monday with an 84–68 home win over Oakland. Roddy Gayle Jr. and Trey McKenny each scored 17 points, and freshman Quinn Costello added 13 in his first college game. Michigan has now won its season opener for 24 straight years.

The real test comes Friday. The Wolverines face No. 2 UConn in Boston, a rematch of last year's national title game. In an exhibition game last month, Michigan lost to Houston, 66–83.
