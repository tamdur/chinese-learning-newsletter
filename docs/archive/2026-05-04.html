五角大廈批准八家公司在機密網路部署 AI，Anthropic 被排除

來源：Breaking Defense

美國國防部首次正式允許商業 AI 進入軍方最高機密系統。獲准的八家包括 Google、Microsoft、OpenAI、NVIDIA、SpaceX 和 Oracle 等。

Anthropic 明顯被排除在外。五角大廈今年稍早將其列為「供應鏈風險」，原因是雙方在自主武器和監控（監視）政策上有分歧。這代表美軍正加速走向「AI 優先」的方向。

The U.S. Department of Defense has formally authorized commercial AI into its most sensitive military systems for the first time. The eight approved companies include Google, Microsoft, OpenAI, NVIDIA, SpaceX, and Oracle. Anthropic has been notably excluded. The Pentagon flagged the company earlier this year as a "supply chain risk" due to disagreements over autonomous weapons and surveillance policies. The move signals the U.S. military's accelerating pivot toward an "AI-first" strategy.

川普稱美伊談判「非常正面」，同時啟動護航行動

來源：CNN

美伊衝突第 65 天，川普改變語氣，形容與伊朗的對話「非常正面」，但仍拒絕伊朗的停火提案。

同時，美軍宣布「自由計畫」，從週一起派出驅逐艦、1.5 萬名軍人和超過 100 架飛機，護送商船通過荷莫茲海峽。自開戰以來，約兩萬名船員被困在那裡。

On day 65 of the U.S.-Iran conflict, Trump shifted his tone, describing talks with Iran as "very positive," while still rejecting Tehran's ceasefire proposal. Simultaneously, the U.S. military announced "Operation Freedom," deploying destroyers, 15,000 troops, and over 100 aircraft starting Monday to escort commercial vessels through the Strait of Hormuz. Since the conflict began, roughly 20,000 sailors have been stranded there.

美國油價飆到每加侖 4.39 美元，開戰後漲近五成

來源：NBC News

伊朗戰爭讓荷莫茲海峽關閉，美國汽油均價已漲到每加侖 4.39 美元，比二月開戰前漲了將近 50%。光是上週就跳了 30 美分。

加州最貴，平均要 6.06 美元。高油價也推高了運費、機票和食物價格，影響每個人的日常生活。

War with Iran has closed the Strait of Hormuz, pushing the U.S. average gasoline price to $4.39 per gallon—nearly 50% higher than before fighting erupted in February. Last week alone saw a 30-cent jump. California tops the nation at $6.06 per gallon on average. The spike has rippled through shipping costs, airfares, and food prices, touching nearly every aspect of daily life.

SpaceX-xAI 合併後出手，六百億美元買下 Cursor

來源：TechCrunch

Elon Musk 今年二月把 SpaceX 和 xAI 合併，市值達 1.25 兆美元。現在這家超級公司又拿到以 600 億美元收購 AI 程式工具 Cursor 的選擇權。Cursor 已被超過一半的財星 500 大企業使用。

這筆交易讓 Musk 同時掌握火箭、衛星、AI 模型和開發工具，加上本週拿到的五角大廈機密網路許可，成為最大的國防 AI 承包商。

Elon Musk merged SpaceX and xAI in February of this year, creating a supercompany valued at $1.25 trillion. Now the combined entity has secured an option to acquire Cursor, an AI coding tool, for $60 billion. Cursor is already used by more than half of Fortune 500 companies. The deal gives Musk control over rockets, satellites, AI models, and developer tools—and combined with this week's Pentagon classified network clearance, positions him as the largest defense AI contractor.

小熊橫掃響尾蛇，主場十一連勝創十八年紀錄

來源：ESPN

芝加哥小熊週日在 Wrigley Field 以 8比4 擊敗響尾蛇，完成三連勝橫掃。Michael Busch 打出滿壘三壘打，貢獻 4 分打點；Ballesteros 也轟出全壘打。

小熊主場已連贏 11 場，是 2008 年以來最長紀錄。今晚將在主場迎戰紅人隊。

The Chicago Cubs swept the Diamondbacks on Sunday with an 8-4 victory at Wrigley Field, completing a three-game sweep. Michael Busch hit a grand slam, driving in 4 runs, while Ballesteros also went deep. The Cubs have now won 11 straight games at home, their longest streak since 2008. They'll host the Reds tonight at home.

兵工廠三球完勝 Fulham，備戰歐冠四強

來源：Arseblog News

兵工廠線——Arsenal 3比0 輕鬆擊敗 Fulham，在英超領先曼城六分。Gyokeres 進了兩球，Saka 也進一球。

主帥 Arteta 大讚 18 歲小將 Lewis-Skelly 表現「不可思議」，他首次以中場先發，傳球成功率高達 97%。週二兵工廠將在歐冠四強次回合對決 Atletico Madrid，目前兩隊 1比1 平手。

Arsenal beat Fulham 3-0 comfortably to extend their lead over Manchester City to six points in the Premier League. Gyokeres scored twice, with Saka adding the third. Manager Arteta praised 18-year-old prospect Lewis-Skelly as "incredible" in his first start in midfield, completing 97 percent of his passes. Arsenal face Atletico Madrid in the Champions League semi-final second leg on Tuesday, with the tie level at 1-1.
