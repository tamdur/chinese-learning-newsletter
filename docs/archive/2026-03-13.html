密西根狼獾隊以第一種子出戰大十聯盟錦標賽

來源：ESPN

密西根狼獾（wolverines）隊今天以第一種子的身分，在 Big Ten 錦標賽打第一場比賽。他們這季打出 29 勝 2 敗的成績，是球隊歷史上最好的紀錄。

密西根被大家看好會拿下聯盟冠軍。今年真的是歷史性的一季，希望他們能一路打到最後！

The Michigan Wolverines took the court today as the No. 1 seed in the Big Ten Tournament for their first game. With a 29-2 record this season, they're on pace for the best season in program history.

Michigan is widely expected to win the conference championship. This really has been a historic season, and everyone's hoping they can go all the way!

中東戰爭造成史上最大石油供應中斷

來源：Reuters

因為中東的戰爭，伊拉克的石油產量每天少了 300 萬桶，這是歷史上最嚴重的石油供應中斷。比 1990 年波斯灣戰爭那時候還要嚴重。

全球能源市場受到很大的影響，油價上漲也可能帶動通貨膨脹（就是東西都變貴），對全球經濟是一大挑戰。

Due to the war in the Middle East, Iraq's daily oil production has dropped by 3 million barrels, marking the most severe oil supply disruption in history. This is even worse than what happened during the 1990 Persian Gulf War.

The global energy market has been significantly affected. Rising oil prices could also drive inflation (meaning everything becomes more expensive), presenting a major challenge to the global economy.

卡達氦氣停產，晶片供應鏈只剩兩週存量

來源：Tom's Hardware

卡達停止生產氦（helium）氣，很可能跟中東戰爭有關。做晶片需要用到非常純的氦氣，所以這對全球半導體（semiconductor）產業是很大的問題。

目前業界的氦氣庫存大約只夠用兩週。如果不快解決，晶片生產線可能要停下來。

Qatar has stopped producing helium gas, likely related to the Middle East conflict. Chipmaking requires extremely pure helium gas, so this is a major problem for the global semiconductor industry.

Currently, industry helium reserves are roughly sufficient for only two weeks of supply. If not resolved quickly, chip production lines may have to shut down.

ATM沒有消滅銀行櫃員，但智慧型手機做到了

來源：Hacker News

你可能以為 ATM 出現以後，銀行櫃員就變少了，但其實相反。ATM 讓開分行的成本降低，銀行反而開了更多分行，所以櫃員人數還增加了。

真正讓櫃員工作大量減少的，是智慧型手機。這個故事告訴我們：AI 對工作的影響，可能跟我們想的很不一樣。

You might have thought that once ATMs appeared, the number of bank tellers would have decreased. But the opposite happened. ATMs lowered the cost of opening branches, so banks actually opened more branches, and the number of tellers increased.

What really caused a sharp drop in teller jobs was the smartphone. This story teaches us something important: AI's impact on work might be very different from what we expect.

大都會博物館公開140件藝術品的3D掃描

來源：The Verge

紐約的大都會博物館（The Met）把 140 件有名的藝術品做成高畫質的 3D 掃描，免費給大家看。

裡面有古代的雕塑、盔甲（armor）、樂器等等，你可以從任何角度仔細看每個細節。不用飛到紐約，在家就能逛博物館，還滿酷的。

The Metropolitan Museum of Art in New York has released high-quality 3D scans of 140 famous artworks, available for free to everyone.

The collection includes ancient sculptures, armor, musical instruments, and more. You can examine every detail from any angle. You don't need to fly to New York — you can explore the museum from home. Pretty cool, right?
