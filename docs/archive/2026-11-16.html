Anthropic上市大熱門，股價可能再往上

來源：Bloomberg

人工智慧公司Anthropic，就是做Claude聊天機器人的公司，準備在美國那斯達克股市上市，股票代號是ANT。彭博報導，想買的人比能賣的股票多出好幾倍。

原本訂的價錢是每股70到78美元，現在銀行在考慮賣得更貴。這樣算下來，公司價值可能超過1.5兆美元。

價錢預計11月18日決定，19日開始買賣。這是历上最大的上市案之一，也是市場第一次真正替頂尖AI公司打分數。

Anthropic, the company behind the Claude chatbot, is preparing to go public on the Nasdaq under the ticker ANT. Bloomberg reports that buyers outnumber available shares by several times.

The original price range was $70 to $78 per share, but banks are now considering pricing the shares higher. At that level, the company could be valued at more than $1.5 trillion.

Pricing is expected on November 18, with trading beginning on the 19th. It is one of the largest IPOs in history, and the first real market verdict on a leading AI company.

兵工廠兩名主力後衛同時受傷

來源：Sports Illustrated

兵工廠線——國家隊比賽週帶來壞消息。兵工廠兩名主力中後衛，薩利巴（Saliba）和加布里埃爾（Gabriel），都在幫國家隊踢球時受傷。薩利巴傷到腳蹝（腳和小腿相接的地方），加布里埃爾傷到大腿。

教練阿特塔（Arteta）說，球隊會有很長一段時間沒有他們。他也替因傷退出國家隊的其他球員說話。

兵工廠21日要去客場踢紐卡索，24日還有歐洲冠軍聯賽對多特蒙德。頭號射手薩卡要到12月初才回來，排名第一的兵工廠得想辦法守住領先。

The Arsenal beat: The international break has brought bad news. Two of Arsenal's first-choice centre-backs, Saliba and Gabriel, were both injured while playing for their national teams. Saliba hurt his ankle (the joint where the foot meets the lower leg), and Gabriel injured his thigh.

Manager Mikel Arteta said the team will be without them for a long time. He also spoke up for other players who have had to withdraw from their national squads through injury.

Arsenal travel to Newcastle on the 21st and face Borussia Dortmund in the Champions League on the 24th. Their top scorer, Saka, will not be back until early December, and Arsenal, top of the table, will have to find a way to protect their lead.
