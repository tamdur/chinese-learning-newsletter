Anthropic 發布 Claude Opus 4.8 新模型

來源：Hacker News

Anthropic 公司在五月底發布了 Claude Opus 4.8，這是他們最新的 AI 模型，距離上一代 Opus 4.7 只過了 41 天。新模型在寫程式和多種工作上都有進步，在 SWE-Bench Pro 測試中拿到 69.2%，超過了 GPT-5.5 和 Gemini 3.1 Pro。

這次同時推出了「動態工作流」功能，可以同時跑好幾百個小助手來處理大型程式。Anthropic 也同時宣布拿到 650 億美元的新資金，AI 競爭越來越激烈。

Anthropic released Claude Opus 4.8 at the end of May, their latest AI model arriving just 41 days after the previous Opus 4.7 generation. The new model shows improvements across coding and diverse tasks, scoring 69.2% on the SWE-Bench Pro benchmark and surpassing both GPT-5.5 and Gemini 3.1 Pro. The release simultaneously introduces a "Dynamic Workflows" feature that can run several hundred small agents in parallel to handle large-scale programming tasks. Anthropic also announced securing $65 billion in new funding, underscoring the intensifying competition in the AI space.

歐洲央行維持利率不變，六月可能升息

來源：European Central Bank

歐洲央行（ECB）在四月底決定把存款利率維持在 2%，這是今年以來連續第二次不動。中東戰爭讓能源價格大漲，四月通膨（物價上漲的速度）升到 3%。

市場現在預測六月十一日的會議有 91% 的機會升息，這會是歐洲央行一年多來第一次加息。

The European Central Bank (ECB) decided at the end of April to hold its deposit rate steady at 2%, marking the second consecutive month without a change this year. Middle East tensions have driven energy prices sharply higher, pushing April inflation (the rate of price increases) up to 3%. Markets are now pricing in a 91% probability that the bank will raise rates at its June 11th meeting, which would be the ECB's first rate hike in over a year.

美軍週末再度空襲伊朗，國會差一票未能阻止戰爭

來源：CBS News

美軍這個週末對伊朗南部的雷達站和指揮中心發動了新一波空襲，目標在荷莫茲海峽（Strait of Hormuz）附近。軍方說這是「自衛」行動，因為伊朗打下了一架美國無人機。

同時，美國眾議院上週要投票決定是否限制總統的戰爭權力，但共和黨領袖在快要輸的時候緊急取消了投票。六月二日復會後必須重新表決。

The U.S. military conducted a new round of airstrikes against radar stations and command centers in southern Iran over the weekend, targeting areas near the Strait of Hormuz. The military said the operation was an act of "self-defense" in response to Iran shooting down an American drone. Meanwhile, the U.S. House of Representatives was set to vote last week on whether to limit the president's war powers, but Republican leadership abruptly canceled the vote as they faced likely defeat. The measure will need to be reconsidered when Congress reconvenes on June 2nd.

熊隊球場案卡關，議會最後一夜提出新方案

來源：Chicago Sun-Times

伊利諾州議會春季會期在五月三十一日結束，但芝加哥熊隊（Bears）的新球場案沒有通過。參議員 Cunningham 說，原本的減稅方案「沒有足夠的票」，因為很多人反對給球隊四十年高達 15 億美元的房產稅優惠。

晚上十一點，Cunningham 提出了新方案：讓地方政府擁有球場的土地，球隊出錢蓋，但不用付房產稅。這場球場大戰還沒結束。

Illinois's spring legislative session ended on May 31st, but the Chicago Bears' new stadium proposal failed to pass. Senator Cunningham said the original tax-abatement plan "didn't have the votes" because many opposed giving the team a $1.5 billion property-tax exemption over forty years. At 11 p.m., Cunningham floated a new proposal: the municipality would own the land, the team would pay to build the stadium, but wouldn't owe property taxes. The stadium battle isn't over yet.

Crow-Armstrong 四安加全壘打，小熊連勝兩場

來源：MLB.com

小熊隊外野手 Pete Crow-Armstrong 五打數敲出四支安打，包括一支 444 英尺的全壘打，是他職業生涯打最遠的一球。小熊以 6 比 1 打敗紅雀隊。

小熊之前曾經連輸十場，現在終於連贏兩場。Crow-Armstrong 最後還飛撲接殺了最後一個出局數，攻守都很精彩。

Cubs outfielder Pete Crow-Armstrong went 4-for-5 with a home run, including a 444-foot blast that was the longest of his professional career. The Cubs defeated the Cardinals 6-1. After a ten-game losing streak, the Cubs have now won back-to-back games. Crow-Armstrong capped off the performance with a diving catch to record the final out, delivering an impressive display on both offense and defense.

兵工廠歐冠決賽點球惜敗，仍辦冠軍遊行

來源：Arsenal.com

兵工廠線——兵工廠在布達佩斯的歐冠決賽中和 PSG 踢成 1 比 1，Havertz 開場六分鐘就進球，但 Dembele 靠點球追平。點球大戰中，Gabriel 踢失最後一球，PSG 以 4 比 3 贏得冠軍，連續兩年稱霸歐洲。

雖然心碎，兵工廠五月三十一日在 Islington 舉辦了英超冠軍巴士遊行，慶祝拿下聯賽冠軍的精彩一季。

Arsenal Line — Arsenal drew 1-1 with PSG in the Champions League final in Budapest, with Havertz scoring just six minutes into the match, but Dembélé equalized from the penalty spot. In the shootout, Gabriel missed the final kick, and PSG claimed the trophy 4-3 on penalties, winning the European crown for the second consecutive year. Despite the heartbreak, Arsenal held a Premier League champions bus parade in Islington on May 31st to celebrate a brilliant season that culminated in clinching the league title.
