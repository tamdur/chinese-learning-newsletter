川普抵達北京　與習近平展開高峰會談

來源：CNBC

美國總統川普今天抵達北京，這是近十年來第一位到訪北京的美國總統。他帶了Tesla的馬斯克和Nvidia的黃仁勳等科技大老一起來。

兩天的會談要談關稅（進出口的稅）、稀土、AI安全和台灣問題。習近平開場就問：美中兩國能不能避開大國對抗的老路？

US President Trump arrived in Beijing today, making him the first American president to visit the city in nearly a decade. He brought along tech titans including Tesla's Elon Musk and Nvidia's Jensen Huang. The two-day summit will cover tariffs (import and export taxes), rare earth minerals, AI safety, and the Taiwan question. Xi Jinping opened by asking: Can the US and China avoid the old path of great power confrontation?

LinkedIn裁員900人　為AI轉型瘦身

來源：Yahoo Finance

微軟旗下的求職平台LinkedIn宣布裁掉大約900個工作，大約是全球員工的5%。公司說要組成更小、更快的團隊，大量使用AI工具。

有趣的是，LinkedIn上一季收入還成長了12%。今年科技業已經裁了超過十五萬人，Meta下週也要裁八千人。

Microsoft's job-search platform LinkedIn announced layoffs of approximately 900 employees, about 5% of its global workforce. The company said it wants to build smaller, faster teams that make heavy use of AI tools. Interestingly, LinkedIn's revenue grew 12% last quarter. The tech industry has already laid off more than 150,000 people this year, and Meta is cutting 8,000 employees next week.

百度執行長：AI競爭進入「超級個人」時代

來源：TechNode

百度執行長李彥宏今天在北京的Create 2026大會上說，AI的重心已經從比誰的模型大，變成比誰的AI助手好用。

他提出「超級個人」的想法：未來一個人加上一群AI助手，就能做到一個小公司的事。他還說，以後衡量AI成功要看「每天有多少AI助手在工作」，全球可能會超過一百億。

Baidu's Chief Executive Officer Li Yanhong said today at the Create 2026 conference in Beijing that the focus of AI competition has shifted from building the largest models to creating the most useful AI assistants. He introduced the concept of the "Super Individual": in the future, one person equipped with a team of AI assistants will be able to accomplish what a small company does today. He also said that success in AI should be measured by "how many AI assistants are working each day," predicting the global figure could exceed ten billion.

經濟學家：AI不會讓大家失業，但有三個麻煩

來源：Marginal Revolution

知名經濟學家Tyler Cowen在他的部落格上寫道，他不認為AI會造成大量失業，但短期內有三個問題。

第一，新工作多在醫療和能源這些管很嚴的行業，所以開出來的速度會很慢。第二，工作變化太快，找到對的人來做對的事變難了。第三，被取代的人心理壓力很大。

Renowned economist Tyler Cowen wrote on his blog that he doesn't believe AI will cause widespread unemployment, but there are three problems in the near term. First, new jobs are concentrated in heavily regulated industries like healthcare and energy, so they'll be created slowly. Second, job changes are happening too fast, making it harder to match the right people to the right roles. Third, people being displaced face considerable psychological stress.

小熊隊簽下前明星終結者Hendriks

來源：Bleacher Nation

芝加哥小熊隊簽下三次入選明星賽的終結者（closer）Liam Hendriks，是小聯盟合約。他在2023年打敗了癌症，正在努力回到大聯盟。

小熊目前27勝14敗，領先釀酒人3.5場，但目前有10個投手受傷，需要補人。

The Chicago Cubs signed three-time All-Star closer Liam Hendriks on a minor league contract. He beat cancer in 2023 and is working his way back to the majors. The Cubs currently stand at 27-14, leading the Brewers by 3.5 games, but with 10 pitchers on the injured list, they need reinforcements.
