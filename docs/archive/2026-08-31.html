美軍空襲伊朗火箭發射車

來源：CNN

美軍星期天攻擊了伊朗拉拉克島上的兩輛火箭發射車。美軍中央司令部說，島上的革命衛隊正準備把裝了水雷（放在海裡的炸彈）的火箭，射進荷莫茲海峽。

這是一個多月來，美國第一次公開出手打伊朗。上星期美軍才剛把海峽的航道清乾淨，這次動手是為了保住這條油輪水道。

伊朗革命衛隊說島上有人傷亡，一定會報復。

The US military attacked two rocket launcher vehicles on Iran's Larak Island on Sunday. According to US Central Command, the Revolutionary Guards on the island had been preparing to launch rockets equipped with naval mines into the Strait of Hormuz.

This marks the first time in over a month that the US has publicly struck Iran. Last week, the US military cleared the strait's shipping lanes, and this action is aimed at keeping this crucial oil tanker route open.

Iran's Revolutionary Guards said there were casualties on the island and vowed to retaliate.

參議院通過短期預算案

來源：CBS News

美國參議院以九十票對六票，通過一份短期預算案，讓聯邦政府的經費撐到十二月十一日，避開九月三十日的關門危機。法案已送到眾議院，眾議員今天休假結束，回來表決。

票數看起來很和氣，真正的爭執藏在條文裡：法案要限制白宮的預算辦公室，不准它擋下國會已經批准的錢。部分眾議員反對這一條。

The Senate approved a short-term budget bill 90 to 6, funding the federal government through December 11 and sidestepping a shutdown crisis looming for September 30. The bill now heads to the House, where members return from recess today to vote.

The lopsided vote makes it sound harmonious, but the real fight is buried in the details: the bill would restrict the White House's budget office from withholding funds Congress has already approved. Some House members already oppose this measure.

OpenAI 斷掉 Cursor 的模型供應

來源：OpenAI

SpaceX 在八月十四日用換股的方式，買下寫程式工具 Cursor ，身價高達六百億美元。幾天後 OpenAI 就通知對方：十一月十二日起，不再把自己的模型借給 Cursor 用。

OpenAI 說，它不相信馬斯克旗下的公司會守規矩。大型 AI 公司只因為「客戶被誰買走」就斷貨，這還是頭一次。不過 OpenAI 的模型只佔 Cursor 大約百分之五的用量。

On August 14th, SpaceX acquired the coding tool Cursor through a stock swap, valuing it at up to 60 billion dollars. Days later, OpenAI notified them that starting November 12th, it would no longer provide its models to Cursor.

OpenAI said it doesn't trust companies under Musk's ownership to comply with its terms. This is the first time a major AI company has cut off supply solely because a customer was acquired by someone. However, OpenAI's models account for only about 5% of Cursor's total usage.

芝加哥用市府合約向廠商施壓

來源：Associated Press

芝加哥市長強生簽了一道行政命令：想跟市府做生意的大公司，必須交出領導階層和董事會的組成資料，也要說明有沒有跟移民及海關執法局（ICE）往來。不交，就拿不到合約。

一個民間團體算出，有四十二家市府廠商同時接 ICE 的生意，金額約三十一點九億美元。聯邦國土安全部立刻開罵，說市長是在處罰幫政府做事的公司。

Chicago Mayor Brandon Johnson signed an executive order requiring large companies seeking to do business with the city to disclose details about their leadership and board composition, and to reveal whether they have any ties to Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE). Companies that don't comply won't be awarded city contracts.

A nonprofit group found that forty-two city contractors simultaneously work with ICE, with a combined value of roughly $3.19 billion. The federal Department of Homeland Security was quick to criticize, accusing the mayor of penalizing companies that do government work.

熊隊裁到五十三人，傷兵先擺著

來源：Chicago Sun-Times

星期天是 NFL 的裁員大限。芝加哥熊隊放掉三十多名球員，才把人數壓到五十三人。

被裁的反而有季前賽表現最好的幾個人：跑鋒 Salvon Ahmed 、接球數第一的 Maurice Alexander ，還有在附近長大的老將 Scotty Miller 。

受傷的角衛 Kyler Gordon 則被放上傷兵名單。球隊沒有補新人，只是先把傷兵擺著。九月十三日開季，熊隊要到卡羅萊納作客。

Sunday was the NFL's roster cut deadline. The Chicago Bears released over 30 players to trim their roster to 53.

Surprisingly, among those cut were several of the team's best preseason performers: running back Salvon Ahmed, leading receiver Maurice Alexander, and veteran Scotty Miller, who grew up in the Chicago area.

Injured cornerback Kyler Gordon was placed on injured reserve instead. The team didn't bring in replacements; rather, it's holding off on filling those gaps. The season opens September 13th, with the Bears heading to Carolina.

十八年畫下句點：尼爾森告別兵工廠

來源：Yahoo Sports / ESPN

兵工廠線——二十六歲的邊鋒 Reiss Nelson 和球隊談妥，撕掉最後一年合約，立刻變成自由球員。

他從小在球隊的 Hale End 青訓營長大，一待就是十八年，替一隊出賽九十場、進八球，也是隊上最後一個在溫格手下踢過球的人。

走的時機很巧：轉會截止前一天。兵工廠整個夏天都想買邊鋒，卻一個也沒買到。

The Arsenal beat — 26-year-old winger Reiss Nelson has agreed with the club to terminate the final year of his contract, immediately becoming a free agent.

He grew up through the club's Hale End academy from childhood, spending eighteen years there. He made ninety appearances for the first team and scored eight goals, and was the last remaining player at the club to have played under Wenger.

The timing of his departure was particularly notable: it came the day before the transfer deadline. Arsenal had pursued signing a winger all summer long but failed to land one.
