熊隊四分衛威廉斯可望復出

來源：Sports Illustrated

芝加哥熊隊的四分衛（負責傳球的主將）威廉斯，九月底拉傷大腿肌肉，已經休息好幾場。NFL電視網記者拉波波特說，他很可能在10月18日星期日先發，對上亞特蘭大獵鷹隊。

他不在的時候，由替補巴金特上場。熊隊先打贏噴射機隊，上週卻以7比31大輸給綠灣包裝工隊。總教練強森還沒有正式說誰會先發。

專家的預測沒有改變，仍然看好熊隊多贏約3分。獵鷹隊的四分衛彭尼克斯也是帶著肋骨的傷在比賽。

Caleb Williams, the Chicago Bears' quarterback, strained a thigh muscle at the end of September and has been sidelined for several games. NFL Network reporter Ian Rapoport says he is likely to start this Sunday, October 18, against the Atlanta Falcons.

In his absence, backup Tyson Bagent has taken the snaps. The Bears beat the Jets first, but last week they were routed 31-7 by the Green Bay Packers. Head coach Ben Johnson has not yet officially said who will start.

Expert forecasts have not changed. They still favor the Bears by about three points. Falcons quarterback Michael Penix Jr. is also playing through a rib injury.

兵工廠作客諾丁漢森林

來源：Flashscore

兵工廠線——兵工廠剛在歐洲冠軍聯賽以3比0打敗里爾，已經連贏14場歐冠比賽，排名第一。這個星期六（10月17日），他們要回到英超，到諾丁漢森林隊的主場「城市球場」作客。

不過，後衛薩利巴背部受傷，預計要到月底才能回來。四天後，球隊還要去德國打拜仁慕尼黑，比賽一場接一場。

兵工廠在歐洲幾乎不會輸，但在英超客場的表現時好時壞。這場比賽可以看出，他們能不能把好狀態帶回聯賽。

The Arsenal beat: Arsenal have just beaten Lille 3-0 in the Champions League, have now won 14 straight European matches, and sit top of the table. This Saturday (October 17), they travel to the City Ground to face Nottingham Forest in the Premier League.

However, defender Saliba has a back injury and is not expected back until the end of the month. Four days later, the team must also travel to Germany to play Bayern Munich, with one match following another.

Arsenal are almost unbeatable in Europe, but their away form in the Premier League has been hit and miss. This match will show whether they can carry their good form back into the league.
