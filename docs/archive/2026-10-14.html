IMF警告：AI股價太高，可能突然大跌

來源：Gulf News / IMF Global Financial Stability Report

國際貨幣基金組織（IMF）這週在曼谷發表金融報告，說現在的股票價格，已經比公司真正的價值高出很多。

報告說，美國「標普500」指數的本益比（股價和獲利的比例），比1990年以來96%的時候都高。「七大科技股」在市場裡占的比重非常高，讓人想起2000年網路泡沫（股價吹太大後破掉）的時候。

IMF最擔心的是：如果AI公司賺的錢不如預期，或AI沒有讓工作更有效率，科技股可能會又快又亂地大跌。

The International Monetary Fund (IMF) released a financial report in Bangkok this week, saying that stock prices today are well above the true value of the companies behind them.

The report notes that the price-to-earnings ratio (the relationship between a stock's price and the profit it earns) for the S&P 500 index in the United States has been higher than it was 96% of the time since 1990. The "Magnificent Seven" tech stocks take up a very large share of the market, which brings to mind the dot-com bubble of 2000, when stock prices were pushed too high and then burst.

The IMF's biggest worry is this: if AI companies earn less money than expected, or if AI fails to make work more efficient, tech stocks could fall quickly and chaotically.
