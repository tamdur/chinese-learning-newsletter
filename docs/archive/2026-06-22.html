哥倫比亞大選右派險勝

來源：NPR

南美洲國家哥倫比亞六月二十一日舉行總統選舉。右派候選人德拉埃斯普列利亞拿到百分之四十九點六五的票，只贏對手不到二十五萬票。

他得到美國總統川普支持，主張用軍隊打擊武裝幫派。輸的左派候選人不服氣，要求重新算票。這也是最近拉丁美洲向右轉的又一個例子。

Colombia held a presidential election on June 21st. The right-wing candidate De la Espriella secured 49.65% of the vote, winning by less than 250,000 votes over his opponent. He enjoyed the backing of US President Trump and campaigned on using the military to combat armed gangs. The defeated left-wing candidate refused to concede and demanded a recount. This marks another example of Latin America's recent rightward shift.

美司法部挑戰身障者權利

來源：NPR

美國司法部六月發出一份法律意見，挑戰過去的規定：身障者（身體或心智有障礙的人）應該能住在自己家和社區裡。

這份意見說，各州不一定要幫他們住在社區。為身障者說話的團體很擔心，怕幾十萬人會被送回大型機構住。

The U.S. Department of Justice issued a legal opinion in June that challenges a long-standing rule: people with disabilities should be able to live in their own homes and communities. The opinion argues that states are not necessarily required to help them live in community settings. Advocacy groups for people with disabilities are alarmed, fearing that hundreds of thousands could be sent back to large institutional facilities.

芝城福特城商場關門

來源：Block Club Chicago

芝加哥西南區的福特城商場，六月二十二日中午在法院命令下關門了。這家商場從一九六五年開到現在，已經六十年了。

城市說商場有漏水、電線外露、消防系統壞掉等危險。關門後，建商想蓋倉庫工業區，但超過六百位居民連署，希望改蓋住宅和商店。

Chicago's Ford City Mall in the Southwest Side closed at noon on June 22nd under a court order. The mall had been operating since 1965, a span of sixty years. The city cited water leaks, exposed wiring, and a broken fire suppression system as safety hazards. Following the closure, developers plan to build a warehouse industrial complex, but over six hundred residents have signed a petition hoping for residential and retail development instead.

研究：減稅讓更多人願意工作

來源：Marginal Revolution

經濟學家柯文分享了一份新研究，談美國二〇一七年的減稅（少收人民的稅金）帶來的影響。

研究發現，稅金每少收人民收入的百分之一，出來工作的人就多了大約百分之零點七到一。這讓大家重新討論：減稅是不是真的能把人拉回職場。

Economist Tyler Cowen shared a new study about the effects of America's 2017 tax cut. The research found that for every one percentage point reduction in taxes relative to people's income, the workforce participation rate increased by approximately 0.7 to 1 percent. This has reignited debate about whether tax cuts can genuinely draw people back into the labor force.

另一名球員強森，本季平均得十三點一分、搶下七點三個籃板，也確定參加選秀。兩人離開後，教練梅伊要重新補強球隊。

Two Michigan basketball stars are heading to the NBA draft following the Wolverines' national championship win this year. Forward Lendeborg is considered a first-round prospect after working out for the Golden State Warriors. His teammate Johnson, who averaged 13.1 points and 7.3 rebounds this season, has also committed to entering the draft. Coach May will need to rebuild the roster after their departures.
