熊隊21比13打敗聖徒隊，拿下五連勝

來源：ESPN

芝加哥熊隊週日在主場以21比13打敗紐奧良聖徒隊，拿下五連勝，戰績來到8勝2負。

四分衛威廉斯（Caleb Williams）傳球23次、成功15次，傳了181碼，還有兩次達陣。他只被抄截（球被對手接走）一次，比上一場的四次少很多。

不過真正的功臣是防守，全場擒殺（把對方四分衛撲倒）六次。威廉斯賽後也說，進攻還可以打得更好。

The Bears beat the Saints 21–13 at home on Sunday to win their fifth straight game, improving to 8–2. Quarterback Caleb Williams completed 15 of 23 passes for 181 yards and two touchdowns. He was intercepted only once, a big drop from the four interceptions he threw in the previous game. The real heroes, though, were on defense, which brought down New Orleans quarterbacks for six sacks. After the game, Williams said the offense could still play better.

門將受傷，兵工廠週二迎戰多特蒙德

來源：Sky Sports

兵工廠線——兵工廠週二晚上要在主場迎戰德國的多特蒙德，這是歐洲冠軍聯賽的第五輪。兵工廠前四場拿了10分，排第三。

麻煩的是，一號門將拉亞（David Raya）週六對紐卡索時肩膀受傷，還在等檢查結果。預計由替補門將凱帕（Kepa）上場。薩卡和祖比門迪也還在養傷。

好消息是，英超打完11場，兵工廠拿了28分，還是比曼城多6分。

The Arsenal beat: Arsenal host Germany's Borussia Dortmund at home on Tuesday night in the fifth round of the Champions League. Through their first four matches, Arsenal have collected 10 points and sit third. The trouble is that No. 1 goalkeeper David Raya hurt his shoulder in Saturday's game against Newcastle and is still waiting for test results. Backup keeper Kepa is expected to start. Saka and Zubimendi are also still recovering from injuries. The good news is that after 11 Premier League matches, Arsenal have 28 points, still six points ahead of Manchester City.
