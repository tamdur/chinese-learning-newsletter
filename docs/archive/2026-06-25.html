最高法院判決：綠卡族更難過關

來源：SCOTUSblog

美國最高法院六月二十三日做出一個重要判決。以後拿綠卡（長期居留證）的人出國回來時，如果被懷疑犯過罪，官員不用拿出很強的證據，就能把他們擋在門外。

這個判決是六比三通過的。三位反對的法官說，這等於給了政府一張空白支票，讓住很久的居民更沒有保障。

The U.S. Supreme Court issued a significant ruling on June 23rd. Going forward, when green card holders (permanent residents) return from abroad and are suspected of having committed a crime, officials no longer need to present strong evidence to deny them entry.

The decision passed 6-3. The three dissenting justices argued that this amounts to giving the government a blank check, leaving long-term residents with far fewer protections.

On the same day, the Court also narrowed the scope of an older law in another case, making it harder for foreigners to sue multinational corporations in American courts for aiding wrongdoers in illegal activities.

This ruling was one of a series of decisions issued as the term drew to a close. The case everyone is most anxious about—birthright citizenship—has not yet been announced. As the government intensifies immigration enforcement and deportations, these decisions will directly impact the lives of many people.

OpenAI 推出資安新模型

來源：Build Fast With AI

OpenAI 在六月二十二日推出新的人工智慧叫 GPT-5.5-Cyber，專門做電腦資安（保護電腦不被攻擊）的工作。它能讀很長的程式碼，找出漏洞再幫忙修好。

在一項叫 CyberGym 的測驗裡，它拿到 85.6 分，比別家的模型還高。同一天也傳出，太空公司 SpaceX 每個月要花一億五千萬美元租用電腦算力，看得出大家都在搶運算資源。

OpenAI launched a new artificial intelligence model called GPT-5.5-Cyber on June 22nd, designed specifically for computer security work—protecting computers from attacks. It can read lengthy code, identify vulnerabilities, and help fix them.

In a test called CyberGym, it scored 85.6 points, higher than competing models.

This represents a new direction. Previously, companies competed on general-purpose models that could do everything. Now they're building specialized models that excel at one specific task. Cybersecurity is a double-edged sword: the same capability can protect computers or attack them.

That same week, SpaceX and a startup called Reflection AI signed a contract spending $150 million per month to rent computing capacity. This shows how fiercely companies are competing for computer processing power.

市議員不滿：停車費案太神秘

來源：Block Club Chicago

芝加哥有二十二位市議員在六月二十四日指責市長強森，說他偷偷重談那個很不受歡迎的停車費合約，還用保密協議當理由不給大家看內容。

這份合約是二○○八年簽的，把全市的停車收費租給私人公司七十五年，一直被市民罵。議員說市長不說清楚，他們就不會投票同意。

Twenty-two Chicago aldermen accused Mayor Johnson on June 24th of secretly renegotiating the widely unpopular parking meter contract and using confidentiality agreements as an excuse to keep the details hidden from the public.

The contract, signed in 2008, leased the city's parking revenue to a private company for seventy-five years and has been criticized by residents ever since. The aldermen said that unless the mayor explains what he's doing, they won't vote to approve any new arrangement.

Johnson himself had previously criticized the original contract, calling it "the worst deal in municipal history." Now he finds himself facing accusations of lacking transparency.

The dispute has deepened the already strained relationship between the mayor and the city council.

酒吧外槍擊案後　議員想管夜店

來源：Block Club Chicago

六月七日凌晨，芝加哥林肯公園區一家酒吧外發生槍擊，一名三十九歲的女子中了好幾槍，情況危急。

事情傳出是從附近一家酒吧的爭吵引起的。市議員克努森在六月二十四日提議，想對深夜營業的酒吧加上更嚴的規定，讓夜生活和居民安全的老問題又被提出來。

In the early morning hours of June 7th, a shooting occurred outside a bar in Chicago's Lincoln Park neighborhood, leaving a 39-year-old woman critically injured with multiple gunshot wounds.

The incident reportedly stemmed from a dispute that began inside a nearby bar. On June 24th, alderman Knudsen proposed stricter regulations for late-night bars, reviving the long-standing tension between vibrant nightlife and resident safety.

His proposal includes requiring bars in certain neighborhoods to close earlier. Supporters argue this would improve safety, while opponents worry it could harm local businesses.

This dilemma — balancing lively entertainment with peace and safety — is one that Chicago's many bustling neighborhoods grapple with regularly.

芝加哥公牛隊在二○二六年 NBA 選秀中，用第四順位選中了北卡羅來納大學的前鋒威爾森。

威爾森身高六呎九吋，大學時每場平均得十九點八分、搶下九點四個籃板，彈跳力和防守都很出色。他是公牛重新打造年輕球隊的重要基石。

The Chicago Bulls selected forward Wilson from the University of North Carolina with the fourth overall pick in the 2026 NBA Draft.

Standing 6'9", Wilson averaged 19.8 points and 9.4 rebounds per game in college, with exceptional athleticism and defensive prowess. He is a cornerstone piece in the Bulls' rebuild of their young roster.

兵工廠出手　天價搶阿瓦雷茲

來源：TeamTalk

兵工廠線——夏天轉會市場一打開，阿森納就開出一億零四百萬英鎊的大手筆，想買下馬德里競技的前鋒阿瓦雷茲。

阿瓦雷茲是阿根廷的世界盃冠軍球員，巴黎、巴塞隆納和切爾西也都想要他，四隊搶一人。這是阿森納這個夏天最大膽的一步。

Arsenal beat — as soon as the summer transfer window opened, Arsenal made their boldest move yet, tabling a £104 million bid to sign Atlético Madrid's striker Álvarez.

Álvarez is Argentina's World Cup champion forward, and Paris, Barcelona, and Chelsea are also vying for him — four clubs chasing one player. This is Arsenal's most audacious statement this summer.

Arsenal won the Premier League title last season, and now they're looking to bolster their attacking firepower, proving they're serious about sustaining their winning ways.

A sum this large shows Arsenal's ambition in unmistakable terms. Whether they can outbid the other three clubs will become clear in the coming weeks.
