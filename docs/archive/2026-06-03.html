川普簽令：政府可提早看最強AI

來源：NPR / CNBC

六月二日，美國總統川普簽了一道命令，請最厲害的AI公司在新模型（AI系統）上市前三十天，先給政府看一看。

這是為了國家安全，由國安局來決定哪些AI太強、需要檢查。不過命令說這是自願的，政府不會強迫公司交出來。

On June 2, US President Trump signed an order asking the most advanced AI companies to show the government their new models thirty days before launch. This is for national security, with the National Security Agency deciding which AI systems are too powerful and need review. However, the order states this is voluntary, and the government won't force companies to hand over their work.

The move aims to give authorities a head start in evaluating cutting-edge AI capabilities before they become widely available. It reflects growing concerns about potential risks from increasingly powerful artificial intelligence systems. The voluntary framework suggests the administration is seeking cooperation from tech companies rather than imposing mandatory oversight requirements at this stage.

微軟推自家AI，想少靠OpenAI

來源：CNBC / TechTimes

六月二日的Build大會上，微軟推出兩個自己做的AI：會寫程式的MAI-Code-1，和會思考的MAI-Thinking-1。

執行長納德拉說，微軟從「用別人的AI」變成「自己做AI」。這代表它想少靠合作夥伴OpenAI，也能省下很多錢。

At its Build conference on June 2nd, Microsoft unveiled two AI models of its own: MAI-Code-1, which writes code, and MAI-Thinking-1, which reasons through problems. CEO Nadella said Microsoft is shifting from "using other people's AI" to "building our own AI." This signals the company wants to rely less on its partner OpenAI and save significant costs in the process.

The move reflects a broader industry trend: major tech companies are increasingly developing proprietary AI systems rather than depending on external providers. For Microsoft, reducing reliance on OpenAI gives it more control over its AI capabilities and spending, while maintaining its competitive edge in enterprise AI applications.

歐盟通過嚴格移民新法

來源：NPR / Euronews

六月初，歐盟談成了一套很嚴的移民（從別國搬來的人）新規定。以後可以把被拒絕的人送到歐盟以外的地方關起來。

關押時間從六個月拉長到兩年，不准再進來的時間也從五年變十年。只有西班牙投下反對票。

In early June, the European Union finalized a strict new set of immigration rules. Going forward, people whose asylum applications are rejected can be detained outside EU territory. Detention periods have been extended from six months to two years, and the ban on re-entry has doubled from five years to ten years. Only Spain cast a dissenting vote.

俄國大舉空襲烏克蘭

來源：ABC News / Reuters

六月二日晚上，俄國對烏克蘭發動了開戰以來最大的空襲之一，一口氣射出七十三枚飛彈和六百多架無人機。

首都基輔是主要目標，至少十七人死亡、一百多人受傷，還有十四萬人一度沒電。烏克蘭再次向美國求援武器。

On the evening of June 2nd, Russia launched one of its largest air assaults since the war began, firing 73 missiles and over 600 drones in a single barrage. The capital Kyiv was the primary target, with at least 17 people killed and over 100 wounded. The attack left 140,000 people without power. Ukraine once again appealed to the United States for additional weapons.

公牛想換到選秀狀元籤

來源：Bleacher Nation

芝加哥公牛隊正在想辦法，要用交易把手上的第四順位換成第一順位（狀元籤）。他們已經找過手握狀元籤的華盛頓巫師隊談。

今年最被看好的新人，是身高兩百公分的大學前鋒迪班塔。公牛這麼做，是想擺脫這幾年的平庸成績。

The Chicago Bulls are working to trade their fourth overall pick for the first overall pick (the top selection). They've already initiated talks with the Washington Wizards, who hold the number one pick. This year's most highly-regarded prospect is Dybantar, a seven-foot college forward. The Bulls are making this push to break free from years of mediocre results.

兵工廠追求阿瓦雷茲

兵工廠線——六月三日，阿森納（兵工廠）出手詢問能不能簽下馬德里競技的前鋒、阿根廷世界盃冠軍阿瓦雷茲。

傳聞轉會費高達八千多萬英鎊。不過巴塞隆納也在搶，而且已經和球員談好個人條件，這筆交易並不好談。

Arsenal — June 3rd. Arsenal has made inquiries about signing Atletico Madrid's striker and Argentine World Cup champion Alvarez. Transfer fees are rumored to exceed 80 million pounds. However, Barcelona is also in the race and has already agreed personal terms with the player, making this deal difficult to conclude.
