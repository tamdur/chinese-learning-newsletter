巴西週日選總統，波士尼亞同天投票

來源：AS/COA、European Western Balkans

巴西這個星期天（10月4日）要選總統。80歲的現任總統魯拉（Lula）想拿下第四個任期，主要對手是前總統波索納洛的兒子弗拉維歐（Flávio Bolsonaro）。

民調顯示魯拉領先。如果沒有人拿到超過一半的票，前兩名要在10月25日再選一次。

同一天，歐洲的波士尼亞京要投票。那裡的總統由三個人一起當，各自代表一個民族，這次一共要選出518個職位。

Brazil will hold its presidential election this Sunday, October 4. Eighty-year-old incumbent President Lula is seeking his fourth term, with his main challenger being Flávio Bolsonaro, the son of former President Bolsonaro. Polls show Lula with a commanding lead. If no candidate wins more than half the votes, the top two will compete in a runoff on October 25. On the same day, Bosnia in Europe will also hold elections. The Bosnian presidency is shared by three people, each representing a different ethnic group. In this election, a total of 518 positions will be filled.

中國黃金週開跑，考驗大家肯不肯花錢

來源：中國交通運輸部、中國文化和旅遊部

中國的國慶「黃金週」從10月1日放到7日。官方估計，第一天就有3.4億人次出門，七天加起來超過21億人次。

北京用補貼（政府幫忙出一部分錢）鼓勵大家買東西，因為工廠和投資的成長，都掉到差不多二十年來最低。

去年的長假，國內旅遊一共花了8000多億人民幣。今年補貼有沒有用，這一週就能看出一點答案。

China's National Day "Golden Week" runs from October 1st to 7th. Officials estimate that on the first day alone, 340 million trips were made, and over the seven days the total exceeds 2.1 billion trips.

Beijing is using subsidies—government funding to help offset costs—to encourage spending, because both factory output and investment growth have fallen to near their lowest levels in roughly twenty years. Last year's extended holiday saw domestic tourism spending exceed 800 billion yuan.

This week will reveal whether the government's subsidies this year are actually working.

歐盟給中國的期限到了

來源：Euronews

歐盟之前要求中國在10月初拿出成果，現在期限到了。

歐盟從中國買的東西，比賣給中國的多出3600多億歐元，差不多每天10億。歐盟也擔心中國再次停止出口稀土（做電動車和武器需要的特別金屬）。

歐盟貿易官員謝夫喬維奇（Maroš Šefčovič）10月8日到9日要去北京。他警告，如果中國沒有改変，歐盟會用「更強硬的手段」。

The EU had set an early October deadline for China to show concrete progress, and that moment has now arrived. Europe's trade imbalance with China is stark: its purchases from China outpace sales by over 360 billion euros—roughly one billion per day. The EU is also anxious that China might once again restrict rare earth exports, the specialized metals essential for electric vehicles and weapons. EU Trade Commissioner Maroš Šefčovič will travel to Beijing on October 8–9, where he warned that if China doesn't change course, the EU will deploy "tougher measures."

最高法院開新會期，芝加哥槍枝禁令上場

來源：WTTW、SCOTUSblog

美國最高法院10月5日星期一開始新的一年。芝加哥人最該注意的，是庫克郡（芝加哥所在的郡）禁止AR-15這類步槍的規定。

這個案子叫 Viramontes v. Cook County，會和康乃狄克州一個類似的案子一起，在12月2日開庭辩論，判決大概明年夏天出來。

這一年法院還會處理宗教學校，以及跟氣候變遷有関的官尸。

The U.S. Supreme Court begins its new term on Monday, October 5. For Chicagoans, the most significant case is Cook County's ban on rifles like the AR-15.

The case—Viramontes v. Cook County—will be argued alongside a similar Connecticut case on December 2, with a decision expected next summer.

The Court will also hear cases this term involving religious schools and climate change.

熊隊迎戰噴射機，威廉斯還在養傷

來源：Chicago Bears、Sports Illustrated

芝加哥熊隊這個星期天中午，要在主場對上紐約噴射機隊。

大家最關心的是四分衛（負責傳球的球員）凱勒布．威廉斯（Caleb Williams）。他在第二週對維京人隊時大腰後側拉傷，上週一第一次在正式比賽缺席，星期三還是沒有練球。

跑衛儲威夢特（D'Andre Swift）臝蓋受傷，也沒有練球。總教練班．強森說，威廉斯的情況要「一週一週看」。

The Chicago Bears host the New York Jets at home this Sunday at noon. All eyes are on quarterback Caleb Williams, who suffered a hamstring injury in Week 2 against the Vikings. He missed his first official game last Monday and remained out of practice on Wednesday. Running back D'Andre Swift is nursing a knee injury and also sat out practice. Head coach Ben Johnson said Williams' status will need to be evaluated on a week-to-week basis.
