特斯拉第三季賣得變少

來源：Electrek / Tesla 財報

特斯拉（Tesla）10月21日公布第三季成績。營收（公徸收進來的錢）大約280億美元，跟去年差不多；但是淨賣的錢少了43%，只剩7.87億美元。

這一季賣出約48.65萬輛車，比大家輜的多，卻比去年少了2%。研發新技術花的錢也變多了。

特斯拉希望大家把它看成一家 AI 和機器人公徸。可是無人計程車 Cybercab 和機器人 Optimus，到現在都還没有大量生產。

Tesla released its third-quarter results on October 21. Revenue (the money the company brings in) came to about $28 billion, roughly flat from last year. But net profit fell 43%, to just $787 million.

The company sold about 486,500 vehicles this quarter. That was more than people had expected, but 2% fewer than a year earlier. Spending on developing new technology also rose.

Tesla wants people to see it as an AI and robotics company. But the Cybercab robotaxi and the Optimus robot have yet to reach mass production.

校園槍擊对學生的長期影響

來源：Marginal Revolution

經濟學家 María Medellín-Esguerra 研究了1990年到2025年美國大學發生的槍擊案，想知道這些事件會不會長久改變學生的人生。

她發現，經歷過槍擊的學生，畢業的時間跟別人差不多，没有拖得比較久。不過，他們之後繼續讀研究所，或去念法律、醫學等學校的人變少了。

經濟學家 Tyler Cowen 在部落格上介紹了這篇論文。

Economist María Medellín-Esguerra studied campus shootings at American universities from 1990 to 2025, looking at whether these incidents permanently alter students' lives.

She found that students who lived through a shooting graduated on about the same schedule as everyone else, with no evidence of delay. However, they were less likely to go on to graduate school, or to enroll in law school, medical school, and similar programs.

Economist Tyler Cowen featured the paper on his blog.

熊隊今晚主場迎戰愛國者

來源：Bleacher Nation

芝加哥熊隊（Bears）今天晚上在士兵球場（Soldier Field）對上新英格蘭愛國者隊（Patriots）。兩隊目前都是4勝2敗。

上週，四分衛（負責傳球的球員） Caleb Williams 傷好回來，傳球超過300碼，帶領熊隊以31比23贏了亞特蘭大獻齰隊。愛國者隊則已經連贏三場。

這是熊隊連續對上同區對手之前的最後一場比賽，先拿下這一勝非常重要。

The Chicago Bears host the New England Patriots tonight at Soldier Field. Both teams are currently 4-2.

Last week, quarterback Caleb Williams returned from injury and threw for more than 300 yards, leading the Bears to a 31-23 win over the Atlanta Falcons. The Patriots, meanwhile, have won three games in a row.

This is the last game before the Bears face a stretch of division opponents, so it is important to come away with this win.

兵工廠客場2比2逼和拜仁

來源：ESPN

兵工廠線——兵工廠（Arsenal）10月21日在歐冠（歐洲冠軍聯賽）客場對上德國的拜仁慕尼黑，最後2比2打平。

Eze 先替兵工廠進球，拜仁很快追平。下半場，Harry Kane 踢進罰球，讓拜仁領先。到了第82分鐘，Declan Rice 踢進一球，幫球隊救回一分。

兵工廠在歐洲比賽的14連勝停了下來。不過，少了兩名主力後衛，還能在很難打的客場拿到一分，表現不錯。

The Arsenal beat: Arsenal drew 2-2 away to Bayern Munich in the Champions League on October 21.

Eze put Arsenal ahead, but Bayern equalized quickly. In the second half, Harry Kane converted a penalty to give Bayern the lead. In the 82nd minute, Declan Rice scored to rescue a point for his side.

The result ends Arsenal's 14-match unbeaten run in European competition. Still, taking a point at a very difficult away ground without two first-choice defenders was a solid performance.
