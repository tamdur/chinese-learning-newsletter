美國與伊朗接近達成核子協議

來源：Axios

美國和伊朗正在談一份重要的核子協議。根據報導，伊朗可能同意停止提煉（把鈾變純）至少十二年，接受聯合國隨時檢查，不建地下核設施。美國則會取消經濟制裁。

川普說協議「大部分已經談好」，可能很快就會宣布。如果成功，這會是中東和平的一大步。

The United States and Iran are negotiating an important nuclear agreement. According to reports, Iran may agree to halt uranium enrichment for at least twelve years, accept UN inspections at any time, and refrain from building underground nuclear facilities. In return, the United States would lift economic sanctions. Trump said the agreement is "mostly done" and could be announced soon. If successful, this would be a major step toward Middle East peace.

SpaceX 星艦試飛後在印度洋爆炸

來源：Euronews

SpaceX 的星艦火箭昨天完成第十二次試飛，飛了大約六十六分鐘後降落在印度洋，碰到水面幾秒後就爆炸了。

SpaceX 表示這是計畫中的結果，火箭本來就不會被回收。這次還成功放出了模擬的 Starlink 衛星。星艦是 NASA 登月計畫的關鍵火箭。

SpaceX's Starship rocket completed its twelfth test flight yesterday, flying for approximately sixty-six minutes before landing in the Indian Ocean. It exploded just seconds after hitting the water. SpaceX said this was the planned outcome—the rocket was never intended to be recovered. The test successfully deployed mock Starlink satellites. Starship is a critical rocket for NASA's lunar landing program.

中國山西煤礦爆炸至少八十二人死亡

來源：NPR

中國山西省長治市的柳深煜煤礦在星期五晚上發生瓦斯（氣體）爆炸，造成至少八十二人死亡，超過一百二十人住院。這是中國十多年來最嚴重的礦災。

這座煤礦在 2024 年就被列為「高瓦斯」危險礦場。習近平已下令全力搜救並徹底調查。

A gas explosion at the Liushenyu coal mine in Changzhi, Shanxi Province, China occurred Friday evening, killing at least 82 people and hospitalizing more than 120. This is China's deadliest mining disaster in over a decade. The mine had been classified as "high-gas risk" since 2024. Xi Jinping has ordered an all-out rescue effort and a thorough investigation.

川普新規：綠卡申請人必須離開美國

來源：NPR

川普政府宣布，以後在美國的外國人想拿綠卡（永久居留權），必須先回自己的國家申請。這改變了超過五十年的舊規定。

每年大約六十萬人用舊方法在美國國內申請，他們現在都會受到影響。政府還沒說新規定什麼時候開始。

The Trump administration announced that foreign nationals in the United States seeking green cards (permanent residency) must now return to their home countries to apply. This overturns a practice that has been in place for over fifty years. Roughly 600,000 people per year have used the previous method to apply from within the United States, and all of them will now be affected. The government has not yet specified when the new rule will take effect.

來源：Bleacher Nation

芝加哥小熊隊昨天在主場被休士頓太空人隊以 3-0 完封（一分都沒得）。太空人的 Christian Walker 打了兩支全壘打。

小熊已經連輸七場，是四年來最長的連敗。最近十三場輸了十一場，球迷很著急。

The Chicago Cubs were shut out at home yesterday by the Houston Astros, 3-0. Christian Walker hit two home runs for the Astros. The Cubs have now lost seven straight games, their longest losing streak in four years. Over their last thirteen games, they've won just two, leaving fans anxious.
