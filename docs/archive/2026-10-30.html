密西根萬聖節出戰羅格斯，想找回信心

來源：CBS Sports

上星期六，密西根大學美式足球隊在主場以6比13輸給印第安那大學，而且是在延長賽輸的。這場比賽讓很多球迷明白，十大聯盟（Big Ten）的強弱已經跟從前不一樣了。

這是新教練惠廷翰（Kyle Whittingham）帶隊的第一年。球隊常常發生失誤，季中還把先發四分衛換成一年級新生。

這個星期六剛好是萬聖節，密西根要到客場挑戰羅格斯大學。賽前分析都看好密西根，而且密西根已經連續贏了羅格斯五次。這場比賽是重新站穩的好機會。

Last Saturday, the University of Michigan football team lost 13-6 to Indiana University at home, and the game was decided in overtime. For many fans, the result made it clear that the balance of power in the Big Ten has shifted from what it used to be.

This is the first season for new head coach Kyle Whittingham. The team has committed turnovers often, and midseason it even replaced its starting quarterback with a freshman.

This Saturday is Halloween, and Michigan travels to Rutgers. Pre-game analysis favors Michigan, which has won five straight against Rutgers. This game is a good chance for the team to get back on its feet.
