AI公司說要放慢，亞洲股市跟著跌

來源：The Jakarta Post

AI公司 Anthropic 的老闆阿莫迪週末說，大家開發 AI 的速度該慢下來。OpenAI 的奧特曼和 xAI 的馬斯克，隔天都說同意。

星期一亞洲股市一開盤就跌。日本軟銀最多跌了百分之十三，韓國 SK 海力士跌百分之五點三，台灣的台積電也跌了百分之一點二。

奧特曼還說，OpenAI 今年不會上市（把股票賣給大家），因為安全更重要。一句講安全的話，變成了股價。

Anthropic's CEO Amodei said over the weekend that companies should slow down their AI development. OpenAI's Altman and xAI's Musk both agreed the next day. When Asian stock markets opened on Monday, they fell. Japan's SoftBank dropped the most, down 13%; South Korea's SK Hynix fell 5.3%; and Taiwan's TSMC also declined 1.2%. Altman also said that OpenAI won't go public this year—won't sell shares to the public—because safety is more important. A single comment about safety had moved the market.

三大對手密談，想合開AI檢查機構

來源：Seoul Economic Daily

Anthropic、OpenAI 和 Google 從七月起就一直在開會，想一起成立一個機構，在新的 AI 模型上市前先做測試。這件事星期一才第一次被報導出來。

這個點子是 Google DeepMind 的老闆哈薩比斯在七月十四日提出的，做法學美國管股票經紀人的那種自律團體。

三家公司爭的不是要不要有規矩，而是政府該管多少：Anthropic 希望政府一起來，OpenAI 想先用自願的標準。

Since July, Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google have been in talks about jointly creating an organization to test new AI models before they're released to market. The talks were first publicly reported on Monday.

The idea was proposed by Demis Hassabis, head of Google DeepMind, on July 14th, and is modeled on the kind of self-regulatory bodies that oversee securities brokers in the United States.

The three companies aren't arguing about whether rules should exist—they're divided on how much government oversight is needed. Anthropic wants government involvement, while OpenAI prefers to start with voluntary standards.

瑞典大選，左派聯盟小幅領先

來源：Novus Exit Poll

瑞典星期天舉行國會大選。出口民調（投完票後馬上問選民）顯示，在野的紅綠聯盟拿到百分之五十一點三，執政的一邊拿百分之四十七。

不過差距很小。瑞典國會有三百四十九席，電視台的民調算出來兩邊只差一席。

所以最後誰來組政府，要等這星期票全部數完才知道。

Sweden held parliamentary elections on Sunday. Exit polls—surveys conducted right after voters cast their ballots—showed the opposition Red-Green alliance with 51.3 percent support, versus 47 percent for the ruling coalition. But it's a razor-thin margin. With 349 seats in parliament, the two sides are separated by just one seat according to television projections. So who will actually form the government won't be clear until all votes are fully counted this week.

男子被推下列車，十五歲少女被控殺人

來源：Chicago Sun-Times

芝加哥警方說，一名十五歲少女星期五被捕，被控殺人和聚眾滋事（一群人一起動手）。

勞動節那天下午兩點四十分，二十九歲的比克姆下班回家，在紅線車廂裡和人起衝突，被三個人推下行駛中的列車，在六十九街附近被另一班車撞到，當場死亡。

少女星期六首次出庭，法官裁定收押到開庭。另外兩個人是誰，警方還沒說。

Chicago police arrested a 15-year-old girl on Friday, charging her with murder and mob action. On Labor Day afternoon at 2:40 p.m., a 29-year-old man named Bickham was heading home from work when he got into a confrontation with someone in a Red Line train car. He was pushed from the moving train by three people and struck by another train near 69th Street, dying at the scene. The girl made her first court appearance on Saturday, and the judge ordered her held in custody pending trial. Police have not yet identified the other two individuals involved.

熊隊開幕戰大勝，創下NFL最高分紀錄

來源：ESPN

芝加哥熊隊星期天到夏洛特作客，以五十九比三十七打敗卡羅萊納黑豹隊。兩隊加起來九十六分，是 NFL 開幕戰史上最高的分數。

第三年的四分衛威廉斯傳球二百六十九碼、兩次達陣，自己又跑了六十五碼、再拿兩次，一場球就包辦四次達陣。

只是防守讓對手拿下三十七分，這場慶祝還是留了一個問號。

The Chicago Bears traveled to Charlotte on Sunday and defeated the Carolina Panthers 59-37. The two teams combined for 96 points, marking the highest-scoring opening game in NFL history. Third-year quarterback Williams delivered an outstanding performance, throwing for 269 yards and two touchdowns while rushing for 65 yards and two additional scores—accounting for all four of the team's touchdowns. However, the defense's inability to contain the Panthers' 37-point output tempered the celebration with some concern.

兵工廠線：布魯諾對上老東家紐卡索

來源：Pain in the Arsenal

兵工廠線——兵工廠現在排在英超第一，但下一場對紐卡索，人手有點緊。

中後衛薩利巴背部受傷，要到冬天才能回來；艾澤小腿受傷還要四到六週；莫斯蓋拉和廷伯能不能上場也還不確定。教練阿爾特塔得重新拼出一條後防線。

另一個看點是布魯諾。他八月才用大約七千五百萬英鎊的價錢，從紐卡索轉到兵工廠，這場要面對把他賣掉的老東家。

Arsenal sit top of the Premier League, but with Newcastle looming, their squad is stretched thin. Centre-back Saliba is out with a back injury until winter; Eze is sidelined with a calf injury for another four to six weeks; it remains uncertain whether Mosquera and Timber will be available. Manager Arteta faces the task of improvising with a makeshift defence. The subplot, though, is Bruno. In August, he arrived from Newcastle for around £75 million—now he faces the club that sold him.
