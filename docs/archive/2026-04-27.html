來源：CNBC

國際油價因此大漲。布蘭特原油升到每桶一百零七美元，自從戰爭開始以來已經漲了百分之二十七。美國的汽油平均價格也來到每加侖四點一美元，讓許多人感到壓力。

Iran has proposed a new plan willing to reopen the Strait of Hormuz, but refuses to discuss nuclear weapons. US President Trump canceled a planned peace envoy to Pakistan, escalating tensions. International oil prices have surged as a result. Brent crude has climbed to $107 per barrel, up 27% since the war began. US gasoline prices have also reached an average of $4.10 per gallon, putting pressure on many consumers.

Officer Bartholomew, 38, was shot in the head and tragically died before 1 p.m. He was a veteran officer with ten years of experience. Another officer with twenty-one years on the force remains in critical condition. The suspect has been arrested. The tragedy unfolding inside the hospital has shocked the entire community.

Starting pitcher Shota Imanaga had a poor outing. Although the winning streak has ended, the Cubs' overall performance this season continues to excite fans.
