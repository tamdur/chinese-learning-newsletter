油價漲到六週新高，沙烏地煉油廠傳出被打中

來源：CNBC

美國和伊朗週末互相攻擊，戰火燒到了第三個國家。英國金融時報說，沙烏地阿拉伯石油公司的吉贊煉油廠週一被打中，這是一個月內第二次。

油價因此連漲三天。布蘭特原油每桶漲到九十七點七三美元，是七月二十三日以來最高。荷姆茲海峽幾乎停止通行，過去十天平均每天只有十艘貨船通過，是五月以來最少。

The US and Iran exchanged attacks over the weekend, with the conflict spilling into a third country. According to the Financial Times, Saudi Aramco's Jizan refinery was struck on Monday, marking the second attack within a month. Oil prices surged as a result, gaining for three consecutive days. Brent crude climbed to $97.73 per barrel, the highest level since July 23rd. The Strait of Hormuz has nearly ground to a halt, with an average of only ten tankers passing through daily over the past ten days—the lowest volume since May.

俄軍一夜放出九十二架無人機，和談停住

來源：Kyiv Independent

烏克蘭空軍說，俄羅斯一個晚上發射九十二架攻擊無人機，其中七十一架被攔了下來，南部城市札波羅熱有一棟公寓被打壞。

美國前幾天才幫忙安排和談，兩邊也講好暫時不打對方的首都。但烏克蘭總統澤倫斯基說，他「預期戰爭會繼續」，等於親口承認這次談判沒有成功。

The Ukrainian Air Force said Russia launched 92 attack drones in a single night, with 71 intercepted. An apartment building in the southern city of Zaporizhzhia was damaged in the strikes. The U.S. had arranged talks just days earlier, and both sides had agreed to temporarily avoid targeting each other's capitals. But Ukrainian President Zelenskyy said he "expects the war to continue" — essentially admitting that this round of negotiations has failed.

郵寄投票新規定，送上美國最高法院

來源：SCOTUSblog

美國政府今天向最高法院遞出申請，希望郵寄投票的新規定能在期中選舉前上路。新規定要求郵局只能收下國土安全部公民名單上有名字的人寄回的選票。

反對的二十三個州要在今天上午十點前回覆。麻州法官先前只幫這些州擋下規定，所以同一場全國選舉，可能會有兩套不同的規則，而選票已經寄出去了。

The US government filed an application with the Supreme Court today, seeking to implement new mail-in voting regulations before the midterm elections. The new regulations require the postal service to accept only returned ballots from people whose names appear on the Department of Homeland Security's citizenship list. Twenty-three opposing states must respond before 10 AM this morning. A Massachusetts judge previously blocked the regulations only for those states, creating a situation where the same national election could result in two different sets of rules—while ballots have already been mailed out.

普里茲克在勞工野餐會上開打連任戰

來源：WVIK (NPR)

伊利諾州州長普里茲克週一到東莫林，參加第五十七屆「向勞工致敬」野餐會，正式對上十一月的共和黨對手貝利。

他強調自己把全州最低工資提高到每小時十五美元，今年一月一日已經上路，還通過工作安全法律和保障工人談判權利的修憲案。聯邦參議員達克沃斯也到場幫忙。

Illinois Governor Pritzker took on Republican opponent Bailey on Monday at the 57th annual "Salute to Labor" picnic in East Moline, formally launching his November campaign. He emphasized his record raising the statewide minimum wage to fifteen dollars per hour—a measure that took effect on January 1st of this year—along with new workplace safety legislation and a constitutional amendment protecting workers' collective bargaining rights. U.S. Senator Duckworth also attended to show her support.

小熊隊克羅阿姆斯壯挑戰四十轟四十盜

來源：MLB.com Cubs

小熊隊中外野手克羅阿姆斯壯本季打出第四十支全壘打，再加上三十三次盜壘、打擊率兩成八。他是小熊隊史上第一個單季四十轟加三十盜的球員。

球季只剩十八場，只要再成功盜壘七次，他就會成為大聯盟歷史上第八位「四十轟四十盜」的球員，而他今年才二十四歲。

Cubs center fielder Pete Crow-Armstrong hit his 40th home run this season, adding 33 stolen bases and a .280 batting average to his resume. He's the first player in Cubs franchise history to record 40 home runs and 30 steals in a single season. With 18 games remaining, he needs just seven more stolen bases to become the eighth player in MLB history to achieve the 40-40 milestone—and he's only 24 years old.

兵工廠線：歐冠首戰作客拿坡里

來源：Yahoo Sports

兵工廠線——球隊週三晚上作客義大利的拿坡里，踢新球季歐洲冠軍聯賽的第一場。兵工廠近六場五勝一和，上週日還以二比一逆轉切爾西。

拿坡里由阿萊格里帶隊，兩名中後衛布翁久爾諾和馬里亞努奇都受傷缺陣。兵工廠真正的難題是人手：球隊賣掉耶穌和馬丁內利，卻沒有補進任何前鋒，聯賽和歐冠要靠同一批人撐完。

Arsenal travels to Naples on Wednesday night for their first Champions League match of the new season. The team arrives in excellent form—five wins and a draw from their last six games, including a 2-1 comeback against Chelsea last Sunday. Naples is managed by Allegri, though centre backs Buongiorno and Marianucci are both sidelined by injury. Arsenal's real headache is squad depth: having sold Jesus and Martinelli, they brought in no new forwards, leaving the same group to carry both the domestic league and the Champions League.
