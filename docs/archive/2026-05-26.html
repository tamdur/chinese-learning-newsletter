教宗良十四世發表首份 AI 通諭，呼籲全球管制

來源：Vatican News / NCR

教宗良十四世五月二十五日發表他的第一份通諭（教宗寫給全世界的重要信），主題是 AI。這份文件長達兩百三十五頁，名叫「偉大的人性」，警告 AI 如果沒有嚴格的規定，可能變成「控制和排斥」的工具。

特別的是，教宗和 Anthropic 共同創辦人 Chris Olah 一起在梵蒂岡發表這份文件。三十三歲的科技人和教宗坐在一起，呼籲教會和科技業合作保護人類安全。

來源：Vatican News / NCR

Pope Leo XIV issued his first encyclical on May 25th—a major letter from the Pope to the world—focused on artificial intelligence. The 235-page document, titled "The Greatness of Humanity," warns that AI could become a tool of "control and exclusion" without strict regulation. Notably, the Pope released the document at the Vatican alongside Anthropic co-founder Chris Olah. The 33-year-old technologist sat beside the Pope to call for cooperation between the Church and the tech industry to safeguard human security.

美國與伊朗達成和平框架，荷乘茲海峽可望重開

來源：CBS News

美國和伊朗的談判代表同意了一個和平框架：先停火六十天，再重新開放荷乘茲海峽（Strait of Hormuz，全球最重要的石油運送通道）讓船通行，同時讓伊朗自由賣石油。

不過雙方在核武問題上還有很大的分歧（不同意見）。就在談判進行的同時，美國還對伊朗的飛彈基地發動了攻擊，情況很複雜。

來源：CBS News

Negotiators from the United States and Iran have agreed on a peace framework: a sixty-day ceasefire first, followed by the reopening of the Strait of Hormuz—the world's most critical oil shipping channel—to allow vessels to pass through, while also permitting Iran to sell oil freely. However, the two sides remain deeply divided on the nuclear weapons issue. Complicating matters, the United States launched attacks on Iranian missile bases even as negotiations were underway. The situation is complex.

OpenAI 完成史上最大募資，估值八千五百二十億美元

來源：CNBC / OpenAI

OpenAI 完成了人類歷史上最大的一次募資（向別人要錢來做事）：一共拿到一千兩百二十億美元，公司估值達到八千五百二十億美元。Amazon 出了五百億，Nvidia 和 SoftBank 各出三百億。

OpenAI 也計畫在今年底或明年初上市（讓一般人也能買股票），這將會是美國歷史上最大的上市案。

來源：CNBC / OpenAI

OpenAI has completed the largest fundraising round in human history, raising a total of $122 billion and reaching a company valuation of $852 billion. Amazon contributed $50 billion, while Nvidia and SoftBank each invested $30 billion. OpenAI also plans to go public by the end of this year or early next year, which would make it the largest initial public offering in US history.

聯準會八比四決定不降息，內部意見嚴重分裂

來源：Federal Reserve / Al Jazeera

美國聯準會（管錢的中央銀行）以八票對四票決定維持利率在 3.5%-3.75%，不降也不升。四票反對是自一九九二年以來最多的，顯示內部意見很不一樣。

問題出在中東戰爭讓石油變貴，推高了物價。聯準會把通膨（東西越來越貴）從「有點高」改成「高」，讓市場很緊張。

來源：Federal Reserve / Al Jazeera

The U.S. Federal Reserve voted 8 to 4 to keep interest rates steady at 3.5%-3.75%, neither raising nor lowering them. The four dissenting votes represent the most opposition since 1992, signaling serious internal disagreement. The core problem: conflict in the Middle East has driven up oil prices, pushing inflation higher. The Fed upgraded its inflation assessment from "somewhat elevated" to "elevated," rattling markets.

小乘隊吞下九連敗，創四年來最長連敗紀錄

來源：ESPN / Bleacher Nation

芝加哥小乘隊（Cubs）以一比二輸給海盜隊（Pirates），連續輸了九場，是二〇二二年以來最慘的一次。海盜隊的 Henry Davis 在第七局打出全壘打，逆轉了比賽。

小乘隊先發投手 Ben Brown 其實投得很好，六局只失一分，但打線只得一分實在太少了。

來源：ESPN / Bleacher Nation

The Chicago Cubs fell to the Pirates 1-2, extending their losing streak to nine games—their worst stretch since 2022. Henry Davis hit a go-ahead home run for Pittsburgh in the seventh inning. Cubs starter Ben Brown actually pitched well, allowing just one run over six innings, but the offense managed only one run, which simply wasn't enough.

兵工廠等了二十二年，終於再奪英超冠軍

來源：ESPN / Arsenal.com

兵工廠線——兵工廠（Arsenal）在英超最後一天以二比一打敗水晶宮（Crystal Palace），拿下自二〇〇四年以來的第一個英超冠軍！等了整整二十二年。Gabriel Jesus 和 Noni Madueke 各進一球。

隊長 Odegaard 在客場舉起獎盃，球迷歡呼。但故事還沒結束：五月三十日，兵工廠將在歐冠決賽對上巴黎聖日耳曼（PSG），挑戰雙冠王！

來源：ESPN / Arsenal.com

Arsenal beat Crystal Palace 2–1 on the final day of the Premier League season, claiming their first top-flight title since 2004. Twenty-two years of waiting came to an end. Gabriel Jesus and Noni Madueke scored the goals. Captain Odegaard lifted the trophy away from home as fans erupted. But the story isn't finished yet: on May 30th, Arsenal will face Paris Saint-Germain in the Champions League final, chasing the double.
