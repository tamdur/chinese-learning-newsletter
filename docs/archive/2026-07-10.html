川普對巴西課五成關稅 只因波索納洛案

來源：CBS News

美國總統川普在七月九日再次確認，要對所有巴西商品課徵百分之五十的關稅。他說這不是因為貿易吵架，而是因為巴西正在「政治追殺」前總統波索納洛。

巴西總統魯拉很生氣，說這是在威脅巴西的主權（自己國家作主的權利），還說會用「經濟對等法」反擊，對美國商品也課一樣的稅。這是美洲兩個最大經濟體的一場硬碰硬。

US President Trump on July 9th once again confirmed plans to impose 50% tariffs on all Brazilian goods. He said this isn't about a trade dispute, but rather Brazil's "political persecution" of former president Bolsonaro. Brazilian President Lula was furious, calling it a threat to Brazil's sovereignty—the right of a country to govern itself—and vowed to strike back with an "economic reciprocity law," imposing the same tariffs on American goods. This is a head-to-head clash between the two largest economies in the Americas.

xAI 推出 Grok 4.5 主打便宜寫程式

來源：TechCrunch

馬斯克的公司 xAI 在七月八日推出新的 AI 模型 Grok 4.5，專門用來寫電腦程式。馬斯克說它「跟最強的模型差不多，但是更快」。

最特別的是價格。它每一百萬個字的收費只要兩美元，比對手便宜六成以上。這讓 AI 公司之間的比賽，從「誰最強」變成「誰最便宜」。

Musk's company xAI launched a new AI model called Grok 4.5 on July 8th, specifically designed for writing code. Musk says it "matches the strongest models out there, but it's faster." What really stands out is the price: just two dollars per million tokens—over 60% cheaper than competitors. It marks a telling shift in how AI companies compete: from "who's strongest?" to "who's cheapest?"

參議院預算談判破局 恐再度政府停擺

來源：The Hill

美國參議院兩黨的預算談判在週四破局。共和黨的柯林斯和民主黨的莫瑞談不攏，柯林斯只好取消四個預算案的審查會。

兩黨吵的是錢要怎麼花。民主黨說國防花太多、民生太少，共和黨不肯讓步。很多議員擔心，今年秋天政府可能又要停擺（沒錢運作）。

U.S. Senate budget talks between the two parties collapsed on Thursday. Republican Collins and Democrat Murray failed to reach common ground, forcing Collins to cancel the markup sessions on four budget bills. The dispute hinges on spending priorities. Democrats argue that defense is being overfunded while social programs are underfunded, but Republicans refuse to compromise. Many lawmakers now fear that the government could shut down again this fall for lack of funding.

芝加哥移民逮捕再增加 四社區傳查緝

來源：Block Club Chicago

芝加哥的移民逮捕又變多了。一個叫「復活計畫」的團體說，六月有一百七十人來求助，比五月的大約一百人還多。

這禮拜，移民官員出現在蓋吉公園、皮爾森等四個社區，至少抓了三個人。自從「中途突襲」行動去年結束後，查緝反而悄悄回升，讓幫忙的團體忙得團團轉。

Immigration arrests in Chicago are on the rise again. An organization called the Resurrection Project reports that 170 people sought help from them in June, up from roughly 100 in May.

This week, immigration officers appeared in four neighborhoods including Gage Park and Pilsen, making at least three arrests. Since the "Midway Blitz" operation ended last year, crackdowns have quietly resumed, leaving aid organizations stretched thin.

熊隊補進中鋒布萊柏瑞 門票卻出包

來源：Bleacher Nation

芝加哥熊隊補進資深中鋒布萊柏瑞，要加強保護年輕的四分衛。這是新教練強生上任後，球隊陣容的一個重要動作。

不過就在同一天，熊隊的訓練營門票開賣卻出了問題，讓想看球的球迷很不開心。這也是熊隊最近難得的一則球員新聞。

The Chicago Bears signed veteran center Bradbury to strengthen protection for their young quarterback. This is an important roster move by new head coach Johnson since he took the helm. However, on the same day, the Bears' training camp ticket sales ran into problems that left fans wanting to watch the team disappointed. It's also a rare bit of player news for the Bears lately.
