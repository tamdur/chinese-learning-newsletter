美國國防部把 ChatGPT 和 Grok 發給三百萬人

來源：Fortune

美國國防部九月一日宣布，把 OpenAI 的 ChatGPT Mil 和 xAI 的 Grok 加進自己的安全網站 GenAI.mil，之前上面只有 Google 的 Gemini。

國防部有大約三百萬名軍人和職員，已經有一百七十萬人在用這個網站。這些模型通過了安全審核，可以處理敏感的公文。

值得注意的是，Anthropic 的 Claude 沒有入選。願不願意讓軍方使用，成了各家公司的分界線。

The US Department of Defense announced on September 1st that it has added OpenAI's ChatGPT Mil and xAI's Grok to its secure platform, GenAI.mil, which previously offered only Google's Gemini.

The Pentagon has roughly three million military personnel and civilian staff, of whom 1.7 million are already using the platform. These AI models have cleared security reviews and can handle sensitive government documents.

Notably, Anthropic's Claude was not selected. Willingness to permit military use has emerged as a dividing line among the major AI companies.

最高法院放行　白宮宴會廳繼續施工

來源：NPR

美國最高法院以五比四判決，讓白宮那座造價四億美元的新宴會廳繼續蓋下去。

法官沒有說這棟建築合不合法，只說提告的「國家歷史保護信託」大概沒有資格告。判決書寫著，單純的不喜歡不算真正受到傷害。

四位反對的法官裡有首席法官羅伯茲。他們認為這個工程很可能違法。等於是說，因為沒人有資格告，一棟可能違法的建築就照蓋。

The US Supreme Court ruled 5-4 to let construction continue on the White House's new $400 million ballroom.

The justices did not rule on whether the building is lawful, only that the plaintiff — the National Trust for Historic Preservation — likely lacks standing to sue. The opinion stated that simply disliking something does not amount to genuine harm.

Chief Justice Roberts was among the four dissenters. They concluded the project is likely unlawful. In effect: because no one has standing to challenge it, a probably illegal building goes up anyway.

上合組織峰會　普丁會見伊朗總統談繞開美元

來源：The Moscow Times

上海合作組織的高峰會九月一日在吉爾吉斯的比什凱克結束。俄羅斯總統普丁和伊朗總統佩澤希齊揚見面，這是二月伊朗戰爭開打後兩人第一次會談。

會議重點是減少對美元的依賴，讓石油買賣不必經過美國的金融系統。中國的習近平、印度的莫迪也都到場。

伊朗被鄰國切斷貿易以後，回應不是打仗，而是和莫斯科、北京另外建一套付錢的方法。

The Shanghai Cooperation Organisation summit concluded on September 1st in Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan. Russian President Putin and Iranian President Pezeshkian met face to face for the first time since the war with Iran broke out in February.

The summit's central focus was reducing reliance on the US dollar, so that oil trading need not pass through America's financial system. China's Xi Jinping and India's Modi also attended.

After Iran's neighbours cut off trade, its response was not military — it was to build an alternative payments system together with Moscow and Beijing.

今年一半以上的裁員　公司說是因為 AI

來源：IBTimes UK

追蹤裁員的網站 Layoffs.fyi 九月一日公布：今年到現在，有百分之五十四的裁員案，公司自己說跟人工智慧或自動化有關，影響超過十七萬人。

去年這個比例還不到百分之八。寫程式、客服和內容製作最容易被砍，AI 安全和技術工人還在找人。

可是不久前紐約聯邦準備銀行的研究說，整體數字裡看不出 AI 造成失業。公司說的和數據看到的對不上，這才是重點。

On September 1st, the layoff-tracking site Layoffs.fyi reported that 54 percent of this year's job cuts have been attributed by the companies themselves to artificial intelligence or automation, affecting more than 170,000 people.

Last year that share was under 8 percent. Programming, customer service and content production are the most exposed, while AI safety and skilled technical roles are still hiring.

Yet recent research from the Federal Reserve Bank of New York found no sign of AI-driven unemployment in the aggregate data. The gap between what companies say and what the numbers show is the real story.

密西根新教頭首戰在即　主力左截鋒受傷

來源：ClickOnDetroit

密西根大學美式足球新總教練惠廷厄姆九月一日說，負責保護四分衛的左截鋒巴巴洛拉受傷，會缺席星期六的開幕戰。

他同時宣布馬歇爾是先發跑鋒。這份名單他從八月底就一直不肯公開，這次被傷勢逼著提早說出來。

惠廷厄姆在猶他大學帶了二十一年，開幕戰十七勝四敗。星期六對上西密西根大學，是他新時代的第一次考試。

Michigan's new head football coach, Kyle Whittingham, said on September 1st that left tackle Andrew Babalola — the man responsible for protecting the quarterback — is injured and will miss Saturday's season opener.

He also named Jordan Marshall the starting running back. Whittingham had refused to release this depth chart since late August; the injury forced him to reveal it early.

Whittingham coached at the University of Utah for twenty-one years, going 17-4 in season openers. Saturday's game against Western Michigan is the first test of his new era.

兵工廠關窗前再送走一人　努瓦內里外借多特蒙德

來源：Yahoo Sports

兵工廠線——轉會窗九月一日關門，兵工廠最後一筆交易是把十九歲的努瓦內里外借給德國的多特蒙德，租一整季，沒有買斷條款，明年夏天一定回來。

多特蒙德因為前鋒受傷缺人，會付他全部薪水，兵工廠則留了提前把人叫回來的權利。

同一天，熱蘇斯轉去巴塞隆納，維艾拉去漢堡。一天送走三名攻擊球員，一個也沒買進；隊上最被看好的自家小將，反而去別隊磨練。

The Arsenal beat — the transfer window closed on September 1st, and Arsenal's final piece of business was loaning nineteen-year-old Ethan Nwaneri to Borussia Dortmund of Germany for the full season. There is no option to buy, so he returns next summer.

Dortmund, short of forwards through injury, will pay his wages in full, while Arsenal kept the right to recall him early.

The same day, Gabriel Jesus moved to Barcelona and Fabio Vieira to Hamburg. Three attacking players out in a single day and none brought in — and the club's most highly rated academy prospect goes elsewhere to develop.
