輝達今天交成績單，AI泡沫再受考驗

來源：S&P Global

美國晶片大廠輝達（Nvidia）今天股市關門後，會公布最新一季的成績。公司自己預估，這一季營收（賣東西收到的錢）大約1080億美元，比上一季多12%。

這個月初，AI股票一週內總共跌掉大約1兆美元，輝達也跌了7%左右。很多人擔心AI是一個「泡沫」：看起來很大，其實裡面是空的。

輝達的晶片是AI資料中心的心臟。如果數字比預期好，大家會比較放心；如果不好，市場可能又要緊張了。

Nvidia, the American chip giant, reports its latest quarterly results after the stock market closes today. The company itself expects revenue (the money it takes in from sales) of about $108 billion this quarter, up 12% from the previous quarter. Earlier this month, AI stocks lost roughly $1 trillion in value over a single week, and Nvidia fell around 7% as well. Many people worry that AI is a "bubble": it looks big, but there is nothing solid inside. Nvidia's chips are the heart of AI data centers. If the numbers beat expectations, people will feel more reassured; if they fall short, the market may get nervous again.
