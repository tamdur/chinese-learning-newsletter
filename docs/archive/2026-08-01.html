哈瑪斯首次同意交出武器

來源：CNN

七月三十一日，哈瑪斯第一次答應了一份具體的交出武器計畫。這份計畫由美國支持的「和平理事會」提出，一共十五點。哈瑪斯要在九十天內交出重型武器，還有地道（地下通道）的地圖。

哈瑪斯也開出條件：以色列要停止空襲、退回「黃線」，並讓更多救援物資進入加薩。聯合國最高負責人古特雷斯說，這是「最近少有的好消息」。

On July 31st, Hamas agreed for the first time to a concrete plan for handing over its weapons. Presented by the US-backed "Board of Peace," the plan contains fifteen points. Hamas would surrender its heavy weapons within ninety days, along with maps of the tunnels.

Hamas also set conditions: Israel must cease airstrikes, withdraw to the "yellow line," and allow more humanitarian aid into Gaza. UN Secretary-General Guterres said this is "rare good news in recent times."

DeepSeek 便宜版模型打敗自家旗艦

來源：MarkTechPost

中國人工智慧公司 DeepSeek 在七月三十一日推出新版本 V4-Flash-0731。這個又快又便宜的版本，在九項寫程式和「代理」（讓電腦自己動手做事）的測驗裡，全部贏過公司自己最貴的旗艦模型。

在一項叫 Terminal Bench 的測驗上，分數從六十一點八跳到八十二點七。兩個版本的架構一樣，進步全靠訓練方法。不過這些數字是公司自己測的，還沒有別的實驗室證實。

Chinese AI company DeepSeek launched a new version, V4-Flash-0731, on July 31st. This faster, cheaper version outperformed the company's own most expensive flagship model across all nine tests for coding and "agents" — systems that let a computer carry out tasks on its own.

On a benchmark called Terminal Bench, the score jumped from 61.8 to 82.7. Both versions share the same architecture, so the improvement comes entirely from training methodology. However, these numbers come from the company's own testing and have not yet been verified by any independent laboratory.

伊利諾州立法管社群媒體怎麼對待兒童

來源：Capitol News Illinois

伊利諾州長普利茲克在七月三十一日簽署《兒童社群媒體安全法》。以後平台要把未成年人的帳號預設成不公開，晚上十點到早上七點不能傳通知，也要限制他們在應用程式裡買虛擬貨幣。

這條法律在州議會兩院都是全票通過，很少見。不過要等到二〇二八年一月一日才開始實施，由州檢察長負責執行。

Illinois Governor Pritzker signed the Children's Social Media Safety Act on July 31st. Going forward, platforms must set minors' accounts to private by default, cannot send them notifications between 10 p.m. and 7 a.m., and must restrict their ability to buy virtual currency inside apps.

The law passed unanimously in both chambers of the state legislature, which is rare. However, it does not take effect until January 1, 2028, and will be enforced by the state attorney general.

全球第一個「光子時間晶體」誕生

來源：ScienceDaily

法國巴黎綜合理工學院和德國 HZDR 研究中心的團隊，在《自然》期刊發表世界第一個用光做成的「光子時間晶體」。一般的晶體是原子在空間裡排得很整齊，這種新材料則是性質在時間裡規律地變化。

他們用兆赫（一種頻率很高的電波）照射黃金和半導體做的材料，把光的損耗減少了一半以上。這條路未來可能做出超快的雷射，還有用光計算的電腦。

A team from France's École Polytechnique and Germany's HZDR research centre has published the world's first "photonic time crystal" — one made out of light — in the journal Nature. Where an ordinary crystal has its atoms arranged in a neat pattern in space, this new material has properties that vary regularly in time.

The researchers used terahertz radiation, a very high-frequency radio wave, to illuminate a material made of gold and semiconductor, cutting the loss of light by more than half. The approach could eventually lead to ultra-fast lasers and computers that calculate with light.

美國職棒的球員交易在八月三日下午六點截止。芝加哥小熊目前五十九勝四十五敗，排在國聯外卡第一，不過在中區還落後密爾瓦基五場半。

Major League Baseball's trade deadline falls at 6 p.m. on August 3rd. The Chicago Cubs currently sit at 59-45, first in the National League wild card race, though they still trail Milwaukee by five and a half games in the Central.

Reports have named the pitchers the club is after for the first time: Peralta, Holmes, Gausman, and Detroit's ace Skubal. More importantly, the front office is willing to give up a top prospect to land one — provided that pitcher can play for the team for several more years.
