川普在白宮會見澤倫斯基 烏克蘭想要更多防空武器

來源：Kyiv Post

美國總統川普七月二十八日上午在白宮會見烏克蘭總統澤倫斯基。這是本月美國同意烏克蘭自己生產「愛國者」防空飛彈之後，兩人第一次面對面談話。

烏克蘭希望再拿到更多防空和遠程攻擊的武器。最近烏克蘭的無人機一再打中俄羅斯的石油碼頭，俄羅斯也連續兩年取消海軍節閱兵（軍隊排隊遊行給大家看），等於承認自己的後方已經不安全。

U.S. President Trump met with Ukrainian President Zelensky at the White House on the morning of July 28. This was the first face-to-face meeting between the two leaders since the U.S. agreed this month to let Ukraine manufacture its own Patriot air-defense missiles.

Ukraine is seeking additional air-defense and long-range strike weapons. Ukrainian drones have repeatedly struck Russian oil terminals in recent weeks, and Russia has canceled its Navy Day military parade for a second consecutive year — a tacit admission that its own rear areas are no longer safe.

郵寄選票禁令上訴 政府要求最高法院八月前決定

來源：Washington Post / AP

美國司法部七月二十七日向最高法院提出緊急申請，希望解除下級法院的禁令。川普三月簽署的命令規定，郵局只能把郵寄選票送給政府新建的「公民名單」上的人。

二十三個州和華盛頓特區的官員已經告上法院，認為憲法把選舉規則交給各州和國會，不是交給總統。政府要求最高法院八月上旬就給答案，好趕在十一月期中選舉（總統任期中間的國會選舉）之前實施。

On July 27 the U.S. Justice Department filed an emergency application with the Supreme Court seeking to lift a lower court's injunction. An executive order Trump signed in March restricts the Postal Service to delivering mail ballots only to people whose names appear on a newly created government "citizenship list."

Officials from 23 states and Washington, D.C. have gone to court, arguing that the Constitution assigns election rules to the states and Congress, not to the president. The government is asking the Supreme Court for an answer by early August, so the changes can be in place before November's midterm elections — the congressional races held midway through a presidential term.

輝達與韓國SK集團簽下五千億美元AI大單

來源：Nvidia Newsroom

美國晶片公司輝達（Nvidia）和韓國SK集團七月二十五日簽下意向書，總金額超過五千億美元。SK電訊要在韓國蓋一座超大的AI工廠，用電量高達兩百萬千瓦（2GW），第一期預計二○二七年啟用。

這座工廠會用輝達下一代的 Vera Rubin 平台，記憶體由韓國的SK海力士長期供應。最近兩週股市正擔心AI投資過熱，產業這邊卻還在繼續砸錢蓋廠。

U.S. chipmaker Nvidia and South Korea's SK Group signed letters of intent on July 25 covering more than $500 billion. SK Telecom will build an enormous AI factory in South Korea drawing up to 2 gigawatts of power, with the first phase expected to come online in 2027.

The facility will run on Nvidia's next-generation Vera Rubin platform, with memory supplied long-term by South Korea's SK Hynix. Over the past two weeks the stock market has been worrying that AI investment is overheating — yet the industry itself keeps pouring money into new plants.

伊利諾新法 全州公立學校上課全程禁用手機

來源：CBS Chicago

伊利諾州長普利茲克簽署 SB 2427 法案，全州大約八百五十個公立學區都要實施「從上課鐘到放學鐘」的手機禁令，從二○二七學年開始。

小學到國中的學生整天都不能用手機，高中生在午餐和下課時間比較有彈性。醫療需要、特殊教育、正在學英文和要照顧家人的學生可以例外，學校也不准用罰錢或停學來處罰。芝加哥教師工會反對，認為執行的壓力都落在老師身上。

Illinois Governor Pritzker signed Senate Bill 2427, requiring all roughly 850 public school districts in the state to adopt a bell-to-bell phone ban starting with the 2027 school year.

Elementary and middle school students may not use phones at any point in the day, while high schoolers get more flexibility at lunch and between classes. Exceptions are allowed for students with medical needs, those in special education, English learners, and students caring for family members, and schools may not punish violations with fines or suspension. The Chicago Teachers Union opposed the bill, arguing the burden of enforcement falls entirely on teachers.

小熊客場七比三擊敗紅雀 交易截止前先贏一場

來源：MLB.com

芝加哥小熊七月二十七日到聖路易的布希球場，以七比三打敗紅雀，拿下四連戰的第一勝。全隊敲出十四支安打，二壘手霍納在比賽尾聲擊出全壘打，把比數拉開。

這是小熊最近四場比賽的第三勝。離八月三日的交易截止日只剩六天，小熊正在爭外卡（進季後賽的另一張門票）。先在客場贏下第一場，球團就能帶著好成績去補強球隊，而不是急著救火。

The Chicago Cubs traveled to Busch Stadium in St. Louis on July 27 and beat the Cardinals 7-3, taking the opener of a four-game series. The team collected fourteen hits, and second baseman Nico Hoerner homered late in the game to stretch the lead.

It was the Cubs' third win in their last four games. With just six days left until the August 3 trade deadline, Chicago is chasing a wild-card spot — the other ticket into the postseason. Winning the first game on the road means the front office can go shopping from a position of strength rather than scrambling to put out a fire.

The good news is on the injury front: defenders Ben White and Jurriën Timber are both back in full training and could feature on Saturday.
