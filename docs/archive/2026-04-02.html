法院擋下國防部禁用Anthropic AI命令

Just Security

一位美國法官做出了一個很重要的決定。川普政府之前命令所有政府機關不可以使用Anthropic的AI工具，但法官林麗達說這違反了言論自由。這是第一次有法院把政府禁止使用某家AI公司當作言論自由的問題來看。目前川普政府面對超過700件法律挑戰，這件是其中最受關注的之一。

A U.S. judge made a momentous ruling. The Trump administration had previously ordered all government agencies to stop using Anthropic's AI tools, but Judge Linda Lin said this violated free speech rights. This is the first time a court has examined a government ban on a specific AI company's tools through the lens of free speech. The Trump administration currently faces more than 700 legal challenges, and this case is among the most closely watched.

聯準會左右為難：油價飆漲加上失業潮

FinancialContent / CNBC

美國的中央銀行現在很頭痛。因為伊朗戰爭，油價已經漲到每桶100美元，物價越來越高。同時，二月少了9.2萬個工作機會。物價在漲但經濟在變差，這叫做「停滯性通膨」。聯準會把利率維持在3.5%-3.75%，但理事鮑曼暗示今年可能降息三次。市場昨天因為停火消息小漲了一點。

The Federal Reserve is now in a bind. Because of the Iran war, oil prices have surged to $100 per barrel, and inflation is rising. Meanwhile, February saw 92,000 job losses. When prices rise while the economy weakens, that's called stagflation. The Fed is holding interest rates steady at 3.5%-3.75%, but Fed Governor Bowman hinted that three rate cuts may come this year. Markets ticked up slightly yesterday on ceasefire news.

CNN Politics

The U.S. Supreme Court heard arguments yesterday in a major case. Trump previously signed an executive order trying to eliminate the automatic right to citizenship for anyone born in the United States. This right is written in the Fourteenth Amendment to the Constitution. This is the first time the Supreme Court has directly addressed whether birthright citizenship itself is constitutional, not just procedural questions. The outcome will affect many people.

The five key points are: an immediate ceasefire, resumption of peace negotiations, protection of civilians, ensuring safe passage for ships through the Strait of Hormuz, and establishing a multilateral cooperation framework. This represents a diplomatic response to U.S. military operations.

伊朗戰爭與油價：荷莫茲海峽關閉，全球發抖

來源：CNN、CNBC、CBS News

今天油價大漲近8%，布蘭特原油快要到110美元一桶了。原因很簡單：川普昨晚對全國講話，說戰爭還要打兩三個星期，而且可能會打伊朗的油田。市場聽了嚇壞了。

荷莫茲海峽從三月初關閉到現在，全世界兩成的石油都出不來。國際能源署警告四月會更糟，因為之前還在路上的船到了，但新的船已經沒有了。美國加油站的油已經漲到每加侖4.08美元，比開戰前貴了一塊多。分析師說如果海峽繼續關，油價可能衝到150到200美元。簡單說，很不妙。

Oil prices surged nearly 8% today, with Brent crude approaching $110 per barrel. The reason is straightforward: Trump addressed the nation last night, saying the war will continue for two to three more weeks and may target Iran's oil fields. Markets were shaken by the news.

The Strait of Hormuz has been closed since early March, blocking about 20% of the world's oil supply. The International Energy Agency warns that April will be worse, as previously-shipped vessels are arriving while new shipments have stopped. Gas prices at US pumps have climbed to $4.08 per gallon, more than a dollar higher than before the war began. Analysts say if the strait remains closed, oil prices could spike to $150 to $200 per barrel. In short, the outlook is bleak.
