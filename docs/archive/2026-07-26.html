法國西班牙森林大火，二十六萬多人逃離家園

來源：CNN

歐洲正碰上破紀錄的熱浪，法國和西班牙同時燒起森林大火。到七月二十五日，兩國已經有超過二十六萬五千人被迫離開家園。

法國西南部吉倫特省的火勢最猛，燒掉三萬兩千七百公頃，大約是巴黎市的三倍大，還燒毀一百四十間房子。法國總理說，這種規模「以前從來沒有見過」。

法國今年燒掉的森林將近九萬八千公頃，創下新高。科學家說，長期乾旱加上一波又一波的熱浪，和氣候變遷有關。

Europe is experiencing record-breaking heat waves, and forest fires are raging simultaneously in France and Spain. By July 25th, over 265,000 people from both countries have been forced to flee their homes. The fires are most intense in the Gironde region of southwestern France, burning some 32,700 hectares—roughly three times the size of Paris—and destroying 140 houses. France's Prime Minister said this scale of fire "has never been seen before." France has burned nearly 98,000 hectares of forest this year, setting a new record. Scientists say the prolonged drought combined with successive heat waves are linked to climate change.

油價破百，聯準會下週可能升息

來源：The Motley Fool

美國的中央銀行「聯準會」七月二十八、二十九日要開會。一個星期前，大家都以為利率不會動；現在市場認為升息的機會大約有三分之一。

原因是油價。國際油價本週一度突破一桶一百美元，是二零二二年以來第一次，星期五又跌回九十七美元左右。中東的戰事讓石油運送變得不順。

聯準會理事庫克說，美國物價一年漲了百分之三點七，離百分之二的目標還很遠。

The Federal Reserve, America's central bank, will meet July 28 and 29. A week ago, markets expected rates to hold steady; now traders give a roughly one-in-three chance of a rate hike. The culprit: oil prices. International crude briefly topped $100 a barrel this week—the first time since 2022—before settling back around $97 on Friday. Middle East tensions have disrupted oil shipments. Fed Governor Cook noted that U.S. inflation rose 3.7% over the past year, still well above the Fed's 2% target.

上訴法院擋下川普郵寄投票新規定

來源：US News (AP wire)

美國聯邦上訴法院七月二十五日以二比一裁定，川普總統想收緊郵寄投票的行政命令，在十一月期中選舉前不能執行。

這道命令要政府做一份合格選民名單，名單上沒有的人，郵局就不能把選票送到他手上。六月時波士頓的法官已經判定它違憲（違反憲法），範圍涵蓋將近二十四個州和華盛頓特區。

這是法院第一次真的攔下政府在選舉規則上的動作。所以十一月投票，各州還是照原本的規矩辦。

On July 25th, a federal appeals court ruled 2-1 that President Trump's executive order to tighten mail-in voting rules cannot take effect before the November midterm election. The order would require the government to compile a list of eligible voters, and those whose names don't appear on it would be barred from receiving their ballots by mail. A Boston judge had already ruled the order unconstitutional in June, a decision covering nearly 24 states and Washington D.C. This marks the first time the court has actually blocked the government's action on voting rules. For the November elections, then, states will continue operating under their existing procedures.

芝加哥一夜六起槍擊，五人死亡

來源：Chicago Sun-Times

七月二十四日傍晚到二十五日凌晨，芝加哥發生六起槍擊案，五個人死亡，還有人受傷，其中包括一名十三歲男孩。

死者分別倒在海德公園、上城和西加菲爾公園。最早一起在下午五點三十五分，一對年輕男女坐在車上被人開槍，警方認為和家庭糾紛有關。

這一夜也讓十一月的公投更受注意：芝加哥要決定，要不要把之前停用的「槍聲偵測系統」裝回來。

Between the evening of July 24 and the early morning of July 25, Chicago experienced six shooting incidents that killed five people and wounded several others, including a thirteen-year-old boy. The victims were found in Hyde Park, Uptown, and West Garfield Park. The first shooting occurred at 5:35 p.m., when a young couple was shot while sitting in their car; police suspect it was connected to a domestic dispute. The violence has also drawn increased attention to an upcoming November ballot measure: whether Chicago should restore its previously deactivated gunshot detection system.

熊隊開訓，首度以分區冠軍身分備戰

來源：ChicagoBears.com

芝加哥熊隊的新球員七月二十五日到訓練基地報到，二零二六年的夏季訓練營正式開始。這是總教練強森帶隊的第二年。

心情不太一樣。去年熊隊拿下二零一八年以來第一座國聯北區冠軍，今年是來守冠軍的，不是來重建的。

球隊也公布開放給球迷看的練習時間表，一共十場，免費入場，第一場在七月三十一日，最後一場在八月二十五日。

Chicago Bears newcomers reported to the training facility on July 25th, officially kicking off the 2026 summer training camp. This marks head coach Johnson's second year at the helm, and the atmosphere feels different. Last year, the Bears clinched their first NFC North division title since 2018, so this year is about defending the crown rather than rebuilding. The team also unveiled its open practice schedule for fans — ten sessions in all, with free admission, running from July 31st through August 25th.

兵工廠線：阿森納把目標指向維尼修斯

來源：Sports Mole

兵工廠線——七月二十六日有多家媒體報導，阿森納正在研究引進皇家馬德里的巴西邊鋒維尼修斯。這是球隊今年夏天被傳出的最大名字。

機會來自合約。維尼修斯和皇馬的合約只剩一年，續約談了很久都沒進展，雙方星期一還要再談。阿森納打算等這場談判的結果再決定。

要提醒的是，這目前只是傳聞，兩家球會還沒有接觸。同時阿森納也準備對紐卡索中場布魯諾提出第三次出價，大約七千萬英鎊。

Arsenal Line — Multiple media outlets reported on July 26 that Arsenal is exploring a move for Real Madrid's Brazilian winger Vinícius Júnior. This is the biggest name linked to the club all summer. The opportunity stems from contract circumstances: Vinícius's contract with Real Madrid has just one year remaining, renewal negotiations have stalled despite lengthy discussions, and both sides are scheduled to meet again on Monday. Arsenal is waiting to see how those talks unfold before making a decision. It's worth noting that this remains speculation at this stage, and the two clubs have not yet made contact. Meanwhile, Arsenal is also preparing to submit a third bid for Newcastle midfielder Bruno, approximately £70 million.
