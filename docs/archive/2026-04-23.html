伊朗在荷莫茲海峽搶走兩艘船，美國封鎖不停

來源：NBC News / CNN

美國總統Trump把和伊朗的停火延長，沒有時間限制，但美國海軍的封鎖（不讓船進出）繼續不變。伊朗說這是「戰爭行為」，馬上在荷莫茲海峽搶走兩艘國際貨船。

到目前為止，美軍已經攔下31艘船。白宮說，被搶的不是美國船，所以不算違反停火。雙方還是沒辦法談好。

President Trump extended the ceasefire with Iran indefinitely, but the U.S. Navy's blockade—preventing ships from entering or leaving—remains unchanged. Iran called this an "act of war" and immediately seized two international cargo vessels in the Strait of Hormuz. So far, U.S. forces have intercepted 31 ships. The White House said the seized vessels weren't American, so they don't violate the ceasefire. The two sides still can't reach a deal.

阿里巴巴推出新AI模型，小體積打敗大對手

來源：AI Daily Post / Hacker News

中國的阿里巴巴在4月22日發布了 Qwen3.6-27B，一個只有270億參數的AI模型。雖然體積小，它在寫程式的測試裡贏過了大十幾倍的模型，包括自家的3970億參數版本。

這個模型用 Apache 2.0 開放授權，任何人都可以免費下載使用。開放AI的比賽越來越激烈。

China's Alibaba released Qwen3.6-27B on April 22, an AI model with only 27 billion parameters. Despite its compact size, it outperformed models more than ten times larger in programming tests, including Alibaba's own 397-billion-parameter version. The model uses an Apache 2.0 open license, allowing anyone to download and use it freely. The race in open AI is intensifying.

美國參議院取消邊界水域採礦禁令

來源：MPR News

美國參議院以50比49票，取消了明尼蘇達州「邊界水域」(Boundary Waters)附近的採礦禁令。這個禁令是2023年設的，原本要保護這片森林和水源20年。

取消後，一家智利公司的子公司 Twin Metals 可以在那裡開採銅和鎳。環保團體很擔心會破壞這個珍貴的自然區。

The U.S. Senate voted 50-49 to overturn a mining ban near Minnesota's Boundary Waters. The ban, established in 2023, was originally intended to protect the forest and water resources for 20 years. With the ban rescinded, Twin Metals, a subsidiary of a Chilean company, can now extract copper and nickel from the region. Environmental groups are concerned about damage to this precious natural area.

加拿大新公司賣「沒有科技」的拖拉機，便宜一半

來源：Hacker News

加拿大亞伯達省的小公司 Ursa Ag 賣一種很特別的拖拉機：沒有GPS、沒有電腦、沒有感應器，用的是1990年代的柴油引擎。150馬力的型號只要大約9.5萬美元，是大品牌的一半。

很多農夫受夠了機器壞了不能自己修，還要付月費。一次訪問之後，就有400個美國農夫來問。

A small company called Ursa Ag in Alberta, Canada is selling a remarkably different kind of tractor: no GPS, no computers, no sensors—just a diesel engine from the 1990s. The 150-horsepower model costs only about $95,000, half the price of major brands. Many farmers are fed up with machines they cannot repair themselves and the monthly fees that come with them. After one visit, 400 American farmers came asking about it.

The Chicago Cubs defeated the Philadelphia Phillies 7-4 on Wednesday night, extending their winning streak to eight games. It's their longest streak since 2023. Busch delivered a crucial two-run single, while Suzuki and Hoerner each hit home runs. The Cubs are now 14-9, playing hot baseball. Over the eight-game run, they've scored 58 runs while allowing just 20.
