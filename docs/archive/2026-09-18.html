華為公布新AI晶片 挑戰輝達地位

來源：Bloomberg

九月十七日，華為在上海的年度大會上，公布新一代AI晶片「昇騰960」。主管汪濤說，960DT會在2027年第一季推出，比原本的計畫早了三季。

新晶片的運算速度和記憶體，都比上一代快一倍。華為還推出「超級節點」，能把4096顆晶片連成一台大電腦。美國管制晶片出口，華為想在國內做出自己的一整套系統。

On September 17th, Huawei unveiled its new generation AI chip, the "Ascend 960," at its annual conference in Shanghai. Executive Wang Tao said the 960DT will launch in the first quarter of 2027—three quarters ahead of the original plan. The new chip's computing speed and memory are both twice as fast as the previous generation. Huawei also introduced the "super node," which can connect 4,096 chips together into a single massive computer. As the US tightens restrictions on chip exports, Huawei is building out its own complete ecosystem domestically.

研究指暖化助長尼泊爾山崩洪災

來源：World Weather Attribution

八月二十六日，尼泊爾朗塘山有一大塊岩石和冰塊崩落，引發洪水沖進山谷，造成超過一千三百人死亡、五千多人失蹤。

九月十七日，「世界天氣歸因」團隊公布研究，指出當地七、八月的氣溫比平常高了約五度，冰川變薄，凍土（長年結冰的泥土）也融化，山坡因此失去支撐。科學家說，這是長期暖化累積出來的災難。

On August 26th, a large section of rock and ice broke away from Nepal's Langtang Mountain, triggering floods that swept into the valley and killing over 1,300 people while leaving more than 5,000 missing. On September 17th, the World Weather Attribution team released research showing that temperatures in the region during July and August were approximately 5 degrees Celsius higher than normal. The unusually warm conditions thinned glaciers and melted permafrost—soil that remains frozen year-round—removing critical support from the mountain slopes. Scientists point to this as a disaster accumulated from long-term climate warming.

十五名伊利諾居民 向國土安全部求償

來源：WTTW Chicago

九月十七日，十五名伊利諾州居民向美國國土安全部提出賠償申請，說去年秋天「中途突襲行動」期間，聯邦探員對他們動粗，還把人抓走關起來。

提出申請的人包括橡樹園鎮的民選代表穆尼奧斯、記者昆克爾和律師莫瑞亞蒂。依照法律，他們要先等六個月，看政府怎麼回覆，才能正式向聯邦法院提告。

On September 17th, fifteen Illinois residents filed a compensation claim with the U.S. Department of Homeland Security, saying that federal agents used force against them and arrested and detained people during last fall's "Operation Midway Blitz." Among those filing the claim were Munoz, an elected representative from Oak Park, journalist Quinkel, and attorney Moriarty. Under the law, they must first wait six months to see how the government responds before they can formally file suit in federal court.
