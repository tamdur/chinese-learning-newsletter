中國回應美國AI老闆的警告

來源：AP News

Anthropic公司的老闆阿莫代伊上週六發表一篇長文，說中國如果在人工智慧上領先，對美國和全世界都很危險。他要求美國繼續管制，不把最先進的晶片賣給中國。

中國外交部發言人郭嘉昆星期一回應，說這種「散播恐懼」只會破壞全球一起訂規則的過程。中國國家安全部長陳一新也警告，外國的AI可能被壞人利用，動搖中國的政治和思想安全。

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei published a lengthy essay last Saturday arguing that Chinese dominance in artificial intelligence would pose a danger to both the United States and the world. He called on Washington to maintain export controls and prevent the sale of advanced semiconductors to China. On Monday, Foreign Ministry spokesperson Guo Jiakun responded, saying such "fear-mongering" would only undermine global efforts to establish shared AI governance rules. State Security Minister Chen Yixin also warned that foreign AI systems could be exploited by malicious actors to threaten China's political and ideological security.

最高法院擋下郵寄投票新規定

來源：NPR

美國最高法院九月十四日拒絕政府的緊急請求，讓郵局的郵寄選票新規定在十一月期中選舉前繼續被凍結。法官們表示，政府這場官司不太可能打贏。

大法官卡瓦諾另外寫了意見，他認為新規定本身也許合法，但是來得太晚，各州辦選舉的人來不及準備。大法官阿利托和湯瑪斯則不同意這個決定。

The U.S. Supreme Court on September 14 rejected the government's emergency request, allowing the Postal Service's new mail-in ballot regulations to remain frozen ahead of the November midterm elections. The justices indicated the government is unlikely to win its case. Justice Kavanaugh wrote a separate opinion, arguing that while the new rules might be legal in themselves, they came too late for state election officials to prepare. Justices Alito and Thomas disagreed with the decision.

留學生簽證新制上路前一天被擋

來源：DHS / Study in the States

美國原本要從九月十五日起改變留學生簽證：一百二十萬名F-1學生不再是「讀多久就待多久」，改成最多四年的固定期限，畢業後能留下的時間也從六十天減為三十天。

但是九月十四日，麻州的聯邦法官塞勒發出全國性的暫時禁令，把新規定擋了下來。官司結束前，舊制度繼續使用。

The U.S. was scheduled to overhaul international student visas beginning September 15th. The 1.2 million F-1 students would no longer enjoy unlimited duration of stay; instead, they would face a fixed four-year limit. The grace period to remain in the country after graduation would also shrink from 60 days to 30 days. However, on September 14th, Massachusetts federal judge F. Dennis Saylor IV issued a nationwide temporary injunction blocking the new rules. Until the case is resolved, the current system remains in effect.

中國八月出口大增，內需更冷

來源：CNBC

中國九月十五日公布八月的經濟數字：出口比去年同期成長兩成五，工廠賣到國外的貨很暢銷。可是國內零售只成長百分之零點四，比七月的零點六還要低。

今年前八個月的固定資產投資更掉了百分之七點二。外國人猛買、自己人不敢花錢，這樣的落差讓北京壓力更大，也讓美國在談關稅時更有話說。

China released August's economic figures on September 15th, painting a sharp contrast. Exports jumped 25 percent year-over-year, with factories finding eager buyers abroad. Domestic retail sales, however, managed just 0.4 percent growth—falling from July's already-weak 0.6 percent. Fixed asset investment for the first eight months tumbled 7.2 percent. The picture is clear: foreigners are buying, but Chinese consumers aren't spending. This growing divergence ramps up pressure on Beijing and gives Washington more leverage in tariff talks.

The Chicago Bears dominated Carolina 59-37 in week one, marking the highest-scoring NFL season opener on record, with quarterback Williams single-handedly accounting for four touchdowns. In week two, they head to Detroit to face the Lions. Bears head coach Johnson previously served as the Lions' offensive coordinator—and the offensive scheme he's running is his own design. The Lions understand Johnson's thinking better than any team they'll face. This is the real test.
