美國和伊朗在巴基斯坦面對面談判，停火能維持嗎？

來源：Al Jazeera

美國副總統Vance、特使Witkoff和Kushner今天到了巴基斯坦首都，和伊朗外交部長直接見面。這是四月七日停火以來，兩國第一次坐下來談。巴基斯坦是中間人，幫了很大的忙。

但雙方立場差很遠。伊朗要求保留鈾（一種核能材料）濃縮的權利，還要全面取消制裁（sanctions）。美國說這兩點都不能接受。而且黎巴嫩要不要算在停火裡面，也吵得很兇。談判才剛開始，路還很長。

U.S. Vice President Vance, envoy Witkoff, and Kushner arrived in Pakistan's capital today for direct talks with Iran's Foreign Minister. This marks the first face-to-face meeting between the two countries since the ceasefire on April 7th. Pakistan is serving as a crucial intermediary. However, the two sides remain far apart on key issues. Iran is demanding the right to continue uranium enrichment and seeks a comprehensive lifting of sanctions. The U.S. says both demands are unacceptable. Additionally, there is fierce disagreement over whether Lebanon should be included in the ceasefire. The negotiations have only just begun, and the road ahead is long.

猶他州開先例：AI可以幫你續開處方藥

來源：Axios / Washington Post

美國猶他州成為全國第一個讓AI自己決定能不能續開處方藥的地方。一家叫Doctronic的公司和州政府合作，用AI幫病人續開190種常見藥物，像是糖尿病和高血壓的藥。

不過，這只是續開，第一次看病還是要找真的醫生。管制藥品也不包括在內。有些醫生擔心病人安全，但支持的人說，這能讓看病變得更快、更方便。

Utah has become the first state in the nation to allow AI to independently decide whether prescription drug refills are permitted. A company called Doctronic is partnering with the state government to use AI to help patients refill 190 common medications, such as drugs for diabetes and high blood pressure. However, this only applies to refills; patients still need to see a real doctor for their initial visit. Controlled substances are not included. Some doctors worry about patient safety, but supporters say this can make healthcare faster and more convenient.

IMF報告：貿易不確定，全球經濟成長放慢

來源：IMF

國際貨幣基金組織（IMF）這個星期發了四月的世界經濟報告，把全球成長的預測往下調了。主要原因是美國的關稅（tariff）政策讓貿易變得不確定，加上中東的戰爭也有影響。

好消息是，全球物價上漲的速度在慢慢降下來，預計今年通膨（inflation）大概3.1%。中國的經濟成長預測是4.8%，美國是2.4%。整體來說，世界經濟不差，但風險很多。

The International Monetary Fund released its April World Economic Outlook this week and downgraded its forecast for global growth. The main reason is uncertainty in trade stemming from US tariff policy, along with the impact of conflict in the Middle East. On the positive side, the pace of global price increases is gradually slowing, with inflation expected to reach around 3.1% this year. China's economic growth forecast is 4.8%, while the US is projected at 2.4%. Overall, the global economy is not in bad shape, but there are significant risks ahead.

川普政府告伊利諾州：預測市場到底誰來管？

來源：NPR / ABC7 Chicago

川普政府向法院告了伊利諾州，原因是「預測市場」的管理問題。預測市場是一種網路平台，讓人用錢來下注未來會發生什麼事。

伊利諾州說這是賭博，應該由州來管。但聯邦政府說這是金融商品，像期貨（futures）一樣，歸聯邦管。Kalshi和Polymarket這些平台都被捲進來了。州長Pritzker批評川普是在幫這些公司說話。

The Trump administration has filed a lawsuit against Illinois over the regulation of prediction markets. Prediction markets are online platforms where people can place bets with money on what will happen in the future. Illinois claims this constitutes gambling and should be regulated by the state. However, the federal government argues that these are financial instruments, similar to futures contracts, and fall under federal jurisdiction. Platforms like Kalshi and Polymarket have been drawn into the dispute. Governor Pritzker has criticized Trump for appearing to advocate on behalf of these companies.

小熊隊好消息和壞消息一起來了。好消息是他們9比2大贏坦帕灣光芒隊。Bregman打破了連續13個打席沒安打的低潮，一場打出三支安打。投手Assad也投了5又2/3局沒失分的好球。

Combined with Boyd's arm injury, the Cubs' pitching rotation faces significant pressure.

兵工廠線｜兵工廠在歐冠（Champions League）八強第一回合客場1比0贏了葡萄牙的士砵丁。比賽打到最後，替補上場的Havertz在補時接到Martinelli的傳球，一腳射進唯一的進球。
