川普取消攻擊伊朗，稱協議架構已談成

來源：CBS News

美國總統川普八月二日宣布，取消原本要對伊朗發動的攻擊。他說，各方已經談出協議的大方向，包括「立刻、完整打開荷姆茲海峽」，還要結束伊朗的核子威脅。

不過伊朗談判團隊的一名成員說，他們並沒有答應重新開放海峽。這場打了五個月的戰爭，第一次走向談判，而不是繼續擴大。

U.S. President Trump announced on August 2 that he was canceling a planned military strike against Iran. He said that all parties had reached agreement on the basic framework of a deal, including "immediately and completely opening the Strait of Hormuz," and ending Iran's nuclear threat.

However, a member of Iran's negotiating team said they had not agreed to reopen the strait. For the first time in five months of conflict, the war is turning toward negotiations rather than escalation.

OpenAI 新模型解開十道數學難題

來源：AI 產業新聞彙整

OpenAI 說，公司還沒推出的新模型「Astra」一口氣解開十道數學和電腦科學的老難題。其中一題和數學上的「群」有關，從一九九九年起就沒人解得開；另一題把球體堆疊密度的上限往前推，是一九七八年以來第一次進步。

得過費爾茲獎的數學家高爾斯等人，親自檢查後把結果公開。機器寫出來的證明，第一次得到數學界自己人的認可。

OpenAI says its yet-to-be-released new model "Astra" has solved ten longstanding problems in mathematics and computer science in one stroke. One involves the mathematical concept of "groups" and has resisted solution since 1999; another advances the upper bound for sphere packing density, marking the first progress on that problem since 1978.

Fields Medal-winning mathematician Tim Gowers and colleagues personally verified the results before making them public. For the first time, machine-generated proofs have received validation from the mathematics community itself.

兩大藥廠傳出談合併，規模上看四千億美元

來源：CNBC

英國藥廠阿斯特捷利康傳出和美國的必治妥施貴寶談合併。《金融時報》說，合併後的公司價值大約四千億到四千五百五十五億美元，會是史上最大的藥廠合併之一。

兩家公司都有重要的癌症和心臟病藥，專利（發明的保護期）快要結束，到時候別人就能做一樣的便宜藥。買下對方，比自己重新研發又快又省。談判還在早期，也可能談不成。

British pharmaceutical giant AstraZeneca is reportedly in talks to merge with American competitor Bristol Myers Squibb. According to the Financial Times, the combined company would be valued at roughly $400 billion to $455.5 billion, making it one of the largest pharmaceutical mergers ever.

Both companies have key cancer and heart disease medications, but the patents protecting these drugs are about to expire. Once they do, competitors will be able to manufacture cheaper generic versions. Merging is a faster and more economical alternative to developing new drugs independently. The negotiations are in early stages and may still fall through.

法官駁回選民名冊訴訟，聯邦政府連輸十九次

來源：WBEZ Chicago

聯邦法官勞里斯上週五駁回司法部的訴訟。司法部要求伊利諾州交出完整的選民名冊，裡面有住家地址、駕照號碼和部分社會安全號碼。法官說司法部沒有這個權力，而且這個案子不能再告一次。

這是川普政府這項行動在全國第十九次連輸。到目前為止，還沒有任何一州被法院要求交出這些資料。

Federal Judge Lawless dismissed the Justice Department's lawsuit last Friday. The department had sought to compel Illinois to turn over its complete voter registration rolls, which contain home addresses, driver's license numbers, and partial Social Security numbers. The judge ruled that the Justice Department lacked the authority to make such a demand, and that the case cannot be filed again.

This marks the Trump administration's nineteenth consecutive loss on this effort nationwide. So far, no state has been ordered by a court to turn over such information.

佩拉塔轉戰光芒，小熊補強落空

來源：MLB.com

交易大限前，投手佩拉塔被大都會隊送到光芒隊，換回三名小聯盟球員。他是市場上最搶手的先發投手之一，小熊隊也一直想要他。

小熊目前排在國聯外卡第一，但今年有六名先發投手受過傷。八月一日球隊才說願意用頂級新秀換投手，結果對手先一步成交。剩下的人選不是年紀大，就是要價高。

Before the trade deadline, pitcher Peralta was sent from the Mets to the Rays in exchange for three minor league players. He is one of the most sought-after starting pitchers on the market, and the Cubs have long wanted him.

The Cubs currently sit in first place for the National League wild card, but six starting pitchers have been injured this year. On August 1, the team said it was willing to trade a top prospect for a pitcher, but a rival closed the deal first. The remaining options are either aging or carry high asking prices.

兵工廠線：維尼修斯轉會，兩邊說法打架

來源：Sky Sports

兵工廠線——球隊傳出對簽下皇家馬德里前鋒維尼修斯越來越有信心。但八月一日晚上，球員的經紀團隊公開否認雙方已經談好個人條件。

兩邊的說法不可能同時是真的。報導說維尼修斯其實比較想留在皇馬，只是續約一直談不攏；真的談不成，他才願意來倫敦。這種互相矛盾，正是多數轉會消息的真實樣子。

The Arsenal beat — reports suggested the club was growing increasingly confident about signing Real Madrid forward Vinícius. But on the evening of August 1st, the player's agents publicly denied that the two sides had already agreed on personal terms.

Both sides' accounts can't possibly be true at the same time. According to reports, Vinícius actually prefers to stay at Real Madrid, but contract extension talks have stalled; he would only be willing to move to London if those negotiations definitively collapse. This kind of contradiction is precisely what most transfer news actually looks like.
