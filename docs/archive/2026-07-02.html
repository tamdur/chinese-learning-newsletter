俄軍大舉空襲基輔，波蘭緊急派戰機

來源：PBS NewsHour / CNBC

七月二日清晨，俄羅斯用飛彈和無人機大規模攻擊烏克蘭首都基輔，至少八人死亡，許多人受傷，還有住宅大樓起火。

爆炸從半夜兩點一直響到清晨四點。因為攻擊太靠近邊界，鄰國波蘭趕緊派出戰鬥機在空中巡邏，芬蘭也短暫關閉東部部分空域，保護自己的領空。

In the early morning hours of July 2nd, Russia conducted a massive assault on Kyiv, Ukraine's capital, using missiles and drones. The attack killed at least eight people, wounded many others, and set residential buildings ablaze. Explosions continued from 2 a.m. until 4 a.m. Because the strikes came dangerously close to its border, Poland swiftly deployed fighter jets for aerial patrols, while Finland temporarily closed portions of its eastern airspace to safeguard its own territory.

美國不再續簽美墨加貿易協定

來源：NBC News

美國貿易代表葛里爾七月一日宣布，不再續簽「美墨加貿易協定」（美國、墨西哥、加拿大三國的自由貿易條約）。

這份協定不會馬上結束，但改成每年檢討一次，若三國一直談不攏，最長可能拖上十年才失效。這讓北美的工廠和生意都陷入長期的不確定。美國預計七月二十日再和墨西哥談判。

U.S. Trade Representative Greer announced on July 1st that the United States will not renew the U.S.-Mexico-Canada Trade Agreement (USMCA), the free trade treaty among the three countries. The agreement won't terminate immediately but will instead be reviewed annually. If the three countries continue to fail to reach agreement, it could take as long as ten years to finally expire. This leaves North American factories and businesses facing prolonged uncertainty. The U.S. plans to resume negotiations with Mexico on July 20th.

人工智慧衝擊職場，科技金融每月少兩萬多個工作

來源：Bloomberg

彭博報導，二○二六年美國的金融和資訊業工作快速減少，平均每個月少掉大約兩萬八千個職位。

史丹佛大學的研究發現，最容易被人工智慧（會自己學習做事的電腦）取代的工作，人數縮得最快。二十二到二十五歲的年輕人，這類工作每年就少了百分之三點八。連大銀行的高層都承認，這種技術真的會讓一些職位消失。

Bloomberg reports that in 2026, jobs in America's financial and information sectors are shrinking rapidly—shedding roughly 28,000 positions per month on average. Research from Stanford University shows that jobs most vulnerable to replacement by artificial intelligence, computers that can teach themselves to perform tasks, are disappearing at the fastest rate. Workers aged 22 to 25 are being hit hardest, seeing these types of positions decline by 3.8 percent annually. Even senior executives at major banks are acknowledging that this technology will indeed eliminate certain positions.

伊利諾多項新法七月一日上路

來源：CBS Chicago

七月一日起，美國伊利諾州有十幾項新法律生效，同時啟用大約五百六十億美元的新年度預算。

這些新規定和大家的生活有關：暫停調漲汽油稅、把疫情時的「外帶調酒」變成永久合法、把用人工智慧做的假圖片欺負同學列為霸凌，另外也開始管理「預測市場」（一種下注猜結果的生意）。

Starting July 1st, more than a dozen new laws take effect in Illinois, alongside a new annual budget of roughly 56 billion dollars. These regulations touch everyday life in several ways: a freeze on gasoline tax increases, permanent legalization of takeout cocktails (a pandemic-era practice), classifying AI-generated fake images used to bully classmates as harassment, and new oversight of "prediction markets"—platforms where people wager on real-world outcomes.

密西根公布新賽季賽程，九月對戰奧克拉荷馬

來源：MGoBlue

密西根大學美式足球隊公布二○二六年的單場門票，七月七日到十日開賣。最受矚目的是九月十二日在主場對上奧克拉荷馬，球隊還請球迷一起穿上代表色黃色加油。

更大的看點是，這將是新總教練惠廷漢帶隊的第一個球季，象徵球隊進入全新的時代。

The University of Michigan football team has released single-game tickets for the 2026 season, going on sale from July 7 to 10. The most eagerly anticipated matchup is a home game against Oklahoma on September 12, with the team asking fans to wear the school color yellow in support. The bigger story, however, is that this marks new head coach Whittingham's first season at the helm—a symbolic entry into a new era for the program.

萊斯傷勢好轉，可望出戰世界盃

來源：Arsenal.com

兵工廠線——阿森納（兵工廠）和英格蘭的中場球員萊斯傳出好消息。他在小組賽對迦納時小腿被踢傷，腫了三天，還缺席了對巴拿馬那一場。

不過萊斯說，那只是為了保險，傷勢並不嚴重。英格蘭準備在世界盃三十二強淘汰賽對上民主剛果，他可望回到先發，再次坐鎮中場。他說：「我現在感覺很好，又強壯又健康。」

Arsenal beat — Good news for Arsenal and England midfielder Rice. He sustained a shin injury from a kick during the group-stage match against Ghana, which caused three days of swelling and kept him sidelined for the Panama match. However, Rice says it was merely precautionary; the injury is not serious. With England set to face the Democratic Republic of Congo in the World Cup Round of 32, he is expected to return to the starting lineup and control the midfield again. "I feel great right now—strong and healthy," he said.
