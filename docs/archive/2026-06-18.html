不是攻擊　只是改程式　Anthropic為隱藏設定道歉

來源：Hacker News

本月美國政府要求人工智慧公司Anthropic把最強的模型Fable 5關掉。原本大家以為是有人破解了它。

但研究人員說，真正的原因只是有人叫它「幫我改程式」，結果碰到了藏起來的安全設定。Anthropic也公開道歉，承認沒有告訴用戶這些看不見的設定。

This month, the U.S. government asked artificial intelligence company Anthropic to shut down its most powerful model, Fable 5. Everyone initially thought someone had hacked it. But researchers said the real reason was simpler: someone asked it to "help me modify code," and it triggered hidden safety settings. Anthropic publicly apologized, acknowledging that it hadn't disclosed these invisible settings to users.

AI太熱門　電腦不夠用　公司花大錢搶算力

來源：Economist desk

AI用的人太多，連大公司的電腦都快撐不住了。微軟的程式網站GitHub被AI擠爆，只好改用對手亞馬遜的電腦幫忙。

為了搶電腦的運算力，Anthropic每個月付給SpaceX約十二億美元，谷歌也付了不少。高盛銀行估計，未來六年全球會為AI花掉七兆六千億美元。

AI has become so popular that even big companies' computers can barely keep up. Microsoft's GitHub code platform got overwhelmed by AI demand, so it had to turn to rival Amazon's computers for help. To secure computing power, Anthropic pays SpaceX roughly 1.2 billion dollars a month, and Google has also spent heavily. Goldman Sachs estimates that over the next six years, the world will spend 7.6 trillion dollars on AI.

歐巴馬中心今開幕　明天對外開放

來源：Billboard

蓋了好多年的歐巴馬總統中心，今天在芝加哥南區的傑克森公園舉行開幕典禮。

這座園區大約有十九英畝，花了八億五千萬美元。歐巴馬夫婦都會上台說話，歌手史普林斯汀和史提夫汪達也會表演。中心明天六月十九日才對大家開放。

The Obama Presidential Center, which took years to build, held its opening ceremony today in Jackson Park on Chicago's South Side. The campus spans approximately nineteen acres and cost $850 million. Both President and Mrs. Obama will speak, and musicians Bruce Springsteen and Stevie Wonder will perform. The center opens to the public tomorrow, June 19th.

擔心油價　英國利率不動　三大央行同步觀望

來源：Bank of England

英國的中央銀行今天決定把利率（借錢的成本）維持在百分之三點七五。就算物價已經降了一點，他們還是擔心中東打仗會讓油價變貴。

這個星期，美國和日本的央行也都沒有動利率，三家大銀行一起先觀望。只有歐洲央行上週調高了利率。

Britain's central bank decided today to keep interest rates (the cost of borrowing) steady at 3.75 percent. Even though prices have fallen a bit, officials worry that fighting in the Middle East could push oil prices higher. This week, the central banks of the United States and Japan also held rates steady, with all three major institutions taking a wait-and-see approach together. Only the European Central Bank raised rates last week.

小熊再見安打勝洛磯　克勞阿姆斯壯完成完全打擊

來源：MLB.com

芝加哥小熊隊的克勞阿姆斯壯，一場比賽裡打出一壘、二壘、三壘安打和全壘打，完成很難得的「完全打擊」。

他是隊史第十二位做到的球員。小熊最後在第九局靠著再見安打，以五比四逆轉打敗科羅拉多洛磯隊。

Pete Crow-Armstrong of the Chicago Cubs hit a single, double, triple, and home run in a single game, completing the rare feat of the "cycle." He is the twelfth player in franchise history to achieve this. The Cubs ultimately pulled off a comeback win against the Colorado Rockies, 5-4, with a walk-off hit in the ninth inning.

兵工廠女足奪下首屆世界冠軍盃

來源：Arsenal.com

兵工廠線——兵工廠女子足球隊拿下了第一屆「國際足總女子冠軍盃」，成為這個新比賽的第一個世界冠軍。

決賽在大雨中進行，對手是巴西的哥林多隊。兵工廠兩度領先又被追平，最後在延長賽由福德踢進致勝球，以三比二贏得冠軍。

The Arsenal beat — Arsenal's Women's football team has claimed the inaugural "FIFA Women's Champions Cup," becoming the first world champion of this new competition. The final took place in heavy rain against Brazil's Corinthians. Arsenal went ahead twice but was drawn level both times, before Foord scored the winning goal in extra time to secure a 3-2 victory.
