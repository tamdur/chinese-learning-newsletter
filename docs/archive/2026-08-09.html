布蘭奇以一票之差出任美國司法部長

來源：Washington Post / NPR

美國參議院在八月八日清晨投票，以五十票對四十九票，通過布蘭奇出任司法部長。他以前是川普總統的私人律師。

只有柯林斯和穆考斯基兩位共和黨參議員投下反對票。關鍵的一票來自卡西迪，他先要求政府公開艾普斯坦案的檔案，才答應支持。

這是這個職位有史以來最接近的票數之一。布蘭奇一上任，就要處理出生公民權和關稅的大官司。

The U.S. Senate voted early on August 8th, confirming Blanche as Attorney General by a vote of 50 to 49. He was previously President Trump's personal lawyer. Only two Republican senators—Collins and Murkowski—voted against. The deciding vote came from Cassidy, who demanded that the government release the Epstein case files before agreeing to support. This is one of the closest votes in the history of this position. Upon taking office, Blanche will have to tackle major court cases involving birthright citizenship and tariffs.

網路攻擊能力太強，OpenAI 放慢新模型

來源：TechCrunch / Axios

OpenAI 在八月七日說，公司測試還沒發表的新模型 Astra 時，發現它寫程式和攻擊網路的能力進步很快，可能到達公司自己訂的安全標準裡最高的「危險」等級。

這個等級的意思是，模型不用人幫忙，就能自己找出並利用大系統的漏洞（安全上的破洞）。

公司決定先放慢開發，加強測試。這是第一次有大型 AI 公司因為模型太會攻擊，主動把產品往後延。

On August 7th, OpenAI announced that while testing its unreleased new model Astra, the company discovered its capabilities for writing code and attacking networks were advancing rapidly—potentially reaching the highest "dangerous" level under the company's own safety standards. That level means the model could independently find and exploit vulnerabilities (security holes) in large systems without human help. In response, the company decided to slow development and strengthen testing. This marks the first time a major AI company has proactively delayed a product because the model was too capable at attacking.

晶片需求帶動，中國七月出口大增

來源：CNBC / Reuters

中國海關八月七日公布，七月出口比去年同月多了百分之二十三點九，順差（賣出去的錢減掉買進來的錢）衝到一千一百二十五億美元，創下新高。

成長最快的是科技產品。半導體（也就是晶片）的出口金額幾乎多了一倍，高科技產品成長百分之四十點七。

全世界蓋 AI 資料中心的熱潮，正在改變全球貿易的樣子。

China's customs administration announced on August 7 that July exports rose 23.9% compared with the same month last year, while the trade surplus—export earnings minus import spending—hit a record $112.5 billion. Technology products led the growth. Semiconductor (chip) export value nearly doubled, and high-tech products overall rose 40.7%. The global rush to build AI data centres is reshaping the landscape of world trade.

法官擋下新命令，保護在美出生的孩子

來源：NPR / ACLU

川普政府這個星期簽了新命令，想用不發簽證等方式，限制「在美國出生就有公民身分」的規定。

八月七日，一位聯邦法官把所有在美國出生的孩子認定成一個團體，並下令暫時擋住這些命令。這是第一次有案子拿到這麼完整的團體資格。

去年最高法院限制了法官發出全國性禁令，律師只好改用這個辦法，一次保護所有孩子。

The Trump administration signed new executive orders this week seeking to restrict the rule that anyone born in the United States is a citizen, using measures such as withholding visas. On August 7th, a federal judge certified all children born in the US as a single class and issued an order temporarily blocking those directives.

This marks the first time a case has received such comprehensive class certification. After the Supreme Court last year limited judges' power to issue nationwide injunctions, lawyers turned to this approach instead, protecting all children at once.

安全衛接連受傷，熊隊急著補人

來源：Chicago Sun-Times

芝加哥熊隊訓練營第九天，安全衛（守在最後一線的球員）路易斯在練習尾聲受傷，被小車載離球場。

兩個星期內，這已經是第三位倒下的安全衛。先發的布萊恩至少要休兩個月，歐文斯也被球隊放棄。熊隊只好馬上簽下希爾和角衛萊特來補位。

好消息是四分衛威廉斯當天練得不錯。

On the ninth day of Chicago Bears training camp, safety Lewis—the player who guards the last line of defence—was injured late in practice and carted off the field. That is the third safety to go down in two weeks. Starter Bryant will be sidelined for at least two months, and Owens has been let go by the team. The Bears immediately signed Hill and cornerback Wright to fill the gaps. The good news: quarterback Williams had a solid practice that day.

兵工廠七千五百萬英鎊簽下布魯諾

來源：Arsenal.com

兵工廠線——阿森納在八月八日正式宣布，用大約七千五百萬英鎊從紐卡索簽下巴西中場布魯諾，合約五年。

這個夏天球隊追過維尼修斯和羅傑斯，兩次都落空，布魯諾是他們真正買到的大牌。他擅長搶球和分配傳球，將和萊斯、厄德高一起守中場。

八月十六日對曼城的社區盾，會是他的第一場大考。

The Arsenal beat — Arsenal officially announced on August 8 the signing of Brazilian midfielder Bruno from Newcastle for roughly £75 million on a five-year contract. After chasing Vinicius and Rogers this summer and missing out both times, Bruno is the marquee name the club actually landed. Strong at winning the ball back and distributing passes, he will hold the midfield alongside Rice and Odegaard. The Community Shield against Manchester City on August 16 will be his first big test.
