會自己複製的 AI 病毒出現了

來源：Hacker News

研究人員今天發現第一隻會自己複製（就是一直複印自己）的 AI 病毒，大家叫它「蠕蟲」。

它會在人們自己電腦上跑的 AI 模型之間傳來傳去，把自己複製到別的機器。因為這些模型放在每個人家裡，沒有一個總開關可以關掉它，所以很難擋住。

Researchers today discovered the first self-replicating AI virus, which people are calling a "worm." It spreads between AI models running on people's own computers, copying itself from one machine to another. Because these models sit on individual devices with no central kill switch, they're extremely difficult to stop.

日本兩週內將用上最強 AI

來源：LLM Stats

日本財政大臣說，日本政府和大銀行兩週內就能用到 Anthropic 公司最強的 AI 模型 Mythos。

這個模型本來只給大約五十個挑選過的夥伴用。這次是在美國財政部長和日本見面後才開放的。才過幾天，美國才要求把同一種模型在國內關掉，看得出最強的 AI 已經變成國家之間談判的籌碼。

Japan's finance minister says the Japanese government and major banks will be able to use Anthropic's most powerful AI model, Mythos, within two weeks. The model was previously available only to about fifty selected partners. This access came after the U.S. Treasury secretary met with Japan. Just days earlier, the U.S. had required that the same kind of model be shut down domestically, underscoring how the most powerful AI has become a bargaining chip in negotiations between nations.

芝加哥慶祝六月節

來源：City of Chicago / The Hill

今天是六月節（Juneteenth，紀念黑人不再當奴隸的日子）。芝加哥舉辦第十二屆非裔遊行，博物館也有活動。

不過在全國這一天卻有爭議。川普政府把這天從國家公園「免門票日」的名單拿掉，被很多人批評，雖然它還是國定假日。

Today is Juneteenth, which commemorates the day Black Americans were freed from slavery. Chicago is hosting its twelfth annual African American parade, with museum events as well. Yet nationwide the day has become controversial. The Trump administration removed it from the National Park Service's list of fee-free days, drawing criticism from many — even though it remains a federal holiday.

川普取消 AI 新規定

來源：LLM Stats

川普總統取消了一份本來要管 AI 的命令。他擔心規定太多會綁住美國公司，讓中國追上來。

這樣一來，美國就沒有全國統一的 AI 規則。偏偏這時候科羅拉多州自己在立法，歐洲也開始嚴管。到底誰來管美國最強的 AI，現在更難說了。

President Trump canceled an executive order that was meant to regulate AI. He worried that too many rules would tie down American companies and let China catch up. As a result, the US now lacks a unified national framework for AI. Yet at the very same time, Colorado is passing its own legislation and Europe is beginning to regulate strictly. Who will govern America's most powerful AI is now harder than ever to say.

小熊王牌投手受傷

來源：MLB.com Cubs

芝加哥小熊隊的終結者（負責最後關頭守住勝利的投手）帕倫西亞手肘發炎，被放進十五天傷兵名單，球隊叫回了霍洛威爾頂替。

今天小熊在主場迎戰藍鳥隊，由布朗先發，他的防禦率只有 1.74，非常好。球隊目前三十九勝三十六敗，少了終結者，教練要更傷腦筋了。

Chicago Cubs closer Daniel Palencia has an inflamed elbow and has been placed on the 15-day injured list, and the team has recalled Gavin Hollowell to fill in. Today the Cubs host the Blue Jays at home, with Ben Brown starting; his ERA is just 1.74, which is excellent. The team currently sits at 39 wins and 36 losses, and without their closer, the manager has a bigger headache on his hands.

帕茲拒絕兵工廠

來源：Arsenal.com / Just Arsenal

兵工廠線——二十一歲的好手尼可．帕茲拒絕加入兵工廠（阿森納隊），他想留在義大利的科莫隊，跟著教練法布雷加斯踢球。

這對想補強中場的兵工廠是個打擊。有消息說，球隊這個夏天可能賣掉自家青年隊出身的恩瓦內里，來調整陣容。兵工廠上季在歐冠決賽輸給巴黎，轉會市場六月十五日才剛開門。

Arsenal beat — The talented 21-year-old Nico Paz has rejected a move to Arsenal, choosing to stay at Italy's Como and play under manager Cesc Fabregas. It's a blow to Arsenal, who were hoping to strengthen their midfield. There are reports that the club may sell its own academy product, Ethan Nwaneri, this summer to reshape the squad. Arsenal lost last season's Champions League final to Paris, and the transfer market only just opened on June 15.
