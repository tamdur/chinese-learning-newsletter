美加談判破裂，美國課加拿大五成關稅

來源：Bloomberg

美國和加拿大的談判在星期五晚上破裂。八月二十二日起，美國對大約兩百八十億美元的加拿大貨物收百分之五十的關稅，包括曲棍球棒、建材和衣服。

加拿大總理卡尼說這是「錯誤的判斷」。他宣布加拿大會一塊錢對一塊錢還手，九月八日開始對美國的鋼鐵、電子產品和農業機具課稅。

這對芝加哥很重要：伊利諾州最大的貿易對象就是加拿大。

US-Canada negotiations collapsed Friday evening. Starting August 22, the United States is imposing a 50% tariff on roughly $28 billion in Canadian goods, including hockey sticks, building materials, and clothing. Canadian Prime Minister Carney called it "a misguided decision." He announced that Canada will respond dollar-for-dollar, beginning September 8 with tariffs on US steel, electronics, and agricultural machinery. This matters greatly for Chicago: Canada is Illinois' largest trading partner.

恆大創辦人許家印被判無期徒刑

來源：Bloomberg

中國深圳的法院判恆大集團創辦人許家印無期徒刑（關到老死）。他承認把公司的財產灌水做大，還藏起超過三千億美元的欠債。

法院一共開出二十三億五千萬美元的罰款，也判了五十多人有罪，包括許家印的兩個兒子。

許家印曾經是中國首富。北京這次選擇用法律追究，而不是拿錢救公司。

A Shenzhen court has sentenced Evergrande Group founder Hui Ka Yan to life imprisonment. He admitted to inflating the company's assets and hiding over $300 billion in debt. The court imposed a combined fine of $2.35 billion and convicted more than 50 people, including Hui's two sons. Hui was once China's richest person. This time, Beijing chose legal accountability over a corporate bailout.

聯邦法官推翻七十五國簽證禁令

來源：華盛頓郵報

八月二十二日，美國紐約的聯邦法官巴爾加斯判決，政府必須恢復處理七十五個國家人民的移民簽證。

今年一月，國務院以這些國家的人民可能會靠政府救濟過日子為理由，停發簽證。法官說，法律明白禁止用國籍差別對待，這個做法「明顯違法」。

許多已經通過審查的家庭團聚和工作申請人，因此卡了半年多。

On August 22nd, federal judge Vargas in New York ruled that the government must resume processing immigration visas for people from seventy-five countries. In January, the State Department suspended visa issuance, citing concerns that residents of these nations might rely on government assistance. The judge stated that the law clearly prohibits discrimination based on nationality, calling the policy "flagrantly illegal." Many family reunification and employment applicants who had already passed background checks were delayed for over six months as a result.

以色列空襲敘利亞基地，阻擋土耳其進駐

來源：NPR

八月十九日凌晨，以色列戰機對敘利亞西北部伊德利卜省的阿布杜胡爾空軍基地投下八枚炸彈，跑道受損，沒有人傷亡。

以色列說，敘利亞快要讓土耳其軍隊進駐這個基地，威脅到自己的安全。敘利亞和土耳其都強烈譴責（大聲責備）。

少見的是，美國也公開批評以色列，說這是「不必要的升高」。土耳其是北約成員，也是美國的盟友。

In the early morning of August 19th, Israeli fighter jets struck the Abu Duhur air base in Idlib Province in northwest Syria with eight bombs, damaging the runway but causing no casualties. Israel claimed that Syria was about to allow Turkish military forces to establish a presence at the base, posing a threat to its security. Both Syria and Turkey strongly condemned the strikes. Notably, the United States also publicly criticized Israel, calling the action an "unnecessary escalation." Turkey is a NATO member and a US ally.

熊隊季前賽大敗，教練說「感覺很差」

來源：芝加哥太陽報

八月二十二日，芝加哥熊隊在季前賽以九比二十七輸給辛辛那提孟加拉虎隊。全隊被吹了十一次犯規，第三檔進攻十四次只成功兩次。

總教練班強森賽後直說：「不管是不是季前賽，這樣輸球，休息室裡的感覺就是很差。」

熊隊去年拿下國聯北區冠軍，幾天後就要把球員名單砍到五十三人。

On August 22nd, the Chicago Bears lost to the Cincinnati Bengals 9-27 in a preseason game. The team was called for eleven penalties, and on third down they converted just 2 of 14 attempts. Head coach Ben Johnson said bluntly after the game: "Whether it's preseason or not, losing like this—the feeling in the locker room is just terrible." The Bears won the NFC North last year, and in a few days they'll need to cut their roster down to 53 players.
