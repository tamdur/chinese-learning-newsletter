南極冰下抓「鬼粒子」，美國教授得諾貝爾物理獎

來源：Nobel Prize / Scientific American

今年的諾貝爾物理獎在10月6日公布，得獎的只有一個人：美國威斯康辛大學的哈爾岑（Francis Halzen）。他今年82歲，在比利時出生。

他帶頭在南極的冰裡放了五千多個感測器，做成一個很大的「冰立方」。它專門抓中微子，這是一種很小、可以直接穿過地球的粒子，所以也叫「鬼粒子」。

2013年，冰立方第一次抓到從太陽系外面飛來的高能中微子。這等於幫人類多開了一扇看宇宙的新窗戶。

This year's Nobel Prize in Physics was announced on October 6, going to just one person: Francis Halzen of the University of Wisconsin-Madison. At 82, Halzen was born in Belgium. He led the installation of over 5,000 sensors deep beneath the Antarctic ice to create a massive "ice cube." It is designed to catch neutrinos—tiny particles so small they can pass directly through the Earth, earning them the nickname "ghost particles." In 2013, the ice cube detected high-energy neutrinos from beyond our solar system for the first time. This amounts to opening an entirely new window for humanity to observe the universe.

退休金投錯了？最高法院審英特爾案

來源：SCOTUSblog / 401(k) Specialist

美國最高法院在10月6日聽了一個和退休金有關的案子。英特爾的員工說，公司把大家的 401(k) 退休金，放了很多在「避險基金」和「私募基金」這類收費很高的投資裡。

員工說，這些投資花錢多、賺得又比一般基金少，所以告了公司。這個官司從2019年就開始打了。

法官要決定的是：員工告公司時，是不是一定要先找出一個「好的比較對象」，證明別的基金真的做得更好？答案會影響很多美國人的退休錢。

The U.S. Supreme Court heard a case related to retirement funds on October 6. Intel employees contend that the company placed a significant portion of their 401(k) retirement savings into high-fee investments such as hedge funds and private equity funds.

According to the employees, these investments carry steep costs while generating lower returns than standard mutual funds. The employees sued the company over this issue, with the case having been in litigation since 2019.

The central legal question before the justices is whether employees bringing such a lawsuit must first identify a "suitable alternative" investment that demonstrably outperformed the funds in question. The court's ruling will have implications for the retirement savings of millions of Americans.

公牛今晚開打季前賽，新秀第一次上場

來源：SI.com / ABC7 Chicago / NBA.com

芝加哥公牛隊今晚7點在聯合中心（United Center）打新球季的第一場季前賽，對手是太陽隊。季前賽就是正式比賽開始前的熱身賽。

這也是兩位新秀第一次穿上公牛球衣比賽：選秀第4個被選上的威爾森（Caleb Wilson），和第15個被選上的史威恩（Dailyn Swain）。他們會和吉迪（Josh Giddey）、布澤利斯（Matas Buzelis）一起打球。

太陽隊有球星布克（Devin Booker），今年夏天又換來了布里吉斯（Miles Bridges）。年輕的公牛打得怎麼樣，今晚就能看到。

The Chicago Bulls play their first preseason game of the new season tonight at 7 p.m. at the United Center, facing the Suns. Preseason games are warm-up matches before the official season begins. It will also be the first game for two rookies wearing a Bulls jersey: Caleb Wilson, the 4th overall draft pick, and Dailyn Swain, selected 15th overall. They'll be playing alongside Josh Giddey and Matas Buzelis. The Suns have star player Devin Booker, and this summer they also acquired Miles Bridges. We'll find out how the young Bulls look tonight.
