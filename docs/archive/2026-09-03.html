Anthropic 同時推出兩款新模型

來源：Anthropic / Hacker News

人工智慧公司 Anthropic 這星期推出兩款新模型，名叫 Claude Fable 5.1 和 Claude Mythos 5.1。前者人人可用，後者只開放給通過審查的資安和生命科學研究者。

公司說，新模型在寫程式和處理長時間的難題上都比舊版更強，還把快取（重複讀取資料）的費用砍掉七成五。消息一出，馬上衝上科技論壇 Hacker News 第一名。

時機也是重點：Anthropic 正在和投資人開會，準備十月上市，公司價值被估到接近九千六百億美元。

AI company Anthropic released two new models this week: Claude Fable 5.1 and Claude Mythos 5.1. The first is available to everyone, while the second is restricted to vetted security and life sciences researchers. According to the company, the new models outperform earlier versions in code writing and tackling long-running complex problems, while slashing caching costs by 75 percent. The announcement immediately topped Hacker News. Timing is significant too: Anthropic is currently meeting with investors ahead of an October IPO, with the company valued at roughly 960 billion dollars.

法官判 Google 不必賣掉廣告平台

來源：CNBC

美國法官布林克瑪九月二日判決，Google 不必賣掉旗下的廣告交易平台 AdX。法院早就認定 Google 在網路廣告技術上非法壟斷（一家獨大、排擠對手），這次卻沒有下令拆開公司。

法官改要求 Google 換做法：把即時出價的資料開放給對手的系統，也讓網站能對不同買家設不同底價。

過去大家以為，只要被判壟斷就會被迫拆賣。這個判決把兩件事分開，也會成為其他科技公司官司的參考。

On September 2, U.S. Judge Brinkema ruled that Google does not have to sell off its AdX advertising exchange platform. The court had already found that Google illegally monopolized web advertising technology—dominating the market and squeezing out competitors—but this time declined to order the company broken up. Instead, the judge required Google to change course: opening real-time bidding data to rival systems and allowing websites to set different minimum prices for different buyers. Everyone once assumed that antitrust findings automatically led to forced divestitures. This ruling separates the two issues and will likely become a reference point in future tech industry antitrust cases.

ComEd 要花百億美元強化電網，錢誰出？

來源：WBEZ Chicago

芝加哥地區的電力公司 ComEd 向伊利諾州的主管機關提出計畫，打算在二零二八到二零三一年花掉一百五十三億美元，讓電網更耐風雨。

今年伊利諾州的龍捲風次數全美第一，熱浪也一再出現。ComEd 說，如果計畫全部通過，住家每月大約多付兩塊半到三塊美元。

但公民團體不服氣：民眾前幾年已經付了好幾十億，停電卻沒有明顯變少。有團體指出，其中十億美元以上的花費說不清楚。

ComEd, Chicago's utility company, has submitted a plan to Illinois regulators to spend $15.3 billion between 2028 and 2031 strengthening the electrical grid against severe weather. Illinois has recorded the nation's highest tornado count this year, and heat waves have repeatedly occurred as well. If the plan is fully approved, residential customers would pay approximately $2.50 to $3 more per month, according to ComEd. But civic groups remain unconvinced: consumers have already paid billions over recent years, yet outages have not noticeably decreased. Some groups point out that over a billion dollars of the proposed spending lacks clear justification.

印第安納州八萬多人失去食物補助

來源：Indiana Capital Chronicle

印第安納州領糧食券（政府補助買食物的錢，英文簡稱 SNAP）的人，比去年十月少了大約八萬二千人，等於掉了百分之十四點五。

川普簽的「大而美法案」去年十月上路，五十五到六十四歲的人也要符合工作規定，還得重新申報資料。多數人不是變有錢了，而是文件沒補齊就被刷掉。

今年十月起，州政府要自付的行政費用從一半提高到七成五，一年多花約四千七百萬美元。

Indiana has lost approximately 82,000 SNAP recipients since last October—a decline of 14.5%. The federal 'One Big Beautiful Bill Act,' which Trump signed and which took effect last October, now requires people aged 55–64 to meet work requirements and recertify their eligibility. Most didn't become better off; they simply lost benefits due to incomplete paperwork. Beginning this October, Indiana's share of administrative costs rises from 50% to 75%, costing the state roughly $47 million more per year.

熊隊發問卷，問球迷搬到印第安納好不好

來源：ABC7 Chicago

芝加哥熊隊把問卷寄給季票球迷，問他們對在印第安納州哈蒙德市蓋新球場的看法。哈蒙德就在州界另一邊，離現在的士兵球場約二十五英里。

球隊的構想是一座有屋頂的室內球場，除了比賽，還能辦演唱會和大型活動。

印第安納州的議員一直在拉攏熊隊，伊利諾州的方案卻還沒談成。離九月十三日開季只剩十天，熊隊最大的新聞竟然和蓋房子有關。

The Chicago Bears mailed a survey to season ticket holders asking for their thoughts on building a new stadium in Hammond, Indiana—just across the state line, roughly 25 miles from Soldier Field. The team's vision is a climate-controlled indoor venue capable of hosting not just games but also concerts and major events. Indiana lawmakers have been actively courting the Bears, while Illinois still hasn't finalized its proposal. With just ten days remaining until the September 13 season opener, the Bears' biggest headline has nothing to do with football—it's about stadium construction.

兵工廠線：馬競逼阿瓦雷斯表態

來源：Goal.com / Sky Sports

兵工廠線——西班牙的馬德里競技（簡稱馬競）給前鋒阿瓦雷斯下了最後期限，要他在星期天前決定：留在馬競，還是爭取轉會到兵工廠。

奇怪的是，轉會窗九月一日就關了。兵工廠這個夏天走了三名前鋒，一個也沒補進來，所以這件事最快也要等明年一月才可能成真。

馬競執行長說，阿瓦雷斯已經好幾天沒練球。對想保住聯賽冠軍的兵工廠來說，前鋒不夠會是整個冬天的難題。

The Arsenal Beat — Spain's Atlético Madrid has given forward Álvarez an ultimatum: decide by Sunday whether to stay or pursue a transfer to Arsenal. The odd catch: the summer transfer window closed on September 1st. Arsenal lost three forwards this summer and brought in none, so any deal couldn't happen before January at the earliest. Atlético's chief executive says Álvarez hasn't trained in days. For Arsenal, chasing the league title, a striker shortage could be a headache all winter.
