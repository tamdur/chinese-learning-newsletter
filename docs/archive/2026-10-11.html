Anthropic傳準備上市，估值可能近2兆美元

來源：Bloomberg

據彭博社報導，AI公司Anthropic邀請一群大型投資人，10月14日到舊金山總部開會，討論公開上市（把股票賣給大眾）的計畫。Anthropic就是做出聊天機器人Claude的公司。

報導說，投資人估計公司價值大約1.8兆到2兆美元，比今年5月的9650億美元多了快一倍。不過，公司還沒有證實。

對手OpenAI走另一條路：它說今年不上市，而是私下再找投資人拿至少300億美元。兩家最大的AI公司，找錢的方法正好相反。

According to Bloomberg, AI company Anthropic has invited a group of major investors to its San Francisco headquarters on October 14 to discuss plans for an IPO (selling shares to the public). Anthropic is the company behind the Claude chatbot. The report says investors estimate the company's value at roughly $1.8 trillion to $2 trillion, nearly double the $965 billion valuation from May. The company has not confirmed the reports, however. Rival OpenAI is taking a different path: it says it will not go public this year, and instead is seeking at least $30 billion more from private investors. The two biggest AI companies are going about raising money in exactly opposite ways.

世界衛生高峰會今天在柏林登場

來源：World Health Summit

一年一次的世界衛生高峰會，今天在德國柏林開幕，一共舉行3天。這次的主題是「從危機到韌性」（韌性就是被打倒後很快站起來的能力），有超過4000人參加，400位講者上台。

開幕的重頭戲，是醫學期刊 The Lancet 的一個專家小組要公布研究結果：21世紀人類健康最大的威脅是什麼？

美國已經退出世界衛生組織，很多國際組織也正在缺錢。這場會議要回答的問題是：少了美國，世界要怎麼一起保護大家的健康？

The World Health Summit opened today in Berlin, Germany. The annual event runs for three days. This year's theme is "From Crisis to Resilience" (resilience means the ability to bounce back quickly after being knocked down). More than 4,000 people are attending, and 400 speakers will take the stage. The highlight of the opening is a report from an expert panel from The Lancet, a medical journal, that will reveal the answer to a big question: what is the greatest threat to human health in the 21st century? The United States has already withdrawn from the World Health Organization, and many international organizations are also short on money. The summit will try to answer this question: without the United States, how can the world work together to protect everyone's health?

芝加哥馬拉松今天開跑，跑過29個社區

來源：Chicago Marathon / Time Out Chicago / NBC Chicago

第48屆芝加哥馬拉松今天早上7點30分起跑。超過5萬5千名跑者要跑42.195公里，一路經過城市裡的29個社區。

從早上6點到下午4點，市中心和比賽路線上的很多街道都會封路。今天要出門的人，最好改搭捷運。

這場比賽是世界六大馬拉松之一，也是芝加哥的一門大生意。一份研究發現，去年的比賽為芝加哥地區帶來將近7.56億美元的收入，飯店和餐廳都是大贏家。

The 48th Chicago Marathon kicks off this morning at 7:30 a.m. More than 55,000 runners will cover 42.195 kilometers, passing through 29 of the city's neighborhoods. From 6 a.m. to 4 p.m., many streets downtown and along the course will be closed, so anyone heading out today should take the train instead. The race is one of the world's six major marathons, and it is also big business for Chicago. A study found that last year's race brought in nearly $756 million for the Chicago area, with hotels and restaurants as the biggest winners.

聯邦政府第三次拒絕伊州救災申請

來源：NPR Illinois / Capitol News Illinois

美國聯邦緊急事務管理署（FEMA，負責救災的政府單位）上週四拒絕了伊利諾州的申請，不給庫克郡、威爾郡等8個受災的郡聯邦救災錢。

8月11日，一場暴風雨帶來龍捲風，風速接近每小時160公里。但FEMA在信裡說，這次不需要聯邦幫忙。

這是川普第二任內，伊州第三次被拒絕。州長普立茲克說，很多社區到現在還在清理災後的破壞。少了聯邦的錢，修理和清理的費用只能由州政府、郡和居民自己出。

Last Thursday, the Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA), the federal government agency responsible for disaster relief, rejected Illinois's request for federal disaster aid for eight affected counties, including Cook and Will counties. On August 11, a storm brought tornadoes with winds approaching 160 kilometers per hour. But in its letter, FEMA said federal help was not needed this time.

This is the third time during Trump's second term that Illinois has been turned down. Governor Pritzker said many communities are still cleaning up the damage from the storm.

Without federal money, the costs of repairs and cleanup will fall entirely on the state government, the counties, and residents themselves.

熊隊今天到蘭博球場，挑戰老對手包裝工

來源：Packers.com / Bleacher Nation

芝加哥熊隊今天中午要到綠灣的蘭博球場，對上包裝工隊。這是兩隊第214次交手，也是NFL歷史最久的對決。

歷史上包裝工領先，109勝98敗6和。包裝工在主場更強，最近10次在家對熊隊贏了9次。

不過今年熊隊3勝1敗，包裝工只有2勝2敗。今年1月的季後賽，熊隊也以31比27打敗過包裝工。如果今天再贏，熊隊就能在分區裡領先包裝工兩場，證明好成績不是運氣。

The Chicago Bears travel to Lambeau Field in Green Bay this afternoon to take on the Packers. It is the 214th meeting between the two teams, making it the longest-running rivalry in NFL history. The Packers hold the all-time edge at 109 wins, 98 losses, and 6 ties. Green Bay is even tougher at home, winning nine of its last ten games against the Bears there. This season, though, the Bears are 3-1 while the Packers sit at 2-2. The Bears also beat Green Bay 31-27 in the playoffs back in January. A win today would put Chicago two games ahead of Green Bay in the division, which would show that their good start is no accident.

兵工廠逆轉里茲聯，積分追平曼城

來源：ESPN / beIN Sports

兵工廠線——兵工廠週六在主場酋長球場以2比1逆轉里茲聯。下半場第55分鐘，里茲聯的賈斯丁先進球，全場一下子安靜下來。

幾分鐘後，後衛卡拉菲奧里把比分追平。第70分鐘，替補上場的吉馬良斯接到萊斯的傳球，把球射進球門角落，完成逆轉。

這是里茲聯本季第一次輸球。兵工廠拿到15分，和曼城同分，只因為淨勝球（進球減掉失球）比較少，暫時排第二。

Arsenal beat: Arsenal came from behind to beat Leeds United 2–1 at the Emirates Stadium on Saturday. In the 55th minute of the second half, Leeds' Justin scored, and the whole stadium fell silent. A few minutes later, defender Riccardo Calafiori equalized. In the 70th minute, substitute Guimarães received a pass from Declan Rice and drove the ball into the corner of the net to complete the comeback.

It was Leeds' first defeat of the season. Arsenal have 15 points, level with Manchester City, but sit second for now because their goal difference (goals scored minus goals conceded) is slightly smaller.
