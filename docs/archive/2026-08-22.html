Anthropic 準備上市　規模可能寫下史上最大紀錄

來源：Bloomberg

彭博社報導，美國人工智慧公司 Anthropic 最快八月底就會正式申請上市，十月在股市亮相，目標身價大約兩兆美元。如果成功，這將是史上最大的一次上市，超過六月才創下紀錄的 SpaceX。

報導說，高盛、摩根士丹利和摩根大通三家大銀行負責這件事。到七月底，公司一年的收入大約是六百五十億美元。兩天前對手 OpenAI 才說要等到二〇二七年才上市，等於被搶先了一年多。

Bloomberg reports that U.S. artificial intelligence company Anthropic could file for an initial public offering as soon as late August, with a stock market debut targeted for October and a valuation goal of around $2 trillion. If successful, it would be the largest IPO in history, surpassing the record SpaceX set just this June.

The report says Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley, and JPMorgan Chase are handling the offering. As of the end of July, the company's annualized revenue was roughly $65 billion. Just two days ago rival OpenAI said it would not go public until 2027 — meaning Anthropic is beating it to market by more than a year.

大法官放行　白宮宴會廳繼續施工

來源：CNN

美國最高法院首席大法官羅伯茲星期五下令，暫時擋下兩個下級法院的判決，讓白宮那座造價四億美元的新宴會廳可以繼續蓋。

下級法院本來認為這個工程不合法，因為國會從來沒有同意過。到八月十四日，工程已經完成六成五，兩百五十個工人每天做二十個小時。這道命令只是暫時的，但在大法官們想清楚以前，房子會一天天蓋得更高。

U.S. Supreme Court Chief Justice Roberts issued an order on Friday temporarily blocking two lower-court rulings, allowing construction of the White House's new $400 million ballroom to continue.

The lower courts had found the project unlawful because Congress never approved it. As of August 14th, the work was already 65 percent complete, with 250 workers putting in twenty-hour days. The order is only temporary — but until the justices make up their minds, the building goes up a little more each day.

歐元區工廠回溫　景氣升到九個月新高

來源：S&P Global

標普全球在八月二十一日公布，歐元區八月的採購經理人指數（一種看景氣好壞的分數，超過五十分代表在成長）升到五十二點一，是去年十一月以來最高。工廠那一項更是四年半來最好。

原因很具體：德國工廠接到很多和人工智慧有關的科技產品訂單，加上各國增加國防設備的需求。今年以來第一次有公司開始增加人手，出口訂單也是四年多來第一次成長。

S&P Global reported on August 21st that the eurozone's August Purchasing Managers' Index — a score of business conditions, where above 50 means growth — climbed to 52.1, the highest since November of last year. The manufacturing component was its strongest in four and a half years.

The reasons are specific: German factories have taken in a wave of orders for AI-related technology goods, on top of rising demand for defense equipment across the region. For the first time this year companies began adding staff, and export orders grew for the first time in more than four years.

世界最老銀行出手　同時要買兩家對手

來源：Bloomberg

義大利的西雅那銀行（一四七二年成立，是世界上還在營業的最老銀行）星期五一口氣對 Banco BPM 和 Banca Generali 兩家同業出價，合計三百四十億歐元，全部用自己的股票支付。

這是為了自保。最大對手聯合聖保羅銀行想用三百六十億歐元把它吃下來，所以它先把自己變大，大到別人吞不下。兩筆若都談成，新銀行會變成義大利第三大。

Italy's Monte dei Paschi di Siena — founded in 1472 and the world's oldest bank still in business — made bids on Friday for two rivals at once, Banco BPM and Banca Generali, for a combined 34 billion euros, paid entirely in its own shares.

This is self-defense. Its biggest rival, Intesa Sanpaolo, wants to swallow it for 36 billion euros, so it is making itself bigger first — big enough that no one can swallow it. If both deals go through, the combined bank would become Italy's third largest.

熊隊想搬去印第安納　還得過老闆們這關

來源：Bleacher Nation

芝加哥熊隊董事長麥卡斯基八月二十一日說，球隊「就是要往哈蒙德走」，還點名兩塊可能的地：迷失沼澤高爾夫球場和狼湖碼頭。印第安納州也開出最多十億美元的好處來搶人。

但有一關是兩個州都管不到的。照聯盟規定，球隊搬家要有三十二隊裡至少二十四隊同意。把熊隊搬出芝加哥這個大市場，其他老闆不一定會點頭。

Chicago Bears chairman McCaskey said on August 21st that the team is "moving forward in Hammond," and named two possible sites: Lost Marsh Golf Course and Wolf Lake Terminals. Indiana has offered up to $1 billion in incentives to land the franchise.

But there is one hurdle neither state controls. Under league rules, a relocation needs the approval of at least 24 of the 32 clubs. Moving the Bears out of Chicago, a major market, is not something the other owners will necessarily sign off on.

兵工廠三比零勝考文垂　衛冕之路開好頭

來源：ESPN

兵工廠線——上季冠軍兵工廠星期五晚上在主場以三比零打敗剛升上來的考文垂，為英超新球季開了個好頭。進球的是哈維茨、薩卡和厄德高，三個人上季就在隊上，夏天新買的人一個都沒得分。

考文垂上半場連一次搶球都沒有，一直到第六十九分鐘才搶下第一球。教練阿爾特塔稱讚球隊踢得很順，卻還是要求前鋒再積極一點，並說轉會市場還剩十天，補強還沒做完。

The Arsenal beat — defending champions Arsenal beat newly promoted Coventry 3-0 at home on Friday night, opening the new Premier League season on the right foot. The scorers were Havertz, Saka, and Odegaard, all three already on the squad last season; none of the summer signings got on the scoresheet.

Coventry did not make a single tackle in the first half, and only won their first ball in the 69th minute. Manager Arteta praised how smoothly the side played but still asked his forwards to be sharper, noting that the transfer window has ten days left and the squad's reinforcement is not finished.
