巴西明天選總統，兩人幾乎打平

來源：Reuters / Rio Times

巴西星期天舉行總統第二輪投票，現任總統魯拉（Lula）對上參議員弗拉維歐（Flávio Bolsonaro）。弗拉維歐的爸爸是前總統波索納洛，因為想推翻2022年的選舉結果被判有罪，現在被關在家裡。

第一輪投票時，弗拉維歐拿到約47%，小贏魯拉。最新民調顯示兩人幾乎打平，弗拉維歐只多一點點。

魯拉贏的話，將是他第四次當總統；弗拉維歐贏的話，波索納洛家族就回到權力中心。新總統明年1月5日上任。

Brazil holds the second round of its presidential election on Sunday, with incumbent President Lula facing Senator Flávio Bolsonaro. Flávio's father is former president Jair Bolsonaro, who was found guilty of trying to overturn the 2022 election result and is now under house arrest.

In the first round, Flávio took about 47% of the vote, narrowly beating Lula. The latest polls show the two nearly tied, with Flávio only slightly ahead.

If Lula wins, it will be his fourth time as president. If Flávio wins, the Bolsonaro family will be back at the center of power. The new president takes office on January 5 next year.

熊隊30比24打敗愛國者隊

來源：NFL.com / Yahoo Sports

芝加哥熊隊星期四晚上在主場，以30比24打敗新英格蘭愛國者隊，戰績來到5勝2敗。

四分衛威廉斯（Caleb Williams）受傷回來的第二場，就傳出308碼和兩次達陣（把球帶進得分區）。新人波登（Luther Burden）接球跑了130碼。第四節，桑托斯（Cairo Santos）踢進58碼的射門，是主場史上第三遠。

不過，球員克梅特（Cole Kmet）腿部受傷，被車子載下場。熊隊下一場是11月2日對上西雅圖海鷹隊。

The Chicago Bears beat the New England Patriots 30–24 at home on Thursday night, improving their record to 5–2.

Quarterback Caleb Williams, in only his second game back from injury, threw for 308 yards and two touchdowns. Rookie Luther Burden caught passes for 130 yards. In the fourth quarter, Cairo Santos hit a 58-yard field goal, the third-longest in the history of the team's home stadium.

But Cole Kmet left the game with a leg injury and was taken off the field on a cart. The Bears' next game is against the Seattle Seahawks on November 2.
