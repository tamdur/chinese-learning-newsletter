企業愛用 Claude，Anthropic 首度超越 OpenAI

來源：VentureBeat / Ramp

根據Ramp公司追蹤五萬家企業的最新調查，付費使用 Anthropic Claude 的美國企業已達 34.4%，首次超過 OpenAI 的 32.3%。一年前 Anthropic 的比例還不到 8%，成長速度驚人。

分析指出，主要原因是 Claude Code 這款 AI 寫程式工具大受歡迎，目前全球 GitHub 上約 4% 的公開程式碼都由 Claude 產出。

According to the latest survey by Ramp, which tracks fifty thousand companies, 34.4% of paid-tier US enterprises now use Anthropic's Claude, surpassing OpenAI's 32.3% for the first time. Just a year ago, Anthropic's share stood below 8%, marking remarkable growth. Analysts attribute the surge primarily to Claude Code, an AI programming tool that has become wildly popular—currently accounting for roughly 4% of all public code on GitHub globally.

沃爾許今天正式接任聯準會主席

來源：CNBC / NPR

鮑爾的任期今天到期，沃爾許（Kevin Warsh）正式成為美國聯準會（央行）新主席。他在五月十三日以 54 比 45 的票數通過參議院確認，是近代最接近的一次投票。

沃爾許面對不少挑戰：通膨（物價上漲）仍在 3.8%，川普總統又一直要求降低利率。他的第一次會議在六月十六日。

Powell's term ended today, and Kevin Warsh officially became the new chairman of the U.S. Federal Reserve. He was confirmed by the Senate on May 13 with a vote of 54 to 45, one of the closest votes in recent history. Warsh faces considerable challenges: inflation remains at 3.8%, and President Trump has been consistently pushing for lower interest rates. His first meeting is scheduled for June 16.

Anthropic 與蓋茲基金會投入兩億美元推動 AI 助人

來源：Anthropic

Anthropic 和蓋茲基金會宣布一項為期四年、共兩億美元的合作計畫，要把 Claude AI 用在全球公共衛生、教育和農業上。

全球有 46 億人缺少基本醫療，這項計畫會先從小兒麻痺等疾病開始，也會在非洲和印度用 AI 幫小學生學讀書寫字。

Anthropic and the Gates Foundation announced a four-year, $200 million partnership to deploy Claude AI across global public health, education, and agriculture. With 4.6 billion people lacking access to basic medical care, the initiative will begin with diseases like polio, while also using AI to help elementary students in Africa and India learn reading and writing.

普林斯頓大學廢除 133 年榮譽制度，考試改為監考

來源：Daily Princetonian

美國名校普林斯頓大學從 1893 年起就讓學生考試時不用監考，靠的是「榮譽制度」——信任學生不會作弊。但這項 133 年的傳統本週正式結束了。

校方說，因為 AI 工具太容易在手機上使用，作弊變得很難被發現。調查發現，近三成畢業生承認自己曾經作弊。七月一日起所有考試都要有老師在場。

Princeton University, a top American university, has ended its 133-year-old honor system this week. Since 1893, the school had allowed students to take exams without proctors, relying on an "honor code" that trusted students not to cheat. The administration cited the ease of using AI tools on smartphones, which makes cheating difficult to detect. A survey found that nearly 30 percent of graduates admitted to cheating at some point. Starting July 1st, all exams will require a teacher to be present.

小乖隊完封勇士隊 2-0，終結四連敗

來源：ESPN

芝加哥小熊隊昨天以 2 比 0 完封（讓對手零分）亞特蘭大勇士隊，終結了四場連敗。Ian Happ 轟出全壘打，五名投手聯手只被打出五支安打。

另外，小熊也簽下三屆全明星終結者 Liam Hendriks，加強後援投手陣容。

The Chicago Cubs shut out the Atlanta Braves 2-0 yesterday, ending a four-game losing streak. Ian Happ hit a home run, and five pitchers combined to allow just five hits. The Cubs also signed three-time All-Star closer Liam Hendriks to bolster their relief pitching corps.

兵工廠線——Saka、Odegaard 和 Saliba 三名核心球員昨天都沒有出現在兵工廠的團體訓練中，只做了個人練習。兵工廠只要再贏一場，就能拿下自 2004 年以來的第一個聯賽冠軍。
