川普延長伊朗最後期限到四月六日

來源：來源：Yahoo Finance

川普本來要伊朗在三月二十七日前打開荷莫茲海峽，但結果他多給了十天，延到四月六日。川普說：「他們要七天，我給十天，因為他們放了油船走。」

副總統Vance也去了巴基斯坦，可能會和伊朗官員見面。不過伊朗已經拒絕了美國的和平方案。結果金價先跌到四千零九十八美元，後來又回到四千四百七十美元左右。油價也在一百零八美元上下。

Trump originally demanded that Iran open the Strait of Hormuz by March 27th, but he ended up giving them ten extra days, extending the deadline to April 6th. Trump said: "They asked for seven days, I'm giving them ten, because they released the oil tankers."

Vice President Vance also traveled to Pakistan and may meet with Iranian officials. However, Iran has already rejected the U.S. peace proposal. As a result, gold prices initially fell to $4,098 but later bounced back to around $4,470. Oil prices hovered around $108.

美國最高法院審理總統能不能開除官員

來源：來源：SCOTUSblog

美國最高法院正在看一個很重要的案子：總統可不可以隨時開除政府機關的官員？川普去年開除了兩個FTC的委員，說這個法律不合法。

從一九三五年開始，法律就說總統不能想開除就開除這些官員。另外也有人問：總統能不能開除聯準會的人？如果法院改變了舊的規定，結果會很大——很多機關的人員都可能被換掉。

The U.S. Supreme Court is hearing a very important case: can the president fire government agency officials whenever he wants? Trump fired two FTC commissioners last year, saying that law was illegal.

Since 1935, the law has said the president cannot fire these officials whenever he wants. There's also a question about whether the president can fire people at the Federal Reserve. If the court changes the old rule, the consequences will be huge — people at many agencies could potentially be replaced.

小熊隊開幕戰輸球，鈴木受傷

來源：來源：ESPN

小熊隊在Wrigley球場的開幕戰，結果以四比十輸給國民隊。三萬九千多人來看球。投手Boyd一開始很厲害，前十個打者三振了七個，但第四局就不行了，被打了六分。

好消息是Crow-Armstrong打了兩支安打，也有兩分打點。新隊員Bregman也打了一支安打。不過鈴木誠也因為膝蓋受傷，要休息十天。今年也是小熊隊一百五十週年！

The Cubs lost their opening game at Wrigley Field, falling 4-10 to the Nationals. Over 39,000 people came to watch. Pitcher Boyd started strong, striking out seven of the first ten batters, but fell apart in the fourth inning, giving up six runs.

On the bright side, Crow-Armstrong went 2-for-2 with two RBIs. New teammate Bregman also had a hit. However, Suzuki Seiya suffered a knee injury and will need to rest for ten days. This year also marks the Cubs' 150th anniversary!

AI寫的病毒攻擊了四十七個軟體套件

來源：來源：BleepingComputer

三月十九到二十一日，一群駭客用AI寫了一個叫CanisterWorm的網路病毒。這個病毒攻擊了四十七個npm軟體套件，也就是工廠用的程式工具。它會自己到處散開，偷走工程人員的密碼。

特別的是，這個病毒用區塊鏈來藏自己的指令站，所以很難找到。網路安全人員說，這是第一次有人這樣做。結果很多公司的網站也被影響了。

From March 19 to 21, a group of hackers used AI to create a network virus called CanisterWorm. This virus attacked 47 npm software packages — that is, programming tools used in factories. It spreads on its own and steals engineers' passwords.

What's particularly notable is that this virus uses blockchain to hide its command center, making it extremely difficult to find. Cybersecurity experts say this is the first time someone has done this. As a result, many companies' websites were also affected.

芝加哥公車局派警員上火車

來源：來源：Chicago Tribune

芝加哥的CTA因為安全問題，差一點被聯邦政府拿走五千萬美元的錢。去年十一月，有人在藍線火車上放火燒一個女人，聯邦官員很不高興。

現在CTA的新計畫是：派警員和警隊每個月在車站巡四千四百小時，也加了高門和查票員。結果很不錯，今年到現在暴力犯罪少了百分之十九。

Chicago's CTA nearly had the federal government take away $50 million because of safety concerns. Last November, someone set a woman on fire on a Blue Line train, which made federal officials very unhappy.

Now the CTA's new plan is: deploy police officers and security teams to patrol stations for 4,400 hours per month, and they've also added high gates and ticket checkers. The results have been pretty good — violent crime is down 19% so far this year.
