美國兩位學者拿下諾貝爾經濟學獎

來源：NBC News / CNBC / Axios

今年的諾貝爾經濟學獎在10月12日公布，得主是徲丹佛大學的霞爾（Robert Hall）和哥倫比亞大學的伍德福德（Michael Woodford）。兩人會一起分1200萬瑞典克朗，大約120萬美元。

他們研究的是：家庭和公御每天做的小決定，怎麼加起來影響整個國家的經濟。像伍德福德發現，央行（國家的中央銀行）先告訴大家以後打算怎麼做，就能影響物價。

今年油價大漲，物價跟著上升，各國央行用的正是他們這一套方法。

This year's Nobel Prize in Economics was announced on October 12. The winners are Robert Hall of Stanford University and Michael Woodford of Columbia University. They will share 12 million Swedish kronor, about 1.2 million US dollars. Their research looks at how the small daily decisions of households and companies add up to shape the whole economy. Woodford, for example, found that when a central bank (the institution that manages a country's money) announces in advance what it plans to do, it can influence prices. Oil prices have surged this year and prices have risen with them, and central banks around the world are using exactly the framework these two helped develop.

法官叫停政府用稅金打的電視廣告

來源：CNBC / CBS News / Forbes

紐約聯邦法官羅雄（Jennifer Rochon）10月11日下令，川普政府要馬上停播國土安全部的電視廣告。這筆廣告錢有2000萬美元，已經花掉大約1900萬。

告政府的團體說，這些廣告用的是人民繳的稅，卻像在幫政府拉票。11月就要舉行期中選舉（總統任期中間的國會選舉）。

不過，命令下來以後，電視上還是一直有這些廣告。司法部說，負責的律師到隔天早上才知道這個命令。

On October 11, New York federal judge Jennifer Rochon ordered the Trump administration to stop airing the Department of Homeland Security's TV ads immediately. The ad campaign had a budget of $20 million, and about $19 million of it has already been spent. The groups suing the government argue that these ads use money from taxpayers but work like campaign materials for the government. The midterm elections, the congressional elections held in the middle of a president's term, are coming in November. Even so, the ads have kept appearing on television since the order was issued. The Justice Department says the lawyers responsible did not learn of the order until the next morning.

IMF：今年全球經濟成長3.1%

來源：AP / CNBC

國際貨幣基金（IMF）10月13日在泰國曼谷公布最新預測：今年全世界的經濟會成長3.1%，比7月時想的多一點，但還是比去年的3.5%慢。

美國今年預計成長2.3%。因為油和天然氣變貴，全球物價今年預計會上漲4.7%。IMF假設今年油價平均每桶96美元。

AI熱潮帶來很多投資，幫忙撑住了經濟。不過IMF也提隨，各國欠的錢越來越多，要小心。

The International Monetary Fund (IMF) announced its latest forecast in Bangkok, Thailand, on October 13: the world economy will grow 3.1% this year. That is slightly more than the IMF expected in July, but still slower than last year's 3.5%. The United States is projected to grow 2.3% this year.

Because oil and natural gas have become more expensive, global prices are expected to rise 4.7% this year. The IMF assumes that oil will average $96 per barrel this year.

The AI boom has brought a lot of investment, which has helped keep the economy afloat. However, the IMF also warns that countries are borrowing more and more money, and that they should be careful.

薩利巴背傷不用開刀，最快月底回來

來源：90min / TEAMtalk / Al Jazeera

兵工廠線——兵工廠後衛薩利巴（William Saliba）的背傷還沒好。他今年夏天在世界盃受傷，專家檢查後說不用開刀，只要慢慢做復健。

報導說，球隊在考慮兩個日子讓他回來：10月24日在主場对艾佛頓的英超比賽，或是27日到客場，在聯賽盃对比較弱的弗利特伍德。

11月初兵工廠還要去利物浦打一場大戰，所以球隊寧可慢慢來，不想讓他太早上場。

The Arsenal beat: William Saliba's back injury has not healed yet. He picked it up at the World Cup this summer, and after examinations, specialists said he does not need surgery and can recover with gradual rehabilitation.

Reports say the club is weighing two dates for his return: October 24, at home against Everton in the Premier League, or October 27, away to the weaker Fleetwood Town in the League Cup. Arsenal still have a big clash at Liverpool in early November, so the club would rather bring him back slowly than risk putting him on the pitch too soon.
