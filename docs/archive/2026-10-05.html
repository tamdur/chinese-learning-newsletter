最高法院新年度第一案：城市能告石油公司嗎？

來源：Bloomberg Law

美國最高法院今天開始新的年度，第一個案子就跟氣候有關。科羅拉多州的波德（Boulder）在2018年告了艾克森美孚（ExxonMobil）和森科（Suncor）兩家石油公司，說他們明知道燒石油會讓地球變熱，還騙大家。

石油公司說，氣候是全國的事，應該由聯邦法律來管，地方不能用州法來告。科羅拉多州最高法院之前以5比2，讓這個官司繼續打。

全美還有很多城市和州在等結果，包括芝加哥所在的庫克郡。如果城市贏了，石油公司可能要賠一大筆錢。

The U.S. Supreme Court begins a new year today, and its first case involves climate change. In 2018, Boulder, Colorado sued two oil giants—ExxonMobil and Suncor—accusing them of knowingly deceiving the public about the climate impact of burning petroleum, despite understanding that fossil fuels warm the planet. The oil companies counter that climate is a national issue governed by federal law, not state jurisdiction. Colorado's state supreme court previously ruled 5-2 in favor of allowing the lawsuit to proceed. Many cities and states across the country are watching closely for the outcome, including Cook County, which encompasses Chicago. If the municipalities prevail, the oil companies could face substantial financial liability.

少了威廉斯，熊隊照樣贏噴射機

來源：ESPN

芝加哥熊隊星期天在主場以23比12打敗紐約噴射機隊，戰績變成3勝1敗。先發四分衛威廉斯（Caleb Williams）腿傷還沒好，這場沒有上場。

替補的貝根特（Tyson Bagent）傳球268碼，是他生涯最多。跑衛莫南蓋（Kyle Monangai）跑了146碼，還達陣兩次。

熊隊拿球將近43分鐘，整場一次都沒有棄踢（把球踢給對手），這是他們2014年以來第一次。

The Chicago Bears defeated the New York Jets 23-12 at home on Sunday, improving to 3-1. Starting quarterback Caleb Williams was unavailable due to a lingering leg injury. Backup Tyson Bagent threw for 268 yards, the best of his career, while running back Kyle Monangai rushed for 146 yards and scored twice. The Bears controlled the ball for nearly 43 minutes and did not punt once all game—their first such feat since 2014.
