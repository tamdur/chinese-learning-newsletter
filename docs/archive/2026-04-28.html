川普審查伊朗和平提案，聯合國要求重開荷莫茲海峽

來源：Al Jazeera

伊朗透過巴基斯坦提出和平方案：願意重新開放荷莫茲海峽，條件是美國解除海上封鎖，但核問題先不談。川普正在白宮戰情室和幕僚討論這份提案。

不過，消息人士說川普不太可能接受，因為他堅持核問題必須一起解決。聯合國秘書長警告，海峽持續關閉可能引發全球糧食危機。

Iran has submitted a peace proposal through Pakistan: willing to reopen the Strait of Hormuz in exchange for the US lifting its maritime blockade, but sidelining the nuclear issue for now. Trump is reviewing the proposal with staff in the White House situation room. However, sources say Trump is unlikely to accept, insisting that the nuclear question must be resolved simultaneously. The UN Secretary-General warned that prolonged closure of the strait could trigger a global food crisis.

白宮記者晚宴槍擊嫌犯被控企圖刺殺總統

來源：U.S. Department of Justice / NPR

美國司法部正式起訴31歲的 Cole Tomas Allen，罪名是企圖刺殺總統，最重可判終身監禁。

4月25日晚間，Allen 帶著散彈槍衝過白宮記者晚宴的安檢站，開槍擊中一名特勤人員的防彈衣。特勤局立刻開火還擊並逮捕了他。川普和第一夫人當時已被安全撤離。

The U.S. Department of Justice has formally charged 31-year-old Cole Tomas Allen with attempted assassination of the president, a crime carrying a maximum sentence of life imprisonment. On the evening of April 25, Allen rushed through a security checkpoint at the White House Correspondents' Dinner carrying a shotgun and fired at a Secret Service agent, striking the agent's body armor. The Secret Service immediately returned fire and arrested him. President Trump and the First Lady had already been safely evacuated at the time.

54國齊聚哥倫比亞，討論如何告別化石燃料

來源：NPR / Common Dreams

哥倫比亞和荷蘭在聖瑪爾塔共同主辦了史上第一場專門討論「淘汰化石燃料」的國際會議，54國派代表參加。會議重點不是「要不要」退出化石燃料，而是「怎麼做」。

不過，美國、中國、俄羅斯、印度和日本這幾個最大的排放國都沒有出席。主辦方還禁止石油公司相關人士入場。

Colombia and the Netherlands co-hosted the world's first international conference dedicated specifically to "phasing out fossil fuels" in Santa Marta, with representatives from 54 countries in attendance. The conference's focus was not on "whether" to exit fossil fuels, but rather "how" to do it. However, the world's largest emitters — the United States, China, Russia, India, and Japan — were absent. The organizers also barred fossil fuel industry representatives from the venue.

熊隊新球場法案送進州參議院，球團要求更多優惠

來源：Axios Chicago

伊利諾州眾議院以78比32票通過了大型開發法案，讓芝加哥熊隊可以在 Arlington Heights 蓋新的巨蛋球場。法案現在交給州參議院審議。

但熊隊說還需要更多修改，尤其反對球場周邊餐廳和酒吧要被課9%的娛樂稅。印第安納州也在積極招手，讓談判更加緊張。

The Illinois House passed a major development bill 78-32 that would allow the Chicago Bears to build a new domed stadium in Arlington Heights. The bill now heads to the state senate for review. But the Bears say they need more modifications, particularly opposing a 9% entertainment tax on restaurants and bars around the stadium. Indiana is actively courting the team, adding pressure to the negotiations.

芝加哥小熊寫下本季最精彩的一頁！面對洛杉磯道奇，小熊一度落後四分，卻在七到九局連得六分逆轉，終場6比4勝出。

This marks the Cubs' longest winning streak since their 2016 World Series championship season. Fans are beginning to wonder: could this be a special year?

伊朗和談破局 油價飆破每桶一百一十美元

來源：CNBC

伊朗戰爭的和談這個週末又卡住了。伊朗外交部長阿拉奇提前離開巴基斯坦，川普於是取消了兩位特使魏特科夫和庫許納的行程，兩邊根本沒坐下來談。

伊朗提出一個新方案：先把荷姆茲海峽重新打開、結束戰爭，核武的問題以後再談。川普不買帳，說德黑蘭內部「吵得很兇、混亂不堪」。一名伊朗官員也放話：海峽「在任何情況下」都不會恢復原狀。週六只有 19 艘商船通過這個原本運送全球五分之一石油的水道。

市場立刻反應。布蘭特原油星期二漲了 3.2%，每桶衝破 110 美元，是三個禮拜以來第一次；美國西德州原油也站上 100 美元。戰爭開打以來布蘭特已經漲超過 55%，最高逼近 120 美元。更糟的是，阿拉伯聯合大公國本週宣布退出石油輸出國組織（OPEC），這個產油大聯盟也開始裂開了。

Talks over the Iran war stalled again this weekend. Iran's Foreign Minister Araghchi left Pakistan early, prompting Trump to cancel the itineraries of his two envoys, Wittekoff and Kushner—the two sides never even sat down to negotiate.

Iran proposed a new plan: reopen the Strait of Hormuz first, end the war, and discuss the nuclear issue later. Trump rejected it, saying Tehran's internal situation was "very contentious and chaotic." An Iranian official fired back, saying the strait would not return to normal "under any circumstances." On Saturday, only 19 merchant vessels passed through the waterway that normally carries one-fifth of the world's oil supply.

Markets reacted immediately. Brent crude surged 3.2% on Tuesday, breaking through $110 per barrel for the first time in three weeks; U.S. West Texas crude also crossed $100. Since the war began, Brent has climbed more than 55%, approaching $120 at its peak. To make matters worse, the United Arab Emirates announced this week that it would withdraw from the Organization of the Petroleum Exporting Countries (OPEC)—the oil producers' alliance is beginning to fracture.
