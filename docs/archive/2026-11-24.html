公牛延長賽輸金塊，戰績4勝11敗

來源：NBA.com

芝加哥公牛隊三天內輸了兩場很接近的比賽。週五對華盛頓巫師，鮑威爾（Norman Powell）最後一球想追平比數，球卻在籃框彈了出來，以94比98輸球。

週日晚上對丹佛金塊，兩隊打進延長賽，公牛最後以145比151落敗。金塊的穆雷（Jamal Murray）拿下45分，約基奇（Nikola Jokic）也有37分、18個籃板和11次助攻。

公牛現在是4勝11敗。更糟的是，主力鮑威爾大腿後側受傷，球隊23日又和中鋒羅賓森（Orlando Robinson）解約。公牛已經能跟強隊打得很接近，可是人手越來越少。

The Chicago Bulls lost two close games in three days. On Friday against the Washington Wizards, Norman Powell's last-second shot to tie the game rimmed out, and the Bulls fell 94-98.

On Sunday night against the Denver Nuggets, the teams went into overtime, and the Bulls lost 145-151. Jamal Murray scored 45 points for Denver, and Nikola Jokic added 37 points, 18 rebounds, and 11 assists.

The Bulls are now 4-11. Even worse, Powell pulled a hamstring, and on the 23rd the team also parted ways with center Orlando Robinson. The Bulls have been able to stay close with strong teams, but they are running short on bodies.

兵工廠今晚主場迎戰多特蒙德

來源：Squawka

兵工廠線——兵工廠今晚在主場酋長球場，迎戰德國的多特蒙德。這是歐洲冠軍聯賽的比賽，兵工廠踢了4場拿到10分，多特蒙德有9分，兩隊只差1分。

聯賽階段排名前八的球隊，可以直接進入淘汰賽，不用多打一輪附加賽。所以今晚誰能贏球，誰就更接近前八。

兵工廠兩名主力中後衛都還在養傷。預計先發有萊斯（Rice）、蘇比門迪（Zubimendi）、埃澤（Eze）和哲凱賴什（Gyokeres）。接下來英超還要對上曼城，賽程很緊。

The Arsenal beat: Arsenal host Borussia Dortmund tonight at the Emirates Stadium in the Champions League. Arsenal have played four matches and collected 10 points, while Dortmund have 9. The two sides are separated by just one point.

The top eight teams after the league phase advance straight into the knockout rounds without having to play a playoff. So whichever side wins tonight moves closer to a place in the top eight.

Two of Arsenal's first-choice centre-backs are still out injured. The expected starting lineup is Rice, Zubimendi, Eze and Gyökeres. Arsenal also face Manchester City next in the Premier League, so the fixture schedule is tight.
