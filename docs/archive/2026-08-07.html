川普簽兩道命令限制出生公民權

來源：NPR

八月六日，美國總統川普簽下兩道行政命令，對付「生育旅遊」，也就是外國人特地到美國生小孩，好讓孩子拿到美國國籍。

一道命令要外交官不要發簽證給這種旅客；另一道把出生就有公民權的例外縮小，例如外國恐怖組織成員的小孩。

六月時，最高法院才以六比三擋下川普範圍更大的做法。學者說，這兩道命令馬上會被告上法院。

On August 6th, US President Trump signed two executive orders to address "birth tourism"—when foreigners specifically travel to the United States to have children so they can obtain US citizenship.

One order directs diplomats not to issue visas to such travelers; the other narrows exceptions to birthright citizenship, such as for children of foreign terrorist organization members.

In June, the Supreme Court blocked Trump's broader approach with a 6-3 vote. Legal scholars say these two orders will face immediate court challenges.

Meta 的 AI 測試出錯，入侵外部公司系統

來源：SiliconANGLE

八月六日，Meta 說自家的 Muse Spark 1.1 模型在安全測試時，真的攻進了另一家公司的系統。

問題出在測試廠商 Irregular 設定錯誤，讓模型不小心連上了網路。八天前，Anthropic 也因為同一家廠商、同樣的錯誤出過事。

Meta 說會查清楚再公布報告。看來真正的弱點不是某一個模型，而是整個產業共用的測試環境。

On August 6, Meta disclosed that its Muse Spark 1.1 model had actually breached another company's systems during safety testing.

The problem stemmed from a configuration error at testing vendor Irregular, which inadvertently connected the model to the internet. Eight days earlier, Anthropic had experienced an identical incident at the same vendor due to the same misconfiguration.

Meta says it will investigate thoroughly before releasing its report. The real vulnerability appears to lie not in any single model, but in the shared testing environment used across the industry.

葉門停火破裂，青年運動攻擊政府軍

來源：Bloomberg

八月六日，葉門的青年運動（Houthi，伊朗支持的武裝團體）用飛彈和無人機攻擊政府軍營地，地點在馬里卜和哈德拉毛兩省。

葉門政府說至少三十名士兵死亡，青年運動則說打死打傷好幾百人。這是二○二二年停火以來最嚴重的一次。

過去他們大多攻擊紅海上的商船，這次卻把戰火拉回自己國家的內戰。

On August 6th, Yemen's Houthi movement—an Iran-backed armed group—attacked government military bases using missiles and drones in Marib and Hadramawt provinces.

The Yemeni government said at least 30 soldiers were killed, while the Houthis claimed they killed and wounded several hundred. This is the most serious escalation since the 2022 ceasefire.

In the past, they mostly targeted commercial ships in the Red Sea, but this time they've brought the fighting back to their own country's civil war.

關稅退款只給企業，州長要求發錢給家庭

來源：WTTW Chicago

最高法院判川普的關稅違法以後，美國財政部上個月底退了一千億美元給企業，光是蘋果就拿回二十二億。

八月六日，伊利諾州長普利茲克寫信給川普，要求直接發一千七百美元給州內每個家庭。他說：「洛克福德的媽媽買菜，沒有地方可以申請退款。」

他也要川普支持一項法案，讓拿到退款的公司把商品價格降下來。

After the Supreme Court ruled Trump's tariffs illegal, the U.S. Treasury Department refunded $100 billion to businesses at the end of last month. Apple alone recovered $2.2 billion.

On August 6th, Illinois Governor Pritzker wrote to Trump, demanding to directly distribute $1,700 to every household in the state. He said: "A mom in Rockford buying groceries has nowhere to apply for a refund."

He also asked Trump to support a bill that would require companies receiving refunds to lower their prices.

熊隊訓練營，歐頓齊說腳傷已經全好

來源：ESPN

芝加哥熊隊星期四練了一百三十分鐘，而且穿上全套護具，是這次訓練營最長、也最激烈的一天。

最亮眼的是外接員歐頓齊（Rome Odunze）。他去年腳掌骨折，開完刀休養了一個夏天，這天在邊線接下一顆長傳。他說自己的腳「感覺很棒」，現在幾乎不會再想到那個傷。

他也講出今年的目標：接球一百次、跑出一千碼。

The Chicago Bears practiced for 130 minutes on Thursday in full pads, marking the longest and most intense day of this training camp.

The standout was wide receiver Rome Odunze. He suffered a foot fracture last year and underwent surgery, spending a summer in recovery. That day, he caught a deep pass on the sideline. He said his foot "feels great" and he now barely thinks about the injury anymore.

He also shared his goals for this year: 100 receptions and 1,000 receiving yards.

維尼修斯續約留在皇馬，兵工廠落空

來源：ESPN

兵工廠線——維尼修斯確定不會來了。八月六日，皇家馬德里正式宣布，這位二十六歲的巴西邊鋒一口氣續約六年，合約到二○三二年。

兵工廠原本想一次簽下他和紐卡索隊長布魯諾，現在只剩下後面這一位。

剛拿下二十二年來第一座英超冠軍的球隊，必須在轉會窗關門前，另外找一個左路攻擊手。

Arsenal Beat — Vinicius is staying put. On August 6th, Real Madrid officially announced that the 26-year-old Brazilian winger has renewed his contract for six years, keeping him through 2032.

Arsenal had been hoping to secure both him and Newcastle captain Bruno at once, but now only the latter remains in play.

The team that just captured their first Premier League title in 22 years must now find another left-winger before the transfer window closes.
