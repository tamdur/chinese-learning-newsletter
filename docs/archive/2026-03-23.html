密西根狼獾隊以第一種子闖進甜蜜十六強

來源：mgoblue.com

密西根大學籃球隊今年打得很好，是 NCAA 的第一種子隊。他們已經贏了三十三場，只輸了三場，這也是學校的歷史記錄。

球員 Lendeborg 在第二場得了二十五分，而 Johnson 在第一場八投八中，結果拿了二十一分和十個籃板。教練 Dusty May 也創了記錄：他是第一個在頭兩年都帶隊打進甜蜜十六強的密西根教練。下一場在芝加哥，等於是主場比賽！

The University of Michigan basketball team had an excellent season this year and earned the number one seed in the NCAA tournament. They've won thirty-three games with only three losses, a school record.

Player Lendeborg scored twenty-five points in the second game, while Johnson shot eight for eight in the first game, finishing with twenty-one points and ten rebounds. Coach Dusty May also made history — he's the first Michigan coach to lead his team to the Sweet Sixteen in both of his first two years. The next game is in Chicago, which essentially feels like a home game!

美以伊戰爭第二十二天：伊朗打以色列核設施

來源：Reuters

伊朗向以色列南部兩個城市發射了飛彈，其中一個是 Dimona，那裡有以色列最重要的核研究站。結果至少一百八十人受傷，但核設施沒有被打到。

伊朗說這是對美軍打伊朗核工廠的回應。Trump 在網站上說，如果伊朗不在四十八小時內打開荷莫茲海峽（全世界最重要的石油通道），美軍就會打伊朗的電廠。伊朗也說，如果被打，他們會打美軍基地所在國的電廠。

Iran fired missiles at two cities in southern Israel, one of which is Dimona, home to Israel's most important nuclear research facility. At least 180 people were injured as a result, but the nuclear facility was not hit.

Iran said this was in response to a U.S. military strike on Iran's nuclear facilities. Trump stated on his website that if Iran does not open the Strait of Hormuz (the world's most critical oil passage) within 48 hours, the U.S. military will strike Iran's power plants. Iran also said that if attacked, they will strike power plants in countries where U.S. military bases are located.

用筆電跑三千九百七十億個參數的 AI 模型

來源：GitHub

一個叫 Flash-MoE 的新工具，可以在 MacBook Pro 筆電上跑一個有三千九百七十億個參數的 AI 模型。這個模型的文件有兩百多 GB，但每次只用一小部分，所以不需要太多記憶。

開發員 Dan Woods 和 Claude AI 一起花了大約二十四小時就做出來了。他試了很多方法，結果發現最好的方法是「相信作業系統」。這個專案也在 GitHub 網站上得到了快一千顆星。

A new tool called Flash-MoE makes it possible to run an AI model with 397 billion parameters on a MacBook Pro laptop. The model's file is over 200 GB, but it only uses a small portion at a time, so it doesn't require too much memory.

Developer Dan Woods and Claude AI spent about 24 hours building it together. He tried many approaches and found that the best method was to "trust the operating system." The project also gained nearly 1,000 stars on GitHub.

數學天才陶哲軒談 AI 與數學的未來

來源：Marginal Revolution

世界有名的數學家陶哲軒最近說，AI 已經讓「想新點子」變得很便宜，現在最難的是「確認對不對」。他說如果沒有 AI，他現在寫的論文要花五倍的時間。

不過他也指出，目前的 AI 比較像「人工小聰明」，會不停試，但還不會真正學習。結果是，AI 可以很快地產生想法，但人員還是要負責判斷哪些是對的。

Renowned mathematician Terrence Tao said recently that AI has made "coming up with new ideas" very cheap, but now the hardest part is "verifying if they're correct." He noted that without AI, the papers he's writing now would take five times longer to complete.

However, he also pointed out that current AI is more like "artificial shallow cleverness"—it keeps trying things but hasn't truly learned. The result is that AI can quickly generate ideas, but people still have to be responsible for judging which ones are actually right.

CERN 發現新粒子：雙魅重子

來源：CERN

CERN 的科學家用大型對撞機（讓粒子高速撞在一起的機器）找到了一種新的粒子。這個粒子有兩個「魅」夸克（charm quark）和一個「下」夸克，重量大約是一個質子的四倍。

這也是 LHC 發現的第八十個新粒子。它的「兄弟」粒子在二零一七年就被找到了，但這個新粒子壽命短六倍，所以更難找到。這個結果也解開了科學家二十年來的爭論。

Scientists at CERN used the Large Hadron Collider — a machine that smashes particles together at high speeds — to discover a new particle. This particle contains two charm quarks and one down quark, with a mass roughly four times that of a proton.

This is the 80th new particle discovered by the LHC. Its "sibling" particle was found back in 2017, but this new particle has a lifetime six times shorter, making it much harder to detect. The discovery also resolves a scientific debate that has lasted for twenty years.
