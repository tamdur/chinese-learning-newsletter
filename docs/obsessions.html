霧裡的歌聲：落差草原的《霧海》

來源：GuruGuru Brain／Rush Hour

落差草原 WWWW 是台北的一個樂團，在 2010 年成立。2016 年，他們在日本唱片公司 GuruGuru Brain 推出《霧海》，這張唱片只有五首歌。

他們把長笛、吉他和電子聲音疊在一起，再加上像祭典（拜神的大活動）一樣的唱聲和鼓聲，靈感來自台灣原住民和民間音樂。有人說，這像一場慢慢走的送葬（送死去的人最後一程）隊伍。

聽的時候，聲音好像被一片大霧蓋住，看不清楚，卻讓人一直想往前走。

Falls Grassland WWWW is a band from Taipei, formed in 2010. In 2016, they released Sea of Mist on the Japanese label GuruGuru Brain. The album has just five songs. They layer flute, guitar, and electronic sounds, then add festival-style chanting and drums, drawing inspiration from Taiwanese Indigenous and folk music. Some listeners say it sounds like a slow funeral procession winding its way to the grave. As you listen, the sound seems to be wrapped in a thick fog, hard to see through, yet it keeps pulling you forward.

一位醫生拼出來的國家史詩

來源：Parppeinvaara Rune Singer's Village／Visit Finland

很多芬蘭人以為《卡勒瓦拉》是一首很古老的史詩（很長、講英雄故事的詩），其實它是鄉下醫生倫洛特一點一點拼起來的。

他十一次走進東邊的卡累利亞，聽村民唱歌，再把各地的歌接成一個長故事。1835 年先出第一版，1849 年又出了更長的新版。很多詩句都來自同一位歌手：佩爾圖寧。

今天，在伊洛曼齊的「歌者村」，還能聽到這種老唱法，也能聽傳統樂器康特勒彈出的音樂。

Many Finns assume the Kalevala is a very ancient epic (a long poem about heroes), but it was actually pieced together bit by bit by a country doctor named Elias Lönnrot. He made eleven trips into eastern Karelia, listened to villagers sing, and then stitched the songs from different places into one long story. The first edition appeared in 1835, and a much longer new edition followed in 1849. Many of its verses come from the same singer, Perttunen. Today, in the "Village of Singers" in Ilomantsi, you can still hear this old style of singing, along with music played on the traditional kantele.

星期天的熱派課：從麵皮開始

來源：Chicago Botanic Garden

11 月 22 日星期天早上十一點，芝加哥植物園在 Glencoe 開一堂做菜課。

大家要自己動手，從麵皮開始做兩個鹹派：一個放雞肉、大蔥、豌豆（小小圓圓的綠豆子）和菇；另一個放南瓜、藍紋起司（有藍色花紋、味道很重的起司）和牛小排。

一堂課大約 95 到 119 美元，要先報名，位子不多了。天氣變冷了，剛出爐的熱派正好。

On Sunday, November 22, at 11 a.m., the Chicago Botanic Garden in Glencoe is holding a cooking class. Participants will make their own dough from scratch and bake two savory pies. One is filled with chicken, scallions, peas (small round green beans), and mushrooms. The other is filled with pumpkin, blue cheese (a strong-flavored cheese with blue veining), and beef short ribs. The class costs about $95 to $119 and requires advance registration, and spots are running low. With the weather turning cold, a hot pie straight from the oven is just right.

一位老師畫下五百個句子

來源：german-latin-english.com

美國退休的外語老師 Eugene Moutoux 做了一個個人網站，放了五百多張「句子圖」。

這種畫法用一條條橫線，把一句話拆成主角、動作和小句子，好像畫出句子的骨頭。它在 1847 年出現，1870 年代由 Reed 和 Kellogg 兩位老師改好，以前美國學校都會教。

網站最精彩的一頁，把林肯有名的〈蓋茲堡演說〉（在大家面前的講話）一句一句畫出來。看著這些線，你會發現好句子也有漂亮的形狀。

Eugene Moutoux, a retired American foreign language teacher, has built a personal website with more than 500 "sentence diagrams." The method uses a series of horizontal lines to break a sentence into its subject, its action, and smaller phrases, like sketching the skeleton of a sentence. It first appeared in 1847, was refined by teachers Reed and Kellogg in the 1870s, and was once taught as a matter of course in American schools. The site's standout page diagrams Lincoln's famous Gettysburg Address one sentence at a time. Looking at those lines, you'll see that good sentences have beautiful shapes too.
