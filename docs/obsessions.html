用打鐵聲做成的唱片

來源：Red Dot Design Award

2019年，台中亞洲大學的四個學生做了一套黑膠唱片，叫「Track Back To」。它得到紅點設計大獎的最高獎。

他們去找老師傅錄音：打鐵的敲打聲、做西裝的剪刀聲、刻神像的鑿子（刻木頭的工具）聲。然後把聲音做成新音樂：打鐵配龐克，做衣服配爵士，刻神像配南管（台灣傳統音樂）。

這些手藝快要消失了，但它們的聲音被留了下來。

In 2019, four students at Asia University in Taichung made a vinyl record called "Track Back To." It won the top prize at the Red Dot Design Award. They went to find master craftspeople and recorded their work: the clang of a blacksmith's hammer, the snip of a tailor's scissors, and the chisel of a woodcarver who carves religious statues. Then they turned these sounds into new music: blacksmithing paired with punk, clothes-making with jazz, and statue carving with nanguan, a traditional Taiwanese music style. These crafts are close to disappearing, but their sounds have been preserved.

這片土地是我們的孩子

來源：Siida

在芬蘭北方的小村伊納里，有一座薩米人（住在北極圈一帶的民族）的博物館，叫 Siida。它在2024年得到「歐洲年度博物館」大獎。

館裡的展覽叫「這片土地是我們的孩子」。特別的是，由薩米人自己決定怎麼說自己的故事：養馴鹿的生活，還有他們和北極大地的關係。

三月去拉普蘭時，可以來這裡慢慢看，再去拜訪養馴鹿的人家。

In Inari, a small village in northern Finland, there is a museum of the Sámi people (an Indigenous people who live around the Arctic Circle) called Siida. In 2024 it won the European Museum of the Year award. The museum's exhibition is titled "This Land Is Our Child." What makes it special is that the Sámi themselves decided how their own story would be told: the life of herding reindeer, and their relationship with the vast Arctic land. If you visit Lapland in March, you can come here to take your time looking around, and then visit a family that herds reindeer.

一起看月亮和土星

來源：Triton College Cernan Earth and Space Center

11月21日星期六晚上6點半到9點，芝加哥天文學會在 Triton 學院的 Cernan 太空中心外面，辦免費的看星星活動。

會員會帶自己的望遠鏡來，讓大家看月亮和土星的環。不用報名，也不用自己帶望遠鏡，4歲以上都可以來。

這種老派的天文之夜已經辦了很多年，卻很少人知道。如果下雨、多雲或風太大，就會取消。

On Saturday, November 21, from 6:30 to 9:00 p.m., the Chicago Astronomical Society will hold a free stargazing event outside the Cernan Space Center at Triton College. Members will bring their own telescopes so visitors can look at the Moon and the rings of Saturn. No reservation is needed, and you don't need to bring a telescope yourself. Anyone age 4 and up is welcome. This old-fashioned night of astronomy has been running for many years, yet few people know about it. If it rains, is cloudy, or the wind is too strong, the event will be cancelled.

一個人騎車拍下一座城

來源：TOBuilt

2006年，多倫多的 Bob Krawczyk 開始騎著腳踏車，在市中心拍房子。他不只拍有名的大樓，也拍小店、公寓，還有後來被拆掉的老房子。

到了2013年，他一個人做了大約一萬筆資料。後來一個保護老建築的團體接手，叫它 TOBuilt。現在網站上有一萬五千多棟建築，每棟都有地址、年代和照片。

按下「Surprise Me」，它會帶你看一棟你可能每天走過、卻從沒注意過的房子。

In 2006, Bob Krawczyk of Toronto began riding his bicycle through the city center, photographing buildings. He did not only shoot the famous towers. He also photographed small shops, apartments, and older houses that were later torn down. By 2013, he had compiled about 10,000 entries on his own. Later, a group dedicated to protecting historic architecture took over the project and named it TOBuilt. The website now lists more than 15,000 buildings, and each one comes with an address, a construction date, and photographs. Press "Surprise Me," and it will show you a building you may walk past every day without ever having noticed it.
