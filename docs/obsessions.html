台北噪音牆：一張以紐約為名的夢

來源：Manic Sheep Bandcamp / beehype

二〇一六年，台北四人樂團「躁羊」（Manic Sheep）發行了第二張專輯，名字叫《Brooklyn》，也就是紐約的布魯克林區。有趣的是，團員們從來沒去過那裡。

主唱 Chris Lo 從貝斯（低音吉他）改彈吉他，讓聲音變得又厚又重，像一面噪音做成的牆，人聲在裡面輕輕飄浮。這張專輯被選為當年台灣最好的搖滾專輯之一，也是少數受到海外注意的台灣搖滾作品。

In 2016, the Taipei four-piece band Manic Sheep released their second album, titled Brooklyn—named after New York's Brooklyn borough. Ironically, the band members had never actually been there. Lead singer Chris Lo switched from bass to guitar, creating a thick, heavy sound like a wall of noise, with vocals floating delicately within it. The album was chosen as one of Taiwan's best rock albums that year and became one of the rare Taiwanese rock works to gain overseas attention.

雪地列車帶你去挖寶石

來源：Lampivaara Amethyst Mine (amethystmine.fi)

在芬蘭北部的拉普蘭（Lapland），有一座全歐洲唯一對外開放的紫水晶礦場。它在呂斯托國家公園裡的蘭皮瓦拉山丘頂上，那裡的紫水晶是二十億年前火山岩石裡長出來的。

冬天去的話，你可以坐上一輛有暖氣的雪地列車，穿過冰雪覆蓋的森林到達山頂。在那裡，你可以親手從土裡挖出自己的紫水晶，然後帶回家當紀念。三月時白雪還很深，風景非常美。

In Lapland in northern Finland, there is Europe's only publicly accessible amethyst mine. Perched atop Lampivaara Hill within Pyhä-Luosto National Park, the amethysts here formed within volcanic rock two billion years ago. If you visit in winter, you can board a heated snowmobile train that winds through snow-blanketed forests to reach the summit. There, you can dig your own amethyst directly from the earth and take it home as a souvenir. In March, the snow lies deep and the landscape is breathtaking.

一天學會做麵包，還能帶酵母回家

來源：The Chopping Block

五月二十三日，芝加哥的 The Chopping Block 烹飪（煮菜）教室會舉辦一整天的手工麵包課。地點在 Merchandise Mart 大樓裡，從早上十點到下午四點半，費用三百美元。

課程會教你發酵（讓麵團膨脹）的科學原理，還有揉麵和塑形的方法。最棒的是，每個人可以帶一罐活的酸麵團酵母回家，繼續自己烤麵包。

On May 23rd, The Chopping Block cooking school in Chicago will host a full-day artisanal bread-making class. Located in the Merchandise Mart building, the class runs from 10 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. and costs $300. The course teaches the science behind fermentation—how to make dough rise—along with kneading and shaping techniques. The best part: each participant gets to take home a jar of live sourdough starter to continue baking bread at home.

一個人聽完一千多張唱片，然後寫下來

來源：Between Sound and Space: ECM Records and Beyond

ECM 是一家來自北歐的唱片公司，五十多年來專門出版爵士樂和現代古典音樂。它的聲音風格很特別：安靜、空曠、有很深的迴音。

二〇一〇年起，學者 Tyran Grillo 開始在他的網站 ecmreviews.com 上寫每一張 ECM 唱片的評論。到二〇一五年，他寫完了超過一千張的全部目錄，至今還在持續更新。每篇評論都像一篇小文章，認真對待每張唱片。

ECM is a record label from Northern Europe that has specialized in jazz and contemporary classical music for over fifty years. It has a distinctive sonic signature: quiet, spacious, with deep reverb. Starting in 2010, scholar Tyran Grillo began writing reviews of every ECM album on his website, ecmreviews.com. By 2015, he had completed reviews of over one thousand records from the complete catalog, and continues updating to this day. Each review reads like a short essay, treating every album with serious attention.
