一張只有海浪的唱片

來源：Sound Traces (soundtraces.tw)

一九九四年夏天，錄音師傅崑民在花蓮錄完阿美族的豐年祭。天快亮時，他一個人走到東海岸，只錄下海浪的聲音。

這張《浪來了》長四十七分鐘，沒有音樂，也沒有人聲，被看作台灣第一張純自然聲音的專輯。

出版的水晶唱片是台灣獨立音樂的先鋒（走在最前面的人）。在解嚴（結束軍事管制）後的年代，他們把系列取名「傾聽，台灣的話」，讓海浪也替台灣說話。

In the summer of 1994, sound engineer Kun-min recorded the Amis harvest festival in Hualien. Just before dawn, he walked alone to the east coast and recorded nothing but the sound of the waves. The resulting album, Waves Are Coming (浪來了), runs 47 minutes with no music and no human voice. It is regarded as Taiwan's first album of pure natural sound. The label that released it, Crystal Records, was a pioneer of Taiwan's independent music. In the years after martial law was lifted, the label named its series "Listening: Taiwan's Words," letting the waves speak for Taiwan too.

從一顆蛋誕生的森林之家

來源：Finnish Architecture Navigator (finnisharchitecture.fi)

在赫爾辛基城外的努克西奧國家公園旁，有一座像蛋的木頭建築，叫做「哈爾蒂亞」。它是芬蘭的國家自然中心，二〇一三年完成，也是芬蘭第一座大型全木造的公共建築。

它的樣子來自芬蘭史詩（很長的古老故事詩）《卡勒瓦拉》：空氣女神膝上的一顆鴨蛋破開，變成了世界。館內還有一間蛋形的木頭展覽室。

屋頂長滿青草，用地熱和太陽能取暖。三月來這裡，可以穿上雪鞋，走進古老的森林。

On the edge of Nuuksio National Park, just outside Helsinki, stands an egg-shaped wooden building called Haltia. It is Finland's national nature centre. Completed in 2013, it was the country's first large all-timber public building. Its form draws on the Kalevala, Finland's epic poem (a very long, ancient narrative poem): a duck's egg resting on the knee of the air goddess cracks open and becomes the world. Inside, there is also an egg-shaped wooden exhibition room. The roof is covered in grass, and the building is heated by geothermal and solar energy. Visit in March, and you can strap on snowshoes and walk into the ancient forest.

相撲力士來到芝加哥

來源：Audacy WXRT Chicago

十一月七日，全新的「相撲冠軍聯盟」要在芝加哥的室內體育館比賽。比賽是真打，不是事先排好的表演。這可能是美國第一次在大型球館舉辦職業相撲。

聯盟簽下來自九個國家的四十多位重量級力士（相撲選手），他們整季爭排名，也爭獎金。十月十日，聯盟已經在德州奧斯汀附近打完第一場。

門票已經開賣。不用飛到東京，就能親眼看到這項古老的日本運動。

On November 7, the brand-new Sumo Champion League will stage its matches at an indoor arena in Chicago. These are real contests, not pre-arranged exhibitions. It may be the first time professional sumo has been held in a major arena in the United States. The league has signed more than forty heavyweight rikishi (sumo wrestlers) from nine countries. They compete all season for rankings and for prize money. On October 10, the league already held its first event near Austin, Texas, and tickets for Chicago are now on sale. You won't have to fly to Tokyo to watch this ancient Japanese sport in person.

地圖上消失的鐵路

來源：FRRandP (Forgotten Railroads, Roads & Places)

鐵路迷安德魯‧葛利格沒有任何機構幫忙，花了好幾年做出一張網路地圖，畫出已經廢棄（不再使用）的鐵路。

地圖上已有大約兩千五百條舊鐵路，總長超過六萬英里，橫跨美國、英國和加拿大。他靠老的空中照片、鐵道迷社群和舊的政府資料，一條一條找回來。

有一條消失的鐵路，現在藏在高速公路、住宅區和電影院底下。這張地圖永遠不會完成，新的虛線還在一直出現。

Railfan Andrew Gregory, with no institution helping him, spent several years building an online map of abandoned railways, the lines that are no longer in use. The map already shows roughly 2,500 old rail lines, totaling more than 60,000 miles across the United States, Britain, and Canada. He tracked them down one by one, using old aerial photographs, railfan communities, and old government records. One vanished line now lies beneath a highway, a housing development, and a movie theater. The map will never be finished, because new dotted lines keep appearing.
