落日飛車的城市流行夢

來源：Spotify、Audiotree、金曲獎

二〇〇九年，一群台北年輕人組了落日飛車樂團。他們解散過，又重新回來。二〇一八年的專輯 Cassa Nova 是他們最好的作品：溫暖的薩克斯風、復古合成器、迪斯可節奏，在倫敦傳奇的 Abbey Road 錄音室完成母帶處理。九首歌，三十五分鐘，像深夜開車穿過城市的感覺。

他們也是第一個在芝加哥音樂節目 Audiotree 演出的台灣樂團。單曲 My Jinji 在 Spotify 上已有超過三千兩百萬次播放。城市流行不只是日本的事——台灣也有自己的聲音。

Sunset Rollercoaster, a Taipei-based band formed by a group of young musicians in 2009, has had a tumultuous journey—disbanding and reuniting along the way. Their 2018 album Cassa Nova stands as their finest work: warm saxophone, vintage synthesizers, and disco rhythms mastered at the legendary Abbey Road Studios in London. Nine tracks spanning thirty-five minutes, the album captures the feeling of driving through the city late at night.

They also became the first Taiwanese band to perform on Chicago's music program Audiotree. Their single "My Jinji" has accumulated over 32 million plays on Spotify. City pop is not solely a Japanese phenomenon—Taiwan has its own distinctive sound.

拉普蘭的雪橇犬

來源：Bearhill Husky、Visit Rovaniemi

在芬蘭北部的拉普蘭，有一種旅行方式比什麼都安靜：狗拉雪橇。 Bearhill Husky 農場在羅瓦涅米附近，養了大約一百隻阿拉斯加雪橇犬。兩個人各自駕一隊狗，穿過冰雪森林，中途交換。最長的行程跑三十到四十公里，中間在營火旁吃午餐。

三月是最好的時間：雪很厚，白天有十二小時光，夜晚又夠暗，可以看極光。芬蘭人叫極光「狐火」——傳說是狐狸尾巴掃過天空的光。回到小木屋，喝一杯熱莓果汁，整個世界都安靜了。

In Lapland in northern Finland, there is a way to travel that is quieter than anything else: dog sledding. Bearhill Husky Farm, located near Rovaniemi, raises about a hundred Alaskan sled dogs. Two people each drive a team of dogs, traveling through snowy forests, and switch teams midway. The longest routes cover thirty to forty kilometers, with lunch by a campfire along the way.

March is the best time: the snow is thick, there are twelve hours of daylight, and the nights are dark enough to see the aurora borealis. Finns call the northern lights "fox fire"—legend says it is the light of a fox's tail sweeping across the sky. Back at the cabin, sipping a cup of hot berry juice, the whole world falls silent.

芝加哥紀錄片影展十歲了

來源：Doc10 Film Festival、doc10.org

Doc10 是芝加哥唯一的紀錄片影展，今年剛好第十屆。規則很簡單：每年只選十部片，都是從日舞、翠貝卡等大影展挑出來的。少，但每一部都值得看。

放映地點在 Davis Theater（Lincoln Square）和 Gene Siskel Film Center。過去選過的片包括《一胎中國》和《美國工廠》，都是很有力量的故事。今年四月二十四日到五月三日，票可以在 doc10.org 買。

Doc10 is Chicago's only documentary festival, and this year marks its tenth edition. The concept is simple: just ten films selected annually from major festivals like Sundance and Tribeca. Small, but every single one is worth watching.

Screenings take place at Davis Theater (Lincoln Square) and the Gene Siskel Film Center. Past selections have included "One Child Nation" and "American Factory"—both powerful stories. This year runs April 24 through May 3, and tickets are available at doc10.org.
