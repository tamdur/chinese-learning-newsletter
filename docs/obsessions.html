蒙懂的道火：蒙眼演奏的台灣迷幻兄弟

來源：Bandcamp Daily

台北有一對兄弟，叫做夢東（Mong Tong）。他們表演的時候會蒙住眼睛，用鍵盤和取樣機做出像夢一樣的音樂。

二〇二三年他們在東京的 Guruguru Brain 唱片公司發行了《道火》這張專輯。裡面混合了甘美朗（印尼傳統音樂）、印度鼓和台灣民間樂器的聲音，還加入東南亞街頭婚喪儀式的錄音。他們把道教當作活的聲音材料，而不只是歷史。

Taipei is home to a duo called Mong Tong. When they perform, they cover their eyes and create dreamlike music using keyboards and sampling machines. In 2023, they released an album called "Dao Huo" (道火) through the Tokyo-based record label Guruguru Brain. The album blends the sounds of gamelan (Indonesian traditional music), Indian drums, and Taiwanese folk instruments, layered with field recordings from Southeast Asian street ceremonies for weddings and funerals. They treat Daoism not as history, but as living sonic material.

在冰上滑行：芬蘭圖爾庫群島的冬日冒險

來源：Visit Finland

芬蘭西南方有一片圖爾庫群島，由四萬個大大小小的島嶼組成。到了冬天，海面結冰，渡船停開，取而代之的是直接鋪在冰面上的公路。

三月是最好的時機：白天變長了，但冰還很厚。你可以穿上長刀冰鞋，在結冰的海灣上滑行好幾公里。這裡很多居民的母語其實是瑞典語，和赫爾辛基的氣氛很不一樣。

In southwestern Finland lies the Turku Archipelago, a collection of forty thousand islands of varying sizes. When winter arrives, the sea freezes over, ferries cease operations, and in their place emerge roads laid directly atop the ice. March offers the ideal window: daylight stretches longer, yet the ice remains thick enough. You can don long-bladed ice skates and glide for kilometers across the frozen bays. Swedish is the native tongue for many residents here, giving the region an atmosphere quite distinct from Helsinki.

用金子修補裂痕：芝加哥金繕工作坊

來源：Japanese Culture Center

金繕（Kintsugi）是日本一種古老的技術：把破掉的碗盤用金色漆（一種含金粉的膠）黏起來，讓裂痕變成美麗的金線。

芝加哥日本文化中心正在開設初學者課程，由藝術家高橋真美指導。近期場次是六月五日和六月二十日，地點在 N. Lincoln Ave. 2940 號。每人一百二十美元，包含全部材料，完成後可以把作品帶回家。

Kintsugi is an ancient Japanese technique: broken bowls and plates are mended with gold-tinted lacquer—a resin infused with gold powder—transforming the cracks into beautiful golden seams. The Chicago Japanese Cultural Center is launching a beginner workshop led by artist Mami Takahashi. Upcoming sessions are June 5th and June 20th, held at 2940 N. Lincoln Ave. The cost is $120 per person, including all materials, and participants take home their finished pieces.

一位機長的飛行地圖：用數據畫出一整個職業生涯

來源：jameshard.ing

James Harding 是英國航空的 A350 機長。他花了好幾年的時間，把自己飛過的每一趟航班都記錄下來，然後做成了一個個人網站。

網站上有會轉動的立體地球，你可以看到他的航線一條一條畫過地球。還有像 GitHub 那樣的活動色塊圖，顏色深淺代表每天飛了多少小時。這就是網路最迷人的地方：一個人因為熱愛，把平凡的工作變成了藝術。

James Harding is an A350 captain at British Airways. Over several years, he meticulously recorded every flight he took, then turned the data into a personal website. The site features an interactive 3D globe where you can watch his flight routes trace across the planet. There's also an activity heatmap reminiscent of GitHub, where color intensity represents the hours he flew each day. This is what makes the internet so captivating: one person, driven by passion, transforms ordinary work into art.
