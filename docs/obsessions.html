夢東：用合成器打開台灣的神秘世界

來源：Bandcamp Daily

台北的兩兄弟組合「夢東」花了六個月走遍全台灣，拍下佛教木乃伊、奇特的廟宇物件，還有民間信仰中最不可思議的角落。他們把這些照片做成兩本書，再為書寫了一張專輯做配樂。

這張專輯叫 Music from Taiwan Mystery，混合了慢節奏電子音樂和台灣老電玩、老電影的聲音片段。聽起來像八零年代深夜電視裡的鬼故事節目，模糊又迷人。音樂就是田野調查，用聲音記錄台灣民間信仰的另一面。

The Taipei duo Meng Dong spent six months traveling across Taiwan, photographing Buddhist mummies, peculiar temple objects, and the most remarkable corners of folk belief. They turned these photos into two books, then composed an album to accompany them. The album, titled Music from Taiwan Mystery, blends slow-tempo electronic music with sound clips from vintage Taiwanese video games and old films. It sounds like ghost story programs from late-night eighties television—hazy and mesmerizing. The music itself is fieldwork, using sound to document another side of Taiwan's folk traditions.

世界最大的木造教堂，冬天沒有暖氣

來源：Finnish Architecture Navigator

在芬蘭東部的賽馬湖（Lake Saimaa）邊，有一座黃色的巨大教堂。凱里邁基教堂（Kerimäki Church）是全世界最大的木造教堂，建於一八四四年到一八四七年，可以坐三千人，站著能容納五千人。教堂有四十五公尺長、二十七公尺高。

但這座教堂完全沒有暖氣。從一九五三年起，冬天的禮拜改到旁邊一間小教堂舉行。三月的時候，那巨大的黃色空間靜靜地被雪覆蓋，空無一人。

On the shores of Lake Saimaa in eastern Finland stands an enormous yellow wooden church. Kerimäki Church is the world's largest wooden church, built between 1844 and 1847, with seating for 3,000 people and standing room for 5,000 more. The structure stretches forty-five meters long and twenty-seven meters high. Yet the church has no heating system whatsoever. Since 1953, winter services have been held in a small adjacent chapel instead. In March, that vast yellow space sits silent and empty, blanketed in snow.

芝加哥歌舞秀週：五十場表演，每場三十美元以下

來源：Working In Concert

第五屆芝加哥歌舞秀週（Chicago Cabaret Week）將在五月八日到十七日舉行，超過五十位表演者會在夜店、鋼琴酒吧、教堂和小劇場演出。每場票價都在三十美元以下。

節目很多樣：有經典美國老歌，有百老匯致敬秀，也有把變裝秀、滑稽表演和現場音樂混在一起的跨界演出。場地都很小很親密，是體驗芝加哥夜生活的好機會。

The fifth Chicago Cabaret Week runs May 8-17, featuring over fifty performers in intimate venues—nightclubs, piano bars, churches, and small theaters. Every show costs thirty dollars or less. The lineup spans the full spectrum: classic American standards, Broadway tributes, and boundary-pushing acts that blend drag, comedy, and live music. These close-quarters settings offer a genuine taste of Chicago's nightlife scene.

七萬八千種顏料的家

來源：Artist Pigments

有人用自己的空閒時間建了一個網站，收集了七萬八千多種畫家用的顏料。這個資料庫記錄了九百多種色素（顏色的原料），來自一千三百多個品牌。

最特別的是它的調色工具：不是用電腦螢幕的顏色去混合，而是用光學物理來計算真正顏料混在一起的樣子。網站上還有三千多張手工製作的色票（顏色樣本），全部是一個人的熱情之作。

Someone built a website in their spare time that catalogues over 78,000 paint pigments used by artists. The database records more than 900 distinct pigments—the raw materials that create color—sourced from over 1,300 brands. What makes it truly special is its color-mixing tool: rather than blending colors as they appear on a computer screen, it uses optical physics to calculate how actual pigments behave when mixed together. The site also features over 3,000 handmade color swatches, all painstakingly created by a single passionate individual.
