一位警察唱出大海的聲音

來源：Discogs

一九九九年，住在台東的卑南族警察陳建年（族名 Pau-dull）錄了一張專輯叫《海洋》。他用木吉他輕輕彈奏，歌聲溫柔又深遠，好像能聽見台東的山和太平洋的海風。

這張專輯在金曲獎打敗了張學友和王力宏，拿下最佳男歌手。後來台灣樂評人投票，選它為那十年間最好的台灣專輯。一個平時巡邏（在街上走動看守）的警察，用最安靜的歌震動了整個樂壇。

In 1999, Pau-dull, a Puyuma police officer living in Taitung, released an album called "Ocean." With a gentle touch on his wooden guitar and a voice both tender and distant, he seemed to capture the mountains of Taitung and the Pacific winds. At the Golden Melody Awards, the album defeated Jacky Cheung and Wang Leehom to win Best Male Singer. Years later, Taiwanese music critics voted it the best Taiwanese album of the decade. A police officer who spent his days patrolling the streets had moved the entire music world with the quietest of songs.

芬蘭湖裡的秘密小海豹

來源：Atlas Obscura

在芬蘭東部有一個很大的湖叫塞馬湖。大約一萬年前，這個湖和波羅的海分開了，一群環斑海豹從此留在淡水裡，全世界只有這裡有。芬蘭人叫牠們 norppa。

三月正是最神奇的時刻：海豹媽媽躲在湖邊的雪洞裡，照顧只有四到五公斤重的小寶寶。旅人看不見牠們，但這個秘密世界就藏在冰雪之下。

In eastern Finland, there is a vast lake called Saimaa. About ten thousand years ago, this lake became separated from the Baltic Sea, and a group of ringed seals found themselves stranded in freshwater—a phenomenon unique to this location in the entire world. The Finnish call them norppa. March is the most magical time: seal mothers hide in snow caves along the lake's edge, caring for tiny pups weighing only four to five kilograms. Travelers cannot see them, but this secret world lies hidden beneath the ice and snow.

五十年的異鄉人之舞

來源：Heritage Museum of Asian Art

五月九日下午，日本舞踏（一種前衛舞蹈）藝術家大竹永子將在芝加哥文化中心演出。她用舞蹈、影片和說故事，回顧自己在美國當了五十年外國人的日子。

演出後還有一場對談，她和芝加哥爵士貝斯手兼太鼓大師青木達一起聊戰後日本的前衛藝術。時間是下午兩點半到四點半，免費入場。

On the afternoon of May 9th, Japanese butoh artist Nagako Otake will perform at the Chicago Cultural Center. Through dance, film, and storytelling, she reflects on five decades of life as a foreigner in America. After the performance, she'll join a discussion with Chicago jazz bassist and taiko master Tatsuo Aoki, exploring postwar Japanese avant-garde art. The event runs from 2:30 to 4:30 p.m. and is free to attend.

用符號追上說話的速度

來源：Stenophile

在打字機和電腦出現之前，人們發明了速記（用特別的符號快速寫字），讓手能跟上嘴巴說話的速度。一個叫 Sean 的人獨自建了一個網站 Stenophile，收集了超過三十種語言的速記系統。

從英文、中文到阿拉伯文，都有人設計過專用的速記法。這些古老的手冊記錄了一個幾乎消失的技術，而 Sean 把它當作活的手藝在保存。

Before typewriters and computers existed, people invented shorthand—using special symbols to write quickly—so their hands could keep pace with their spoken words. A person named Sean single-handedly built a website called Stenophile that collects shorthand systems in more than thirty languages. From English and Chinese to Arabic, people have designed specialized shorthand methods for each. These old manuals document a technique that has nearly vanished, and Sean preserves it as a living craft.
