把紐約放進吉他：瘋羊的《布魯克林》

來源：beehype

瘋羊（Manic Sheep）是二○一○年在台北成立的樂團。他們很喜歡美國紐約布魯克林的夢幻流行樂，所以把二○一六年的第二張專輯（唱片）取名叫《布魯克林》。

這張專輯的吉他聲像一大片牆，又厚又好聽。其中一首歌叫 No More Anger，彈得最過癮。後來他們受邀去美國 SXSW 和日本富士音樂祭演出，成了台灣很重要的一張作品。

Manic Sheep is a band formed in Taipei in 2010. They fell in love with the dreamy pop sound of Brooklyn, New York, so they named their second album, released in 2016, "Brooklyn." The guitar work on this album sounds like a massive wall of sound—thick and deeply satisfying. One standout track, "No More Anger," is particularly exhilarating to hear. The album later earned them invitations to perform at SXSW in the United States and Japan's Fuji Rock Festival, cementing it as one of Taiwan's most important works.

焦油之都：芬蘭奧盧的黑色財富

來源：Oulu2026（歐洲文化之都）

在一八六○年代，芬蘭北部的奧盧（Oulu）是全世界賣出最多焦油（一種黑色黏液）的城市。每年運出八萬桶，大多賣給英國海軍，用來塗在木頭船上防水。

這門生意讓許多當地人脫離貧窮。今年奧盧被選為二○二六年歐洲文化之都。城裡有一條「焦油故事」步道，用手機掃一掃就能讀到十個老地點的歷史。焦油的味道至今還做成芬蘭人愛吃的糖果呢！

In the 1860s, Oulu in northern Finland was the world's largest exporter of tar, a thick black liquid. The city shipped out eighty thousand barrels annually, most destined for the British Navy, where it was used to waterproof wooden ships. This lucrative trade lifted many local residents out of poverty. This year, Oulu was named European Capital of Culture for 2026. The city features a "Tar Story" walking route where visitors can scan QR codes with their phones to discover the history of ten historic sites. The distinctive scent of tar has even been transformed into a candy that Finnish people love to eat.

在城市裡採野菜：芝加哥街頭的一頓晚餐

來源：Eventbrite / Odd Produce

六月二十八日星期天下午，採野食的高手戴夫（Dave Odd）會帶大家在芝加哥的阿爾巴尼公園區散步三小時。

他會教你認出五十多種長在城市裡、可以吃的野草和野菇。走完以後，大家會到一家小餐館，吃一頓用剛採到的食材做的飯。只散步是四十美元，加上晚餐是四十五美元。

On Sunday, June 28th, Dave Odd, an expert forager, will lead a three-hour walk through Chicago's Albany Park neighborhood. He'll teach you to identify over fifty edible wild plants and mushrooms that grow right here in the city. After the walk, everyone heads to a small restaurant for dinner prepared entirely from that day's harvest. The walk alone costs forty dollars; walk plus dinner is forty-five dollars.

一個人搶救三十六年的世界資料

來源：worldfactbookarchive.org

美國中情局有一本《世界概況》，免費介紹每個國家的地理和經濟。今年二月，他們突然把它關掉了。

在資料消失之前，一位叫 MilkMp 的研究者自己把三十六年的版本全部存了下來，整理出超過一百萬筆資料，做成一個可以查詢的網站。你可以看出同一個國家的說法，幾十年來是怎麼一點一點改變的。

The U.S. Central Intelligence Agency published the World Factbook, a free resource introducing the geography and economics of every country. In February this year, they suddenly took it offline. Before the data disappeared, a researcher named MilkMp single-handedly preserved all 36 years of versions, organizing over a million data points into a searchable website. You can now see how descriptions of the same country have shifted subtly over decades.
