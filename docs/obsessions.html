高凌風的迪斯可時代

來源：South China Morning Post

一九八〇年，台灣電視明星高凌風出了一張專輯叫「Disco」。他把西方流行歌曲全部改成中文：ABBA的歌變成了「惱人的秋風」，New Order七分鐘的電子樂被他剪成一半，加上女聲合唱，歌詞寫的是青橄欖（一種苦苦的水果）和失敗的愛情。

這些唱片沒有留下任何製作紀錄，但「惱人的秋風」到今天還是華語流行音樂的經典。

In 1980, Taiwanese television star Gao Lingfeng released an album called "Disco." He transformed Western pop songs into Chinese versions: ABBA's hit became "The Tiresome Autumn Wind," and New Order's seven-minute electronic track was cut in half, layered with female vocals, and reimagined with lyrics about unripe olives—a bitter fruit—and failed romance. These records left behind no production documentation, yet "The Tiresome Autumn Wind" remains a classic of Mandarin pop music to this day.

赫爾辛基最老的游泳池重新開放

來源：Wallpaper*

赫爾辛基的尤爾約街游泳館建於一九二八年，是芬蘭第一座公共游泳池。經過兩年整修（修理），它在二〇二六年二月重新開放。裡面有漂亮的裝飾藝術瓷磚，二樓有咖啡座可以看到整個泳池。

這裡至今保留著一九二〇年代的傳統：男女分開使用，可以選擇不穿泳衣游泳。三月去芬蘭的旅人不該錯過。

Helsinki's Yörjö Street Swimming Hall, built in 1928, was Finland's first public swimming pool. After two years of renovation, it reopened in February 2026. The facility features beautiful Art Deco tiles, and a café on the second floor overlooks the entire pool. It preserves a tradition from the 1920s: separate hours for men and women, with the option to swim without a bathing suit. Travelers visiting Finland in March shouldn't miss it.

划船十八英里：美國第二老的獨木舟賽

來源：canoemarathon.com（via Block Club Chicago）

五月十七日，第六十九屆德斯普蘭河獨木舟馬拉松就要開始了。選手要從Libertyville划到Mount Prospect，全長十八點五英里。這是全美國第二老的獨木舟比賽，從一九五〇年代辦到現在。

報名費只要四十到五十美元，任何人都可以參加。如果覺得太長，也有五英里的短程版本。

On May 17th, the 69th Des Plaines River Canoe Marathon will begin. Paddlers will travel from Libertyville to Mount Prospect, covering a total of 18.5 miles. This is the second-oldest canoe race in the entire United States, running continuously since the 1950s. The entry fee is only $40 to $50, and anyone can participate. If the full distance seems too long, there's also a shorter 5-mile version available.

一個數學家花三十年記錄全世界的撲克牌遊戲

來源：pagat.com

一九九五年，英國數學家John McLeod建了一個網站叫pagat.com。他一個人花了三十年，收集並寫下超過五百種來自世界各地的撲克牌遊戲規則。

網站名字來自中歐紙牌遊戲「塔羅克」裡最小的王牌。紙牌歷史學家稱它是「網路上最重要的紙牌遊戲網站」。

In 1995, British mathematician John McLeod created a website called pagat.com. Over the course of thirty years, he single-handedly collected and documented the rules for more than five hundred card games from around the world. The website's name comes from "tarok," a Central European card game, referring to its lowest trump card. Card historians call it "the most important card game website on the internet."
