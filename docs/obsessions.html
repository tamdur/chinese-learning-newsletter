在漢堡王誕生的後搖傳奇：甜梅號

來源：Discogs

一九九零年代末，四個台北大學生在漢堡王裡想出了一個團名：甜梅號。二零零一年八月，他們在水晶唱片發行了首張專輯《是不是少了什麼》，一張完全沒有人聲的後搖滾（一種用層層音色代替歌詞的搖滾樂）專輯。

這張唱片時而柔和、時而尖銳，成為台灣後搖的起點，影響了一整個世代的樂團。後來甜梅號還為周美玲導演的電影《刺青》創作了原聲帶。

In the late 1990s, four Taipei university students came up with a band name at Burger King: Vast and Hazy (甜梅號). In August 2001, they released their debut album "Is Something Missing?" on Crystal Records—an entirely instrumental post-rock album (a style of rock music that uses layered textures in place of vocals). The record alternated between gentle and sharp moments, becoming the starting point for Taiwan's post-rock scene and influencing an entire generation of bands. Vast and Hazy later composed the soundtrack for director Sylvia Wei's film "Tattoo."

滑雪才能到的咖啡屋：芬蘭的森林小屋

來源：thisisFINLAND (finland.fi)

芬蘭是全世界每人喝最多咖啡的國家。他們有一種特別的地方叫 latukahvila，是藏在森林裡的咖啡小屋，只有滑雪才能到。在Levi、Yllas等滑雪地區，滑到半路就能停下來喝杯淺烘（輕烤的）咖啡，配肉桂麵包和馴鹿三明治。

最特別的是赫爾辛基的海上版：冬天波羅的海結冰時，有人會在冰面上開一間咖啡屋，漂浮在海面上，直到冰融化才關門。

Finland is the world's largest coffee consumer per capita. They have a special type of establishment called a latukahvila — a coffee cabin hidden deep in the forest, accessible only by ski. In ski regions like Levi and Yllas, you can stop halfway down the slopes for a cup of light roast coffee, paired with cinnamon bread and reindeer sandwiches. What's truly remarkable is Helsinki's seaside version: when the Baltic Sea freezes over in winter, someone opens a coffee cabin right on the ice, floating on the sea surface until the ice melts and the cabin closes for the season.

黑洞專家來芝加哥：阿德勒天文館公開演講

來源：Adler Planetarium

五月十九日（星期二）晚上六點，史丹佛大學天文學家Roger Blandford將在芝加哥阿德勒天文館演講。他最有名的研究是解釋旋轉中的黑洞如何射出強大的光束。

這次講題是「極端宇宙」，他會講宇宙加速膨脹（越來越快變大）的發現，以及科學家尋找暗能量的故事。任何人都可以參加，需要先上網買票。

On Tuesday evening, May 19th at 6 p.m., Stanford University astronomer Roger Blandford will give a lecture at the Adler Planetarium in Chicago. He is best known for his research explaining how rotating black holes emit powerful jets of radiation. This talk, titled "The Extreme Universe," will cover the discovery of cosmic acceleration—the universe expanding faster and faster—and the story of scientists' search for dark energy. The event is open to the public, and tickets must be purchased online in advance.

一個人、三十年、一百二十國的撲克牌百科

來源：The World of Playing Cards

英國人 Simon Wintle 從一九九六年開始，獨自建造了一座撲克牌的網路博物館：wopc.co.uk。到今天已經收錄了將近五千篇文章，記錄一百二十個國家的撲克牌。

這裡不只記錄誰做了哪副牌，還說明牌面上的圖案在不同時代代表什麼意思：有的是政治漫畫，有的是廣告，有的是塔羅牌的藝術。Wintle 幾乎每天都在更新。

British collector Simon Wintle has spent the past three decades building a sprawling online encyclopedia of playing cards at wopc.co.uk, starting in 1996. The site now contains nearly five thousand articles documenting playing cards from one hundred twenty countries. It goes far beyond simply cataloging who made which deck—Wintle meticulously explains what the imagery on each card meant in its era: political cartoons, advertisements, tarot artwork. He updates the site almost daily.
