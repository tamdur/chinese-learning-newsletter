《十殿》：把二二八寫成地獄故事

來源：StreetVoice 吹音樂

二〇〇九年，台灣樂團閃靈推出專輯《十殿》。故事裡，埔里的廟童潘承元闖進地獄十殿，偷走生死簿，想救回在二二八事件中被殺的朋友。

他們用台語演唱，配上快速的鼓聲和電吉他，還有一把二胡（傳統的弦樂器），像在替死者送行。這是「鎮魂」三部曲之一。主唱林昶佐後來當上立法委員，讓這張專輯更像信念，而不是裝扮。

In 2009, the Taiwanese band Chthonic released the album Mirror of Retribution (十殿, "Ten Courts of Hell"). In its story, Pan Cheng-yuan, a temple boy from Puli, breaks into the ten courts of hell and steals the Register of Life and Death, hoping to bring back friends killed in the February 28 Incident.

They sing in Taiwanese, over fast drumming and electric guitar, with an erhu — a traditional stringed instrument — running through it like a funeral procession for the dead. It is one part of the band's "Requiem" trilogy. Frontman Freddy Lim later became a member of the Legislative Yuan, which makes the album feel less like costume and more like conviction.

克瓦肯：還在長大的群島

來源：Visit Vaasa / UNESCO World Heritage Centre

芬蘭西岸瓦薩外海有一片群島，叫克瓦肯，共約五千六百座島。上一次冰河時期，三千公尺厚的冰把海底壓下去五百多公尺。

冰融化後，土地慢慢彈回來，每年上升約九公釐（不到一公分），一年多出七百公頃新陸地。這裡是世界遺產，四條步道冬天也開放，路上有小木屋可以生火取暖。

Off the coast of Vaasa, on Finland's western shore, lies an archipelago called Kvarken — roughly 5,600 islands. During the last ice age, ice three kilometres thick pressed the seabed down by more than 500 metres.

After the ice melted, the land began slowly springing back, rising about 9 millimetres a year — less than a centimetre — and adding some 700 hectares of new land annually. It is a UNESCO World Heritage site, and four of its trails stay open through the winter, with small cabins along the way where you can build a fire and warm up.

芝加哥西區的免費舞廳課

來源：TaskForce Chicago

芝加哥西區西塞羅大道九號，社區團體 TaskForce 每週一和週三下午五點到九點開免費的 Vogue 舞蹈課，一直上到十二月十七日。

Vogue 來自「舞廳文化」，人們組成「家族」，在伸展台上比腳步、手勢和下腰。不用報名，也不用學過跳舞，穿上好活動的衣服就能進場。下一堂是八月三日，星期一。

At 9 N Cicero Avenue on Chicago's West Side, the community group TaskForce runs a free Vogue dance class every Monday and Wednesday from 5 to 9 p.m., continuing through December 17th.

Vogue comes out of ballroom culture, where people form "houses" and compete on the runway with footwork, hand performance and dips. No registration is needed and no dance experience is required — just wear something you can move in and walk in. The next session is Monday, August 3rd.

一個人寫了十七年的街頭字典

來源：Green's Dictionary of Slang

英國學者格林一個人花了十七年，整理出將近十萬條英文俚語（街頭用語），還附上四十多萬筆寫明年代的例句，最早可以追到西元一千年。

二〇一六年，他把全部資料免費放上網。網站上有時間軸，你能看見一個字怎麼從古代小偷的黑話，一路走進今天的嘻哈歌詞。本來只想查一個字，一小時後才回過神。

The British lexicographer Jonathon Green spent seventeen years, working alone, assembling nearly 100,000 entries of English slang, along with more than 400,000 dated example citations reaching as far back as the year 1000.

In 2016 he put the whole thing online for free. The site has a timeline that lets you watch a single word travel from the cant of old thieves all the way into today's hip-hop lyrics. You come to look up one word, and an hour later you finally come to.
