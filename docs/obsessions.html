沒有吉他的搖滾樂團：鐵之貝克的鋼琴革命

來源：Taipei Times

一九九九年，三個台北年輕人做了一個大膽的決定：組一個沒有吉他手的搖滾樂團。 Tizzy Bac （鐵之貝克）只有鍵盤、貝斯和鼓，所有的旋律都由鍵盤手陳惠婷負責。

他們的第三張專輯《如果看見地獄，我就不怕魔鬼》在二〇〇九年發行，用活潑的合成器音色搭配詭（奇怪的）異的鋸琴聲，再爆發成壯闊的高潮。不需要吉他，他們依然是台灣獨立音樂中最特別的聲音之一。

In 1999, three young people from Taipei made a bold decision: form a rock band without a guitarist. Tizzy Bac has only keyboards, bass, and drums, with all melodies handled by keyboardist Chen Hui-ting. Their third album, "If I See Hell, I Won't Fear the Devil," released in 2009, pairs lively synthesizer tones with eerie saw-like sounds that build into sweeping crescendos. Without a guitar, they remain one of Taiwan's most distinctive voices in independent music.

坦佩雷市場裡的黑色香腸

來源：Visit Tampere

芬蘭第二大城坦佩雷（Tampere）有一座一九〇一年開幕的室內市場，是北歐最大的市場大廳。三月來訪，這裡是躲避寒風的好去處。

來這裡一定要試試黑香腸（mustamakkara），一種用豬血和裸麥做成的深色香腸，從十七世紀就是當地人的最愛。點餐時可以說 "menopaluu"，意思是「去又回」，指的是 U 形的整條香腸。搭配越橘（一種小紅莓）果醬一起吃，是這座城市最道地的味道。

Finland's second-largest city, Tampere, is home to an indoor market that opened in 1901 and remains Northern Europe's largest market hall. A visit in March makes it an ideal refuge from the biting cold. The must-try here is mustamakkara—a dark sausage made from pork blood and rye that has been a local favorite since the seventeenth century. When ordering, you can ask for "menopaluu," which means "goes and comes back," referring to the U-shaped whole sausage. Served with lingonberry jam, it's the most authentic taste the city has to offer.

母親節的湖邊探險：北島公園的秘密活動

來源：Chicago Park District

五月九日星期六上午十點到下午一點，芝加哥的北島公園（Northerly Island）將舉辦免費的母親節探險活動。

活動內容包括近距離觀察爬蟲類、射箭、釣魚、手工藝和自然導覽。公園就在阿德勒天文館旁邊，可以看到美麗的湖景。射箭和釣魚只開放八歲以上的小朋友參加，其他項目不限年齡。

On Saturday, May 9th from 10 a.m. to 1 p.m., Northerly Island in Chicago will host a free Mother's Day exploration activity. The program includes up-close observation of reptiles, archery, fishing, crafts, and nature walks. The park sits next to the Adler Planetarium with beautiful lake views. Archery and fishing are limited to children eight and older, while other activities are open to all ages.

消失的聲音：一個人收藏全世界的老唱片

來源：Excavated Shellac

從二〇〇七年開始，洛杉磯的 Jonathan Ward 獨自經營一個叫 Excavated Shellac 的網站，專門分享來自八十九個國家的老蟲膠（一種早期唱片材料）唱片。

每一篇文章介紹一首很少人聽過的歌曲，時間橫跨一九〇七到一九六七年，都是他親手從自己的收藏中轉錄的。這些是全球音樂工業把一切變得一樣之前，世界各地真正的聲音。

Since 2007, Jonathan Ward in Los Angeles has operated a website called Excavated Shellac on his own, dedicated to sharing old shellac records—an early record material—from eighty-nine countries. Each article introduces a rarely heard song spanning the period from 1907 to 1967, all personally transcribed by him from his own collection. These are the authentic voices of the world from before the global music industry made everything uniform.
