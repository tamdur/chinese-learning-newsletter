繞過地球才被記住的歌聲

來源：Discogs

一九七八年，台東馬蘭部落的阿美族農夫郭英男（阿美族名叫 Difang ），和太太一起唱了古老的「飲酒歌」，錄音被送到法國保存。

一九九四年，德國樂團 Enigma 把這段歌聲放進新歌，還成了一九九六年奧運的主題曲，卻沒人知道是誰在唱。打完官司後，這對七十多歲的夫妻自己出了專輯《生命之環》，在日本和台灣都拿下第一名。

In 1978, Guo Yingnan, an Amis farmer from Malan village in Taitung (known by his Amis name as Difang), and his wife recorded an ancient "drinking song." The recording was sent to France for safekeeping.

In 1994, the German band Enigma sampled this recording in a new song that became the 1996 Olympics theme—yet no one knew who the original singers were. After winning a legal battle, the couple, then in their seventies, released their own album titled "Circle of Life," which topped the charts in both Japan and Taiwan.

讓薩米人自己說故事的博物館

來源：Museums Association / Siida

在芬蘭北方的伊納里（Inari），有一座叫 Siida 的薩米人博物館。薩米人是住在北極圈的原住民。二〇二四年，它得到「歐洲年度博物館」大獎。

這是第一次，這個獎頒給由原住民自己設計的博物館。三百多位薩米人一起幫忙布置，展出他們養馴鹿、唱傳統歌謠的生活，也說出以前被迫改變文化的傷心過去。

In Inari in northern Finland stands a Sámi museum called Siida. The Sámi are Indigenous people who live in the Arctic Circle. In 2024, it received the "European Museum of the Year" award.

This was the first time the award was given to a museum designed by Indigenous people themselves. More than three hundred Sámi people worked together to create the exhibition, which showcases their lives herding reindeer and singing traditional songs, while also sharing the painful history of being forced to abandon their culture.

在圖書館認識能吃的野菇

來源：Illinois Mycological Association

七月二十九日，伊利諾真菌學會的莉茲（Liz），會在道納斯格羅夫（Downers Grove）的公共圖書館，免費教大家怎麼認出森林裡能吃的野菇。

學會帶隊採菇的活動只給會員參加，但這種演講開放給每一個人。她會分享怎麼分辨野菇、提醒哪些長得像卻有毒，還有好吃的煮法。

On July 29th, Liz from the Illinois Mycological Society will offer a free workshop at the Downers Grove Public Library on identifying edible wild mushrooms found in the forest.

While the society's guided foraging trips are members-only, this lecture is open to everyone. She'll explain how to distinguish different mushroom species, point out dangerous look-alikes, and share delicious preparation methods.

二十一棵知識樹上的每日猜謎

來源：GuessKin

GuessKin 是一個免費的每日猜謎遊戲，像 Wordle 一樣。你每猜錯一次，它就告訴你，你猜的東西和答案在分類樹上最近的共同祖先在哪裡。

特別的是，它有二十一種主題，每一種都用真正的學術分類法：植物用 APG IV 系統，鳥類跟著世界鳥類名錄，礦物用一九四一年的分類法。沒有廣告，只是一個人用心做的小專案。

GuessKin is a free daily guessing game, much like Wordle. Each time you guess wrong, it shows you where your guess and the answer meet on the classification tree—their closest common ancestor.

What makes it special is its twenty-one categories, each using genuine academic classification systems: plants follow APG IV, birds follow the World Bird List, minerals follow the 1941 system. No ads, just a passion project by one person.
