王若琳的太空冒險：一張用自由換來的專輯

來源：YAM Magazine

二〇一三年，台灣歌手王若琳做了一件大膽的事。她的第一張專輯在亞洲賣了超過二十萬張，但她和 Sony 談了一個交易：先錄幾張普通的翻唱（唱別人的歌）專輯，換一張完全由自己決定的作品。

結果就是《Galaxy Crisis》——十六首歌，三十一分鐘，用電子遊戲音樂和六〇年代的唱法，打造了一個太空小宇宙。歌名像「火星花園派對」和「太空酒館」，在華語流行音樂裡獨一無二。

In 2013, Taiwanese singer Joanna Wang made a bold move. Her debut album had sold over 200,000 copies across Asia, but she negotiated a deal with Sony: first record a few standard cover albums, then earn the right to create a work entirely of her own design. The result was Galaxy Crisis—sixteen songs, thirty-one minutes, built from video game soundscapes and 1960s vocal aesthetics into a miniature cosmos of sound. Track titles like "Mars Garden Party" and "Space Tavern" were utterly singular in Mandarin pop music.

冰下的透明湖：芬蘭七百年的冬季捕魚

來源：Elävän perinnön wikiluettelo

芬蘭東部的普魯維西湖是全國最清的大湖，光線可以穿過二十公尺深的水。從西元一三〇〇年起，漁民就在冬天的冰面下拉大網，捕一種叫「穆伊庫」的小銀魚。因為湖水太清了，魚群會跟著網走而不會穿過去。

每個早上，漁民在港口交換冰的厚度、天氣和魚的位置等消息，這些知識一代傳一代，如今已被列入芬蘭國家無形文化遺產（看不見摸不到但很珍貴的傳統）。

Lake Puruvesi in eastern Finland is the country's clearest major lake, with light penetrating twenty meters below the surface. Since the year 1300, fishermen have cast large nets beneath the winter ice to catch a small silvery fish called muikku. Because the water is so transparent, fish schools follow the net rather than pass through it. Every morning, fishermen at the harbor exchange knowledge about ice thickness, weather conditions, and fish locations—wisdom passed down through generations and now recognized as part of Finland's National Intangible Cultural Heritage (traditions that are invisible and intangible, yet profoundly precious).

走進芝加哥最老的消防局，看一場夢一樣的戲

來源：Choose Chicago

芝加哥劇團 Chicago Shpatz 正在一棟一八七三年建造的老消防局裡演出沉浸式（觀眾走進故事裡的）戲劇《Fire House》。這棟在羅斯福路上的建築是原來的第十八號消防站。

觀眾小組一間一間地走，故事從消防員的日常慢慢變得像做夢一樣奇怪。每個人看到的都不一樣。演出日期：五月十五、十六、十七、二十一、二十四、二十五、二十八日，晚上八點。

Chicago Shpatz theater company is performing an immersive drama called "Fire House" inside a historic firehouse built in 1873. The building, located on Roosevelt Road, was originally the city's 18th fire station. Small groups of audience members move from room to room as the story gradually transforms from the mundane routines of firefighters into something dreamlike and surreal. Each person experiences something different. Performances: May 15, 16, 17, 21, 24, 25, and 28 at 8 p.m.

九千張時刻表：一個人建的交通歷史博物館

來源：Timetable World

二〇〇八年金融危機的時候，一位英國銀行分析師失業了。他開始掃描（把紙變成電腦圖片）舊的火車、公車和飛機時刻表，當作學寫程式的練習。

十五年後，這個網站 Timetable World 已經收集了來自九十五個國家的九千多張時刻表，全靠志工維持。每一張舊時刻表都記錄了一個社會怎麼安排人們的移動。

When the 2008 financial crisis hit, a British bank analyst lost his job. He started scanning old train, bus, and airplane timetables as a way to teach himself programming. Fifteen years later, his website, Timetable World, has collected over 9,000 timetables from 95 countries, all maintained by volunteers. Each old timetable is a snapshot of how a society organized the movement of its people.
