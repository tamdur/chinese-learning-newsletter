小毛驢變成橄欖樹：齊豫的第一張唱片

來源：放言Fount Media

一九七八年，音樂家李泰祥請作家三毛連夜寫下九首歌詞。其中一首本來叫〈小毛驢〉，寫的是三毛在西班牙鄉下看見的驢子（一種像馬的動物），那一帶到處都是橄欖樹。

李泰祥把它改名、重寫成〈橄欖樹〉，民歌手楊祖珺又改了開頭，把小毛驢和西班牙女孩的大眼睛，換成「天空飛的小鳥」和「山間的小溪」。一九七九年七月九日，齊豫在滾石唱片唱下這首歌，成了她第一張專輯的名字。

它是台灣校園民歌的起點，也證明了一件事：認真寫的音樂，一樣可以人人愛唱。

In 1978 the composer Li Tai-hsiang asked the writer Sanmao to write nine sets of song lyrics overnight. One was originally called "Little Donkey" — about donkeys Sanmao had seen in the Spanish countryside, in a region covered with olive groves.

Li renamed and rewrote it as "Olive Tree," and the folk singer Yang Tsu-chun later revised the opening, replacing the little donkey and the Spanish girl's big eyes with "birds flying in the sky" and "streams among the mountains." On 9 July 1979, Chyi Yu recorded the song for Rock Records, and it became the title of her debut album.

It marks the beginning of Taiwan's campus folk movement, and it proved something: music written seriously can still be music everyone wants to sing.

走進自己剛剛敲破的海

來源：The Icebreaking Company

芬蘭北邊有個小港叫凱米。每年冬天，桑波號從這裡出發，它是全世界唯一開放給遊客搭乘的破冰船。四個小時裡，船身壓過一公尺厚的海冰，發出低沉的聲音，把白色的冰面壓成一塊一塊。

航程走到一半，船會停下來。旅客換上橘色的救生衣（會浮起來的厚衣服），從船邊爬下去，躺進剛剛被船敲開的海裡。衣服讓人沉不下去，你就在碎冰之間慢慢漂著。

破冰季從聖誕節前一直到三月底。很多人說三月最好：海冰還很厚很穩，白天卻已經變長了，看得清楚船怎麼把冰一路推開。

In northern Finland there is a small port called Kemi. Every winter the Sampo sets out from here — the only icebreaker in the world open to tourists. Over four hours the hull grinds through sea ice a metre thick, groaning as it presses the white surface into slabs.

Halfway through the voyage the ship stops. Passengers put on orange survival suits — thick garments that float — climb down the side, and lie back in the sea the ship has just broken open. The suit makes it impossible to sink, and you drift slowly among the fragments of ice.

The icebreaking season runs from just before Christmas to the end of March. Many say March is best: the sea ice is still thick and steady, but the days have already lengthened, so you can clearly see the ship pushing the ice aside.

小盆裡的大樹：第四十八屆中西部盆栽展

來源：Midwest Bonsai Society

八月十四日到十六日，第四十八屆中西部盆栽展在芝加哥植物園登場。這是全美最大的地區性盆栽（把樹種在小盆裡，修剪成大樹的樣子）展覽之一。名家 Boon Manakitivipart 會一棵一棵評分，並講解好在哪裡。

星期六下午一點到四點，Alsdorf 講堂有免費的現場修剪示範。名家 Jonas Dupuich 親手做好的一棵樹，當天抽獎送出。

院子裡還有大約三十家攤位，賣樹、花盆、工具和泥土。

From 14 to 16 August, the 48th Mid-America Bonsai Exhibition opens at the Chicago Botanic Garden. It is one of the largest regional bonsai shows in the country — bonsai being the art of growing a tree in a small pot and pruning it into the shape of a full-grown one. The master Boon Manakitivipart will judge each tree in turn and explain what makes it good.

On Saturday from 1 to 4 pm there are free live styling demonstrations in Alsdorf Auditorium. A tree shaped by the master Jonas Dupuich will be raffled off that day.

Around thirty vendors fill the courtyards, selling trees, pots, tools and soil.

一年三百六十五天，每天一首怪歌

來源：WFMU Beware of the Blog

WFMU 電台一九五八年在美國新澤西州的厄普薩拉學院開播，本來是學生電台。一九九五年學院倒閉，主持人們沒有散去，改由聽眾捐錢養活這個電台，繼續放別人不敢放的音樂。

在電台的部落格「Beware of the Blog」上，他們做了一件傻事：二〇〇七年那一年，每天挖出一段被遺忘的錄音貼上網——搞笑的小唱片、路邊錄下的聲音、消失的廣播歌。每一段都附上主持人親手寫的說明。

這就是網路最好的樣子：有人只因為喜歡，就默默做了一整年。快二十年過去，這些聲音還安靜地留在 blog.wfmu.org，等你一天一天翻。

WFMU went on the air in 1958 at Upsala College in New Jersey, originally a student station. When the college closed in 1995 the DJs did not scatter; listeners' donations kept the station alive, and it went on playing the music nobody else dared to play.

On the station's blog, "Beware of the Blog," they did something gloriously foolish: through all of 2007 they dug up one forgotten recording every day and posted it — comic novelty records, sounds captured at the roadside, vanished radio jingles. Each came with notes the DJs wrote themselves.

This is the internet at its best: someone, purely out of love, quietly kept it up for a whole year. Nearly twenty years on, those sounds are still sitting there at blog.wfmu.org, waiting for you to work through them one day at a time.
