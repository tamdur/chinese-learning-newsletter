落日飛車的《金桔希子》：台北亞熱帶靈魂的歸來

來源：Bandcamp Daily

二〇一六年三月一日，落日飛車發行了三首歌的小專輯《金桔希子》。原本的三人組解散後，主唱曾國宏重組樂團，丟掉車庫搖滾的吉他噪音，改以電鋼琴、哇哇放克吉他與柔軟的合成器重新出發。

專輯名來自靈魂歌手瓊．盧西安把巴西名曲〈Dindi〉唱成了「Jinji」；樂團把這個幻聽的音節寫成「金桔希子」。這張小專輯後來在全球意外爆紅，也定義了台北的「亞熱帶靈魂」聲音。

On March 1, 2016, Sunset Rollercoaster released Jinji Kikko, a three-song EP. After the original trio disbanded, lead singer Tseng Kuo-hung reassembled the band, ditching the garage-rock guitar noise in favor of electric piano, wah-wah funk guitar, and soft synthesizers for a fresh start.

The EP's title comes from soul singer Jon Lucien, who sang the Brazilian standard "Dindi" as "Jinji"; the band wrote out that misheard syllable as "Jinji Kikko." The EP would go on to become an unexpected global hit, and it came to define Taipei's "subtropical soul" sound.

在世界最北的中世紀城堡裡，吃一頓謀殺懸疑晚餐

來源：Visit Savonlinna

奧拉維城堡建於一四七五年，由艾瑞克．托特下令興建，立在薩伊馬湖中一座小岩島上。它是舊瑞典王國保存最完整的中世紀石造堡壘，也是全世界位置最北的一座。

三月底，湖面仍結著厚冰，城堡推出限成人的「黑暗導覽」，講述最陰森的歷史；也開放平日禁入的窄道密室行程。最特別的是在拱頂的王廳裡舉辦的謀殺懸疑晚餐，客人一邊用餐，一邊在石牆之間追查案情。

Olavinlinna Castle was built in 1475 on the orders of Erik Axelsson Tott, perched on a small rocky islet in Lake Saimaa. It is the best-preserved medieval stone fortress of the old Kingdom of Sweden, and the northernmost castle in the world.

At the end of March, with the lake still locked under thick ice, the castle is rolling out an adults-only "Dark Tour" recounting its grimmest history, along with access to narrow passageways and hidden chambers that are normally off-limits. The highlight is a murder mystery dinner held in the vaulted King's Hall, where guests work their way through a meal while hunting down clues among the stone walls.

一磚一瓦讀橋港：四月十八日的徒步導覽

來源：Chicago Humanities Festival

四月十八日星期六上午十一點到中午，芝加哥人文節的「橋港日」將以一場徒步導覽開場，由知名社群帳號「Brick of Chicago」的團隊帶路。他們專門記錄芝加哥的磚石工藝和被遺忘的建築小細節。

導覽從拉莫瓦劇院出發，走一小時，把橋港這個南區老工人社區，讀成一部移民勞動與工業的歷史。當天還有巴士行程、劇場講座和共榮藝文中心的節目。

On Saturday, April 18, from 11 a.m. to noon, the Chicago Humanities Festival's "Bridgeport Day" kicks off with a walking tour led by the team behind the well-known social media account "Brick of Chicago," which specializes in documenting the city's masonry craftsmanship and the forgotten architectural details hiding in plain sight.

Starting from the Ramova Theatre, the hour-long walk reads Bridgeport — this old working-class neighborhood on the South Side — as a history of immigrant labor and industry. The day also features a bus tour, a theater talk, and programming at Co-Prosperity.

過時媒介博物館：一個人，幾百種死去的格式

來源：obsoletemedia.org

英國人賈森．柯蒂斯一個人做了一座「過時媒介博物館」，網址是 obsoletemedia.org。他把人類用過的幾百種聲音、影像、膠卷和資料儲存格式一一整理出來。

從最古老的蠟筒（蠟做的錄音滾筒）、鋼絲錄音，到雷射影碟、迷你光碟，以及一九八〇年代早已被遺忘的家用電腦磁帶。每種格式都有照片、規格、商業壽命的年份，以及它為何死去的故事。這座網站讀起來，像一部二十世紀媒體內臟的自然史。

A British man named Jason Curtis has single-handedly built the "Museum of Obsolete Media" at obsoletemedia.org. He has painstakingly catalogued several hundred of the audio, video, film, and data storage formats humanity has used over the years.

The collection runs from the oldest wax cylinders (recording drums made of wax) and wire recordings, through laserdiscs and MiniDiscs, all the way to the home computer cassette tapes of the early 1980s that the world long ago forgot. Every format comes with photos, specifications, the years of its commercial lifespan, and the story of how it died. Reading through the site feels like a natural history of the twentieth century's media entrails.
