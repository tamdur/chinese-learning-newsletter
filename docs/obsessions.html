《台灣之聲》：一封用聲音寄回家的信

來源：Pure Person Press (Bandcamp)

二零二五年十一月，一張叫《台灣之聲》的專輯發行了。故事的開頭，是住在美國的音樂人林安琪太想家，寫信問台灣電子音樂前輩林強：你有沒有家鄉的錄音？

林強寄回他錄下的檔案：家人喝咖啡時吵架、媽祖進香的念經聲、老糖廠裡的椅子聲、竹林裡小孩的叫喊。

林安琪再請來許多音樂人，把這些真實的聲音改成新歌。整張專輯，就是用台灣本來的聲音，拼成的一封長長的家書。

In November 2025, an album called "Voices of Taiwan" was released. The story begins with Lin An-qi, a musician living in the United States, missing home so deeply that she wrote to Lin Qiang, a pioneering figure in Taiwanese electronic music, with a simple question: Do you have any recordings from home? Lin Qiang sent back his recordings: family members arguing over coffee, chanting from a Mazu temple pilgrimage, the sound of chairs creaking in an old sugar factory, children calling out in a bamboo grove. Lin An-qi then invited numerous musicians to transform these authentic sounds into new songs. The entire album becomes one long letter home—pieced together from the authentic voices of Taiwan itself.

開往北極的旅館房間

來源：VR (Finnish Railways)

芬蘭國鐵 VR 在二零二六年要換上全新的臥鋪車廂（火車上的睡覺房間），和設計公司 Kuudes 一起做。最大的改變是：每一間都有自己的廁所，高級的還附上淋浴。

顏色變得柔和，家具也能移動，住起來像旅館，不像火車。赫爾辛基到羅瓦涅米這條線要開十二個小時：晚上在城市睡著，早上醒來，窗外就是北方的雪地。

Finnish Railways VR will be installing brand new sleeper car compartments in 2026, developed together with design company Kuudes. The biggest change is that every room has its own bathroom, with the premium units also featuring showers. The colors are softer, the furniture is movable, and it all feels more like a hotel room than a train. The Helsinki-to-Rovaniemi route takes twelve hours—you fall asleep in the city at night and wake in the morning to snowy northern landscapes right outside your window.

捏一朵菊花：和菓子裡的秋天

來源：Heritage Museum of Asian Art

九月十二日星期六，芝加哥的亞洲藝術博物館有一堂和菓子（日本傳統點心）課。老師是點心師傅 Toshiko Sugii Steffes ，教大家用練切（一種豆沙做成的軟麵團）捏出兩種菊花。

一種叫「著綿」，花上蓋著棉花，是在祝人長壽；另一種叫「光琳菊」，樣子來自江戶時代的畫家。

日子挑得剛好：九月九日是日本的重陽菊花節，已經過了一千多年。材料都準備好，沒學過的人也能參加。

On Saturday, September 12th, the Asian Art Museum in Chicago is hosting a wagashi class—that's traditional Japanese sweets. The instructor is confectioner Toshiko Sugii Steffes, who will teach everyone how to shape two types of chrysanthemums using nerikiri, a soft dough made from sweet bean paste. One variety is called "chakuwata," adorned with cotton atop the flower, symbolizing wishes for longevity. The other is the "Korin chrysanthemum," inspired by an Edo-period painter. The timing couldn't be better: September 9th marks Japan's Chongyang chrysanthemum festival, a tradition spanning over a thousand years. All materials will be provided, so even newcomers can join in.

讓電腦學會投票的一頁動畫

來源：thesecretlivesofdata.com

二零一四年左右，程式設計師 Ben Johnson 做了一個網站，叫「資料的秘密生活」。它只有一頁，卻把很難的 Raft 演算法（一群電腦互相商量、得到同一個答案的辦法）講清楚了。

你只要往下捲動，畫面上的小圓圈就開始「投票」選出領袖、互相抄寫紀錄，連網路斷掉後怎麼恢復都演給你看。

沒有廣告，也沒有公司，只是一個工程師的業餘作品。後來很多程式設計師第一次看懂這個題目，都是靠它；還有粉絲自己把它翻成中文。

Around 2014, programmer Ben Johnson created a website called "The Secret Life of Data." It's just one page, but it clearly explains the notoriously difficult Raft algorithm—a method for getting a group of computers to consult with each other and reach the same answer. Scroll down, and little circles on the screen begin to "vote" for a leader, copy each other's records, and even demonstrate how they recover when the network goes down. No ads, no corporate backing—just one engineer's weekend project. Later, many programmers learned about this topic for the first time through it, and fans even translated it into Chinese on their own.
