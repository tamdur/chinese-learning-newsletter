金韻獎：一家唱片公司打造的民歌時代

來源：中國時報、臺北廣播電臺

1977年，新格唱片辦了一個大學生唱歌比賽，叫做「金韻獎」，一直辦到1984年。每年得獎的歌手，都被收進一張有編號的紀念專輯。歌迷就像收集卡片一樣，一張一張買回家。

第一屆冠軍是陳明韶，她打敗了大約一千個對手。同一批歌手裡，包美聖唱的〈捉泥鰍〉（泥鰍是一種小魚）和〈看花〉，成了校園民歌的經典。後來，齊豫也是從這裡被大家認識的。

今天回頭看，決定一整代人聽什麼聲音的，不只是寫歌的人，也是一家唱片公司。

In 1977, Sinn Ger Records ran a singing contest for college students called the Golden Rhyme Award (金韻獽). It went on until 1984. Each year's winners were collected on a numbered commemorative album, and fans bought them one by one, the way people collect trading cards. The first champion was Chen Mingshao, who beat about a thousand rivals. Among the singers from that same batch, Bao Meisheng's "Catching Loaches" (a loach is a small fish) and "Looking at Flowers" became classics of campus folk music. Qi Yu also first became known to the public through the contest. Looking back today, what decided which sounds an entire generation listened to was not only the songwriters, but also a record company.

黃色渡輪串起的群島環線

來源：Saariston Rengastie / Visit Parainen

在芬蘭西南方的圖爾庫（Turku）外海，有一條大約250公里的「群島環線」。它用幾座橋和八、九艘免費的汽車渡輪，把十幾個有人住的小島連在一起。

渡輪都漆成亮黃色，遠遠就看得見。免費的船不能預約，開車到岸邊排隊就好，有時旁邊只有幾輛車和一兩台腳踏車。

三月去要先算好時間：冬天班次比較少，而且有一段航線只在夏天開。不過，一個小島接一個小島地過海，平常的開車日子就變成了一場小冒險。

Off the coast of Turku in southwestern Finland, there is an archipelago loop roughly 250 kilometers long. A few bridges and eight or nine free car ferries link more than a dozen inhabited small islands together. The ferries are painted bright yellow, so they are easy to spot from far away. Free ferries cannot be booked in advance. You simply drive to the shore and join the queue, which sometimes contains only a few cars and perhaps a bicycle or two. If you go in March, plan your timing carefully: winter schedules run less often, and one route operates only in summer. Still, crossing the water from one island to the next turns an ordinary drive into a small adventure.

在圖書館門口看星星

來源：Time Out Chicago

11月16日星期一晚上6點到8點，阿德勒天文館（Adler Planetarium）的天文學家會帶著望遠鏡，來到芝加哥公共圖書館的 Bucktown-Wicker Park 分館，免費開講。

這是今年秋天「一書一芝加哥」活動的一部分。今年全市一起讀的書是《軌道》（Orbital），寫的是一群住在太空站上的人。這個秋天，望遠鏡已經去過好幾間分館。

不用買票，也不用去博物館。只要帶著好奇心，就能在家附近的人行道上看見宇宙。

On Monday, November 16, from 6 to 8 p.m., astronomers from the Adler Planetarium will bring their telescopes to the Bucktown-Wicker Park branch of the Chicago Public Library for a free public program. It is part of this fall's One Book, One Chicago program. This year's citywide shared read is Orbital, a novel about a group of people living aboard a space station. This fall, the telescopes have already visited several branches. No tickets are needed, and no trip to a museum. All it takes is curiosity, and you can see the universe from the sidewalk near your home.

從A到Z的郵票博物館

來源：alphabetilately.org

有一個網站叫 Alphabetilately，用26個英文字母，帶你走進集郵（收集郵票）的世界。A是集郵本，Z是齊柏林飛船（Zeppelin）送的信。每個字母都配上真的郵票和一小段文章。

這個作品由 Bill Senkus 設計，2008年到2015年在美國國家郵政博物館展出了七年。

展覽結束了，網站卻留了下來，現在由一群志工照顧。它像一本用心做的小百科，讓你發現：原來集郵比想像中有趣得多。

There is a website called Alphabetilately that uses the 26 letters of the English alphabet to take you into the world of philately, or stamp collecting. A stands for stamp album, and Z stands for a letter carried on a Zeppelin. Each letter is paired with real stamps and a short essay. The project was designed by Bill Senkus, and from 2008 to 2015 it was shown for seven years at the U.S. National Postal Museum. The exhibition has ended, but the website has stayed online, and a group of volunteers now looks after it. It works like a carefully made little encyclopedia, and it shows you that stamp collecting is far more interesting than you might expect.
