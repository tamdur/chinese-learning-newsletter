三天錄製的夢境：雷光夏的第一張專輯

來源：FOND/SOUND

一九九五年六月，台北歌手雷光夏只花了三天，就錄完她的第一張專輯《我是雷光夏》。這些歌是她十幾歲時寫的民謠，但重新加入了大提琴、手風琴和合成器的聲音。

其中一首五段的口白（說話而不是唱歌）作品《時間》，像一場半夢半醒的旅行。她的聲音安靜而迷人，在台灣音樂史上獨一無二。

In June 1995, Taipei singer Lei Guangxia recorded her debut album "I Am Lei Guangxia" in just three days. The songs were folk compositions she had written in her teens, but she reimagined them with the addition of cello, accordion, and synthesizer sounds. One of the pieces, a five-part spoken-word work titled "Time" (spoken rather than sung), feels like a journey caught between dream and wakefulness. Her voice is quiet and mesmerizing, utterly unique in Taiwan's music history.

一把刀的秘密：芬蘭刀匠的鋼鐵配方

來源：Finland, Naturally

在芬蘭小鎮Harmoinen，刀匠Heimo Roselli花了幾十年研究一種特別的鋼。他的超高碳鋼含碳量接近百分之二，硬度極高，刀刃（刀最尖的邊）可以很久不變鈍。

芬蘭傳統腰刀叫 puukko，已有幾百年歷史。Roselli的配方只有他自己知道，從不公開。如果三月去芬蘭旅行，一把Roselli刀是很好的紀念品。

In the small Finnish town of Harmoinen, knife maker Heimo Roselli has spent decades researching a special type of steel. His ultra-high carbon steel contains carbon content close to two percent, giving it exceptional hardness so the blade edge stays sharp for a very long time. The traditional Finnish hunting knife, called a puukko, has a history spanning several centuries. Roselli's formula is known only to him and has never been made public. If you travel to Finland in March, a Roselli knife makes an excellent souvenir.

五月十八日：在芝加哥學打鐵鍛刀

來源：CourseHorse / Fire Arts Center of Chicago

五月十八日星期一下午一點到三點，芝加哥西北邊的 Fire Arts Center 提供一堂兩小時的鍛刀入門課。學生會在真正的鍛爐（加熱金屬的火爐）旁，用鐵錘把一塊鋼打成刀的形狀。

不需要任何經驗，完全從零開始。火花、鐵錘聲、還有自己做的刀帶回家。

Monday, May 18th, from 1 to 3 p.m., the Fire Arts Center on Chicago's northwest side is offering a two-hour introduction to blacksmithing. Students will work beside a real forge—the furnace that heats metal—using an iron hammer to shape a block of steel into a knife. No experience necessary; you start from scratch. Sparks flying, hammer ringing, and you take home a knife you made yourself.

十五萬張卡片裡的民謠世界

來源：ibiblio.org / Folk Music Index

一九七零年代末，美國西北部的民謠老師 Jane Keefer 開始把歌曲來源寫在小卡片上，方便學生找歌。到了一九八五年，她的卡片已經裝滿十二個抽屜。

現在這些資料全部在網上，收錄超過四千三百張唱片和四百一十本書，共十五萬筆資料。你可以查一首民謠出現在哪些唱片裡——只有真正的民謠人才會做這種事。

In the late 1970s, Jane Keefer, a folk music teacher in the Pacific Northwest, began writing down song sources on small index cards to help her students find music. By 1985, her collection had filled twelve drawers. Today, all of that material is online, cataloging over 4,300 records and 410 books, comprising 150,000 entries in total. You can search for a folk song and see which records it appears on—only a true folk music devotee would do something like this.
