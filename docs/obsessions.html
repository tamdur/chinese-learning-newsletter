台北地下的迷信音樂

來源：Bandcamp Daily

夢東是一對台北兄弟組成的樂團。二〇一七年，他們回到台北，開始把台灣民間信仰和老電視恐怖片的聲音，混入電子合成器和鼓機裡。

二〇二〇年的專輯《秘神》由東京廠牌 Guruguru Brain 發行，他們稱自己的音樂為「迷信音樂」，把民俗當作聲音的材料。他們還親手做了攝影書，拍下台灣各地的佛教和民間廟宇。

Meng Dong is a band formed by two brothers in Taipei. In 2017, they returned to Taipei and began blending sounds from Taiwanese folk religious practices and vintage television horror films into electronic synthesizers and drum machines. Their 2020 album "Secret Gods" was released by Tokyo label Guruguru Brain, and they describe their music as "superstition music," treating folk traditions as sonic material. They also personally created a photography book documenting Buddhist temples and folk shrines across Taiwan.

芬蘭史詩的故鄉：庫赫莫小鎮

來源：Visit Kuhmo

芬蘭東部邊境有一個小鎮叫庫赫莫，那裡是芬蘭國家史詩（很長的古老故事詩）《卡勒瓦拉》的故鄉。鎮上的文化中心屋頂種著野莓，遊客可以學唱古老的吟唱歌曲。

三月時湖面結冰，湖邊的旅館外形像一隻巨大的神話之鳥，在那裡可以看北極光。畫家加倫—卡萊拉當年還特地選這裡當蜜月地點呢！

In Finland's eastern borderlands sits a small town called Kuhmo, the birthplace of the Kalevala — Finland's national epic, an ancient and sprawling verse narrative. The town's cultural center features a roof planted with wild berries, and visitors can learn to sing the old traditional chants. In March, the lake freezes over, and beside the lake stands a hotel shaped like a giant mythical bird, where you can watch the northern lights. The painter Gallen-Kallela even chose this very place for his honeymoon.

芝加哥最老魔術社開門教你變牌

來源：S.A.M. Assembly 3 of Chicago

六月一日晚上六點半，芝加哥歷史最久的魔術社會對外開放一堂課。地點在北區有名的 Magic, Inc. 魔術店，門票二十美元。

魔術師 Tony Gerard 會教大家「經典強迫選牌」——就是讓觀眾覺得自己自由選了一張牌，其實那張牌早就被魔術師決定好了。這是紙牌魔術中最難的技巧之一。

On the evening of June 1st at 6:30 p.m., Chicago's oldest magic club will open its doors to the public for a lesson. The class takes place at Magic, Inc., a well-known magic shop in the North Shore, with tickets priced at twenty dollars. Magician Tony Gerard will teach everyone the "classic force" — a technique that makes the audience feel they freely chose a card, when in fact the magician had already decided which card it would be. This is one of the most difficult techniques in card magic.

一個人收藏四百張古地圖的網站

來源：Cartographic Images (myoldmaps.com)

Jim Siebold 是一位退休的美國海軍軍官，從一九六〇年代開始拍攝古老的地圖。一九九四年他把這些資料放上網路，現在已經有超過一萬一千頁。

網站收集了從西元前六千年到一八〇〇年的四百多張地圖，從巴比倫的泥板到文藝復興（歐洲文化重生時代）的航海圖都有。每一張都附上作者的故事和詳細說明。

Jim Siebold is a retired U.S. Navy officer who began photographing ancient maps in the 1960s. In 1994, he launched a website to share his collection, which has since grown to over 11,000 pages. The site houses more than 400 maps spanning from 6000 BCE to 1800 CE, ranging from Babylonian clay tablets to Renaissance-era nautical charts. Each map comes with the cartographer's story and detailed annotations.
