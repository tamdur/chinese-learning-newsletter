一首阿美族的歌，飄進了奧運會場

來源：聯合報 udn 報時光

一九八八年，阿美族長老郭英男在法國唱了〈老人飲酒歌〉。那是族人一起喝酒時對唱的歌，沒有樂器，只有一層層疊起來的人聲。

德國樂團 Enigma 把這段錄音取樣（拿別人的錄音來用），做成自己的新歌，一九九六年還成了亞特蘭大奧運的主題曲，卻沒說出歌者的名字。郭英男夫婦一九九七年在美國打官司，對方最後正式署名並賠償。他們用這筆錢，替阿美族的孩子設了獎學金。

In 1988, Amis elder Difang Duana (Kuo Ying-nan) sang the “Elders' Drinking Song” in France. It is a call-and-response song the community sings while drinking together — no instruments, just layer upon layer of human voices. The German group Enigma sampled the recording, built their own track from it, and in 1996 that track became the theme of the Atlanta Olympics — without ever naming the singer. In 1997 Difang and his wife Igay took the case to court in the United States; the other side eventually credited them formally and paid a settlement. They used the money to set up a scholarship for Amis children.

茶杯裡的祕密：赫爾辛基大飯店的禁酒年代

來源：Hotel Kämp — Iconic Stories

一九一九年到一九三二年，芬蘭全國禁酒。可是赫爾辛基最華麗的康普飯店從沒真的停過。服務生端出「硬茶」：茶、糖，加上烈酒，裝在茶杯裡，看起來像人人都在喝下午茶。

私人小房間裡還有加了酒的咖啡，還有靠商船從愛沙尼亞偷偷運來的白蘭地。後來走私（偷偷運貨）太嚴重，一九三二年全國投票，禁酒令就結束了。今天飯店還在營業，客人可以在同一個大廳裡點一杯調酒。

From 1919 to 1932 Finland banned alcohol nationwide. Yet Helsinki's most opulent hotel, the Kämp, never really stopped serving. Waiters brought out “hard tea”: tea and sugar with spirits added, poured into a teacup so that everyone appeared to be taking afternoon tea. In the private rooms there was also coffee laced with liquor, and brandy smuggled in from Estonia aboard merchant ships. Eventually the smuggling grew so serious that a national referendum in 1932 ended prohibition altogether. The hotel is still open today, and a guest can order a cocktail in that very same hall.

洪堡公園的蘑菇散步

來源：Chicago Mushroom Club

八月二十二日星期六早上十點，芝加哥蘑菇社在洪堡公園帶大家找菇。地點在公園的活動中心前，免費，也不用報名，走過去就行。

芝加哥的公園不准採摘（把東西摘走），所以他們只看、只拍照，認出草地和樹皮堆裡冒出來的菇。前陣子下過雨，正是菇長得最多的時候。主辦人說，這是一場氣氛輕鬆、大人小孩都歡迎的散步，不認識菌類也沒關係。

At 10am on Saturday, August 22nd, the Chicago Mushroom Club is leading a mushroom hunt in Humboldt Park. Meet in front of the park fieldhouse — it is free, no registration required, just walk up. Chicago parks do not allow foraging, so the group only looks and photographs, identifying whatever has pushed up through the grass and the bark mulch. It rained not long ago, which is exactly when mushrooms are most abundant. The organisers describe it as a relaxed walk, welcoming to adults and children alike — knowing nothing about fungi is perfectly fine.

用滑鼠捏出人的聲音

來源：dood.al (Neil Thapen)

二〇一七年，捷克科學院的數學家 Neil Thapen 做了一個網頁小玩具，叫 Pink Trombone，中文是「粉紅長號」。畫面上是一張人頭的側剖圖，看得到舌頭、喉嚨和鼻腔。

你用滑鼠拖動這些地方，電腦就馬上算出聲音：母音、子音，哼一聲、喊一聲都行。裡面沒有任何錄好的聲音檔，全靠公式模擬（用數學算出真實的樣子）聲波在喉管裡跑動。玩上幾分鐘，你會忽然明白自己說話時，嘴裡到底發生了什麼事。

In 2017, Neil Thapen, a mathematician at the Czech Academy of Sciences, built a little web toy called Pink Trombone. The screen shows a cross-section of a human head, with the tongue, throat and nasal cavity all visible. Drag those parts around with your mouse and the computer works out the sound in real time: vowels, consonants, a hum, a shout. There are no recorded audio files inside it at all — everything comes from formulas simulating how sound waves travel through the vocal tract. Play with it for a few minutes and you suddenly understand what is actually going on inside your mouth when you speak.
