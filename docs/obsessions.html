躁動綿羊的布魯克林夢

來源：A-indie

二〇一〇年在台北成立的躁動綿羊，是台灣瞪鞋（一種用厚重吉他音牆包圍聽眾的搖滾風格）最重要的樂團。二〇一六年他們發行了專輯《Brooklyn》，用紐約那個獨立音樂聖地來命名。

這張專輯的特別之處在於：吉他手寫貝斯的部分，主唱寫吉他的旋律。團員互相交換角色，做出了緊密交織的聲音。二〇二三年這張專輯再版為黑膠唱片，證明它經得起時間考驗。

Restless Sheep's Brooklyn Dream

Restless Sheep, formed in Taipei in 2010, is Taiwan's most important shoegaze band—a rock style characterized by heavy walls of guitar sound that envelope the listener. In 2016 they released the album "Brooklyn," named after New York's independent music mecca.

What makes this album special is that the guitarist wrote the bass parts while the vocalist composed the guitar melodies. Band members swapped roles with each other, creating densely interwoven sound. In 2023 the album was reissued as a vinyl record, proof that it has stood the test of time.

坦佩雷的黑血腸：芬蘭最有爭議的街頭小吃

來源：Helsinki Times

芬蘭第二大城坦佩雷有一種出名的食物叫黑血腸，用豬血、豬肉、黑麥和麵粉做成。當地人從十六世紀就開始吃了。

一九五三年，一個叫塔波拉的家族在一間二十五平方公尺的地下室，用母親的配方開始大量生產。今天在市場廣場上，攤車一字排開，人們配著越橘（一種北歐小紅莓）果醬和冰牛奶一起吃。這道小吃讓坦佩雷嘗起來和赫爾辛基完全不同。

Finland's second-largest city, Tampere, is famous for a food called black blood sausage, made from pork blood, pork meat, rye, and flour. Local people have been eating it since the sixteenth century.

In 1953, a family named Tapola began mass production in a twenty-five-square-meter basement using their mother's recipe. Today in the market square, food carts line up, and people eat it with lingonberry jam and cold milk. This street food makes Tampere taste completely different from Helsinki.

芝加哥昆蟲館：親手製作蜘蛛標本

來源：Eventbrite / The Insect Asylum

四月四日星期六下午一點到三點，位在密爾沃基大道上的 The Insect Asylum 將舉辦一場狼蛛（大型毛蜘蛛）標本製作工作坊。

老師會教你如何固定、擺放和保存一隻永續來源的狼蛛，所有工具都提供，完成品可以帶回家。票價三十一到一百五十五美元，含昆蟲館門票。

The Insect Asylum in Chicago, located on Milwaukee Avenue, will host a tarantula specimen-mounting workshop on Saturday, April 4th from 1 to 3 p.m.

An instructor will teach you how to mount, position, and preserve a sustainably-sourced tarantula. All tools are provided, and you can take your finished specimen home. Tickets range from $31 to $155 and include admission to the insect museum.

有人搶救了 CIA 的世界資料庫

來源：Show HN / Hacker News

CIA 的《世界概況》是一本收集全球各國基本資料的公開手冊，運作了三十六年。二〇二六年二月，它悄悄關閉了。

一位網友把一九九〇年到二〇二五年的全部資料重建成可搜尋的網站，涵蓋二百八十一個國家、超過一百萬筆資料。你可以看一個國家的 GDP、軍費或人口組成如何在三十六年間變化——像是一張世界政治地理的 X 光片。

Someone rescued the CIA's world database.

The CIA's World Factbook was a public handbook collecting basic data on countries worldwide, in operation for thirty-six years. In February 2026, it quietly shut down.

An internet user rebuilt all the data from 1990 to 2025 into a searchable website, covering 281 countries and over a million data points. You can see how a country's GDP, military spending, or population composition changed over thirty-six years—like an X-ray of world political geography.
