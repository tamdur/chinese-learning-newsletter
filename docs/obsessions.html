警察的蘭嶼之歌

來源：中央通訊社

陳建年是台東的卑南族歌手，也當了三十年警察。他常常自己要求去很遠的地方工作，還在蘭嶼的小派出所待了十多年。那座島只能坐船或小飛機才到得了。

2017年退休後，他把島上的寂靜（非常安靜）寫成專輯《pongso no Tao》，意思是「人之島」。歌裡有藍調的味道，也有達悟族的語言和節奏。這張專輯在2022年得到金曲獎最佳原住民語專輯。

發片演唱會上，他把蘭嶼派出所搬上舞台，連旗桿都有。

Chen Jian-nian is a Puyuma singer from Taitung who also spent thirty years as a police officer. He often asked to be posted to remote places, and he spent more than ten years at a small police substation on Orchid Island (Lanyu). The island can only be reached by boat or small plane. After retiring in 2017, he turned the island's stillness into an album, pongso no Tao, which means "island of people." The songs have a bluesy flavor, and they also draw on the language and rhythms of the Tao (Yami) people. The album won the Golden Melody Award for Best Indigenous Language Album in 2022. At its launch concert, he brought the Lanyu police substation onto the stage, flagpole and all.

會吱吱叫的起司

來源：Falstaff Nordics

在芬蘭北方的拉普蘭，有一種「麵包起司」，芬蘭話叫 leipäjuusto。它用牛奶做成，烤到表面金黃，咬下去會在牙齒間吱吱作響。

在瑞典那一邊的拉普蘭，人們叫它「咖啡起司」：把小塊起司直接丟進熱咖啡，泡軟了再吃。以前烤過的起司放得久，又方便攜帶（帶在身上），很適合雪地裡的生活。

在芬蘭，常見的吃法是把它加熱，再淋上酸酸的雲莓果醬。三月去北方，別忘了點一份。

In northern Lapland, in Finland, there is a "bread cheese" that the Finns call leipäjuusto. It is made from milk and baked until the surface turns golden, and when you bite into it, it squeaks between your teeth. On the Swedish side of Lapland, people call it "coffee cheese": small pieces are dropped straight into hot coffee and eaten once they have softened. Baked cheese keeps well and is easy to carry on your person, which made it well suited to life in the snow. In Finland, the usual way to eat it is warmed, with a drizzle of tart cloudberry jam on top. If you travel north in March, don't forget to order a serving.

副總統寫的冠軍歌

來源：Evanston History Center

在埃文斯頓的湖邊，有一棟1894年蓋的大房子，有二十五個房間和十二個壁爐。主人查爾斯·道斯當過美國副總統，也得過諾貝爾和平獎。

道斯也愛彈鋼琴，在1911年寫了一首曲子。他過世後，有人替它填上歌詞，改名〈It's All in the Game〉。1958年，這首歌登上美國流行歌排行榜第一名。

10月15日到18日下午1點、2點、3點都有導覽（有人帶你參觀），不用預約，門票10美元，12歲以下免費。

On the lakeshore in Evanston stands a large house built in 1894, with 25 rooms and 12 fireplaces. Its owner, Charles Dawes, served as U.S. vice president and won the Nobel Peace Prize. Dawes also loved playing the piano, and in 1911 he composed a piece for it. After his death, someone added lyrics and renamed it "It's All in the Game." In 1958, the song reached No. 1 on the American pop chart. Guided tours run at 1, 2, and 3 p.m. on October 15 through 18. No reservations are needed. Admission is $10, and children under 12 get in free.

從網路廢墟救回的摺紙

來源：erikdemaine.org

丹麥魔術師 Thoki Yenn（1919到2004年）也是畫家。他在1958年愛上摺紙，後來做了一個好玩的個人網站，放滿自己畫的摺紙圖解。

他過世後，網站慢慢壞掉了。麻省理工學院教授 Erik Demaine 也是有名的摺紙研究者。他從網路檔案館的舊存檔，一頁一頁把網站拼回來。

讀者也來幫忙找遺失的圖。其中有一匹2002年為馬年做的搖搖馬；還有一個玩具沒留下圖，只好看著舊照片，反過來推出摺法。

Danish magician Thoki Yenn (1919-2004) was also a painter. He fell in love with origami in 1958 and later built a playful personal website filled with his own illustrated folding diagrams. After he died, the website slowly fell apart.

Erik Demaine, a professor at MIT, is also a well-known origami researcher. He pieced the website back together page by page, using old snapshots from the Internet Archive. Readers also pitched in to help find the missing diagrams. One was a rocking horse folded in 2002 to celebrate the Year of the Horse. Another toy left no diagram behind, so people had to study old photographs and work backward to figure out how it was folded.
