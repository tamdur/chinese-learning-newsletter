閃靈《高砂軍》：為被忘記的人唱歌

來源：PopMatters

台北樂團「閃靈」在2011年發行《高砂軍》。這是他們歷史三部曲的最後一張，前兩張是《賽德克巴萊》和《十殿》。

「高砂義勇隊」是二戰時被日本找去當兵的台灣原住民。封面上，一位隊員偷偷刺上被日本禁止的賽德克族紋面（臉上的圖案）。

音樂裡，飛快的鼓聲和台灣傳統樂器、民謠旋律混在一起。主唱Freddy後來當上立法委員。他用最猛烈的聲音，把被淡忘的歷史拉回大家眼前。

In 2011, Taipei band Sinn Sing (閃靈) released Takasago Army (《高砂軍》). It is the final installment of their historical trilogy; the first two albums were Seediq Bale (《賽德克巴萊》) and Ten Courts (《十殿》).

The Takasago Volunteer Corps were Taiwanese Indigenous men whom Japan recruited to fight in World War II. On the cover, one soldier has secretly tattooed a Seediq facial tattoo (the traditional designs on the face), a practice Japan had banned.

The music mixes rapid-fire drumming with Taiwanese traditional instruments and folk melodies. Lead vocalist Freddy later became a member of the Legislative Yuan. He used the most fierce sound he could to drag forgotten history back into the public eye.

為了一座燈塔，畫一條彎彎的國界

來源：Atlas Obscura

芬蘭和瑞典之間的海上，有一座小島叫 Märket。1885年，俄國人在島上蓋燈塔，卻量錯了邊界，把燈塔蓋到瑞典那一邊。

兩國沒有吵架。1985年，他們把島上的國界改成彎彎的 S 形，讓燈塔回到芬蘭，兩國的土地和海岸線也還是一樣多。

從奧蘭群島可以租船去看。奧蘭本身也很特別：1921年，國際聯盟決定它屬於芬蘭，但島上不能有軍隊，居民說瑞典語。

Somewhere in the sea between Finland and Sweden sits a small island called Märket. In 1885, Russians built a lighthouse on the island, but they had surveyed the border wrong and put the lighthouse on the Swedish side.

Neither country went to war over it. In 1985, they redrew the border around the island into a winding S-shape, so the lighthouse ended up back in Finland, and the two countries kept the same amount of land and coastline as before.

You can rent a boat from the Åland Islands to go and see it. Åland is special in its own right: in 1921, the League of Nations decided that it belonged to Finland, but the islands were not allowed to have a military, and the residents speak Swedish.

冬日窗前的一片彩色玻璃

來源：CourseHorse (Evanston Art Center)

12月12日星期六，Evanston 藝術中心有一堂彩色玻璃入門課，從早上10點到下午4點。

你會自己畫圖，再切玻璃、包銅邊，用焊錫（熔化後能黏住東西的金屬）把它們接起來，做出聖誕節或光明節的作品。不需要經驗。

學費190美元，18歲以上都能報名。多付50美元，還能裝上框掛在窗上。陽光照進來時，家裡就有一小片教堂的光。

On Saturday, December 12, the Evanston Art Center is offering an introductory stained glass class from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m.

You will sketch your own design, then cut the glass, wrap the edges in copper foil, and join the pieces with solder (a metal that melts so it can bond things together). The result is a piece for Christmas or Hanukkah. No experience is needed.

Tuition is $190, and anyone 18 or older can sign up. For an extra $50, you can have your piece framed and hung in a window. When the sunlight streams through, your home gets a small patch of church light.

只有八個指令的程式語言

來源：Esolang Wiki (esolangs.org)

Esolangs.org 是網友一起寫的維基網站，收集了幾百種「怪怪的」程式語言。它們不是拿來工作的，而是為了好玩、搞笑或挑戰自己。

最有名的一種叫 Brainfuck。1993年，瑞士學生 Urban Müller 發明了它，只有八個符號指令。他想為 Amiga 電腦做出最小的編譯器（把程式翻成電腦看得懂的工具），最後只有大約240個位元組。

他把它放上網，邀陌生人用它寫出真正的東西，然後就離開了。幾十年後，志工們還在這裡照顧它啟發的幾百種語言。

Esolangs.org is a wiki built collaboratively by volunteers, cataloguing several hundred "weird" programming languages. They are not meant for real work. People make them for fun, for laughs, or to test their own skills.

The most famous is Brainfuck. In 1993, Swiss student Urban Müller invented it with just eight symbols for commands. He wanted to write the smallest possible compiler (a tool that translates a program into something a computer can run) for the Amiga computer. The result was only about 240 bytes.

He posted it online, invited strangers to use it to build real things, and then walked away. Decades later, volunteers are still looking after the several hundred languages it inspired.
