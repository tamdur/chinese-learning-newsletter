廟會鼓聲裡的搖滾樂

來源：Bandcamp Daily

「裝咖人」是一個台灣樂團，由幾位愛讀文學的學生在二○一七年組成。

他們在二○二一年的專輯《夜官巡場》，說的是團長小時候在嘉義「火燒庄」聽到的神明和鬼故事。

最特別的是，他們把廟會用的「北管」鼓樂放進搖滾樂裡，聽起來就像走在夜市的遊行隊伍中。

"Zhuang Ka Ren" (裝咖人) is a Taiwanese band formed in 2017 by a group of literature-loving students. Their 2021 album "Night Officials on Patrol" (夜官巡場) draws on stories of deities and ghosts the band leader heard as a child in the "Burning Village" (火燒庄) of Chiayi. What makes them truly distinctive is how they weave the "Beiguan" (北管) percussion of temple festivals directly into rock music, so it sounds like walking through a procession at a night market.

難民帶來的國民點心

來源：Atlas Obscura / Gastro Obscura

芬蘭最有名的點心「卡累利亞派」，是用黑麥（一種麥子）做皮，裡面包著米粥的小餅。

它本來只是「卡累利亞」這個地方的食物。一九四○年這塊土地被蘇聯拿走，四十二萬人逃到芬蘭各地，才讓它變成全國人都愛吃的點心。

三月到芬蘭，市場裡就有熱熱的餅，大家會抹上奶油和蛋一起吃。

Finland's most famous snack, the Karelian pasty, is a small pie with a rye crust filled with rice porridge. It was originally just a local food of the region of Karelia. When that territory was seized by the Soviet Union in 1940, 420,000 people fled to various parts of Finland — and that is how it became a snack the whole country loves. Visit a market in March and you'll find them warm, and people spread them with butter and egg before eating.

走進大腦的劇場

來源：Theater of the Mind Chicago

在芝加哥有一個很特別的展覽，叫《心智劇場》。它是由有名的音樂人大衛．拜恩和作家一起做的。

每一場只讓十六個人走進一間間房間，花七十五分鐘用眼睛、耳朵和舌頭去體驗大腦怎麼記住事情。

票價是六十九到九十九美元，展到二○二六年八月底，還有很多時間可以買票。

Chicago is home to a very special exhibition called "Theater of the Mind." It was created by the renowned musician David Byrne together with a writer. Each session admits only sixteen visitors into a series of rooms, where over seventy-five minutes they use sight, hearing, and taste to experience how the brain remembers things. Tickets run from sixty-nine to ninety-nine dollars, and the show runs through the end of August 2026 — so there is still plenty of time to buy tickets.

一張地圖看遍全美礦場

來源：mines.fyi（Show HN）

有一個人做了一個很厲害的網站，叫 mines.fyi。他把美國九萬多個礦場（挖礦的地方）全部放在一張地圖上。

你可以用州、挖的東西、或是公司來找。想看內華達州的鋰礦（做電池的金屬），或是你家附近的金礦，都找得到。

每個礦場還有每週更新的資料，讓人看見這個平常看不到的世界。

One person built a remarkable website called mines.fyi. They put all of America's 90,000-plus mines (the places where mining happens) onto a single map. You can search by state, by what is being mined, or by company. Want to see the lithium mines (the metal used to make batteries) in Nevada, or a gold mine near your home? You can find them all. Each mine even comes with weekly-updated data, revealing a world most people never get to see.
