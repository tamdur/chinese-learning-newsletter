鐵琴上的五聲音階

來源：TVBS 新聞

魯千千是台灣的鐵琴手。鐵琴是一種用小棒子敲的金屬琴。她在台北藝術大學學古典音樂，後來到美國念爵士樂。

2020年，她發行第一張專輯《行 The Path》。她把東方的五聲音階放進爵士樂團，聽起來像兩種語言自然地說在一起。這張專輯在美國爵士排行榜前20名，一待就是20週。

後來，她和貝斯手一起做的《繫 Connected》，還拿下了金曲獎。

Lu Qianqian is a vibraphone player from Taiwan. The vibraphone is a metal-keyed instrument played by striking its bars with small mallets. She studied classical music at Taipei National University of the Arts, and later went to the United States to study jazz.

In 2020, she released her first album, Xing (The Path). She brought the five-tone scale of Eastern music into a jazz band, and the result sounds like two languages speaking together naturally. The album stayed in the top 20 of the American jazz charts for 20 weeks. Later, Ji (Connected), which she made with a bassist, won a Golden Melody Award.

掛在天花板上的麵包

來源：Wikipedia / finland.fi

在芬蘭西部，以前的農家一年只烤兩次黑麥麵包。麵包做成扁扁的圓圈，中間有一個洞。

人們把麵包穿在長長的木棍上，掛在廚房的天花板下面，讓它慢慢變乾變硬，可以放好幾個月。芬蘭東部剛好相反，每個星期都烤新鮮的圓麵包。

黑麥不怕冷，也不怕土地不好。2017年，黑麥麵包被選為芬蘭的國家食物。

In western Finland, farmhouses used to bake rye bread only twice a year. The loaves were shaped into flat rings with a hole in the middle. People threaded them onto long wooden poles and hung them from the kitchen ceiling, where they slowly dried and hardened and could keep for several months. Eastern Finland did the opposite, baking fresh round loaves every week. Rye does not mind the cold or poor soil. In 2017, rye bread was named Finland's national food.

教堂裡的女巫故事

來源：Court Theatre (Chicago)

1692年，美國塞勒姆發生了有名的「女巫審判」。第一個被指控（被說做了壞事）的人，是一位叫蒂圖巴的女人。

12月10日到20日，芝加哥的宮廷劇院要在芝加哥大學的老教堂裡，演出一個人的戲《蒂圖巴》。這是它第一次在美國演出，音樂由爵士歌手 esperanza spalding 寫成。

12月14日，她會和大家談戲裡的音樂。沒有買票的人也可以來聽。

In 1692, the famous "witch trials" took place in Salem, Massachusetts. The first person accused of doing evil was a woman named Tituba. From December 10 to 20, the Court Theatre in Chicago will stage a one-woman show, Tituba, in the old chapel at the University of Chicago. It is the play's first performance in the United States, and the music was written by jazz singer esperanza spalding. On December 14, she will talk with the audience about the music in the play. You do not need a ticket to come and listen.

自己做天線，一起找閃電

來源：Blitzortung.org

Blitzortung.org 是一個由志工建立的閃電地圖。世界各地的愛好者自己做天線和小機器，最多花大約355美元。

每台機器收到閃電的訊號，就傳到同一個系統。系統比較訊號到達的時間，馬上算出閃電打在哪裡，再畫在免費的地圖上。

這個網站從德國開始，現在全世界有幾百個站。2023年的研究發現，日本的站能找到大約九成的閃電，位置誤差（算錯的距離）不到5.6公里。

Blitzortung.org is a lightning map built by volunteers. Enthusiasts around the world make their own antennas and small devices, spending up to about US$355 each. Each device picks up lightning signals and sends them to a single shared system. The system compares the times at which the signals arrive, works out where the lightning struck almost instantly, and plots it on a free map. The network started in Germany and now has several hundred stations around the world. A 2023 study found that Japan's stations detect about 90% of lightning, with location errors (how far off the calculated position is) of less than 5.6 kilometers.
