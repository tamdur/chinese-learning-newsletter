林強的《娛樂世界》：被遺忘的電子奇作

來源：Rate Your Music

一九九三年，台灣歌手林強飛到英國倫敦，找了名製作人John Fryer一起錄音。隔年他帶回了《娛樂世界》這張專輯（唱片）。

這張唱片把流行搖滾和電子、噪音（很吵的聲音）混在一起，歌詞大罵當時的媒體和娛樂圈。一九九四年幾乎沒人買，現在卻被看作台灣最有想法的作品之一。

後來林強替侯孝賢的電影《千禧曼波》配樂，在世界各地出了名。

In 1993, Taiwanese singer Lim Giong flew to London to record with producer John Fryer. The following year, he returned with the album Entertainment World. The record blended pop rock with electronic music and noise, its lyrics harshly criticizing the media and entertainment industry of the time. In 1994, hardly anyone bought it—but today it's regarded as one of Taiwan's most thoughtful works. Later, Lim Giong gained international recognition after composing the score for director Hou Hsiao-hsien's film Millennium Mambo.

芬蘭魯亞毯：從維京船到婚禮的織毯

來源：Kotona Living

魯亞毯（ryijy）是芬蘭很古老的厚毛毯。最早是維京水手在船上蓋著睡覺用的。

到了十七世紀，它變成婚禮的重要東西。新娘會親手織一張，兩人在典禮上站在毯子上，之後就當作床蓋。毯上織著心、皇冠和生命樹的圖案。

蜜月時可以去于韋斯屈萊的芬蘭手工藝博物館，看這段老故事。

The rya rug (ryijy) is an ancient thick woolen blanket from Finland. Originally, Viking sailors used it to sleep on aboard ships. By the seventeenth century, it had become an essential part of wedding ceremonies. The bride would weave one by hand, and the couple would stand on the blanket during the ceremony before using it as a bedcover afterward. The blankets were woven with patterns of hearts, crowns, and trees of life. During a honeymoon, visitors can see this old story at the Finnish Handicrafts Museum in Jyvaskyla.

夏至聚會：在芝加哥森林裡迎接白晝

來源：Friends of the Forest Preserves

六月二十日，芝加哥南邊的博比恩森林保護區會辦一場免費的夏至（一年中白天最長的一天）聚會，需要先報名。

那天下午有輕鬆的森林散步、動手整理土地、種植活動，還有免費的食物。主辦單位希望大家停下腳步，和大自然重新親近。

這片森林在卡魯梅河和湖交會的地方，是市內生物最多、卻最少人去的角落之一。

On June 20th, the Beaubien Woods Forest Preserve south of Chicago will host a free summer solstice gathering — the day with the longest daylight of the year — with advance registration required. That afternoon features easy forest walks, hands-on land stewardship work, planting activities, and free food. The organizers hope visitors will pause and reconnect with nature. The forest sits where the Calumet River meets the lake, making it one of the city's most biodiverse yet least-visited corners.

一個人的搶救：被刪掉的世界資料庫

來源：worldfactbookarchive.org (via Show HN)

美國中情局的《世界概況》，是一本介紹兩百八十一個國家的公開資料。二○二六年二月，它忽然被全部刪掉了。

一位叫MilkMp的工程師出手了。他把一九九○年到二○二五年的每一版都存進資料庫，超過一百萬筆資料，還做了一個網站。

現在你可以查一個國家在不同年代的人口和經濟變化。這一切，只因為有人覺得這份紀錄不該消失。

The U.S. Central Intelligence Agency's World Factbook is a public database introducing two hundred eighty-one countries. In February 2026, it was suddenly deleted entirely. An engineer named MilkMp stepped in. He preserved every version from 1990 to 2025 in a database—over a million records—and built a website around it. Now you can track how a country's population and economy have changed across different eras. All of this happened because one person believed this record shouldn't disappear.
