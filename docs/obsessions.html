一把月琴，唱出台灣的根

來源：中央社 CNA

一九六七年，兩位學者許常惠和史惟亮到南部採集民歌，遇見了恆春的陳達。他眼睛快看不見了，靠一把月琴走遍半島賣唱。

一九七一年，他們替他錄了唱片。他唱的〈思想起〉沒有固定歌詞，想到什麼就唱什麼：思念、往事、村裡的故事。同一首歌，每次都不一樣。

一九七八年，雲門舞集請他上台北，為舞劇《薪傳》現場伴奏。台灣後來的民謠，幾乎都從這位老歌手身上長出來。

In 1967, two scholars, Xu Changhui and Shih Wei-liang, travelled to southern Taiwan to collect folk songs and encountered Chen Da of Hengchun. His eyesight had nearly failed him, yet he crossed the peninsula with a moon lute, busking for a living. In 1971 they recorded an album for him. The song he sang, "Sixiangqi" (Thinking Back), had no fixed lyrics; he sang whatever came to mind — longing, memories, stories from his village. The same song was never the same twice. In 1978, Cloud Gate Dance Theatre invited him to Taipei to play live accompaniment for the dance drama "Legacy". Nearly all of the Taiwanese folk music that followed grew out of this old singer.

沒有圍牆的監獄

來源：thisisFINLAND

從赫爾辛基坐船，十五分鐘就到芬蘭堡。這座十八世紀的海上城堡是世界遺產，遊客整年不斷。

島上還有一座監獄，一九七一年開辦，沒有圍牆，也沒有鐵門。十幾位受刑人（關在監獄裡的人）住在這裡，白天修補古老的石牆、照顧島上的草木，有人還能搭船到市區上班。

芬蘭認為，處罰不該拿走一個人的責任。你拍照的那面牆，也許昨天才被他們補好。

A fifteen-minute boat ride from Helsinki brings you to Suomenlinna, an eighteenth-century sea fortress and UNESCO World Heritage site that draws visitors year-round.

The island is also home to a prison, opened in 1971, with no walls and no iron gates. A dozen or so inmates live here, spending their days repairing the old stone ramparts and tending the island's greenery. Some even take the boat into the city for a day job.

Finland's principle is simple: punishment should not take away a person's responsibility. That wall you photographed may have been repointed by them only yesterday.

十萬雙球鞋，一天換現金

來源：Sneaker Con

八月二十九日星期六，中午到晚上七點，球鞋大會來到芝加哥郊區的紹姆堡會議中心。兩百多個攤位，帶來超過十萬雙球鞋，還有球員卡和潮流服飾。

場內有一組「收購小隊」，看見你袋子裡的鞋，就當場出價、直接付現。主辦單位估計，一天會付出超過二十五萬美元。

平常球鞋都在手機上買賣。這一天，它變回一個看得見、摸得到的市場。

On Saturday, August 29th, from noon to 7pm, Sneaker Con comes to the Schaumburg Convention Center in Chicago's suburbs. More than two hundred vendor booths will bring over one hundred thousand pairs of sneakers, along with trading cards and streetwear. A roaming "cash-out crew" works the floor — spot the shoes in your bag, make an offer on the spot, and pay in cash. The organizers estimate they will hand over more than $250,000 in a single day. Sneakers usually change hands on a phone. For one day, the market becomes something you can see and touch.

四千萬年前的小蟲，一張張被拍下來

來源：amber-fossils.com

琥珀（樹脂變成的化石）裡，常常封著幾千萬年前的小蟲和蜘蛛。一位丹麥的琥珀專家花了很多年，把牠們一隻一隻拍成清楚的特寫。

他曾幫丹麥自然史博物館收下七萬件的琥珀收藏，也為默恩島的地質中心做了常設展。後來他把所有知識放上自己的網站。

網站上沒有廣告，也不賣飾品。首頁只寫著一件事：他想讓每個人都能讀到這些知識。

Amber — fossilized tree resin — often seals in insects and spiders from tens of millions of years ago. A Danish amber specialist spent years photographing them one by one in sharp close-up. He helped the Danish Natural History Museum acquire a collection of 70,000 amber pieces, and built the permanent exhibition at the geological centre on the island of Møn. Eventually he put all of that knowledge on his own website. There are no advertisements, and nothing is for sale. The homepage says only one thing: he wants everyone to be able to read it.
