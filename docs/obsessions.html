台灣狄斯可：被遺忘的七〇年代女聲

來源：Spectrum Culture / Aberrant Records

二〇二〇年，一家愛挖老唱片的公司「 Aberrant 」，發行了一張黑膠合輯，叫《台灣狄斯可》。裡面收了七〇到八〇年代初，台灣女歌手的冷門歌曲，像電視綜藝天后崔苔菁，還有歌手吳秀珠。

那時台灣還在戒嚴（社會管得很嚴），可是西方的放克、搖滾卻悄悄跑進錄音室。鼓聲很野，吉他毛毛的，配上緊張又不安的唱腔，變成一種奇怪又好聽的混血音樂。

In 2020, Aberrant—a label known for scouring forgotten vinyl—released a compilation LP titled "Taiwan Disco." It collected obscure songs by Taiwanese female singers from the 1970s through the early 1980s, including TV variety show host Tsui Tai-ching and singer Wu Hsiu-chu. Taiwan was under martial law at the time, with society tightly controlled, yet Western funk and rock quietly seeped into recording studios. The drums were wild, the guitars fuzzy, paired with tense, uneasy vocals, creating a strange yet captivating hybrid sound.

偵探社的祕密：赫爾辛基的禁酒年代

來源：MyHelsinki / Trillby & Chadwick

一九一九到一九三二年，芬蘭全國禁酒（不准喝酒）。可是酒還是一船一船從外國偷偷運進來，多到歷史學家把波羅的海叫做「伏特加海」。

赫爾辛基有一家酒吧 Trillby & Chadwick ，藏在一扇沒有招牌的木門後面。傳說一九二四年，這裡假裝是偵探社，其實是全城最賺錢的地下酒館，警察從來沒抓到過它。

From 1919 to 1932, Finland enforced a nationwide alcohol ban. Yet spirits still flowed in by the shipload from abroad—so much so that historians nicknamed the Baltic Sea the "Vodka Sea." In Helsinki, a bar called Trillby & Chadwick operated behind an unmarked wooden door. Legend has it that by 1924, the place posed as a detective agency but was actually the city's most lucrative speakeasy—and the police never once raided it.

從菜園到餐桌：植物園的現摘料理秀

來源：Chicago Botanic Garden

七月二十五日星期六，芝加哥植物園有一場免費的戶外做菜表演。 The Chopping Block 烹飪學校的廚師 Lisa Counts ，會用當天早上剛從園裡摘下的蔬菜，現場煮給大家看。

這是「花園主廚系列」的活動，夏天每個月的第二、第四個週末，早上十一點半和十二點半各一場，先到先坐。隔天七月二十六日，換另一位廚師上台，兩天都很精彩。

On Saturday, July 25th, the Chicago Botanic Garden is hosting a free outdoor cooking demonstration. Chef Lisa Counts from The Chopping Block cooking school will prepare a live show using vegetables freshly picked from the garden that morning. This is part of the "Garden Chef Series," held on the second and fourth weekends of each summer month at 11:30 a.m. and 12:30 p.m., with seating on a first-come, first-served basis. The next day, July 26th, features a different chef, and both days promise to be excellent.

番茄醬的博物館：一個省錢念頭的二十年

來源：condimentpacket.com

住在美國費城郊區的 Chris Harne ，二〇〇三年發現只要收集免費的番茄醬包，就不用再花錢買醬。他把每一包小心倒空，再把包裝紙收進球員卡的塑膠套裡，照口味或餐廳分類。

網站裡最珍貴的，是一位老太太花三十年蒐集的二百六十八件收藏。她過世後，管理員接手了這些寶貝。這個網站已經開心地經營了二十多年。

Chris Harne, who lives in the suburbs of Philadelphia, discovered in 2003 that he could collect free ketchup packets instead of buying sauce. He carefully emptied each packet and stored the wrappers in plastic sleeves for sports cards, organizing them by flavor or restaurant. The site's most prized possession is a collection of 268 items gathered by an elderly woman over thirty years. After her death, the website administrator took over these treasures. The website has been happily maintained for over twenty years.
