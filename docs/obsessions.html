當鋼琴遇見笙：卡到音的慢速實驗

來源：Ka Dao Yin / Bandcamp

「卡到音」是一個來自台灣的四人樂團，團名是台語「被迷住了」的意思。他們用鋼琴、薩克斯風、古箏和笙（一種中國吹管樂器）來即興演奏，把東方和西方的聲音混在一起。

2013年的專輯《慢》靈感來自台灣路上的「慢」字標誌，十首歌都用很慢的速度展開，像在安靜的房間裡呼吸。這張專輯在2014年拿下第25屆金曲獎最佳跨界專輯。

"Stuck in Sound" is a four-member band from Taiwan, with a name that means "captivated" in Taiwanese. They improvise with piano, saxophone, guzheng, and sheng—a traditional Chinese wind instrument—blending Eastern and Western soundscapes together. Their 2013 album Slow drew inspiration from the "slow" traffic signs seen on Taiwan's roads. All ten tracks unfold at a measured pace, like breathing in a quiet room. The album won the Best Crossover Album award at the 25th Golden Melody Awards in 2014.

住進芬蘭四百年莊園

來源：Tertti Manor (tertinkartano.fi)

芬蘭湖區的米凱利附近，散落著許多古老的莊園（大農場）。特爾蒂莊園建於十七世紀，那時芬蘭還屬於瑞典王國，貴族（有錢的家族）用供應軍馬來換免稅的權利。

這座莊園經歷過俄國占領和二戰，現在由第四代家族經營有機農場旅館。三月來訪，可以住在改建的倉庫房間裡，吃農場自產的新鮮食物，窗外是一片白雪覆蓋的湖泊。

Near Mikkeli in Finland's lake region, ancient estates (large farms) are scattered throughout the landscape. Terttu Manor was built in the seventeenth century, when Finland was still part of the Swedish kingdom, and nobles (wealthy families) exchanged the supply of military horses for tax exemption rights. The estate has survived Russian occupation and World War II, and is now run by the fourth generation of the family as an organic farm inn. Visit in March to stay in converted barn rooms, eat fresh food grown on the farm, and gaze out at snow-covered lakes beyond the windows.

海德公園花市：芝加哥最老的植物市集

來源：Hyde Park Garden Fair (hydeparkgardenfair.org)

這個週末（5月16至17日），芝加哥最古老的社區植物市集將在海德公園的Kimbark Plaza舉行，免費入場。這個花市從1959年開始，今年已經第67屆了。

最受歡迎的是香草區，有玫瑰味百里香、巧克力薄荷等少見的品種，還有數百株乳草（蝴蝶最愛的植物）和番茄苗。所有收入都捐給社區公園，賣不完的植物送給學校和教堂。

This weekend (May 16-17), Chicago's oldest community plant market will be held at Kimbark Plaza in Hyde Park, with free admission. The flower market, which started in 1959, is now in its 67th year. The most popular section is the herbs area, featuring rare varieties like rose-scented thyme and chocolate mint, along with hundreds of milkweed plants (a butterfly favorite) and tomato seedlings. All proceeds go to community parks, and unsold plants are donated to schools and churches.

一個人，二十五年，四萬五千種植物用途

來源：Botanical Research Institute of Texas (BRIT)

密西根大學的人類學家Daniel Moerman花了25年，從200多份歷史文獻中，一筆一筆整理出美洲原住民使用植物的方式：食物、藥物、染料、纖維。

這個免費資料庫記錄了291個部落、4029種植物、共45000筆用途，任何人都可以上網搜尋。一位工程師後來自己做了一個備份網站，因為他覺得這些知識太珍貴，不能消失。

A University of Michigan anthropologist named Daniel Moerman spent 25 years systematically cataloging how Native Americans used plants, combing through over 200 historical documents entry by entry. His meticulous work documented their applications as food, medicine, dye, and fiber. The free database he created records 45,000 plant uses across 4,029 species from 291 tribes—accessible to anyone online. An engineer later built a backup website independently, convinced that knowledge this valuable was too precious to risk losing.
