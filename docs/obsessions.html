林強的聲音世界：用台灣的聲音畫一幅畫

來源：In Sheep's Clothing HiFi

林強是台灣最特別的音樂人之一。他最早唱閩南語流行歌，後來為侯孝賢（知名導演）的電影《千禧曼波》做配樂，走向電子音樂。

二〇〇五年，他出了《驚蟄》這張專輯。他在台灣各地收集了幾千個真實的聲音——寺廟的鑼（一種金屬樂器）、歌仔戲的片段、大自然的環境音——再混合成電子音樂，他叫它「立體音畫」。這張專輯拿了金曲獎最佳跨界專輯，還在坎城影展現場演出。

Lin Strong is one of Taiwan's most distinctive musicians. He initially sang Hokkien pop songs, later composed the soundtrack for director Hou Hsiao-hsien's film "Millennium Mambo," and moved toward electronic music.

In 2005, he released the album "Awakening of Insects." He collected thousands of authentic sounds from across Taiwan—temple gongs, segments from Taiwanese opera, ambient sounds from nature—and mixed them into electronic music, which he called "three-dimensional sound paintings." The album won the Golden Melody Award for Best Crossover Album and was performed live at the Cannes Film Festival.

煙燻桑拿：芬蘭最古老的溫暖

來源：Kakslauttanen Arctic Resort / Visit Finland

在芬蘭北部的拉普蘭地區，有一座叫 Kakslauttanen 的度假村。這裡有三間煙燻桑拿（一種沒有煙囪的傳統蒸氣浴室），最大的一間能容納兩百人。木柴先燒好幾個小時，煙排出去以後，留下的是柔和、帶著木頭香氣的熱氣。

客人住在玻璃屋頂的小屋裡，躺在床上就能看北極光。蒸完桑拿後，可以跳進冰湖或在雪地裡打滾。三月是好時機：雪還很厚，極光還在，白天也開始變長了。

In Lapland in northern Finland, there is a resort called Kakslauttanen. It has three smoke saunas—traditional steam baths without chimneys—with the largest capable of holding two hundred people. The wood is burned for several hours, and once the smoke clears, what remains is gentle heat infused with the aroma of wood.

Guests stay in small cabins with glass roofs, where they can watch the Northern Lights from bed. After a sauna session, they can jump into an icy lake or roll around in the snow. March is an ideal time: the snow is still thick, the Northern Lights are still visible, and the days are starting to grow longer.

David Byrne的「心靈劇場」：你的感官在說謊

來源：Theater of the Mind Chicago / Goodman Theatre

搖滾樂團 Talking Heads 的主唱 David Byrne 和作家 Mala Gaonkar 一起做了一個很特別的展覽，叫「心靈劇場」（Theater of the Mind），現在在芝加哥展出。

每次只有十六個人一起進去，花七十五分鐘走過一個巨大的空間。裡面的每個房間都根據神經科學研究設計，會騙你的眼睛、耳朵和觸覺，讓你發現自己的感官其實很容易被騙。地點在 LaSalle 街三三三號，票價六十六美元起，到七月十二日為止。

David Byrne, the lead singer of the rock band Talking Heads, has partnered with writer Mala Gaonkar to create a special exhibition called "Theater of the Mind," now on display in Chicago.

Only sixteen people enter at a time, spending seventy-five minutes walking through a vast space. Each room inside is designed based on neuroscience research and will deceive your eyes, ears, and sense of touch, revealing how easily your senses can be fooled. Located at 333 LaSalle Street, tickets start at sixty-six dollars and run through July 12th.
