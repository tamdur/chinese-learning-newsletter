為台灣的鬼怪傳說配樂

來源：WV Sorcerer Productions (Bandcamp)

台北的樂團「夢東」是兩兄弟組成的，還有一位神祕的第三人，連他們的唱片公司老闆都不知道他是誰。

兄弟倆花了半年多，走遍台灣奇怪的角落，拍下地獄主題的廟、木乃伊（乾掉的和尚身體）和路邊的小神壇，做成一本攝影書叫《台灣謎景》。他們還把老電玩和恐怖電影的聲音混在一起，變成慢慢的電子迷幻曲。這張專輯先在二〇一九和二〇二一年出過卡帶，後來重新做成黑白花紋的黑膠唱片。

Mong Tong (夢東), a Taipei band made up of two brothers plus a mysterious third member whose identity even their record label boss doesn't know, spent over half a year exploring Taiwan's strangest corners. They photographed hell-themed temples, mummified monks, and roadside spirit shrines, then compiled the images into a photography book called "Taiwan Mystery." The band also mixed sounds from vintage video games and horror films into slow, hypnotic electronic tracks. The album first appeared on cassette in 2019 and 2021, then was reissued as a black-and-white marbled vinyl record.

「請安靜」：芬蘭把寂靜變成招牌

來源：Helsinki Times

二〇一一年，芬蘭的觀光單位推出新的宣傳，照片上只有一個人站在空曠的風景裡，寫著簡單的一句話：「請安靜」。

別的國家覺得不愛講話很奇怪，芬蘭卻把它變成賣點。他們不再抱歉「這裡都沒有人在聊天」，反而大方接受，後來還推出「寂靜晚餐」，大家安安靜靜吃好幾道菜。對三月來度蜜月的人來說，這是一個提醒：在三溫暖、火車或餐廳裡不說話，不是冷淡，而是芬蘭人表達親近和尊重的方式。

In 2011, Finland's tourism board launched a campaign featuring photographs of a lone figure standing in empty landscape, accompanied by simple text that read "Silence, Please." While other countries found it strange that people didn't talk much, Finland turned silence into a selling point. Rather than apologizing for the lack of conversation, they embraced it openly—and later even introduced a "Silence Meal," where everyone ate multiple courses together in total quiet. For couples arriving in March on their honeymoon, this serves as a reminder: not speaking in a sauna, on a train, or in a restaurant isn't coldness but the Finnish way of expressing closeness and respect.

邊跑邊讀：恩格塢的說書路跑

來源：Read & Run Chicago

七月十二日星期天早上九點，「讀跑芝加哥」要在恩格塢（Englewood）辦一場免費的路跑導覽。

這次的主題來自一本書《別走》，書裡談為什麼有些芝加哥人選擇留在被忽略的社區，有些人卻搬走。這個團體從二〇二一年到現在，已經在三十多個社區跑過一百二十多條和書有關的路線，一邊帶大家跑步，一邊聊社區的歷史和故事。

On Sunday, July 12th at 9 a.m., Read & Run Chicago will host a free guided run in Englewood. The theme comes from a book called Don't Go, which explores why some Chicago residents choose to stay in overlooked neighborhoods while others move away. Since 2021, the group has organized runs on over 120 book-related routes through more than 30 neighborhoods, combining the runs with conversations about each neighborhood's history and stories.

尋找消失的畫面：失落媒體維基

來源：The Lost Media Wiki

「失落媒體維基」是一個有一萬七千名志工的網站，專門找回不見了的電影、電視和遊戲畫面。他們像偵探一樣，分析資料、找當年的工作人員、翻私人收藏。

其中一個有名的案子是《芝麻街》一九七六年的第八四七集：一位演員在裡面扮演《綠野仙蹤》的女巫，把小朋友嚇壞了，電視台從此不再播出。這集消失了四十六年，直到二〇二二年六月，有人在網路論壇上匿名（不寫名字）分享，才終於被找到。

The Lost Media Wiki is a website with 17,000 volunteers dedicated to recovering lost films, TV episodes, and video game footage. They work like detectives—analyzing information, tracking down original crew members, and combing through private collections. One famous case is a 1976 episode of Sesame Street (episode 847) in which an actor played the Wicked Witch from The Wizard of Oz so convincingly that it frightened young viewers, and the network never aired it again. The episode disappeared for 46 years until June 2022, when someone anonymously shared it on an online forum, finally allowing it to resurface.
