解嚴後最大膽的唱片：《抓狂歌》

來源：Wikipedia / 中央社 / Mirror Media

1987年，台灣解除戒嚴（由軍隊管理社會的命令）。兩年後，王明輝和陳明章用「黑名單工作室」的名字，推出專輯《抓狂歌》。

他們用台語饒舌（很快地念出歌詞），再加上搖滾和民謠，唱出農民上街、小人物生活的苦與笑。在那個年代，這樣敢說真話的唱片非常少見。

今天，很多人說它點燃了「新台語歌」運動，是一張值得一聽再聽的老唱片。

In 1987, Taiwan lifted martial law, the decree under which the military had managed society. Two years later, Wang Ming-hui and Chen Ming-chang released the album Crazy Songs under the name "Blacklist Studio." They used Taiwanese rap, delivering lyrics at breakneck speed, and combined it with rock and folk to sing about farmers taking to the streets and the joys and sorrows of ordinary people's lives. In those days, albums that dared to speak the truth this openly were very rare. Today, many people say it ignited the "New Taiwanese Song" movement, and it is an old record worth listening to again and again.

一個紙箱，芬蘭寶寶的第一張床

來源：Kela

從1938年起，芬蘭政府送給準媽媽一份免費的「媽媽禮盒」。裝禮物的紙箱放上床墊，就成了寶寶的第一張床。

2026年的紙箱是淡淡的桃子色，畫著太空旅行，由年輕畫家 Eveliina Muotkavaara 設計。裡面有52樣東西，還第一次放進小玩具球。

第一次當媽媽的人，九成五選紙箱，不拿現金。它不只是禮物，也是芬蘭人愛護孩子的象徵。

Since 1938, the Finnish government has given expectant mothers a free "maternity box." The cardboard box, once a mattress is placed inside, becomes the baby's very first bed. The 2026 box is a soft peach color decorated with space travel, designed by young artist Eveliina Muotkavaara. It contains 52 items, and this year for the first time includes a small toy ball. Ninety-five percent of first-time mothers choose the box over the cash alternative. It is more than a gift; it is also a symbol of how Finns cherish their children.

古地圖的一日市集

來源：Newberry Library

10月31日星期六，紐貝瑞圖書館（60 W. Walton St.）舉辦一年一次的古地圖與古書市集，早上9點到下午5點，免費入場。

今年剛好是芝加哥地圖學會成立50年。十幾家來自全美的書商會擺出老地圖和地圖集，現場還可以動手做地圖，聽愛荷華州地圖的故事。

想先熱身的話，前一晚有收費的開幕酒會，預售票40美元。

On October 31 (Saturday), the Newberry Library (60 W. Walton St.) will host its annual antiquarian map and book fair, running from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. with free admission. This year also marks the 50th anniversary of the Chicago Map Society. More than a dozen booksellers from across the United States will set out old maps and atlases. Visitors can also try their hand at mapmaking on site and hear stories about Iowa's maps. For a warm-up, there is a paid opening reception the night before, with presale tickets at $40.

走進火山的地下長廊

來源：Arizona State University Lava Tube Database

地質學家 Ronald Greeley 花了幾十年研究熔岩管（火山岩漿流過後留下的長山洞）。他想用地球上的山洞，了解火星和月球上的火山。

亞利桑那州立大學把他一櫃一櫃的筆記放上網，做成「熔岩管資料庫」，收錄34個國家的山洞，長度和坡度都查得到。

最有名的是夏威夷的 Kazumura 洞，長達65公里以上，是地球上已知最長的熔岩管。

Geologist Ronald Greeley spent decades studying lava tubes, the long caves left behind after volcanic magma has flowed through. He wanted to use caves on Earth to understand volcanoes on Mars and the Moon. Arizona State University has put his filing cabinets full of notes online as a "lava tube database," which catalogs caves in 34 countries, with their lengths and slopes searchable. The most famous is Kazumura Cave in Hawaii, more than 65 kilometers long and the longest known lava tube on Earth.
