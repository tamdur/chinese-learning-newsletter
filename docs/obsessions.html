陳揚的電子四季

來源：Fond Sound

一九八七年，台灣作曲家陳揚出了一張專輯，叫《現代四季》。整張音樂都是用電子合成器（一種用電來做出聲音的樂器）做出來的。

他的聲音冷冷的，像霧一樣慢慢散開，有人拿它和德國的「橘夢樂團」相比。可是旋律裡，聽得到中國的四季和老民謠。

陳揚後來為電影《桂花巷》寫音樂，很有名。但這張電子專輯，到今天還很少人知道。

In 1987, Taiwanese composer Chen Yang released an album called Modern Four Seasons. The entire record was made with electronic synthesizers, instruments that create sound using electricity. His sound is cool and distant, spreading slowly like fog, and some have compared it to the German group Tangerine Dream. Yet within the melodies, you can hear China's four seasons and old folk songs. Chen Yang later wrote music for the film Osmanthus Lane (桂花巷), and he became well known for it. But this electronic album is still little known today.

奧盧，黑色焦油的港口

來源：Oulu2026.eu

芬蘭北邊的城市奧盧，旁邊有一座小島，叫 Pikisaari，意思是「焦油島」。兩百年來，島上日夜都在煮松樹焦油（從松木燒出來的黑色黏油）。

這種黑油塗在木頭帆船上，可以防水。一八六〇年代，奧盧是芬蘭最大的焦油港口，英國、法國和美國的船都用過它。

後來鐵船取代了木船，這門生意很快就消失了。現在，你可以沿著「焦油故事」步道，走一走當年運油船的路。

Oulu, a city in northern Finland, has a small nearby island called Pikisaari, which means "Tar Island." For two hundred years, workers there boiled pine wood into tar, a thick black oil made by burning pine, day and night. The tar was painted onto wooden sailing ships to keep the water out. In the 1860s, Oulu was Finland's largest tar port, and ships from Britain, France, and the United States all took on cargo there. Later, iron ships replaced wooden ones, and the trade disappeared quickly. Today, visitors can follow the "Tar Stories" trail and walk the route the tar-carrying ships once used.

印在布上的六百年

來源：Newberry Library

十二月十日起，芝加哥紐伯瑞圖書館有一個新展覽，叫「印在布上」。它告訴大家，從一四〇〇年代到今天，人們怎麼把圖和字印在布上。

策展人（安排展覽的人） Suzanne Karr Schmidt 特別提到，很多女性曾經出錢請人印布，可是她們的故事常被忘記。

展覽免費，開到明年四月三日。冬天到市中心散步時，可以進去看看。

Starting December 10, the Newberry Library in Chicago will open a new exhibition called "Printed on Cloth." It traces how people printed images and words on fabric from the 1400s to today. Suzanne Karr Schmidt, the curator who organized the show, points out that many women once paid for cloth to be printed, yet their stories are often forgotten. Admission is free, and the exhibition runs through April 3 of next year. If you are walking around downtown this winter, it is worth stopping in.

看守森林的眼睛

來源：University of Idaho CDIL

美國愛達荷大學有個小團隊，在二〇二一年夏天走上山，去找還留下來的防火瞭望塔（在山頂看有沒有起火的高塔）。

他們做了一張互動地圖。每個點不只是位置，還畫出當年看守員能看到的整片森林。點進去，還能聽到老看守員說自己的故事。

愛達荷以前有將近一千座有人看守的塔，現在剩下不到兩百座。這個網站，留住了一種孤單又安靜的看山方式。

In the summer of 2021, a small team from the University of Idaho climbed mountains to track down the fire lookout towers (tall towers on mountaintops used to spot wildfires) that are still standing. They built an interactive map. Each marker shows more than a location: it also depicts the whole stretch of forest a lookout could see from that spot. Click on a marker and you can hear former lookouts tell their own stories. Idaho once had nearly a thousand staffed towers. Fewer than two hundred remain. The website preserves a lonely, quiet way of watching over the mountains.
