歌仔戲遇見爵士樂

來源：誠品線上

《歌仔曹七巧》是一張把歌仔戲（台灣的傳統戲曲）和爵士樂放在一起的專輯。故事來自張愛玲的小說《金鎖記》，音樂由柯智豪設計。

2026年8月，它在第37屆傳藝金曲獎拿下最佳跨界音樂專輯，主唱李靜芳也得到最佳演唱獎。

它好聽的地方是：歌仔戲長長的轉音和爵士的和聲像一對平等的朋友，誰也不是配角。

The album Gezai Cao Qiqiao puts Taiwanese gezai opera (a traditional Taiwanese form of musical theater) and jazz side by side. The story comes from Eileen Chang's novella The Golden Cangue (金鎖記), and the music was designed by Ke Zhihao. In August 2026, the album won Best Crossover Music Album at the 37th Traditional Arts Golden Melody Awards, and its lead vocalist, Li Jingfang, also took the Best Vocal Performance award. What makes it work is that the long, sliding ornaments of gezai singing and the harmonies of jazz feel like two equal friends, with neither one cast as the supporting player.

一百七十歲的松木屋頂

來源：歐洲文化遺產獎（Europa Nostra）

在芬蘭東部的湖區小鎮凱薩拉赫蒂，有一座1836年蓋的木造鐘樓。1941年，旁邊的教堂被炸彈燒毀，鐘樓卻留了下來。

它的屋頂有2800片手工劈開（用力切開）的松木瓦片，塗著黑黑的松焦油，已經大約170歲了。

這個只有約2000人的小教區，沒有改用鐵皮，而是訓練工匠用老方法修好它，還得到歐洲文化遺產大獎。

In Kesälahti, a lakeside town in eastern Finland, stands a wooden bell tower built in 1836. In 1941, the church next to it was burned down by a bomb, but the bell tower survived. Its roof is covered with 2,800 hand-split pine shingles coated in black pine tar, and it is roughly 170 years old. Rather than switching to sheet metal, this small parish of about 2,000 people trained craftsmen to repair it using the old methods, and it went on to win the European Heritage Award.

用金色補好破盤子

來源：芝加哥日本文化中心

10月24日星期六下午三點半到六點，芝加哥日本文化中心要開一堂「金繼」入門課。金繼是日本的老手藝，用加了金粉的漆把破掉的碗盤補起來，裂痕變成金線。

不需要經驗，材料都準備好了。這次用新式的膠土，不是傳統的漆，大家可以帶走自己修好的盤子。

老師高橋麻美是古物修復專家，在日本學過日本畫，平時住在芝加哥和東京兩地。

On Saturday, October 24, from 3:30 to 6:00 p.m., the Japan Cultural Center in Chicago will host a beginner's class in kintsugi. Kintsugi is an old Japanese craft in which broken bowls and plates are mended with lacquer mixed with gold powder, so the cracks become golden lines. No experience is needed, and all the materials are provided. This time the class uses a newer adhesive clay instead of traditional lacquer, and participants can take home the plate they repaired.

The instructor, Mami Takahashi, is an expert in restoring antiquities. She studied Japanese-style painting in Japan and divides her time between Chicago and Tokyo.

收藏所有日曆的圖書館

來源：MetaFilter

「時間圖書館」（Library of Time）是一個網站。一位不知名的作者想收集人類用過的每一種曆法（記日子的方法）。

這裡有古羅馬和馬雅人的曆法，有法國大革命時一天只分十小時的時鐘，甚至還有電影裡想像出來的日曆。

作者擔心，很多一個人做的網站，會在主人忘了付錢或過世後消失。所以這個網站只是一個簡單的檔案，下載後就能永遠打開。

The Library of Time is a website. An anonymous author set out to collect every calendar humanity has ever used, meaning every way people have kept track of days. Visitors will find the calendars of ancient Rome and the Maya, a clock from the French Revolution that split the day into just ten hours, and even calendars invented for movies. The author worries that many one-person websites disappear once the owner forgets to pay the bills or dies. So this site is just a simple file: download it, and you can open it forever.
