卡帶裡的革命：王福瑞與台灣噪音地下

來源：White Fungus

一九九三年，聲音藝術家王福瑞在台北成立了NOISE，這是台灣第一個實驗音樂廠牌。當時戒嚴（政府限制人民自由的時期）剛結束六年，各種新聲音開始出現。

王福瑞用郵寄的方式把手工包裝的卡帶寄到世界各地，每一捲都像一件小小的藝術品。他一九九七年的作品後來被收進比利時Sub Rosa唱片的中國實驗音樂選集，讓全世界聽見了台灣的聲音。

In 1993, sound artist Wang Fu-rui founded NOISE in Taipei, Taiwan's first experimental music label. Martial law had ended just six years earlier, and new sounds were beginning to emerge. Wang Fu-rui mailed handcrafted cassettes to listeners around the world, each one a small work of art in itself. His 1997 work was later included in the Belgian label Sub Rosa's anthology of Chinese experimental music, bringing Taiwan's sound to the world.

國王之路：穿越冰海的古老郵路

來源：Finnish Postal Museum

芬蘭的「國王之路」是一條七百年歷史的古道，從西邊的圖爾庫沿著南部海岸一路通往俄國邊界。最危險的一段是五十三公里的冰海橫渡（從結冰的海面上走過去），郵差要用手推著小船在浮冰上前進。

一七一一年冬天，十七名郵差在這段路上失去了生命。今天這條路已成為芬蘭第一條官方風景公路，沿途有中世紀城堡和美麗的海邊小鎮。

Finland's "King's Road" is an ancient route with seven centuries of history, stretching from Turku in the west along the southern coast all the way to the Russian border. The most perilous stretch is a fifty-three-kilometer crossing over frozen sea—travelers walked across the ice and postal carriers pushed small boats across the floes by hand. In the winter of 1711, seventeen postal carriers lost their lives on this segment. Today, the route has become Finland's first official scenic highway, lined with medieval castles and picturesque coastal towns.

吃掉你的鄰居：芝加哥野外採集散步

來源：Eventbrite / Odd Produce, Inc.

五月三十日，Dave Odd將帶你沿著伊利諾州巴達維亞的狐狸河步道散步兩小時，認識至少五十種可以吃的野生植物。

他是芝加哥唯一的全職採集師（專門在野外找食物的人），能認出超過一千種可食用植物，替三百多家餐廳供應野菜食材。有趣的是，他最初是一個窮困的喜劇演員，因為沒錢買菜才開始在巷子裡採花來吃。

On May 30th, Dave Odd will lead you on a two-hour walk along the Fox River trail in Batavia, Illinois, introducing you to at least fifty edible wild plants. He is Chicago's only full-time forager—a specialist in hunting for food in the wild—who can identify over a thousand edible plant species and supplies foraged vegetables to more than three hundred restaurants. Interestingly, he started out as a struggling comedian who, unable to afford groceries, began picking flowers from alleys to eat.

一個人的鐘塔普查：六十年的全球鐘聲記錄

來源：TowerBells.org

Carl Scott Zimmerman從一九六二年開始演奏鐘琴（裝在高塔上的一組大鐘），同時也開始記錄全世界的鐘塔資料。因為資料更新太快，印成書永遠來不及，他在一九九六年把它搬上了網路。

今天towerbells.org有超過三千五百頁，記錄了全球每一座已知的鐘塔樂器，從傳統銅鐘到瓷器鐘和玻璃鐘都有。一個人花六十年做一件事，這就是網路最美的樣子。

Carl Scott Zimmerman has been playing the carillon—a set of large bells mounted in a tower—since 1962, and he's been documenting bell towers around the world the whole time. Because the data was changing too fast to keep up with in print, he moved it online in 1996. Today towerbells.org has over three thousand five hundred pages, cataloging every known bell tower instrument globally, from traditional bronze bells to ceramic and glass ones. One person spending sixty years on a single project—that's the internet at its best.
