鬼島之聲：台灣電子音樂的黑暗面

來源：Resident Advisor

台灣年輕人有時開玩笑叫自己的國家「鬼島」。音樂家 Meuko! Meuko! 用這個名字做了她的第一張專輯。

這張專輯有五首歌，把台北街上的聲音、佛教的號角、快速的節拍和像鬼一樣的唱聲混在一起。聽起來像是在一個又美又可怕的夢裡走過台北的夜晚。

這張專輯由瑞士的 Danse Noire 唱片公司在 2018 年發行，後來在歐洲的音樂節上表演，讓世界聽見了台灣最前衛的聲音。

Young Taiwanese sometimes jokingly call their own country "Ghost Island." Musician Meuko! Meuko! named her first album after this epithet.

The album contains five tracks that blend street sounds from Taipei, Buddhist horns, rapid beats, and ghostly vocals. It sounds like walking through a Taipei night in a dream that is both beautiful and terrifying.

Released by Swiss label Danse Noire in 2018, the album later found performance opportunities at European music festivals, bringing Taiwan's most avant-garde sounds to the world's attention.

芬蘭的公共三溫暖：下班後的新去處

來源：Travel + Leisure

在芬蘭，三溫暖不只是洗澡的地方，更是交朋友的地方。現在城市裡的公共三溫暖越來越受歡迎，很多人下班後不去酒吧，而是去蒸氣房。

赫爾辛基的 Löyly 是最有名的一間。它建在海邊，蒸完以後可以直接跳進波羅的海裡。先熱再冷，這是芬蘭人幾百年來的傳統。

新式三溫暖還有餐廳和酒吧，讓古老的文化變成了現代人放鬆的新方式。

In Finland, saunas are not just a place to bathe — they're a place to make friends. Public saunas in cities are becoming increasingly popular, with many people heading to the steam room instead of the bar after work.

Löyly in Helsinki is the most famous one. It sits on the waterfront, and after steaming, you can dive straight into the icy Baltic Sea. Heat followed by cold — this has been a Finnish tradition for hundreds of years.

Modern saunas also feature restaurants and bars, transforming an ancient cultural practice into a contemporary way for people to unwind.

花的雨：芝加哥春天花展免費看

來源：Garfield Park Conservatory

芝加哥的加菲爾德公園溫室正在舉辦春天花展，一直到五月十日。今年的主題叫「花之雨」。

走進去會看到八十多個花籃掛在空中，藤蔓從上面垂下來，像是綠色的雨。地上還有鬱金香、蘭花和各種春天的花。

芝加哥市民免費入場。三月的芝加哥還很冷，走進溫暖的溫室看花，是最好的週末活動。

Chicago's Garfield Park Conservatory is hosting a spring flower exhibition running through May 10th. This year's theme is called "Flowers in the Rain."

Upon entering, you'll see over eighty hanging flower baskets suspended in the air, with vines cascading down from above like green rain. The ground level displays tulips, orchids, and various other spring flowers.

Admission is free for Chicago residents. March in Chicago is still cold, so stepping into the warm conservatory to view the flowers is the perfect weekend activity.
