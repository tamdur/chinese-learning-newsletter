金屬樂裡的神明廟會

來源：Blabbermouth.net

2018年，台北黑金屬樂團「閃靈」推出第八張專輯《政治》。十一首歌像一座廟，請來台灣民間的神明：帝爺公、王爺公、城隍爺，都在吉他和鼓聲裡現身。

王爺公是驅趕瘟疫（傳染病）的神。團員葉湘怡說，這張專輯也獻給1920年代爭取台灣權利的人。香港歌手何韻詩也來合唱，讓廟裡的神話和台灣的歷史，在重金屬裡相遇。

In 2018, Taipei black metal band Chthonic (閃靈) released its eighth album, Politics. Its eleven songs are built like a temple, inviting the folk deities of Taiwan: Emperor God, Lord Wang, and City God all make their appearance amid the guitars and drums. Lord Wang is the god who drives away plagues (infectious diseases). Band member Doris Yeh says the album is also a tribute to the people who fought for Taiwan's rights in the 1920s. Hong Kong singer Denise Ho also joins in on vocals, bringing the temple myths and Taiwan's history together in heavy metal.

海上孤塔裡的七間房

來源：Bengtskär Lighthouse

在芬蘭西南方的群島上，有一座51公尺高的燈塔，叫Bengtskär。它在1906年完工，當時是北歐最高的燈塔。1941年，這裡發生過海戰，牆上到今天還看得到砲彈的痕跡。

1968年，燈塔改成自動，守塔人離開，房子荒廢了二十多年。1992年，一所大學接手修復，三年後重新開放。現在舊宿舍只有七間客房，要從Hanko搭船才到得了，很適合安靜的蜜月。

On an archipelago southwest of Finland stands Bengtskär, a 51-meter lighthouse. It was completed in 1906, when it was the tallest lighthouse in Northern Europe. In 1941, a naval battle was fought here, and traces of shell damage can still be seen on the walls today. In 1968, the lighthouse was automated, the keepers left, and the buildings sat empty for more than twenty years. In 1992, a university took on the restoration, and the site reopened three years later. Today the old keepers' quarters hold just seven guest rooms. You have to take a boat from Hanko to get there, which makes it a quiet place for a honeymoon.

皮革手作的第一個晚上

來源：Chicago School of Shoemaking

10月26日星期一晚上七點到九點，位在Ravenswood大道的芝加哥製鞋學校開一堂「皮革入門課」。不需要任何經驗，學費72美元，工具和材料都由學校準備。

兩個小時裡，你會學到怎麼切皮、釘鉚釘（固定皮革的小金屬釘）、裝扣子和鞋帶孔。學校在2011年由製鞋師傅Sara McIntosh創立，她從1974年就開始做鞋。下課時，你會帶著親手做的皮件回家。

On Monday, October 26, from 7 to 9 p.m., the Chicago School of Shoemaking on Ravenswood Avenue is offering an introductory leather class. No experience is needed. Tuition is $72, and the school supplies all the tools and materials. Over the two hours, you will learn how to cut leather, set rivets (small metal studs that hold leather pieces together), and attach buttons and shoelace eyelets. The school was founded in 2011 by shoemaker Sara McIntosh, who has been making shoes since 1974. When class ends, you will take home a leather piece you made with your own hands.

一位鷹獵師的網上百科

來源：The Modern Apprentice

「The Modern Apprentice」是鷹獵大師Lydia Ash親手做的網站。鷹獵就是訓練老鷹幫人打獵。她和兩位鷹獵朋友一起寫，沒有任何機構支持。

網站像一套小課本：有古老用語的解釋、各種鳥的介紹、工具和繩結的教學。歷史頁一路追到西元前722年到705年的亞述（古代西亞的國家）石雕，上面就刻著帶鷹打獵的人。中文維基百科也引用過它。

"The Modern Apprentice" is a website built by falconry master Lydia Ash with her own hands. Falconry means training hawks to hunt on people's behalf. She writes it together with two falconer friends, without backing from any institution. The site works like a small textbook: it explains old falconry terms, introduces many kinds of birds, and teaches tools and knots. Its history section reaches back to Assyrian stone carvings from 722 to 705 BCE (Assyria was an ancient kingdom in West Asia), and one of them shows a man hunting with a hawk. Chinese Wikipedia has even cited the site.
