電線接出的聲音：台灣模組合成器卡帶

來源：ato.archives / Tobira Records

東京的小唱片公司 ato.archives 出了一卷卡帶《Pulses on the Horizon》，收了九首台灣「模組合成器」（用電線接來接去做出聲音的電子樂器）的作品。

這卷卡帶由住在台灣的音樂家 Ryan J Raffa 整理。藝術家 weiii 的〈Veil〉用電線一條條接出聲音；HISS 的〈Whispers of the Sands〉像細沙在耳邊流動。

這些音樂都是一次接好就錄，錄完不再加東西。它們告訴我們：台灣有自己的電子聲音，不只是學柏林或東京。

Tokyo's small label ato.archives has released a cassette, Pulses on the Horizon, featuring nine works from Taiwan's "modular synthesizers," electronic instruments that make sound through cables patched together. The tape was compiled by Ryan J Raffa, a musician based in Taiwan. Artist weiii's "Veil" builds its sound one cable at a time, while HISS's "Whispers of the Sands" drifts past the ear like fine sand. Each piece was patched once and recorded straight through, with nothing added afterward. Together they show that Taiwan has an electronic sound of its own, not just an echo of Berlin or Tokyo.

雪國的探戈：芬蘭人的慢舞情歌

來源：Wikipedia / Christian Science Monitor / Tangomarkkinat

探戈來自阿根廷，但芬蘭人也愛它。1913年，一對丹麥舞者在赫爾辛基的飯店跳了探戈，芬蘭人從此愛上這種舞。

到了1930年代，芬蘭探戈變得更慢、更悲傷，唱的是想念和冬天的孤單。二次大戰後，鄉下到處蓋起戶外舞台，大家在夏夜裡一起跳舞。

從1985年起，每年七月，小鎮塞伊奈約基都會辦探戈節，吸引十萬多人，還會選出當年的「探戈國王」和「探戈皇后」。

Tango came from Argentina, but Finns love it too. In 1913, a pair of Danish dancers performed the tango at a hotel in Helsinki, and Finns fell in love with the dance from that moment on. By the 1930s, Finnish tango had grown slower and more melancholy, its lyrics about longing and the loneliness of winter. After World War II, outdoor dance stages sprang up across the countryside, and people gathered to dance together on summer nights. Since 1985, every July, the small town of Seinäjoki has hosted a tango festival that draws more than 100,000 people, and it crowns each year's "Tango King" and "Tango Queen."

在圖書館門口看星星

來源：Time Out Chicago

10月19日星期一晚上6點到8點，阿德勒天文館（Adler Planetarium）的天文學家會把望遠鏡搬到芝加哥公共圖書館 Portage-Cragin 分館的門外。

這個活動叫「Scopes in the City」，不用買票，也不用報名，走過去就能看月亮和星星。之後還有兩場：11月2日在 Obama 分館，11月16日在 Bucktown-Wicker Park 分館。

要記得：如果天氣不好，活動可能會取消。

On Monday, October 19, from 6 to 8 p.m., astronomers from the Adler Planetarium will set up their telescopes outside the Portage-Cragin branch of the Chicago Public Library. The event, called "Scopes in the City," requires no tickets and no registration. Just walk up and look at the moon and stars. Two more sessions follow: November 2 at the Obama branch, and November 16 at the Bucktown-Wicker Park branch. Keep in mind that if the weather is bad, the event may be canceled.

新字獵人：一個人追了二十七年的英文新詞

來源：Word Spy (wordspy.com)

從1998年開始，住在多倫多的作家 Paul McFedries 一個人經營網站 Word Spy，專門收集英文新字，像「doomscrolling」（一直滑手機看壞消息）和「hangry」（餓到生氣）。

他的規定很嚴格：一個字出現不能超過二十年，而且過去一年要在不同地方出現至少三次，才算「新字」。每個字都寫上意思、最早在哪裡出現，還有真實的例句。

這個網站到今天還是免費的，像一本會長大的英文字典。

Since 1998, Toronto-based writer Paul McFedries has run the website Word Spy on his own, collecting new English words such as "doomscrolling" (endlessly scrolling through bad news) and "hangry" (angry because you are hungry). His rules are strict: a word must be no more than twenty years old, and it must have shown up in at least three different places over the past year to count as "new." Each entry gives a meaning, the earliest known use, and real example sentences. The site is still free today, like a dictionary of English that keeps growing.
