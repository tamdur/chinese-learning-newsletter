洗衣盆當貝斯，嗩吶闖進爵士樂

來源：Wikipedia / Onion Design Associates

二〇〇二年，一群住在台北的美國人和英國人組成「泥灘地浪人」樂團。他們的專輯《擒虎記》，靈感來自一九一七年的美國爵士老歌。

樂團找來廟會出身的樂手，吹嗩吶（很響亮的傳統吹管樂器）和笙，讓美國南方藍調和台灣廟口的熱鬧撞在一起。有人乾脆用洗衣盆當貝斯。

專輯外盒做成道教符咒（廟裡求平安的紙符）的樣子，二〇二〇年入圍第六十二屆葛萊美獎最佳唱片包裝。

In 2002, a group of Americans and Britons living in Taipei formed a band called The Muddy Basin Ramblers. Their album Hold That Tiger took its inspiration from a 1917 American jazz standard. The band brought in musicians who came up through temple festivals to play the suona — a piercingly loud traditional wind instrument — and the sheng, letting American Delta blues collide with the clamor of a Taiwanese temple courtyard. One member simply used a wash basin as a bass. The album's packaging was made to look like a Taoist talisman, the paper charm people get at temples for protection, and in 2020 it was nominated for Best Recording Package at the 62nd Grammy Awards.

為一場沒有舉行的奧運，蓋了一座塔

來源：thisisFINLAND

赫爾辛基的奧林匹克體育場，本來要辦一九四〇年奧運，卻因為第二次世界大戰取消。十二年後，芬蘭才在一九五二年真的辦成。

場外站著跑者努爾米的銅像，他拿過九面奧運金牌。這座一九二五年完成的裸體銅像，當年被嫌太不雅，先在美術館裡放了二十七年才搬出來。

體育場的塔高七十二公尺，冬天也開放。三月上去，可以看見結冰的港口，而且不必人擠人。

Helsinki's Olympic Stadium was originally built to host the 1940 Olympics, but the Games were cancelled because of the Second World War. Twelve years later, in 1952, Finland finally got to hold them. Outside the stadium stands a bronze statue of the runner Paavo Nurmi, who won nine Olympic gold medals. Finished in 1925, the nude bronze was considered too indecent at the time and sat inside an art museum for twenty-seven years before being moved outdoors. The stadium's tower is seventy-two meters tall and stays open in winter. Go up in March and you can see the frozen harbor — without the crowds.

開鎖不是為了偷東西，是為了解謎

來源：Pumping Station: One / TOOOL Chicago

芝加哥北愛爾斯頓大道三五一九號，有一間叫「一號抽水站」的自造空間。每個月第一個星期三晚上八點，一群開鎖同好會在這裡聚會。

他們把喇叭鎖、掛鎖都當成謎題，比誰先打開，不碰任何違法的事。

新手第一次來有免費教學，沒工具也可以借。不用會員，不用報名，走進去就好。下一場是二〇二六年九月二日。

At 3519 North Elston Avenue in Chicago there is a makerspace called Pumping Station: One. On the first Wednesday of every month at 8 p.m., a group of lock-picking enthusiasts gathers there. They treat knob locks and padlocks as puzzles, racing to see who opens them first — nothing illegal involved. First-time beginners get a free lesson, and tools are available to borrow if you don't own any. No membership, no registration — just walk in. The next meetup is September 2, 2026.

八千五百面公路標誌的網路博物館

來源：AARoads Shield Gallery

一九九八年秋天，一個愛看公路的年輕人尼茲曼，開始做網頁收集公路標誌。隔年變成「路標畫廊」，後來和另一位同好的網站合併，二〇〇〇年成立 AARoads。

現在這裡收了超過八千五百面盾牌形的路牌：州際公路、國道、州道、郡道，還有加拿大、澳洲、墨西哥和紐西蘭的標誌，全部來自一萬多張照片。

沒有廣告，只有一群人二十幾年的喜歡。

In the autumn of 1998, a young highway enthusiast named Nitzman started building web pages to collect road signs. The following year it became the "Route Sign Gallery," and later merged with another hobbyist's site to become AARoads in 2000. Today it holds more than 8,500 shield-shaped route markers — Interstate highways, U.S. routes, state roads, county roads, plus signs from Canada, Australia, Mexico and New Zealand — all drawn from over 10,000 photographs. No advertising, just a group of people who have loved this for more than twenty years.
