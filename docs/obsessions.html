被禁的抓狂歌：一九八九年的台語搖滾

來源：Wikipedia / KKBOX

一九八九年十一月七日，黑名單工作室推出專輯《抓狂歌》。這張唱片做了五年，把台語唸唱（饒舌）、藍調和搖滾混在一起，〈台北帝國〉、〈民主阿草〉直接笑罵當時的政治。

那時候審查歌曲的制度還在，電台和電視都不敢播，它只能在地下流傳。後來大家才明白，它開啟了「新台語歌」運動。王明輝、陳明章、林暐哲日後都成了重要的音樂人，第三十屆金曲獎也頒給他們特別貢獻獎。

On November 7, 1989, Blacklist Studio released the album Songs of Madness (抓狂歌). After five years in the making, the record blended Taiwanese-language rap, blues and rock. Tracks like “Taipei Empire” and “Democracy A-Chao” openly mocked the politics of the day.

Song censorship was still in force then — radio and television would not touch it, so it spread entirely underground. Only later did people recognise that it had launched the “New Taiwanese Song” movement. Wang Ming-hui, Chen Ming-chang and Lin Wei-che all went on to become major figures in music, and the 30th Golden Melody Awards gave them a Special Contribution Award.

藍色時刻：芬蘭冬天最溫柔的光

來源：Reissu Ruoti

芬蘭人給冬天的一段光取了名字：sininen hetki，「藍色時刻」。太陽還躲在地平線下面，照過來的光大多是最短的那一種，整片天地就被染成淡藍色。它從十一月底一直出現到四月，三月正好在季節裡。

東邊的塞馬湖和中部的派揚內湖最有名，結冰的湖面平得像鏡子，把藍光變成兩倍。芬蘭人習慣在這個時候，在家裡的窗邊點上蠟燭，陪天空慢慢暗下來。

The Finns have a name for one stretch of winter light: sininen hetki, the “blue moment.” The sun is still hidden below the horizon, so most of the light that reaches you is of the shortest wavelengths, and the whole landscape is washed pale blue. It appears from late November all the way through April — March falls squarely inside the season.

Lake Saimaa in the east and Lake Päijänne in the centre of the country are the most celebrated spots: the frozen surfaces lie flat as mirrors and double the blue glow. Finns traditionally light candles in their windows at this hour, keeping the sky company as it slowly goes dark.

沙特爾的地板，就在密西根大道旁邊

來源：Fourth Presbyterian Church

第四長老教會的葛拉茲中心二樓，布坎南禮拜堂的地上鋪著一個大圖案。它照著法國沙特爾大教堂大約一二二〇年的石頭地板做成：一條彎彎曲曲的路，沒有岔路，也不會走錯。

走的人慢慢往中間的玫瑰花圖形前進，再照原路走回來，當作一種安靜的練習。每週二到週四下午一點到三點開放，免費，不管信什麼都可以來。入口在東栗街（126 E. Chestnut St.），去之前先問一下櫃台，因為有時候會有告別式。

On the second floor of the Gratz Center at Fourth Presbyterian Church, the floor of Buchanan Chapel carries a large pattern. It is modelled on the stone floor laid into Chartres Cathedral in France around 1220: a single winding path, with no branches and no wrong turns.

Walkers move slowly toward the rosette at the centre, then retrace the same path back out — a form of quiet practice. It is open Tuesdays through Thursdays, 1 to 3 p.m., free, and open to anyone whatever they believe. The entrance is at 126 E. Chestnut St.; check with the receptionist before you go, since memorial services sometimes take the room.

一個人蓋的煉金術大教堂

來源：alchemywebsite.com

一九九五年起，蘇格蘭學者麥克林（Adam McLean）一個人打造了網路上最完整的煉金術（古人想把金屬變成黃金的學問）資料庫：兩千五百多頁，收了兩百五十多本完整的古書和五百多張圖片。

不少插圖是他自己一張張上色的，包括十七世紀的《亞特蘭妲奔逃》，那本書把煉金術的比喻配上真正的賦格音樂。他早在一九七八年就辦了《赫密士期刊》，比很多大學開始數位化這些古書還早很多年。

Since 1995, the Scottish scholar Adam McLean has single-handedly built the most complete alchemy archive on the internet — more than 2,500 pages, holding over 250 complete early books and more than 500 images.

He hand-coloured many of the illustrations himself, one at a time, including those from the seventeenth-century Atalanta Fugiens, a book that set alchemical allegory to real fugal music. He had founded the Hermetic Journal as early as 1978, many years before most universities began digitising these old texts.
