盲眼歌手的月琴聲

來源：中央通訊社

一九六七年，兩位音樂學者到屏東恆春找老歌，遇見六十一歲的街頭歌手陳達。他一隻眼睛看不見，也不識字，卻能一邊彈月琴（一種圓形的琴），一邊唱出自己編的歌。

一九七一年，他的歌被錄成唱片《民族樂手：陳達和他的歌》。最有名的一首是〈思想起〉，歌聲粗粗的，像在路邊哭著說故事。

一九七八年，雲門舞集的舞作《薪傳》（意思是代代相傳）用了他的歌聲，他的聲音從此成了台灣的聲音。

In 1967, two music scholars traveled to Hengchun in Pingtung to look for old folk songs and met Chen Da, a 61-year-old street singer. He was blind in one eye and could not read, yet he could play the yueqin (a round, moon-shaped lute) and sing songs he had composed himself. In 1971, his songs were released on the record Folk Musicians: Chen Da and His Songs. The most famous of them, "Sixiangqi" (思想起, "Thinking Back"), has a rough, gravelly voice, like someone weeping by the roadside while telling a story. In 1978, Cloud Gate Dance Theatre's work Legacy (薪傳, meaning "handing down from generation to generation") used his singing, and from then on his voice became the voice of Taiwan.

一張藍色的巧克力紙

來源：myHelsinki

一八九一年，Karl Fazer 在赫爾辛基市中心開了一家點心店和咖啡館。一百多年後，這家店還開著，客人可以坐下來喝咖啡、吃甜點。

一九二二年，Fazer 公司推出牛奶巧克力，包裝紙是很特別的深藍色。二〇〇一年，這個藍色成為芬蘭第一個註冊（向政府登記）的顏色商標，別的公司不能隨便拿來用。

來赫爾辛基度蜜月，不妨走進這家老店，嚐一口藍色包裝裡的甜。

In 1891, Karl Fazer opened a confectionery shop and café in the center of Helsinki. More than a century later, the shop is still open, and customers can sit down for a cup of coffee and a dessert. In 1922, Fazer launched its milk chocolate, wrapped in a distinctive deep blue paper. In 2001, that blue became the first color in Finland to be registered as a trademark (officially recorded with the government), so other companies could not simply use it. If you are honeymooning in Helsinki, consider stepping into this old shop and tasting a little of the sweetness wrapped in blue.

芝加哥的黑暗散步

來源：Choose Chicago

十月二十三日和二十四日下午四點，「Haunted by Our Dark Side」導覽（有人帶路介紹）將帶大家走過市中心約三公里的路。每場只收二十人，票價三十五美元。

其中一站是 Congress Plaza 飯店，傳說它的四四一號房常常鬧鬼。導遊也會講兩場大災難：一九〇三年 Iroquois 劇院大火，死了六百零二人；一九一五年 Eastland 號遊船在芝加哥河翻覆（翻過來沉下去），八百四十四人死亡。

On October 23 and 24 at 4:00 p.m., the "Haunted by Our Dark Side" guided walk will take visitors on a roughly three-kilometer route through downtown Chicago. Each tour is limited to twenty people and costs $35. One stop is the Congress Plaza Hotel, where legend has it that room 441 is often haunted. The guide will also tell two stories of great disasters: the 1903 Iroquois Theatre fire, which killed 602 people, and the 1915 Eastland disaster, when the excursion steamer rolled over and sank in the Chicago River, killing 844 people.

寫給未來自己的信

來源：FutureMe.org

二〇〇二年左右，Matt Sly 想起小時候老師要學生寫信給未來的自己，就和朋友 Jay Patrikios 做了一個網站：FutureMe。

在這裡，你可以寫一封電子信，選一天寄給未來的自己，最短三十天，最長可以等好幾十年。如果把信設成「公開」，陌生人也能讀到，但看不到你的名字。

二十多年來，這個小網站沒有大公司幫忙，卻已送出幾千萬封信。讀著別人十年前的願望，好像那封信是寫給你的。

Around 2002, Matt Sly remembered how his teachers had students write letters to their future selves when he was a kid. Together with his friend Jay Patrikios, he built a website called FutureMe. There, you can write an electronic letter, choose a day to have it sent to your future self, and pick a delivery date at least thirty days away, or wait several decades if you like. If you mark the letter "public," strangers can read it too, though they won't see your name. For more than twenty years, this small website has run without help from any big company and has delivered tens of millions of letters. Reading what someone else wished for ten years ago, you feel as though the letter was written to you.
