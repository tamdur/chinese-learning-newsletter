唱給傷口聽的金屬樂

來源：Crescent Lament（恆月三途）Bandcamp / Taipei Times

台北樂團「恆月三途」成立於2007年。主唱周慕姿白天是心理師（幫人照顧心情的專家），還寫過一本很多人讀的書；晚上，她把二胡的旋律放進重金屬的吉他聲裡。

2020年的專輯《噤夢》（噤：不敢出聲）有十首歌，唱的是二二八事件和白色恐怖，其中一首寫的是戰爭剛結束時的基隆港。

周慕姿說，音樂和她的工作做的是同一件事：讓傷口慢慢好起來。

Taipei band Hengyue Santu (恆月三途) was formed in 2007. Its lead vocalist, Chou Mu-tzu, works as a psychologist by day, helping people look after their emotional well-being, and has written a book that many readers have picked up. By night, she brings the melodies of the erhu (a two-stringed Chinese fiddle) into the guitar roar of heavy metal. The band's 2020 album, Silenced Dreams (噤夢, where 噤 means "not daring to make a sound"), has ten songs about the 228 Incident and the White Terror. One of them is set at Keelung harbor just after the war ended. Chou says that music and her work do the same thing: they help wounds slowly heal.

狐狸點亮的北方天空

來源：Wikipedia — Firefox (mythology)

芬蘭人把北極光叫做「revontulet」，意思是「狐狸的火」。傳說有一隻火狐狸跑過拉普蘭的雪地，跑得太快，尾巴把火花掃上了天空。

住在北方的薩米人卻有另一種說法：那些光是死去的人的靈魂。老人提醒大家，看到極光時不要吹口哨、唱歌或揮手，免得惹靈魂生氣。

三月去拉普蘭，你會聽到當地人每天都還這樣叫極光。

Finns call the northern lights "revontulet," which means "fox fire." According to legend, a fire fox ran across the snow of Lapland so fast that its tail swept sparks up into the sky. The Sámi, who live in the far north, tell a different story: the lights are the souls of the dead. Elders warn that when you see the aurora, you should not whistle, sing, or wave, or you may anger the souls. Travel to Lapland in March and you will still hear locals speaking of the aurora this way every day.

修道院裡的拉丁晚禱

來源：Monastery of the Holy Cross (chicagomonk.org)

芝加哥橋港區（Bridgeport）有一座小小的「聖十字修道院」。每年「基督君王節」前一天，修士們會打開大門，請大家免費參加用拉丁文唱的晚禱（晚上的祈禱）。

修士們唱古老的葛利果聖歌，合唱團 Schola Laudis 則唱四百多年前的多聲部歌曲。小教堂裡充滿了香的氣味。

照往年的習慣，今年應該在11月21日星期六下午5:15。不過日期還沒公布，出門前請先上 chicagomonk.org 確認。

On Chicago's Bridgeport side, there is a small place called Holy Cross Monastery. On the day before the Feast of Christ the King, the monks open their doors and invite everyone to attend Vespers, the evening prayer sung in Latin, free of charge. The monks chant ancient Gregorian chant, and the choir Schola Laudis performs polyphonic music more than four hundred years old. The small chapel fills with the scent of incense.

Following past custom, this year's service should be held on Saturday, November 21, at 5:15 in the afternoon. The date has not yet been announced, though, so please check chicagomonk.org before you head out.

一個人的老辦公室博物館

來源：Early Office Museum (officemuseum.com)

「早期辦公室博物館」是一個網站，由一位收藏家自己經營。這裡只收1920年以前的辦公用品：打字機、加法機、削鉛筆機、紙夾，每一件都有老照片和詳細的歷史說明。

你會在這裡讀到很特別的小故事。例如1855年，緬因州的 Walter Kittredge Foster 拿到了美國第一個削鉛筆機的專利（政府給發明人的保護）。

館主到現在還在請大家捐出老東西：只要是1920年前做的、輕於25磅的機器，他都歡迎。

The Early Office Museum is a website run by a single collector. It features only office equipment made before 1920: typewriters, adding machines, pencil sharpeners, and paper clips. Each item comes with old photographs and detailed historical notes, and the site is full of charming little stories. For example, in 1855 Walter Kittredge Foster of Maine received the first U.S. patent for a pencil sharpener, a government-granted protection for an inventor. The curator is still looking for donations of old items. As long as a machine was made before 1920 and weighs less than 25 pounds, it is welcome.
