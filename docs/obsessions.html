島嶼的聲音：林強的錄音，交給世界改寫

來源：Pure Person Press (Bandcamp)

林強是台灣有名的電子音樂人，電影《千禧曼波》的配樂就是他做的。二零二零到二零二一年，他帶著錄音機走遍台灣，錄下廟會、夜市、家人說話，還有山上的風聲。

這些聲音沒有直接發行。二零二五年十一月，唱片公司 Pure Person Press 找來 CFCF、Point Hsu 等音樂人，用這些錄音寫成八首新曲。其中一首改編自 CFCF 的〈Life is Perfecto〉，連林強自己的聲音也織進了歌裡。

Lin Qiang is a renowned Taiwanese electronic music producer, known for composing the soundtrack to the film "Millennium Mambo." From 2020 to 2021, he traveled across Taiwan with a recording device, capturing the sounds of temple festivals, night markets, family conversations, and wind whistling through the mountains. These recordings were not initially released commercially. In November 2025, record label Pure Person Press enlisted musicians including CFCF and Point Hsu to compose eight new songs from these recordings. One of the pieces is an adaptation of CFCF's "Life is Perfecto," and it weaves in Lin Qiang's own voice as well.

三月的馴鹿：頭上有角的，其實是媽媽

來源：StayLapland

公馴鹿在十一、十二月交配季一結束，就會把角掉光。懷孕的母鹿卻會把角留到四、五月。

因為冬天最難過的那幾個月，牠要用角挖開結硬的雪，找底下的地衣（一種貼在地上長的小植物）來吃，也用角守住自己的食物。

所以三月在拉普蘭坐雪橇（鹿拉的車）時，看到頭上還有大角的，幾乎都是快當媽媽的母鹿，和魯道夫的故事正好相反。當地的薩米人很早就用這個方法認鹿。

Male reindeer drop their antlers shortly after mating season ends in November and December. But pregnant females keep theirs until April or May. In winter's harshest months, they need their antlers to break through frozen snow and find lichen—a low-growing plant—to eat, and to defend their food. So in March, when riding a reindeer sleigh in Lapland, the animals with large antlers are almost always expectant mothers—the opposite of the Rudolph story. The local Sami people have long used this knowledge to identify reindeer.

地下室裡的煤礦山：四百公尺長的小火車

來源：Garfield-Clarendon Model Railroad Club

芝加哥北邊 Uptown 的 Clarendon 公園活動中心，地下室藏著一個一九四七年成立的模型火車俱樂部。每個星期五晚上七點到九點，大門打開，不用預約，敲門就能進去看，想捐錢再捐。

裡面的鐵道超過四百公尺長，做的是二十世紀中期美國東部的產煤山區。每一根枕木（鐵軌下面那排小木條），都是會員把舊木門一根一根切出來，再釘上去的。

In the basement of Clarendon Park Recreation Center in Uptown, north of Chicago, sits a model train club founded in 1947. Every Friday evening from 7 to 9 p.m., the doors open to the public—no reservation required, just knock to come in and look around. Donations are welcome if you'd like to contribute. Inside, the track stretches over 400 meters long, recreating a coal-mining mountain region of mid-20th-century eastern America. Every single railroad tie—those small wooden pieces running beneath the tracks—was cut by club members from old wooden doors, piece by piece, and then carefully nailed into place.

一個人的埃及王表：從亞歷山大到最後的女王

來源：The Egyptian Royal Genealogy Project

二零零一年，自學出身的埃及研究者 Chris Bennett 開始做一個網站，把托勒密王朝的家族關係和在位年份一筆一筆排清楚。那是亞歷山大大帝之後統治埃及將近三百年的希臘王室，最後一位就是有名的女王 Cleopatra。

他比對石碑上的刻字、古老的紙上文件和錢幣上的日期，連學者吵不清的共治（兩個國王同時在位）問題都理出答案，正式的學術論文也引用他。

Bennett 二零一四年過世，網站卻被喜歡它的人一次次搬家，到今天還在。

In 2001, self-taught Egyptian researcher Chris Bennett created a website to systematically map out the family relationships and reign dates of the Ptolemaic dynasty. This Greek royal house ruled Egypt for nearly three centuries following Alexander the Great, with its last ruler being the famous queen Cleopatra. By comparing inscriptions on stone monuments, ancient papyrus documents, and dates on coins, Bennett even resolved scholarly disputes about co-regency—periods when two kings reigned simultaneously. His work was cited in formal academic papers. Though Bennett died in 2014, the website was preserved by admirers who migrated it repeatedly from server to server. It remains online today.
