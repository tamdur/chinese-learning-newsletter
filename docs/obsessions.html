先聽見，才看見：蟬的第一張專輯

來源：VERSE

台灣樂團 Cicada 的名字意思是「蟬」。蟬總是先被人聽見，才被人看見。四位團員都學過古典音樂，2011年發行第一張專輯《散落的時光》。

整張專輯沒有人唱歌，只有小提琴、大提琴、鋼琴和木吉他。九首曲子，說的是回想一段過去的感情。

這張專輯停賣多年，2020年 White Wabbit 唱片重新推出，只做350張純白色的黑膠唱片。後來，他們把台灣的海岸、鯨魚和山林都寫進音樂裡，一切都從這裡開始。

Taiwan's band Cicada takes its name from the insect that is always heard before it is seen. All four members studied classical music, and in 2011 they released their debut album, Scattered Time. The entire album contains no vocals—only violin, cello, piano, and acoustic guitar. The nine pieces tell the story of remembering a past relationship. Out of print for years, the album was reissued in 2020 by White Wabbit Records as just 350 pure white vinyl records. Later, they wove Taiwan's coastlines, whales, and forests into their music, and that is where everything began.

赫爾辛基的百年電車

來源：Helsinki Tram Museum

在赫爾辛基的托洛區，有一座1900年蓋的電車車庫，現在是電車博物館。裡面放著六輛老電車，最老的一輛是用馬拉的。

還有一輛德國做的電車，城市剛開始用電的時候，它就在街上跑了。另一輛在1952年奧運時，載過去看比賽的人。

館裡還有模擬器（像真的一樣的機器），你可以坐上司機的位子，「開」過奧運時和戰爭時的街道。三月外面很冷，這裡溫暖又安靜。

In the Töölö district of Helsinki stands a tram depot dating back to 1900, now serving as a tram museum. Inside, six vintage trams are on display, with the oldest being horse-drawn. There's also a German-made tram that began running the city's streets when Helsinki first switched to electric power. Another carried Olympic spectators during the 1952 Games. The museum features simulators—remarkably lifelike machines where you can take the driver's seat and 'operate' a tram through streets as they appeared during the Olympics and wartime. Outside, March brings frigid temperatures, but inside here it's warm and peaceful.

一枝花的線條

來源：Ikebana International Chicago Chapter #27

10月24日星期六，國際插花協會芝加哥分會在帕拉廷的哈珀學院辦活動，請來花藝老師艾琳·韋伯。

日本插花不像西方的花束那樣圓圓滿滿，而是看重線條。左右不一樣高，還要留下空白，讓每一枝花都被看見。

老師會先現場示範，再讓大家自己動手插一盆花。這個活動開放給所有人參加。

On Saturday, October 24, the Chicago branch of the International Flower Arrangement Association is hosting an event at Harper College in Palatine, featuring floral artist Erin Weber. Japanese flower arrangement differs from Western bouquets in that it emphasizes line rather than fullness. Rather than creating a perfectly rounded arrangement, the design plays with asymmetrical heights and deliberately leaves empty space—allowing each individual flower to be seen and appreciated. Weber will begin with a live demonstration, then invite participants to arrange their own vase of flowers. The event is open to anyone interested in attending.

把鳥的歌寫成樂譜的人

來源：davidhindley.org

大衛·欣德利（1933-2018）是英國劍橋的音樂老師。1985年他提早退休，把後半生都用來做一件事：把鳥叫聲寫成樂譜。

他最愛雲雀，那像流水一樣停不下來的歌聲。他發明了「雙面樂譜」，名字來自有兩張臉的羅馬神雅努斯：一面記下真的鳥聲，一面讓電腦照著譜「唱」出鳥叫。

他還用這個方法，讓紐西蘭已經滅絕（完全消失）的垂耳鴉再次「唱歌」。現在，他的女兒把他的樂譜和文章都放在網站上，大家都能看。

David Hindley (1933–2018) was a music teacher at Cambridge University in England. In 1985, he retired early and devoted the rest of his life to one task: transcribing bird songs into musical notation. His favorite was the lark, with its endless, water-like song. He invented the "two-faced score," named after Janus, the Roman god with two faces: one side recorded the actual bird calls, while the other allowed a computer to "sing" the bird sounds by following the notation. He even used this method to resurrect the song of New Zealand's huia, a species that has since gone extinct. Now his daughter has made all his scores and articles publicly available on a website.
