木偶的搖滾史詩

來源：聯合新聞網部落格

霹靂布袋戲（把手伸進木偶裡操作的戲）不只會演戲，還會出唱片。從一九八〇年代末開始，霹靂公司推出《霹靂英雄音樂精選》，到二〇二二年已經出到第九十二集。

每個木偶角色都有自己的主題音樂。作曲家把重金屬電吉他、管弦樂，和南管、北管等傳統戲曲的聲音混在一起，聽起來就像一場搖滾歌劇。

一九八八年登場的素還真，他的主題音樂幾十年來一再被重新編曲。一張木頭刻成的臉，竟然帶出了一整套跨越三十多年的音樂故事。

Pili glove puppetry (in which the performer slips a hand inside each puppet to work it) does more than stage shows. It also puts out records. Starting in the late 1980s, the Pili company released the Pili Heroes Music Selections series, and by 2022 it had reached its 92nd volume. Every puppet character has its own theme music. The composers mix heavy-metal electric guitar and orchestral strings with the sounds of traditional opera styles such as nanguan and beiguan, so the result sounds like a rock opera. Su Huanzhen, who debuted in 1988, has had his theme rearranged again and again over the decades. A face carved from wood has, somehow, given rise to a whole body of music spanning more than thirty years.

在冰上踢著走

來源：This is Finland

在芬蘭，以前冬天的路還沒人鏟雪，大家就靠「踢雪橇」（在雪上滑的小車）出門。它有兩條長長的鐵條，人站在後面，用一隻腳踢冰面往前滑；前面有座位，傳統上坐在那裡的人可以一邊喝咖啡。

踢雪橇還有世界大賽。二〇二六年二月，選手就在凱烏魯一座結冰的大湖上比賽。

三月來度蜜月，可以到湖區的林南薩里國家公園，跟著嚮導（帶路的人）在冰上踢雪橇，又輕鬆又好玩。

In Finland, before anyone had cleared the winter roads of snow, people got around by "kicking sleds," small sleds that glide over the snow. They have two long iron runners. The rider stands at the back and pushes off the ice with one foot to move forward. There is a seat at the front, and traditionally the person sitting there could enjoy a cup of coffee along the way. Kick sledding even has a world championship. In February 2026, competitors raced on a frozen lake in Keuruu. For a honeymoon in March, you can visit Linnansaari National Park in the lake district and, led by a guide, go kick sledding across the ice. It is both relaxing and fun.

怪奇秀裡的科學課

來源：Time Out Chicago

十一月十四日星期六晚上七點，表演者布里頓要在小熊隊球場附近的「星星與吊襪帶」，演出《怪奇秀與說明》。

他會吞火、吞劍，還把釘子敲進自己的鼻子裡。每做完一招，他就停下來講解背後的物理和人體構造，告訴你這不是魔法，而是讓他平安活下來的科學。

這場秀只限二十一歲以上的人，票價大約十五到四十美元，地點在北克拉克街三九一四號。

On Saturday, November 14, at 7 p.m., performer Britton will stage Freakshow & Tell at Stars & Garters, a venue near Wrigley Field. He swallows fire and swords, and hammers nails up his nose. After each stunt, he pauses to explain the physics and human anatomy behind it, telling you this is not magic but the science that keeps him alive. The show is limited to people 21 and older. Tickets cost roughly $15 to $40, and the venue is at 3914 N. Clark Street.

一顆彈珠的身世

來源：MarbleCollecting.com

美國彈珠收藏家協會成立於一九七五年。他們的網站像一座彈珠博物館，有將近四百張圖片教你認出古董彈珠。不知道手上的彈珠是什麼？網站還能免費幫你找到同一州的收藏家來看看。

最精彩的一頁，講的是克里斯坦森瑪瑙（一種有花紋的石頭）公司。它一九二五年在俄亥俄州開業，一九三三年就倒閉了。可是它的彈珠有一種像通了電的彩色漩渦（水轉圈的樣子），別家都做不出來，現在是最貴的彈珠之一。

The American Marble Collectors Society was founded in 1975. Its website works like a marble museum, with nearly 400 pictures to help you identify antique marbles. Not sure what marble you have? The site can also connect you, for free, with collectors in your own state who can take a look. The most fascinating page is about the Christensen Agate Company (agate is a type of patterned stone). It opened in Ohio in 1925 and went out of business as early as 1933. Yet its marbles had a colorful swirl that seemed to crackle with electricity, a look no other maker could reproduce, and today they are among the most valuable marbles in the world.
