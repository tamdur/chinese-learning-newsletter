二胡與重金屬：一位藝旦的紅線

來源：Crescent Lament (Bandcamp) / UniteAsia

Crescent Lament 在2007年成立於台北。他們把二胡、弦樂和重金屬音樂放在一起，不唱奇幻故事，而是唱台灣被忘記的歷史。

2015年的專輯《Elegy for the Blossoms》說的是阿香的故事。她是日本時代的藝旦（陪客人唱歌的女子），被賣掉，不能決定自己的人生。樂團用她來比喻台灣的命運。

2020年的《噤夢》（噤：不敢出聲）接著說她沒有完成的愛情，跟著一條「紅線」走過時間的河。

Crescent Lament was formed in Taipei in 2007. They bring together the erhu, string instruments, and heavy metal music—not to tell fantasy tales, but to sing Taiwan's forgotten histories. Their 2015 album Elegy for the Blossoms centers on the story of Axiang, a courtesan during Japan's colonial period who was sold into service and robbed of agency over her own life. The band uses her story as a metaphor for Taiwan's fate. Their 2020 album Silenced Dreams continues with her uncompleted love story, tracing a "red thread" through the river of time.

圖爾庫老木屋裡的百年藥局

來源：City of Turku / Pharmacy Museum and Qwensel House

在芬蘭的圖爾庫，有一棟大約1700年蓋的木頭房子，叫做 Qwensel House。它是這座城市最老的木造建築。

屋子裡的藥局櫃台、藥櫃和草藥房都不是假的。它們是1858年的真藥局，1930年代從北方城市奧盧整間買下來，搬到這裡。

現在屋裡有一間咖啡店，賣老式點心。你可以坐在從前藥師調藥的房間裡，慢慢喝一杯咖啡。

Turku, Finland, is home to a wooden house built around 1700 called the Qwensel House — the city's oldest wooden building. The pharmacy counter, medicine cabinets, and herb room inside are entirely authentic, not reconstructions. They date from 1858 and were purchased wholesale from the northern city of Oulu in the 1930s, then transported here. Today, a café operates within the house, offering traditional pastries and sweets. Visitors can sit in the very room where pharmacists once prepared their medicines and slowly enjoy a cup of coffee.

相撲大力士小錦，帶著壽司來芝加哥

來源：Time Out Chicago

10月2日到4日，The Fields Studios（2828 N. Pulaski）有一場「Sumo + Sushi」相撲表演。主持人是小錦，他在夏威夷出生，是第一位升上「大關」（相撲第二高的等級）的外國選手。

真正的職業相撲選手會上場比賽，觀眾一邊看，一邊吃芝加哥餐廳做的便當和壽司。每一場還有六位觀眾可以付錢上台，親自和大力士比一比。

週五晚上8點、週六5點和8點45分、週日下午1點半都有表演。

From October 2-4, The Fields Studios (2828 N. Pulaski) will host a "Sumo + Sushi" sumo performance. The host is Konishiki, who was born in Hawaii and became the first foreign wrestler to achieve Ozeki—sumo's second-highest rank. Real professional sumo wrestlers will compete on stage while audiences watch and enjoy bento and sushi prepared by Chicago restaurants. At each performance, six audience members can also pay for the chance to go on stage and challenge a sumo wrestler themselves. Shows are scheduled for Friday at 8 PM, Saturday at 5 PM and 8:45 PM, and Sunday at 1:30 PM.

火星運河：騙了人們一百年的地圖

來源：The Public Domain Review

1877年，義大利天文學家 Schiaparelli 在火星上畫出一些細線，叫它們「canali」。這個字的意思是「水道」，卻被翻成英文的「運河」，好像是人挖出來的。

美國人 Percival Lowell 相信了這個錯誤。他在亞利桑那州蓋了天文台，畫了很多地圖，說火星人挖了這些河，把南北兩極的冰水送到沙漠城市。

The Public Domain Review 收集了這些老地圖。最有趣的是：Lowell 的助手用同一台望遠鏡，卻從來看不到那些線。

In 1877, Italian astronomer Schiaparelli drew some faint lines on Mars, calling them "canali"—a word meaning "water channels" that was mistranslated into English as "canals," implying human construction. American astronomer Percival Lowell accepted this error as fact. He built an observatory in Arizona and produced numerous maps claiming that Martians had excavated these channels to transport water from the polar ice caps to desert cities. The Public Domain Review has collected these historical maps. What's most fascinating is that Lowell's own assistant, using the same telescope, could never actually see these lines.
