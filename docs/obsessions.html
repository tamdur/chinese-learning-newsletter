五十五歲才出的第一張專輯：胡德夫的《匆匆》

來源：That's Mags

胡德夫是台東的卑南族（Puyuma）歌手，族名 Ara Kimbo。年輕時他發起了「唱自己的歌」運動，又花了三十年為原住民爭取權利。直到五十五歲，他才終於在 2005 年出了第一張專輯《匆匆》。

整張專輯只有鋼琴和他低沉的歌聲，唱的是卑南族和排灣族的古老故事。有人說那聲音像海洋藍調，也像山裡的歌。這張專輯拿下了 2006 年金曲獎最佳年度歌曲和最佳作詞兩項大獎。

Hu De-fu is a Puyuma singer from Taitung, with the tribal name Ara Kimbo. As a young man, he launched the "Sing Your Own Song" movement and spent thirty years fighting for indigenous rights. Not until he was fifty-five did he finally release his debut album, In Haste, in 2005. The entire album features only piano and his deep, resonant voice, singing ancient stories of the Puyuma and Paiwan peoples. Some say that voice sounds like ocean blues; others hear the songs of the mountains. The album won two Golden Melody Awards in 2006: Best Song of the Year and Best Lyrics.

芬蘭鍛刀師復活兩千年前的印度古鋼

來源：Finland, Naturally

在芬蘭西部的考哈瓦（Kauhava）小鎮，有一位自學的鍛刀師叫 Heimo Roselli。他從 1974 年開始打鐵，因為買不到夠好的刀，就自己做。後來他花了好幾年研究兩千年前印度的烏茲鋼（Wootz），最後成功做出了比古鋼更硬、更利的刀刃。

據說他的刀薄到可以浮在水面上。這裡是芬蘭傳統小刀 puukko 的故鄉，旅客可以到當地的鐵匠鋪（打鐵的工作坊）參觀，甚至親手試做一把刀。

In the small town of Kauhava in western Finland, there is a self-taught bladesmith named Heimo Roselli. Starting in 1974, he began forging steel because he couldn't find knives sharp enough to suit his needs, so he made his own. Over the years, he spent considerable time studying Wootz steel from ancient India two thousand years ago, and eventually succeeded in creating blades that were harder and sharper than the ancient steel. His knives are reportedly so thin they can float on water. Kauhava is the birthplace of the traditional Finnish puukko knife, and visitors can tour the local smithies—the blacksmith workshops where blades are forged—and even try their hand at crafting a knife themselves.

在林肯公園賞梅：芝加哥的梅見活動

來源：Japanese Culture Center

4 月 26 日星期天，日本文化中心和林肯公園協會將在 Alfred Caldwell Lily Pool 舉辦免費的「梅見」（賞梅花）活動。這座花園建於 1930 年代，是美國國家歷史地標。

在日本，賞梅的歷史其實比賞櫻更早，氣氛也更安靜、更私密。林肯公園裡的野梅樹正好開花，隨時可以來走走，不用報名。

On Sunday, April 26, the Japanese Cultural Center and Lincoln Park Conservancy will host a free "ume-mi" (plum blossom viewing) event at the Alfred Caldwell Lily Pool. Built in the 1930s, this garden is a U.S. National Historic Landmark. In Japan, the tradition of viewing plum blossoms actually predates cherry blossom viewing and carries a quieter, more intimate atmosphere. The wild plum trees throughout Lincoln Park are in bloom right now, so you can visit anytime without needing to register.

一個人寫的天空百科全書

來源：Atmospheric Optics (atoptics.co.uk)

英國退休物理學家 Les Cowley 在 1970 年代的某個冬天，看到天空出現了一圈冰暈（陽光穿過冰晶形成的光圈），從此就迷上了光和大氣的關係。

他花了幾十年建了一個網站 atoptics.co.uk，收集了彩虹、光暈、綠閃光等幾十種天空光學現象的照片和解說。他還寫了一套模擬（模仿）軟體，可以用數學追蹤光線怎麼穿過冰晶。這是一個人用一輩子的熱情蓋出來的天空博物館。

British retired physicist Les Cowley became captivated by light and atmospheric phenomena one winter in the 1970s when he spotted an ice halo—a luminous ring formed by sunlight passing through ice crystals—in the sky. Over decades, he built a website, atoptics.co.uk, that has become a comprehensive archive of sky optical phenomena: photographs and detailed explanations of rainbows, halos, green flashes, and dozens of other light effects. He also developed simulation software that uses mathematics to trace how light travels through ice crystals. It's a sky museum built over a lifetime of passion by a single person.
