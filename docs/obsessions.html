拼貼出來的歌聲：珂拉琪的科幻神話

來源：鳴人堂 / Apple Music

「珂拉琪」是台北的雙人樂團，團名就是「拼貼」的意思。2022年，他們用第一張專輯《MEmento MORI》拿下金曲獎最佳新人。主唱夏子有客家和阿美族的血統，她把台語、日語和阿美族語一起唱進歌裡。

為了寫科幻故事，有些阿美族語的詞還不存在，夏子就自己造新詞。2024年的新專輯《Deus Ex Machina》有十一首歌，繼續用語言的切換來說性別和族群的故事。他們說：語言不是風格，是每天的生活。

珂拉琪 (Collage) is a two-person band from Taipei — the band name itself means "collage." In 2022, they won Best New Artist at the Golden Melody Awards with their debut album MEmento MORI. Lead singer 夏子 (Natsuko) is of Hakka and Amis heritage, and she weaves Taiwanese, Japanese, and the Amis language together into their songs.

To write science-fiction stories, she found that some Amis words simply didn't exist yet — so she coined new ones herself. Their 2024 album Deus Ex Machina holds eleven tracks that keep using language-switching itself to tell stories about gender and ethnicity. As they put it: language isn't a style. It's daily life.

揹著新娘跑：芬蘭的世界錦標賽

來源：Eukonkanto.fi

芬蘭中部的小鎮松卡耶爾維，從1992年起，每年七月舉辦「揹（背在身上）妻子世界錦標賽」。男生揹著女伴，衝過沙地、水池和欄杆，比誰最快。

比賽來自十九世紀的傳說：強盜羅恩凱寧要手下揹著沉重的袋子練體力。今天的冠軍不拿獎金，而是拿到和妻子體重一樣重的啤酒。被揹的人至少要四十九公斤，太輕就再背一個重背包。

In Sonkajärvi, a small town in central Finland, the Wife-Carrying World Championship has been held every July since 1992. Men carry a female partner through sand pits, water hazards, and over hurdles, racing to see who finishes fastest.

The competition traces back to a 19th-century legend about a bandit named Ronkainen, who had his men carry heavy sacks to build their strength. Today's champion wins no prize money — instead, he receives beer equal to his wife's weight. The person being carried must weigh at least 49 kilograms; if lighter, a weighted backpack makes up the difference.

舞者走進爵士：孟格斯樂章的重生

來源：See Chicago Dance

八月二十五、二十六日兩個晚上，在芝加哥西部大道的小場地 Constellation，編舞家歐祖祖和薩克斯風手沃德帶回舞作《觸碰我愛人的思緒》。

這支舞是照著爵士大師孟格斯1963年的專輯《黑聖人與罪女》編出來的。作品最早在千禧公園首演，還得過「黑人卓越獎」。這次走進小小的店面場地，舞者離現場樂團只有幾步遠。

On the evenings of August 25th and 26th, at Constellation — a small venue on Chicago's Western Avenue — choreographer Onye Ozuzu and saxophonist Greg Ward bring back the dance work Touch My Beloved's Thought.

The piece was choreographed to jazz master Charles Mingus's 1963 album The Black Saint and the Sinner Lady. It first premiered at Millennium Park and went on to win a Black Excellence Award. In this small storefront space, the dancers stand only a few steps from the live band.

一個人的世界鈔票百科

來源：RealBanknotes.com

2008年底，一位只用「Proteus」這個名字的收藏家，開始在網路上做一件事：把全世界的紙鈔一張一張記下來。哪一國、哪一年、哪一個版本、上面是誰簽的名，全都整理得清清楚楚。

這個網站叫 RealBanknotes，完全免費，只靠一點點廣告收入維持。世界各地的同好會寄照片來幫忙補充。十幾年下來，它成了網路上最完整的紙鈔紀錄之一。

In late 2008, a collector known only by the name "Proteus" set out on an ambitious project: documenting the banknotes of the entire world, one at a time. Which country, which year, which version, whose signature is on it — all of it neatly catalogued.

The site, called RealBanknotes, is entirely free, sustained only by a trickle of advertising revenue. Fellow collectors from around the globe send in photographs to help fill the gaps. Over more than a decade, it has become one of the internet's most complete records of paper money.
