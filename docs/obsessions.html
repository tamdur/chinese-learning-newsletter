鹽埕區長的老歌新夢

來源：Refuge Worldwide / NTS Radio

「鹽埕區長」是一九六四年的一首台語歌。歌詞太大膽，被當時的政府禁唱。連真的區長都生氣，說要告唱片公司。

高雄音樂人 Vicki Huang 用這首歌當作自己廠牌（唱片公司）的名字。她把收藏的老唱片轉成數位檔，再加上慢慢的電子節拍，聽起來像一部老台灣電影。

她的第一張十吋黑膠今年在歐洲巡迴，也上了 Angie QQ 的 NTS 電台節目。老歌沒有死，只是睡著了。

"Yanchen District Chief" is a Taiwanese-language song from 1964. Its lyrics were considered too daring, and the government of the day banned it. Even the real district chief was angry and threatened to sue the record company. Kaohsiung musician Vicki Huang named her own record label after the song. She converted her collection of old records into digital files and added slow electronic beats, so the result sounds like an old Taiwanese film. Her first 10-inch vinyl record went on tour in Europe this year, and it also featured on Angie QQ's show on NTS Radio. The old song never died; it was only asleep.

森林屬於每一個人

來源：Yle

在芬蘭，有一條沒寫在法律裡的老規矩，叫「每個人的權利」。任何人都可以走進森林、在湖邊搭帳篷過夜，還能採莓果和蘑菇，不用問地主，也不用付錢。

但權利也有責任：不能吵到住戶和動物，不能折活的樹枝，沒有地主同意也不能生火。用小瓦斯爐煮飯就沒問題。

別人家的院子、軍事區和自然保護區不在這條規矩裡。三月到芬蘭，你們可以自己滑雪，穿過整片安靜的雪林。

In Finland, there is an unwritten old custom called "everyone's right." Anyone may walk into the forest, pitch a tent by a lake and spend the night, and pick berries and mushrooms without asking the landowner or paying anyone. But rights come with responsibilities: you must not disturb residents or animals, you must not snap off living branches, and you may not light a fire without the landowner's consent. Cooking on a small gas stove is fine. Other people's yards, military zones, and nature reserves are not covered by this custom.

If you visit Finland in March, you can ski on your own and glide through an entire stretch of quiet, snowy forest.

星期天的陌生人棋局

來源：US Chess / Rizal Center Chess Club

芝加哥的黎剎（菲律賓的民族英雄）中心，是美國第一個菲律賓裔（祖先來自菲律賓）的西洋棋社。

十月二十五日星期天，他們在 Lakeview 的 1332 W. Irving Park Rd. 舉辦今年第四場公開比賽。早上九點報到，十點開始第一輪。分成公開組和兒童組，有沒有等級分都可以參加。

當天報名三十到五十美元，早點報名少五元，還包午餐和點心。坐下來，和陌生人下一盤棋吧。

Chicago's Rizal Center, named for the Filipino national hero José Rizal, is the first Filipino-American chess club in the United States, founded by people of Philippine ancestry. On Sunday, October 25, the club will host its fourth open tournament of the year at 1332 W. Irving Park Rd. in Lakeview. Check-in is at 9 a.m., and the first round starts at 10. There are open and children's sections, and players with or without a rating are welcome. Entry on the day costs $30 to $50, with $5 off for early registration, and lunch and snacks are included. Pull up a chair and play a game against a stranger.

三十萬張地名小卡

來源：Archif Melville Richards (Bangor University)

威爾斯學者梅爾維爾·理查茲（1910—1973）一生都在做一件事：把威爾斯地名的老寫法，一張一張用手抄在小卡片上。

一座山、一個農場、一條河，名字在幾百年裡怎麼變，都記在卡片上。他過世時，已經存了大約三十萬張。

後來，班戈大學把全部卡片放上網站，免費讓大家查。現在任何人都能看到一個地名在古老文件裡的樣子。

Welsh scholar Melville Richards (1910–1973) spent his life doing one painstaking thing: copying the old spellings of Welsh place names by hand, one at a time, onto small index cards. A mountain, a farm, a river: he recorded how each name had shifted over the centuries, card by card. When he died, he had amassed roughly 300,000 of them. Bangor University later put the entire collection online, free for anyone to search. Today, anyone can see what a place name looked like in old documents.
