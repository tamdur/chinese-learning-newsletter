電子琴裡的台灣四季

來源：FOND/SOUND

一九八七年，作曲家陳揚（一位很會寫歌的音樂家）為雲門舞集（台灣有名的現代舞團）寫了兩首曲子，叫「春」和「夏」。後來他把這兩首當種子，做出了一張專輯，名字是《現代四季》。

他用當時很新的電子琴和機器，做出安靜、乾淨的聲音，像「心洞」和「秋」這些曲子。有趣的是，海的另一邊，日本也有人在做很像的音樂，但陳揚一個人在台灣，可能從來沒聽過他們。

In 1987, composer Chen Yang wrote two pieces for Cloud Gate Dance Theatre—Taiwan's celebrated modern dance company—called "Spring" and "Summer". He later used these works as the foundation for an album titled Modern Four Seasons.

Drawing on the cutting-edge electronic keyboards and machines of that era, he created quiet, crystalline soundscapes in works like "Heart Cave" and "Autumn". Interestingly, across the sea in Japan, musicians were making remarkably similar music at the same time—though Chen Yang, working alone in Taiwan, probably never heard them.

世界最後一艘運麥帆船

來源：Ålands Sjöfartsmuseum

在芬蘭的奧蘭群島（一大片會說瑞典話的島），港口停著一艘大帆船，叫波曼號。它有四根桅杆（掛帆的大柱子），是世界上唯一還保留原本樣子的這種商船。

波曼號在一九〇三年於英國造好。一位奧蘭的船王買下它，讓它在澳洲和歐洲之間比賽運送麥子，還兩次得到冠軍。從赫爾辛基坐船就能到，很適合來看看。

In the Åland Islands of Finland—a Swedish-speaking archipelago—lies a magnificent four-masted sailing ship named the Pommern. It is the world's only merchant vessel of its kind that has preserved its original form.

The Pommern was built in Britain in 1903. An Åland shipowner purchased it and entered it in races transporting wheat between Australia and Europe, winning the championship twice. Easily reached by ship from Helsinki, it's well worth visiting.

賞鳥人的冰淇淋約會

來源：Chicago Ornithological Society

七月二十三日，芝加哥的賞鳥學會要辦一場輕鬆的聚會，地點在 Bartleby's 手工冰淇淋店。大家可以一起吃冰淇淋，聽現場的鄉村音樂。

店裡有很多口味，也有不含麩質（一種麵粉裡的東西）、全素和不含堅果的選擇。這場活動不用錢，也不用先報名。不管你會不會賞鳥，都歡迎來玩。

On July 23rd, the Chicago Bird-Watching Society is hosting a casual gathering at Bartleby's Artisanal Ice Cream Shop. Everyone can enjoy ice cream together and listen to live country music.

The shop offers many flavors and has gluten-free, vegan, and nut-free options. The event is free and requires no advance registration. Whether you're an experienced bird watcher or not, everyone is welcome.

一個人寫下全世界的紙牌遊戲

來源：pagat.com

從一九九五年開始，英國數學家 John McLeod 就一個人在網站 pagat.com 上，記下全世界超過五百種紙牌遊戲的玩法和故事。

有些遊戲很少見，只有他一個人在網路上寫下來。另一位研究紙牌的學者說，這是網路上唯一這麼認真的紙牌網站。他還一起寫了兩大本很專業的塔羅牌遊戲歷史書。

Since 1995, British mathematician John McLeod has single-handedly documented over five hundred card games from around the world on his website, pagat.com, recording both their rules and histories.

For some of these games, he is the only person online to have written about them. According to another scholar who studies card games, pagat.com is the only card game website on the internet that operates with this level of rigor. He has also co-authored two substantial, scholarly books on the history of tarot card games.
