自己跟自己說話的機器

來源：ATTN Magazine

台灣聲音藝術家 Lucia H Chung 住在倫敦，用 en creux 這個名字做音樂。她的方法很特別：不接任何樂器，只把一個吉他效果器和一台混音器（把很多聲音混在一起的機器）互相接起來，讓機器自己發出聲音。

2020年，她在 Falt 唱片出了卡帶《PHASE》。裡面有三段現場錄音，最長的一段超過 20 分鐘，完全沒有剪接。英國雜誌 ATTN 說，這是「此時此地」最直接的紀錄。

Lucia H Chung, a Taiwanese sound artist based in London, makes music under the name en creux. Her method is unusual: she uses no musical instruments at all. Instead, she connects a guitar effects pedal and a mixer (a machine that blends many sounds together) to each other and lets the machines generate sound on their own. In 2020, she released the cassette PHASE on the Falt label. It contains three live recordings, the longest running more than 20 minutes, with no editing at all. The British magazine ATTN called it the most direct record of "the here and now."

岩石底下的游泳池

來源：This is Finland

赫爾辛基在岩石底下蓋了將近 500 個地下空間，可以讓快一百萬人躲進去，比全市 70 萬人口還多。

最有名的是 Itäkeskus 游泳館。平常大家在這裡游泳、洗三溫暖。但是芬蘭法律規定，如果需要，這裡必須能在 72 小時內把水放乾，變成可以住 3800 人的避難所（躲避危險的地方）。

地底下還有球場、教堂和攀岩場。入口的通道故意蓋成彎的，為的是擋住爆炸的力量。

Helsinki has carved nearly 500 underground spaces out of the bedrock, enough to shelter almost one million people, more than the city's population of 700,000.

The best known is the Itäkeskus swimming hall. Ordinary days see people swimming and relaxing in the sauna. But Finnish law requires that, when needed, the pool be drained within 72 hours and converted into a shelter for 3,800 people. Deep underground there are also a ball court, a church, and a climbing wall. The shelter entrances are deliberately built with bends, so that they block the force of an explosion.

只有四分鐘的鬼故事

來源：Authors Guild Chicago Chapter

11 月 1 日星期日下午 1 點到 3 點，Berwyn 的 The Pile 書店要舉辦「亡靈節四分鐘恐怖故事挑戰」。

八位作家每人只有四分鐘，大約 350 個英文字，朗讀自己寫的恐怖小故事。時間一到就要停！

之後，任何人都可以在門口報名上台。因為書店裡可能有小孩，故事不能太血腥（太多血、太可怕）。想來聽的人不用預約，直接來就好。

On Sunday, November 1, from 1 to 3 p.m., The Pile bookstore in Berwyn will host a "Day of the Dead Four-Minute Horror Story Challenge." Eight writers will each get just four minutes, or about 350 English words, to read aloud a short horror story they wrote themselves. When time is up, they have to stop! Afterward, anyone can sign up at the door to take a turn at the microphone. Since children may be in the bookstore, the stories cannot be too gory (no excessive blood or anything too frightening). Listeners do not need a reservation, so just show up.

一個人的頭骨博物館

來源：skullsite.co.uk

英國網站 skullsite.co.uk 是一個叫 Will 的人做的。一開始，他只是拍下自己收集的動物頭骨，想跟大家分享這個少見的愛好。

後來網站越長越大，教你認出頭骨屬於哪種動物、怎麼清理，還有動物腳印的圖片。撿到頭骨的人也可以寄照片請他幫忙認。

這個網站已經被點開超過三百萬次，還被大英圖書館選進網站保存計畫。沒有機構幫忙，全靠一個人的熱情。

The British website skullsite.co.uk was built by a man named Will. At first, he simply photographed the animal skulls he had collected, hoping to share this unusual hobby with others. Over time the site grew. It now teaches readers how to identify which animal a skull came from and how to clean one, and it includes pictures of animal footprints. People who find a skull can also send him a photo and ask for help identifying it. The site has been visited more than three million times, and the British Library has included it in its web archiving program. No institution is behind it; it runs entirely on one person's enthusiasm.
