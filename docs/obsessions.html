解嚴之後的吵鬧青春：濁水溪公社

來源：Wikipedia / LTK Commune Official Blog

一九九五年，台北一群高中生組成的「濁水溪公社」，發行了第一張專輯。那時台灣剛解除戒嚴（政府放寬管制）不久，他們把夜市裡常聽到的「那卡西」老歌，和吵鬧的龐克、爵士樂拼在同一首歌裡。

專輯的名字故意取得很粗俗，就是要刺激大家。樂團說，他們要和社會底層的人站在一起，建立新的秩序。三十年後，這張唱片仍然是台灣地下音樂最重要的起點之一。

In 1995, a group of Taipei high school students formed the Turbid Water River Commune (LTK Commune) and released their debut album. Taiwan had just lifted martial law, and they mixed the old, nostalgic songs commonly heard in night markets with loud punk and jazz on the same tracks. The album title was deliberately crude — a provocative attempt to shock audiences. The band said they wanted to stand alongside society's underclass and build a new order. Thirty years later, this record remains one of the most important starting points in Taiwan's underground music.

最後一個冬天：芬蘭的海上冰路

來源：Yle News

在芬蘭北部，海水冬天會結冰。將近六十年來，只要冰厚超過四十公分，政府就會在海面上鏟出一條七公里長的路，通往哈伊盧奧托島。工作人員每週鑽洞量冰，確定安全才開放。

二○二六年六月，兩座新橋通車，這條冰路正式走入歷史。二○二七年三月來的人雖然錯過了，但芬蘭還有別的冰路，像科利湖上那條，是全歐洲內陸最長的冰路。

In northern Finland, winter transforms the sea into solid ice. For nearly sixty years, whenever the ice grew thicker than forty centimeters, the government would plow a seven-kilometer-long road across the frozen surface to Hailuoto Island. Workers would drill holes and measure the ice weekly, opening the route only after confirming safety. In June 2026, two new bridges opened to traffic, and this ice road officially became history. Those visiting in March 2027 will have missed it, but Finland still has other ice roads — like the one on Koli Lake, Europe's longest inland ice road.

划過候鳥的水面：斯科基湖區獨木舟

來源：The Urban Birding Festival

九月二十日是芝加哥「城市賞鳥節」的最後一天。這個三天的活動以自然博物館為中心，當天有一場特別的行程：划著獨木舟，進入斯科基湖區平靜的水面。

那裡風浪很小，新手也能安心。船慢慢滑過樹叢旁，正好遇上往南飛的鶯鳥（一種小型候鳥）。同一個週末還有看老鷹遷徙和湖邊騎車，但只有這趟划船，能讓人這麼安靜地靠近小鳥。

September 20th is the final day of Chicago's "Urban Birding Festival," a three-day celebration centered around the Nature Museum. On that day, there's a special outing: kayaking through the calm waters of Skokie Lagoons. The waves are gentle there — even beginners can paddle with ease. As your boat drifts slowly past groves of trees, you're likely to encounter warblers migrating south (small migratory birds). The same weekend offers eagle-watching and lakeside cycling, but only this kayaking trip lets you draw this close to birds in such peaceful silence.

程式碼是一幅畫：Piet

來源：Esolang Wiki

在一個由志工經營的「怪語言維基」網站上，有一頁介紹 Piet 這個程式語言。它由摩根馬發明，名字來自畫家蒙德里安。最特別的是，它的程式碼不是文字，而是一張用二十種顏色畫成的抽象圖。

電腦執行時，會有一個小點在相鄰的色塊之間移動，靠它走的方向決定要做什麼。還有人用這種「畫」寫出另一個語言的翻譯程式，證明它什麼都算得出來。

On a volunteer-run wiki for esoteric programming languages, there's a page about Piet, a programming language invented by David Morgan-Mar and named after painter Piet Mondrian. What makes it special is that the code isn't written in text — it's an abstract image painted in twenty colors. When the computer executes it, a small pointer moves between adjacent colored blocks, and the direction it travels determines what operation to perform. Someone has even written an interpreter for another language using this visual 'painting,' proving that it can compute anything.
