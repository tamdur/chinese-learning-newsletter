送走亡魂的歌，唱給不改選的國會

來源：臺灣音聲100年（國立臺灣歷史博物館）

「牽亡歌」是台灣民間喪禮上的歌。法師（做儀式的人）用尖尖的鼻音，帶著剛過世的人走過地府的關口。

一九八九年，作曲家邱晨把它改寫成抗議歌。歌詞問：一九四七年選出來、四十多年都不必再改選的「萬年國會」，什麼時候也要接受人民的審判？

一九九零年三月，野百合學運的學生在中正紀念堂廣場一起唱它。一首送葬的歌，最後送走了一個舊時代。

The spirit-guiding song is a folk funeral chant sung at Taiwanese wakes. A ritual priest, in a sharp nasal tone, leads the newly dead through the gates of the underworld. In 1989 the composer Chiu Chen rewrote it as a protest song. The lyrics ask a pointed question: when would the "ten-thousand-year congress" — elected in 1947 and never required to face re-election for more than forty years — finally submit to the people's judgment? In March 1990, students of the Wild Lily Movement sang it together in the plaza at the Chiang Kai-shek Memorial Hall. A song for escorting the dead ended up escorting away an entire era.

花瓶上的曲線，來自芬蘭的湖岸

來源：Alvar Aalto Foundation

一九三六年，芬蘭建築師阿爾托（Alvar Aalto）參加玻璃設計比賽，得了第一名。作品的名字很怪：「愛斯基摩女人的皮褲」。

那就是今天全世界都認得的阿爾托花瓶。瓶身彎彎曲曲，像芬蘭幾萬個湖不規則的岸邊。

最早的花瓶至今還放在赫爾辛基的薩佛伊餐廳。餐廳也是阿爾托和妻子艾諾設計的，就在中央大道公園旁，現在還開著，你可以走進去坐下來。

In 1936 the Finnish architect Alvar Aalto entered a glass design competition and took first prize. The piece bore an odd name: "The Eskimo Woman's Leather Breeches." It is the Aalto vase, the one the whole world now recognises. The body curves and ripples, like the irregular shorelines of Finland's tens of thousands of lakes. The earliest vases still sit in Restaurant Savoy in Helsinki, which Aalto designed together with his wife Aino. Beside Esplanade Park, the restaurant is still open today — you can walk in and sit down.

把布綁起來，泡進藍色的染缸

來源：Tock（芝加哥紡織中心活動行事曆）

九月十三日星期六上午十一點半到下午兩點，芝加哥的 Field Theory 有一場藍染工作坊，主辦的是永續時尚團體 ALL WE REMEMBER，一個人一百一十五美元。

學的是日本的「絞染」：先把布折起來、扭起來、綁起來，再放進活的藍靛染缸。綁住的地方染不到顏色，拿出來就是別人複製不了的花紋。

沒有經驗也能報名，帶一件白衣服去就好。

On Saturday, September 13th, from 11:30 a.m. to 2 p.m., Field Theory in Chicago hosts an indigo-dyeing workshop run by the sustainable fashion collective ALL WE REMEMBER, at $115 per person. What you learn is the Japanese technique of shibori: fold the fabric, twist it, bind it, then lower it into a living indigo vat. Where the bindings hold, the dye cannot reach; unfold it and you have a pattern nobody else can copy. No experience needed — just bring a white garment.

寫了十九年的草稿：程式設計師談錢

來源：interfluidity

Steve Randy Waldman 不是經濟學家，他是寫程式的人。二零零七年，他開始在自己的部落格上寫貨幣（錢的制度）和貧富差距，一寫十九年，沒有編輯、沒有廣告，也沒有機構養他。

克魯曼、柯文和《經濟學人》都引用過他的想法。

他現在的網站就叫「草稿」。每篇文章他都當成還沒寫完，過幾年再回來改：一篇二零二五年四月的文章，二零二六年六月又被他大改了一次。

Steve Randy Waldman is not an economist; he writes code. In 2007 he began writing on his own blog about money — the monetary system — and about inequality, and has kept it up for nineteen years, with no editor, no advertising and no institution paying him. Krugman, Cowen and The Economist have all cited his ideas. His current site is simply called "drafts." He treats every essay as unfinished, coming back years later to change it: one piece from April 2025 was substantially rewritten in June 2026.
