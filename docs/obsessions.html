《抓狂歌》：台語搖滾的第一聲雷

來源：Wikipedia (Chinese)

一九八九年十一月，台灣解除戒嚴（政府放寬管制）才兩年，「黑名單工作室」推出專輯《抓狂歌》。這張唱片做了五年，成員有王明輝、陳明章，還有原住民歌手胡德夫。

他們把台灣的唸歌（一種說唱故事的老調）和搖滾、饒舌、放克、雷鬼混在一起，幾乎全用台語唱出多年不能說的話。這張專輯點燃了「新台語歌」運動，後來的林強和伍佰都受它影響。

In November 1989, just two years after Taiwan lifted martial law and the government began relaxing its controls, Blacklist Studio released the album Songs of Madness. The record took five years to produce and featured Wang Ming-hui, Chen Ming-zhang, and indigenous singer Hu De-fu. They fused Taiwan's traditional narrative folk form with rock, hip-hop, funk, and reggae, singing almost entirely in Taiwanese to express words that had been forbidden to speak for years. The album ignited the 'New Taiwanese Song' movement, influencing later artists like Lin Chiang and Wu Bai.

卡勒瓦拉珠寶：戴在身上的青銅時代

來源：Kalevala (Kalevala Koru)

一九三五年，芬蘭作家赫波勞塔和一群女性成立了卡勒瓦拉珠寶公司，本來是想募款，為史詩《卡勒瓦拉》裡的女性蓋一座雕像。她們沒有設計新款式，而是照著考古學家挖出的青銅、鐵器時代飾品，一件一件複製。

九十年後，它仍是芬蘭最大的珠寶公司，在赫爾辛基的科納拉工坊，用銀和青銅手工打造那些古老的手環與胸針。市中心中央街三號的旗艦店還設有二手區，讓老件再找到新主人。

In 1935, Finnish writer Elsa Heporauta and a group of women founded Kalevala Jewelry Company with a singular purpose: to raise funds for a statue honoring the women celebrated in the epic Kalevala. Rather than designing original pieces, they painstakingly replicated bronze and iron age ornaments that archaeologists had uncovered. Ninety years later, the company remains Finland's largest jewelry house. At their Konala workshop in Helsinki, artisans continue to hand-craft those ancient bracelets and brooches in silver and bronze. The flagship store at 3 Central Street in the city center also maintains a vintage section, giving treasured pieces a second life with new owners.

在墓碑之間找蘑菇

來源：Chicago Mushroom Club

九月二十六日下午兩點，芝加哥蘑菇俱樂部要在西北區的波希米亞國家公墓，辦一場免費的採菇導覽，大家在墓園主建築前集合。

因為是墓地，不可以採摘，只能觀察、拍照，一起討論那些老捷克墓碑旁長出了什麼菇。俱樂部全由志工組成，不用報名，也不用經驗，時間到了直接出現就行。

On September 26th at 2 PM, the Chicago Mushroom Club is hosting a free guided walk through Bohemian National Cemetery in the northwest part of the city. Everyone meets at the main cemetery building. Since it's a cemetery, mushrooms can only be observed and photographed—no picking allowed. The group will discuss what fungi have grown up beside the old Czech gravestones. The club runs entirely on volunteers, so there's no need to sign up or bring any experience. Just show up when it's time.

一個人，四十年，一整座藥瓶博物館

來源：curesalldiseases.com

一位英國收藏家花了四十多年，從河床、維多利亞時代的垃圾堆、舊貨店和古董市集裡，一個一個撿出老藥瓶。這些瓶子曾經裝著號稱能治百病的藥水和藥丸，有的還加了鴉片（一種會上癮的藥）。

他把它們全放上自己做的網站，照形狀、顏色、廠商標記和年代分類，從十八世紀初一直排到第一次世界大戰。本來只是為了寫書做的功課，現在成了認老藥瓶最好用的工具書。

Over four decades, a British collector painstakingly gathered old medicine bottles from riverbeds, Victorian-era dumps, secondhand shops, and antique markets. These bottles once held patent remedies and pills touted as cure-alls, some laced with opium—an addictive drug. He uploaded his entire collection to a website he created, organized by shape, color, manufacturer marks, and era, spanning from the early 18th century through World War I. What started as research for a book has become the definitive guide for identifying old medicine bottles.
