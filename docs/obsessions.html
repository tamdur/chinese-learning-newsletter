台灣地下電子聲響：模組合成器的島嶼紀錄

來源：ato.archives / Bandcamp

二〇二五年七月，日本唱片廠牌 ato.archives 發行了一張特別的卡帶合輯：《Pulses on the Horizon》，收錄九首來自台灣的模組（把不同零件自由組合的）合成器音樂。

住在台北的美國藝術家 Ryan J Raffa 花了好幾年記錄這個小而熱情的圈子。專輯中的藝人包括 weiii（張永威）、MADZINE，還有夢東樂團的 Hom Yu。他們把合成器當作一種記錄環境聲音的工具，在台北的小型藝術和噪音場地演出。

In July 2025, Japanese record label ato.archives released a special cassette compilation: "Pulses on the Horizon," featuring nine tracks of modular synthesizer music from Taiwan. Ryan J Raffa, an American artist based in Taipei, spent several years documenting this small but passionate community. The album includes artists such as weiii (Zhang Yongwei), MADZINE, and Hom Yu from Dream East Band. They use synthesizers as a tool to capture environmental sounds, performing at small art and noise venues across Taipei.

千年羊肉的秘密：芬蘭萊米村的白樺木槽

來源：Finnish Intangible Cultural Heritage Wiki

在芬蘭東南方的萊米村，有一道叫做「särä」的羊肉料理，已經用同樣的方法做了超過一千年。做法是：先把一根白樺（一種北歐常見的樹）木頭挖成長槽，泡鹽水一星期，再塗上羊油，然後把醃好的羊肉放進去慢慢烤。

這道菜在一九七二年被選為「芬蘭七大奇蹟」之一。現在村裡的 Säräpirtti Kippurasarvi 餐廳還在用傳統方式做這道菜，從赫爾辛基開車大約兩小時就能到。

In the village of Lemi in southeastern Finland, there is a lamb dish called "särä" that has been prepared using the same method for over a thousand years. The process involves carving a birch wood — a tree common throughout Northern Europe — into a long trough, soaking it in salt water for a week, coating it with sheep fat, and then slowly roasting the cured lamb inside. This dish was selected as one of the "Seven Wonders of Finland" in 1972. Today, the restaurant Säräpirtti Kippurasarvi in the village continues to prepare this dish using traditional methods, located about two hours' drive from Helsinki.

用手做一本小誌，畫出你的街區地圖

來源：Zine Club Chicago

六月八日，Zine Club Chicago 將在芝加哥西南區舉辦一場免費的小誌（zine，自己動手做的小書）工作坊。主題是畫出你自己住的街區地圖：你常走的路、你喜歡的地方、你的回憶。

不管你以前有沒有做過小誌都歡迎參加。這個團體最近還成立了芝加哥西南區專屬的小誌圖書館。

On June 8th, Zine Club Chicago will host a free zine-making workshop in Chicago's southwest side. The theme is creating a map of your own neighborhood: the routes you walk regularly, the places you love, your memories. Whether or not you've made a zine before, you're welcome to join. The group recently established a zine library dedicated to Chicago's southwest side.

Wiby：一個只找得到「老網路」的搜尋引擎

來源：Wiby.me

你還記得早期網路的感覺嗎？那時候很多人自己做網站，只因為喜歡某個東西。Wiby.me 是一個搜尋引擎，專門收錄這種簡單、沒有廣告的個人網頁。

住在西雅圖的 Eric K. Hill 從二〇一七年開始經營這個網站，靠捐款維持運作。它完全不收錄 YouTube、Reddit 等大平台。最好玩的是它的「驚喜」按鈕，會隨機帶你去一個完全沒想過的頁面。

Do you remember what the early internet felt like? Back then, many people built their own websites simply because they loved something. Wiby.me is a search engine dedicated to indexing exactly that kind of simple, ad-free personal webpage. Eric K. Hill, who lives in Seattle, has run the site since 2017, supported entirely by donations. It deliberately excludes major platforms like YouTube and Reddit. The best part is its "surprise" button, which randomly takes you to a completely unexpected page.
