盲眼國寶的祕密歌本

來源：中央社

楊秀卿四歲就看不見了，卻抱著月琴唱了一輩子的「唸歌」，也就是一邊彈琴、一邊說故事的台灣民間歌唱。2009年，她被選為「人間國寶」。

製作人柯智豪發現，她心裡藏著一批只給大人聽的老歌詞，花了好幾年才說服她錄音。這張《未公開的綺麗》只用月琴和胡琴伴奏，故事裡的男男女女，全由她一個人用聲音演出來。

Yang Hsiu-ching lost her sight at the age of four, yet she spent her whole life singing niàn gē, the Taiwanese folk form in which a performer plays the yueqin (moon lute) while telling a story. In 2009 she was named a "Living National Treasure." The producer Ke Chih-hao discovered that she carried a collection of old song texts meant only for adult ears, and it took him several years to persuade her to record them. The album Unpublished Splendor is accompanied only by the yueqin and the huqin, and every man and woman in its stories is voiced by her alone.

拉普蘭的冰凍瀑布

來源：Visit Finland

在芬蘭北方的拉普蘭，有一條長30公里的峽谷（兩邊都是高山壁的深谷），叫做科羅烏瑪。每年冬天，山壁上流下來的水會結成高大的冰牆，最高的有60到70公尺。

三月正適合來這裡攀冰（爬上冰牆）。從羅凡涅米出發的一日行程，就算沒有經驗也能參加。不想爬的人，也可以沿著步道散步，走過三座最大的冰瀑布。

In Lapland, in northern Finland, there is a 30-kilometer-long gorge (a deep valley with high mountain walls on both sides) called Korouoma. Every winter, water running down the cliff walls freezes into towering walls of ice, some reaching 60 to 70 meters high. March is the ideal time to come here for ice climbing (climbing up the ice walls). A day trip from Rovaniemi is open to beginners, so no prior experience is needed. Those who would rather not climb can simply walk the trails and pass three of the largest ice falls.

民主是怎麼慢慢死去的？

來源：Chicago Humanities Festival

11月7日星期六下午，芝加哥大學有一場特別的對談。主持人是廣播節目主持人彼得．薩格爾，來賓是兩位法律教授：阿齊茲．胡克和湯姆．金斯伯格。

兩位教授合寫了《如何拯救憲政民主》。他們發現，很多國家的民主不是被政變（用武力搶走政權）推倒，而是被一條條新法律慢慢掏空。當天晚上，薩格爾還會主持另一場談《獨立宣言》的講座。

On the afternoon of Saturday, November 7, the University of Chicago hosted a special conversation. The moderator was Peter Sagal, host of a radio program, and the guests were two law professors, Aziz Huq and Tom Ginsburg. The two professors co-wrote How to Save a Constitutional Democracy. They argue that in many countries, democracy has not been toppled by coups, meaning seizures of power by force, but has been slowly hollowed out by a series of new laws. That same evening, Sagal will also moderate another event, a lecture on the Declaration of Independence.

飛進蟲洞的網站

來源：spacetimetravel.org

德國物理學家烏特．克勞斯和科文．贊恩，做了一個叫「時空旅行」的網站。你可以拖動一個黑洞，看星光怎麼被它拉彎；也可以坐上模擬飛船，直接飛進蟲洞（連接遠方兩地的時空隧道）。

網站上的每一張圖，都是用愛因斯坦的相對論真的算出來的，不是畫家的想像。這個網站沒有廣告，完全免費，是兩位科學家為了教學，用熱情一點一點做出來的。

German physicists Ute Kraus and Corvin Zahn have built a website called "Spacetime Travel." You can drag a black hole around and watch starlight bend as it passes by. You can also board a simulated spaceship and fly straight into a wormhole (a tunnel through spacetime that connects two distant places). Every image on the site is calculated from Einstein's theory of relativity, not drawn from an artist's imagination. The site has no ads and is completely free. The two scientists built it bit by bit, out of enthusiasm, for the purpose of teaching.
