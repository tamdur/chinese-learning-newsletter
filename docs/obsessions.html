三天錄成的夢：雷光夏的第一張專輯

來源：FOND/SOUND

一九九五年，台灣歌手雷光夏把自己做的音樂一次又一次寄給水晶唱片公司。後來老闆親自打電話給她，於是有了這張《我是雷光夏》。

這張專輯只花三天就錄好了，聲音卻慢得像做夢。裡面有一首長長的〈時間（I）〉，她輕輕唸著詩，配上柔和的旋律。三十年後再聽，還是不像任何時代的音樂。

In 1995, Taiwanese singer Lei Guangxia sent her music repeatedly to Crystal Records. Eventually the owner called her personally, and that's how her debut album "I Am Lei Guangxia" came to be.

The album was recorded in just three days, yet its sound unfolds like a dream. It contains a long track called "Time (I)" where she recites poetry softly over gentle melodies. Thirty years later, listening again, it still sounds like music from no particular era.

開車走過結冰的海：芬蘭最後的冰路

來源：Europa Tips

在芬蘭北部的奧盧附近，有一條全國最長的冰路。冬天海面結冰後，人們可以開車走過七公里的冰面，到海克魯奧托島。冰上還插著交通標誌，車子之間要隔五十公尺。

可惜這是最後一季了。天氣越來越暖，冰路能用的日子從好幾個月縮短到只剩幾個星期。二○二六年底會蓋好一座大橋，以後就不必再走冰上了。

Near Oulu in northern Finland, there is the country's longest ice road. When the sea freezes over in winter, people can drive across seven kilometers of ice to reach Hailuoto Island. Traffic signs are planted on the ice, and vehicles must maintain a fifty-meter distance from one another.

Unfortunately, this is the final season. As temperatures warm, the ice road's usable period has shrunk from several months to just a few weeks. A major bridge will be completed by the end of 2026, so people will no longer need to drive across the ice.

近百年老戲院，添了第三個放映廳

來源：Block Club Chicago

芝加哥的音樂盒戲院（Music Box Theatre）在一九二九年開幕，是當地人最愛的老電影院。六月二十六日，它要開出近一百年來第一個新放映廳，有九十八個座位。

這個新廳是用隔壁兩間店面改建的，座位一排比一排高，最後一排還放了雙人沙發。它能放數位電影，也能放老式膠卷底片。

Chicago's Music Box Theatre, which opened in 1929, is the city's most beloved vintage cinema. On June 26th, it will debut its first new screening room in nearly a century, with seating for ninety-eight guests.

The new theater was built by converting two adjacent storefronts. The seats are arranged in a stadium configuration, with each row elevated above the last, and the back row features a pair of loveseat sofas. It can show both digital films and 35mm film stock.

一個人、二十年：伊利諾野花大百科

來源：Illinois Wildflowers (illinoiswildflowers.info)

從二○○二年開始，一位叫約翰・希爾蒂（John Hilty）的人，自己一個人做了一個網站，叫「伊利諾野花」。裡面記錄了好幾百種植物，每一種都有他自己拍的照片。

最特別的是，他還寫下每一種花會招來哪些蜜蜂、蝴蝶和甲蟲。這個網站沒有廣告，也不用付錢，只是一個人二十年的用心。

Since 2002, a man named John Hilty has single-handedly built a website called "Illinois Wildflowers." It documents several hundred plant species, each with photographs he took himself.

What makes it truly special is that he also recorded which bees, butterflies, and beetles each flower attracts. The site has no ads, costs nothing to use—it's simply one man's careful work over twenty years.
