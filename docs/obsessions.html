雷光夏的夢境之聲：一九九五年的台灣環境音樂

來源：FOND/SOUND

一九九五年，雷光夏在台北的水晶唱片，只花三天就錄好了她的第一張專輯《我是雷光夏》。她把這種風格叫做「人物音樂」：民歌慢慢融化成像夢一樣的聲音，再加上她輕輕念出的詩。專輯最重要的作品是分成五段的〈時間（一）〉，像導演剪接回憶的碎片。這是華語流行樂裡最特別的一張處女作。

Summer Lei's Dreamscape Sounds: 1995 Taiwanese Ambient Music

In 1995, Summer Lei spent just three days at Taipei's Crystal Records laying down her debut album, I Am Summer Lei. She called the style "character music": folk song slowly melting into dream-like sound, layered with her softly-recited poetry. The album's centerpiece is "Time (I)", a work split into five parts, like a director splicing together fragments of memory. It stands as one of the most distinctive debut albums in Mandarin pop.

菲斯卡村：一六四九年的鐵工廠變成設計小鎮

來源：Visit Finland

在芬蘭首都赫爾辛基西邊大約九十公里的森林裡，有一座菲斯卡村。一六四九年，這裡是一座鐵工廠，也是全世界都認得的橘色剪刀品牌最早的家。現在只住著六百多人，其中超過一百位是藝術家、設計師和手工藝人。走進老廠房，可以看到吹玻璃的師傅、做陶器的人、還有打鐵的工匠（手藝人）認真工作。三月時村子還覆蓋著一層薄雪。

Fiskars Village: A 1649 Ironworks Turned Design Town

In the forest about ninety kilometers west of Finland's capital Helsinki sits Fiskars Village. In 1649, this was an ironworks, and it was also the original home of the world-famous orange scissors brand. Today only a little over six hundred people live here, more than a hundred of whom are artists, designers, and craftspeople. Walk into the old factory buildings and you can see glassblowers, potters, and blacksmiths (artisans) hard at work. In March, the village is still covered in a thin layer of snow.

作家用腳走完芝加哥的海岸線

來源：Chicago Public Library

四月十六日禮拜四，作家麥考利會在芝加哥公共圖書館，談他的新書《走過芝加哥海岸：到印第安納沙丘的六十三英里旅程》。這本書一半是回憶錄，一半是環境報導。他從自己在城北的家出發，沿著密西根湖一路走到印第安納沙丘國家公園，一邊走一邊想這座城市一層一層的歷史和裂痕（分開的地方）。活動免費，只要先報名。

A Writer Walks Chicago's Coastline on Foot

On Thursday, April 16, writer McColly will appear at the Chicago Public Library to discuss his new book, Walking the Chicago Shore: A Sixty-Three Mile Journey to the Indiana Dunes. The book is half memoir, half environmental reporting. Starting from his home on the North Side, he walked along Lake Michigan all the way to Indiana Dunes National Park, reflecting as he went on the city's layered history and its fractures — the places where it is divided. The event is free, but registration is required in advance.

一個人，七千多座老橋，二十三年

來源：MetaFilter

在網路上有一個叫做「歷史橋樑」的網站。一位叫侯爾斯的先生，花了二十三年，一個人整理出北美洲七千七百多座老橋的資料，還親自走到其中一千九百多座橋前面拍照。每一座橋都有全景照、細節照，還有工程故事：桁架橋（鐵條交叉做成）、懸臂橋、石拱橋，很多都快要消失了。沒有任何一間大學或博物館，做得到這件事。

One Man, Over Seven Thousand Old Bridges, Twenty-Three Years

There is a website on the internet called HistoricBridges.org. A man named Nathan Holth has spent twenty-three years, all by himself, compiling information on more than seven thousand seven hundred old bridges across North America, and he has personally traveled to over nineteen hundred of them to take photographs. Every bridge has wide-angle shots, detail shots, and an engineering story: truss bridges (made of crisscrossing iron bars), cantilever bridges, stone arch bridges — many of them on the verge of disappearing. Not a single university or museum has managed to do what he has done.
