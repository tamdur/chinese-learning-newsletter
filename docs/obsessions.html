太空熱潮吹進台南舞廳

來源：獨立評論 CommonWealth Opinion

一九六九年，台南的陽光合唱團因為臨時替補一支缺席的外國樂團，意外走紅。當時大部分樂隊只能幫歌星伴奏，他們卻能自己錄下整張唱片。

那年他們一年內錄了三張唱片。第三張叫《太陽神十一號》，一九六九年九月一日發行。主打歌改編自英國樂團的〈電星〉。人類登月才過一個月，太空的聲音就進了台灣的舞廳。

In 1969, the Sunshine Chorus from Tainan unexpectedly rose to fame when they stepped in as last-minute replacements for a foreign band that had cancelled. While most bands at the time could only provide accompaniment for singers, they were able to record an entire album of their own.

That year they recorded three albums within twelve months. The third was called Apollo No. 11, released on September 1, 1969. The lead track was an adaptation of The Tornados' "Telstar". Just one month after humans landed on the moon, the sounds of space had arrived in Taiwan's dance halls.

只在冬天出現的橋

來源：This is FINLAND

每年十一月，赫爾辛基（芬蘭的首都）會在海上架起一座浮橋，把岸邊和一座小島連起來。這座島叫烏尼島，橋只留到四月中，然後就拆掉，改由船來接送。

島上有餐廳，也有一群愛冬泳的人，年紀從十二歲到七十五歲都有。三月來剛剛好：橋還在，海冰上的泳洞也還開著。

Every November, Helsinki — Finland's capital — erects a floating bridge across the sea, connecting the shore to a small island. The island is called Uunisaari, and the bridge stays up only until mid-April, when it is dismantled and replaced by boat service.

The island has a restaurant and a community of winter swimmers, ranging in age from twelve to seventy-five. March is exactly the right time to visit: the bridge is still there, and the swimming hole in the sea ice is still open.

週三夜晚的免費棋局

來源：Chicago Chess Center

每個星期三晚上，芝加哥西洋棋中心都有免費的「四人賽」：四個人輪流對下三盤快棋。地點在市中心西華盛頓街一六六號，六點到六點十五分現場報名，六點半開始。

這裡最歡迎新手，工作人員會從規則教起。下一場是九月九日星期三，走出捷運站就到。

Every Wednesday evening, the Chicago Chess Center runs free "Quads" — four players taking turns over three fast games. It is at 166 West Washington Street downtown, with on-site registration from 6:00 to 6:15 p.m. and play starting at 6:30.

Beginners are especially welcome, and the staff will start you from the rules. The next one is Wednesday, September 9th, and it is right outside the L stop.

一個人畫出的沉船地圖

來源：Sunken Ships of the Second World War

有位網友自稱「地圖人」。他發現網路上的資料庫只記下被德國潛艇（水面下的軍艦）擊沉的船，其他的都漏掉了，於是決定自己動手。

他一個人，把第二次世界大戰裡一萬五千多艘沉船，一艘一艘標在世界地圖上。地圖還能播放動畫，讓你看著船隻一週一週沉沒。

There is a hobbyist online who goes by "mapsterman". He found that the databases on the internet recorded only the ships sunk by German U-boats — everything else was left out — so he decided to do it himself.

Working alone, he has plotted more than 15,000 ships sunk in the Second World War onto a world map, one by one. The map can also play an animation, letting you watch the vessels go down week by week.
