被遺忘的舞曲：七○年代台灣的迪斯可女聲

來源：Teleport City / Aberrant Records

你以為七○年代的台灣歌曲都是慢慢的情歌嗎？其實那時候有一群女歌手在唱很會跳舞的迪斯可（一種熱鬧的舞曲）。

一張叫《Taiwan Disco》的合輯（把很多人的歌放在一起的唱片）把這些歌找了回來，收了吳秀珠、陳蘭麗等十一位女歌手。吳秀珠的〈我不知道我愛你〉電吉他的聲音又炸又猛，好聽又好特別。

You thought all 1970s Taiwanese songs were slow ballads? Actually, back then a group of female singers were performing disco — lively, danceable music that got people moving. A compilation album called "Taiwan Disco" has rescued these forgotten tracks, featuring eleven female vocalists including Wu Xiuzhu and Chen Lanli. Wu Xiuzhu's "I Don't Know That I Love You" features electric guitar that's explosive and powerful — catchy and genuinely distinctive.

門不上鎖：芬蘭五百間免費山中小屋

來源：thisisFINLAND (finland.fi)

在芬蘭的大自然裡，有五百多間小木屋。門不用上鎖，不用先訂位，也不用付錢。走累了，任何人都可以進去過夜。

這裡只有一個沒寫出來的規矩：用掉柴火的人，要記得補好柴，留給下一個陌生人。三月的雪最好，滑雪的人會在這些小屋歇腳過夜。

Deep in Finland's wilderness, more than five hundred small wooden cabins stand waiting. The doors don't need locks. There's no reservation system. No fee. When hikers grow tired, anyone can step inside and spend the night. There is only one unwritten rule: if you burn firewood, you refill it for the next stranger. March snow is best — skiers stop at these cabins to rest and sleep through the night.

一本護照，吃遍全世界：芝加哥的美食小旅行

來源：Humanitix / North River Commission

六月二十五日晚上五點到八點，芝加哥的Albany Park會辦一場美食活動。每個人會拿到一本「護照」，照著路線走，一家一家嘗小店。

這一區是全美國人種最多的地方之一。走幾步路，就能吃到波斯烤肉、薩爾瓦多的玉米餅，還有東南亞的家常菜。大家在3403 W Lawrence的停車場報到。

On the evening of June 25th, from 5 to 8 PM, Albany Park in Chicago will host a food festival. Each visitor receives a "passport" and follows a route, sampling small restaurants along the way. This neighborhood is one of the most ethnically diverse in the United States. Within just a few blocks, you can taste Persian grilled meat, Salvadoran corn tortillas, and Southeast Asian home cooking. Check-in happens at the parking lot at 3403 W Lawrence.

一個人的二十二萬張浮世繪

來源：ukiyo-e.org

浮世繪是日本的木刻版畫（用刻好的木板印出來的圖）。寫出有名程式jQuery的工程師John Resig，花了好幾年做了一個網站ukiyo-e.org，把全世界超過二十二萬張版畫放在一起，一查就找得到。

最厲害的是，電腦會自己比對每張圖，把同一塊木板印出來、卻分散在各國的畫找在一起。連博物館的學者都用它來改正弄錯的資料。

Ukiyo-e are Japanese woodblock prints — images created by carving designs into wooden blocks and printing from them. John Resig, the engineer who created the famous jQuery programming library, spent several years building a website called ukiyo-e.org that brings together over 220,000 prints from around the world in one searchable collection. What's remarkable is that the computer automatically compares each image and groups together prints made from the same wooden block, even when they're scattered across different countries. Museum scholars now use it to correct their own mistaken records.
