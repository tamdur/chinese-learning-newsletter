甜梅號：在速食店誕生的台北後搖滾

來源：Bandcamp Daily

一九九七年，三個台北大學生的主唱沒來練團，他們就自己玩出了沒有歌詞的音樂。後來在漢堡王取名「甜梅號」。

二〇〇一年第一張專輯《是不是少了什麼》用電吉他堆出細緻又爆裂的聲音，沒有電台幫忙，全靠現場演出傳開。他們是台灣後搖滾的起點。

In 1997, the lead singer of three Taipei university students' band didn't show up to practice. They decided to play anyway, and stumbled into a wordless instrumental sound. Later they named themselves Sugar Plum Ferry over a meal at Burger King.

Their 2001 debut album Lack of Something layered electric guitars into textures that were both delicate and explosive. With no radio support, the album spread entirely through live shows. They became the starting point of Taiwanese post-rock.

放下手機，用紙地圖走進芬蘭森林

來源：Time Out

芬蘭人把「定向越野」（看地圖找點的運動）當作國民活動，全國有六萬個會員。二〇二五年世界錦標賽就在庫奧皮奧市舉行。

只要十五歐元就能參加城市短程賽，用紙地圖在二十八分鐘內找到八個點。三月蜜月時，這是走入冬日森林、用身體感受芬蘭的好方法。

Finns treat orienteering — the sport of using a map to find checkpoints — as a national pastime, with 60,000 federation members across the country. The 2025 World Championships were held in the city of Kuopio.

For just fifteen euros you can join a city sprint, finding eight checkpoints in twenty-eight minutes with only a paper map. For a couple on a March honeymoon, it's a wonderful way to step into the winter forest and feel Finland with your whole body.

一天走進八棟萊特私人老屋

來源：Wednesday Journal (Oak Park)

五月十六日，芝加哥附近的橡樹園會把八棟私人的萊特老屋開放一天。其中一九〇二年的赫特利屋是最早的「草原風格」代表作。

預售票已經賣完，當天早上八點在萊特故居會放出七十五張現場票。八棟老屋都在幾條街內，慢慢走就能看完。

On May 16, Oak Park, just outside Chicago, opens eight privately owned Frank Lloyd Wright houses to the public for a single day. Among them is the 1902 Heurtley House, one of the earliest examples of his Prairie Style.

Advance tickets are sold out, but at 8 a.m. that morning the Wright Home & Studio releases seventy-five day-of tickets. All eight houses sit within a few blocks of each other — an easy walk to see them all.

天空中那隻被忘記的貓和熱氣球

來源：Ian Ridpath's Star Tales

英國天文作家瑞巴斯花了幾十年做了一個網站，記下天上八十八個星座的故事。最有趣的是那些「被淘汰的星座」。

十七、十八世紀的繪圖師在夜空畫過一隻貓、一台印刷機，還有一七九八年法國人為熱氣球兄弟而畫的熱氣球星座。一九三〇年後這些星座就從天空消失了。

The British astronomy writer Ian Ridpath has spent decades building a website that records the stories of all eighty-eight constellations in the sky. The most fascinating section is on the "retired constellations."

In the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries, mapmakers drew a cat, a printing press, and — in 1798 — a hot-air-balloon constellation that the French created to honor the Montgolfier brothers. After 1930 these constellations vanished from the sky.
