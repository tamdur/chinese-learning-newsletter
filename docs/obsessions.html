老菸樓裡錄出來的山歌

來源：Wikipedia / 報導者 The Reporter

一九九九年，高雄美濃的農民想擋下一座水庫。林生祥和鍾永豐組了交工樂隊，把專輯《我等就來唱山歌》錄在一座沒人用的菸樓（以前用來烘菸葉的老磚房）裡。磚砌的煙道讓聲音變得又深又厚，一般錄音室做不出來。

他們把客家八音的鑼、嗩吶（一種很響亮的吹管樂器）和月琴，配上搖滾的吉他與鼓。其中一首歌，唱的就是農民在台北立法院外面遊行的樣子。這張專輯拿下兩座金曲獎。

In 1999, farmers in Meinong, Kaohsiung set out to stop a dam. Lin Sheng-hsiang and Chung Yung-feng formed the Labor Exchange Band and recorded their album We Are Going to Sing Mountain Songs inside a disused tobacco barn — an old brick building once used to cure tobacco leaf. The brick flues gave the sound a depth and thickness an ordinary studio could not produce. They set the gong, suona (a loud, piercing horn) and moon lute of Hakka bayin folk music against rock guitar and drums. One of the songs is simply the sound of farmers marching outside the Legislative Yuan in Taipei. The album won two Golden Melody Awards.

出海前，先走一遍石頭迷宮

來源：Out in the Nature / National Nordic Museum

芬蘭的波羅的海沿岸，散著一百四十多座石頭迷宮，當地人叫它「巨人的花園」。那是漁民用拳頭大的石塊排成的螺旋，最早可以追到青銅器時代，一直排到一八○○年代。

出海前，漁民會先走完那條彎彎曲曲的路，相信這樣能把愛搗蛋的小妖怪關在身後，換來好風和滿船的魚。最好找的一座在圖爾庫外海的納沃島，從林間小路走六百公尺就到，坐免費渡輪就能去。

Scattered along Finland's Baltic coast are more than 140 stone mazes, known locally as "the giant's garden." They are spirals laid out by fishermen from fist-sized stones, the oldest reaching back to the Bronze Age and the practice continuing into the 1800s. Before putting out to sea, a fisherman would walk the whole winding path, believing it would shut the mischievous little spirits in behind him and buy him good winds and a full boat. The easiest one to find is on Nauvo island off Turku — 600 metres along a forest path, and reachable by free ferry.

中國城公園，火光轉起來

來源：Flow Fests / do312

八月二十九日，芝加哥中國城的譚繼平紀念公園要辦第八屆「流動藝術節」。玩雜耍、轉火球和轉呼拉圈的人會聚在一起，等太陽下山，再來一場火焰表演。

進場免費。今年的主題叫「共生」，把傳統的流動技藝和繩鏢、龍火球的課排在一起，還請來一整團舞龍。想動手學的人，可以買三十五元的全日通行證，三十多堂課隨你上。

On August 29, Ping Tom Memorial Park in Chicago's Chinatown hosts the eighth Flow Fest. Jugglers, fire spinners and hoop dancers gather, wait for the sun to go down, and finish with a fire performance. Admission is free.

This year's theme is "Symbiotic," pairing traditional flow arts with classes in rope dart and dragon poi, plus a full dragon dance troupe. Anyone who wants to learn hands-on can buy a $35 all-access pass and take their pick of more than thirty workshops.

一個人做出來的搜尋引擎

來源：Marginalia Search

二○二一年疫情期間，瑞典工程師 Viktor Löfgren 自己動手做了一個叫 Marginalia 的搜尋引擎。頭兩年，整套系統就跑在他家的普通電腦上，用自家的網路，慢慢爬過好幾億個網頁，直到二○二三年十月才搬進真正的伺服器。

他想找的是「小網路」：個人部落格，還有那些字很多、沒有廣告的網站。這些頁面常常被大公司的搜尋結果埋住。沒有投資人，沒有公司，只有一個人和他自己寫的程式。

In 2021, during the pandemic, Swedish engineer Viktor Löfgren built a search engine called Marginalia by himself. For the first two years the whole system ran on an ordinary computer in his home, over his own internet connection, slowly crawling several hundred million pages; only in October 2023 did it move to a real server.

What he was after was the "small web": personal blogs, and text-heavy sites without advertising — the kind of page that tends to get buried under the big companies' search results. No investors, no company. Just one person and the code he wrote himself.
