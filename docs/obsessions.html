電路裡的島嶼之聲：台灣模組合成器音樂

來源：ato.archives / Bandcamp

你聽過用電壓做出來的音樂嗎？模組合成器（一種用電線連接不同零件來造聲音的機器）在台北已經有一群熱情的玩家。

二〇二五年七月，住在台北的 Ryan J Raffa 替日本廠牌 ato.archives 策劃了一張九首歌的合輯，叫做 Pulses on the Horizon。其中藝術家 weiii 把自然錄音和電子聲混在一起，聽起來不像歌，更像島嶼本身在呼吸。

Have you ever heard music made from voltage? Modular synthesizers—machines that create sound by connecting different components with cables—have attracted a passionate community of players in Taipei. In July 2025, Ryan J Raffa, who lives in Taipei, curated a nine-track compilation for Japanese label ato.archives called Pulses on the Horizon. Among the artists, weiii blends field recordings with electronic sounds in a way that doesn't quite sound like a song—it sounds more like the island itself breathing.

會唱歌的鋼管：赫爾辛基西貝流士紀念碑

來源：HAM Helsinki

赫爾辛基有一座很特別的紀念碑，是為了紀念芬蘭最有名的作曲家西貝流士。雕塑家 Eila Hiltunen 用六百多根空心鋼管焊（用高溫把金屬接在一起）成波浪形狀，重達二十四噸。

一九六七年揭幕時，很多人不喜歡，覺得應該做一個人像才對。但三月的早上，波羅的海的風吹過鋼管，會發出輕輕的哼聲——這座紀念碑真的會唱歌。

Helsinki has a very special monument dedicated to Finland's most famous composer, Sibelius. Sculptor Eila Hiltunen welded over six hundred hollow steel tubes into a wave-like shape, weighing twenty-four tons total. When it was unveiled in 1967, many people disliked it, feeling it should have been a portrait instead. But on March mornings, when the Baltic wind blows through the steel tubes, they emit a gentle humming sound — this monument truly sings.

一個字一個字印：活版印刷工作坊

來源：Spudnik Press

五月十七日（星期六）中午十二點到下午六點，芝加哥的 Spudnik Press 有一堂活版印刷（用一個個金屬字排列再壓印到紙上）課。

設計師 Amira Hegazy 會帶你用手一個字一個字排好，再操作老式印刷機。Spudnik 是一個非營利的版畫工作室，保存著很多老機器，任何人都可以來學。

On May 17th (Saturday) from noon to 6 PM, Spudnik Press in Chicago is offering a letterpress printing workshop — that's arranging individual metal type and pressing it onto paper. Designer Amira Hegazy will walk you through setting type by hand, one letter at a time, then operating a vintage printing press. Spudnik is a nonprofit printmaking studio that maintains an impressive collection of old machinery and welcomes anyone who wants to learn.

三十五年的世界檔案：CIA 世界概況資料庫

來源：worldfactbookarchive.org

美國中央情報局（CIA）曾經出版一本叫 World Factbook 的書，記錄全世界每個國家的基本資料。二〇二六年二月停刊後，有一位工程師把一九九〇年以來的全部資料存了下來。

這個網站收了二百八十一個國家、超過一百萬筆資料。你可以一行一行看任何國家三十五年來的變化——像翻開一本用數字寫的歷史書。

The U.S. Central Intelligence Agency once published a book called the World Factbook that recorded basic information about every country in the world. After it ceased publication in February 2026, an engineer preserved all the data dating back to 1990. The website contains records for 281 countries with over one million data points. You can scroll through any country's changes over the past 35 years line by line—like flipping through a history book written in numbers.
