當夜市遇見倫敦迷霧：林強的《驚蟄》

來源：Bandcamp Daily

林強是台灣音樂的傳奇人物。他先用台語搖滾改變了流行音樂，後來又走向電子音樂。2005年，他飛到倫敦，和英國名製作人 John Fryer 一起錄了專輯《驚蟄（春天蟲醒的日子）》。

這張專輯把夜市的聲音、戲曲的鑼鼓和台灣民謠，混進冰冷的電子音樂裡。它先在法國發行，還在坎城影展上演出，當時幾乎沒人買，卻拿下了第十七屆金曲獎最佳跨界專輯。走在時代前面的作品，往往要等很久才被聽見。

Lim Giong is a legendary figure in Taiwan's music scene. He first revolutionized popular music with Taiwanese-language rock, then ventured into electronic music. In 2005, he flew to London and recorded the album "Awakening of Insects (The Day Spring Awakens the Bugs)" with renowned British producer John Fryer. This album wove together the sounds of night markets, the gongs and drums of traditional opera, and Taiwanese folk melodies into cold electronic music. It was first released in France and performed at the Cannes Film Festival, selling almost nothing at the time, yet it won Best Crossover Album at the 17th Golden Melody Awards. Works ahead of their era often take a long time before they are truly heard.

藍色時刻：芬蘭冬天最美的十五分鐘

來源：Reissu Ruoti

芬蘭人有一個專門的詞叫 sininen hetki，意思是「藍色時刻」。每年九月到三月間，太陽落到地平線下方不遠處時，天空會變成一種很深的寶石藍，持續大約十五到三十分鐘。

湖面會像鏡子一樣映出藍光，整個世界好像泡在藍色裡。三月二十日春分（日夜一樣長的那天）之後，這個現象就消失了。三月的蜜月旅行，剛好能趕上冬天最後也最動人的藍色。

Finns have a special word for it: sininen hetki, meaning "the blue hour." Between September and March each year, when the sun dips just below the horizon, the sky transforms into a deep jewel blue, lasting roughly fifteen to thirty minutes. The lake surface mirrors the blue light like a sheet of glass, and the entire world seems immersed in blue. After the spring equinox on March 20th—when day and night are equal—the phenomenon vanishes. A March honeymoon arrives just in time to catch winter's final and most moving blue.

兩位天才獎得主聊藝術為何重要

來源：Chicago Humanities Festival

5月9日晚上七點，芝加哥人文節將在 Lakeview 的 Athenaeum Center 舉辦一場特別對談。廣播節目 Radiolab 的創辦人 Jad Abumrad，和漫畫家兼教育家 Lynda Barry，都是麥克阿瑟「天才獎」得主。

他們將用影像和聲音，聊藝術如何幫助我們面對恐懼、找回想像力。這是人文節春季 Lakeview Day 的一部分，也是天才獎四十五週年的紀念活動。有興趣的人別錯過。

On the evening of May 9th at 7 p.m., the Chicago Humanities Festival will host a special conversation at the Athenaeum Center in Lakeview. Jad Abumrad, founder of the radio program Radiolab, and Lynda Barry, cartoonist and educator, are both MacArthur Fellowship recipients. They will explore, through images and sound, how art helps us confront fear and reclaim our imagination. This event is part of the festival's spring Lakeview Day programming and marks the 45th anniversary of the MacArthur Fellowship. Don't miss it if you're interested.

一個人畫出北美所有口音的地圖

來源：aschmann.net

語言學家 Rick Aschmann 花了幾十年，用最簡單的畫圖軟體，一層一層地畫出北美洲每一種英語口音的分布地圖。網站上有大約九百段真人說話的錄音，從賽車手到政治人物都有。

這個網站的樣子像九〇年代的網頁，字很小，設計很舊，但裡面裝的是一個人對語言的全部熱情。四億人怎麼說同一個字，他都記下來了。

Linguist Rick Aschmann spent decades mapping every English dialect across North America using the simplest drawing software, layering together the geographic distribution of each regional accent. His website hosts roughly nine hundred audio recordings of real speakers—from race car drivers to politicians. The site looks like a relic from the 1990s, with tiny text and dated design, but it contains one man's complete passion for language. He documented how four hundred million people pronounce the same words in different ways.
