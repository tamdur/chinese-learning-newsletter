陳揚《現代四季》：台灣被遺忘的空靈之聲

來源：FOND/SOUND

一九八七年，台北的作曲家陳揚用兩台合成器（一種電子樂器），做出了《現代四季》這張專輯。他本來是為雲門舞集寫的兩首曲子，後來長成了一整張唱片。

這裡面幾乎沒有鼓聲，只有一片一片、慢慢飄動的聲音，像雲一樣。其中一首歌叫〈心洞〉，聽起來空空的，很安靜。那時候還沒有人把這種音樂叫做「氛圍音樂」，但陳揚已經走在很前面了。

In 1987, composer Chen Yang from Taipei created the album "Modern Four Seasons" using two synthesizers—electronic instruments that would become his signature. What started as two pieces written for the Cloud Gate Dance Theatre evolved into a complete album. It features almost no drums, only layered sounds that drift slowly like clouds. One track, "Heart Cave," sounds hollow and profoundly quiet. At the time, no one had yet coined the term "ambient music," yet Chen Yang was already pioneering the form.

開車過海：芬蘭最長的冰上道路走入歷史

來源：Europa Tips

每年冬天，芬蘭人會開車走過大約七公里的海面，到一座叫海盧奧托的小島。因為海水結成很厚的冰（至少七十公分厚），車子可以在上面跑，時速五十公里，車與車還要保持五十公尺的距離，冰才不會裂開。

但二零二六年是最後一次了。一座新的大橋蓋好以後，這條冰路就會永遠消失。以後想去島上看紅色的小木屋和一八七二年的老燈塔，一年四季都到得了。

Every winter, Finnish people drive across approximately seven kilometers of seawater to reach a small island called Hailuoto. The seawater freezes into remarkably thick ice—at least 70 centimeters deep—allowing vehicles to travel across it at 50 kilometers per hour, though drivers must maintain 50 meters distance from one another to prevent the ice from cracking. But 2026 will be the last time. Once a new bridge opens, this ice road will disappear forever. Afterward, those wanting to visit the island's distinctive red wooden houses and the old lighthouse dating from 1872 will be able to reach it in any season.

「魔法樹叢」的清晨：密西根湖畔的免費賞鳥之旅

來源：Chicago Ornithological Society

七月十一日星期六早上九點到中午，芝加哥鳥類學會要帶大家免費去賞鳥，不用報名。地點在密西根湖邊一塊叫「魔法樹叢」的小樹林。

這裡是候鳥（會飛很遠的鳥）飛過大湖時，少數可以停下來休息的地方。因為附近幾公里都沒有別的樹，有時會出現一些迷路、很少見的鳥，所以這塊看起來普通的樹叢，在賞鳥人心中很有名。

On Saturday, July 11th, from 9 AM to noon, the Chicago Ornithological Society will host a free bird-watching walk — no registration needed. The location is a small wooded grove by Lake Michigan known as the 'Magic Thicket.' It's one of the few places where migratory birds (birds that travel long distances) can pause and rest during their crossing of the Great Lake. Because the surrounding area for several kilometers has virtually no other trees, the grove sometimes attracts lost or rarely-spotted bird species. This seemingly ordinary patch of trees has become quite well-known among birdwatchers.

聽見地球的心跳：一個把地震變成音樂的網站

來源：Sounds of Seismic (allshookup.org)

有位藝術家叫SHOOK，花了三十年，把科學和音樂放在一起。他做的網站Sounds of Seismic，接上了全世界的地震感應器，把地球的搖動變成一直在變化的音樂。

地震的波動太慢了，人的耳朵聽不到，所以網站把它加快、切碎，讓我們聽見大地在地震後慢慢安靜下來，就像敲一下鐘，聲音慢慢消失一樣。

There is an artist named SHOOK who has spent three decades merging science and music. His website, Sounds of Seismic, connects to seismic sensors around the world, transforming Earth's tremors into constantly shifting music. Because seismic waves oscillate too slowly for human ears to detect, the website accelerates and segments them, allowing us to hear how the ground gradually quiets after an earthquake—like the slow fade of a struck bell.
