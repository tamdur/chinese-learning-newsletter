用噪音重建自己：午夜乒乓的《心建築》

來源：Mando Gap (Michael Hong)

台北樂團「午夜乒乓」在二零二三年推出第二張專輯《心建築》，還先提醒歌迷：這張可能「太吵」了。專輯一開場，就是尖叫和轟隆隆的吉他聲。

不過，比起二零一六年首張專輯用兩把吉他互相較勁，這次他們把舊架構整個拆掉，改用一圈一圈慢慢繞的旋律，把噪音當成支架（架子），撐起樂團最柔軟、最誠實的心事。

Taipei band Midnight Ping Pong released their second album, Heart Architecture, in 2023, but gave fans a fair warning first: this one might be "too loud." The album opens with shrieks and thunderous guitar walls. But compared to their 2016 debut—which showcased two guitars locked in battle—they've completely dismantled the old structure. This time, they've built with slowly spiraling, layered melodies, turning noise itself into scaffolding that supports the band's most tender and authentic emotions.

冰冷海底的幽靈船：芬蘭的水下時光膠囊

來源：Meriarkeologia.fi

一七七一年十月，一艘荷蘭商船「瑪麗亞號」在暴風雨中沉入芬蘭外海，船上載著要送給俄國女皇凱薩琳的名畫。它的位置消失了兩百多年，直到一九九九年才被潛水員找到。

波羅的海又冷又暗，鹽分低、氧氣也少，讓平常會啃木頭的蛀蟲活不下來，所以船身到今天還幾乎完好。現在它受到群島海國家公園保護，研究人員用立體影像慢慢記錄它。

In October 1771, a Dutch merchant ship named the Vrouw Maria sank during a storm off the Finnish coast, carrying valuable paintings intended as a gift for Russian Empress Catherine the Great. The ship's location was lost for over two centuries until divers discovered it in 1999. The Baltic Sea's cold, dark waters, combined with low salinity and minimal oxygen, create conditions hostile to the wood-eating shipworms that normally devastate submerged wooden vessels. Consequently, the hull has remained remarkably well preserved to this day. Now protected by the Archipelago Sea National Park, the wreck is being gradually documented by researchers using 3D imaging technology.

學看蜂王的健康：芝加哥蜂蜜合作社的養蜂課

來源：Chicago Honey Co-op

今年夏天到七月的週末，芝加哥蜂蜜合作社在西區的養蜂場開設養蜂體驗課。花九十五美元，就能拿到煙燻器和工具，親手打開一箱活生生的蜜蜂。

老師會教一種很難的本領：看懂蜂王健不健康、幼蟲排得好不好，好在蜂群分家逃走前先發現問題。不需要任何經驗，午餐大家帶菜分享。合作社每堂課還提供兩個免費名額。

This summer through July, on weekends, the Chicago Honey Co-op is offering beekeeping experience classes at its West Side apiary. For ninety-five dollars, you get a bee smoker and tools—and the chance to personally open a living hive. The instructor teaches a challenging skill: how to spot whether the queen bee is healthy and the larvae are properly arranged, so you can catch problems before the colony swarms. No experience necessary. Lunch is potluck-style. The co-op also provides two free spots per class.

藏在紐約書商倉庫裡的失落劍術手稿

來源：FencingClassics — The Secret History of the Sword

安伯格在一九九零年代辦過一份談劍術歷史的刊物，現在他一個人經營部落格，整理幾百年來的劍術教本和決鬥畫作。

最精彩的是一份十六世紀的劍術與摔角手稿。它曾被誤認為是畫家杜勒的作品，二零零五年在紐約一位書商的倉庫裡意外被人發現。學者查出它出自德國一位貴族梅爾，他從一五四零年代開始收購舊劍術書，想編出當時最豪華的武術大全。

Amberger published a journal on fencing history in the 1990s. Now he runs a solo blog cataloging centuries of sword-fighting manuals and paintings of duels. The most spectacular is a sixteenth-century manuscript on swordplay and wrestling. Once mistaken for a work by painter Albrecht Dürer, it was unexpectedly discovered in 2005 in a New York bookseller's warehouse. Scholars traced it to a German nobleman named Mair, who starting in the 1540s began acquiring old fencing texts, aiming to compile the most elaborate martial arts encyclopedia of his time.
