黃韻玲的《平凡》：藏在鋼琴裡的熱帶節奏

來源：FOND/SOUND

一九九一年九月，台北的小個子鋼琴手黃韻玲推出了第四張專輯《平凡》。大家本來以為她只會唱傷心情歌，沒想到她是一位真正的作曲家。

在〈胡思亂想〉和〈遠方〉這幾首歌裡，她把拉丁和加勒比海的節奏輕輕放進國語靈魂樂裡。三十多年後再聽，那份爵士味的從容依然很迷人。

In September 1991, the diminutive Taipei pianist Huang Yun-ling released her fourth album, Ordinary. Everyone had assumed she only sang sad love songs, never realizing she was a genuine composer.

On tracks like "Wandering Thoughts" and "Faraway," she gently folded Latin and Caribbean rhythms into Mandarin soul. More than thirty years on, that jazzy, unhurried elegance remains utterly captivating.

四千片松木編成的海邊外套：赫爾辛基的Löyly

來源：ArchDaily / Avanto Architects

在芬蘭首都赫爾辛基市中心南邊兩公里的海邊，有一座奇特的建築叫Löyly。建築師用了超過四千片處理過的松木片，把裡面的桑拿房和餐廳包起來，像一件木頭外套。

這件外套同時是一個大階梯，人人都能爬上去看海。三月天還很冷，海面上會飄著大塊浮冰，很適合坐在屋裡喝一碗熱熱的鮭魚湯看風景。

Two kilometers south of downtown Helsinki, the Finnish capital, there stands a peculiar building by the sea called Löyly. The architects used over four thousand treated pine slats to wrap the saunas and restaurant inside, like a wooden coat.

This coat is also a giant staircase, which anyone can climb to look out at the sea. March is still cold, and large chunks of ice drift on the water's surface — a perfect time to sit indoors with a hot bowl of salmon soup and take in the view.

四月十一日：把家庭相簿當成小說的工作坊

來源：Museum of Contemporary Photography

這個星期六，也就是四月十一日中午十二點到兩點半，芝加哥當代攝影博物館會舉辦一場免費的小型工作坊。

藝術家Matthew Finley會帶大家看他怎麼用別人丟掉的老照片編出一個假的人生故事，這就是他的書《An Impossibly Normal Life》。活動在博物館二樓的版畫研究室，記得先上網報名。

This Saturday, April 11th, from noon to 2:30 PM, the Museum of Contemporary Photography in Chicago will host a free small workshop.

Artist Matthew Finley will show everyone how he uses old photographs that others have thrown away to construct a fabricated life story — this is his book "An Impossibly Normal Life." The event takes place in the print study room on the museum's second floor; remember to register online in advance.

把快要消失的聲音收進博物館：Conserve the Sound

來源：Conserve the Sound

兩位德國人Daniel Chun和Jan Derksen從二零一三年開始做一件很浪漫的事。他們把快要消失的老東西的聲音一個一個錄下來，放在網站上。

轉盤電話、隨身聽、老打字機、幻燈片機、還有舊手機的按鍵聲，每一樣都配上乾淨的照片和短短的人物訪問影片。

Two Germans, Daniel Chun and Jan Derksen, have been doing something very romantic since 2013. One by one, they record the sounds of old things that are about to disappear, and put them on a website.

Rotary telephones, Walkmans, old typewriters, slide projectors, and the button-press sounds of old mobile phones — each one is paired with clean photographs and short interview videos with people.
