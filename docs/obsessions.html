月琴上的藍調：街頭歌手陳達

來源：Wikipedia / Taipei Times

一九六七年夏天，兩隊台灣學生背著錄音機走遍鄉下，錄下一千多首民間歌謠，這就是「民歌採集運動」。

在恆春，他們遇到看不見的街頭歌手陳達。他抱著月琴（一種兩條弦的樂器），用沙啞的聲音唱出〈思想起〉。那自由的曲調，像美國的藍調（一種憂傷的音樂）。

一九七七年，他錄下唱片《民族樂手：陳達和他的歌》，成為台灣民謠最重要的聲音。

In the summer of 1967, two teams of Taiwanese students traveled throughout the countryside with recording machines, capturing over a thousand folk songs. This was the beginning of the Folk Song Collection Movement. In Hengchun, they encountered a blind street singer named Chen Da. Holding his moon lute—a two-stringed instrument—he sang 'Si Xiang Qi' in a raspy voice. The free-flowing melody was like American blues, that mournful kind of music. In 1977, he recorded an album titled 'Folk Musicians: Chen Da and His Songs,' and became the most important voice in Taiwanese folk music.

在貨櫃裡熟成的芬蘭威士忌

來源：Teerenpeli Distillery, Lahti

芬蘭湖區的城市拉赫蒂，有一家叫 Teerenpeli 的酒廠。從二○○二年開始，他們用當地大麥和冰河地下水，做出得過國際大獎的威士忌。

特別的是，他們不用石頭倉庫，而是把酒桶放進貨櫃裡。芬蘭冬天很冷、夏天溫暖，一冷一熱之間，酒熟成得更快，這是蘇格蘭做不到的。

酒廠在赫爾辛基北邊約一百公里，往拉普蘭的路上，正好可以順道參觀，喝上一杯。

In Lahti, a city in Finland's lake region, there's a distillery called Teerenpeli that has been producing internationally award-winning whisky since 2002, using local barley and glacial groundwater. What makes them distinctive is their unconventional approach: rather than aging barrels in traditional stone warehouses, they use shipping containers. The benefit is tangible—Finland's extreme swings between harsh winters and warm summers accelerate maturation far more effectively than Scotland's milder climate permits. Positioned about 100 kilometers north of Helsinki on the route toward Lapland, the distillery makes for a perfect stop on the way north, well worth a visit and a dram.

壞人的故事：芝加哥記者走上舞台

來源：Athenaeum Center for Thought & Culture

九月十八日晚上，芝加哥故事節回到 Athenaeum 劇院，今年的主題是「壞人」：我們害怕什麼、誤會什麼，又會變成什麼樣的人。

平常躲在文章後面的記者，這次自己站上台。來自 WBEZ、芝加哥論壇報、Block Club 和太陽報的記者，說出真實的親身故事，中間還有舞者、喜劇演員和音樂人的表演。

晚上七點開門，八點準時開演，早鳥票三十美元起。

On the evening of September 18th, the Chicago Story Fest returns to the Athenaeum Theater with this year's theme: "Bad People"—exploring what we fear, what we misunderstand, and what kind of people we might become. For once, reporters who usually work behind the scenes will take the stage themselves. Journalists from WBEZ, the Chicago Tribune, Block Club, and the Sun-Times will share real personal stories, punctuated by performances from dancers, comedians, and musicians. Doors open at 7 PM, the show begins at 8 PM sharp, with early-bird tickets starting at $30.

椅子底下的祕密：七千五百件木雕

來源：Misericords of the World

中世紀的教堂裡，唱詩班要站著很久。把椅子翻起來，底下有一塊小木板，可以偷偷靠一下，名字叫 misericord，意思是「憐憫」（體諒別人的心）。

因為木板朝下，神父看不見，木匠就刻上頑皮的圖案：美人魚、長滿樹葉的綠臉人，還有一隻對著鵝講道的狐狸。這些在教堂別的地方都不准出現。

英國人 Dominic Strange 一個人跑遍近一百五十座教堂，拍下並整理了超過七千五百件這樣的木雕。

In medieval churches, choirs stood for hours at a time. Flip up the seat of a chair, and beneath it sits a small wooden ledge—called a misericord, meaning "mercy" (compassion for others). Invisible to the priest, the carvings could be delightfully irreverent: mermaids, green-faced men sprouting leaves, a fox preaching to a goose. These images appeared nowhere else in the church. Englishman Dominic Strange visited nearly 150 churches, single-handedly photographing and cataloging over 7,500 such wooden carvings.
