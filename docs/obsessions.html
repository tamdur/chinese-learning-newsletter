桑布伊：用母語唱到金曲獎最高殿堂

來源：Wind Music / Taiwan ASEAN Music Action

桑布伊是台東卑南族歌手，他只用卑南族的語言唱歌。他說，聽的人不需要懂每一個字，只要用心去感受就好。

二〇二一年，他的第三張專輯《得力量》拿下金曲獎年度最佳專輯。這是他第二次得到這個大獎。專輯裡有電子音樂、搖滾、非洲風格，還有管弦樂，但每首歌的根都在部落的古老吟唱裡。

Sangpuy is a Puyuma indigenous singer from Taitung. He sings only in the Puyuma language. He says listeners don't need to understand every word — they just need to feel the music with their hearts.

In 2021, his third album "Pulu'em" (Gain Strength) won Album of the Year at the Golden Melody Awards. It was his second time winning this top prize. The album blends electronic music, rock, African-influenced sounds, and orchestral arrangements, but the root of every song lies in the ancient chanting traditions of his tribe.

阿爾托之城：世界上最多大師建築的小城

來源：Visit Jyväskylä / Aalto2 Museum

芬蘭湖區有一座叫于韋斯屈萊的小城，這裡是全世界阿爾瓦．阿爾托建築最密集的地方，超過三十棟建築都出自他的手。

最新開幕的「阿爾托二號」博物館，把兩座他設計的老建築用一個白色玻璃中庭連在一起。三月的芬蘭還是冬天，你可以在雪地裡散步，走進一棟又一棟大師的作品。

In Finland's lakeland region, there is a small city called Jyväskylä. It has the world's densest concentration of buildings designed by Alvar Aalto — more than thirty structures bear his mark.

The newly opened "Aalto2" museum connects two of his older buildings with a white glass atrium. In March, Finland is still deep in winter. You can walk through the snow, stepping into one masterwork after another.

美食界的奧斯卡回來了：詹姆斯．比爾德獎週末

來源：James Beard Foundation

六月十三日到十五日，美國最重要的美食獎「詹姆斯．比爾德獎」又要在芝加哥舉辦了。星期一晚上的大典在歌劇院舉行，全國最好的廚師都會來。

典禮結束後，聯合車站會變成一個大型試吃會，得獎廚師們會現場做菜給你吃。週末還有免費的公開座談會。今年是第一次在網路上直播。

From June 13 to 15, America's most important food award — the James Beard Award — returns to Chicago. Monday evening's main ceremony takes place at the Lyric Opera, drawing the nation's finest chefs.

After the ceremony, Union Station transforms into a grand tasting event where award-winning chefs cook for you on the spot. Free public panel discussions run throughout the weekend. This year marks the first time the ceremony will be live-streamed.

一位哲學教授的打字機王國

來源：The Typewriter Database

理查．波特是美國澤維爾大學的哲學教授，從一九九四年開始收集打字機。他建了一個網站，收集了全世界最完整的打字機序號資料。

世界各地的收藏家把自己的打字機照片和序號寄給他，一起為這個正在消失的機器做人口普查。三十年過去了，這個網站已經是打字機世界裡最大的寶庫。

Richard Polt is a philosophy professor at Xavier University in the United States. He has been collecting typewriters since 1994. He built a website that houses the world's most complete collection of typewriter serial numbers.

Collectors from around the world send him photos and serial numbers of their machines, together conducting a census of a vanishing technology. Thirty years on, the site has become the greatest treasure trove in the typewriter world.
