客家水牛的新靈魂之聲

來源：Taiwan Beats

在新竹，有一位客家歌手叫黃子軒。他的專輯《牛騷》把客家民謠和neo-soul、電子音樂混在一起，聽起來很新鮮。「牛騷」是客家話裡水牛低沉叫聲的意思，帶著固執（不輕易改變）的力量。

黃子軒在第三十六屆金曲獎拿下最佳客語歌手。他想告訴大家：客家文化不是博物館裡的展品，而是活的、有世界觀的聲音。

In Hsinchu, there's a Hakka singer named Huang Zixuan. His album "Niu Sao" blends Hakka folk ballads with neo-soul and electronic music in a way that sounds entirely fresh. "Niu Sao" is the Hakka word for the deep, guttural call of a water buffalo—it carries a stubborn, unyielding kind of power. Huang Zixuan won Best Hakka Singer at the 36th Golden Melody Awards. He wants people to understand: Hakka culture isn't a museum artifact gathering dust. It's alive, it has a worldview, and it has a voice.

鐵匠村的冬日寂靜

來源：Finland Naturally

從赫爾辛基往西開車大約一小時，有一座叫Fiskars的小村莊。它在一六四九年建成，最早是一座煉鐵（把鐵礦變成鐵）工廠。現在，一百多位藝術家和設計師住在那些紅磚老房子裡工作。

三月去最好：夏天的遊客都走了，你可以安靜地走在結霜的小路上，逛陶瓷工作室、鐵匠鋪和老穀倉咖啡廳。工業歷史、當代設計和芬蘭的深冬寧靜，在這裡同時遇見。

About an hour's drive west of Helsinki is a small village called Fiskars. Founded in 1649, it began as an iron smelting factory—a place where iron ore was transformed into usable iron. Today, more than a hundred artists and designers live and work in those red-brick historic buildings. March is the best time to visit: the summer tourists have gone, and you can walk quietly along the frost-covered paths, browsing ceramic studios, blacksmith shops, and converted barn cafés. Here, industrial history, contemporary design, and the deep winter stillness of Finland converge.

不怕斷糧的紀錄片節

來源：Block Club Chicago

Doc10紀錄片影展從四月二十四日到五月三日，在芝加哥北邊的Davis Theater（Lincoln Ave. 4614號）放映十二部長片。主題包括越戰中第一支黑人精英特種部隊，以及在加薩工作的志願醫生。

去年影展期間，聯邦政府突然取消了補助金。主辦單位沒有縮小規模，反而靠個人捐款多辦了一整週。明天開幕，值得一看。

Doc10 Documentary Film Festival runs from April 24 to May 3, screening twelve feature films at Davis Theater (4614 Lincoln Ave.) on Chicago's North Shore. The program spans themes including the first Black elite special forces unit in the Vietnam War, and volunteer doctors working in Gaza. Last year, the federal government abruptly cut the festival's funding. Rather than scale back, organizers expanded the program by an entire week through individual donations. Opens tomorrow—worth checking out.

十萬頁的個人電腦革命

來源：byte.tsundoku.io

有人把BYTE雜誌全部二百七十七期——從一九七五年到一九九八年——做成了一張可以放大縮小的巨大畫布。超過十萬頁，像衛星地圖一樣可以自由移動和放大。

你可以從遠處看到二十多年的封面排成一片，也可以一路放大到一九八一年某篇Pascal教學的每一個字。一個人做的網站，只因為熱愛。

Someone has taken all 277 issues of BYTE magazine — from 1975 to 1998 — and created a single zoomable canvas. Over 100,000 pages, navigable and scalable like a satellite map. You can view two decades of covers arrayed across the canvas from a distance, or zoom in all the way to read every character of a Pascal tutorial from 1981. One person built the website, driven purely by passion.
