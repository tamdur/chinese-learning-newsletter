用外婆的話唱電子歌

來源：Asian Pop Weekly

排灣族歌手阿爆發現，自己的族語說得不好，連媽媽都笑她。從2017年起，她花了兩年，和媽媽、外婆長長地聊天，把快要消失的族語錄下來。

她把這些話放進電子節拍（音樂的拍子）裡，做成專輯《母親的舌頭》（Kinakaian）。2020年，它成為第一張拿下金曲獎「年度專輯」的原住民族語唱片。

這份光榮也提醒大家：會說族語的人，已經越來越少了。

Paiwan singer A-Bao discovered she wasn't fluent in her tribe's language—even her mother laughed at her. Starting in 2017, she spent two years having long conversations with her mother and grandmother, recording their ancestral language before it disappeared.

She embedded these recordings into electronic beats to create the album "Mother's Tongue" (Kinakaian). In 2020, it became the first indigenous-language album to win the Golden Melody Award for Album of the Year.

This recognition also serves as a reminder: speakers of indigenous tribal languages are becoming increasingly rare.

三國交會處的極光小村

來源：University of Helsinki

在芬蘭西北角，有一個小村子叫 Kilpisjärvi。它夾在挪威和瑞典中間，正好在極光常出現的地帶下方，天空也常常很晴朗。

村旁的 Saana 山高1029公尺，是薩米人（北歐的原住民）心中的聖山。赫爾辛基大學在山下設了研究站，科學家在這裡研究旅鼠（一種北方的小動物）。

附近還有一個石堆，標出芬蘭、挪威、瑞典三國相交的地方。三月的晴朗夜晚，很適合在這裡等極光。

In the northwestern corner of Finland sits a small village named Kilpisjärvi, wedged between Norway and Sweden. It lies just below the zone where auroras commonly appear, and the skies there are often remarkably clear.

The Saana mountain beside the village rises 1,029 meters and holds sacred significance for the Sámi people, the indigenous inhabitants of northern Europe. Helsinki University has established a research station at the mountain's base, where scientists study lemmings, small arctic animals.

A stone cairn nearby marks the exact point where Finland, Norway, and Sweden converge. Clear March evenings make it an ideal location to watch for the northern lights.

在老教堂裡聽爵士樂

來源：Hyde Park Jazz Festival

海德公園爵士音樂節今年20歲了！9月26日到27日，37場表演分散在這一區有名的老建築裡，像美術館、教堂，還有一家舊銀行。

星期六晚上10點，薩克斯風（一種吹奏樂器）名家 Joshua Redman 會在洛克斐勒教堂獨奏一小時。高高的石頭屋頂，讓聲音聽起來特別飽滿。

所有表演都免費，主辦單位建議每人捐10美元。星期天的最後一場，會在戶外大草地上演出。

The Hyde Park Jazz Festival is celebrating its 20th anniversary this year! From September 26 to 27, 37 performances will take place across the neighborhood's iconic buildings—including art museums, churches, and a historic bank.

On Saturday night at 10 p.m., acclaimed saxophonist Joshua Redman will perform a solo recital for one hour at Rockefeller Chapel. The soaring stone ceiling creates particularly resonant acoustics.

All performances are free, though organizers suggest a $10 donation per person. The festival concludes with a final performance on Sunday outdoors on a large lawn.

一位教授的繩結寶庫

來源：Peter Suber (Earlham College)

Peter Suber 是一位哲學教授。他最有名的事，是推動讓大家免費讀到科學論文。

但從1996年起，他也一直在照顧一個網頁，叫「網上的繩結」（Knots on the Web）。裡面整理了打結的方法，也有繩結的數學、藝術、軟體和歷史。

他還自己發明一套符號，能用文字寫出任何一個結該怎麼打。快三十年了，這個網頁還在，像一座愛繩子的人留下的秘密花園。

Peter Suber is a philosophy professor, best known for championing free public access to scientific papers.

But since 1996, he has also been quietly maintaining a website called "Knots on the Web." It catalogues methods for tying knots and covers their mathematics, art, software, and history.

He even developed his own notation system—one that can describe, in text, how to tie any knot. Nearly thirty years later, the website endures, a secret garden left behind by someone truly devoted to rope.
