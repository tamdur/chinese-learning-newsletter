江文也的《台灣舞曲》：被遺忘的奧運獎牌

來源：Music Tales

一九三六年，德國柏林奧運舉辦藝術比賽。在台灣出生的作曲家江文也，用一首《台灣舞曲》得到了榮譽獎，成為第一位得獎的亞洲人。

他十三歲就離開台灣，卻把原住民舞蹈的節奏，和新潮的管弦樂（大型樂團的音樂）合在一起，寫出了他心中的島嶼。

後來他搬到北京，命運坎坷（很不順利），一九八三年去世時幾乎被人遺忘。如今他的作品重新錄音，終於再次被人聽見。

In 1936, the Berlin Olympic Games hosted an art competition. Chiang Wen-yeh, a composer born in Taiwan, won an honorable mention with his composition "Taiwan Dance," becoming the first Asian to receive the award. Though he left Taiwan at just thirteen, he wove the rhythms of indigenous dance together with contemporary orchestral music—creating what he envisioned as his island home. Later, he moved to Beijing, where his life took difficult turns, and when he died in 1983, he had largely faded from memory. Now, with his works newly recorded, his music is finally being heard again.

走進芬蘭最後一座玻璃工廠，看花瓶誕生

來源：Iittala Village

在赫爾辛基（芬蘭首都）北邊約一百二十五公里的小村伊塔拉，有芬蘭僅存的玻璃工廠。平日免費開放，遊客可以站在高台上往下看。

六、七位師傅一起工作，花十個小時、十二道步驟，才能做出一個有名的阿爾托花瓶。每位師傅都要學五年才能上手。

工廠裡藏著兩百多種顏色的配方，有的花了好幾年才調出來。這裡不只是工廠，更像一座活的實驗室。

About 125 kilometers north of Helsinki, Finland's capital, lies the small village of Iittala, home to Finland's last remaining glassworks. The factory is open to the public free of charge on weekdays, and visitors can watch from an elevated platform. Six or seven master craftsmen work together, spending ten hours and completing twelve steps to produce a single renowned Aalto vase. Each craftsman requires five years of training before mastering the craft. The factory safeguards formulas for over two hundred colors, some of which took years to develop. This place is more than just a factory—it's a living laboratory.

三天寫歌營：老城民謠學校的創作盛會

來源：Old Town School of Folk Music

六月五日到七日，芝加哥的老城民謠學校（Old Town School）要舉辦第二屆寫歌論壇。地點在林肯廣場的校區，林肯大道四五四四號。

這三天有教人寫歌的課、開放麥克風、現場錄製的廣播節目，還有晚上的演唱會。

星期五由小提琴手貝曼開場，談「寫歌裡的詩意」；星期六晚上由普格和莫蘭壓軸（最後登場）。可買三日通票，也能單買一場。

From June 5th to 7th, the Old Town School in Chicago will host its second annual Songwriting Forum. The event takes place at the Lincoln Square campus, located at 4544 North Lincoln Avenue. Over the three days, there will be songwriting workshops, open mic sessions, live radio broadcasts, and evening concerts. Fiddle player Rachel Baiman will open on Friday, discussing "Poetry in Songwriting," while Joe Pug and John Moreland will headline Saturday night. Three-day passes are available, as well as individual show tickets.

海洋發光網站：一位科學家的二十五年筆記

來源：The Bioluminescence Web Page (UCSB / MBARI)

海洋生物學家哈達克從一九九○年代起，自己動手做了一個網站，記錄海裡會發光的生物，一做就是二十五年。

這裡不只講水母。有一種黑龍魚會發出紅光，別的動物看不見，就像牠私人的手電筒；魷魚會讓肚子發亮，藏起自己的影子。

網站還說了一種少見的「乳海」：大量細菌一起發光，把好幾千平方公里的海面都點亮。讀它，就像翻看科學家的私人筆記本。

Marine biologist Steve Haddock has been maintaining a website documenting bioluminescent creatures in the ocean since the 1990s—a quarter-century project entirely of his own making. It covers far more than jellyfish. There's a black dragonfish that emits red light invisible to other animals, like a private flashlight; squid that illuminate their bellies to hide their shadows. The site also describes a rare phenomenon called "milky seas": vast colonies of bacteria glowing together, illuminating thousands of square kilometers of ocean surface. Reading it feels like paging through a scientist's personal notebook.
