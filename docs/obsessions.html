台灣迪斯可：被埋了四十年的迷幻放克

來源：Teleport City

「台灣迪斯可」是一張二〇二〇年發行的合輯（把很多首歌收在一起的唱片），裡面全是一九七〇到八〇年代初，台灣女歌手唱的放克和迷幻搖滾。

像田路路的歌，吉他聲又粗又糊，聽起來不太像跳舞的音樂，反而更像特攝英雄的主題曲。

這些歌當年被國語情歌蓋過去，幾乎沒人記得。後來是挖黑膠唱片的人一張一張找回來，才讓它們重見天日。

"Taiwan Disco" is a compilation album released in 2020 that collects funk and psychedelic rock songs sung by Taiwanese female vocalists from the 1970s to early 1980s. Songs like those by Tian Lu-Lu feature guitar sounds that are thick and murky—they don't really sound like dance music at all, but rather resemble theme songs from tokusatsu hero series. These tracks were overshadowed by Mandarin love songs at the time and were almost completely forgotten. It was only later, when vinyl record collectors dug them up one by one, that they saw the light of day again.

三個人一起織的毛衣

來源：Finnish Heritage Agency Living Heritage Wiki

芬蘭西岸有個小鎮叫柯斯奈斯，人很少，講的是瑞典語。從一八七〇年代起，那裡的人就一直織同一款毛衣：米白色的底，加上紅、綠、黃的星星和跳舞的小女孩。

最特別的是做法。傳統上三個人一起做一件，膝蓋對著膝蓋圍成一圈織；肩膀、腰和袖口則改用鉤針（用有小勾子的針勾出花樣），這是別的地方沒有的手藝。

這款毛衣已經列入芬蘭的文化資產名單，鎮上的協會還在教新的織者，好讓這一百五十年不要斷掉。

On the west coast of Finland sits a small town called Korsnäs, sparsely populated and Swedish-speaking. Since the 1870s, its residents have been knitting the same style of sweater: a cream-colored base adorned with red, green, and yellow stars and dancing little girls. What makes it truly distinctive is the technique. Traditionally, three people work together on a single sweater, sitting knee to knee in a circle as they knit. The shoulders, waist, and cuffs are finished using crochet hooks—a craft unique to this place—to create the pattern. This sweater style is now inscribed on Finland's cultural heritage list, and the town's association continues to teach new knitters, working to keep this 150-year tradition alive.

九月三十日，帶你的蛇來開會

來源：Chicago Herpetological Society

芝加哥爬蟲學會（研究蛇、蜥蜴、烏龜和青蛙的團體）從一九七〇年代就在了。每個月最後一個星期三晚上七點半到九點半，他們在諾特巴特自然博物館開會。

任何人都能走進去，不用是會員，也不用錢。來演講的是世界各地的學者，主題可能是保護毒蛇，也可能是怎麼養育稀有的陸龜。開講前，會員常常把自己養的蛇和蜥蜴帶來給大家看。

這個月的聚會是九月三十日。

The Chicago Herpetological Society, a group dedicated to studying snakes, lizards, turtles, and frogs, has been around since the 1970s. Every month on the last Wednesday from 7:30 to 9:30 PM, they hold meetings at the Notebaert Nature Museum. Anyone can attend—you don't need to be a member, and there's no admission fee. The speakers are scholars from around the world, discussing topics that range from snake conservation to raising rare terrestrial turtles. Before the lecture, members often bring their own snakes and lizards to show everyone. This month's gathering is September 30th.

一個人收集了四百首國歌

來源：nationalanthems.info

從一九九九年開始，加拿大研究者肯德爾一個人做了一個網站，收集四百多個國家、地區，還有已經消失的國家的國歌。每一首都有樂譜、錄音、歌詞和翻譯。

他還記下二十六首「沒有歌詞」的國歌，西班牙就是其中一個。網站上甚至有西蘭公國的國歌。那是英國外海一座二戰防空平台上的「國家」，沒有人承認它。

肯德爾還自己造了一個字，來稱呼這個嗜好：國歌學。

Since 1999, a Canadian researcher named Kendall has single-handedly built a website collecting the national anthems of over four hundred countries and regions—including those of nations that have ceased to exist. Each anthem comes with sheet music, recordings, lyrics, and translations. He has also documented twenty-six "lyric-free" national anthems, Spain being one of them. The website even features Sealand's national anthem—a "nation" based on an abandoned World War II anti-aircraft platform off the British coast that no one officially recognizes. Kendall has even coined his own term for this passion: anthemology.
