抽屜裡的十年：一卷舊錄音終於出土

來源：Manic Sheep (Bandcamp)

二○一四年，台北樂團 Manic Sheep 錄了九首歌，卻一直沒有完成。吉他手洪宇離開後，這些歌就躺在他的硬碟裡，一躺就是十年。

二○二四年，主唱羅郁婷和鼓手吳柏廷找回洪宇，三個人一起把舊錄音做完，取名《Rewind 2014》。

前半張是他們最有名的瞪鞋（吉他聲很厚、像蒙著一層霧）風格；後半張卻轉成冷硬的工業聲響。十年的等待，讓舊歌長出了新的面貌。

In 2014, Taipei band Manic Sheep recorded nine songs that never got finished. When guitarist Hong Yu left the project, those tracks were abandoned on his hard drive—for ten years. Then in 2024, vocalist Lo Yu-ting and drummer Wu Bo-ting tracked him down. The three reunited and completed the old recordings, releasing them as Rewind 2014. The first half showcases the shoegaze sound the band is known for: thick, layered guitar tones that feel shrouded in fog. The second half takes a sharp turn into cold, industrial textures. A decade of waiting has given these old songs an entirely new face.

冰湖上的駕訓班：芬蘭的賽車冠軍從哪裡來

來源：Visit Finland / Lapland Driving, Rovaniemi

芬蘭全國四十五萬公里的路，有三十五萬公里沒有鋪柏油，全是碎石子。很多小孩還沒考駕照，就先在結冰的湖面上學會開車。

所以這個只有五百五十萬人的國家，出了三位 F1 世界冠軍、七位拉力賽（在碎石山路上比快的賽車）冠軍。

在北邊的羅瓦涅米，離市區十分鐘就有一所拉力學校，十二月開到三月底。教練先教你在冰道上煞車、轉彎、滑行，再讓你真的開一趟。

Finland has 450,000 kilometers of roads, but 350,000 of them are unpaved—nothing but gravel. Many children learn to drive on frozen lake surfaces before they even get their licenses. That's why this nation of just 5.5 million people has produced three F1 world champions and seven rally racing champions (drivers competing for speed on gravel mountain roads). In Rovaniemi in the north, just ten minutes from the city center, there's a rally school that runs from December through March. Instructors first teach you how to brake, turn, and slide on ice tracks, then send you out to drive for real.

鼓聲與橘衣：芝加哥的原住民聚會日

來源：Chicago American Indian Community Collaborative

九月二十六日，芝加哥美國原住民健康服務中心會在德文大道的 Caldwell Woods 舉辦一年一次的「Powwow」（原住民的傳統歌舞聚會）。

上午十一點到下午五點，免費，隨時可以走進去。現場有傳統舞蹈、鼓隊、原住民食物和說故事的人。

聚會的名字叫「每個孩子都重要」，來自橘衣日運動，紀念從前被送進寄宿學校的原住民孩子。所以這一天是慶祝，也是記得。

On September 26th, Chicago's American Indian Health Service Center will host its annual Powwow at Caldwell Woods on Devon Avenue. Running from 11 a.m. to 5 p.m., the event is free and open to drop-in visitors. You'll find traditional dancing, drum circles, Native American food, and storytellers. The gathering is titled "Every Child Matters," drawing from the Orange Shirt Day movement, which commemorates Indigenous children who were sent to residential boarding schools. So this day is both celebration and remembrance.

地底下的鬼車站：一個人的二十五年記錄

來源：Abandoned Stations (Joseph Brennan / Columbia University)

高中時，Joseph Brennan 每天從紐約郊外搭地鐵上學。後來他花了二十幾年，把紐約地底那些廢棄車站一個一個寫下來，放在自己的哥倫比亞大學網頁上。

像市政廳站，彎彎的月台又漂亮又華麗，一九四五年關閉，現在只有博物館的少數導覽進得去；還有一九六二年停用的沃斯街站。

每個車站都有自己的頁面：老照片、手畫的軌道圖，還有從當年公文裡找出來的施工紀錄。

In high school, Joseph Brennan commuted daily by subway from the New York suburbs. Later, he devoted over twenty years to systematically documenting the city's abandoned subway stations and posting them on his personal Columbia University website. Consider City Hall Station: its gracefully curved, ornate platforms closed in 1945 and today remain accessible only through select museum tours. Or Worth Street Station, decommissioned in 1962. Each station has its own page, complete with archival photographs, hand-drawn track diagrams, and construction records he unearthed from historical documents.
