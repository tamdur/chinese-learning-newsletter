廟裡的回音：夢東的台灣謎景

來源：Bandcamp (Mong Tong / WV Sorcerer Productions)

台北的洪御、洪郡奇兩兄弟，花了半年多，走遍台灣最奇特的廟：地獄主題的遊樂園、路邊小廟，還有肉身菩薩（不會腐壞的高僧遺體）。他們把這些做成一本書，也做成二〇一九年的第一張專輯《台灣謎景》。

音樂裡，厚重的電子琴聲混著走音的誦經錄音，還有七〇年代驅魔（趕走鬼怪）電影的對白。慢速的迷幻搖滾把聲音包起來，每首歌都不像歌，比較像一間你不小心走進去的密室。

Two Taipei brothers, Hong Yu and Hong Junqi, spent over half a year traveling through Taiwan's most extraordinary temples: hell-themed amusement parks, small roadside shrines, and mummified Buddhist monks—venerable remains that never decay. They turned their findings into a book and crafted them into their 2019 debut album, Music from Taiwan Mystery. The music mixes heavy electronic organ sounds with out-of-tune chanting recordings and dialogue from 1970s exorcism films. Slow psychedelic rock envelops the sound, making each track feel less like a song than like a hidden chamber you've stumbled into by accident.

二十公斤紅莓，換來全芬蘭的自由

來源：Visit Finland / thisisFINLAND

一九一四年九月四日，寡婦（丈夫過世的女子）林格倫在芬蘭塞馬湖區的小島上，採了二十公斤越橘（一種紅色小漿果）。地主說果子長在他的地上，全部搶走。

林格倫打了五年官司，一路告到最高法院，贏了。這個判決成為芬蘭「人人共享的權利」的起點，一九二〇年寫進法律。今天任何人都能在別人的土地上採果子、健行、露營。三月來度蜜月的人，走出湖邊小屋就能進森林，不必問誰。

On September 4, 1914, a widow named Lindgren gathered twenty kilograms of lingonberries on a small island in Finland's Saimaa Lake region. The landowner claimed the berries grew on his land and took them all. Lindgren fought the case for five years, taking it all the way to Finland's Supreme Court—and won. This verdict became the foundation of Finland's "Right to Roam," which was written into law in 1920. Today, anyone can pick berries, hike, and camp on someone else's land. A couple on their honeymoon in March can step out of a lakeside cottage and walk straight into the forest without asking anyone.

公園裡的望遠鏡：南區的免費星空夜

來源：Chicago Park District

九月二十五日星期五，晚上七點到九點，「芝加哥天文人」把專業望遠鏡搬到南區的格雷佛公園（西一〇二街一五一八號）。這個團體全部由志工組成。

活動免費，任何年紀都能來。他們會教你怎麼用望遠鏡，帶大家看當晚出現的行星和星團，還會講幾段短短的天文課。這個團體幾乎每星期換一座公園，不用自備器材，也不用懂天文，天氣好就行。

On Friday, September 25th, from 7 to 9 PM, "Chicago Astronomers" will set up professional telescopes at Graver Park on the South Side (1518 West 102nd Street). The organization is run entirely by volunteers. The event is free and open to people of all ages. They'll show you how to use a telescope, point out the planets and star clusters visible that evening, and share a few short astronomy talks. The group visits a different park almost every week — no need to bring equipment or know anything about astronomy, just hope for clear skies.

三千一百個嘔吐袋：一座網路上的博物館

來源：Air Sickness Bag Virtual Museum

一九八一年，美國波士頓的席柏格第一次搭聯合航空，順手收了一個沒用過的嘔吐袋，從此停不下來。一九九七年起，他把收藏放上網站，照航空公司排好，每個袋子都註明年代、誰送的，還加上他自己的評語。

現在超過三千一百個。袋子收在他家，內容也延伸到船、火車、恐怖片、電玩，甚至選舉。喜歡這件事的人自稱「袋迷」。

In 1981, Silberberg from Boston took United Airlines for the first time and casually picked up an unused airsickness bag—and hasn't stopped since. Beginning in 1997, he posted his collection online, organized by airline, with each bag dated, sourced, and annotated in his own voice. Today it exceeds 3,100.

The collection lives in his home, but has since expanded to include bags from ships, trains, horror films, video games, and even political campaigns. Enthusiasts call themselves "baggists."
