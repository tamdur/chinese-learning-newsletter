9m88的靈魂樂電台

來源：Bandcamp / NME

9m88本名湯毓綺，原本在台北念服裝設計。25歲那年，她跑到紐約的爵士樂學校學唱歌。2018年，她翻唱日本老歌〈Plastic Love〉，在網路上爆紅。

2022年的第二張專輯《9m88 Radio》是她自己製作的。她找來五個國家的音樂人合作，像美國饒舌（一種用說的唱法）歌手Oddisee和韓國歌手SUMIN。

最特別的是，有些歌差點被丟掉。大學時錄的放克（節奏很強的舞曲）小歌〈Love Is So Cruel〉，和一首差點不要的〈Star〉，最後都成了專輯裡最好聽的歌。

9m88's real name is Tang Yu-qi. She originally studied fashion design in Taipei before moving to New York at 25 to attend jazz school and pursue singing. In 2018, her cover of a Japanese classic, "Plastic Love," went viral online. Her second album, "9m88 Radio," released in 2022, was self-produced. For the project, she collaborated with musicians from five countries, including American rapper Oddisee and Korean singer SUMIN. What makes the album particularly special is that some of its best tracks almost never made it. A funky song called "Love Is So Cruel" that she recorded in college, and another track titled "Star" that she nearly cut entirely, ended up becoming some of the album's finest pieces.

購物廣場裡的木頭安靜

來源：designboom

在赫爾辛基最熱鬧的購物廣場中間，有一座沒有窗戶的木頭小教堂，叫做「康比安靜教堂」。它蓋於2012年，那年赫爾辛基是「世界設計之都」。

外牆用雲杉（一種松樹）木板一片片接起來，看不到一根釘子。裡面是上過油的厚木板，彎成像蛋一樣的形狀。整座教堂約十一公尺高，找不到一個直角。

不管你信什麼宗教，都可以走進去安靜坐五分鐘。彎彎的木牆，把城市的吵鬧全都擋在外面。

In the heart of Helsinki's busiest shopping district stands a small wooden chapel without windows, called the Kamppi Chapel of Silence. Built in 2012—the year Helsinki was named World Design Capital—its exterior walls are constructed from sheets of spruce wood fitted piece by piece with no visible nails. Inside, thick oiled wooden boards curve into an egg-like form. The entire chapel rises roughly eleven meters high with not a single right angle to be found. Anyone, regardless of their faith, can step inside and sit quietly for five minutes. The curved wooden walls shut out all the city's noise and clamor.

只演一次的老膠卷怪片夜

來源：Chicago Film Society

9月26日星期六，芝加哥的Music Box電影院要放一場很特別的老影片。這是Heather McAdams和Chris Ligon第三次辦16釐米（膠卷的寬度）影片大會。

這些影片是McAdams從小收集的，很多來自二手店和學校丟掉的舊東西。有好笑的廣告歌、奇怪的預告片、早就消失的商品廣告，還有古怪的音樂片段。

門票15美元，在門口買。因為每一卷都是真的膠卷，這場表演結束後，就再也不能一模一樣地重來了。

On Saturday, September 26th, Chicago's Music Box Theater is hosting a very special screening of vintage films. This marks the third time Heather McAdams and Chris Ligon have organized a 16mm film gathering—referring to the width of the film stock. McAdams has been collecting these films since childhood, many sourced from secondhand shops and discarded school materials. The program features funny commercial jingles, bizarre trailers, forgotten product advertisements, and peculiar music clips. Tickets are $15 and available at the door. Because each reel is actual physical film, once this screening concludes, it can never be shown in exactly the same form again.

壓了四十年的糖果紙

來源：Candy Wrapper Archive

1983年夏天，一個小男孩把爸爸買給他的Whatchamacallit巧克力棒包裝紙，夾在筆記本裡壓平。這就是「糖果包裝紙檔案館」（收藏資料的地方）網站的開始。

他一直沒有停下來。四十多年後，他收集了五千多張糖果紙，從1900年代到今天都有。每一張都被掃描、整理，放上網路。

這些小紙片，記下了包裝設計的歷史。沒有人打算蓋這座博物館，它只是一個人四十年的小習慣。

In the summer of 1983, a young boy took a Whatchamacallit chocolate bar wrapper—a gift from his father—and pressed it flat between the pages of his notebook. This was the birth of what would become the Candy Wrapper Archive website. He never stopped. Over forty years later, he has collected more than 5,000 candy wrappers, ranging from the 1900s to today. Each one has been scanned, catalogued, and posted online. These small pieces of paper chronicle the history of packaging design. No one planned to build this museum—it was simply one man's forty-year habit.
