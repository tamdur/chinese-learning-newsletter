月琴裡的思想起：陳達的歌重新出版

來源：VERSE

一九六七年，兩位音樂學者到屏東恆春「採集」（收集）民間歌謠，遇見一位半盲的老街頭歌手陳達。他抱著月琴，隨口唱出動人的「思想起」。

一九七一年，他被請到台北錄音，留下專輯《民族樂手－陳達和他的歌》，還得了第一屆金鼎獎。這張唱片絕版四十五年後，如今終於重新出版，讓我們再次聽見他一生流浪的歌聲。

In 1967, two musicologists traveled to Hengchun in Pingtung to collect folk songs and met Chen Da, an elderly street singer who was partially blind. He held his moon lute and casually sang the moving "Thinking of You" (Sixiangqi). In 1971, he was invited to Taipei to record, leaving behind the album "Folk Musician — Chen Da and His Songs," which won the inaugural Golden Tripod Award. After the record went out of print for forty-five years, it has finally been republished, allowing us to hear once more the wandering songs of his lifetime.

靜默教堂：赫爾辛基裡沒有直角的木蛋

來源：Archello

在赫爾辛基最熱鬧的廣場中央，站著一座像蛋一樣的木頭建築－「康比靜默教堂」。它沒有窗戶，外牆用雲杉木片一片片接成滑順的曲線，裡面的牆是溫暖的赤楊木，屋頂藏著一圈天窗透進光。

二〇一二年落成，它不屬於任何宗教，只是城市送給大家的安靜角落。走進去，購物中心的吵鬧全都留在門外。

In the heart of Helsinki's bustling plaza stands an egg-shaped wooden building—the Kamppi Chapel of Silence. It has no windows; its outer walls are built from spruce planks layered into smooth curves, the interior walls are warm alder wood, and the roof conceals a ring of skylights that let light filter through.

Completed in 2012, it belongs to no religion—simply a quiet sanctuary the city offers to everyone. Step inside and all the noise of the shopping center remains outside.

一起摺紙：芝加哥北區摺紙同好的週日聚會

來源：OrigamiUSA / JASC Chicago

這個星期天，七月二十六日下午一點到五點，芝加哥北區摺紙同好會在「日美協會」聚會，只要五美元，人人都能參加。

不管你是第一次摺紙，還是摺了一輩子的高手，都很歡迎。記得帶自己的紙，還有想分享的書和作品。多年來熱心帶領大家的山崎小姐，會在旁邊教新手認識最基本的「山摺」和「谷摺」。

This Sunday, July 26th from 1 to 5 PM, Chicago's North Side origami enthusiasts will gather at the Japan America Society—admission is just five dollars, open to everyone. Whether you're folding for the first time or a lifelong master, all are welcome. Bring your own paper and any books or finished pieces you'd like to share. Ms. Yamasaki, who has warmly led the group for many years, will be on hand to teach newcomers the fundamentals: mountain folds and valley folds.

接線生的世界：一九一五年老電話總機模擬器

來源：Durgadas.com Simulations

在自動撥號還沒發明的年代，打電話要靠「接線生」幫忙接通，這份工作幾乎都由年輕女生擔任，人們叫她們「哈囉女孩」。

有位愛好者用電腦重現了一台一九一五年的老式總機，放在網頁上，你可以親手把線插進插孔，像當年的接線生一樣接電話。這只是他收藏的眾多老機器模擬器之一，全放在他的個人網站上。

Before automatic dialing was invented, making a phone call required the help of "operators" to connect your call. This job was almost exclusively performed by young women, and people called them the "Hello Girls." One enthusiast has recreated a 1915-era switchboard on a computer and placed it on a webpage, where you can manually insert the cords into the sockets just as operators did back then. This is just one of many vintage machine simulators in his collection, all hosted on his personal website.
