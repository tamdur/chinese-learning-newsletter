唱給大海的歌

來源：Songlines

2022年，「小島大歌」這個計畫出了一張專輯《我們的島》。台灣阿美族歌手Putad Pihay和排灣族歌手Sauljaljui，跟大溪地、模里西斯的音樂人一起合唱。

他們不進錄音室，而是到每座島的海邊錄音。歌裡有真的海浪、風聲和珊瑚礁（海裡的岩石）的聲音。其中一首〈Lament for a Dying Ocean〉用阿美語，唱出對海水上升的心痛。

這張專輯入圍了2022年金曲獎的最佳原住民語專輯。

In 2022, the "Island Songs" project released an album called Our Islands. Taiwanese Amis singer Putad Pihay and Paiwan singer Sauljaljui sang together with musicians from Tahiti and Mauritius. They did not record in a studio. Instead, they recorded on the shore of each island. The songs include the real sounds of waves, wind, and coral reefs (the rocky formations under the sea). One track, "Lament for a Dying Ocean," is sung in Amis and expresses heartache over rising seawater. The album was nominated for Best Indigenous Language Album at the 2022 Golden Melody Awards.

岩石裡的游泳池

來源：Finland.fi

在赫爾辛基的地下，有一座挖在大石頭裡的游泳館，叫做伊塔凱斯庫斯（Itäkeskus）游泳館。它在1993年開放，有50公尺的泳道、滑水道和六間桑拿。

但它其實也是防空洞（躲避轟炸的地方）。只要大約72小時，就能把水放掉、關上厚重的鐵門，讓3800人躲進來。

赫爾辛基還有很多地下空間：教堂、冰球場、停車場都有。三月去，外面下雪，你卻可以在石頭裡游泳！

Deep beneath Helsinki, a swimming hall has been carved into solid bedrock. It is called the Itäkeskus swimming hall. It opened in 1993 and has a 50-meter lap pool, a water slide, and six saunas. It is also a bomb shelter. In roughly 72 hours, the pools can be drained and the heavy steel doors sealed, and the space can hold 3,800 people taking cover. Helsinki has many other underground spaces, including churches, ice hockey rinks, and parking garages. Visit in March, when it is snowing outside, and you can go swimming inside the rock!

雪花的未解之謎

來源：SnowCrystals.com

美國加州理工學院的物理學家利布雷克特（Kenneth Libbrecht），做了一個專門研究雪花的網站snowcrystals.com。

網站上有一張「中谷圖」，是日本科學家中谷宇吉郎在1930年代畫的。它告訴我們，溫度和濕度會決定雪花的樣子：零下2度左右，會長出薄片和星星；零下5度左右，卻變成細長的柱子和針。

奇怪的是，到今天還沒有人完全知道為什麼。網站用很多照片，讓你親眼看見這個謎。

Kenneth Libbrecht, a physicist at the California Institute of Technology, runs a website devoted to snowflakes, snowcrystals.com. On it is a "Nakaya diagram," drawn by the Japanese scientist Ukichiro Nakaya in the 1930s. It shows how temperature and humidity shape snowflakes: at about -2°C, they grow into thin plates and little stars; at about -5°C, they turn into slender columns and needles. Strangely, even today, nobody fully understands why. The website uses many photographs to let you see this mystery for yourself.

怪奇博覽會來到羅斯蒙特

來源：World Oddities Expo

12月5日到6日，「世界怪奇博覽會」（World Oddities Expo）將在芝加哥旁邊的羅斯蒙特舉行，地點是歐海爾機場附近的會議中心。

現場有攤位賣動物標本、骨頭做的藝術品和古老的醫療用品，還有像馬戲團一樣的舞台表演。也有專家來講法醫（用醫學幫忙查案的人）的歷史和民間巫術（神祕的法術）。

開放時間是早上11點到晚上7點，門票大約48美元起。

On December 5 and 6, the World Oddities Expo will be held in Rosemont, a suburb next to Chicago, at a convention center near O'Hare Airport. Vendors will sell taxidermied animals, art made from bones, and antique medical tools, and there will be circus-style stage performances. Experts will also talk about the history of forensic science (people who use medicine to help solve crimes) and folk witchcraft (mysterious magic practices). The expo runs from 11 a.m. to 7 p.m., and tickets start at about $48.
