最後被查禁的那張唱片

來源：The News Lens 關鍵評論網

一九八九年，龐克歌手趙一豪（Chao I-hao）發行了專輯《把我自己掏出來》。這張唱片講的是傷害自己、還有一些大人才懂的話題。

第二年，政府把它查禁（不准再賣），還在公告裡說歌詞是「垃圾」，該丟去燒掉。這是戒嚴時代最後一張被禁的唱片。後來它重新發行，改名叫《把我自己收回來》，像是在笑當年那些做法。

In 1989, punk singer Chao I-hao released the album 'Pulling Myself Out.' The record dealt with self-harm and other mature themes.

The following year, the government banned it, prohibiting further sales, and announced in its notice that the lyrics were 'garbage' deserving to be burned. It was the last album banned during the martial law era. Later it was reissued under a new title, 'Taking Myself Back,' as if to mock the censorship of that era.

追極光的百年科學站

來源：University of Oulu / Sodankylä Geophysical Observatory

在芬蘭北方的拉普蘭，有一座一九一三年蓋的科學站，叫索丹屈萊（Sodankylä）。它是北極圈裡第一座觀測地球磁場的地方。

最早的紀錄從一九一四年開始，大部分由站長的太太西麗（Siiri）親手完成。今天這裡的相機和儀器還在日夜工作，幫忙預報極光。白天你可以來看這些儀器，晚上再坐馴鹿雪橇到草原上，看它們量測的那道光。

In Lapland, in Finland's far north, stands a scientific station built in 1913 called Sodankylä—the first place in the Arctic Circle to observe Earth's magnetic field.

The earliest records began in 1914, mostly made by hand by Siiri, the station director's wife. Today the cameras and instruments here still run around the clock, helping forecast auroras. By day you can visit the equipment; by night, ride a reindeer sleigh out to the tundra to see the very light they measure.

騎單車走過被埋起來的歷史

來源：Chicago Race Riot of 1919 Commemoration Project

七月二十五日早上十點，芝加哥有一場免費的單車導覽。大家跟著歷史學家，騎過南邊的兩個老社區，重走一九一九年種族衝突（不同膚色的人打起來）發生的那條路。

那年一個叫威廉斯（Williams）的黑人男孩，游過一條看不見的界線，溺水死了，才引起這場五天的衝突。你可以自己帶單車，也可以坐車跟著走，不用先懂歷史。

On the morning of July 25th at 10 AM, Chicago is hosting a free bike tour. Riders will follow historians through two old South Side neighborhoods, retracing the route where the 1919 race riots unfolded.

The violence was sparked that year when a Black boy named Williams drowned after swimming across an invisible boundary line, igniting five days of conflict. You can bring your own bike or follow along on the trolley—no prior knowledge of the history required.

讓老電腦回到過去的網路

來源：The Old Net (theoldnet.com)

有一個叫 The Old Net 的網站，是一位叫 Rich 的工程師一個人做的。它像一道橋，把舊網頁從網路檔案館（專門存放舊網頁的地方）找出來，還拿掉新的程式，讓一九九四年的老電腦也打得開。

你可以選一個年份，看看那時候的網路長什麼樣子——閃亮的動畫、會跑的字，全都回來了。很多喜歡老電腦的人，都愛拿它出來玩。

There's a website called The Old Net, made by a single engineer named Rich. It works like a bridge, pulling old web pages from the Internet Archive—a place dedicated to storing historical web pages—and stripping away the new code so that even a 1994-era computer can open them.

You can pick a year and see what the internet looked like back then—shiny animations, scrolling text, it all comes back. Many vintage-computer enthusiasts love to bring it out and play with it.
