一卷不完美的現場錄音

來源：Soundtraces.tw

1989年5月18日晚上，歌手陳明章在中國文化大學開了一場演唱會。現場用很簡單的器材錄下聲音，音質並不好。

1990年1月，水晶唱片把這卷錄音出成專輯《現場作品I》。他們相信，陳明章用台語唱的民謠，還有現場的氣氛，比乾淨的錄音更重要。

這張專輯證明了：便宜的現場錄音也能有真正的力量。它也讓水晶唱片成為台灣獨立音樂的起點。

On the evening of May 18, 1989, singer Chen Mingzhang gave a concert at Chinese Culture University. The show was recorded on very simple equipment, and the sound quality was not good. In January 1990, Crystal Records released the recording as the album Live Works I. They believed that Chen's folk songs, sung in Taiwanese, and the atmosphere of the live performance mattered more than a clean recording. The album showed that a cheap live recording could still have real power. It also made Crystal Records the starting point of Taiwan's independent music.

戰火沒有燒到的村莊

來源：Arctic Centre, University of Lapland

芬蘭北部的拉普蘭，有一個叫蘇萬托（Suvanto）的小村子。這裡只住了大約30個人，卻留著一百多棟木頭房子，最老的蓋於1700年代。

1944年，德軍撤退（往後退走）時，燒掉了拉普蘭幾乎所有的村莊，只有蘇萬托被留了下來。

1980年代起，人們開始修復這些老房子。1991年，它得到歐洲保護文化遺產（前人留下的東西）的大獎。三月來這裡，可以看見雪中的老木屋。

In Lapland, in northern Finland, there is a small village called Suvanto. Only about 30 people live there, yet it still has more than a hundred wooden houses, the oldest built in the 1700s. In 1944, when German troops retreated, they burned almost every village in Lapland. Only Suvanto was spared. Starting in the 1980s, people began restoring these old houses, and in 1991 the village won a major European award for cultural heritage conservation. Visit in March and you can see the old wooden houses standing in the snow.

把芝麻醬帶進西方廚房的人

來源：Chicago Humanities Festival

10月12日星期一晚上7點半，來自以色列、住在英國的名廚奧托倫吉（Yotam Ottolenghi），會來到芝加哥Southport大道上的Athenaeum中心。

他讓芝麻醬和中東的「薩塔」（一種混合香料）走進了西方人的廚房。這次他會談新書《Simple Too》，講怎麼用簡單的方法做出好菜。

這是芝加哥人文節的活動，任何人買票都能參加。現場有賣書，但票價不包含書。

On Monday, October 12, at 7:30 p.m., Yotam Ottolenghi, the Israeli-born chef who lives in Britain, will appear at the Athenaeum Center on Southport Avenue in Chicago. He is the cook who brought tahini and za'atar, a Middle Eastern spice blend, into Western kitchens. At this event he will talk about his new book, Simple Too, which shows how to make good food with simple methods. The event is part of the Chicago Humanities Festival, and anyone can attend by buying a ticket. Books will be sold at the venue, but the ticket price does not include a copy.

用頭髮編成的思念

來源：Sentimental Locks

一百多年前的英國，人們會把過世親人的頭髮編成項鍊、頭飾，甚至做成掛在牆上的畫，用來懷念他們。

2014年，研究歷史的學生Kate Moore做了網站「Sentimental Locks」，專門收集這種「髮藝」。她把一本十九世紀的編髮圖樣書全部放上網，也介紹怎麼在圓形木桌上，用掛著重物的線軸（捲線的小棒子）編頭髮。

網站還連到另一個從1990年代開到現在的個人網站，今天還在賣編髮用的木桌。

More than a century ago in Britain, people would weave the hair of deceased loved ones into necklaces and hair ornaments, and even into framed pictures hung on the wall, as a way to remember them. In 2014, Kate Moore, a history student, created a website called "Sentimental Locks" dedicated to collecting this kind of "hair art." She posted an entire 19th-century hairwork pattern book online, and also explained how to weave hair on a round wooden table using bobbins (small spools of thread) weighted with hanging weights. The site also links to a personal website that has been running since the 1990s and still sells the wooden tables used for hairwork today.
