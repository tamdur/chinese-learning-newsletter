般若波羅蜜多心經

故知般若波羅蜜多，是大神咒，是大明咒，是無上咒，是無等等咒，能除一切苦，真實不虛。故說般若波羅蜜多咒，即說咒曰：揭諦揭諦，波羅揭諦，波羅僧揭諦，菩提薩婆訶。

Therefore, know that Prajna Paramita is the great transcendent mantra, the great bright mantra, the supreme mantra, the unequalled mantra, which can remove all suffering and is true, not false. Therefore he proclaimed the Prajna Paramita mantra, saying: Gate gate paragate parasamgate bodhi svaha.

孟子 · 滕文公下

孟子曰：「昔齊景公田，招虞人以旌，不至，將殺之。志士不忘在溝壑，勇士不忘喪其元。孔子奚取焉？取非其招不往也，如不待其招而往，何哉？且夫枉尺而直尋者，以利言也。如以利，則枉尋直尺而利，亦可為與？昔者趙簡子使王良與嬖奚乘，終日而不獲一禽。嬖奚反命曰：『天下之賤工也。』或以告王良。良曰：『請復之。』彊而後可，一朝而獲十禽。嬖奚反命曰：『天下之良工也。』簡子曰：『我使掌與女乘。』謂王良。良不可，曰：『吾為之範我馳驅，終日不獲一；為之詭遇，一朝而獲十。《詩》云：「不失其馳，舍矢如破。」我不貫與小人乘，請辭。』御者且羞與射者比。比而得禽獸，雖若丘陵，弗為也。如枉道而從彼，何也？且子過矣，枉己者，未有能直人者也。」

Mencius said: "In the past, Duke Jing of Qi went hunting and summoned his gamekeeper with a pennant. The gamekeeper would not come, and the duke was about to have him killed. 'A man of resolve never forgets he may end up in a ditch; a brave man never forgets he may lose his head.' What did Confucius admire in this? He admired that the man would not go when summoned improperly. What would it mean to go without even waiting for a proper summons? Furthermore, this talk of 'bending one foot to straighten eight feet' is reasoning based on profit. If we follow profit, then bending eight feet to straighten one foot would also be acceptable, so long as it were profitable — would you do that too? In the past, Zhao Jianzi had Wang Liang drive a chariot for his favorite, Xi. They went out for a whole day without catching a single bird. Xi reported back: 'He is the worst driver in the world.' Someone told Wang Liang about this. Liang said: 'Let me try again.' He insisted, and Xi reluctantly agreed. In a single morning they caught ten birds. Xi reported back: 'He is the best driver in the world.' Jianzi said: 'I will assign him to drive for you regularly.' He told Wang Liang, but Liang refused, saying: 'When I drove properly for him, we went a whole day without catching anything. When I used irregular methods, we caught ten in a single morning. The Odes say: "He does not miss his course; his arrows fly as if to shatter the target." I am not accustomed to driving for petty men — please let me decline.' Even a chariot driver would be ashamed to conspire with a hunter. If by conspiring one could pile up game like hills, a true driver still would not do it. If you bend the Way to follow such people, what is the point? Besides, you are wrong: no one who bends himself has ever been able to straighten others."

師示眾云：「善知識！何名坐禪？此法門中，無障無礙，外於一切善惡境界，心念不起，名為坐；內見自性不動，名為禪。善知識！何名禪定？外離相為禪，內不亂為定。外若著相，內心即亂；外若離相，心即不亂。本性自淨自定，只為見境，思境即亂。若見諸境心不亂者，是真定也。善知識！外離相即禪，內不亂即定。外禪內定，是為禪定。《菩薩戒經》云：『我本元自性清淨。

'If you fixate on mind or fixate on purity, this obstructs the Way.' The Master addressed the assembly: 'Good friends! What is meant by sitting meditation? In this Dharma gate, there are no obstructions and no hindrances. Externally, when confronting all conditions of good and evil, not a single thought arises in the mind—this is called sitting. Inwardly, seeing one's own nature without being moved—this is called meditation. Good friends! What is meant by meditative concentration? Externally, being free from forms is meditation; inwardly, being undisturbed is concentration. If you attach to external forms, the inner mind becomes disturbed. If you are free from external forms, the mind is undisturbed. The original nature is inherently pure and inherently settled. It is only because of perceiving sense objects and thinking about them that disturbance arises. If one can see all conditions and the mind remains undisturbed, this is true concentration. Good friends! Being free from forms externally is meditation; being undisturbed internally is concentration. External meditation and internal concentration—this is meditative concentration. The Bodhisattva Precepts Sutra says: "Our original self-nature is inherently pure."'
