孟子・離婁上

孟子曰：「自暴者，不可與有言也；自棄者，不可與有為也。言非禮義，謂之自暴也；吾身不能居仁由義，謂之自棄也。仁，人之安宅也；義，人之正路也。曠安宅而弗居，舍正路而不由，哀哉！」孟子曰：「道在爾而求諸遠，事在易而求之難。人人親其親、長其長而天下平。」

Mencius said: "One who does violence to himself cannot be engaged in conversation; one who throws himself away cannot be engaged in action. To speak against ritual propriety and righteousness is what I call doing violence to oneself; to say 'I am unable to dwell in benevolence and follow righteousness' is what I call throwing oneself away. Benevolence is the peaceful dwelling of a person; righteousness is the proper path of a person. To leave the peaceful dwelling empty and not live in it, to abandon the proper path and not follow it—how sad!" Mencius said: "The Way is near, yet people seek it afar; the task is easy, yet people make it hard. If everyone simply treated their parents as parents and their elders as elders, the whole realm would be at peace."
