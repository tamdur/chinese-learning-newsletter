般若波羅蜜多心經

無罣礙故，無有恐怖，遠離顛倒夢想，究竟涅槃。三世諸佛，依般若波羅蜜多故，得阿耨多羅三藐三菩提。

Without hindrance, there is no fear. Far from all inverted views, one attains nirvana. All buddhas of past, present, and future rely on Prajna Paramita and attain supreme, perfect enlightenment.

孟子

孟子曰：「不挾長，不挾貴，不挾兄弟而友。友也者，友其德也，不可以有挾也。孟獻子，百乘之家也，有友五人焉：樂正裘、牧仲，其三人，則予忘之矣。獻子之與此五人者友也，無獻子之家者也。此五人者，亦有獻子之家，則不與之友矣。非惟百乘之家為然也。雖小國之君亦有之。費惠公曰：『吾於子思，則師之矣；吾於顏般，則友之矣；王順、長息則事我者也。』非惟小國之君為然也，雖大國之君亦有之。晉平公之於亥唐也，入云則入，坐云則坐，食云則食。雖疏食菜羹，未嘗不飽，蓋不敢不飽也。然終於此而已矣。弗與共天位也，弗與治天職也，弗與食天祿也，士之尊賢者也，非王公之尊賢也。舜尚見帝，帝館甥于貳室，亦饗舜，迭為賓主，是天子而友匹夫也。用下敬上，謂之貴貴；用上敬下，謂之尊賢。貴貴、尊賢，其義一也。」

Mencius said: "In friendship, one should not rely on being older, or on being of noble rank, or on having powerful brothers. Friendship is a matter of befriending a person's virtue — there must be no leveraging of advantages. Meng Xianzi was the head of a family with a hundred chariots, and he had five friends: Yue Zheng Qiu, Mu Zhong, and three others whose names I have forgotten. Xianzi's friendship with these five men was conducted as if his family's wealth did not exist. If these five men had given weight to Xianzi's family status, they would not have been his friends. This is true not only of families with a hundred chariots. Even the ruler of a small state has such relationships. Duke Hui of Bi said: 'With Zisi I stand as a pupil before a teacher. With Yan Ban I stand as an equal friend. But Wang Shun and Chang Xi are men who serve me.' This is true not only of rulers of small states. Even rulers of great states have such relationships. Duke Ping of Jin, in his dealings with Hai Tang, entered when told to enter, sat when told to sit, and ate when told to eat. Even when it was coarse rice and vegetable broth, he always ate his fill, for he did not dare not eat his fill. Yet it went no further than this. He did not share Heaven's position with him, did not share Heaven's duties with him, did not share Heaven's emoluments with him. This was a scholar honoring a worthy man — it was not the way a king or duke honors a worthy man. When Shun went up to see Emperor Yao, Yao lodged his son-in-law in a secondary palace, and also feasted Shun, with the two of them taking turns as host and guest. This was a Son of Heaven befriending a commoner. When the lower shows deference to the higher, it is called honoring rank. When the higher shows deference to the lower, it is called honoring worth. Honoring rank and honoring worth — their principle is the same."

金剛般若波羅蜜經

時，長老須菩提，在大眾中，即從座起，偏袒右肩，右膝著地，合掌恭敬。而白佛言：「希有世尊！如來善護念諸菩薩，善付囑諸菩薩。世尊！善男子、善女人，發阿耨多羅三藐三菩提心，應云何住？云何降伏其心？」佛言：「善哉，善哉。須菩提！如汝所說：如來善護念諸菩薩，善付囑諸菩薩。汝今諦聽，當為汝說：善男子、善女人，發阿耨多羅三藐三菩提心，應如是住，如是降伏其心。」「唯然，世尊！願樂欲聞。」

At that time, the elder Subhuti, who was among the great assembly, rose from his seat, bared his right shoulder, knelt on his right knee, and with palms joined in reverence, addressed the Buddha: "How rare, World-Honored One! The Tathagata skillfully protects and is mindful of all bodhisattvas, and skillfully entrusts and instructs all bodhisattvas. World-Honored One, when good men and good women give rise to the mind of anuttara-samyak-sambodhi, how should they abide? How should they subdue their minds?" The Buddha said: "Excellent, excellent! Subhuti, it is just as you say — the Tathagata skillfully protects and is mindful of all bodhisattvas, and skillfully entrusts and instructs all bodhisattvas. Now listen carefully, and I shall explain it for you. When good men and good women give rise to the mind of anuttara-samyak-sambodhi, they should abide thus, and thus subdue their minds." "Yes, World-Honored One! We joyfully wish to hear."
