般若波羅蜜多心經

無眼耳鼻舌身意，無色聲香味觸法，無眼界，乃至無意識界。無無明，亦無無明盡，乃至無老死，亦無老死盡。

There are no eyes, ears, nose, tongue, body, or mind; no sights, sounds, smells, tastes, touches, or objects of mind; no realm of sight, and so on up to no realm of consciousness. There is no ignorance and no end of ignorance, and so on up to no old age and death and no end of old age and death.

孟子

萬章下

「大國地方百里，君十卿祿，卿祿四大夫，大夫倍上士，上士倍中士，中士倍下士，下士與庶人在官者同祿，祿足以代其耕也。次國地方七十里，君十卿祿，卿祿三大夫，大夫倍上士，上士倍中士，中士倍下士，下士與庶人在官者同祿，祿足以代其耕也。小國地方五十里，君十卿祿，卿祿二大夫，大夫倍上士，上士倍中士，中士倍下士，下士與庶人在官者同祿，祿足以代其耕也。耕者之所獲，一夫百畝。百畝之糞，上農夫食九人，上次食八人，中食七人，中次食六人，下食五人。庶人在官者，其祿以是為差。」

"In a large state of a hundred li square, the ruler received ten times a minister's salary. A minister's salary was four times a grand officer's. A grand officer received double a senior scholar's, a senior scholar double a middle scholar's, a middle scholar double a junior scholar's, and a junior scholar received the same salary as a commoner employed in government service — salary sufficient to replace what he would have earned from farming. In a middle state of seventy li square, the ruler received ten times a minister's salary. A minister's salary was three times a grand officer's. A grand officer received double a senior scholar's, a senior scholar double a middle scholar's, a middle scholar double a junior scholar's, and a junior scholar received the same as a commoner in government service — salary sufficient to replace farming income. In a small state of fifty li square, the ruler received ten times a minister's salary. A minister's salary was double a grand officer's. A grand officer received double a senior scholar's, a senior scholar double a middle scholar's, a middle scholar double a junior scholar's, and a junior scholar received the same as a commoner in government service — salary sufficient to replace farming income. As for what farmers produced: one man worked a hundred mu. With proper fertilization of a hundred mu, a top-grade farmer could feed nine people, the next grade eight, a middle-grade farmer seven, the next six, and the lowest grade five. Commoners employed in government service had their salaries scaled accordingly."

信心銘

契心平等所作俱息狐疑淨盡正信調直一切不留無可記憶虛明自照不勞心力非思量處識情難測真如法界無他無自要急相應唯言不二不二皆同無不包容十方智者皆入此宗宗非促延一念萬年無在不在十方目前極小同大忘絕境界極大同小不見邊表有即是無無即是有若不如是必不須守一即一切一切即一但能如是何慮不畢信心不二不二信心言語道斷非去來今

Align the mind with equanimity,
and all striving comes to rest.
Doubt is cleansed completely;
true faith stands upright.

Nothing whatsoever is retained,
nothing that can be remembered.
Empty, luminous, self-illuminating,
no effort of the mind is needed.

This is not a place of thinking —
consciousness and sentiment cannot fathom it.
In the true suchness of the Dharma-realm,
there is no other and no self.

To swiftly accord with this,
only say: not two.
In not-two, all is the same;
nothing is excluded.

The wise in all directions
all enter this teaching.
This teaching is beyond haste or delay;
one thought encompasses ten thousand years.

It is nowhere, yet it is everywhere;
the ten directions stand before your eyes.
The infinitely small equals the infinitely large,
when the boundaries of the world are forgotten.

The infinitely large equals the infinitely small,
when edges can no longer be seen.
Existence is precisely emptiness;
emptiness is precisely existence.

If it is not like this,
you must not hold to it.
One is all;
all is one.

If you can simply be like this,
why worry that it is not complete?
Faith and mind are not two;
not-two is the faithful mind.

Words cannot open this road;
it is not of the past, the present, or the future.
