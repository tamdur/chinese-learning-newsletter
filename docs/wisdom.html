般若波羅蜜多心經

觀自在菩薩，行深般若波羅蜜多時，照見五蘊皆空，度一切苦厄。

Avalokitesvara Bodhisattva, when practicing the profound Prajna Paramita, illuminated the five aggregates and saw that they are all empty, thus overcoming all suffering and distress.

孟子・公孫丑下

孟子去齊。充虞路問曰：「夫子若有不豫色然。前日虞聞諸夫子曰：『君子不怨天，不尤人。』」曰：「彼一時，此一時也。五百年必有王者興，其間必有名世者。由周而來，七百有餘歲矣。以其數則過矣，以其時考之則可矣。夫天，未欲平治天下也；如欲平治天下，當今之世，舍我其誰也？吾何為不豫哉？」孟子去齊，居休。公孫丑問曰：「仕而不受祿，古之道乎？」

Mencius left Qi. Chong Yu asked him on the road: "Master, you seem to have an unhappy expression. The other day I heard you say: 'A gentleman does not blame Heaven or resent others.'" Mencius said: "That was one time; this is another. Every five hundred years a true king must arise, and in the interval there must be those who are renowned in their age. From the Zhou dynasty to now, it has been over seven hundred years. By the count, the time is overdue; by the circumstances, the moment is right. But Heaven does not yet wish to bring peace and order to the world. If it did wish to bring peace and order to the world, who in this present age is there besides me? Why should I be unhappy?" Mencius left Qi and settled in Xiu. Gongsun Chou asked: "To serve in office yet not accept a salary — is that the Way of the ancients?"

六祖大師法寶壇經・般若品第二

若百物不思，當令念絕，即是法縛，即名邊見。善知識！悟無念法者，萬法盡通；悟無念法者，見諸佛境界；悟無念法者，至佛地位。

「善知識！後代得吾法者，將此頓教法門，於同見同行，發願受持。如事佛故，終身而不退者，定入聖位。然須傳授從上以來默傳分付，不得匿其正法。若不同見同行，在別法中，不得傳付。損彼前人，究竟無益。恐愚人不解，謗此法門，百劫千生，斷佛種性。

'But if you think of nothing whatsoever and completely cut off all thought, this is bondage to dharma, and is called a one-sided view. Good friends, one who awakens to the dharma of no-thought penetrates all dharmas. One who awakens to the dharma of no-thought sees the realm of all buddhas. One who awakens to the dharma of no-thought attains the stage of buddhahood.' 'Good friends, those in future generations who receive my teaching should take this Sudden Teaching Dharma-gate and, together with those of the same understanding and same practice, vow to receive and uphold it. Serve it as you would serve the Buddha, and never retreat throughout your life — you will surely enter the holy stage. However, this must be transmitted and entrusted quietly, following the tradition from the beginning; do not conceal the true Dharma. If someone does not share the same understanding and same practice, being in a different lineage, do not transmit it to them. It will harm those who came before and ultimately bring no benefit. I fear that deluded people may fail to understand and slander this Dharma-gate, thereby cutting off the buddha-seed for hundreds of kalpas and thousands of lifetimes.'
