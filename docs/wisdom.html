般若波羅蜜多心經

觀自在菩薩，行深般若波羅蜜多時，照見五蘊皆空，度一切苦厄。

Avalokitesvara Bodhisattva, when practicing the profound Prajna Paramita, illuminated the five aggregates and saw that they are all empty, thus overcoming all suffering and distress.

孟子．萬章上

孟子曰：「否，不然。伊尹耕於有莘之野，而樂堯舜之道焉。非其義也，非其道也，祿之以天下，弗顧也；繫馬千駟，弗視也。非其義也，非其道也，一介不以與人，一介不以取諸人，湯使人以幣聘之，囂囂然曰：『我何以湯之聘幣為哉？我豈若處畎畝之中，由是以樂堯舜之道哉？』湯三使往聘之，既而幡然改曰：『與我處畎畝之中，由是以樂堯舜之道，吾豈若使是君為堯舜之君哉？吾豈若使是民為堯舜之民哉？吾豈若於吾身親見之哉？天之生此民也，使先知覺後知，使先覺覺後覺也。予，天民之先覺者也；予將以斯道覺斯民也。非予覺之，而誰也？』思天下之民匹夫匹婦有不被堯舜之澤者，若己推而內之溝中。其自任以天下之重如此，故就湯而說之以伐夏救民。吾未聞枉己而正人者也，況辱己以正天下者乎？聖人之行不同也，或遠或近，或去或不去，歸潔其身而已矣。吾聞其以堯舜之道要湯，未聞以割烹也。伊訓曰：『天誅造攻自牧宮，朕載自亳。』」

Mencius said: "No, that is not so. This is the fabrication of meddlesome people. Yi Yin was farming in the wilds of the state of You Xin, and he delighted in the Way of Yao and Shun. If something was contrary to righteousness or contrary to the Way, even if you offered him the revenues of the whole realm, he would pay no attention; even if you tethered four thousand horses before him, he would not give them a glance. If something was contrary to righteousness or contrary to the Way, he would neither give so much as a trifle to another person nor take so much as a trifle from another person. Tang sent envoys with gifts to invite him. He said contentedly: 'What would I do with Tang's gifts? Would I not rather stay here amid the fields and delight in the Way of Yao and Shun?' Only after Tang sent envoys three times did he change his mind and say: 'Rather than staying amid the fields delighting in the Way of Yao and Shun, would it not be better to make this sovereign into a sovereign like Yao or Shun? Would it not be better to make these people into people like those of Yao or Shun? Would it not be better to see this with my own eyes in my own lifetime? Heaven, in producing these people, intended those who understand first to awaken those who understand later, and those who are aware first to awaken those who are aware later. I am one of Heaven's people who has been among the first to be awakened. I shall use this Way to awaken this people. If not I, then who?' He thought that if among the common men and women of the realm there were any who had not received the blessings of Yao and Shun, it was as if he himself had pushed them into a ditch. Such was the weight of responsibility he took upon himself for the whole realm. And so he went to Tang and persuaded him to launch the campaign against the Xia to save the people. I have never heard of someone who bends himself being able to straighten others — how much less someone who disgraces himself in order to set the realm right. The actions of sages differ — some stay far away, some stay near; some depart, some remain — but all alike return to keeping their persons pure. I have heard that Yi Yin sought Tang's attention through the Way of Yao and Shun; I have not heard that he did so through cooking. The 'Instructions of Yi' says: 'Heaven's punishment was to be carried out, beginning from the Mu Palace. I, for my part, began from Bo.'"

六祖大師法寶壇經．付囑品第十

蓋為汝等信根淳熟，決定無疑，堪任大事。然據先祖達磨大師付授偈意，衣不合傳。偈曰：

吾本來茲土，傳法救迷情，一華開五葉，結果自然成。」

師復曰：「諸善知識！汝等各各淨心，聽吾說法。若欲成就種智，須達一相三昧、一行三昧。若於一切處而不住相，於彼相中不生憎愛，亦無取捨，不念利益成壞等事，安閒恬靜，虛融澹泊，此名一相三昧。若於一切處行住坐臥，純一直心，不動道場，真成淨土，此名一行三昧。

'This is because your roots of faith are pure and mature, you are resolute and without doubt, and you are capable of the great task. Moreover, according to the verse left by our first patriarch Bodhidharma for the transmission, the robe should not be passed on further. The verse says: "I originally came to this land to transmit the Dharma and save the deluded. One flower opens five petals, and the fruit ripens of itself."' The Master further said: 'Good friends! Each of you purify your minds and listen to my teaching. If you wish to accomplish the wisdom of all modes, you must penetrate the Samadhi of One Characteristic and the Samadhi of One Practice. If in all places you do not attach to characteristics, and within those characteristics you give rise to neither aversion nor love, and there is no grasping or rejecting, and you do not think of benefit, success, or destruction — peaceful, at ease, tranquil, open, and serene — this is called the Samadhi of One Characteristic. If in all places — walking, standing, sitting, or lying down — you maintain a single, straightforward mind, never departing from the place of awakening, truly accomplishing the Pure Land — this is called the Samadhi of One Practice.'
