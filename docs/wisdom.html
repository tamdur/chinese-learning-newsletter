般若波羅蜜多心經 — Heart Sutra — Wednesday

舍利子，是諸法空相，不生不滅，不垢不淨，不增不減。是故空中無色，無受想行識。

Shariputra, all dharmas are marked with emptiness — they are neither born nor destroyed, neither defiled nor pure, neither increasing nor decreasing. Therefore, in emptiness there is no form, no feelings, perceptions, mental formations, or consciousness.

孟子 — Mencius — 公孫丑下

他日，王謂時子曰：「我欲中國而授孟子室，養弟子以萬鍾，使諸大夫國人皆有所矜式。子盍為我言之？」

Another day, the king said to Shi Zi: "I wish to give Mencius a house in the center of the capital, and to support his disciples with a stipend of ten thousand zhong of grain, so that all the officials and people will have someone to look up to and emulate. Would you convey this to him for me?"

六祖大師法寶壇經 — Platform Sutra — 般若品第二

若大乘人，若最上乘人，聞說《金剛經》，心開悟解。故知本性自有般若之智，自用智慧，常觀照故，不假文字。譬如雨水，不從天有，元是龍能興致，令一切眾生、一切草木、有情無情，悉皆蒙潤。百川眾流，却入大海，合為一體。眾生本性般若之智，亦復如是。善知識！小根之人，聞此頓教，猶如草木根性小者，若被大雨，悉皆自倒，不能增長。小根之人，亦復如是。

'If those of the Great Vehicle or the Supreme Vehicle hear the Diamond Sutra explained, their minds open in awakening and understanding. Therefore we know that one's original nature inherently possesses prajna-wisdom. Through the constant use of this wisdom in contemplation, one does not depend on written words. It is like the rain: it does not come from heaven, but is actually brought forth by the dragons, causing all living beings, all grasses and trees, all sentient and insentient things to be nourished. The hundred rivers and countless streams all flow back into the great ocean, merging into one body. The prajna-wisdom of beings' original nature is also like this. Good friends, those of lesser capacity who hear this sudden teaching are like grasses and trees with shallow roots — if drenched by a great rain, they all topple over and cannot grow. Those of lesser capacity are also like this.'
