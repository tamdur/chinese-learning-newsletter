般若波羅蜜多心經 — Heart Sutra — Friday

無苦集滅道，無智亦無得。以無所得故，菩提薩埵，依般若波羅蜜多故，心無罣礙。

There is no suffering, no cause of suffering, no cessation, no path; no wisdom and no attainment. With nothing to attain, a bodhisattva relies on Prajna Paramita, and the mind is without hindrance.

孟子 — 梁惠王下

莊暴見孟子，曰：「暴見於王，王語暴以好樂，暴未有以對也。」曰：「好樂何如？」孟子曰：「王之好樂甚，則齊國其庶幾乎！」他日，見於王曰：「王嘗語莊子以好樂，有諸？」王變乎色，曰：「寡人非能好先王之樂也，直好世俗之樂耳。」曰：「王之好樂甚，則齊其庶幾乎！今之樂猶古之樂也。」曰：「可得聞與？」曰：「獨樂樂，與人樂樂，孰樂？」曰：「不若與人。」曰：「與少樂樂，與眾樂樂，孰樂？」曰：「不若與眾。」

Zhuang Bao went to see Mencius and said, "I had an audience with the king, and the king told me of his love for music. I had no reply to offer." He then asked, "What about loving music?" Mencius said, "If the king's love of music were great enough, then the state of Qi would be close to good governance!" On another day, Mencius had an audience with the king and said, "Your Majesty once told Zhuang Bao of your love for music—is this true?" The king's expression changed, and he said, "I am not able to love the music of the ancient kings. I merely love popular music." Mencius said, "If Your Majesty's love of music were great enough, then Qi would be close to good governance! The music of today is just like the music of antiquity." The king said, "May I hear more about this?" Mencius said, "Which is more enjoyable—to enjoy music alone, or to enjoy it with others?" The king said, "It is better to enjoy it with others." Mencius said, "Which is more enjoyable—to enjoy music with a few, or to enjoy it with many?" The king said, "It is better to enjoy it with many."

金剛般若波羅蜜經 — Diamond Sutra

如來說：世界，非世界，是名世界。須菩提！於意云何？可以三十二相見如來不？」「不也，世尊！不可以三十二相得見如來。何以故？如來說：三十二相，即是非相，是名三十二相。」「須菩提！若有善男子、善女人，以恆河沙等身命布施；若復有人，於此經中，乃至受持四句偈等，為他人說，其福甚多！」。

The Tathagata says: a world is not a world, and is therefore called a world. Subhuti, what do you think? Can one perceive the Tathagata through the thirty-two physical marks? No, World-Honored One! One cannot perceive the Tathagata through the thirty-two marks. Why? Because what the Tathagata calls the thirty-two marks are not marks, and are therefore called the thirty-two marks. Subhuti, if a good man or good woman were to sacrifice as many lives as there are grains of sand in the Ganges River, and another person were to receive and uphold even just four lines of verse from this sutra and explain them to others, the merit of the latter would be far greater.
