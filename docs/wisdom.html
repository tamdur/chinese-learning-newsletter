般若波羅蜜多心經

觀自在菩薩，行深般若波羅蜜多時，照見五蘊皆空，度一切苦厄。

Avalokitesvara Bodhisattva, when practicing the profound Prajna Paramita, illuminated the five aggregates and saw that they are all empty, thus overcoming all suffering and distress.

孟子 · 萬章下

曰：「不可。《康誥》曰：『殺越人于貨，閔不畏死，凡民罔不譈。』是不待教而誅者也。殷受夏，周受殷，所不辭也。於今為烈，如之何其受之？」曰：「今之諸侯取之於民也，猶禦也。苟善其禮際矣，斯君子受之，敢問何說也？」曰：「子以為有王者作，將比今之諸侯而誅之乎？其教之不改而後誅之乎？夫謂非其有而取之者盜也，充類至義之盡也。孔子之仕於魯也，魯人獵較，孔子亦獵較。獵較猶可，而況受其賜乎？」

Mencius said: "No, it would not. The Announcement to Kang says: 'Those who kill and rob others for their goods, fierce and fearless of death — there are none among the people who do not detest them.' Such people are to be punished without waiting to instruct them. This system was received by the Yin from the Xia, and by the Zhou from the Yin — no one disputes it. It has continued fiercely to the present day — how could one accept such gifts?" Wan Zhang said: "But today's feudal lords take from the people in ways no different from robbery. If they are scrupulous about ritual proprieties in their exchanges, then the gentleman accepts — may I ask how you explain this?" Mencius said: "Do you think that if a true king arose, he would line up all the current feudal lords and execute them? Or would he first instruct them and only execute those who refused to reform? To call it theft whenever someone takes what is not rightfully his — that is pushing the category to its logical extreme. When Confucius held office in Lu, the people of Lu were holding a competitive hunt, and Confucius also joined the competitive hunt. If even a competitive hunt was acceptable, how much more so accepting their gifts?"

金剛般若波羅蜜經 · 妙行無住分第四

「復次，須菩提！菩薩於法，應無所住，行於布施。所謂不住色布施，不住聲、香、味、觸、法布施。須菩提！菩薩應如是布施，不住於相。何以故？若菩薩不住相布施。其福德不可思量。」「須菩提！於意云何？東方虛空，可思量不？」「不也，世尊！」「須菩提！南、西、北方，四維上下虛空，可思量不？」「不也，世尊！」「須菩提！菩薩無住相布施，福德亦復如是不可思量。須菩提！菩薩但應如所教住。」

"Furthermore, Subhuti, a bodhisattva should practice giving without abiding in anything. That is to say, one should give without abiding in form, and give without abiding in sound, smell, taste, touch, or mental objects. Subhuti, a bodhisattva should give in this way — without abiding in any characteristic. Why? If a bodhisattva gives without abiding in characteristics, the merit is beyond reckoning." "Subhuti, what do you think? Can the space to the east be measured?" "No, World-Honored One." "Subhuti, can the space to the south, west, north, or in the four intermediate directions, above and below, be measured?" "No, World-Honored One." "Subhuti, the merit of a bodhisattva who gives without abiding in characteristics is likewise beyond reckoning. Subhuti, a bodhisattva should abide only as instructed."
