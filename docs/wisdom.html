般若波羅蜜多心經

無苦集滅道，無智亦無得。以無所得故，菩提薩埵，依般若波羅蜜多故，心無罣礙。

There is no suffering, no cause of suffering, no cessation, no path; no wisdom and no attainment. With nothing to attain, a bodhisattva relies on Prajna Paramita, and the mind is without hindrance.

孟子 · 滕文公下

曰：「子何以其志為哉？其有功於子，可食而食之矣。且子食志乎？食功乎？」曰：「食志。」曰：「有人於此，毀瓦畫墁，其志將以求食也，則子食之乎？」曰：「否。」曰：「然則子非食志也，食功也。」萬章問曰：「宋，小國也。今將行王政，齊楚惡而伐之，則如之何？」

Mencius said: "Why must you judge by intentions? If someone renders you a service, you may feed him and that is that. Besides, do you feed people based on their intentions, or on their actual contributions?" Peng Geng said: "Their intentions." Mencius said: "Suppose someone here smashes your tiles and scrawls over your freshly plastered walls, and his intention is to earn food by it — would you feed him?" Peng Geng said: "No." Mencius said: "Then you do not feed based on intentions after all — you feed based on actual contributions." Wan Zhang asked: "Song is a small state. Now if it tries to implement true kingly government and Qi and Chu become hostile and attack it, what should be done?"

六祖大師法寶壇經

云何名懺？云何名悔？懺者，懺其前愆，從前所有惡業，愚迷憍誑嫉妬等罪，悉皆盡懺，永不復起，是名為懺。悔者，悔其後過，從今以後，所有惡業，愚迷憍誑嫉妬等罪，今已覺悟，悉皆永斷，更不復作，是名為悔。故稱懺悔。凡夫愚迷，只知懺其前愆，不知悔其後過。以不悔故，前愆不滅，後過又生。前愆既不滅，後過復又生，何名懺悔？

「善知識！既懺悔已，與善知識發四弘誓願，各須用心正聽。

What is meant by 'repentance' (chan) and what is meant by 'reform' (hui)? 'Repentance' means to repent of past transgressions — all previous evil karma born of ignorance, arrogance, deceit, jealousy, and envy is entirely repented, never to arise again. This is called repentance. 'Reform' means to reform future conduct — from this moment forward, all evil karma born of ignorance, arrogance, deceit, jealousy, and envy, now that one has awakened, is entirely and forever cut off, never to be committed again. This is called reform. Therefore it is called 'repentance and reform.' Ordinary people in their delusion only know how to repent past transgressions but do not know how to reform future conduct. Because they do not reform, past transgressions are not eliminated and future offenses continue to arise. If past transgressions are not eliminated and future offenses continue to arise, how can this be called repentance and reform? Good friends! Now that the repentance is complete, I will make the Four Great Vows together with you. Each of you must listen attentively with a sincere mind.
