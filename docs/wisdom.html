般若波羅蜜多心經 — 星期四

無眼耳鼻舌身意，無色聲香味觸法，無眼界，乃至無意識界。無無明，亦無無明盡，乃至無老死，亦無老死盡。

Heart Sutra — Thursday

There are no eyes, ears, nose, tongue, body, or mind; no sights, sounds, smells, tastes, touches, or objects of mind; no realm of sight, and so on up to no realm of consciousness. There is no ignorance and no end of ignorance, and so on up to no old age and death and no end of old age and death.

孟子 — 梁惠王上

「今恩足以及禽獸，而功不至於百姓者，獨何與？然則一羽之不舉，為不用力焉；輿薪之不見，為不用明焉，百姓之不見保，為不用恩焉。故王之不王，不為也，非不能也。」曰：「不為者與不能者之形何以異？」

Mengzi — 梁惠王上

'Now, your kindness is sufficient to reach animals, yet its benefits do not reach the common people — why is this the sole exception? A feather not lifted — it is because one does not use one's strength. A cartload of firewood not seen — it is because one does not use one's sight. The people not cared for — it is because one does not use one's kindness. Therefore, Your Majesty's failure to be a true king is because you choose not to act, not because you cannot.' The king asked, 'How does the form of choosing not to act differ from that of being unable?'

Diamond Sutra — 一相無相分第九

"Subhuti, what do you think? Can a non-returner have the thought 'I have attained the fruit of non-returning'?" Subhuti said: "No, World-Honored One. Why? A non-returner is called 'one who does not return,' yet in truth there is no non-returning — therefore one is called a non-returner." "Subhuti, what do you think? Can an arhat have the thought 'I have attained the way of the arhat'?" Subhuti said: "No, World-Honored One. Why? There is in truth no dharma called 'arhat.' World-Honored One, if an arhat were to think 'I have attained the way of the arhat,' that would be clinging to self, person, sentient being, and life span. World-Honored One, the Buddha has said that I have attained the samadhi of non-contention, that I am foremost among people, the foremost arhat free from desire. Yet, World-Honored One, I do not think 'I am an arhat free from desire.'"
