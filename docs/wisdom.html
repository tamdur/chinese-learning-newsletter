般若波羅蜜多心經

無罣礙故，無有恐怖，遠離顛倒夢想，究竟涅槃。三世諸佛，依般若波羅蜜多故，得阿耨多羅三藐三菩提。

Without hindrance, there is no fear. Far from all inverted views, one attains nirvana. All buddhas of past, present, and future rely on Prajna Paramita and attain supreme, perfect enlightenment.

孟子：萬章上

孟子曰：「否，不然也。天與賢，則與賢；天與子，則與子。昔者舜薦禹於天，十有七年，舜崩。三年之喪畢，禹避舜之子於陽城。天下之民從之，若堯崩之後，不從堯之子而從舜也。禹薦益於天，七年，禹崩。三年之喪畢，益避禹之子於箕山之陰。朝覲訟獄者不之益而之啟，曰：『吾君之子也。』謳歌者不謳歌益而謳歌啟，曰：『吾君之子也。』丹朱之不肖，舜之子亦不肖。舜之相堯，禹之相舜也，歷年多，施澤於民久。啟賢，能敬承繼禹之道。益之相禹也，歷年少，施澤於民未久。舜、禹、益相去久遠，其子之賢不肖，皆天也，非人之所能為也。莫之為而為者，天也；莫之致而至者，命也。匹夫而有天下者，德必若舜禹，而又有天子薦之者，故仲尼不有天下。繼世以有天下，天之所廢，必若桀紂者也，故益、伊尹、周公不有天下。伊尹相湯以王於天下。湯崩，太丁未立，外丙二年，仲壬四年。太甲顛覆湯之典刑，伊尹放之於桐。三年，太甲悔過，自怨自艾，於桐處仁遷義；三年，以聽伊尹之訓己也，復歸于亳。周公之不有天下，猶益之於夏，伊尹之於殷也。孔子曰：『唐虞禪，夏后、殷、周繼，其義一也。』」

Mencius said: "No, that is not so. When Heaven gives the realm to the worthiest, it goes to the worthiest; when Heaven gives it to a son, it goes to the son. In ancient times, Shun recommended Yu to Heaven. Seventeen years later, Shun died. When the three years of mourning were complete, Yu withdrew from Shun's son to Yang Cheng. The people of the realm followed him, just as after Yao's death they had followed Shun rather than Yao's son. Yu recommended Yi to Heaven. Seven years later, Yu died. When the three years of mourning were complete, Yi withdrew from Yu's son to the north of Ji Mountain. But those who came to pay court and bring legal disputes went not to Yi but to Qi, saying: 'He is our lord's son.' Those who sang songs of praise sang not of Yi but of Qi, saying: 'He is our lord's son.' Yao's son Dan Zhu was unworthy, and Shun's son was also unworthy. Shun's service as minister to Yao and Yu's service as minister to Shun lasted many years, and their blessings to the people endured long. Qi was worthy and able to respectfully carry on Yu's way. Yi's service as minister to Yu lasted few years, and his blessings to the people had not endured long. The great distances in time between Shun, Yu, and Yi, and the worthiness or unworthiness of their sons — all of this was Heaven's doing, not something humans could bring about. What comes about without anyone making it happen is Heaven. What arrives without anyone summoning it is fate. For a commoner to possess the realm, his virtue must equal that of Shun or Yu, and he must also have the recommendation of a Son of Heaven — that is why Confucius did not possess the realm. For one who inherits the realm through succession to be deposed by Heaven, he must be as wicked as Jie or Zhou — that is why Yi, Yi Yin, and the Duke of Zhou did not possess the realm. Yi Yin served as minister to Tang and helped him become king over the realm. When Tang died, Tai Ding had not yet been installed. Wai Bing reigned for two years and Zhong Ren for four. Then Tai Jia overturned the statutes and standards of Tang, and Yi Yin banished him to Tong. After three years, Tai Jia repented of his errors, blamed himself and reformed, and while at Tong he cultivated benevolence and moved toward righteousness. After three years of heeding Yi Yin's instructions, he was restored to Bo. The Duke of Zhou's not possessing the realm was like Yi's case with the Xia and Yi Yin's case with the Yin. Confucius said: 'The abdications of Tang and Yu, and the hereditary successions of the Xia, Yin, and Zhou — the principle behind them is one and the same.'"

六祖大師法寶壇經：付囑品第十

若著相於外，而作法求真；或廣立道場，說有無之過患。如是之人，累劫不得見性。但聽依法修行，又莫百物不思，而於道性窒礙。若聽說不修，令人反生邪念。但依法修行，無住相法施。汝等若悟，依此說、依此用、依此行、依此作，即不失本宗。若有人問汝義，問有將無對，問無將有對，問凡以聖對，問聖以凡對。二道相因，生中道義。如一問一對，餘問一依此作，即不失理也。設有人問：『何名為闇？』答云：『明是因，闇是緣，明沒即闇。

'If you attach to forms externally and devise practices to seek the truth, or if you extensively establish places of practice and debate the faults of existence and non-existence — people like this, even after countless eons, will never see their true nature. Simply listen and practice according to the Dharma, and do not think about nothing at all, for that would obstruct the nature of the Way. If you only listen to teachings without practicing, it will cause people to develop wrong thoughts. Simply practice according to the Dharma — bestow the Dharma without attachment to any form. If you all awaken, then teach according to this, practice according to this, act according to this, and function according to this, and you will not lose the essential teaching of our school. If someone asks you about meaning, when asked about existence, respond with non-existence; when asked about non-existence, respond with existence; when asked about the ordinary, respond with the sagely; when asked about the sagely, respond with the ordinary. From the mutual causation of these two aspects, the meaning of the Middle Way arises. For each single question and answer, handle the rest in the same way, and you will not lose the principle. Suppose someone asks: "What is darkness?" Answer: "Light is the cause, darkness is the condition. When light vanishes, there is darkness.'
