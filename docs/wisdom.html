般若波羅蜜多心經

心經 — 星期六

無罣礙故，無有恐怖，遠離顛倒夢想，究竟涅槃。三世諸佛，依般若波羅蜜多故，得阿耨多羅三藐三菩提。

Without hindrance, there is no fear. Far from all inverted views, one attains nirvana. All buddhas of past, present, and future rely on Prajna Paramita and attain supreme, perfect enlightenment.

孟子 告子上

孟子．告子上

孟子曰：「牛山之木嘗美矣，以其郊於大國也，斧斤伐之，可以為美乎？是其日夜之所息，雨露之所潤，非無萌櫱之生焉，牛羊又從而牧之，是以若彼濯濯也。人見其濯濯也，以為未嘗有材焉，此豈山之性也哉？雖存乎人者，豈無仁義之心哉？其所以放其良心者，亦猶斧斤之於木也，旦旦而伐之，可以為美乎？其日夜之所息，平旦之氣，其好惡與人相近也者幾希，則其旦晝之所為，有梏亡之矣。梏之反覆，則其夜氣不足以存；夜氣不足以存，則其違禽獸不遠矣。人見其禽獸也，而以為未嘗有才焉者，是豈人之情也哉？故苟得其養，無物不長；苟失其養，無物不消。孔子曰：『操則存，舍則亡；出入無時，莫知其鄉。』惟心之謂與？」

Mencius said: "The trees on Ox Mountain were once beautiful. But because it borders a great city, axes and hatchets cut them down. Could they remain beautiful? With the respite that day and night provide, and the moisture of rain and dew, buds and sprouts did grow again—but then cattle and sheep came to graze on them. That is why the mountain looks so bare. People see its bareness and suppose it never had timber. But is this the nature of the mountain? And as for what is preserved in a person, how could there be no heart of benevolence and righteousness? The way one lets go of one's good heart is like the axes and hatchets on the trees: hack at it day after day, and can it remain beautiful? With the restorative rest of day and night, and the air of the calm dawn, one's likes and dislikes come close to those of other people—but just barely. Then what one does during the day shackles and destroys it again. When this shackling is repeated over and over, the restorative night air is no longer enough to preserve it. When the night air is no longer enough, one is not far from an animal. Others see one's animality and suppose there was never any good endowment there. But is this truly the nature of a human being? Therefore, given the right nourishment, there is nothing that will not grow; deprived of nourishment, there is nothing that will not wither away. Confucius said: 'Hold on to it and it is preserved; let it go and it is lost. It comes and goes without fixed times, and no one knows its direction.' This was spoken of the heart, was it not?"

金剛般若波羅蜜經／能淨業障分第十六

能淨業障分第十六

「復次，須菩提！善男子、善女人，受持讀誦此經，若為人輕賤，是人先世罪業，應墮惡道，以今世人輕賤故，先世罪業則為消滅，當得阿耨多羅三藐三菩提。」「須菩提！我念過去無量阿僧祇劫，於燃燈佛前，得值八百四千萬億那由他諸佛，悉皆供養承事，無空過者；若復有人，於後末世，能受持讀誦此經，所得功德，於我所供養諸佛功德，百分不及一，千萬億分、乃至算數譬喻所不能及。」「須菩提！若善男子，善女人，於後末世，有受持讀誦此經，所得功德，我若具說者，或有人聞，心則狂亂，狐疑不信。須菩提！當知是經義不可思議，果報亦不可思議！」

Furthermore, Subhuti, if a good man or good woman receives, upholds, reads, and recites this sutra, yet is scorned and looked down upon by others, know that this person's karmic offenses from previous lives, which would have led to rebirth in evil realms, are being dissolved through the contempt suffered in this present life, and that person will attain anuttara-samyak-sambodhi. Subhuti, I recall that in the immeasurable asamkhyeya kalpas of the past, before the time of Dipankara Buddha, I encountered and made offerings to eighty-four billion nayutas of buddhas, serving each one without exception. Yet if someone in the final age can receive, uphold, read, and recite this sutra, the merit obtained will be a hundred times — a thousand, ten thousand, a billion times — greater than the merit of my offerings to all those buddhas, so great that no number, no calculation, no analogy can approach it. Subhuti, if I were to fully describe the merit that such a good man or good woman in the final age would obtain by receiving, upholding, reading, and reciting this sutra, there are those who, upon hearing it, would become bewildered, filled with doubt and disbelief. Subhuti, know that the meaning of this sutra is inconceivable, and its fruition is equally inconceivable!
