般若波羅蜜多心經

https://pages.ucsd.edu/~dkjordan/chin/chtxts/ShinJing.html

舍利子，是諸法空相，不生不滅，不垢不淨，不增不減。是故空中無色，無受想行識。

Shariputra, all dharmas are marked with emptiness — they are neither born nor destroyed, neither defiled nor pure, neither increasing nor decreasing. Therefore, in emptiness there is no form, no feelings, perceptions, mental formations, or consciousness.

孟子 · 萬章上

https://ctext.org/mengzi

曰：「天子能薦人於天，不能使天與之天下；諸侯能薦人於天子，不能使天子與之諸侯；大夫能薦人於諸侯，不能使諸侯與之大夫。昔者堯薦舜於天而天受之，暴之於民而民受之，故曰：天不言，以行與事示之而已矣。」曰：「敢問薦之於天而天受之，暴之於民而民受之，如何？」

Mencius said: "The Son of Heaven can recommend a person to Heaven, but cannot make Heaven give that person the realm. A feudal lord can recommend a person to the Son of Heaven, but cannot make the Son of Heaven give that person a fief. A minister can recommend a person to a feudal lord, but cannot make the feudal lord give that person a ministry. In ancient times, Yao recommended Shun to Heaven, and Heaven accepted him. He presented Shun to the people, and the people accepted him. Therefore I say: Heaven does not speak — it simply reveals its will through actions and affairs." Wan Zhang said: "May I ask how Heaven accepted him when he was recommended to Heaven, and how the people accepted him when he was presented to the people?"

六祖大師法寶壇經 · 付囑品第十

入是十二入——外六塵——色、聲、香、味、觸、法；內六門——眼、耳、鼻、舌、身、意是也。界是十八界——六塵、六門、六識是也。自性能含萬法，名含藏識。若起思量，即是轉識。生六識，出六門，見六塵，如是一十八界，皆從自性起用。自性若邪，起十八邪；自性若正，起十八正。若惡用即眾生用，善用即佛用。用由何等？由自性有。對法外境，無情五對：天與地對，日與月對，明與暗對，陰與陽對，水與火對；此是五對也。

'The sense-fields are the twelve sense-fields: the six external objects — form, sound, smell, taste, touch, and mental objects; and the six internal faculties — eye, ear, nose, tongue, body, and mind. The sense-realms are the eighteen realms: the six objects, the six faculties, and the six consciousnesses. The self-nature can contain all dharmas and is called the storehouse consciousness. If thinking arises, that is the transforming consciousness. It generates the six consciousnesses, which go out through the six faculties and perceive the six objects. Thus the eighteen realms all arise from the functioning of the self-nature. If the self-nature is deviant, it gives rise to eighteen deviations; if the self-nature is correct, it gives rise to eighteen correct functions. If used for evil, that is the functioning of a sentient being; if used for good, that is the functioning of a Buddha. What determines the use? The self-nature. For the pairs concerning external objects and non-sentient phenomena, there are five: heaven and earth, sun and moon, light and darkness, yin and yang, water and fire — these are the five pairs.'
