般若波羅蜜多心經

舍利子，色不異空，空不異色，色即是空，空即是色，受想行識，亦復如是。

Shariputra, form is not different from emptiness, emptiness is not different from form. Form is emptiness, emptiness is form. The same is true of feelings, perceptions, mental formations, and consciousness.

孟子 · 滕文公上

「吾聞用夏變夷者，未聞變於夷者也。陳良，楚產也。悅周公、仲尼之道，北學於中國。北方之學者，未能或之先也。彼所謂豪傑之士也。子之兄弟事之數十年，師死而遂倍之。昔者孔子沒，三年之外，門人治任將歸，入揖於子貢，相向而哭，皆失聲，然後歸。子貢反，築室於場，獨居三年，然後歸。他日，子夏、子張、子游以有若似聖人，欲以所事孔子事之，彊曾子。曾子曰：『不可。江漢以濯之，秋陽以暴之，皜皜乎不可尚已。』今也南蠻鴃舌之人，非先王之道，子倍子之師而學之，亦異於曾子矣。吾聞出於幽谷遷于喬木者，末聞下喬木而入於幽谷者。《魯頌》曰：『戎狄是膺，荊舒是懲。』周公方且膺之，子是之學，亦為不善變矣。」

'I have heard of using the ways of the Central States to transform barbarians, but I have never heard of being transformed by barbarians. Chen Liang was a native of Chu. Delighting in the Way of the Duke of Zhou and Confucius, he traveled north to study in the Central States. Among the scholars of the north, none had been able to surpass him. He was what one would call a truly outstanding man. You and your brother studied under him for decades, and when your teacher died you turned your back on him. In the past, when Confucius died, after three years his disciples packed their bags to go home. Before leaving, they went in to bow to Zigong, and facing one another they wept until they lost their voices, and only then departed. Zigong went back and built a hut by the burial ground, where he lived alone for three more years before going home. On another occasion, Zixia, Zizhang, and Ziyou, thinking that You Ruo resembled the sage, wished to serve him as they had served Confucius, and tried to compel Zengzi to join them. Zengzi said: "That must not be. Washed by the waters of the Yangtze and the Han, bleached by the autumn sun — so dazzling white that nothing can surpass it." Now here is a man of the southern barbarians with a twittering tongue who denounces the Way of the ancient kings, and you turn your back on your teacher to study under him — you are quite different from Zengzi. I have heard of birds leaving dark valleys to move to tall trees, but I have never heard of them descending from tall trees into dark valleys. The Lu Hymns say: "Strike the Rong and Di, punish Jing and Shu." The Duke of Zhou was about to strike them — yet you choose to learn from them. This is hardly a change for the better.'

六祖大師法寶壇經 · 定慧品第四

善知識！道須通流，何以却滯？心不住法，道即通流；心若住法，名為自縛。若言常坐不動是，只如舍利弗宴坐林中，却被維摩詰訶。善知識！又有人教坐，看心觀靜，不動不起，從此置功。迷人不會，便執成顛。如此者眾，如是相教，故知大錯。」

師示眾云：「善知識！本來正教，無有頓漸，人性自有利鈍。迷人漸修，悟人頓契。自識本心，自見本性，即無差別，所以立頓漸之假名。

'Good friends! The Way must flow freely — why would you cause it to stagnate? When the mind does not dwell on dharmas, the Way flows freely. When the mind dwells on dharmas, that is called binding yourself. If you say that constantly sitting without moving is correct, consider that Shariputra was sitting quietly in the forest and was scolded by Vimalakirti. Good friends! There are also those who teach sitting meditation by watching the mind and contemplating stillness, remaining motionless and unmoving, and devoting their efforts to this. Deluded people who do not understand latch on to this and become confused and unbalanced. There are many such people. Those who instruct others in this way are, one should know, gravely in error.' The Master addressed the assembly: 'Good friends! Originally the true teaching has no distinction of sudden and gradual — it is human nature that has sharpness and dullness. The deluded cultivate gradually; the awakened achieve sudden realization. When you recognize your own original mind and see your own original nature, then there is no difference. Therefore the labels "sudden" and "gradual" are merely provisional designations.'
