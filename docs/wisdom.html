般若波羅蜜多心經

觀自在菩薩，行深般若波羅蜜多時，照見五蘊皆空，度一切苦厄。

Avalokitesvara Bodhisattva, when practicing the profound Prajna Paramita, illuminated the five aggregates and saw that they are all empty, thus overcoming all suffering and distress.

孟子 · 滕文公下

景春曰：「公孫衍、張儀豈不誠大丈夫哉？一怒而諸侯懼，安居而天下熄。」孟子曰：「是焉得為大丈夫乎？子未學禮乎？丈夫之冠也，父命之；女子之嫁也，母命之，往送之門，戒之曰：『往之女家，必敬必戒，無違夫子！』以順為正者，妾婦之道也。居天下之廣居，立天下之正位，行天下之大道。得志與民由之，不得志獨行其道。富貴不能淫，貧賤不能移，威武不能屈。此之謂大丈夫。」周霄問曰：「古之君子仕乎？」

Jing Chun said: "Are not Gongsun Yan and Zhang Yi truly great men? When they are angry, the feudal lords tremble; when they are at ease, the world is at peace." Mencius said: "How can they be considered great men? Have you not studied the rites? When a young man is capped, his father instructs him; when a young woman is married, her mother instructs her. Seeing her off at the gate, the mother warns: 'When you go to your husband's house, you must be respectful and careful — never disobey your husband!' To take obedience as the standard — that is the way of a concubine. A true great man dwells in the broad house of the world — benevolence; stands in the correct position of the world — propriety; walks the great road of the world — righteousness. When he achieves his ambitions, he shares the Way with the people; when he does not, he walks the Way alone. Wealth and rank cannot corrupt him, poverty and low station cannot sway him, power and force cannot bend him. This is what I call a great man." Zhou Xiao asked: "Did the gentlemen of antiquity serve in government?"

六祖大師法寶壇經 · 坐禪品第五

』善知識！於念念中，自見本性清淨，自修自行，自成佛道。」。

'Good friends! In each successive thought, see for yourselves that your original nature is pure. Cultivate and practice by yourselves, and accomplish the Buddha Way by yourselves.'
