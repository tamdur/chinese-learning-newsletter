般若波羅蜜多心經

Tuesday — 般若波羅蜜多心經

舍利子，色不異空，空不異色，色即是空，空即是色，受想行識，亦復如是。

Shariputra, form is not different from emptiness, emptiness is not different from form. Form is emptiness, emptiness is form. The same is true of feelings, perceptions, mental formations, and consciousness.

孟子：告子下

孟子 · 告子下

「交聞文王十尺，湯九尺，今交九尺四寸以長，食粟而已，如何則可？」

Cao Jiao said: 'I have heard that King Wen was ten feet tall and Tang was nine feet tall. Now I am over nine feet four inches, yet all I do is eat grain. What must I do to become like them?'

金剛般若波羅蜜經：無法可得分第二十二

金剛般若波羅蜜經 · 無法可得分第二十二

須菩提白佛言：「世尊！佛得阿耨多羅三藐三菩提。為無所得耶？」佛言：「如是，如是。須菩提！我於阿耨多羅三藐三菩提乃至無有少法可得，是名阿耨多羅三藐三菩提。」

Subhuti addressed the Buddha, saying: "World-Honored One! When the Buddha attained anuttara-samyak-sambodhi, was there truly nothing attained?" The Buddha said: "Just so, just so. Subhuti, with regard to anuttara-samyak-sambodhi, there is not even the slightest dharma to be attained. This is what is called anuttara-samyak-sambodhi."
