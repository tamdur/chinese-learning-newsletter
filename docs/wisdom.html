般若波羅蜜多心經 — Heart Sutra — Wednesday

舍利子，是諸法空相，不生不滅，不垢不淨，不增不減。是故空中無色，無受想行識。

Shariputra, all dharmas are marked with emptiness — they are neither born nor destroyed, neither defiled nor pure, neither increasing nor decreasing. Therefore, in emptiness there is no form, no feelings, perceptions, mental formations, or consciousness.

孟子・離婁上 — Mencius 131

孟子曰：「求也為季氏宰，無能改於其德，而賦粟倍他日。孔子曰：『求非我徒也，小子鳴鼓而攻之可也。』由此觀之，君不行仁政而富之，皆棄於孔子者也。況於為之強戰？爭地以戰，殺人盈野；爭城以戰，殺人盈城。此所謂率土地而食人肉，罪不容於死。故善戰者服上刑，連諸侯者次之，辟草萊、任土地者次之。」

Mencius said: "Ran Qiu served as steward of the Ji family. Unable to improve their virtue, he doubled the grain taxes compared to before. Confucius said: 'Qiu is no disciple of mine. My young students, you may beat the drum and attack him.' From this we can see that any ruler who does not practice benevolent government but is enriched by his ministers—all such ministers were rejected by Confucius. How much more so those who wage aggressive war on his behalf? When wars are fought over territory, the dead fill the fields; when wars are fought over cities, the dead fill the cities. This is what is called leading the land to devour human flesh—a crime for which death is not punishment enough. Therefore those who are skilled at war deserve the heaviest punishment; those who form alliances among the feudal lords for war deserve the next; and those who open up wastelands and exploit the land through heavy taxation deserve the next."
