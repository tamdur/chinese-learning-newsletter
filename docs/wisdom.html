般若波羅蜜多心經

舍利子，是諸法空相，不生不滅，不垢不淨，不增不減。是故空中無色，無受想行識。

Shariputra, all dharmas are marked with emptiness — they are neither born nor destroyed, neither defiled nor pure, neither increasing nor decreasing. Therefore, in emptiness there is no form, no feelings, perceptions, mental formations, or consciousness.

孟子 — 萬章上

孟子曰：「否，不然也。好事者為之也。於衛主顏讎由。彌子之妻與子路之妻，兄弟也。彌子謂子路曰：『孔子主我，衛卿可得也。』子路以告。孔子曰：『有命。』孔子進以禮，退以義，得之不得曰『有命』。而主癰疽與侍人瘠環，是無義無命也。孔子悅於魯衛，遭宋桓司馬將要而殺之，微服而過宋。是時孔子當阨，主司城貞子，為陳侯周臣。吾聞觀近臣，以其所為主；觀遠臣，以其所主。若孔子主癰疽與侍人瘠環，何以為孔子？」

Mencius said: "No, that is not so. This is the fabrication of meddlesome people. In Wei, Confucius lodged with Yan Chou You. The wife of Mi Zi and the wife of Zi Lu were sisters. Mi Zi said to Zi Lu: 'If Confucius lodges with me, he can obtain the position of minister of Wei.' Zi Lu reported this to Confucius. Confucius said: 'That is a matter of fate.' Confucius advanced according to propriety and withdrew according to righteousness. Whether he obtained office or not, he said: 'That is a matter of fate.' Had he lodged with the eunuch Yong Ju or the attendant Ji Huan, that would have been to abandon both righteousness and fate. When Confucius was out of favor in both Lu and Wei, he encountered the Song minister of war Huan Tui, who plotted to intercept and kill him. He passed through Song in disguise. At that time, Confucius was in dire straits and lodged with Sicheng Zhen Zi, who was a minister of the Marquis of Chen. I have heard that one judges a minister at court by whom he serves, and a minister abroad by whom he lodges with. If Confucius had lodged with the eunuch Yong Ju and the attendant Ji Huan, how could he have been Confucius?"

六祖大師法寶壇經 — 付囑品第十

師於太極元年壬子延和七月命門人往新州國恩寺建塔，仍令促工，次年夏末落成。七月一日，集徒眾曰：「吾至八月，欲離世間。汝等有疑，早須相問，為汝破疑，令汝迷盡。吾若去後，無人教汝。」法海等聞，悉皆涕泣。惟有神會，神情不動，亦無涕泣。師云：「神會小師却得善不善等，毀譽不動，哀樂不生；餘者不得。數年山中竟修何道？汝今悲泣，為憂阿誰？若憂吾不知去處，吾自知去處。吾若不知去處，終不預報於汝。

In the first year of the Taiji era, the year renzi, in the seventh month of the Yanhe period, the Master ordered his disciples to go to Guoen Temple in Xinzhou to build a stupa, and urged them to hasten the work. It was completed by the end of summer the following year. On the first day of the seventh month, he gathered his disciples and said: 'In the eighth month I wish to leave this world. If you have doubts, ask your questions soon so I may resolve them for you and put an end to your confusion. Once I am gone, there will be no one to teach you.' When Fahai and the others heard this, they all wept. Only Shenhui remained unmoved in spirit, without tears. The Master said: 'Young Master Shenhui alone has attained equanimity toward good and bad, is unmoved by praise or blame, and does not give rise to sorrow or joy. The rest of you have not achieved this. After all these years of practice in the mountains, what Way have you been cultivating? Now you weep — for whom are you worried? If you are worried that I do not know where I am going, I know where I am going. If I did not know, I would not have told you in advance.'
