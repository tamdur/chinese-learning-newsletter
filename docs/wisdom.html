般若波羅蜜多心經 — Heart Sutra — Wednesday

舍利子，是諸法空相，不生不滅，不垢不淨，不增不減。是故空中無色，無受想行識。

Shariputra, all dharmas are marked with emptiness — they are neither born nor destroyed, neither defiled nor pure, neither increasing nor decreasing. Therefore, in emptiness there is no form, no feelings, perceptions, mental formations, or consciousness.

孟子 — 公孫丑上

孟子曰：「以力假仁者霸，霸必有大國，以德行仁者王，王不待大。湯以七十里，文王以百里。以力服人者，非心服也，力不贍也；以德服人者，中心悅而誠服也，如七十子之服孔子也。《詩》云：『自西自東，自南自北，無思不服。』此之謂也。」

Mencius said: "One who uses force while feigning benevolence is a hegemon—a hegemon must have a large state. One who practices benevolence through virtue is a true king—a true king need not have a large state. Tang became king with only seventy li, King Wen with only a hundred li. One who subdues people by force does not win their hearts—they submit because their own strength is insufficient. One who wins people through virtue gains their heartfelt and sincere submission, as the seventy disciples submitted to Confucius. The Odes say: 'From west and east, from south and north, none thought of not submitting.' This is what it means."

六祖大師法寶壇經 — Platform Sutra

「祖，三更喚秀入堂，問曰：『偈是汝作否？』秀言：『實是秀作，不敢妄求祖位，望和尚慈悲，看弟子有少智慧否？』祖曰：『汝作此偈，未見本性，只到門外，未入門內。如此見解，覓無上菩提，了不可得；無上菩提，須得言下識自本心，見自本性不生不滅；於一切時中，念念自見萬法無滯，一真一切真，萬境自如如。如如之心，即是真實。若如是見，即是無上菩薩之自性也。

At the third watch, the Patriarch summoned Shenxiu into the hall and asked, 'Did you write that verse?' Shenxiu said, 'It was indeed written by Shenxiu. I dare not presume to seek the patriarchship. I only hope the Master, in his compassion, will judge whether your disciple has some small wisdom.' The Patriarch said, 'This verse of yours shows that you have not yet seen your original nature. You have reached the gate but have not entered it. With such understanding, you cannot attain supreme bodhi. Supreme bodhi requires that in the very moment of hearing the words, you recognize your original mind and see your original nature — which is neither born nor destroyed. At all times, in every thought, you see for yourself that the myriad dharmas are unobstructed. The one truth is all truth, and all things are just as they are. The mind of suchness — that is the real. If you see in this way, that is the self-nature of supreme bodhi.
