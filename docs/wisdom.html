般若波羅蜜多心經

無苦集滅道，無智亦無得。以無所得故，菩提薩埵，依般若波羅蜜多故，心無罣礙。

There is no suffering, no cause of suffering, no cessation, no path; no wisdom and no attainment. With nothing to attain, a bodhisattva relies on Prajna Paramita, and the mind is without hindrance.

孟子 — 梁惠王上

孟子見梁惠王。王曰：「叟不遠千里而來，亦將有以利吾國乎？」孟子對曰：「王何必曰利？亦有仁義而已矣。王曰『何以利吾國』？大夫曰『何以利吾家』？士庶人曰『何以利吾身』？上下交征利而國危矣。萬乘之國弒其君者，必千乘之家；千乘之國弒其君者，必百乘之家。萬取千焉，千取百焉，不為不多矣。苟為後義而先利，不奪不饜。未有仁而遺其親者也，未有義而後其君者也。王亦曰仁義而已矣，何必曰利？」

Mencius visited King Hui of Liang. The king said, 'Sir, you have not considered a thousand li too far to come — surely you have something that will profit my state?' Mencius replied, 'Why must Your Majesty speak of profit? There is only benevolence and righteousness, nothing more. If the king says, "How can I profit my state?" the great officers say, "How can I profit my house?" and the common scholars and people say, "How can I profit myself?" — then those above and below will compete with one another for profit, and the state will be endangered. In a state of ten thousand chariots, the one who murders its ruler will surely be the head of a house with a thousand chariots; in a state of a thousand chariots, the one who murders its ruler will surely be the head of a house with a hundred chariots. To take a thousand from ten thousand, or a hundred from a thousand — that is no small share. But if righteousness is put last and profit is put first, they will not be satisfied without seizing everything. There has never been a benevolent person who abandoned his parents, nor a righteous person who neglected his ruler. Let Your Majesty speak only of benevolence and righteousness — why must you speak of profit?'

禪宗經典 — 信心銘

至道無難唯嫌揀擇但莫憎愛洞然明白毫釐有差天地懸隔欲得現前莫存順逆違順相爭是為心病不識玄旨徒勞念靜圓同太虛無欠無餘良由取捨所以不如莫逐有緣勿住空忍一種平懷泯然自盡止動歸止止更彌動唯滯兩邊寧知一種一種不通兩處失功遣有沒有從空背空多言多慮轉不相應絕言絕慮無處不通歸根得旨隨照失宗須臾返照勝卻前空

The Great Way is not difficult — it only avoids picking and choosing. Simply do not hate or love, and it will be thoroughly clear. A hair's breadth of difference, and heaven and earth are set apart. If you wish it to appear before you, do not hold to for or against. Conflict between for and against — this is the mind's disease. Without recognizing the profound principle, you toil in vain to quiet your thoughts. Perfect like great emptiness, with nothing lacking, nothing in excess. Only because of grasping and rejecting do we fail to see its suchness. Do not chase after entanglements, do not dwell in the patience of emptiness. In oneness and equanimity of heart, all things dissolve of themselves. When you stop movement to return to stillness, that stopping only creates more agitation. If you linger in either extreme, how will you ever know oneness? Without penetrating oneness, both paths lose their function. Banish existence and you drown in it; follow emptiness and you turn your back on it. Excessive words, excessive thinking — only take you further from accordance. Cut off words, cut off thinking, and there is nowhere you cannot penetrate. Return to the root and grasp the meaning; chase after appearances and lose the source. One moment of inward reflection surpasses the emptiness that came before.
