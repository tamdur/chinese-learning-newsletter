般若波羅蜜多心經

觀自在菩薩，行深般若波羅蜜多時，照見五蘊皆空，度一切苦厄。

Avalokitesvara Bodhisattva, when practicing the profound Prajna Paramita, illuminated the five aggregates and saw that they are all empty, thus overcoming all suffering and distress.

孟子 — 梁惠王下

魯平公將出。嬖人臧倉者請曰：「他日君出，則必命有司所之。今乘輿已駕矣，有司未知所之。敢請。」公曰：「將見孟子。」曰：「何哉？君所為輕身以先於匹夫者，以為賢乎？禮義由賢者出。而孟子之後喪踰前喪。君無見焉！」公曰：「諾。」

Duke Ping of Lu was about to go out when his favorite attendant, Zang Cang, asked, "On other occasions when Your Lordship goes out, you always instruct the officials where you are going. The carriage is already harnessed, but the officials do not yet know the destination. May I ask where you are going?" The duke said, "I am going to see Mencius." Zang Cang said, "What is this? That you would lower yourself to call first on a commoner—is it because you consider him worthy? Propriety and righteousness come from the worthy. Yet Mencius's funeral for his mother exceeded his funeral for his father. I urge Your Lordship not to see him." The duke said, "Very well."

金剛般若波羅蜜經 — 法身非相分第二十六

「須菩提！於意云何？可以三十二相觀如來不？」須菩提言：「如是！如是！以三十二相觀如來。」佛言：「須菩提！若以三十二相觀如來者，轉輪聖王則是如來。」須菩提白佛言：「世尊！如我解佛所說義。不應以三十二相觀如來。」爾時，世尊而說偈言：「若以色見我，以音聲求我，是人行邪道，不能見如來。」

"Subhuti, what do you think? Can the Tathagata be perceived through the thirty-two marks?" Subhuti said: "Yes! Yes! The Tathagata can be perceived through the thirty-two marks." The Buddha said: "Subhuti, if the Tathagata could be perceived through the thirty-two marks, then a wheel-turning sage king would be a Tathagata." Subhuti addressed the Buddha: "World-Honored One! As I understand the meaning of what the Buddha has said, one should not perceive the Tathagata through the thirty-two marks." At that time, the World-Honored One spoke this verse: "If one seeks me through form, or seeks me through sound, this person walks a misguided path and cannot see the Tathagata."
