般若波羅蜜多心經

無眼耳鼻舌身意，無色聲香味觸法，無眼界，乃至無意識界。無無明，亦無無明盡，乃至無老死，亦無老死盡。

There are no eyes, ears, nose, tongue, body, or mind; no sights, sounds, smells, tastes, touches, or objects of mind; no realm of sight, and so on up to no realm of consciousness. There is no ignorance and no end of ignorance, and so on up to no old age and death and no end of old age and death.

孟子 · 離婁下

孟子告齊宣王曰：「君之視臣如手足，則臣視君如腹心；君之視臣如犬馬，則臣視君如國人；君之視臣如土芥，則臣視君如寇讎。」王曰：「禮，為舊君有服，何如斯可為服矣？」

Mencius said to King Xuan of Qi: "When a ruler regards his ministers as his hands and feet, the ministers regard their ruler as their heart and belly. When the ruler regards his ministers as dogs and horses, the ministers regard their ruler as any common man. When the ruler regards his ministers as dirt and weeds, the ministers regard their ruler as an enemy." The King said: "According to ritual, one wears mourning for a former ruler. Under what conditions does one do so?"

By chance Xuance, a disciple of the Master, came to visit, and the two fell into vigorous discussion. Xuanjue's words, without his intending it, accorded with those of the patriarchs. Xuance asked, "Virtuous one, from whom did you receive the Dharma?" He replied, "I have studied the Vaipulya sutras and treatises, each of which I received through a proper line of teachers. Later, through the Vimalakīrti Sutra, I awakened to the Buddha's mind-teaching, but I have had no one to confirm it."

Xuance said, "Before the time of the Buddha King of Awesome Voice, that would have been acceptable; but since the King of Awesome Voice, anyone who awakens on his own without a teacher is nothing but a self-styled heretic." Xuanjue said, "Then I ask you, virtuous one, to be my witness and confirm me." Xuance said, "My word carries too little weight. At Caoxi there is the Great Master, the Sixth Patriarch. People gather there like clouds from every direction, all of them coming to receive the Dharma. If you will go, I will travel with you."
