般若波羅蜜多心經

舍利子，色不異空，空不異色，色即是空，空即是色，受想行識，亦復如是。

Shariputra, form is not different from emptiness, emptiness is not different from form. Form is emptiness, emptiness is form. The same is true of feelings, perceptions, mental formations, and consciousness.

孟子 — 離婁下

公都子曰：「匡章，通國皆稱不孝焉。夫子與之遊，又從而禮貌之，敢問何也？」

Gongdu Zi said: "Kuang Zhang — the whole country calls him unfilial. Yet you, Master, associate with him and even treat him with courtesy. May I ask why?"

六祖大師法寶壇經 — 頓漸品第八

見性之人，立亦得、不立亦得，去來自由，無滯無礙，應用隨作，應語隨答，普見化身，不離自性，即得自在神通游戲三昧，是名見性。」志誠再啟師曰：「如何是不立義？」師曰：「自性無非、無癡無亂，念念般若觀照，常離法相，自由自在，縱橫盡得，有何可立？自性自悟，頓悟頓修，亦無漸次，所以不立一切法。諸法寂滅，有何次第？」志誠禮拜，願為執侍，朝夕不懈。 僧志徹，江西人，本姓張，名行昌，少任俠。

'One who has seen their nature can establish or not establish — coming and going freely, without obstruction or hindrance. In action one responds spontaneously, in speech one answers spontaneously. Seeing transformation bodies everywhere, yet never departing from self-nature — this is to attain the samadhi of sovereign spiritual power and playful freedom. This is called seeing one's nature.' Zhicheng again addressed the Master: 'What is the meaning of not establishing?' The Master said: 'When self-nature is without wrong, without delusion, without disturbance, and every thought is illuminated by prajna, always transcending dharma-characteristics, free and at ease, penetrating everywhere without obstruction — what is there to establish? Self-nature is self-awakened; awakening is sudden, practice is sudden. There are no gradual stages, and therefore not a single dharma is established. All dharmas are quiescent — what stages could there be?' Zhicheng bowed in reverence and vowed to serve as the Master's attendant, diligent morning and evening without slackening.
