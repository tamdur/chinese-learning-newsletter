般若波羅蜜多心經

無罣礙故，無有恐怖，遠離顛倒夢想，究竟涅槃。三世諸佛，依般若波羅蜜多故，得阿耨多羅三藐三菩提。

Without hindrance, there is no fear. Far from all inverted views, one attains nirvana. All buddhas of past, present, and future rely on Prajna Paramita and attain supreme, perfect enlightenment.

孟子 — 萬章下

孟子曰：「伯夷，目不視惡色，耳不聽惡聲。非其君不事，非其民不使。治則進，亂則退。橫政之所出，橫民之所止，不忍居也。思與鄉人處，如以朝衣朝冠坐於塗炭也。當紂之時，居北海之濱，以待天下之清也。故聞伯夷之風者，頑夫廉，懦夫有立志。

Mencius said: "Bo Yi would not look at improper sights, nor listen to improper sounds. He would not serve a ruler he did not approve of, nor command people he did not approve of. He advanced when there was order and withdrew when there was chaos. He could not bear to dwell wherever oppressive government held sway or oppressive people resided. He thought that being among common villagers was like sitting in mud and ashes while wearing court robes and cap. During the time of the tyrant Zhou, he lived on the shores of the North Sea, waiting for the world to become pure again. Therefore, those who heard of Bo Yi's example — the greedy became incorruptible, and the cowardly found resolve."

師春秋七十有六，年二十四傳衣，三十九祝髮，說法利生三十七載。得旨嗣法者，四十三人，悟道超凡者，莫知其數。

'After my passing, practice according to this as though I were still present. Even if you go against my teaching, then even if I were present in the world, it would be of no benefit.' He further spoke a verse: "Sitting utterly still, not cultivating good; free and at ease, not creating evil. In quietude, cutting off seeing and hearing; vast and open, the mind has no attachments." After speaking the verse, the Master sat upright until the third watch of the night, then suddenly said to his disciples: 'I am going now!' and passed away serenely. At that time, a strange fragrance filled the room, a white rainbow touched the earth, the trees turned white, and the birds and animals cried out in mourning. The Master lived seventy-six years. He received the robe at age twenty-four, was ordained at thirty-nine, and taught the Dharma for the benefit of sentient beings for thirty-seven years. Forty-three people received and continued his Dharma transmission, while those who awakened to the Way and transcended the ordinary were beyond counting.
