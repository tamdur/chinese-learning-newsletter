般若波羅蜜多心經

無眼耳鼻舌身意，無色聲香味觸法，無眼界，乃至無意識界。無無明，亦無無明盡，乃至無老死，亦無老死盡。

There are no eyes, ears, nose, tongue, body, or mind; no sights, sounds, smells, tastes, touches, or objects of mind; no realm of sight, and so on up to no realm of consciousness. There is no ignorance and no end of ignorance, and so on up to no old age and death and no end of old age and death.

孟子 — 告子上

孟子曰：「乃若其情，則可以為善矣，乃所謂善也。若夫為不善，非才之罪也。惻隱之心，人皆有之；羞惡之心，人皆有之；恭敬之心，人皆有之；是非之心，人皆有之。惻隱之心，仁也；羞惡之心，義也；恭敬之心，禮也；是非之心，智也。仁義禮智，非由外鑠我也，我固有之也，弗思耳矣。故曰：『求則得之，舍則失之。』或相倍蓰而無算者，不能盡其才者也。《詩》曰：『天生蒸民，有物有則。民之秉夷，好是懿德。』孔子曰：『為此詩者，其知道乎！故有物必有則，民之秉夷也，故好是懿德。』」

Mencius said: "As far as our natural feelings are concerned, we are capable of being good. That is what I mean by calling human nature good. As for those who do what is not good, that is not the fault of their native endowment. The feeling of compassion—all people have it. The feeling of shame and disgust—all people have it. The feeling of respect and reverence—all people have it. The feeling of right and wrong—all people have it. The feeling of compassion is benevolence. The feeling of shame is righteousness. The feeling of respect is propriety. The feeling of right and wrong is wisdom. Benevolence, righteousness, propriety, and wisdom are not welded onto us from outside—we inherently possess them. It is simply that we do not reflect upon this. Therefore it is said: 'Seek and you will find them; neglect and you will lose them.' That people differ from one another by twice, five times, or even incalculably—that is because they cannot fully develop their native endowment. The Book of Odes says: 'Heaven gave birth to the multitudes of people; where there are things, there are standards. The people's constant nature is to love admirable virtue.' Confucius said: 'The one who wrote this poem truly understood the Way! Therefore where there are things there must be standards, and because the people hold to their constant nature, they love admirable virtue.'"

金剛般若波羅蜜經 — 持經功德分第十五

「須菩提！若有善男子、善女人，初日分以恆河沙等身布施，中日分復以恆河沙等身布施，後日分亦以恆河沙等身布施，如是無量百千萬億劫，以身布施；若復有人，聞此經典，信心不逆，其福勝彼，何況書寫、受持、讀誦、為人解說。」「須菩提！以要言之，是經有不可思議、不可稱量、無邊功德。如來為發大乘者說，為發最上乘者說。若有人能受持讀誦，廣為人說，如來悉知是人，悉見是人，皆得成就不可量、不可稱、無有邊、不可思議功德。

Subhuti, if a good man or good woman were to give away as many bodies as there are grains of sand in the Ganges in the morning, give away as many bodies as there are grains of sand in the Ganges at midday, and give away as many bodies as there are grains of sand in the Ganges in the evening, and continued such bodily sacrifice for immeasurable hundreds of thousands of millions of billions of kalpas — yet if another person, upon hearing this sutra, gave rise to faith without opposition, that person's merit would surpass the former's, to say nothing of one who copies, receives, upholds, reads, recites, and explains it to others. Subhuti, to put it simply, this sutra possesses inconceivable, immeasurable, and boundless merit. The Tathagata has spoken it for those who have set out on the Great Vehicle, for those who have set out on the Supreme Vehicle. If there are those who can receive, uphold, read, and recite it, and widely explain it to others, the Tathagata will fully know and fully see that such persons will achieve immeasurable, incalculable, boundless, and inconceivable merit.
