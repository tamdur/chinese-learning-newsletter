般若波羅蜜多心經

Heart Sutra · Tuesday segment (2/7)

舍利子，色不異空，空不異色，色即是空，空即是色，受想行識，亦復如是。

Shariputra, form is not different from emptiness, emptiness is not different from form. Form is emptiness, emptiness is form. The same is true of feelings, perceptions, mental formations, and consciousness.

孟子・離婁上

Mencius · 離婁上 · passage 125 of 269

孟子曰：「人有恆言，皆曰『天下國家』。天下之本在國，國之本在家，家之本在身。」孟子曰：「為政不難，不得罪於巨室。巨室之所慕，一國慕之；一國之所慕，天下慕之；故沛然德教溢乎四海。」孟子曰：「天下有道，小德役大德，小賢役大賢；天下無道，小役大，弱役強。斯二者天也。順天者存，逆天者亡。齊景公曰：『既不能令，又不受命，是絕物也。』涕出而女於吳。

Mencius said: "People have a common saying—they all speak of 'the realm, the state, the family.' The foundation of the realm lies in the state; the foundation of the state lies in the family; the foundation of the family lies in the individual." Mencius said: "Governing is not difficult—do not offend the great families. What the great families admire, the whole state admires; what the whole state admires, the whole realm admires. And so virtue and moral instruction will overflow to fill the four seas." Mencius said: "When the realm has the Way, those of lesser virtue serve those of greater virtue, and those of lesser ability serve those of greater ability. When the realm lacks the Way, the small serve the great, and the weak serve the strong. Both of these are the workings of Heaven. Those who follow Heaven survive; those who go against Heaven perish. Duke Jing of Qi said: 'Since we can neither command others nor accept commands from them, this will cut us off entirely.' He wept as he sent his daughter to marry into the state of Wu.

六祖大師法寶壇經・機緣品第七

Platform Sutra · 機緣品第七 · passage 125 of 178

口誦心行，即是轉經；口誦心不行，即是被經轉。聽吾偈曰：心迷法華轉，心悟轉法華，誦經久不明，與義作讎家。無念念即正，有念念成邪，有無俱不計，長御白牛車。」達聞偈，不覺悲泣，言下大悟，而告師曰：「法達從昔已來，實未曾轉法華，乃被法華轉。」再啟曰：「經云：『諸大聲聞乃至菩薩，皆盡思共度量，不能測佛智。』今令凡夫但悟自心，便名佛之知見。自非上根，未免疑謗。

'When you recite with your mouth and practice with your mind, that is turning the sutra. When you recite with your mouth but do not practice with your mind, you are being turned by the sutra. Listen to my verse: When the mind is deluded, it is turned by the Lotus; when the mind is awakened, it turns the Lotus. Reciting for so long without understanding makes one an enemy of the teaching's meaning. Without thought-attachment, thought is correct; with thought-attachment, thought becomes deviant. When neither being nor non-being is calculated, one rides forever in the white ox cart.' Upon hearing the verse, Fada wept without realizing it and experienced great awakening. He said to the Master: 'From the very beginning until now, Fada has in truth never turned the Lotus Sutra — rather, the Lotus Sutra has been turning me.' He then asked further: 'The sutra says: "All the great shravakas and even the bodhisattvas, pooling all their thought, cannot fathom the Buddha's wisdom." Yet now you say that ordinary people need only awaken to their own mind and it is called the Buddha's knowledge and insight. Unless one is of the highest capacity, this will inevitably give rise to doubt and slander.'
