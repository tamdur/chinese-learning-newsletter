般若波羅蜜多心經

觀自在菩薩，行深般若波羅蜜多時，照見五蘊皆空，度一切苦厄。

Avalokitesvara Bodhisattva, when practicing the profound Prajna Paramita, illuminated the five aggregates and saw that they are all empty, thus overcoming all suffering and distress.

孟子・離婁下

孟子曰：「原泉混混，不舍晝夜。盈科而後進，放乎四海，有本者如是，是之取爾。苟為無本，七八月之閒雨集，溝澮皆盈；其涸也，可立而待也。故聲聞過情，君子恥之。」孟子曰：「人之所以異於禽於獸者幾希，庶民去之，君子存之。舜明於庶物，察於人倫，由仁義行，非行仁義也。」

Mencius said: "A spring that flows copiously, never ceasing day or night, fills each hollow before moving on, and eventually reaches the four seas. What has a source is like this — that is all he was noting. If something has no source, it is like the rain that collects in the seventh and eighth months, filling all the ditches and gutters — but its drying up can be expected in no time. Therefore, for one's reputation to exceed one's substance is something an exemplary person finds shameful." Mencius said: "What distinguishes human beings from the birds and beasts is but slight. The common people lose it; the exemplary person preserves it. Shun clearly understood the myriad things and had keen insight into human relationships. He acted from benevolence and righteousness, rather than merely performing acts of benevolence and righteousness."

六祖大師法寶壇經・機緣品第七

」隍於是大悟，二十年所得心，都無影響。其夜河北士庶，聞空中有聲云：「隍禪師今日得道。」隍後禮辭，復歸河北，開化四眾。一僧問師云：「黃梅意旨，甚麼人得？」師云：「會佛法人得。」僧云：「和尚還得否？」師云：「我不會佛法。」

師一日欲濯所授之衣而無美泉，因至寺後五里許，見山林欝茂，瑞氣盤旋。師振錫卓地，泉應手而出，積以為池，乃跪膝浣衣石上。

At this Zhihuang was greatly awakened, and the mind he had attained over twenty years left not the slightest trace behind. That night the officials and common people of Hebei heard a voice in the sky saying, “Chan Master Zhihuang has attained the Way today.” Later Zhihuang made his bows, took his leave, and returned to Hebei, where he taught and transformed the fourfold assembly.

A monk asked the Master, “Who is it that has obtained the essential meaning of Huangmei?” The Master said, “One who understands the Buddha-dharma has obtained it.” The monk said, “Has the Venerable obtained it?” The Master said, “I do not understand the Buddha-dharma.”

One day the Master wished to wash the robe that had been transmitted to him, but there was no fine spring nearby. So he went about five li behind the monastery, where he saw the mountain woods lush and thick, with auspicious vapors swirling above them. The Master shook his staff and struck it into the ground, and a spring welled up at the touch of his hand, gathering into a pool. Then he knelt down and washed the robe upon a stone.
