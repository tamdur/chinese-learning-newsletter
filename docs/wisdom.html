般若波羅蜜多心經

觀自在菩薩，行深般若波羅蜜多時，照見五蘊皆空，度一切苦厄。

Avalokitesvara Bodhisattva, when practicing the profound Prajna Paramita, illuminated the five aggregates and saw that they are all empty, thus overcoming all suffering and distress.

孟子：告子上

曰：「耆秦人之炙，無以異於耆吾炙。夫物則亦有然者也，然則耆炙亦有外與？」孟季子問公都子曰：「何以謂義內也？」曰：「行吾敬，故謂之內也。」「鄉人長於伯兄一歲，則誰敬？」曰：「敬兄。」「酌則誰先？」曰：「先酌鄉人。」「所敬在此，所長在彼，果在外，非由內也。」

Mencius said: "Our enjoyment of a man of Qin's roast meat is no different from our enjoyment of our own roast meat. Things can be like this too. So is the enjoyment of roast meat also external?" Meng Jizi asked Gongduzi: "Why do you say righteousness is internal?" Gongduzi said: "I am acting on my own feeling of respect, so I call it internal." "If a fellow villager is one year older than your eldest brother, whom do you show respect to?" "I show respect to my brother." "But when pouring wine, whom do you serve first?" "I serve the villager first." "Your real respect is directed at one person, but the honor you show goes to another. So it is determined by external circumstances, not from within."

離相寂滅分第十四

所以者何？我相，即是非相；人相、眾生相、壽者相，即是非相。何以故？離一切諸相，則名諸佛。」佛告須菩提：「如是，如是！若復有人，得聞是經，不驚、不怖、不畏，當知是人，甚為希有。何以故？須菩提！如來說：第一波羅蜜，即非第一波羅蜜，是名第一波羅蜜。須菩提！忍辱波羅蜜，如來說非忍辱波羅蜜。是名忍辱波羅密。何以故？須菩提！如我昔為歌利王割截身體，我於爾時，無我相、無人相、無眾生相、無壽者相。

Why is this? The mark of self is not a mark; the marks of person, sentient being, and life span are not marks. Why? Because to be free of all marks is to be called a buddha. The Buddha told Subhuti: So it is, so it is! If someone hears this sutra and is not alarmed, not frightened, not afraid, know that such a person is most rare. Why? Subhuti, what the Tathagata calls the first paramita is not the first paramita, and is therefore called the first paramita. Subhuti, the paramita of patience, the Tathagata says, is not the paramita of patience, and is therefore called the paramita of patience. Why? Subhuti, in the past, when King Kalinga cut my body to pieces, at that time I had no mark of self, no mark of person, no mark of sentient being, and no mark of life span.
