般若波羅蜜多心經

Heart Sutra · Monday segment (1 of 7)

觀自在菩薩，行深般若波羅蜜多時，照見五蘊皆空，度一切苦厄。

Avalokitesvara Bodhisattva, when practicing the profound Prajna Paramita, illuminated the five aggregates and saw that they are all empty, thus overcoming all suffering and distress.

孟子・滕文公下

Mencius · 滕文公下 · passage 117 of 269

孟子曰：「於齊國之士，吾必以仲子為巨擘焉。雖然，仲子惡能廉？充仲子之操，則蚓而後可者也。夫蚓，上食槁壤，下飲黃泉。仲子所居之室，伯夷之所築與？抑亦盜跖之所築與？所食之粟，伯夷之所樹與？抑亦盜跖之所樹與？是未可知也。」曰：「是何傷哉？彼身織屨，妻辟纑，以易之也。」

Mencius said: "Among the scholars of Qi, I would certainly regard Zhongzi as the foremost. Nevertheless, how can Zhongzi truly be called a man of integrity? To fully carry out Zhongzi's principles, one would have to be an earthworm to succeed. An earthworm eats dry earth above and drinks from the yellow springs below. But the house Zhongzi lives in — was it built by a Bo Yi, or by a Robber Zhi? The grain he eats — was it planted by a Bo Yi, or by a Robber Zhi? This we cannot know." Kuang Zhang said: "What does that matter? He weaves sandals himself and his wife spins hemp thread, and they trade these for what they need."

六祖大師法寶壇經・懺悔品第六

Platform Sutra · 懺悔品第六 · passage 117 of 178

雖去吾千里，如常在吾邊。於此言下不悟，即對面千里，何勤遠來。珍重！好去。」

一眾聞法，靡不開悟，歡喜奉行。

Even if you are a thousand miles from me, it will be as though you are constantly at my side. But if you cannot awaken at these very words, then even face to face you are a thousand miles away — so why bother making the long journey here? Take care of yourselves! Farewell and go well.' The entire assembly, upon hearing the Dharma, was without exception awakened. They accepted it with joy and put it into practice.
