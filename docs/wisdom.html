般若波羅蜜多心經

Heart Sutra · Sunday segment (7 of 7)

故知般若波羅蜜多，是大神咒，是大明咒，是無上咒，是無等等咒，能除一切苦，真實不虛。故說般若波羅蜜多咒，即說咒曰：揭諦揭諦，波羅揭諦，波羅僧揭諦，菩提薩婆訶。

Therefore, know that Prajna Paramita is the great transcendent mantra, the great bright mantra, the supreme mantra, the unequalled mantra, which can remove all suffering and is true, not false. Therefore he proclaimed the Prajna Paramita mantra, saying: Gate gate paragate parasamgate bodhi svaha.

孟子・滕文公下

Mencius · 滕文公下 · passage 116 of 269

「昔者禹抑洪水而天下平，周公兼夷狄驅猛獸而百姓寧，孔子成《春秋》而亂臣賊子懼。《詩》云：『戎狄是膺，荊舒是懲，則莫我敢承。』無父無君，是周公所膺也。我亦欲正人心，息邪說，距詖行，放淫辭，以承三聖者；豈好辯哉？予不得已也。能言距楊墨者，聖人之徒也。」匡章曰：「陳仲子豈不誠廉士哉？居於陵，三日不食，耳無聞，目無見也。井上有李，螬食實者過半矣，匍匐往將食之，三咽，然後耳有聞，目有見。」

"In the past, Yu suppressed the floodwaters and the world was at peace. The Duke of Zhou subjugated the barbarians and drove away the fierce beasts, and the people found tranquility. Confucius completed the Spring and Autumn Annals, and rebellious ministers and unfilial sons were struck with fear. The Odes say: 'He struck the Rong and Di, he punished Jing and Shu, and none dared stand against us.' Those who deny father and ruler — they are the ones the Duke of Zhou struck down. I too wish to rectify people's hearts, put an end to heresies, oppose crooked conduct, and banish licentious words, in order to carry on the work of these three sages. How could I be fond of disputation? I have no choice. Whoever can argue against Yang and Mo is a disciple of the sages." Kuang Zhang said: "Is Chen Zhongzi not truly a man of integrity? Living in Yuling, he went three days without eating — his ears could not hear, his eyes could not see. Above a well there was a plum tree, its fruit more than half eaten by worms. He crawled over, took it, and ate it. After three bites, his ears could hear and his eyes could see again."

六祖大師法寶壇經・懺悔品第六

Platform Sutra · 懺悔品第六 · passage 116 of 178

頌曰：

迷人修福不修道，只言修福便是道，布施供養福無邊，心中三惡元來造。擬將修福欲滅罪，後世得福罪還在，但向心中除罪緣，名自性中真懺悔。忽悟大乘真懺悔，除邪行正即無罪，學道常於自性觀，即與諸佛同一類。吾祖惟傳此頓法，普願見性同一體，若欲當來覓法身，離諸法相心中洗。努力自見莫悠悠，後念忽絕一世休，若悟大乘得見性，虔恭合掌至心求。」

師言：「善知識！總須誦取，依此修行，言下見性。

The verse says: Deluded people cultivate blessings but not the Way, / Saying that cultivating blessings itself is the Way. / Though the blessings of giving and offerings are boundless, / The three evils in the mind were there all along. / If you try to use cultivated blessings to eliminate offenses, / In future lives you gain blessings but the offenses remain. / Simply remove the causes of offenses within your mind — / This is true repentance within your own self-nature. / Suddenly awakening to the Great Vehicle's true repentance, / Remove the deviant, practice the right, and there are no offenses. / Studying the Way, always contemplate your own nature — / Then you are of the same kind as all Buddhas. / Our patriarchs only transmitted this sudden teaching, / Universally wishing that all who see their nature share one substance. / If in the future you wish to find the Dharma-body, / Wash away attachment to dharma-characteristics within your mind. / Strive to see it yourselves, do not be idle, / For if in the next thought-moment life is suddenly cut off, all is lost. / If you awaken to the Great Vehicle and see your nature, / Join your palms with sincerity and seek it wholeheartedly.' The Master said: 'Good friends! All of you must recite this and practice accordingly. Seeing your nature will happen the moment these words are spoken.
