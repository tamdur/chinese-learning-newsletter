般若波羅蜜多心經 — Heart Sutra — Sunday

故知般若波羅蜜多，是大神咒，是大明咒，是無上咒，是無等等咒，能除一切苦，真實不虛。故說般若波羅蜜多咒，即說咒曰：揭諦揭諦，波羅揭諦，波羅僧揭諦，菩提薩婆訶。

Therefore, know that Prajna Paramita is the great transcendent mantra, the great bright mantra, the supreme mantra, the unequalled mantra, which can remove all suffering and is true, not false. Therefore he proclaimed the Prajna Paramita mantra, saying: Gate gate paragate parasamgate bodhi svaha.

孟子 — 梁惠王下

「昔者齊景公問於晏子曰：『吾欲觀於轉附、朝儛，遵海而南，放于琅邪。吾何脩而可以比於先王觀也？』晏子對曰：『善哉問也！天子適諸侯曰巡狩，巡狩者巡所守也；諸侯朝於天子曰述職，述職者述所職也。無非事者。春省耕而補不足，秋省斂而助不給。夏諺曰：「吾王不遊，吾何以休？吾王不豫，吾何以助？一遊一豫，為諸侯度。」今也不然：師行而糧食，飢者弗食，勞者弗息。睊睊胥讒，民乃作慝。方命虐民，飲食若流。流連荒亡，為諸侯憂。從流下而忘反謂之流，從流上而忘反謂之連，從獸無厭謂之荒，樂酒無厭謂之亡。先王無流連之樂，荒亡之行。惟君所行也。』景公說，大戒於國，出舍於郊。於是始興發補不足。召大師曰：『為我作君臣相說之樂！』蓋徵招角招是也。其詩曰：『畜君何尤？』畜君者，好君也。」

"In ancient times, Duke Jing of Qi asked Yanzi, 'I wish to visit Mount Zhuanfu and Mount Chaowu, then follow the coast southward to Langya. How should I conduct myself so as to compare with the tours of the ancient kings?' Yanzi replied, 'An excellent question! When the Son of Heaven visits the feudal lords, it is called an inspection tour—he inspects what they are guarding. When the feudal lords go to the Son of Heaven, it is called a report on duties—they report on their assigned duties. None of these travels is without purpose. In spring, they inspect the plowing and supply what is lacking; in autumn, they inspect the harvest and assist those in need. The saying of the Xia dynasty goes: If our king does not tour, how shall we have rest? If our king does not travel, how shall we have help? Each tour and each journey set the standard for the feudal lords. But now it is not so: armies march and provisions are consumed, the hungry are not fed, the weary are not rested. People eye each other resentfully and slander grows, and the people turn to wickedness. Commands are flouted, the people are oppressed, and food and drink flow like water. Drifting, indulging, going wild, getting lost—these become a worry for the feudal lords. Going downstream and forgetting to return is called drifting. Going upstream and forgetting to return is called indulging. Hunting without limit is called going wild. Drinking without limit is called getting lost. The ancient kings had no pleasure in drifting or indulging, and no practice of going wild or getting lost. It is for Your Lordship to decide which path to take.' Duke Jing was pleased. He issued a great proclamation throughout the state, then went out and lodged in the suburbs. He then began to open the granaries to relieve the needy. He summoned the Grand Music Master and said, 'Compose for me music of harmony between ruler and minister!' This was presumably the Zhizhao and Jiaozhao. The lyrics said, 'What fault is it to restrain the ruler?' To restrain the ruler means to love the ruler."

金剛般若波羅蜜經 — 能淨業障分第十六

「復次，須菩提！善男子、善女人，受持讀誦此經，若為人輕賤，是人先世罪業，應墮惡道，以今世人輕賤故，先世罪業則為消滅，當得阿耨多羅三藐三菩提。」「須菩提！我念過去無量阿僧祇劫，於燃燈佛前，得值八百四千萬億那由他諸佛，悉皆供養承事，無空過者；若復有人，於後末世，能受持讀誦此經，所得功德，於我所供養諸佛功德，百分不及一，千萬億分、乃至算數譬喻所不能及。」「須菩提！若善男子，善女人，於後末世，有受持讀誦此經，所得功德，我若具說者，或有人聞，心則狂亂，狐疑不信。須菩提！當知是經義不可思議，果報亦不可思議！」

Furthermore, Subhuti, if a good man or good woman receives, upholds, reads, and recites this sutra, yet is scorned and looked down upon by others, know that this person's karmic offenses from previous lives, which would have led to rebirth in evil realms, are being dissolved through the contempt suffered in this present life, and that person will attain anuttara-samyak-sambodhi. Subhuti, I recall that in the immeasurable asamkhyeya kalpas of the past, before the time of Dipankara Buddha, I encountered and made offerings to eighty-four billion nayutas of buddhas, serving each one without exception. Yet if someone in the final age can receive, uphold, read, and recite this sutra, the merit obtained will be a hundred times — a thousand, ten thousand, a billion times — greater than the merit of my offerings to all those buddhas, so great that no number, no calculation, no analogy can approach it. Subhuti, if I were to fully describe the merit that such a good man or good woman in the final age would obtain by receiving, upholding, reading, and reciting this sutra, there are those who, upon hearing it, would become bewildered, filled with doubt and disbelief. Subhuti, know that the meaning of this sutra is inconceivable, and its fruition is equally inconceivable!
