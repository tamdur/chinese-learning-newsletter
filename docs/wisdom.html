般若波羅蜜多心經

觀自在菩薩，行深般若波羅蜜多時，照見五蘊皆空，度一切苦厄。

Avalokitesvara Bodhisattva, when practicing the profound Prajna Paramita, illuminated the five aggregates and saw that they are all empty, thus overcoming all suffering and distress.

孟子・離婁上

孟子曰：「居下位而不獲於上，民不可得而治也。獲於上有道：不信於友，弗獲於上矣；信於友有道：事親弗悅，弗信於友矣；悅親有道：反身不誠，不悅於親矣；誠身有道：不明乎善，不誠其身矣。是故誠者，天之道也；思誠者，人之道也。至誠而不動者，未之有也；不誠，未有能動者也。」

Mencius said: "If you occupy a low position and do not win the confidence of your superiors, the people cannot be governed. There is a way to win the confidence of your superiors: if your friends do not trust you, you will not win the confidence of your superiors. There is a way to win the trust of your friends: if serving your parents does not please them, you will not win the trust of your friends. There is a way to please your parents: if you reflect on yourself and find a lack of sincerity, you will not please your parents. There is a way to become sincere: if you do not understand what is good, you cannot make yourself sincere. Therefore, sincerity is the Way of Heaven; striving for sincerity is the Way of human beings. There has never been one who was perfectly sincere and yet failed to move others; without sincerity, no one has ever been able to move others."

六祖大師法寶壇經・機緣品第七

一日參禮，師問曰：「汝從何來？欲求何事？」曰：「學人近往洪州白峯山禮大通和尚，蒙示見性成佛之義。未決狐疑，遠來投禮，伏望和尚慈悲指示。」師曰：「彼有何言句？汝試舉看。」曰：「智常到彼，凡經三月，未蒙示誨。為法切故，一夕獨入丈室，請問：『如何是某甲本心本性？』大通乃曰：『汝見虛空否？』對曰：『見。

One day he came to visit the Master, who asked: 'Where do you come from, and what do you seek?' He answered: 'Recently I went to Mount Baifeng in Hongzhou and paid respects to Master Datong, who kindly showed me the meaning of seeing one's nature and becoming a Buddha. But my doubts remain unresolved, so I have come from afar to pay my respects, humbly hoping the Master will compassionately instruct me.' The Master said: 'What did he say to you? Try to recount it for me.' Zhichang said: 'When I arrived there, after a full three months I had not yet received any instruction. Being earnest in seeking the Dharma, one evening I went alone to his chamber and asked: "What is my original mind, my original nature?" Master Datong then asked: "Do you see empty space?" I replied: "Yes, I see it."'
