孟子・告子下

曰：「先生之志則大矣，先生之號則不可。先生以利說秦楚之王，秦楚之王悅於利，以罷三軍之師，是三軍之士樂罷而悅於利也。為人臣者懷利以事其君，為人子者懷利以事其父，為人弟者懷利以事其兄。是君臣、父子、兄弟終去仁義，懷利以相接，然而不亡者，未之有也。先生以仁義說秦楚之王，秦楚之王悅於仁義，而罷三軍之師，是三軍之士樂罷而悅於仁義也。為人臣者懷仁義以事其君，為人子者懷仁義以事其父，為人弟者懷仁義以事其兄，是君臣、父子、兄弟去利，懷仁義以相接也。然而不王者，未之有也。何必曰利？」

{'translation_html': "

Mencius said: 'Your intention is grand, sir, but your banner is wrong. If you persuade the kings of Qin and Chu through profit, and the kings halt their armies because they are pleased by profit, then the soldiers of those three armies will delight in the ceasefire because they too are pleased by profit. Ministers will serve their lords with profit in mind, sons will serve their fathers with profit in mind, younger brothers will serve their elder brothers with profit in mind. Ultimately, rulers and ministers, fathers and sons, brothers—all will abandon benevolence and righteousness, dealing with each other purely through calculations of profit. No state that does this has ever escaped ruin. But if you persuade the kings of Qin and Chu through benevolence and righteousness, and the kings halt their armies because they are pleased by benevolence and righteousness, then the soldiers will delight in the ceasefire because they too are pleased by benevolence and righteousness. Ministers will serve their lords with benevolence and righteousness in mind, sons will serve their fathers so, brothers will serve each other so. Rulers and ministers, fathers and sons, brothers—all will set aside profit and deal with each other through benevolence and righteousness. No one who does this has ever failed to become a true king. Why must you speak of profit?'

金剛般若波羅蜜經・法身非相分第二十六

「須菩提！於意云何？可以三十二相觀如來不？」須菩提言：「如是！如是！以三十二相觀如來。」佛言：「須菩提！若以三十二相觀如來者，轉輪聖王則是如來。」須菩提白佛言：「世尊！如我解佛所說義。不應以三十二相觀如來。」爾時，世尊而說偈言：「若以色見我，以音聲求我，是人行邪道，不能見如來。」

"Subhuti, what do you think? Can the Tathagata be perceived through the thirty-two marks?" Subhuti said: "Yes! Yes! The Tathagata can be perceived through the thirty-two marks." The Buddha said: "Subhuti, if the Tathagata could be perceived through the thirty-two marks, then a wheel-turning sage king would be a Tathagata." Subhuti addressed the Buddha: "World-Honored One! As I understand the meaning of what the Buddha has said, one should not perceive the Tathagata through the thirty-two marks." At that time, the World-Honored One spoke this verse: "If one seeks me through form, or seeks me through sound, this person walks a misguided path and cannot see the Tathagata."
