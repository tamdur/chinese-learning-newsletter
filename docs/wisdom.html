般若波羅蜜多心經

故知般若波羅蜜多，是大神咒，是大明咒，是無上咒，是無等等咒，能除一切苦，真實不虛。故說般若波羅蜜多咒，即說咒曰：揭諦揭諦，波羅揭諦，波羅僧揭諦，菩提薩婆訶。

Therefore, know that Prajna Paramita is the great transcendent mantra, the great bright mantra, the supreme mantra, the unequalled mantra, which can remove all suffering and is true, not false. Therefore he proclaimed the Prajna Paramita mantra, saying: Gate gate paragate parasamgate bodhi svaha.

孟子

曰：「吾明告子。天子之地方千里；不千里，不足以待諸侯。諸侯之地方百里；不百里，不足以守宗廟之典籍。周公之封於魯，為方百里也；地非不足，而儉於百里。太公之封於齊也，亦為方百里也；地非不足也，而儉於百里。今魯方百里者五，子以為有王者作，則魯在所損乎？在所益乎？徒取諸彼以與此，然且仁者不為，況於殺人以求之乎？君子之事君也，務引其君以當道，志於仁而已。」

Mencius said: 'Let me explain it plainly. The territory of the Son of Heaven is a thousand li square; without a thousand li, it would not be enough to receive the feudal lords. The territory of a feudal lord is a hundred li square; without a hundred li, it would not be enough to maintain the ancestral temples and their records. When the Duke of Zhou was enfeoffed in Lu, his territory was a hundred li square. The land was not insufficient, yet it was kept to only a hundred li. When Taigong was enfeoffed in Qi, his territory was also a hundred li square. The land was not insufficient, yet it was kept to only a hundred li. Now Lu has five times a hundred li. Do you think that if a true king arose, Lu would gain territory or lose it? Even taking from others to give to the deserving, a benevolent man would not do—how much less would he do so by killing people to get it? The way a gentleman serves his ruler is to guide him onto the proper path and set his will on benevolence, nothing more.'

六祖壇經

時，有一客買柴，使令送至客店；客收去，惠能得錢，却出門外，見一客誦經。惠能一聞經語，心即開悟，遂問：『客誦何經？』客曰：『《金剛經》。』復問：『從何所來，持此經典？』客云：『我從蘄州黃梅縣東禪寺來。其寺是五祖忍大師在彼主化，門人一千有餘；我到彼中禮拜，聽受此經。大師常勸僧俗，但持《金剛經》，即自見性，直了成佛。』惠能聞說，宿昔有緣，乃蒙一客，取銀十兩與惠能，令充老母衣糧，教便往黃梅參禮五祖。

At that time, a customer bought firewood and had me deliver it to his lodging. After the customer took the firewood and I received my payment, I went out the door and encountered another man reciting a sutra. The moment I heard the words of the sutra, my mind was opened to awakening. I asked, 'What sutra are you reciting?' The man said, 'The Diamond Sutra.' I asked further, 'Where have you come from, carrying this scripture?' He replied, 'I come from Dongchan Monastery in Huangmei County, Qizhou. The Fifth Patriarch, Great Master Hongren, presides there, with over a thousand disciples. I went there to pay my respects and received this sutra. The Master constantly urges both monks and laypeople to simply uphold the Diamond Sutra, and they will see their own nature and directly realize Buddhahood.' Hearing this, I felt a karmic connection from past lives. Then a patron gave me ten taels of silver for my mother's clothing and provisions, and told me to go to Huangmei to pay respects to the Fifth Patriarch.
