般若波羅蜜多心經

故知般若波羅蜜多，是大神咒，是大明咒，是無上咒，是無等等咒，能除一切苦，真實不虛。故說般若波羅蜜多咒，即說咒曰：揭諦揭諦，波羅揭諦，波羅僧揭諦，菩提薩婆訶。

Therefore, know that Prajna Paramita is the great transcendent mantra, the great bright mantra, the supreme mantra, the unequalled mantra, which can remove all suffering and is true, not false. Therefore he proclaimed the Prajna Paramita mantra, saying: Gate gate paragate parasamgate bodhi svaha.

孟子 告子下

任人有問屋廬子曰：「禮與食孰重？」曰：「禮重。」「色與禮孰重？」曰：「禮重。」曰：「以禮食，則飢而死；不以禮食，則得食，必以禮乎？親迎，則不得妻；不親迎，則得妻，必親迎乎！」

A man from Ren asked Wulu Zi: 'Which is more important, ritual propriety or food?' Wulu Zi replied: 'Ritual propriety is more important.' 'Which is more important, sex or ritual propriety?' 'Ritual propriety is more important.' The man pressed: 'If following ritual propriety means starving to death, but ignoring it means getting food, must one still insist on propriety? If performing the formal wedding ceremony means not getting a wife, but skipping it means getting one, must one still insist on the ceremony?'

金剛經 離色離相分第二十

「須菩提！於意云何？佛可以具足色身見不？」「不也，世尊！如來不應以具足色身見。何以故？如來說：具足色身，即非具足色身，是名具足色身。」「須菩提！於意云何？如來可以具足諸相見不？」「不也，世尊！如來不應以具足諸相見。何以故？如來說：諸相具足，即非具足。是名諸相具足。」

"Subhuti, what do you think? Can the Buddha be seen through his complete physical form?" "No, World-Honored One! The Tathagata should not be seen through his complete physical form. Why? Because what the Tathagata calls the complete physical form is not the complete physical form — it is merely called the complete physical form." "Subhuti, what do you think? Can the Tathagata be seen through the perfection of all marks?" "No, World-Honored One! The Tathagata should not be seen through the perfection of all marks. Why? Because what the Tathagata calls the perfection of all marks is not truly perfection — it is merely called the perfection of all marks."
