般若波羅蜜多心經

無苦集滅道，無智亦無得。以無所得故，菩提薩埵，依般若波羅蜜多故，心無罣礙。

There is no suffering, no cause of suffering, no cessation, no path; no wisdom and no attainment. With nothing to attain, a bodhisattva relies on Prajna Paramita, and the mind is without hindrance.

孟子 · 離婁下

孟子曰：「西子蒙不潔，則人皆掩鼻而過之。雖有惡人，齊戒沐浴，則可以祀上帝。」孟子曰：「天下之言性也，則故而已矣。故者以利為本。所惡於智者，為其鑿也。如智者若禹之行水也，則無惡於智矣。禹之行水也，行其所無事也。如智者亦行其所無事，則智亦大矣。天之高也，星辰之遠也，苟求其故，千歲之日至，可坐而致也。」

Mencius said: "If Xi Shi were smeared with filth, people would all hold their noses passing by. Yet even an ugly person, if he fasts and bathes ritually, may serve in sacrifice to the Lord on High." Mencius said: "When people in the world discuss human nature, they rely on precedent, and precedent takes natural tendency as its basis. What is objectionable in cleverness is its forced contrivance. If the clever were to act as Yu did in channeling water, there would be nothing objectionable in cleverness. Yu channeled water by guiding it along its natural course. If the clever likewise followed the natural course, their wisdom would be great indeed. The heavens are high and the stars are distant, yet if one traces their patterns, the solstices for a thousand years can be calculated while sitting at one's desk."

One day, Shenxiu instructed his disciple Zhicheng: 'You are clever and perceptive. Go to Caoxi on my behalf and listen to the teaching. If you hear anything, remember it thoroughly and relate it to me.' Zhicheng followed these instructions and went to Caoxi, joining the assembly without revealing where he had come from. At that time the Patriarch told the assembly: 'There is someone here today who has come to steal the Dharma, hiding in this gathering.' Zhicheng immediately came forward, bowed, and explained the whole matter. The Master said: 'You come from Yuquan — you must be a spy.' Zhicheng replied: 'No, I am not.' The Master said: 'How can you say you are not?' Zhicheng replied: 'Before I spoke, I was. Now that I have spoken, I am not.' The Master asked: 'How does your teacher instruct the assembly?' Zhicheng replied: 'He constantly teaches the great assembly to fix the mind and contemplate stillness, to sit for long periods without lying down.'
