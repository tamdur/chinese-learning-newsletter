般若波羅蜜多心經 — Heart Sutra — Friday

無苦集滅道，無智亦無得。以無所得故，菩提薩埵，依般若波羅蜜多故，心無罣礙。

There is no suffering, no cause of suffering, no cessation, no path; no wisdom and no attainment. With nothing to attain, a bodhisattva relies on Prajna Paramita, and the mind is without hindrance.

孟子 — 公孫丑上

孟子曰：「尊賢使能，俊傑在位，則天下之士皆悅而願立於其朝矣。市廛而不征，法而不廛，則天下之商皆悅而願藏於其市矣。關譏而不征，則天下之旅皆悅而願出於其路矣。耕者助而不稅，則天下之農皆悅而願耕於其野矣。廛無夫里之布，則天下之民皆悅而願為之氓矣。信能行此五者，則鄰國之民仰之若父母矣。率其子弟，攻其父母，自生民以來，未有能濟者也。如此，則無敵於天下。無敵於天下者，天吏也。然而不王者，未之有也。」

Mencius said: "If you honor the worthy and employ the capable, placing outstanding men in office, then all the scholars in the world will be delighted and will wish to serve at your court. If in the marketplace you charge rent on stalls but do not tax the goods, or enforce regulations but do not charge rent, then all the merchants in the world will be delighted and will wish to store their goods in your markets. If at the border passes you inspect travelers but do not tax them, then all the travelers in the world will be delighted and will wish to travel your roads. If farmers are asked only for communal labor and not taxed on their harvest, then all the farmers in the world will be delighted and will wish to till your fields. If there are no additional levies on households, then all the people in the world will be delighted and will wish to become your subjects. If you can truly carry out these five things, then the people of neighboring states will look up to you as a parent. To lead their own children to attack their parent—since the beginning of humankind, no one has ever succeeded at this. In this way, you will be without rival in the world. One who is without rival in the world is an agent of Heaven. Having all this yet failing to become a true king—that has never happened."

」神秀上座，於南廊壁上，書無相偈，大師令人皆誦，依此偈修，免墮惡道；依此偈修，有大利益。』惠能曰：『上人！我此踏碓，八箇餘月，未曾行到堂前。望上人引至偈前禮拜。』童子引至偈前禮拜，惠能曰：『惠能不識字，請上人為讀。』時，有江州別駕，姓張名日用，便高聲讀。惠能聞已，遂言：『亦有一偈，望別駕為書。』別駕言：『汝亦作偈？其事希有。』惠能向別駕言：『欲學無上菩提，不得輕於初學。

Senior monk Shenxiu wrote this formless verse on the south corridor wall. The Great Master ordered everyone to recite it, saying that those who practice according to this verse will avoid evil destinies, and those who practice according to it will gain great benefit.' Huineng said, 'Sir, I have been pounding rice here for over eight months and have never once gone to the front hall. I beg you to lead me to the verse so I may pay my respects.' The boy led him to the verse, where Huineng bowed. Huineng said, 'I cannot read. Would you please read it for me?' At that time, there was a vice-prefect from Jiangzhou, surnamed Zhang, given name Riyong, who read it aloud. After hearing it, Huineng said, 'I also have a verse. Would the vice-prefect write it down for me?' The vice-prefect said, 'You — composing a verse? That would be extraordinary indeed!' Huineng said to the vice-prefect, 'If you wish to attain supreme bodhi, do not look down on a beginner.
