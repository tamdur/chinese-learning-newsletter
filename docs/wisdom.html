般若波羅蜜多心經 — 星期二

舍利子，色不異空，空不異色，色即是空，空即是色，受想行識，亦復如是。

Shariputra, form is not different from emptiness, emptiness is not different from form. Form is emptiness, emptiness is form. The same is true of feelings, perceptions, mental formations, and consciousness.

孟子 · 梁惠王上 · 第12段

曰：「王無異於百姓之以王為愛也。以小易大，彼惡知之？王若隱其無罪而就死地，則牛羊何擇焉？」王笑曰：「是誠何心哉？我非愛其財。而易之以羊也，宜乎百姓之謂我愛也。」曰：「無傷也，是乃仁術也，見牛未見羊也。君子之於禽獸也，見其生，不忍見其死；聞其聲，不忍食其肉。是以君子遠庖廚也。」

Mencius said, 'Your Majesty should not be surprised that the people thought you stingy. You replaced the large with the small — how could they understand? If Your Majesty was pained that an innocent animal was going to its death, then what was there to choose between the ox and the sheep?' The king laughed and said, 'What was my true motive, I wonder? I was not grudging the expense when I exchanged it for a sheep — but I suppose it was natural for the people to call me stingy.' Mencius said, 'There is no harm in it. This was in fact the method of benevolence: you had seen the ox but had not seen the sheep. The gentleman, in his relation to animals — having seen them alive, he cannot bear to see them die; having heard their cries, he cannot bear to eat their flesh. That is why the gentleman keeps his distance from the kitchen.'

金剛般若波羅蜜經 · 第12段

「須菩提！於意云何？若人滿三千大千世界七寶以用布施，是人所得福德，寧為多不？」須菩提言：「甚多，世尊！何以故？是福德即非福德性，是故如來說福德多。」「若復有人，於此經中受持，乃至四句偈等，為他人說，其福勝彼。何以故？須菩提！一切諸佛，及諸佛阿耨多羅三藐三菩提法，皆從此經出。須菩提！所謂佛法者，即非佛法，是名佛法。」

"Subhuti, what do you think? If someone filled the three-thousand-fold great-thousand world with the seven precious treasures and used them for giving, would the merit gained by this person be great?" Subhuti said: "Very great, World-Honored One. Why? This merit is not the nature of merit itself; therefore the Tathagata says the merit is great." "If, on the other hand, someone receives and upholds even as few as four lines of verse from this sutra and explains them to others, that person's merit surpasses the former. Why? Subhuti, all Buddhas and all the Dharma of anuttara-samyak-sambodhi of all Buddhas come forth from this sutra. Subhuti, what is called the Buddha-Dharma is not the Buddha-Dharma; it is called the Buddha-Dharma."
