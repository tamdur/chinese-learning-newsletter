般若波羅蜜多心經 — Heart Sutra — Sunday

故知般若波羅蜜多，是大神咒，是大明咒，是無上咒，是無等等咒，能除一切苦，真實不虛。故說般若波羅蜜多咒，即說咒曰：揭諦揭諦，波羅揭諦，波羅僧揭諦，菩提薩婆訶。

Therefore, know that Prajna Paramita is the great transcendent mantra, the great bright mantra, the supreme mantra, the unequalled mantra, which can remove all suffering and is true, not false. Therefore he proclaimed the Prajna Paramita mantra, saying: Gate gate paragate parasamgate bodhi svaha.

孟子 — 梁惠王下 — Mencius

孟子對曰：「昔者大王居邠，狄人侵之。事之以皮幣，不得免焉；事之以犬馬，不得免焉；事之以珠玉，不得免焉。乃屬其耆老而告之曰：『狄人之所欲者，吾土地也。吾聞之也：君子不以其所以養人者害人。二三子何患乎無君？我將去之。』去邠，踰梁山，邑于岐山之下居焉。邠人曰：『仁人也，不可失也。』從之者如歸市。或曰：『世守也，非身之所能為也。效死勿去。』君請擇於斯二者。」

Mencius replied, "In ancient times, King Tai lived in Bin, and the Di tribes invaded. He tried to buy them off with hides and silks, but could not avoid their demands. He tried to buy them off with dogs and horses, but could not avoid their demands. He tried to buy them off with pearls and jade, but could not avoid their demands. Then he gathered the elders and announced, 'What the Di people want is our land. I have heard it said: a gentleman does not use that which nourishes people to harm people. My friends, why should you worry about having no ruler? I shall leave.' He left Bin, crossed Mount Liang, and built a settlement at the foot of Mount Qi. The people of Bin said, 'He is a benevolent man. We must not lose him.' Those who followed him were like people flocking to market. Others said, 'This land has been guarded by our family for generations—it is not ours alone to abandon. We should defend it to the death.' Your Lordship, please choose between these two courses."

金剛般若波羅蜜經 — 化無所化分第二十五 — Diamond Sutra

「須菩提！於意云何？汝等勿謂如來作是念：『我當度眾生。』須菩提！莫作是念。何以故？實無有眾生如來度者。若有眾生如來度者，如來則有我、人、眾生、壽者。須菩提！如來說：『有我者，則非有我，而凡夫之人以為有我。』須菩提！凡夫者，如來說則非凡夫，是名凡夫。」

"Subhuti, what do you think? You should not say that the Tathagata has this thought: 'I shall deliver sentient beings.' Subhuti, do not think this way. Why? There are in truth no sentient beings for the Tathagata to deliver. If there were sentient beings for the Tathagata to deliver, then the Tathagata would have a self, a person, a being, and one who lives a lifespan. Subhuti, when the Tathagata speaks of 'having a self,' it is not truly having a self, yet ordinary people take it as having a self. Subhuti, as for ordinary people — the Tathagata says they are not ordinary people; they are merely called ordinary people."
